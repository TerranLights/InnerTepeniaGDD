## Session 1 — 2026-08-31, during Phase 1 (Constraint & Capability)

**Deficit/question being researched against:** Phase 1's G2/G4 generators established that Mountain Pass
sits at extreme altitude and cold, was chosen specifically for geological stability over any human-comfort
factor, and is a genuine bi-national (two-city) joint venture — no city-specific real-world basis exists
for it, unlike Vostok (Vostok Station, Russia) and Kunlun (Kunlun Station, China), which already have
their own dedicated real-world anchors used elsewhere. Needed a real-world comparable that is *itself* a
bi-national joint venture at extreme altitude, to avoid re-using either founding city's own basis and to
find concrete texture the founding-condition paragraph alone doesn't supply.

**⚠ Deliberate real-world/in-fiction naming collision, flagged so it is never mistaken for an error:**
the real-world research station used as this outpost's comparable is itself named "Concordia Station,"
which is coincidentally identical to Tepenia's own in-fiction capital city. **These are unrelated.**
Every reference to the real station below is written as "the real-world Concordia Station (Dome C)" in
full, and this pass never treats the coincidence as meaningful in-fiction. Recorded here explicitly so a
future reader does not assume a deliberate connection that was never intended.

**What was withheld versus omitted:** nothing withheld deliberately. One detail **omitted** as not
transferable: the real Concordia Station's specific scientific research programs (astronomy, glaciology,
human biology) are its actual mission, which does not match Mountain Pass's own established mission
(chamber manufacturing). The comparable was used for its *structural* facts (bi-national governance,
seasonal crew swing, altitude/humidity physiology, isolation psychology, the midwinter tradition) — never
for its research-station content, which would be an uncharacteristic import per `Cultural_Synthesis_
Techniques.md` §0.

**Divergences from source, stated explicitly per the source-not-specification rule:** Mountain Pass is
smaller in every dimension than the real station (no summer crew of dozens is plausible for a component-
manufacturing site the size implied by canon); it manufactures physical objects rather than conducting
open science; and it answers to two cities rather than two nations. The *shape* of the comparison (bi-
national, extreme-altitude, seasonally-swinging, psychologically demanding) transfers; the *scale* and
*mission* do not, and are not imported.
