# Phase 1 — Constraint & Capability (the spine)

**Asks:** What can this place do without trying, what can it not do at all, what must it keep paying, and
what does it permit but punish?

Five generators selected (`00_Frame_and_PreFlight.md`): **G2** physical/environmental, **G3** function/
purpose, **G4** founding condition, **G5** network position, **G8** demographic composition. Each run to a
full four-quadrant profile **separately, before comparing**, per Step 2A.

---

## A. Five separate profiles

### G2 — Physical & environmental constraint

East Antarctic plateau interior, on Hwy 37 between Kunlun (at/near Dome Argus, ~4,093 m — the ceiling of
sustained human habitation, per `Specs/Kunlun.md`) and Vostok (Earth's coldest naturally recorded surface
temperature, −89.2°C). The route itself, per `Highways.md`, never returns below ~3,200 m after its first
quarter and closes through the deep polar night.

- **What does this environment give freely?** Extraordinary atmospheric purity and stillness — extreme
  cold and altitude together produce very low humidity and minimal atmospheric turbulence. *(Real-world
  grounding: the actual Dome C site this pass researches against is, for the identical reason, considered
  Earth's best location for submillimeter astronomy — see research log.)*
- **What does it withhold absolutely?** Liquid water in any form (permanently frozen); a breathable oxygen
  margin without acclimatization (moderate-to-chronic hypobaric hypoxia at this altitude, per the
  real-world comparable's own documented physiology); any local food source; a way out once the route
  closes for the season.
- **What does it demand continuously?** Heat and pressure-adjusted life support; continuous snow/ice
  clearance for the airstrip; structural vigilance against cold-embrittlement; acclimatization discipline
  for anyone new to the altitude.
- **What does it permit but punish?** Going outside unprepared (severe, fast-onset harm at this
  cold/altitude combination); staying past the safe pre-closure changeover window (not forbidden by
  anyone — simply means being unable to leave until the route reopens); working the outdoor airstrip
  alone (no policy against it, no one enforces a buddy system, but a fall or equipment failure with no one
  nearby is materially worse here than almost anywhere else in Tepenia).

**Profile:** STRENGTH — atmospheric purity/stillness (freely given, unusual). DEFICIT — no self-
sufficiency in water, food, or egress. STANDING COST — continuous life-support and airstrip maintenance,
continuous acclimatization discipline. GRUDGING TOLERANCE — the changeover-timing trap (see §D below).

### G3 — Function & purpose

Split per `02` §2 G3: what the place is for on its own terms, versus what its parent(s) need from it.

- **On its own terms:** precision fabrication — building Cradle chambers to the tolerances the nationwide
  network depends on. The staff's own self-conception is almost certainly this: *we are the people who
  build the thing correctly.*
- **What Vostok and Kunlun actually need from it:** the manufacturing output matters, but so do two things
  the workforce itself may not foreground — (1) the airstrip keeps Hwy 37's only truly impassable interior
  stretch usable at all, a service to the whole corridor, not just to the two founding cities; (2) per
  canon's own framing, the outpost is "a concrete, physical expression of the already-established 'two
  loneliest outposts' relationship" — meaning part of what the two cities need from this place is
  something closer to companionship infrastructure than manufacturing infrastructure.
- **The divergence, and it is real:** the workforce experiences itself as a manufacturing operation. The
  founding cities experience it as (at minimum) three things at once — manufacturing, corridor
  infrastructure, and mutual-isolation relief — and only ever tell the workforce about the first one.

**Profile:** STRENGTH — high-precision fabrication capability; the corridor's only functioning waypoint.
DEFICIT — no civic apparatus or independent existence rationale outside the joint task; nobody here would
describe themselves as building a community. STANDING COST — precision-tolerance maintenance that cannot
lapse without a shipped chamber failing somewhere else in the country; year-round airstrip readiness
regardless of week-to-week demand. GRUDGING TOLERANCE — off-hours exist and are permitted, but "off-hours"
here has nowhere to go and nothing to be spent on that isn't still, in some sense, the same small building.

### G4 — Founding condition

Who, why, under what constraint, with what, and without what (`02` G4). Canon gives an unusually specific
answer for a non-city location: a joint Vostok-Kunlun venture, originally slated for a third site
(Fort McMurdo) that was **rejected specifically for its volcanic/seismic instability** (Mount Erebus's
active-lava-lake hazard, both from ashfall/lava risk and from ongoing seismic vibration — the harder of
the two to mitigate). Mountain Pass was chosen instead **because it is geologically calm**, with no
volcanic activity nearby.

- **With what:** the two cities' own scientists and engineers, and by implication their shared technical
  standards and equipment.
- **Without what — this is the yield.** Founded with **zero consideration for livability**. The site-
  selection criterion that actually decided the location was vibration-freedom for precision synthesis
  equipment, not habitability, climate tolerability, or anything to do with the people who would work
  there. The founders explicitly traded human comfort for manufacturing reliability, and did so
  *knowingly* — the Fort McMurdo rejection proves the decision process weighed physical-site properties at
  all, which means the livability question was considered and set aside, not simply never asked.

**Profile:** STRENGTH — chosen for the one property (geological stability) that makes the highest-value
work here actually reliable; this is a strength earned from an explicit trade-off, not an accident.
DEFICIT — the founding provision includes no comfort infrastructure, no long-term-settlement provision,
no civic apparatus, because none was ever the criterion. STANDING COST — everyone who works here
continuously pays the livability cost the founders decided, once, was worth it — a cost distributed onto
every subsequent worker rather than absorbed by anyone who made the choice. GRUDGING TOLERANCE — silent;
this generator speaks mainly to the other three quadrants.

### G5 — Network position

Sole interior stopping-place on Hwy 37's entire length; per `Highways.md`, the highway's **only**
hitchhiking-viable node. Positioned between Kunlun and Vostok, reasonably accessible to Dome Fuji and (via
the Hwy 22 dual-junction) Amundsen Station. Chambers ship outward via this same highway/airport network.

- **What connects here:** the corridor's only rest/resupply/emergency point, in either direction.
- **What it lies between:** two isolated cities that otherwise have no other stop between them across a
  plateau traverse that never drops below ~3,200 m.
- **What flows through, and direction/volume:** manufactured chambers ship outward (Mountain Pass → the
  rest of Tepenia); people and supplies move both directions between Vostok and Kunlun via this node;
  volume is plausibly low but non-zero, since it is the *only* viable stop, not merely a convenient one.
- **Asymmetry:** everything that crosses the interior of Hwy 37 depends on this place existing, whether or
  not the traveler ever interacts with its manufacturing function at all. It is a chokepoint of relational
  importance wildly disproportionate to its own tiny population.

**Profile:** STRENGTH — sole functioning waypoint on the harshest interior corridor in the country;
outsized relational importance for its size. DEFICIT — purely a through-point with no independent pull of
its own; nobody arrives here *choosing* it as a destination (see Phase 2's arrival-mode read). STANDING
COST — must remain passable and staffed reliably, because the corridor's usability as a whole depends on
this one node; a service performed for two cities and every traveler on the route, without anyone
necessarily framing it as a service. GRUDGING TOLERANCE — travelers are permitted to stop and wait here,
but the wait is punished by scarcity: per `Highways.md`, this is already "the hardest route in Tepenia to
hitchhike... without being formally closed to it," even in this pass's own active, pre-war frame.

### G8 — Demographic composition

Two founding populations, and they are not symmetric. **Kunlun: 0 humans / 123,449 robots (Census I)** —
entirely robot, a deliberately curated astronomy-and-comms-heritage population. **Vostok: 129,617 humans /
259,644 robots (Census I)**, mixed, USA/Japan Primary. A jointly-staffed outpost drawing proportionally
from both contributing cities inherits this asymmetry directly: any human staff can only ever be
Vostok-origin, while robot staff can come from either city.

**Profile:** STRENGTH — two different flavors of technical excellence in one small crew: Kunlun's curated
scientific/technical specialization meeting Vostok's broader industrial and survival-hardened population.
DEFICIT — humans at the outpost are structurally a minority drawn from a single source population, with no
possibility of "someone from my home city" solidarity across the human/robot line the way robot staff have
it; this is a real, checkable social asymmetry, not a generic diversity statement. STANDING COST — silent;
this generator does not independently populate this quadrant, and that silence is recorded rather than
forced. GRUDGING TOLERANCE — silent, for the same reason.

## C. Resolving the one real disagreement — and confirming the rest are agreement, not conflict

**No quadrant contains a genuine three-way conflict.** Every cell above is either agreement, independent
addition, or silence — an honest, if unusual, result: five generators run on a small, function-first
Installation converge unusually hard rather than fight. Per `02` §5.2, "all three agree" is used as the
spine directly, without needing the both-are-true test.

**The one place worth applying it anyway** is G8's DEFICIT cell against the general DEFICIT convergence.
G2/G3/G4/G5 all describe the deficit as *structural and impersonal* — the place lacks home-ness because of
what it is, not because of who is in it. G8's deficit is *social and specific* — a particular asymmetry
between two named populations. **Applying the both-are-true test:** these are not competing readings of
the same fact; they are the same underlying condition read at two different scales — G2–G5 describe the
deficit at the *structural* scale (the place itself has no home-making apparatus), and G8 describes its
*consequence* at the *social* scale (given that structural absence, the specific asymmetry between
Kunlun's all-robot and Vostok's mixed population determines exactly *whose* isolation is worse). Both
stand. This is folded into Phase 2 and Phase 9 rather than treated as a contradiction.

## D. Reading the shape

**All four quadrants are populated, from independent generators, with the STRENGTH row showing genuine
three-way agreement and the STANDING COST row showing the densest, most cross-generator-supported content
of any quadrant.** Per `02` §4, this reads as **Cost-dominant**: *is this place doing anything other than
continuing to exist?*

**Applying `02` §4.0 before trusting the reading** (mandatory: a shape is a property of the admitted input
set, never of the location alone). STANDING COST here fills from **G2 (physical), G3 (functional), G4
(founding-condition reasoning), and G5 (network)** — four independent generator classes, not from a single
retrofit of known institutional facts. This passes §4.0's own check: the cost is genuinely imposed by the
place's own physical and founding conditions, not merely voluntary or artifact-of-admission. **Cost-
dominant is the correct read, not a default.**

**No sibling exists to check for a repeated shape** (per `01` §5.3a, substitutes used — see Frame
Declaration). The nearest analogous location at a different scale, Byrd's Chamber Works, is embedded
inside a full city and does not carry a comparable cost-dominant profile of its own — Byrd's Division of
Industry is diversified, and its Chamber Works is one sector among several, not the totality of the
location's existence the way it is here. No collision to differentiate against; recorded rather than
assumed.

**The matching question, answered:** *is this place doing anything other than continuing to exist?* —
**Barely, and everyone here knows it in a specific, quiet way.** The manufacturing output is real and
matters nationally, but the effort required simply to keep four to eight people (closure season) or
fifteen to twenty-five (open season) alive, acclimatized, and working at this altitude and cold **is
itself most of what the place does, day to day** — the chamber output is what the effort is *for*, but the
effort's actual content, hour to hour, is survival-and-tolerance-maintenance, not manufacturing in any
hands-on sense for most of the crew. This is Phase 4's own finding waiting to happen, seeded here.

## E. Deficit addresses, counted

The single converged deficit ("this place has no home-ness, only function") is addressed at:

> **In the parent(s).** *(`02` §4.1.)* The remedy — civic life, comfort, community, a place people are
> actually *from* — exists in full, close by, and is not abstract: it is Vostok and Kunlun themselves, the
> outpost's own two founding cities, both a highway-trip away in either direction. **This sharpens toward
> "in a neighbor's present"** (the newer address type, `02` §4.1): the comparison population never
> departed and is not a departed witness or an absent authority — Vostok's and Kunlun's own residents are
> living, ordinary, physically proximate people who have exactly what this place's own workers lack,
> including, for many of the workers, their own homes and families at one end of the road or the other.
> **The count is exactly one address**, which per `02` §4.1's "a count above one should raise suspicion"
> rule is itself worth noting as unusually clean — there is no ambiguity about where the remedy lives; it
> is precisely legible, which makes its absence here sharper, not softer. The workers are not looking for
> an answer they cannot locate. They know exactly where it is. They are simply not there.

**G8's sharper sub-deficit** (humans structurally single-sourced) has the same address, refined: for a
human worker, "the parent" specifically means Vostok, since Kunlun offers no human community to draw on at
all. A Kunlun-origin robot worker has *two* possible homes to feel homesick for; a Vostok-origin human
worker has exactly one. **This is recorded as a genuine, checkable asymmetry** in how sharply the same
deficit-address bites depending on which founding city a given worker is from.

## F. Research, aimed at what Step 2 named — see `MountainPassAirport_Research_Log.md`, Session 1

Per Step 3.1, research targets the deficit the profile actually named: a place with genuine geological/
environmental grounds for existing but explicitly no grounds for being lived in as a home, at extreme
altitude, jointly run by two separate authorities. **The real-world Concordia Station (Dome C, French-
Italian joint venture)** was researched as the comparable — chosen specifically because it is *itself* a
genuine bi-national joint venture at comparable extreme altitude, avoiding re-use of either Vostok's or
Kunlun's own already-assigned real-world basis (Vostok Station and Kunlun Station respectively).

**What the research changed, not merely ornamented (Gate 7):**
1. **The population/status model** — from a flat, unsourced 8–20 guess to a genuine seasonal-swing model
   (closure-season skeleton crew ~4–8; open-season crew ~15–25), grounded in the real station's documented
   13–16 (winter) vs. up to 70 (summer) swing, scaled down to match this location's much smaller implied
   size. Already folded back into `00_Frame_and_PreFlight.md`.
2. **The changeover-timing trap's real physiological stakes** — the real station's documented chronic
   hypobaric hypoxia at comparable altitude, and its own recorded psychological effects (claustrophobia,
   altered time perception, interpersonal friction over a 9-month, 4-month-dark winterover) ground G2's
   STANDING COST and GRUDGING TOLERANCE quadrants in real physiology rather than assumed hardship.
3. **A genuine Retroactive Mechanism finding** (`Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` technique 5): the real
   station's documented extreme low humidity **both** threatens digital-record static discharge and fire
   risk **and** is exactly what makes the site valuable for its actual work (submillimeter astronomy,
   really; precision fabrication-synthesis tolerance, here). **One physical property, two opposite
   effects on two different objects** — the both-are-true pattern (`02` §5.3), generated by research
   rather than by an internal contradiction. Carried into Phase 3.
4. **A real, sourced observance for Phase 6E's required small/unserious category** — the real station's
   own Midwinter Day tradition (Scott's 1902 faux-Christmas, marking the isolation period's midpoint) is
   fused, not copied, into a Mountain Pass equivalent in Phase 6.

## G. The Unrecognized Instrument — run last, per `02` §4.2

**Named deficit:** no home-making apparatus; no civic/comfort infrastructure of any kind.

**Sweep of the outpost's own institutions, trades, and processes for anywhere this faculty is already
being exercised, unrecognized:**

The chamber-manufacturing process itself, per canon, requires **precision tolerance maintained continuously
across a long, unmonitored shipping journey** — the pass's own Tier-3 interrogation of the Calethina-
chamber discoverable detail (see `00_Frame_and_PreFlight.md`) established that a chamber made here has to
survive a months-long, unaccompanied transcontinental trip intact. **That is, structurally, a caretaking
discipline** — checking, wrapping, cushioning, and verifying something fragile and irreplaceable so it
arrives whole at a destination the makers will never see, made by people who will also never meet whoever
receives it. **The outpost already has, in exactly one process, the emotional and procedural shape of
sending someone you care about somewhere better than here — and does not recognize it as anything but
quality control.**

**Why it did not spread:** nobody classified the shipping-preparation ritual as an instance of *caring for
something you cannot follow*, because it was never framed as anything but an engineering procedure. The
faculty the place lacks (home-making, sending care outward) is being exercised daily, on cargo, by people
who would be startled to hear it described that way.

**What this converts:** the deficit from tragic to addressable, without importing anything foreign to the
place — per `02` §4.2's own governing logic. The change a player could cause here is not to give the
outpost a home it structurally cannot have, but **to help someone notice that the outpost already knows
how to say goodbye carefully to something it made** — which is closer than the profile alone would suggest
to knowing how to do the same for a person. Carried forward as seed material for Phase 6 and Phase 9.

## Standing reminders honored in this phase

- Filled STANDING COST from physical/functional/founding constraint (G2/G3/G4), never from function alone
  (`02` §3.1) — G3's own quadrant is kept distinct from its STRENGTH entry precisely to avoid this
  conflation.
- No prohibitions written into this frame — Phase 7 owns those.
- The profile is written as strengths/deficits with **one consequence each**, not as a diagnosis; where a
  consequence risked reading like another location's (isolation, hardship), it was pushed to the specific
  mechanism (the changeover trap, the shipping-caretaking Unrecognized Instrument) that could not transfer
  unchanged to Highway 37 or Cape Adare.
