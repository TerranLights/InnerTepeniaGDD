# Phase 7 — Order

**Optional for Corridor**, per `03` §0.1. Run in full anyway, since real material exists for most of its four
components.

---

## 7a — Work and economy

**Division of labor, already established:** convoy crews (transit-only) and Mountain Pass Airport's staff
(Band 1 resident-on-rotation), per Phases 2 and 4. **What it makes:** fabrication-synthesis chambers, shipped
outward. **The non-thematic export**, required per `03` Phase 7a: not the chambers themselves (thematic — the
outpost's headline function), but **informal personal correspondence and small trade goods carried between
Vostok and Kunlun by whatever convoy happens to be running** — care packages, letters, small requested items
between two isolated research communities with no other regular physical contact. **Ordinary, emotionally
neutral, unrelated to the fabrication headline, and it proves the corridor is a real channel between two living
communities, not merely a supply line for one product.**

## 7b — Governance and decision

**What is unadministrable, per the phase's own most productive question:** per the diffuse-parent finding
(Phase 5 §5c) and the still-open maintenance-authority REQUESTED item (pre-flight), **nobody has clear authority
to formally declare Highway 37 open or closed.** The seasonal-closure finding (Phase 3 §E) is a physical
reality; **the decision of when exactly to stop or resume running it is not, per any canon found, vested in any
named office.** This produces exactly the "edge case nobody wrote" the phase asks for: **each closure and
reopening is effectively decided informally, crossing by crossing, by whichever crew or city is about to send
one** — not a policy failure, simply a genuine administrative gap nobody has ever needed to close, since no
single crisis has forced the question yet within this pass's frame.

## 7c — Knowledge and transmission

**How someone learns:** per Phase 5 §5d's membership-conversion finding, a new convoy driver or outpost
technician's first rotation is paired with someone experienced — an apprenticeship structure implied directly
by the "full rotation, including the worst stretch" membership mechanism, since surviving a first rotation
well requires someone else's guidance through it. **What becomes of the ones who cannot learn it:** per the
project's standing non-malice discipline, **not punished — simply reassigned.** Someone who cannot manage the
altitude, the isolation, or the procedural discipline this route demands is rotated to a posting elsewhere; this
is an ordinary personnel outcome, not a shameful one, and this pass does not invent drama where none is implied.

## 7d — Private life, minorities, and counterculture

**Check canon for an existing counterculture first:** none exists (expected — no culture-pass conclusions exist
for this location at all, per the inbound readiness check).

**Derive from the specific pressure, per the phase's own instruction — never from generic rebellion.** What this
place requires of everyone: continuous procedural discipline under altitude and cold that punishes even small
lapses (Phase 1's grudging-tolerance quadrant). **Who cannot or will not give it, fully?** Per the sanction-
proportionality check (`00d`), and given this population's own tiny scale (Band 0-1): **the honest answer is
that any dissent here is individual, not organizational.** A convoy driver or outpost technician who quietly
cuts a corner — skipping a precaution, trusting personal tolerance over protocol — is not building a movement;
there is nobody to organize with, and the population is too small and too rotation-clocked for a stable
counterculture faction to ever form. **Per Phase 7d's own explicit instruction to check whether the
contradiction is a population at all: it is not.** This location **cannot be reformed from within and cannot be
opposed from within either, because there is nobody to organize** — a genuine, characterizing finding rather
than a gap, and a sharper one than inventing a faction this population's own scale cannot support.

**Sanction proportionality, checked directly:** what would this corridor's own conditions do to someone caught
cutting a serious corner? Given the physical stakes established in Phase 1 (unacclimatized travel, off-route
deviation both genuinely lethal), **the "sanction" that actually applies is not administrative — it is
physical, and it is the environment's own, not a policy anyone imposed.** No invented punitive institution is
needed or appropriate here; the road itself already enforces the discipline it demands, which is a stronger,
more honest finding than manufacturing a disciplinary body for a population this small.

## Differentiation axis

**Named axis: "enforcement without an enforcer."** Every governance-shaped finding in this phase (7b's
unadministrable open/closed decision, 7d's sanction-proportionality finding) traces to the same structure: the
corridor's own physical conditions do the disciplining work a named office or institution would do elsewhere.
**This distinguishes Highway 37 from a location whose order is either genuinely absent (lawless) or genuinely
administered (a real governing body)** — it is neither; the discipline is real and consistently applied, but
its author is the environment, not an institution.
