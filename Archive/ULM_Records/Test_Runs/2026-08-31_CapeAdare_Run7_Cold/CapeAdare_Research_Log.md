**Deficit/question being researched against:** Phase 1 §F's open question (what replaces a dominant national
anchor?) and the concrete texture of penguin-rookery coexistence flagged in Phase 1 §A.

**Fact → finding table:**

| What came back | Which finding it became |
|---|---|
| Real, specific Boulders Beach rules: no alcohol/smoking, no feeding, a mandatory 2–3 m minimum distance, boardwalks as the designated viewing/movement corridor keeping people off penguin routes entirely | Phase 1 §A's "unwritten space-sharing convention" made concrete: Phase 3/8's texture work can now use a real, specific rule-set rather than a vague gesture at "coexistence" |
| The Yalta Conference's site was chosen specifically for a mild climate AND for lending "a sense of seclusion and neutrality" to a meeting between three powers with competing interests | Directly fuses with Cape Adare's own "no dominant nation, founded around precedence" reading (Phase 1 §B/§C) — a real-world precedent for a place whose value lies partly in belonging to no single power, used in Phase 6/7 for the civic-identity/authority question Phase 1 §F left open |

**Divergence stated, per source-not-specification:** Yalta was still sovereign Soviet territory during its
conference — not neutral in a legal sense, only in a functional/diplomatic one. Cape Adare's own "neutrality"
(no dominant founding nation) is structural, not merely functional, which is a stronger, cleaner version of the
same idea rather than a direct copy.

**Open threads — surfaced but not chased:** the specific mechanism by which Boulders Beach's rules are
enforced/who enforces them (park authority vs. informal community norm) was not fully chased — relevant to
Phase 7 (Order) if a future pass wants to derive Cape Adare's own enforcement mechanism in more depth than this
pass's own timeframe allows.
