## PHASE 1 — CONSTRAINT & CAPABILITY (the spine)

### A. Four generators run separately

#### G2 — Physical & environmental constraint

*What does the environment give freely, withhold absolutely, demand continuously, and permit-but-punish?*

- **Gives freely:** an ice-free oasis (~40 km²) in a nearly all-ice continent — bare rock, exposed ground,
  something to build directly on rather than engineering around permanent ice. Maritime access to Prydz Bay and
  the Indian Ocean via Chinese icebreaker routes with pre-exile heritage. ~150 lakes across the oasis.
- **Withholds absolutely:** **a developed drainage network.** The research log's key physical fact: the oasis
  has ~150 lakes but drainage between them is **not developed** — water does not reliably flow from one lake
  to the next; each basin is closer to a closed system than a network.
- **Demands continuously:** individual monitoring of water quality per lake, because lake chemistry is driven
  independently by sea spray, local geology, and **periodic seawater surges from Dålk-glacier calving** at the
  oasis's eastern corner — a shock that can hit one lake and not its neighbor a few hundred meters away.
- **Permits but punishes:** treating the ~130 islands and multiple peninsulas (Stornes, Broknes, plus smaller
  Grovnes, Brattnevet, Stinear) as a single contiguous surface. Geography permits crossing between them, but at
  a real cost in time and exposure — this is an archipelago wearing a city's shape, not a plain with a city on it.

**Third-order chase (LAW 0):** no drainage network → water quality is a per-basin fact, not a city-wide one →
therefore **there is no such thing as "the city's water,"** only "this basin's water, right now" → therefore
any civic mechanism claiming to certify water safety city-wide is either false or is actually a
per-basin process wearing a city-wide name. **This is the deficit Phase 1 will spine on.**

#### G4 — Founding condition

*Who, why, under what constraint, with what, and without what?*

- **Who/why:** Sinian Federation (formerly Chinese) exiles, settling on pre-existing CHINARE Zhongshan Station
  infrastructure (operating since 1989), via a **diplomatic partition** at the Jeju-do International Court of
  Diplomacy — the same institution behind the 2318 robot-personhood ruling — which confirmed China's claim as
  exclusive and settled Japan's and Korea's claims to the neighboring sites in the same process.
- **With what:** continuous Chinese/Sinian habitation and administration is treated as plausible across the
  entire First Interwar Period (the Specs file's own reasoning: generation-to-generation habitation by
  descendants of the same national population, not one fixed institution persisting unchanged) — reinforced,
  not substituted, by the later Jeju-do ruling.
- **Without what:** **a losing claimant.** As noted in Phase 0 above, no rival claim is on record as having been
  pressed and refused. Compare this to Shirayuki, founded on the Bharati site allocated as **unoccupied** — a
  different founding shape (allocation of an empty site vs. confirmation of an existing one). Zhongshan's
  founding condition therefore differs from at least one of its two immediate neighbors in kind, not degree:
  **its city was already there and had to be recognized; Shirayuki's had to be granted.**

**Third-order chase:** recognition of an existing claim, rather than grant of an empty one, means Zhongshan's
founding generation never had a first arrival moment the way a granted-site population does → there is no
"day one" in the founding mythology, only a legal ratification of something already true → **the founding story
has no arrival scene**, which is an unusual property for a Settlement's origin account and worth carrying into
Phase 6 (Meaning) and Phase 10 (Catalog — is there a founding monument, and what does it actually depict if
there was no arrival to depict?).

#### G7 — Real-world inspiration (research run per `Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md`)

*Three picks, all already researched in the admissible research log — read here as attributes, not re-derived
as conclusions.*

- **Vilnius [Primary] → Užupis:** a self-declared micro-"republic," constitution written in three hours,
  engraved on multilingual plaques, an annual Independence Day where the "border" is performed rather than
  enforced.
- **Austin, Texas [Secondary]:** a civic identity ("Keep Austin Weird") that is an ex-post label for a quality
  produced by an accident of conditions (cheap housing after a bust, plus a university) — and whose founding
  preservation campaign (the Barton Creek Greenbelt) has **"largely faded from public memory"** even though its
  result remains central to the city's identity.
- **Yekaterinburg [Secondary]:** the world's highest concentration of Constructivist architecture (140+
  structures), produced by building enormously in one narrow window when one architectural approach was simply
  the obvious one — an *arrived-at* monoculture, not a chosen one.

**Fusion question, run per concrete detail, not per pick (Step C):** what do these three share structurally,
independent of their surface content? **All three are places whose defining civic fact was never decided by
anyone in particular** — Užupis's founders wrote a joke constitution that calcified into real local identity;
Austin's defining campaign is now unremembered by the people benefiting from it; Yekaterinburg's architectural
character was never a design brief, only what was available in one building window. **The common shape: civic
character as residue of a forcing condition, not as an authored choice.** This is the generator's genuine
contribution — a directly-usable structural echo of G2's own finding (no drainage network → no single city-wide
water fact) and G4's (recognition rather than grant → no founding arrival scene). **All three generators are
independently pointing at the same shape: absence of a deciding author.**

#### G8 — Demographic composition + census-change

- **Composition (Census II shares, per Specs):** China 35.83% (Primary), South Korea 9.70%, Japan 7.30%,
  Germany 8.47%, Russia 5.72%, Australia 3.66% (all Significant), then twelve Notable-tier nations at 1.2–3.6%
  each. **Genuinely multinational** despite a strong Chinese plurality — not a single-nation city.
- **Census-change (retention), per `02` G8's technique — hand-checked against the source table directly above,

    robots 474,312/647,448 ≈ 73.3%.
  - **Z-scored against all 33 cities is not computed in this pass** — flagged as an open task before this figure
    is used to support any finding, per `00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 7's z-score requirement. Recorded here as a raw

### E. Deficit address (`02` §4.1)

**The deficit:** no city-wide water fact / no single-basin water can stand for the whole.

**Address, peer-free:** **diffuse.** No single lake, no single institution, no single national-origin group is
"the" address for this deficit — every basin is independently subject to it, and the shock (glacier calving)
arrives unpredictably and locally. Per `02` §4.1's harder case: **check whether anyone has ever had a reason to
formulate this as a problem.** Water is checked per-basin as a matter of course (a practical necessity, not a
perceived deficiency) — there is no natural moment at which "we have no single water fact" would occur to a
resident as a complaint, because nobody expects a citywide fact from an archipelago in the first place.
**Tentative reading: this deficit may be unspeakable rather than merely unaddressed** — flagged for
confirmation once Phase 4 (Ordinary Life) is written and can test whether any resident's daily routine would
ever surface the question.

**A second deficit — no founding arrival scene:** address is **in its own past**, but per the harder check in
`02` §4.1, ask whether the evidence is also in the past. **Here it is not** — the population that would
"remember" an arrival scene never had one to remember; this is not an exodus-shaped deficit (nobody left who
knew otherwise) but a **never-occurred** deficit. This is closer to `diffuse` than to `in its own past`,
structurally, though its *content* (a historical absence) resembles the latter. **Recorded as a case the `02`
§4.1 taxonomy does not cleanly cover** — a deficit that is historical in subject matter but diffuse in address,
because there was never a witness position from which the missing thing could have been perceived. **Flagged
as a methodology finding for the observations log.**
