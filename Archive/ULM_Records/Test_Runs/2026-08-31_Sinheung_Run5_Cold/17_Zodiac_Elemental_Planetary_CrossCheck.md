## Cross-sign combinatorial pass — new step, added per the developer's subagent-parallelization proposal

**Not part of the original single-session procedure** — added when the developer proposed running this
extension as twelve parallel subagents, one per sign, which makes a final cross-sign synthesis step necessary
in a way it wasn't when one session held all twelve signs in a row. This step is run once, holding all twelve
signs' results at once, looking for combinations no single sign's own internal synthesis could reach.

**⭐ The Asteroid Belt convergence — the strongest cross-sign finding.** Four signs, checked independently and
in isolation from each other, each produced a decentralization finding specifically when crossed against the
Asteroid Belt: Leo's record display (many neighborhood boards, not one monument), Virgo's inspection process
(sequential distributed checkpoints, not one examiner), Scorpio's violator registry (several independent
private ledgers, not one gatekeeper), and Aquarius's technical society (non-hierarchical, consensus-governed).
**No single one of these was written with the others in view.** Read together, they describe a real, city-wide
structural pattern: **every institution actually checked against the Asteroid Belt decentralizes**, even
though Sinheung's own central civic myth — the Jeju-do court's founding ruling — is a singular, centralized
act. The city's foundational legitimacy is monolithic; nearly everything built on top of it, once examined
closely enough, is not. This is a genuinely new, emergent finding that required cross-referencing four separate
signs' results to see at all.

**A second, concrete link: the reconstruction corps and the salvage trade may be the same people.** Aries's
Mars-crossed finding (a dedicated war-damage reconstruction corps) and Pisces's Mars-crossed finding (a
war-debris salvage sub-trade) were derived independently, from different signs, and both anchor to the same
underlying fact — Sinheung's "damaged, partially operational" status. Read together: it is a natural, well-
grounded inference that these are not two separate populations but one, wearing two hats — officially clearing
and rebuilding war damage by day, informally salvaging and reselling the material that clearing turns up. This
gives both findings a sharper, more specific shape than either had alone.

**Checked and not combined:** Libra's Zhongshan-seniority finding (Jupiter) and Scorpio's founding-elder-circle
(speculative) were considered together, on the theory that the elders might be the specific mechanism enacting
deference to Zhongshan — but both sides of that link are already flagged as comparatively weak or speculative
individually, and stacking two speculative findings into a combined claim would compound rather than earn
confidence. Not adopted.
