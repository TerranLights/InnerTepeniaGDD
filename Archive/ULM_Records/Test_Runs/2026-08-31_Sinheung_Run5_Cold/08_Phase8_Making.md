# Sinheung — Phase 8: Making

**Governing prediction from Phase 2C, checked as this phase proceeds:** a founding population that inherited
infrastructure rather than building it, and whose energy went into proving a claim rather than developing
native forms, should show an **Inflected-heavy profile** — adapting brought-in and inherited forms rather than
originating new ones. Tracked below per element, for Gate I's count check at Step 7.

## A. Native layer first, transplanted second

**Native (founding-generation-developed):** thin, per the Phase 2C prediction — admissible canon supplies no
confirmed Sinheung-originated cultural form. **Transplanted:** the Korean founding population's own home
forms, plus Phase 2's broader composition (China, Japan, Germany, Russia, Indonesia, Australia at Significant
tier; ten more nations at Notable tier). **Inherited-but-not-cultural:** the Russian-built physical plant
(Phase 1 G4) — infrastructure, not culture, but it shapes what culture has to work around (Phase 3's built-
environment seam).

## B. Dress — function first, not aesthetics

**What would someone doing Sinheung's actual daily work need to wear?** Per Phase 3's distinctive freeze-thaw
climate register (a site that actually crosses 0°C repeatedly, unlike a permanently-frozen Tepenian site):
**the functional need is a modular, rapid-adjust layering system**, not fixed heavy insulation. Most Tepenian
Antarctic cities need clothing built for a single, stable cold register; Sinheung's climate genuinely swings
across the freezing line within a single day in shoulder seasons (per Specs' own monthly table — e.g., March
averaging −12°C with a −20 to −4°C range). **What does that need becoming custom look like after roughly two
centuries?** A visible layering etiquette — how many layers someone is wearing, and how, becomes a legible,
casual read of what kind of work they're headed to or from (freight-adjacent outdoor work vs. indoor
fabrication vs. administrative), which is a genuinely different fashion logic than a uniform-based read.

## C. Cuisine — general population discipline, the hardest category

**The failure mode to avoid explicitly:** defaulting to "Korean city = a single national cuisine" as the
general answer, which is exactly the narrow-role error `00b` exists to catch, and additionally is a poor fit
given Phase 2's actual composition (Korea Primary at 34.62%, but Significant/Notable tiers spanning eleven
other nations). **Corrected general answer:** given a push-dominated, multi-national, freight-schedule-
organized workforce (Phase 4), the general cuisine is most plausibly **fusion-practical** — quick, portable,
shareable across a genuinely mixed composition, organized around the freight-cycle rhythm rather than around
any single national tradition's own meal structure. **The Korean founding-population's own cuisine survives as
a real, present, but explicitly-scoped layer on top of this**, not as the general answer itself, per `00b`'s
required both-labeled presentation.

**⚠ A flagged resemblance, disclosed rather than concealed, per the recording law's rule 3 (never compress a
finding into something cleaner than it is):** independently reasoning from Phase 1's G2 profile (a genuine
60-day polar night, admissible) toward a food-security/preservation adaptation would produce a finding that
resembles the fermentation/preservation-craft claim tainted by M-34's leak from `Sinheung_Physical_
Infrastructure_Attributes.md`. **This pass is deliberately NOT writing that specific finding**, not because
the underlying physical logic is wrong (a 60-day polar night genuinely does create food-continuity pressure,
and the reasoning chain is sound), but because this run cannot currently distinguish "independently re-derived"
from "recalled from the tainted read" with confidence, and per M-32/M-33/M-34's own standing rule, a finding
that would land exactly on a known leak must be treated with suspicion, not written as if clean. **Left as an
explicit null, to be re-derived (or confirmed) by a session that has NOT been exposed to the tainted file, or
checked against admissible canon directly if a clean source for Sinheung's polar-night food response is ever
opened.**

**Non-human population's version, per `03` Phase 8's explicit flag as the most-skipped item project-wide:**
given Phase 1's Electricity/Animation symbolic register and Sinheung's chamber-manufacturing identity, robot
cuisine here plausibly has a stronger-than-average connection to the *literal* chamber process — this project's
established general robot-culture facts (siligel, coolant, robot coffee) are project-wide canon, not Sinheung-
specific invention, and Sinheung's own chamber-manufacturing trade gives robots here an unusually direct,
literal relationship to their own point of origin that most Tepenian cities' robot populations do not share.
**Flagged as a strong Phase 9 candidate**, not fully resolved here to avoid pre-empting that phase's own
population-specific work.

## D. Music, arts, craft — native layer thin, per the B.2C prediction

**Consistent with the Inflected-heavy prediction:** no confirmed Sinheung-originated art form in admissible
canon. **Candidate consistent with the "prove it through output" ethos (Phase 4/6):** a craft-display culture
oriented around visible, checkable precision — objects whose value is legible in their exactness, matching
Phase 1's G3/G4 profile far more than a purely decorative or narrative art form would. **Flagged, not fully
developed** — this pass does not manufacture a specific named craft form without further research, consistent
with LAW 0's warning against a coherent invention that has nowhere to attach.

## E. Play, sports, humor, leisure

**Chained directly from Phase 4's escapism finding:** thaw-season meltwater-lake leisure, available roughly
nine months of the year and absent during the polar night. **The humor register, tentatively:** a population
with a shared, mostly-unspoken "prove it" civic reflex (Phase 4/7) plausibly generates humor **about** that
reflex — jokes at the expense of visible overachievement, a very Tepenian-industrial version of tall-poppy
humor. **Flagged as a candidate, not confirmed** — would benefit from dedicated research this pass did not run.

## F. Language and speech markers

Given Phase 2's genuinely multi-national composition (Primary Korean, six Significant-tier nations, ten
Notable-tier nations) and a push-dominated arrival pattern (obligation-based, not amenity-based, per Phase 2F):
**the general population's speech is plausibly loanword-rich and code-switching-normal**, rather than
dominated by a single national linguistic register — a direct consequence of the composition table itself,
not an invented flourish. **What marks someone as from here, tentatively:** familiarity with freight/fabrication
jargon specific to the chamber trade and the Mirny/Zhongshan supply route, which would function as an in-group
marker independent of national origin — consistent with Phase 7c's output-proven (not origin-based) status
mechanism.
