## 5b — The three-way differentiation set (run before 5a, since it supplies the category 5a needs)

**Category chosen: how does each city relate to being fully known/understood by outsiders?** Read directly off
the geometry above, with no invented content:

- **Zhongshan (Saturn/Metal): resists full disclosure and is comfortable with it.** Saturn's registered meaning
  is explicit — "doesn't care to be fully known," "impressive from a distance, insubstantial up close." Metal
  reinforces this from the other symbol: precision and cutting-away read as a preference for a controlled,
  edited self-presentation over an open one.
- **Shirayuki (Uranus/Fire): performs its outsider status outward, attractively.** Uranus supplies the
  anomaly/outsider base; Fire's registered positive pole — "the courage to act and actually be seen doing it" —
  converts that outsider status into something actively displayed rather than hidden. **This independently
  derives, from registered symbol content alone, the same shape as Shirayuki's known in-fiction "clearest
  attraction case" reputation — without reading the inadmissible source that states it directly.** Recorded as
  corroboration between two independent readings, not as evidence this pass secretly used the withheld fact.
- **Sinheung (Uranus/Electricity): normalizes its outsider status rather than either hiding or performing it.**
  Uranus's positive pole is specifically about *settling into* a post-event identity as the ordinary baseline —
  not concealment (Zhongshan's mode) and not display (Shirayuki's mode), but **quiet normalization**: the
  anomaly stops reading as anomalous, to itself first and to outsiders second. Electricity's negative pole
  supplies the twist — that normalized calm rests on an animating current that could fail all at once, meaning
  **the normalization is a genuine achievement, not a comfortable one.**

**The axis, in three or four words: concealment / performance / normalization.** Genuinely divergent — three
cities that share a founding mechanism (the Jeju-do court) and, in two cases, a planetary symbol, produce three
structurally different relationships to their own outsider condition. **Tense checked, per `04` III.2's
required fourth axis:** all three readings are stated in the present tense (an ongoing disposition, not a past
event) — this is a genuine gap in the comparison and is flagged rather than papered over: **none of the three
readings currently distinguishes *when* each city's relationship to its outsider status was established versus
how it reads now**, which the founding-condition material (Phase 1, G4) could sharpen at Step 7 differentiation.

## 5a — Peers

**Zhongshan** (near-identical coordinates, a few hundred meters) and **Shirayuki** (~8km, per Specs). **What
flows, and what does each side refuse to develop, supplying it from the other instead (`03`'s core move —
mechanism, not rivalry)?**

- **Sinheung ↔ Zhongshan:** Phase 1's G3 already establishes this concretely and admissibly — Sinheung's raw
  material is "trucked in from Mirny via the Zhongshan tri-junction." **Zhongshan supplies the physical transit
  corridor Sinheung's entire fabrication economy depends on**, without Zhongshan itself being the source of the
  material. This is a real, named, non-hostile dependency — the "quiet friction" `03` Phase 5a asks to
  distinguish from loud friction: Sinheung needs a piece of Zhongshan's own territory to function economically,
  every single day, and this is stated as ordinary infrastructure, not as a negotiated concession or grievance
  anywhere in admissible canon. **Flagged as a genuine "in a neighbor's present" dependency** (per Phase 1's own
  new methodology observation) — the reliance is structural and constant, not dramatic, which is exactly the
  kind of relation this project's shadow discipline (`00d`) predicts will be invisible from inside.
- **Sinheung ↔ Shirayuki:** no admissible economic or logistical fact links them directly (Shirayuki sits
  further off, on the far side of the highway per Hwy 4's stated routing: Sayowa → Mawson → Sinheung →
  Shirayuki — Sinheung is *between* Mawson and Shirayuki on the same road). **What Sinheung refuses to develop
  that Shirayuki supplies instead, per the 5b axis above:** if Shirayuki performs its outsider status outward
  and Sinheung normalizes its own inward, then Shirayuki is plausibly the cluster's outward-facing "face" for
  outsider visitors specifically curious about the Larsemann Hills' unusual three-way founding — a role
  Sinheung's own Uranus/Electricity disposition (settle, don't perform) would not want and would not compete
  for. **This is inference from the geometry, flagged as a Phase 5 candidate finding, not yet corroborated by
  an independent generator** — weaker grounding than the Zhongshan relation above, which rests on a Tier-1
  economic fact.

## 5c — The parent (Mirny subnet)

**What the parent needs from Sinheung:** per Phase 1's G3, national chamber-manufacturing capacity — a
disproportionate dependency relative to Sinheung's stated "modest political profile." **What Sinheung supplies
on its own terms vs. what it believes about the parent:** Mirny subnet is not written in comparable depth
(Tier 2 absence, noted at Frame stage) — running the provisional-assumption protocol (`01` §5.2): **provisionally
assumed** that Sinheung regards Mirny primarily as a raw-material source rather than a cultural peer, since the
cluster's own daily identity-negotiation (per Specs' Character & Culture section, treated as light attribute-
adjacent scene-setting) is explicitly with Zhongshan and Shirayuki, not with Mirny. **This assumption is
flagged for Gate P** if a Mirny-subnet-level pass is ever written — it should confirm, contradict, or leave open
whether Sinheung's relationship to its own parent subnet is this thin.

## 5d — Edge and crossing

**Route:** Hwy 4 (Mawson–Sinheung Highway), positioning Sinheung between Mawson and Shirayuki on the same road,
plus the Zhongshan tri-junction for the Mirny freight line. **Arrival types, enumerated rather than assumed
singular:** (1) freight/logistics traffic via the Mirny/Zhongshan route — the dominant, routine arrival type
given the established economy; (2) highway travelers via Mawson/Shirayuki; (3) DLC-specific: the airstrip's
historical Dome Fuji supply route (Specs, Economy section) — a third, air-based arrival type entirely distinct
from the other two, though currently an Open Question per Specs (broken plane, player-repairable).

**What converts a visitor into a member?** Per `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` Technique 10 (Membership by
Unremarked Persistence), derived from the local economy rather than a ceremony: given Phase 1's founding-
condition finding (a paper claim that had to be proven through output), the plausible mechanism is **having a
piece of one's own work accepted into the fabrication/freight chain without correction** — the same shape as
the guild/craft-based conversion row in the technique's own table, fused with Sinheung's specific "prove it
through output" civic disposition. **This is not yet independently confirmed by a second generator** — flagged
as Phase 5/7 candidate, to be checked against Phase 7's Order work.

## 5e — Dependency

**Named, not shadowed — both dependencies are openly stated in admissible canon, which per `02` §3.3 places
them as politics/logistics, not `00d` shadow material:**
1. **Mirny** — raw material. Named, routine, non-hostile (§5a above).
2. **Neumayer** — the Mark IV schematic, Sinheung's single-address deficit from Phase 1. **This is the
   strongest, most actionable relational thread this pass has produced** — a real, named counterparty holding
   exactly what Sinheung's G3 profile says it lacks (engineering origination). Per `02` §4.1, a single address
   is the *best* case (a grievance that can eventually be acted on). **Corroborated by G7 research**: the
   Volgograd Tractor Plant / Albert Kahn precedent (an externally-designed flagship facility under full local
   operational control) is the strongest real-world shape for exactly this relation, and its own history (later
   partial reclamation, per the Córdoba FMA precedent's ownership-cycle pattern) suggests a plausible future
   arc for Sinheung/Neumayer that this pass is not resolving, only flagging for `Storyline/` consideration.

  the Tier 2 gap on Mirny/Shirayuki specifically — what each neighbor might need from Sinheung); the asymmetry
