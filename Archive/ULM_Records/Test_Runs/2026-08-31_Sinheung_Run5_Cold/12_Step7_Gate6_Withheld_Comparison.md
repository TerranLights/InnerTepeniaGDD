## The one genuine mismatch — both-are-true test run per `02` §5.3 / `04`'s Gate 6 note

**This pass (Phase 7d):** no organized counterculture — the "prove it" pressure is internalized individually,
distributed rather than organized, and the place "cannot be reformed from within and cannot be opposed from
within either, because there is nobody to organize."

**The withheld file (§19):** *"Modest but real... deliberately unproductive, openly resistant to the output-as-
legitimacy ethic... a small, quietly resented minority who opted out of proving anything to anyone."*

**Running the required test before declaring either wrong.** Checking the candidate scales in order, per `04`'s
Gate 6 note:

1. **Public/mainstream vs. private/minority-community — this is the reconciling scale.** This pass's Phase 7d
   finding describes the *mainstream population's* internal psychological structure (why the pressure is
   diffuse and unorganized *for most people*). The withheld file's finding describes a *specific, small,
   minority population* that has, exceptionally, organized around refusing that pressure. **These are not
   competing claims about the same population — they are claims about two different population shares.**

**Reconciliation, and it sharpens both findings rather than killing either:** a population where the "prove it"
pressure is genuinely diffuse and unorganized (this pass's finding) makes organizing an explicit refusal of it
*rare and difficult* — which is exactly consistent with the withheld file's own description of the
counterculture as **"small"** and **"quietly resented"** (resented specifically *because* refusing the norm
that almost everyone else silently absorbs individually is conspicuous in a way that organized dissent against
an externally-imposed rule would not be). **This pass's finding explains why the withheld file's counterculture
is shaped the way it is — small, quiet, resented — rather than contradicting its existence.** Adopted
reconciled version: **the "prove it" reflex is diffuse and unorganized for the mainstream population (this
pass's Phase 7d finding, standing as written), and a small, genuinely exceptional counterculture exists
specifically because organizing against a diffuse pressure is unusually hard to do at all (the withheld file's
§19, now explained rather than merely stated).**
