### The swap-test risk flagged in Phase 9, resolved here

**Phase 9 flagged honestly that its second-strongest finding, and by extension part of Phase 1's central
finding, might describe the Jeju-do diplomatic-founding condition generally (shared with Shirayuki) rather than
Sinheung specifically.** Running the resolution now, using the geometry already established rather than
inventing new material:

**What single property would produce both a Sinheung-specific reading and a Jeju-do-general reading?** Per
Phase 5's three-way axis (concealment/performance/normalization), **Shirayuki and Sinheung share the founding
mechanism but diverge sharply on what they DO with the resulting anxiety.** Both cities inherit the same root
condition (a paper claim, secured before the population existed) — this part of the finding genuinely is
Jeju-do-general, and should be **relabeled as such, not claimed as Sinheung-unique.** But Phase 5's registered-
symbol geometry shows the two cities process that shared condition differently: Shirayuki's Uranus/Fire
disposition converts it into outward-performed attraction (the "clearest attraction case" reading, corroborated
independently in Phase 5b); Sinheung's Uranus/Electricity disposition converts it into inward normalization
resting on a fragile animating current. **The reconciliation: the "prove it" ANXIETY is shared with Shirayuki
(and structurally, though less directly, with Zhongshan, whose founding claim was confirmed rather than
allocated). The specific FORM that anxiety takes at Sinheung — quiet, internalized, distributed rather than
organized, expressed as continuous demonstrable output rather than as outward display — is what Phase 1/9
actually established, and that specific form does not swap cleanly onto Shirayuki.**

**Correction applied:** Phase 1's central finding and Phase 9's second-strongest finding are **re-scoped, not
discarded** — from *"Sinheung uniquely pays to be believed to deserve to exist"* to *"Sinheung shares the
Jeju-do cluster's general founding-legitimacy anxiety, but is the one member of the cluster that internalizes
it as continuous, distributed, individually-carried demonstrable-output pressure rather than externalizing it
as performance (Shirayuki) or concealment (Zhongshan)."* **This is a stronger, more precise finding than the
original, not a weaker one** — the both-are-true test, run honestly, sharpened rather than killed the pass's
best material. Per `04`'s own note on this exact test: *"very often not a wrongness but a scale mismatch."*
Here the "scale" was cluster-general vs. city-specific, and both readings survive at their correct scale.
