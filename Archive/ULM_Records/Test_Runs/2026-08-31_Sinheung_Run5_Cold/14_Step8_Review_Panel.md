# Sinheung — Step 8: The Review Panel

**Run 2026-08-31.** Flat Archetypes cast as: **Child** = a resident's kid, too young for the fabrication floor
· **Lover** = a courting pair with nowhere private · **Parent** = a logistics worker on the freight cycle with
a young dependent · **Ruler** = the freight dispatcher (Phase 10's own cast) · **Elder** = a Census-I-era
founding-generation worker, now past output capacity · **Mentor** = the Chamber Works quality examiner (Phase
10). Plus the mandatory **Passer-Through** (a Hwy 4 traveler, Mawson→Shirayuki, one day) and **Neighbor** (a
Zhongshan resident, cast across the tri-junction per Phase 5's cluster geometry).

## The Child

*"What am I not allowed to touch, and what happens if I do it anyway?"* **Objection — addition:** this pass
never addresses childhood directly. Given Phase 1's land-scarcity deficit and Phase 4's near-universal "prove
it" civic reflex, a child here plausibly absorbs the output-proving disposition **without being taught it**
(exactly `00d`'s unintended/unnoticed shadow shape) and has nowhere formal to play, given the density flag.
**→ accepted**, folds into Phase 4/9 as a new finding: the "made here" generation absorbs the civic anxiety
ambiently, before they can articulate it, which sharpens Phase 6A's stake finding into something inherited
rather than chosen even by adults.

## The Lover

*"Could I build a life with someone here, and where would we actually go?"* **Objection — a genuine want:**
privacy for courtship, given the land-scarcity/access-road congestion (Phase 4B) and the independently-derived
high-mutual-visibility civic culture (output is legible, contribution is publicly legible, per Phase 4C/6A).
**Running Rule 3 before accepting:** would satisfying this make Sinheung more like its cluster siblings? **Yes**
— Zhongshan's Saturn-register comfort with not being fully known (Phase 5's geometry) implies exactly the kind
of private, un-surveilled social space Sinheung structurally lacks; giving Sinheung a private-courtship venue
would move it toward Zhongshan's own character, not develop Sinheung's own. **→ unmet.** Written as
characterization, per `00f`'s own model: **Sinheung has no concept of private courtship space, and land
scarcity plus a civic culture where contribution must be visible make privacy itself read as slightly suspect
— a pair here does not get to disappear from view, and courtship happens in public, legible, contribution-
adjacent contexts or not at all.** The seasonal meltwater-lake leisure spot (Phase 4E) is the closest available
exception, and it is explicitly seasonal and outdoor, not a real solution.

## The Parent

*"Where are the children right now, and who has them?"* **Objection — a genuine coverage gap, not a
characterization:** nothing in admissible canon establishes crisis-time childcare institutions. **→ noted** —
real but deferred; this pass's admissible-canon-only scope does not supply the composition-level institutional
detail needed to answer honestly, and inventing an answer here would violate LAW 0's anti-invention discipline.
Flagged as a genuine REQUESTED-adjacent gap for a future pass with fuller access.

## The Ruler

*"Where does it come from, and who settles up?"* **Largely satisfied already** — Phase 1 G3, Phase 5, and
Phase 7a cover resource flow (Mirny import, Neumayer schematic import, chamber/finished-goods/quarry export) in
real depth. **One genuine addition:** what happens if Neumayer stops updating the schematic? Phase 7b's
unadministrable-rule finding covers the shared right-of-way but not this contingency. **→ noted** — real,
minor, deferred; a legitimate REQUESTED item rather than a finding this pass should manufacture.

## The Elder

*"Was that actually always true — and what am I now, if I can't do that any more?"* **Objection —
sharpens an existing finding rather than adding a new one:** Phase 6D's Failure-State-of-the-Core-Value table
already predicts, in the abstract, that "productivity/self-reliance" failing produces "a dependency nobody will
name; euphemism as institution." **The Elder makes this personal and concrete:** a founding-generation worker
who can no longer produce demonstrable output confronts Sinheung's central civic value's own failure state more
directly and more personally than almost any other resident could — precisely because the civic value here is
output-legitimacy, not lineage or seniority, an Elder's decades of prior contribution do not automatically
convert into continued standing the way they might in a lineage-valuing culture. **→ accepted**, strengthens
Phase 6D into a sharper, character-facing finding rather than standing as a separate one.

## The Mentor

*"How does someone learn this, and what becomes of the ones who can't?"* **Already substantively answered** by
Phase 5d/7c's output-proven apprenticeship and quiet-lateral-reassignment findings. **→ accepted as
confirmation**, per `00f` §8's own note that panel-derived material strengthening an existing finding is an
equally good outcome as a standalone new one — no new content forced to justify running the position.

## The Passer-Through

**A Hwy 4 traveler, crossing in one day.** What they see: industrial activity, freight congestion at the
Zhongshan tri-junction (Phase 7b), the freeze-thaw texture (Phase 3), a city that reads as busy and legible from
outside. **Discoverability check (`00d` test 3):** does the shadow (asymmetric record-keeping, Phase 6C) show
on arrival? **No** — a passer-through would read the posted-output display (Phase 10) as ordinary, even
admirable, civic pride, not as evidence of suppression; the asymmetry is only visible on investigation.
**→ PASS.** The shadow has been written as a shadow, not as the surface.

## The Neighbor (cast as a Zhongshan resident)

**Biased by design, and the bias is informative.** Given Zhongshan's Saturn-register comfort with not being
fully known (Phase 5's geometry) set against Sinheung's visible, output-legible civic culture: a Zhongshan
resident plausibly reads Sinheung's civic pride with mild, unspoken skepticism — the same "impressive from a
distance, insubstantial up close" instinct Saturn's own registered meaning carries, projected outward onto a
neighbor rather than held about itself. **The sharp part:** an outside observer suspecting that Sinheung's
visible pride is compensating for something **would, per Phase 6A's own stake finding, be correct** — the
Neighbor's biased read partially discovers the shadow without ever being told it, from a standpoint the pass
did not construct to reveal it. **→ accepted**, recorded as external corroboration of Phase 6A from a genuinely
independent standpoint (the panel's own attention, not a new generator, but a real strengthening of confidence
in the finding).

## The Lover faculty (Moore & Gillette) — run every time

*"Is this place alive, and could anyone love it?"* **A genuinely mixed answer, not a simple yes or the
Impotent-Lover no.** Real vitality exists — the thaw-season meltwater-lake leisure (Phase 4E), the milder-
than-Antarctic-average climate, genuine daylight for a third of the year, a craft-display pride in precision
work (Phase 8D). **But it is conditional and partially colonized by the output-proving reflex** — Phase 4C
already flagged that even hobby/chore contexts carry a faint overcorrection toward demonstrable output. **→
accepted**, written as the pass's answer to this question specifically: Sinheung is alive, and its joy is real,
but even its leisure is not fully off-duty from the civic anxiety underneath it — which is a more particular,
earned answer than either "yes, unreservedly" or "no, it is dead."

## One shadow position — the Denying "Innocent" One (Magician's passive shadow)

*"What does this place refuse to know that it already knows?"* **Verdict: guilty, directly.** Phase 6C's
asymmetric record-keeping finding (successes posted, failures under-recorded) and Phase 10's record-hall
landmark **are, structurally, exactly this shadow's mechanism** — a place that maintains a permanent, visible
record of one kind of information and conspicuously does not maintain the other is refusing to know, at the
institutional level, precisely what it would need to know to address the founding-legitimacy anxiety at its
own root (Phase 6A). **No villain required** — nobody decided to suppress failure records; the record simply
grew that way, in exactly `00d`'s no-author shape.

## Four-corner check

**The Child, Lover, and Parent objections share a root cause (Phase 1's land-scarcity deficit) but attack
genuinely different weaknesses** — the Child's angle (ambient absorption of civic anxiety, nowhere to play),
the Lover's angle (romantic privacy, resolved as `unmet`), and the Parent's angle (crisis-time institutional
support, resolved as `noted`) are not one objection wearing three hats; they are three genuinely different
consequences converging on the same underlying constraint. **Per `00f` §5, this convergence from different
corners onto the same root is the strongest signal available**, not a collapsed panel — recorded as such.

## Life Arcs consulted, briefly

**The Maiden** (*can a young person separate here?*): plausibly difficult, given land scarcity and a civic
culture where standing is earned through visible local contribution — leaving might read as abandoning
unfinished proof, a genuine tension not fully resolved this pass. **Silence recorded**, not developed further.
**The King** (*what does this place ask its powerful to give up?*): no clear governance-elite figure has been
established in admissible canon to test this against — **silence recorded**, consistent with Phase 7b's thin
governance material generally.

## Refereed conflicts

**None** — no two panel positions directly conflicted this run; the Lover's `unmet` and the Neighbor's
corroboration both point the same direction (a real, characteristic limitation) rather than opposing each
other.
