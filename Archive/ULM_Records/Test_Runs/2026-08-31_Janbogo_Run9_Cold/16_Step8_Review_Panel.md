## The Six Flat Archetypes

**1. The Child** (cast as a founding-core household's kid)
> *"Nobody's written down where I go when it's too windy and too dark to be outside for two straight
> months. I know I'm not supposed to go out alone during a wind warning — that part's obvious, the wind

  ground an answer. Real gap, correctly left open rather than invented here either.
- **Asks**: *What am I not allowed to do here, and what happens if I do it anyway?* — Answered by the
  pass's own G2 finding: the environment itself punishes (Phase 1's Grudging Tolerance), no social
  enforcement needed. **A genuinely satisfying answer**, not a gap.

**2. The Lover** (cast as a mixed founding/grown-in courting pair)

> each other. I don't know if that's romantic or just weather."*

  is *possible* indoors but never states whether it's *private from the community* — Phase 6's own record
  that Janbogo's public/private divide runs thin (per the frame's own admissible material, not the withheld
  file) leaves this genuinely ambiguous. **REJECTED as a doubt, with reason**: this is not a coherence
  failure in the pass — it correctly declined to invent a specific answer where none was grounded, and the
  Lover's question surfaces a REQUESTED item rather than a contradiction.

**3. The Parent** (cast as an outdoor-exposed labor parent)

**4. The Ruler** (cast as the Subnet Administration Seat)

**6. The Mentor** (cast as a testbed knowledge-holder)
