**Capricorn's registered character, condensed for reference (all terms read from the substrate file):**
the organizer who makes other people's visions structurally real; motive = standing that cannot be
revoked, dues paid and never taken away; civic vow = "make society a safe and orderly place for its
hard-working, law-abiding members"; nightmare = "a lawless world run amok"; cardinal earth = duty plus
the push to make things happen; ruler Saturn = time, limitation, accountability, structure; tenth house
(career/public status, "House of Enterprise") and twelfth house (confinement/isolation/solitude, "Saturn's
joy") as public face vs. private weather; Saturn return at 29.5 years as a real, non-arbitrary maturity
threshold. Light: builds things designed to outlast their builders, winter competence ("always has a
plan"), calculated (never faith-based) courage, integrity as reputation management, institutional memory
as affection, dry load-bearing humor, quiet authority exerted without a title. Shadow: patronage/favor-
ledger sincerity, appearance-over-substance, "the schedule as a comfort object" (a stale calibration never
revisited because change is felt as loss of safety), homogeneity pressure/distrust of outsiders, guilt as
value system, exhaustion displayed as credential, the unforgetting (grudges), isolation dressed as self-
sufficiency, vindicated pessimism, perfectionism with no floor, test-and-admit socialization. Core need:
sincere recognition for dedicated effort within a chosen field — dues acknowledged as real, not applause.
Myth: Pricus (a father memorialized for a failure of protection, from repeatedly resetting time to save
children who could not be saved, until he stopped) and Saturn-as-Cronus (a father who consumes his
successors to keep his seat) — the sign owns both halves of the generational problem. Correspondences:
fortresses (ruled place), the sea-goat, "record over rite."
