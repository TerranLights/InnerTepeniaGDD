**Virgo's registered character, in brief, as the filter for everything below:** the Analyzer, "I serve"/"I
Analyze," motive = catastrophe forestalled at the smallest scale ("even big, complex machinery can come to a
screeching halt should nobody notice one loose screw"), motto "my best can surely be better." Light: preventative
culture, the "dimmer switch" (regulates so nothing is prematurely overstimulated/worn out), radical
self-sufficiency (doesn't trust others' expertise, teaches itself), composure under load, mediation without
leadership, audits upward and inward alike, criticism delivered privately. Shadow: assumes universal
incompetence, the censor (including of itself), "the auditor that cannot be audited" (total inspection
privilege, zero inspection exposure), peevishness/grudge-keeping in writing (the ledger, the red gel pen),

anxiety, denial as emotional practice, diagnostic gift curdled into manipulation, workaholism/no natural
stopping point ("no job is ever finished"), "the unsigned work" (a job that cannot close without external
sign-off). Core need: usefulness *witnessed*, to be signed off, autonomy inside the assignment, order as an
operating requirement (not a preference), control of the threshold, rules that protect the work's own standing,
to mean something structurally, to not be owned. Structural position: mutable earth = **sand** — yields to
pressure, does not resist head-on; negative/yin/receptive; ruler Mercury as **scribe**, not messenger. Myth:
Ishtar's descent (one goes into the dark so the surface has light) and Astraea's withdrawal (the archetype's
ultimate weapon is departure, not sabotage).

## STEP 1 — Base prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Virgo's own registered character, what would it be?

**Reasoning.** Virgo's defining motive — continuous, small-scale vigilance that forestalls catastrophe before
it happens, distrust of ordinary/ambient judgment, a job that structurally never finishes — maps with unusual
precision onto Phase 1's own G2 profile: Janbogo's katabatic wind hazard specifically **defeats the ordinary
human heuristic** ("check the sky, check the thermometer... the practical distinction between 'blizzard' and
'high-wind clear conditions' collapses"), and Phase 1's own GRUDGING TOLERANCE names this exactly: "trusting
one's own senses about outdoor conditions is permitted... and punished unpredictably." Phase 1's STANDING COST
(G2, agreeing with G5) is explicitly continuous and non-seasonal: "continuous wind-forecast/warning
infrastructure... none of this is seasonal, it never stops." Specs' own admissible Gameplay Notes independently
support *some* wind-forecast/warning function ("the game signals incoming events") — a clean source, distinct
from the M-65-excluded "Katabatic Warning Towers" institution this run cannot honestly use (Phase 10's own
provenance flag).

**Candidate:** a wind-forecast/dispatch role — an analyst whose entire civic function is the Virgo motive made
literal: continuous, obsessive small-scale monitoring (instrument readings, pressure trends, indirect signals)
precisely because direct sensory/ambient judgment is established as untrustworthy here. The job Virgo describes
— "devises a rating system... knows the value of eliminating the superfluous," never satisfied, "my best can
surely be better" — is structurally the same job this population needs someone to hold, independent of any
excluded institution's name.

**Filter check:** characteristically inevitable, not generic — it is derived directly from Phase 1's own G2
STANDING COST/GRUDGING TOLERANCE, not invented from Virgo alone. Pushed player-facing: **spoken/heard** — an
NPC role the player can find at a monitoring post and talk to; the wind-status signal itself is something
**heard/seen** throughout the city.

**KEPT — Finding 1: the wind-forecast analyst/dispatcher** (person, role-archetype). Category: person.

**Contradiction check (Step 4, run here since Step 1 produces a kept result):** opposite register — informal/
private vs. institutional/formal. Candidate: an unlicensed elder or "weather-sense" old-timer who reads
conditions by informal, sensory means and is trusted *over* the official system. **Rejected** — this directly
contradicts Phase 1's own G2 finding that trusting personal/sensory judgment about conditions is specifically
unreliable and unsafe at Janbogo; an informally-trusted "reads the wind by feel" tradition would be
uncharacteristic, not a safe parallel, given the established profile.

**Step 5 stopping reason for Step 1:** a candidate was generated and kept (the analyst role), independently
re-derived from Phase 1's own admissible G2 material rather than the excluded Katabatic Warning Towers file; its
opposite-register contradiction candidate was generated and rejected because it contradicts Phase 1's own G2
finding.
