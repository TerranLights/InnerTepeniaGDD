## 7a — Work and economy

maritime, and overland logistics — the admissible Economy & Industry material ("a significant node in

Janbogo's own inherited scientific-instrumentation/data-service tradition, persisting alongside its supply-
hub identity rather than because of it — **exactly the "ordinary, emotionally neutral, unrelated to the
headline function" shape `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` §9 asks for**, and it did not need to be
invented; it was sitting in Phase 1's own research log (open thread #3), now formalized here per `03` §0.2

## 7b — Governance and decision

**The most productive question, per `03` 7b: what is unadministrable, not who holds power.** Phase 1's G5
deficit supplies a genuine, structural answer: **Janbogo administers the Janbogo Subnet but does not hold
its own subnet's Arcanet relay nexus** (that hardware sits in Concordia's Gemini district). This is a real
governance oddity independent of any invented mechanism — whatever Janbogo's subnet administration actually
controls, it structurally cannot administer its own network's physical center. **What this produces, per
`03`'s own framing**: a genuine edge case nobody wrote — REQUESTED, since admissible material does not
establish how (or whether) this gap is formally handled versus simply lived with.

## 7c — Knowledge and transmission

**Two plausible channels, neither invented from nothing.** Maritime/port trades (loading, logistics,
navigation of the polynya's variable ice edge) — apprenticeship-shaped, given the physical-skill nature of
the work. The scientific-testbed tradition (7a) — plausibly transmitted through a more formal,
qualification-based track, given its research character.

**⚠ Amended, Review Panel (`16_Step8_Review_Panel.md`, the Mentor position, ACCEPTED).** What becomes of
someone who cannot learn either track: an informal, ungrandiose drift toward the port/logistics track — not
a named institution or a formal washout mechanism, simply an ordinary settling into the more physically-
skill-based of the two channels when the more formal, qualification-based testbed track doesn't take. **No
invented specifics beyond this** — the Mentor's own words state it plainly: "they just... end up doing
something else, port work usually." Closes the open question `03` 7c asks after, with the modest answer the
pass's own established material actually supports.

## 7d — Private life, minorities, and counterculture

**Derived from specific pressure, per `03`'s own discipline — not from generic rebellion.** Phase 4
established a real, unremarked-on civic norm: minimizing outdoor threshold crossings, batching movement to
reduce wind exposure. **The counterculture candidate is people who conspicuously do not minimize exposure**
— not the outdoor-exposed labor minority (Phase 4), whose relationship to the norm is occupational
necessity, not choice, and therefore not counterculture in `03`'s own sense. A genuine counterculture would
be people who *could* follow the general norm and deliberately do not. **Left as a named candidate rather
than asserted**: no admissible material yet grounds who these people are or why, and forcing a specific
answer here would risk exactly the "generic rebels" failure `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` §11 warns
against. **REQUESTED.**

**A second, distinct candidate, flagged but explicitly not adopted**: Phase 1's G8 deficit (the founding
nation's civic template surviving without demographic backing) could seed a "preserve what's being diluted"
counterculture among founding-descended residents. **Deliberately not developed further here** — this is
close to the same shape as the excluded, banded memory conclusions about Janbogo's Korean civic identity
(M-63/M-64), and developing it without new grounding risks reconstructing the excluded material from
adjacent inference rather than genuinely deriving it fresh. **Flagged as a live but higher-risk candidate**,
should a future session with fresh, independently-researched Phase 8/9 material want to test it properly.

**Sanction proportionality** (`00d`'s hard check): not yet applicable — no specific sanction has been
