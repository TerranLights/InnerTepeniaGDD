## STEP 1 — Base Cancer prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Cancer's own registered character, what would
that be?

**F1 — The Registry.** Two independently-grounded Janbogo facts converge on the same shape without either
one importing from the other: Phase 6C's own hypothesis (frozen/rocky ground makes conventional burial
infeasible, so Janbogo's dead are plausibly held in a **record** rather than marked by a **rite** — "record
over rite") and Phase 3D's seam finding (the founding-era three-wing core, ~4,000 m², a literal, bounded,
enterable physical edge that everything since has grown around). Cancer's own light expression is explicitly
about **institutional memory** — "records, albums, custodianship, continuity, the honoring of ancestors... a
memory like a steel trap" — and its Design Hook #10/#11 explicitly frame civic memory as a curated, kept,
never-fully-released archive ("trust the past, distrust the present... archives curated to keep only the
good, the painful record sealed, not destroyed"). **Result: a civic archive/registry — arrivals, guaranteed
passages, and the city's dead — sited at or grown from the founding-era core, that only ever accumulates and
never prunes**, mirroring the same "no natural end point" shape Phase 1 already found in G4's own STANDING
COST (continuous outward growth away from an inherited footprint). Category: **PLACE** (institution).
Player-facing: **entered** (a hall/office), **handled** (records requested/browsed).

**Filter check:** characteristically inevitable, not generic — it is the only object in Janbogo's own
established material that satisfies Cancer's institutional-memory light expression AND Cancer's
never-fully-lets-go shadow AND Janbogo's own already-flagged record-over-rite death hypothesis AND the
already-bounded, physical founding-core setting, all four at once, without inventing any new fact.

"passive-aggressive, could erupt" coloring is plausible narrative embellishment, not a new fact grounded in
any additional admissible material. Held to the same standard as every other cross-check in this pass, not
kept.

**3. Fire (elemental).** No candidate generated. Fire's registered content (passion, visibility, courage to

**5. Wood (elemental).** **F3 — The Annexation-as-Shelter Growth Pattern.** Wood's negative ("expansion

Category: **THING** (a civic growth pattern/rhetoric). Player-facing: **seen** (readable in the city's own
successive construction rings) and **spoken** (the "we shelter more" justification given for each expansion).

**6. Metal (elemental).** No candidate generated. Metal's positive register (graceful letting-go, honoring
what has genuinely ended) runs directly counter to Cancer's own shadow (curated nostalgia, sealed-not-

**7. Electricity.** No candidate generated. Electricity's registered content (animation, total dependency on

**8. Electromagnetism.** Candidate generated: "a signal is also a vulnerability — anything reachable by an

### Planetary Symbols

**15. Saturn (planetary).** **F10 — The Uninvited Gathering.** Saturn's positive ("beauty built from
