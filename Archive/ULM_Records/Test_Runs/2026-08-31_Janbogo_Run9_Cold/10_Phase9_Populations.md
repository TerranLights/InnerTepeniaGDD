# Phase 9 — Populations

**Asks:** What is life here like for each distinct kind of person present, and how do those kinds relate?

---

## A. Populations and lens

**Robots and humans** — the only representation category in active use project-wide (standing canon, not
Janbogo-specific). **Lens decision**: the outer-city convention anchors robot culture in founding-nation
threads (`03` Phase 9's own note). Given Phase 1's G8 finding (a plural, twelve-nation composition with no
dominant majority, founding nation included), **the founding-nation anchor does not collapse to one thread
here — it plausibly produces a genuinely plural robot culture**, mirroring the human population's own
layered composition (Phase 2: founding-descended minority vs. grown-in majority) rather than one dominant
national throughline.

## C. Inter-population relations — the local inflection

**A genuine "different axis" finding, per `03` 9C's own strongest-move preference**: rather than restating
the national baseline, Phase 4's own established divide — the outdoor-exposed labor minority (whose day
cannot follow the general "minimize threshold crossings" rhythm) versus the interior-buffered majority —
plausibly cuts **across** both national origin and human/robot lines more sharply than either does day to
day. A dock worker's shared condition with other dock workers (regardless of nation, regardless of human or
robot) is plausibly a more immediate, lived commonality than shared national origin with someone who never
leaves the interior-buffered majority's routine. **This is Phase 9's own "is there a category here that
matters more than kind?" question, answered from material already established rather than invented fresh.**

---

## D. Dual-tag

**The outdoor-exposed/interior-buffered axis**: inheritance = **emergent** (arises from Janbogo's own G2
profile, not inherited from any single national tradition or transplanted institution); depth = **deep**
(a structural, lived daily condition, not a costume-level or surface detail). **Per the Sinheung Run 5
clarification** (`03` Phase 9D's own note), one strong emergent/deep finding is a good result on its own
merits, not something requiring artificial balancing against other findings.

## E. Swap test — run honestly, including where it may fail

**The axis itself (exposure-role cutting across kind) is somewhat generic to any hostile-environment
settlement** — this alone would not strongly differentiate Janbogo from a comparable polar city. **The
specific mechanism grounding it (Phase 1's G2 deficit — wind-chill/spindrift collapsing the ordinary
ambient-temperature safety heuristic) is more specific, but is explicitly shared, climate-identically, with
Zukelli** (Phase 5's own flagged near-duplicate risk — Zukelli's Specs states its climate is "not
meaningfully distinguishable from Janbogo's"). **Honest conclusion: this finding would likely NOT survive a
swap test against Zukelli specifically**, though it would very plausibly survive a swap against a
non-Terra-Nova-Bay comparable. **This does not kill the finding** — per `04` Gate 4, the value is in
recording which finding is weakest under swap, not in only reporting successes — but it sharpens the
already-flagged Gate 6 risk (Phase 5) into something concrete: **whatever differentiates Janbogo from
Zukelli will have to come from G4/G8 (founding, composition), not from this G2-rooted population-axis
finding**, which the two cities structurally share.
