## Cross-sign combinatorial synthesis — run once, by the coordinating session, holding all twelve results at once

**⭐⭐ Headline convergence: SEVEN of twelve independent signs, zero shared visibility, converged on the same
underlying institutional shape — an inherited, unrevised documentary authority paired with an informal,
peer-carried workaround layer that quietly patches its gaps without ever reconciling with it.**

- **Taurus** — the frozen founding manual (shadow: "first-impression lock," stillness curdling to stagnation)
  paired with living peer-taught craft knowledge as its counterweight.
- **Gemini** — an unwritten body of workaround-knowledge that "occasionally quietly contradicts" the inherited
  manuals, with nobody positioned to reconcile the two (Jupiter in detriment: "can retrieve anything and rank
  nothing").
- **Virgo** — the two ledgers: dockside manual vs. clifftop manifest, both Mercury-as-scribe, one never
  formally closed out ("unsigned work").
- **Sagittarius** — the manual held as de facto scripture (the sign's own core need for "a warrant to believe
  something... external and written down"), paired with an oral amendment layer that "outruns" it.
- **Capricorn** — the manual as a literal enactment of the sign's own Pricus myth (resetting to a configuration
  that no longer holds rather than revising it), paired with private veteran notebooks as an earned-access
  counterpart never merged into the official text.
- **Scorpio** — the uncredentialed "true keeper" whose real, undocumented authority fills exactly the gap the
  manual leaves and never formalizes.
- **Aquarius** — the annotated manual as the sign's own Saturn-code standing in for an absent external
  authority, paired explicitly with the unwritten workaround layer as "the same people," not two factions.

**This exceeds every prior convergence this methodology has produced** — Janbogo Run 9's six-sign convergence
(M-71) and Mountain Pass Airport Run 10's seven-sign chamber-departure convergence (M-76), which was itself
the prior record. **Seven independent agents, briefed only with this location's own established facts and
each sign's own registered file, with no visibility into each other's work and explicit instructions never to
reference Concordia's application of the signs, arrived at the identical two-part institutional shape**: this
port has exactly one written authority (a manual inherited from a dead institution, never revised) and exactly
one living authority (peer-taught workaround knowledge, never formalized) — and nothing anywhere reconciles
the two. **This is not a restatement of Phase 1's G4 finding (self-taught knowledge, no external institution)
— it is an independent, much sharper elaboration of it**, specifying a mechanism (two parallel, non-
communicating knowledge systems) that Phase 1 itself never derived. Recorded as this run's own strongest
single result, to be logged as a new M-number finding.

**⭐ Second convergence: SIX of twelve signs independently found deep, specific resonance in the port's own
already-established veteran-status mechanism** (Aries, Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Sagittarius, Capricorn) — each
reading it through a genuinely different registered lens (crisis-competence-with-a-hidden-physical-cost;
"standing that cannot be revoked"; sideways-entry-into-family; rank-blind private correction authority; a
generosity/mentorship register; an earned-access threshold) without producing the same specific finding twice.
**This is the strongest evidence this run's Phase 5d finding (Membership by Unremarked Persistence) is
genuinely load-bearing for this location**, not merely a plausible technique application — six independent
symbolic readings converge on treating it as real and structurally central, from six different angles, none
of which were told to expect it.

**Third convergence: FIVE signs (Aries, Cancer, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces) independently found the port's
own established schedule-protecting counterculture to be a genuinely rich site**, each supplying a distinct
elaboration (individualist self-possession; Uranus-in-Cancer's rejection of "any nest"; a Scorpio peer who
shields them out of earned loyalty; Capricorn's own core-need material naming exactly what they're
protecting; Pisces's own non-punitive standing economy). **This corroborates Phase 7d's finding that the
tension here is genuinely dual — both an organized minority and an unorganized private cost most workers
carry alone** — the five-sign spread supports both readings simultaneously rather than favoring one.

**Fourth convergence, weaker but worth naming: FOUR signs (Taurus, Virgo, Pisces, Aquarius) independently
found the crate-waste household-goods trade (Phase 7a's non-thematic export) meaningful**, each through
different registers (Taurus's "I have" motive resurfacing narrowly; Virgo's Venus-in-fall recovery; Pisces's
scavenger instinct; Aquarius's "what the flow carries away"). **Scorpio explicitly tested and rejected the
same detail** (too "unsolemn" for Scorpio's own exclusive, solemn economic register) — this divergence is
itself informative: the trade's defining texture (practical, unceremonious, unrelated to the port's formal
identity) is corroborated exactly as much by which signs it fits as by which one it doesn't.

**A genuine negative corroboration, worth recording alongside the positive convergences**: Aquarius explicitly
tested its own "equality trap" shadow (uniform treatment ignoring individual variation) against the port's
"never refuse at the point of contact" ethos and found the shapes did not match — a deliberate, reasoned
rejection rather than a missed connection, and useful confirmation that the port's own core ethos is
specifically about universal non-refusal, not about a failure to differentiate between cases.
