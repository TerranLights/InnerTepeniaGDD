# 7a · Work and economy

## The three tiers

`16` §18 is a dated developer determination specific to this city, and it governs.

| Tier | Share | On corrected Census I |
|---|--:|--:|
| **Baseline** — the civic load, D = 1.25, GROWER, food term 100% | **41.2%** | 357,244 |
| **Mandated** — technical/scientific, three-quarters of the 35% sector | **15.4%** | 133,533 |
| **Free — the character budget** | **43.4%** | 376,320 |

**Two reconciliations, recorded rather than quietly resolved.**

`09_Per_City_Baseline_Run.md` puts this city's baseline at **35.6%**, not 41.2%. That is not a contradiction
once both are read: `09` is dated 2026-09-01 and uses the old B1 food rate of 53, which `11` rebuilt to 120.7
the same day, and it does not apply the GROWER classification §18 carries. **§18 is later, city-specific, and
developer-ruled. It governs; `09`'s percentage for this city is superseded** — while everything in `09` §3.5
below, which does not depend on the food rate, stands.

And the headcounts everywhere in both files are **pre-−10%** (`M-199`). Corrected Census I is 568,786 humans
and 582,703 robots, so `workforce = robots + 0.5 × humans` = **867,096**, not the 963,441 printed. The
percentages are ratios and survive; the absolute numbers do not. All figures above are recomputed.

*(This also closes `M-198`: Half B's row reads 41.2 / 11.8 / 47.1 with `src: H`, an estimate; §18 reads
41.2 / 15.4 / 43.4 as a determination. **The baseline agrees in both.** Only the mandate split differs, and
the determination wins.)*

## ⭐ The marine sector is not fishing, and the arithmetic that proves it is already in canon

`16` §18 lists **Marine 15%** as FREE, describing it as *"fishing, and `04` §3 does not list Zhongshan among
the national fish providers."* The label comes from §15's *"Prydz Bay fishing and related industries."*

`11` establishes the test. Real fleets land ~250 t per fisher-year; Tepenia's entire sustainable national
catch is 3.0 Mt krill + 0.25 Mt finfish, needing **13,000 fishers nationally**. Read as fishing crews, the
canon marine sectors would land **53× what the Southern Ocean can yield**, and `11` resolves the identical
case at another city as *"a port, shipping, and marine-science sector"* — a category error, not a
contradiction.

**Run the same test here.** Marine is 15% of the distinctive tier (58.8% of 867,096 = 509,852), so **76,478
workers**. At 250 t each that is **19.1 Mt — 5.9× the entire national sustainable catch, from one city.** The
finding holds on the pre-−10% figures too (6.5×), so it does not depend on which census basis is used.

> **This city's marine sector cannot be fishing crews either. By `11`'s own resolution it is a port, shipping
> and marine-science sector — which is exactly the workforce Phases 5 and 6 needed and could not name.**

⚠ **Per the object-colonization rule, the sector is written at its full breadth first.** Marine at 15% means
harbor operation, cargo handling, vessel and mooring maintenance, hydrography and ice observation, marine
science, and the seasonal construction trade the CONSTRUCTED tier requires — **and a genuinely small fishing
component**, since `11` puts the food-capture fraction of such a sector in single digits. **The annual harbor
rebuild is a scoped specialization inside that sector, not the whole of it.** Writing the rebuild as the
sector would be the same error as pointing an entire heritage sector at one hut.

## The dual economy, and the part that is not optional

`City_Logistics.md`'s general section: what is shared is most services, transit, governance and commerce; what
is separate is **food — entirely — plus robot maintenance and repair as specialist industries.** This city runs

Census II 47.59%, the sign inverted). The robot half's cost is sized: **B3 maintenance 12.5 + B4 sustenance
3.25 = 15.75 per 1,000 robots**, inside the baseline above.

And `00`'s **C4** carries the developer's 2026-09-08 ruling — *"Tepenia doesn't have the option to 'export'
their waste"* — which makes materials recovery a closed loop rather than a service line: **every output is an
input to something, or it accumulates somewhere a person has to live next to.** The register notes C4 is still
*"absent corpus-wide."* It is present here at 5 per 1,000 residents and unwritten everywhere.

# 7b · Governance and decision

> The spine: *"The most productive question here is not who holds power but **what is unadministrable**."*

## The largest baseline industry in this city is record-keeping

`00` §A is emphatic, and it is a real-world inversion rather than an import. Remote settlements run *low*
healthcare and *high* administration — Svalbard under 2% health, ~50% public sector; Nunavut 60% — because
**"allocation, rationing, permitting, registry, and logistics coordination are what make remote habitation
possible at all. It is not bureaucratic bloat; it is life support with a filing cabinet."**

**C3 Administration is 65 per 1,000 residents — the single largest rate in the entire baseline**, larger than
food, health, transport or construction. And the register's own list of what it covers includes **registry**.

## And this city's defining civic law is that nobody asks

`City_Origin_Factions_Second_Interwar.md`, "The Larsemann Hills Compact":

> *"Zhongshan's defining civic law is a **strict non-prying norm — nobody asks about your past, ever**, and
> hospitality is ceremony rather than personal warmth. In a cluster this small and this permanent, Zhongshan's
> read is that privacy is the only thing that makes proximity survivable over two centuries."*

> ## The city's largest single industry is the keeping of records about people, and its defining civic law forbids anyone from asking about them.

**Both are true at once, and neither is a violation of the other.** The registry is administrative and
necessary — nobody can be housed, rationed, warmed or treated without it. The norm is social. **The city knows
who you are and does not discuss it.** What is unadministrable here is not the record; it is the question.

That is also a prohibition with a peculiar shape. The spine distinguishes a prohibition — *"a chosen policy
with an author"* — from grudging tolerance. Canon calls this a **civic law**, and names no legislator, no
penalty and no enforcing body. **It is a law in the sense that everyone obeys it, and in no other sense.**

**Three separate domains — researched, constitutional, and social — and the same mechanism in all three.** This
city is not under-governed. It is governed by expectation, consistently, at every level anyone has looked at.

# 7c · Knowledge and transmission

## The thing this city cannot teach

`National_Medical_and_Care_Institutes.md` is canon and unambiguous. Care delivery cannot be centralized —
**"every city staffs its own clinicians, always"** — and Tepenia cannot import a trained one, because there is
no off-continent residency and no outside profession to recruit from. Therefore **every medic, nurse, field
responder and robot-care technician in the Federation was trained at one of three institutes.**

**None of the three is here.** And the third — the Sinheung Institute of Cybernetics and Robotic Care, whose
subject is *"the physical AND EMOTIONAL care of, and for, robots"* and which canon calls **the mental-health
system of the national majority** — is in the city across the fjord, 450 m from this city's own shore.

> **A city whose mandated national export is technical people cannot train a single one of the people who look
> after its own.** Half its population is robot; the discipline that tends them is taught by the neighbor that
> also holds port dominion.

Education here is 7% and free — schooling and trade training, not professional formation. That is the whole of
what this city passes on internally. **It makes engineers and researchers and sends them away; it receives its
clinicians made.**

## What becomes of the ones who cannot learn it

⚠ **Derived, and marked as such.** The mandate is the largest in the city and the identity is, in the
developer's words, *"fundamentally"* scientific. Not everyone in a population of 1.15 million completes that
formation.

The non-prying norm decides what happens next, and it cuts both ways. **Nobody asks why you left the track**,
which is the kindest possible treatment of a private failure — and it is also the reason nothing follows it.
There is no conversation in which someone notices, and therefore no mechanism by which anyone is redirected.
**The same rule that spares you the question withholds the help the question would have started.**

# 7d · Private life, minorities, and counterculture

> The spine: **check canon for an existing counterculture before deriving one.** Canon has one, and deriving a
> different one would have been the error this instruction exists to prevent.

## It is already written

Same source, the same passage's closing argument:

> *"Whether Zhongshan's reserve is wisdom earned through two centuries of proximity or **a habit that's quietly
> cost it some of its own smaller heritage communities, who feel slowly absorbed rather than protected by the
> non-prying norm**."*

**The pressure is reserve. The population it does not fit is the smaller heritage communities.** That is
exactly the derivation the spine asks for — *what does this place require of everyone, and who cannot or will
not give it?* — and canon did it first.

## Which of the three kinds it is, checked deliberately

The spine warns against defaulting to refusal, and names a rarer third option: **demanding the place's own
stated rule be applied more literally than the mainstream applies it.**

**This is that one.** These communities are not refusing reserve and not performing something the city cannot.
Phase 6 established the city's standard of worth: *nothing here ever had to start over* — continuity, held in
the station, the site claim, the unbroken research heritage. **The heritage communities are asking for the
city's own standard to be extended to them.** The city applies continuity to its institutions and forbids the
one question that would apply it to people.

## And Phase 2 supplies the mechanism

Phase 2 found **18 nations summing to exactly 100%**, one at 35.83%, a 26.13-point gap to second, and then
**twelve nations packed inside a 2.36-point band.** Twelve communities of nearly identical size, none large
enough to be self-evident.

> **A norm forbidding anyone to ask where you are from is precisely the instrument that turns twelve
> distinguishable communities into one undifferentiated remainder** — not by suppressing anything, and with
> nobody intending it. Their origins are in the registry; C3 is the largest industry in the city. **They are
> documented and not discussed.** That is the whole of "absorbed rather than protected."

## Is the dissent a population, or is it inside each person?

The spine requires the check. **Here, honestly, it is both**, and the second half is what keeps it from being a
faction.

It is a population — canon names them. But the norm is genuinely protective as well as erosive, and it is
protective *of the same people*: in a cluster this dense, being un-askable is a real shelter. **So the same
resident can want the norm kept and want their own history known**, and there is no coherent position that
delivers both. That is why it has stayed a two-century argument rather than a movement: **the contradiction
runs through each person, so there is nobody for a faction to organize against.**

**Kept sympathetic, not criminal.** Nobody here is breaking a rule. They are people the rule does not fit.

## Sanction proportionality

Priced in this location's physical conditions, as required. This is a functioning GROWER city with a food term
of 100%, three highways, a shared airport and a shared port. **Exclusion here is not lethal and would be wildly
disproportionate to a social breach.**

⚠ **Derived, and lightly:** canon says hospitality here *"is ceremony rather than personal warmth."* A city
whose warmth is formal has a sanction ready-made and costless — **the ceremony continues and nothing behind it
does.** No exclusion, no penalty, nothing anyone could appeal, and nothing anyone could point to. It fits both
the physical conditions and a civic law that has no enforcing body.

Checked against `04`: this is not "bureaucratic," not "secretive," not "insular." **The record exists and is
complete. The prohibition is on asking, not on knowing.**
