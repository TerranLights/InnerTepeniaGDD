# ⭐⭐⭐ HEADLINE FINDING

> # **Nobody's presence at Davis was ever decided about them individually — and the one clause that made the two populations different is the same clause that guaranteed they would live in the same houses.**

**Three sourced legs, none of them inferred:**

1. **Article II.2.2** — *"This provision applies to robots as a class. No individual designation, accusation, or finding of wrongdoing is required, sought, or relevant to its application; nor shall the absence of any such finding be grounds for exception."*
2. **Article II.3.2** — the population *"departing under Article II.2 shall be settled at such existing scientific and research installations as are presently found upon that continent, **each population being assigned to a station**."*
3. ⭐⭐ **Article II.2.3** — a human may accompany the robot population only if they have *"taken up residence among robot communities, married or partnered with a robot, borne or raised children in a robot household, or otherwise established their life among the robot population."*

**The third is the one nothing in this pass had opened.** ***The founding instrument's eligibility criterion for a human being at Davis is a documented relationship with a robot.*** **So the human/robot line at Davis is not, at the founding, a line between two communities. It is a line running through the middle of households — by law, on day one, with no exceptions available.**

### And what does that cause — asked more than once

- **First order.** Davis's founding human population is not a population of individuals. It is a population of **halves of mixed households**, admitted on that basis and on no other.
- **Second order.** The census's two columns are therefore **not two neighborhoods.** Whatever else `DR-4`'s deferral has cost this pass, it cannot have hidden a residential segregation at the founding, because the admission criterion forbade one.
- ⭐⭐ **Third order.** When the orbital destination opens and the city retains **77.61% of its humans against 57.87% of its robots — a spread of 19.74 pp** — **that spread does not run between two separated groups. It runs through homes.** A differential exit rate between two populations the founding instrument made co-resident *by definition* is a differential exit rate inside families.
- ⭐⭐⭐ **Fourth order.** And `02` §8.1 has already established what Davis does with a structural charge: *"Davis converts structural charges into character judgements about persons — authorlessly, in complete good faith, and invisibly from inside."* ⇒ **A departure that splits a household is exactly the class of event Davis's own method cannot adjudicate** — fire #1 records that the census gives *a destination, not a gate*, so there is **no entry to check.** ***In a city whose characteristic argument ends with "show me the entry," the largest thing that has ever happened to its families left no entry.***
- ⚠ ⛔ **AND THE PHASE STOPS THERE.** **What Davis says about such a household, or whether it has a word for one, is Phase 6 and Phase 9 material and is NOT written here.** *Stating the constraint is legal; converting it into a practice is foreclosure.*
