### Note A-1 · The Type, and what it obliges the later phases to ask

- **Why dual, and why it is one piece of evidence, not two.** The Installation half and the *resettled*
  modifier rest on the same admitted spans (spec L150 chars 362–612 and 717–786, via CON L75–78). They are two
  questions pointed at one fact, not two independent confirmations of it.
- **The inflection this Installation half carries**, stated as a question the type obliges and not as a finding:
  01's Installation question is *"What happens to people who live inside somebody else's purpose?"* (01 L102).
  For Mirny the admitted input gives **a purpose-built structure and its paperwork that persisted, and a
  controlling institution that did not** (CON L142–143: "no living institutional knowledge survived the
  operator chain"). Whether an in-frame Tepenian institution later ran the site's installation-like functions (the
  relay, the quarry-to-chamber supply chain, CON L388) is not in the admitted input → **R-10** (§G).
- **The resettled question in its GPS-legal form** (01 L135): *what did the founding population inherit, misread or
  fail to notice* — asked only of **the persistent materials** (DRL L341–346, L376–380), **never** of the prior
  occupants' nationality or character (DRL L354).
- **Modifiers considered and NOT assigned** (none admitted ≠ none possible): *enclosed* — no admitted envelope; the
  airlock detail (spec L162) is excluded (CON L335), and a wind-shedding ring is not an envelope · *orbital*,
  *mobile* — no · *ruined* — the only ruin material is post-war (spec L4, L206–L208; CON L150–151, L361) · *contested*
  — the founding contradiction was a source defect, settled by DR-9, not an in-world contest · *seasonal /
  rotational* — the population is permanent; only sea access is seasonal (CON L390) · *restricted / sacred* — none
  admitted.
- **Types considered and NOT assigned:** *Network locus* — the relay is physical hardware in this city; an Arcanet
  region would be a different location (RB L1962). *Polity* — the treaty draft's local-council clause (FTD L37) is
  unratified and would not displace Settlement as the primary type.
