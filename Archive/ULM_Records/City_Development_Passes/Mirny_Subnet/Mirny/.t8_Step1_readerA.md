## 3. Row detail where the matrix can't hold the evidence

L230 says the composition "bears no real resemblance to the Russian station/ship the current name and founding story are built around." In-world, the file writes only the continuity reading (L154) and the resonance reading (L216). Nothing is written about how the name sits with a population the file itself makes mostly non-Russian. Gate 9 asks this question. This step does not answer it, and the rename stays deferred per L230.

**Row 4 (war damage).** Every consequence of the status change is open: infrastructure, population and relay (L208), governance (L223), whether the solstice tradition "has changed since the Long Night War" (L224), and whether the marker survives (L225). The favorable description of the city (L162–L176) was written for the undamaged city, and nothing connects it to the damaged one. This is a post-war status change (Q1).

**Row 7 (Hwy 110).** Both states are left open, and that part is symmetric. The asymmetry is in the valuation. L144 and L222 weigh the road for Concordia ("the most strategically significant open question about Mirny is now the physical one", L222, weighed as "the actual mechanism connecting the two cities"). The file also gives Mirny road-dependent flows: "raw materials trucked in from quarries to the south" and materials "for the eastern highways" (L168), plus a "near-exclusive top-tier" supply role (L176). It never says which roads carry them. I have not written that connection. Step 1 audits and does not write new findings (RB L2530).

**Row 8 (refugees).** The file does not say when or why they left. "pre-war connections" (L202) implies a war but does not name one, and I have not inferred one.

**Row 11 (Antarctic Circle), recorded but not scored.** This is the city's defining threshold ("the geographic threshold of the polar zone", L8; "For practical purposes, Mirny is on the line", L53). The gate's question of "someone it decides against" has no subject here. If the developer rules that the gate covers the civic use of the threshold (Q3), the inherited material shows this:
- L164 writes one side as "the threshold": "The winter solstice sun-grazing is observed as a threshold: the shortest light, the most marginal sun, the day that proves the city exists in the space between the polar world and the world outside it."
- The corrected geography puts the two solstices on opposite sides of the polar zone. In winter, "**there is no polar night here at all.**" In summer, "**~29 days of midnight sun.**" (L55; see also L85–L86)
- L164's "neither fully inside the polar zone nor fully outside it" was written before that correction.
- No civic treatment of the summer side is written in L164. L138 and L183 name both solstices only as events and a site, and their content is TBD (L224).
