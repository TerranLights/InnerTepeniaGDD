**Chain G2-a: the lifted snow**
1. Snow arrives on the wind rather than out of the sky (L76–L79), and whiteout comes under a clear sky (L82).
2. The sky stops being a forecast. Ground-level visibility depends on surface wind and on how much loose snow
   lies upwind, not on cloud. A person at a window on a clear morning cannot tell whether the next hour outdoors
   is survivable.
3. Whether it is safe to go out becomes a matter of measurement or report, not of looking. The first place a
   whiteout can be seen is upwind: south and southeast (L71). **The eye loses authority; whoever reads the wind
   upstream gains it.**
   - *Step 3 target:* whether a lifted-snow front can be observed upwind in advance here, and with what lead
     time. Do not assume one.

**Chain G2-b: the lee**
1. The wind strips open ground, which is why the coast stays rocky and exposed (L57, L78).
2. What the wind carries, it drops wherever the flow slows: behind anything that stands up. This is general
   drift physics; the site pattern is a Step 3 target. Open ground stays clear; built ground collects snow.
3. **Mirny's snow load is set by what Mirny builds, not by the weather alone.** Every new structure is a new
   drift. The clearing burden grows with the built extent.

**Chain G2-c: half a polar year**
1. No polar night, and ~29 days of midnight sun.
2. Winter keeps a short day with a sunrise, every day. It is summer that loses the daily cycle, and summer
   (calm, warm, open water) is when outside work is possible.
3. **The natural stop that darkness provides is there in the hard season and missing in the easy one.** In
   Mirny, rest is what has to be imposed by people rather than by the sky. It has to be imposed exactly when the
   pressure to keep working is highest.

**Chain G2-d: light and cold out of phase**
1. The winter is a five-month plateau within 1.0 °C. The coldest mean month is August (−16.4), and the record
   low, −36.5 °C, is also in August (L70, L114). That is two months after the June solstice.
2. After June the light returns, but conditions do not improve until October (−13.4) and November (−6.8). The sea
   stays shut until the October breakup (L116).
3. **At Mirny the solstice turns the light and nothing else.** Anything that marks midwinter as a turning point
   is making a promise the next three months will not keep.
   - Demoted L224 (a solstice tradition) is read here only as a prompt. It grounds nothing.

   running machine, and the unwritten parts came back only through failure.**
   - Candidate for later phases, not a finding: in Mirny, "why do we do it this way?" has two honest answers,
     "the record says so" and "we tried otherwise and it broke." There is no answer of the form "the people
     before us did it this way", because at the founding there were no people before.
   - *Flag:* this chain lands near a conclusion in an excluded span of L150. It was derived from the admitted

1. Three nations' exiles founded the city together.
2. No one stock could hold the place as the continuation of its own origin by right of founding. What the three

3. **The first shared ground was operational rather than ancestral.** Belonging entered through practice, not
   lineage.

**Chain G5-b: stone up the wind** *(depends on G2-S4, so it is not an independent agreement)*

## 5. Conflicts: the both-are-true test (§5.3)

**C1. Connected vs closed** (G5-S1/S3 vs G2-D1/D3)
- *The two objects:* signals, and bodies and goods. **Both stand.**
- Mirny can be in contact with every place in its subnet on a day when it cannot safely put anyone outdoors
  (L131, admitted part) or bring a ship in (L143).
- The place the region talks through is, on some days, the place nobody can reach.

**C2. Continuous obligations vs seasonal access** (G5-C1/C2 vs G2 seasonality)
- *The two scales:* the relay and the stone are owed year-round; safe outdoor access is seasonal. The strong
  katabatic season runs May–Aug (L111–L114), with katabatics intensifying from Feb (L108) and moderating from
  Sep (L115). **Both stand.**
- Outdoor work owed to others is either done in the dangerous season or put off into the calm one, and the calm
  one is the nightless one (G2-c). Feeds shadow candidate (a) in §10.

**C3. Wind as gift vs wind as cost** (G2-S1 vs G2-C2)
