## 2. The findings that fire

### A1: War damage ("Damaged; partially operational")
- **What the file says.** L4: `**Status:** Damaged; partially operational`. L208: "Mirny was damaged during the Long Night War but remains partially operational."
- **What is missing.** The war decided against part of the city, but the file writes only that some of it survived. The part that was lost is left explicitly blank. L208: "The extent of its survival — what infrastructure is functioning, what population remains, whether the subnet relay hardware is operational — is TBD". L223: "who is there and what are they doing? TBD".
- **The city's roles are written only in their undamaged state.** That covers the subnet hub (L174: "its role as the Mirny subnet hub"), the quarry-to-construction engine (L168, L174), and the "near-exclusive top-tier industrial/quarrying hub" that supplies Sinheung's chamber manufacturing (L176). The file does not say what the damage did to any of them.
- **Durable? Route back?** How long the damage lasts is not written. No repair, resettlement or return is written.
- **Open question for the pass:** *What did the damage take, from whom, and can any of it be recovered?*

### A2: The Split Brain cut
- **What the file says.** L140: "the Split Brain severed all inter-subnet Arcanet links without exception". L198: "Concordia has access only to Janbogo subnet records, same as every other subnet is cut off from every other." L200: "it is a road, not a communication channel."
- **Whose side is written.** Every mention (L8, L140, L184, L198, L214, L222) is a correction note about Concordia's link. None is written from the side of anyone in Mirny who was cut off.
- **The people it cut off are in the file, but never connected to it.** L202 puts Mirny refugees in Concordia. L198 says Concordia can reach only Janbogo subnet records. The file never says what that means for those refugees (it only shows up by reading L198 and L202 together).
- **Durable? Route back?** "without exception" (L140, L198) says how wide the cut is, not whether it is permanent. No route back is written, and L200 rules the road out as a way to carry information.
- **Candidate shadow.** By `G10` :: L39 this is a **candidate textbook shadow**: a cut with no written route back, which the file treats only as a correction to get right, never as a problem for anyone in Mirny. The label is a candidate, not a verdict; the pass decides.
- **Open question for the pass:** *Is the cut permanent? What do Mirny's own people lose across it, and does anything get across at all?*

### A3: The relay hardware threshold, and the hub status that depends on it
- **What the file says.** L184: "if it fails, the whole Mirny subnet loses contact with itself, not with Concordia". So the failure case is written, but only for the subnet as a whole.
- **What is missing: Mirny's own standing.** The file never says what failure does to Mirny itself. Its hub status is written only for the case where the hardware works: L5 "subnet hub", L166 "the subnet's industrial and communications hub", L174 "its role as the Mirny subnet hub".
- **Durable? Route back?** How long a failure would last is not written. Only prevention is written: L192 (a placeholder figure) is "the current administrator keeping the damaged subnet relay hardware functional post-war". Meanwhile L208 leaves "whether the subnet relay hardware is operational" as TBD. Nothing is written about restoring the hardware after a failure.
- **Open question for the pass:** *If the relay goes down, is Mirny still the hub? Can it be brought back, and by whom?*

### A4: Whether Hwy 110 is passable
- **What the file says.** L140: "whether the highway itself is passable post-war is TBD". L222 calls it "the most strategically significant open question about Mirny".
- **Only one case has consequences.** L144: "a passable Hwy 110 spur would be Concordia's most direct physical supply/evacuation route to an external city". The impassable case has no consequences written.
- **Mirny's side is empty.** Neither case is written from Mirny's point of view. L144 is Concordia's view. That is relation, which is allowed, but it leaves Mirny's own stake unwritten.
- **Durable? Route back?** Neither is written for the impassable case.
- **Open question for the pass:** *If the spur is cut, what does Mirny lose, and can it be reopened?*

### A5: Refugees
- **What the file says.** L202 says "Mirny refugees in Concordia exist" and that the community is spread out.
- **What is missing.** This is the only place the file names people the war decided against. It says they exist and where they are. It does not say whether their displacement is permanent or whether they can go home.
- **Both possible routes home are blank in this same file.** The physical route is Hwy 110, which is TBD (L140, L222). The informational route is the Arcanet, which is cut (L198).
- **Open question for the pass:** *Can Mirny's refugees go home, or even find out what happened to it?*

### A6: Founded by exile under the Falkland Treaty
- **What the file says.** L150: "Settled: Post-Falkland Treaty." … "Russian exiles built on the genuinely substantial, functional station that remained, and developed their own scientific and operational culture from there, independently." L152: "Founding population: Primarily Russian exiles alongside a broader mix of communities in the East Antarctic coastal area."
- **Two mechanisms, one side each.** The file names a verdict (exile) and an admission (settlement under the treaty). Both are written only from the favorable side: what the site offered the people who arrived.
- **What is missing.** Nothing says what exile cost the founders, whether it was permanent, or whether there was any way back. Nothing says whom the treaty admitted here and whom it did not.
- **This finding depends on another one.** The file contradicts itself about who founded the city (§5, O2). The pass has to settle who was exiled here before it can write the other side of A6.
- **Open question for the pass:** *What did exile cost the people who founded this city, and was it for life?*
