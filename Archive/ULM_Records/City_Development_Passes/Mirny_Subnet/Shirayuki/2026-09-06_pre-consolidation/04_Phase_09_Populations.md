# C · INTER-POPULATION — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the local inflection, and it is a different axis entirely**

> **`03` C: *"Restating the setting's baseline is not a finding. The strongest available move is that the place
> reorganizes its population along a DIFFERENT AXIS entirely — ask **is there a category here that matters more
> than kind?**"***

**The national baseline** *(canon)*: **"egalitarian, skewed in favor of robots"** — roughly equal labor
division, robots holding slightly better positions, genuine mutual appreciation. ⛔ **Restating that is not a
finding.**

> # **THE CATEGORY THAT MATTERS MORE THAN KIND HERE IS: HOW WELL YOU KEEP THINGS.**
>
> **Phase 7 §7c found the shadow — *if standing comes from how well you keep things, someone whose hands are
> poor has no route to standing*.** ⭐⭐⭐ ***And that axis is KIND-BLIND.*** **A human with good hands and a
> robot with good hands stand identically. A clumsy robot and a clumsy human are equally unremarked.**
> **The inter-kind question then answers itself underneath: kind is not the salient category, because a more
> salient one is running.**

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND FUNDAMENTAL LAW 2 GUARANTEES THE AXIS CUTS BOTH WAYS

> **U `Laws_of_Robotics.md`, Law 2:** ***"A robot will understand that anything she is capable of doing to
> others, it is also realistically possible for others to do to her."***

**This is not a safety rule. It is a law of mutual vulnerability and reciprocity, hard-coded as civic
procedure by robots themselves.** ⭐⭐ **Applied to a city that quietly grades people by their hands:**

> ## ***THE GRADERS ARE THE GRADED. NOBODY CAN STAND OUTSIDE THE AXIS, AND EVERYONE KNOWS IT.***
> ⭐ **This does not dissolve the shadow — it explains why it has no villains** *(`00d`'s hardest requirement)*.
> **Everyone is exposed to the same judgment they are making, and Law 2 means they understand that they are.**
> ⭐⭐ **It also explains why nothing organizes against it** *(`04_Phase_07` §7d)*: **you cannot form a faction
> against a standard you are also applying.**

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ H · TWO DEVELOPER RULINGS ARRIVED AFTER THIS PHASE WAS WRITTEN — **and they unlock material this pass has been refusing for eleven pieces**

## H.1 — The sequencing ruling, and the GPS law's actual PURPOSE

> ***"Once the various nationality-origins, and their proportionate-percentage compositions, have been
> established… THEN it's acceptable to take into consideration their origin-ethnicities, ethnic-cultures,
> etc."***
> ***"You shall NOT incorporate 'Russian heritage' into the establishment of the city of Shirayuki… Once the
> population composition has [been] established, then, yes, you can start incorporating aspects of Japanese
> culture, traditions, history, social norms… because **Shirayuki is a fundamentally Japanese city**. THAT'S
> the purpose of the 'GPS purposes only' Law."***

| ⛔ What the law blocks | ✅ What it never blocked |
|---|---|
| **the real SITE's operator nationality** — *Bharati's builders, and by the same rule Progress's and Zhongshan's for the neighbors* | ⭐⭐ **THE CITY'S OWN ETHNIC CHARACTER, which comes from its FOUNDING POPULATION and is canon** |

> ## ***THE LAW EXISTS SO A CITY IS CHARACTERIZED BY WHO LIVES THERE, NOT BY WHOSE STATION IT OCCUPIES.***
> ✅ **This pass obeyed the blocked half correctly** — *`04_Phase_02` refused the Bharati line entirely, per the
> spec's own CANON NOTE.* ⛔ **And it over-applied the other half: composition here has been settled since
> 2026-07-06, so ethnic-cultural material was admissible from Phase 2 onward and was not used.**

## H.2 — ⭐⭐⭐ THE DIVERGENCE PRINCIPLE — **and it answers the question this phase asked**

**I asked whether robots carry origin-culture personally, offering three readings.** ***The answer is none of
the three: the axis I chose was wrong.***

> ***"This nuance actually depends partially upon WHEN a robot was built, in addition to WHERE, and to that
> extent, **the principle applies to humans as well**… after enough generations, [people] will develop into
> new, unique cultures shaped by their surroundings, their hardships and struggles, their common life
> objectives."***
> ***"~250 years since these people's ancestors actually moved to Antarctica. Their cultures will be
> different."*** *— with Québécois, Canadian, American, Australian and Singaporean given as the real cases.*

> # **COMPOSITION NAMES THE STOCK. TIME AND PLACE PRODUCE THE CULTURE.**
> **Operator inputs, the developer's own list:** ***time · separation · local environmental setting · local
> struggles and hardships · local goals · local sensibilities and habits.***
> ⛔ **Neither extreme:** *refusing ethnic material gives a placeless city; transplanting Japanese culture
> intact is `Step 3.5`'s "costumed version of somewhere real."*

### ⚠ WHAT THIS MEANS FOR §A–§G ABOVE

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **The robot/human axis I built the question on is SECONDARY** | ***The primary axis is TIME.*** *"While robots live longer, if they're built in Tepenia and also learn from the humans and other robots they're in the community of, their cultures will be different as well."* ⭐ **So `WHEN` a robot was built matters as much as `WHERE`** |
| ✅ **§C, §D and the swap tests STAND** | *They rest on the merger, Law 2, the keptness axis and Ch. 13 — none of which the rulings touch* |
| ⛔ **§A's lens needs one amendment** | **The lens is still *the built environment under a shared exclusion* — but the STOCK it operates on is now nameable: a Japan-founded city, 250 years diverged** |

### ⭐⭐⭐ AND IT HANDS THE PASS A GATE 6 TEST IT DID NOT HAVE

> ⛔ **If a later pass produces similar-shaped Japanese-descended cultures for both, at least one is wrong.**
> ⭐ **The divergence principle is therefore an ANTI-CONVERGENCE ENGINE, not a license to converge.**
