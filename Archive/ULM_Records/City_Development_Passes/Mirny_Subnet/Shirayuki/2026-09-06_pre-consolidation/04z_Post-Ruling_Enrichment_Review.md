# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 1 · THE SINGLE BIGGEST DISCOVERY — **the grievance has no PARTIES by Act 2**

**`01_Inherited.md` §4 A1 found the pass's strongest asymmetry: the Jeju-do allocation admitted Japan and
refused China, and I wrote that the refusal has *"no route back — the deciding body has no successor with
standing over this place,"* so it *"can only be lived, and it is lived at fifteen kilometers."***

## ⭐ What the Acts add — and it is not a refinement, it is a different ending

| | |
|---|---|
| **The Jeju-do decision happened** | ⭐ **BEFORE Act 1** — *on Upper Earth, pre-exile, between **Upper Earth nations***  |
| **The parties were** | **Japan and the Sinian Federation** — *national actors, in a world that still had them* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **By Act 2** | ***Those categories do not exist in Tepenia.*** **There is no "Japanese population" and no "Chinese population" here — there are Tepenians, whose ancestry differs** |

> # **THE GRIEVANCE DOES NOT GET RESOLVED. IT LOSES ITS PARTIES.**
> ***An exclusion enacted between two nations, inherited by two populations who have stopped being those
> nations, becomes a grievance nobody is standing on either side of.***
>
> ⭐⭐ **This is STRONGER than "no appellate body," which is what I had.** *A grievance with no court is still a
> grievance.* ***A grievance with no plaintiff and no defendant is a historical fact about a place.***
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And it explains something `04_Phase_05` could only date:** *the Tri-Cities merged `~2780s`, deep in
> Act 2* — **you cannot merge with a city you are still nationally aggrieved at, and by then nobody was.**

⚠ **AND THE ASYMMETRY CHECK STILL FIRES — differently.** ⛔ *The inverse is no longer "what became of the
refused party."* ⭐ **It is: *what becomes of a foundational injury when the people who could feel it have
become someone else?*** **Written as a Phase 6/9 question, not answered here.**

**`04_Phase_02` §E.2 found that the table is proportional, so it shows the national mix as *identical* across
a `38%` loss — *"the one demographic event in this city's history is invisible in the one document that
describes its demographics."***

## ⭐⭐⭐ The Acts add a second, independent defect in the same instrument

> **`Japan 36.27% · Australia 8.76 · Russia 8.61 · South Korea 7.89 · China 7.18…`** ***By the time most of
> this frame is running, those are ANCESTRY LABELS being used as if they were identities.***

| The record | What it fails at |
|---|---|
| **Proportional** | ⛔ **cannot show the migration** *(the original finding)* |
| ⭐ **National-tier** | ⛔⛔ **cannot show the Act transition either** — *it describes an Act-2 city in the vocabulary its people had stopped using* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ SO THE INSTRUMENT IS SILENT ABOUT BOTH OF THE CITY'S TWO GREAT CHANGES.
> **`03_Research.md` §D established that this population *"perceives the symptom and cannot name the
> cause."*** ***Here is the second reason why: the only record it has speaks a language about who its people's
> ancestors were, not about who its people are.***
> ⚠ **Corpus-wide artifact, not a Shirayuki property** — *every city's table has it.* ⭐ **What is specific is
> that this city has the largest hidden departure in its oasis.**

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 3 · THE SEAM IS ALSO A TIME BOUNDARY — **and the only old things are the Act-1 things**

**`04_Phase_03` §D found the seam is a GROUND boundary: the founding station sits on ROCK; everything after
sits on ICE, and the boundary is re-asserted every rebuild.** **`03_Research.md` §C: *nothing on ice gets to
be old.***

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **THE FOUNDING BUILD IS ON ROCK, BECAUSE THAT IS WHERE A STATION GOES. SO THE PHYSICALLY OLDEST GROUND CARRIES THE ONLY PHYSICALLY OLD THINGS — AND THOSE ARE ALL ACT-1 THINGS.**
>
> **`~9–13%` of this city can hold antiquity. The other `87–91%` structurally cannot.**
> ⭐⭐ **So the rock is not merely the secure ground and the class line** *(`Extent` §10)* — ***it is the only
> place the city's ethnic-fresh era physically survives.***

⭐⭐⭐ **AND IT SHARPENS THE STRATIFICATION FINDING.** *`Extent` §10: "an inherited address becomes inherited
security."* ***An inherited address on this city's rock is also an inherited address in Act 1*** — **the one
place where the founding generation's own built world is still standing.** ⚠ **`[DERIVED]`** — *canon gives
the ground split and the Acts separately; the alignment is this pass's.*

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 · "NOTHING HERE IS OLD" IS TRUE IN **BOTH** SENSES — **and I only had one**

**`04_Phase_06` §A's proposition — *"Nothing here is old. Things are only well-kept"* — was derived entirely
from PHYSICAL facts: ice, grit, no shore, no antiquity from the founding.**

## ⭐ The Acts supply a second, cultural derivation of the same sentence

| This city cannot reach a past, in four independent ways |
|---|
| ⛔ **Physically** — `87–91%` on ice; nothing outdoors is ever finished, only recently attended to |
| ⛔ **Foundationally** — *the claim was settled elsewhere by people who never came* |
| ⛔ ⭐ **Ethnically** — *by Act 2 the origin culture is **ancestry, not identity**. The deep past is real and is no longer THEIRS in the first person* |
| ⛔ ⭐⭐ **And the one era that IS theirs — Act 1 — survives only on the `9–13%` that is rock** *(§3)* |

> # ***THE PROPOSITION IS NOT ABOUT OBJECTS. IT IS THE CITY'S WHOLE RELATIONSHIP TO THE PAST.***
> **A place with no reachable antiquity of any kind will find its standard of worth in the only temporal
> register available to it: *the recent, the attended-to, the currently-kept*.**
> ⭐⭐ **This makes the Phase 6 finding considerably stronger and it survives the portability check even better
> than before** — *Sinheung and Zhongshan hold their shores from 2564 and their rock frontage still; **their
> Act 1 is not confined to a tenth of their ground.***

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 · THE FOUNDING ARGUMENT DID NOT GO UNREHEARSED — **IT EXPIRED**

**`02_Spine.md` §2b: *"a city that never had to argue for itself has no rehearsed account of why it should
exist, and when one is eventually needed there is nothing to reach for."***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ THE ACTS MAKE THIS SHARPER AND SADDER.
> ***The only argument this city was ever handed was a NATIONAL one*** — *a Japanese claim, settled against a
> Chinese one, by an Upper Earth court.*
> ## **AND BY ACT 2 THAT ARGUMENT IS NOT MERELY UNPRACTICED. IT IS UNAVAILABLE IN PRINCIPLE.**
> **You cannot make a national-origin claim in a country where national origin has become ancestry.**

⭐ **So the deficit is not a skill the city failed to learn. It is a title deed written in a language that
stopped being legal tender.** ⭐⭐ **And `03_Research.md` §J's Unrecognized Instrument lands harder for it:**
***the city built a new account of itself — the upkeep — precisely because the old one expired, and it never
noticed it was doing so.***
