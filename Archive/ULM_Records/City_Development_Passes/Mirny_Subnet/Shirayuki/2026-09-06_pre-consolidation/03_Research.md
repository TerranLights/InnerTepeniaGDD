## R-1 · **PRIORITY 6** *(new at RE-RUN 3)* — abrasive particulate against MACHINE bodies

⭐⭐ **This was `N-5`'s generating question and it is now answered, and the answer is a COMPOUNDING MECHANISM

|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Wind-borne grit permanently damages lenses and windows *"especially in areas with no snow cover,"* turning them to *frosted glass*** | Antarctic operational practice |
