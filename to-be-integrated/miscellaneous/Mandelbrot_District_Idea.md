# Mandelbrot Idea — Held Open

**Status:** unintegrated, deliberately tentative. Raised 2026-07-09 during the Concordia district Megasheet pass.

The developer wants to work a Mandelbrot set into the game somewhere in Concordia, possibly as a holographic representation. No district has been chosen, and it isn't yet decided whether it should belong to one specific district at all — explicitly left open until more of the project is built out and the right home becomes clearer.

## Candidates discussed, neither chosen

- **Pisces (The Markets)** — the stronger thematic fit. Pisces' own established Syncretic Religion centers on "the dissolution of the boundary between individual and collective identity"; a Mandelbrot's infinite self-similarity (the whole endlessly echoed in every part) reads as a near-perfect visual language for that specific doctrine. Dream-tech dens are an existing, natural venue for a holographic version.
- **Aquarius (The Labs)** — the more expected/literal fit as the experimental-science district, but reads more as a math/research Easter egg than something earned by an in-world belief system.

## Revisit when

- A future district (especially whichever of the above, or the eventual Concordia Ultra-Megasheet) surfaces a natural opening for it
- The developer brings up "the Mandelbrot idea" again
