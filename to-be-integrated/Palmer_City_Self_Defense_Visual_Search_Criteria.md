## Full Description

The concept is a woman who is warm, pleasant, and comes across as genuinely loving — someone who looks completely at home around humans and in a lively, social setting like Palmer City — but who is also capable of defending herself in an instant, up to and including lethal force, if the moment demanded it. That capability should sit quietly underneath her presentation, not visible on the surface. She should not look tough, gritty, hardened, or combat-worn — none of that reads as "at home in Palmer City" or as someone humans feel drawn to and safe around. Instead, her warmth should be the dominant, immediate impression; the capacity for sudden violence is a private truth about her, not a visual style choice.

In practice, this likely means: soft, approachable, genuinely warm facial expressions (not guarded or watchful — that was the earlier draft's mistake); clothing that could range anywhere from casual to elegant, but should feel socially at-ease rather than deliberately practical/utilitarian; a build that can be conventionally attractive/soft rather than visibly toned or athletic, since the "capable of violence" quality doesn't need to show in her physique at all — it can be entirely latent. What should NOT change from a normal, pleasant, loving-reading reference set is any visible toughness, edge, or guardedness. If anything, the more disarming and genuinely warm she looks, the better the eventual dramatic irony of what she's actually capable of.

Setting and props are unimportant either way — Palmer City's own register (arts, culture, nightlife, a "Neutral Zone" municipality akin to Las Vegas/Atlantic City/New Orleans) supports almost any glamorous, social, or intimate setting. Nothing here should be read as needing an urban/gritty backdrop or combat-adjacent prop — that was also part of the earlier over-correction and no longer applies.

## Point-by-Point Checklist

**Expression & presence**
- Warm, pleasant, genuinely inviting expression — soft and open, not guarded or watchful
- Someone humans would feel immediately comfortable and safe around
- No visible toughness, edge, or hardness in her bearing

**Build**
- Conventionally soft/attractive is fine — no requirement for visible athleticism or muscle tone
- The self-defense capability is latent and invisible, not a physical "tell"

**Styling**
- Anything from casual to elegant works, as long as it reads as socially at-ease rather than deliberately practical/tactical
- No requirement to avoid delicate jewelry, soft fabrics, or glamorous styling — those are fine here

**Setting/props**
- Unimportant either way — Palmer City's own glamorous/social register supports almost anything
- No need for urban/gritty backdrops or combat-adjacent props (this was part of the earlier over-correction)

**Tone to avoid**
- Toughness, edge, hardened/combat-worn styling, guarded or watchful expressions
- Anything that visibly signals "capable of violence" at a glance — the whole point is that it shouldn't show

**Tone to match**
- Warm, loving, pleasant, at-home-in-a-social-setting — with the self-defense capability existing only as a hidden, latent truth underneath that surface
