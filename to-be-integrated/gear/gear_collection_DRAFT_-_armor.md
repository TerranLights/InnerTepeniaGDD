# Gear Collection (DRAFT) — Armor

**STATUS: ONGOING — genuinely incomplete, not a finished deliverable.** The developer's own assessment,
2026-08-16: this is a nice start but far from satisfactory as-is. Treat this as a standing work area to
revisit repeatedly, not something to close out. See also `Worldspace/Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`'s own matching
status note.

**What this is, and what it is deliberately not.** Per direct instruction: this is **not military armor** in
the usual sense. This is heavy-duty *clothing* — cold-weather gear, industrial safety equipment, work suits —
at varying degrees of hardness and rigidity, governed by the exact same restriction as the weapons catalog:
**almost none of it was designed to protect anyone from a person.** It was designed to protect someone from
Antarctica, or from the specific hazards of a specific job. Combat protection is what circumstance does to it,
the same way combat *use* is what circumstance does to a tool that becomes a weapon.

**Explicitly distinct from `Game-Mechanics/Combat/District_Armor_Augmentations_and_Protection.md`.** That file
covers something categorically different: rare, named wearable armor *sets* per Concordia district ("Core
Guardian Plating," "Overclocked Reactor Harness"), scarce loot tied to district reputation and exploration —
already itself a designed-as-armor system, not a repurposed-tool one. This file is the base layer underneath
that: common, everyday, city-specific protective clothing every resident of a given city might plausibly
already own, organized around Tepenia's 36 outside-world cities (the district system belongs to Concordia
specifically). The two systems can coexist without contradiction — this one just isn't the same thing, and
shouldn't be read as competing with or replacing it.

**The rigidity scale**, loose and civilian, not a locked mechanical tier list:
1. **Soft/insulated** — parkas, thermal layers, wool/synthetic insulation. Minimal physical protection;
   environmental (cold) protection only.
2. **Padded/reinforced** — quilted work jackets, padded coveralls, leather aprons. Real blunt-force cushioning
   as an incidental property of insulation bulk.
3. **Semi-rigid** — hard hats, reinforced work boots, cut-resistant gloves, welding aprons with metal mesh.
   Moderate protection against a specific, named workplace hazard.
4. **Rigid** — heavy industrial exosuit shells, pressure/dive suits, blast-shielding aprons, radiation-shielding
   suits. Highest protection, heaviest, least mobile — genuinely rare even in civilian life, reserved for the
   most dangerous specific jobs.

**Structure:** organized by subnet and city, same as the weapons file, drawing on each city's own established
industry (per `Local_Cultures/` Division of Industry sections) and climate.

## Palmer Subnet

**Palmer City** — entertainment/hospitality. *Soft:* performer's costume layers, doubling as concealment.
*Padded:* stagehand's rigging-work jacket. Not a heavy-armor city — its whole civic identity runs warm and
indoor, so the rigid tier genuinely doesn't fit here without forcing it.

**Rothera** — industrial/manufacturing. *Padded:* fabrication-floor coveralls. *Semi-rigid:* hard hat and
steel-toe boots, vault-access reinforced gloves. *Rigid:* underground-vault heavy-impact apron for the
component-lifting floor.

**Marambio** — aviation/maritime dual hub. *Semi-rigid:* dockworker's cut-resistant gloves, ground-crew hi-vis
padded jacket. *Rigid:* shipyard welding apron/faceshield.

**Port Lockroy** — heritage/small maritime town. *Soft:* museum-keeper's layered wool. *Padded:* harbor-work
oilskin coat. Kept modest, matching the town's own small scale.

**Sejong** — diplomatic/customs island. *Soft:* diplomatic formalwear layered for warmth. *Padded:*
customs-post duty coat. *Semi-rigid:* the same institutional gear the Small/Long Guns category already
implies exists here (DLC-specific per that catalog's own availability note).

**Signy** — isolated biological research/fishing. *Padded:* fishing oilskins. *Semi-rigid:* marine sample-work
gloves and boots, built for cold-wet conditions specifically rather than dry cold.

**Esperanza** — guardian-charter childcare economy. *Soft:* the civic identity itself argues against heavy
armor here — a city organized around family/guardianship should skew toward its softest end, deliberately.
*Padded:* harbor trans-shipment work coat.

**Juan Carlos** — fishing/hosted-gathering. *Padded:* fishing oilskins (same family as Signy). *Semi-rigid:*
net-handling gloves.

## Halley Subnet

**Halley** — the relocating city, ice engineering. *Semi-rigid:* structural-relocation crew's reinforced
coveralls and hard hat — a city that moves its own buildings needs genuinely serious protective gear for the
crews doing it.

**Belgrano** — Air Force exile discipline, aviation/garage culture. *Semi-rigid:* flight-line coveralls,
steel-toe boots. *Rigid:* engine-bay welding apron.

**Lazar** — largest city, post-war reconstruction. *Padded:* reconstruction-crew jacket. *Semi-rigid:*
demolition-site hard hat and gloves.

**Neumayer** — precision instrumentation. *Soft:* lab-coat-style layered indoor wear. *Semi-rigid:* structural
survey crew's field gear — this city's protective clothing skews toward precision-work protection (hands,
eyes) over blunt-impact bulk.

**Princess Elisabeth** — renewable energy, off-grid. *Semi-rigid:* turbine-maintenance harness gear,
insulated linesman's gloves.

**Sanay** — port/shipyard, actual Arcanet nexus. *Rigid:* shipyard welding suit — same family as Marambio's,
Sanay's own version scaled to the subnet's real shipbuilding volume.

**Troll** — contested freight hub, DLC5's own conflict anchor. *Semi-rigid to rigid:* ground-crew gear here
can plausibly run harder than most cities', given the canon-established contested-territory framing already
in place for this specific location.

**Abowasa** — small joint founding. *Soft:* household-scale cold-weather layers only, matching the city's own
modest established size — don't force a heavier tier onto a city this small.

## Janbogo Subnet

**Janbogo** — port/teahouse hospitality. *Soft:* teahouse-keeper's layered indoor wear. *Padded:* dockside
cargo-work coat.

**Cape Adare** — heritage gateway. *Soft:* preservation-worker's layered wool, matching Port Lockroy's family.

**Denison** — windiest ground on Earth, sealed single structure. *Semi-rigid:* the city's entire built
environment is one sealed structure against extreme wind — its own protective clothing should reflect that
directly: wind-sealed exterior-work suits, reinforced against the specific hazard of being physically thrown
by gusts, not generic cold gear.

**Dumont d'Urville** — marine/wind engineering, penguin management. *Padded:* wind-damage repair-crew coat.
*Semi-rigid:* wildlife-handling gloves (penguin-tagging work has its own specific protective need — beak/claw
resistant, not blunt-impact).

**Fort McMurdo** — industrial extraction, biggest built thing in Tepenia. *Semi-rigid:* Dry Valleys
extraction-crew gear, hard hat and steel-toe boots. *Rigid:* the closest thing to genuine heavy industrial
armor in the whole catalog — a real extraction operation at this city's scale would need real protective
bulk.

**Scott** — quiet, volcanic-fallout collection. *Semi-rigid:* heat-resistant collection-crew gloves and apron,
matching the weapons file's volcanic-ash tongs — the same one real industry, protective side.

**Zukelli** — destroyed, post-destruction salvage register. Whatever protective clothing shows up here should
be scavenged and mismatched rather than a coherent civilian outfit, the same framing as its weapons entry.

## Mirny Subnet

**Mirny** — comms hub, now also the subnet's quarrying city. *Semi-rigid:* quarry-crew hard hat, gloves, boots.
*Rigid:* the windbreak-ring's own structural-maintenance gear, working directly against the katabatic wind the
city's whole architecture is built to shed.

**Casey** — transit junction, Splinters' home city. *Soft:* bar-back's layered indoor wear. Not a heavy-armor
city — its civic identity is social/transit, not industrial.

**Davis** — breadbasket, greenhouse agriculture. *Soft:* greenhouse-worker's layered wear (indoor, warm,
humid — genuinely the softest-skewing industrial city in the catalog). *Semi-rigid:* limnological
field-research cold-weather gear for the lake-sampling side of the work.

**Kunlun** — astronomy, extreme altitude. *Rigid:* altitude-survival suit — Dome A's ceiling-of-habitability
conditions demand real, serious protective gear, not a soft layer.

**Vostok** — coldest city on Earth, subglacial genetics research. *Rigid:* the single most extreme cold-weather
suit in the entire catalog, matching the city's own extreme-cold survival infrastructure being a genuine
quarter of its whole economy. Deep-drilling crew gear sits alongside it.

**Zhongshan** — precision manufacturing, Prydz Bay fishing, a culture that concentrates care in unseen
details rather than visible display (per its own established architecture/fashion philosophy). *Soft:*
Zhongshan's own muted, detail-concentrated clothing tradition — dark blues, deep greys, charcoal — already
functions as the soft tier without modification; care shows in maintenance and repair, not bulk. *Padded:*
fishing-crew oilskins for the Prydz Bay economy. *Semi-rigid:* precision-manufacturing floor gear, gloves and
apron, kept as unornamented and functional as the rest of the city's own aesthetic instinct. Deliberately no
"rigid" tier pushed here — a city whose whole identity resists visible display would read wrong in heavy,
conspicuous armor plating.

**Sinheung** — heavy industrial fabrication, an output-as-legitimacy civic identity that wants competence
*seen*. *Semi-rigid:* fabrication-floor coveralls and hard hats, worn visibly and without Zhongshan's
concealment instinct — per the city's own established character, protective gear here is allowed to look
like exactly what it is. *Rigid:* the fabrication-synthesis-chamber section's own reinforced suiting,
matching Sinheung's status as one of the handful of cities that manufactures the actual apparatus that
creates robots — the same register as Byrd's equivalent entry above, since both cities do this work.

**Shirayuki** — mild climate, art/fashion/research economy, the one Mirny subnet city that never needed
harsh-survival gear to begin with. *Soft:* the city's own established homemade Alt-Rock/Alt-Grunge/Alt-Pop
fashion sensibility — genuinely the least protection-oriented clothing tradition in the entire catalog,
existing for self-expression rather than function. *Padded:* university/studio work smocks for
fabrication-and-design coursework. Deliberately thin beyond that — Shirayuki's mild oasis climate and
research/arts civic identity give it the weakest tool-first justification for anything past the soft tier of
any city in this file, and that thinness is itself accurate to the city rather than a gap to fill.

## Mawson Subnet

**Mawson** — honeymoon destination, subnet hub. *Soft:* hospitality-staff and honeymoon-suite linens — the
softest-skewing city in the whole catalog by civic design, matching its own established romantic-hospitality
identity.

**Dome Fuji** — Ice-Cold Buddhism monastery, extreme altitude. *Rigid:* altitude-legacy survival gear
inherited from the original research population, now worn by devotees; worth writing this tension
deliberately — serious survival equipment maintained by a population that arrived for faith, not science.

**Sayowa** — fabrication/trucking triple-junction. *Semi-rigid:* fabrication-floor gear, trucking/dispatch
hi-vis padded coats.

---

## Byrd Subnet

**Byrd** — underground fabrication, grounded aviation salvage. *Rigid:* the reinforced chamber-manufacturing
section's own protective suiting — the highest-stakes rigid gear in the catalog outside Kunlun/Vostok's
extreme-altitude/cold suits, since this is the section producing the actual apparatus that creates robots.
*Semi-rigid:* general underground-plant coveralls and hard hats for the rest of the facility.

---

## Amundsen Station

Rotating-staff relay station, no civilian economy. *Semi-rigid:* whatever minimal, spare protective gear
station staff actually need for relay maintenance and Tower operations work — kept deliberately unelaborate,
matching the station's own "almost everything shipped in, nothing made here" character. Same historical/salvage
register as its weapons entry, if pulled into present-day use.
