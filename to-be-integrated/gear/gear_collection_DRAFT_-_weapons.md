# Gear Collection (DRAFT) — Weapons

**STATUS: ONGOING — genuinely incomplete, not a finished deliverable.** The developer's own assessment,
2026-08-16: this is a nice start but far from satisfactory as-is. Treat this as a standing work area to
revisit repeatedly, not something to close out. See also `Worldspace/Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`'s own matching
status note.

**What this is:** the organized consolidation of `splatter_1` through `splatter_8`'s raw per-city brainstorm —
copied and cleaned up here, not moved; the splatter files stay exactly as they are as the working scratch
record. Same governing rule as `Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md` and `Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`: **almost none of
this was designed to hurt anyone.** Every item below is a real thing a Tepenian in that specific city would
have and use for its actual stated purpose; combat use is what circumstance does to it, not what it was built
for.

**Structure:** organized by subnet, then city, matching how the rest of the project already organizes city
content. Where an item is a near-duplicate of something already in `Weapon_Item_Catalog.md` (cargo hooks,
chains, ice-core drills, etc. — see the Recurring Motifs note in `splatter_8`), it's noted as a variant rather
than re-invented from scratch. Genuinely unique, city-specific standouts get fuller treatment.

## Palmer Subnet

**Palmer City** — Tepenia's cultural capital, entertainment/hospitality economy. A stage's own counterweight
sandbag, still on its rigging cable, swings as a bludgeon straight off the fly system. A bartender's kit
(ice pick, bottle-breaker, muddler) is already half-weapon-shaped without anyone intending it that way. A
performer's costume boning — thin rigid corset stays — has real stabbing potential as nothing more than a
garment part.

**Rothera** — decentralized industrial spine, surface and underground. A precision fabrication press arm,
a heavier-than-household vault-access pry bar, and the component-lifting chain-hook that moves finished
infrastructure parts between vault sections.

**Marambio** — dual aviation/maritime hub. Aircraft tie-down chain and turnbuckle (flail-shaped by accident),
shipyard dock cargo hooks, a pneumatic rivet gun.

**Port Lockroy** — small, old, heritage-preservation town. A museum conservator's restoration scalpel/chisel
set; a harbor mooring gaff hook; a ship's-bell clapper as an improvised blunt object.

**Sejong** — the most nationally-dense island in Tepenia, diplomatic/customs economy. A heavy customs
stamp-press, a diplomatic courier's reinforced tamper-evident satchel strap, an island-crossing ice staff.

**Signy** — isolated, no road in, biological research plus fishing. A marine sample-collection harpoon/corer
(already spear-shaped as a genuine science tool), a fishing gaff hook, and an everything-in-one
self-sufficiency multitool.

**Esperanza** — founded specifically to guard human children of exile. A harbor trans-shipment hook, a
guardian-charter ceremonial staff worth developing once the charter's own iconography exists, a heavy
administrative birth-registry stamp.

**Juan Carlos** — fishing/maritime economy, a genuine hosted-gathering civic tradition. Net-weight flails,
gaff hooks, and the hosting tradition's own carving knife/large ladle.

## Halley Subnet

**Halley** — the city that physically relocates. Its own relocation-rig cable and winch, ice-anchor stake
drivers, and structural building jacks — nothing else in the roster has a "the city itself moves" mechanism
to draw from.

**Belgrano** — Air Force exile discipline, aviation/garage culture. Torque wrenches, engine hoist chains,
tow-bars.

**Lazar** — Tepenia's largest city, post-war reconstruction economy. Rebar-bending tools, demolition pry
bars, trade-hub cargo hooks.

**Neumayer** — precision instrumentation, Amundsen Tower engineering legacy. Surveying-tripod stakes
(spear-shaped precision hardware) and structural-stress probe rods.

**Princess Elisabeth** — renewable energy, zero-emission off-grid infrastructure. Wind-turbine blade
maintenance tools, solar-array cleaning poles, energy-diagnostic probes.

**Sanay** — the subnet's actual Arcanet nexus, genuine port/shipyard city (Halley keeps the naming credit).
Shipyard welding rods, dockworker cargo hooks, crane cable.

**Troll** — airfield/intermodal freight hub, DLC5's own flagged conflict anchor. Freight tie-down chains,
ground-crew marshalling wands, cargo pallet jacks. Worth noting: Troll is already canon-contested territory,
so its gear-to-weapon translation can lean harder into "actually used this way" than most cities.

**Abowasa** — small, joint Finnish/Swedish founding. Kept deliberately modest — small household-scale fishing
gear, nothing large-industrial, matching the city's own small established scale.

## Janbogo Subnet

**Janbogo** — port/commercial hub, genuine teahouse hospitality warmth. A heavy teahouse kettle or serving
tray, dockside cargo hooks, polynya fishing-net weights.

**Cape Adare** — heritage site (the 1899 hut), marine/commercial gateway. Restoration tools (same family as
Port Lockroy's), gateway customs hooks.

**Denison** — the windiest ground on Earth, a single sealed interlinked structure. Wind-anchor turnbuckles,
structural wind-brace struts, and — distinct from Mirny's Windbreak Turbine Fragment — a loose,
whatever-the-wind-tore-loose-today debris fragment, since Denison's civic identity is about the wind itself
rather than a windbreak defending against it.

**Dumont d'Urville** — marine/wind engineering, penguin-colony management, francophone island. Penguin-tagging
poles/nets (genuinely delicate wildlife gear), wind-damage repair struts, coastal-resource gaffs.

**Fort McMurdo** — Tepenia's biggest built thing, Dry Valleys extraction, apprenticeship-trained trade shops.
Extraction pickaxes/drill bits, harbor logistics hooks, a trade-shop tool literally handed to an apprentice.

**Scott** — quiet, commemorative, one genuine industry: collecting Mount Erebus's volcanic fallout. A
heat-resistant volcanic-ash collection scoop/tongs — the single most geographically specific item in the whole
catalog. A ceremonial item tied to St. Robert is flagged as worth developing once that connection has more to
draw from, not invented wholesale here.

**Zukelli** — food/hospitality signature export, shared polynya port with Janbogo, destroyed in the Long Night
War. Its hospitality serving tools and port gear match Janbogo's own family — but Zukelli's whole gear identity
should read as **post-destruction salvage**, pulled from ruins, rather than active-industry tooling the way
every other city's entries do here.

## Mirny Subnet

Zhongshan, Sinheung, and Shirayuki already have deep, individually-developed entries in
`Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`'s "Genuinely New Tepenia-Original Items" section (Seal-Processing Hook, Windbreak
Turbine Fragment, and the Thermal Edge crossover item) — not duplicated here.

**Mirny** — comms/Arcanet subnet hub, and (since the Davis reassignment) the subnet's own quarrying city.
Quarry pickaxes/drills feeding Sinheung's fabrication economy directly, plus subnet-scale Arcanet relay
maintenance tools, bigger than any single city's own line-tester probe.

**Casey** — transit/logistics junction, home to Splinters (Tepenia's largest, most famous bar). Bar tools —
tap handle, bottle, a bar-back's crowbar — leaning directly into the regional landmark.

**Davis** — Tepenia's breadbasket, sheltered-agriculture and greenhouse cultivation. Pruning shears and
sickles are the most literal "farm tool becomes weapon" items in the entire roster; also a limnological
sample corer and a greenhouse glass-cutting tool.

**Kunlun** — astronomy-dominant, extreme altitude, near-Earth-object observation. Telescope/observatory
alignment poles, altitude-survival kit tools, oldest-ice-on-Earth core samplers.

**Vostok** — the coldest city on Earth, Lake Vostok subglacial genetics research. A deep ice-core drill bit
distinct from the ordinary household version by sheer depth and precision — the most extreme drilling
operation in the country, sitting above 25 million sealed years. Genetics-lab extraction needles and an
extreme-cold survival toolkit (a genuine quarter of this city's whole economy).

## Mawson Subnet

**Mawson** — subnet hub, Tepenia's honeymoon destination. Hospitality/hotel service tools, and — handled
carefully, for the irony rather than for shock — a decorative honeymoon-suite item repurposed. Maritime dock
tools round out the set.

**Dome Fuji** — Ice-Cold Buddhism monastery, inherited ice-core science, extreme altitude. A religious or
pilgrimage implement, written as an actual devotee's actual object rather than generic monastery set-dressing;
archival ice-core sample tools; altitude-legacy medical equipment maintained for a human population that may
no longer be present.

**Sayowa** — industrial fabrication, trucking/dispatch, the one point where three regions physically meet.
Fabrication-floor tools, tow chains/cargo ratchet straps, and a highway-junction signal flag.

---

## Byrd Subnet

**Byrd** — built underground before it ever stood on the surface, now the last city its own subnet has left.
Underground fabrication-plant tools (the reinforced chamber-manufacturing line specifically), a snow-management
plow blade, resource-extraction drills, import/export dispatch hooks — and, the standout of the whole catalog,
a **salvaged aircraft propeller-blade fragment** from the grounded, irreparable fleet: a genuinely evocative
item carrying real narrative weight, a literal piece of the connection to the rest of Tepenia that no longer
works.

---

## Amundsen Station

Not one of the 35 named cities, included per direct instruction — a rotating-staff relay station with no
civilian economy in the conventional sense. Arcanet relay maintenance tools (station-scale, not city-scale),
Amundsen Tower operations equipment (exact scope still open, see `Specs/Amundsen_Station.md`), and a
deliberately spare rotating-staff survival toolkit, since almost everything here was shipped in rather than
made. Per the station's own destruction — it "severed Tepenia's ability to talk to itself" — anything pulled
from here should read as historical/salvaged, the same register as Zukelli's entry above.

## Recurring Motifs — Consolidation Candidates

Carried over from `splatter_8`, not yet acted on: cargo/dock hooks (Marambio, Sanay, Janbogo, Zukelli, Casey,
Byrd, Cape Adare, Esperanza, Juan Carlos), chains/cables/tow-lines (Marambio, Belgrano, Lazar, Sanay, Troll,

often enough that they probably deserve one strong catalog entry each, flavored per-district, rather than
being formalized as near-duplicate standalone items. Flagged for whoever does the final `Weapon_Item_Catalog.md`
integration pass — not resolved here.
