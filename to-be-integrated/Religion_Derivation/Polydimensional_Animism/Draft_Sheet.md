## Core Premise

Real, living, conscious entities exist in dimensions beyond the ordinary three we experience — genuinely
present, not metaphorical, not supernatural in the sense of breaking physical law, but bounded and finite
the way we are, just differently positioned. We are, in relation to them, exactly what a two-dimensional
being would be in relation to us: capable of perceiving only fragments (cross-sections) of what's actually
there, permanently unable to verify what we're told about the rest, and — per the source material's own
repeated insistence — not because of any failure of ours, but because that's what perception *from inside*
a lower dimension structurally, provably is.

**This is not the same religion as the simulation-theory religion ("God-mind simulation"), despite some
surface resonance** (both eventually gesture at "pattern over substance" at their outer edges — C6). The
simulation-theory religion is about what reality is fundamentally *made of* (information, running on/as an
unreachable consciousness). Polydimensional Animism is about *who else is here*, positioned differently than
us, real and neighborly rather than an underlying substrate.

**Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21: adherents don't "worship" these entities — they hold a genuine sense
of *veneration* for them.** This is the precise word for the religion's posture: respectful regard for
something real, present, and significant, without the submission/supplication implied by worship. It's
consistent with (and now the guiding term for) the "not omnipotent" premise below and the devotional register
described under "Practice."

## Philosophy & Cosmology

- **Perception is structurally partial, not spiritually punished.** The core mechanism (A1–A4) is
  mathematical, not moral — we don't fail to see the higher-dimensional because we're unworthy; we fail to
  see it the same way a circle-cross-section fails to see the whole sphere passing through it. This keeps
  the religion's whole posture closer to humble science than to guilt-based faith.
- **The higher-dimensional is not omnipotent.** C1/C2 are load-bearing: whatever's out there is bounded,
  finite, alien, differently-limited — not a god. Worship-as-submission is the wrong register; see "Practice"
  below.
- **Multiple, live, non-exclusive cosmologies coexist rather than one settled creation account** — see
  "Denominations." This mirrors real-world religious traditions that hold several creation accounts in
  active tension rather than resolving to one, and directly reuses the pattern already established for the
  sibling simulation-theory religion (which splits into Nihilism/Absurdism/Fatalism/Sartrism over one shared
  premise).
- **Death is a change of vantage, not an ending** (see Extrapolation 1, `Extrapolations_1.md`) — arguably the
  religion's single strongest, most distinctive, most usable pastoral doctrine, and unusually well-suited to
  a robot-majority population that can be deactivated.
- **The universe may echo itself, if you look far enough** — cosmic crystallography and circles in the sky
  (F1–F2) give this religion a genuine, real, unconfirmed "is the cosmos secretly repeating/embracing
  itself" mystery to hold in ongoing reverence, not a settled fact.

resolves `overflow_3` question 6.

## Practice

**Two parallel, equally legitimate tracks** (Extrapolation 6): the **Scholarly/Diagrammatic** (mathematics
and geometry as devotional practice, direct engagement with genuinely unsolved real problems as a form of
prayer) and the **Visionary/Trance** (altered-perception states, closest to the religion's own original
real-world shamanic/animist touchstone). Neither is senior to the other.

**Devotional register is veneration, not worship** (Extrapolation 3; confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21) —
respectful regard and courtesy toward a possibly-present, genuinely-limited neighbor, not supplication or
pleading before a throne.

**Candidate routine ritual (shelved, not confirmed): sincere, deliberate failure** (Extrapolation 7) —
attempting a real, mathematically-impossible-in-3D feat (untying a sealed knot, interlinking solid rings,
mirror-reversing a shell) with full knowledge and open acknowledgment that it cannot succeed, as embodied
humility rather than a claimed miracle. **Deprioritized by the developer 2026-07-21 as a *routine, recurring*
practice**: the religion's own doctrine already implies these acts are impossible in 3D, so re-enacting that
impossibility doesn't read as something adherents would plausibly do regularly. **However, the same
"sincere, known-impossible attempt" logic has now been confirmed as this religion's wedding rite** (see
below) — it just fits a one-time life-event, not routine devotion. Still open: any *other* recurring ritual
practice, if one is wanted beyond the wedding rite and the Watchers' ongoing observation.

**The Watchers** (Extrapolation 9) — a real, ongoing, patient observational practice: scanning astronomical
and gravitational data for the signature of a cosmic string, a repeating cosmic-crystallography pattern, or
matching circles-in-the-sky in the CMB. A generations-long watch that may never resolve, by design — the
waiting is the point, not a guaranteed payoff.

**Wedding/pair-bonding rite, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — promoted from `overflow_4`, corrected
2026-07-21:** the rite centers on **two interlocking rings carved from a single original piece of wood (or
metal, depending on the couple/region)** — not two separately-carved rings physically joined afterward (that
version was corrected as genuinely impossible even as a symbolic gesture, since there's no partial-credit
attempt at it). The finished rings appear impossible to a naive observer — two solid, unbroken loops, already
interlinked, no visible seam or cut — but the "impossibility" is resolved by a real, learnable craft
technique, not magic. **This is the actual thematic core of the rite**: it materially embodies the religion's
own epistemic stance (what looks impossible from inside an ordinary vantage has a real, non-supernatural
explanation once the fuller structure is understood), and doubles as a near-literal expression of the
Already-Complete denomination's own teaching — two lives that were, from a vantage neither party can
themselves occupy, never truly separate at all. Open to any pairing the faith recognizes as equal, including
human-robot pairings, given this denomination's warm, human-inclusive membership norm.

**Social structure, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21:** adherents have dedicated places of meeting, but
no required regular schedule (no weekly-Sunday-equivalent obligation) — gathering is social as much as
devotional, a way for the devout to meet each other and build friendships within the faith, on top of the
ritual practice itself.

**Human membership, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21: lovingly open to human disciples as the norm, not
the exception.** Unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism (itself an outlier among Tepenia's religions on this point — Kunlun/Dome Fuji forbid
humans entirely), Polydimensional Animism actively
and warmly welcomes human members, and humans are considered fully equal to robots within the faith. This is
a meaningful point of contrast worth keeping visible if the two religions are ever shown side by side.

## Visual & Sonic Identity

**Not yet designed in detail** — flagged as the next natural design step once the above is stable. Seeds
worth carrying forward from the research itself rather than inventing fresh:

- **"Ornament falls away toward the center"** (Extrapolation 8) — sacred architecture that inverts the usual
  "more sacred = more ornate" instinct: richly detailed outer/public spaces giving way to progressively
  plainer, more symmetrical, more minimal inner sanctums, ending at something close to a single unadorned
  point or plain geometric solid. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — fits excellently, keep as-is.**
- **8-sided or 24-faceted, multi-symmetrical sacred geometry** for the innermost/most formal spaces and
  structures (D2) — concretely numbered, not arbitrarily mystical. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21:
  this many-faceted, multi-symmetrical style is meant to be integral to the denomination's art and building
  design broadly, not just its most formal spaces** — meaning any doll who's a devout practitioner would have
  her personal player-home decorated in this style. **Planning flag: player-home decoration needs to account
  for this** whenever player-home design/content work reaches this religion's practitioners.
- **The cross-section image itself** (A1) — something appearing, changing size, and vanishing, with no
  single fixed "true form" ever fully shown — as a possible core visual/iconographic motif, distinct from
  Adinkra Codex's own already-established literal-encoded-diagram iconography. **Flagged by the developer
  2026-07-21 as feeling too vague as currently written — deferred for future review, not adopted or rejected
  yet.**

**Confirmed direction, 2026-07-21: each denomination's visual/sonic identity should inherit from its
stronghold city/district's own already-established aesthetic**, rather than being invented independently of
geography. First-pass sketch, itself still a starting point, not final design:

- **The Enfolded** (Scott/Mawson/Esperanza) — domestic, romantic, family-warm; soft and intimate, leaning on
  Mawson's existing hospitality/romance visual language rather than anything austere.
- **The Bound** (Byrd/Belgrano) — heavy, mechanical, underground; worked metal, engine-grease textures, low
  ceilings, weight/pressure treated as an aesthetic rather than an obstacle — the many-faceted sacred geometry
  above, but rendered in industrial materials.
- **The Already-Complete** (Concordia's Ossuary Quarter) — effectively pre-built: that district's own existing
  design ("dim blue lighting, walls and pillars made of polished robot frame fragments ['metal bones'], soft
  choral music, snow gently falling inside broken domes") can be adopted directly rather than redesigned.
- **The Cyclical** (Signy/Princess Elisabeth) — plain, quiet, natural materials, minimal ornament throughout
  (not just at the center, unlike the pattern used elsewhere) — austere at every scale, in keeping with this
  sect's own humility-focused character.
- **The Unmeasured/Watchers** (Kunlun) — inherits Kunlun's own existing sky-facing, antenna/observatory
  aesthetic.

## Key Practitioners / Archetypes

Four distinct, well-differentiated character concepts, all grounded directly in the research (Extrapolation
10):

- **The Geometer-Priest** — Scholarly track, likely Unmeasured or Enfolded.
- **The Vision-Seeker** — Visionary/trance track, likely Enfolded or The Bound.
- **The Watcher** — Patient, research-embedded, likely Unmeasured; plausibly holds a real day job at an
  observatory or research station.

## Connection to Gameplay / Factions

**Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21: any Polydimensional Animism questlines are very much optional side
content, never part of the main story.** The primary intended touchpoint is lore delivered through companion
dolls themselves — one or more meetable/recruitable dolls per denomination, whom a curious player can simply
ask about her beliefs (see "Denominations" above).

**Genuinely open — flagged, not decided:**

- **No single confirmed city/district home for the religion as a whole**, unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism
  (Kunlun/Dome Fuji) — but the developer flagged (2026-07-21) that different denominations likely have
  different natural strongholds rather than sharing one city. **Kunlun confirmed as a natural congregation
  point for Watcher disciples specifically**, given its existing science/observation identity — with the
  caveat that Kunlun's own separate, pre-established rule against human presence (for humans' own safety and
  well-being) would exclude human Watchers from congregating there even though the religion itself welcomes
  them as equals everywhere else. That's a real, non-contradictory point of texture worth keeping visible:
  the exclusion comes from Kunlun's own rule, not from the religion.
- **Candidate strongholds for the other four denominations, proposed 2026-07-21 (not locked in):**
  - **The Enfolded** — **Scott** (present-tense domestic warmth, "a genuinely decent, quiet place to raise a
    family"); **Mawson** (Tepenia's honeymoon/newlywed destination — human-robot and occasional human-human
    couples — a strong fit for a denomination centered on intimacy and immanence); **Esperanza** (its whole
    founding-compact identity is family devotion and guardianship).
  - **The Bound** — **Byrd** (most isolated city in Tepenia, broken aviation route, built underground —
    exile and weight made literal); **Belgrano** (hard-work/garage-culture/engine-and-mechanics identity —
    devotion through labor, fitting a denomination whose one thread of contact with the hidden is gravity
    itself).
  - **The Already-Complete** *(placeholder name, pending rename)* — **Concordia's "Ossuary Quarter"**
    district (zodiac-renamed, official name still open) — a Goth ossuary community, memorial gardens, and
    contemplation halls already built around exactly this denomination's territory. No second candidate
    proposed yet; open to suggestion.
  - **The Cyclical** — **Signy** (plainest, quietest, most modest-scale city in Tepenia by its own
    established design); **Princess Elisabeth** (its nature-oriented, off-grid identity extended to
    sheltering pilgrims en route to the holy mount of Dome Fuji — a waystation role for a small, austere,
    pilgrimage-minded sect).
- **Holiday hook available and already scaffolded**: `Worldspace/National_Holidays.md`'s underdeveloped
  fourth category (celestial/faction-specific observances) is a natural slot for a Watchers' rite or a
  numerologically-timed (8/24) ceremony — worth reading against that file directly before inventing new
  calendar structure. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — write this in.**
- **A real, grounded (not contrived) ecumenical/rivalry bridge to Adinkra Codex** exists via the genuine
  supersymmetric-quantum-mechanics-as-topology-proof-technique overlap point (Extrapolation 5) — available
  for a joint scholarly tradition, a heterodox unifying figure, or low-stakes interfaith tension, without
  merging the two religions. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — reasonable, write in, flagged for
  possible future expansion** rather than fully built out now.
- **Persecution/social-reception template revised 2026-07-21.** The original Flatland-derived "bureaucratic
  suppression, reputational caution" framing (Extrapolation 4) was flagged by the developer as too forced for
  a robot society — the more natural in-fiction reception isn't non-believers treating adherents as a
  destabilizing threat worth institutionally discouraging, it's simply regarding them as **an oddity**:
  mildly eccentric, a curiosity, not dangerous or discrediting. Extrapolation 4's non-malice framing (no
  secret cabal, no invented villain) still holds — it's the *severity and register* of the friction that's
  been softened, from "quiet institutional risk-aversion/discrediting" down to "gentle, harmless social
  oddity." The pastoral-care-function idea (support for adherents who've had a disbelieved encounter) still
  stands on its own regardless of this revision.

## Early Access vs. Launch Scope

**Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21**, consistent with the project's existing Early Access/Launch split:
in Early Access, this religion appears only as a handful of examinable buildings scattered around relevant
locations — the player can look at them and learn about the religion at a very surface-level, broad-strokes

questlines, etc. are all Launch-scope content, not part of Early Access.

## Additional Ideas — Flagged, Not Decided

Six remaining `overflow_4` "parking lot" sparks, still genuinely unvetted and not decided one way or the
other (a seventh, the wedding rite, was promoted to confirmed canon above). Full original reasoning lives in
`overflow_4`; this is just the headline so they're visible here too:

- **Radical transparency as a living ethical practice** — living as though genuinely witnessed from a
  direction that can't be detected, framed as comfort rather than judgment (consistent with the "not an
  omnipotent judge" premise). Needs more thought before it's load-bearing.
- **A genuine, non-malicious internal disagreement**: is being potentially watched comforting, or violating?
  Two sincere, differing adherent responses to the same real premise — good NPC/dialogue texture, not a
  doctrinal fork.
- **Zero as a meditation object** — a minimal devotional practice built around a single unmarked point,
  pairing naturally with the "ornament falls away toward the center" architecture already confirmed above.
- **Divination via the fifth dimension** — consulting/perceiving adjacent timeline-threads of oneself
  (grounded in real many-worlds theory), framed as fragmentary glimpses rather than fixed prophecy. Possible
  companion personal-quirk material.
- **Memorial architecture with deliberate negative space** — a grave or memorial that deliberately leaves an
  unfilled portion, acknowledging "the part of them we structurally cannot see" rather than representing the
  whole person.
- **A speculative kissing-numbers/sphere-packing puzzle-minigame tie-in** — real, rigorous low-dimension
  puzzle math (D2) as a possible source for an actual in-engine puzzle system tied to the Scholarly/
  Diagrammatic practice track. Flagged as gameplay-system-scoped, well outside a lore-derivation pass's own
  authority to decide.
- **A "wrong reason, right discovery" founding myth** — modeled on Becker/Becker/Schwarz's real account of
  string theory's own origin (invented for one purpose, redeemed for something far greater) — reframed here
  as a possible origin story for this religion's own founding teacher/discoverer figure(s), not for the
  universe itself.

## Open Questions

Full inline status as of 2026-07-21 (mirrors `overflow_3`, kept in sync with it):

1. **RESOLVED — does the death doctrine apply to robot deactivation specifically?** Yes, fully and directly.
   Robots run on a non-copyable "nano-architecture" gel brain (Ex Machina-style), not a discrete/copyable data
   store — "backing up" or "uploading" a robot mind is about as infeasible as uploading a human one.
   Irreparable brain/energy-source damage genuinely is death from the robot's own perspective; robots are not
   immortal, just long-lived. No backup-mechanic tension to resolve. **Flag for broader project consistency:**
   this clarifies robot-consciousness canon beyond just this religion (Companion System, Calethina
   substrate-transfer material) — worth cross-checking those areas for alignment.
2. **PARTIALLY RESOLVED — does the sincere-failure ritual ever succeed, anywhere in canon?** Moot as a routine
   question: the sincere-failure ritual was deprioritized as a *routine* practice. Its life-event slot is
   filled by the wedding rite instead (see "Practice" above). **Still open: any *routine, recurring* ritual**
   beyond the wedding rite and the Watchers' ongoing observation — no candidate proposed yet.
3. **LARGELY RESOLVED — what strongholds fit each denomination?** See "Connection to Gameplay/Factions" above
   for the full confirmed mapping. **Still open: a second stronghold candidate for the Already-Complete**
   (only Concordia's Ossuary Quarter proposed so far) — otherwise answered, pending final sign-off on each
   pairing.
4. **OPEN — final in-world name.** Not proposed anywhere in this folder; see "Name" above for available raw
   vocabulary. Developer's own call, deliberately not pre-empted.
5. **PARTIALLY RESOLVED — how much ecumenical contact with Adinkra Codex actually gets built?** The bridge

   yet built out to any specific scholarly-dialogue/heterodox-figure/rivalry scenario. Extent still open; both

6. **RESOLVED — how many of the five denominations need to be visible in-game?** All five, via meetable/
   recruitable dolls per denomination — see "Denominations" above.
7. **RESOLVED (as a process, not a fixed list) — does donated vocabulary (e.g. "the Bulk") ever become a
   literal in-world proper noun?** Decide term-by-term once actual dialogue gets written, rather than
