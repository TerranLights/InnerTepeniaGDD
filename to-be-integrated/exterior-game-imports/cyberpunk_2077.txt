## 5. ASSAULT → Guns (Nerve) — sustained/suppressive-fire flavor, same governing skill as Handguns
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Cross-note before drafting:** Assault's CP2077 reward ladder is byte-for-byte identical to Handguns'
(source file literally reuses the table). Per Section 3, both land on Inner Tepenia's single Guns skill —
there is no Inner Tepenia equivalent of CP2077's Handguns/Assault split. Rather than draft two duplicate
perk sets off one identical numeric pool, the same pool is split into two distinct flavors that won't
compete for design space once both live under Guns: Handguns above leans precision/quickdraw (single
well-placed shots), Assault below leans sustained/suppressive fire (staying on target across a burst or
turn). Flagging this explicitly rather than quietly diverging — a future pass may decide these should
simply merge into one perk family instead of staying split.

"Sustained Fire Control" (placeholder, not developer-chosen) — from Assault Lv4/8/13/14/19/16/17 (Recoil -10% x4, -15% x1, Spread -25%, -10%)
  Diegetic: trained bracing and burst discipline for rifles/carbines — staying on target across multiple
  rounds rather than a single shot.
  Effect (by rank): R1: the accuracy penalty for a second attack against the same target in the same turn
  is halved when using a rifle/carbine. R2: no such penalty, and a third attack against the same target
  in the same turn only takes the normal (not stacked) penalty.
  Ranks: 2
  Unlock condition: land X hits with rifles/carbines during turns where 2+ attacks were made (40 / 120).
  Note: deliberately mirrors Suppression Discipline's (Annihilation) mechanical shape but scoped to
  rifles/carbines instead of heavy/automatic weapons — same underlying "stay on target" idea, different
  weapon-category gate, consistent with Section 6's note that CP2077's weapon-category granularity is
  itself useful seed material.

"Rapid Target Acquisition" (placeholder, not developer-chosen) — from Assault Lv2/7/11 (Aiming speed +20%, Crit chance +5%, Crit damage +15%)
  Diegetic: fast target-to-target reacquisition — snapping the sights from a downed target to the next
  threat without losing tempo.
  Effect (by rank): R1: -1 AP cost on the first rifle/carbine attack made against a new target after
  downing a different target in the same turn. R2: +5% crit chance and +15% crit damage on that follow-up
  attack.
  Ranks: 2
  Unlock condition: land killing blows on X separate targets within the same combat turn/encounter using
  rifles/carbines (15 / 50).
  Note: this is the one Assault perk that reads meaningfully different from its Handguns counterpart even
  though the source numbers are identical — "target switching under fire" versus "first-shot precision"
  are genuinely different playstyles being pulled out of the same raw ladder.

content — Minigame time +5% (x5 lines), Data mining materials +10% (x4 lines), Max RAM +1 (x2 lines),

or invent a dedicated Hacking-specific resource) — not resolved by this pass. Do not treat "+1 RAM"-style
bonuses as portable until that question is answered.

ALSO FLAGGED: the source table itself notes Breach Protocol's Lv20 reads "Perk Point" rather than "Trait,"
an apparent inconsistency in the CP2077 source data (every other skill's Lv20 is "Trait"). Preserved as-is
in ORIGINALS above per the retention policy — not silently corrected, since it may just be an accurate
quirk of the actual game data rather than a transcription error.

"Combat Ice" (placeholder, not developer-chosen) — from Cold Blood, Collagulant
  Diegetic: a Nerve-linked combat-cooling response — the system gets colder and steadier with

  Effect: each kill grants a stacking buff (max 3 stacks baseline): +10% movement speed per

  Diegetic: the same cooling response deepening with practice — composure under fire becoming a
  trained reflex rather than an adrenaline accident.
  Effect:
    Rank 1 (Will to Survive + Blood Brawl + Defensive Clotting): each Combat Ice stack grants
    +3% Resistances, +10% melee damage, and +8% Armor.
    Rank 2 (Predator + Frozen Precision + Pain is an Illusion): each stack additionally grants
    +30% attack speed and +5% headshot damage, and -5% damage taken while any stacks are active.
    Rank 3 (Coldest Blood + Critical Condition + Cold and Calculating + Merciless): +2 max
    stacks (5 total), +50% stack duration, critical hits grant an extra stack on top of kills,

  Note: ten separate CP2077 perks collapsed into 3 ranks — the single largest consolidation in
  this whole batch, justified because the source tree is genuinely one mechanic (stack count /
  duration / what-each-stack-grants) escalating across ten small entries rather than ten
  distinct ideas. Rank 3's "crits grant a stack" creates a self-sustaining loop at full

"Iron Calm" (placeholder, not developer-chosen) — from Immunity
  Diegetic: full status-hardening once the cooling response is fully engaged.
  Effect: immune to Bleed, Poison, Burn, and Shock status effects while Combat Ice has at least

  Gate: N 7, Level 18.

"Last Ditch Countermeasure" (placeholder, not developer-chosen) — from Cheat Death (Stealth

  Effect: Rank 1 (Cheat Death): while below 50% HP, incoming damage is reduced by 50% for 2

  Note: CP2077 independently put a near-death safety net in two different trees — Cheat Death in
  Stealth, Bloodswell in Cold Blood. Rather than draft two overlapping "prevent death" perks
  under two different skills, they're combined into one progression here, filed under Nerve/Cold
  Blood since Bloodswell's version is the more significant capstone effect and both trees map to
  the same Cool attribute in CP2077 anyway.
