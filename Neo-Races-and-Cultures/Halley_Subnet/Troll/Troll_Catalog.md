## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** elevated bedrock nunatak group in dramatic mountain terrain, with a major
high-altitude airfield as a defining feature — the real-world matches should favor populated
high-altitude mountain communities, ideally ones built around aviation access.

- **USA (Primary):** Leadville, Colorado — the highest incorporated city in the US, with its own
  high-altitude municipal airport; a strong match for the "elevated settlement built around aviation
  access" angle.
- **Germany (Significant):** Berchtesgaden / Zugspitze region (Bavarian Alps) — populated high-alpine
  communities on comparable bedrock.
- **France (Significant):** Courchevel (French Alps) — real high-altitude settlement known specifically
  for its dramatic mountain airport.
- **UK (Significant):** Cairngorms — same bedrock-highland match used elsewhere in this subnet.
- **Russia (Significant):** Caucasus mountain valleys near Mount Elbrus — elevated bedrock terrain with
  real mountain communities, though noted here for elevation/exposure rather than the meltwater angle
  used at Lazar.
- **Brazil (Significant):** Serra da Mantiqueira (Brazilian Highlands) — weakest match, flagged for
  Phase 2 review.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type:** aviation/airfield hub functioning as a genuine intermodal freight center — "the
airfield [is] the effective center of a major share of Tepenia's actual real-economy
import/distribution network."

- **USA (Primary):** Memphis, Tennessee — FedEx's global air-cargo hub; an extremely strong, direct
  real-world match for "airfield as the center of a major freight/logistics network."
- **Germany (Significant):** Leipzig/Halle Airport — DHL's major European air-cargo hub, a strong
  match.
- **France (Significant):** The Roissy/Charles de Gaulle cargo zone — a real major French air-freight
  node, a decent match.
- **UK (Significant):** East Midlands Airport — the UK's major dedicated air-freight hub (home to DHL
  UK), a strong match.
- **Russia (Significant):** No strong domestic dedicated air-cargo-hub match — flagged as weak.
- **Brazil (Significant):** No strong domestic match — flagged as weak.
