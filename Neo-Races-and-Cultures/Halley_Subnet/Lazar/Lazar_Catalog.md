- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA. Significant — Germany, France, UK, Russia (founding

  Lithuania, Slovenia, Latvia, Estonia. Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Lazar.md` and
  `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Schirmacher Oasis — one of Antarctica's rare ice-free areas, ~34km² of exposed
  bedrock and meltwater lakes surrounded by ice sheet on all sides. Holds two real-world station sites
  close together (Novolazarevskaya, Maitri).
- **Geological composition:** Ice-free oasis bedrock with meltwater channels in summer — a genuinely
  different landscape from the surrounding plateau, comparable in kind to Sinheung's Larsemann Hills
  oasis in the Mirny subnet.
