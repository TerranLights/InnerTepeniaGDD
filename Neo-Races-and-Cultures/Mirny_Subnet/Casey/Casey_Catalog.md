## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-16)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Casey's own
conditions (its highway-junction civic role and its Splinters-anchored entertainment identity) that a
given population's originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X
because they're from nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in:** Casey's Enneagram profile is **Feeling (Heart) / Assertive /
Positive Outlook** — the same three traits as Palmer City, a city organized around felt experience and
projecting its own cultural identity outward. `Casey_Mega_Init.md`'s own Inspirational Influences
research already identifies Casey's *actual* established founding tension, directly from its own
Post-Culture Identity section: **destination versus waypoint** — "residents live full lives in a city
whose external reputation reduces it to a highway junction." Three real-world precedents ground this,
all tied to the USA population specifically: Flagstaff, Arizona (a literal crossroads by both history
and design, civic identity and junction function treated as the same fact); Memphis, Tennessee (a
multi-modal crossroads where Beale Street's live-music scene sits directly at the junction and became
the city's own defining civic landmark — proof of exactly Casey's own Splinters dynamic); and Reno,
Nevada (the closest real precedent for the destination-vs-waypoint tension itself — a real 1931 pivot
from stopover to destination via legalized gambling, not just an invented parallel).
