## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — South Korea. Significant — China, Japan, Germany, Russia
  (station operator only, not a founding population), Indonesia, Australia (founding wave). Notable —
  Thailand, Estonia, Hungary, Lithuania, Malaysia, Vietnam, Philippines, Bulgaria, Latvia, South Africa.
  Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Sinheung.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Larsemann Hills, Ingrid Christensen Coast, Princess Elizabeth Land, Prydz Bay — a
  small ice-free oasis (~34km²), part of the Mirny subnet's densest multi-city cluster (with Zhongshan
  and Shirayuki, all three founded via the same Jeju-do diplomatic allocation).
- **Geological composition:** Ice-free oasis, exposed bedrock, meltwater lakes — comparable in scale to
  the Schirmacher Oasis (Lazar).

## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

Zhongshan (see Lazar's own entry for the general hydrological framing).

- **South Korea (Primary):** No true glacial-meltwater match domestically (Korea has no active
  glaciers); the closest conceptual parallel is Baekrokdam, the crater lake atop Hallasan on Jeju

  match as at Lazar/Shirayuki.

- **Australia (Significant):** No match — flagged as weakest.
