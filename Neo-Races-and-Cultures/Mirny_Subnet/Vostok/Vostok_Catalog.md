mismatch against the founding legend's Russian character, given the present composition is
USA/Japan-led. This Phase 1c pass describes the present-day population honestly rather than inventing a
resolution to that gap. The resident geneticist, Charlene (XT-17), is a named individual character
rather than a population-level cultural finding and belongs in future Course of Events or companion
material, not this catalog.

infrastructure relationship (per ChemMatCARS) rather than only emotional companionship — worth treating
