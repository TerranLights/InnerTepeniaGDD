## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-16)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Zhongshan's
own conditions (its unbroken founding-to-present Chinese demographic continuity, its self-sufficient
Prydz Bay fishing economy, and its "chaos that is actually structure" civic character) that a given
population's originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X because
they're from nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Zhongshan's Enneagram profile,
recorded in `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Tri-Cities_Region.md`, is **Instinctive (Gut) / Withdrawn /
Competency** — "the Quiet City," a personality organized around autonomy, presence, and simply *being*
rather than analyzing or performing. Zhongshan is the only Tepenian city where the founding operator
nation stayed demographically Primary from founding through the present, unbroken — the city never lost
its identity, only refined it, always in close proximity to two very different neighbors (Sinheung,
Shirayuki) without ever blending into either. A genuine "double life" dynamic governs daily life: a
shared public rhythm (the non-prying social contract — nobody asks a single personal question) with
one's own heritage kept quietly at home. `Zhongshan_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences research
grounds Zhongshan's own political stance toward the Sinian Federation directly in Vilnius's real
precedent (bold defiance shaped by a complicated history with larger neighboring powers) and its own
semi-gridlike, "never fully abandoned even as later construction bent and broke it" street layout in
Yekaterinburg's own real grid-plus-organic-growth history. Its Cross-Reference Synthesis identifies the
**Crossing Quarter** (an unbroken artisan neighborhood where the city's central cultural fact — an
identity never diluted, only refined — is directly visible in a handful of workshops) and the **Loud
Quarter** (a counterculture district deliberately left without an official name, since formalizing it
would violate the same non-prying norm that governs individual privacy elsewhere in the city) as the
two most thematically concentrated sites in Zhongshan.

## Synthesis Notes (Phase 2 — draft, not yet developer-confirmed as final)

**Working name: "Zhongshanese"** *(the developer's own established term for this city's people —
reused directly here, not a placeholder, per the project's own flagship worked example status).*

Zhongshan is the developer's own designated flagship worked example, and its neo-culture reflects that:
a single, unbroken founding-to-present Chinese demographic continuity — the only Tepenian city where
this was never displaced — refined rather than diluted across the exile era, expressed through a
genuine "double life" dynamic (shared public rhythm, heritage kept quietly at home) that every other
population here integrates into rather than competes with. Every Significant-tier population beyond
China (South Korea, Germany, Japan, Russia, Australia) reads comparatively thin at the Surface/Deep
Culture level specifically because Zhongshan's own cultural framework was already fully formed by the
time each arrived — this thinness is not a research gap the way it reads elsewhere in this project, but
a genuine, established structural fact about how this particular city absorbs newcomers. China alone
carries the city's real cultural weight, grounded in three distinct real-world precedents: Ningbo-
Zhoushan (maritime trade), Vilnius (bold political defiance toward the Sinian Federation), and
Yekaterinburg (the grid-plus-organic-growth architecture underlying the city's own "chaos that is
actually structure" street layout).

**Two concrete sites worth carrying into future Course of Events or level-design work:** the
**Crossing Quarter**, an unbroken artisan neighborhood condensing Zhongshan's central cultural fact
into touchable objects, and the **Loud Quarter**, a Southeast-Asian-inflected counterculture district
deliberately left without an official name — not an oversight, but the same non-prying, "what happens
in your neighborhood is your business" civic principle applied at district scale rather than
person-to-person scale. A future formal renaming or official designation of the Loud Quarter would
itself be a violation of one of Zhongshan's own core values, a genuinely usable in-world reason a
character might resist "cleaning up" the district's status.

**Worth flagging for the eventual Tri-Cities cross-referencing pass:** Zhongshan shares its Instinctive
(Gut)-centered Major Theme with Sinheung, but expresses it in nearly the opposite direction — Zhongshan's
quiet, self-contained Withdrawn/Competency energy versus Sinheung's forceful, outward-projecting
Assertive/Reactive energy. Shirayuki, founded via the same Jeju-do mechanism that produced Sinheung
(though allocated to Japan there, and never invoked for Zhongshan — China's claim predates the
diplomatic partition entirely, the very reason the partition was needed), stands apart from both on a
Feeling-centered axis. Worth a dedicated cross-city comparison now that all three Larsemann Hills
cities (Zhongshan, Sinheung, Shirayuki) have completed Phase 1c.
