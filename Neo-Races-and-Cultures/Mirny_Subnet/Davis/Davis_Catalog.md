## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — China. Significant — Japan, Germany, UK, South Korea,

- **Geography:** Vestfold Hills, Ingrid Christensen Coast, Prydz Bay — one of the largest ice-free

  lakes and fjord-like inlets.
- **Geological composition:** Ice-free oasis, exposed rock — significantly larger and more varied than
  the smaller oasis sites (Schirmacher/Lazar, Larsemann Hills/Sinheung cluster) elsewhere in Tepenia.

## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** a large, varied ice-free oasis (~400km²) with freshwater/saltwater lakes and
fjord-like inlets — matches should favor genuine lake-district terrain rather than a single-lake
oasis.

- **China (Primary):** Qinghai Lake basin — a large high-plateau lake oasis surrounded by arid
  terrain, exposed rock, genuine scale comparable to Davis.
- **Japan (Significant):** Shiretoko Peninsula lake district, Hokkaido — a modest match; smaller
  scale than Davis but a real lake-and-rock terrain.
- **Germany (Significant):** Mecklenburg Lake District — a genuine large lake district, though much
  wetter/greener than Davis; flagged as a partial match for Phase 2 review.
- **UK (Significant):** The English Lake District — fells and lakes, a real varied terrain and a
  fitting name-parallel, though again much greener than Davis's exposed rock; flagged as partial.
- **South Korea (Significant):** No strong domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** No strong match — flagged as weakest.
- **Australia (Significant):** Lake Eyre basin — Australia's own rare desert lake system, though
  usually dry rather than perennially lake-filled; flagged as a partial match.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type, resolved 2026-07-16:** a combined ecological/limnological research hub AND Tepenia's
foremost sheltered-agriculture/greenhouse city — the "breadbasket of Tepenia." Both functions grow
directly out of the same underlying fact: Davis sits on the largest ice-free oasis in the country
(~400km², diverse lakes and fjord-like inlets), giving it more exploitable fresh water and usable land
than any other city in Tepenia. Real-world matches below draw on both functions per nation, since a
single city rarely embodies both in the real world.

- **China (Primary):** Ecological — Poyang Lake, China's largest freshwater lake and a major wetland
  research area. Agriculture — Shandong's greenhouse vegetable belt, the core of China's status as the
  world's largest greenhouse-vegetable producer; an excellent match on both fronts, especially fitting
  given China's own Primary-tier presence here.
- **Japan (Significant):** Ecological — Lake Biwa, Japan's largest lake and home to a major
  limnological research institute. Agriculture — Kōchi Prefecture's greenhouse horticulture industry.
- **Germany (Significant):** Ecological — Lake Constance (Bodensee) research. Agriculture — the Lower
  Rhine greenhouse-vegetable region, a modest but real match.
- **UK (Significant):** Ecological — the English Lake District's Freshwater Biological Association at
  Windermere, a strong match that also ties back to the Lake District terrain match already used in
  this city's own Real-World Parallel Locations section. Agriculture — the historic Lea Valley
  glasshouse district, once Britain's defining greenhouse-growing region.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Ecological — no strong domestic large-lake-research match; flagged as
  weak. Agriculture — Korea's Jeolla province greenhouse/smart-farm belt, a solid match.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Ecological — Lake Toba, Indonesia's largest lake, a real research site.
  Agriculture — no strong domestic greenhouse-agriculture identity given the tropical climate; flagged
  as weak on this front specifically.
- **Australia (Significant):** Ecological — no strong domestic large-permanent-lake-research match;
  flagged as weak. Agriculture — the Bowen/Bundaberg horticulture belt, a modest match.
