conditions (its windbreak-architecture industrial core, its expanded status as a top-tier

nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Mirny's Enneagram profile is
**Instinctive (Gut) / Withdrawn / Competency** — a city engineered, literally architecturally, to turn
inward against a hostile environment: a fortified outer ring built to shed katabatic wind, sheltering
huge industrial yards and residential space alike inside. Its pride is explicitly *unforced* — felt and
lived, not performed, a calm confidence in its own capability that never needs defending. Mirny shares
this exact profile with Byrd, Sanay, Denison, Davis, and Zhongshan, distinguished among them by
**industrial shelter** specifically. `Mirny_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences research grounds
this directly in Yakutsk's real precedent (severe conditions managed as routine, not spectacle; a city
transformed by resource extraction rather than founding glory) and Nizhny Tagil's real precedent (a
single dominant industrial complex effectively defining a whole city's economy) for Russia specifically,
and in Wolfsburg's real precedent (a city physically organized as an enclosure around its own industrial
core) as general structural flavor for the windbreak-architecture identity, not tied to any specific
population here. Mirny's own industrial role was elevated 2026-07-16 to national significance — its
quarries now feed both the established eastern-highway construction-materials chain and Sinheung's
fabrication-synthesis-chamber manufacturing, a role previously misattributed to Davis.
