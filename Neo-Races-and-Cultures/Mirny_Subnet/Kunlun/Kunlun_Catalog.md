- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA, Russia, China. Significant — Canada, Japan, UK,

  presence is forbidden as a safety measure (altitude + cold).
- **Geography:** Dome A (Dome Argus), highest point on the Antarctic Ice Sheet, ~4,093m — effective

  astronomy and ice-core drilling.
- **Geological composition:** East Antarctic Plateau ice-dome, the most extreme version — highest
  elevation and likely coldest sustained environment of any Tepenian city.
