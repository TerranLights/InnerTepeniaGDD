For You, Made Into a Place You'd Choose"** — an imposed, external founding (the pre-exile Jeju-do
diplomatic allocation) transformed, over generations, into a genuinely, authentically chosen identity.

Harajuku) whose own real origin stories — external influence reworked into authentically local

**A quiet structural irony worth naming, not resolved here:** Shirayuki's entire founding purpose was

yet China remains present at Significant tier (7.18%) — deliberately counterbalanced by design rather
than absent, a different outcome from simply excluding the population the allocation was meant to
check.
