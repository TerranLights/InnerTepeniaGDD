## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type:** university/education center — education is a genuine economic pillar, drawing students
from across Tepenia — PLUS an arts/cultural identity anchored around a downtown gallery district
(city-wide sculpture/murals).

- **Japan (Primary):** Kyoto — a real Japanese city whose national identity combines a major
  university-town character with a strong arts/cultural-heritage reputation; an excellent dual match.
- **Australia (Significant):** Melbourne — a genuine Australian university-and-arts city.
- **Russia (Significant):** Saint Petersburg — a real Russian university-and-arts city with a strong
  gallery/museum culture.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Daegu, or more specifically Seoul's Hongik University arts district —
  a real Korean university-plus-arts-district match.
- **China (Significant):** Hangzhou — a real Chinese city with a strong university-and-arts-culture
  reputation.
- **Germany (Significant):** Heidelberg — a classic real German university-town match, though with a
  weaker specifically-gallery-district identity; flagged as a partial match.
