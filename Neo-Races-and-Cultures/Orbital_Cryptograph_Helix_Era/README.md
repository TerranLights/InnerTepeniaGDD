# Orbital Era — Scope Boundary and Handoff

**Rescoped 2026-08-29.** An earlier version of this file was written as a development brief for orbital
neo-cultures. **That overstepped this repo's remit** and has been cut back.

---

## ⚠ What this repo does and does not own

**This repo is the Inner Tepenia CRPG Game Design Document.** Being what it is, it also establishes the
foundational reference for the **Southern Lights** TV series and for the **opening situational setting** at the
start of the **Cryptograph Helix** novel series.

It does **not** develop orbital society.

> **Orbital-era questions — who those people become, what culture they build, how they process what happened,
> what their institutions and neo-cultures look like — are the domain of the Cryptograph Helix design-docs
> repo.** They are not answered here and should not be.

What lives here is only the **foundation** the Cryptograph Helix repo starts *from*: facts established by, and
load-bearing for, Inner Tepenia's own story. This folder is therefore a **boundary marker and handoff**, not a
work queue.

`../README.md` still lists an orbital extension as Phase 3 of the Neo-Races and Neo-Cultures project. Read that
as *"the same method will eventually be applied there"* — the application itself happens in the other repo.

## What Inner Tepenia establishes (and the other repo inherits)

Foundational, and referenced by this repo's own material.

### 1. Orbital infrastructure exists, from early in the Second Interwar Period

Not a late development. It is in place well before the war.

### 2. Contact with the surface splits sharply by era

- **Early Second Interwar → 2812 (Tepenia as a proper, functioning country).** The link is **rich**. Tepenia
  transmits **enormous** volumes of data to orbit, and where bandwidth is insufficient it **physically
  transports datadrives** up. What moves includes **backups and archives of the Arcanet itself** — which is not
  only Antarctican in content, being supplemented by datadrives carried down from **Upper Earth and the old
  pre-war human internet**, layered with **Tepenians' own original cultures** and native Antarctican network
  activity.
- **2812 onward (post-Long-Night-War, post-Planetary Split Brain).** Transmission capability degrades badly;
  the only robot-occupied part of the planet is Antarctica.
- **The channel never fully closes.** Specific people and characters can still transmit to orbit regularly. It
  simply stops being an ordinary, society-wide occurrence.

This matters *to Inner Tepenia* because it sets what any surface character can and cannot reach, and when.

### 3. Orbit watched the war, and was helpless

**Established canon.** Orbit observed the Long Night War and could not intervene, watching its progenitive —
or sister — civilization die.

Recorded here because it is foundational and because this repo's characters touch it. **Whether Tepenia is
orbit's progenitor or its sister is undecided**, and that decision belongs to the Cryptograph Helix repo.

### 4. Individual ties crossed the gap, and persist

Both directions, both already in this repo's character canon:

- A Zukelli-descended refugee fled **to** orbit for gravity-related medical relief — lower gravity eased the
  physical pain of an illness (TCY-45 seed,
  `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Methodology/00e_Quick_Capture_Seed_Batch_Input_[rewritable].txt`).
- A surface researcher's entire life's work is developing material-efficient farming and harvesting methods and
  **transmitting the schematics up**, so space-dwelling Tepenians can better provide for themselves
  (STP-06, `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/TBN [STP-06 thicc Chinese Hao]/README.md`).

### 5. Orbit is not a knowledge recipient

It originates knowledge the surface physically cannot produce — there are real categories of research possible
only in low or zero gravity. It also produces its own ordinary cultural output: entertainment, literature,
music, humour, much of which has no reason to ever reach Earth.

### 6. There is no real-world orbital basis to research, and none is needed

The only real-world referent is the **ISS** — roughly seven people at a time, on months-long rotations, never
multi-generational, no children born aboard, no economy, no elders. It is a workplace with a crew roster, not a
society. No multi-generational orbital population has ever existed, so the absence of source material is a fact
about the world rather than a gap in research. Derivation runs from conditions, not from comparanda.

## Handed off to the Cryptograph Helix repo

Questions raised while establishing the above. **Recorded so they are not lost, explicitly NOT to be answered
here.**

- Progenitor or sister — which relationship, and how it colours everything downstream.
- Why orbit was helpless: no descent capability, no ability to affect ground events, deliberate
  non-intervention, or something else. The emotional core is fixed; the mechanism is not.
- What orbit's vantage point means for the historical record — plausibly the most complete observational
  account of the war anywhere, including material the surface does not have.
- Whether pre-2812 archives in orbit hold clean copies of what the surface has since lost. Set against the
  Circuit's established data-archaeology work recovering what the **Great Corruption** took, this is a
  potentially significant connection — but it is a Cryptograph Helix decision, and it would reach back into
  Inner Tepenia canon, so it needs coordinating across both repos rather than deciding unilaterally in either.
- How a witnessing population differs from a surviving one. Surface Tepenians are survivors; orbit are
  witnesses. Different wound, different people.
- Whether orbit still knows how much survived, given that transmission degraded precisely when it most needed
  to check.
- How the plural orbital neo-cultures diverge from each other, given minimal physical travel between locations.

## Dependency, and where the method lives

**Orbital population composition is unstarted** — its own deliberately-reserved high-token task
(`project_orbital_composition` in project memory). Any orbital work in either repo is gated on it.

The derivation method itself is not orbital-specific and already exists here:
`../_Method/Cultural_Iceberg_Method.md` (Hall's surface/deep-culture sorting) and
`../_Method/Human_Universals_Culture_Framework.md` (Brown's universals as a believability floor). Both are
sorting and question-generating frameworks, so neither depends on having a real-world referent. The robot
counterpart is `Outside-World/.../Local_Robot_Culture_Methodology/`.

The **neo-culture quality bar** is set in `../README.md`: a genuine new people — *"a real, new 'third thing,'
culturally distinct from any of the origin populations that fed into it"* — the way Taiwanese, Singaporean,
Québécois, and Afrikaner are, rather than variant flavors of a parent nationality.
