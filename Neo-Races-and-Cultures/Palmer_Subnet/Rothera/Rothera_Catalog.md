- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA. Significant — Germany, France, UK, Canada, Mexico,

- **Geography:** Rothera Point, eastern shore of Adelaide Island, western Antarctic Peninsula —
  sheltered relative to the open Drake Passage; Marguerite Bay to the south, the Lemaire Channel to the
  north. Paved runway (Bonner Laboratory airstrip) made it a secondary aviation hub.

  mildest climate type in Tepenia alongside Palmer City, mean annual temp ~-9°C.
