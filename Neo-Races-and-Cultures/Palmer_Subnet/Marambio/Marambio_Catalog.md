## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA. Significant — Germany, UK, Spain, Canada, Mexico,
  Brazil. Notable — Argentina (founding wave), Chile (founding wave), Uruguay. Exact per-nation share %
  in `Specs/Marambio.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Seymour Island (Isla Marambio), eastern Antarctic Peninsula coast, Weddell Sea — south
  of Esperanza but still north of the Antarctic Circle. Geologically extraordinary: Eocene-era fossil
  beds (~34-56 million years old).
- **Geological composition:** Sedimentary rock island — a genuine geological archive of Antarctica's
  pre-ice, forested past; runway infrastructure made it the subnet's eastern aviation hub.
