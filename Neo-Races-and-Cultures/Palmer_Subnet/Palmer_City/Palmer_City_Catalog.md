## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA. Significant — Germany, France, UK, Canada, Mexico,
  Brazil. Notable — all remaining master-list nations (43-nation roster total — Tepenia's most diverse
  city by design). Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Palmer_City.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Palmer_City.md`,
  and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Anvers Island, off the Antarctic Peninsula — the northernmost finger of the continent,
  reaching toward South America; the most temperate and accessible Antarctic station of all. First point
  of arrival for the original Falkland Treaty exiles in 2564.
- **Geological composition:** Temperate coastal island terrain — rarely drops below -20°C in winter,
  occasionally above freezing in summer; the mildest and most accessible geography in Tepenia.
