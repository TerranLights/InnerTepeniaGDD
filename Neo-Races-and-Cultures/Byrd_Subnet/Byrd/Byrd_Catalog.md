## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA, Canada. Significant — Australia, Japan, South Korea,
  China. Notable — 35 additional nations (Byrd carries the master-list roster nearly as broadly as
  Palmer City; see `Specs/Byrd.md` for the full list). Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Byrd.md` and
  `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Deep interior of Marie Byrd Land, West Antarctica, ~1,530m elevation — not coastal,
  not at a dramatic feature, in the relatively featureless interior of the West Antarctic Ice Sheet.
  Progressively buried by snow accumulation over generations, giving the city a layered, tunneled
  understructure.
- **Geological composition:** West Antarctic marine ice sheet — bedrock lies below sea level, inherently
  less stable than East Antarctica's continental bedrock, with some areas volcanically active. Lower
  altitude than Concordia but still deep inland and isolated.

## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, Primary+Significant complete 2026-07-17)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Byrd's own
conditions (its underground, progressively-buried civic structure, its self-sufficiency born of
necessity rather than pride, and its integrated fabrication-and-dispatch economy) that a given
population's originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X because
they're from nation Y." This entry covers Primary + Significant tier only, per the current phase's
scope; the 35-nation Notable-tier roster is reserved for a later, much larger pass on this city,
comparable in scale to Palmer City's own undertaking. **Byrd carries a genuine, developer-flagged
internal contradiction over whether it has overland highway access at all** — standing, unresolved by
the developer's own choice, not something this catalog attempts to settle.

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Byrd's Enneagram profile is
**Instinctive (Gut) / Withdrawn / Competency** — "Built From Below, Then Left Alone," raw endurance and
presence rather than analysis or self-image. Self-sufficiency began as sheer necessity ("less pride
than necessity") and only became pride over time, hardship absorbed and metabolized into identity
without much reflection along the way. Byrd is the most isolated surviving city in Tepenia, isolated by
identity as much as circumstance — its hospitality character is "reserved but genuine," since visitors
are rare enough to be treated as a real event. `Byrd_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences research
grounds this directly: Derinkuyu, Turkey (an ancient 18-level underground city holding up to 20,000
people with genuine planned civic logic — the single tightest structural match available anywhere in
the real world for an underground settlement at real civic scale) and the Ruhr Valley, Germany (a
dense, interconnected industrial mega-region where multiple manufacturing sectors feed each other in
the same physical space — the clearest real-world model for Byrd's own genuinely integrated fabrication
economy) are both general civic-identity flavor not tied to a specific Primary/Significant nation here
(neither Turkey nor Germany holds Significant tier or above). Chelyabinsk, Russia ("Tankograd," a city
that became a nation's critical manufacturing chokepoint under wartime pressure and never fully
returned to peacetime scale) offers a fitting historical echo for Byrd's own post-war role as one of
only two places left that can manufacture a robot from nothing, and Norilsk, Russia's own inverted
isolation precedent (depending on the connection it doesn't have, keeping the one it does) sharpens
exactly how precarious Byrd's own remaining lifeline is — though Russia itself sits at Notable tier only
here.
