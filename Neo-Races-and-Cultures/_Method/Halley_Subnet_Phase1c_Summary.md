## Recurring Methodology Findings — Validated Across a Full Subnet

Ready to carry into the remaining five subnets (Janbogo, Mawson, Mirny, Palmer, Byrd) as settled
methodology, not re-derived from scratch per city:

1. **Founding nation ≠ present-day cultural majority.** Sanay (South Africa) and Abowasa
   (Finland/Sweden) both show founding nations drifting down to Notable tier while surviving only as
   civic mythology, not lived-culture majority.
2. **Population share and narrative/geographic-match weight regularly decouple.** Confirmed at Abowasa
   (Germany), Belgrano (UK), and Princess Elisabeth (UK) — small populations repeatedly carry
   disproportionate thematic weight when their real-world match happens to be unusually precise.
3. **Brazil's City-Type matches track a real, predictable rule, not random weakness:** weak at small,
   purpose-built, single-mission settlements (Belgrano, Halley, Neumayer, Princess Elisabeth, Troll —
   five confirmed instances); strong at large, organic, multi-purpose cities (Sanay, Abowasa, Lazar).
   This should now be treated as a predictive expectation, not re-derived from scratch per city.
4. **Multiple populations converging on the same civic value via genuinely different real-world routes
   is common and worth calling out explicitly.** Belgrano and Troll both show three-way convergence
   ("maker city," "freight precision"); Neumayer shows the opposite pattern — four populations reaching
   the same abstract value (research prestige) via genuinely competing specific traditions rather than
   converging cleanly.
