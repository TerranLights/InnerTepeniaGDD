# City Functional/Civic Type Reference

Compiled 2026-07-16, alongside the Phase 1b-ii "City-Type Parallels" pass. This is a standing
reference of each of the 35 cities' defining functional/economic role or disproportionately-famous
cultural/leisure landmark — independent of geography/geology (contrast the terrain-based Real-World
Parallel Locations sections in each city's own Catalog). Saved here so this doesn't need to be
re-derived from scratch later. Source: each city's own `Specs/[City].md`, specifically the
"Significance" line, "Economy & Industry" section, "Nationally known for" line, and "Notable
Locations" section.

Two worked examples that established the category: **Sanay** (major shipping/logistics port city —
endless shipyards, receiving South African freighter shipments) and **Casey** (nationally famous for
"Splinters," its huge live-music bar — a disproportionately famous site of leisurely-cultural
relevance).

---

## Halley Subnet
- **Abowasa** — Resolved 2026-07-16: a quiet twin-settlement residential/commuter community — a
  scaled-down twin-city-merger identity (echoing Budapest/Minneapolis–St. Paul/Kansas City, downsized
  to fit Abowasa's own small scale and genuinely dual founding, real Aboa+Wasa stations ~200m apart).
  A residential community with its own local industry and culture, whose working population largely
  commutes to Sanay's port or Troll's airfield. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Belgrano** — Aviation/airfield hub + major port (one of two South African freighter receiving
  ports, alongside Sanay), PLUS a fairly well-developed secondary garage/warehouse/industrial culture
  *(developer note added 2026-07-16)* — present but not as developed as its aeronautics identity. ALSO
  an Atlantic-coast receiving point for the established South America shipping corridor via Marambio
  *(clarified 2026-07-16, sourced from `City_Vision_Notes/Marambio.md`, not a new addition — corrects
  an earlier Phase 1c pass that flagged Brazil as unanchored here before this corridor was accounted
  for)*. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Halley** — Pure highway-gateway town; no significant independent economy beyond its Hwy 59 gateway
  role to the South Pole route. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Lazar** — Administrative/commercial hub at megacity scale; Tepenia's largest city by population,
  genuine megacity-scale commercial density (holographic ads, multicolored lights). No single named
  leisure landmark — the commercial glow itself is the identity.
- **Neumayer** — Scientific research center (atmospheric/glaciology + precision engineering R&D,
  including fabrication-synthesis chamber schematics). No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Princess Elisabeth** — Scientific research center with a renewable-energy specialization; genuine
  traded expertise in Antarctic energy systems. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Sanay** — Major port/shipping hub (the project's own worked example) + Arcanet relay/comms hub. No
  notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Troll** — Aviation/airfield hub functioning as a genuine intermodal freight/logistics center. No
  notable leisure/cultural landmark (Troll Airfield itself is infrastructural, not a leisure site).

## Janbogo Subnet
- **Cape Adare** — Historic/heritage site; Borchgrevink's Hut (1899), the oldest human structure on
  the Antarctic continent, is the city's most historically important pre-exile site.
- **Denison** — Industrial (structural/wind engineering, historically chamber manufacturing) +
  scientific research. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Dumont d'Urville** — Resolved 2026-07-16: a wildlife-tourism/nature-reserve gateway city (echoing
  Kaikōura/Simon's Town/Punta Tombo — the penguin colonies are the main draw), organized around a
  small, walkable "Quebec City-like" town core with a genuine arts/music/culture identity of its own,
  not just a research outpost with penguins nearby.
- **Fort McMurdo** — Mining/heavy industrial center (Tepenia's primary heavy industrial center) +
  historical administrative/governance role (former national capital). No notable leisure landmark
  (Mount Erebus is geological, not civic).
- **Janbogo** — Major port/shipping and supply hub. Landmark: Majyao's Teahouse, an establishment
  famous throughout Tepenia.
- **Scott** — Administrative/garrison partner-city to Fort McMurdo + minor industrial (volcanic
  material collection) + religious/memorial site: Hut Point is Scott's most sacred civic location, a
  national remembrance pilgrimage site tied to Independence Day.
- **Zukelli** — Food/hospitality industry (own food, craft, and design industries grown from a
  hospitality-centered civic culture). Landmark: "the cultural quarter" (food halls/communal spaces,
  not one single named site).

## Mawson Subnet
- **Dome Fuji** — Fundamentally, foundationally a religious site/city *(reinforced 2026-07-16: this is
  not a co-equal dual type — established by and for the Ice-Cold Buddhists, arguably the primary
  sacred site in all of Tepenia; that is the city's core identity)*. Ice-core science is a genuine but
  distant-second economic activity, not a co-equal identity.
- **Mawson** — Administrative/Arcanet hub + scientific research + tourism/hospitality — Tepenia's
  go-to honeymoon destination for newly-married human-robot couples, a genuine resort-town character.
- **Sayowa** — Highway junction/transit hub (Mawson subnet's western-end logistics) + scientific
  research + maritime trade. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.

## Mirny Subnet
- **Casey** — Highway junction/transit hub, PLUS the project's own second worked example:
  "Splinters," Tepenia's largest and most famous bar (Classic Rock/Jazz/Blues/Acoustic Folk music
  scene), famous along the whole Dumont d'Urville Sea and Indian Ocean coastline.
- **Davis** — Resolved 2026-07-16: a combined ecological/limnological research hub AND Tepenia's
  foremost sheltered-agriculture/greenhouse city — the "breadbasket of Tepenia." Both functions grow
  from the same fact: Davis sits on the largest ice-free oasis in the country (~400km², diverse lakes
  and fjord-like inlets), giving it more exploitable fresh water and usable land than any other
  Tepenian city.
- **Kunlun** — Scientific research (astronomy/ice-core science) + religious/pilgrimage center (Kunlun
  Observatory, arguably *the* primary sacred site in all of Tepenia) + industrial (joint
  chamber-manufacturing outpost with Vostok). 100% robot population.
- **Mirny** — Administrative/comms hub (subnet hub function) + industrial (quarrying raw materials,
  processed into construction materials for the eastern highways).
- **Shirayuki** — University/education center (education is a genuine economic pillar, drawing
  students from across Tepenia) + arts/cultural. Landmark: the downtown gallery district
  (city-wide sculpture/murals).
- **Sinheung** — Mining/heavy industrial fabrication (manufactures fabrication-synthesis chambers) +
  minor aviation. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Vostok** — Scientific research center (genetics research is the dominant activity) + industrial
  (joint chamber-manufacturing outpost with Kunlun). No notable leisure/cultural landmark.
- **Zhongshan** — Maritime trade/logistics (Prydz Bay) + scientific research; cluster economy shared
  with Sinheung. No notable leisure/cultural landmark.

## Palmer Subnet
- **Esperanza** — Port/maritime trans-shipment hub (natural trans-shipment point between the Halley
  and Palmer subnets) + family-settlement/civic-historical significance. No notable leisure landmark.
- **Juan Carlos** — Maritime/fishing + administrative (origin of Tepenia's first bureaucratic/customs
  archive). No notable leisure landmark.
- **Marambio** — Aviation/airfield hub (the Palmer subnet's primary aviation hub) + maritime trade via
  the established South America shipping corridor (clarified 2026-07-16, sourced from
  `City_Vision_Notes/Marambio.md` — ships onward to Atlantic coastal cities including Belgrano and
  Sanay). The Eocene fossil beds are a genuine geological/scientific fact about the city but *not* a
  defining cultural characteristic of its population *(developer correction 2026-07-16)* — kept as a
  geography note, not a City-Type pillar. No notable leisure landmark.
- **Palmer City** — Cultural/entertainment capital of pre-war Tepenia (entertainment and hospitality
  industries central to its economy) + port-of-entry/administrative (first customs point; site of the
  original Falkland Treaty exile arrival). Landmark: Miles Davis Boulevard, a real jazz-record-culture
  civic identity.
- **Port Lockroy** — Maritime/heritage-tourism port. Landmark: the post office, a real heritage
  courier institution central to the city's identity.
- **Rothera** — Mining/industrial center (the Palmer subnet's industrial hub — raw materials become
  finished infrastructure components here) + aviation (Bonner Laboratory airstrip). No notable leisure
  landmark.
- **Sejong** — Transit/trade hub (multinational — King George Island's favorable position made it a
  trade and transit hub). No notable leisure landmark.
- **Signy** — Scientific research (marine biology/ecology) + fishing/maritime resources (Scotia Sea).
  No notable leisure landmark.

## Byrd Subnet
- **Byrd** — Industrial/manufacturing (mechanized fabrication-synthesis chambers) + logistics/dispatch
  hub — import/export & dispatch are Byrd's real dominant sectors. No notable leisure landmark.
