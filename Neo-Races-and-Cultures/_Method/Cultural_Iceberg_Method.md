## The core idea

Culture is like an iceberg: only about 10% is visible above the waterline at any given time. The
other ~90% is invisible unless you go looking for it. Most surface-level comparative-culture work
(the kind that produces a fictional culture that's really just "the same values, different food")
never gets below the waterline. This project is explicitly trying not to make that mistake.

**A genuine limitation of the analogy, worth remembering:** the iceberg is a static image; real culture
is dynamic and constantly changing. Don't treat any single cataloging pass as a permanently fixed
description — a neo-culture, once synthesized, should be understood as a living thing that could keep
developing, not a locked specification.

---

## Surface Culture (~10%, visible)

- Food
- Flags
- Festivals
- Fashion
- Holidays
- Music
- Performances
- Dances
- Games
- Arts & Crafts
- Literature
- Language

This is the layer most comparative research naturally gravitates toward, and the layer least useful on
its own for actually synthesizing a distinct neo-culture. Necessary, but never sufficient.

---

## Deep Culture (~90%, invisible)

**Communication styles and rules:**
- Facial expressions, gestures, eye contact
- Personal space, touching, body language
- Conversational patterns in different social situations
- Handling and displaying of emotion
- Tone of voice

**Notions of:**
- Courtesy and manners
- Friendship, leadership
- Cleanliness, modesty, beauty

**Concepts of:**
- Self, time, past and future
- Fairness and justice
- Roles related to age, sex, class, family, etc.

**Attitudes toward:**
- Elders, adolescents, dependents
- Rule expectations, work, authority
- Cooperation vs. competition
- Relationships with animals, age, sin, death

**Approaches to:**
- Religion, courtship, marriage
- Raising children, decision-making, problem solving

This is the layer that actually produces a genuinely distinct people, per the real-world Taiwanese/
Singaporean/Québécois/Afrikaner precedent this whole project is modeled on. A real neo-culture needs
real, specific answers in this layer, not just a new surface-culture coat of paint over an unchanged
deep-culture core.

---

## How this applies per city

For each nationality present in meaningful proportion in a given Tepenian city:
1. Identify one or more real-world communities of that same nationality/ethnicity living in
   geographically/geologically comparable conditions (same terrain type, same geological character —
   coastal, volcanic, mountainous, etc. — obviously without the extreme cold and wind, since the
   comparison is about land, not climate).
2. Research that real-world community using the full iceberg — both layers, not just the surface one.
3. Record findings in the city's own folder, sorted by iceberg layer, following
   `City_Catalog_Template.md`.
4. Repeat for every nationality present in the city at a meaningful population share before moving to
   synthesis (Phase 2) for that city.

See `Progress_Tracker.md` for the full city checklist.
