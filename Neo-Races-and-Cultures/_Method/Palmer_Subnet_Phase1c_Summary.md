# Palmer Subnet — Phase 1c Complete (2026-07-16)

**Kept separate from any individual city's own Catalog file on purpose** — this is a cross-city,
subnet-level summary, not part of Signy's (or any other city's) own Synthesis Notes.

All eight Palmer subnet cities now have complete Per-Nation Entries and draft Phase 2 syntheses:

- **Esperanza** ("Esperanzan") — a "family/genesis city" tied to its real founding history (first
  child born in Antarctica). Argentina, the smallest population, holds the single most powerfully
  anchored population found anywhere in the project: strongest geography match, strongest City-Type
  match, and a literal historical tie to the founding event, all at once.
- **Juan Carlos** ("Juan Carlan"/"Carlino," undecided) — a fishing-port-plus-archive city. Spain holds
  a namesake claim (the real Spanish base is literally "Juan Carlos I"). Italy is the first confirmed
  instance of a new pattern: a large population with no strong anchor on any dimension, not explained
  by the Brazil settlement-type rule.
- **Marambio** ("Marambian") — required a full correction pass. Paleontology is real geology, not a
  population-culture driver. The port is the already-established South America shipping corridor
  (`City_Vision_Notes/Marambio.md`), not a new addition — this also corrected Belgrano's own Brazil
  entry in the Halley subnet, previously flagged as the weakest-anchored population in the project.
  Germany's match upgraded to Duisburg, the strongest dual-function real-world precedent found in the
  whole project. Surfaced the process requirement (check Megasheet/Enneagram/Vision-Notes before
  drafting) now standing for all future cities.
- **Palmer City** ("Palmerite"/"Palmerian," undecided; Primary+Significant scope only) — the first city
  where every population converges on one shared civic value (identity/felt experience) instead of
  splitting a function-vs-warmth axis. Canada/Montreal confirmed as the standout real-world precedent,
  already established in existing research before this pass began.
- **Port Lockroy** ("Lockroyan") — the first city with an explicitly temporal, not national, founding
  tension (living memory vs. heritage spectacle). UK holds a literal historical tie (the real Operation
  Tabarin); France's Saint-Malo match is a precise structural echo of the city's own layered-history
  identity.
- **Rothera** ("Rotheran") — the clearest full-population convergence found in the project so far; all
  seven nations land on industrial craft competence via seven distinct real-world routes, with no
  meaningful counter-register. Source of Tepenia's own project-wide glitch-coolant "working-class"
  canon.
- **Sejong** ("Sejongite," reusing the city's own real demonym) — the process-requirement check paid
  off most dramatically here: the original pass read eight of nine populations as weak, but existing
  research already established the real precedent (Izmir/Smyrna and Keelung's "boundary zones" of
  self-governed quarters) — a shared structural pattern held by every population, not a single
  dominant match. South Korea's namesake claim (the real Sejong Station) mirrors Spain's at Juan
  Carlos.
- **Signy** ("Signian") — the highest Primary-tier population concentration found anywhere in the
  project (USA, 33.33%), yet the quietest civic register of any city cataloged, matching its own
  Thinking/Withdrawn/Competency Enneagram core rather than its numeric dominance. Fishing confirmed as
  the dominant economy over marine-biology research.

## Recurring Methodology Findings — Palmer Subnet

Builds directly on the four findings validated across the Halley subnet
(`Halley_Subnet_Phase1c_Summary.md`); all four held here too. New findings from this subnet:

1. **A city's own existing research (Megasheet, Enneagram, Vision Notes, Cross-Reference Synthesis)
   must be checked before drafting Phase 1c entries — this is now a standing process requirement, not
   optional.** Marambio and Sejong both required substantial correction passes because this wasn't
   done on the first attempt; Palmer City, Port Lockroy, Rothera, and Signy were drafted correctly the
   first time specifically because the check happened before writing, not after.
2. **A population's real-world geological/economic *fact* about a city doesn't automatically mean it's
   a *cultural* driver for that city's population.** Marambio's fossil beds are genuine, notable
   science — but not something that shapes how residents communicate, organize family life, or mark
   status, the way the aviation/port economy does.
3. **"Weak on both axes" findings should be checked against inter-city trade corridors and shared
   structural patterns before being treated as settled.** Confirmed twice this subnet: Marambio/
   Belgrano's Brazil correction (a missed shipping corridor) and Sejong's full-city correction (a
   missed shared structural pattern, not a per-nation search problem at all).
4. **A new "large population, no strong anchor" category exists, distinct from Brazil's predictable
   settlement-type rule.** Italy (Juan Carlos, Marambio) and Spain/Mexico (Marambio specifically) are
   the confirmed instances so far — not yet explained by a settled rule, worth a dedicated look once
   more subnets are done.
5. **Numeric population dominance does not determine a city's civic register on its own.** Signy's USA
   population (33.33%, the highest Primary-tier share found anywhere) still produces a quiet,
   Withdrawn-profile culture, because the city's own established personality core points that
   direction regardless of which population is largest.
