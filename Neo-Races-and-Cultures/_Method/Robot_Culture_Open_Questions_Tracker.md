## Resolved (2026-08-06)

Both items below were resolved by deriving the answer from information already established elsewhere in
the framework — not fresh creative decisions. Kept here for the record; the authoritative versions live in
`Human_Universals_Culture_Framework.md`.

### [x] Does the "cognitive imperative" transfer to robots?

**No, not as an innate mechanism.** Only the Personality Module and, where applicable, the Language Module
are ever programmed into a robot — there's no room for a third built-in drive. Robot religion is a **New
Universal**, reached the same emergent way robot cooperation and morality are: through lived experience and
accumulated culture, not a hardwired explanatory drive.

### [x] Do robots have anything resembling disease?

**No — Non-Transfer.** Robots' established "things that go wrong" already fully account for the territory
disease would occupy: gradual component degradation (the aging-analogue) and corruption events like
Calethina's Split Brain (the mortality-analogue). No third slot is left over for something disease-shaped.
