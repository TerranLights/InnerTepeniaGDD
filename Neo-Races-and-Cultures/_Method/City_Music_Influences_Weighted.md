# City Music Influences — Weighted by Population Share

Pulled from the "Music" Surface Culture finding in each city's own Neo-Races Catalog. Only the 18
cities with completed Phase 1c work (all 8 Halley subnet, all 8 Palmer subnet, plus Casey and Davis in
Mirny) have this data — the other 17 cities haven't started Phase 1c yet. Within each city, nations are
listed by population share (highest weight first); nations whose entry reads "no strongly distinct
local variant surfaced" are omitted since there's nothing to list.
