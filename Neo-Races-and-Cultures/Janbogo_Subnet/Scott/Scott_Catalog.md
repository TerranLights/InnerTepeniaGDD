## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — China, USA. Significant — Japan, UK, South Korea, Canada,
  Indonesia, Australia (founding wave). Notable — Philippines, Malaysia, New Zealand (founding operator
  heritage), Chile. Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Scott.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Hut Point Peninsula, Ross Island — ~3km from Fort McMurdo on the same volcanic
  promontory; shares Fort McMurdo's geography (McMurdo Sound, Erebus, Dry Valleys) but at much smaller,
  more compact scale.
- **Geological composition:** Same volcanic rock promontory as Fort McMurdo — see that city's own entry
  for full geological detail; the two cities share a microclimate and terrain.

## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** identical volcanic-peninsula profile to Fort McMurdo (see that city's entry for
the general framing); this entry covers Scott's own distinct nation set.

- **China (Primary):** Changbaishan — same match as Fort McMurdo.
- **USA (Primary):** Hawaii's Big Island — same match as Fort McMurdo.
- **Japan (Significant):** Sakurajima, Kagoshima — same match as Fort McMurdo.
- **UK (Significant):** Weakest match, as at Fort McMurdo — no genuine active-volcanism domestic
  parallel; flagged for Phase 2 review.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Jeju Island (Hallasan) — a dormant shield volcano with a substantial
  real population living on its slopes and surrounding coast.
- **Canada (Significant):** No strong domestic match — Canada's volcanic terrain (e.g. Mount
  Garibaldi, BC) is largely unpopulated; flagged weakest, likely needs a diaspora-community approach.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Merapi, Java — among the most densely populated active-volcano slopes
  anywhere on Earth; an outstanding match, arguably the strongest "living under an active volcano"
  parallel available in the whole project.
- **Australia (Significant):** No active-volcanism domestic match — closest is the dormant Newer
  Volcanics Province in Victoria, sparsely populated; flagged as weak.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type:** administrative/garrison partner-city to Fort McMurdo, with a genuine religious/memorial
identity — Hut Point is "Scott's most sacred civic location," where residents lay items of
remembrance during Independence Day observances.

- **China (Primary):** No single directly comparable national-remembrance memorial-pilgrimage site —
  flagged as weak.
- **USA (Primary):** Arlington National Cemetery — a real, defining American site of national
  remembrance and civic pilgrimage, a strong structural match.
- **Japan (Significant):** Yasukuni Shrine — a real, nationally significant Japanese site of civic
  remembrance (acknowledging its own real-world political complexity, noted here strictly for its
  structural role as a national memorial-pilgrimage site).
- **UK (Significant):** The Cenotaph, London — the UK's defining national remembrance site, a strong
  match.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Seoul National Cemetery — a real Korean national remembrance site.
- **Canada (Significant):** The National War Memorial, Ottawa — a real Canadian civic remembrance
  site.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Kalibata Heroes Cemetery, Jakarta — a real Indonesian national
  remembrance site.
- **Australia (Significant):** The Australian War Memorial, Canberra — a strong, direct match.
