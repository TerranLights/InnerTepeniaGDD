## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — China, USA. Significant — Japan, UK, South Korea, Canada,
  Indonesia, Australia (founding wave). Notable — Philippines, Malaysia, New Zealand (earliest founding
  wave), Chile. Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Cape_Adare.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Headland at the northern tip of the Adare Peninsula, where Victoria Land meets the
  Ross Sea — the most accessible point on the Ross Sea coast from the open Southern Ocean, the first
  natural landfall for ships from the north.
- **Geological composition:** Low, rocky, exposed coastal headland — hosts one of the world's largest
  Adélie penguin rookeries; more maritime-exposed than the sheltered Terra Nova Bay cities to the south.
