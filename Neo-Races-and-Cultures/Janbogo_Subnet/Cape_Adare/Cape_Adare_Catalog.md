## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — China, USA. Significant — Japan, UK, South Korea, Canada,
  Indonesia, Australia (founding wave). Notable — Philippines, Malaysia, New Zealand (earliest founding
  wave), Chile. Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Cape_Adare.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Headland at the northern tip of the Adare Peninsula, where Victoria Land meets the
  Ross Sea — the most accessible point on the Ross Sea coast from the open Southern Ocean, the first
  natural landfall for ships from the north.
- **Geological composition:** Low, rocky, exposed coastal headland — hosts one of the world's largest
  Adélie penguin rookeries; more maritime-exposed than the sheltered Terra Nova Bay cities to the south.

## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-17)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Cape Adare's
own conditions (its "big city, small-town feel," its acoustic-folk music culture, and its established
relationship to the Adélie penguin rookery as neighbors rather than wildlife) that a given population's
originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X because they're from
nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Cape Adare's Enneagram profile is
**Feeling (Heart) / Compliant / Positive Outlook** — a warm, community-first identity, vast but
low-density, organized around belonging and shared feeling rather than function or risk. An
acoustic-instrument folk music culture (guitars, violins, cellos, tagelharpas) is a genuine, established
civic strength, and penguins are kept as outdoor pets by some residents — the rookery treated "not as
tourists but as neighbors." `Cape_Adare_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences research (Yalta, Lahti,
Boulders Beach/Simon's Town) is general civic-identity flavor rather than tied to any specific
Primary/Significant nation here — none of the three real-world picks belong to a nation present at
Significant tier or above — but grounds the city's own established traits in genuine precedent: Yalta's
own transformation from exclusive retreat to open, communal place mirrors the "big city, small-town
feel"; Lahti's own disproportionate musical reputation for a city its size grounds the acoustic-folk
identity as something genuinely achievable, not decorative; and Boulders Beach's own documented
penguin-neighborhood integration is the single most precise real-world precedent found in this project
for "not as tourists but as neighbors."
