**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Zukelli's own
conditions (its hospitality-centered civic culture, its organically-tangled city layout, and its final
act of sheltering AWOL Upper Earth defectors) that a given population's originating patterns fed into or
reinforced — never as "this population does X because they're from nation Y." **Status note:** Zukelli

Significant-tier) — corrected 2026-07-13 from an earlier, inaccurate "Italian-rooted" framing. Its
destruction came from sheltering AWOL Upper Earth defectors — an act of real care and risk taken on
behalf of others, at the highest possible cost. At its established peak (72.5% human retention, "near
peak, not in decline"), Zukelli read as genuinely vibrant rather than embattled, a tangled, organic city
full of music and warmth, navigable only to residents who knew and loved it — vitality that makes its

from sheltering AWOL Upper Earth defectors, an act of real care and risk taken on behalf of others at
