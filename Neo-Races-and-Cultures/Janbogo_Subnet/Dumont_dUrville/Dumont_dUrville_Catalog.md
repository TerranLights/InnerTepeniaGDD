## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — China, USA. Significant — Japan, France (founding operator
  heritage), South Korea, Indonesia, Australia (founding wave). Notable — Thailand, Vietnam,
  Philippines, Malaysia, New Zealand. Exact per-nation share % in `Specs/Dumont_dUrville.md` and
  `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Petrel Island, Géologie Archipelago, ~5km off the Adélie Land coast — not built on the
  continent itself, giving it a maritime-plus-Antarctic character. Adélie Land is among the windiest
  regions on Earth.
- **Geological composition:** Small island exposed rock, somewhat sheltered by the archipelago relative
  to the open coast but still subject to extreme, persistent katabatic winds.

## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** small rocky island within a sheltering archipelago, offshore rather than
continental, still subject to persistent severe wind.

- **China (Primary):** Zhoushan Archipelago, Zhejiang — real small rocky islands off the mainland
  coast with a genuine offshore-island population pattern.
- **USA (Primary):** Isles of Shoals (Maine/New Hampshire) — small, rocky, wind-exposed offshore
  islands with a real historical population.
- **Japan (Significant):** Gotō Islands, Nagasaki — small rocky archipelago islands, real populated
  communities.
- **France (Significant):** Ouessant / Île de Sein, Brittany — small, famously wind-battered French
  islands ("who sees Ouessant sees his own blood" is a real local proverb about the danger of its
  waters); an especially strong match given France's own founding-operator heritage at this city.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Baengnyeongdo or Marado — small, remote, rocky Korean offshore
  islands.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** No strong match — Indonesia's small islands are tropical and lack the
  severe-wind archipelago profile; flagged weakest, may need a diaspora-community approach.
- **Australia (Significant):** Kent Group / Hogan Group, Bass Strait — small, remote, genuinely
  storm-exposed Australian islands.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type, resolved 2026-07-16:** a wildlife-tourism/nature-reserve gateway city — the penguin
colonies are the main draw, same civic register as Kaikōura, Simon's Town, and Punta Tombo — organized
around a small, walkable town core with a genuine arts/music/culture identity of its own, in the vein
of Quebec City: a cultured small city that happens to be built around a natural reserve, rather than a
plain research outpost with penguins nearby.

*Nature-reserve-gateway angle (per nation):*
- **China (Primary):** Wòlóng, Sichuan — a real Chinese wildlife-reserve gateway town (giant pandas),
  a strong structural match.
- **USA (Primary):** Homer, Alaska — a real American coastal wildlife-tourism gateway town.
- **Japan (Significant):** Shiretoko, Hokkaido — a real Japanese UNESCO wildlife-reserve gateway town.
- **France (Significant, founding operator heritage):** Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, Camargue — a real
  French wildlife-reserve gateway town (flamingos, wild horses) with a genuine small-town cultural
  identity of its own; an especially fitting match given France's own founding-operator heritage here.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Suncheon — a real Korean wetland-reserve gateway town (migratory
  birds).
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Labuan Bajo — a real Indonesian wildlife-reserve gateway town (Komodo
  National Park), an excellent match.
- **Australia (Significant):** Phillip Island — home to Australia's own real "Penguin Parade" tourist
  attraction; an outstanding, almost literal match.

*Cultured-small-city angle (general framing, not tied to a specific nation in this roster):* Quebec
City itself is the reference point for DdU's own internal register — a small, walkable historic core
with a genuine, disproportionate arts/music/culture identity layered on top of its main tourist draw,
rather than a research outpost that happens to have penguins.
