## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** sheltered bay coastline subject to strong, persistent katabatic (downslope)
winds funneling off nearby mountains — calmer than the fully open coast (contrast Denison) but
defined by the same wind mechanism.

- **China (Primary):** Dalian's bay coastline, Liaoning — a real sheltered-bay port city with
  significant seasonal winds off the surrounding hills.
- **USA (Primary):** Juneau, Alaska / Prince William Sound — genuinely subject to the real "Taku
  winds," true katabatic winds funneling down from nearby ice fields into a sheltered bay; an
  excellent mechanistic match.
- **Japan (Significant):** Toyama Bay — a real sheltered bay subject to strong seasonal "oroshi"
  downslope winds off the nearby mountains.
- **Germany (Significant):** No strong domestic match — Germany has no comparable
  mountain-into-sheltered-bay wind system; flagged as weak.
- **Italy (Significant):** Gulf of Trieste — home to the real Bora wind, a textbook katabatic wind
  funneling down from the Dinaric Alps into a sheltered bay; one of the strongest mechanistic matches
  in the whole project.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Sokcho and the east coast near the Taebaek Mountains — real seasonal
  mountain-downslope winds into a coastal town.
- **Canada (Significant):** Kitimat, British Columbia — subject to real, documented katabatic
  "Squamish winds" funneling down coastal mountains into a sheltered inlet.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** No meaningful match — tropical Indonesia lacks any katabatic bay
  system; flagged weakest, likely needs a diaspora-community approach.
- **Australia (Significant):** No strong domestic match — Australia lacks a comparable
  mountain-fed katabatic bay system; flagged as weak.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type:** major port/shipping and supply hub, PLUS a nationally-famous leisure landmark:
Majyao's Teahouse, "the establishment famous throughout Tepenia."

- **China (Primary):** Guangzhou's famous historic teahouse culture (e.g. the long-running teahouses
  of the Liwan district) — a genuine, nationally-known Chinese teahouse-culture match, especially
  fitting given the "Majyao's Teahouse" landmark itself.
- **USA (Primary):** New Orleans — a real major US port city with its own nationally-famous
  food/hospitality landmark culture (French Quarter), a strong dual match.
- **Japan (Significant):** Yokohama — a genuine major Japanese port city.
- **Germany (Significant):** Hamburg — same major German port match used at Sanay/Belgrano.
- **Italy (Significant):** Genoa — a real major Italian port city.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Busan — South Korea's largest port city, a strong match.
- **Canada (Significant):** Vancouver — a genuine major Canadian port city.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Surabaya — a real major Indonesian port city.
- **Australia (Significant):** Melbourne — a genuine major Australian port city.
