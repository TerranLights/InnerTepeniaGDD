## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-17)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Janbogo's own
conditions (its shielded-commercial-hall indoor urbanism, its Majyao's Teahouse landmark, and its heavy
emotional weight as the city deliberately spared to witness Zukelli's destruction) that a given
population's originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X because
they're from nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Janbogo's Enneagram profile is
**Feeling (Heart) / Compliant / Reactive** — a genuinely fused fashion sensibility developed
independently as the city's own indoor-warmth style choice (happening to echo Chinese and French design
sensibilities as convergent development, not a direct import from either), expressed through great
shielded commercial halls that function as real social/commercial gathering spaces. Janbogo carries a
genuinely heavy emotional weight: it was deliberately left standing after Zukelli's destruction
specifically to witness the strike and carry its deterrent message forward — not a burden processed with
calm detachment, but something felt and reacted to. `Janbogo_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences
research grounds Majyao's Teahouse directly in the Central Asian Chaykhana tradition (teahouse as real
civic infrastructure, a genuine hub for storytelling, business, and news exchange) and the Ottoman/
Turkish Kahvehane tradition (a specific cultural institution transcending its origin to become universal
and ethnicity-agnostic — directly relevant given the teahouse tradition has already grown to belong to
the whole city regardless of ancestry). Dubai Mall culture grounds the "great shielded commercial halls"
in a genuine real-world precedent (climate extremity, regardless of direction — extreme heat or extreme
cold — producing indoor-hub urbanism at civic scale), and Murmansk grounds Terra Nova Bay's own polynya
in a precise real-world match (a current-maintained ice-free water access point granting a maritime
advantage). New Orleans jazz culture parallels how Janbogo's own warmth and hospitality became
genuinely city-wide rather than staying confined to its Korean founding population.
