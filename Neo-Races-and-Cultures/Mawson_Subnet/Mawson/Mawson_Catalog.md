## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-17)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** every finding below is framed as arising from Mawson's own
conditions (its resort-town hospitality character, its status as Tepenia's honeymoon destination for
newly-married human-robot couples, and its "Name That Outlasted the Founders" continuity theme) that a
given population's originating patterns fed into or reinforced — never as "this population does X
because they're from nation Y."

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Mawson's Enneagram profile is
**Feeling (Heart) / Compliant / Positive Outlook** — "relatively speaking, Antarctica's closest thing to
a resort town," a place defined by hospitality and warmth rather than function or security, with a
civic function oriented entirely toward serving visitors. `Mawson_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational
Influences research grounds this directly: Jeju City (South Korea) is the single most precise match of
any pick found in this research pass — the real, actual honeymoon capital of South Korea, a reputation
deliberately built and decades-earned starting in the 1970s-80s, exactly matching Mawson's own
established "genuinely a planned destination... earned over decades" identity, plus a genuine structural
echo (a warm, celebratory surface sitting atop serious, only-partially-acknowledged history) worth
developing further rather than copying directly. Odessa, Varna, and Trogir are general civic-identity
flavor not tied to a specific Primary/Significant nation here (Ukraine, Bulgaria, and Croatia all sit at
Notable tier only), but ground the city's own combination of genuine demographic plurality and a real,
documented hospitality reputation (Odessa), its multi-millennial unbroken cultural centrality still
functioning as a genuinely lived destination today (Varna), and its own defining theme, "The Name That
Outlasted the Founders" — Trogir's own 2,300 years of unbroken urban continuity beneath completely
different rulers and populations, an almost exact structural mirror.

**Worth flagging as a genuinely rich thematic thread, not resolved here:** Jeju's own real layering (a
warm, celebratory surface reputation sitting atop serious, only-partially-acknowledged history) offers a
genuine structural echo of Mawson's own established "Name That Outlasted the Founders" theme and its
quiet tension between founding memory and demographic present — worth developing further in future
Course of Events work rather than treated as a coincidental parallel. Trogir's own 2,300 years of
unbroken urban continuity beneath completely different rulers gives that theme a precise, documented
real-world shape: Mawson's continuity was never about which nation happened to be living there at any
given time, the oldest continuously-occupied site in its subnet (1954 founding) carrying forward
regardless of demographic shift.
