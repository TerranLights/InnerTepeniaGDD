## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** rugged coastal terrain with real mountains inland (the Prince Charles Mountains
visible to the south) — matches should favor places where mountains meet the sea directly, not flat
coastline.

- **China (Primary):** Rugged Fujian coast near Pingtan/Xiamen — real rocky, cliff-heavy Chinese
  coastline.
- **Japan (Significant):** Sanriku Coast, Iwate — a genuinely rugged Japanese coastline where steep
  terrain meets the sea directly; a strong match.
- **Germany (Significant):** Weakest match — Germany's own coasts (North Sea, Baltic) are largely
  flat; the closest available is Rügen's chalk cliffs, a modest match at best. Flagged for Phase 2
  review.
- **France (Significant):** Côte de Granit Rose, Brittany — a real rugged granite coastline with a
  genuine coastal population.
- **UK (Significant):** Wester Ross, Scottish west coast — real rugged mountain-meets-fjord terrain.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Taebaek coastal range / Ulleungdo — genuine rugged mountain-to-sea
  Korean coastline.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** Sumatra's western coast — rugged volcanic coastal terrain, though a
  much warmer climate register; flagged for Phase 2 review.
- **Australia (Significant):** Tasmania's west coast — genuinely rugged, storm-exposed Australian
  coastline with mountains close to the sea.
