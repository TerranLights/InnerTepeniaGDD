## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** extreme high-altitude ice-dome polar desert, ~3,810m nominal elevation with an
effective physiological altitude near 4,500m — one of the hardest terrain types in the whole project
to find genuine real-world matches for, since almost nowhere combines this altitude with permanent
population outside a narrow band of the world.

- **USA (Primary):** No strong domestic match — Leadville, Colorado (the highest US incorporated
  city, ~3,094m) falls well short of the target altitude; flagged as weak, likely needs a
  diaspora-community approach in Phase 2.

  4,500m+ in genuine cold desert conditions; the single strongest real-world match found anywhere in
  this project for this terrain type.
- **Japan (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak, likely needs a diaspora-community
  approach.
- **Germany (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **UK (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **France (Significant):** No true domestic match; the highest staffed French Alpine sites (e.g. the
  Aiguille du Midi station) are not permanent civilian populations. Flagged as weak.
- **South Korea (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **Russia (Significant):** No true domestic match at this altitude — the closest Russian highland
  settlements (Caucasus valleys, already used at Lazar) fall well short of 4,500m; flagged as weak.

may be worth keeping in mind for Phase 2 synthesis even though no Dome Fuji nation traces to them
directly.

## Per-Nation Entries (Cultural Iceberg findings — Phase 1c, complete 2026-07-17)

**No National Stereotypes compliance note:** Dome Fuji has no human residents (0 humans / 55,072
robots) — every finding below is framed as arising from Dome Fuji's own conditions (its foundational
identity as an Ice-Cold Buddhist pilgrimage/devotional site, its extreme altitude, and its minimal
secular economy) as inherited through robot-population heritage-tracking rather than lived human
experience, the same framing established at Kunlun and Vostok. A given nation's inclusion reflects
genuine real-world Buddhist-pilgrimage or research heritage (per the population's own tiered
composition), not a literal resident population of that nationality.

**Pre-existing research folded in (per the process requirement):** Dome Fuji's Enneagram profile is
**Instinctive (Gut) / Withdrawn / Positive Outlook** — a Zen-like peace where "the air and sky itself"
seem to participate in meditation alongside the devotees, a merging quality that dissolves the boundary
between self and environment. Sparse white robes exist specifically "to commune with the cold,"
reframing what would be a survival problem anywhere else in Tepenia into an act of devotion and
meaning. `Dome_Fuji_Mega_Init.md`'s Inspirational Influences research grounds this directly in three
real-world Buddhist pilgrimage sites: Paro Taktsang, Bhutan (a genuinely difficult climb where the
difficulty itself is the meaning — "the crossing is the filter" — supporting Dome Fuji's own speculated
"Deepest Cold" observance timed to polar night's depth as the natural high season); Potala Palace,
Tibet (the world's highest palace at 3,700m, built as the Dalai Lamas' winter residence — directly
reinforcing Dome Fuji's own "winter palace, most fully itself when the cold is deepest" framing, and a
genuine structural parallel to Dome Fuji's own research-station-to-monastery identity shift); and
Angkor Wat, Cambodia (cosmology of crossing a symbolic ocean to ascend a sacred mountain at the center —
Dome Fuji's own flat, featureless plateau, crossed via a genuinely dangerous interior journey, is
literally one of the highest points in Tepenia by elevation despite carrying none of a mountain's visual
drama, an inversion worth reading as a more advanced, more ascetic theological statement). None of these
three sites ties to a specific Primary/Significant nation here except Potala Palace's own Tibetan
connection to China.

## Synthesis Notes (Phase 2 — draft, not yet developer-confirmed as final)

**Working name: "Fujian devotees"** *(placeholder; not yet developer-confirmed — note the real-world
homophone with China's actual Fujian province, worth flagging for the developer to consider a different
term).*

Dome Fuji is structurally similar to Kunlun and Vostok — zero human residents, every finding tracking
robot heritage-inheritance rather than lived human culture — but with a genuinely different center of
gravity: where Kunlun and Vostok converge on a shared *scientific* ethos, Dome Fuji converges overwhelmingly
on a shared *devotional* one. Every population here shares the same sparse-white-robes dress, the same
"Deepest Cold" observance timed to polar night, and the same core theological stance (hardship reframed
as the point, not endured) — Surface and Deep Culture findings read almost identically across all eleven
populations, with national distinctiveness concentrated almost entirely in which specific real-world
Buddhist pilgrimage site or research institution each population's presence echoes. China stands apart
as the single most richly anchored population in the entire project: the strongest geography match found
anywhere (Tibetan Plateau) combined with a direct Buddhist pilgrimage match and Potala Palace's own
uniquely precise altitude-plus-religion-plus-identity-shift precedent. Japan and South Korea each hold
genuine, direct Buddhist-pilgrimage City-Type anchors (Mount Kōya, Haeinsa Temple) reflecting Buddhism's
real-world geographic center of gravity in East Asia. USA, France, Germany, UK, and Russia all carry the
secondary, explicitly distant-second research-town dimension rather than the primary religious one.

**Worth preserving as a genuinely rich thematic core, not resolved here:** the Angkor Wat cosmological
inversion (Dome Fuji as a summit with all the elevation and none of the visual drama of a mountain) is
one of the single richest pieces of real-world-grounded flavor found anywhere in this project — worth
building future Course of Events or companion material directly around this image rather than letting it
sit as a one-off research note. Similarly, Potala Palace's own real identity-shift (residence to museum)
validates Dome Fuji's own research-station-to-monastery history as a genuine, documented pattern rather
than an invented convenience.
