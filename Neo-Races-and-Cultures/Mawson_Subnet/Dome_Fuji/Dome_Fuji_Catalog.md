## City Snapshot

- **Nations present, by tier:** Primary — USA, China. Significant — Japan, Germany, UK, France, South
  Korea, Russia, Brazil, Indonesia, Australia. Notable — 24 additional nations (see `Specs/Dome_Fuji.md`
  for the full list; Dome Fuji has the widest Notable-tier spread of any Tepenian city). Exact
  per-nation share % in `Specs/Dome_Fuji.md` and `Official_Population_Census.md`.
- **Geography:** Near Valkyrie Dome, East Antarctic Ice Sheet, ~3,810m elevation — one of the two or
  three highest-elevation settlements in Tepenia; effective physiological altitude closer to 4,500m due
  to polar atmospheric compression.
- **Geological composition:** East Antarctic Plateau ice-dome — extreme high-altitude polar desert,
  nearly identical terrain type to Concordia and Vostok.

## Real-World Parallel Locations (Phase 1b — location identification only, no cultural findings yet)

**Terrain profile:** extreme high-altitude ice-dome polar desert, ~3,810m nominal elevation with an
effective physiological altitude near 4,500m — one of the hardest terrain types in the whole project
to find genuine real-world matches for, since almost nowhere combines this altitude with permanent
population outside a narrow band of the world.

- **USA (Primary):** No strong domestic match — Leadville, Colorado (the highest US incorporated
  city, ~3,094m) falls well short of the target altitude; flagged as weak, likely needs a
  diaspora-community approach in Phase 2.
- **China (Primary):** Tibetan Plateau — Nagqu or Amdo county, real permanent populations living at
  4,500m+ in genuine cold desert conditions; the single strongest real-world match found anywhere in
  this project for this terrain type.
- **Japan (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak, likely needs a diaspora-community
  approach.
- **Germany (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **UK (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **France (Significant):** No true domestic match; the highest staffed French Alpine sites (e.g. the
  Aiguille du Midi station) are not permanent civilian populations. Flagged as weak.
- **South Korea (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **Russia (Significant):** No true domestic match at this altitude — the closest Russian highland
  settlements (Caucasus valleys, already used at Lazar) fall well short of 4,500m; flagged as weak.
- **Brazil (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weakest, likely needs a diaspora-community
  approach.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** No domestic match — the Puncak Jaya highlands are extreme in
  elevation but lack a comparable permanent cold-desert population at this altitude; flagged as weak.
- **Australia (Significant):** No domestic match — flagged as weakest.

**Design note carried forward:** beyond the primary Tibetan Plateau match, general reference points for
"world's highest permanent human settlements" (La Rinconada, Peru, ~5,100m; El Alto, Bolivia, ~4,150m)
may be worth keeping in mind for Phase 2 synthesis even though no Dome Fuji nation traces to them
directly.

## City-Type Parallels (Phase 1b-ii — functional/civic identity, not geography)

**City type:** fundamentally, foundationally a religious site/city — Dome Fuji was explicitly
established by and for the Ice-Cold Buddhists, and that is its core identity and reason for existing
*(developer note reinforced 2026-07-16 — this is not a co-equal dual type; the religious/pilgrimage
identity is primary, and the ice-core scientific research economy, while genuinely present, is a
distant second)*. The pilgrimage/religious-site matches below are therefore the primary real-world
parallel for this city; the research-town matches are secondary/supporting only.

*Pilgrimage/religious-site angle (primary):*
- **China (Primary):** Mount Wutai or Mount Emei — genuine major Chinese Buddhist pilgrimage sites, a
  strong direct match given Dome Fuji's own Buddhism-derived religious identity.
- **Japan (Significant):** Mount Kōya — a real major Japanese Buddhist pilgrimage site, an excellent
  match.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Haeinsa Temple — a real major Korean Buddhist pilgrimage site.
- **Others:** the remaining nations in this city's roster have no comparably strong domestic Buddhist
  pilgrimage-site match; flagged as weak rather than forced, since Buddhism's real-world geographic
  center of gravity is genuinely concentrated in East/Southeast Asia.

*Research-town angle (secondary, distant second to the religious identity above):*
- **USA (Primary):** Los Alamos, New Mexico — same dedicated research-town match used at Neumayer.
- **China (Primary):** Golmud or a Tibetan Plateau research outpost town — a real Chinese
  high-altitude scientific outpost, a strong thematic match.
- **Japan (Significant):** Tsukuba Science City — same match used at Princess Elisabeth/Denison.
- **Germany (Significant):** Garching bei München — same match used at Neumayer.
- **UK (Significant):** Harwell, Oxfordshire — same match used at Neumayer/Princess Elisabeth.
- **France (Significant):** Saclay — same match used at Neumayer/Princess Elisabeth.
- **South Korea (Significant):** Daedeok Innopolis — a real dedicated Korean science-research city.
- **Russia (Significant):** Akademgorodok — a real dedicated Soviet/Russian science-research town, an
  excellent match.
- **Brazil (Significant):** No strong domestic dedicated-research-town match — flagged as weak.
- **Indonesia (Significant):** No strong domestic match — flagged as weak.
- **Australia (Significant):** No single dedicated research-town match — flagged as weak.
