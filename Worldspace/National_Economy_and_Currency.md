## ⭐⭐ A STANDING CONSTRAINT ON WHATEVER THE ANSWER TURNS OUT TO BE

> # ***Whatever currency came into national common use is the currency Tepenians carried with them into orbital infrastructure, following Amundsen Tower's completion.***

⇒ ⭐ **The national system was never a planet-side-only system.** **Whatever it was had to survive being taken
off the continent and used in orbit** — by a population living on infrastructure the Tower made reachable.

⚠ **This does not tell us what the currency was. It tells us something true about the unknown thing, and it
narrows the answer:** ⛔ **an answer that only works on the continent is wrong.**

⭐ **And it puts the whole question inside one structure's lifetime.** **The Tower makes orbital access
possible; the national currency is in common use by the late-Second Interwar and goes up with the people who
go up; the Tower's destruction ends space access, fractures the grid and severs the Arcanet in the same
event — and the currency re-fractures with them.** ⛔ **Carried as shape, not as mechanism.**

⏸️ **Same gate. This is a note for later development, after every city and the subnet meta-cultures are
done.**

## ⭐ THE RULING — `DR-3`, developer, verbatim

> *"In the very early stages of the Tepenian Federation (let's say, the very first one to three generations,
> before people began finding common ground and unifying), **each individual subnet had roughly what was its
> own local 'currency'**, which would've been based on how that particular socio-geopolitical entity understood
> the concept of its own economy, inter-city trade, and what had value. Then later, as the country unified
> socially and culturally (in addition to politically, as per the Falkland Treaty), **they had to start
> harmonizing their money system**."*

⭐ **The differences between subnet currencies are CHARACTEROLOGICAL, not nominal.** **Each one is downstream of
how that subnet understood its own economy, its inter-city trade, and what had value** — so they cannot be
derived until those things are known.

## ⛔⛔ THE GATE — this work cannot start yet, and the reason is structural

> **Developer:** *"This absolutely cannot be accomplished until **all 38 cities have fully and completely
> undergone the ULM, CST, and RWBEM**. So this is definitely a task for the future."*

**The dependency runs in one direction and cannot be inverted:**

```
every city completes ULM / CST / RWBEM
        ↓
what each city values and trades is known
        ↓
municipal neo-cultures are derived
        ↓
each subnet harmonizes into its own GEOGRAPHICAL NEO-CULTURE
        ↓
what economic system and currency THAT culture would arrive at
```

⛔ **Writing the money first inverts the derivation and forces every city in the corpus to conform to a system
invented before they existed.** *(Subnet regional identity is one of the things canon already records as
downstream of the completed city corpus — see the city-derived dependency set.)*

## ⏸️ WHAT THE FUTURE WORK WILL HAVE TO ANSWER

**Questions, held open. None of these has a current answer:**

- **What each subnet's early currency was based on** — per subnet, derived from that subnet's own geographical
  neo-culture.
- **How harmonization actually worked** — the mechanism, the sequence, what drove it, how long it took, and
  what it produced.
- **What the national system rested on**, once there was one.
- **How the dual economy was settled** — humans and robots spend on completely different things; whatever the
  system was, it had to bridge that.
- **Whether cities within a subnet share one currency or hold their own.**
- **Exchange mechanics at subnet boundaries**, in every era that had boundaries.
- **How Upper Earth trade interacts with any of it** — plausibly a separate settlement layer entirely.
- **Cost-of-living variation by city type**, once individual city economies exist to compare.
- ⭐⭐ **How the national currency actually functioned IN ORBIT**, given it went up with the people who went up —
  and whether orbital infrastructure settled in the same system, a derived one, or something of its own.
- ⭐ **What happened to orbital holdings of that currency when the Tower fell** and space access ended, given
  the same event fractured the planet-side system.
