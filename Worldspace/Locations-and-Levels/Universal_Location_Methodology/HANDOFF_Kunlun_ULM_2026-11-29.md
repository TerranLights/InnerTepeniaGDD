### 2.3 The web budget (measured since the startup file was written)

- **The budget refills.** Measured on Casey's research (2026-11-20/21): about **1,000 at a session's start plus ~97 an hour**; the first
  refusal came at 1,528 searches 5 h 26 min in. A long session gives about 2,000. The startup file's **"above 850, stop"** rule is
  replaced by the Casey pacing rule: **capacity = 1,000 + ~97 × hours since the session started; dispatch a unit-round only when its
  worst case (270 searches for a pick unit) plus a ~100 reserve fits, with no credit for refill during its run.** When even that does not
  fit, **stop at the unit boundary, record exactly where the run stands, and continue in a fresh session** (or wait for the refill if the
  window has hours left). **A run that spans several sessions is normal and approved (`DR-146`).** Never thin, skip, merge or shorten a
  unit, a round or a list to fit a session.
- **The counter:** `python3 Tools/web_quota_count.py --tasks <this session's tasks folder>`. On Casey's research the tool and the agents'
  own counts agreed within about 1%; trust the tool's figure when they differ. Its "quota/rate-limit" flags were false positives every
  time (fishing quotas, the word "quotation", HTTP 429 from websites, the project's own "There is no search budget" line): inspect before
  believing one.
- ⚠ **A second kind of stop: the account's 5-hour usage limit** (first met 2026-11-21, while another session was also running). It reaches
  agents as a WebSearch refusal: *"You've hit your 5-hour limit · resets …"*. **Handle it like a budget refusal:** the agent writes a STOP
  note; stop dispatching; after the reset, send a fresh agent the same prompt plus a resume line naming the STOP note (on 2026-11-21 the
  resume took 12 minutes). The error's time zone (America/Vancouver) differs from the machine's clock (MST). The counter does not flag
  this text, so read every STOP note.
- **Sites that refuse every automated fetch:** CBC (all pages) and gov.nu.ca; facts sourced only there stay UNVERIFIED.
