# City Input Audit — the 30 November cities (2026-10-21)

**What this is.** A mechanical check of every city in the November run against the ULM's own list of required inputs
(`ULM_Input_Required_Reference.md`), plus a check that each city's Pre-Trip sheet, ledger and 19 datasheets exist and are sound.
Run by `Tools/audit_city_inputs.py` (new, read-only). 40 tests per city. **It tests presence, not correctness or quality**, and it
never compares one city with another.

## Result in one paragraph
**No city is missing a blocking input.** Every city has its spec, census entry, subnet, founding row in the Register, access type,
tiered inspiration picks, a Division-of-Industry row and a climate table. Every Pre-Trip sheet resolves all its addresses with no
missing inputs and no failed line claims (Mirny's older hand-built sheet uses a different format and carries no count). Every
datasheet set has 19 files, none a stub. What follows are the thin spots, in the order they matter.

## What this does NOT tell you
- **Whether any value is right.** The datasheets still need their three-reader checks; the Pre-Trip sheets were generated by script with its own address and line-number checks, not read by three readers.
- **"Named in" tests count mentions, not membership**, except where a per-city row is required (census, Register, Division-of-Industry, Inspirational-Influences), which are tested as rows.
- **Findings from earlier that this tool cannot see:** three spec composition tables disagree with the census (Byrd, Palmer City, Contrapunto); Palmer City and Rothera have concept-art filenames with later-era words in them.
- **A correction to something I said earlier:** I listed "Byrd and Dome Fuji have no Census II row" as a finding. The census documents it and Census I governs, so it is not a gap.
