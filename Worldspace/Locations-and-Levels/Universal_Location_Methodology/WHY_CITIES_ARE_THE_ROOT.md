## 3 · THE LIST, AS IT NOW STANDS — the two statements above, merged for practical use

**Everything below derives its attributes, qualities, characteristics and traits from what the 38 cities end
up being, once the full ULM/CST/RWBEM run is genuinely complete.** *(Explicitly non-exhaustive — the
developer's own "etc etc etc etc etc." Treat this as the representative shape of the dependency, not a closed
list.)*

- **Weapon/armor specs** — culturally, geographically, situationally and logistically consistent with each
  city. *A city on exposed rock doesn't build "ice-blasting explosives"; a coastal city's ergonomics aren't
  built around reindeer meat.*
- **Any districts that may exist within cities**, once they reach that scale
- **What settings are present in those cities after the Long Night War**
- **What neo-cultures and neo-races these communities become**
- **How those municipal neo-cultures reshape, adjust and further evolve in orbital civilization**, and then in
  **colonized (and, further-later, terraformed) Mars civilization**, on the timeline of *The Cryptograph
  Helix* novel series
- **How those later-future neo-cultures and neo-races further reshape, adjust and evolve in orbital
  civilization around Jupiter** in *Outer Tepenia 1*, and then in *Outer Tepenia 2* — the first two
  installments of the Outer Tepenia game trilogy
- **What cultural influences these communities would have on their newly-adopted districts of residence in
  Concordia** (whichever districts they end up living in, post-migration)
- **What factions would appear in these municipal neo-cultures**, and any conflicting sub-ideologies that
  might arise within the same faction
- **Possible new robot religions** that may appear from a given community
- **What music scenes these communities would have**
- ⭐ **ADDED 2026-09-14 — SUBNET-LEVEL REGIONAL IDENTITY.** ***Each subnet (Halley, Janbogo, Mawson, Mirny,
  Palmer, Byrd, and any others) should initially develop as its own distinct region — not a uniform blend.***
  **The reasoning:** everyone in Tepenia fundamentally agrees on robot personhood, and everyone is there for
  one of two shared reasons — *robots forced there by the Falkland Treaty, or humans who love and support
  robots.* **But underneath that shared premise, the founding populations still come from genuinely different
  origin cultures.** The aggregate cultural composition of a subnet's own cities will therefore differ from
  every other subnet's, and **each subnet should end up looking, feeling and *vibing* differently** as a
  result — a real regional character, not an administrative grouping with no texture of its own. **Like
  everything above it, this is only discoverable once the full ULM/CST/RWBEM run exists for all 38 cities** —
  a subnet's regional character is an emergent property of its cities' actual compositions, not something that
  can be authored in advance of them.

## 4 · WHY THIS GOVERNS PACING, NOT ONLY SCOPE

> *"This is why I keep telling you, 'I don't want you to do it quickly; what I want is for you to do it
> RIGHT.' And just the same, that's why 'one single, individual step for one city in 8 hours is completely
> unsustainable.' At 11 steps per city (and 9 phases for just Step 4), at 1 step per day, this will never get
> finished."*

**Both halves of that sentence are true at once, and neither excuses the other:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **Depth is non-negotiable** | A shallow city produces shallow weapons, shallow districts, shallow factions, shallow music scenes, shallow subnet identity — **multiplied across every later system that reads from it.** The cost of rushing a city is paid everywhere downstream, not just in that city's own files |
| ⛔ **Pace overhead that adds no depth is still waste** | Hours spent hand-reconstructing a process that already had a working template (see `PRE-TRIP_INSPECTION_RECIPE_TRIAL.md`) buys nothing — it is not the "depth" `LAW 0` asks for, it is friction `LAW 0` never asked for. **See `feedback_read_existing_instructions_before_improvising` for the concrete 2026-09-14 instance and its fix.** |

**How to apply, for any future session touching this project:**
1. **Any candidate task from the list in §3 is gated on substantial 38-city completion**, unless the
   developer explicitly asks for it despite the dependency — that is their call, never an assumption to make
   on their behalf.
2. **`LAW 0` stays absolute.** Nothing in this file is license to move faster through a city pass itself.
3. **Process overhead is a separate problem from depth, and is fair game to fix aggressively** — a range-aware
   dispatch template, a corpus-wide disposition table for recurring questions, a correctly-scoped Round 3, are
   all pace fixes that cost the *pass itself* nothing in depth. Keep hunting for more of these.
