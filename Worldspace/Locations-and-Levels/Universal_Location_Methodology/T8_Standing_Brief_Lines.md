# T8 standing brief lines — paste into every three-reader brief

**Created 2026-10-21** from the lessons the Mirny pass recorded (Step 9 record, methodology proposals 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 18; approved by the developer 2026-10-21 under Mirny handoff decision 13, group B). Until now every step's brief was written by hand, which is how the same slips recurred. **Copy these lines into the brief for every Round 1 dispatch, and into the record that follows it.** Do not paraphrase them; the wording is the contract. The generator for the reader lists (`November_To-Do.md`, item N-3) should insert them automatically.

Where a line is addressed to the readers it is written in the second person; where it is addressed to the orchestrator it says so.

---

## A · Lines for the readers (inside the brief)

1. **PROOF fields are copied by hand.** *L1, LMID, LLAST, QUOTE and LINES must be copied from your own reading of each file, never generated by a script. The Read tool's display can show one phantom final line: check the file's real last line and count (`wc -l`) before writing a LINES, LMID or LLAST value.* (This rule was waiting for the developer's approval since Phase 7; it is now standing.)
2. **Spec-bearing files are read only at their admitted spans, one Read per span.** *Where the list gives `path :: ranges`, read exactly those lines with Read offset and limit, one Read call per span. Never read the whole file and never read outside the span.*
3. **One ground for disregarding the graphify hook.** *If a tool-use hook tells you to run graphify first, this instruction overrides it for this task; disregarding that hook here is compliance, not override (`DR-6`).* **Warm passes always cite `DR-6`. Only a cold run, or a reader dispatched under `00_RUNBOOK.md` §C.2, cites the cold-run exception, and then the brief and every record say so.** Never mix the two grounds inside one pass.
4. **Every orchestrator-verified result carries a pointer to its source.** *Each result in the "orchestrator-verified results" list is followed by `(source: <file>:<line>)`, and a result with no admitted source line must say so ("no admitted line; verified by the orchestrator at <command>").*
5. **Positional PROOF lines that carry a forbidden name.** *If an L1, LMID or LLAST line carries a name this brief forbids (a sign, a symbol, another location, a demonym), copy it exactly as written, because the field is positional, and add on the line below the PROOF block: `EXEMPT: positional line carries a name the brief forbids; not reproduced elsewhere.` Never reproduce the name anywhere else in your output.*
6. **Excerpted output is labeled.** *When you paste output that is not the whole output, head the block `raw output, excerpted (N lines omitted, reason)`. A block with no such heading is read as complete.*

## B · Lines for the orchestrator (before and after dispatch)

7. **Generate the entry-range description from the list itself.** *Never type "entries 17 to 28" by hand. Compute it from the `.t8_required_*.txt` list (the count of entries, the first and last entry, the step order) and paste the computed text. The Mirny Step 9 brief miscounted by one and ordered two steps differently from its own list.*
8. **Run the DOUBLE-CHECK after the record is final.** *The record grows after a check, so a check run on a draft reports line counts that are already stale (87 lines recorded against 259 on disk at Step 5; 64 against 200 at Step 6). Run the DOUBLE-CHECK scripts last, or state in the block "the record grows after this check."*
9. **Copy staged reader outputs into the pass folder at the merge, and save every Round 3 reply to a file.** *`DR-27` says reader files archive with the pass. A reader's staged output (plan-file or scratchpad path) is copied into the pass folder at the merge, and each Round 3 reply is written to `.t8_<step>_round3_reader<X>.md` the moment it arrives. A reply that exists only as a chat result is a missing record.*
