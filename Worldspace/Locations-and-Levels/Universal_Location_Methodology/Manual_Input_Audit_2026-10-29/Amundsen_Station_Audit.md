Method: `README.md` in this folder. Read by hand: the spec in full (230 lines), the whole Pre-Trip (567 lines; the standard text compared line for line with Rothera's — same builder form — and only the differing lines re-read), all 19 datasheets (`Step_5`–`Step_10` are identical to Rothera's after the city name is swapped, apart from the culture-file path), both pass READMEs and the coverage ledger; every cited line opened against the live file. **Nothing was edited**; the edits are in `FIX_LEDGER.md` (entries `AS-…`). Pass folder: `City_Development_Passes/Amundsen_Station/Amundsen_Station/` (one level deeper than the outer README says). `LOC` = `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations`, `CITIES` = `LOC/Cities`.

**Context.** Amundsen Station is "a facility, not a city" (no subnet layer in the folders) and **its pass is on hold by the developer's choice**: it is excluded from the November run so it can be **expanded first**, and on 2026-10-29 the developer recorded a *current thinking, not a ruling* that it may become a "town" with infrastructure above and below the surface (`Research_Logs/Amundsen_Station_Developer_Framing_2026-10-29.md`; additive to everything on file). A 740-line **canon inventory of the same date** (`Research_Logs/Amundsen_Station_Canon_Inventory_2026-10-29.md`, §3, contradictions `C-1` … `C-38`) already lists many of the contradictions below; this audit cites it (`Inv C-n`) and adds what it did not test: the Pre-Trip and sheets line by line, the arithmetic, and the shared files that name the station only in other spellings.

**Verdict.** **Nothing blocks a run.** The spec's census rows, tier table, per-nation shares, mean-temperature column, precipitation column and record extremes are clean; the city's own `09`, `16` Half B (which here **equals** its own determination §35), census and Register rows are current as printed. What is left: **(1)** the **2026-10-28 census rows attributed to the station** — a maritime fleet of 22,982 (Census I) and a space workforce of 7,923 plus a maritime fleet of 7,922 (Census II), "Amundsen Station's share of the Santa Luce redistribution" — are in **no list, no sheet and not in the spec**; **(2)** the name search misses the station's own lines in **other spellings**: `16` §35 (upper case, L3445 / L3451), **`Stations` L29 (en dash: the Pole's own skiway) — the Pre-Trip says "zero hits"**, `Precipitation` L216 (en dash), COMNAP pages 8 and 81 (no row at all in `Step_1_and_2`); **(3)** `Step_3` cites the picks 31 lines early (XS-15) **and carries none of the seven picks** (only the heading and two alias-note lines); **(4)** `Step_-1` copies a **post-war** Significance line (Planetary Split Brain, Kendra Heinrich) and withholds the highway line as "compares"; **(5)** the spec and RelDB still call the station a **three-highway node** while `Highways.md` and `16` §35 say **one** (Inv C-2); **(6)** stale line lists after later edits; **(7)** "datasheets not yet built" in three places; **(8)** six research files made 2026-10-29 are in neither the Pre-Trip nor any sheet, and `Step_3` says no research log exists.
