# Tier U shared sheets — manual input audit (2026-10-30)

Method: `README.md` in this folder. The 16 sheets in `Universal_Location_Methodology/Corpus_Reference_Sheets/` are **shared by every city** (Tier U: "identical for every city"), so an error in one is an error in 38 places. Each sheet was read by hand; its stated sources were opened; cited line numbers, source line counts and the sheet's own dates were compared with the live files; and the sheet's content was compared with the source text — **word for word where the sheet says "verbatim"**, and **claim by claim (not word for word)** where it condenses. **Nothing was edited**; the edits are in `FIX_LEDGER.md` (shared entries `SH-015` … `SH-022`). The sheets were extracted 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-21; most of the drift below was caused by runbook edits of 2026-10-21 and by the alias-table and Register edits of 2026-10-23 and later. `RUNBOOK` = `Universal_Location_Methodology/00_RUNBOOK.md` (now **3,179** lines).

**Verdict.** **Nothing blocks.** Of the 16 sheets, **seven are right as written** (Doll Representation Categories, Zodiac Signs, Robot Religions Roster, Social Contract Term, Phase 7 Roster and Model Files, Robot Physiology, Second Interwar Period), **six carry stale line ranges or counts, or a single misreading**, and **three carry stale or wrong content** — the two process sheets for **Step 9** and **Step 10** (the runbook text they "extract in full" was rewritten on 2026-10-21) and the **Founders and Renames** sheet (its statement about the renamed folders was overtaken on 2026-10-23). Step 10 item 7 is the one that can mislead a reader into a wrong verdict: the sheet says a pre-seeded output folder is "a contamination risk and a status lie", while the runbook now says the Pre-Trip, ledger, README and `Datasheets/` set **may** be pre-seeded (every city folder is).
