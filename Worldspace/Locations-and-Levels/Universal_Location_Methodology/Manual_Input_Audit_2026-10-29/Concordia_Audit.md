# Concordia — manual input audit (2026-10-30) — a FINISHED city (district method): drift since the district passes

Method: `README.md` in this folder. Concordia is worked by the **district method** (`Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/Phase_Instructions/00_RUNBOOK.md`), not by the ULM: there is **no Pre-Trip and no datasheet set**, so there is no Part A. **Twelve of the thirteen districts were completed and QA-passed by 2026-08-29** (Cancer, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aries, Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, Gemini, Pisces, Sagittarius, Virgo); **the Hub is deferred by the developer** (not run). The question is only *has anything the district work rests on changed since?* **Finished district files are never edited** (and the megasheets are "not edited" by standing instruction); each drift item is an `AMEND` entry in `FIX_LEDGER.md` (entries `CNC-…`). **Limit, stated plainly:** the district material (13 Full Extrapolations, the Ultra-Megasheet, 13 Deep Dives, the Cultural Sheet — several MB) was *not* re-read line by line. Drift was found by (1) `git` history and a diff of the Concordia spec between the district-pass period (commit `7e259979`, the last change before 2026-09-03) and now, (2) counting old values and old names across `Concordia-City/`, (3) comparing the Concordia-level files (`Concordia_Second_Interwar_Cultural_Sheet.md`, `Concordia_Altitude_and_Atmosphere.md`, the Ultra-Megasheet front matter) with the live spec, census, Register and climate rebuild. `LOC` = `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Outside-World/Tepenian-Federation/Locations`, `CITIES` = `LOC/Cities`, `CC` = `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City`.

**Verdict.** **The district method is not harmed**, and the district files that name no figures are unaffected. What has moved is the **Concordia-level data underneath them**: **(1)** the 2026-10-28 census scenario (`DR-68`) changed Concordia's own counts; **(2)** the **climate was rebuilt on 2026-09-04**, a week after the twelve districts closed (polar night and midnight sun, record extremes, wind, precipitation, the monthly table); **(3)** the Ultra-Megasheet front matter says "altitude acclimatization is a real physiological fact", which the **altitude file of 2026-09-04 reverses** (humans do not acclimatize); **(4)** the **Cultural Sheet still calls Concordia "French- and Italian-founded"**, which the Founding Register (`DR-44`, 2026-10-03; `DR-19`) rules out; **(5)** the diaspora composition that feeds every district's outer-city intake was weighted on **Census I as it stood on 2026-07-31**, before the −10% cut, the Port Lockroy reduction (128,887 → 929) and the Santa Luce reduction (−58%); **(6)** retired city names remain in the Hub material, Deep Dives and Staging. Nothing blocks any later district or Hub work.
