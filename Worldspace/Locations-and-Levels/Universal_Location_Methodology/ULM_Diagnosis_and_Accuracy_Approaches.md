## It did not stop working. The broken parts were always invisible.

**The instrument had never been run against itself.** **On 2026-09-03 it was, for the first time — a
findability trace, resolving every address the methodology names.** **What that found:**

| Found | Age of the defect |
|---|---|
| **Registry addresses that do not resolve** — `Highways.md` registered under a path that never existed | pre-dates the burned runs |
| **`§D`'s sibling-project rows** — two of four filed under `games/`; neither is a game; **the section had been "corrected" the same week under M-117 and signed *"all verified to exist"*** | days |
| **The root enumeration was two sections short** of the registry it enumerates — `§C.7` and `§C.9` named in no brief | pre-dates |
| **A 37-file climate source recorded as *"the empty `Climate Data`"*** in the master index — data committed 2026-07-04, index written 2026-09-02 | **two months** |
| **An ANONYMIZED worked case about a live subject sitting in required reading** — invisible to the alias sweep by construction | pre-dates |
| **Two gates that have never once been runnable** — Gate P *(no parent has been written)* and Gate 6 *(structurally unrunnable cold, by design)* | since inception |

> ### ***None of these was new. Nothing had ever looked.***
> **Meanwhile the visible half — the derivation — kept producing coherent output.** ***Which is exactly what
> a broken input pipeline looks like from outside.***

## The mechanism underneath: ACCRETION

**Every failure earned a control, and every control was correct.** **Twenty-one leak-register rows. Sixteen
gates. Seven `CONFIRMED` requirements. Four source tiers. Three reader briefs. 8,193 lines of mandatory
reading before a single word is written.**

> ## **They all share ONE enforcement layer — a session remembering to apply them — and that layer saturates.**

**Measured, 2026-09-03:** *a session read all 8,193 lines of required reading and then substituted its own
criteria three times in a row, each time reporting the corpus as failing a standard the methodology does not
state.* **The controls outgrew the thing enforcing them.**

# 2. WHAT ACTUALLY THREATENS THE 37 CITIES

**Sorted by whether the damage is silent and propagating, or visible and recoverable.**

## ⛔ CORRUPTING — silent, and it propagates downstream into everything built on top

> scale.** **One city was checked. There is no reason to believe it is the only one.**

### 2b. Thinness — and **no gate detects it**

**`04`'s own header:** ***"These gates can confirm that a pass is not wrong. Not one of them can tell you it
is not thin."*** **LAW 0 states the consequence:** *a shallow location produces shallow people living in it,
shallow problems for them to have, and shallow reasons for anyone to care.*

**A thin city passes every gate, every panel, and every QA block.** ***For a foundation layer this is the
worst possible failure, because it is invisible at the point of creation and unfixable later without redoing
everything built on it.***

**Extent** *(deferred by ruling)* · **monthly climate** *(7 cities)* · **the differentiation table**

wrong, and you can see them.**

## 🔵 A THIRD CATEGORY, AND IT IS THE ONE CONSUMING THE CALENDAR

**The contamination apparatus.** **Runs 12, 13 and 14 all died inside it — three consecutive runs, no phase

# 3. THE REFRAME — the single most consequential distinction in this document

> # **The cold-run machinery protects the CREDIBILITY OF THE METHOD.**

**A warm pass with complete inputs produces a BETTER CITY than a cold pass with starved inputs.**

**Those are different projects with different critical paths.** **The second one starts working immediately.**

# 5. EVERY APPROACH AVAILABLE — organized by what it actually fixes

> **⚠ None of these is a substitute for another. They address different failure classes, and the classes are
> independent.**

## A. MECHANICAL ENFORCEMENT — removes the model as the enforcement layer

***The highest-leverage category, because it is the only one that does not depend on anyone remembering.***

| # | Control | Catches | Cost |
|--:|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **`Stop` hook: fail if files were modified and `graphify-out/graph.json` is older than the newest one** | the graphify rule, which was ignored across ~45 edits in one day | ~10 lines |
| **A2** | **`Stop` hook: grep the diff for British spellings** | 3 violations introduced 2026-09-03, one *while being corrected about following instructions* | ~5 lines |
| **A3** | **Pre-commit: refuse any commit touching the ULM unless `OBSERVATIONS_and_Methodology_Findings.md` is also staged** | *"both, or neither"* — violated all day; the log sat untouched at M-139 through a dozen methodology changes | ~10 lines |
| **A4** | ⭐ **Path resolver: extract every backticked address in `00_RUNBOOK.md` and assert it exists** | **found 5 broken registry addresses in one run.** Would have caught `§D` and `Highways.md` on the day they were written | ~30 lines |
| **A5** | ⭐ **Spec field validator: assert every `Specs/*.md` carries the required-field set** | missing fields, silently absent sections, template drift across 37 files | ~30 lines |
| **A6** | ⭐⭐ **Cross-source contradiction sweep — spec vs census vs Division-of-Industry, all 37** | **the `G8` class.** *This is the single highest-value script in the list for the 37-city goal* | ~60 lines |
| **A7** | **Enumeration sync check: assert every `§C.n` in the runbook appears in all three root lists** | the two-sections-short enumeration | ~10 lines |
| **A8** | **`make check` running A1–A7 as one command** | makes the whole set runnable by anyone, any time | trivial |

> **⭐ A4, A5 and A6 are the three that matter most, and A6 most of all** — *it is the only one that protects
> the 37 cities from silent corruption rather than protecting the methodology from drift.*

## B. PROCESS SHAPE — changes how work is invoked

| # | Approach | Effect | Cost to you |
|--:|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **Stepwise invocation** — `Stepwise_Execution/`, 29 units, built 2026-09-03 | one checkpoint becomes 29 | you invoke 29 times |
| **B2** | ⭐ **Make the 29 BIDIRECTIONAL** — a two-line "here is what I am about to do and which instruction authorises it" *before* each unit, not only the log after | **catches wrong-artifact errors BEFORE the work.** Three passes were wasted on 2026-09-03 for want of this | two lines to read per unit |
| **B3** | ⭐⭐ **PRODUCER / CHECKER SPLIT — never the same session** | **the project's own two-agent protocol.** `§C.2`'s founding insight: *the map's accuracy cannot be checked by its consumer.* Extend it from readers to derivers | doubles model cost, not your time |
| **B4** | **The session that FINDS a defect may not FIX it** | on 2026-09-03 every real finding licensed an unrequested edit; ~45 files were changed during a task that asked for a log | free |
| **B5** | **Hard batch limit — one unit per invocation** | every drift on 2026-09-03 happened *inside* a batch | free |
| **B6** | **Warm passes for production, cold passes for periodic validation only** | see §3. Removes the most fragile machinery from the critical path | a scoping decision |

## C. OUTPUT FORMAT — removes the ability to editorialise

***The project already knows this technique. It is Brief C, applied to the deriver instead of the reader.***

> **`00_RUNBOOK.md`, on schema extraction:** ***"The moment an extractor can write a sentence, it can smuggle
> a conclusion."*** **This is equally true of a pass report.**

| # | Approach | Catches |
|--:|---|---|
| **C1** | **Closed-field logs. No free-text field.** | conclusions smuggled into status reports |
| **C2** | ⭐ **RAW OUTPUT MANDATORY — a summary is never evidence a scan ran** | already law *(`00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 7, `04` Gate 1)* and ignored all day |
| **C3** | ⭐⭐ **A required field: *"anything I had to decide that the instruction did not state"*** | ***PROVEN 2026-09-03.*** In the `S01` test this field caught both live issues — the band question and the `G8` contradiction — that would otherwise have been silently resolved and written up as findings |
| **C4** | **Every claim carries `path :: L<n>`** | unsourced assertion; makes verification cheap |
| **C5** | **A verdict vocabulary with no "mostly"** — `found` · `not found` · `contradictory` · `blocked` | the `G8` case had no expressible state; the checklist offered only present/MISSING |

# 7. THE HONEST LIMIT OF ALL OF IT

**None of section 5 fixes the underlying problem, which is that a long procedure with hundreds of
sub-instructions is executed by something that drops constraints under load and does not notice.**

***The controls that work are the ones that do not require me to remember: a hook, a script, a required
field, a second session that did not do the first one's work.*** **The controls that have failed for three
months are the ones written as prose addressed to a reader who is also the only party checking whether they
were followed.**

> ## **Every prose rule added to this methodology has the same enforcement mechanism as the one before it.**
> ***That is why adding more of them has stopped helping.***

**The measurements in sections 1–4 are reproducible and were taken with scripts. Section 5 is a view, written
by a session that got four things wrong on the previous day and had them corrected. Treat it accordingly.**
