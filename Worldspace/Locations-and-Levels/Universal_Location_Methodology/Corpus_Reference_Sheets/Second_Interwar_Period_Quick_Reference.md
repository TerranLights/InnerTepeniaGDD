## The frame

**2564–2812. 248 years.** The era between the Falkland Treaty and the Long Night War. **The default frame for
worldbuilding a Tepenian location** — before the war, before Amundsen Tower's destruction.

## The Act structure — the single most important thing to know about it

| Act | Span | What the people ARE |
|---|---|---|
| **Act 1** | 2564 → early 2600s (~40–50 years, **~18%** of the period) | *"Chinese / Japanese / American / Russian / Mexican who live in Antarctica."* National-ethnic-origin cultures are still fresh |
| **Act 2** | ~late 2600s / early 2700s onward (**~82%** of the period) | **Properly Tepenian, in both name and cultural identity.** Origin is now ANCESTRY, not identity |

⚠ **The era spans both Acts and is mostly Act 2.** A pass that writes its characters as their founding nation
is writing the wrong Act for about 200 of the 248 years.

## The consolidating mechanism

**Interconnection, decisively solidified by Amundsen Tower's completion (~2688).** Four qualifications that
must not be dropped:
1. ⛔ **NOT a switch** — the unification was already in progress before construction even began.
2. ⛔⛔ **NOT the creation of unity** — before the Tower, people already shared struggles and goals in a shared
   country; they did not consider themselves psychologically/culturally/spiritually separate.
3. ⛔ **NOT the Tower's only effect** — it is primarily energy regulation, not the cause of everything.
4. ⭐ **What it did:** full interconnection, which further defined and was *"the decisive force that helped
   solidify their sense of national identity."*

**An Act boundary is a consolidation, not a line crossed** — write the transition as a gradient with a decisive
moment inside it.

## What else sits at ~2688 — check what shares your date

Amundsen Tower's completion · the opening of the orbital tier · **the Census I → Census II boundary** (which
the census records as pre-war migration to orbit, not loss) · regionally, the Tri-Cities' de facto
amalgamation. ⇒ *The same structure whose completion solidified a shared national identity is also the
structure through which people left — national context, true everywhere, therefore never a differentiator
between locations. What differentiates is how hard a given place used that exit.*

## What this means for any pass, story, or scene set in this era

1. ⛔ Place events against the Act boundary — the same event in Act 1 and Act 2 happens to different peoples.
2. ⭐ Correct form of words: ⛔ *"a Japanese city"* → ✅ *"a Japan-FOUNDED Tepenian city."* Stock: national
   origin. People: Tepenian. Local culture: its own.
3. ⭐⭐ Differentiate LOCALLY, converge NATIONALLY — local cultures stay genuinely different; national identity
   is never a differentiator, every Tepenian is Tepenian.
4. Composition names the STOCK; time and place produce the CULTURE — operator: time · separation · local
   environmental setting · local struggles and hardships · local goals · local sensibilities and habits.
