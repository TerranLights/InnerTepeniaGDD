> # Doll Representation Categories
>
> Every character in the Dolliverse is based on a real-world, physically-existing doll. How that doll is
> *represented* as an in-universe person is not fixed to one form — there are four total representation
> categories ("Cat.s"). Only one is currently in active use; the other three are established canon, reserved for
> future characters and projects.
>
> ## The Four Categories
>
> 1. **Robots** — the only category currently in active use across the Dolliverse. Originally built by humans;
>    later came to be built by each other. See `Robot_Universals/` for the shared cross-series robot canon
>    (biology, culture, the Fundamental Laws), and each project's own repo for project-specific robot mechanics.
>
> 2. **Anthros** — genetically engineered by humans, by splicing human and animal DNA into human-animal hybrids.
>    Most prominently (but not limited to) "dog-people," "cat-people," and "rabbit-people." Later developed into
>    full races via natural reproduction, rather than remaining a manufactured population the way robots did.
>
> 3. **Elves** — genetically engineered by aliens, on a parallel Earth, in another parallel dimension.
>
> 4. **Aliens** — origin unknown.
>
> ## Status
>
> Not yet in use: as of 2026-08-09, every character across every Tepenian Universe project is a Doll represented
> as a robot (Category 1). Categories 2-4 are confirmed canon but have no characters assigned to them yet.
>
> ## Open Questions
>
> - No character has been assigned to Categories 2, 3, or 4 yet — first use of each is undecided.
> - The `seed-template-init.txt` / `seed-to-README-process.md` intake process (see
>   `Worldspace/Characters/`) has no field for which of the four categories a Doll belongs to — it implicitly
>   assumes robot. Once a non-robot character is added, that process will need an explicit category field; not
>   added preemptively since only one category is in use right now.
> - The parallel-Earth/parallel-dimension mechanism behind the elves' origin, and the aliens' own origin, are
>   not yet developed.
>
