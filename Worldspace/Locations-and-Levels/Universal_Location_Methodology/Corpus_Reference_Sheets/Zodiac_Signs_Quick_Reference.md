## The pattern worth carrying into every application

**Nearly every sign's negative attributes aren't a separate trait bolted on beside the positive ones — they're
the SAME trait pushed past its useful range.** Aries' courage becomes recklessness; Virgo's precision becomes
paralysis; Cancer's protective instinct becomes a wall; Libra's fairness becomes an inability to ever decide.
**A location's own "Zodiac-appended" reading isn't necessarily gaining a new quality it didn't have — it may
just be naming which direction an already-established trait could tip toward, for better or worse, if
pushed.**
