## Density check — run here, per `00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 2.6, not deferred to QA

**One division, no interpretation, per Gate 11's own worked precedent on Zhongshan.**

Population (Census II, combined): **888,292.**
Larsemann Hills total oasis extent (Specs, geographic basis): **~34 km²**, shared among three cities
(Sinheung, Zhongshan, Shirayuki) at near-identical coordinates.

| Assumption | Density |
|---|---|
| Sinheung alone occupies the full 34 km² | ~26,126/km² — comparable to Paris (~21,000/km²) or central Manila |
| Sinheung occupies a roughly equal one-third share (~11.3 km²) | ~78,600/km² — denser than any real city on Earth at metro scale; comparable only to the single densest slum districts (e.g., Dharavi pockets), not to any city-scale figure |

**This cannot be resolved by invention** — extent is a Tier 0 blocking input per `05` §2.1 and it is not stated
for Sinheung specifically anywhere in admissible canon. **Emitting a REQUESTED input** (see §5 block below)
rather than picking a number. **Provisional working assumption for this pass, stated as provisional and
revisited at Step 7/Gate 11:** given the oasis is described as shared roughly evenly by three cities of
comparable stated population (Zhongshan's own prior runs describe a similarly large population — not consulted
here, but the shared-coordinates fact alone implies comparable footprint), this pass proceeds on the **full-
34km² assumption** (~26,126/km²) as the less extreme and better-evidenced reading, while flagging explicitly
that if Sinheung's true footprint is closer to a one-third share, the density is in territory this project has
never had to write for and Phase 3/4/9 would need a fundamentally different texture (vertical, subterranean, or
extremely enclosed construction) to be physically plausible at all.

**This is not a fresh Gate-11-style catch — it is expected, not surprising**, given Sinheung shares Zhongshan's
coordinates and Zhongshan's own prior run already found and corrected a comparable order-of-magnitude density
issue for the same physical site. Recorded here as confirmation the same physical constraint applies to
Sinheung, not as a novel plausibility failure.
