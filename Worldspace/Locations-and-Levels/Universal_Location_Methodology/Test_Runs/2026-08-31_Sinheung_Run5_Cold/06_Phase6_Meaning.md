# Sinheung — Phase 6: Meaning

## A. The Naming technique, run first

**Canon check for religious-register language attached to non-religious objects, in admissible material:**
Specs' own "Legacy" section states the Jeju-do allocation *"standing alongside Shirayuki's own diplomatically-
engineered founding"* — attribute-level, no sacred/oath/witnessed-register vocabulary present. **The search
comes back empty in admissible canon** — Sinheung's culture-pass files almost certainly contain this kind of
language (most Tepenian cities' completed culture sheets do), but those are precisely the quarantined files.
**Proceeding to generate, per the technique's own fallback rule.**

**The unnamed load-bearing thing, stated as one sentence a resident would agree with:** *"A claim that was
given to you, rather than earned by you, has to be re-earned constantly, or it was never really yours."*

**This is a STAKE, not a belief system** (`03` Phase 6 §A's genre distinction) — *what is at risk here that is
at risk nowhere else.* Chained directly from every phase so far: Phase 1's G4 founding deficit (no organic
claim), Phase 1's central cost-dominant finding (paying, on four fronts, to be believed to have deserved to
exist), Phase 4's civic "prove it" reflex, and Phase 5's Zhongshan-tri-junction dependency (a claim to land
that is itself partly borrowed daily). **What's at stake, specifically, that is not at stake in a city that
grew organically:** the entire legitimacy of Sinheung's presence on this ground is retroactive and conditional
— it was granted on the expectation of output, and admissible canon gives no indication the grant is
permanent or unconditional. **Counter-check (per Phase 6 §A's portability test):** would this stake work
equally well for a sibling? **No** — Zhongshan's Saturn/Metal geometry (Phase 5) reads as comfortable with
mystery and self-editing, not anxious about re-earning anything; Shirayuki's Uranus/Fire geometry performs its
outsider status as attraction, which is close to Sinheung's Uranus half but diverges sharply on the Fire/
Electricity axis (charismatic display vs. quiet dependent animation). **The stake is Sinheung-specific.**

## C. Death and the dead

**The disposal-of-the-dead mechanism is a standing project-wide RESERVED question** (`05` §3) — not decided
here. **What can be written around it, per the reserved protocol:** given Sinheung's stake (§A) and its
inherited-not-built founding (Phase 1, G4), the more interesting question this phase can actually answer is
**who is not mourned properly, and why not** (`03` Phase 6 §C, flagged as usually the most productive sub-
question). **Candidate, chained from Phase 5's dependency finding:** anyone whose life's output was judged to
have failed to "prove the claim" — a low-output worker, a discontinued project, a schematic revision that
never shipped — plausibly receives a thinner public record than someone whose output was visibly successful.
This directly extends Phase 1's G8-adjacent "Asymmetric Record-Keeping" candidate (`Cultural_Synthesis_
Techniques.md` Technique 8): **Sinheung likely permanently records successes and conspicuously under-records
failures**, which is the same asymmetry Cancer's own worked example uses, but derived here independently from
Sinheung's own founding-legitimacy stake rather than copied from that instance. **Flagged for Phase 7/10
development, not fully resolved** — this is a candidate, not yet corroborated by a second generator.

**Is the absence itself the finding?** Per Phase 6 §C's own distinction (three different reasons a place might
outsource its dead: lack of room, lack of anyone yet to bury, or not wanting to look) — **Sinheung's candidate
reason is a fourth, not yet named in the rule file: not wanting to look at a record of insufficient output.**
Flagged as a possible addition to `03` Phase 6 §C's enumerated reasons, pending confirmation on a second
location.

## D. The Failure State of the Core Value

**Core value/promise:** the claim was worth securing — Sinheung will demonstrably justify its own existence
through output. **Under what circumstance does it become impossible to keep?** When output cannot be produced
— a chamber-line failure, a schematic mismatch with Neumayer's updates, a freight interruption from Mirny/
Zhongshan (Phase 5's named dependency). **What grew up around that circumstance?** Per Phase 1's G1 profile
(Electricity's negative pole: instability, total dependency, collapse the instant the current is withdrawn),
the failure state is not gradual decline but **sudden, total interruption** — the same shape as the symbolic
substrate's own negative pole, corroborating rather than deriving (`02` §5.2's "two agree" pattern, now visible
a third time across phases). **Does the culture ritualize the failure or refuse to?** Per §C's asymmetric-
record-keeping candidate, the tentative answer is **refuse** — a place that under-records failure is
structurally unlikely to have built a comfortable ritual around it. **This is the stronger half, per `03`
Phase 6 §D's own note** ("the refusal is often the strongest half") — Sinheung's culture has had roughly two
generations (Census I era to now) to develop a failure-ritual and, per the pattern established across every
other phase, appears to have developed record-suppression instead.
