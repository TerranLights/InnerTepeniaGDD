# Sinheung — Phase 10: Catalog

**Governing premise checked:** Sinheung is built around a theme (continuous self-proof through demonstrated
output), not a thing — the catalog below refracts that theme rather than restating "the Chamber Works" over
and over.

## 1. Named places and landmarks

- **The Chamber Works** — already established in admissible canon (Specs, Economy & Industry). This pass adds:
  a legible interior/exterior architectural seam (Phase 3C) between the externally-schematic-matched interior
  and the inherited/adapted exterior city fabric.
- **The Zhongshan tri-junction** — already established (Specs, Economy). This pass adds: the unadministrable
  right-of-way finding (Phase 7b) as its defining civic character, distinct from its bare logistics function.
- **A dimension-stone works / quarry site**, drawing on the Larsemann Hills' exposed bedrock (Phase 1 G2,
  Phase 7a's non-thematic export). Not yet officially named — flagged as a REQUESTED naming opportunity rather
  than invented outright.
- **A record hall or posted-output board**, refracting Phase 6C/8's asymmetric-record-keeping finding — a
  public place where completed, successful output is displayed (chamber shipments, fabrication milestones),
  with no equivalent public record of failure. Directly parallel in *shape* to Cancer's own "successes posted,
  failures unrecorded" finding (`Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` Technique 8's worked instance) — **flagged
  explicitly as a shape-repeat requiring the three-step rule** (`02` §4, `00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 6): the *shape*
  (asymmetric record-keeping) is shared, but the *instrument* here is Sinheung's specific founding-legitimacy
  stake (Phase 6A), not Cancer's continuation-value failure state — genuinely different generators producing a
  structurally similar-looking output. **To be written up as an explicit comparison table at Step 6, not
  silently allowed to stand as if novel.**

## 2. Physical things

- **A layered, rapid-adjust garment system** (Phase 8B) — the functional dress response to the freeze-thaw
  climate register.
- **A quarried-stone household or civic object** (Phase 7a's non-thematic export, made concrete) — something a
  person elsewhere in Tepenia might own without knowing its origin, the same shape as Cancer's cast-metal
  export (Technique 9's worked instance), again a shape-repeat to be checked at Step 6, not written as if new.

## 3. People as role-archetypes — no invented proper names

- **A freight dispatcher** — the person whose schedule actually organizes most residents' days (Phase 4A),
  functionally more load-bearing to daily life than any Chamber Works role, despite being far less nationally
  visible.
- **A quality examiner at the Chamber Works** — the gatekeeper of Phase 5d/7c's "accepted without correction"
  membership/apprenticeship mechanism; the person whose judgment converts a newcomer's or apprentice's work into
  proof of belonging.
- **A "made-here" robot** and **a "made-elsewhere" robot** — not named individuals but the two sides of Phase
  9's strongest population-level distinction; genuinely worth casting as recurring background-NPC archetypes
  given how directly it falls out of established fact.
- **A quietly-reassigned worker** — someone who did not clear the output bar and now works the quarry or an
  administrative role, per Phase 6C/7c's no-villain lateral-reassignment finding; sympathetic, not disgraced,
  consistent with `00d`'s discipline.

**Existing placeholders, not renamed or expanded beyond their current status (RESERVED):** Chief Engineer Baek
Ji-hoon, Foreman Dae-ho Richter (Specs' Notable Figures — both explicitly marked placeholder in canon already).

## 4. Settings

- **The shared right-of-way** (Phase 7b) — a recurring situational texture: two freight loads, one narrow
  access route, an unwritten and unenforceable priority custom.
- **A meltwater-lake leisure spot** (Phase 4E) — seasonal, present roughly nine months a year, absent during
  the polar night; a genuine amenity most Tepenian Antarctic cities cannot offer.
- **The Chamber Works' interior/exterior threshold** (Phase 3C) — a literal architectural expression of Phase
  1's G3 deficit (design authority elsewhere).
