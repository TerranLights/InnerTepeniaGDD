# Phase 5 — Relation & Geometry

**Named axis: "who remembers founding it vs. who now outnumbers them."** Mandatory. **Isolated-location
weighting applies**: Cape Adare's sibling set (other Janbogo-subnet cities) exists but their culture-pass
material is inadmissible for this pass (Frame Declaration), so 5a runs thin by necessity, and 5c/5e carry the
real weight, per `03` Phase 5's own weighting table for isolated locations.

---

## 5a — Peers (thin, by necessity, not by choice)

**No admissible peer material exists.** The other six Janbogo-subnet cities' own culture passes are withheld
for the identical reason Cape Adare's is. **Substitutes used, per `01` §5.3a**: own-eras (not much history to
compare within this single-frame pass, genuinely thin here) and the real-world comparables already researched
(Yalta, Boulders Beach). **Stated honestly rather than forced.**

## 5c — The parent (primary, per the weighting table)

**What the Federation/Janbogo subnet needs from Cape Adare, inferable from its own established position**:
first-landfall processing for ships approaching from the open north, and — per Phase 1 §D — the earliest
warning of incoming weather anywhere on this stretch of coast. **What Cape Adare's own civic memory holds, and
whether it's accurate**: per Phase 2's arrival-order mismatch, the community that carries the founding
precedence forward (New Zealand's own early-arriving population) is not the community that now holds the
numbers (USA, China). **The real relational question this produces**: does the parent (the subnet, the
Federation) treat Cape Adare's significance as belonging to its founding narrative, or to its current
demographic weight? **Not resolved by this pass's admissible material — flagged forward to Phase 6/7**, since
this is precisely the "no anchor" question Phase 1 §F left open, now sharpened into a genuine relational
mismatch rather than a purely internal one.

## 5d — The edge and the crossing

**Arrival types, enumerated**: by sea (the historic default, the "first landfall" route every founding account
centers on) and by land (via the Hwy 183 connector — Phase 1 §D's spur finding). **What converts a visitor into
a member**: per Technique 10, fused to Cape Adare's own specific economic rhythm (Phase 4 §C's arrival-dependent
feast-or-famine work cycle) rather than copied from any other location's own mechanism — **having weathered one
full cycle of the arrival-dependent economy**, plausibly a season or more, marks someone as no longer needing
the boardwalk-corridor system explained to them and no longer treated as new. **Genuinely distinct from Highway
37's own rotation-clock mechanism** (Run 6) despite a surface similarity — Cape Adare's version is about a
stable resident absorbing an unpredictable economic rhythm, not a posted worker completing a fixed-length
assignment; there is no clock here, only an accumulated familiarity.

**The asymmetry check, run directly (`04` Gate 9)**: what happens to someone who arrives and leaves before
completing a full cycle? **Low-stakes by this mechanism's own nature** — unlike Highway 37's rotation
interruption, nothing is lost or reset; the newcomer simply remains a newcomer for as long as they stay, with
no penalty and a trivial route back (staying longer eventually crosses the same threshold). **No asymmetry
problem exists here to fix — checked and confirmed clean, not skipped.**

## 5e — Dependency

**What Cape Adare needs from elsewhere and cannot make itself**: at Band 5 (over a million people) in an
extreme southern climate, large-scale food and resource import dependency is a near-certain structural fact,
though not quantified anywhere admissible — **REQUESTED**, not invented to false precision. **A second,
canon-confirmed dependency, structurally unusual**: Cape Adare's own physical integration into the wider
Janbogo subnet road network is **incomplete in canon itself** — the coastal road south to the Terra Nova Bay
cities is explicitly "not yet established; distance and route TBD" (Open Questions). **This is not a narrative
gap this pass is filling in — it is a genuine open dependency the city's own canon has never resolved**,
worth carrying forward as a REQUESTED item with unusually high stakes: until it's answered, Cape Adare's entire
southward physical relationship to its own subnet is undefined.
