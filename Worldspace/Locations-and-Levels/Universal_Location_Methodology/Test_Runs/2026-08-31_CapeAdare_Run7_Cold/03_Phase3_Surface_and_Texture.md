# Phase 3 — Surface & Texture

**Named axis: "the rookery sets the terms; the city fits around it."** Mandatory for Settlement.

---

## A. Formalize first

**Already established, admissibly:** headland terrain, low and rocky; a mean annual temperature of −12°C
(genuinely milder than every interior city this methodology has examined); pronounced storm exposure from the
north; a ~87-day polar night and ~98-day midnight sun, real but "not as extreme as the deep interior cities";
and the rookery's own scale (250,000+ breeding pairs) as the dominant sensory fact.

## B. The four sensory sub-fields

**Sound.** The rookery is loud and constant — a real Adélie colony of this scale is a continuous, layered
sound of vocalizing birds, never fully absent. Storms, when they arrive (often, per Phase 1's first-hit
finding), add wind and surf on top of it, not instead of it.

**Smell.** Guano and colony smell at this scale is, per every real-world account of comparable Adélie
rookeries, inescapable and genuinely strong — the file's own admissible language calls it *"the dominant
sensory fact... for any visitor,"* and this pass takes that at face value rather than softening it.

**Feel.** Markedly milder than the interior cities examined so far, but exposed and windswept — the cold here
is a wet, ocean-driven cold rather than the interior's dry extreme, a genuinely different physical sensation
even at a similar or milder temperature.

**First impressions.** Arrival by sea puts a newcomer at the actual literal threshold of the continent — the
first landfall point — immediately inside a rookery whose scale most newcomers will not have any real
preparation for. **The geography and the wildlife arrive as one single overwhelming impression, not two.**

## C. Boardwalk corridors — a real, researched fusion, not an assumption

**Per Technique from `Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md`, fused against Cape Adare's own rookery-scale
fact (Phase 1 §A) and the researched Boulders Beach precedent** (`CapeAdare_Research_Log.md`, Session 1): real
Boulders Beach enforces movement via **fixed boardwalks, a 2–3 m minimum distance, and a strict no-feeding
rule** — not vague etiquette but a specific, physical, enforced corridor system. **Fused, not copied**: at Cape
Adare's much larger scale (250,000+ pairs against Boulders' colony of a few thousand), the same logic — fixed,
marked movement corridors that keep human/robot traffic off penguin routes entirely — is not a visitor
amenity, as at Boulders, but **basic civic infrastructure**, load-bearing for the city to function at all.
**Divergence stated explicitly**: Boulders' rules exist to protect a delicate, comparatively small colony from
tourist pressure; Cape Adare's marked-corridor system, at rookery scale, more plausibly exists to keep the city
itself physically navigable at all, with penguin welfare a secondary though real benefit.

## D. Public/institutional split, checked but genuinely thin

**No admissible material establishes indoor/heated institutional space in any specific form** — the "warm
microclimate via heating infrastructure" claim exists only in the excluded Character & Culture section (`00`'s
self-correction) and is **not used here**. **What this pass can say instead, independently derived**: Cape
Adare's own milder coastal climate (§A) means the *degree* of heating infrastructure required is plausibly less
than at the deep interior cities this methodology has already examined — a genuine, independently-reached
divergence from those prior findings, not a restatement of the excluded section's own (much stronger) claim.
**REQUESTED**: the actual scale and character of Cape Adare's built/heated interior spaces, which this pass
does not invent past this cautious baseline.

## E. Retroactive Mechanism — checked, genuine null

**Nothing in Cape Adare's admissible material presents as an unexplained established effect** the way
Highway 37's "bidirectional junction" phrasing did — every admissible fact here already carries its own stated
physical cause (the rookery's own scale, the coastal storm exposure). **Recorded as a genuine null rather than
forced.**

## F. Seasonal variance

**Polar night (~87 days) and midnight sun (~98 days), genuinely present but milder than the deep interior.**
The rookery itself is seasonal in the real world (Adélie colonies are occupied primarily during the breeding
season, largely empty in deep winter) — **REQUESTED**: whether this pass's admissible material or any future
research confirms the same seasonal occupancy pattern for Tepenia's rookery, which would materially change
what "the dominant sensory fact" actually means across the year rather than as a year-round constant.
