# The transferable lesson — this is why the entry is worth keeping

**The technique itself is sound and general, and survives without the instance:**

- **Run BOTH halves of the arrival pair.** *Pull* — the place offers something obtainable nowhere else.
  *Push* — the person was leaving somewhere else and this was the destination.
- **They differ in DURABILITY, not only direction.** *A population assembled by attraction has already
  demonstrated willingness to relocate for something better. It will demonstrate it again.*
- **Pull locations accumulate low-switching-cost attachments** — pleasant, genuinely good, costless to give
  up. **Push locations accumulate high-switching-cost attachments** — a trade, a claim, an obligation, people
  who know your business — unpleasant, and they hold.
- **The finding this produces is often that a location's greatest strength and its largest structural
  vulnerability are the same property**, running in two directions.
- **Cross-check against `G8`'s census-change technique**: pull/push predicts retention, retention is
  measurable, so this is one of the few places the methodology can check its own reading against a number.
