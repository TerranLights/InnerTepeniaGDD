**Scope note.** This file runs ONE sign (Aquarius) of the Zodiac Lens technique family against Janbogo's
established, admissible profile (Phase 0–10 base content, `00`–`11` in this folder, `01` Ordering Collision
Log excluded as not needed). Sources: Aquarius's own registered character read from
`Concordia-City/Districts/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/11_Aquarius.md` (never from tradition, never from the

(`City_Symbolic_Substrate/Robot_Elementals.md`); the 10 Robot Planetary Symbols

**Note on Janbogo's own assigned symbols.** Janbogo's `City_Symbol_Assignments.md` row carries Earth (planet)
and Air (element) — already used at Phase 0 §4 in a G1 pairing reading (Earth's redundant-convergence promise
undercut by Air's own diffuse-connection negative, read as "Ironic"). Both are cross-checked again below

system from Earth-the-Wu-Xing-elemental — genuine, non-circular cross-checks, not restatements. Where a

## STEP 1 — Base Aquarius prompt

**Prompt:** If Janbogo had to produce something embodying Aquarius's own registered character — the
water-bearer who walks farther than anyone to bring back what the collective needs; "improvement of the
collective by correct thinking"; the Saturn/Uranus split (institution vs. rogue); the sign's own historical
first-association with *empirical weather prediction* (Eustemon of Athens, §13); mental stimulation pursued
with fixity-as-follow-through — what would that be, given Janbogo's own established profile?

**Candidate generated.** Three independently-admissible facts converge without any one requiring the others:
(a) Phase 1's own researched G4/G7 finding — the real Jang Bogo Station's documented research foci
(space/astronomy, glaciology, meteorite study, climate/cryosphere, applied multidisciplinary testbed work),
already grounding a "scientific/applied-research tradition" at Phase 7a/8; (b) Phase 1's own G2 STANDING
COST — a continuous, admissible need for wind-forecast/warning function (sourced cleanly from Specs' Gameplay
Notes, "the game signals incoming events," not from the excluded M-65 file); (c) Aquarius's own registered
material independently supplies the *specific* historical hook — this sign is the corpus's own first
recorded association with empirical weather forecasting, not science in general. **HIT 1**: an
atmospheric/applied-research institution — Janbogo's own version of "the water-bearer who walks farther than
anyone to bring back what the collective needs" — grounded in material Janbogo already has on its own terms,
sharpened (not invented) by Aquarius's specific weather-forecasting myth-strand.

**Filter.** Characteristically inevitable, not generic: this is not "Janbogo has science and Aquarius likes
science" (too generic) — it is the specific convergence of a real historical research-station heritage, a
continuous physical necessity for empirical atmospheric prediction, and Aquarius's own uniquely specific
historical tie to that exact practice. **Kept**, pushed toward player-facing form: **entered** — a place,
distinct from (but overlapping with) the already-catalogued "scientific/testbed craft workshops" (Phase
7a/8/10); this lens sharpens *why* that existing catalog entry is characteristically Aquarian rather than
inventing a new one. No proper name assigned (consistent with the base run's own name-poor discipline).

**Step 5 — stopping reason for Step 1.** Generation stopped after HIT 1 because the three converging facts
(research heritage, forecasting necessity, Aquarius's own myth-strand) are already a maximally tight fit;
pushing further at the base-prompt level (before running the 18 cross-checks) would mean inventing additional
Aquarian content not grounded in Janbogo's own established profile, which the discipline this run has held
throughout (REQUESTED over invented) forbids. Further candidates from this same prompt are deferred to the
contradiction check (Step 4), which is where a second, opposite-register finding belongs.

## STEP 3 — Within-sign combinatorial synthesis

Checked all pairs among HITs 1–9 for combinations producing something neither hit implies alone. Three
survived; others considered and found to add nothing beyond what the individual hits already state (e.g.
HIT 1 × HIT 6 — the institute's proximity to the undocumented core — was already folded into HIT 6's own
write-up above, not a fresh third thing; HIT 4 × HIT 9 and HIT 5 × HIT 9 were considered and found to require
forcing an unsupported cross-city mechanism, rejected).

**COMBO 1 (HIT 2 × HIT 5).** The informal, unlicensed local relay (HIT 2) is not merely a Uranus-rogue
convenience for its own sake — it is plausibly a direct, trust-driven response to the con-artist/
info-broker vulnerability (HIT 5): Janbogo's research-and-coordination community routes around the
officially-sanctioned, externally-hosted nexus specifically *because* that channel is the known avenue for
manipulated data. The "unlicensed lab" becomes a rational security practice growing directly out of the very
Aquarian-gullibility exploit that makes the sanctioned channel dangerous — a causal link neither hit implies
alone.

**COMBO 2 (HIT 3 × HIT 7).** The uniform housing/allocation policy (HIT 3) is plausibly not a separate
administrative decision but the direct mechanical output of the committee/consensus governance body (HIT 7)
itself: a co-authored, no-single-authority process naturally converges on uniform, universally-applicable
rules, since no individual has standing to grant a case-by-case exception. This is precisely the mechanism by
which Aquarius's own light expression (rule by co-authorship, not decree) produces its own shadow (the
equality trap) — the same institution, seen from two angles, not two separate facts.

**COMBO 3 (HIT 8 × HIT 9).** HIT 8 (Janbogo's own self-presentation oversells its distinctiveness) and HIT 9
(the Federation/subnet administratively treats Janbogo as interchangeable with Zukelli) combine into a
standing, low-grade civic grievance: the oversell is not free-floating civic pride but plausibly a direct,
ongoing rhetorical response to the unconsented classification — a real political friction neither fact
implies on its own.
