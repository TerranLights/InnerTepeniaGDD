## C. One honest divergence — tested both-are-true, not simply killed

**Phase 5d proposed**: sustained economic participation (continuing to work port/logistics past an initial
arrival purpose) as Janbogo's membership mechanism, per `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` Technique 10.

**The culture sheet's own §28 states a different, more specific mechanism**: *"you become a Janbogo resident
the moment you start hosting someone else rather than only being hosted."* A reciprocity-of-care ritual, not
an economic threshold.

**Both-are-true test, run per `02` §5.3 and `04` Gate 6's own required check before declaring a kill**: are
these about different objects or different scales? **Plausibly yes** — economic persistence (staying past a
work term) is a structural *precondition* that puts someone in a position to eventually host rather than
only be hosted; the culture sheet's own reciprocity marker is the *specific, humanized, recognized* version
of becoming settled enough to give rather than only receive. **Not contradictory, but also not confirmed as
the same finding** — this pass's own candidate was a reasonable structural guess that turned out to be one
layer beneath the actual, more specific canon answer. **Recorded honestly as a partial miss, not smoothed
into a false match**: the economic-participation mechanism was never adopted as more than a flagged
candidate in Phase 5d, so nothing here needs correcting — but it is worth naming precisely, since a future
session reaching for this technique on a similar location should expect the *economic* answer to be a floor,
not necessarily the actual recognized marker.
