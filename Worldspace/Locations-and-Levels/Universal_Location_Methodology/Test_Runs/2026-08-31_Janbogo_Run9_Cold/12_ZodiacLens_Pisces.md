**Sign:** Pisces (raw substrate only — `Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/12_Pisces.md` — never the Concordia
Pisces district's own culture-pass conclusions). **Location:** Janbogo, admissible material only (Phase

**Reading discipline held throughout:** all Pisces content read from the substrate file's own registered
terms (light/shadow/core need/behavior/socialization/conflict-affinity/structural-position/myth/
correspondences), never from traditional astrology. All Elemental/Planetary content read from

## Step 1 — Base Pisces prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Pisces's own registered character — dissolution
of fixed shape, "the only place that will still transact with you after everywhere else has stopped,"
genuine redistribution, absorbing without being able to source it — given Janbogo's own established
profile, what would that be?

**Candidate generated and kept.** Pisces's light expression names, as its clearest recognition marker,
being the door that stays open when every other door has shut: "Nobody is turned away... the only place
that will still transact with you after everywhere else has stopped" (§2). Janbogo's own single most
repeated, most distinctive established fact — present in Phase 0's frame, Phase 1's G2 STRENGTH, Phase 3's
first-impressions, Phase 5's peer comparison, Phase 8's cuisine finding — is the Terra Nova Bay polynya:
the **only year-round ice-free maritime access point on this stretch of Ross Sea coast.** For most of the
year, Janbogo genuinely is the one place on this coast still open when the rest have iced shut. Reading
this through Pisces produces a role, not a restatement of the polynya itself:

**HIT 1 (kept). A harbor/bay pilot whose defining function is bringing a vessel in during conditions that
would see any other Ross Sea port refuse entry** — because Janbogo, structurally, is often the only
functioning option. Category: **PERSON** (role-archetype, no invented name). Player-facing form: encountered
at the docks; a figure who has personally witnessed every kind of desperate, last-resort arrival Janbogo
receives, precisely because the city cannot close the way its neighbors can.

**Filter applied:** characteristically inevitable, not generic — grounded in three separately-established
Janbogo facts converging (G2 STRENGTH's unique year-round access; G2's own STANDING COST, the continuous
wind-forecast/shelter-routing infrastructure that makes "bringing a ship in under hazard" an institutionalized,
repeated judgment call rather than an ad hoc one; G5's genuine multi-modal port function) — not simply
"every port has a pilot."

**Alternative considered and set aside for Step 1 itself, not discarded**: an informal secondhand/salvage
exchange fed by outgrown retrofit material and by the belongings of people who never finished passing
through. This candidate is real and well-grounded, but is developed properly at Step 2 (Elemental
Earth), where it emerges more precisely from Janbogo's own G4 findings than from the base Pisces prompt
alone — recorded here so the reasoning trail is visible, not silently dropped.

**Step 5 stopping reason for the base prompt:** a candidate was generated and kept (HIT 1); a second
candidate (the salvage exchange) was identified but deliberately deferred to a cross-check where its
grounding is sharper, per the "formalize before inventing, use the strongest available derivation" discipline
this whole run has held.
