# Phase 8 — Making

**Asks:** What does this place cook, sing, wear, build, play, and say?

**Governing discipline: native, not transplanted.** Given the excluded `Character & Culture` material would
have been the most likely source of transplanted-nation-specific texture (M-64), this phase leans
deliberately on Janbogo's own *native* material — what its own physical/functional situation produces —
which happens to align with `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md` §12's own preference to develop the native
layer first regardless.

---

## Dress — started from function, per `03` 8B

**A genuine two-register wardrobe, falling directly out of Phase 3's established exterior/interior split.**
Function first: anyone crossing the threshold needs sealed, wind/cold-resistant outerwear capable of
surviving a katabatic event's collapsed blizzard/clear distinction (Phase 3, G2) — this is not fashion, it
is survival equipment, and per `03` 8B's own instruction ("what does that need becoming custom look like
after two centuries?") it plausibly hardens into a recognizable, near-universal exterior layer regardless of
who is wearing it or why. **Indoor dress, by contrast, is unconstrained by this same pressure** — interiors
are established as genuinely sealed and stable (Phase 3), so indoor appearance has no equivalent physical
driver and is not asserted here (general-population discipline: absence of a grounding fact is a reason not
to invent one, not an invitation to).

**General-population check, run per `03` 8C's own hardest-here warning**: this finding describes the whole
population's *outerwear*, not one profession's uniform — the wind-hazard is universal (Phase 1, G2), not
role-specific, so the exterior layer is plausibly general rather than narrow. **Passes the discipline
because the underlying driver (G2) is itself universal, not because a professional garment was mistakenly
generalized.**

## Cuisine

**A genuine, non-invented differentiator**: the Terra Nova Bay polynya's year-round openness (Phase 1, G2
STRENGTH) means Janbogo plausibly receives fresh maritime goods more consistently than almost any other
Tepenian coastal city, most of which face seasonal ice closure. **This is a real structural advantage with a
direct cuisine consequence** — the *timing and consistency* of fresh-goods arrival, not any specific dish,
is the differentiator, which keeps this finding at the correct general-population altitude rather than
reaching for a specific named food (per `00b`'s object-colonization warning, applied here to avoid the
"narrow object standing in for a sector" failure at Phase 8's own scale).

**All populations, per `03` 8's own requirement**: no admissible material establishes a distinct
robot-population cuisine angle for Janbogo specifically — **REQUESTED**, flagged rather than invented,
consistent with `03` 8's own note that this is "the most skipped item in this project's entire template"
and should not be skipped silently here either.
