## STEP 1 — Base Leo prompt

**Prompt:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Leo's own registered character — fixed fire that needs to be *witnessed* being itself, cannot tell unwitnessed from wronged, event-manufacture out of ordinary moments, morale infrastructure, dignity maintained under exposure (the Merrick reading), refusal of the menial/subordinate, binary health with no middle gear — what would that be, given Janbogo's own established profile?

**Reasoning.** Janbogo's admissible profile is dominated by hazard-vigilance, threshold-crossing cost, a hub-without-its-own-nexus governance gap, and (Phase 6A) a genuine NULL on sacred/civic-register language — none of that is naturally Leo-shaped, and a "Leo landmark building" is actively *contradicted* by Phase 3's own first-impressions finding (arrival by sea shows "low-profile, wind-braced, aerodynamically oriented" structures — the opposite of Leo's "loudest, most exquisite... refusal to blend"). But two genuine, non-forced fits exist, both keyed to specific established facts rather than to Leo's name alone.

**KEPT — Candidate 1A. A "captive-season" morale host (PERSON, role/archetype).** Phase 3E establishes Janbogo's worst stretch as polar night and peak katabatic intensity overlapping almost exactly (~May–July, no offset, no reprieve); Phase 4/8 establish that recreation is disproportionately concentrated indoors into that same six-to-eight-week window; Phase 8's own leisure-specifics slot is explicitly REQUESTED ("what specifically fills that time is not groundable"). This is precisely the setup Leo's light expression is built for — "tries to create events from the smallest moments," "wonderful morale booster," and the specific behavioral note "leaves early if under-attended, stays all night if fed" describes an audience that is, for six to eight weeks, literally captive. A role whose civic function is to manufacture occasion and hold morale during that overlap is characteristically inevitable here, not generic — it answers an established gap with an established constraint. **Player-facing form:** heard (announcements, a voice associated with the dark stretch), seen (a recurring host/presence at whatever indoor venue fills that REQUESTED gap).

**KEPT — Candidate 1B. The outdoor-exposed worker, witnessed without choosing (PERSON, role/archetype — the Merrick pattern).** Snodgrass's chosen "Leo at its best" exemplar is Joseph Merrick — spectacle imposed, not chosen, met with courteous, generous dignity. Phase 4/9 establish the outdoor-exposed labor minority (dock/highway workers) as Janbogo's single sharpest social division, one whose day cannot follow the "minimize threshold crossings" rhythm the rest of the city runs on — visible exposure that is occupational necessity, never chosen performance. Read through Leo's "dignity under exposure" register rather than its "wants the spotlight" register, this population is a genuine, non-forced candidate for involuntary witness. **Flagged for its own contradiction-check below (§4) — Phase 4's own word for this divide is "unremarked-on," which cuts directly against the "witnessed/admired" reading and produced a second, independently surviving finding.**

**Rejected within Step 1, with reason:** a Leo-shaped landmark building — rejected because Phase 3B's admissible first-impressions material states the opposite architectural signature directly (low-profile, wind-braced, non-context-refusing), a genuine contradiction rather than a silence. A Leo-shaped "hide economy" (status objects taken off defeated predecessors) — rejected, no established status-object economy exists in admissible material to hang it on; would be invented from nothing.
