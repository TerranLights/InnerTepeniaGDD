## STEP 1 — Base Libra prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Libra's own registered character — the
Balancer; a fair procedure that keeps a community from tearing itself apart; civility as load-bearing
structure; the core need to be the indispensable third party in someone else's arrangement; the structural
fact that Libra's ruling Sun is in fall ("no center of its own, no sovereign self, only the relation") —
what would show up at Janbogo, given Janbogo's own established profile?

**Finding kept — HIT 1: The Subnet Administration Seat.** Janbogo's own established G5 deficit
(`02_Phase1_Constraint_and_Capability.md`) is that it administers the Janbogo Subnet but does **not** hold
its own subnet's Arcanet relay nexus — that hardware sits in Concordia's Gemini district. Read through
Libra's own structural position (§10: "Sun in fall... the district has no center of its own, no sovereign
self, only the relation") this is not a generic bureaucratic footnote — it is Janbogo's own literal
instantiation of Libra's defining structural fact: an administrative authority that IS the relation
(coordination, protocol, subnet governance) while holding none of the network's actual material center.
Pushed to player-facing form: an enterable administrative hall/office (unnamed — proper-name reservation
per `05` §3) where subnet business is conducted, staffed by whoever actually does this coordination.
**Filter:** characteristically inevitable — grounded directly in Janbogo's own established G5 deficit, not
invented; not generic — the specific "hub without its own center" shape is Janbogo's own fact, independently
arrived at in Phase 1 before this Zodiac Lens pass began. **Category: place (+ implied administrator role).**

**Stopping reason:** kept, with the above grounding.

**Earth (element).** Positive: reliability, patience, "capacity to support and sustain others without
needing credit for it," stabilizing centeredness. Negative: stagnation, paralysis, stubbornness.

needing credit") is structurally anti-Libra: Libra's own core need (§5) is explicitly the opposite —
"standing," "to be the indispensable third party," "Libra needs to be needed." Janbogo's own established G2
STRENGTH quadrant (Phase 1) reads as textbook Earth-element logic: "several independent systems each capable
of covering for the others," no single point of failure, nobody specifically credited. If HIT 1's office is
staffed by people doing genuinely Libra-shaped relational labor, Janbogo's own Earth-quadrant civic idiom
(redundancy, no single point of failure, no individual indispensability) structurally denies them the
recognition their own psychology requires. Fresh relative to HIT 1 — HIT 1 is about structural position;
this is about the individual staffing it. **Category: person (role-archetype: a subnet protocol officer /
hub administrator).**

**Air (element).** Positive: "the invisible connective medium that carries a voice, a signal... without
either end needing to touch the other directly." Negative: "a connection so diffuse it commits to nothing...
carry a signal, [or] carry nothing at all, with no way to tell the difference from the outside."
**Stopping reason — reinforces HIT 1, no new independent finding kept.** Air is one of Janbogo's own two
assigned symbols (`00_Frame_and_PreFlight.md` §4), and Libra's own out-of-fiction structural element is
*also* Air (§10) — a genuine three-way convergence (Libra's element, Janbogo's assigned symbol, and HIT 1's
grounding all land on the same "connective medium with no confirmed delivery" idea). The content this

material confirming infrastructure sits in Concordia) restates HIT 1's own Sun-in-fall reading rather than
adding anything HIT 1 does not already cover. Not kept as a separate hit; folded into HIT 1's grounding.

**Fire.** Positive: passion, charisma, "the courage to act and actually be seen doing it." Negative:
recklessness, "an inability to ever simply rest," impatience.
**Finding kept — HIT 3: The Unreviewable Closure Order.** Fire's impulsive-action shadow prompted a check

M-65-excluded file) that *some* wind-warning/signaling function exists. Prediction: Janbogo's administrative
apparatus chronically under-responds to the diffuse ambient-heuristic hazard until a bad-enough incident
forces one sudden, sweeping, unreviewable closure/lockdown order rather than a negotiated, incremental
policy. **Category: thing/practice** (an institutional behavior pattern, catalogable, dramatically usable).

**Water.** Positive: wisdom, adaptability, "yield without breaking." Negative: fear, paranoia, "an inability

pass). Rejected on that basis, not because the thematic connection is implausible.

**Wood.** Positive: growth, vision, initiative, "bending in the wind without breaking," capacity "to plan
and pursue genuine new beginnings." Negative: rigidity disguised as flexibility, overreach.
**Finding kept — HIT 4: The Seam Allotment Office.** Wood's growth/initiative content, read alongside
Libra's own cardinal modality (§10: "initiating, goal-oriented... marking a seasonal hinge" — the correction

genuine housing-stock disparity. A Libra-shaped body whose specific portfolio is adjudicating competing

(institution/office).**

**Metal.** Positive: precision, "honoring what has genuinely ended," letting go gracefully. Negative:
rigidity, "grief that can't actually move on," melancholy.
**Stopping reason — no candidate generated.** Metal's defining correspondence is the Chinese Autumn/Grief

**Electricity.** Positive: "the single current that runs through and unifies an entire system end to end."

## STEP 3 — Within-sign combinatorial synthesis

Checked all pairs among HITs 1–8 for combinations neither hit implies alone.

**Finding kept — HIT 9: The Crisis Window** (HIT 3 × HIT 5). During and immediately after an Unreviewable
Closure Order (HIT 3), the administration is forced into abnormal, fast, unilateral action — the normal,
slower, harder-to-fake relational-verification process (HIT 1's own everyday mode) is suspended. This is
precisely the moment the Spoofed Seal vulnerability (HIT 5) is most exploitable: authority is most
concentrated and least verified exactly when scrutiny is lowest. Neither HIT 3 (a crisis-response mismatch)
nor HIT 5 (a standing structural vulnerability) implies this timing relationship alone. **Category:
thing/situation** (an exploitable window, catalogable as a smuggling/fraud opportunity).

**Finding kept — HIT 10: Necessary and Uncredited** (HIT 2 × HIT 7). HIT 2 established that Janbogo's
Earth-quadrant civic idiom structurally denies individual recognition to the people doing Libra-shaped
relational labor. HIT 7 independently established that this same labor is, in fact, genuinely functionally
necessary — the actual mechanism preventing Janbogo's no-majority population from Saturn's own dispersal
risk. Combined, this sharpens into a crueler, more specific irony than either hit alone: the work is real
and load-bearing, not merely redundant-and-replaceable, and it *still* goes uncredited, because Janbogo's
own civic vocabulary has no category for crediting indispensable individuals even when the function is
empirically indispensable. **Category: person** (role-archetype, sharpens HIT 2's description).

**Candidate considered and rejected** (HIT 4 × HIT 6): seam-zoning disputes (HIT 4) escalating under a
crisis-instrument mismatch (HIT 6) during, e.g., emergency post-storm housing reallocation. Rejected as a
near-duplicate in shape of HIT 9 — both describe Libra's dither-then-unilateral-swing failure mode colliding
with a standing structural condition under compressed timelines; keeping only one avoids the redundant-
near-duplicate-finding failure this project's own methodology flags elsewhere (cf. Phase 5's near-duplicate
risk concern, this same file set).

No further pairs produced fresh material — checked exhaustively across the remaining C(8,2) combinations not
listed above; each either restated an already-kept hit's grounding or required stacking un-grounded
assumptions in the same way the Water cross-check was rejected.
