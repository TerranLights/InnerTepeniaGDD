## Step 1 — Base Sagittarius prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Sagittarius's own registered character, what
would that be, given Janbogo's own established profile?

### BASE-1 — KEPT — a place: the Departure Yard

Sagittarius's core motive is "meaning obtained by going far enough away to get it" — "the journey itself,
rather than the goal, which is important," ninth-house domain of long-distance travel, and its own most
reliable light-expression trait is "field competence over credential" ("a pragmatic field knowledge that
undergirds thorough book training"). Read against Janbogo's own established STRENGTH quadrant (Phase 1,
G2/G5): the Terra Nova Bay polynya is the coast's only year-round ice-free access point, and Hwy 183's
Janbogo spur is "the most heavily used connector" into Concordia among the highway network, with genuine
multi-modal throughput (sea + road + subnet administration) simultaneously. Janbogo's own civic function is
therefore structurally an onward-passage point, not a destination in itself — exactly the register
Sagittarius's core motive describes.

**Filter:** characteristically inevitable — not generic. This isn't "any port city would have a dock"; it's
specifically grounded in Janbogo's own uniquely-established year-round access plus its documented status as
the Federation's busiest connector, which no other admissible Ross Sea city shares at this exact combination
(Phase 5a: Zukelli shares the climate but not this specific G5 profile).

**Player-facing form (kept):** a place — an expedition-outfitting/staging yard where crews, cargo captains,
and travelers headed further along Hwy 183 or the coast provision, hire field-experienced guides/pilots
(not credentialed administrators — matching Sagittarius's "field competence over credential"), and get
cleared to go. Entered, handled.

### BASE-2 — KEPT — a thing/civic memory: the disputed hazard-dismissal disaster

Sagittarius's specific, well-documented shadow is "boredom as negligence" — Design Hook #2, "The Mere Fog":
"the district's civic shame is not a defeat but a dismissal: a slow environmental hazard that leadership
called trivial because it was boring, and that then killed people... Everyone knows the name; nobody agrees
who said it." This maps with unusual precision onto Janbogo's own established G2 deficit (Phase 1): "the
practical distinction between 'blizzard' and 'high-wind clear conditions' collapses," and "trusting one's
own senses about outdoor conditions is permitted... and punished unpredictably." Sagittarius's "immunity
delusion" ("seldom feels it's in mortal danger — after all, how could something that's so much fun be
perilous?") is the exact psychological shape of a resident who checks the sky, sees nothing alarming, and
steps out.

**Filter:** characteristically inevitable — this is arguably the single tightest fit in this entire pass:
Janbogo's own best-grounded, most-textured established finding (the collapsed ambient-temperature heuristic)
and Sagittarius's own most specific, named shadow pathology (the Mere Fog) describe the identical mechanism
from two independent directions.

**Player-facing form (kept):** a thing/event — a specific, still-disputed katabatic disaster remembered
civic-wide as the moment a hazard was publicly dismissed as ordinary before it killed people, with no public
consensus on who is to blame. Spoken of, heard. (Left unnamed and not tied to any specific memorial
structure, respecting Phase 6C's own REQUESTED status for a memorial/record institution and this run's
proper-name-reservation discipline.)
