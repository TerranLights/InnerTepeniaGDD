## STEP 1 — Base Aries prompt

**Prompt:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Aries's own registered character, what would it be?

Aries's core is the fused laborer/warrior (the Babylonian substitution — the oldest layer of this archetype
is a worker, the ram is a later overlay; hero-image and labor-image are the same icon, §1/§14.1), cardinal
fire's "gotta get moving, now" urgency (§10), crisis competence expressed in short, violent, effective bursts
(§2), and no inherited wisdom / no self-reflection (§5, §1).

Janbogo's Phase 4 already independently produced its **sharpest finding of that phase**: the outdoor-exposed
labor minority (port/dock, Hwy 183 maintenance) cannot follow the general population's "minimize threshold
crossings" rhythm — their day is instead "organized entirely around wind-exposure windows, working when
conditions allow, regardless of clock time" (Phase 4 differentiation axis, verbatim). This is, independently
of Aries, already a cardinal-fire-shaped labor pattern: work happens in short, urgent, all-or-nothing bursts
the instant a window opens, not on a schedule — precisely Aries's "gotta get moving, now."

**Result, KEPT — H1 (person).** *The wind-window foreman/crew* — the outdoor-exposed labor population's own
leading figure, who calls and leads the loading/maintenance run the instant a break in the wind opens, works
at a dead sprint for its duration, and stops the instant it closes. Player-facing form: seen/heard barking a
go-order the moment a wind gauge or a spotter signals a lull; a role/archetype, not an invented name.

**Filter.** Characteristically inevitable, not generic: it is a direct synthesis of Phase 4's own strongest,
independently-derived finding with Aries's own registered laborer-warrior/cardinal-fire core, not imported
from outside Janbogo's established profile.

**Stopping reason (Step 5, prompt 1 of 19):** kept — see H1. Contradiction check run at Step 4 below.

## STEP 3 — Within-sign combinatorial synthesis

**H1 × H3.** Both are "committed, single-direction burst action within a narrow hazard window," in different
domains (land cargo vs. sea piloting). Aries's own Design Hook #10 (the battering ram: "a single trunk,
rounded end, carved ram's head, curling horns, hung on heavy ropes... a district monument, a crew crest, or a

structure — but rather than importing the ram wholesale, the combination is legitimate here because H1 and H3

**Result, KEPT — H12 (thing/practice).** *A shared "commitment" ritual-object or practice across Janbogo's
hazard-window trades* — something recognized by both dock crews and ice-edge pilots alike, marking the
instant of full commitment (the loading run begins; the ice gap is taken), echoing Aries's own guild-object
hook but derived independently from two of Janbogo's own established hazard trades rather than imported from
myth.

**H4 × H5 × H6.** H4 is the blunt written registry. H5 is the ignorant/overconfident victim-type. H6 is the
principled-defiance counterculture. From outside, and in the registry itself, these two very different kinds
of death — a person who simply misjudged the wind, and a person who defied it on principle — read identically
("went out against the wind-window, died of exposure"). The registry's own uncompromising honesty (Metal,
H4's basis) cannot actually recover *motive*, only outcome.

**Result, KEPT — H13 (civic-epistemic fact).** *The registry's permanent fool/martyr ambiguity* — however
honest and blunt, the accident-registry can never actually settle whether a given name belonged to H5's
overconfidence or H6's principled refusal, and Janbogo's civic memory has to live with that ambiguity
permanently. Neither H4, H5, nor H6 alone implies this; it only appears once all three are held together.

**No further combinatorial pairs kept.** H9 (structural accountability gap) and H11 (principled non-
participation in the Saints framework) share a loose family resemblance — both are about Janbogo lacking or
declining external validation from a framework above it — but on inspection this is a shared *theme* across
two already-independent findings, not new content; noted here rather than minted as a separate hit, to avoid
padding.
