## B. Arrival-mode taxonomy (`03` Phase 2)

**Founding generation (2564, shortly after the Falkland Treaty):** a mix of two modes, not one. **Fled to
it** — the founding population is explicitly exile-origin, per standing project-wide canon (the Falkland
Treaty exile mechanism, rank-1/2 canon, not a Janbogo-specific conclusion) — robots from Korean society and
their human partners/supporters arrived because the alternative (remaining under the prior order) had
already failed them, not by ordinary free choice of destination. **Inherited it**, in the narrower physical
sense — the founders inherited the real Jang Bogo Station's existing built infrastructure (Phase 1, G4)
rather than an empty site, which is a distinct fact from *why* they were there at all.

**Across the pre-war Second Interwar Period (the ~250 years this pass's frame actually covers):** the
G2/G5 profile from Phase 1 — a genuine, functioning port with unique year-round maritime access, the
Federation's most heavily used highway connector, and a subnet-hub administrative role — plausibly
generates two further, later modes:

- **Chose it.** A functioning trade node ("goods moving between the Ross Sea cities and the inland stations
  passed through or alongside Janbogo," admissible Economy & Industry material) is exactly the kind of place
  that draws voluntary economic migration over two and a half centuries, independent of any founding-nation
  identity.
- **Stopped while passing.** Heavy, genuine through-traffic (year-round shipping, the Federation's busiest
  highway connector) is the precise condition `03` Phase 2 names as producing this mode — people who meant
  to continue and did not. **This is a testable candidate, not asserted as certain**: Census I→II retention
  is 73.05% (26.95% departed via the standard project-wide orbital-emigration pattern, not war loss) — a
  population that stays at roughly three-quarters is consistent with, though not proof of, a real
  "stopped while passing" population mixed among genuine choosers.

**Assigned/posted** is a weaker candidate here than at an Installation-type location — nothing in admissible
material suggests Janbogo received population via institutional assignment the way a purely functional
station might; its Settlement-type character (people building a life, not rotating staff) argues against it
as anything beyond a minor thread.

**The mix is the finding, per `03`'s own governing rule.** Janbogo is not one arrival story — it is a
founding population that fled here and inherited a mismatched physical shell (Phase 1's own G4 finding),
overlaid by two and a half centuries of people who came because the port/highway function genuinely worked,
and some genuine fraction who simply never finished leaving.

## C. Native layer vs. transplanted layer, kept explicit

**The native/founding layer**: South Korea, 10.23% by Census II — the founding operator nation, but (per
Phase 1's G8 finding) not the demographic majority.

**The transplanted/grown layer**: China (20.63%) and USA (18.39%) together hold 39.02%, nearly four times
the founding nation's own share, having arrived and grown across the pre-war centuries via the "chose it" /
"stopped while passing" modes above — **this is read directly from the raw tier table, not from the
excluded conclusion prose that made a similar-shaped claim** (see Phase 1's own provenance caveat, M-66, for
why this distinction is being kept unusually explicit in this run).

**What this composition rules out, stated per `03` Phase 2 step C's own warning**: a culture whose only
content is "the founding nation's traditions plus the arrived nations' traditions, side by side" would be
exactly the failure this step exists to prevent — "a place whose only culture is its immigrants' cultures
has no culture." **Not resolved here** — Phase 8 (Making) is where native-vs-transplanted material actually
gets written; this phase only establishes that the layers are real and distinct, and flags the trap for
later.

## E. Who is not here

**No Upper Earth-origin population, ordinary immigration.** Per standing, project-wide, non-location-specific
canon (the post-Split-Brain exile mechanism), Tepenia's population is exile-descended; nobody arrives from
Upper Earth by ordinary voluntary immigration in this era. This is true of every Tepenian city and is not a
Janbogo-specific finding — recorded per `03` Phase 2 step E's instruction to ask the question explicitly,
not because the answer is surprising.

**No stated non-Primary/Significant/Notable-tier population at all** — the census table's own three tiers
plus "Notable" appear to be exhaustive of Janbogo's admissible national composition; no absence beyond the
general exile-population fact is currently visible in admissible material. **Left open rather than forced**:
a genuinely interesting "who is not here" finding may exist but is not visible from the census table alone;
flagged as a candidate to revisit once Phase 5 (Relation) establishes what Janbogo's neighbors and network
partners look like, since an absence is often sharpest by contrast with an established peer.

## Differentiation axis

**Named per `03` Phase 2's own requirement**: Janbogo's mode-mix is **"exile-founded-on-inherited-shell,
overtaken by voluntary throughput-migration."** Distinct in kind from a purely exile-founded city with no
subsequent voluntary layer, and distinct from a purely functional/assigned population — the combination of
a founding exile population that lost demographic primacy to genuine economic choosers is the specific
shape. **Not yet checked against any sibling's own mode-mix** (no completed sibling culture pass is
admissible input for this run) — flagged for Gate 6 at Step 7, when Cape Adare's completed material (Run 7,
same methodology) becomes available for comparison.
