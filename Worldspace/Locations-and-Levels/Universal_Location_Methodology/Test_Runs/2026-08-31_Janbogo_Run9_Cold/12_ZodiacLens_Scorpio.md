**Scope.** One sign's worth of the Zodiac Lens technique family (`03` Phase 10 Step B2), run as its own
dedicated file per `RESUME_HERE.md` §5 step 7a. Input: Janbogo's admissible established profile only
(`00_Frame_and_PreFlight.md` through `11_Phase10_Catalog_Base.md`, `01_Ordering_Collision_Log.md` excluded
per instruction) — the withheld `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Janbogo.md` and all M-64/M-65-excluded
material are NOT used here either, consistent with the base run. Scorpio's own character is read only from
`Concordia-City/Districts/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/08_Scorpio.md` §1–§13 (character) — §14 ("Design Hooks
for District Use") is read as evidence of how this sign's traits tend to institutionalize in general, not
copied as Concordia's District 4 content; no completed Concordia-Scorpio-district culture-pass file was
opened. Robot Elementals and Planetary Symbols read from their own registered files, in full.

**Method note on register.** Janbogo's own established profile is thin on institutional/cultural specifics
(M-64/M-65 exclusions) and rich on physical/structural/demographic fact (G2/G4/G5/G8, the seam, the
exposed-labor divide). Most of what follows is therefore extrapolated FROM those structural facts using
Scorpio's own registered psychology as the interrogating lens — flagged as extrapolation throughout, never
asserted as if it were already-admissible canon.

## Step 1 — Base Scorpio prompt

**Prompt:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Scorpio's own registered character, what would
that be, given Janbogo's own established profile?

**Reading.** Scorpio's own root image (`08_Scorpio.md` §10: fixed water = ice, "the hardest, least fluid
form of water... the mass below the waterline undetectable... where what you don't see can be more dangerous
than what you do") lands almost exactly on Janbogo's own established G2 hazard profile: a coastline that
*reads* as merely cold and calm while katabatic spindrift collapses "the practical distinction between
'blizzard' and 'high-wind clear conditions'" (`02` G2), so that trusting one's own senses is "permitted...
and punished unpredictably" (Phase 1, Grudging Tolerance) with **no correlation to how careful someone was
being**. This is Scorpio's own defining epistemic condition (danger that doesn't announce itself, doesn't
correlate with virtue) made literal at Janbogo, not imported from outside it.

Scorpio's own light expression supplies the response: crisis competence, diagnostic/brutal honesty ("if you
don't want to know the truth, don't ask a Scorpio"), and control-as-safety. Phase 6C already hypothesizes,
independently, that Janbogo's dead are not conventionally buried (frozen/rocky ground) and that a formal
**record** may carry more civic weight than a grave — a "record over rite" reading Scorpio's own material
directly reinforces (§10's Detriment-Venus/Fall-Moon finding: this archetype produces comfort in abundance
but cannot export it past a trusted boundary, and what it exports instead is **structure**, not warmth — a
registry is structure, not warmth).

**HIT 1 — The Hazard Registrar.** A civic role/office, low-visibility rather than ceremonial (Scorpio's own
"authority without visibility... pulling strings behind the scenes, because that is where true power lies"),
that does two fused things: issues Janbogo's unsentimental, non-negotiable go/no-go verdicts on outdoor
conditions (the closest thing to control over an otherwise senses-defeating hazard), and keeps the plain,
unflattering registry naming exactly how and why each collapsed-heuristic death happened — not a grave, a
record. Category: person (role) + place/thing (the registry itself, an enterable records office). Player-
facing: an NPC whose verdict overrides personal judgment before any outdoor excursion; an enterable ledger-
room the player can consult or need something from.

**Filter check.** Characteristically inevitable, not generic: it is built from Janbogo's own named G2
Grudging Tolerance (unpredictable, un-correlated-with-care punishment) and Phase 6C's own hypothesis (record
over grave), not from a generic "weather station" idea. Pushed to player-facing form per the base-prompt
instruction (seen/entered/spoken to/hooked).

## Step 2 — Eighteen cross-checks

Each cross-check reads Scorpio's registered character together with one Robot Elemental or Planetary Symbol's
own registered meaning, asking whether something fresh and characteristically consistent appears at Janbogo
that neither Step 1 nor an earlier item in this list already produced. Robot Elementals first (8), then
Planetary Symbols (10).

### 2.1 — Earth (elemental)

Earth-elemental's negative pole (stagnation, hoarding, paralysis, an inability to change when change is
needed) reads directly onto Phase 1's own G4 Grudging Tolerance: "repurposing or building over the original
station footprint informally, without a plan reconciling old and new construction, is permitted... and
punished by structural incoherence" — an ungoverned, purely additive pattern with no mechanism for actually
removing what has decayed. Scorpio's own elimination function (purging what has "become poisonous... so the
system as a whole can be purged, cleansed and healed") is the missing half of that picture: not building-
over, but deliberate, sanctioned condemnation.

**HIT 2 — The Core Clearance Officer.** A role authorized to formally condemn and order removal of decayed,
structurally incoherent sections within the founding-era core (Phase 3D's own seam), converting Janbogo's
established "build-over-and-hope" ungoverned pattern into Scorpio's deliberate-elimination-for-the-health-of-
the-whole logic. Category: person/role. Player-facing: a red condemnation notice on a rime-cracked wall
inside the core; an NPC who can be petitioned, feared, or bribed; an enterable section under active
demolition order.

### 2.2 — Air (elemental)

Air's own negative pole ("a connection so diffuse it commits to nothing... the same invisibility that lets
Air carry a signal also lets it carry nothing at all, with no way to tell the difference from the outside")
restates the same G2 finding already captured in Hit 1 (the wind's own unreliable signal) — noted, not
re-kept, to avoid duplication. A second, more productive fusion: Air's positive pole ("the atmosphere that
quietly makes every other kind of contact between two separated things possible") read against Phase 5d's
established arrival-mode mix (a genuine "stopped while passing" population, Phase 2) and Scorpio's own
Eighth-House material (§10: "resources, finances, karma," "manages money more effectively for others than
for self," an office administering what is owed after an ending).

**HIT 3 — The Waybond / Unclaimed-Freight Keeper.** An office holding bonded custody of cargo, effects, and
claims belonging to ships, travelers, and shipments that never finished their journey — the material residue
of Phase 2's own "stopped while passing" arrival mode — administering property that is never Janbogo's own,
the way Scorpio's Eighth House administers others' estates, trusted because its own claim on the goods is
nil. Category: place/institution (an enterable warehouse-office of unclaimed manifests) with an implied role.

### 2.3 — Fire (elemental)

Fire's positive pole is explicitly about being seen acting (passion, charisma, "the courage to act and
actually be seen doing it") — the near-opposite of Scorpio's own "not a fan of the limelight... pulling
strings behind the scenes" (the same axis as Scorpio's own canonical Leo square). **No candidate.** Janbogo's
admissible material contains no established Fire-adjacent finding — no visible-passion culture, no
charisma-authority figure, no festival of display — for Scorpio's visibility-avoidance to interact with.
Forcing one would require inventing a "display culture" the admissible profile does not supply. Null, with
stated reason.

### 2.4 — Water (elemental — the in-fiction robot element, kept separate from Scorpio's own out-of-fiction
classical "Water" structural position per `08_Scorpio.md` §10's own instruction)

Water-elemental's negative pole (fear, paranoia, isolation, secretiveness, being "swept along by
circumstance rather than navigating it") overlaps heavily with Scorpio's own shadow (§3: "paranoia and
rat-smelling... sometimes accurately, sometimes not, and it cannot tell which from the inside"). Read against
G2's own Grudging Tolerance (the *official* heuristic for danger is unreliable, punishment doesn't correlate
with care) this predicts exactly the soil Scorpio's own tribe-secrecy grows in: people who no longer fully
trust the official channel developing their own private, guarded alternative.

**HIT 4 — Private Weather-Reading Folk Practice.** An informal, secretive body of hazard-reading knowledge —
unofficial signs, private records of near-misses, warnings passed only within a small trusted circle — held
in quiet parallel to (not necessarily hostile to) the official forecast/warning system, directly mirroring
Scorpio's own admission-to-tribe secrecy ("if you're not part of the tribe, you won't even know it exists").
Category: practice, carried by a person/small circle. Player-facing: an NPC or small circle the player can
be let into after earning trust, offering hazard information the official system doesn't have.

### 2.5 — Wood (elemental)

Wood's positive pole (flexibility toward a fixed goal, "bending in the wind without breaking") strongly
echoes Scorpio's own "method flexibility in service of a fixed objective" (§2: "you find a score of ways to
get your goal"). A candidate was generated: a faction within Janbogo's subnet administration whose real
method is Scorpio's own manufactured-crisis playbook (§3), deliberately picking at friction points — small
administrative snags, withheld cooperation — to force Federation/Concordia attention onto G5's own named,
real deficit (Janbogo administers the Janbogo Subnet but does not hold its own Arcanet relay nexus, sited in
Concordia; Phase 1 names this deficit's address as "in the parent," "a shadow with no author").

**Generated and rejected.** Uses a genuine, specific Janbogo fact (G5's nexus deficit), but the actual
mechanism — a faction manufacturing crises for infrastructural leverage — is not anchored in any other
established Janbogo texture (no faction, agitator, or political actor of any kind is named anywhere in
Phases 0–10) and reads as a generic political trope fitted onto a structural fact rather than something the
profile makes inevitable. Also risks duplicating Hit 2's "elimination applied to an ungoverned deficit" shape
without adding real specificity. Rejected.

### 2.6 — Metal (elemental)

Metal's positive pole ("the capacity to let go gracefully... honoring what has genuinely ended... cutting
away what's no longer necessary") is close to a restatement of Scorpio's own diagnostic honesty and

**HIT 5 — The Closed-File Convention.** Janbogo's civic record-keeping treats emigration (orbital departure)
with the same unsentimental, procedural finality it applies to death (Hit 1): a resident's file is formally
**closed**, not left open-ended, no institutional language of "expected to return." Category: thing/practice
(a bureaucratic convention), player-facing via a records clerk/office or a "closed" stamp/ledger entry a
player might encounter while trying to trace a departed person.

### 2.7 — Electricity

Electricity's negative pole (surge and blackout as "two faces of the same underlying fragility," total
dependency on a removable power source) has no admissible Janbogo grounding — no admissible material
establishes anything about Janbogo's power/electrical infrastructure specifically. A softer resonance
(Electricity's "rapid, decisive transformation" against Phase 4's own wind-exposure-window labor pattern) was
considered and found to only re-describe an already-established finding (Phase 4's "organized around
wind-exposure windows, not clock time") without adding anything new. **No candidate**, stated reason: no
admissible infrastructure to fuse with, and the abstract resonance duplicates existing Phase 4 content.

### 2.8 — Electromagnetism

Electromagnetism's negative pole ("dependency on an external field rather than one's own internal
compass"... "a signal is also a vulnerability — anything reachable by an invisible field can be jammed,
intercepted, or spoofed") maps almost verbatim onto G5's own established deficit: Janbogo's own subnet

**No new candidate.** This produces the same SHAPE as Hit 4 (a secretive, trusted-circle-only alternative
information channel) — grounded in a different established deficit (network vulnerability rather than
weather-heuristic failure), but structurally identical. Treated as extending Hit 4's scope (from weather-only

### 2.9 — Mercury (planetary)

Mercury's own registered profile — "endurance at the edge... a rhythm entirely its own, dictated by proximity
to something overwhelmingly larger, but never fully surrendered to it"; negative: "no middle ground, no

relaxation," and §10's structural finding that this archetype's civic export is **structure**, not comfort).

**HIT 6 — Mandatory Structured Rest Protocol.** A rigid, non-negotiable, non-optional schedule of enforced
downtime for exposed labor (dock/highway/port work) — imposed as compliance, not offered as care, because a
population that (per the corpus's own logic) would otherwise push itself until collapse needs an imposed
structure rather than an invitation to relax, and experiences it as discipline rather than kindness even
though it is functionally protective. Category: thing/practice with an implied enforcing role. Player-facing:
a supervisor NPC enforcing mandatory shelter rotations; a rule the player (if working the docks) must obey or
sees enforced on others, resented as "babying" despite being severe.

### 2.10 — Venus (planetary)

Venus's negative pole ("beauty from a distance concealing something genuinely hostile up close") duplicates

### 2.11 — Earth (planetary — Janbogo's own assigned symbol per `City_Symbol_Assignments.md`)

for the Earth/Air symbol pairing. Category: person/role. Player-facing: an inspector NPC; a questline built
around a specific weak point they've found (G5's Concordia-hosted nexus, or G4's undersized core, would both
supply real teeth).

## Step 3 — Within-sign combinatorial synthesis

Looking at Hits 1–12 together for pairs that combine into something neither implied alone:

**Combo A — The Closure Registry (Hit 1 + Hit 5).** Both hits independently produced the same underlying
administrative logic (unsentimental, structural, final record-closing) applied to two different populations
(the dead, by hazard, per Hit 1; the departed, by emigration, per Hit 5) that neither hit named as connected
on its own. Combined, they suggest a single unified civic institution — a Closure Registry — that
administers BOTH forms of "a person's file being closed" under one roof and one procedural culture, rather
than two separate offices that happen to behave similarly. This is new content: neither Hit 1 nor Hit 5 alone
asserted that these are literally the same office.

**Combo B — An integrated oral tradition (Hit 4 + Hit 10).** Both hits are small-trusted-circle,
insider-only cultural phenomena — Hit 4 carries practical hazard-survival knowledge, Hit 10 carries
affectionate/aesthetic appreciation for the patchwork core. Combined, they suggest Janbogo's small trusted
circles plausibly pass down a single blended oral heritage — elders' stories mixing "how to read the wind"
with "why we love this ugly old building" — rather than two separate, unrelated bodies of private knowledge.
This is new content (a specific cultural FORM, oral and blended) that neither hit implied alone, and it is a
candidate answer to Phase 8's own REQUESTED "language and speech markers" gap.

**Connective note, not a separate finding (Hit 3 + Hit 8 + Hit 11).** The Waybond Keeper's function
(administering unclaimed things that "stopped while passing" and stayed) thematically echoes the city's own
founding story (Hit 11 — inherited infrastructure never meant to hold a city, which stayed and became vast
anyway). A natural siting detail follows: the Waybond office plausibly sits within a reclaimed section of the
founding-era core (Hit 8), and locals may read its function as a living echo of Janbogo's own founding story.
Recorded as connective texture, not run through Step 4 separately, since it does not introduce a new
checkable claim beyond re-siting two already-kept results.

## Step 4 — Per-hit contradiction checks

For every kept result from Steps 1–3, one deliberate opposite-register candidate, tested against the same
"characteristically consistent" filter.

**Hit 1 (formal/solemn/official) → informal/unserious.** Scorpio's own same-sign-massing material states
outright that dark humor is "public infrastructure rather than personal style" for this archetype when
concentrated. Candidate: a gallows-humor tradition around katabatic near-misses and notorious wind-death
spots — informally, communally told, shocking to outsiders, normal insider coping. **Survives the filter,
kept as an independent second finding** (Hit 1c).

**Hit 2 (formal/official, visible authority) → informal/communal.** Candidate: consensus-driven, grassroots
core-abandonment (residents collectively deciding a section is "dead," no formal office). **Generated and
rejected** — this duplicates the pre-existing baseline (Phase 1's own Grudging Tolerance: informal build-over
IS the ungoverned status quo). Hit 2 already IS the opposite-register response to that baseline; testing back
to it returns to the starting condition rather than producing anything new.

**Hit 3 (formal/institutional/visible office) → informal/concealed.** Candidate: a quiet, tolerated,
unofficial "finders-keepers" salvage norm for genuinely abandoned freight, running ahead of the formal
office's own claim process. Grounded in the same general Janbogo tendency toward informal appropriation that
Phase 1's G4 Grudging Tolerance already establishes for the built environment. **Survives, kept** (Hit 3c),
moderate strength — more expected/generic than most other kept results, flagged as such.

**Hit 4 (informal/private/small-circle) → formal/public.** The natural formal counterpart to this hit already
exists, independently, as Hit 1 (the official Hazard Registrar). **No fresh candidate generated** — would
simply duplicate an already-kept result.

**Hit 5 (formal/administrative/solemn) → informal/private.** Candidate: a private, small-circle, informal
farewell/mourning custom for someone within one's own trusted group, incompatible with and separate from the
impersonal official closure — directly sourced from Scorpio's own comfort-rationed-and-terminated-
deliberately material (§7/§10). **Survives, kept** (Hit 5c).

**Hit 6 (formal/disciplinary/institutional) → informal/leisure.** Candidate: exposed workers informally
repurpose the enforced rest windows into genuine social/leisure time, subverting the protocol's intended
joylessness. **Survives, kept** (Hit 6c) — flagged as the weakest/most expected of the kept contra-findings,
since "workers make the best of enforced downtime" is a fairly natural countertendency in any comparable
setting, not sharply Janbogo-specific.

**Hit 7 (formal/official, visible role) → informal/concealed.** Candidate: an informal rumor/whisper culture
about which of Janbogo's systems are "really" fragile, sometimes agreeing with and sometimes contradicting
the official auditor — directly sourced from Scorpio's own paranoia/rat-smelling shadow (§3). **Survives,
