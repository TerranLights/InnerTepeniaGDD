## 5a — Peers

**Zukelli, ~8km away, sharing Terra Nova Bay and its polynya.** This is a genuine, close, structurally
matched peer relationship, not a loose regional one: both cities' founders inherited existing real-world
station infrastructure (Jang Bogo Station / Mario Zucchelli Station) rather than building from nothing; both
sit on identical climate (Zukelli's own Specs states its climate is "not meaningfully distinguishable from
Janbogo's"); both are reached via "a connecting road off Hwy 183," never a direct main-line stop; and their
Census II populations are nearly identical — **Janbogo 957,570 vs. Zukelli 937,659, within 2.1% of each
other.**

**What flows, and what does not**: shared physical infrastructure (the same port waters, the same highway
spur pattern) — but no admissible material establishes actual trade/goods flow *between* the two cities
specifically, as opposed to each independently connecting to the wider network. **REQUESTED.**

**⚠ A genuine Gate-6-relevant risk, flagged now rather than only at Step 7.** Two cities this closely matched
on G2 and G5 are exactly the configuration where near-duplicate findings are likeliest (`04` Gate 6's own
concern). **Differentiation has to come from elsewhere** — and G4/G8 already supply it: Janbogo's founding
nation (South Korea) sits at 10.23% of its own population, first in the Significant tier; **Zukelli's
founding nation (Italy) sits at 6.24%, fourth in its own Significant tier** — a real, arithmetic difference
(both raw census tables, admissible, no interpretation required) showing Zukelli's founding-nation dilution
is considerably *more* pronounced than Janbogo's own already-notable dilution (Phase 1, G8). **Two
structurally near-identical port-neighbor cities, differentiated by degree on the same axis rather than by
a different axis entirely** — worth stating plainly as its own finding, since "both cities show the same
underlying pattern, just at different magnitudes" is itself a real, specific, non-generic relationship
between them, distinct from the generic "they're neighbors" framing either city's excluded Character section
would have supplied instead.

**The quiet/loud friction distinction** (`03` Phase 5a): no admissible material yet distinguishes a loud
friction from a quiet one between the two cities — left open, REQUESTED.

## 5d — The edge and the crossing

**Arrival types, enumerated** (drawing on Phase 2's own arrival-mode work): **by sea**, via the polynya —
Janbogo's structurally strongest arrival channel (G2/G5's own STRENGTH quadrant), available year-round unlike
most of the Ross Sea coast. **By land**, via the Hwy 183 spur — structurally secondary (G5's own DEFICIT:
a connecting road, not the main line).

**What converts a visitor into a member — a mechanism, not a ceremony, per `03` Phase 5d's own preference.**
Given the port/trade economy (Economy & Industry, admissible) and Phase 2's "chose it" arrival mode as a
genuine, substantial population layer, the most economy-derived candidate is **sustained participation in
the port/supply economy itself** — continuing to work cargo, logistics, or subnet administration past
whatever the initial arrival purpose was. **This is directly parallel to `Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md`
Technique 10 (Membership by Unremarked Persistence)'s own "continuous labor needs → a work term, you're a
local once you're still there after it" pattern** — named here as a candidate rather than asserted as
settled, since a genuine run of that technique belongs at Phase 8 (Making) where the rest of the toolkit
gets applied.
