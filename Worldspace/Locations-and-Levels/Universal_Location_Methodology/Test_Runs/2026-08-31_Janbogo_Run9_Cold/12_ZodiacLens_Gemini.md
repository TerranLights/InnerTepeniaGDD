## STEP 1 — Base Gemini prompt

**Question:** if Janbogo had to produce something embodying Gemini's own registered character — transmission
over custody (Mercury the messenger, not the scribe), stimulation via connection rather than truth, mediation
without taking sides, amoral non-ranking, mutable relocation rather than defense of a position, "nothing said
is binding and everyone locally knows it," the core need for novelty plus freedom of mobility, and the
Jupiter-in-detriment gap (no authority to say *this matters more than that*) — what would it be, given
Janbogo's own established profile?

**Reasoning.** The single sharpest point of contact is Phase 1's own G5 finding: *"Janbogo administers the
Janbogo Subnet but does not hold its own subnet's Arcanet relay nexus... the hub does not hold the thing that
makes it a hub in the network-topology sense. Whatever 'hub' means for Janbogo, it is not 'holds the
network's own central point.'"* This is structurally identical to Gemini's own registered self-description:
messenger, not scribe; a district that can retrieve and transmit anything and rank nothing (§10, Detriment —
Jupiter). Layered onto this: Janbogo's G5 STRENGTH is genuine multi-modal throughput (sea, road, admin) — the
physical form of Gemini's own "cross-fertilization... the value produced is adjacency" (§2). A second,
independent point of contact: Phase 2's arrival-mode taxonomy names **"stopped while passing"** as a real,
grounded population mode (heavy through-traffic producing people who meant to continue and did not,
consistent with a 73.05% retention rate) — this is close to a direct restatement of Gemini's own "aimless
transit is normal behavior here, not a lapse" (§4) and "nothing said is binding... a statement is a readout
of the current state, not a promise about a future one" (§4) applied to a person's own stated travel plans,
not just their small talk.

**Result F1 [Place].** A dispatch/relay point at Janbogo's port-and-highway junction — where cargo manifests,
wind-condition bulletins, and traveler routings are logged and passed onward, but not archived, prioritized,
or ranked, because (per G5's own established deficit) the actual decision-making anchor for the subnet sits
elsewhere. Player-facing form: an enterable checkpoint/office the player passes through on arrival, staffed
by people who process a constant stream of information without authority to hold or rank any of it.

**Result F2 [Person].** A role-archetype: a longtime Janbogo resident who still describes themselves as "just
passing through," decades on — grounded directly in Phase 2's admissible "stopped while passing" arrival
mode, not invented from nothing. Player-facing form: a hookable NPC whose own stated plans ("I'll be moving
on once...") are locally understood as a readout of current disposition, not a commitment — exactly Gemini's
own registered convention (§4) applied to a real, already-admissible Janbogo population layer.

**Filter applied to both:** characteristically inevitable, not generic — both derive from Janbogo's own
established deficits/findings (G5's hub-without-nexus; Phase 2's arrival-mode taxonomy), not from a
generic "messenger" or "drifter" trope imported from outside the admissible material.

**Step 5 stopping reason (base prompt).** Two genuinely fresh, differently-categorized candidates (place,
person) were kept. Further base-prompt candidates were considered and rejected: (a) the compounded
dark-season/downtime concentration (Phase 3E/4/8) was considered as a Gemini "restlessness confined indoors"
candidate but rejected as duplicating Phase 4/8's own already-stated findings rather than adding anything
Gemini-specific; (b) a "Janbogo speaks in many languages" candidate was considered from G8's plural
composition but rejected as not yet distinctively Gemini-shaped at the base-prompt level — the genuinely
Gemini-flavored version of G8's plurality only emerges later, at the Earth-planet cross-check (F7). Stopped
at F1/F2 because no further base-level candidate cleared the "not already covered by an existing Phase
finding" bar.

## STEP 4 — Per-hit contradiction check

Run once for every result kept from Steps 1–3 (F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9, F10 — 10 items; F4 and F6

**F6 (outsiders over-trust assurances — outsider-disadvantaged register).** Opposite candidate: the reverse

**F7 (cross-national circulation market — communal/public register).** Opposite candidate: a private/
solitary-register counterpart — quiet, one-on-one barter or favor networks outside the public market.
**Rejected.** Gemini's own file is explicit that its register is public and circulatory, not private or
domestic: "at home... loses interest in domestic tasks... home is where the sign loses... life is in its

**F8 (unlicensed-veneration keeper — solemn/private register).** Opposite candidate: a light, public, unserious

registers of the same thing side by side without contradiction ("even enjoys the juxtaposition of shadow and

**F10 (generalized outsider calibration disadvantage — "outsiders suffer" register).** Opposite candidate: a
deliberate, exploitative counterpart — someone who uses the "nothing said is binding" local convention as

have slipped out of malice" (§3). A deliberately exploitative version directly contradicts Gemini's own
registered, textually-sourced character rather than offering a second legitimate register of it.
