## Step 1 — Base Taurus prompt

**Question:** If Janbogo had to produce something embodying Taurus's own registered character — the
consolidator, durable possession of something real, "I have," finishing what others abandon, the
first-impression lock, the Midas block (preservation that kills the thing preserved), fences-before-laws —
what would it be, given Janbogo's own established profile?

**Finding, T-1 — "The Retained Core."** Phase 3D established a concrete, physical seam: the real founding
footprint (16 buildings, ~4,000 m², three wings — Phase 1's own researched G4 finding) is a literal, boundable
core, with everything built beyond it across two and a half centuries of growth being definitionally later
construction. Phase 1's own G4 STANDING COST names this as a burden with "no natural end point" — the gap
between what the original structure holds and what the city needs only ever widens. Taurus's registered
character supplies the missing *why* this core still stands at all rather than having been demolished and
efficiently replaced: Taurus's whole drive is durable possession of something real, and its Midas-block shadow
(Design Hook #5 — "a home, workshop, or relationship preserved so completely that the person it was preserved
for cannot live in it... the district reads this as devotion and cannot be argued out of that reading") is
precisely the mechanism that would keep a 250-year-old, undersized, structurally awkward core standing and
load-bearing to the city's self-image, at real ongoing cost, simply because it is genuinely, originally
*theirs* in a way nothing built since can be.

**Category:** place. **Player-facing form:** enterable — a locatable, physically distinct original core,
recognizable by construction era/materials/scale, kept and built around rather than replaced.

**Filter check:** characteristically inevitable, not generic — this is not "old buildings survive," it is the
specific Taurus mechanism (possession, not nostalgia or preservation-law) explaining an already-established,
otherwise-unexplained Janbogo fact (Phase 3D flags the core's survival as a REQUESTED naming/detail question
without characterizing *why* it persists). Kept.

## Step 2 — Eighteen cross-checks

### Elementals (8)

**1. Taurus + Earth (elemental).** *No candidate — reason: not fresh.* Earth's registered content (reliability,
patience, nourishment, groundedness, stagnation/hoarding shadow) overlaps almost completely with Taurus's own
base character, which the substrate file itself notes must be kept distinct from the in-fiction elemental
system. The one candidate considered — Janbogo's scientific/testbed export (Phase 7a) delivered reliably
"without needing credit" — turned out, on inspection, to add nothing Taurus's own base trait ("delivers the
goods... doesn't take commitments lightly," already available at Step 1) didn't already supply on its own;
Earth's specific nourishment vocabulary contributed no independent grounding beyond what Taurus alone gives.
Rejected as a restatement of Taurus's own base character, not an elemental-specific contribution.

**2. Taurus + Air (elemental).** *Candidate generated and kept — T-2.* Air's registered content (the invisible
connective medium carrying voice/signal/warning; negative: "a connection so diffuse it commits to nothing...
carry nothing at all, with no way to tell the difference from the outside") maps directly onto an established,
literal signal-infrastructure fact: Phase 1's G5 governance oddity — Janbogo administers the Janbogo Subnet but
does not hold its own subnet's Arcanet relay nexus (sited in Concordia's Gemini district instead). Taurus's
core need ("what is genuinely its own... by its own measure") and its possession-slippage/grudge-bearing shadow
("grudges will cause you to lose your focus," held like property, indefinitely, passed on) explain why this
specific, already-established deficit would sting a Taurus-tempered civic psychology in a way it wouldn't sting
a less possessive temperament: the one thing that makes a "hub" a hub is exactly the thing Janbogo cannot hold.

**Finding: a subnet-administration role who nurses a long-running, inherited grudge that Janbogo's own relay
hardware sits in Concordia.** Category: person. Player-facing form: spoken to, a quest-adjacent grievance.

**3. Taurus + Fire (elemental).** *No candidate — reason: no established anchor, and the trait Fire would
contribute (charismatic, showy self-display) is available to Taurus's own hospitality trait without Fire's
help.* Taurus's "hospitality with no restraint switch... over-hosts" could combine with Fire's warmth/
illumination, but nothing in Janbogo's admissible material (the compounded dark/high-wind season, Phase 3E)
establishes a showy, Fire-flavored public display — only an established fact that recreation concentrates
indoors (Phase 3E/4/8), which Taurus's own hospitality trait already accounts for without needing Fire's
register. No fresh, Fire-specific content found.

**4. Taurus + Water (elemental).** *Candidate generated and kept — T-3.* Water's registered content (deep
resilience, "the path of least resistance without ever actually giving up," calm persistence) maps onto
Janbogo's single most literal water-fact: the Terra Nova Bay polynya, a permanent, year-round ice-free body of
open water — flagged in Phase 0 itself as a "firsts/onlys/lasts" item (the only year-round ice-free Ross Sea
access point). Taurus's core archetype is literally "durable possession of something real" and its house
position is the second house, "of possessions, personal wealth, security" — applied to Janbogo's one
undisputed, permanently-held natural asset, this produces a civic-pride reading distinct from a generic "nice
harbor" fact.

**Finding: the polynya treated by Janbogo as a possessed birthright — the one asset no other Ross Sea coast
city has, held with proprietary civic pride.** Category: place/thing. Player-facing form: seen/handled —
dockside civic-pride markers, toasts, launch customs oriented around "we have this, permanently, and no one
else on this coast does."

**5. Taurus + Wood (elemental).** *No candidate — reason: duplicates T-1's own grounding.* Wood's growth/
expansion register maps onto the same Phase 1 G4 material (continuous outward growth away from the founding
footprint, informal building-over "without a plan reconciling old and new construction... punished by

forward vision) does not surface any additional established Janbogo particular beyond what T-1 already covers;
recorded as a duplicate rather than a second independent hit.

**6. Taurus + Metal (elemental).** *Candidate generated and kept — T-4.* Metal's registered content (letting go
gracefully, honoring what has ended, precision/structure, grief) is on-topic for Phase 6C's own genuinely open

**Finding: the moment of formal death-recording is accompanied by a private, comforting untruth spoken to (or
about) the dead — a rite dressed as paperwork.** Category: thing/practice. This is offered as a hypothesis

base Janbogo material already holds itself to.

**7. Taurus + Electricity (elemental).** *No candidate — reason: provenance risk, explicitly declined.* The
most obvious material this pairing could reach for is a wind-forecast/warning signal system. Phase 10's own
M-66 note flags that this exact institution ("the Katabatic Warning Towers") is provenance-compromised — this

**8. Taurus + Electromagnetism (elemental).** *No candidate — reason: duplicates T-2's own grounding.* The only

### Planetary Symbols (10)

**10. Taurus + Venus (planetary).** *Candidate generated and kept — T-5.* Read strictly from its own file, not

missing *human* mechanism: the first-impression lock ("once it has an image firmly planted in its head, the
Bull won't easily be swayed by any updated evidence to the contrary. It sticks to its first impressions").

**Finding: a resident who forms one fixed read of the sky ("it looks calm, it's just cold") and is
constitutionally resistant to revising it even against incoming wind data — the specific psychological
mechanism behind Phase 1's G2 GRUDGING TOLERANCE and a concrete candidate for Phase 6D's own open "what grows

**11. Taurus + Earth (planetary, "Oasis").** *No candidate — reason: over-abstraction, no new grounded object.*

**12. Taurus + Mars.** *No candidate — reason: duplicates T-2's alternate-anchor material (Earth-elemental

**13. Taurus + Asteroid Belt.** *No candidate — reason: would require over-reaching into deliberately-withheld

**14. Taurus + Jupiter.** *Candidate generated and kept — T-6.* Jupiter's registered content — "gathering, not

**15. Taurus + Saturn.** *Candidate generated and kept — T-7.* Saturn's registered content — "beauty built from

**18. Taurus + Pluto.** *Candidate generated and kept — T-8.* Pluto's registered content — the Charon co-orbit,

**Finding: a precisely marked, jealously defended administrative/jurisdictional line with Zukelli (harbor

## Step 3 — Within-sign combinatorial synthesis

**Pairs tested:**

- **T-1 (retained core) + T-7 (loose sprawl) → kept, SYNTH-1.** Together these describe a single, coherent,
  whole-city spatial reading that neither implies alone: a Taurus-tempered Janbogo holds its literal center
  (the founding core) with total, devoted, unrevisited possessiveness, while the same temperament's refusal to
  concede a slow-building problem lets everything beyond that center accumulate as unplanned, loosely-held
  fragments. **"The Held Center, The Loose Edge"** — a visitor moving outward from the core would physically
  cross from tightly-guarded, coherent, ancient construction into looser, patchwork, unplanned additions, with
  the transition itself being legible. Category: place (a city-scale build-fabric reading).
- **T-2 (nexus grudge) + T-8 (Zukelli boundary) → considered, not kept.** Both are about Janbogo's identity
  being administratively whole yet functionally incomplete at its edges (a network node it doesn't hold; a
  near-twin it must territorially distinguish itself from). This is a real thematic echo but does not combine
  into anything more concrete than restating "Taurus is chronically thwarted at the periphery" in two different
  vocabularies; no new catalogable object emerges. Not kept as a separate synthesis — noted here rather than
  silently dropped.
- **T-4 (whispered lie) + T-6 (silent verdict on belonging) → considered, not kept.** Both are one-time,
  irreversible, quietly-delivered judgments (on death, on belonging). The combination mostly restates Taurus's
  own base first-impression-lock trait applied twice to two different established facts, rather than producing
  anything neither implied alone. Not kept.
- **T-3 (polynya) + T-2 (nexus grudge) → considered, not kept.** Both illustrate a possession/non-possession
  asymmetry (natural asset fiercely held vs. network infrastructure denied), but this restates the same
  asymmetry SYNTH-1 already captures in a different domain (geography/infrastructure rather than architecture);
  keeping a third near-identical "Taurus has some things and lacks others" synthesis would be padding rather
  than a genuinely separate result. Not kept.

## Step 4 — Per-HIT contradiction check

For each kept result (T-1 through T-8, SYNTH-1), one deliberate opposite-register candidate was generated and
tested against the same characteristic-fit filter.

**T-1 (Retained Core — public/civic/monumental register).** Opposite-register candidate: private/domestic —
individual old-core residents who could move to later-built, better-designed housing (Phase 4's own established
housing-stock disparity) but refuse to, out of the same retention instinct that keeps the whole core standing.
**Survives — grounded directly in Phase 4's own established finding.** Kept as **T-1b**, category: person (a
role: an elderly resident of the original core who won't relocate despite worse conditions).

**T-2 (Nexus Grudge-Holder — serious/institutional/individual register).** Opposite-register candidate:
unserious/communal — a wry, semi-affectionate running joke among residents about not holding their own subnet's
"brain." **Survives** — Taurus's own file explicitly supports both registers simultaneously ("grudges" held
indefinitely *and* "fights are frequent, loud, and short — a Taurus' temper dies down quickly"), so a lighter
communal version alongside the serious institutional one is textually consistent, not invented from nothing.
Kept as **T-2b**, category: thing (a piece of local color/folk custom), offered as speculative texture
consistent with established facts rather than an asserted specific.

**T-3 (Polynya as possessed birthright — proud/celebratory register).** Opposite-register candidate:
solemn/anxious — a private civic worry that the polynya's permanence isn't guaranteed. **Rejected — no
candidate.** Janbogo's admissible material states the polynya's year-round status as a settled fact, not a
qualified or fragile one; Earth(planetary)'s own negative pole ("hasn't failed yet") is a property of the
symbol in general, not asserted as manifesting at Janbogo specifically. Asserting civic anxiety here would
exceed what the source supports. T-3 stands alone.

**T-4 (Whispered Record — private/intimate register).** Opposite-register candidate: public/formal — the exact,
unsentimental, itemized public registry entry as a separate, cold civic counterpart to the private comforting
untruth. **Survives** — Phase 6C's own material already frames a record/rite distinction, and Metal's own dual
poles (grief *and* precision/structure) plus Taurus's payment-in-kind/ledger instinct ("an object, a shift, or
a meal... not moral") together support a literal, exact public record existing alongside the private rite, not
replacing it. Kept as **T-4b**, category: thing/institution (the formal registry itself, as a public
counterpart to the private rite).

**T-5 (Resident who trusts the calm sky — individual/tragic register).** Opposite-register candidate:
communal/institutional and corrective rather than tragic — mandatory outdoor check-in or buddy-verification
infrastructure that exists precisely because the city cannot trust any single resident's fixed first read.
**Survives** — grounded in Phase 1's own G2 STANDING COST ("continuous wind-forecast/warning infrastructure...
never stops"); the specific compensating-institution reading is a reasonable, well-motivated inference from that
established standing cost. Kept as **T-5b**, category: thing/institution.

**T-6 (Silent verdict on belonging — slow/permanent register).** Opposite-register candidate: fast/loud/
short-cycle — Taurus's own file states both slow permanent verdicts *and* "fights are frequent, loud, and
short... dies down quickly" as genuine registers. Applied to Phase 4/9's own already-established distinct
population (the outdoor-exposed dock/highway labor minority, whose day is organized around shared, acute,
Taurus-favorable "excellent in acute crisis" conditions rather than slow social vetting), a fast, loud,
quickly-formed-and-dissolved solidarity pattern specific to that population is well grounded and genuinely
different in kind from the general population's slow verdict. **Survives.** Kept as **T-6b**, category: person
(a social pattern centered on the dock/highway labor archetype already catalogued at Phase 10).

**T-7 (Loose Sprawl — physical/macro-scale register).** Opposite-register candidate: internal/psychological
(an individual's identity solid-from-a-distance, fragmented-up-close) or leisure-domain parallel. **Rejected —
no candidate.** Neither has any anchor in admissible material, which grounds this finding specifically in the
physical building fabric (Phase 1 G4); forcing a private-psychological or recreational counterpart would exceed
what the source supports. T-7 stands alone.

**T-8 (Zukelli Boundary — formal/administrative/serious register).** Opposite-register candidate: informal/
warm — ordinary residents on both sides maintaining easy, unbureaucratic personal ties across the line, undercutting
the civic seriousness of the boundary. **Rejected — no candidate.** Phase 5a explicitly leaves "what flows,
what does not" between Janbogo and Zukelli as REQUESTED/unestablished; asserting casual cross-boundary ties
where the source is agnostic would exceed the admissible material. T-8 stands alone.

**SYNTH-1 (Held Center / Loose Edge — spatial/architectural register).** Opposite-register candidate: a social
parallel, with the founding-descended minority occupying a "held, fixed" social position mirroring the core,
and the grown-in majority occupying a "loose, still-forming" position mirroring the sprawl. **Rejected —
contradicts established material.** Phase 1's own G8 finding states explicitly that the founding nation's civic
template *lacks* demographic/institutional weight rather than holding a solid, dominant position — the social
parallel does not survive contact with this established deficit. SYNTH-1 stands alone.
