# Phase 9 — Populations

**Asks:** What is life here like for each distinct kind of person present, and how do those kinds relate?

**Type note applied directly, per `03` §0.1:** for an Installation, the population split is often staff/
non-staff or permanent/rotational rather than by kind — confirmed here (Phase 2's open-season/closure-
season split already carries real weight), but this phase focuses specifically on the robot/human
question the project's own representation framework requires at every location.

---

## A. Populations present, and the lens for each

**Robots and humans**, per the project's standard categories. **The lens decision, made explicitly per
`03`'s own instruction:** neither the outer-city anchor (founding-nation threads) nor the district anchor
(theme/role) applies cleanly here, because Mountain Pass Airport has *two* founding populations rather
than one. **This pass anchors both populations' culture in origin-city** — which of the two joint founders
a given worker's own background traces to — because that axis, not human/robot kind, is what this
location's own Phase 1/2 findings actually organize around.

**⭐ A real, checkable inversion worth naming before anything else:** per Phase 1's G8 reading, **Kunlun
contributes zero humans**. A jointly-staffed crew drawn from both cities is therefore **numerically robot-
majority, plausibly robot-supermajority** — the opposite of treating robots as the setting's usual "non-
dominant" population requiring special lens attention. **At Mountain Pass Airport specifically, humans are
the numerically secondary population**, not robots. This is stated explicitly because it inverts the
framing most Settlement-type locations this methodology has run would default to.

## B. Per-population culture

**Humans — mono-sourced, unlike almost anywhere else in Tepenia.** Every human worker at this location is
Vostok-origin, because Kunlun supplies none. **This is a genuinely sharp, uncommon condition**: most
locations' non-dominant or minority population is itself a blend of several diaspora sources; here it is
exactly one. Vostok's own established character (industrial, survival-hardened, USA/Japan Primary) is
imported in miniature, unmixed with any second source. Directly continuous with Phase 1's finding that
human workers have exactly one direction of homesickness, not two.

**Robots — dual-sourced, and this is the more textured population here.** Kunlun's own robots carry that
city's specifically curated astronomy-and-comms technical specialization; Vostok's robots carry its
broader, more generalist industrial/survival profile. **A real, specific, checkable texture emerges from
this meeting**: informal shop-talk comparing "how Kunlun does it" against "how Vostok does it" — a small,
professional-register cultural exchange unique to this location, since nowhere else in Tepenia puts these
two specific robot populations into sustained daily contact with each other.

## C. Inter-population relations — the local inflection

**Restating the setting's baseline (egalitarian human-robot relations, skewed toward robot population per
established project canon) is not a finding on its own** — it is directly inherited and correctly tagged
as such below. **The genuine local inflection, per the phase's own strongest-available-move instruction:**
***origin-city, not kind, is the category that actually organizes daily social texture at Mountain Pass
Airport.*** A Kunlun-origin robot and a Vostok-origin robot have more in common, day to day, with a
Vostok-origin human (shared homesickness for a specific place, shared "how we do it back home" habits)
than a Vostok-origin robot necessarily has with a Kunlun-origin robot on pure kind-solidarity alone. **The
human/robot axis genuinely under-explains this location's social fabric; the origin-city axis over-
explains it, in the productive sense the phase asks for.**

**Would "origin-city outranks kind" survive being swapped onto the nearest comparable — Vostok or Kunlun
themselves?** No, cleanly. At either home city, human/robot kind *is* the locally salient population axis,

populations, so "which other city are you from" has no referent there at all. **This finding does not
transfer, which is the swap test's own definition of a genuinely localized result.**

## Amendment — Zodiac Lens person-shaped results, folded in at Step 5 (`03` §0.4)

Per `11_Zodiac_Lens.md`'s own verified, genuine person-shaped results (all twelve signs — see that file's
execution note for the contamination-recovery provenance), eight role-archetypes are added to this phase's
own population read. None contradict the findings above; several sharpen them directly.

- **Virgo — the outpost's own quality-control-minded technician.** Gives Phase 8's already-catalogued

  legitimate ways the crew manages Phase 6D's own euphemism-as-institution finding around the compact
  nobody fully controls — one imagining where a shipment ends up, one refusing to.
- **Scorpio — the closure-season senior hand.** Someone who has weathered several closures, embodying
