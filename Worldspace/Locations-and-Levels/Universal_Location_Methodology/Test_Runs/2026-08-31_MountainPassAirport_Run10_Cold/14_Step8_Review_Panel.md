## Panel A — the six Flat Archetypes

**1. The Child.**

**Cannot be cast at all.** Per `00f`'s own rule, a position with no standpoint to speak from is itself a
finding, and a strong one — checked directly, not assumed: no children exist at Mountain Pass Airport
(Phase 2's own "Born here" null, Phase 4's explicit "no children" finding), and none are plausible given
the seasonal, rotational, Band-1 staffing model. **This is the second, fully independent instrument to hit
Phase 1's own central converged deficit** — after the Zodiac Lens's own Cancer total-domestic-null — from a
completely different direction (a review panel position rather than a symbol-driven search). Recorded as a
result, not a gap in the panel's own execution.

**2. The Lover** *(cast as: one half of a rotation-era pair, if one exists — not confirmed, plausible)*.

**Asks:** *Could I build a life with someone here, and where would we actually go?* **Answer, direct: no,
and the place already knows it.** Phase 6B's compact states plainly that MPA was never meant to be a home.
Private domesticity exists narrowly (Phase 7d) — a personalized locker, a private habit — but "a life
together" specifically is foreclosed by the same founding trade-off that forecloses everything else
domestic. → **unmet.** The location knowingly does not provide this, per the compact every worker silently
accepts on arrival; satisfying it would require MPA to become something closer to a Settlement, undoing the
very thing that makes it what it is.

**3. The Parent** *(cast as: a Vostok-origin or Kunlun-origin worker whose own children are at home)*.

**Asks:** *Where are the children right now, and who has them?* **A real, sharp answer, unlike the Child's
uncastable silence:** the Parent knows exactly where their children are — at home, with family, in Vostok
or Kunlun — and knows exactly that they are not there. This sharpens Phase 1's own "in a neighbor's
present" deficit-address finding into its most personal possible form: the remedy is not merely nearby and
legible, it is a specific person's own children. → **unmet.** Consistent with the Lover's own reading —
knowingly refused, not an oversight.

**4. The Ruler** *(cast as: the senior on-site engineer during a changeover, per Phase 10's own role-
archetype)*.

**Asks:** *Who administers that, who funds it, and what happens when they stop?* **No clean answer exists**
— this is Phase 7b's own governance-vacuum finding, independently reconfirmed by the one panel position
built to notice exactly this. **Unlike the Lover's and Parent's readings, this is not knowingly protected**
— per `00d`'s own shadow test, nobody at MPA perceives the absence of a formal administrator as a defended
choice; it simply has never been needed until it is. → **noted.** Real, deferred rather than resolved here,
because resolving it would require an actual RESERVED-tier developer ruling on relative seniority between
Vostok and Kunlun (already flagged as a sensitivity note in Step 5b) — not something this pass presumes to
settle on its own authority.

**5. The Elder** *(cast as: a long-tenured worker across many rotations — Taurus's own "gravity well"
figure from the Zodiac Lens, Phase 9 amendment)*.

**Asks:** *Was that actually always true — and what am I now, if I can't do that anymore?* Applied to the
founding trade-off itself (stability chosen over livability, Phase 1 G4): the Elder is the one person
positioned to have watched that trade-off hold, or not, across enough rotations to actually judge it —
and the pass has no canon either way on whether anyone ever has. → **noted**, same underlying open
question as the Ruler's, from a temporal rather than institutional angle — satisfies the four-corner
requirement that these be genuinely different directions of attack, not one objection in two costumes.

**6. The Mentor** *(cast as: an experienced hand overseeing a seasonal crossover, per Phase 7c)*.

**Asks:** *How does someone learn this, and what becomes of the ones who can't?* **Already fully answered**
by Phase 7c (apprenticeship-shaped, forced overlap at changeover) and Phase 6D (euphemized early rotation-
out for those who can't sustain it). The Mentor's own read confirms rather than adds. → **accepted, folded
into the existing Phase 7c/6D findings rather than producing a standalone new one** — per `00f`'s own note
that this is an equally good outcome, not a weak run.

## The mandatory positions

**The Passer-Through.** A genuine bifurcation, not a single verdict: a traveler who crosses in a single day,
on schedule, sees only the surface (a functional, stark, sole-stop industrial waypoint) — the shadow (the
governance vacuum, the euphemism, the compact) is invisible to them, correctly, since discoverability
requires more than a passing glance. **But** the same traveler, caught by a missed changeover window (Phase
5d's own established mechanism), is folded into the crew for the duration and sees everything, immediately
— the surface/shadow boundary at Mountain Pass Airport is not a matter of degree, it is a matter of exactly
which side of one seasonal deadline a visitor happens to be on. **Both readings are correct and are kept
side by side, per `00d`'s own two-mode discoverability test.**

**The Neighbor.** Cast as Dome Fuji (the nearest connected city on Hwy 37 that is not one of MPA's own two
founders). **Their honest position is indifference, not grievance** — consistent with Phase 5a's own
finding that MPA's real relational weight sits entirely with Vostok and Kunlun; anyone once-removed simply
has little stake in it. This is a quieter, thinner Neighbor reading than most locations this methodology
has run, and it is recorded honestly as such rather than manufactured into a grievance that Phase 5's own
material does not support. → **noted**, minor addition folded into Phase 5a rather than a standalone
finding.

## The Lover faculty's question, run every time

**Is this place alive, and could anyone love it?**

**Answered directly, not deflected.** Mountain Pass Airport cannot be loved as a home — every instrument
this pass has run, from the Phase 1 generator stack to Cancer's own total-domestic-null to the Child's
uncastable panel position, converges on the same answer. **But the chamber-departure convergence — this
run's own headline finding, independently reached by seven zodiac signs — is real evidence of a different
kind of aliveness**: care directed outward, at the thing made, done carefully by people who will never
know it mattered. **The honest answer is not "no" and not "yes" — it is that Mountain Pass Airport's
capacity to be loved is real but entirely redirected**, from the place itself onto what the place produces.
A resident could not say they love living here. Several of this pass's own findings suggest they could say,
without exaggeration, that they love the work.

## Four-corner check

**Confirmed, not assumed.** The six positions' objections came from six genuinely different directions:
structural absence (Child), personal/emotional (Parent), institutional (Ruler), temporal/reflective (Elder),
confirmatory (Mentor), and perception-timing (Passer-Through) — no two positions raised the same objection
in different costumes. The Lover's and Parent's shared `unmet` disposition is not a four-corner failure:
they arrive at the same verdict from clearly different standpoints (romantic life vs. specific dependents),
which is convergence from genuinely opposed corners, per `00f`'s own strongest-signal case.

## Panel-derived findings, tallied

**Dispositions:** 2 `unmet` (Lover, Parent) · 2 `noted` (Ruler, Elder — same open question, different
angles) · 1 `accepted`-folded (Mentor) · 0 `rejected` · 0 `refereed` · 0 `declined`.

**Read per `00_RUNBOOK.md`'s own diagnostic** (a low `unmet` count means the location's problems are
absences it does not know it has, not evidence of a soft panel): **the `unmet` count here is not low, and
that is itself informative** — Mountain Pass Airport, unlike a district that doesn't know what it's
protecting, *does* know what it's refusing (the compact is explicit, self-aware, silently accepted by every
worker on arrival). This is consistent with, and further confirms, Phase 1's own G4 finding that the
founders' trade-off was made knowingly, not stumbled into.

**No `declined` verdicts** — every objection this panel raised was either knowingly protected (`unmet`) or
a genuine, real gap the location has not yet perceived (`noted`), never a want whose only fault is making
MPA resemble a sibling it does not actually have. Consistent with Frame Declaration's own note that no
sibling set exists to homogenize toward.
