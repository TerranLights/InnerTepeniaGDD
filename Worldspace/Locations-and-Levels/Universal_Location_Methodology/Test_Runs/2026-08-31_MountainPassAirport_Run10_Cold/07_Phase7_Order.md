# Phase 7 — Order

**Asks:** How does this place organize work, decide things, pass on skill, and handle the people who do
not fit? **Primary phase for Installation type**, per `03` §0.1.

---

## 7a — Work and economy

**Division of labor, at Band 1: named roles, not sectors**, per `01` §2.3's inversion — this pass does not
assign percentage breakdowns to a population this small. The work clusters into three functions already
established (Phase 1, Phase 3): precision fabrication, life-support/structural maintenance, and airstrip
operation — with real overlap, since at this scale the same handful of people cover more than one.

**What it makes:** fabrication-synthesis chambers, per canon.

**The non-thematic export — checked against `00b`'s own warning not to force export into goods where the
real export is people or expertise:** the headline export (chambers) is entirely thematic. **The genuine
non-thematic export is trained, altitude-and-isolation-proven personnel.** A worker who completes a
rotation here — surviving the acclimatization discipline, the closure season's Band-1 fragility, the
changeover-timing risk — carries a specific, verifiable, portable competency that both founding cities'
own broader technical workforces value independently of chamber-building itself. This is stated explicitly
as **people, not goods**, per the same discipline that flagged Cape Adare's own sector-breadth error.

## 7b — Governance and decision

**Who decides, per the real-world-grounded reading from Phase 5b:** joint, shared, non-hierarchical
technical authority during the open season; singular, unaccountable authority by physical necessity during
closure. **No single administrator exists at any point in the year** — canon states the venture was
"jointly established and staffed," with neither city named as senior partner, and the real-world comparable
researched in Phase 1 (the actual Concordia Station, Dome C) confirms this is a genuinely plausible
governance shape for a bi-national joint venture, not an invented gap.

**What is unadministrable — the phase's own most productive question:** ***what happens when Vostok's and
Kunlun's on-site engineers genuinely disagree on a technical call, and there is no route to escalate
quickly?*** No canon answers this, and this pass does not invent an answer — it names the gap as a real,
structural absence rather than papering over it with an invented adjudication process. **This is the
unadministrable edge case**, and per `04` Gate 9's own asymmetry check, it is worth running directly: *the
mechanism runs both ways — did the file write both?* **It does not, because no mechanism exists at all.**
Whoever's judgment does not prevail in a joint disagreement has no formal appeal, no named authority above
the two engineers themselves to rule, and — during closure season specifically — no way to escalate to
either home city in time to matter. **A textbook `00d` shadow**: unintended (nobody designed an
adjudication vacuum on purpose), unnoticed (the "equal partnership" framing reads as a strength, not an
absence, until someone actually needs the missing mechanism), and discoverable rather than announced.

## 7c — Knowledge and transmission

**How someone learns this work:** apprenticeship-shaped, by necessity — precision fabrication is not a
skill a newcomer arrives already fully able to perform at this specific site's own tolerances, and the
seasonal crew swap (Phase 5b) structurally forces at least a partial overlap period between outgoing and
incoming staff during open-season changeovers, even if canon does not specify its exact length.

**What becomes of the ones who cannot learn it, or cannot sustain it:** directly continuous with Phase
6D's Failure State finding — euphemized rotation, not formal dismissal. There is no institutional
mechanism named "you have failed to qualify"; there is only "your rotation is ending a little early this
time," which may be true for entirely legitimate scheduling reasons and also, sometimes, is not.

## 7d — Private life, minorities, and counterculture

**Checked first, per the phase's own instruction: is the dissent a population at all, or does the
contradiction sit inside each person?** Given the population's own composition (Phase 2 — small, entirely
assigned/posted, no native layer, no factions with competing claims on the place), **there is no
counterculture in the organized, factional sense.** The contradiction Phase 6B's compact describes sits
**inside each individual worker**, not between groups. Per `03` Phase 7d's own consequence for this case:
**such a place cannot be reformed from within and cannot be opposed from within either, because there is
nobody to organize against — everyone accepted the same compact on the way in.**

**What exists instead, and it is real:** small, private, unofficial, individually-scaled acts of
domesticity the compact does not forbid but also does not support — a personal item kept somewhere it
technically shouldn't be, a locker or work-surface quietly personalized past what function requires. **Not
rebellion.** Nobody is refusing anything the place demands; this is the closest thing to a counterculture
Band 1 supports, and it is closer to what Phase 1's Unrecognized Instrument already found (displaced
home-making, redirected into caretaking a shipped chamber) — here it surfaces again, redirected a second
time, into the smallest possible personal spaces.

## Sanction proportionality — the hard check, and a genuine inversion

**Priced against this location's own physical conditions, per `00d`'s own rule.** Most locations this
methodology has run treat exclusion as the harshest available sanction — exile into a hostile environment.
**Mountain Pass Airport inverts this structurally.** Per Phase 6B's own compact, being sent away from here
means returning to Vostok or Kunlun — a real city, safety, and (per Phase 1's deficit-address finding) the
one thing everyone here already wants and cannot have. **"Exclusion" is not a punishment at this location.
It is closer to a reward, or at minimum a mercy — which means this place structurally cannot use its own
harshest available tool (removal) as a deterrent at all**, because removal is what the compact already
promises everyone eventually, and nobody dreads it.

**What can function as a consequence, given that inversion:** proportionate, small, and social — reduced
trust in independent technical judgment, or being denied the more autonomous closure-season postings in
favor of the more closely-supervised open season. Priced honestly: at Band 1, a group this size and this
interdependent cannot afford formal, escalated punishment for anything short of a genuine safety violation
— peer disappointment and reduced autonomy are what the place's own physical conditions can actually
support, and this pass does not scale a minor lapse into anything larger, per the Frostlands precedent this
discipline is named for.

## Band variance

**Band 1, per `03`'s own note:** governance here is a conversation between named people, not an
institution with a title — confirmed directly throughout 7b above. The interesting question this band
predicts (*what happens when two of twelve disagree permanently?*) is answered precisely by the
unadministrable gap named in 7b.
