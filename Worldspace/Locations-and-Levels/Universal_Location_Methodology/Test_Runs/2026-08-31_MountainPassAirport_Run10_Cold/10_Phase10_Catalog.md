# Phase 10 — Catalog

**Asks:** What is actually here — named, specific, and enterable?

**Governing premise applied:** this is a place built around a theme (precision under hostile conditions,
joint-venture interdependence), not wall-to-wall manufacturing floors. The catalog below refracts the
theme rather than restating it.

---

## A. Implied-but-unnamed material, listed from Phases 3–8 before inventing

Already established and simply gathered here: the manufacturing floor, the life-support core, the
airlock/suiting-up transition (Phase 3B), the airstrip itself, the redundant paper log and its margin
notes (Phase 3F, Phase 8), the comms terminal/social lifeline (Phase 4), anti-static gear (Phase 8), the
fabrication-synthesis chambers as objects (Phase 1, Tier-3 interrogation).

## B1. Named places and landmarks

- **The Bench.** Informal working name for the manufacturing floor — specific, not "the factory."
- **The Threshold.** Informal name for the airlock/suiting-up chamber between the pressurized interior and
  the lethal exterior — given how load-bearing this transition is (Phase 3B), it plausibly earns its own
  name rather than staying generic.
- **The Margin Log.** The redundant, hand-annotated paper record from Phase 3F/Phase 8 — named specifically
  because it is both a functional safety instrument and, unofficially, the outpost's only durable trace of
  who was actually here.
- **The strip.** Informal shorthand for the airstrip itself.

## B2. Physical things

The fabrication-synthesis chambers, mid-assembly and finished, ready for shipment (the object whose
transcontinental journey grounded Phase 1's Unrecognized Instrument finding). Anti-static grounding gear.
The Margin Log's own physical form — a bound paper ledger, deliberately non-digital. The comms terminal.

## C. People as role-archetypes — no invented proper names, per the binding rule

- **A Kunlun-origin robot technician, well into a long rotation** — carries the "two homesick directions"
  finding lightly, since Kunlun-origin robots have Vostok's proximity too, but this is still not home.
- **A Vostok-origin human engineer serving a first closure season** — the sharpest available cast for
  Phase 6D's Failure-State finding and Phase 1's single-address deficit.
- **The joint venture's senior on-site engineer during a changeover** — the role that most directly lives
  Phase 7b's unadministrable governance gap, since this is the person who would be asked to rule on a
  Vostok/Kunlun technical disagreement with no formal authority to do so.
- **A traveler stopped by a missed changeover window** — the "membership by unremarked persistence" case
  from Phase 5d, folded into the crew's routine by necessity rather than choice.

## D. Settings — atmospheric/situational, not landmarks

- **The suiting-up sequence before stepping onto the strip** — a specific, physically enactable moment
  (Phase 3B/G).
- **The first shared meal after a changeover**, when the crew size has just changed and everyone is still
  adjusting to who is and is not present (Phase 8).
- **The quiet after the comms window closes** — the specific texture of Phase 4's social-lifeline finding,
  the moment it is not available.
