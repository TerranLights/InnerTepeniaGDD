# Phase 9 — Populations

**Named axis: "rotation status outranks kind."** Stated here before the analysis, per `03` §0.2 item 3 — this
phase's real finding (§C) is that a social category this location itself produced (time-in-posting) matters
more day to day than either origin city or robot/human kind, both of which are inherited categories rather than
ones this location generated.

**Optional for Corridor**, per `03` §0.1. Run in full — the composition asymmetry established in Phase 2 gives
this phase real, specific material rather than a generic human/robot restatement.

---

## A. Populations present, and the lens

**Two kinds present, but not cleanly split by origin city — worth stating precisely rather than conflating the
two axes.** Kunlun contributes robots exclusively (its own census is 0 humans). Vostok contributes a mix of
humans and robots. **This means the population is not simply "Kunlun robots vs. Vostok humans" — it is a
majority-robot crew drawn from both cities, plus a human minority that can only ever come from Vostok's side.**

**Lens declared: origin city (Kunlun vs. Vostok), not theme/role (everyone shares the same fabrication work) and
not founding-nation (this is a bi-city joint venture, not a nationally-anchored population).** This matches the
outer-city convention (origin-anchored) rather than the district convention (theme/role-anchored), consistent
with `03` Phase 9's own instruction that the lens must be decided per location type rather than assumed.

## B. Per-population culture

**Robot majority** (drawn from both founding cities): coolant maintenance and siligel-related routines are the
shared baseline, per established project canon (`Robot_Biology_and_Culture/`). **Human minority** (Vostok-origin
only): ordinary food, sleep, and psychological-support needs a Band-1, isolated posting does not automatically
provide for, and which the robot majority does not share. **Neither is subordinate to the other** — per Phase 4
§B, they run as parallel daily rhythms in the same small space, not a dominant culture with an accommodated
minority.

## C. Inter-population relations — the local inflection

**Restating the project's own human-robot relations baseline** (egalitarian, robot-population-skewed) is not a
finding on its own, per this phase's explicit warning. **The sharper question: is there a category here that
matters more than kind?**

**Yes — rotation status.** Per Phase 5 §5d's membership-conversion finding (full rotation completed vs. not
yet), **a newly-arrived Vostok-origin human and a newly-arrived Kunlun-origin robot have more in common with
each other, day to day, than either has with an already-"member" colleague of their own kind.** Both are being
paired with an experienced mentor (Phase 7c), both are still learning the corridor's own procedural discipline
(Phase 1), and neither yet has the standing the completed-rotation status confers. **Rotation status, not
origin city and not robot/human kind, is the operative social category for how this small crew actually treats
each other day to day** — the inter-kind question (robot vs. human) answers itself underneath this sharper
axis, exactly as `03` Phase 9 predicts when this kind of category exists.

## D. Dual-tagging the findings

- **The robot-coffee custom (Phase 4 §B):** inheritance **Adapted** (a real, pre-existing robot cultural
  practice, carried here and reduced to a single practitioner by the outpost's own scale); depth **Deep**
  (Band-1 fragility, genuinely load-bearing daily texture, not decorative).
- **Rotation status as the operative social category (§C above):** inheritance **Genuinely Emergent** (this is
  not inherited from either founding city's own culture — it arises specifically from this joint venture's own
  rotation-clocked structure); depth **Deep**.
- **The parallel-not-subordinate daily rhythms finding (§B):** inheritance **Genuinely Emergent** (a direct
  consequence of this specific composition asymmetry, not a restatement of the national baseline); depth
  **Deep**.

**Per the developer's own standing clarification (M-not-applicable here, but the same rule): multiple
genuinely-emergent, deep findings clustering in one phase is a good result, not a ratio needing correction** —
this phase's findings are not artificially hedged against each other, and no weaker finding is manufactured
merely to balance the count.

## E. Swap test

**Would the rotation-status finding (§C) survive being swapped onto a comparable remote outpost?** Partially,
and honestly reported as such: the *general* pattern (shared newcomer status cutting across other social axes)
is plausible at any small, rotation-based posting. **But the specific composition asymmetry it is built on —
one founding city contributing robots exclusively, the other contributing a genuine mix — is Kunlun/Vostok-
specific and does not transfer.** The finding survives at the level of mechanism; it does not survive at the
level of its own sharpest, most specific detail, which is the correct and expected outcome for a well-localized
finding.
