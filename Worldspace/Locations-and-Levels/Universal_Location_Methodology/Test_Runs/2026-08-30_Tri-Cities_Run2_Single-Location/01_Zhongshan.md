# PHASE 1 — CONSTRAINT & CAPABILITY

## The generators, run separately before comparison

**G1 — Saturn + Metal**, read from file per `02` §6.0, not from familiarity.
**Saturn:** *Mystery · doesn't care to be fully known · beauty built from fragments rather than requiring
wholeness · **held together only loosely, no actual cohesion, one disruption away from dispersing;
impressive from a distance, insubstantial up close.*** **Metal:** *precision · integrity · discernment ·
refinement · **the capacity to let go gracefully, the way autumn releases its own leaves** · honoring what has
genuinely ended.* Excess: *rigidity · grief that cannot move on · isolation through over-refinement.*
**Emotion: grief.**

**G2 — Physical.** Ice-free bedrock oasis *(no ice-movement correction, the easiest ground in Tepenia)* ·
Prydz Bay harbor and its own fishery · −9.9 °C mean · ~60-day polar night · Hwy 110 western terminus at the
tri-junction · **and the Standing Stone: a natural bedrock outcrop the founding build organized itself
around rather than clearing.**

**G4 — Founding.** Claim *confirmed*, not won — China's presence was ratified by the Jeju-do court, uncontested.
**And the "only": continuous Chinese/Sinian habitation straight through the First Interwar Period** — roughly
481 years unbroken, where every other founding site passed through unrelated operators.

**G8 — Composition.** China 35.83% Primary; **eighteen nations total**, the most in the cluster; Germany
(8.47%) outranks both Korea and Japan. Retention **77.9%, z = +0.76** against a 71.9% national mean *(n=33)*.
*(Human-vs-robot gap +9.4 pp, z = +0.82 — **not nationally remarkable; not used.**)*

> ### Proposed Finding Z-1 — Zhongshan cannot end things. `[new]`
> **Zhongshan is the only city in Tepenia whose thread was never cut**, and the cost of that is invisible
> because it has never been paid. **Every other Tepenian city has institutional experience of discontinuity —
> of losing a lineage and rebuilding. Zhongshan has none.** It has no practice of ending, no procedure for it,
> and no living memory of having survived one.
>
> **And the symbol says it precisely:** Metal's positive virtue is ***"the capacity to let go gracefully, the
> way autumn releases its own leaves."*** **Zhongshan holds the element whose central virtue is release and
> possesses only the other half of it** — the precision, the refinement, the discernment. **It has the whole
> of Metal except the letting go.**

## The four quadrants

| Quadrant | Reading |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **Continuity, and the memory that comes with it.** The Long Record; an unbroken craft lineage in the Crossing Quarter; a founding structure still in use as a building rather than a monument. **Others come here to find out what happened.** |
| **DEFICIT** | **Ending.** No mechanism for discontinuation, deaccession, demolition, or closure — **and no perception that this is a capacity at all**, because nothing has ever forced it. |
| **STANDING COST** | **Maintenance that only ever grows.** A city that never discards accumulates obligations monotonically: the record must be kept, the garments mended, the Hall maintained, the fleet serviced. **Nobody has proposed reducing it, because reduction is the thing the city cannot do.** |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Proposing that something stop.** Nobody forbids it. There is simply **no procedure that can receive the proposal** — so it is not rejected, it is *unprocessable*, and the person who keeps making it becomes someone quietly not consulted. |

**Shape: `Cost-dominant`** *(`02` §4)* — the shape the district set could not produce. **The question it forces:
*is this place doing anything other than continuing to exist?*** **Largely, no — and it experiences that as
virtue rather than as drift.**

## Deficit address — peer-free set run first, per `02` §4.1 `[new]`

**Where does "the capacity to end things" live?** **Not** in its own past *(its past is more continuous, not
less)*. **Not** in the parent. **Not** in a named peer — **every other city in Tepenia has this capacity**,
because every other city had its thread cut.

> **Address: DIFFUSE.** *"No counterparty, no politics, **no grievance ever forms.** Produces drift."*
> **Exactly right.** The remedy is held by thirty-four cities and therefore by nobody in particular, so there
> is no one to ask, nothing to resent, and no moment at which the lack becomes a question. **Zhongshan
> accumulates in a direction nothing selects.**

**Methodology note:** the peer-free half of the address axis produced this **without using either neighbor** —
which is the single-location claim in `00_RUNBOOK` working as stated.

## ⚠ The pre-war reading, and what the frame makes visible

**Saturn's negative: *"one disruption away from simply dispersing."*** **A city cohered entirely by
continuity has no second binding.** Its unity is not ideology, institution, or shared origin — 64% of it is
not Chinese — **it is simply that nothing has ever stopped.**

**This pass is pre-war. The residents cannot know what is coming.** The reader can. **Zhongshan's single
point of failure is the exact thing the Long Night War is about to hit**, and the city has no concept of
recovery because it has never needed one. *(Written as anticipation, not as history — `01` §4 rule 1.)*

# PHASE 2 — COMPOSITION & ARRIVAL

**Arrival-mode mix:** overwhelmingly **born here** — the most under-written mode per `03`, and here it is the
*dominant* one, which is rare in Tepenia. Secondary: **inherited it** *(the Jeju-do confirmation handed the
site to a population already on it)*. Minimal *chose it*; minimal *fled to it*.

> **Proposed Finding Z-2. `[new]`** **Zhongshan is the only Tri-City whose population is mostly native**, and
> in a country of exiles that is a genuine anomaly. **Its residents did not arrive; they continued.** The
> founding is not a story about a journey, which every other Tepenian founding is.

**Who is not here:** **no significant refugee layer, and no rotational or posted population.** Nobody is on a
clock. **A city with no rotation has no built-in mechanism for turnover of ideas** — a second-order support for
Z-1's drift.

**The 64% question.** Eighteen nations; China at 35.83%; **Germany third at 8.47%, ahead of both Korea and
Japan** despite Korea and Japan each being Primary in a city a few kilometers away. **`[re-derived]`** The gap
between *who lives here* and *whose city this is* is the widest in the cluster.

  used to be.
- **Sound.** The quietest of the three, and **the Long Winter Suite is the exception that proves it** — during

- **Smell.** **Fermentation.** Preservation infrastructure tied directly to Prydz Bay fishing — the smell of
  things deliberately set aside and left to change. **This is the olfactory signature of a city that does not
  discard.** `[new]`

**The seam** *(two-stage lens)*: **there isn't one, and the absence is the finding. `[new]`** Every other

fabric is continuous because its habitation was** — the Founding Hall is contiguous with what grew around it.
**A stranger cannot tell which parts are oldest, and neither, increasingly, can anyone else.**

# PHASE 4 — ORDINARY LIFE

**Headline function stated, then written away from:** *maritime logistics, precision craft, and archive.*

**An ordinary day is organized around the light cycle, not a shift** — 60 days of dark, 61 of unbroken sun, and
work rhythms that flex around them rather than against them. **Differentiation axis: *what the day is organized
around* — here, the sky.**

- **Routine.** Long, unhurried, self-paced. Work that is finished when it is right rather than when a bell
  goes.
- **Mundane concern.** **Upkeep.** Things needing mending, servicing, re-listing, re-shelving. **The ambient
  background task of a Zhongshan life is maintenance of objects that already exist.**
- **Personal struggle. `[new]`** **Being the one who wants something to change and having nowhere to put it.**
  Not oppression — there is no one refusing. The proposal simply has no address *(the Grudging Tolerance
  quadrant, cashed out in somebody's Tuesday)*.
- **Escapism.** **The Loud Quarter** — see Phase 7.

**What the rhythm fails to provide:** anything for a person who needs a *deadline*. **A city with no endings has
no due dates**, and someone who works to pressure will find nothing here to push against.

# PHASE 5 — RELATION & GEOMETRY

**Clustered location — so 5a and 5b are mandatory** *(`03`'s new weighting table)*. **Written from Zhongshan's
side only.** The neighbors appear as canon attributes, never as Run 2 conclusions.

**5a — Peers.** *Mechanism, not rivalry.*
- **Sinheung (~0.3 km).** Zhongshan supplies **the road and nothing else** — Sinheung's raw materials cross
  Zhongshan's own tri-junction and Zhongshan holds no stake. **What Zhongshan refuses to develop: any
  participation in fabrication.** It never needed to, because bedrock and a fishery made it self-sufficient.
  **Outward direction:** canon records Sinheung reading Zhongshan's quiet as *"performing quiet superiority."*
  **Inward direction — the finding `[re-derived]`:** it is not superiority; it is a city with no attention
  spare. **Zhongshan does not know it is being read this way**, and could not correct it without explaining a
  norm the norm forbids explaining.
- **Shirayuki (~8 km).** Almost no traffic in either direction that canon records. **A quiet non-relation.**

**5b — Three-way set.** Category: ***what a departure is allowed to mean.*** **Zhongshan's answer: nothing is
said and nothing is concluded.** The non-prying norm covers leaving as completely as it covers heritage, so the
city has never assembled a picture of who leaves or why. **A departure is indistinguishable from a silence.**
*(Independently corroborated by canon's own diaspora note — the fled/stayed guilt that "is not discussed.")*

**5c — The parent.** *(Provisional; parent unwritten.)* The Federation needs **very little in particular** from
Zhongshan. **And Zhongshan does not appear to notice.** A city that is not needed and does not feel the lack is
unusual — **it is the only relation in this pass with no tension in it at all**, and that flatness is itself
characterizing.

**5d — Edge and crossing.** All arrivals reach the cluster through **one shared airport belonging to none of
the three**, then cross boundary zones where three building traditions meet without blending. **What converts a
visitor into a member: the withdrawal of attention.** There is no moment, no acknowledgement, and no way to ask
whether it has happened. `[re-derived]`

**5e — Dependency.** **Materially, almost none** — the rarest answer in Tepenia and a direct product of G2.
**Non-materially: it depends on nothing ever stopping.** **Unnamed → shadow**, per `03` 5e. `[new]`

# PHASE 6 — MEANING

**Naming technique run first**, per `03` Phase 6A — searching canon for religious-register language attached to
non-religious objects. **It returns immediately:** *The Long **Record*** · *The **Founding** Hall* · *The
**Standing** Stone* · a **custodian** institution. **These are reverent names for an archive, a building, and a

> ### Proposed Finding Z-3 — the compact, stated as a resident would. `[new]`
> **Zhongshan has no named faith, so per `03` Phase 6A expect a *compact* — what it agreed to and never wrote
> down:**
>
> > ***"What has been continuous is not ours to break."***
>
> **Not a doctrine and not a rule** — nobody would recognize it as either. It is why the rock was built around,
> why the garment is mended, why the record is kept, and why the proposal to stop something has nowhere to go.
> **Counter-check per `03`: not portable.** It would be meaningless in any city whose thread was already cut,
> **which is every other city in Tepenia.**

**Death and the dead.** **The disposal mechanism is RESERVED and is not answered here.** What *can* be said
without touching it: `03` Phase 6C distinguishes the **record** from the **rite**, and **Zhongshan is
overwhelmingly weighted to the record.** The dead persist in the Long Record with the same completeness as
everything else. **Who is not mourned properly:** anyone who left. **They are not in the record as departures,
because departures are not discussed** — so the city's memory, its single greatest asset, has a hole in exactly
the shape of the people who chose to go. `[new]`

**The failure state of the core value.** The promise is *nothing is lost.* **It becomes impossible to keep the
moment something is destroyed rather than ended** — and the culture has no ritual for that, because it has
never happened. **The refusal is the stronger half:** Zhongshan would not ritualize it. It would repair what
could be repaired and decline to mark the rest.

**Observance — one small and unserious, per `03` Phase 6E.** The **first performance night of the Long Winter
Suite** doubles as an ordinary, slightly silly social occasion: people arrive early, eat too much preserved
fish, and talk through the opening movement, which everyone has heard every year of their lives. **Nobody
treats it as solemn except visitors.** `[new]`

# PHASE 7 — ORDER

**7a — Work.** Precision manufacture; Prydz Bay maritime logistics and icebreaker servicing; fishing;
fermentation and preservation; the archive. **Non-thematic export, per `03`:** **preserved fish.** Ordinary,
emotionally neutral, unrelated to the headline identity — and it proves the economy is real rather than
allegorical.

**7b — Governance.** **The most productive question is what is *unadministrable*** — and here it is exact:
**there is no office empowered to end anything.** Not by prohibition; the role simply was never created,
because the need never arose. **A decision to close, demolish, or deaccession has no venue.**

**7c — Transmission.** Apprenticeship in the Crossing Quarter, unbroken across the First Interwar. **What
becomes of those who cannot learn it:** they are not excluded, corrected, or told. **They are simply given
less to do, gradually, by people being kind.** `[new]`

**7d — Counterculture. `[new]` — and canon already supplied it, so the job is to explain why here.**

**The Loud Quarter is established canon:** a deliberately unofficial counterculture district existing openly
alongside the formal civic character, *"rather than a suppressed or hidden one."* **Formalize before
inventing** — the question is why *Zhongshan* was the city this happened to.

> **Because the Loud Quarter is where the non-prying norm is suspended.**
>
> Its content is not noise, rebellion, or vice. **It is that in the Loud Quarter you may ask.** You may ask
> where someone is from, what they keep at home, why they are unhappy, and whether something should stop.
> **It is the only address in the city where a proposal to end something can actually be received.**
>
> **This is `03` 7d's rarer second type** — a counterculture that **performs what the place cannot**, rather
> than refusing what it demands. `03` says to check for it deliberately *"wherever the capability reading has
> named a faculty the place lacks."* **The capability reading named ending, and here it is.**
>
> **And it explains the tolerance:** the Quarter is unsuppressed because **the city needs it and cannot say
> so.** Nobody planned it, nobody defends it, and nobody would describe it this way.

**Sanction proportionality.** Zhongshan has no exclusion mechanism to price, **because exclusion is a form of
ending.** The heaviest available sanction is the one already described: *being gradually given less to do.*
**It has no name, no appeal, and no reversal** — and it is not perceived as a sanction at all.

# PHASE 9 — POPULATIONS

**Lens decision, stated explicitly per `03`:** the outer-city convention anchors robot culture in
**founding-nation** threads. **For Zhongshan that lens is unusually strong**, because the founding nation
thread was never broken — **robots here inherit the same continuous lineage humans do.**

**Inter-population relations — find the local inflection, not the national baseline.** The strongest available

> **Proposed Finding Z-4. `[new]`** **In Zhongshan the category that matters more than kind is *how long your
> line has been here.*** Continuity is the city's actual status axis, and it cuts across robot and human
> identically — a robot of unbroken local lineage and a human of unbroken local lineage share something neither
> shares with a recent arrival of their own kind. **The inter-kind question answers itself underneath it.**

> **Dual-tagged** per `03` 9D: **genuinely emergent** *(not inherited from the national baseline)* · **deep**

on the 481-year continuous-habitation exception, which no other Tepenian city has.
