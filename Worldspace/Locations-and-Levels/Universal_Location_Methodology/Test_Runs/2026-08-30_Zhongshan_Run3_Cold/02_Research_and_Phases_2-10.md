# STEP 3 — RESEARCH, AIMED AT THE DEFICIT

**The deficit Phase 1 named:** *no forcing function — therefore no signal that a practice has lapsed.*
**Research was run only after the profile named it** *(Gate G)*.

## 3.1 The deficit research — real cultures that MANUFACTURE a forcing function

**The move (`00_RUNBOOK.md` 3.1): the profile says what the place cannot do; find a real culture that has it,
and the contrast writes the finding.** Two were found, and they are the same instrument at different scales.

**Ise Jingū — the Shikinen Sengū.** The shrine has been demolished and rebuilt on an adjacent site **every
twenty years for ~1,300 years**, alternating between two sacred plots. **Its stated primary purpose is not
piety but transmission**: the rebuild is what keeps the carpentry, the sacred-treasure making and the apparel
crafts alive, because the skills must be exercised to survive. *"Repeated rebuilding renders sanctuaries
eternal."*

**Beating the bounds.** An annual walk of a parish boundary, tapping each marker — stone, post, wall, tree —
with sticks. **Before maps, it was the mechanism by which boundary knowledge crossed a generation**: old
parishioners walked with young ones, and **children's heads were bumped against the boundary stones so the
location would be imprinted in memory.**

> ### The contrast that writes the finding
>
> **Both are scheduled events whose real function is to detect and prevent silent lapse** — and both exist in
> places where nothing else would have raised the alarm. A skill nobody exercises does not announce that it has
> died; a boundary nobody walks does not announce that it has been forgotten. **So the culture builds a clock.**
>
> **Zhongshan's deficit is precisely the absence of such a clock**, and — per LAW 0's rule that the third-order
> answer is where a place stops resembling anywhere else — **the interesting question is not whether Zhongshan
> would build one. It is what a place with no natural deadline would hang its artificial one on.**

# PHASE 2 — COMPOSITION & ARRIVAL

## ⭐ Finding I — Zhongshan was not founded. It was joined.

**A rank-1 canon fact, reconciled against a rank-2 one, per `00_RUNBOOK.md` §E question 2.**

**The contradiction, stated rather than smoothed:**

| Source | Rank | Says |
|---|---|---|
| `No_National_Stereotypes.md` §17 *(universe repo, binding, cross-project)* | **1** | Zhongshan **"stayed under continuous Chinese/Sinian habitation throughout the First Interwar Period"** — 2083–2564, ~481 years — one of exactly two named exceptions to the rotating-operator model |
| `Specs/Zhongshan.md` | 2 | **"Founding population:** Sinian Federation exiles," settled post-Falkland Treaty on Zhongshan Station infrastructure |

**Both are true, and they are about different objects at different scales** *(`02` §5.3)*. The continuously
resident population was a **station population — small, on the order of a few hundred.** The 2564 exile wave was
**the mass arrival**, orders of magnitude larger. `Specs` describes the wave; the binding rule describes the
residents who were already standing there when it landed.

> **The consequence, and it makes Zhongshan structurally unlike every other city in the Federation.**
> **Everywhere else, exiles arrived at a maintained but empty station.** The rotating-operator model exists
> precisely to explain how the infrastructure was still working with nobody's institution attached to it.
> **At Zhongshan, the exiles arrived somewhere people already lived.**
>
> **And the arriving population included robots.** Every other Tepenian city's humans and robots arrived
> *together*, as co-exiles, into an empty place — a shared beginning. **Zhongshan's robots arrived into a
> human town that had been there for five centuries.**

**Third-order.** The long-resident population held ~481 years of accumulated, specific, local knowledge — which
lake freezes first, which rock face sheds ice in October, where the katabatic runs. **And from the first day of
the exile era they were a rounding error in their own town.** They were not resented, excluded, or overruled.
**They were simply outnumbered, immediately and permanently, by people who were glad to be there.**

> **This is the spine's origin event.** The one group positioned to notice things had no demographic weight to
> be noticed *with* — and there was never a conflict about it, because there was never a vote. **Zhongshan's
> characteristic failure begins here: not a silencing, but an absence of amplification.**

## Finding IV — who is not here *(`03` Phase 2 step E)*

**Nobody from the Peninsula.** Zhongshan's eighteen communities contain **no Argentina, no Chile, no Uruguay,
no Brazil, no USA** — the founding-wave nations of the entire Palmer subnet. The Federation's largest single
national population is American *(2.53 million)* and **Zhongshan has none of it.**

picture of "what a Tepenian is like" is drawn entirely from one half of the continent.

# PHASE 3 — SURFACE & TEXTURE

## Finding V — A city on a hundred and thirty islands, holding a hundred and fifty separate waters

**Formalize first, then extend.** Canon establishes bedrock foundation and a semi-gridlike order laid down early.
**The research supplies what canon never had: the actual shape of the ground.**

**Zhongshan is not built on a site. It is built across an archipelago** — two large peninsulas, three small ones,
about 130 islands, ~40 km² total, with about 150 lakes distributed through it and **no drainage between them.**

**Sensory sub-fields, answered separately** *(`03` step B — do not collapse them)*:

- **Sight.** **Water everywhere and none of it moving.** No river, no current, no falls — a hundred and fifty
  still surfaces at a hundred and fifty different levels. In winter they are the flattest, whitest things in the
  city; in the thaw each goes at its own rate, so the city has **no single date on which spring arrives.**
- **Sound.** **Quiet in a specific and unusual way: there is no water noise.** Every other coastal Tepenian city
  has surf. Zhongshan has a bay that freezes and lakes that do not flow. **What fills the gap is ice** —
  thermal cracking across the lakes at night, which is loud, irregular, and comes from no fixed direction.
- **Smell.** Mineral and saline rather than organic. Sea spray reaches inland across the low ground; the eastern
  lakes carry a genuine sea smell that the western ones do not — **the Dålk glacier's calving surges push
  seawater into them.** *The city smells different at its two ends, and the reason is a glacier nobody sees.*
- **Feel.** **Rock underfoot, not ice, not decking.** Zhongshan is one of the few places in Tepenia where the
  ground is simply *ground*.

**The seam** *(step D — the two-stage lens)*. Not a defensive seam and not a crisis seam. **The seam is the
waterline of the founding wave**: a small, old, tight, five-century-old core — and everything else, built fast
and to one plan, when the population multiplied.

## Finding VI — An architectural monoculture nobody chose *(Yekaterinburg, fused)*

**Yekaterinburg holds the highest concentration of Constructivist architecture on Earth — 140+ structures — not
because it legislated a style, but because it built enormously in one短 window while one approach was the
obvious one.** The Uralmash ensemble and Chekists' Village are whole neighborhoods produced by one idea arriving
at one moment with enough money behind it.

**Zhongshan's version, and the mechanism is the plurality ratio from Phase 1.** When the founding wave
multiplied the city's population, there was **one group at 35.83% and no second group above 9.70%** — so there
was never a competing proposal to accommodate. **The city was built fast, by people who agreed, because the
question of whether to agree never arose.**

> **The result is a city of remarkable visual consistency that nobody designed and nobody imposed.** Ask a
> resident why the buildings match and the honest answer is *"they were built at the same time"* — which is
> true and is not an explanation. **The consistency is the physical form of the deficit: this is what a place
> looks like when nothing forces a second opinion.**

**Player-facing** *(§0b)*: **seen** immediately, from anywhere; **entered**, because the repetition is
navigational — a stranger cannot tell districts apart by their buildings and must learn the *water* instead.

# PHASE 4 — ORDINARY LIFE

**Headline function, in one sentence:** *Prydz Bay maritime logistics and scientific research.*
**Now write away from it** *(`03` Phase 4 step A)*.

## ⭐ Finding VII — The Round: the city's manufactured clock

**This is the pass's central finding.** It fuses the deficit *(no signal of lapse)*, the hydrology
*(150 unconnected lakes)*, and the forcing-function research *(Ise Jingū, beating the bounds)*.

**Necessity first, meaning after** *(Technique 6)*. The lakes are the freshwater supply, and their chemistry
**genuinely varies and genuinely changes** — sea spray, local geology, and periodic seawater intrusion when the
Dålk glacier calves. **A city drinking from 150 unconnected sources has to check them individually, because
there is no downstream to check instead.** So a circuit exists: a fixed order, a fixed interval, someone walks
it, reads each water, moves on.

**That is the whole of the practical justification, and it is sufficient.** Everything below is what the
practice became.

**Second order — the circuit is the only thing in Zhongshan that touches every part of the city on a schedule.**
Nothing else does. The trades are local. The neighborhoods are separate. The plurality's default needs no
meetings. **The Round arrives, and its arrival is the only regular external contact most of the city has.**

**⭐ Third order — what The Round actually verifies has drifted from the water to the people, and nobody has
said so.** The reading takes minutes. The walk takes days. **What the walker actually accumulates is a picture
of who was where, what was still running, and what had quietly stopped** — the shop that is shuttered on a
working day, the maintained thing that is no longer maintained, the person who used to be at that lake and is
not.

> **The Round is Zhongshan's answer to a problem it has never named.** It is the city's only mechanism for
> noticing that something has lapsed — and it is filed, in every official sense, as **water quality
> monitoring.** Nobody in Zhongshan would describe it as a social institution. **Ask, and they will tell you
> about salinity.**

**Fourth order.** The people who hold The Round are therefore **the only residents with a whole-city view** —
not officials, not elected, not senior. Just whoever has the circuit. **And because the role is understood as
technical, that view is not treated as authority and is never asked for.** *(Role, not name — Phase 10
discipline.)*

**Player-facing, in five channels:** **entered** (the circuit is a literal route through the whole map) ·
**done** (the player can walk a leg, or skip one) · **spoken** (a walker will tell you what has changed, if you
ask about the *water*) · **hooked** (the fastest way to find out what is wrong anywhere in Zhongshan is to read
a Round log) · **and the violation is the gameplay** — a leg not walked is a part of the city nobody has looked
at.

## Finding VIII — The four elements of an ordinary day *(`03` Phase 4 step B — all four required)*

- **Routine.** Days are organized around **thaw state, not clock or shift.** Which crossings are passable
  changes through the year as lakes freeze and open at their own individual rates. **A Zhongshan resident's
  commute is seasonal in a way no other Tepenian city's is** — the same two points are eight minutes apart in
  August and forty in December.
- **Mundane concern.** **Which way round.** Because the ground is an archipelago, almost every trip has two
  plausible routes of similar length, and the choice is genuinely re-made each time. Residents hold strong,
  unexamined, entirely trivial opinions about this.
- **Personal struggle.** **Nothing here tells you that you are falling behind.** No shift bell, no quota, no
  visible failure state. **A person can stop doing what they used to do — a trade, a habit, a friendship — and
  encounter no consequence at all for a long time.** The struggle is not hardship. **It is that effort and
  drift feel identical from the inside.**
- **Escapism / downtime.** **The lakes, unromantically.** Swimming is not possible; skating, walking out onto a
  frozen surface, and sitting on rock above one are. **The characteristic Zhongshan leisure is going somewhere
  slightly out of the way and staying there a while** — which is available because the terrain supplies a
  hundred and thirty places to be slightly out of the way in.

**Different populations, different days** *(step C)*. The long-resident families' day is organized around
things they have always done; the founding-wave majority's around the built city; the seventeen smaller
communities' around each other. **These do not conflict. They simply do not intersect, and nothing in the city
makes them.**

**What the rhythm fails to provide** *(step E)*: **anyone whose life needs a deadline.** Zhongshan supplies none.

# PHASE 5 — RELATION & GEOMETRY

**Weighted per `03`'s clustered-location column — this is the rare dense case, and it is declared exceptional.**

**5a — Peers.** *Mechanism, not rivalry.* **What each side refuses to develop, and who supplies it instead:**
Zhongshan does not grow food and **Davis does** — 340 km down Hwy 110, the Federation's breadbasket. **The

because it has never once been interrupted or bargained over. *(Compare `03` Phase 5e: a dependency nobody has
named is a shadow; a dependency everyone has named is politics. **This one is a shadow.**)*

**5b — The three-way set: run against Zhongshan's own eras, per the substitute**, not against its neighbours —
whose material is withheld. **Category chosen first: how the city learns that something has stopped.**

| Era | How lapse is detected |
|---|---|
| **The long residence** *(2083–2564)* | **Directly.** A few hundred people; everything is witnessed. `01` §2.2's Band-1 condition — **no unobserved behavior is possible** |
| **The founding wave** *(2564 onward)* | **Not at all, and nobody notices the loss.** Population multiplies past every threshold at once. The witnessing that had worked for five centuries silently stops working, because the mechanism was *smallness* and smallness is gone |
| **Now** *(Orbital Era)* | **By proxy, through a water-testing circuit that nobody classifies as social** |

> **The substitute did real work, and this is worth recording for the methodology: the three-era set produced a
> sharper result here than a three-city set would have.** The eras differ on the chosen axis *by construction* —
> they are the same place under three population regimes — whereas three neighbouring cities differ on many axes
> at once and the comparison has to be disentangled. **`01` §5.3a calls own-eras "the strongest substitute."
> On this evidence it may not be a substitute at all.**

**5c — The parent. PRIMARY for an isolated location, and near-null here** *(a recorded null, per `03` §0.2.4)*.
The Mirny subnet requires nothing of Zhongshan that Zhongshan uniquely supplies, and supplies nothing Zhongshan
could not obtain otherwise. **This is *covered in substance, absent in the expected form*, not a hole:** the
reason for the emptiness is that both parents are unwritten *(provisional assumptions 1–4)*, and assumption 4 is
registered precisely because **if the subnet does adjudicate between member cities, this section becomes the
most important one in the pass and the deficit's address moves from `diffuse` to `in the parent`.**

**5d — The edge and the crossing.** **Arrival types are unusually varied for a Tepenian city** — three highways,
a shared airport, and a seaport. **And every one of them arrives at somewhere shared.** The junction belongs to
three cities; the airport belongs to three cities. **The first thing a visitor to Zhongshan meets is not
Zhongshan.**

**Membership** *(Technique 10 — mechanism, never ceremony)*: **you are local once a leg of The Round is yours.**
Nobody grants it. A walker stops doing a leg; someone nearby picks it up; a year later the list has your name on
it. **There is no announcement and often no awareness that it has happened** — which is the technique's own
requirement, and it falls straight out of the local economy rather than being invented for the slot.

**5e — Dependency, and both directions written.** Outward: food from Davis; manufactured goods along the same
road. **Inward — and `03` insists both halves be written:** *what does anyone out there think about Zhongshan?*
**Very little, and specifically:** it is the far end of Hwy 110. **The Coastal Cut Highway's two endpoints are
the capital and here** — so to the rest of the Federation, Zhongshan's meaning is largely *the place the road
stops.* **A city that matters intensely to itself and is, elsewhere, mostly a terminus on a map.**

# PHASE 6 — MEANING

**Step A first: run the Naming technique before generating anything.** Sweeping the admissible canon for
religious-register language attached to non-religious objects returns **almost nothing.** Zhongshan's own
material is notably secular in register.

> **So the load-bearing thing here is not a belief. It is a COMPACT** *(`03` Phase 6A's own alternative)* —
> *what the place agreed to and never wrote down.*

## Finding IX — The compact: *you will be here when it is your turn*

**The whole of Zhongshan's civic morality reduces to one unstated obligation, and it is The Round's.** Not
honesty, not diligence, not hospitality — **presence on a schedule.** The city's single genuinely shared
practice is a rota, and the only way to fail it is to not be there.

**Counter-check, per `03` Phase 6A: the name must not be portable.** *Would this work for a sibling?* **No** —
it requires a place whose parts do not connect, whose schedule is self-imposed, and whose failure mode is
absence rather than error. **A city with a shift bell already has presence enforced and needs no compact
about it.**

## Finding X — The Failure State of the Core Value, and the refusal

**The promise:** *nothing here is left unlooked-at.*
**It becomes impossible when:** the thing that lapsed is not on the circuit. A practice held by one household,
a trade with one practitioner, a person who is not near a lake.
**What grew up around it:** nothing. **And the refusal is the finding.**

> **Zhongshan has never extended The Round beyond the water.** It could. Nobody has proposed it. **The city
> that built an accidental instrument for noticing loss has never once pointed it at anything it actually
> cares about**, because doing so would require admitting that the instrument is not about water — and that
> sentence has never been said out loud by anyone.

## Finding XI — Death, written *around* the mechanism *(RESERVED — not decided here)*

**The disposal-of-the-dead mechanism is developer-reserved and is not invented.** *(`05` §3.)* What is written
is the practice around it:

- **The record and the rite are separate**, and Zhongshan keeps the record well and the rite barely at all.
- **Who is not mourned properly:** *(and `03` calls this the most productive of the death questions)* —
  **the ones whose absence was noticed late.** In a city where lapse is detected on a circuit's interval, some
  deaths are found on a schedule rather than at the time. **There is no shame attached and no ritual to repair
  it.** It is simply a known category, and the city has never developed language for it.

## Finding XII — Observance: one, and it is small and unserious *(step E requires this)*

**The Turn of the Round** — the day the circuit's order resets. **It is not a festival.** It is an evening on
which walkers hand over their legs, and the handover has accreted the mildest possible ceremony: the outgoing
walker tells one true and boring thing about their lake that is not in the log. **It is genuinely enjoyed and
completely unimportant**, which is what `03` step E asks for.

# PHASE 7 — ORDER

**7a — Work, and the non-thematic export.** The headline economy is maritime logistics and research. **The
non-thematic export, per Technique 9: cut and dressed stone.** Zhongshan sits on orthogneiss, pegmatite and
granite and had to quarry to build; **the surplus goes out along Hwy 110 and ends up as thresholds, kerbs and
counter-slabs in cities that have no rock of their own.** Ordinary, emotionally neutral, unrelated to the
headline function — and it proves the economy is real rather than allegorical. **A resident of Mirny owns a
Zhongshan doorstep and has never thought about where it came from.**

**7b — Governance, and the thing that is unadministrable.** Decision-making is the plurality's default,
exercised without machinery. **What cannot be administered: a dispute between two parts of the city that do not
share anything.** There is no procedure, because there has never been a case that produced one. **The city is
not ungoverned; it is under-governed in exactly one dimension, and has never needed the missing part.**

**7c — Knowledge and transmission, and what becomes of those who cannot learn.** Trades pass by proximity — you
learn from whoever is nearest, because the archipelago makes *nearest* a strong constraint. **The consequence is
that skill distribution is geographic rather than meritocratic**, and **someone who cannot learn the trade near
them has to physically move to learn a different one** — which, in a city with four cheap exits, frequently
means leaving instead. *(This is the Mentor's standing question, and it is answered before the panel asks it.)*

**7d — Counterculture: derived from the specific pressure** *(Technique 11 — the precise negative)*.
**What Zhongshan requires of everyone: presence on a schedule, and agreement by default.**
**Who cannot or will not give it:** people who want a decision to be *made* rather than defaulted into.

> ### Finding XIII — The Standing Objection *(Užupis, fused — form borrowed, scene discarded)*

> **Užupis supplies the form: a self-declared polity occupying one neighborhood, with a constitution written in
> three hours, and a bridge where you are admitted only if you accept being part of the performance.**
>
> **Zhongshan's version is not artistic and not bohemian** *(divergence stated)*. It is **procedural parody**:
> a group who have constituted themselves as a formal opposition **to nothing in particular**, with offices, a
> charter, minutes, and a standing objection lodged against whatever the city has most recently done by default.
> **They are entirely sincere about the procedure and entirely aware that it has no force.**
>
> **They exist because dissent here has nowhere to go** *(Phase 1, G8's grudging tolerance)*. Unable to form a
> bloc — there is no second bloc to form — **they perform the mechanism the city lacks.** `03` Phase 7d's rarer
> and likelier-to-be-missed option: **not refusal, but performing what the place cannot.**
>
> **And they are the only body in Zhongshan that keeps minutes of decisions.** Which means the parody archive
> is, in practice, **the city's only record of what was decided and when** — a fact nobody has noticed, because
> the record is filed as a joke. *(Technique 8, Asymmetric Record-Keeping: what an outsider would wrongly
> conclude from the surviving record is that Zhongshan was governed by its comedians.)*
>
> **Kept sympathetic, not criminal** *(Technique 11's binding rule)*. Nobody is harmed. Nobody is opposed.

**Is the contradiction between groups, or inside each person?** *(`03` Phase 7d's final check.)* **Inside each
person, mostly** — the same resident finds the default convenient and the absence of a decision faintly
disquieting. **Which is why the Standing Objection is small and tolerated rather than large and resented.**

**Sanction proportionality** *(`00d`, hard check)*. **Zhongshan has no exclusion-shaped sanction, and inventing
one would have been wrong.** Priced in this location's conditions: with four cheap exits and a mild climate,
exclusion here is not lethal — **it is just leaving, which people already do freely.** **The city has no penalty
because it has no leverage**, and that is a truthful and characterizing answer rather than a gap.

# PHASE 8 — MAKING

**Native layer first, transplanted second** *(Technique 12)*.

- **Cuisine — and the axis is NOT one already used elsewhere.** `03` lists eleven district food axes, all taken.
  **Zhongshan's axis is *which water it was made with*.** With 150 lakes of genuinely different chemistry, the
  same preparation differs measurably across the city, and residents can taste it. **Food here is identified by
  provenance-of-water the way a wine is by slope.** It is unpretentious and entirely ordinary to them.
  ⚠ **`Robot_Biology_and_Culture/` is the mandatory check before any siligel/coolant claim** — **no such claim
  is made here**, and the robot population's version is written in Phase 9 rather than assumed.
- **Music.** **Ice, not voice.** The lakes crack thermally at night, loudly and irregularly. **Zhongshan's
  native music is built around that** — long-form, non-metrical, with performers waiting on the ice rather than
  keeping time against it. *(This is the one place the pass touches the cluster's "Alternative Culture"
  reputation, and it deliberately does not resolve whether that reputation is Zhongshan's — **RESERVED.**)*
- **Dress — function first, per step B, and general population, per `00b`.** The daily requirement is **walking
  on rock and on ice on the same trip**, repeatedly, in mild-but-not-warm conditions. **The universal item is
  therefore footwear, and specifically the practice of re-soling rather than replacing** — a whole-city habit,
  visible on everyone, not a trade's uniform.
- **Language.** **The speech marker is directional.** With no grid legible enough to navigate by and no
  through-route, Zhongshan gives directions **by water** — *"past the second one, the one that opens early"* —
  and the habit does not survive contact with other cities. **It is the single fastest way to identify a
  Zhongshan native anywhere in Tepenia.**
- **Play.** **Route-argument as a genuine pastime** — the "which way round" question from Phase 4, formalized
  into a low-stakes running dispute that nobody wants settled.

# PHASE 9 — POPULATIONS

**The lens decision, stated explicitly** *(`03` Phase 9's requirement)*. **Neither founding-nation nor
theme/role. Zhongshan's non-dominant population organizes around *arrival order*.**

## ⭐ Finding XIV — The category that matters more than kind

**`03` Phase 9C: the strongest available move is that the place reorganizes its population along a different
axis entirely — *is there a category here that matters more than kind?*** **Here, yes.**

**Zhongshan's robots arrived in 2564 into a town that already existed** *(Finding I)* — unlike every other
Tepenian city, where humans and robots arrived together into an empty one. **So the salient distinction in
Zhongshan is not human/robot. It is *whether your people were here before the wave*.**

**And that line does not run between kinds — it runs across them.** The long-resident families are human. The
founding wave was both. **A robot and a human who both arrived in 2564 have more in common, in Zhongshan's own
terms, than either has with a family whose ancestors watched them arrive.**

> **The consequence, and it is the inter-population finding:** **Zhongshan has an unusually relaxed
> human–robot relationship, and it is an accident.** The axis that organizes the city was fixed before robots
> were a category anyone here had to think about. **Nobody decided to be egalitarian. The available distinction
> was simply already occupied.**

**Dual tags** *(step D)*: **inheritance = genuinely emergent** *(the arrival-order axis exists nowhere else)*;
**depth = deep** *(it governs an unstated social sorting, not a surface custom)*.
**Swap test** *(step E)*: **fails to survive a swap, correctly** — no other Tepenian city has a pre-exile
resident population for the axis to be built on.

**Where the long-resident layer is visible at all:** in The Round. **They hold a disproportionate share of the
circuit**, because the legs nobody else wanted were the awkward old ones near the original station, and those
families never stopped walking them. **Nobody has read anything into this.**

# PHASE 10 — CATALOG

**Governing premise: a place is built around a theme, not a thing.** Named places and things are named; **people
are role-archetypes only, permanently** *(binding — `05` §3)*.

**Named places**
- **The Round** — the circuit itself; a route, not a building, and the city's most important institution.
- **The Turn** — where the circuit's order resets; a plain room near the old core, used one evening a year.
- **The Eastern Waters** — the lakes the Dålk glacier's surges reach; brackish, different-smelling, first to
  break up.
- **The Standing Objection's chambers** — a small, scrupulously-maintained office with a brass plate,
  minute-books going back generations, and no authority whatsoever.
- **The Quarter of One Trade** *(Yekaterinburg, fused — Chekists' Village)* — a neighborhood built in one go for
  the quarry workers and their families, still housing their descendants, still recognizably a single design.

**Physical things**
- **A re-soled boot** — the near-universal worn object; wear pattern reads as age and as route.
- **A Round log** — a bound water-reading book. **The city's real chronicle, filed as a utility record.**
- **A dressed stone kerb** — the non-thematic export, seen in other cities, unremarked.
- **A route-token** — a small worn marker carried to indicate which way you came; used in the route-argument,
  entirely trivial, universally understood.

**People as role-archetypes** *(no invented proper names)*
- **A walker holding several legs** — knows more about the state of the city than anyone, is asked about
  salinity.
- **A long-resident family's eldest** — carries five centuries of local specificity and no standing.
- **The Standing Objection's clerk** — keeps the only minutes in the city, sincerely, as a joke.
- **A quarry foreman** — runs the export nobody thinks about.
- **A newcomer three years in** — has been handed a leg and has not registered that this made them a local.

**Settings**
- **A crossing that is a bridge in winter and a boat in summer**, and the argument about when it changes.
- **A shuttered workshop on a working day**, seen from the circuit, that nobody has yet asked about.
- **A lakeside sit** — rock above still water, somebody there for no reason, the characteristic downtime image.

**Significant events** — **The year two legs went unwalked** *(a real gap in the logs, cause unrecorded)*.
**Notable figures** — placeholders only, per the binding rule.
