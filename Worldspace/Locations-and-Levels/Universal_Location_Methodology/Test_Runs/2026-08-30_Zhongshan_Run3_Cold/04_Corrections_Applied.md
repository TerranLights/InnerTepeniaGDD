# Zhongshan — Run 3 (cold) — Corrections forced by the gates

**Eight corrections, from five gate fires.** Written as a separate document rather than edited silently into the
pass, so that **what the gates actually changed is visible** — which is the point of running them on an
untested instrument.

---

## C-1 *(Gate 11 — scale)* — Zhongshan is a dense city, and the pass wrote a scattered one

**The error.** Every texture claim in Phases 3, 4 and 7 was written as though Zhongshan were a settlement
scattered across an open archipelago — lakeside sits, walks slightly out of the way, a circuit taking days.

**The arithmetic.** **996,684 people on ~40 km² of ice-free ground = 24,917 per km².** Denser than Paris,
approaching Manhattan. **~6,645 people per lake.**

**The correction, and it makes the material better rather than weaker:**

> **The lakes are not out in a landscape. They are inside the city, hemmed by it.** A Zhongshan lake is a
> **public square that happens to be water** — overlooked on all sides, crossed by whoever is going somewhere,
> busy. In winter the frozen ones are the only large flat open spaces in a very crowded place, and they are
> used accordingly: as thoroughfares, as markets, as the only ground where a crowd can stand.
>
> **The islands are city blocks.** ~130 of them. Getting anywhere means crossings, and crossings are
> chokepoints — which is why "which way round" is a real daily question and not a rambler's musing.
>
> **The mildness now reads differently, too.** −9.9 °C mean and a 0 °C summer month, in a city this dense,
> means **the outdoors is genuinely usable and genuinely crowded.** Zhongshan is not a sealed interior city
> like Concordia. **It is a street city, in the cold.**

**And the same correction applied to Phase 4's escapism**, which had been "going somewhere slightly out of the
way." **In a city of 24,917 per km² there is nowhere out of the way.** The corrected answer:
**escapism here is going somewhere *empty*, and the only reliably empty places are the far ends of frozen
lakes in the dark months.** Which is available, unromantic, free, and specific to this city.

## C-2 *(Gate 11 — scale)* — The Round is a distributed rota, not one person's walk

**The error.** The pass wrote *"someone walks it"* and *"the walk takes days."* **A single circuit cannot serve
a million people**, and both source comparanda are parish-scale and shrine-scale.

**The correction — scale it the way parishes actually scale, by multiplying rather than lengthening:**

> **The Round is ~150 waters, each divided into several legs, held by **several hundred** walkers.** Nobody
> walks the whole thing; nobody ever has. **A leg is small — one water, one frequency, a reading that takes
> minutes and a walk of a few streets.**
>
> **The whole-city view therefore belongs to nobody, and that is the sharper version of the finding.** What
> exists instead is **the log**, which aggregates what several hundred people separately noticed. **The city's
> only instrument for detecting lapse is a document that no single person reads end to end.**

**This strengthens the spine rather than weakening it.** A place with no forcing function now also has **no
single reader of its own only record** — the drift is not merely undetected, it is *distributed past the point
where anyone could detect it.*

## C-3 *(Gate 2 — general population)* — Music, rewritten from the general population outward

**The error.** The pass gave **performers waiting on the ice, playing non-metrically against thermal cracking**
as Zhongshan's music. **That is a performance practice.** `00b` names music among its worst offenders and states
plainly that a place's professional performers are not its musical culture.

**The general answer, written properly:**

> **Zhongshan's general musical culture is domestic, vocal, and unaccompanied — and it is loud.** In a city at
> this density, with thin construction over rock, **everyone can hear their neighbours**, and the ordinary
> response to that across two and a half centuries has not been quiet. **Singing over each other is normal,
> non-competitive, and completely unremarkable to residents** — a household's noise is not an intrusion because
> everyone's is audible and nobody's is private.
>
> **What a visitor notices within an hour** is that a Zhongshan street at evening has several unrelated songs
> audible at once from different windows, and **nobody is coordinating and nobody minds.**

**The narrow case is retained, explicitly scoped, on top of the general answer** — as `00b` step 3 requires:

> **Specifically for the small number of people who perform on the ice** — not the city default — the practice
> is to wait for the lake's own thermal cracking and play around it. **It is a minority practice, and residents
> regard it as slightly precious.**

> **Note for the differentiation instrument at Step 7:** the axis Zhongshan's music answers on is
> **audibility-without-privacy**, not performance. Check it against siblings.

## C-4 *(Gate 9, second pass)* — ⭐ Finding XV: there is no way to ask to belong

**Forced by Gate 9's own-material pass**, which `04` warns is the half that usually goes unrun.

> ### Finding XV — The membership mechanism has no application, and no route in
>
> **Phase 5d established that you become a local when a leg of The Round becomes yours.** Legs are never
> granted — **they are picked up**, when someone nearby stops walking one and a neighbour quietly takes it on.
>
> **So membership requires being near a lapse at the right moment.** And a person whose part of the city is
> well covered — because its walkers are reliable, because nothing there has gone wrong — **is never handed
> anything.** There is no register, no waiting list, no office, **and no way to ask**, because asking presumes
> a granter and there isn't one.
>
> **These people are not excluded. They are not resented, refused, or even identified.** They live in
> Zhongshan for decades, are perfectly well-liked, and remain — in the only sense the city actually operates —
> **not from here.** And because the mechanism has no author, **nobody has ever perceived this as a problem
> anyone could fix.**
>
> **`00d`'s three tests:** unintended ✓ · unnoticed in-world ✓ · discoverable rather than announced ✓.
> **Non-malice** ✓ — it works with everyone acting in good faith, and there is nobody to blame.
>
> **The cruel symmetry, and it is the finding's point: the surest route to belonging in Zhongshan is to live
> somewhere that is going slightly wrong.**

**Player-facing:** **spoken** (a long-resident non-member will say they're "not really from here" and be unable
to explain why) · **hooked** (a player can *cause* a leg to become available, or hand over their own) · **done**
(the player themselves becomes local by this mechanism, without being told).

## C-5 *(Gate I — Inflected under-used)* — what Zhongshan does with a national observance

**The error.** Seven Originated elements, one Inflected. `01` §5.1 predicted this exact ratio as the diagnostic
of a pass working harder for a worse result. **The pass never asked what Zhongshan does with a Federation-wide
observance.**

> ### Finding XVI — The city that observes national days by not stopping
>
> **`National_Holidays.md` establishes Federation-wide civic observances.** Zhongshan's inflection of them is
> **structural rather than attitudinal, and it is a direct consequence of The Round.**
>
> **A national holiday is a day on which the ordinary rhythm stops. Zhongshan's ordinary rhythm is a rota that
> cannot stop** — waters do not observe holidays, and a leg missed is a reading lost. **So Zhongshan is the
> city where a national day is worked through by whoever is on their leg, and celebrated afterwards, by them,
> late.**
>
> **The result is that Zhongshan observes every national holiday twice** — once on the day, thinly, by the
> majority who are not walking; and once in the following days, in scattered small gatherings, by walkers
> coming off their legs. **Nobody planned the doubling. Nobody experiences it as a grievance.** And the
> second, later, smaller version is the one residents actually mean when they talk about the holiday.

**Class: Inflected** — the parent supplies the form, the location supplies its version. **This is exactly the
class the gate said was being skipped, and it produced a better finding than a wholly invented one would have.**

## C-6 *(Gate F — Band 4)* — pattern the uniform claims

**The error.** `01` §5.4: a Band 4+ pass must answer **patterned**, not **uniform**, and *"a Band 4+ pass that
answers everything as Uniform has not been written at its own scale."* The pass asserted a universal footwear
item, one compact and one observance city-wide.

**The pattern, named:** **Zhongshan varies by *how far a neighbourhood is from a water*, and by nothing else
that matters.**

| Category | Uniform / Patterned / Delegated |
|---|---|
| Re-soled footwear | **Patterned.** Universal among the ~40% of the city whose daily route crosses ice; **near-absent in the dense interior blocks**, where residents cross no lake in an ordinary week and wear ordinary boots |
| The compact *(presence on your turn)* | **Patterned.** Binding and felt where legs are held; **an abstraction in the interior**, where many residents know no walker personally |
| The Turn observance | **Patterned.** A real evening near the waters; **not observed at all in parts of the city** |
| Food-by-water-provenance | **Patterned, and it is the sharpest axis:** strongly held near the waters, and in the interior it becomes **a market claim rather than a taste** — people buy "eastern water" bread on the label, and could not tell blind |
| Climate · currency · calendar · robot personhood | **Determined** by the parent — correctly not varied |

> **And the pattern names the city's real internal division, which the pass had missed entirely: Zhongshan is
> split between *the waters* and *the interior*, and the interior is the majority.** The Round, the compact and
> the observance are all **waterside institutions that the interior participates in at second hand.**
> **The city's central institution does not reach most of its population** — which is not a contradiction of the
> spine but its completion.

## C-7 *(Gate 1)* — the prohibition null, explained rather than merely absent

**`prohibit=0`.** The pass argued Zhongshan has no *sanction* because it has no *leverage* — four cheap exits, a
mild climate, exclusion costing nothing. **It never closed the loop to prohibitions.**

> **Recorded as a null, with its reason: Zhongshan has very few prohibitions, because a prohibition is only
> worth writing if something happens when it is broken.** With no sanction available, **a rule here is
> indistinguishable from a preference**, and the city has largely stopped producing them. **What it has instead
> is defaults** — and the difference between a default and a rule is that nobody notices when a default is not
> followed.

**This is *covered in substance, absent in form*, and now says why** — which is `03` §0.2.4's one-question test.

## C-8 *(Gates 3 and 4)* — two smaller corrections

**C-8a — the compact/no-deadline reconciliation, written into the text** rather than left as an apparent
contradiction: *Zhongshan has exactly one deadline; it is long, it is owed to one other person, and **the person
who misses it is not the person who finds out.*** Both claims stand and the tension is the point.

**C-8b — the route-argument demoted**, per Gate 4. It was the weakest finding under the swap test — any city on
complex terrain could hold trivial opinions about which way round. **It is no longer a named pastime.** It
survives as one line of texture inside the crossings/chokepoints material, where the archipelago actually earns
it.
