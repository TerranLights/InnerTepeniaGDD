## Panel A — the six Flat Archetypes

### The Child — *addition*

> *"Everything here is a list somebody else is on. The waters have people who look after them and I'm not one
> and I won't be for years. Where's the bit that's mine? Not a place they let me into. A place that's mine."*

**Disposition: `accepted`** → folds into **C-1** rather than becoming a standalone finding *(`00f` §8.7 — an
equally good outcome)*.

**Zhongshan's answer already existed and the pass had not noticed it was an answer.** In a city this dense, the
**frozen lakes are the only large flat open ground there is** — and for the months they hold, they are not
programmed, not owned, and not supervised. **They are the children's ground by default, because the adults are
using them to get somewhere and the children are already there.** The city never provided this and does not
think of it as provision.

### The Lover *(position)* — *addition*

> *"Everyone can hear everything. That's fine, that's home, I don't mind the singing. But there is nowhere in
> this entire city that two people can be that isn't somebody's route to somewhere."*

**Disposition: `accepted`** → folds into **C-1**.

**The answer is the same terrain and the opposite season: the far ends of the lakes, in the dark months.** Not
romantic — cold, unlit, and genuinely inconvenient. **Zhongshan's courtship happens in the two months a year
when the crowded places are unpleasant enough to be empty**, which is why the city's private life has a season
and its public life does not.

> ⚠ **Four-corner note, recorded honestly:** the Child's and the Lover's objections **converge on the same
> resource** — unprogrammed space — and were answered from the same terrain. **That is two positions producing
> one objection**, and `00f` §5 says to say so rather than count it twice.

### The Parent — *inconsistency*

> *"You have built a machine for noticing that something has stopped. It notices ice. It notices salt. If my
> daughter doesn't come back, that machine has nothing to say about it, and neither does anyone else, because
> the only thing this city checks on a schedule is water."*

**Disposition: `accepted` → new material, folded into Finding VII.**

**This is a genuine inconsistency and the pass had missed it.** Zhongshan has exactly one instrument for
detecting that something has lapsed, and **it is pointed at the least important thing it could be pointed at.**
The Parent is the only position that can see this, because the Parent is the only one holding something whose
absence would matter within hours rather than seasons.

**It sharpens Finding X's refusal considerably:** the city's failure to extend The Round beyond the water is not
merely an unexploited opportunity. **There is a category of loss it structurally cannot notice in time**, and no
one has ever put that sentence together.

### The Ruler — *doubt*

> *"Who pays for it? Nobody. Who reads the whole log? Nobody. Who can compel a leg to be walked? Nobody. And
> the food — we eat because Davis sends food down a road, and there is no agreement, no term, no price
> negotiated by anyone I could name. You have described a city that functions and shown me nothing holding it
> up."*

**Disposition: `refereed`** *(`00f` §4d — two positions right and both excessive)*, **against the pass itself.**

- **The Ruler is right** that a city of a million people with no administration of its central institution and
  no instrument governing its food supply is under-specified. **The pass overreaches in implying nothing
  administrative exists at all.**
- **The pass is right** that Zhongshan's characteristic property is the absence of a forcing function, and
  giving the Ruler a functioning apparatus to negotiate with would destroy the spine.

**The settlement:** **Zhongshan has ordinary municipal administration — collection, distribution, roads, the
harbour — and it is unremarkable, competent, and completely uninvolved in The Round.** The Round is not
administered because **nobody ever classified it as a civic function**; it is filed with water quality, and
water quality is a technical matter. **The food arrangement with Davis is similarly ordinary and similarly
unexamined: it is a standing logistics relationship nobody has ever had cause to renegotiate.**

> **The Ruler's real finding is not that these things are missing. It is that Zhongshan's administration
> administers everything except the one institution that actually holds the city together** — because that one
> has never been recognized as an institution.

### The Elder — *addition, and the strongest single objection of the run*

> *"You keep saying nobody notices when something stops. I notice. My grandmother walked two legs on the
> eastern waters and one of them hasn't been walked since before you were born, and I could take you to it.
> There's four of us left who could. Nobody has ever asked."*

**Disposition: `accepted` → new material, and it completes Finding I.**

**Finding I established that the long-resident families were outnumbered from day one and had no weight to be
heard with. The Elder supplies the consequence the pass had not reached:** they are not merely un-amplified.
**They are the city's only surviving record of what has already lapsed** — a living index of dropped legs,
abandoned crossings and discontinued practices, held in a handful of families, **entirely uncatalogued and never
consulted.**

> **The Round detects what stops *now*. The long-resident families remember what stopped *before*. Zhongshan
> has both halves of a memory and has never connected them** — which is the Unrecognized Instrument technique
> firing a third time, and this one is the most poignant because it is people rather than a process.

**Player-facing: this is the single best quest hook the pass produced.** An Elder can name what is missing; the
log can prove when; **the player can be the connection the city never made.**

### The Mentor — *doubt*

> *"Ask me how someone learns a trade here and I'll tell you: they stand next to someone doing it. Fine. Now
> ask me how someone learns to hold a leg. They don't. Nobody teaches it. It gets picked up. The one thing in
> this city that decides whether you're from here is the one thing there is no way to be taught."*

**Disposition: `accepted` → sharpens **Finding XV** (C-4).**

**The Mentor's standing question — *what becomes of the ones who cannot learn it?* — has an unusual answer
here: there is nothing to fail at.** There is no instruction, no standard, no examination and no rejection.
**Which is precisely why the exclusion is invisible:** a person who never gets a leg has not failed a test.
**They have simply never been near one.**

## The mandatory two

### The Passer-Through — the discoverability check *(`00d` test 3)*

> *"Came off the Twenty-Two about noon, stopped four hours, went on. Big place. Warmer than I expected. Loud —
> people singing out of windows in the middle of the day, and nobody looking up. Water everywhere, all of it
> flat and none of it going anywhere. Kids all over the ice. Bought a meal, it was fine. Nice enough. Wouldn't
> know what to tell you about it."*

**Verdict: PASS on both failure modes, and they are opposite ones.**

- **Do they see the shadow?** **No.** No drift, no exclusion, no un-walked legs, nothing sinister. **The shadow
  is discoverable rather than announced**, as `00d` requires.
- **Do they see nothing at all?** **No.** They see a specific, legible face — dense, mild, loud, water
  everywhere, children on the ice. **The city has a first impression.**

> **This is the strongest single confirmation the panel produced**, because these are the two failures that
> `00f` §6 says are opposite and that a pass can very easily commit either of.

### The Neighbor — *biased by design, and the bias is the instrument*

> *"They talk about the road ending at Zhongshan. It ends at all three of us. The junction is not theirs. The
> airport is not theirs. Hwy 110's endpoint has their name on it and they have been quietly acting like the
> whole convergence does, for two hundred years, and they are not even doing it on purpose — which is somehow
> worse."*

**Disposition: `noted` — real, deferred, and the reason is a limitation of this run.**

⚠ **Casting limitation, recorded per `00f`.** **This position could only be cast from admissible infrastructure
facts** — the shared junction, the shared airport, Hwy 110's named endpoint. **Sinheung's own culture material
is withheld under the cold protocol**, so the Neighbor here has a *positional* standpoint but not a *cultural*
one.

**The objection stands anyway, and it lands on something real**: the pass repeatedly writes "Zhongshan is the
end of three highways" when the accurate statement is "the Tri-Cities junction is." **Corrected in the text.**
**Must be re-run properly when Sinheung is written.**

## The Lover *faculty* — run every time *(Moore & Gillette; `00f` §3)*

> ***Is this place alive, and could anyone love it?***

**Yes — and specifically for the noise.**

**A city where you can hear four unrelated songs from four windows at once and nobody coordinates and nobody
minds is a place with genuine aliveness in it.** It is not beautiful and it is not dramatic. **It is
companionable**, in a way that is rare and easy to underwrite.

> ⚠ **And this is worth recording as a methodology observation: the Lover faculty's question was answered by
> accident, by a correction made for a different reason.** The general-population music finding (C-3) was
> forced by **Gate 2**, on a technical discipline grounds — performers are not a musical culture. **The
> replacement happened to supply the pass's whole answer to "could anyone love it."**
>
> **Before C-3, the honest answer to the Lover faculty would have been "competent, and no."** `00f` names
> *"flattened affect… competent and joyless"* as the Impotent Lover's signature and says to ask it of exactly
> such a pass. **The pass would have failed this question, and the gate that saved it was aimed at something
> else entirely.**

## Panel C/D — the shadow bench

**Position chosen per `00f`: the one most obviously suited to the location's reputation, "because the obvious
charge failing is usually where the real one is."**

### The Denying "Innocent" One — *what does this place refuse to know that it already knows?*

> *"You hold an instrument that tells you when things stop. You point it at water. You have pointed it at water
> for two hundred and fifty years while everything else in this city quietly went un-looked-at. Do not tell me
> nobody thought of it."*

**Verdict: NOT GUILTY — with the conditions for guilt stated** *(the conditional-acquittal technique, `00f`)*.

**Refusal requires a proposition to refuse, and there has never been one.** Nobody has proposed extending The
Round, so nobody has declined to. **This is not denial; it is a thought that has not occurred to anyone**, which
is a different and less culpable thing — and it is the only reading compatible with `00d`'s non-malice
discipline.

> ⭐ **The conditions for guilt, stated so the acquittal is live rather than decorative:** **the moment one
> person says out loud that The Round is not really about the water, Zhongshan becomes guilty of this charge
> from that day forward.** **The player can be the person who says it.** That is a playable trajectory rather
> than a verdict — which is exactly the value `00f` records the technique having produced before.

### The redirect — The Weakling *(the King faculty's passive shadow)*

**Per `00f`'s redirect technique: the literal charge failed, so an adjacent one was tried, and it lands.**

> *"Where does authority exist on paper and nobody exercises it?"*

**Guilty.** **The Round's aggregated log is the most complete picture of Zhongshan's condition that exists, it
is compiled, it is kept — and no one reads it end to end and no one is empowered to act on it.** The authority
is real, written down, and unexercised. **Nobody abdicated; the office was never created.**

## Dispositions summary, and a methodology problem with `unmet`

| Position | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Child | `accepted` → C-1 |
| Lover *(position)* | `accepted` → C-1 *(converges with Child — counted once)* |
| Parent | `accepted` → new material into Finding VII |
| Ruler | **`refereed`** — settlement written |
| Elder | `accepted` → **completes Finding I** |
| Mentor | `accepted` → sharpens Finding XV |
| Passer-Through | **PASS** on both modes |
| Neighbor | `noted` — casting limited by the cold protocol; re-run when Sinheung is written |
| Denying Innocent One | **not guilty, conditionally** |
| The Weakling *(redirect)* | **guilty** |

### ⚠ `unmet` count: **one** — and the low count exposes a real contradiction inside `00f`

**Genuine `unmet`: the Standing Objection.** Asked whether the city should stop tolerating a parody opposition
that keeps minutes and lodges pointless objections, **Zhongshan would decline** — and it would decline
consciously. **That is a thing the city knows it holds and would refuse to surrender.**

**But three further objections *look* like `unmet` and are not, and the difference matters:**

> **`00f` supplies two definitions of `unmet` and they disagree for a location with low self-knowledge.**
>
> - **Rule 3 (procedural):** *"would satisfying this make the location more like the others? If yes, it is
>   almost certainly `unmet`."* → **By this test, the Hero's silence, the Ruler's administration and the
>   Mentor's teachable-membership are all `unmet`: satisfying any of them would homogenize Zhongshan. Count = 4.**
> - **The twelve-district retrospective (diagnostic):** *"`unmet` measures what a location knowingly protects…
>   you cannot refuse to surrender something you do not know you hold."* → **By this test they are not `unmet`
>   at all, because Zhongshan does not know it holds any of them. Count = 1.**
>
> **Both readings are in the same file and neither is wrong.** They simply measure different things — **one
> is a rule about the writing, the other a diagnostic about the place** — and for a location whose defining
> property is that it does not perceive its own condition, **they diverge by a factor of four.**
>
> **Proposed: split the disposition.** `unmet` for what the location **knowingly** protects *(the diagnostic;
> Zhongshan scores 1, correctly, and that is informative)*, and a separate **`declined`** for objections the
> pass refuses on anti-homogenization grounds where the location has no awareness to protect anything
> *(Zhongshan scores 3)*. **Collapsing them loses exactly the signal `00f` says `unmet` is for.**

## Four-corner check *(`00f` §5)*

**Did the objections come from genuinely different directions?** **Mostly yes, with one honest failure.**

- **Genuinely distinct:** the Parent (nothing notices a person's absence) · the Ruler (nothing holds it up) ·
  the Elder (the memory of what already stopped) · the Mentor (the decisive thing is unteachable) · the
  Neighbor (the assets are not yours). **Five different directions on one location.**
- ⚠ **Collapsed:** **Child and Lover produced one objection wearing two hats** — both want unprogrammed space,
  both answered from the same terrain. **Recorded rather than counted twice.**
- ⭐ **Convergence from opposed corners, which `00f` calls the strongest available signal:** **the Elder and the
  Parent — who share nothing** — arrived at the same gap from opposite ends of a life. The Elder: *this city
  cannot tell you what already stopped.* The Parent: *this city cannot tell you what stopped an hour ago.*
  **That gap is real.**
