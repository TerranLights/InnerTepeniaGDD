# PART B — MISMATCHES, INCLUDING TWO LIVE CANON BUGS

## ⚠ B-1 — The culture pass runs on a polar night three times longer than the city has

**The culture pass says "six months of dark" or equivalent at least four times**, and **builds load-bearing
findings on it**:

- §5: *"living through six months of dark every year"* — the origin of the whole "Zhongshan Austere" identity
- §9: *"large shared rooms where a hemmed-in population could sit out a six-month dark together"* — architecture
- §12: *"something to sit with during a six-month dark"* — **the entire rationale for the musical tradition**
- §10: *"feed itself through a six-month polar night"* — **the entire rationale for the cuisine**

**Zhongshan's polar night is ~60 days.** Its own `Specs/Zhongshan.md` states it: *"Polar night: approximately
May 22 → July 21 (~60 days)."* At 69°22'S that is correct and physically necessary — six months of darkness
requires being at or very near the pole.

> **Four of the culture pass's most load-bearing findings — identity, architecture, music and cuisine — are
> each justified by a physical fact that is wrong by a factor of three, and the correct figure is in the city's
> own primary spec sheet.**
>
> **The findings may well survive** — a 60-day night is still a long dark, and Run 3's own Phase 4 and Phase 6
> use it. **But the reasoning must be re-derived rather than patched**, because "sit out six months together"
> and "sit out two months together" are different arguments about how much shared indoor space a city needs.

**Recorded as a canon defect. NOT fixed here** — it touches four sections of a rank-2 canon file and is a
developer call, not a test run's.

## ⚠ B-2 — "One hundred and thirty years of exile"

The culture pass says **"130 years"** repeatedly, and **"five generations."** **The Falkland Treaty is 2564.**
The pass frame is Orbital Era, post-Amundsen-Tower (~2688) and pre-war (before 2812).

**2564 → the pre-war present is ~248 years, not 130.** To the game's own present (~2822–2827) it is
**258–263 years.** **"130 years" appears to be a leftover from a superseded timeline** — and it is used to
justify how deep the cultural refinement runs.

**Recorded. Not fixed here** — same reason.

> ### Both bugs are the same kind, and the kind is the point
>
> **Neither is an interpretive error. Both are a number, contradicted by the city's own primary source, used as
> the premise of a cultural argument.** **And both survived a documented six-pass integrity check** *(M-8)*.
>
> **What caught them was not a gate. It was a session that had never read the file before**, and therefore read
> "six months of dark" as a claim rather than as familiar furniture. **This is the third and fourth live canon
> error a cold run has surfaced in one session** *(M-7)*.

## B-3 — A genuine contradiction: is Zhongshan quiet?

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| Culture pass §14 | *"Zhongshan is quiet by the standards of any other Tepenian city. Voices are kept low in public."* Identity: *the Quiet City* |
| Run 3, correction C-3 | Domestic singing, audible from windows, several unrelated songs at once, nobody minds |

**Both-are-true test applied** *(`02` §5.3 — different objects, not different truths)*:

> **They are about different objects, and together they are better than either alone.** **Public voice is low;
> private noise is inescapable and unremarked.** A city at 24,917 people per km² with thin construction over
> rock **cannot be acoustically private**, whatever its manners are. **So Zhongshan is a place where people are
> quiet in the street and audible in their homes, and the etiquette is not silence — it is not
> commenting on what you can obviously hear.**
>
> **That reading strengthens the non-prying norm considerably:** the norm is not a preference for privacy.
> **It is the only available response to a city where privacy is physically impossible.** Necessity before
> meaning *(Technique 6)*, arrived at from the density figure the culture pass never computed.

## ⭐⭐ THE HEADLINE COMPARATIVE FINDING: two passes on one city produced OPPOSITE shapes

**Run 2 read Zhongshan as `cost-dominant` — a city whose obligations only ever grow.**
**Run 3 read Zhongshan as `cost-absent` — a city nothing continuously threatens.**

**These are not different opinions. They are the arithmetic consequence of different input sets.**

- **Run 2 admitted the Tier-3 particulars** — the Founding Hall, the Long Record, the Mending Houses, the
  Standing Stone. **Every one of those is a maintenance obligation.** Feed them into the STANDING COST quadrant
  and it fills. **Cost-dominant follows necessarily.**
- **Run 3 quarantined them** as culture-pass conclusions and ran G2/G4/G5/G8 alone — physical site, founding,
  network, demography. **None of those generates a maintenance obligation.** **Cost-absent follows necessarily.**

> ### **The shape is not a property of the location. It is a property of the admitted input set.**
>
> **`02` §4 presents the shape typology as a reading of the place.** **It is not.** It is a reading of what was
> put in front of the reader — and **this is now demonstrated, not argued, because the same city under the same
> methodology on the same day produced two opposite shapes from two input sets.**
>
> **The methodology has no warning about this anywhere.**

**And the honest synthesis neither run could reach alone:**

> **Zhongshan's standing cost is near-zero in its environment and high in its institutions.** The site demands
> almost nothing; the things the city has chosen to keep demand a great deal. **That is a genuinely
> characterizing split** — a place under no external pressure that has accumulated a large voluntary burden —
> **and it is the correct reading. Neither pass had the inputs to see it.**

**The primary question `RESUME_HERE.md` §6 asks: *can the methodology carry one location from start to finish?***

### Which gates fired, and what that cost or bought

**Five fired. Two produced findings that would not otherwise exist** *(Gate 9 → Finding XV; Gate I → Finding
XVI)*. **One produced the internal division the pass had entirely missed** *(Gate F → the waters/interior
split)*. **Gate 11 caught a factor-of-25 scale error.** **Gate 2 caught the same failure `00b` was written
about, in a pass whose author had read `00b` that morning.**

> **On this run the gates were not a formality. They were roughly a quarter of the pass's value.**

### What was unrunnable, and why

- **Gate 6** — **structurally unrunnable in a cold pass.** It requires the sibling differentiation instrument,
  which is exactly what the cold protocol withholds. **Run at Step 7 instead, where it immediately found two
  collisions** — one of which Gate 4 had already caught blind.
- **Gate P** — correctly N/A on a child pass.
- **Gate 0's inward direction** — run, and failed, twice, caught externally rather than by the gate.

### The instruments that outperformed their billing

1. ⭐ **Phase 5b's own-eras substitute.** `01` §5.3a calls it *"the strongest substitute."* **On this evidence
   it is not a substitute at all — it is the better instrument.** The three eras differ on the chosen axis *by
   construction*; three neighbouring cities differ on many axes at once and must be disentangled.
2. ⭐ **The z-score rule.** Killed the same attractive finding in two independent sessions.
3. ⭐ **Gate 4's swap test.** Flagged a collision with the city's own canon **before that canon was visible.**
4. ⭐ **The Unrecognized Instrument.** Fired three times — The Round, the parody minute-books, the Elder's index.

### The instruments that under-performed

1. **G1, the symbol substrate.** Selected, then never used; contributed nothing to any claim. **And its
   provenance is compromised** (M-1b).
2. **Gate 0.** Failed in the direction it is specifically warned about.
3. **`03` Phase 8.** The one phase that failed a discipline gate.

## The closing test *(`00_RUNBOOK.md` LAW 0)*

> ***Could a person live an entire life here — and would that life be unlike a life in any comparable place?***

**Yes, and yes — after the corrections, and not before them.**

A life here: born on an island block; child on the winter ice because it is the only open ground; courting at
the far end of a lake in the dark months because it is the only empty place; handed a leg of The Round in your
twenties because someone near you stopped walking theirs, or **never handed one, and never able to ask**;
working a trade you learned from whoever was nearest; arguing about water provenance in bread; hearing four
songs from four windows and commenting on none of them; observing the national days late, with the other
walkers; and handing your leg on when you can no longer walk it, which is the one ceremony the city has.

**Unlike anywhere comparable?** **Yes** — and the sharpest evidence is Gate 4: of six findings swapped onto
Casey, the closest climate match in the country, **five could not survive the move**, and the one that could
had already been demoted.

**But the honest qualifier `RESUME_HERE.md` demands:** **Zhongshan is a best-case location.** All eight
generators present, a canonical "only," a dense cluster, and deep attribute canon. **This run demonstrates the
methodology runs. It does not demonstrate that it runs on a thin location** — and that is the next test, not
this one.
