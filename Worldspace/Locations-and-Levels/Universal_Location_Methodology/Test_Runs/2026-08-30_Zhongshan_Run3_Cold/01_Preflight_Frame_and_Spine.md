## Why NOT "enclosed" — a deliberate modifier refusal, and it changes the phases

Tepenia's settlements invite the **enclosed** modifier by default: sealed, pressurized, domed. `01` §1.2 hangs
real obligatory questions on it — envelope failure modes, what finite volume does to disposal, noise, smell,
privacy and burial.

**Zhongshan does not get it.** The site is an **ice-free bedrock oasis** with a mean annual temperature of
**−9.9 °C** and a **warmest month at 0 °C**. That is not a place that must be sealed against its surroundings;
it is a place with weather. Assigning "enclosed" here would import Concordia's mortuary problem, Concordia's
privacy-by-volume problem and Concordia's envelope anxiety into a city that has none of them.

> **This is Gate F's type check, run at declaration time instead of at QA — and it changed the pass.** The
> refusal is the reason Phase 3 can ask about *outdoor* texture at all, and the reason Phase 6's death question
> does not inherit the sealed-city framing.

# PHASE 1 — CONSTRAINT & CAPABILITY *(the spine)*

## 1A — The four generator profiles, run separately, before comparison

### G2 — Physical & environmental constraint

*Larsemann Hills ice-free bedrock oasis · Prydz Bay · −9.9 °C mean · warmest month 0 °C, coldest −22 °C ·
200–300 mm precipitation · polar night ~60 days · midnight sun ~61 days · 10th warmest of 35 Tepenian cities.*

| Quadrant | Content |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **It builds on rock.** Ice-free terrain means foundations that do not move, are not re-levelled, and do not bury. Add a deepwater bay, real topography with an actual horizon, summer meltwater, and a light cycle that is *moderate* by Tepenian standards — 60 days of night where the plateau cities get months |
| **DEFICIT** | No soil, no wood, no growing season — **it cannot feed itself.** And the bay freezes, so **the one route that is unambiguously Zhongshan's own is seasonal** |
| **STANDING COST** | ⚠ **Conspicuously LOW.** No ice-shelf correction (Halley), no extreme-cold regime (Vostok −54.8, Kunlun −58, Dome Fuji −54, Concordia −52.7), no Denison-scale katabatic engineering, no island resupply cap. The continuous obligations are ordinary: heat, power, snow, keep the harbor open |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Being outdoors.** At 0 °C in January it is genuinely pleasant by national standards; at −22 °C in July it is not. Nothing forbids outdoor life, and for ~60 days a year it simply stops |

**Third-order chase** *(LAW 0)*: ice-free bedrock → **the environment is survivable with ordinary competence** →
**nothing about the site forces anyone to coordinate with anyone.**

### G4 — Founding condition

*Post-Falkland settlement on Zhongshan Station infrastructure. Claim confirmed by the International Court of
Diplomacy at Jeju-do as part of a single three-way partition with Japan and Unified Korea — **settled before the
exile era began.** One of exactly two named exceptions to the rotating-operator model: ~481 years of continuous
single-population habitation, 2083–2564.*

| Quadrant | Content |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **A claim nobody has ever contested.** Confirmed externally, in advance, by a neutral court, and independently reinforced by unbroken physical occupancy. Zhongshan is the one Tepenian city that never had to establish that it was entitled to be where it is |
| **DEFICIT** | **It has no proving mechanism, because it has never had to prove anything.** There is no founding trial in its history — no crisis of legitimacy, no contested first winter, no moment the city nearly failed |
| **STANDING COST** | Maintaining continuity itself: the records and the structures that evidence it. **Low, and self-reinforcing — continuity maintained is continuity easier to maintain** |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Arriving.** The other seventeen national communities all arrived *after* the claim was settled, into a city whose identity was already finished. Nothing excludes them; there is simply nothing left for them to found |

> ⚠ **Discipline check, run here rather than at QA.** `No_National_Stereotypes.md` is binding, and everything
> above is deliberately written as **mechanism, not inheritance**: a court ruling, an occupancy record, an
> arrival sequence. **None of it says anything about Chinese culture, and none of it would change if the
> founding nation were swapped.** That is the rule's own stated self-check, and it passes.

### G5 — Network position

*Hwy 4, Hwy 22 and Hwy 110 all **terminate** at one shared physical point. Tri-Cities Airport, 1 of 9 nationally.
Prydz Bay harbor. Two of Tepenia's five hitchhiking-valid highways end here.*

| Quadrant | Content |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **Reachability, at a level only the capital matches.** Three highway endpoints converge here and at exactly one other place in Tepenia — Concordia — **and Concordia has no airport.** Zhongshan has road, air and sea |
| **DEFICIT** | ⚠ **It is a terminus, not a corridor. Nothing passes through it.** All three highways *end*; past the junction there is sea and two neighbours. Maximal arrival, **zero transit** |
| **STANDING COST** | Keeping the junction and the harbor open — **and the junction is jointly held**, so the duty belongs to three cities and therefore to nobody in particular |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Leaving.** Four exit routes, two of them hitchhiking-valid. Departure from Zhongshan is unusually cheap and nothing stands in its way |

**Third-order chase**: three roads end here → **Zhongshan is the far end of the country** — Hwy 110's two
endpoints are Zhongshan and the capital, so its national meaning is *the place at the other end of the road* →
**and it does not even hold that alone**: the junction and the airport belong to three cities, leaving the
harbor and Hwy 110's named endpoint as the only infrastructure that is unambiguously Zhongshan's.

### ⚠ The shape: this is the inverse of the shape the methodology expects

`02` §4 supplies **cost-dominant** — STANDING COST as the largest quadrant — and calls it *"the expected shape
for hostile-environment outposts, declining settlements, and anything on life support."* **Tepenia is a
continent of hostile-environment outposts. Zhongshan should be cost-dominant. It is the opposite.**

> ### **PROPOSED ADDITION TO THE SHAPE TYPOLOGY — `cost-absent`**
>
> **STANDING COST conspicuously thinner than every other quadrant, across independent generators.**
>
> **The question it unlocks:** *if nothing here has to be paid continuously, what is holding this place
> together — and how would anyone know if it stopped?*
>
> **Why it is not simply "one absence."** An empty quadrant asks *what does it mean to lack this?* A **thin**
> cost quadrant is different and worse: the location **does** have obligations, they are simply never
> load-bearing. **There is no failure signal.** A cost-dominant location learns instantly when it stops paying;
> a cost-absent one learns nothing, because nothing breaks.
>
> **Registered as a Run 3 methodology finding.** See `00_Observations_Run3.md`.

## 1C — Conflict resolution: the both-are-true test *(`02` §5.3)*

**One real conflict.** G5 says the deficit is **positional** (a terminus — nothing passes through). G8 says it is
**procedural** (no mechanism for a contested decision). G4 says it is **historical** (nothing was ever proven).
G2 says it is **material** (it cannot feed itself).

**Do not ask which is right. Ask what single property produces all four.**

> **The property: Zhongshan has never been required to resolve anything.**
>
> - **materially** — the site does not force it *(G2)*;
> - **historically** — the founding did not force it *(G4)*;
> - **positionally** — nothing crosses it, so no through-traffic imposes anyone else's problem *(G5)*;
> - **procedurally** — the demography does not force it *(G8)*.
>
> **They are four objects at four scales, and all four claims stand.** The material deficit is real and separate
> — Zhongshan genuinely cannot feed itself and genuinely depends on Davis's agriculture down Hwy 110. **It is
> not, however, a *contested* dependency, and that is the point: even Zhongshan's one hard material need arrives
> without an argument.**

## 1D — THE SPINE

> ## Zhongshan is the one Tepenian city that nothing continuously threatens — and it therefore has no way to tell a practice that is working from a practice that has quietly stopped.
>
> Every other city in the Federation is held together by something that would kill it if it lapsed. Halley's ice
> moves. Vostok's cold is −54.8 °C. Denison meets katabatic wind with an uncompromising single structure.
> Concordia is sealed. The island cities are capped by resupply. **Each is told immediately, and physically, when
> its attention lapses.**
>
> **Zhongshan is told nothing.** It sits on rock that does not move, in the mildest weather on the East
> Antarctic mainland, at the convergence of three highways it does not have to maintain alone, with a plurality
> large enough that no decision ever requires assembling anyone. **Its cohesion is not enforced by anything. It
> is chosen, or it is habitual — and neither leaves a mark when it ends.**
>
> **So the characteristic failure is not collapse. It is lapse.** A practice here does not get abandoned after
> an argument; it gets performed less often, then rarely, then not, and the city finds out years later or never.
> **`02` §3 gives the deficit rule as *"its characteristic failure is what the deficit was supposed to prevent."*
> Zhongshan's deficit is the absence of a forcing function, and what a forcing function prevents is drift.**

> notice.**
