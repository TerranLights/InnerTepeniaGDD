### Divergences from source, stated explicitly

- **Sanay's admissible climate table (Specs/Sanay.md lines 64-90) is stated for the nunatak position, not
  necessarily the dockside.** Real Antarctic coastal ports experience katabatic wind ACCELERATION toward the
  coast (a well-documented physical phenomenon, not this session's invention) and sea-ice-driven seasonality
  that an inland-station climate table does not capture. This pass will therefore NOT assume the port shares
  the exact monthly figures given for the nunatak summit — it will treat the port's own microclimate as a
  REQUESTED item (exact coastal data not in admissible canon) while using the general physical-geography
  principle (coastal wind acceleration, sea-ice seasonality) as an admissible G2 physical-constraint inference,
  not a specific number.
- **SANAP's real resupply pattern (single seasonal voyage per station) diverges from Sanay's own admissible
  "rotating crews... active at seemingly all hours" framing** — resolved as a both-are-true regional-hub
  divergence (see table above), not a contradiction requiring a kill.
