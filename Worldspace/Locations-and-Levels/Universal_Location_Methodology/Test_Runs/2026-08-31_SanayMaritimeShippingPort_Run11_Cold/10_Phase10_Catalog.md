# Phase 10 — Catalog

**Asks:** What is actually here — named, specific, and enterable?

**Governing premise applied**: this location is built around a theme (continuous, self-reliant freight
service, Phase 1's cost-dominant shape), not one thing. The catalog below refracts through that theme rather
than restating it literally.

this is the honest outcome, not a gap in research effort.

## C. Catalog, organized under the four headings

### 1. Named places and landmarks

- **The Shipyard Complex** and **the Trade Yard** — already admissible/named (`Sanay_Physical_Infrastructure_
  Attributes.md` items 3-4), carried forward as this pass's own primary named locations.
- **The Long Grade** *(working name, PRODUCED this pass — places are producible per `05` §4; not a person's
  proper name)* — the vertical connector implied by Phase 3 F's elevation-based functional split. A switchback
  road or cable-lift structure joining the lower-city dockside to the upper-clifftop administrative tier,
  carrying both the vertical commute (Phase 4 A) and the paperwork-vs-cargo logistics chain (Phase 3 F).

### 2. Physical things

- **Packing-scrap ware** *(working name, PRODUCED)* — the repurposed-dunnage household goods from Phase 7a's
  non-thematic export. Simple, ordinary objects made from cargo-packing waste, traded around Sanay through
  channels unrelated to the port's own formal identity.

### 3. People as role-archetypes — no invented proper names, per `05` §3

- **A veteran dock crew chief** who has survived enough rotations, including a genuinely bad one, to hold
  informal standing per Phase 5d's membership mechanism.
- **A Trade Yard clerk** who works the formal, bounded-protocol business register established in Phase 3 C.
- **A newly-posted worker on their first rotation** — has not yet crossed Phase 5d's unremarked threshold.
- **An informal safety authority** (a captain, a dock supervisor) empowered by practice, if not by any
  confirmed formal rule (Phase 7b), to refuse a docking attempt in marginal weather.
- **A worker who protects a fixed block of time against the rotation clock** — Phase 7d's counterculture
  population, sympathetically drawn, not criminalized.

### 4. Settings

- **The bounded-contact register of the Trade Yard** vs. **the general industrial bustle of the Shipyard
  Complex** — two distinct atmospheric textures already established in Phase 3 C, catalogued here as
  situational settings a player could move between.
- **A lull between shifts** — an unscheduled, unpredictable pocket of downtime (Phase 4/8), distinct from any
  planned leisure period, arising whenever the freighter-tied rhythm happens to slacken.

### Significant local events and notable figures

**Both REQUESTED, honestly recorded as absent rather than invented.** Admissible canon supplies no specific
historical incident and no notable figure specific to the port. Phase 6 D's hypothetical "compounding failure"
scenario (all three of Phase 1's Grudging-Tolerance costs landing at once) is a strong candidate shape for a
significant local event IF canon ever confirms one occurred — flagged as a candidate, not asserted as fact.
