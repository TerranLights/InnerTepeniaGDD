# Tri-Cities Test Run — Phase 5: Relation & Geometry

**TEST RUN. NOT CANON.** All findings `Proposed:`.

**Why this phase was run next, out of order.** `03` §0 restores Phase 5 because its absence at district scale
was *measured* — a completed district file mentioning its own opposite district zero times. **A cluster of
three cities eight kilometres apart is the sharpest available test of whether the restoration was worth
making.** If Phase 5 produces nothing here, it produces nothing anywhere.

**The core move, per `03`:** *write a relation as a mechanism, not a rivalry.* Never *who dislikes whom* —
always ***what does each side refuse to develop, and who supplies it instead?***

---

# 5a — Peers: the three pairs, and they are three different shapes

**Checked first for the failure `03` names: writing rivalry instead of mechanism, and writing only the outward
direction.** All three pairs below are written in both directions.

## Zhongshan ↔ Sinheung — an asymmetric non-relationship at extreme proximity

**A few hundred metres apart. Effectively no exchange.**

- **What Zhongshan refuses to develop:** any stake in fabrication. Sinheung's raw materials cross **Zhongshan's
  own tri-junction** and Zhongshan takes nothing from them. *(Canon records the fact — Overlap 7. The physical
  generator predicts it independently: bedrock plus a fishery means Zhongshan never needed to trade.)*
- **What Zhongshan supplies:** the road. Only the road.
- **What Sinheung supplies Zhongshan:** nothing. There is nothing Zhongshan needs.

### ⚠ Correction — canon already holds most of this, and one part better than the draft did

**The first draft of R-1 claimed this contrast as new. The verification scan says otherwise**, and the
correction is recorded rather than quietly absorbed. Sinheung's own spec sheet carries all three of:

- *"a city that doesn't apologize for wanting to be seen working, **the opposite of Zhongshan's deliberate
  quiet next door**"* (§137)
- *"**Unlike Zhongshan's muted, detail-concentrated restraint**, this city's fashion doesn't mind being
  noticed"* (§115)
- *"people comfortable with a city that wears its ambition openly **rather than performing quiet superiority
  the way Zhongshan does next door**"* (§83)

**The third line is better than anything the draft produced**, and it is now used rather than replaced:
**Sinheung does not read Zhongshan's silence as indifference. It reads it as superiority.**

> **Proposed Finding R-1 (revised) — canon has the contrast; the mechanism is what was missing.**
> **Canon states the opposition as temperament** — one city loud, one quiet, each defined against the other.
> **The capability frame states it as a supply relationship that cannot complete.**
>
> **Sinheung's standing cost is being witnessed** — output *is* legitimacy, and legitimacy requires an
> observer. **Zhongshan's standing cost is teaching every generation not to look.** They are neighbours at a
> few hundred metres, and **Zhongshan is structurally incapable of supplying the one thing Sinheung most
> needs** — not from coldness, but because not-looking is the discipline holding eighteen national communities
> together *(Phase 1, Finding Z-1)*.
>
> **And canon supplies the sting the draft missed:** Sinheung interprets the withholding as *quiet
> superiority*. **It is not.** It is a city too busy not-asking about itself to have any attention left over.
> **Sinheung has assigned a motive to a structural fact, and the misreading is entirely reasonable from where
> it stands.** No malice, no author, no policy — and neither city can correct it, because the correction would
> require Zhongshan to explain the norm, which is the one thing the norm prevents.

**Third order:** this is why Sinheung ships nationwide rather than locally. **A city that needs witnesses and
lives beside one that will not look must find its audience further away** — and the Federation-wide chamber
distribution is, read this way, not only an industry but a solution to a neighbour problem.

## Zhongshan ↔ Shirayuki — reciprocal, and therefore the most stuck

**`02` §4.1: reciprocal is "the most stuck, because there is no asymmetry and therefore no petitioner —
neither side can ask without conceding the whole axis."** This pair is a clean instance.

| | Holds | Lacks |
|---|---|---|
| **Zhongshan** | attachments that cost something to leave | a public life that crosses heritage lines |
| **Shirayuki** | a public life that crosses heritage lines | attachments that cost something to leave |

**Each holds precisely the other's remedy, and neither can ask for it:**

- **Zhongshan asking *"how do you get people to share a public life?"*** concedes that its quiet is not
  contentment — the one thing its whole civic self-image rests on.
- **Shirayuki asking *"how do you make people stay?"*** concedes that being loved is not sufficient — the one
  thing its whole civic self-image rests on.

> **Proposed Finding R-2.** The two cities in the cluster that could most directly repair each other are the
> two with the least possible conversation, and the obstacle is not hostility — **it is that the question
> itself is the admission.** Eight kilometres, one highway, one airport, and an unaskable question in each
> direction.

## Sinheung ↔ Shirayuki — the same symbol at two different stages

**Both are Uranus. Both are outliers. The Element decides which kind** *(see Phase 1 §1.4)*. **This pair is not
an exchange relation at all — it is a developmental one**, and that is why it reads differently from the other
two.

- **Sinheung is what Shirayuki would be if it had ever had to prove it belonged here.** Its 83.1% retention is
  the direct product of a claim still being established.
- **Shirayuki is what Sinheung would be if it ever stopped proving.** Its 61.8% retention is the direct product
  of a claim that never needed defending.

**Neither can adopt the other's answer, and the reason is exact:**
- Sinheung cannot accept Shirayuki's ease, because **accepting it means the proving was never necessary** —
  which retroactively empties every generation of effort that constitutes the city's identity.
- Shirayuki cannot adopt Sinheung's friction, because **the friction is the thing it was founded to not be.**
  Its founding gift was ground nobody contested.

> **Proposed Finding R-3.** Each is a live demonstration of the other's road not taken, eight kilometres away,
> continuously visible, and **useless to both** — because what each would have to learn is the thing that would
> unmake it.

# 5b — The three-way differentiation set

**`03` §5b: pick the category first, then read all three off the geometry.** Deriving one city's answer alone
tends to produce something generic.

**Category chosen: *what happens to a person who leaves.*** Selected because **canon has no answer for it in
any of the three files**, and because the retention spread *(Phase 1 §1.4)* makes it the cluster's live
question. *(Deliberately NOT the "stranger asks a personal question" category — canon's Overlap 3 already
answers that one, and duplicating it would be exactly the collision Gate 6 exists to prevent.)*

| | **What a departure means** |
|---|---|
| **Zhongshan** | **Nothing is said, and nothing is concluded.** The non-prying norm covers leaving as completely as it covers heritage — you are not asked why, so there is no account of it. **A departure here is indistinguishable from a silence**, and the city has therefore never assembled a picture of who leaves or why. |
| **Sinheung** | **A defection from the claim.** You will be asked, audibly and in public, and no answer will be adequate — because the claim is collective and leaving subtracts from it. **The high retention is partly this**, and the cost is borne by the people who stay for reasons that are not really theirs. |
| **Shirayuki** | **You will be wished well, sincerely, and helped to pack** — and the city will draw no conclusion whatsoever. Thirty-eight per cent left and Shirayuki learned nothing, because warmth toward the departing is genuine here and **it is also the mechanism by which no departure is ever read as data.** |

**Axis named:** *what a departure is allowed to mean* — unspeakable · disloyal · unremarkable.
**Checked against the differentiation instrument:** the Overlap Guide's existing rows cover the Gut-theme
expression, the heritage boundary, the founding relationship, the war response, and the built environment.
**No existing row covers departure.** No collision.

# 5c — The parent, and the divergence that matters

**`03` §5c: what does the containing polity require, what does it supply, and where do those disagree?**

**Parent is unwritten at Band 6** *(the Federation has no pass)*, so the provisional-inheritance protocol
applies. **Provisional assumptions, registered per `01` §5.2:** the Federation determines currency, law,
calendar, and the Falkland Treaty settlement; the Mirny subnet determines Arcanet topology and highway
maintenance.

| | What the Federation needs from it | What the city believes |
|---|---|---|
| **Zhongshan** | Very little in particular | Believes itself substantial — and it is, but not *needed* |
| **Sinheung** | **The chambers. Genuinely critical infrastructure** — a robot can be built anywhere in Tepenia because these ship from here | Believes it is indispensable |
| **Shirayuki** | Nothing material; supplies the region's "Alternative Culture" reputation | Believes it is a destination |

> **⚠ Proposed Finding R-4 — the most structurally alarming thing this run produced, and it is derived rather
> than invented.**
> **Sinheung's belief that it is indispensable is *almost* true, and the gap is fatal.** The Federation needs
> **the chambers.** It does not need **Sinheung.** The Mark IV schematic is **designed at Neumayer**, in the
> Halley subnet — *"Sinheung manufactures the chambers, but the underlying engineering credit belongs
> elsewhere."*
> **If Neumayer ever licensed the current-generation schematic to a second manufacturer, Sinheung's entire
> claim to significance would evaporate without anyone having acted against it.**
> This is the fourth-order consequence of the origination deficit, and **it is precisely the kind of fact a
> multiply-addressed location cannot perceive** — Sinheung reads its position in the supply chain as proof of
> importance, when the same position is the measure of its replaceability.
> **Nobody in Sinheung is wrong about any individual fact. The conclusion is still incorrect.**

# 5d — The edge and the crossing

**One airport serves all three cities.** *(`Tri-Cities Airport` — canon.)* One highway tri-junction
*(Hwy 4/22/110)*. **So every visitor to any of the three arrives at the same place.**

### The first thing anyone sees is the seam

**Canon already supplies the material and does not draw the consequence.** At the cluster's edges *"three
distinct building traditions meet at negotiated boundary zones, none blending into the others."*

> **Proposed Finding R-5.** **A visitor's first experience of the Tri-Cities is not any of the three cities —
> it is the refusal to merge.** You land at an airport that belongs to nobody, and cross ground where three
> architectures meet and decline to blend. **The cluster introduces itself, accurately, as three things that
> will not become one thing** — in a frame where they are, unknown to themselves, roughly a century from
> becoming exactly that.

**⚠ And the airport is an Interstitial location in the `01` §1.3 sense** — defined by what it is between,
belonging to none of them, under constant pressure to acquire an identity and somehow resisting. **Logged as a
REQUESTED sub-location pass; not run here.**

### What converts a visitor into a member — mechanism, not ceremony

| | The mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Zhongshan** | **When people stop noticing you.** There is no moment, no acknowledgement, and no way to ask whether it has happened. Consistent with the non-prying norm: membership is conferred by the withdrawal of attention. |
| **Sinheung** | **When your output is counted in the city's figures.** A literal ledger entry. Unambiguous, dated, and — uniquely among the three — *checkable*. |
| **Shirayuki** | **When something you made is used or displayed by someone else.** Not when you arrive, not when you are welcomed — welcome is instant and means nothing. **Membership begins when the city picks up your work.** |

**Axis named:** *what confers membership* — the withdrawal of attention · an entry in a ledger · someone else
using your work.

# 5e — Dependency, and who knows about it

**`03` §5e: a dependency nobody has named is a shadow; a dependency everyone has named is politics.**

| | The dependency | Named? | Therefore |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zhongshan** | A shared public life it cannot generate from within | **No — the norm forbids naming it** | **Shadow** |
| **Sinheung** | Materials from Mirny, route through Zhongshan | **Yes, loudly** | Politics *(and a source of pride)* |
| **Sinheung** | **The design itself, from Neumayer** | **No** | **Shadow — and the load-bearing one** |
| **Shirayuki** | Continuous in-migration to sustain the scene | **No** | **Shadow** |

> **Proposed Finding R-6 — Sinheung is the only city of the three carrying both kinds at once**, and the
> visible one conceals the invisible one. Its openly-discussed logistics dependency *(materials, routes)* is a
> point of civic pride and constant conversation. **The conversation about where things come from is
> permanently occupied by the answer that does not matter.**

> **Proposed Finding R-7 — Shirayuki's dependency is invisible for a structural reason, not a cultural one.**
> **A dependency on continuous arrival is undetectable for exactly as long as people keep arriving.** It
> becomes visible only when they stop — at which point the thing that would have revealed it is also the thing
> that has ended. **The city cannot receive the warning before it is too late to use it**, and the orbital
> exodus was that warning, delivered and unread.

# Methodology verdict on Phase 5

**The restoration was worth making, and this run is evidence.** Phase 5 produced **seven findings**, including
the two the run rates highest *(R-4, the replaceability gap; R-1, the witness/not-looking mechanism)*, and
**none of them were reachable from any single city's own material** — every one required the geometry.

**Three specific mechanics earned their place:**
1. **"Mechanism, not rivalry"** prevented the obvious wrong output. The tempting write-up — *three proud
   neighbouring cities with national rivalries* — is generic, and canon explicitly warns the cluster's weight
   is diplomatic rather than a rivalry. **The mechanism framing produced non-rivalrous relations that are
   nonetheless painful.**
2. **"Write both directions"** produced R-1, which exists *only* in the asymmetry: Sinheung's need and
   Zhongshan's norm are individually unremarkable and jointly a permanent injury.
3. **5b's "pick the category first"** worked, and the check against the existing differentiation instrument
   stopped a collision — *stranger asks a personal question* was already taken.

**One weakness found:** **5c depends on a parent that does not exist.** The Federation has no pass, so R-4 —
the run's strongest finding — **rests on a provisional assumption about what the Federation needs.** Per `01`
§5.2 rule 4, *do not build the location's single strongest finding on a provisional assumption.* **This run
violated that rule.** The finding is flagged accordingly, and the violation is recorded in the observations
file rather than quietly tolerated.
