# PHASE 0 — FRAME

## 0.1 The frame decision, and why it was not obvious

**"Run the methodology on the three Tri-Cities" is ambiguous, and the declaration block caught it before any
content was written.** The cluster passes through four distinct states:

| Period | What exists | Type | Sibling set | Children |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Founding → ~2688 | **Three separate cities** | 3 × Settlement | each other + 32 outer cities | none |
| ~2688 → ~2780s | One continuous urban area, three legal cities | ambiguous | — | — |
| ~2780s → 2812 | **One city**, three sub-districts | 1 × Settlement | the 32 outer cities | **3** |
| 2812 → present (~2822-27) | One city, war-damaged, **no Census III exists** | 1 × Settlement, *damaged* | — | 3 |

**`01` §4 rule 2 is explicit: a location at two eras is two documents, not one hedged across time.**

> **DECLARED FRAME FOR THIS RUN: pre-unification — three separate cities.**
> **Inferred from:** the existing outer-city convention *(outer cities are written pre-war; Concordia's
> districts post-war)*, the fact that every input file writes them as three, and the developer's own recorded
> sequencing — *finish developing each of the three cities' individual identities first.*
> **The post-unification pass is a genuinely different document** and is logged as a REQUESTED output.

**Epistemic horizon for this frame:** residents know the Jeju-do partition as their founding fact and live
beside two cities founded by the same act. **Amundsen Tower is built and orbital migration is under way or
recent** *(Census II is the Orbital-Era snapshot)*. **The Long Night War has not happened.** Unification has
not happened, or is not yet complete — **nobody in this frame knows these three will become one city**, which
matters: every relation written in Phase 5 is between polities that still believe they are separate.

# PHASE 1 — CONSTRAINT & CAPABILITY *(the spine)*

## 1.1 ZHONGSHAN — Saturn + Metal · *ironic*

**Pairing relation: IRONIC** *(`02` §6.3)*. **Metal promises a refined, integrated core** — precision,
discernment, cutting away what is unnecessary. **Saturn undercuts exactly that promise:** *"beauty built from
fragments rather than requiring wholeness"* · *"held together only loosely — no actual cohesion"* ·
***"impressive from a distance, insubstantial up close."***

| Quadrant | Reading |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **Work that requires no explanation.** Precision manufacture and a craft tradition that is its own justification. It is trusted with things that must simply be correct. [G1 Metal-positive; G2 the only city of the three with its own food supply] |
| **DEFICIT** | **Cohesion — and the inability to notice it.** 64% of Zhongshan is not Chinese; its entire public identity is. Eighteen national communities present as one thing *from a distance*. [G8 + G1 Saturn-negative] |
| **STANDING COST** | **Teaching every generation not to ask.** The non-prying norm is not a mood; it is maintenance, paid continuously by everyone, and invisible as work. **What fails first if it lapses for a week: somebody asks, and the fragments become visible.** |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Keeping your own heritage.** Nobody forbids it. There is no public venue for it, nobody inquires, and the entire cost falls on you. **Read in-world not as a cost but as a private choice** — which is precisely `00d`'s authorless, unenforced, unnoticed harm. |

### The three-generator comparison — all three agree, so build hard *(`02` §5.2)*

| | G1 Symbol | G8 Composition | G2 Physical |
|---|---|---|---|
| Says | Metal's **emotion is grief**; excess Metal = *"isolation through over-refinement — cutting away so much it severs real connection"* | 18 nations, one public face; the largest gap in the cluster between *who lives here* and *whose city this is* | Bedrock + its own fishery = **the only city of the three that does not need its neighbors** |
| Verdict | **agree** | **agree** | **agree** |

> **Proposed Finding Z-1 — the quiet is not contentment.**
> **Zhongshan's celebrated calm is the sound of eighteen communities not discussing what they are.** The
> non-prying taboo reads in-world as good manners and is structurally the mechanism that prevents the city
> from ever examining its own composition. **Saturn's "impressive from a distance, insubstantial up close" is
> not a metaphor here — it is a description of a population.**

**Third order:** because self-sufficiency is *geological* rather than temperamental — bedrock to build on,
a bay that feeds the city — Zhongshan is the one city in the cluster that **participates without depending.**
**G2 predicts, independently, the thing canon records as Overlap 7:** goods bound for Sinheung pass through
Zhongshan's own tri-junction and Zhongshan has no economic stake in them whatsoever.

### Deficit address — and this run proposes a new address type

Where does the remedy for "no shared public life across communities" live? **Shirayuki has it exactly** — a
shared creative language anyone can enter regardless of heritage. **It is 8 km away.**

**`02` §4.1's existing types do not fit.** It is not *diffuse*, not *in the parent*, not *in its own past*, and
**not across an antagonism — there is no antagonism.** The three cities are peers, founded by one act, on good
terms.

> **PROPOSED NEW ADDRESS TYPE — "adjacent but unaskable."**
> **The remedy is nearby, identified, reachable, and on friendly terms — and the location's own social
> mechanism forbids the asking.** Worse than *across an antagonism*, because an antagonism can at least be
> named and resented. **Here the barrier is internal and presents as courtesy**, so no grievance ever forms
> and no petition is ever made. The city can see its own answer from its own highway junction.

## 1.2 SINHEUNG — Uranus + Electricity · *reinforcing*

**Pairing relation: REINFORCING.** Both symbols say *sudden · energetic · animating · impossible to ignore.*
Per `02` §6.3 a reinforcing pair has **no internal counterweight**, and the question is **what this place
cannot moderate.**

| Quadrant | Reading |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **Making, at national scale.** The fabrication-synthesis chambers — the apparatus that physically creates robots — ship from here to every city in the Federation. **A robot can be built anywhere in Tepenia because Sinheung exists.** |
| **DEFICIT** | **Origination.** It builds **Neumayer's** design *(Mark IV)*, from **Mirny's** materials, on **Russia's** foundations, on ground a **court** allocated, under a name its founders never chose. **Sinheung has never originated anything it is known for.** |
| **STANDING COST** | **Proving the claim. Continuously, and forever.** Canon's own founding typology: *"a claim that has to keep proving itself."* Output *is* legitimacy, so the output can never stop. |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Rest. Not producing. Being unremarkable.** Nobody forbids it; there is simply no venue in which a non-producing person registers as a member — and in a city where *"people know each other's business,"* the non-production is visible. |

### Deficit address — multiply addressed, and that is the whole finding

**`02` §4.1 warns that a count above one should raise suspicion rather than reassurance:** *"three suppliers
and receipt from none reads as being well-connected, so no deficit is ever perceived."*

**Sinheung's origination deficit is addressed four ways at once** — design at Neumayer, materials at Mirny,
ground from the Jeju-do court, name from nowhere.

> **Proposed Finding S-1 — the pride is not a temperament; it is the only thing Sinheung makes by itself.**
> A city that originates nothing, yet must continuously prove a claim, will assert identity loudly — **because
> assertion is the single product it does not have to import.** Canon records the outsized national pride as an
> outlier trait and leaves it as a given. **The capability frame says it is load-bearing infrastructure.**

**Third order — and it is the reason the deficit is invisible from inside:** Sinheung ships to every city in
Tepenia and flies supplies to Dome Fuji. **It is, by any reasonable measure, a hub.** That connectedness is
exactly what conceals the fact that everything flowing through it was designed, mined, or decided elsewhere.
**Abundance of options reads as abundance of supply**, precisely as `02` §4.1 predicts.

**Fourth order — the shadow:** in a city where worth is output, **the unproductive are not persecuted; they
are simply not counted.** The old, the ill, the disabled, the child who is not good with their hands. No
policy, no enforcer, no malice — and the low-privacy norm means everyone can see who is not producing.

## 1.3 SHIRAYUKI — Uranus + Fire · *tensioned*

**Pairing relation: TENSIONED**, which `02` §6.3 calls usually the richest. **Uranus pulls away** *(anomaly,
outsider, the one that does not fit)*; **Fire pulls toward** *(warmth, charisma, "the courage to act and be
seen")*. **The contradiction it must live with: it is genuinely unlike anywhere else, and that is the reason
people want in rather than the reason they stay out.**

| Quadrant | Reading |
|---|---|
| **STRENGTH** | **Converting difference into draw.** Alone among the three it makes its outlier status *attractive.* Schools that pull students from across Tepenia *"for the culture and soul of the place, not academic ranking."* |
| **DEFICIT** | **Retention — and this one is measured, not inferred.** 61.8% orbital-era retention, **z = −1.26, third lowest of 33 cities**, against Sinheung's 83.1% eight kilometers away. |
| **STANDING COST** | **Being worth coming to.** A reputation is not owned; it is re-earned every generation. **What fails first if it lapses: nothing visible — which is why nobody is watching it.** |
| **GRUDGING TOLERANCE** | **Living here without making anything.** The civic identity *is* a shared creative language; someone outside it has no route in. **The city famous for being easy to like is hardest on the person who does not like what it likes.** |

### The generator conflict — and the both-are-true resolution

**G1 and the census disagree, sharply.** Uranus-positive says *"an identity built after a defining event —
reorientation as the new baseline rather than damage waiting to be corrected."* **The census says the city
lost 38% of its people.**

Per `02` §5.3 — *do not ask which is right; ask what single property produces both:*

> **Proposed Finding SH-1 — the same property fills the city and empties it.**
> **A population assembled by attraction is a population that has already demonstrated willingness to
> relocate for something better.** They did it once. Orbit was simply the next Shirayuki. **The pull that
> fills a city in good times empties it when a stronger attractor opens**, and both readings stand because
> they describe the same mechanism running in two directions.

**Third order — why it is structurally unfixable here:** every attachment Shirayuki offers is a
**low-switching-cost** attachment. Art, schools, ease of friendship, an outdoor gallery — all genuinely good,
all costless to give up. **Sinheung's are all high-cost**: a trade, a plant, a claim, and neighbors who know
your business. **Shirayuki optimized entirely for arrival and never built anything that hurts to leave.**

**Fourth order — and this is where it stops resembling anywhere else:** the city still draws students
nationwide **on a reputation built by a population that has already left.** Newcomers arrive for a scene at 62%
of the strength that made it famous.

### Deficit address — "in its own past," and sharper than the type as written

**`02` §4.1 added this address with an explicit note that the district set *could not* generate it.** Shirayuki
produces it on the first available case, and adds something the type description does not yet contain:

> **It is not only that the remedy is historical. The *evidence* is historical.**
> **Everyone who could explain what Shirayuki lost is someone who left.** The remaining population is composed
> entirely of people for whom the city worked. **A place cannot learn a deficit whose only witnesses have
> already gone** — survivorship bias operating as a civic blind spot, with no author and nobody at fault.

> **PROPOSED refinement to `02` §4.1's "in its own past" entry:** add — *"Check separately whether the
> location's **evidence** is also in its past. Where the people who could name the deficit are the people who
> left, the location is structurally incapable of diagnosing itself, and the nostalgia is not merely factually
> correct but epistemically unchallengeable."*

### ⚠ Three cities, three deficits, and not one of them can be perceived from inside

**This was not designed and was not visible in any input.** It fell out of running the address axis on all
three. **The shared *shape* is a genuine regional finding**, and per `00e` §5's three-step rule the shape is
the only shared part — **the instrument and the consequence differ completely in each case**, which is exactly
the test the rule sets.

> **Proposed regional finding TC-1.** The Larsemann Hills cluster's real common property is not the shared
> airport, the shared founding, or the shared population. **It is that each city is structurally blind to its
> own central problem, by a different mechanism, and each city's blind spot is plainly visible from the other
> two.** Zhongshan's answer is in Shirayuki. Shirayuki's warning is written in Sinheung's retention figure.
> Sinheung's originality problem is obvious to a Zhongshan craftsman who has never once discussed it.
> **They are 8 km apart and none of them can say it.**

**And the unification, in this frame, is unknowable to them** — but the reader knows it is coming. **These
three become one city.** Whether that fixes anything is a Phase 5 question and a post-unification-pass
question, and this run does not answer it.

## 1.5 Tense — the axis `00e` says is most often skipped

**Run deliberately, because the three losses look similar and are not.**

- **Zhongshan loses continuously and invisibly** — heritage by osmosis, a little each generation, never at a
  moment anyone could point to. **There is no event to grieve, which is why the grief has nowhere to go.**
- **Sinheung never finishes** — the claim is proved daily and is never proved. **A standing cost with no
  terminal state.**
- **Shirayuki lost once, decisively, and it is over.** 38% left and the city that remains is a different city
  wearing the reputation of the old one. **The loss is complete; the recognition has not started.**
