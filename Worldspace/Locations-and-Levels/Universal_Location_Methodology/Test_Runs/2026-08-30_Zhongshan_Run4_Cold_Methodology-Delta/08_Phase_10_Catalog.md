## C. Four catalog headings

### 1. Named places and landmarks

- **The tri-junction itself** (Hwy 4/22/110 convergence, Phase 5a) — the single most load-bearing named place
  this pass has produced: where the Transcontinental Highway from the Pole meets the Coastal Cut to the
  capital and the cluster road to Sinheung/Shirayuki. **Candidate name, grounded rather than arbitrary:** given
  Sun Yat-sen's courtesy-name naming convention already established for the city itself (Specs, Founding), a
  plausible in-world name for the junction leans on the same register — **not finalized here**, since place
  names beyond the city itself edge toward the kind of specific proper-noun commitment better left to a
  developer pass; recorded as a REQUESTED naming task rather than invented outright.
- **The rotating market** (Phase 8C) — the venue where craft/goods composition changes because no national-
  origin group holds a fixed majority style. A genuinely new, legitimately Producible institution per `05` §4
  ("new institutions... provided each is named, defined and cross-referenced") — **naming also left as a
  REQUESTED task**, same reasoning as above.
- **The original CHINARE Zhongshan Station infrastructure site** (Specs, Founding) — already named in canon,
  admissible as-is; the physical anchor for the "recognized, not granted" founding finding (Phase 1/G4).

### 2. Physical things

- **A per-basin status marker or posted board** — the physical object that would make the ambient water-basin
  awareness (Phase 4/7b) legible and player-facing, per the player-facing test (`Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques`
  §0b). **Implied by Phase 4's routine, not yet independently confirmed against any admissible source** —
  flagged as a plausible design object, not asserted as canon.
- **The candidate fusion dish** (Phase 8B) — physically real per the finding's own logic, though its specific
  name/recipe is explicitly left unnamed (Phase 8B already states this is a REQUESTED next step, not decided).
- **A glitch-coolant sampling flight or comparable serving format** — direct, grounded consequence of the
  already-canon "wide variety over raw potency" bohemian glitch-coolant designation (Phase 8A): a bohemian
  drinking culture with genuine variety plausibly has *some* format for trying several without committing to
  one, the same way a real-world tasting flight works. **Candidate, not confirmed against any admissible robot-
  culture source beyond the general canon fact already cited.**
