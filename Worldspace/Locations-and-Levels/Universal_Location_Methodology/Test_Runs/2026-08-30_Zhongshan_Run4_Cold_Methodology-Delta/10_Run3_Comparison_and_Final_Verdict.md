## The highway/relational finding — also reconciled, not contradictory

**Run 3's G5 read:** *"It is a terminus, not a corridor. Nothing passes through it... past the junction there
is sea and two neighbours."*

**This pass's Phase 5a read:** Zhongshan is "a mechanism, not a destination" for a traveler going from the
South Pole to the capital — implying real transit traffic.

**These look directly contradictory and are not, once the object is specified.** Run 3's claim is about each
**named highway** individually — Hwy 22 and Hwy 110 each genuinely terminate at that point, as named roads.
This pass's claim is about an end-to-end **journey** that switches from one named highway to the other at that
exact point. **Both are true, about different objects** (a road segment vs. a journey) — precisely the
`02` §5.3 shape, now also the shape `04`'s new Gate 6 rule asks to check for. **Reconciled reading: Zhongshan
is a terminus for every individually-named road and simultaneously the switching point where interior traffic
becomes coastal traffic for anyone making that specific journey** — which is arguably a sharper, more specific
finding than either half alone, since it explains exactly why Run 3's "zero transit" reading and this pass's
"mechanism" reading could both be defensible from the same highway file.
