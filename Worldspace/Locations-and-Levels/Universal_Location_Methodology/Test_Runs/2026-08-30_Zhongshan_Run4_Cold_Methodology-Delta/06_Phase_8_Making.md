## B. Cuisine — general population first, per the corrected Run 3 lesson

**The general answer, derived by fusion (Step C) against a real researched model, not invented from nothing:**
Yekaterinburg's **posikunchiki** — a genuine, real-world Ural-regional dish that is a *hybrid* of a fried patty
and a pelmeni dumpling, born of local necessity from two available food traditions rather than importing either
whole — is the structural model, **not the specific dish transplanted.**

**Fusion question, run properly (Step C):** does Zhongshan's own established character plausibly produce its
own version of a *necessity-fusion* dish, and if so what would it actually be? **Yes, and the composition table
answers it directly:** with a Chinese plurality (35.83%) large enough to set a base but nowhere near a majority,
and Korean/Japanese/German shares each large enough to be a routine daily-life presence (9.70% / 7.30% / 8.47%),
**Zhongshan's general cuisine plausibly runs on routine, unremarked fusion between Chinese cooking technique and
whichever other tradition is locally in easy reach — not a "fusion restaurant" novelty, but the ordinary
default**, precisely because no single tradition has the numbers to stay unmixed as a matter of course.
**This is the general answer — everyone eats some version of this, not a specialist or festival food.**

**Necessity Before Meaning technique, applied:** the practical necessity is demographic (no single-origin
household density is high enough to sustain an unmixed food culture at neighborhood scale); the moralization
that would follow — a value like "nothing here is exactly anywhere else's" — is plausible but **not asserted as
settled**, flagged as a Phase 6 cross-check for a future session rather than decided here.

**What this is NOT:** a claim that any specific real dish (posikunchiki itself, or a specific Chinese-Korean or
Chinese-German fusion food named in the real world) is transplanted wholesale. Per the source-not-specification
rule, **the mechanism (necessity-fusion, demographically forced) is taken; the specific dish is Zhongshan's own
and is not derived here** — naming an actual specific dish is left as a REQUESTED next step, since inventing one
without further research would be exactly the kind of decorative invention LAW 0 warns against.

**General-population check (Gate 2, run inline rather than deferred to QA):** this finding is stated as what
*everyone* eats, derived from the composition table (a population-wide fact), not from one restaurant, one
festival, or one profession's version. **Passes the discipline that failed Run 3 at this exact phase.**

## C. Music, arts, and craft

**General answer, fusion-derived:** Vilnius/Užupis's artisan-workshop culture (one-off handcrafted items, not
mass reproduction) and Yekaterinburg's **Sandarina Market** (a quarterly designer market mixing fashion, crafts,
and food, **never the same lineup twice**) fuse cleanly against Zhongshan's own established "no city-wide fact"
pattern from Phase 1: **a rotating craft/goods market whose exact composition changes because the population
contributing to it has no fixed majority style to converge on.** This is not invented from nothing — it is the
same structural shape (no single dominant answer) that Phase 1's water-basin deficit and Phase 8B's cuisine
finding both already independently produced, now showing up a third time in a third register. **Three
independent phases converging on the same underlying shape from different generators is strong grounding**,
not coincidence — worth flagging as the pass's most structurally consistent finding.

**Arts, specifically:** Užupis's street-art culture is explicitly **DIVERGED FROM** rather than transplanted
(per the research log) — a fixed mural only reaches whoever crosses to that specific island, which is a poor
fit for Zhongshan's archipelago-fragmented geography (Phase 1's grudging-tolerance finding). **The plausible
Zhongshan equivalent is itinerant rather than fixed** — art, performance, or craft display that moves between
islands rather than staying put, a direct consequence of the physical constraint rather than a borrowed form.
**Genuinely derived, not transplanted — the divergence is the finding, per the source-not-specification rule.**

## D. Dress and appearance

per the research log), and a seasonal extreme (60-day polar night / 61-day midnight sun). **A plausible

occasional standing water, functioning across both extremes of the light cycle. **Not researched to a specific

note), since no dress-specific search was run this session and inventing a specific named garment without
research would repeat exactly the invented-texture failure mode LAW 0 warns against.

**What the 18-origin composition plausibly does to dress, tentatively:** given no majority beyond a 36%

without any one national tradition read as the default. **Consistent with, and not yet confirmed by, the
same shape found in Phase 8B/C** — flagged as a hypothesis, not a finding, since it has not been checked
against actual research.

**Humor, fusion-derived and specific:** Austin's "Keep Austin Weird" register — self-aware, punny, playfully

without the developer's involvement, given how load-bearing a piece of invented in-world slang can become) —
**flagged as a strong candidate finding with a clear derivation, not a finalized name.**
