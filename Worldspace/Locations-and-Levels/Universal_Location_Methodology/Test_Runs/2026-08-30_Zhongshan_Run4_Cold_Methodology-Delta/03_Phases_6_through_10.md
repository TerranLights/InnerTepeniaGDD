likely institutional/collective rather than personal, since the personal-veneration slot the rest of the

**C. Death and the dead:** **RESERVED — the disposal-of-the-dead mechanism is explicitly reserved per
`RESUME_HERE.md` §6 and `05` §3.** Per that reservation's protocol: **write practice around the dead, do not
invent the mechanism.** What can be written without deciding the mechanism: *who is not mourned properly, and
why not* is a legitimate question independent of the mechanism itself. Given Phase 2's finding — a city that

candidate for "not mourned properly" is **whoever dies before completing the arrival-to-membership conversion**

practice, whatever their legal residency. **Flagged as a candidate finding, not confirmed**, since it depends on
Phase 5d material this pass has already flagged as unconfirmed.

**D. Failure State of the Core Value:** central promise candidate, per Phase 1/2/6 material so far: *this is a
place whose claim to exist is not in question.* Failure state: **what happens if the claim ever WERE
contested** — genuinely untested in this city's history (Phase 0's null: no losing claimant on record). A
place whose core promise has never actually been tested is a specific and useful finding in itself: **its
confidence in its own legitimacy is unearned in the sense that it has never had to be defended**, which is a
different thing from confidence that has survived a real challenge. Does the culture ritualize this untested
confidence, or refuse to acknowledge that it IS untested? **Candidate reading: refusal** — a city with no
arrival myth and no personal Saint is not a city inclined to dwell on its own founding conditions at all, which
would make this a case of simply not discussing it rather than of deliberately ritualizing a non-event.
**Flagged as the strongest available Phase 6 finding this pass has produced, and genuinely independent** (not
present in any exposed Run 3 material).

**E. Observance:** Federation-wide Independence Day (June 21, winter solstice) applies uniformly (Determined,

local, small, unserious observance is not yet derivable from admissible material** — flagged as a REQUESTED
input rather than invented, since Phase 6E explicitly requires at least one small/unserious entry and this pass

economy shared with Sinheung and Shirayuki; "Full detail TBD for DLC 7" — **the source itself flags this as

**not yet confirmed against any admissible materials-and-trade canon**, flagged as a REQUESTED cross-check
against `Per_City_Weapons`/materials files this pass has not opened.

**7b — Governance and decision.** *What is unadministrable here?* Candidate, following directly from Phase 1's
spine finding: **a city-wide water-safety guarantee is unadministrable**, because the underlying fact (basin
quality) is not a city-wide fact at all. **Any governance body claiming to certify "the water is safe" citywide
is either lying, or is quietly operating basin-by-basin under a citywide name** — this is precisely the kind of
authorless, good-faith-everyone shadow `00d` asks for: nobody decided to mislead anyone, the *administrative
unit* (the city) simply does not match the *physical unit* (the basin) that the claim is actually about.
**Strong `00d` shadow candidate — carried forward to QA.**

**7c — Knowledge and transmission.** How does someone learn which basin is currently good? Per Phase 4/5d
material: apparently by ambient local knowledge (the weather-awareness analogy), not formal instruction.
**What becomes of someone who cannot learn it** — a newcomer with no access to the ambient information network
(per Phase 5d, someone who hasn't yet started tracking it) — is at genuine, unglamorous risk in a way locals
are not. **Flagged for the Review Panel's Ruler/Parent questions.**

**7d — Private life, minorities, counterculture.** Per `02` §5.1's order of attempts (check what the parent
determines → what it supplies to inflect → who arrived carrying a form → is the place already doing it
somewhere → only then invent): **not yet run to completion** — deferred, since Phase 7d depends on Phase 6's
belief-landscape material, which this pass left as an open null. Recorded as a genuine gap rather than
invented content.

**Sanction proportionality:** no sanction/exclusion finding has been written yet for this pass, so this check
does not yet apply — flagged for the next session, since inventing a sanction merely to have something to
proportion-check would violate LAW 0's anti-decoration discipline.

maritime operations in enough depth to say**, and per the technique's own failure-mode warning, this must not
be asserted without real grounding, since a false-positive Unrecognized Instrument finding would be worse than
