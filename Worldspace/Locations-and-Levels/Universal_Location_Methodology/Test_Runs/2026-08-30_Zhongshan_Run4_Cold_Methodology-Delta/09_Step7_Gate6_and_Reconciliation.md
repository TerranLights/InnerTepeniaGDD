## Mismatches — killed findings, the most valuable entries per the recording law

### ⭐⭐ Partially killed, partially reconciled: "no founding arrival scene" / "untested legitimacy" (Phase 1, Phase 6, the previous Gate 8 standout)

**Not a flat kill either — the same both-are-true discipline applies.** Two of this finding's claims were
stated at different scales and need to be judged separately rather than as one bundled assertion.

**What this pass claimed:** Zhongshan has no founding arrival scene and no canonized personal founding figure
(Phase 1/G4, Phase 6), producing a city whose confidence in its own legitimacy has never been tested — the
strongest finding of the pre-Step-7 run, and its Gate 8 standout.

**What the culture sheet actually contains:** a real founding-arrival narrative — *"the first wave of Sinian
defectors establishing the settlement; the origin of the name Zhongshan"* — and a named (placeholder) founding
figure, **Founding Elder Mèi Sun**, *"credited with choosing the name Zhongshan and articulating the founding
political stance... considered the city's moral anchor."* **This directly contradicts the claim of no arrival
scene and no founding-personal-anchor.**

**Where the reasoning actually went wrong:** this pass correctly identified a real structural fact (the
*legal/diplomatic* claim was recognition of an existing presence, not the grant of an empty site) and then
over-extended it into a claim about the *narrative* register (no arrival story, no personal founding figure) —
**conflating a procedural/legal fact with the presence or absence of a human founding myth.** The two are
genuinely independent: a claim can be legally "always already there" while still having a rich, specific human
arrival narrative layered on top of it (people fleeing persecution, choosing a name, being led by a
remembered figure). **This pass never checked for the second thing before asserting its absence** — exactly
the kind of unverified negative Gate C's own discipline warns against ("a zero from a scan is not a result
until you have proved the scan could have found a hit"), applied here to a *narrative* absence rather than a
canon-figure absence.

**What DOES survive:** the narrower, literally-true claim that Zhongshan has no Saint from the Federation-wide
real-world-explorer roster (Scott/Shackleton/Amundsen/Mawson/Byrd) — that check against `National_Holidays.md`
was accurate as far as it went. **What it should have concluded, and did not:** Zhongshan substitutes a
**local** founding-elder tradition (Mèi Sun) for the Federation-wide Saint tradition, which is a genuinely
interesting and different finding — a substitution, not an absence — and this pass simply stopped one step
short of the correct conclusion by not opening the culture sheet before asserting the absence.

### ⭐⭐⭐ Reconciled via the both-are-true test, not simply killed — the strongest methodological finding of this run: the "no single dominant answer" shape (Phase 8B cuisine, Phase 8C the rotating market)

**Corrected mid-analysis, per developer instruction:** a first pass at this section flatly killed this
finding. **That was the wrong move.** `02` §5.3's own binding rule — *"do not ask which generator is right,
ask what single property would produce both readings, then check whether the two claims are about different
objects or at different scales"* — applies exactly here, and `00f`'s `refereed` disposition exists for
precisely this situation. A contradiction at one social level does not disprove a claim at another level; it
is frequently the sharper finding once both are held at once. Reconciled properly below.

**What this pass claimed:** given 18 distinct national-origin groups with no majority beyond a 36% plurality,
Zhongshan's general cuisine plausibly runs on routine, unremarked fusion, and its craft/goods economy plausibly
runs as a rotating market — both derived from demographic diversity alone.

**What the culture sheet establishes, at the PUBLIC/mainstream level specifically:** cuisine, crafts, music,
fashion, and architecture are unbroken, unmixed, continuously Chinese at that level — *"the population making
and wearing these clothes is genuinely, unbrokenly Chinese, nine or ten generations on"* · minority-origin
traditions are explicitly kept **private**: *"In private: the food cooked at home, the language spoken with
family... The city's public face is Chinese."* **At the level this pass's claim was actually stated — the
general, public-facing answer — the claim is genuinely wrong, and that half is retracted, not reconciled away.**

**But the culture sheet also supplies exactly the missing property that makes both readings true at once, at
different scales — the same move `02` §5.3 asks for:** §20 states outright that the privacy the non-prying
norm affords is not static across generations — *"second and third-generation immigrant families sometimes
feel their heritage being absorbed by osmosis... Some families know exactly [how much of it is left]. Others
genuinely aren't sure anymore, and the culture that would never pry is also the culture that will never help
them find out."* **This is a population, already named in existing canon, whose private heritage is
degrading and blurring precisely because nobody ever asks about it or compares notes.** That is the natural
home for a genuine, uncontradicted fusion finding: **not a public rotating market, but a private, unannounced,
half-remembered blending — a fourth- or fifth-generation Korean-descended household's home cooking that has
quietly absorbed pieces of a neighbor's or a spouse's tradition, unrecorded, because the same privacy norm that
protects a heritage from being pried into also means nobody is checking it against anything else.** **This
survives Step 7 as a genuinely new, uncontradicted, and arguably richer finding than the one it replaces** —
private-sphere, generationally-scaled fusion, rather than public-sphere, demographically-scaled fusion.

**The reconciling property, stated the way `02` §5.3 requires:** the single property that produces both
readings is the **non-prying norm itself** — it is what keeps the public sphere pure (nobody imports outside
practice into the shared, watched public register) and simultaneously what lets the private sphere drift
unchecked (nobody polices what happens behind closed doors, including drift away from a claimed heritage's
original form). **One disposition, two opposite-looking effects, at two different scales — exactly the
shape `02` §5.2's both-are-true test predicts, not a coincidence.**

**Logged to the shared observations file as M-29**, reframed from "diversity doesn't predict fusion" (the
over-corrected version) to the more precise and more useful lesson: **a composition-derived prediction that
conflicts with established public-facing canon should be tested for a scale mismatch (public/private,
mainstream/counterculture, older-generation/newer-generation) before being either accepted or killed — the
conflict is very often a sign the claim belongs one level down or over, not that it is baseless.**

**What this does to Phase 8's other findings:** the glitch-coolant "bohemian variety" canon hit (Phase 8A) gets
the identical treatment, not a kill: the variety is real but lives at counterculture scale (§19's Southeast
Asian-community-driven loud/varied scene), not general-population scale — the same public/private,
mainstream/counterculture axis reconciles it exactly as it reconciles cuisine.

**Phase 8E's self-deprecating-humor finding remains genuinely killed**, not reconciled — it depended
specifically on the false "no founding myth" premise (Phase 6's kill, above), and the culture sheet's
established expressive register (understatement, restraint) has no scale at which a joke-forward mainstream
humor culture becomes true. Where the loud counterculture's own humor register might plausibly sit is not
derived by this pass — flagged as a REQUESTED next step rather than forced.
