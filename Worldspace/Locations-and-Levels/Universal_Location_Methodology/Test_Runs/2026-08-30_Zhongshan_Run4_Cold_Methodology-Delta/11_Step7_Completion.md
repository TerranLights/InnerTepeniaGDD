about it.

oldest, most symbolically central structures (the Founding Hall, the Standing Stone) are plausibly among its

not contradict, this run's own highway finding** — the tri-junction is not just relationally significant, it

Founding Elder Mèi Sun was found. **Opening the megasheet folder shows the retraction should have gone

from "untested legitimacy" and "no founding myth" as a location can get. **This run's original Phase 1/Phase 6
finding was wrong at every level once the full withheld set is considered, not merely wrong at the public/
private scale `09` identified** — recorded plainly rather than softened, since the both-are-true test does not
guarantee every finding survives it (per `04`'s own new caveat), and this one, on fuller information, does not
survive at all.
