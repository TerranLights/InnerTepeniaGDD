# 6 · ⛔ STANDING LAWS THAT OVERRIDE EVERYTHING ABOVE

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **LAW 0 — DEPTH OVER SPEED** | ***Never rush to a fast result.*** Contemplate before writing. Research rather than recall. Chase third-order effects. **There is no credit for finishing quickly** — *the QA gates can confirm a pass is not **wrong**; none of them can tell you it is **thin*** |
| ⛔ **GPS PURPOSES ONLY** | **A real SITE's coordinate is the only admissible import. Not its nationality, not its history, not its operators, not its fate.** ⭐⭐ **AND ITS PURPOSE, STATED 2026-09-06:** ***the law exists so a city is characterized by WHO LIVES THERE, not by WHOSE STATION IT OCCUPIES.*** ⛔ **It governs `G7` (the site). It does NOT forbid ethnicity.** ✅ **Once composition is established, the founding population's origin-culture IS admissible** — *`§C.9b`/`§C.9c`.* ⚠ **A pass that refuses all ethnic material is MISREADING this law and will produce placeless cities** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **COMPOSITION NAMES THE STOCK; TIME AND PLACE PRODUCE THE CULTURE** | ***~250 years of local divergence is applied to the origin culture, never skipped.*** **Operator inputs: time · separation · local environmental setting · local struggles and hardships · local goals · local sensibilities and habits.** ⛔ **Neither extreme is the answer** — *refusing ethnic material gives a placeless city; transplanting the source culture intact gives "a costumed version of somewhere real" (`Step 3.5`).* ⭐ **And it is an ANTI-CONVERGENCE engine: two cities founded by the same nation MUST diverge, because the operator's inputs differ.** *(Developer, 2026-09-06 — `§C.9d`.)* |
| ⛔ **SECOND INTERWAR IS THE FRAME** | **Four spec fields are post-war and are NEVER inputs:** `Status` · `Current Status` · `Legacy` · `Connection to Concordia` |
| ⛔ **NEVER CARRY ONE LOCATION'S ANSWERS TO ANOTHER** | ⭐ **Enforced by NOT READING the other location.** *You cannot carry across an answer you never read.* ⛔ **The "at least one is wrong" check is itself a comparison — ⏸️ TERMINAL, never in-run** *(2026-09-06)* |
| ⛔⛔⛔ **ONE LOCATION, ON ITS OWN TERMS — NO CITY IS EVER COMPARED TO ANY OTHER** | ***"Do NOT compare any city to any other city. Ever. That is for much, much, much later in development. For the ULM/CST/RWBEM meta-methodology, for each location, create that one location on its own terms."*** *(Developer, 2026-09-06.)* ⛔ **No ranking, no z-scores, no "nth of N," no "unlike X," no other city as a control, a baseline or an implicit normal.** ✅ **The location's OWN figures, stated flatly — and `01` §5.3a's peer-free substitutes.** ⭐ **This is `§C.8d` generalized from Concordia to every city, and the runbook's own "THE UNIT IS ONE LOCATION" made explicit.** ✅ **RULED 2026-09-06 — DIFFERENTIATION IS A TERMINAL CHECK:** *"during the course of the ULM/CST/RWBEM, just follow the data wherever it leads for one single location on its own terms, and we'll worry about differentiation later."* **All five comparison instruments (`03` §5b · Gate 6 · Step 6 + the table · `02` §4's three-step rule · `02` §G8's z-score) move OUT of the per-location pass; the table becomes WRITE-ONLY in-run.** ⭐ **RELATION stays legal — the one-sentence test: delete every other city's name; if the claim survives, it was relation.** ⚠ **Scope is `ULM/CST/RWBEM` only — the district runbook is untouched.** **Full statement: `00_RUNBOOK.md`** |
| ⛔⛔ **NO FORCED FIT — AN EMPTY SLOT IS A RESULT** | ***Never force a location into a category it does not naturally occupy, in EITHER direction*** — *neither assigning an existing roster member that does not fit, nor inventing a bespoke one to fill a blank.* ⭐ **Rosters are OPEN and expected to grow, so their SIZE is never the test.** **When nothing fits: say so, and LEAVE THE SLOT EXPLICITLY OPEN** — *"none is sited here" must never read as "none is possible here."* **Write what the place actually has — a stake, a compact, a debt, a standard of worth — as what it is, without promoting it into the empty category.** *(Developer ruling, 2026-09-06. Full statement: `00_RUNBOOK.md`; operational form: `03` §Phase 6 B.)* |

| ⭐ **A REVIEW PANEL POSITION IS NOT GUARANTEED WHAT IT WANTS** | *If satisfying an objection would make the place more like the others, the disposition is **`unmet`**, and the refusal is written as characterization* |
