# 2 · THE 11 PHASES — the writing *(`03_The_Phase_Spine.md`)*

| Phase | Name | Asks |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **FRAME** | *What kind of thing is this, at what scale, in what state, in what era, and inside what?* |
| **1** | ⭐ **CONSTRAINT & CAPABILITY** *(the spine)* | *What can this place do without trying, what can it not do at all, and what must it keep paying?* |
| **2** | **COMPOSITION & ARRIVAL** | *Who is actually here, in what proportion, from where — and by what route did they come?* |
| **3** | **SURFACE & TEXTURE** | *What is this place made of, and what is it like to be inside it — to the eye, ear, nose, and skin?* |
| **4** | **ORDINARY LIFE** | *What is an ordinary person's day here, apart from what this place is famous for?* |
| **5** | **RELATION & GEOMETRY** | *What is this place to the places around it, to the thing that contains it, and to anyone crossing it?* |
| **6** | **MEANING** | *What does this place believe, and what does it hold sacred without saying so?* |
| **7** | **ORDER** | *How does it organize work, decide things, pass on skill, and handle those who do not fit?* |
| **8** | **MAKING** | *What does this place cook, sing, wear, build, play, and say?* |
| **9** | **POPULATIONS** | *What is life here like for each distinct kind of person present, and how do those kinds relate?* |
| **10** | **CATALOG** | *What is actually here — named, specific, and enterable?* |

# 4 · THE 8 GENERATORS — what produces findings *(`02_Generators_Capability_and_Symbols.md`)*

**Step 2 needs at least three, or there is no capability profile.**

| | Name | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** | **Assigned symbolic substrate** | Planet + Element for cities. ⭐ *The only generator with **no obligation to be plausible*** — it constrains toward the meaningful. ⚠ For these cities it is **provenance-downstream of a personality read**, so corroboration only |
| **G2** | **Physical & environmental** | Terrain, climate, altitude, exposure, hazards — **what the site provides and what it withholds** |
| **G3** | **Function / purpose** | What it is for — *and separately, **what its parent needs from it**. The two disagreeing is itself the generator* |
| **G4** | **Founding condition** | Who, when, why, under what constraint, **with what and WITHOUT what.** ⭐ *The absences are the yield* |
| **G5** | **Network position** | What connects, in which direction, carrying what volume. ⭐ *Its distinctive yield is **asymmetry*** |
| **G6** | **Defining event** | *A disaster, a war, a discovery, a founding crime.* ⛔ **Push to third order: what practice exists now that only makes sense because of it — and does anyone still know why?** ⏸️ *Deferred out of input prep corpus-wide; belongs with city histories* |
| **G7** | **Real-world inspiration** | Which real case anchors it. ⛔⛔ **GPS PURPOSES ONLY — a coordinate, never a cause, an identity, or a history** |
| **G8** | **Demographic composition** | Who is here, in what proportion, from where |

# 6 · ⛔ STANDING LAWS THAT OVERRIDE EVERYTHING ABOVE

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **LAW 0 — DEPTH OVER SPEED** | ***Never rush to a fast result.*** Contemplate before writing. Research rather than recall. Chase third-order effects. **There is no credit for finishing quickly** — *the QA gates can confirm a pass is not **wrong**; none of them can tell you it is **thin*** |
| ⛔ **GPS PURPOSES ONLY** | A real site's coordinate is the only admissible import. **Not its nationality, not its history, not its operators, not its fate** |
| ⛔ **SECOND INTERWAR IS THE FRAME** | **Four spec fields are post-war and are NEVER inputs:** `Status` · `Current Status` · `Legacy` · `Connection to Concordia` |
| ⛔ **NEVER CARRY ONE LOCATION'S ANSWERS TO ANOTHER** | *If two places produce similar-shaped answers to the same technique, at least one is wrong* |
| ⛔ **PASTE RAW QA OUTPUT** | **Never summarize it.** *Self-audit error in this project has run in one direction — toward flattering the pass — on every occasion it has been measured* |
| ⭐ **A REVIEW PANEL POSITION IS NOT GUARANTEED WHAT IT WANTS** | *If satisfying an objection would make the place more like the others, the disposition is **`unmet`**, and the refusal is written as characterization* |
