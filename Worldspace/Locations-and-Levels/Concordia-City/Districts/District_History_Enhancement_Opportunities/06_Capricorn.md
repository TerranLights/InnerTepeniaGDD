`../District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities.md` for the index and progress checklist, and
`../District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Template.md` for the full method and input list. **Scope:**
Stage 1 only (the ~250-year Second Interwar organic-formation period, 2564–2812). No war fallout, no
present-day material. **Flag only** — no new Stage 1 narrative drafted, no texture woven into the actual
Deep Dive or Megasheet files.

**Extra constraint specific to this district:** Capricorn's own central pre-war conflict — which of seven
documented possibilities (the Capricorn's core injustice [mechanism TBD — 4 contenders shortlisted, see `06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`] or the six `06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`
alternatives) actually happens around c. 2761 — is deliberately unresolved by the developer's own
direction. Every idea below is chosen, the same way the existing Full_Extrapolation and
Cross_Reference_Synthesis already discipline themselves, to hold true regardless of which of the seven
options eventually wins.

**Already covered (Stage 1):** Capricorn was zoned as the heavy manufacturing hub during the Early
Post-Falkland Construction Boom (late 2560s–2580s), its meritocratic "builder" culture forged directly out
of genuine, immediate survival necessity — fast, centralized decisions about labor and resource
allocation that were the right call at the time. The Deep Dive proposes a structural, not malicious, root
cause for whichever specific injustice eventually gets exposed around c. 2761: founding-era arrangements
that made sense under crisis conditions simply never got revisited once the crisis passed, sitting
undisturbed for roughly two centuries because nobody with the power to end them had any incentive to.
`06b`'s own "shared pattern" note independently confirms four of the six alternatives share this exact
shape, and the Full_Extrapolation resolves two genuinely option-neutral facts: the Spire System's literal,
floor-level status architecture exists from the earliest construction years, administratively useful
regardless of what mechanism actually assigns rank underneath it; and Capricorn's founding-era
relationships to the districts it built (power dependency on Aries, infrastructure ties to Virgo,
construction obligations to Cancer and Taurus) are already fully formed by the end of the construction
boom, independent of the eventual c. 2761 reveal. The Cross_Reference_Synthesis further identifies
Capricorn's own pending calcified-injustice pattern as a structural cousin of Libra's already-established
"Perpetual Emergency" — a recurring Second Interwar Period civic shape, not a Capricorn-specific flaw.
Vision Notes confirms atmospheric texture (lit multi-story fabrication facilities, functional window-doors
for repositioning materials, a "driven but not hurried" pace distinct from Aries', a focused rather than
performative mood) while explicitly flagging the street-level status-visibility tension as unresolved.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Full_Extrapolation's own Section III establishes,
   as an option-neutral founding-era fact, that Capricorn held "construction obligations to Cancer and
   Taurus (whose earliest sheltering and residential infrastructure Capricorn quite literally built)" —
   but no document describes what that obligation actually looked like in practice: a formal
   inter-district contract, assigned rotating crews, or something else. A chain establishing the actual
   mechanics of how Capricorn's founding-era labor got allocated to building other districts' own earliest
   infrastructure would use an already-settled fact the source material never makes concrete, and would
   hold regardless of which of the seven c. 2761 options is eventually chosen.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Neumayer — a genuine top-tier Capricorn feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "precision as near-religious civic virtue... responding to [being uncredited] with meticulous internal
   documentation rather than grievance." All seven of Capricorn's own possible c. 2761 conflicts involve
   some form of uncredited or undervalued labor, yet none of Capricorn's own documents mention Neumayer's
   specific coping strategy — document meticulously, don't grieve — which sits in genuine tension with
   whatever specific grievance narrative Capricorn's own population eventually voices. A chain about
   Neumayer-descended arrivals bringing this alternate response to uncredited labor into Capricorn would
   use this unused population contrast, and holds regardless of which option wins, since it's about
   *how people respond* rather than *what the injustice specifically is*.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Dubai's skyscraper
   hierarchy as a Secondary-tier pick, unused anywhere in Capricorn's own documents — and genuinely
   option-neutral, since it grounds the Spire System itself (Full_Extrapolation §II, already confirmed
   independent of which c. 2761 option is chosen), not any specific injustice. Dubai is real-world known
   specifically for literal vertical status hierarchy — floor level and building height as directly legible
   social rank. A chain grounding the Spire System's own founding-era design specifically in a
   Dubai-style vertical-hierarchy precedent would give an already-settled, option-neutral piece of
   Capricorn's architecture a concrete real-world anchor it currently lacks.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Capricorn.md`
   explicitly flags as still open "does the established 'status lighting readable from the street' detail
   need adjusting, or does the developer's vision reflect a specific vantage point/area where it's simply
   less visible than elsewhere in the district?" This is a distinct question from the Spire System's own
   settled option-neutrality (Cross_Reference_Synthesis Finding 2 resolves whether the mechanism is
   visible in principle, not whether it's actually visible from any given street). A chain resolving
   whether founding-era Capricorn's status lighting was legible district-wide or only from specific
   vantage points would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly, and holds regardless of the
   central unresolved question.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Capricorn.md` explicitly leaves open whether "driven" — the
   district's own defining pace — "describes the workers themselves, the general atmosphere, or both."
   This doesn't fit cleanly under any of the other four lenses, and is a genuinely open characterization
   question rather than an economic, population, precedent, or architectural one. A chain resolving
   whether founding-era Capricorn's driven quality was a trait of individual workers, an emergent property
   of the district as a whole, or both simultaneously would use this developer-flagged open question, and
   holds true regardless of which of the seven c. 2761 options is eventually chosen.
