**Already covered (Stage 1):** Taurus is the one founding-era district in this pass so far with no
originating wound at all. Its founding population — ordinary bonded human-robot pairs from the original
exile — was given a deliberately planned, well-insulated residential sector prioritizing family units and
private courtyards, and its defining "stubborn resistance to destabilizing change" is explicitly a product
of 250 comparatively *easy* years, not hardship processed into culture the way Cancer's and Scorpio's own
traits are. The Insulation Schism (Cold Side/Warm Side, tracking insulation material repeatedly diverted
to Capricorn) is confirmed genuine Stage 1 stratification, per the Full_Extrapolation taking roughly a
century to become a recognized social fact rather than a founding-year one. The Full_Extrapolation also
establishes the pre-war Bonded Pair Registry as unremarkable municipal record-keeping (its later
near-sacred status is created entirely by the catastrophic loss still 250 years in the future here), and
dates Taurus/Leo noise friction as immediate and constant from both districts' earliest founding years, no
accumulation required. The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's two findings are the richest material here: the
Cancer-to-Taurus refugee pipeline is a continuous, 250-year-old downstream system (not a post-war
phenomenon), meaning Taurus' own genealogical "old-timer" families quietly include descendants of Cancer's
earliest arrivals; and Taurus' own celebrated household-scale thermal-engineering expertise plausibly
traces to the same c. 2650s crisis (Aries' proposed First Silence) that produced Cancer's own chronic
Aries grievance — two districts' defining traits as independently-discovered sibling adaptations to one
shared originating event. Vision Notes confirms atmospheric texture (a subtle rather than stark
maintenance/weathering gradient tracking the Coldsiders divide, a private household-directed warmth that
doesn't extend to strangers on the street, a "slow-brisk-walk" pace distinct from Cancer's) without adding
new historical material.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md` states, in both
   its draft passes, that Taurus' own "strict conservation and official distribution systems... created
   demand for smuggled comforts and goods, strengthening Pisces' black-market networks" — but this
   relationship appears nowhere in Taurus' own four documents, and the source doesn't specify whether it
   predates the war. Taurus' pre-war conservation culture is independently confirmed (the same
   thermal-engineering pride the Cross_Reference_Synthesis already dates to c. 2650s). A chain resolving
   whether this Taurus/Pisces demand relationship already existed pre-war — the same "give a
   post-war-sounding relationship an appropriately scaled pre-war precedent" move the Cancer and Scorpio
   entries in this pass already used — would use this cross-district detail Taurus' own material never
   acknowledges.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Port Lockroy — a genuine 1st-tier Taurus feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "a city that never demolishes its own history": a communal stone wall, physically added to by
   generations who will never see it finished. None of Taurus' own documents mention Port Lockroy
   specifically, despite this being an almost literal real-world echo of Taurus' own heirloom culture (a
   grandparent's chair as non-negotiable furniture, genealogies spanning four generations). A chain about
   Port Lockroy-descended residents contributing an actual generations-spanning collective-building
   custom to Taurus — distinct from the district's own generic "old-timer" framing — would use this
   unused, thematically-resonant population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists the Kakheti wine
   region, Georgia, as a Supporting-tier pick — unused anywhere in Taurus' own documents. Kakheti is real-
   world famous specifically for qvevri winemaking: a fermentation technique passed down within families
   across generations, using buried clay vessels rather than industrial equipment. Taurus' own established
   culture already includes "extraordinary preserved-food and fermentation cuisine developed out of
   necessity" — confirmed Stage 1 texture with no specific technique or origin story attached to it. A
   chain grounding Taurus' fermentation cuisine specifically in a Kakheti-style family-transmitted
   technique would give an already-established but generic detail a concrete, previously uncited real-world
   anchor.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Taurus.md` explicitly
   lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "whether the Coldsiders divide's visual
   subtlety... says anything about how socially significant the line actually still is day-to-day, versus
   being more a matter of historical record." None of Taurus' four Megasheet documents resolve this either
   — the Full_Extrapolation dates when the Schism became *recognized* (c. mid-2600s) but never addresses
   how seriously later generations, well before the war, actually still treated it day-to-day. A chain
   resolving whether pre-war Taurus residents lived the Cold Side/Warm Side divide as active daily reality
   or largely-inherited historical fact by the war's eve would close a gap the developer's own notes flag
   directly.

5. **Other.** `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md`'s "Scorpio → Taurus" entry — Scorpio's reputation for
   dangerous transformations creating "wariness in Taurus family-oriented communities, influencing housing
   policies and 'newcomer' screening" — appears in none of Taurus' own four documents, and isn't dated to
   either stage. Since Scorpio is confirmed founded in the same late-2560s window as Taurus, a chain
   resolving whether a milder pre-war version of this wariness already shaped Taurus' own founding-era
   housing or screening norms — parallel to how this pass has already resolved comparable undated
   cross-district relationships for Cancer (Aquarius) and Scorpio (Leo) — would extend that same move to a
   relationship Taurus' own material never touches at all.
