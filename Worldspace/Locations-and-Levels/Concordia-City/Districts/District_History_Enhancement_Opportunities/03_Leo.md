**Already covered (Stage 1):** Leo was zoned in the 2570s–2590s explicitly to combat "the psychological
toll of Antarctic exile," but the Deep Dive's own root-cause reading goes deeper than a simple morale
mandate: robots held confirmed legal personhood on Upper Earth since 2318, well before the Falkland
Treaty, yet that legal fact was never the same thing as being *treated* as if it were true — a gap that
recurs across both the original War of Upper Earth and, 250 years later, the Long Night War's own
inciting incident. Leo's founding mission is to close that gap through undeniable, embodied performance,
which is also why the district's established "no audience/performer distinction" reads as the 240-year
logical endpoint of the mission rather than a quirky artistic trait. A live, unresolved "scale vs.
intimacy" philosophical disagreement about how personhood actually gets proven is present from founding
per the Full_Extrapolation, expressed through ordinary patronage and festival-scheduling friction rather
than any named political conflict — the Star War itself is deliberately treated as *not yet having
happened* in this pre-war material, per developer direction, with the founding-era conditions built to
make a conflict shaped like it a natural outcome without committing to its specific documented form. The
Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 places Leo alongside Scorpio as a shared founding-era "confrontation
over avoidance" civic strategy, aimed at opposite audiences (outward vs. inward); Finding 2 establishes
that even a political resolution to the eventual Star War can never resolve the underlying artistic
question, since both scale and intimacy are equally valid proofs of the same founding claim. Vision Notes
confirms atmospheric texture (blue/pink-leaning but broadly rainbow lighting, a district-wide "pull" toward
participation, blended live-music-and-crowd-noise soundscape) without resolving whether that pull reads as
personal or general — left explicitly open even by the developer.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section
   names, as one of the founding-era conditions being deliberately built (without committing to the Star
   War itself), "patron families with real stakes in Leo's council seat and power allocation" — but no
   document, including the Full_Extrapolation, ever explains how patronage actually functions: what a
   patron family provides, what a performer owes in return, or how patronage connects to the district's
   own civic governance seat. A chain establishing the mechanics of founding-era patronage would use a
   detail the source material names as a deliberately-built condition but never elaborates.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Dumont d'Urville — a genuine top-tier Leo feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   a live-music culture ("New Orleans at 1/20th scale," music in nearly every eatery) explicitly proposed
   as "possibly load-bearing to residents' will to stay somewhere this physically demanding." None of
   Leo's own four documents mention Dumont d'Urville, despite this being an unusually direct real-world
   echo of Leo's own "morale is infrastructure" founding logic — performance as literal survival
   mechanism, not just civic culture. A chain about Dumont d'Urville-descended arrivals bringing this
   specific performance-as-survival ethos into Leo's own founding-era culture would use this unused,
   thematically load-bearing population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Andalusian flamenco
   culture, Seville, as a Secondary-tier pick — unused anywhere in Leo's own documents, and distinct from
   the district's Primary-tier scale-coded picks (Ibiza, the Las Vegas Strip). Flamenco is real-world
   known specifically for family-lineage transmission and physically demanding, intensely intimate
   performance — the "impossible to fake" register the Full_Extrapolation already names as one half of
   Leo's own founding-era scale-vs-intimacy tension, with no concrete real-world anchor given to either
   side. A chain grounding the intimate-form side of that tension specifically in a flamenco-style
   lineage-transmitted tradition would tie Leo's own established "artistic lineages taken as seriously as
   blood lineages" texture to a genuine, previously uncited precedent.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Leo.md` explicitly
   lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "whether specific areas of the Golden Ring or
   wider district have any distinguishable character within the overall bright/loud/colorful atmosphere,
   or whether it's uniform throughout." None of Leo's four Megasheet documents address this — the Golden
   Ring is established as "the district's social nervous system" but always described as a single,
   undifferentiated whole. A chain establishing whether founding-era zoning already produced distinguishable
   character by area (a scale-performance quarter versus an intimate-form quarter, say, tracking the
   district's own core tension) would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Leo.md` explicitly leaves open — unresolved even by the developer —
   whether Leo's signature "pull toward participation" feeling is personal (wanting *you* specifically
   involved) or general (built to pull everyone in regardless of who they are). This doesn't fit cleanly
   under any of the other four lenses, and sits directly alongside Leo's own established "no
   audience/performer distinction" without the Deep Dive or any Megasheet document actually engaging it. A
   chain resolving whether founding-era Leo ever articulated this ambiguity explicitly, or whether it was
   productively unresolved from the district's very first years — consistent with Leo's own established
   pattern of tensions that never fully close — would use this genuinely open, developer-flagged question.
