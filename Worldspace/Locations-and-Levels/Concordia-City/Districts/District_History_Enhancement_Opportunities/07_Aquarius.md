**Already covered (Stage 1):** Aquarius was zoned in the 2570s–2590s as the R&D sector for idealists and
rogue engineers, and the Deep Dive identifies it as a fourth, structurally distinct founding-era response
to the original exile wound — not nurturing it (Cancer), confronting it (Scorpio), or suppressing it
through labor (Aries), but trying to engineer past it entirely, on the belief that sufficiently radical
reinvention could make the wound structurally impossible to recur. The Failed Utopias' repeated collapse
(First Collective, Open Frame Project, the still-young Living Network Experiment) is identified as
structural rather than unlucky: every attempt to build internal self-sufficiency remains materially
dependent on Aries, Virgo, and Capricorn regardless of its own ideological independence — true
self-sufficiency was never actually possible inside Concordia's own resource-interdependent structure.
The contamination-event/oversight-evasion cycle is confirmed already mature and self-perpetuating well
before the war, visible as early as the First Collective's own 2590s collapse. The Full_Extrapolation
places the Living Network Experiment's entire pre-war existence in its promising, not-yet-strained
infancy, and dates the oversight-evasion cycle as already a mature, multi-generational institution by the
period's end. The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 proposes a genuine, previously unstated causal
link: Aquarius' own earliest experimental frames plausibly contributed to Scorpio's First Fracture,
two centuries before either district's post-war material connects the two. Finding 2 establishes Aquarius
as a distinct third strategy outside Leo's own confrontation/avoidance axis, expanding the founding-era
wound-response typology to at least five (with Libra proposed as a fifth). Vision Notes confirms
atmospheric texture (literal outdoor ice/snow lab complexes for occasional cold-testing, a genuine visual
patchwork of construction eras, activity mostly happening indoors) while leaving several questions
explicitly open.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section
   names, as explicitly unresolved, "the exact count and rhythm of Contamination Events across two and a
   half centuries" — established only as recurring "multiple times," never pinned to specific dates or
   frequency. No other document resolves this either. A chain establishing an actual documented rhythm —
   evenly spaced, clustering around specific technological pushes, accelerating over time — would close a
   gap the source material itself flags as open, and would give the district's defining institutional cycle
   an actual shape rather than a vague repetition.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Kunlun — a genuine top-tier Aquarius feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes a
   population whose "engagement with its own hardship is joyful and all-consuming... not effortfully
   confrontational," a space/astronomy/comms-heritage community matched to Aquarius' own "visionary/
   scientific character... first." None of Aquarius' own four documents mention Kunlun, despite this being
   a genuine values match for Aquarius' own optimistic engineer-past-the-wound founding mission — joyful
   exuberance rather than the grim determination other districts' founding populations carry. A chain
   about Kunlun-descended arrivals bringing this specific register of hardship-as-joy into Aquarius' own
   founding-era culture would use this unused, thematically distinct population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Biosphere 2, Arizona,
   as a Supporting-tier pick — unused anywhere in Aquarius' own documents. Biosphere 2 is real-world famous
   for a closed-ecological-system experiment that failed to achieve genuine self-sufficiency, undone by
   oxygen depletion and crew conflict despite enormous engineering ambition — an almost exact real-world
   precedent for the Deep Dive's own structural diagnosis of the Failed Utopias (material dependence that
   ideology alone can never engineer away). A chain grounding one of the three Failed Utopias' specific
   proximate collapse in a Biosphere-2-style closed-system failure would give the district's own
   structural pattern a concrete, previously uncited real-world anchor.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Aquarius.md`
   explicitly lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "whether the visually patchwork
   architecture reads as chaotic/uncoordinated, or more like an interesting, legible history." None of
   Aquarius' four Megasheet documents resolve this — the "four or five layered eras, never torn down"
   texture is established as fact but never given an in-world reading. A chain establishing how founding-
   era and early-generation Aquarius residents themselves perceived their own accumulating architectural
   patchwork — pride in visible history, or simple institutional indifference to appearance — would close a
   gap the developer's own notes flag directly.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Aquarius.md` explicitly leaves open "what the indoor atmosphere of
   Aquarius's labs feels like, given that's where most of the real activity actually happens" — the vision
   session's own vantage point only captured the exterior patchwork, not the interior where the district's
   defining work occurs. This doesn't fit cleanly under any of the other four lenses. A chain establishing
   what founding-era lab interiors actually felt like day to day — sterile and controlled, or as
   improvisational and patchwork as the exteriors — would use this genuinely open, developer-flagged
   question about the district's own most functionally important spaces.
