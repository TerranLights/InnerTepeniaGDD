**Already covered (Stage 1):** Sagittarius was established in the late 2560s–2590s as the launch point for
surface expeditions and subnet construction, and the Deep Dive identifies it — alongside Taurus and
Capricorn — as a third genuinely distinct kind of founding-era story: not a response to the exile's
original wound at all, but opportunity-seeking with philosophy as a practical side effect of frontier
stakes. Its own defining wound, the Third Expansion Collapse (sixty-three dead, an eleven-day stranding),
is self-inflicted rather than externally imposed — a direct consequence of the district's own values
working exactly as designed, distinct in shape from every wound-response district's story. The Siligel
Rush and Bust (c. 2772–2777) is established as a live, mechanically grounded grievance for roughly the
last thirty-five years of this document's own timeframe: per the Full_Extrapolation (Section IV),
Capricorn refines Sagittarius' extracted materials into finished siligel, absorbing stable ongoing value
while Sagittarius alone bears the boom-and-bust volatility. The Full_Extrapolation also resolves pace (a
dual rhythm, present from the earliest habitable years — conserving at rest, urgent on the ice), clan
formation (bound by shared risk-history rather than bloodline, from the district's earliest generations),
and living-arrangement architecture (descended directly from expedition equipment, not designed
residential building). The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 places Sagittarius and Capricorn as
mirrored, opposite-facing non-wound-response districts (centrifugal ambition vs. centripetal ambition);
Finding 2 identifies a dated near-coincidence neither district's own material currently notices — Capricorn
entering its most economically fortunate stretch (the Rush, c. 2772) almost exactly a decade after quietly
burying its own documented internal exposure (c. 2761). Vision Notes confirms atmospheric texture (vast,
sparse, wind-driven wilderness, isolated but mutually trusting clan clusters, danger read as environmental
rather than social) without adding beyond what the Full_Extrapolation already resolves.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Full_Extrapolation's own "Notable Figures" section
   names, as an unnamed placeholder, "an early Rush-era resource administrator, watching the boom's
   political power concentrate and then evaporate in real time... plausibly the first person in
   Sagittarius' own institutional memory to articulate" the district's specific grievance against
   Capricorn — but no document narrates the actual moment this figure realized or voiced it. A chain
   dramatizing that first articulation would use a figure and event the source material names as
   significant but never actually shows, the same pattern this pass has already used productively for
   Aries' Political Weapon and Libra's recalled official.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Signy — a genuine top-tier Sagittarius feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "total self-sufficiency, a wildlife-and-weather civic rhythm, genuine peace with chronic isolation...
   patient and content." This is a distinct register from Sagittarius' own established "push further,"
   adrenaline-seeking, restless-expansion ethos. None of Sagittarius' own four documents mention Signy. A
   chain about Signy-descended arrivals bringing this calmer, already-at-peace form of frontier resilience
   into a district otherwise driven by constant forward momentum — a population that finds Sagittarius'
   pace excessive rather than natural — would use this unused population contrast.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Dawson City, Yukon
   (the Klondike Gold Rush) as a Secondary-tier pick, unused anywhere in Sagittarius' own documents. Dawson
   City is real-world specific for exactly the shape the Siligel Rush and Bust already has — a sudden
   boomtown, infrastructure built for a population that didn't last, political and economic power that
   evaporated once the deposits did. A chain grounding the Rush's own specific texture (what the boomtown
   years physically looked like, what got built and then stranded) in a Dawson-City-style precedent would
   give an already-established, mechanically detailed event a concrete real-world anchor it currently
   lacks.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `Regional-Characteristics/District_Prominent_Features.md`
   names "lookout towers and long-range beacons" among Sagittarius' established set-dressing, with no
   origin given — unlike the district's living arrangements, which the Full_Extrapolation already traces
   to expedition equipment. A chain establishing when and why founding-era Sagittarius first built a
   beacon network — a response to a specific lost expedition, or standard practice inherited from the
   earliest scouting crews — would ground an established but originless piece of the district's built
   environment, the same gap this pass has already closed for Cancer's Care Circles and Leo's Golden Ring.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" list names, distinct from the Full_Extrapolation's
   already-resolved question about the Collapse's own fading engineering detail, "whether the oral-
   tradition preservation crisis has already claimed anything specific by the end of this period" more
   broadly. This doesn't fit cleanly under any of the other four lenses — it's a knowledge-transmission
   question, not an economic, population, precedent, or architectural one. A chain establishing one
   specific, non-Collapse-related piece of frontier knowledge already lost to the district's own
   oral-only culture by the war's eve — a good quarry site, a specific survival technique — would use this
   genuinely open question distinct from the one the Full_Extrapolation already answered.
