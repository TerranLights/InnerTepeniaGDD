**Already covered (Stage 1):** Gemini was built in the late 2560s–2580s as the Arcanet's central routing
nexus, and the Deep Dive proposes its defining Twin Channels — an official public network and an
unofficial shadow one, run by the same people — as a 250-year generational echo of the Falkland Treaty's
own founding structure: an official narrative built to look like legitimate negotiation, laid over a
coerced reality underneath it. The Great Corruption (a wiping of pre-treaty archives, plausibly but not
confirmedly targeted) is treated with the same deliberate-non-resolution discipline as Pisces' Flood —
already happened somewhere in this period, timing and mechanism genuinely open. The Mega_Init's own
scope note requires real reinvention, not editing, for the Janbogo-city/Zukelli refugee dynamic, since
Zukelli hasn't been destroyed yet. The Full_Extrapolation gives the Speed Problem a genuine peacetime root
(ordinary continent-spanning distance rewards fast information relay for entirely mundane reasons, long
before any war), and proposes Gemini's internal factions (Speed, Verification, Dual-Network Operators)
already exist as lived professional realities from founding, simply not yet named or institutionalized.
The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 proposes that if the Great Corruption's cover-up motive is real,
Libra's own founding institution — not just Upper Earth — is a plausible party with something to hide,
since Libra is the same institutional lineage born from the Post-Falkland Treaty Negotiations the missing
records would concern. Finding 2 identifies Gemini's Twin Channels and Aquarius' oversight-evasion cycle
as two independent founding-era versions of the same "official story, real story" structure, extending an
already-noted three-district pattern (Gemini, Libra, Pisces) to a fourth. Vision Notes confirms atmospheric
texture (a literal glowing data-circuit built environment, a conversational din distinct from Aries' or
Capricorn's industrial soundscapes) while leaving several questions explicitly open.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** Vision Notes' own recap establishes "Truth Markets
   trading rumored and verified data side by side with reliability prices attached" as founding-era
   texture, but no document — Deep Dive, Mega_Init, Full_Extrapolation, or Cross_Reference_Synthesis —
   explains how a reliability price actually gets set, adjusted, or disputed. A chain establishing the
   actual mechanics of Truth Market pricing would use a named-but-unelaborated economic institution, the
   same pattern this pass has already found productive elsewhere (Cancer's care-debt economy, Scorpio's
   hardware sourcing).

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Sayowa — a genuine top-tier Gemini feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "quiet functional service performed without any interest in credit... small vessel, outsized cargo."
   This is a direct values mismatch with Gemini's own established Speed Faction culture, where being
   first-in and fastest-transmission is the whole point. None of Gemini's own four documents mention
   Sayowa. A chain about Sayowa-descended arrivals bringing this credit-indifferent service ethos into
   Gemini's own founding-era information culture — friction, quiet correction, or a population that never
   fully adopted the district's speed-as-virtue norm — would use this unused population contrast.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Hong Kong as a
   Supporting-tier pick (explicitly "scaled down"), unused anywhere in Gemini's own documents. Hong Kong is
   real-world specific for operating under a genuine "one country, two systems" administrative duality —
   an almost exact structural echo of the Twin Channels' own official/shadow duality, but grounded in real
   legal and civic practice rather than information transmission alone. A chain grounding the Twin
   Channels' actual founding-era operating logic specifically in a Hong-Kong-style dual-system precedent
   would give an already-central district institution a concrete, previously uncited real-world anchor,
   distinct from the treaty-inheritance origin story already covered above.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Gemini.md` explicitly
   leaves open "whether the light-track colors carry deliberate coded meaning (different data types,
   sources, etc.)" — genuinely uncertain even to the developer, framed as something only a longtime Gemini
   resident would actually know. None of the four Megasheet documents address this. A chain establishing
   whether founding-era Arcanet engineers assigned deliberate meaning to the yellow/blue/orange/green
   light-tracks, or whether the color variety is simply an emergent property of the underlying data traffic
   with no intentional code behind it, would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Gemini.md` explicitly notes that pace "was not directly addressed this
   session beyond the conversational soundscape" — genuinely unexplored, unlike every other district's
   Vision Notes file, which at minimum touches pace directly. This doesn't fit cleanly under any of the
   other four lenses. A chain establishing founding-era Gemini's own characteristic pace — driven by the
   speed-as-virtue instinct Full_Extrapolation §II already grounds in ordinary continent-spanning distance,
   or genuinely distinct from it — would use this conspicuously unaddressed gap in the district's own
   developer notes.
