**Already covered (Stage 1):** Libra was established in the late 2560s Post-Falkland Treaty Negotiations
as the central governing hub, and the Deep Dive identifies it as a fifth founding-era response to the
original wound — but unlike Cancer, Scorpio, Aries, and Aquarius' individual/psychological responses,
Libra's is social and procedural: the belief that a fair, orderly process would keep the traumatized exile
community from fracturing internally, genuinely true during the earliest chaotic years when process and
survival really were the same thing. Over 250 years those two things drifted apart without Libra's
founding logic ever developing a way to tell them apart. The Mega_Init's own genuine reinvention (rather
than a light edit) stages a real, functioning Federation Compact pre-war — actual contested elections,
recall procedures, and enforced term limits, making the post-war Suspended Compact a real, earned loss
rather than a paper provision quietly switched off. The Full_Extrapolation elaborates this system (Section
II: growth-stage districts like Aquarius and Sagittarius see more contested races; stable districts like
Cancer and Taurus re-elect familiar incumbents by wide margins), establishes the drift as genuinely
invisible from inside the period rather than a slow-motion crisis (Section III), and proposes the
Perpetual Emergency pattern already holds pre-war, tied to ordinary Antarctic-exile hazards rather than any
single crisis (Section IV) — the war doesn't invent Libra's crisis posture, it's the worst instance in an
unbroken 250-year chain. The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 gives the post-war Forbidden Archive War
a genuine pre-war procedural precedent (Scorpio's "confrontation takes as long as it takes" routinely
colliding with Libra's six-week-minimum process on housing, legal status, and resource requests); Finding
2 establishes Libra as structurally dependent on managing other districts' wounds since its very first
founding decade, never self-sufficient the way its four sibling founding districts are. Vision Notes
confirms atmospheric texture (thoroughly official in both people and space, sincere institutional
conviction, Harmony Plaza's uncanny symmetry) while leaving several tensions explicitly unreconciled.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Full_Extrapolation's own "Notable Figures" section
   names, as an unnamed placeholder, "a recalled official" whose removal became "a piece of Libra's own
   institutional folklore: proof, cited for generations afterward, that the system really did work as
   designed, at least once" — but no document narrates what that actual recall case was, what the official
   did, or how the process played out. A chain dramatizing that specific, precedent-setting recall would
   use a figure and event the source material names as significant folklore but never actually shows.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Juan Carlos — a genuine top-tier Libra feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   the city's archive/records function as "quiet, ordinary administrative work that turned out to be the
   seed of Tepenia's entire institutional memory, and the specific reason Upper Earth destroyed it." None
   of Libra's own four documents mention Juan Carlos, despite the district's own established Treaty Archive
   Vaults sharing this exact tension — record-keeping as simultaneously mundane and existentially
   consequential. A chain about Juan Carlos-descended archivists shaping how founding-era Libra actually
   built its own institutional memory would use this unused, sharply resonant population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Brussels (EU Quarter)
   as a Secondary-tier pick, unused anywhere in Libra's own documents. Brussels is real-world specific for
   housing a supranational parliament whose members are elected separately by each constituent territory —
   an almost exact structural match for the Full_Extrapolation's own described system (each district
   electing its own council representative to Libra's central chamber, subject to real recall and term
   limits). A chain grounding the Federation Compact's own founding-era design specifically in a
   Brussels-EU-Quarter-style precedent would give an already-detailed pre-war institution a concrete,
   previously uncited real-world anchor.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Libra.md` explicitly
   flags as unreconciled the tension between individual staff moving at a "brisk — like people are about
   to be late for something" pace and the district's own established six-week-minimum institutional review
   cycle. None of Libra's four Megasheet documents address this directly. A chain establishing whether this
   dual rhythm — hurried individuals serving an institutionally glacial system — was already visible in
   founding-era Libra, or whether the brisk individual pace is a later development layered onto an
   originally more uniformly deliberate culture, would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Libra.md` explicitly leaves open whether the sincere conviction
   Libra's civil servants project reads as reassuring (competent, dedicated people who care) or unsettling
   (true believers who might not question themselves) — "hard to tell... might be a mix of both," never
   resolved. This doesn't fit cleanly under any of the other four lenses — it's a question about the
   district's own civic temperament, not its economy, population, precedent, or architecture. A chain
   establishing how founding-era Libra's own earliest, genuinely idealistic public servants read to
   outsiders — before two and a half centuries had time to complicate the picture — would use this
   genuinely open, developer-flagged ambiguity about the district's own core character.
