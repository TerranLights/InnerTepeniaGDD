**Already covered (Stage 1):** Virgo was built in the late 2560s–2580s as the district that constructed
the redundant heating loops, waste recyclers, siligel distribution networks, and structural tunnels
making surface habitation possible — a fourth non-wound-response founding-era story, alongside Taurus,
Capricorn, and Sagittarius. The Deep Dive corrects a framing issue: the canon file makes the war sound
like the origin of Virgo's invisible-labor-versus-visible-glamour wound, but the same asymmetry was
already 250 years old by 2812 — the war only supplies its sharpest citable example. The Mega_Init's own
partial reinvention repositions the Year of Silence — a work-to-rule action, undated in post-war canon —
as Virgo's genuine pre-war defining incident: proof of the district's indispensability, demonstrated once,
deliberately, that Virgo never had to repeat, a meaningfully different leverage model than Aries' own
repeatedly-used Political Weapon. The Full_Extrapolation resolves pace (steady by default, snapping to
drilled urgency the instant something fails, needing no war to produce it), proposes a small private
mourning ritual already old practice beneath the district's public gallows humor, and dates the Year of
Silence to the interwar period's final decades with a proposed mechanism (an accumulated threshold, not a
single trigger, with specific two-week downstream effects on Aquarius, Leo, and Aries). The Deep Level's
own origin is deliberately left open, per the same discipline already applied to Pisces' Flood. The
Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 places the Deep Level in the same "existence confirmed, mechanism
open" category as the Flood; Finding 2 identifies Virgo's Year of Silence and Sagittarius' Siligel Rush as
this project's first two confirmed pre-war instances of "proof without payoff," each later doubled by the
war rather than originated by it. Vision Notes confirms atmospheric texture (deliberately, functionally
lit rather than fully dark, a comprehensible maze distinct from Pisces' unstable geography, nobody working
alone given the danger) while leaving one question genuinely distinct from the Deep Level's own deferred
origin.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Full_Extrapolation's own "Notable Figures" section
   names, as an unnamed placeholder, "an early Documentarian, beginning the district's own tradition of
   meticulous incident record-keeping... whose own records plausibly become part of what the Year of
   Silence's organizers draw on" — but no document narrates what this figure actually recorded or how
   founding-era documentation practice first took shape. A chain establishing the origin of Virgo's own
   documentation culture — a specific early incident that convinced the district record-keeping mattered —
   would use a figure the source material names as significant but never actually shows, the same pattern
   this pass has used productively for Aries, Libra, and Sagittarius.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Belgrano — a genuine top-tier Virgo feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "disciplined, function-over-comfort, Air Force-descended... the archive is a duty roster," calling it
   close to "Virgo's own unsupported, unglamorous maintenance culture first." None of Virgo's own four
   documents mention Belgrano. A chain about Belgrano-descended arrivals bringing this specific
   duty-roster, checklist-driven discipline into Virgo's own founding-era maintenance culture would use
   this unused, sharply resonant population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists London's Victorian
   sewer system (Bazalgette's) as a Secondary-tier pick, unused anywhere in Virgo's own documents. Joseph
   Bazalgette's real sewer system dramatically improved public health at unprecedented engineering scale,
   while the engineer himself remained comparatively uncredited next to more visible Victorian
   achievements — an almost exact precedent for Virgo's own invisible-labor-versus-visible-glamour
   founding wound. A chain grounding a specific founding-era Virgo engineering achievement in a
   Bazalgette-style precedent — transformative, essential, and quietly under-credited even at the moment
   of its completion — would give the district's own core wound a concrete real-world anchor two and a
   half centuries before the war supplies its more famous example.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** Vision Notes' own recap establishes "We Keep
   You Alive" motivational posters "displayed completely unironically" as founding-era texture, but no
   document gives their origin — who created them, or when they first appeared. A chain establishing the
   founding-era moment or campaign that produced this specific piece of Virgo's own visual culture would
   ground an established but originless set-piece, the same gap this pass has already closed for Cancer's
   Care Circles, Leo's Golden Ring, and Sagittarius' beacon network.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Virgo.md` explicitly leaves open "whether the Deep Level came up at
   all in the developer's own mental picture, or wasn't part of what was pictured this session" — a
   question distinct from the Deep Level's own deliberately deferred *origin* (untouched here, consistent
   with that discipline). This is about whether the chambers are a live, felt presence in ordinary Virgo
   residents' daily consciousness, or something genuinely marginal even to people who live above them. This
   doesn't fit cleanly under any of the other four lenses. A chain establishing how present the Deep Level
   actually was in founding-era Virgo's own everyday awareness — a known but rarely-discussed fact of life,
   or something closer to active superstition — would use this genuinely open question without touching
   the origin question itself.
