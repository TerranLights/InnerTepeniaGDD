**Already covered (Stage 1):** Aries carries the same original exile wound Cancer and Scorpio each
process, but its founding assignment — immediate, continuous, life-or-death power generation with no
infrastructure margin for reflection — left no room for either nurture or confrontation. What 250 years of
that produces, per the Deep Dive, is a third distinct founding-era strategy: suppression through unceasing
labor. This reframes Aries' established contempt for "soft" districts as unprocessed avoidance rather than
simple workplace pride, and explains its specific fear of silence (not cold or darkness) as the one
condition under which that avoidance mechanism stops working. Rather than repositioning the war-era Black
Silence as a pre-war event, the Full_Extrapolation proposes a genuinely separate, dated founding-era
crisis — the First Silence (c. 2650s) — caused by ordinary infrastructure fatigue in a core that was never
built with real safety margin, not sabotage. The Political Weapon (Aries' documented blackout leverage) is
proposed as a direct, deliberate response to the First Silence, discovered only once Aries witnessed
exactly how much the rest of Concordia depended on it. The Cross_Reference_Synthesis's Finding 1 confirms
Aries as the fourth instance of a shared founding-era four-strategy typology for processing the original
wound (Cancer's nurture, Scorpio's inward confrontation, Leo's outward confrontation, Aries' suppression),
flagging Aquarius and Libra as likely fifth/sixth instances still to check. Finding 2 establishes that
Aries' fear of silence predates Leo's fear of darkness by roughly 140 years — a structural sibling fear,
discovered independently, for opposite founding-era reasons. Vision Notes confirms atmospheric texture
(genuinely hot, dense humming/grinding industrial soundscape, quiet duty-based self-respect rather than
performed pride, workers too absorbed in their own tasks to acknowledge visitors) without resolving three
questions the developer's own notes leave explicitly open.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Full_Extrapolation's own "Notable Figures" section
   names, as an unnamed placeholder, "the first Aries leader to test the Political Weapon" — credited with
   setting the precedent the post-war district still operates from — but no document anywhere describes
   what that first deliberate blackout threat actually looked like: what dispute triggered it, what was
   withheld, how the rest of Concordia responded. A chain dramatizing that first, precedent-setting test of
   Aries' own political leverage would use a figure and event the source material names as significant but
   never actually shows.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning for Denison — a genuine top-tier Aries feeder city, no Stage 2 Override flag — describes
   "pride in extremity performed loudly (reciting wind statistics as social ritual), the explicit opposite
   of the 'competence without commentary' ethos most other cities share." This is a direct clash with
   Aries' own established "quiet, unspoken self-respect rather than anything performed," confirmed
   independently by Vision Notes. Unlike this pass' usual feeder-city finds (a clean cultural match),
   Denison offers a genuine mismatch: a chain about how founding-era Aries absorbed loudly-proud
   Denison-descended arrivals into its own quiet-duty culture — friction, reshaping, or a population that
   never fully assimilated — would use this unused population tension nobody has connected.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Baku, Azerbaijan
   ("Land of Fire") as a Supporting-tier pick, unused anywhere in Aries' own documents. Baku is real-world
   known specifically for ancient fire-worship traditions built around natural gas seeps — devotion to fire
   itself, distinct from any organized religion. Aries' own established texture already includes "a
   devotional-but-not-religious relationship with the machinery itself," confirmed in Vision Notes' own
   recap, sitting uneasily alongside the district's explicit contempt for "impractical" robot religions
   with no account of how the two coexist. A chain grounding Aries' own devotional relationship to its
   power core specifically in a Baku-style fire-veneration tradition — devotion to the thing itself, never
   organized into anything Aries would recognize as religion — would give an established tension a concrete,
   previously uncited real-world anchor.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Aries.md` explicitly
   lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "whether the heat and industrial ambience vary in
   intensity across different parts of the district, or are consistent throughout." None of Aries' four
   Megasheet documents address this — the district's built environment is always described as a single,
   uniformly demanding register. A chain establishing whether founding-era zoning already produced
   distinguishable intensity by area (closer to the core versus outer barracks, say) would close a gap the
   developer's own notes flag directly, matching the same kind of question already resolved for Cancer's
   haze and Leo's Golden Ring.

5. **Other.** `District_Vision_Notes/Aries.md` explicitly leaves open whether the workers' quiet
   self-respect "extends to how they'd treat a visitor who did need help, or is entirely self-directed."
   This doesn't fit neatly under any of the other four lenses — it's a question about the district's own
   social contract, not its economy, population, precedent, or architecture. A chain resolving whether
   founding-era Aries' quiet duty-culture already extended outward to visibly struggling outsiders, or
   whether that impulse was always purely inward and self-contained, would use this genuinely open,
   developer-flagged question about the district's own core character.
