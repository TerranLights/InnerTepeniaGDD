**Part of the District History Enhancement Opportunities pass** — see
`../District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities.md` for the index and progress checklist, and
`../District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Template.md` for the full method and input list. **Scope:**
Stage 1 only (the ~250-year Second Interwar organic-formation period, 2564–2812). No war fallout, no
present-day material. **Flag only** — no new Stage 1 narrative drafted, no texture woven into the actual
Deep Dive or Megasheet files.

**Provenance note:** first drafted as the template's own validation test run
(`../_Method/District_History_Enhancement_Test_Run_Cancer.md`), run twice — once against core inputs, once
more with Vision Notes added. What follows is the composite, best-of-both-rounds version: the strongest-
grounded idea for every lens, preferring an idea a source explicitly flags as open over one this pass had
to infer. The test-run file remains the historical record of how each idea was chosen, including the
alternate that didn't make the final cut.

**Already covered (Stage 1):** Cancer was designated Concordia's sanctuary/integration zone from the
Second Interwar Period's start, built to receive the *original exile's* trauma (the War of Upper Earth and
the Falkland Treaty's own coercive terms), not the still-250-years-distant Long Night War. Across the
following two and a half centuries this population — never a one-time founding wave, but a continuous
intake of Upper Earth exiles, newly-activated robots, and washed-back Sagittarius frontier settlers —
built the district's entire mature caregiving culture: the memorial tradition, mastered lullaby art, the
doors-always-unlocked norm, and a care-debt parallel economy, all read as 250-year accumulated practice
rather than crisis response. The Full_Extrapolation proposes a working pre-war ethos ("there's room," not
yet "No One Left Behind"), an informal verbal-handoff ancestor of the post-war Mother's Circuit, and gives
the long-running Aries energy conflict a specific originating incident (the First Silence, c. 2650s,
cross-referenced directly against Aries' own pre-war document). The Cross_Reference_Synthesis establishes
Cancer and Scorpio as a deliberately designed sibling pair — two different planned responses (nurture vs.
confront) to the same founding wound. Vision Notes confirms and lightly extends the sensory texture (no
straight lines in the architecture, dual-purpose living plantlife, a soft haze with no actual fog, a
specific "available but not hovering" quality of care toward strangers) without resolving the care-debt
economy any further than the Deep Dive already does.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Deep Dive's own Stage 1 section names a "care-debt
   parallel economy" among Cancer's established cultural texture — and Vision Notes independently confirms
   it exists ("in place of credits") without elaborating it any further. Neither document, nor the
   Mega_Init, Full_Extrapolation, or Cross_Reference_Synthesis, ever explains what actually constitutes a
   "debt" in a culture built around never turning anyone away. A chain establishing how it gets tracked or
   repaid without contradicting the district's own "there's room" ethos would use this named-but-empty
   economic detail, now confirmed from two independent sources.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning calls Esperanza — Cancer's #1 feeder city — "about as clean a Cancer match as exists anywhere
   in this project," chartered on the founding principle that exile's children deserved care, with robots
   as primary at-home caregivers. Yet none of Cancer's own Stage 1 documents name Esperanza specifically. A
   chain about a specific Esperanza-originated caregiving practice or founding-era exchange between the two
   communities would use this explicitly-cited-elsewhere, still-undramatized population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Ospedale degli
   Innocenti, Florence, as a Secondary-tier pick — a real Renaissance foundling hospital famous for its
   wheel, a mechanism allowing infants to be left anonymously without stigma. None of Cancer's own
   documents engage with any of its 8 cited real-world inspirations beyond broad architectural mood. A
   chain about a specific pre-war Cancer intake practice echoing the Ospedale's anonymous-admission
   mechanism — for arrivals too raw or ashamed to give their own account of what they survived — would use
   this named, cited, but entirely undramatized precedent.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** `District_Vision_Notes/Cancer.md` explicitly
   lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "the specific source of the ambient haze
   (humidity from the hydroponics, presumably, though not explicitly confirmed as the cause)" — a defining
   sensory feature ("a soft, hazy, fog-like light with no actual fog") with an unconfirmed origin. None of
   Cancer's four Megasheet documents engage the haze's actual mechanism. A chain resolving what actually
   produces it — confirming the hydroponics theory, or revealing a second contributing source no one's
   named yet — would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly, rather than one this pass had to
   infer. *(Alternate considered: the founding-era origin of a specific named set-piece, such as the first
   Care Circle, per `Regional-Characteristics/District_Prominent_Features.md` — still valid, not used here
   only because the haze question has stronger, self-flagged grounding.)*

5. **Other.** `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md` states, in two separate draft passes, that
   "experimental technologies and augmentations were sometimes tested on Cancer's vulnerable populations"
   by Aquarius — but this relationship appears nowhere in Cancer's own four documents, and the source
   doesn't specify whether it predates the war. A chain resolving whether a smaller-scale version of this
   Aquarius/Cancer relationship existed pre-war — parallel to how Full_Extrapolation Section IV resolved
   the Mother's Circuit's own pre-war precedent question — would use this cross-district detail Cancer's
   own material never acknowledges at all.
