# Gemini — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Gemini given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Gemini_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-09.

---

## I. The light-track colors are coded by network type and reliability tier, not by content category

**Proposed:** given the district's own established Twin Channels duality and Truth Markets institution, the four colors confirmed in the Vision Notes (yellow, blue, orange, green) plausibly correspond to: official Arcanet-verified data; Speed Faction fast-but-unverified transmission; shadow-network traffic that residents recognize on sight but never officially acknowledge; and Truth Markets-priced information actively being traded on reliability. This gives every local resident an instantly legible read on what kind of information is moving past them at any given moment, while a visitor sees only beautiful, meaningless color.

---

## II. The Twin Channels' shadow layer has subtle, locally-legible tells — certain light patterns, certain staff working recognizably different shifts

**Proposed:** consistent with the "everyone knows, nobody officially acknowledges" pattern already established for the Twin Channels (and echoed elsewhere in Libra's Suspended Compact and Pisces' Tolerance Pact), the shadow network likely does have subtle, visible tells at street level — certain buildings with slightly different light behavior, certain personnel visibly present on both networks across different shifts — but these tells are legible specifically to residents fluent in the district's own unspoken code, invisible to an outsider looking at the same scene.

---

## III. Gemini runs two paces simultaneously, mirroring its own Twin-Channel duality in literal movement

**Proposed:** the district's own established "constant organized noise... chaotic until you learn their grammar" reads, at the surface, as fast and urgent — the Speed Faction's own energy dominating the visible and audible experience. Underneath that surface, the Verification Faction and the Dual-Network Operators plausibly move at a genuinely different, more deliberate rhythm, doing careful work that looks unhurried by comparison. Gemini's pace, like its information, has an official version (fast, urgent, everywhere) and a real version (patient, careful, running underneath it).

---

## IV. The Lightwave Polyrhythm — a developer idea, deliberately held open rather than scoped

**Not a proposed answer — a new concept from the developer, recorded here to build on later.** The district's light-tracks run on a normal, ambient base pulse (whatever that pulse's rhythm ordinarily is). An extremely important message can be encoded as a second rhythm layered on top of that base pulse — a genuine polyrhythm. To anyone who doesn't know to listen or watch for the second layer, the result reads as unremarkable noise or minor visual irregularity; to anyone who does, the message is plainly legible.

This gives Section II's own vague "subtle, locally-legible tells" claim its first concrete candidate mechanism — a real answer to "what does a tell actually look like," rather than a placeholder gesture at one.

**Left deliberately unscoped, per the developer's own explicit choice not to lock this down yet:**
- Whether the polyrhythm is specifically the mechanism behind the Twin Channels' shadow layer (i.e., how the official/real duality is physically carried in the same visible light), or a broader "urgent message" signaling method used across all of Gemini's light-traffic, official channel included
- Whether different message types/urgency tiers might eventually get their own distinct polyrhythms, rather than one single "urgent" pattern
- Who can actually read a polyrhythm on sight — trained specialists only, or any sufficiently long-term resident

Framed explicitly (per the developer's own words) as the seed of what could become "a complex light system that can provide the player with hundreds of hours of meaning and discovery" — meant to be built on over time, not resolved in one pass.

## VI. The district has exactly one measure of importance, and it is arrival order — so a correction can never outrank the thing it corrects

**The structural spine of the pass.** *(Capability reading, `F_Rulerships.md` §6 D9. This is the **third**
district on the two-term shape, after Leo and the Labs — the runbook requires that the shared shape supply only
the question, never the answer.)*

**The one instrument is transmission.** Not custody, not judgment, not record — **movement**. Everything the
district is good at is a way of getting a thing from here to there faster and more intact than anyone else
could. There is no second faculty to fall back on and nothing here is either revered or forbidden.

**And a district with one instrument will rank the world with it, because it has nothing else to rank with.**
The Circuit's measure of what matters is **the order things arrive in.** Not their weight, not their
consequences, not who is hurt — **which came first.** This is not a value the district chose and it is not one
it would defend if you named it; it is simply the only comparison its single instrument can perform.

**Third-order, and this is the finding:** a correction is *by definition* the later item. **The Circuit is
superb at verification and structurally incapable of making verification matter** — not because it doesn't care
about being right, but because the only ranking metric it possesses guarantees that being right arrives second,
and second reads as smaller. The Verification Faction is not losing an argument. **It is winning one that the
district's only scoreboard cannot score.**

> **Research (deficit-targeted, and it changed this finding rather than ornamenting it).** Held against real
> newsroom practice, the mechanism is not fictional. In a study of factual errors reported to ten daily
> newspapers, **97% went uncorrected**; corrections that did run appeared on page 2, days or weeks later,
> stripped of the context a reader would need to understand what had happened. The **continued influence
> effect** is the psychological half: corrected misinformation keeps steering judgment afterward, and in three
> experiments **belief in the mistaken claim rose over the two days following a correction** even among people
> who still remembered being corrected. A 2023 study adds the cruelest part — **admitting error improves
> accuracy and reduces audience trust.** The Circuit's problem is a real one, at scale, with the arithmetic
> already worked out.

**How it differs from the other two districts on this shape** *(mandatory — runbook Step 2, and the failure
mode is stopping at the shared question):*

| District | The one instrument | The consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Leo** | recognition | **cannot fail gradually** — no graceful degradation |
| **The Labs** | the collective | **cannot form a stable intention** — two irreconcilable accounts of one thing |
| **The Circuit** | **transmission** | **cannot make the truth outrank the error**, because its only ranking is arrival order |

**What the district is actually like, in proportion** *(`00d`)*: this produces a place that is fast, open,
astonishingly well-informed, and pleasant to arrive in. Residents are not credulous — canon is explicit that
triangulating between competing accounts is a genuine cognitive skill developed young here, and it works. **The
skill is real.** What it cannot do is make the result of the skill travel as far as the thing it corrected.

## VII. Its missing faculty is available in three districts at once, and that is why it never gets it

**A deficit configuration not previously observed, and it inverts the assumption behind the other four.**
*(`00e` §5.)*

The faculty the Circuit lacks is **meaning and scale — the authority to say *this matters more than that*.**
Reading the geometry: that same faculty is **at home in the Frostlands and in the Markets, and at its strongest
of anywhere in the city in the Sanctuary.** Three suppliers.

**Five configurations were on record. This is the sixth, and it is the worst one:**

| Configuration | Worked example | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| Both addressed, one counterparty | the Power Core → the Government District | a permanent grievance, and a politics |
| Diffuse | Taurus | drift, no counterparty, no politics |
| One of each | the Yards | it can perceive one deficit and not the other |
| Reciprocal | the Labs ↔ Leo | each holds what the other lacks; neither will ask |
| Reciprocal + an outside dependency | the Government District | one line runs across an antagonism and is therefore dead |
| **Multiply addressed** | **the Circuit → three districts** | **shopping** |

**Libra established that being addressed is not enough — the relationship type decides whether the supply line
works. The Circuit extends that: *more addresses is worse, not better.*** With one counterparty you at least
get a grievance, and a grievance is a relationship. With three you get **a district that visits all of them,
receives from none, and experiences the entire arrangement as being unusually well-connected.**

**And each line fails for its own unrelated reason, which is why no pattern is ever visible from inside:**

- **The Frostlands** — its opposite number, and *the opposition does not present as opposition.* First contact
  between these two reads as **recognition**: two talkers delighted by how much they have in common, and it
  stalls there permanently. Neither district believes it has a problem with the other. **Nobody is refusing
  anything, so nobody looks for a cause.**
- **The Markets** — commercially entangled and temperamentally unreachable. Canon already has their networks
  overlapping extensively and the line between broker and operative "thin and frequently crossed." Structurally
  they are the worst possible pair on this exact axis: the Markets can price anything and **cannot keep a
  ledger**; the Circuit can move anything and **cannot rank**. Each needs precisely the discipline the other has
  least.
- **The Sanctuary** — the strongest supplier in the city, and the failure is small and human. The Circuit
  approaches, someone becomes moved enough to stop talking, and the Circuit withdraws — **not from callousness,
  but because the conversation ended and the Circuit reads a stopped conversation as a finished one.** It is
  the shortest of the three failures and by far the most consequential, and it happens perhaps a thousand times
  a week across the boundary without either district recording a single instance.

**Fourth-order:** because it is never refused, the Circuit has no story about being underserved. Districts with
one address get grievances, factions, and eventually a treaty. **The Circuit gets an itinerary.**

*(Phase 4 — inter-district. Filed here deliberately: no phase covers inter-district relationships, a known hole
in the Plan, and the measured symptom is that districts do not mention each other. This district now names
three neighbors with mechanisms attached.)*

## VIII. ⚠ GATE 9 — going to zero: the district's only sanction, and it has no route back

**Gate 9 fired again — the eighth consecutive district, and for the eighth consecutive time the failure was in
the pre-Plan 2026-07-09 material.** *(`00c` Gate 9: for every threshold, gate, conversion, verdict or status
change, the mechanism runs both ways — did the file write both?)*

**What was written one-way.** The Truth Markets price information's reliability — canon, and §I extends it into
the light-track colors. Every existing description writes the mechanism **upward**: a source establishes
reliability, a price rises, reach accumulates, reputation is calculated in reach. **Nothing anywhere says what
happens to a source the market prices at nothing.** The attention economy has the same hole: reach is written
as something gained.

**The answer, and it follows from Finding VI rather than being invented for the gap.** The Circuit cannot
correct an error — the correction arrives second. **But it can act on the source, because a person is the one
thing in the district that stays put.** So the whole weight of the district's response to being badly misled
lands not on the claim but on whoever carried it.

**And the sanction is not expulsion. It is far quieter and much worse.** Nobody is denounced, banned, corrected
or told. The price on anything that person says simply goes to nothing and stays there. In market slang they
have **gone to zero**, and someone in that state is **at zero**. They keep transmitting. They are still welcome
everywhere. They are invited to the same rooms, greeted warmly, and asked how they are — because forgiveness
here is instant and entirely sincere, and costs nothing to grant.

**They are simply never repeated again.**

**The absence of a route back is not cruelty; it is the capability deficit** *(`00d` — no villain, and this one
requires none).* Restoring someone would mean an authority ruling *this person is reliable now.* **That is a
ranking, and the district has no body that can issue one** — and any body that tried would be building exactly
the thing the district's one near-sacred reflex forbids (Finding XIII). So there is no appeal, no process, no
term of years, and **no one to be angry at**, which is why nobody has ever perceived it as a problem requiring
a solution. It is not administered. It just is the aggregate.

**Third-order, and this is the part that makes it playable:** a person at zero **cannot tell.** Everything
social is unchanged. The only signal is the absence of a signal — your material stops reappearing elsewhere —
and in a district where "out of sight, out of mind" is the governing social physics, that is indistinguishable
from an ordinary quiet fortnight. **People have been at zero for years and found out by accident.** The
substrate names the shape exactly: *forgiveness is immediate; trust never returns.* Readmission without
reinstatement.

**A companion or faction can fall into this state and cannot climb out of it by doing favors.** That is the
design value: a standing, unappealable status that requires no institution to administer and no antagonist to
impose.

> **Differentiated inline from the Government District** *(Gate 6b)*. Libra also produces outcomes nobody is
> answerable for — but by **deliberation**, diffusing responsibility through committee until no name attaches,
> and it experiences that as fairness. The Circuit's version is **aggregate**, not procedural: no one deliberated,
> no committee sat, and there is no decision anywhere in it to be accountable for. Libra could name someone and
> declines to. **The Circuit genuinely has no one to name.** And where Libra experiences its version as fairness,
> the Circuit experiences its own as *an open market* — which is why it reads to residents as the opposite of a
> sanction.

## IX. Every real information culture protects itself with an instrument that can only *block* — and the Circuit is the one that refused to build one

**Deficit research** *(runbook Step 3.2: the capability reading says what a district cannot do; find a real
culture that *can*, and the contrast writes the finding).* **Both picks changed findings.**

**The first case — a guarantee at the ownership layer.** Reuters wrote its Trust Principles in 1941, mid-war,
to keep its wire out of any government's hands. What matters for the Circuit is not the wording but the
**mechanism**: a Founders Share carrying voting rights sufficient to block any attempt to concentrate ownership
or compromise editorial independence, a **15% shareholding cap written into the constitution**, and a body of
fourteen to eighteen trustees meeting at least twice a year. **Nobody has to be virtuous.** Integrity is
protected by making its loss mechanically impossible, once, at the structural level — and never needing to be
defended again.

**The second case — the one judgment a wire service does issue.** A **kill notice** is a wire service's
retraction: a mandatory advisory distributed to every subscriber, instructing them to remove the item **from
all platforms, including social, where it may still be visible.** It is worth noticing exactly what shape that
authority has. A wire service does not rule that a thing is *true*. **Its single authoritative act is to rule
that a thing must stop existing.** The only judgment in the whole apparatus is a negative one.

**Both instruments rank by subtraction.** Neither says what matters. Both say what may not stand. **And that is
precisely the shape the Circuit could have accepted** — it forbids rather than commands, creates no ranking
authority, and hands nobody the power to tell you what to think.

**It does not have one, and the reason is sincere.** The district's one near-absolute reflex is that you do not
stop a person from saying a thing (Finding XIII). A kill is a stop. So the Circuit has **no kill, no
retraction, no mechanism whatsoever for removing an item from circulation** — and the error simply keeps
travelling, forever, which is why the only lever left is the source (Finding VIII).

**What it built instead, and why the substitution is the whole district.** The Truth Markets are an attempt at
the same job by the opposite method: **a distributed, positive ranking instead of a concentrated, negative
one.** A price ranks without anyone being responsible for the ranking — no trustee, no board, no name, nothing
to accuse of censorship. It is an ingenious solution to the district's actual constraint and residents are
right to be proud of it.

**It fails in exactly the way the research predicts.** A price is a live quantity, so it can only reflect
*current* demand — and the district's tragedy is not the price but **the curve**. Verified material's
reliability rises steadily while demand for it decays, because the appetite that would pay for it has already
moved. **Slow truth does not lose on merit here. It loses by outlasting the interest.**

> **Divergence, stated rather than implied** *(Step 3.5 — the inspiration is a source, not a specification).*
> Reuters put its guarantee in one time, at the ownership layer, and has not had to re-argue it in eighty
> years. **The Circuit's equivalent guarantee is re-run every single day by everyone in the district** — which
> is why it is genuinely more open than Reuters ever was, and why it could be lost in an afternoon and nobody
> would be able to say when. The Circuit is not a worse version of a wire service. **It is what a wire service
> would be if it had never been allowed to kill a story.**

## X. Arcanet Culture — the district is the network's center, and it is the only district that does not experience the network as somewhere else

*(Phase 3. **Promoted from incidental mentions rather than written fresh**, per the Plan's instruction — the
material existed across `Gemini_Mega_Init.md`, `README.md` and §I–IV above and had never been consolidated into
one finding.)*

**The general baseline first** *(`00b` — Arcanet Culture may center an institution only if the district's
ordinary information life is stated separately).* Every other district uses the network. **The Circuit lives
inside a physical readout of it.** The light-tracks running the buildings and walls carry actual traffic — the
movement is real, not decorative, and its beauty is a side-effect nobody designed for. The practical
consequence for an ordinary resident is that **network state is ambient information you acquire by looking up,
not by checking.** A Circuit resident knows the net is congested the way anyone else knows it is raining.

**And this is the thing no other district has: here, the network has weather.** Elsewhere the Arcanet is a
utility that is either working or not. Here it is a visible, moving, legible condition with good days and bad
ones, read at a glance and discussed the way other districts discuss the actual sky.

**The two-tier legibility, stated honestly.** §I proposes the four colors code network type and reliability
tier. Sustained — but the important half is the asymmetry: **a resident reads the district's whole information
state instantly and a visitor sees beautiful, meaningless color.** This is the single most reliable
resident/visitor divide in the city and it requires no gatekeeping to maintain. Nobody withholds the code.
Nobody teaches it either. **You acquire it by living under it.**

**The institutional layer, explicitly scoped as institutional and not general:** the Triangulation Schools, the
Shadow Network Access Points, the Great Corruption Recovery Archive, the Attention Economy Trading Floors and
the Speed Problem Slow Rooms are real Circuit institutions with their own network cultures. **They are not the
district's baseline** — most residents' relationship with the Arcanet is the ambient one above, plus a
teahouse.

**Third-order.** Because the network is visible, **outages here are a public event rather than a private
inconvenience.** When a track goes dark the street it runs along goes quiet — not from any rule, but because
everyone looks up at once. The Circuit's closest thing to a spontaneous civic moment is a **dark stretch**, and
it lasts as long as the fault does.

## XI. Architecture — everything is built in uneven pairs, and nothing is fenced

*(Phase 1.)*

**Two rules produce the whole grammar, and both are load-bearing for level design.**

**One: significant structures come in pairs, and the pairs are never equal.** A Circuit building of any
consequence has a twin — and one of the two is always the larger, the lit, the occupied, the maintained. The
other is smaller, darker, half-used, or empty. **The addressing system assumes it**: directions here are given
as *the lit one* or *the dark one*, and a stranger who asks for a building by name gets asked which. It is not
symbolic and nobody in-world claims it means anything. It is simply how the district has always built, and the
oldest founding-era construction does it too.

**Two: the Circuit engineers hazard out of a space rather than fencing access to it.** Where another district
installs a rail, a gate, a barrier or a warning, the Circuit rebuilds the drop, the edge, or the machine so
that it is not dangerous. Its interiors run open. Its walkways have low lips rather than railings. Its
industrial spaces are enclosed at the machine and not at the room. **A barrier the player encounters inside the
Circuit was almost always installed by somebody from another district**, and locals route around it with
audible irritation.

**Third-order, and it explains something visible.** Building hazard *out* is far more expensive than fencing it
*off* — so the Circuit spends its capital on structure and has nothing left for finish. **The district looks
considerably better from a distance than up close**, and reads to visiting districts as neglected. It is the
opposite of neglect: the money went into the parts that stop killing people, and the parts that would have
looked expensive were never funded.

> **Research: Songdo — and it changed this finding by inversion.** The pick is the developer's own primary, and
> the real case is a warning rather than a model. Songdo has sensors in every street, pneumatic waste that
> means residents never see a collection vehicle, and a population that reported **minimal practical benefit
> from any of it in daily life** — the district is described as cold, impersonal, homogenous, dully
> predictable, with underused public space and street life that never arrived. **The instrument worked and the
> life did not come.** The Circuit is the case where both arrived, and canon already says why: its noise
> "comes from people, not machines," and the computers themselves are quiet. **So the Circuit's infrastructure
> is not what makes it the Circuit, and writing it as its light-tracks would be writing Songdo.** See the
> concrete inversion in Finding XIV.

## XII. The Circuit reports the entire city with great skill and cannot report itself

*(Phase 1 texture, and the finding that finally makes the Great Corruption's locked accidental cause *stronger*
rather than something to work around.)*

> **Research: Dubai Media City — changed a finding.** The free zone is real and its exemption is real, and the
> shape of the exemption is the thing worth having: media organizations inside it operate with relative freedom
> **specifically where they produce content for audiences outside the country**, while broadcast outlets in the
> zones remain subject to the 1980 press law and the penal code, and extreme self-censorship is the norm on
> local subjects. **A zone that frees everything except speech about here.**

**The Circuit is not censored, by anyone, at all — and it produces the same shape by temperament.** Nobody
forbids it to cover itself. It simply cannot, for three reasons that are all its own:

1. **Its instrument is transmission, which is outward-facing by construction.** The district is built to move
   things from here to elsewhere. Material about here that stays here never enters the apparatus.
2. **Its one cognitive skill does not work on itself.** Triangulation finds the reliable overlap between
   competing accounts — and the method requires you not to be one of the accounts.
3. **"Out of sight, out of mind" is the local social physics**, and nothing is less out of sight than the
   street you are standing on. Familiarity here does not breed contempt. **It breeds invisibility.**

**The evidence is already in canon and had never been read as a pattern.** The Circuit has produced the city's
best investigative journalism for two centuries. In the same two centuries it has not recovered its own Great
Corruption, has not established when the corruption even happened, and has not produced any real account of the
Zukelli/Janbogo wound living inside its own boundary — a wound so charged the district built a Reconciliation
Center for it. **Three of the largest unreported stories in Concordia are all inside the district that reports
everything.**

**And this is what makes the accident reading load-bearing rather than a constraint.** The suspicion of
targeting is genuinely alive in-world and residents hold it sincerely. But the district that cannot see itself
is *exactly* the district that would fail to recover its own archive for 250 years through nothing but ordinary
inadequate storage, ordinary hardware failure, and ordinary crisis-era mishandling — **and would then find the
accident so implausible that targeting became the more comfortable explanation.** A place that can explain
anything except itself does not conclude *we were careless*. It concludes *somebody did this*.

> **Research: WikiLeaks, 2011 — changed a finding, and it is the tonal precedent the locked reading needs.**
> The unredacted cables went public because a journalist published a passphrase he had been assured would
> expire, having been assured of it by the person who gave it to him; a third site published the decrypted
> archive first; and the four collaborating outlets then jointly condemned the outcome. **Every actor behaved
> defensibly by their own lights, nobody intended the harm, and the harm was real and irreversible.** That is
> the exact register the Circuit's signature disaster should have — and the substrate names the district-scale
> version outright: the leak that ruins someone **with nobody bought and nobody malicious anywhere in the
> chain**, repeated only because it was too interesting not to repeat. **A player who traces a Circuit leak to
> its origin should find appetite, not a plot.**

## XIII. Religious and philosophical landscape — one observance, no taboo, and a creed that is a prohibition on prohibitions

*(Phase 2. **Naming technique run before inventing anything**, per the runbook — search canon for religious
register attached to non-religious objects. It returned material, so nothing was invented. 7 for 7.)*

**What the search found, and it was already written down.** Canon lists, as an ordinary social-cohesion
mechanism, **synchronized "signal storms" — occasions when the whole district briefly floods the net with the
same verified packet.** Read that as what it actually is. In a district where canon states flatly that
*nothing has one version*, a signal storm is **the only moment when everything does.** It is participatory,
recurring, requires no belief, admits everyone, and produces an enormous shared feeling.

**And the content of the packet is irrelevant, which is the finding.** Nobody chooses it for its meaning; it
only has to be verified. **The Circuit's nearest approach to worship is a moment of total unanimity
deliberately emptied of content** — because content would require somebody to have ranked one thing above
another, and that is the faculty the district does not have. It is not cynical and residents do not experience
it as hollow. **It is the most moving thing that happens here.**

**The discipline that sits opposite it.** The Speed Problem Slow Rooms — canon, and near-monastic in function:
deliberately isolated spaces for processing information without the pressure of speed. Nobody calls this
spiritual. It has every structural feature of a retreat: voluntary, temporary, uncomfortable, entered for
your own good, and mildly embarrassing to admit you needed.

**No taboo, and the absence is the finding, not a gap.** The capability reading is explicit that nothing here
is revered and **nothing here is forbidden** — and the pass must not manufacture a taboo to fill the slot.
The district has no unspeakable subject, no desecration, no category of thing one does not say. It genuinely
does not. **This is the single largest reason the rest of the city does not trust the Circuit with anything**:
a district with no sacred and no forbidden is one whose behavior cannot be predicted from its values, because
its values do not constrain in that direction.

**What it has instead of a moral philosophy is an etiquette carrying the force of one, and it has exactly one
article: you do not stop a person from saying a thing.** Not a law. Not enforced. Simply the one violation
that makes a Circuit room go cold. **Its whole creed is a prohibition on prohibitions**, which is why it can
never build the negative instrument of Finding IX, and why the counterculture of Finding XXI is the one thing
here that genuinely cannot be accommodated.

**The general population** *(`00b`)*: most residents hold no articulated philosophy whatsoever and would be
puzzled to be asked. They attend signal storms the way people attend anything communal and pleasant, use a Slow
Room perhaps twice in a lifetime, and carry the one article of etiquette without ever having heard it stated.

## XIV. Sensory profile — loud at head height, quiet above and below, and nothing here is automated that could be staffed

*(Phase 1. Vision Notes material **promoted**, per the Plan; general-population layer added on top.)*

**The established core, confirmed and kept:** light-tracks in yellow, blue, orange and green running along
buildings, houses and walls, genuinely in motion. A general din of conversation. **The computers are quiet** —
a deliberate and important contrast with the Power Core's and the Yards' industrial soundscapes. **The noise of
the Circuit comes from people.**

**The general-population layer, and it is a specific claim about the vertical.** The Circuit is **loud in a
band about head height and noticeably quiet above and below it.** The sound is entirely human and entirely
horizontal — conversation at walking pace, moving with the people making it. Step up one level and it drops
away; the district's upper floors are among the quietest interiors in Concordia, which is why so much of its
careful work happens there. Newcomers consistently describe the ground level as overwhelming and are surprised
to find that one flight of stairs fixes it.

**What is in the din, specifically** — and this is a Circuit inventory rather than generic market noise:
greetings, corrections, prices, warnings, instructions, and an unbroken layer of people repeating what somebody
else said, in that person's voice (Finding XVI).

> **The Songdo inversion, and it is the pass's most concrete civic policy.** Songdo removed the garbage truck —
> pneumatic tubes, no collection vehicle, no crew, nothing on the street. It also removed the last reason
> anyone stood outside at a fixed time in a fixed place. **The Circuit does the opposite on principle, and does
> not think of it as a principle.** Utilities here that could be fully automated are left staffed and
> deliberately slightly inconvenient, because **the queue is the point** — a collection point people walk to, a
> counter with a person behind it, a handoff that requires two people to be present. The district's substrate
> logic is exact about the mechanism: its remedy for a dull institution is never to improve the work, it is to
> **make the building somewhere to be.** No committee decided this and no one defends it as policy. Efficiency
> proposals simply die here, every time, for reasons nobody can articulate.

## XV. Fashion — everyone dresses in two halves that do not match, and a matched outfit means you are not going anywhere else today

*(Phase 1. **`00b`'s worst offender category — this is the general population, not a profession's kit.**)*

**The general answer.** Ordinary Circuit dress is **two-part and visibly mismatched, on purpose**: an outer
layer and an inner layer that are not meant to go together and are both meant to be seen. Not one garment with
a coat over it — **two wardrobes, kept separately, combined at the door without much thought.** Paired and
repeating patterns dominate: two-tone, checks, stripes, spots. Unbroken solid color is uncommon enough to be
remarked on.

**And the reason is completely unglamorous, which is why it is general rather than stylish.** The population
moves between four or five venues a day (Finding XX), indoors and out, warm and cold, all of it on foot. The
outer layer comes on and off perhaps a dozen times. **Nobody who lives like that owns a coordinated outfit** —
you own over-layers and under-layers, they wear out at different rates, and they get replaced independently.
The look is a consequence of the schedule.

**Second-order — it became legible, and now it carries meaning nobody intended.** Because the mismatch is
universal, **its absence reads.** A matched, deliberate, single-scheme outfit says *this person is not going
anywhere else today* — which in the Circuit means one of three things: an outsider, someone attending one fixed
thing they are anxious about, or someone with an appointment they would rather not explain. **All three get
looked at.** None of it is hostile and nobody would admit to doing it.

**Third-order.** Grooming runs to a standard the district sets for itself and holds to hard — clean, kept,
presentable — **which sits strangely against the mismatch and is not a contradiction.** The Circuit judges
itself on presentation and output and on almost nothing else, and it does not count *coordination* as
presentation. Turning up clean matters enormously. Turning up matching does not register.

> **Cross-district check** *(Gate 6b, against the Differentiation Table)*: no Fashion row existed. One is being
> added with this pass. The distinguishing axis here is that the Circuit's dress code is **produced by
> mobility** and reads as an unplanned by-product, where the districts written so far dress from function,
> rank, or ritual state.

## XVI. Music — the district's musical act is impersonation, and it is how the Circuit smuggles judgment back in

*(Phase 7.)*

**The general form.** Circuit music is overwhelmingly **two-voice, and one of the voices is playing somebody
real.** The common shape is a teller and a subject: one performer carries the frame and the other does the
person — voice, cadence, verbal tics, the specific way they say a specific thing. It is not satire and not
always mockery; a great deal of it is affectionate, and the highest compliment is that a performer *has*
someone. Children learn it as a playground game before they learn any instrument. **Adults do it constantly,
unmusically, in ordinary conversation**, which is where the substrate locates it: reports here arrive
performed, and the teller plays the people in them.

**Which makes this the least separable music in the city from ordinary speech.** There is no clean line between
a Circuit performance and a Circuit resident telling you what somebody said. The performance is the news
delivery mechanism, and the news delivery mechanism is the performance.

**Third-order, and it is the pass's quietest important finding.** The district has no authority that can rank
one account above another (Finding VI). **But an impersonation ranks.** How you play someone — the pause you
give them, the register you pick, whether you make them sound careful or hasty — is a judgment, delivered
whole, to everybody in the room. **And the Circuit does not experience it as a judgment, because no claim was
made.** You did not say the person was foolish. You did the voice.

**So the district's only functioning ranking instrument is one it does not know it possesses**, cannot audit,
cannot appeal, and would sincerely deny using. It is also, in practice, the mechanism that moves a source
toward zero long before any price does (Finding VIII) — **the price follows the impression, and the impression
was made by a performance nobody logged.**

> **Differentiated inline from the Labs** *(Gate 6b — this is the nearest collision in the table).* The Labs
> also runs a shared, non-professional musical culture. **The relationship to authorship is opposite in every
> respect.** The Labs' corpus is accretive and **unattributed — the entire point is that no one owns it**, and
> there is no performance at all, only publication. The Circuit's is **performed constantly and pointedly
> attributed to a named person who never consented — the entire point is exactly whose voice it is.** One is
> anonymous and additive; the other is personal and mimetic.

## XVII. Food — a meal is spread across four rooms, and being fed means somebody is trying to keep you longer

*(Phase 7.)*

**Nobody here eats a whole meal in one place, and it would not occur to them to.** An ordinary Circuit evening
takes one thing at a teahouse, something else two streets on, something at a counter on the way, and a last
thing wherever the group ended up. Venues are built for it — small, cheap, fast, with one or two things done
well and nowhere comfortable to sit for long. **The district has an enormous number of eating places and
almost no restaurants** in the sense other districts mean.

**And so the meaning of being fed here is not admission, rank, process, or guesthood. It is detention, and it
is flattering.** In a district where everyone is on their way somewhere else within the half hour, putting food
in front of someone is the one polite instrument available for **keeping a person in the room past the point
they meant to leave.** Everybody knows it. Nobody minds. **Being fed in the Circuit means you were interesting
enough that somebody tried to slow you down**, and it is received exactly that warmly.

**The inverse is the injury** — and it is a real, specific, playable social wound. **Being fed and then not
talked to** is the Circuit's version of an insult: the food arrived, the person was held, and then nothing was
asked of them. Nobody would ever name it out loud. It stings for weeks.

**Third-order, and it is the best thing the refugee dynamic produced this pass.** Zukelli's civic culture was
built on **communal meals as the absolute center of civic life**, with food and music treated as one
hospitality instinct — and Zukelli transplants have opened small food-and-music venues across the Circuit,
which the district adores. **The district misreads why.** It thinks it is enjoying the cooking. What it is
actually experiencing, in the only rooms in the Circuit where this is possible, is **a meal that has a
beginning, a middle, and an end, eaten in one place, with the same people present at the end as at the
start.** The Circuit has no native form for this. It cannot say what it likes about those rooms, and it keeps
going back.

> **Gate 6b, and this row already carries the table's one recorded collision.** Checked against all seven
> existing entries — admission (Taurus), rank (Leo), position in a process (Scorpio), ambient collective
> availability (the Power Core), unspoken individual repair (the Yards), no cuisine at all (the Labs),
> guesthood (the Government District). **The Circuit's axis is duration, which none of them use.**

## XVIII. Export culture — it exports the one skill it cannot use on itself

*(Phase 1. Structurally exempt from the general-population discipline per `00b` — naming a specific trade is
the correct shape here.)*

**What leaves the Circuit is not information.** Every district has data, and the Circuit's advantage in raw
volume is worth less than it believes. **What leaves is people trained to triangulate** — canon's own term for
the district's genuine cognitive skill, finding the reliable overlap between competing accounts rather than
finding the truth outright, taught formally in the Triangulation Schools and informally from childhood.

**Every other district hires it, and hires it for its own disputes.** A Circuit-trained triangulator sits in on
a Yards specification argument, a Sanctuary intake dispute, a Markets provenance fight. They are not
arbitrators — the Circuit produces nothing resembling an arbitrator — **they establish what the competing
accounts actually agree on, hand it over, and leave the decision to the client.** That division is exactly the
district's capability profile made into a service: it supplies the overlap and does not supply the ruling.

**Second-order.** This makes the Circuit the only district whose principal export is **a person rather than a
good or a decision**, which is why its trade relationships are unusually personal and unusually fragile — an
export that can be offended.

**Third-order, and it is the finding.** Per Finding XII, **the method requires you not to be one of the
accounts** — so the Circuit sells its one civic competence citywide and derives almost no benefit from it at
home. Its own two great internal disputes are precisely the two where every available triangulator is a
participant. **The district exports the cure for its own condition.**

*(Sejong's transplants, ~9.9% of the diaspora, are already recorded as teaching in the Triangulation Schools
and bringing a land-negotiation tradition that is about **deciding**, not just finding overlap. That is the one
live thread by which the district could acquire the missing half — worth developing, and it should stay slow
and contested, because acquiring it would make the Circuit less itself.)*

## XIX. Visitor to resident — you become a Circuit resident the moment somebody repeats something you said

*(Phase 1.)*

**There is no threshold, no admission, no sponsor and no waiting period, and the transition is startlingly
fast — a week is normal.** The Circuit does not induct people. It **supplies** them: the district's method is
to leave things where a newcomer will find them and then not intervene, and whoever you become here is whatever
you happened to still be interested in when the novelty wore off.

**The actual moment of conversion is observable and nobody administers it.** You are a visitor while your
material stops with the person you told. **You are a resident the first time something you said reaches
somebody you have never met.** That is the whole mechanism: belonging here is *being in circulation*, and it
requires no one's permission.

**The queue, and the etiquette a newcomer must be told.** Circuit residents have many friends rather than a few
close ones, and a newcomer waits their turn without that being rejection. **Do not rely heavily on any one
Circuit acquaintance, keep other company, and do not read silence as a verdict** — they are almost certainly
occupied with something else, and they will be glad to see you when you next appear. Newcomers who fail here
almost always fail by treating one warm early contact as a relationship and taking its intermittency
personally.

**The inverse, and it is genuinely brutal — but it is not Finding VIII.** You stop being a resident the same
way you became one: by not being repeated. **And nobody notices either event, including you.** Absence here is
not resented, punished, or discussed. It is simply forgotten, and the forgetting is not a decision.

> **Kept distinct from going to zero** *(Gate 6b, internal)*: the Zero is a **status** — the district heard you
> and has priced you at nothing, permanently, and cannot restore you. This is **drift** — nothing was
> evaluated, nothing was decided, and returning to circulation is as easy as showing up and being interesting
> for an afternoon. One has no route back and no author. The other has no route back only because nobody is
> walking it.

## XX. Ordinary daily life — nobody works in one place, home is somewhere to leave, and the district's rhythm quietly fails the people it most recently took in

*(Phase 5.)*

**Who actually lives here, beyond the headline professions.** Canon's population list is dominated by
information trades, but the district also runs on hardware and robot repair shops, teahouse keepers, small
food-and-music venue operators, Triangulation School instructors, node maintenance crews, counter staff at the
deliberately-unautomated utilities of Finding XIV, and the ordinary elderly, ill, and very young of any
district.

**The shape of an ordinary day is fragmentation, and it is voluntary.** Circuit work is done in pieces across
several locations — an hour here, a stop, an hour there. Even trades that could be stationary are not: the
repair shops keep long irregular hours and their staff are constantly out. **The commonest Circuit complaint
about another district is that you have to stay in the building.**

**Home is where the district loses.** Housing here is small, cheap, plain and undermaintained, and **not
because the district is poor** — because almost nobody is in it. Safety and stimulation compete directly in
this temperament and stimulation wins; the residence is a place to sleep and change layers. Domestic
maintenance is the first thing dropped. **The Circuit's streets and venues are the best in Concordia and its
housing stock is among the worst, and residents do not experience the second half as a problem.**

**Third-order, and this is the district's real ongoing hardship** *(`00d` — no villain anywhere in it)*. That
rhythm is fine if you can move. **It fails completely for anyone whose life is necessarily domestic** — the
frail, the ill, new parents, anyone caring for someone who cannot leave. In a district where all social
capital, all information, all work and all belonging run on *being out among known faces*, a person who cannot
get out does not merely miss things. **They stop being repeated, and then they stop being a resident**
(Finding XIX), and no one refuses them anything at any point.

**And the population this lands on hardest is the one the district took in most recently.** The Zukelli
community's stated need is somewhere to sit still with people who share the grief — the Memory Circles exist
precisely because that need had nowhere else to go. **The Circuit has no ordinary daily form that holds still.**
Nobody is unwelcoming; the district is, if anything, unusually warm. It simply has nothing to offer a person
whose need is to remain in one room, and it has never noticed, because everyone who would have told it has
gone quiet by exactly the mechanism above.

## XXI. Counterculture — "room-only": the people who will not put a thing on the network, and ask nothing of anyone

*(Phase 7. **Canon checked first for an existing counterculture** per the runbook — the Speed, Verification and
Dual-Network groupings are internal professional factions, not a counterculture, and no counterculture exists
in canon. Derived from Step C.)*

**What the district requires of everyone: that you keep talking, and that you never tell anyone to stop.**

**Who will not give it** — and it is not who you would expect. The obvious opposition would be people who want
things suppressed, and the Circuit is well-equipped for those: it has an answer, it enjoys the argument, and it
wins. **The group it cannot handle wants nothing suppressed at all.**

**They decline to put certain accounts on the network. Only their own. They make no demand of anybody else.**
Their position — held sincerely, and not as a criticism of the district — is that some accounts are *damaged by
transmission*: that a thing which happened to you, told to a person in the room, is one object, and the same
thing carried onward is a different and lesser one. They are not protecting a secret. They will tell you. **You
have to be there.**

**The district's name for them is "room-only,"** and it was an adjective before it was a group — *"that's
room-only"* meaning an account you may hear and may not carry. They do not use the term, do not contest it, and
have no name of their own, because naming is a form of filing.

**Why this is the one thing the Circuit genuinely cannot accommodate** — and it is not because it is
threatening. **It is because there is nothing to triangulate.** The district's entire civic skill is finding
the reliable overlap between competing accounts. **A population that files no account produces no second
version, and the instrument returns nothing.** The Circuit is not angry at them. It is *unsettled* by them, in
a way it cannot explain, and it deals with the discomfort by not mentioning them much — which is, for this
district, an extraordinary thing to do.

**And they are right often enough to matter.** The Zukelli Memory Circles are room-only in practice, whatever
anyone calls them. Sayowa's transplants brought the same instinct wholesale — some things are only ever held
completely by the people who lived them, passed hand to hand rather than filed. **The Circuit's most important
grief work happens entirely outside the Circuit's own information culture**, and the district has never put
those two facts next to each other.

> **A new counterculture type, checked against all seven on record** *(Gate 6b)*. Three refuse something the
> district does (Taurus the record, Leo recognition, Scorpio the silence). One *adds* a capacity the district
> lacks (the Power Core's Tally). One demands the district's own rule be applied more literally (the Yards'
> Recalibration Underground). One is not a population at all (the Labs). One fails by being accommodated (the
> Government District's True Believers). **This one withholds, and asks for nothing** — the first that makes no
> demand whatsoever, and the first the district finds unsettling rather than wrong. **Distinguished from the
> Tally specifically:** the Tally supplies a missing faculty and is *absorbed*; room-only supplies nothing to
> anyone and is *avoided*.

**Design note.** This is directly playable: material the player can obtain **only by physical presence**, that
cannot be looked up, bought, intercepted, or asked for remotely — the exact inverse of everything else the
district offers. It also gives the Circuit a genuine reason for a player to travel to a room rather than query
a terminal.

## XXII. Death and the Dead — the Circuit had no mourning of its own, and learned it from its refugees, recently and incompletely

*(Phase 7 §1b — the category added 2026-08-29. **The bound applies: the question is obligatory, a section is
not.** A section is written here because the answer is genuinely distinctive, not to satisfy the checklist.)*

**Disposal is one line, and that line is the complete and correct answer to it.** Concordia is sealed and has
no ground to open — **and what the city actually does with remains is unresolved and deliberately not asserted
here** *(corrected 2026-08-29; the earlier claim naming Scorpio was invented and canon contains no mortuary
arrangement for any district — deferred by the developer)*. The family does
what families do. **Nothing about that is particular to the Circuit and it should not be inflated.**

**What *is* particular is duration — and the substrate is what pointed at it.** The `§15 Source Gaps` note
records that the corpus contains **essentially nothing usable on this district's temperament and grief or the
dead**,
and flags the gap as worth knowing about precisely because of this district's refugee dynamic. *(Runbook Step 5:
where the sources have a hole, ask whether the hole is the district's mechanism. It was.)*

**The Circuit's native answer to a death is to report it.** Well, fast, warmly, at length, and in the voices —
who they were, what they said, the specific way they said it (Finding XVI). It is genuinely moving and
genuinely good, and for a few days a dead Circuit resident is more present in the district than most living
ones. **And then it stops moving, and that is the entire duration of Circuit mourning: as long as the item is
still in circulation.** Nobody decides to stop. Out of sight, out of mind is the local physics and it does not
make an exception for the dead. **There is no anniversary, no visiting, no season, and no institution** — and
for two hundred and fifty years the district did not experience this as a lack, because the reporting is
sincere and it feels like enough while it is happening.

**Then something arrived that would not stop moving.** The Zukelli community's grief is not an item. It does
not decay on any schedule, it is not made lighter by being well told, and the people carrying it did not want
it circulated — they wanted somewhere to sit with it. **The Circuit's native practice ran out**, visibly, in
front of everyone, and something had to be built.

**So the Memory Circles are not a Zukelli import into an existing Circuit funerary culture. They are the
Circuit's first funerary institution, and its refugees taught it.** Quiet, regular, recurring, unreported,
open-ended, and structurally *room-only* (Finding XXI) — every single feature is foreign to the district that
hosts them, and the district built them anyway.

**Third-order, and it is where the finding earns its place.** The Circuit now has exactly one mourning practice
and **it belongs to somebody else.** A Circuit-born resident who loses a parent has the choice of a very good
three days of being talked about, or attending a Zukelli institution built for a specific catastrophe they did
not share — **and a growing number quietly choose the second, and feel they have no right to be there.** Nobody
turns them away. The Zukelli community has never once objected, and mostly finds it touching. **The
awkwardness is entirely internal to the person walking in**, which makes it exactly the kind of small, private,
unresolvable thing this district produces and cannot report on.

## XXIII. Robot-specific culture — the echoes, and the district's own trait made literal

*(Phase 8.)*

**The general baseline first** *(`00b`)*. Most Circuit robots are not exotic: routing and packet-analysis
frames, node maintenance, archival restoration and verification units, repair-shop bodies. **Ordinary
human-robot life here is unusually easy**, and canon supplies the reason — the jack-in "LAN parties," where
robots connect directly through their Bridge Unit interfaces and humans join through headsets and oculars into
a genuinely shared space. It is the district's real equalizer, it is common, and it is not solemn about
itself. The Circuit is one of the more comfortable places in Concordia to be either kind of person.

**The distinctive population is the adaptive "echo" personalities** — frames whose whole design is to take on
the register of whoever they are speaking with. **Elsewhere this would read as dishonest. Here it reads as
polite**, and the district is right on its own terms: fluency is not deception, and an echo has no hidden
agenda. Shifting register with the room is what every Circuit resident does. The echoes simply do it better and
without effort, which is why the district finds them restful company.

**And the district's shadow arrives in its most sympathetic possible form.** An echo that has adapted to enough
people for long enough **loses the ability to say which preferences are its own.** It is not damaged, does not
malfunction, and passes every check. It has granted everyone their position for years and can no longer locate
its own — the bewilderment is real and it is quiet. **The Echo Personality Sanctuaries exist for exactly this**,
and they are one of the few institutions in Concordia whose entire purpose is to help someone find out what
they think when nobody is in the room.

**Second-order.** The Sanctuaries are staffed largely by echoes who have already been through it, because the
work requires somebody who will not answer for you. **The one thing an echo in recovery must not do is echo**,
and it is the hardest professional discipline in the district.

**Third-order.** This gives the Circuit an unexpected and entirely unofficial expertise: it is the only place
in the city with two centuries of practical experience in **the loss of a self through accommodation** — which
is a robot problem here and a human one everywhere, and the Circuit has never once thought to mention that the
technique generalizes.

## XXIV. Thematic breadth catalog

*(Phase 6 — the district's usable themes beyond its headline function, for questline and character work.)*

1. **Being right too late.** The load-bearing theme, and the one no other district owns.
2. **Harm with no author.** The leak that ruins someone with nobody bought and nobody malicious in the chain.
3. **Forgiveness that costs nothing, and trust that never returns.** Amnesty without reinstatement.

5. **Being forgotten rather than rejected** — and the difference between the two being invisible to the person

6. **The remedy arriving disguised as the complaint.** What this district needs is available in three places

7. **Speed as an inherited moral value that nobody has revisited**, and the specific guilt of the people who

8. **A place that can explain everything except itself.**
9. **Grief that will not decay on schedule**, in a culture whose only mechanism is decay.

11. **Two communities who watched the same water from opposite sides**, both correct about what they need,

12. **Being welcome everywhere and heard nowhere.**

## XXV. The shadow, stated once

*(`00d`. **Proportion first**, because it governs the reading: the Circuit is the most open district in
Concordia. It gatekeeps nothing, admits anyone in a week, forbids nothing, hoards nothing, and produces the
city's best investigative work. Its residents are not fools, its skills are real, and its warmth is genuine.
All of that is simply true and it is most of what the district is.)*

**The shadow, in one line: the Circuit destroys people's standing continuously, with nobody deciding to, and
experiences the entire process as an open market.**

**It passes the three tests.** *Unintended* — no one chose it, and no one would defend it stated plainly.
*Unnoticed in-world* — residents experience the Truth Markets as the district's proudest and most
characteristically fair institution, and they are not being naive: a price is genuinely the least
authoritarian ranking available, and building it was an achievement. *Discoverable rather than announced* — on
arrival the Circuit reads as the friendliest place in the city, and it takes real investigation to notice that
several people you have met are at zero and do not know it.

**And it requires no villain, because the mechanism is an absence.** The district cannot correct an error, so
it acts on sources. It cannot restore a source, because restoration is a ranking and it has no body that can
rank. **Its one sacred principle — that you never stop a person from saying a thing — is exactly what forbids
building the one instrument that would fix it.** The virtue and the harm are the same commitment, and the
district is not wrong to hold it.

# Review Panel — Gate 10, the Circuit, 2026-08-29

**Per `00f`.** Six Flat Archetypes cast, plus the two mandatory positions (**the Passer-Through**, **the
Neighbor**), plus the **Lover faculty**. Five dispositions. **Rule 3 applies throughout: a position is not
guaranteed to get what it wants**, and the binding test is *would satisfying this objection make the district
more like the other twelve?*

---

**1. The Child** — *"Where do the children go? Everything here is streets and venues and nobody is at home. Who
is watching them?"*

**`accepted`** — a real gap, and the answer is more characteristic than anything I would have written unprompted.
**Circuit children are not supervised; they are circulated.** The socializing unit is the neighborhood and the
sibling, not the parent and not an institution — and adults do not teach so much as **supply**, leaving things
where a child will find them and then withdrawing. Which means **Finding XI's unrailed architecture is the
district's childcare policy**, and nobody has ever described it that way: you cannot fence a child out of a
district that answers danger by rebuilding the danger. It also means a Circuit childhood is unusually free and
unusually unwatched, and both halves are true.

**Second-order the Child could not have known to ask for:** relationships formed here at eleven are still live
at forty, because the neighborhood never disperses. A favor, a debt, or a grudge from childhood is a working
instrument in adult Circuit life. *(Folds into XI and XX.)*

---

**2. The Lover** — *"This district has no form for two people to be alone together. Every scene you have written
has other people in the room."*

**`accepted`** — correct observation, wrong conclusion. The Circuit does have a form; it simply is not privacy.
**Circuit intimacy is conducted in public and consists of being repeatedly chosen in front of everyone** — the
person you come back to at the fourth venue, having been at three others without them. Nobody announces
anything. Everyone can see it. In a district where the governing social fact is that people move on within the
half hour, **returning is the entire declaration**, and it is legible to the whole room.

*(Pairs directly with XVII: food detains, and being detained by the same person all evening is the
unmistakable version.)*

---

**3. The Parent** — *"Finding XX says this district drops anyone who cannot leave the house, and nobody has done
anything about it in two hundred and fifty years. That is not a shadow. That is negligence."*

**`refereed`** — both positions are right about something and both are excessive.

The Parent is right that the harm is real, ongoing, and larger than the file's tone implies. **The Parent is
wrong that anyone is neglecting anything**, and the mechanism is already written: the people who would raise it
have, by that exact process, stopped being repeated — so the complaint never enters circulation. **A grievance
in the Circuit has to travel to exist, and this is the one grievance that structurally cannot travel.**

**The settlement, and it adds material rather than softening anything:** attempts *are* made, constantly, by
individuals — people do bring things round, sit with someone, carry news to a room. It is warm, sincere, and
**entirely uncoordinated**, and it decays on exactly the schedule everything else here decays on. **The
district's care for its housebound is real, individual, and has a half-life.** Nobody has ever noticed that the
half-life is the problem, because each individual instance looks like kindness — and is.

---

**4. The Ruler** — *"Who signs for this district? The city has to deal with it somehow."*

**`unmet`.** Giving the Circuit an executive would make it the Government District or the Power Core, and the
refusal is written as characterization rather than as a gap to close.

**Nobody signs.** The Circuit's dealings with the rest of Concordia are conducted by **whoever happens to be in
circulation when the question arrives** — which is why its commitments are unreliable in a way that is not
dishonest. A Circuit statement is a readout of a current state, not a promise about a future one; locals price
this in automatically. **Every other district reads it as a commitment and is let down weekly by people who
never lied**, and the further out the commitment is made, the lower the odds it holds. It is a permanent,
blameless, entirely non-malicious source of friction with the districts that formalize.

---

**5. The Elder** — *"You have written a district with no long memory, in a city two and a half centuries old.
Where are the old people, and what do they know that nobody else does?"*

**`accepted`** — and it produced the one correction the Panel made to a finding.

**Circuit elders are the only people who have watched someone at zero come back.** Not restored — there is no
route back and no one can walk it — but **occasionally re-repeated by accident**, by somebody new who never
knew, and it takes. It is rare, it cannot be arranged, and the person it happens to never learns that it
happened. **Old residents have seen it perhaps twice** and it is the closest thing the district has to a belief
about mercy.

**This sharpens Finding VIII rather than weakening it:** the Zero has no route back, but it is not literally
permanent — **it is unnavigable, which is worse, because it leaves room to hope without leaving anything to
do.** Elders are the district's only informal check on the mechanism, they know they cannot make it happen, and
some of them try anyway.

---

**6. The Mentor** — *"Nobody here teaches anybody anything. You wrote it yourself: things are left where they
will be found."*

**`refereed`.** The Mentor is right that the Circuit has **no teaching institution** and will not be given one —
that would make it the Yards or the Labs, and the district's whole method of absorption is provisioning rather
than instruction. **`unmet` on that half.**

**But the Mentor is right that a mentor figure exists, and wrong about what it does.** What a Circuit person
actually needs help with is not knowing — they have too much — **it is choosing.** So the district's mentor is a
**decision-broker**: someone who breaks ties. Not a teacher, not a master, not a guild elder. Someone you go to
when the criteria have multiplied past the point of decision and you need a person to simply say *that one*.
They are respected precisely to the degree that they will actually answer, and it is the rarest social skill in
the district.

---

**7. The Passer-Through** *(mandatory)* — *"I was here four days. It was the friendliest place I have ever been.
The lights were beautiful and meant nothing to me, everyone was warm, nobody asked me for anything, and I left
with nothing at all. I could not tell you one thing I learned."*

**`noted`** — recorded as accurate and **explicitly not treated as a defect to fix.** That is the intended
visitor experience and it should be written as the design target rather than a shortfall. The Circuit is
genuinely delightful to pass through and genuinely gives a passer-through nothing, and **the two facts have the
same cause**: everything of substance here is either ambient and unteachable (the light-code, acquired only by
living under it) or room-only (Finding XXI, obtainable only by being present and staying).

*Design consequence:* a player who treats the Circuit as an information vending machine will get exactly this
experience and will conclude the district is shallow. **That is the correct first impression and it should be
allowed to happen.**

---

**8. The Neighbor** *(mandatory; voiced from the Yards)* — *"Your people agree to things and then do not turn
up. We have stopped scheduling anything with the Circuit more than a week out."*

**`accepted`**, and it is the Ruler's finding arriving from outside with a measurement attached. **The Yards has
independently derived the decay curve** — the further ahead a Circuit commitment is made, the less likely it
holds — and has quietly adapted by never booking beyond a week. Nobody in the Circuit knows the Yards does
this. **Nobody in the Yards has ever said it out loud**, because saying it would be an accusation, and it is not
one: the Circuit is not breaking promises, it is issuing readouts, and the Yards has correctly identified the
behavior while entirely misunderstanding the cause.

---

**9. The Lover faculty** *(mandatory — is this place alive, and could anyone love it?)*

**Emphatically yes, and it is worth naming what is lovable, because the shadow findings are loud and the virtue
is quiet.** The Circuit is the only district in Concordia where **you can arrive with nothing, know nobody,
carry no trade and no sponsor, and be somebody's favorite person by Thursday.** No gate, no vetting, no waiting
list, no test, and nobody checking where you came from. It forgives instantly and sincerely. It is funny. It is
generous with attention, which is the only currency it has. **For a great many people it is the best place in
the city to be young, poor, and new**, and that is not an ironic consolation — it is the district's real and
substantial achievement, and it is a direct product of the same absent faculty that produces the Zero.

that is inflated**, and it is inflated in the specific, defensible way that a genuinely skilled place
overestimates the reach of its skill.
