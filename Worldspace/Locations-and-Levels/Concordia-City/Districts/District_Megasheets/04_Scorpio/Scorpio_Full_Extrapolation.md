# Scorpio — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Scorpio given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Scorpio_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-09.

---

## I. Reputation is earned differently depending on whether you're in your own transformation or trying to gain access from outside

**Proposed:** for someone actually undergoing their own transformation, reputation accrues visibly through the district's own established mask culture (descent, integration, emergence) — progress itself is legible and respected. For an outsider or visitor with no transformation of their own underway, reputation is earned through vouching: a resident willing to stake their own standing on introducing someone, or through demonstrated respect for the district's existing social code around secrecy (the established norm that residents have real signals for what can and cannot be discussed publicly, and breaking that code is a serious violation). Simply showing genuine, sustained respect for that code, without ever violating it, is itself how an outsider slowly earns trust.

---

## II. The "peace of knowing" is absorbed by proximity, not formally taught

**Proposed:** consistent with the district having no single unifying institution (the Clinical and Sacred schools coexist without either one being the district's official philosophy), the "peace of knowing" isn't delivered through any curriculum or teaching practice — it's something long-term residents describe, retrospectively, as having picked up simply from being immersed in the district's whole way of operating. Nobody sits anyone down to explain it. It settles in the way any deeply-lived-in worldview does, through years of proximity rather than instruction.

---

## III. The fog thickens with depth, functioning as a literal signal of privacy level

**Proposed:** given the developer's own confirmed detail that doors lead to corridors that lead to invite-only chambers, the district's engineered fog plausibly thickens the further in you go — thin and unremarkable at the district's public edges, genuinely obscuring by the time you reach its deepest, most restricted spaces. This gives visitors (and the player) an intuitive, physical way to read how deep into Scorpio's private layers they've actually gotten, without needing anyone to tell them directly.

## V. Architecture (District Culture Development Plan, Phase 1)

**Proposed:** Scorpio's architecture is already substantially established — this Finding consolidates it into a dedicated treatment. **Baseline:** dark reds and blacks, corridors leading progressively inward to invite-only chambers (Mega-Init), a genuinely nested, layered building logic rather than Cancer's open, spread-out design or Taurus's courtyard-centered one. **Steam is structural, not decorative** — engineered thermal design that physically creates privacy pockets in public space, per `District_Canon_Reference.md`'s own "privacy is a physical property of the district, not a social courtesy." **Depth as an architectural language, already established via Finding III:** the fog thickening with depth means Scorpio's buildings are legible by how obscured they are — a visitor can read their own access level architecturally, without being told. **Purpose-built specialized spaces** (Mask Foundries, Silence Booths, private threshold rooms for near-death simulation) give the district a genuine range of distinct interior architectures serving specific therapeutic functions, not a single uniform building type repeated throughout.

## VIII. Religious/Philosophical Landscape — naming what both schools already agree on (Phase 2)

**Proposed.** Scorpio's canon gives it two named institutions — the **Clinical School** and the **Sacred
School** — coexisting with neither being the district's official philosophy, and one recurring phrase residents
use without explaining: **"the peace of knowing"** (Findings II and VI). Finding II already established that it
is absorbed by proximity rather than taught.

**Applying the Naming technique** (`02_Phase_2` §8): the search for religious-register language attached to
non-religious objects returns that phrase immediately, and — per the Leo refinement — **what it names is not a
religion.** Scorpio does not need one; it has two schools already. What it lacks is a statement of the premise
those two schools share, which is why they can disagree completely about method and still not be rivals.

**The premise, stated as a resident would agree with it:**

> **The worst thing that can happen to you has already been located. Knowing exactly where it is, is better
> than being protected from it.**

That is the whole of "the peace of knowing," and it explains a great deal that was previously just true:

- **Why the two schools coexist without conflict.** They disagree about *how* to bring a person to the thing —
  structured protocol versus rite — and agree entirely that being brought to it is the point. A methodological
  dispute, not a doctrinal one. Neither can win, because neither is arguing about the premise.
- **Why the district's register is "acceptance, not tranquility"** (Finding VI). Peace here is not the absence
  of the bad thing. It is the end of not knowing where it is.
- **Why comfort offered from outside lands as insult** (see Finding XVIII). Reassurance proposes that the thing
  might not be there. To a resident that is not kindness — it is being asked to give up the only thing that
  actually worked.

**Ritual register — conservative liturgy over radical content** *(substrate §7)*. The subject matter is death
and transformation; the ceremonial apparatus around it is deliberately plain, fixed, unchanging, and free of
any consoling overlay. **The flatness is the respect.** A rite here is short, verbally spare, identical every
time, and would strike a visitor as almost bureaucratic given what it is doing.

**No meaningful marginal belief is proposed**, for a structural reason rather than an oversight: an imported
faith would have to offer either consolation or an afterlife, and the premise above forecloses the first while
the district's whole civic practice is already an answer to the second.

## IX. Fashion — correctly scoped, and the mask is not the answer (Phase 2)

**Proposed. This Finding exists partly to correct a standing error** — see the Gate 2 correction appended to
Finding VI. Scorpio is the district where the general-population failure was first caught, and the same failure
had nonetheless persisted inside this file.

**General population, and this is the actual answer:** dark, understated, expensive, and comfortable. Deep reds
and near-blacks *(substrate §12: maroon, burgundy, crimson, rust; white and pastels rejected)* in good
materials — leather, heavy weaves, worked metal fastenings — cut for **warmth retention in a humid, enclosed,
below-grade district** and built to last. **The register is understated luxury, never display.** The
counterintuitive and important note *(substrate §14.12)*: **this is not a poor district, it is a dark and
expensive one**, and its clothing reads that way to anyone who knows fabric.

**Nobody is in costume.** No gothic affect, no ceremonial dress as daily wear, and above all **no general
masking**. An ordinary Scorpio street reads as quiet, well-dressed, and slightly formal — not theatrical.

**The mask, correctly scoped:** worn by residents **actively undergoing a transformation process**, in the
settings where that process happens, and functioning as a **phase signal** — it tells others which stage the
wearer is in (descent, integration, emergence) and therefore how to behave toward them. Three consequences:

- **It is worn by a small minority at any given time**, and only in specific contexts. Most residents are not in
  process (Finding XII), and a visitor may see very few.
- **It is a restraint device, not a privacy screen** *(substrate §3, and this inverts the obvious reading)*. The
  wearer fits it to themselves. Its function is to hold something in during a period when the wearer knows they
  are not reliable — which is why removing it early is a serious matter and why the district treats a masked
  person as owed a specific kind of space rather than as hidden.
- **It retires when the phase does.** A mask ends when the chapter it belonged to ends *(substrate §14.2)*;
  keeping one past its process is legible and slightly alarming.

**Robots wear them on the same terms as humans**, which is worth stating because it would be easy to assume
otherwise given a robot's control over its own visible expression.

## X. Arcanet Culture — the district that records the most and transmits the least (Phase 3)

**Proposed.** Scorpio's information culture is the inverse of Leo's and is *not* a variant of Taurus's.

**Total retention, near-zero transmission.** The Archive of Final Confessions is already canon, and the
district's whole documented instinct is to keep material rather than move it *(substrate §3: it "will save every
valuable piece of information you come across")*. Arcanet here is **storage and retrieval infrastructure**,
deliberately not a network — connected inward, minimally outward, and with the outward connections treated as a
supervised liability rather than a service.

**The distinction from Taurus that must be stated, or the two will be merged** *(Gate 6b)*:

| | **Taurus** | **Scorpio** |
|---|---|---|
| Records are about | property, lineage, objects | **people, and what they said** |
| Access | broadly public within the district | **sealed by default** |
| Duration | permanent, inherited | **held until released or closed** |
| Consent | not a concept; the record is civic | **the subject's, and it is revocable** |

**Privacy norms are the strictest in Concordia and they are procedural rather than polite.** Finding I already
establishes a real social code about what may be discussed; this Finding supplies its infrastructural half.
Material about a person is held under that person's authority — which makes Scorpio the only district where a
resident can compel the deletion of a record about themselves, and makes its archives structurally unlike
anywhere else in the city.

**Relationship to Gemini:** Scorpio is Concordia's least networked district by choice, and consumes the wider
Arcanet almost entirely as capacity rather than as connection. To Gemini this reads as waste — enormous storage
that nothing flows out of. **To Scorpio, a record that can be transmitted is not a secure record**, and the two
districts have never resolved this because it is not a technical disagreement.

**The injury ledger** *(substrate §14.7)* belongs here as real infrastructure rather than temperament: wrongs
are **filed rather than forgiven**, held indefinitely with no statute of limitations, and closed by one of two
routes — settlement, or the offender demonstrating genuine contrition **plus changed conduct.** Elapsed time
closes nothing, which is why waiting a grievance out is the one strategy that reliably fails here. It has
clerks, retention rules, and a redemption procedure, and it is used far more often to close things than
outsiders assume.

## XI. Visitor Experience and Visitor-to-Resident Transition — where the conflict geometry lives (Phase 4)

**Proposed. Per the Mode B rule** (`00e` §11b), the district's inter-district relationships are written **into**
this phase rather than deferred to a substrate section, because Phase 4 is where arrivals, receptions, and
neighbors actually belong.

**Visitor experience, extending Finding I rather than repeating it:** guarded, not hostile; a visitor needs a
known contact or a legible reason to be present. The fog gradient (Finding III) lets them read their own access
level without being told.

**The transition mechanism is admission to a cell, and it is unlike any other district's** *(substrate §7,
which is the richest institutional material in the whole substrate folder)*.

- **Stage one is auditable conduct**: demonstrated trustworthiness, promises kept, willingness to speak
  emotionally. **Clearing it is necessary and explicitly not sufficient.**
- **Stage two is deliberately unfalsifiable**: chemistry, ease, comfortable silence, wordless reading of mood,
  willingness to hold a secret permanently. No applicant can manufacture it and no rejection can be appealed.
- **The verdict is fast** — often formed within moments of meeting — while the process around it is slow.
- **Cells are small (three to ten) and their membership is concealed from everyone**, including family. There is
  no register. A newcomer frequently cannot tell that cells exist at all, which is where the outside
  impression that Scorpians are friendless comes from.

**And the district teaches, as a formal first lesson to newcomers, that non-admission is not an insult.**
*(Substrate §7: "a matter of survival, not snobbery" — an energy budget, not a judgment.)* **This is the single
most institutionally distinctive thing about Scorpio's welcome**: it is the only district in Concordia that
explains its own exclusion mechanism to the people it excludes, on arrival, deliberately, because without the
explanation every non-admission reads as a slight and the whole arrangement becomes unlivable.

**The consequence nobody designed, and it is severe** *(see Finding XIX)*: a resident who is never admitted to
any cell is not merely lonely. Because comfort here is distributed only inside cells (Finding XVIII), they are
**structurally unreachable by any care the district produces**, indefinitely, while living in the district whose
entire civic function is care of the interior.

### Conflict geometry, written in

- **Taurus — the district Scorpio is structurally set against, and its single most important external
  relationship.** The dispute is **retention against elimination**: Taurus holds, Scorpio ends things. Neither
  can be moved by pressure, so when it jams it jams completely. **But the substrate is emphatic that this axis
  has a working mode**, and it matters more than the deadlock: Taurus is the one neighbor whose humor reaches
  Scorpio, the one place anything light gets in, and the district's most-trusted keeper of its confidences. What
  flows back is diagnosis — Scorpio sees what Taurus is refusing to notice about itself. **The same neighbor is
  the pressure valve and the wall**, and which one it is at any moment is the most consequential variable in
  either district's politics. *(This corroborates Taurus's own Finding XX from the opposite side: Taurus's
  strongest faculty is precisely Scorpio's weakest, and the transfer is nearly impossible for both.)*
- **The two hardest frictions.** **Leo** is a visibility conflict, and it is asymmetric in a way that guarantees
  eventual detonation: Scorpio privately finds Leo's bids for control *funny*, manages the relationship with
  deliberate flattery, and considers Leo categorically outmatched. That is exactly the treatment most likely to
  enrage the other party the moment it is noticed, and it always eventually is. **The Labs** is the structurally
  harder of the two *(substrate §8)* — it can drop an attachment overnight at no visible cost, which Scorpio reads as either
  fraud or inhumanity. **And it is simultaneously Scorpio's only available corrective**, the one neighbor that
  supplies the wide view Scorpio's depth-focus cannot generate. The antagonist is the remedy.
- **Natural allies.** **Cancer** — shared emotional read-speed, and the source of the district's only depicted
  ordinary leisure (Finding XII). **Pisces** — same population, opposite method: Scorpio must know *why*
  someone became an outsider, Pisces simply accepts them. Two allied institutions that cannot use each other's
  procedures. **Virgo** is its best working partnership, and the two are noted as sharing a root in the same
  ancient medical symbol. **Capricorn** is complementary and carries a **shared vice** rather than only a
  shared strength — both tend to tyrannize, and together they isolate.
- **The arm's-length rule, which is a staffing principle** *(substrate §9)*: Scorpio's adjacent districts work
  well at a distance and badly up close. **The neighbors it should send envoys to are precisely the ones it
  must never be asked to merge with**, and the district has learned this well enough to staff its liaison posts
  accordingly without ever having written down why.

## XII. Ordinary Daily Life (Phase 5)

**Governing test applied:** nobody in Scorpio is transforming daily. **The overwhelming majority of residents
are not in process at all, and many never have been.** This Finding is about them.

**Routines.** Most residents work the district's service and facility economy — maintenance of humidity and
thermal systems (which in a steam district is constant, skilled, and never finished), records work, food
service, and the medical, pharmaceutical and mortuary trades that are Scorpio's actual employment base. A
substantial number commute out to ordinary jobs elsewhere and treat Scorpio purely as home. **Children go to
school and are taught ordinary subjects**, with two local additions noted below.

**The texture of ordinary leisure, and it is the one thing the substrate depicts warmly** *(substrate §9, on the
Cancer affinity)*:
**shared handwork.** Baking, gardening, sculpting, repair — activities using the hands, done alongside someone
else, where **co-presence matters far more than the activity**. Scorpians will spend an entire evening doing
something mundane together and count it among the better evenings. This is the district's real social baseline,
and it is much quieter and much more domestic than its reputation.

**Two local additions to ordinary schooling** *(substrate §7)*: subjects are **elective-driven to an unusual
degree**, on the working assumption that a pupil's own instinct about what they need is more reliable than a
syllabus; and **intuition is a timetabled subject with actual drills** — paired sender-and-receiver exercises,
examined like anything else. A visiting educator would find the first admirable and the second baffling.

**Mundane concerns.** Whether a facility booking will come through this year or next. Damp getting into
something that matters. A cell that has quietly stopped meeting and nobody wanting to be the one to say so. The
specific social awkwardness of having a neighbor you know is in process and not knowing whether to acknowledge
it. Whether an old filing on the ledger is ever going to close.

**Personal struggles.** A resident who has lived here fifteen years and has never been admitted to a cell, who
does not discuss it and would refuse sympathy if offered. Someone whose process ended without resolving, who is
technically finished and privately isn't. A parent worrying that a child's total withdrawal is the ordinary
local kind and not the other kind, and having no way to tell.

**Escapism/downtime.** The Silence Booths (canon) at one extreme; at the other, and far more common, **the
district's public baths** — see Finding XIV(a), which is where most residents actually go.

## XIV. Cuisine, Music, and Arts — a district that had to invent a way of speaking (Phase 7)

**Proposed — the unifying insight, and it comes from the substrate's own account of the core need.** What this
district most wants is **to be met at full depth and not abandoned for it** — and its own sources state the
obstacle in the same breath: *"you don't have the words to express what you feel."* **The thing wanted most is
the one thing the district has no vocabulary to request.** Every category below is a workaround for that.

**(a) Food — eating as a scheduled instrument.** The substrate delivers Scorpio's dietary material as **rules,
in the same register as its privacy and truth-telling instructions**, not as preferences, and that register is
the finding. Food here attaches to **where a person is in a process**: high-protein, plain, whole-grain, no
large evening meals, spring water rather than tap.

**Fused from Iquitos ayahuasca retreat practice, researched for this pass:** the **dieta** — a restrictive
dietary and conduct regime observed *before* a process and, critically, **continuing well after it ends**, with
a scheduled follow-up long past the event. Scorpio has adopted the same structure. A **dieta card** states the
restrictions attached to a stage, and reading what someone is eating tells you exactly where they are. **Nobody
remarks on it**, which is the local courtesy.

**And residents not in any process eat by the same rules anyway, from habit** — which is why Scorpio's ordinary
cooking is spare, protein-heavy, and slightly severe, offset by the one documented indulgence: **strong
flavor in small quantities**, condiments, and intensely seasoned small dishes. Scorpio does not do the large
communal platter. It does the small, sharp, deliberate thing.

**(b) Robot cuisine and siligel.** Siligel here is **formulated by process stage** in exactly the way human
dieta is, prepared at the same institution that handles pharmacy and mortuary work (Finding XIII). The local
peculiarity: **Scorpio is the only district where a robot's nutritional intake is treated as clinical
information** — logged, sealed under the same consent rules as everything else (Finding X), and legible to a
practitioner as a record of what that robot has recently been carrying.

**Glitch-Coolant** sits with the humor rather than with the ritual — see (c). Consumption is **inside cells**,
in small groups, and the district's reputation for grimness is at its most misleading here.

**(c) The thing outsiders get most wrong: this district is funny.** *(Substrate §8, same-sign massing: dark
humor is **public infrastructure rather than personal style** — the stated stabiliser when two of this
temperament go down together.)*

**Independently corroborated by the sky-burial research**, which is the strongest single external warrant in
this pass: real *rogyapas* — the body-breakers who dismember the dead — are documented as working **not in
solemn silence but cheerfully and light-heartedly**, because a light atmosphere is believed to help the dead
move on. **Two entirely unrelated sources — the design substrate and a real funerary practice — independently say that
the people who handle death do it cheerfully, and that the cheerfulness is functional.**

So: **Scorpio's death-workers and process-facilitators are funny at work, deliberately and as doctrine.** Not
gallows-humor-as-coping; a maintained working atmosphere with a stated purpose. **To an outsider it reads as
monstrous**, and the district knows exactly how it reads and does not modify it for visitors. This is the single
most counterintuitive true thing about the place.

**(d) Music — simultaneous speaking.** *(Fused from Cabaret Voltaire and Dada, researched for this pass.)* The
documented Dada inventions are the **simultaneous poem** — several people reciting different texts at once, in
different rhythms, producing deliberate unintelligibility — and the **sound poem**, which abandons meaning
entirely for voiced syllable, performed *"playful yet deadly serious."*

**This is precisely the form a district would invent if its central problem were that its members have no words
for what they feel and cannot bear to be individually legible.** Scorpio's native practice is a room of people
speaking simultaneously — each saying something true, none individually audible, the whole producing a texture
rather than a statement. **You are heard without being identified**, which is the mask principle applied to
speech and the only way this district can say things aloud at all.

- **The serious register** is what the Simultaneity Room (Finding XIII) is for. Attendance is not performance;
  everyone speaks. There is no audience.
- **The social register** is much lighter and often very funny, and shades directly into (c).
- **Nobody is a musician.** There are no professionals, no bills, and no recordings — recording would defeat
  the entire mechanism.

> **Cross-district contrast, stated so the next reader does not merge these** *(Gate 6b)*. Three districts, one
> category, three incompatible answers: **Taurus** — everyone sings, quietly, and there are no professionals.
> **Leo** — almost nobody plays, and the general population are expert *listeners*. **Scorpio** — everyone
> speaks at once and nobody is individually audible. Each is unusable in either of the others.

**(e) Arts — making as a prescribed channel.** *(Substrate §7: emotion is routed into writing, painting, music,
gardening, photography, on the assumption of native talent needing no instruction.)* **Art here is a sanctioned
discharge route, not a career.** Nearly everyone makes something; almost nobody exhibits; **a substantial
proportion of the work is destroyed once made**, which is understood as the work having functioned rather than
as a loss. Scorpio therefore exports almost no art despite producing more of it per resident than any district
in Concordia — a fact Leo finds incomprehensible and slightly offensive.

## XV. Human-Robot Relations, Counterculture, and Private Life (Phase 7)

**Proposed.**

**(a) Human-Robot Relations — the local inflection is a physiological asymmetry with institutional
consequences.** The district's whole health doctrine holds that **withholding becomes illness** — the substrate
gives two separate somatic routes by which unexpressed feeling turns into physical disease, and Scorpio's
medicine is built to read symptoms as confessions.

**That doctrine applies unevenly across the two populations.** Whatever the equivalent burden is for a robot, it
is not the same one, and it does not arrive by the same route or on the same schedule. **So robots can hold
what humans structurally cannot hold safely** — and the district has organized around that fact without ever
deciding to.

Consequences, all of them consistent with existing canon rather than replacing it:

- **The Archive of Final Confessions' confession-recording archivists (already canon) are disproportionately
  robots, and now there is a structural reason why** rather than an aesthetic one. A human who holds that
  volume of other people's worst material pays for it in a way the district's own doctrine predicts precisely.
- **A quiet, genuinely unresolved question sits under it:** if the keeping costs a robot nothing, is the robot
  *doing* what the district thinks is being done, or only storing it? Both schools have positions. Neither has
  won, and robot archivists themselves are divided — some find the question insulting, others find it the most
  interesting thing anyone has asked about their work.
- **What would look strange to a visitor:** not any inequality between the populations, of which Scorpio has
  notably little, but that the district's most trusted and most burdened profession is quietly understood to be
  a robot occupation for reasons nobody says out loud.

**(b) Counterculture — the Loud.** *(Derived per Step C from the district's specific demand.)*

**What Scorpio requires of everyone is silence about other people.** The code around what may and may not be
discussed (Finding I) is the district's load-bearing social technology, and the substrate is blunt that the
reserve exists *because* the undisciplined version of this temperament is the worst gossip and manipulator in
the set — **the restraint is fitted deliberately, and the whole social order rests on people keeping it on.**

**The counterculture is therefore the people who take it off and talk** — openly, in public, about things the
code seals. And their position is not frivolous: **they hold that a district where nothing may be discussed
cannot correct anything**, that a sealed ledger and a concealed cell system make abuse structurally
undetectable, and that the code protects whoever is worst served by daylight.

**They are right.** That is the point of the finding.

**And the harm they cause is also real** — the substrate is equally clear that unsealed material in this
temperament's hands becomes manipulation and cattiness rather than reform, and some of them know it about
themselves. **Both things are true simultaneously**, which is what makes this the district's genuine political
conflict rather than a grumble.

- **The mainstream calls them the Loud**, dismissively, as a quiet district would.
- **They cannot agree what to call themselves**, and this is characteristic: half consider themselves a reform
  movement and half simply think talking should not be a crime. They have no name, no leadership, and a real
  ongoing internal argument about whether they are one thing at all.

> **Cross-district contrast** *(Gate 6b)*. Taurus's counterculture declines **the record**; Leo's declines
> **recognition**; both are sympathetic and essentially harmless. **Scorpio's is the one that is not harmless**,
> and it is also the only one making an argument the district cannot answer. Different in structure, not just
> in subject.

**(c) Private Life and Minority Communities — the cell, and what it costs the uncelled.**

**The integration bargain is the steepest and the most rewarding in Concordia.** What a cell offers is
genuinely unavailable elsewhere in the city: being known completely by three to ten people, permanently, with
nothing withheld. **What it costs is that the offer cannot be applied for, cannot be appealed, and is
concealed** — so there is no way to know how close you came, or whether anyone considered you.

**The generational cost, unintended and structural:** cells **calcify**. A cell that has admitted nobody in
twenty years is not being cruel; it is at its energy budget, which the district's own doctrine says is the
whole basis of the rule. **Its members then age out together**, and the district loses the material inside
them all at once, because none of it was ever written down anywhere — Scorpio's archives hold what people
formally confessed, not what they told their cell.

**And a district composed of cells has no mechanism whatsoever for a stranger.** That is not a flaw anyone
introduced. It is what the structure is.

## XVI. Municipal Holidays (Phase 7)

**Proposed — three, and the middle one is genuinely a party.**

**1. The Common Session** *(annual, practical, quietly about class).* **Fused from Balinese Ngaben practice,
researched for this pass**, where individual cremation is prohibitively expensive and the ordinary solution is
a **collective ceremony** organized by the local community — with lower-income families approaching a family
that has already scheduled one and asking to be included. Scorpio's canon already has **waiting lists years
long**; this supplies the mechanism that relieves them. Once a year the district runs a **collective process**
at a fraction of the individual cost, and residents who have been waiting join it. **Everyone understands that
joining the Common Session rather than booking privately says something about your circumstances, and it is
considered extremely poor form to notice.**

**2. The Breakers' Night** *(annual, loud, extremely funny, and appalling to outsiders).* The death-workers',
facilitators', and archivists' own night — the one occasion when the district's working cheerfulness (Finding
XIV(c)) goes fully public. It is raucous, the humor is unrepeatable outside the district, and **visitors are
not excluded but are reliably horrified.** Scorpians consider this the best evening of the year and are
entirely unapologetic about it. It is also, not coincidentally, the night on which the profession's actual
casualties are named and drunk to.

**3. Closure Day** *(quarterly, brief, administrative, and enormously charged).* The ledger office (Finding X)
processes closures on set days. A filing closed is read out; the two parties need not attend and often do not.
**It is a five-minute clerical procedure carrying more emotional weight than anything else in the district's
calendar**, and the flat register is deliberate — conservative liturgy over radical content (Finding VIII).

## XVII. Robot-Specific Culture — the capstone (Phase 8)

**Lens:** theme/role-based — Scorpio's robot population organizes around the district's defining vocation,

Finding XV(a). This is the anchor the rest builds from.

**Finding 2 — The confession-recording archivist is a robot vocation with a genuine internal ethic.**
*(Inheritance: adapted pool-draw. Iceberg: Deep Culture.)* Already canon as a robot type; what this pass adds is
the professional culture — consent handling (Finding X), the rule that an archivist never initiates contact
with a subject, and a strong internal norm against discussing holdings even with one's own cell. **The
archivists are, in practice, the strictest keepers of the code the Loud are arguing against**, and several of
them privately think the Loud have a point.

**Finding 3 — Robot Kinship here is rooted in shared load, not shared origin.** *(Inheritance: adapted
pool-draw. Iceberg: Deep Culture.)* Per the Kinship reminder to start from the actual shared experience: robots
who worked the **Identity Collapse Epidemic** together — the emergency years when technique was being invented
under load — share a permanent bond, and it is explicitly **not** based on activation date, model, or facility.
It is based on having been present for the same specific catastrophe and having held the same material.

**Finding 4 — What "transformation" means for a robot is a live and unresolved dispute between the two
schools.** *(Inheritance: genuinely emergent, robot-only. Iceberg: Deep Culture.)* The district's central
service is built on a human process. Whether a robot undergoing it is doing the same thing, an analogous thing,
or something the schools have no framework for is **openly argued and deliberately not settled here** — it is
the most interesting open question in the district and should stay open rather than be resolved by this pass.

**Finding 5 — The bath is where the robot/human distinction is least legible, by design.** *(Inheritance:
adapted pool-draw. Iceberg: Surface Culture over Deep.)* The Common Bath's whole principle is that status,
title, and placement do not apply (Finding XIII). Robots and humans use it identically and the district treats
that as unremarkable — which, given how conscious Scorpio is of category everywhere else, is a deliberate and
protected exception rather than an oversight.

**Consistency checks:**
- **Human-Robot Relations Baseline:** consistent with the egalitarian, robot-skewed baseline; Finding XV(a) is
  an asymmetry of *physiology and consequent occupation*, not of standing.
- **Fragmentation Matrix:** Scorpio's Bond/Grief tagging is **not confirmed in this pass** and is deliberately

- **Robot Elementals & Solar Symbols:** unconfirmed for districts, as with the other three.

**Swap Test:** tested against **Cancer**, chosen per the sharpened rule as the partner most likely to survive —

## XVIII. Capability profile — world-class diagnosis, nowhere to convalesce, and a care surplus that cannot leave the room (Substrate)

**Proposed — the pass's primary generator.** *(Substrate: `F_Rulerships.md` §5-6 D4 row; `08_Scorpio.md` §10,
§14.9.)*

- **Structurally excellent at investigation, controlled force, and elimination.** Precision rather than
  aggression — the surgeon's knife, not the robber's. This is genuine and the city relies on it.
- **Structurally weak in valuing others, and weaker still in care.** Both of the faculties that would soften
  what it does well are at their lowest ebb here.
- **And it has no amplified faculty at all** — nothing works better than its native strength.

**Scorpio is therefore the only district in the system whose capability row is net-negative: one strength, two
deficits, nothing amplified.** Where Taurus's absence made it a donor and Leo's made it non-redundant,
**Scorpio's makes it a district whose civic function requires precisely the two capacities it structurally
lacks.** It is the psychological-care district, and care is its weakest term.

**The characteristic institutional failure follows exactly: world-class diagnosis with nowhere to convalesce.**
It will identify what is wrong with a person more accurately than anywhere in Concordia, and it has no
institution for recovery. Its welfare provision is procedural. **Its care workers burn out, because care here
is delivered as endurance** — the only register the district has for it.

**And the ayahuasca research supplies the exact shape of what is missing.** Real practice devotes as much
structure to *afterward* as to the event: a dieta continuing two weeks past, integration circles, one-to-one
sessions on rest days — where practitioners say most of the actual processing happens — and a scheduled
follow-up a month later. **Scorpio has the ceremony and not the aftercare.** It runs the descent superbly and
the return badly, and the gap is not negligence: it is the capability row made institutional.

**But the deficit is distribution, not production — and this is the correction that makes the finding true
rather than merely bleak.** *(Substrate §10, revised reading.)* This district produces gentleness in **enormous
quantity**. It simply cannot get it past the cell boundary. Inside three to ten people it is among the most
tender environments in Concordia; outside, the same person is indifferent by default. Three consequences, all
already visible in the material above:

1. **An uncelled resident is not merely lonely — they are structurally unreachable by any care the district
   generates** (Finding XI).
2. **Imported comfort fails on contact.** Sympathy offered from outside is refused as condescension, which is
   why well-meant help from Cancer or Pisces reliably lands wrong.
3. **The one form that does travel is structure** — scheduling, procedure, provision. **A district that
   delivers care as logistics and is genuinely offended when this is called cold** is both exactly in character
   and quietly tragic.

**Shadow proportion** (`00d`): **Scorpio works, and the city needs it to.** It does the psychological work no
other district will touch, it does it well, and its residents are neither grim nor damaged — they are, per
Finding XII, mostly people doing ordinary jobs and spending evenings doing handwork with friends. Nothing above
says otherwise. **The shadow is that a place organized to reach people at their worst has no reliable way to
reach the ones who never let it start.** *(Discoverability: invisible on arrival; a visitor sees only
competence.)*

## XIX. Gate 9 finding — the district can read progress, which means it can read failure (Substrate)

**Proposed. Produced by the asymmetry check** (`00c` Gate 9), run first against the pre-Plan findings per §11b.

**Finding I states that for someone in their own transformation, "progress itself is legible and respected."**
It wrote only that direction. **The mechanism is symmetrical: if progress is legible, so is its absence.**

The phase mask (Finding IX) signals which stage its wearer is in. **A mask that has not changed in a long time
says so, publicly, to everyone who passes.** Nobody is monitoring it. No record is kept. The information is
simply *on the person's face*, in a district whose entire population is fluent in reading exactly that signal.

**And the reverse of Finding III's access gradient does the same work architecturally**: if the fog tells you
how deep you have been admitted, then **losing standing is something you walk into.** You do not get told. You
find the depth you used to reach is no longer available to you, and there is no announcement and no appeal
because no decision was made.

**Combined with Finding XVIII, this is the district's specific cruelty and it requires no cruel person:** the
one place in Concordia that can see a stalled recovery with total clarity is also the one with no institution
for what happens next. **Everyone can see it. Nobody's job is to do anything about it. And the doctrine holds
that being seen clearly is itself the good.**

**Design hook, no villain required:** a resident whose mask has not changed in years, who is treated with
complete and genuine respect by everyone, and for whom nothing whatsoever is going to happen.

## XX. Contradiction reconciled, and two findings mined from the source gaps (Substrate)

**Proposed.**

**The contradiction.** The substrate places Scorpio in the most change-resistant modality group — resist,
stabilize, dig in, stalemate — and simultaneously describes the archetype as the system's specialist in
*forced change*. Canon likewise has Scorpio as the district that transforms people for a living.

**Both are true, per the both-are-true shape** (`00e` §7 — *one disposition, two objects*): **the objective
never moves and the method always does.** *(Substrate §2: "you don't just find a way; you find a score of ways
to get your goal.")* Scorpio changes people **because it will not change its mind about what needs to happen.**
Its flexibility is entirely tactical and sits on top of an immovable aim. Which is why the district is
simultaneously the most adaptive institution in Concordia at the level of technique and the most immovable at
the level of purpose — and why arguing with it about method is easy and arguing with it about goals is futile.

**Mined from §15 Source Gaps** *(per the 2026-08-29 rule that a hole in the sources is often the district's
mechanism)*:

- **"Any dispute requiring a party to yield has no native resolution mechanism."** The substrate's massing
  material — the only place any source describes this temperament *concentrated*, which is what a district is —
  states outright that two of these in conflict produce a standoff with no route out, since neither will
  surrender. **Applied: Scorpio has no internal arbitration at all.** Its disputes are settled by the injury
  ledger (deferral), by amputation (Finding XI's cut-off), or by the Taurus axis functioning as an external
  pressure valve — never by anyone conceding. **This is why the district needs its opposite neighbor working**,
  and why a jammed Taurus relationship is a genuine civic emergency here rather than a diplomatic
  inconvenience.
- **The environmental self-contradiction, which the district knows about.** The substrate states that this
  temperament is "vulnerable to dark environments where it's stagnant, heavy, toxic" — **an exact description of
  the architecture it builds by preference** (Findings III and V). **Scorpio builds, by taste, the precise
  conditions its own medicine identifies as making its people ill, and it is aware of this.** Whether it denies
  it, taxes it, or treats the sickness as part of the price is a real and unforced political question, and is
  deliberately left open rather than answered here.

## Worth Your Attention — updated 2026-08-29

**Standout: Finding XVIII — Scorpio produces care in quantity and cannot get it past the cell boundary.**

The capability reading gives Scorpio the only **net-negative** row in the system: one strength, two deficits,
nothing amplified — and the two deficits are precisely the faculties its civic function most requires. Run out
institutionally that yields *world-class diagnosis with nowhere to convalesce*, which the ayahuasca research
then sharpened into something specific and damning: **real transformative practice devotes as much structure to
the aftermath as to the event** — dieta continuing weeks past, integration circles, follow-up a month later, and
practitioners' own view that most of the processing happens on the rest days. **Scorpio has the ceremony and not
the aftercare.**

What makes it the standout rather than merely bleak is the correction underneath it: **the deficit is
distribution, not production.** This district generates enormous tenderness — it simply cannot move it across
the cell line. Inside three to ten people it is among the warmest environments in Concordia. Outside, the same
person is indifferent by default, imported sympathy is refused as condescension, and **the only form of care
that travels is structure** — scheduling, procedure, provision. A district that delivers love as logistics and
is genuinely offended when this is called cold. **No villain, nobody failing at their job, and a real
casualty**: the resident no cell ever admitted, who is unreachable by every good thing the district makes.

**Runner-up: Finding XIV(c) — the district is funny, and this is the strongest externally-corroborated finding
in the pass.** The substrate says dark humor here is *public infrastructure rather than personal style*. The
sky-burial research independently documents that real body-breakers work **cheerfully and light-heartedly, as
doctrine**, because a light atmosphere is held to help the dead move on. **Two unrelated sources — the design
substrate and a real funerary practice — arriving at the same non-obvious claim.** Scorpio's death-workers
are funny at work, deliberately, and know exactly how it reads to outsiders.

**Also worth flagging: Finding XIV(d).** Dada's simultaneous poem turned out to be the exact form this district
needed — many voices at once, none individually audible — because Scorpio's stated core problem is that its
people have no words for what they feel and cannot bear to be individually legible. **You are heard without
being identified.** It is the mask principle applied to speech, and it completes a clean three-district contrast
on a single category that none of the three could borrow from another.

**The original standout still holds** — Finding I's two earned paths to reputation — now with its unwritten
half supplied by Finding XIX.
