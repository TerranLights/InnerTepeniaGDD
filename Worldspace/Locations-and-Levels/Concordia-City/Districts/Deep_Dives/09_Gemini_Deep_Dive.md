## Stage 1 — What Gemini Actually Became, Across 250 Years, Before the War Ever Touched It

**The Great Corruption plausibly wasn't random, and plausibly connects directly to the Falkland Treaty's own confirmed coercion.** The established fact: a massive portion of Janbogo's pre-war archives were corrupted, wiping records of "the period immediately before the Falkland Treaty," and several data archaeologists suspect this was targeted rather than accidental. Worth naming what's actually in that specific window: the Falkland Treaty's own established genre is a victor's settlement dressed in manufactured legitimacy — the New Echota register, "narrated so that the treaty appears to be a genuine negotiation... regardless of how clean that mandate actually was." And, confirmed just yesterday: Maggie Aarden, the treaty's own robot signatory, was "invited" to "volunteer" — coerced, not chosen. If records of exactly how coercive those final pre-treaty negotiations really were ever existed, they're precisely the kind of material someone — Upper Earth, protecting the treaty's own legal legitimacy against a future coercion challenge, or early Tepenian leadership, protecting their own legitimacy as far-sighted negotiators rather than manipulated victims — would have genuine motive to destroy. This doesn't need to be resolved here, but it gives "targeted rather than random" real narrative teeth instead of an unexplained mystery floating free of the rest of the project's own established history.

**The Twin Channels as a generational echo of the treaty's own duplicity, not just a practical information-management adaptation.** Gemini's established defining trait — "everything in Janbogo has a public version and a real version" — reads differently once you notice the founding document itself already had exactly that structure: an official narrative designed to look like legitimate negotiation, and a coerced reality underneath it. Over 250 years, a community founded by a document with an official version and a real version plausibly built an entire civic information culture around maintaining exactly that same duality, without necessarily connecting the two consciously. Gemini's whole culture may be, structurally, what happens when a founding population's descendants keep practicing the one skill their origin document actually taught them: how to hold an official story and a real one at the same time.

## Stage 2 — How the War's Fallout Landed on That Already-Mature, 250-Year-Old Duality

**The Long Night War's Information Warfare phase didn't invent Janbogo's dual-channel culture — it gave a 250-year-old structural habit its highest-stakes possible use.** Propaganda, encrypted orders, real-time crisis broadcasting: all of it depends on exactly the official/shadow duality Stage 1 proposes Gemini had already been practicing since its own founding. The war didn't create the Twin Channels. It's the moment the district's oldest, quietest skill got deployed at national survival stakes.

## New narrative thread — Gemini's data archaeologists are the natural mechanism connecting the Cradle-Keepers to Concordia

Threads 7 and 8 (Capricorn's robot-rights movement and Virgo's own invisible-labor wound, both potentially recognizing a national pattern via Neumayer/Byrd/Sinheung) each currently need some unspecified way for that information to actually reach them. Gemini is the obvious, already-established answer: it's the city's real data-archaeology hub, staffed by people whose established profession is recovering and cross-referencing exactly this kind of buried, cross-city pattern. A Gemini data archaeologist — quite possibly one already working the Great Corruption's own mystery — is a natural, earned character to be the one who first connects Capricorn's Capricorn's core injustice [mechanism TBD — 4 contenders shortlisted, see `06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`] to the wider Cradle-Keepers pattern, giving Gemini genuine narrative load-bearing function that reaches beyond its own self-contained conflicts.
