# Pisces/Libra — The Tolerance Pact: Confirmed Resolution

**Purpose:** resolves the item flagged in `District_Canon_Reference.md` on 2026-07-11 — the developer wasn't sure what the Tolerance Pact actually was, or whether it stayed in canon at all, independent of the non-malice question (which it had already passed cleanly on its own terms: mutually beneficial, consensual, nobody exploited). This file supplies the resolution, derived directly from Pisces' own already-confirmed Black Market Origin rather than from a fresh set of candidates.

**Status: CONFIRMED and PROMOTED, 2026-07-29.** `District_Canon_Reference.md`'s Tolerance Pact bullet already carries the full reframed prose. Downstream consistency fixes also made to `10_Pisces_Deep_Dive.md`, which had grouped the Pact with Gemini's Twin Channels and Libra's Suspended Compact as the same kind of deliberate institutional consensus — it isn't, and has been removed from that comparison.

## The original uncertainty, restated precisely

The Tolerance Pact, as originally written, is a *deliberate, negotiated quid-pro-quo*: "Libra doesn't seriously crack down on Pisces operations; Pisces provides certain services for Libra operatives... Both parties benefit. Both parties would deny it." That's two institutions consciously agreeing to look the other way — an active arrangement, not structural inertia. It's the exact same shape every other item resolved this session started out with (a conscious deal between parties) before being reworked into non-malicious structural inertia instead. The developer's own uncertainty about whether this "stays in canon" reads, in hindsight, as uncertainty about whether Pisces' history actually supports a *deliberate* arrangement at all — a question that couldn't really be answered until Pisces' own Black Market Origin was itself confirmed.

## Why nBeta already answers it

Pisces' Black Market Origin, confirmed as "nBeta" (`10c_Pisces_Black_Market_Origin.md`), already establishes almost everything the Tolerance Pact was trying to describe, without requiring anyone to negotiate anything:

- Libra never built real enforcement capacity toward Pisces in the first place — a founding-era policing gap, never revisited, a surviving provision of the Continuity and Stability Act.
- By the post-war period, already-established canon (the Post-War Institutionalization of the Gray Economy bullet) states outright that "repeated Libra crackdowns failed because too many other districts secretly relied on its services."

There's no room left in that picture for an actual negotiated pact — the outcome the Pact was describing (Libra tolerating Pisces, both sides benefiting, neither admitting it) is already fully accounted for as an emergent equilibrium. Nobody needed to strike a deal for this to be true; it was already going to be true given nBeta's own mechanism.

## The confirmed resolution

**The Tolerance Pact stays in canon — name unchanged, services unchanged — with one change: it is not, and was never, a negotiated arrangement between two institutions.** It is what individual Libra operatives, personally and informally, quietly arrived at over decades of ordinary self-interest, the same way ordinary residents of every other district quietly do. Nobody at Libra ever sat down and decided this. "Both parties would deny it" no longer means "we have a secret and we're protecting each other" — it means there was never anything to admit to in the first place, because nothing was ever agreed to.

**Why the Pact's own established duration locks this in precisely, rather than needing to be adjusted:** the Pact is dated to roughly sixty years before the present day (≈2822–2827), placing its actual origin around 2762–2767 — squarely inside the Second Interwar Period, decades *before* the Long Night War. This is exactly where nBeta's own Section C places Pisces as "a genuine, contained gray economy" — sympathetic, low-stakes, not yet the syndicate-run shadow economy the war-era text describes. The Tolerance Pact isn't a separate arrangement bolted onto Pisces' history after the fact. It's the specific, individual, personal-scale thread running underneath the exact same fabric nBeta already confirmed: a few Libra operatives quietly relying on Pisces' modest pre-war gray-market contacts, a relationship that simply continues and deepens — never renegotiated, never needing to be — once the war hardens the whole district's economy sixty-some years later. It is the missing personal-scale seed nBeta's citywide mechanism grows from, not an addition to it.

## What this changes about the actual text

- **"An arrangement between Libra and Pisces"** → reframed as something that was never arranged at all — an equilibrium that emerged, not a deal that was struck.
- **"Libra doesn't seriously crack down on Pisces operations"** → not a policy choice by Libra as an institution, but the natural, continuing consequence of the enforcement gap it never closed.
- **"Pisces provides certain services for Libra operatives — untraceable goods, information that cannot be officially acquired, political favors at arms length"** → unchanged. These specific services remain entirely plausible; only the reason they continue changes.
- **"Both parties benefit. Both parties would deny it."** → unchanged in substance, reframed in cause: there's nothing to deny in the sense of covering up a scheme — there was simply never a scheme to begin with.

## What doesn't need to change

- **The Tolerance Pact Liaison Office** (`District_Canon_Reference.md`'s own Community Infrastructure additions, an unofficial point of contact managing the unwritten arrangement) still works exactly as written — an informal liaison point can just as easily emerge to manage a decades-old, never-negotiated equilibrium as it could a deliberate pact. No edit needed there.
- **The Pact's sixty-year duration, its specific services, and its total absence from any written record** — all already consistent with the new framing and require no adjustment.
