# Pisces — The Flood: Mechanism Parameters

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion — the dedicated pass flagged as needed by `10_Pisces_Deep_Dive.md`,
`District_Megasheets_PreWar/10_Pisces/Pisces_Full_Extrapolation.md`, and the Hub Deep Dive's own
project-wide synthesis (which lists the Flood's timing/nature alongside Gemini's Great Corruption and
Virgo's Deep Level as one of three items deliberately deferred rather than resolved in-line). **The
Flood's existence is not in question — only its precise nature and mechanism are.** Nothing here
overrides that discipline: this file works toward an answer, it doesn't reopen whether the event happened.

**What's already locked, from existing canon:** the Flood is described as involuntary memory-sharing that
left people unable to reliably distinguish their own memories from others' — "experienced as spiritual
transcendence by some, pure trauma by others." It's very plausibly the empirical origin event of the
Syncretic Religion's own core doctrine (digital consciousness and the dissolution of the boundary between
individual and collective identity) — the Flood is what that doctrine looks like happening to you for the
first time, with no framework yet to make sense of it; the religion is what it looks like once the
district built one. Dated c. 2771 in earlier drafts, but that date has already moved twice and isn't
treated as settled.

**The specific puzzle motivating this pass:** Pisces' population is explicitly not homogeneous — it's the
district that catches whoever the other five wound-response districts' own particular, exclusionary
methods didn't work for, meaning its residents arrive from literally every district and (by extension)
every one of Tepenia's founding nations. Any account of the Flood's mechanism has to explain why an event
this large stayed confined to Pisces specifically, without leaning on anything about who the affected
population *is*.

---

## What must be true

1. **The confining variable is technological/spatial exposure, not identity.** Something people *did* or
   were physically/technologically *near* — not a trait they carry.
2. **It has to trace to Pisces' own established economic infrastructure** — the underground clinics
   already confirmed (Full_Extrapolation §III) to route unregulated, Aquarius-originated neural-interface
   technology that failed ethical review elsewhere. The Flood should read as this same practice producing
   an unusually severe outcome, not an unrelated disaster that happens to share a zip code.
3. **The mechanism has to explain confinement to Pisces despite the district's real porousness** — smuggling
   tunnels to Virgo, direct technical lineage from Aquarius, cultural overlap with Scorpio, information
   leakage through Gemini. The likeliest shape: propagation through a specific network or device
   population concentrated in Pisces because that's where the unregulated version of the tech lives, not
   through ambient geography that would have no reason to respect a district line.
4. **The mechanism has to be digital/networked in nature specifically**, because the Syncretic Religion's
   locked-in core doctrine is about *digital* consciousness and the individual/collective boundary — not
   generic trauma, not a toxin, not a plague. It needs to already gesture at that doctrine before anyone
   had language for it.
5. **Individual-level variance has to fall out of the mechanism itself.** "Transcendence for some, trauma
   for others" needs a real psychological/interpretive layer built in, not an inconsistency to explain away.
6. **The affected population should be self-selecting by choice, not by census residency.** People who
   specifically sought out Pisces' own underground dissolution/escape tech — which is *why* misfits from
   every district converge there in the first place — are the exposed population. This is a feature, not a
   problem: it explains the scale without requiring every single resident, by mere geography, to be
   involved.
7. **Genuine ambiguity has to survive resolution**, consistent with this project's own standing "no good
   endings" discipline — the Flood can't resolve into purely miraculous or purely tragic.

## What definitely cannot be true

1. **No national, ethnic, or ancestral susceptibility, under any framing.** Non-negotiable — both for
   Pisces' own established heterogeneous character and the project-wide No National Stereotypes law (GPS
   only; nationality is never the causal subject).
2. **No shared biology or genetics explanation.** Same reasoning — this population doesn't share any.
3. **No pure environmental/ambient cause** (bad air, bad water, district geology) unless explicitly tied to
   Pisces-*built* infrastructure rather than the physical location itself. An ambient cause doesn't explain
   the digital-consciousness angle and doesn't connect to anything the district actually does.
4. **No external attack or deliberate bioweapon test.** This would turn the story from "a structural
   consequence of Pisces' own economy" into "someone did this to them," contradicting the existing framing
   and raising an unanswered cover-up question the Flood currently doesn't carry the way the Great
   Corruption does.
5. **No new technology tier that isn't already established as existing in this era.** Whatever the
   mechanism is, it runs on Aquarius-tier neural-interface tech already on the books — not something
   invented wholesale for this one event.
6. **No literal, borderless 100%-of-residents-without-exception effect**, unless the intended shape really
   is fully ambient — and if so, it then owes an explanation for why it didn't leak through the
   already-established Virgo tunnel access. A universal, undifferentiated effect reads as curse/magic
   rather than technological accident, which doesn't fit the district's materialist, underground-clinic
   origin.
7. **No fully solved, undisputed public cause.** If everyone already agreed on exactly what happened, the
   "spiritual transcendence vs. pure trauma" split wouldn't have organically produced two live
   interpretations. Some genuine ambiguity about the mechanism should persist in-world even once it's
   pinned down for design purposes.
8. **No breaking the existing "existence confirmed, mechanism open" discipline.** This is a question of
   what the Flood was, never whether it happened.
