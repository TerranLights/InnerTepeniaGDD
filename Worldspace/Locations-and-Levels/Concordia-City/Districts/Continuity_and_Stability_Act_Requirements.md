# The Continuity and Stability Act — Requirements Before Drafting

**What this is:** guardrails, not the document itself. The Continuity and Stability Act was first proposed in `Cross_District_Power_Leverage_Alternatives.md`'s "Big Interconnection Possibility" as a single founding-era piece of emergency legislation that several districts' quiet-power mechanisms could all trace back to, rather than being unrelated coincidences that merely rhyme. It has since picked up confirmed, explicit provisions in real district writeups. This file records the structural format and the "must be true" / "cannot be true" stipulations to satisfy *before* the actual in-world document gets drafted — the same discipline `Deep_Dives/10b_Pisces_Flood_Mechanism.md` used for the Flood before its own mechanism was chosen.

**Status: guardrails confirmed, 2026-07-29. The actual document text has not yet been drafted.**

## Structural format

A genuine in-world legal document from a provisional emergency body, not a single named author: something like *"Emergency Continuity and Stability Act, [ratifying body], late 2560s"* (working title, not final). Format: a short preamble stating the crisis it responds to, followed by a small number of broad, general-purpose Articles — not one clause per district — each granting a category of sweeping operational latitude (resource allocation, record-keeping, safety authority, enforcement discretion) to "any institution the city depends on for continuity of function," never naming Aries, Libra, Cancer, or Pisces specifically. Each district's own confirmed mechanism is that district's own institution independently invoking one Article, in its own bureaucratic language, without ever cross-referencing how anyone else used it.

**Critically: no single complete copy of the Act should exist anywhere as one discoverable object.** The entire design point — nobody has ever seen the whole picture — only holds if the "full text" is something a player or in-world investigator has to reconstruct from scattered partial citations across multiple districts' own paperwork, starting with Pisces' Triage Directive (`District_Canon_Reference.md`'s Libra Treaty Archive Vaults entry). A single findable master copy would collapse the mystery in one pickup.

---

## What must be true

1. **Passed during genuine, undisputed founding-era crisis** — the earliest exile years, before Concordia's district structure had even fully solidified — matching the "reasonable decision under real pressure" shape every other confirmed mechanism this session shares.
2. **Broad and general by construction**, granting sweeping latitude to "essential institutions" as a category, never naming any specific district — this is what allows multiple unrelated-seeming mechanisms to trace back to it independently.
3. **Never formally repealed, for structural reasons only** — no clean end-point to the crisis it addressed, never a decision anyone actively made to keep it in force.
4. **Never fully assembled or seen whole by anyone, ever** — each district inherited and invoked its own piece independently, with no cross-referencing between them. The single most load-bearing constraint, since it's the entire reason the Act is worth having at all.
5. **Purely additive to what's already confirmed** — every district's own mechanism (Aries' review, Libra's clause, Cancer's formula, Pisces' gap) stays exactly as already written; the Act explains *why* the authority existed, it doesn't get to rewrite any mechanism to fit itself.
6. **Genuinely fragment-discoverable** — compatible with Pisces' Triage Directive as the first piece, meaning other districts' own paperwork should plausibly carry their own partial citations too, not the full text.
7. **Attributed to a body, not a person** — a provisional council or emergency committee, never one named individual, so no single figure's intent or motive becomes something to litigate.

## What cannot be true

1. **Cannot have targeted or been authored with any specific district in mind**, as beneficiary or target — breaks the non-malice shape confirmed across every district this session if it did.
2. **Cannot be something anyone today actively invokes as live justification.** Nobody currently says "per the Act, I'm entitled to..." out loud — the moment it becomes a currently-wielded tool, it turns back into the active leverage this whole audit spent the session removing.
3. **Cannot retroactively require rewriting any already-confirmed district mechanism** — Aries, Libra, Cancer, and Pisces stay exactly as written; this is connective tissue, not a revision trigger.
4. **Cannot be dated later than, or after, the founding-era decisions it's meant to explain** — must predate or sit alongside the 2560s–2590s founding window already established for the districts that hold confirmed provisions.
5. **Cannot have ever been formally reviewed and knowingly reaffirmed** — "never revisited" has to stay literally true, not "revisited once and kept on purpose," which would shift it toward institutional culpability.
6. **Cannot explain every district's quiet power with total certainty.** By design, some connections (Aries' underlying grid fragility, Libra's other three tributaries) were deliberately left *outside* the Act — a document that explains literally everything stops feeling like a found object and starts feeling like the designer's hand. That discipline has to survive into the actual text.
7. **Cannot silently assume Capricorn or Leo are settled provisions** — given the inconsistency flagged above, the document must not be drafted as if that question is already decided.

## Does District Under-Questline generation need to wait for this? No — confirmed 2026-07-29

Checked directly against `Storyline/Side-Content/District_Under_Questline_Design_Method.md`'s own tests. Reasons this doesn't block:

- **The method never narrows candidates down** — every passing candidate is kept as real content, so nothing generated now would ever need to be discarded once the Act's own Articles are drafted later. New Act-aware candidates can simply be added on top.
- **Most districts have no dependency on the Act at all.** Only Aries, Libra, Cancer, and Pisces hold confirmed provisions, plus the still-undecided Capricorn/Leo question — the other seven-to-nine districts can run the full method with zero relationship to any of this.
- **Even for the four (or six) districts that do connect to it, this requirements file already supplies enough to satisfy the method's own "non-conflicting" test.** A candidate doesn't need the final Article text to reference "an old piece of forgotten founding-era legislation, exact wording TBD" — it only needs to not contradict what's already locked here (no single findable master copy, never actively invoked today, attributed to a body not a person, etc.).
- **There's a real synergy running the other direction.** Generating Under-Questline candidates for Capricorn and Leo specifically might surface a natural answer to whether either belongs in the Act — the same way this session's own accumulated precedent ended up informing which Flood mechanism candidate fit best. Worth treating that as a possible source of evidence, not just a blocker to clear first.

**One live caution, not a blocker:** any candidate that treats the Act as something with one complete, findable master copy violates requirement 4 above and should be reworked, not used as written.
