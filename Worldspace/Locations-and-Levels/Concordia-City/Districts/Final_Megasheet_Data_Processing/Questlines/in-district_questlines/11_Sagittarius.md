# Sagittarius — Questlines

**What this is:** each of Sagittarius' own Cross-Reference Synthesis findings supplies a 4th-order effect; that effect becomes this document's new 1st-order cause, from which new 2nd-, 3rd-, and 4th-order effects are derived, explicitly framed toward questline potential. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Questline Thread 1 — from Finding 1 (three districts, three pieces of the same unresolved puzzle)

**1st-order cause (= Finding 1's 4th-order effect):** Cancer, Sagittarius, and Gemini each hold one piece of the same unresolved puzzle — Sagittarius the need, Cancer unknowingly the answer, Gemini the institutional capability to connect them — with none of the three currently aware all three pieces exist.

**New 2nd-order effect:** the player, having gathered pieces of this picture across multiple districts, realizes they personally are the missing link the deep dive itself identified — the only party currently positioned to connect Sagittarius' need, Cancer's unknowing answer, and Gemini's own capability.

**New 3rd-order effect:** this opens a fork: actively broker the full connection, bringing a Sagittarius Memory Keeper, a Cancer resident, and a Gemini data archaeologist together directly; handle the information relay personally instead, protecting the Cancer resident's privacy at the cost of a less complete outcome; or decide the knowledge isn't worth disturbing anyone's life to recover, and let it stay lost.

**New 4th-order effect:** full brokering could recover genuinely lost history and validate the Memory Keepers' entire mission, but requires navigating three districts' own sensitivities at once — Cancer's non-interrogative ethos, the resident's own trauma, and Sagittarius' internal Expansionist/Consolidationist politics over what the recovered knowledge would actually mean; personal relay is safer for everyone involved but produces incomplete, secondhand information Sagittarius may not be able to fully use; leaving it lost respects everyone's autonomy but permanently forecloses the one mission the Memory Keepers have organized their whole faction around.

## Questline Thread 2 — from Finding 2 (the least likely of seven instances to ever self-correct)

**1st-order cause (= Finding 2's 4th-order effect):** Sagittarius is structurally the least likely of all seven confirmed "war over-credited as originator" instances to ever correct its own record, since even its own Memory Keepers may no longer hold the specific knowledge needed to contest the wartime narrative.

**New 2nd-order effect:** a specific, aging Memory Keeper — a strong fit for the figure already proposed in Sagittarius' own Full Extrapolation — realizes they personally may be one of the last living links to the caravan network's true pre-war origins, and enlists the player's help before that knowledge is gone entirely.

**New 3rd-order effect:** this opens a fork: help the Memory Keeper create a lasting, physical record, a genuine break from Sagittarius' own oral tradition; help them find and train a successor to carry the knowledge forward the traditional way, preserving cultural continuity but not the underlying fragility; or accept that some knowledge will be lost regardless, and focus the Memory Keeper's remaining effort on preserving only the most critical, truly irreplaceable pieces.

**New 4th-order effect:** a durable record could permanently solve Sagittarius' preservation crisis, but represents a real cultural break the district's own established values may actively resist; training a successor preserves the oral tradition's own character but doesn't resolve the underlying fragility, only delays its recurrence one more generation; triaging to critical-only pieces is the most realistic path, but requires accepting genuine, permanent loss no matter what the player chooses.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Thread 1 is the more hopeful of the two — it's the only path in this document with a genuine chance at full recovery rather than managed, partial loss — but Thread 2 is the more urgent, since it's explicitly running against a living person's own remaining time, not an abstract institutional problem.
