# Cross-District Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors that pass through multiple districts — not things districts have in common (see Patterns for that), but chains showing how a fact in one district leads through another to a consequence in a third, or further. Drawn from all 13 completed District Megasheets, their in-district Throughways files, and `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md`'s own established ripple-effect chains. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Throughway 1: Sagittarius → Cancer → Gemini (the lost-knowledge recovery chain)

**Sagittarius' oral-only knowledge culture puts irreplaceable frontier knowledge (quarry locations, the Third Expansion Collapse's exact cause) at genuine risk as its founding generation ages** (Sagittarius deep dive) → **Sagittarius sends its own wounded and broken settlers to Cancer as an established, ongoing refugee flow** (`District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`) → **those residents are exactly the population most likely to have witnessed Sagittarius' own knowledge-critical events firsthand, but Cancer's caregiving model has no mechanism, and no ethical basis, for debriefing traumatized arrivals for institutional value** (Cancer Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **meaning the knowledge isn't gone, only misplaced, sitting unmarked inside Cancer's own resident population** → **recoverable only through Gemini's own established data-recovery mission, which independently proposed a senior data archaeologist as the natural figure to make exactly this connection** (Gemini Full Extrapolation V).

This three-district chain — need (Sagittarius), unrecognized possession (Cancer), and institutional capability (Gemini) — is complete and already sitting in the project's own material, missing only the moment someone in-fiction actually connects the three pieces.

## Throughway 2: Aquarius → Pisces → Virgo (the Flood's undocumented physical echo)

**Aquarius' experimental culture produces "repeated experimental spills" as an ordinary cost of its own defining risk-taking** (Virgo Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **one such experiment, a malfunctioning neural interface mod, was routed through Pisces' underground clinics, causing the Flood — the involuntary memory-sharing event that would become the empirical origin of Pisces' entire Syncretic Religion** (Pisces Mega-Init, Hard Facts; deep dive) → **the same malfunction plausibly produced an undocumented physical echo elsewhere in the city's infrastructure at the same time — a leak, a containment breach — logged by Virgo crews as a routine incident, with no way of knowing what it was the opening minutes of** (Virgo Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **meaning Virgo's own decades-deep failure archive may already contain the earliest physical record of Pisces' founding-era religious event, connectable only by someone cross-referencing Virgo's incident logs against Pisces' own oral history of the Flood's timeline.**

This chain runs from one district's defining risk-taking, through a second district's defining religious tradition, into a third district's defining institutional habit — three completely different domains (science, faith, maintenance) turning out to share one unexamined origin point.

## Throughway 3: Sagittarius → Capricorn → Libra (extraction funds the leverage that buries an audit)

**Sagittarius absorbs all the volatility of frontier resource extraction — the Siligel Rush's boom, then its bust, then the stranded political power** (Sagittarius Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **Capricorn, per two independently-written deep dives converging on the same claim, is the most likely district refining those raw materials into finished siligel, capturing the stable, ongoing value Sagittarius' own volatile risk makes possible** (Capricorn Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **that same manufacturing dominance is very plausibly the source of the economic leverage Capricorn used over Libra to keep The Narrow Door's damaging findings buried for over sixty years past discovery** (Capricorn Cross-Reference Finding 1, "Synthesis") → **meaning Sagittarius' own uncredited extraction risk indirectly funds the specific leverage that lets Capricorn avoid accountability for a completely unrelated internal failure, three districts and two different kinds of injustice connected by one continuous flow of value.**

## Throughway 4: Cancer / Taurus / Leo → Aries → Virgo (the recognition cascade)

**Cancer, Taurus, and Leo all depend on Aries' power while criticizing its methods, and Aries resents this as ingratitude for genuinely indispensable, dangerous labor** (`District_Canon_Reference.md`, cited in Aries Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **Aries' own culture simultaneously treats Virgo's maintenance labor — no less indispensable, no less dangerous — as "beneath notice," the identical treatment Aries itself resents** (`District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`, cited in Aries Cross-Reference Finding 2; Virgo Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **this habit of discounting Virgo's proven value is the direct behavioral cause of why Aries' own leverage (the blackout threat) needs periodic re-demonstration, unlike Virgo's Year of Silence, which was proven once and never repeated** (Virgo Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **producing a four-district cascade where the exact same under-appreciation, entering the system through Cancer, Taurus, and Leo's treatment of Aries, passes straight through Aries and lands, unexamined, on Virgo.**

This is the clearest available illustration in the whole project of recognition-scarcity as a genuinely structural, city-wide condition rather than three separate districts each having an isolated grievance.

## Throughway 5: Gemini → Leo → Aries / Cancer (amplification destabilizing an already-fragile recognition economy)

**Gemini's "audience-building" relationship with Leo is framed by both districts as stabilizing, but Gemini can destabilize Leo's own internal hierarchy by amplifying rival "new spotlight" figures against established "old glow" ones** (Gemini Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **this makes Gemini a third compromised relationship feeding Leo's recognition economy, alongside Leo's own already-tangled relationships with Aries and Cancer, both of which deliver recognition bundled with criticism of "frivolity"** (Leo Cross-Reference Finding 1, cited in Gemini Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **meaning the specific validation Leo receives from Aries and Cancer is undermined not only by those two relationships' own built-in criticism, but by a third district's amplification effect capable of destabilizing whichever performers Aries and Cancer might otherwise be inclined to recognize** → **leaving Virgo — a district with no current relationship to Leo's recognition economy at all — as the sole potential source of clean validation across all of Leo's major connections** (Leo Cross-Reference Finding 2).

## Throughway 6: The Falkland Treaty's duplicity → three separate branches (Libra, Gemini, Pisces)

**The Falkland Treaty's own official narrative and coerced reality, with Maggie Aarden's coerced-but-outwardly-voluntary signature at its center, is Tepenia's single deepest historical wound** (project canon) → **that one root duplicity branches into three separate districts' own founding structures, each independently arrived at rather than copied from the others:**
- **Libra's** founding logic conflated "protecting the process" with "protecting the community" because, for its first crucial decades, the two were the treaty's own reality — genuinely identical (Libra Mega-Init, "Worth Your Attention").
- **Gemini's** Twin Channels are a direct 250-year generational inheritance of the treaty's own official-narrative-versus-coerced-reality split (Gemini Mega-Init, Hard Facts).
- **Pisces'** Tolerance Pact runs on the identical "everyone knows, nobody officially acknowledges" logic, sixty years old and unwritten (Pisces deep dive, cited in Gemini Full Extrapolation II).

**All three branches predate the war**, and all three represent the same underlying institutional inheritance, expressed in three completely different domains (governance, information, vice) without any of the three districts' own material drawing the connection back to its shared root explicitly.

## Throughway 7: All Thirteen Districts → the Hub (the pass's own closing discovery)

**Twelve districts, over the course of this Megasheet pass, were each sorted into at least one of five recurring founding-era categories** (wound-response, non-wound-response Stage 1, sink district, two-version duality, or the Hub's own keystone position) → **the Hub's own Cross-Reference Synthesis, written last, is uniquely positioned to compare the completed set against itself, since its own founding function is neutral observation rather than any one specific role** (Hub Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **that comparison reveals Leo alone fits none of the recurring categories the rest of the pass identified** (Hub Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **and separately reveals the Hub itself as the logical limit case of the "proof without payoff" pattern found in Pisces, Sagittarius, and Virgo, rather than a simple fourth instance of it** (Hub Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **meaning the entire thirteen-district pass produces, as its very last piece, two genuinely new observations about itself that no single district's own file, read in isolation, could ever have surfaced.**

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 4 and Throughway 7 are the two most significant chains in this file, for opposite reasons. Throughway 4 shows how a single, ordinary-seeming social failure (not thanking your maintenance crew) actually connects four separate districts into one continuous cascade of unexamined ingratitude. Throughway 7 shows the entire thirteen-district analytical project turning back on itself at the very end to discover two things — Leo's own uncategorizable status, and the Hub's own unique structural position — that only existed because all twelve other districts had already been mapped first.
