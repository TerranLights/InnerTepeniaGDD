# Sagittarius — Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors within Sagittarius' own material — chains showing how one fact leads through another to a third. Drawn from `District_Megasheets/11_Sagittarius/README.md` and its source files. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Throughway 1: From opportunity-seeking to a self-inflicted wound and a still-unresolved internal tension

**Sagittarius exists because crowded domes will always produce a population that wants opportunity and space more than safety, with philosophy emerging as a side effect of the stakes, not trauma-processing** (One-Line Pitch) → **that same expansion-driven ethos, pursued exactly as designed, produced the Third Expansion Collapse — a structural miscalculation and an unexpected weather event that stranded hundreds of workers for eleven days, killing sixty-three** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **the survivors' honest reckoning with what expansion costs now sits in permanent tension with the district's own dominant "push further" ethos, embodied in the ongoing Expansionist/Consolidationist divide** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts; `District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`) → **and the Memory Keeper faction exists specifically because neither side of that divide, on its own, addresses the loss of the irreplaceable oral knowledge both events actually depend on preserving** (`District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`, cited in Mega-Init).

## Throughway 2: From two uncredited proofs to a district structurally unable to correct its own record

**The Siligel Rush (beginning c. 2775) gave Sagittarius genuine political and economic power it had never previously held, then depleted, leaving the boom infrastructure without the power** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **decades later, the district's caravan network proved equally indispensable during the war's frontier defense, and was credited neither time** (Mega-Init, "Worth Your Attention") → **this makes Sagittarius the seventh confirmed instance of the cross-district "war over-credited as originator" pattern, and the only instance reinforced from two directions simultaneously** (Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **because the district's own oral-knowledge culture is structurally poor at retaining exactly the kind of pre-war institutional history (who actually built the caravan routes, and why) needed to contest the easier wartime story** (Cross-Reference Finding 2, 3rd-order effect) → **leaving Sagittarius the single least likely of all seven instances to ever correct its own record, since even its own Memory Keepers may no longer hold the specific knowledge required to do so** (Cross-Reference Finding 2, 4th-order effect).

## Throughway 3: From an established refugee flow to an unrealized recovery chain

**Sagittarius sends its wounded and broken frontier settlers to Cancer as an established, ongoing refugee flow** (`District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`, cited in Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **those same residents are very plausibly the population most likely to have witnessed Sagittarius' own defining, knowledge-critical events firsthand, including possibly the Third Expansion Collapse itself** (Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **but Cancer's own caregiving model has no mechanism, and no ethical basis, for debriefing traumatized arrivals for institutional value** (Cancer's own Cross-Reference Synthesis, cited in Sagittarius Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **meaning Sagittarius' own oral-tradition preservation crisis may not be a closed loss at all, but a misplaced one — recoverable only through a currently unrealized three-district chain (Sagittarius/Cancer/Gemini), none of the three aware that all three pieces of the puzzle exist** (Cross-Reference Finding 1, 4th-order effect).

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 3 is the most consequential of the three — unlike Throughway 2, which describes a loss with no clear path to correction, Throughway 3 identifies a specific, already-existing (if currently unrealized) mechanism by which Sagittarius' own preservation crisis could genuinely be resolved, simply by connecting institutions that already exist but have never been placed in the same conversation.
