# Cancer — Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors within Cancer's own material — not things Cancer has in common with itself (see Patterns for that), but chains showing how one fact leads through another to a third. Drawn from `District_Megasheets/01_Cancer/README.md` and its source files. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Throughway 1: From founding population to Libra's dependent legitimacy

**The original exile's traumatized founding population** (late 2560s) → **led Cancer to be designated the primary sanctuary and integration zone** from the district's very first construction phase (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **which, over 250 years, produced a genuine care-debt economy** running on favors and time rather than credits, explicitly resistant to formal taxation or regulation (Mega-Init; Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **which Libra cannot govern, yet depends on** — Cancer's visible suffering is the "face" that makes Libra's abstract fairness feel concrete, meaning Libra is structurally disincentivized from ever fully resolving Cancer's crises (Cross-Reference Finding 1).

This chain runs from a 250-year-old founding fact to a live, present-day political dependency that neither district's own material states was ever the intended outcome.

---

## Throughway 2: From informal information culture to an unnoticed internal power shift

**Cancer's founding population arrived with nothing and no established bureaucracy** → **which produced a lasting district-wide preference for informal, relational, unwritten systems** rather than formal record-keeping (the care-debt economy's own "Green Ledger," never actually written down, per Full Extrapolation I) → **which is the same cultural condition that allowed the Mother's Circuit to cross from ordinary community monitoring into "something approaching a surveillance network" without anyone marking when the line was crossed** (Cross-Reference Finding 3) → **which means the Surveillance Faction's ideology has already become the district's real operating system, undetected by the district's own internal factional language, which still treats all four factions as co-equal** (Cross-Reference Finding 3).

The same informal-by-culture instinct that makes Cancer's care feel genuine and unbureaucratic is also, traced through this chain, the specific mechanism that let one internal faction's infrastructure quietly become load-bearing before anyone noticed.

---

## Throughway 3: From non-interrogative care to permanently unmarked knowledge

**Sagittarius sends Cancer its wounded and broken frontier settlers as a continuous, established refugee flow** (`District_Unity_of_Opposites.md`, cited in Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **Cancer's caregiving model has no mechanism for debriefing or interrogating traumatized new arrivals for institutional value, "because doing so would run against the district's entire ethos"** (Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **which means whatever irreplaceable frontier knowledge those residents carry (per Sagittarius' own oral-culture attrition) goes unasked-for and unmarked, indistinguishable from ordinary biographical detail** (Cross-Reference Finding 2, 4th-order effect) → **which could, in principle, still be recovered — but only if Gemini's own established data-recovery mission (and specifically a data archaeologist, per Gemini's own Full Extrapolation) ever thought to look for it inside Cancer specifically**, a connection no existing institution in either district currently has a reason to make.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 3 is the most consequential of the three because it's the only one with a clear, if currently unrealized, resolution already sitting in the material — Sagittarius' own preservation crisis (see Sagittarius' Throughways) and Cancer's own unmarked knowledge are two ends of the same chain, missing only the middle step (someone from Gemini actually going looking).
