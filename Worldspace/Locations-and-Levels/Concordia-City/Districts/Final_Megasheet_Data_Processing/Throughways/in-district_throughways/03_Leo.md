# Leo — Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors within Leo's own material — chains showing how one fact leads through another to a third. Drawn from `District_Megasheets/03_Leo/README.md` and its source files. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Throughway 1: From a legal gap to a district-wide unresolved anxiety

**Robots' legal personhood was settled in 2318, but lived recognition of that personhood was never fully settled** (deep dive, cited in Mega-Init "Worth Your Attention") → **Leo was founded specifically as the place both humans and robots prove "humanity" through undeniable, embodied performance**, giving the district's whole recognition culture a genuine philosophical stake beneath the glamour (Mega-Init, "Who Lives Here, and Why") → **this produced the founding "no audience/performer distinction," later mastered as a deliberate craft skill rather than left as an accident** (Full Extrapolation I) → **which feeds directly into the district's own unresolved core question: whether two centuries of performing joy for each other produced genuine joy, or a population that's forgotten there was ever a difference** (One-Line Pitch).

---

## Throughway 2: From a patron-house rivalry to today's physical and political geography

**The Star War (mid-2740s) began as a nominally aesthetic dispute — grand spectacle versus intimate form — that was functionally political** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **it resolved roughly eighty years before the present day, and the winning house still holds power today** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **that same decades-old outcome plausibly still shapes the Golden Ring's own physical texture, with Grand Performance areas and Intimate Underground areas reading as genuinely different registers within the district's overall bright/loud atmosphere** (Full Extrapolation II) → **meaning a rivalry that ended generations ago continues to organize both Leo's internal politics and its literal, walkable geography without needing to be actively re-fought.**

---

## Throughway 3: From The Dimming's sacred memory to present-day power politics

**Aries cut Leo's power for six months during the 2789–90 energy crisis** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **the memory of that event became sacred to Leo specifically because it happened to a district whose entire founding mission depends on never letting its own energy — literal and figurative — drop** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts; Full Extrapolation) → **which explains the district's disproportionate, still-active ferocity about power priority today** (Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **layering directly onto the same relationship that already delivers Leo its most tangled recognition — Aries needs Leo's meaning-making, resents its cost, and once nearly extinguished it outright** (Cross-Reference Finding 1).

---

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 2 and Throughway 3 both show decades-old events still actively organizing Leo's present without requiring ongoing conflict to stay operative — but Throughway 3 is sharper, because it shows the exact same district (Aries) sitting at both ends of Leo's history: the one that nearly went dark on Leo once, and the one Leo depends on most heavily for meaning today.
