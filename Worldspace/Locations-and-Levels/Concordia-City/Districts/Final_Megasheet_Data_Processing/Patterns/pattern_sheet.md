## In-District Patterns (4 per district, 52 total)

### 01 — Cancer
1. Visible virtue, hidden cost — recurring at every scale
2. Significant thresholds pass without leaving a record
3. Care that refuses to be conditional, interrogative, or extractive
4. Order and comfort that emerged, rather than were designed

### 02 — Taurus
1. An identity built from ease, not hardship
2. Warmth strictly threshold-gated, never withheld out of hostility
3. Real fault lines kept quiet by cultural habit, not resolved
4. Load-bearing importance to others that Taurus itself doesn't recognize

### 03 — Leo
1. Deliberately blurred boundaries, treated as mastered craft rather than unresolved ambiguity
2. Recognition that is always tangled with need, criticism, or invisibility — never clean
3. Past crises still structure present daily life without needing to be re-litigated
4. The district's own founding question (performed joy vs. real joy) echoes at every other scale examined

### 04 — Scorpio
1. Confrontation as a mature, deliberate 250-year methodology, not a crisis improvisation
2. Access and privacy calibrated by depth and reputation, never by outright hostility
3. Institutional capacity strained by several genuinely distinct demand streams sharing one set of tools
4. Unresolved, ongoing states are tolerated rather than requiring closure

### 05 — Aries
1. Suppression through unceasing labor, and a specific aversion to silence
2. Two operating registers, both expressions of the same underlying philosophy
3. A gap between Aries' self-image and its actual, ongoing need for recognition
4. Aries holds grievances it doesn't recognize itself as equally guilty of

### 06 — Capricorn
1. Institutional failures get disputed and reframed, not corrected
2. Leverage wielded to avoid accountability, not merely to negotiate
3. Systems that compress social information into something only insiders can read
4. A meritocratic self-image resting on foundations considerably less earned than claimed

### 07 — Aquarius
1. Genuine good intentions, paired with a structural inability to see consequences from the receiving end
2. History worn openly and self-deprecatingly, rather than concealed
3. The same structural root cause recurring across three separate, decades-apart attempts at independence
4. The district's core instability threatens to undo its own best opportunities for redemption

### 08 — Libra
1. A founding-era conflation that was never updated, recurring wherever Libra has to let go of control
2. Legitimacy that quietly depends on uncredited contribution from more than one district
3. A single institutional face containing genuinely incompatible internal tones
4. Macro-level slowness built entirely out of micro-level urgency

### 09 — Gemini
1. Duality recurring at every scale — informational, personal, temporal, and physical
2. More information paired with worse judgment, not better
3. Things that read as merely procedural or professional turn out to carry much more weight
4. A rare case of a developer idea deliberately preserved open rather than immediately resolved

### 10 — Pisces
1. A "catching" function that recurs even within Pisces' own internal structure
2. Systems of meaning and enforcement that accrete informally over time, rather than being designed
3. Surface explanations that are simpler and more convenient than the deeper truth underneath
4. Recognition that is structurally impossible to receive without being destroyed by receiving it

### 11 — Sagittarius
1. Trauma that is self-inflicted, not inherited — a genuinely distinct founding category
2. Two coexisting speeds, both expressions of the same survival logic
3. Institutional proof given repeatedly, credited never
4. An oral-knowledge economy that loses information asymmetrically — gone at home, potentially preserved elsewhere

### 12 — Virgo
1. Proof given repeatedly, payoff received never
2. Meaningful practices deliberately kept from surface districts, "because they wouldn't understand"
3. Two operating speeds, both expressions of the same underlying readiness
4. Comprehensive documentation as both Virgo's primary defense and a repository whose full significance exceeds its own understanding

### 13 — Hub (Axis Mundi)
1. Identity built from absence and subtraction, not addition
2. Neutrality sustained only by permanently not resolving its own internal contradiction
3. The Hub is itself the fourth confirmed instance of this entire pass's own drafting bias
4. A uniquely reflexive position, only visible because the Hub comes last
