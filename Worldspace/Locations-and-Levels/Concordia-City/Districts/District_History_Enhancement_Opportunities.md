# District History — Enhancement Opportunities (Index)

**Purpose, method, and full input list:** see `District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Template.md` —
the official, validated spec for this pass.

**Split into one file per district, 2026-07-17,** once it became clear a single combined file (~60 lines
per district × 13 districts) would grow the same way the city pass's own tracker did before its subnet
split. This index holds only the purpose pointer and the progress checklist; per-district flagged ideas
live in `District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities/[NN]_[District].md`.

**Scope, applies to every district file:** all flagged ideas are Stage 1 only — the ~250-year Second
Interwar organic-formation period (2564–2812). No war fallout, no present-day material. **Flag only** — no
new Stage 1 narrative drafted, no texture woven into the actual Deep Dive or Megasheet files. This is a
punch list for a future pass.

## Notes for further elaboration

Tracked at the developer's own request — not part of the flagged-ideas punch list above, but a running
note of where source material was genuinely thin, or where a real creative decision is still pending, so
future direct-conversation sessions (Vision Notes-style) can target the right places. Swept district by
district, Cancer through Hub, by checking each district's own Mega_Init "What's Actually Open" list
against what its own Full_Extrapolation actually resolved. Three labels:

- **[DECISION PENDING]** — a developer-flagged, genuinely undecided design question. Needs the developer's
  own creative call before certain future work (actual chain drafting, etc.) can proceed cleanly.
- **[OPEN DETAIL]** — a smaller, specific unresolved fact the Full_Extrapolation left standing rather than
  answered — lower stakes than a full design decision, but still a real gap, not just an enhancement idea.
- **[THIN MATERIAL]** — nothing unresolved, but this pass found less fresh, unused territory than usual.
- **No entry** — every open item in the Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" list got a proposed answer
  in its own Full_Extrapolation. Nothing outstanding found.

- **Cancer** — no entry. All four of the Mega_Init's own open items (founding-era ethos wording, the
  ongoing-arrivals role's institutional name, the Cancer/Aries relationship's origin, a pre-war Mother's
  Circuit precedent) were resolved by the Full_Extrapolation.
- **Taurus** — no entry. All three open items (the Insulation Schism's founding-era severity, the pre-war
  Bonded Pair Registry's shape, the Taurus/Leo noise friction's origin) were resolved.
- **Leo** — [DECISION PENDING] The Star War (mid-2740s) itself: whether it happens at all in anything like
  its currently-documented form, which house (if any) wins, whether "scale vs. intimacy" ever hardens into
  named factions. The Dimming's own pre-war standing (dated 2789–90, technically inside this period) is
  left equally unsettled, consistent with treating all of Leo's uncertain-canon material the same way.
- **Scorpio** — no entry. All four open items (the First Fracture's trigger, a pre-war confession-
  preservation precedent, the Clinical/Sacred-Goth School split's timing, pre-war Scorpio/Libra friction)
  were resolved.
- **Aries** — [OPEN DETAIL] The Black Silence and the Overclock Martyrs remain deliberately unplaced in
  time, pending two open Staging threads (the Tower-grid connection, the Scorpio/Aries Black Silence link)
  that would need resolving first before either event could be dated with confidence.
- **Capricorn** — [DECISION PENDING] The single largest open item in the whole district set: which of
  seven documented possibilities (the Capricorn's core injustice [mechanism TBD — 4 contenders shortlisted, see `06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`] or the six `06b` alternatives) actually happens
  around c. 2761, or whether two of them merge. Every other Capricorn question is downstream of this one.
- **Aquarius** — [OPEN DETAIL] Two smaller items survive their own Full_Extrapolation: the exact count and
  rhythm of Contamination Events across two and a half centuries (already flagged as this district's own
  Lens 1 idea above, but also a genuine canon gap in its own right), and the Living Network Experiment's
  eventual fate — the Full_Extrapolation confirms it reads as promising within this document's own
  timeframe, but whether it actually collapses post-war, and why, is untouched by any pre-war document.
- **Libra** — [OPEN DETAIL] The Surveillance Files leak's own pre-war dating is explicitly left undated by
  both the post-war canon and this document — the only one of Libra's four open items with no proposed
  answer at all in the Full_Extrapolation.
- **Gemini** — [DECISION PENDING] The Great Corruption's own precise timing and mechanism (targeted vs.
  accidental) are explicitly deferred to "its own dedicated pass" by the Deep Dive itself, not resolved
  anywhere in the pre-war material.
- **Pisces** — [DECISION PENDING, two items] The Tolerance Pact (whether/how it formalizes with Libra) and
  the Flood (the Syncretic Religion's plausible origin event) are both genuinely open, same weight as
  Capricorn's own unresolved item.
- **Sagittarius** — [THIN MATERIAL] The Full_Extrapolation had already resolved most of the Mega_Init's own
  open questions (pace, clan formation, living-arrangement architecture) before this pass began, leaving
  less fresh, unused territory than any other district covered. Worth a fresh Vision Notes-style session if
  the developer wants this district's own future work to have more to draw on.
- **Virgo** — [DECISION PENDING, lower stakes] The Deep Level's own origin (pre-Falkland infrastructure,
  something older, navigable geology, or a Cradle-adjacent fabrication link) is explicitly deferred to its
  own dedicated pass, same discipline as Pisces' Flood.
- **Hub** — no entry, and notably the most fully-resolved district in the set: its own Cross_Reference_Synthesis
  (Finding 2) identifies the Hub as one of only two districts in the whole pre-war series needing no
  reinvention at all to survive the move backward in time — a "structurally complete self" from founding
  onward. One small, explicitly-flagged detail remains (the Grand Transit Exchange and Central Council
  Plaza's own founding-era specifics beyond "smaller, less expanded"), already captured as this district's
  own Lens 1 and Lens 4 ideas above.

**Summary — five items need an actual developer decision before related future work can proceed cleanly:**
Leo's Star War, Capricorn's seven c. 2761 options, Gemini's Great Corruption, Pisces' Tolerance Pact and
Flood, Virgo's Deep Level. Two smaller factual gaps remain open with no decision pending: Aries' Black
Silence/Overclock Martyrs dating, Libra's Surveillance Files leak dating. One district (Sagittarius) would
benefit from a fresh Vision Notes-style session simply for having less unused material to work with, not
because anything is unresolved.
