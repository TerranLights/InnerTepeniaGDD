# District Hostile Actions
**Actions that Turn Locals Immediately Violent**

For each district, these are **specific, culturally violating actions** (beyond generic murder/looting) that would cause nearby locals to turn hostile and attack the player on sight.

**Flagged for future review (2026-07-22):** name tags audited and corrected against current district canon
(Cancer/Capricorn/Libra now use their resolved official names; Gemini's tag clarified as informal, not
official).

**Proposed additions, 2026-07-24 — pending review, not yet decided which to keep/cut/merge.** Cross-referenced
every district against `District_Canon_Reference.md`'s full Historical Pressures/Cultural Texture material.
Each district below now has a "Proposed additions" block appended after its original 5 triggers — these are
new candidates or sharper/more specific versions of existing ones, grounded in canon that didn't exist when
the original list was drafted. Nothing has been removed; the original bullets are untouched pending an actual
decision on which stay, which get replaced, and which get merged.

### 1. Cancer – The Sanctuary (Nurturing / Protective)
- Deliberately damaging or sabotaging shelters, medical facilities, or child-care centers.
- Stealing food, medicine, or supplies meant for the sick, elderly, or children.
- Threatening, harassing, or attempting to abduct vulnerable NPCs (especially children or the infirm).
- Desecrating memorials or care rituals.
- Intentionally spreading disease or contamination in residential areas.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Publicly relitigating or exposing the details of the Overcrowding Decision (c. 2813) in front of survivors
  from either side — Cancer's own secret wound, deliberately never memorialized.

### 2. Taurus – TBN-district (Residential / Stability)
- Breaking into or viciously vandalizing private homes.
- Stealing or destroying sentimental family heirlooms or personal belongings.
- Disrupting or ruining important community gatherings, meals, or traditions.
- Harming or threatening pets and bonded companions.
- Causing major structural damage to residential buildings.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Forcing entry into a home where the norm is that doors are always answered unannounced — the violation
  lands harder here than ordinary trespass, since it breaks a total-openness social contract, not just
  property law.
- Harming or threatening a multi-generational bonded companion specifically (a robot with four generations
  of service to the same household carries real genealogical social status).

### 3. Leo – TBN-district (Cultural / Performative)
- Sabotaging or destroying performances, stages, or major cultural artifacts.
- Publicly humiliating or mocking performers during a live show.
- Stealing or defacing famous artworks, costumes, or cultural relics.
- Disrupting major festivals or performances in a chaotic or destructive way.
- Spreading high-impact false rumors that destroy a performer’s reputation.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Deliberately plunging a performance space into darkness — given The Dimming, dark spaces in Leo are
  understood as psychologically dangerous, not just disruptive. Sharper and more specific than the existing
  generic "disrupting a festival" bullet.

### 4. Scorpio – TBN-district (Psychological / Transformative)
- Violating someone’s psychological privacy (forcefully exposing memories or augmentations).
- Sabotaging or destroying transformation/therapy equipment or ritual spaces.
- Betraying someone during a deeply personal transformation ritual.
- Publicly exposing or weaponizing someone’s darkest traumas or secrets.
- Desecrating spaces meant for confronting inner demons.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Forcibly removing or mocking someone's transformation mask (descent/integration/emergence) in public — a
  real, specific social code, more distinctive than the generic "psychological privacy" bullet already here.
- Attempting to extract or leak a specific confession from the Archive of Final Confessions for political
  use — the Archive's actual, established political sensitivity, sharper than generic "exposing traumas."

### 5. Aries – TBN-district (Raw Survival / Energy)
- Sabotaging critical power infrastructure in a way that endangers lives.
- Stealing high-value power cells or energy reserves in front of workers.
- Intentionally causing blackouts that directly harm the district’s operations.
- Publicly humiliating authority figures during a crisis.
- Showing extreme cowardice or weakness in a way that disgraces the district’s values.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Mocking or dismissing the memory of the Overclock Martyrs — recruits are expected to be able to name all
  of them before their first shift; this is sacred in a way generic "authority figures" isn't. Candidate
  replacement for the existing "publicly humiliating authority figures" bullet.
- Causing even a brief, localized blackout, deliberately evoking every worker's memory of the Black Silence.

### 6. Capricorn – The Yards (Industrial / Ambition)
- Sabotaging production lines or critical machinery.
- Stealing industrial secrets, prototypes, or high-value materials.
- Undermining or publicly disgracing a foreman/supervisor in front of workers.
- Causing major workplace accidents through reckless sabotage.
- Mocking the district’s work ethic or industrial output publicly.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Defacing or publicly mocking a merit board specifically — a real, physical, public institution central to
  Capricorn's identity; sharper than generic "mocking the work ethic." Candidate replacement.
- **Deliberately NOT proposing anything tied to Capricorn's core injustice mechanism itself** — still a
  4-way undecided contender per `06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`; don't build a hostility trigger
  assuming specifics until one is chosen.

### 7. Aquarius / The Labs (Visionary / Experimental)
- Stealing or destroying experimental data, prototypes, or research.
- Sabotaging dangerous but important ongoing experiments.
- Publicly exposing unethical experiments in a way that threatens the district.
- Betraying a researcher mid-experiment.
- Discrediting major research projects in a destructive public manner.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Reframe "publicly exposing unethical experiments" around the established pattern that scandals trigger
  *another* round of external oversight/restriction from Aries/Virgo/Libra — a real recurring historical
  cycle here, sharper than generic embarrassment.
- Sabotaging or exposing the Living Network Experiment specifically — the current ~30-year, possibly-
  collapsing utopia project with genuine true believers.

### 8. Libra – The Government District (Diplomatic / Balance)
- Disrupting official proceedings or diplomatic events violently.
- Stealing or leaking highly sensitive government documents.
- Inciting major unrest or rebellion inside the governmental district.
- Causing severe diplomatic incidents that threaten stability.
- Openly undermining the rule of law in a high-profile way.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- **Resolves the flagged Unity Path tension:** narrow "stealing or leaking highly sensitive government
  documents" to specifically mean *forceful/reckless* leaking — distinct from the sympathetic-archivist-
  mediated version `District_Main_Questlines.md`'s own Libra Unity Path already treats as a legitimate good
  outcome. Same act, different method, different consequence.
- Exposing that the Surveillance Files exist at all, or forcing public acknowledgment of the Suspended
  Compact — both described as the district's "most politely ignored secret[s]," genuinely more severe than
  a generic document leak.

### 9. Gemini – TBN-district (informally "Janbogo Subnet Nexus," not yet an official name) (Information / Communication)
- Deliberately spreading dangerous misinformation on a large scale.
- Hacking and publicly leaking highly sensitive private data.
- Destroying or corrupting critical information archives.
- Betraying high-profile information brokers or journalists.
- Shutting down or sabotaging major communication networks.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Enrich "destroying or corrupting critical information archives" with a direct Great Corruption tie:
  sabotaging the generations-long recovery effort specifically, not just archives in general.
- Trivializing or relitigating the Zukelli/Janbogo-city refugee divide — the district's own most charged
  internal wound per its canon, currently unrepresented anywhere in this list.

### 10. Pisces / The Markets (Black Market / Underworld)
- Snitching on or informing against major criminal operations.
- Stealing from or publicly betraying high-level crime bosses.
- Disrupting major black market deals causing huge losses.
- Exposing hidden criminal safehouses or infrastructure.
- Violating the criminal “code of the streets” in a major public way.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24) — the real fix for this district's one-dimensionality.** Currently 100%
Operators/black-market (the 5 bullets above are arguably five phrasings of the same betrayal); the Believers
and Flood-Traumatized factions have zero representation despite being equally central per the Main
Questline's "Flood Protocols" chain:
- Violating the district's actual information-economy trust — using something someone shared about
  themselves (the real social contract underpinning access to significant Pisces operations) against them.
  Candidate replacement for one of the redundant Operators bullets above.
- Forcibly inducing memory-sharing on someone without consent — deliberately recreating Flood (c. 2771)
  trauma — or publicly dismissing Flood survivors' trauma as fake/exaggerated.

### 11. Sagittarius – The Frostlands (Frontier / Exploration)
- Sabotaging expedition equipment or vehicles in life-threatening conditions.
- Stealing critical survival supplies from fellow explorers.
- Betraying or abandoning people during dangerous expeditions.
- Claiming or stealing territory/resources others fought hard to secure.
- Disrespecting the harsh frontier survival code in a major way.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Claiming or exploiting a depleted quarry site specifically, invoking the Siligel Rush and Bust's lingering
  bitterness — sharper than generic "stealing territory/resources."
- Publicly demanding literal, provable truth from a storyteller's story, disrespecting the actual cultural
  function storytelling serves here (a story doesn't need to be literally true to be valuable).

### 12. Virgo – Concordia Undergrid (Maintenance / Service)
- Sabotaging critical infrastructure (pipes, power lines, support systems).
- Stealing essential maintenance tools or repair materials.
- Attacking or disrespecting maintenance workers during critical repairs.
- Causing major system failures that endanger the Undergrid.
- Publicly humiliating or undermining the importance of maintenance work.

**Proposed additions (2026-07-24):**
- Publicly dismissing or mocking Virgo's wartime contribution relative to Aries' celebrated one — the exact
  defining recognition-gap wound the canon names directly; sharper than generic "undermining the importance
  of maintenance work."
- Trespassing into or publicizing the existence of the Deep Level — a genuinely unique, unexplained piece
  of lore; fits this file's own pattern of "sacred secret, violating it is severe."

### 13. Concordia Central Hub (Axis Mundi) – Neutral Crossroads / Governance Nexus
- Desecrating or defacing the Bridge Memorial, the one site every district has uneasily agreed to leave unclaimed.
- Sabotaging the Grand Transit Exchange or Central Council Plaza in a way that cripples inter-district movement or negotiation.
- Forcing a Hub-born resident to publicly declare exclusive loyalty to one district, especially through threats or violence.
- Exploiting Hub neutrality as cover to smuggle resources, weapons, or information for one district's political advantage.
- Bringing openly partisan violence onto Hub ground — attacking someone specifically for their district origin while standing in neutral territory.

### Quick Summary of Patterns

| District Theme              | What Angers Them Most                     | Typical Trigger Type                  |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Cancer / Taurus             | Harm to the vulnerable or homes           | Violation of care & stability         |
| Leo / Scorpio               | Cultural or psychological desecration     | Violation of identity & self          |
| Aries / Capricorn           | Sabotage of power or production           | Threat to strength & output           |
| Aquarius / Gemini           | Theft or destruction of knowledge         | Threat to progress & truth            |
| Libra / Pisces              | Betrayal of order or criminal code        | Violation of systems / rules          |
| Sagittarius / Virgo         | Sabotage of survival or infrastructure    | Threat to endurance & function        |
