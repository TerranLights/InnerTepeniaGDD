# 1 · THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

> # ⭐⭐ AN ORGAN MUST SAY SOMETHING ITS DISTRICT'S ZODIAC SIGN DOES NOT ALREADY SAY.
> ***Otherwise the second layer is decoration.***

**This did most of the work below, and it is what rules out the obvious pairings — which were mostly wrong.**

**The worked case: Libra.** The sign already means *balance*. **The inner ear is the organ of balance**, so
`Libra = Ears` is the immediately attractive answer — **and it is redundant.** It restates the scales in
anatomy and adds nothing. ⭐ **Libra takes the KIDNEYS instead — filtration against a threshold, applied
continuously, with no appeal** — which is a reading the zodiac does not supply and which the district's own
history already demonstrates.

**Test any future pairing against this. If the organ paraphrases the sign, it is the wrong organ.**

# 3 · THE HUB — **Brain. Developer ruling, verbatim.**

> ***"for the Central Hub, when I say its association is the Brain, I don't mean that it's some sort of a
> 'control center'. That's not my intention. The way that I picture it is that it represents Carl Jung's
> concept of the 'conscious' (at the level[s] where people actually occupy) and the 'subconscious' (deep
> underground, where the badly-damaged energy-grid center is located where the player fights the final battle
> of the game). That's the effect I'm going for when I say 'Brain' ~~> 'Central Hub'."***

⛔ **NOT a control center, NOT governance, NOT a command function.** *(Libra is the government district; the
Hub's zodiac role is transit and crossroads.)*

## What the ruling actually establishes

⭐ **The Hub's organ symbol is a VERTICAL AXIS, not a location.** **Conscious = the inhabited levels.
Subconscious = the deep underground, and specifically the badly-damaged energy-grid center.**

**Three consequences worth holding on to:**

| Order | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **1st** | **Depth in the Hub is depth of mind.** *Going down is not travel; it is descent into what the city does not examine* |
| **2nd** | **The thing in the subconscious is DAMAGED, and the city lives on top of it.** *The Hub's ordinary daily life happens directly above an injury nobody looks at* |
| **3rd** | ⭐ **The final battle is therefore not "the boss lives in the basement." It is the confrontation with the city's own unexamined material** — *and it is the only place in Concordia where the geography and the psychology are the same fact* |

⚠ **This also reframes the Hub's established identity** — *"a place where nobody is fully at home."* **A
conscious mind is exactly that: the part of the psyche everyone passes through and nobody is native to.**

## The reasoning, per district

### ⭐ Aries — **Heart**
**The obvious reading is "power source," and it is the shallow one.** **The heart is not interesting because
it supplies energy; it is interesting because it *cannot stop*, is *never thought about until it hurts*, and
its failure is *immediate and total*.** ⭐ **Aries' canon reading is a district that keeps everyone alive, is
fully aware of it, and carries unresolved resentment at being simultaneously essential and unacknowledged.**
***That is a heart's relationship with the body it serves.***

### Cancer — **Ears**
⛔ **The sentimental answer is the Heart, and it is wrong twice over** — it duplicates the sign's own meaning,
and it is a worse reading of the district. ***Cancer's virtue is not that it loves. It is that it LISTENS.***

⭐⭐ **And the organ carries the district's wound as well as its virtue.** **The Mother's Circuit** — the
caregiving robots' informal network of shared emotional data, which grew benevolently into *"something
approaching a surveillance network, and nobody quite knows when that line was crossed"* — **is listening that
became something else.** *One organ, both faces of the district. That is what a good symbol does.*

⚠ **Robot physiology raises the stakes:** robots perceive sound and vibration across a meaningfully wider
range than humans, structurally. **In Cancer that is not a spec sheet — it is what the district is for.**

### ⭐⭐ Libra — **Kidneys**
**The kidney maintains the body by applying a threshold, continuously, to everything that passes it. It does
not review. It does not hear appeals. It has no mechanism for exception.**

***The Rationing of Grief is a kidney event, in every particular.*** **Cancer filed a formal exemption through
Libra's own process; the request landed in a backlog at the worst possible moment; the compliance formula —
*"with no manual override built in for exceptions"* — flagged Cancer as noncompliant and cut its allocation
**before anyone reviewed it**; the directive was later withdrawn and Cancer's specific allocation was never
individually corrected.**

⭐ **Nobody was malicious. The filter simply ran.** *That is the organ exactly, and the sign's "balance" cannot
express it.*

### Scorpio — **Liver**
**The liver's distinction is that it does not route around the toxin — it takes it in and metabolizes it.**
***Scorpio's canon reading is "the only way through is through," and two centuries of technique for going
through what the rest of the city goes around.***

⭐ **And the liver is the one organ that regenerates**, which is the transformation half of the district's role
stated in tissue.

⚠ **The district's known deficit sharpens the fit rather than softening it:** *Scorpio has the ceremony and
not the aftercare* **(the ayahuasca finding).** **A liver that processes without recovery time is a liver that
scars.**

### ⭐ Gemini — **Spinal cord + peripheral nervous system**
**The nervous system is the obvious home for an information district, so the work is in choosing *which part*.
The brain is taken and it belongs to the Hub. What is left is better anyway:** ***the reflex arc — the loop
that acts before the brain is consulted at all.***

⭐⭐ **That says something specific and true: Gemini reacts faster than Libra can deliberate, and this is
structural rather than a fault of either.** **A reflex is not insubordination. It is what happens when the
signal path is shorter.** *The district's own reading — information as the solution, never reckoning that more
information increases the need for judgment — is the reflex problem stated in its own vocabulary.*

### Sagittarius — **Eyes**
**The city's outward look, at the frontier.** ⭐ **But the reason to choose it is physical, not poetic:
the eye is the organ that must stay EXPOSED in order to work.** *You cannot see through a covering.*

***In the Frostlands that is a real cost, not a metaphor*** — **the eye is the part of a body that cannot be
insulated, and Sagittarius is the district whose function requires standing where insulation is most needed.**
*See `Robot_Cold_Physiology.md` for what exposure costs there.*

### Pisces — **Stomach**
**Canon: *"Every city produces overflow — people, needs, practices, substances, and ideas that the rest of the
city's organized structures cannot accommodate. Pisces is where all of it goes."***

***The stomach receives whatever the body takes in. It does not evaluate, screen, or approve.*** **It accepts
and begins breaking down.** ⭐ **And its medium is acid — which is the honest part: the district is hospitable
and corrosive at once, and one substance does both.**

### Virgo — **Intestines**
**The long, unglamorous stretch that extracts what is usable and moves the rest along** — *"makes everything
else possible and receives the least acknowledgment for it."*

⭐ **The second reading is the better one: the gut holds the ENTERIC NERVOUS SYSTEM, the "second brain," which
knows things the head does not and cannot explain how.** ***Virgo knows where every pipe runs.*** **That
knowledge is not filed anywhere; it is held in the tissue that does the work.**

> ## ⛔⛔ CONTENT BOUNDARY — BINDING
> **This symbol is ABSORPTION and GUT-INSTINCT. It is NEVER elimination.**
> ***The project's standing rule holds without exception: sanitation is infrastructure, never character
> material.*** **Stated here explicitly because this is the one organ on the list that could be misread as
> license, and a symbol file is exactly where such a misreading would take hold.**

### ⭐ Leo — **Lungs**
**Breath is what makes voice. Performance runs on air.**

⭐⭐ **And Concordia makes this concrete rather than figurative.** **The city sits at roughly 3,800 m
physiological altitude, is heated but deliberately NOT pressurized, and no acclimatization is possible**
*(`Concordia-City/Concordia_Altitude_and_Atmosphere.md`)*. ***So sustained singing, brass playing, stage work
and oratory are genuinely harder in Concordia than they would be anywhere at sea level.***

> **Leo's canon premise is that *morale is infrastructure, and like all infrastructure it requires constant
> power and skilled maintenance.*** ⭐ **The organ says what that power actually is: the scarcest thing in the
> city.** *A district whose civic function is performed on a resource the city cannot supply in abundance.*

⚠ **Virgo has a legitimate competing claim on the lungs** *(air handling as maintenance)*. **If a second
association is wanted, this is the strongest candidate for one** — see §7.

### Taurus — **Pancreas**
**The pancreas holds a single critical variable at its set point, against everything the body does.**
***Homeostasis is Taurus's entire personality*** — *"decided a very long time ago what life should look like,
and has been defending that decision against everything the city throws at it ever since."*

⚠ **Its failure mode is the useful part and it is slow:** **pancreatic failure is chronic rather than acute,
and it changes everything downstream while presenting as nothing for a long time.** *A district whose
stability could be eroding invisibly is a more interesting district than one that is simply stable.*

⚠ **This is the weakest pairing on the board** — defensible, least vivid. **First candidate to cut.** See §7.

### ⭐ Capricorn — **Bone marrow** *(addition)*
**Blood is not stored in bone; it is MANUFACTURED there.** ***The body's actual production floor, hidden
inside its hardest structure.*** **The Yards' rigid meritocratic hierarchy is skeletal, and the making happens
inside it.**

> ### ⭐⭐ AND THIS LANDS DIRECTLY ON EXISTING CANON
> ***Robot bones are METAL rather than calcium*** *(the ossuary ruling, CGRM 2026-09-01)*. **So a robot's
> marrow-equivalent is a manufacturing process housed inside a metal frame** — **a genuinely different object
> from a human's, and one this project has the physiology to support.** *See `Robot_Biology_and_Culture/`.*

⚠ **Note the district's known blind spot:** *it "became unable to question whether the hierarchy it built is
actually producing the right results."* **Marrow is the organ where a production fault is invisible from
outside and systemic in effect.**

### ⭐⭐ Aquarius — **Immune / lymphatic system** *(addition)*
***The strongest fit on the board.***

**The immune system is distributed rather than centralized, learns only by exposure, and its characteristic
catastrophic failure is AUTOIMMUNITY — attacking the body it exists to protect.**

> **Aquarius' canon reading, verbatim:** *"A district full of people who are genuinely trying to save the
> world, who are frequently quite intelligent, and who have a systematic inability to consider what their good
> intentions look like from the perspective of the people they are helping."*
>
> ⭐⭐ ***Autoimmunity is that sentence in one word.*** **Not malice, not incompetence — a protective system
> correctly executing its function against the wrong target, and unable to perceive the error from inside.**

⚠ **The district's capability reading is that it *cannot form a stable intention*** *(Step 2, `00_RUNBOOK.md`)*.
**An immune system has no intention at all; it has recognition and response.** *The organ and the capability
row agree without having been derived from each other.*

# 6 · RULINGS AND OPEN QUESTIONS

## ✅ RULED — developer, 2026-09-04

### 1. ⛔⛔ THE ORGAN LAYER IS **REFERENCE-ONLY**. It does NOT exist in-fiction.

> **Developer ruling, verbatim:** ***"yes, reference-only"***

⭐ **Organs inherit the zodiac's standing rule exactly: this is the developer's reference scheme, not
something anyone in Concordia knows about.** **No resident calls their district "the kidneys," no signage
carries an organ, no faction takes one as an emblem, and no character uses the vocabulary.**

> ### ⚠ THE TRANSLATION DISCIPLINE APPLIES — sweep before committing any district text
> **`00e` §8 already requires this for zodiac vocabulary — *"no zodiac vocabulary outside a bracketed citation
> or a header"* — and the runbook records that it catches genuine leaks on EVERY district, 3–7 per pass.**
> ***Organ vocabulary now needs the same sweep, and it is harder***, because **organ words are ordinary
> English in a way "Capricorn" is not.** *"Heart," "gut," "nerve," "lungs," "eyes," "marrow" and "stomach" all
> appear innocently in normal prose.*
>
> ⭐ **So sweep for the ASSOCIATION, not the word.** *A character saying "my heart wasn't in it" is fine. A
> line that presents Aries **as** the city's heart is a leak.* **The test: would this sentence read as a claim
> about the symbol layer to someone who knows it exists?**
>
> ⛔ **`00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 5's word-boundary warning applies doubled here** — *a bare `mars` matches inside
> `grammars`* — **and every organ term on this list has a common-English usage that will flood a naive grep.**

### 2. ✅ THE ASSOCIATIONS STAND AS THEY ARE — all 13, no cuts

> **Developer ruling, verbatim:** ***"the associations work fine as they are"***

**The 13-for-13 completeness is ADOPTED.** ⛔ **The cut order previously proposed here — Taurus/Pancreas, then
Leo/Lungs — is withdrawn and must not be acted on.** *Recorded only so the question is not reopened.*

⚠ **This also settles the multiple-districts question by default: no organ carries more than one district.**
*The developer permitted it; the mapping did not need it. **Virgo's competing claim on the lungs is not
adopted.***
