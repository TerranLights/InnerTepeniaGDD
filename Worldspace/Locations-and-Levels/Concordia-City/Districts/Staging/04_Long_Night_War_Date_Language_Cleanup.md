# Finding 4 — "Long Night War (early 2800s)" Is Stale Language Almost Everywhere

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion, cosmetic/low-urgency

## The issue

Nearly every district entry in `District_Canon_Reference.md` and `Historical_Pressures.md` refers to "the Long Night War (early 2800s)" as a loose historical-pressure header. This phrasing predates the war being pinned down to a single, precise year: **2812**.

Affected districts (non-exhaustive, essentially all of them): Cancer, Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aries, Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, Gemini, Pisces, Sagittarius, Virgo, and the Central Hub.

## Why this isn't a logic break

"Early 2800s" still technically contains 2812 — it's imprecise, not wrong. Nothing here actually contradicts the fixed date; it's just language that was never tightened up after the date got fixed.

## Suggested approach, whenever this gets picked up

A global find-and-tighten pass, not a rewrite — swap "Long Night War (early 2800s)" for "Long Night War (2812)" throughout both files. Low effort, no content changes needed, just precision. Not urgent enough to justify doing today, but worth batching whenever another Concordia file pass happens anyway.
