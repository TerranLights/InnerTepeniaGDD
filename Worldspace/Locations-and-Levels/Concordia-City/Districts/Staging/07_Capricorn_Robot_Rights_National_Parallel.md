# Thread 7 — Capricorn's Recalibration Underground Gains a National Parallel

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion (new conflict thread, not a consistency fix)

**Correction, 2026-07-29:** this thread's original framing as a "robot-rights movement" has been corrected — Tepenia is a fundamentally robot-equal country, so a present-day movement demanding rights already unambiguously held doesn't fit the setting. The underlying content (essential labor, systematically uncredited, at a national scale) is unaffected and still works; only the movement's name and stated demand have changed. See `../Deep_Dives/06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md` Section G.

## What's already established

The Narrow Door (c. 2761) found the district's merit-rating and scheduling system had systematically undervalued workers whose rhythms didn't fit its own founding-era assumptions — human and robot alike, robots most visibly — for decades; leadership disputed the methodology and buried the findings. There's a real, currently-dismissed Recalibration Underground in Capricorn as a result, demanding the merit system measure what it claims to, not petitioning for rights already held.

## What this week's Megasheet work adds

The proposed **Cradle-Keepers** faction pattern (Neumayer designs the current chamber schematic uncredited; Byrd and Sinheung manufacture it, both modest-political-profile cities most of Tepenia barely thinks about) establishes that the exact same pattern — essential labor, systematically uncredited — isn't a Capricorn-specific injustice. It's a *national* one, repeating at the level of entire cities across three different subnets.

## Why this is worth developing

Right now, the Recalibration Underground has one piece of "smoking gun" evidence: The Narrow Door itself. If that movement (or any individual member of it) ever learns how the Cradle network actually works — that the entire country's robot-creation capacity runs on labor nobody credits, at a national scale — that's not just sympathetic context. It's proof their own grievance isn't an isolated local failure to be negotiated away district-by-district; it's evidence of a pattern the whole country is built on.

## Discussion questions

- How would this information plausibly reach the Recalibration Underground? Gemini (information network), a Byrd- or Neumayer-origin character, or something else?
- Does this make the movement more radicalized (national pattern = systemic, not fixable by better Capricorn-only policy) or does it risk diluting the movement's own specific, local grievance into something more abstract?
- Could this tie into the already-flagged questline idea (a robot tracing their own chamber's "Mark" and discovering Neumayer's own uncredited design role) — giving the Underground a genuine external ally or evidence source?
