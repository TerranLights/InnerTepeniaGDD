# Finding 1 — Aries' Post-War Energy Crisis Is Missing an Obvious Cause

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion

## What Aries' own file currently says

`District_Canon_Reference.md`'s Aries entry frames its ongoing post-war energy crisis as internally generated: "Chronic siligel shortages, geothermal instability, and competing demands from expanding districts have entrenched a cycle of boom-and-blackout governance." The Black Silence (a near-total collapse) and the Overclock Martyrs incident are both framed as self-contained Aries events, with no external cause named.

## What's already established elsewhere that this doesn't connect to

- The Amundsen Tower's destruction (2812, the same year as the Long Night War) is explicitly framed as causing **"acute grid overload + chronic instability"** on a continent-wide, Tower-regulated power grid (`Energy_Grid_Failure_Rationale.md` #11, the Amundsen Tower Reframe).
- That exact mechanism is the confirmed reason the Mountain Pass fabrication-chamber outpost died permanently — it ran on residual overflow from the Tower's regulated grid, and when the Tower fell, the outpost's manufacturing capability ended for good, though the facility itself is still standing.
- Concordia is one of Tepenia's largest surviving population centers. It's a reasonable, easy assumption that Concordia's own power core drew on that same continent-wide grid, at least partially.

## Why this matters

If Aries' own crisis has never been explicitly tied to the Tower's collapse, it means one of Concordia's central, defining institutional wounds is missing its most obvious, already-established cause — sitting one cross-reference away and never made. Right now Aries reads as suffering from garden-variety infrastructure problems (shortages, instability) rather than from the same nationally significant catastrophe that also ended Mountain Pass.

## Discussion questions

- Does Concordia's Aries core actually draw on the Tower's regulated grid, or is it meant to be a separate, local Concordia-only power system? (Not yet established either way, as far as I can find.)
- If it does draw on the Tower's grid: should Aries' "Post-War Energy Crises (mid-2800s onward)" be rewritten to explicitly trace to the Tower's 2812 collapse, the same causal chain as Mountain Pass?
- Would this change anything about the Black Silence specifically (currently undated within the war) — could the Black Silence *be* the moment the Tower's collapse hit Concordia's own grid, rather than a separate incident?

**Ripple effect worth noting:** this same gap runs downstream into Cancer's own file, which cites "chronic power allocation disputes with Aries" endangering incubators and medical equipment as an "ongoing since ~2650s" problem. If Aries' post-war crisis specifically gets tied to the Tower's 2812 collapse, Cancer's own post-war version of the same dispute (as distinct from its older, pre-war Second-Interwar-era version) should probably get the same explicit tie-in rather than reading as a separate, coincidental continuation of an older pattern.
