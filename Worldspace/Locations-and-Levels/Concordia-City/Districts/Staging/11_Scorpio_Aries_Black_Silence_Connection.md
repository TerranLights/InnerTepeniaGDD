# Finding 11 — Scorpio's Forbidden Archive War and Aries' Black Silence Are Plausibly the Same Event

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion
**Added:** during a second, more rigorous pass through Districts 1/2/4/6/8/11/12 after the developer flagged that the first pass had gone easier on them than on 3/5/7/9/10/13.

## What each district's file says, read side by side

**Aries' "The Black Silence"** (`District_Canon_Reference.md`, District 5): "The most catastrophic near-failure in Concordia's history. The power core came within minutes of total collapse. The exact circumstances are disputed: Aries workers say it was twelve minutes from failure; Libra's official record says four; the classified version says forty-seven. ... What exactly caused the near-failure — and why Libra's classified files disagree with everyone's account of the timeline — are questions the district has never stopped asking."

**Scorpio's "The Forbidden Archive War"** (District 4): "Scorpio's Archive of Final Confessions received a testament from a dying technician who had witnessed the true cause of **a key Long Night War event** — something that contradicted Libra's official history. Libra demanded the record be destroyed under the Concordia Emergency Information Act. Scorpio refused... The record may or may not still exist in its original form."

## Why this is worth flagging

These were written as two separate, unrelated pieces of district lore. But they line up almost perfectly:

- Both involve a specific, high-stakes Long Night War-era event.
- Both involve a discrepancy between the "official" account and what actually happened.
- Both involve Libra specifically defending an official version and suppressing an alternative one.
- Aries' own file explicitly poses the open question "why do Libra's classified files disagree with everyone's account" — which is exactly the kind of question a suppressed technician's testimony, sitting in Scorpio's own archive, would answer.

Nothing currently states these are the same event. But nothing states they aren't, either, and the shape of both mysteries fits together unusually well for something that was never intentionally designed as a pair.

## Discussion questions

- If these are made the same event: the "dying technician" in Scorpio's archive was presumably an Aries engineer (or someone with direct access to the power core) who ended up in Scorpio's care near death — worth asking whether that's plausible given Scorpio's established role as where Long Night War survivors with "near-death experiences, memory damage, and existential crises" ended up.
- This would give Scorpio genuine leverage over Aries as well as Libra — currently the Forbidden Archive War is framed as purely a Scorpio-vs-Libra standoff; connecting it to the Black Silence pulls Aries into the triangle too.
- Worth deciding whether the record, if it still exists, actually resolves which of the three disputed timelines (12 min / 4 min / 47 min) is true, or whether it complicates the question further rather than answering it.
