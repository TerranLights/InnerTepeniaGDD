# Thread 9 — Sagittarius' Frontier Economy Gets No Comparable National Respect

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion (new conflict thread)

## What's already established

Sagittarius' economy runs on explorer guilds, caravan merchants, long-range scouts, and frontier resource extraction (the Siligel Rush and Bust). Its established grievance is chronic neglect from Libra's central planning and a sense of being treated as a dumping ground/safety valve for the rest of the city, never as a serious contributor.

## What this week's Megasheet work adds

The proposed **Long Haul** faction pattern (Belgrano, Byrd, Sayowa, Lazar, Troll — five cities whose economies all independently depend on keeping the actual overland freight network running, tied together by the Rastra vehicle's own unbroken lineage from Belgrano's founding-era invention) establishes that frontier/freight/logistics work of exactly this kind is treated as *nationally significant* when it happens outside Concordia — Byrd is one of only two places left that can manufacture a robot from nothing, in part because of that same freight network.

## Why this is worth developing

Sagittarius does structurally identical work (moving people and goods through hostile, remote conditions, keeping a periphery connected to a core) and gets none of that same respect — it's read internally as provincial and expendable rather than nationally load-bearing. If word of the wider Tepenian Long Haul network's actual significance reached Sagittarius (via Gemini, via a Sagittarius-origin character with outside contacts, via trade), it could sharpen an existing grievance into something more specific: not just "we're neglected," but "the exact same work we do is celebrated when someone else does it a thousand kilometers away."

## Discussion questions

- Does this thread work better as a source of resentment (why does the *same* work get respect out there and contempt in here) or as a source of pride/legitimacy Sagittarius could actually claim for itself?
- Could Sagittarius' own explorer guilds develop a genuine professional kinship with Long Haul cities specifically, distinct from its existing (and already well-developed) relationship with Pisces and Aries?
- Is there a plausible in-world mechanism for this information to actually reach Sagittarius, given the district's own established "almost no written institutional culture... knowledge is transmitted through story"?
