# Finding 5 — Two Long-Running Institutions Span the War Without Comment

**Status:** 🟡 open for discussion

## The two cases

**Aquarius' "Living Network Experiment"** (District 7): described as "ongoing, semi-secretly... has been running for roughly thirty years and may be approaching collapse." Counting back from present day (≈2822–2827), that's a start around **2792–2797** — meaning it began roughly 15–20 years *before* the war (2812) and, per the text, is still running today, having continued straight through the war without any mentioned interruption.

**The Pisces/Libra "Tolerance Pact"** (District 10): "an arrangement... that has never been written down, never been acknowledged, and has been in effect for roughly sixty years." Counting back, that's a start around **2762–2767** — again predating the war by decades, and, per the text, still quietly in effect today.

## Why this is worth a deliberate call, not necessarily a fix

Neither of these is stated to have been interrupted by the war, which is a real assumption baked into both entries whether or not it was intentional. Given how catastrophic the Long Night War is established to be for Concordia generally (command-nexus role, evacuation through the Tower, "patched infrastructure" everywhere, memorial plaques embedded in load-bearing walls), it's a fair question whether either of these should have survived completely unscathed and unremarked.

## Discussion questions

- Living Network Experiment: does it make more sense that this continued straight through the war (a point in its favor — semi-secret, decentralized things might be exactly what survives chaos best), or should the war have meaningfully disrupted or paused it, with the "thirty years" figure needing adjustment?
- Tolerance Pact: same question — is an *informal, unwritten* arrangement between Libra and Pisces plausibly more resilient through war and reconstruction than a formal one would be (a point that could be made explicit and interesting), or is 60 years of uninterrupted quiet cooperation implausible given everything else Concordia went through?
