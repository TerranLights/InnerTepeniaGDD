**Already covered (Stage 1):** Cancer was designated Concordia's sanctuary/integration zone from the
Second Interwar Period's start, built to receive the *original exile's* trauma (the War of Upper Earth
and the Falkland Treaty's own coercive terms), not the still-250-years-distant Long Night War. Across the
following two and a half centuries this population — never a one-time founding wave, but a continuous
intake of Upper Earth exiles, newly-activated robots, and washed-back Sagittarius frontier settlers —
built the district's entire mature caregiving culture: the memorial tradition, mastered lullaby art, the
doors-always-unlocked norm, and a care-debt parallel economy, all read as 250-year accumulated practice
rather than crisis response. The Full_Extrapolation proposes a working pre-war ethos ("there's room," not
yet "No One Left Behind"), an informal verbal-handoff ancestor of the post-war Mother's Circuit, and gives
the long-running Aries energy conflict a specific originating incident (the First Silence, c. 2650s,
cross-referenced directly against Aries' own pre-war document). The Cross_Reference_Synthesis additionally
establishes Cancer and Scorpio as a deliberately designed sibling pair — two different planned responses
(nurture vs. confront) to the same founding wound, not independently-arrived-at philosophies.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Deep Dive's own Stage 1 section names a
   "care-debt parallel economy" among Cancer's established cultural texture, in the same breath as the
   memorial tradition and mastered lullabies — but unlike those two, it's never elaborated anywhere in
   Cancer's own material (Mega_Init, Full_Extrapolation, and Cross_Reference_Synthesis are all silent on
   it beyond the name). A chain establishing what actually constitutes a "debt" in a culture built around
   never turning anyone away, and how it gets tracked or repaid without contradicting the district's own
   "there's room" ethos, would use this named-but-empty economic detail.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s own per-city
   reasoning calls Esperanza — Cancer's #1 feeder city — "about as clean a Cancer match as exists anywhere
   in this project," chartered on the founding principle that exile's children deserved care, with robots
   as primary at-home caregivers. Yet none of Cancer's own Stage 1 documents name Esperanza specifically;
   the Deep Dive lists it only as an unelaborated entry in a feeder-city list. A chain about a specific
   Esperanza-originated caregiving practice or founding-era exchange between the two communities would use
   this explicitly-cited-elsewhere, still-undramatized population match.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` lists Ospedale degli
   Innocenti, Florence, as a Secondary-tier pick for Cancer — a real Renaissance foundling hospital famous
   for its wheel, a mechanism allowing infants to be left anonymously without stigma. None of Cancer's own
   documents engage with any of its 8 cited real-world inspirations beyond broad architectural mood. A
   chain about a specific pre-war Cancer intake practice echoing the Ospedale's anonymous-admission
   mechanism — for arrivals too raw or ashamed to give their own account of what they survived — would use
   this named, cited, but entirely undramatized precedent.

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** The Mega_Init's "What It Feels Like"
   section establishes the curved, green-and-white architecture and hydroponic haze as present from
   founding, "built into the district's original design brief." `Regional-Characteristics/District_Prominent_Features.md`
   separately lists specific set-dressing features (Safe Haven waystations, Care Circles, memorial walls)
   with no origin given for any single one. A chain about the specific founding-era decision or early
   milestone that produced one of these named features — the first Care Circle, say — would ground an
   established but originless piece of the district's built environment.

5. **Other.** `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md` states plainly, in two separate draft passes, that
   "experimental technologies and augmentations were sometimes tested on Cancer's vulnerable populations"
   by Aquarius — but this relationship appears nowhere in Cancer's own Deep Dive, Mega_Init,
   Full_Extrapolation, or Cross_Reference_Synthesis, and the source document doesn't specify whether this
   predates the war. A chain resolving whether a smaller-scale version of this Aquarius/Cancer relationship
   existed pre-war — parallel to how Full_Extrapolation Section IV resolved the Mother's Circuit's own
   pre-war precedent question — would use this cross-district detail Cancer's own material never
   acknowledges at all.

## Methodology observations (test-run notes, not part of the template itself)

- **The five lenses all produced usable, well-grounded ideas without forcing anything** — the adapted
  set (civic function/economy, feeder-city population, real-world precedent, personality/architecture,
  other) mapped cleanly onto Cancer's actual source material. No lens came up empty or needed a
  workaround.
- **The "Already covered" step took real synthesis work**, more than the city version did — Cancer has
  four separate pre-war documents (Deep Dive Stage 1, Mega_Init, Full_Extrapolation, Cross_Reference_Synthesis)
  where a city had one Catalog plus one Mega_Init. Worth keeping that summary to one dense paragraph
  rather than letting it sprawl per-document, which is what made this test run readable, since the
  district's Deep-Dive-plus-Megasheet-trio genuinely holds more prior synthesis than a city's
  Catalog-plus-Mega_Init did at the same stage.
- **The feeder-city lens (Step 2 in the template's lens table) required reading three separate
  documents** — `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s table, its reverse index, and the specific feeder
  city's own per-city reasoning paragraph — to find one usable, specific, unused detail (Esperanza's
  charter). This is more source-hopping than any other lens needed. Worth flagging in the template as an
  expected extra step, not a sign something's wrong if a future district's write-up takes longer on this
  lens specifically.
- **Untested by this run: the Staging-overlap step.** Cancer has zero Staging entries, so this test never
  actually exercised the "skip or note the overlap" instruction from the template's open-questions
  section. The next district run should deliberately be one *with* Staging entries (Aries, Scorpio, Leo,
  Capricorn, Aquarius, Libra, Gemini, Pisces, or Sagittarius all qualify) specifically to test that step
  before this method is considered fully validated.
- **Verdict: template holds up.** No structural changes needed based on this run. Recommend promoting
  this content into the real tracker file once a second, Staging-overlapping district has also been
  tested, so both untested paths (clean district, Staging-overlapping district) are confirmed before
  treating the template as final.

## Round 2 — re-run with `District_Vision_Notes/Cancer.md` added, 2026-07-17

The Scorpio test run validated the Staging-overlap path; this second Cancer pass tests the newly-added
Vision Notes input instead, added to the template after a source-inventory survey turned up that file as
a real gap. **Files added:** `District_Vision_Notes/Cancer.md` (full read).

**What Vision Notes actually contributed:**

- **Nothing new for Lenses 1, 2, 3, or 5.** Vision Notes restates the care-debt economy ("in place of
  credits") without elaborating it any further than the Deep Dive already does — this *confirms* Lens 1's
  idea is grounded in a genuine, still-unresolved gap rather than something the Deep Dive simply forgot to
  cross-reference, but it doesn't add new material. Esperanza, the Ospedale precedent, and the
  Aquarius/Cancer testing relationship are untouched — Vision Notes is a single conversational session
  about atmosphere and doesn't reach into population, real-world precedent, or cross-district topics at
  all.
- **A substantial upgrade for Lens 4.** Vision Notes turns out to be the single richest atmosphere/
  architecture source of any file read for Cancer — confirmed sensory specifics (deliberately no straight
  lines, living plantlife serving a dual functional/psychological purpose, a soft haze with no actual fog,
  a specific "care available but not hovering" stranger-facing behavior, ambient lullaby-singing, a "slow
  enough" pace) plus, more usefully for this pass, its own **"Still open from this session"** list —
  explicitly unresolved questions the developer flagged in real time, not gaps this pass had to infer.

**Lens 4, revised** to use the strongest of those explicitly-flagged questions in place of the original,
inferred Care Circle origin idea:

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history (revised).** `District_Vision_Notes/Cancer.md`
   explicitly lists, as still open from the developer's own session, "the specific source of the ambient
   haze (humidity from the hydroponics, presumably, though not explicitly confirmed as the cause)" — a
   defining sensory feature of the district ("a soft, hazy, fog-like light with no actual fog") with an
   unconfirmed origin. None of Cancer's four Megasheet documents touch the haze's mechanism at all; the
   Mega_Init only calls it "ambient haze from dense hydroponic cultivation" without engaging the Vision
   Notes' own explicit uncertainty about whether that's really the cause. A chain resolving what actually
   produces the haze — confirming the hydroponics theory, or revealing a second contributing source no one's
   named yet — would close a gap the developer's own notes flag directly, rather than one this pass had to
   infer. *(The original Care Circle-origin idea is still valid and undifferentiated from this one — kept
   as a viable alternate, not discarded, since both are genuine Lens 4 gaps.)*

**Verdict: Vision Notes is worth the added read, but only for Lens 4.** Recommend keeping it in the input
table as written — a mandatory read, not an optional bonus — since for at least one lens it produced
strictly better-grounded material (explicitly flagged as unresolved by the source itself) than what was
available without it. For the other four lenses it cost a read with no payoff this time; that's an
acceptable trade for one lens' improvement, and may pay off differently on other districts depending on
what each one's own Vision Notes session happened to cover.
