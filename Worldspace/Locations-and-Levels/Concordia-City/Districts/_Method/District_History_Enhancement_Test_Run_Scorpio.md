**Already covered (Stage 1):** Scorpio was zoned in the same late-2560s founding window as Cancer, its
deliberate sibling — both built to treat the same original exile wound, Cancer through shelter and
Scorpio through direct confrontation, "rebirth" through facing the wound rather than being sheltered from
it. Its core pre-war institution, the Descent/Integration/Emergence mask culture, was forged not
gradually but under real pressure during the First Fracture (proposed c. 2610s–2630s) — an early,
smaller-scale wave of robot identity fracture caused by crude, rushed early activation technology, decades
before the district had any precedent to draw on. The Full_Extrapolation proposes this same failure mode
(activation/reprogramming technology outrunning what protocols can support) as the shared root cause
later repeated, at wartime scale, in the post-war Identity Collapse Epidemic — per the
Cross_Reference_Synthesis, the same mechanism recurring roughly two centuries apart, not two unrelated
crises. It further proposes a small, private, generations-old testimony-recording practice as the genuine
institutional ancestor of the post-war Archive of Final Confessions; a Clinical/Sacred-Goth School
specialization split that only emerges a generation or two into the period, once population and capacity
allow it, intersecting with "the wider Goth subculture's own separate development elsewhere in
founding-era Concordia"; and a milder, chronic pre-war Scorpio/Libra bureaucratic friction (Libra
periodically attempting to license Scorpio's less clinical practices, Scorpio declining to cooperate) as
precedent for the post-war Forbidden Archive War. The Deep Dive's own Stage 1 section additionally flags,
as a genuinely open and unresolved question in its own text, whether Scorpio's hardware-side rebirth
practitioners draw on Cradle-chamber-adjacent technology or expertise.

1. **District civic function/economy → history.** The Deep Dive's own Stage 1 section explicitly names
   an "open, well-motivated connection nobody's made yet": whether Scorpio's rebirth practitioners' own
   hardware-side reconstructive work draws on Cradle fabrication-synthesis-chamber-adjacent technology or
   expertise, without duplicating the Cradle's own domain. None of Scorpio's three Megasheet documents
   pick this question up at all. A chain resolving where Scorpio's founding-era practitioners actually
   sourced their hardware expertise — a formal early exchange, cross-trained personnel, a licensed
   technique — would close a gap the source material itself already identifies but never answers.

2. **Feeder-city population culture → history.** `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md`'s reverse index

   (Belgrano, Palmer City, Zukelli, Casey) are explicitly flagged as a Stage 2 trauma-driven override, "not
   a cultural affinity in the same sense as the rest of this file." Its real, organic Stage-1 match is
   Vostok (2nd tier): a population with "zero Russian representation inheriting a Russian scientific
   legend that isn't theirs — a legend living in someone else's house." None of Scorpio's own Stage 1
   documents mention Vostok, despite that inherited-identity tension being close kin to Scorpio's own
   founding specialty. A chain about specific Vostok-descended arrivals bringing this particular flavor of
   identity dissonance to Scorpio's confrontation-based practice — distinct from the generic exile-trauma

   founding-era Concordia," without naming a specific real-world anchor for that subculture's own origin.
   A chain grounding that already-proposed-but-uncited connection directly in Cabaret Voltaire's own real
   history — an avant-garde movement born specifically from WWI-era disillusionment and trauma, not
   costume aesthetics — would give the Sacred/Goth School's own founding a concrete precedent instead of a

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history.** The Mega_Init's "What It Feels Like"

## Methodology observations (test-run notes, not part of the template itself)

- **The Staging-overlap step resolved cleanly, but not the way expected.** Finding 11 turned out to be
  entirely Stage 2 material (both the Forbidden Archive War and the Black Silence are Long Night War-era
  events) — so it was automatically out of scope for this Stage-1-only pass, not something requiring a
  judgment call about duplication. Worth updating the template's guidance: for districts, a Staging
  overlap is more likely to resolve as "not applicable, wrong stage" than "same idea, skip it." The
  overlap check is still worth doing (to confirm that, not to assume it), but shouldn't be expected to
  produce the same kind of collision the city pass's Davis precedent did.
- **The feeder-city lens surfaced a real complication the Cancer run didn't have.** Cancer's top feeder
  cities were all genuine Stage 1 matches. Scorpio's are not — its four best-documented feeder cities are
  explicitly a Stage 2 override, described in the source file itself as not a real cultural affinity.
  Using them would have violated the pass's own Stage-1-only scope. **Recommend adding this as explicit
  guidance in the template:** before using a district's top-ranked feeder cities for this lens, check
  whether `City_Refugee_District_Affinities.md` flags them as a "Stage 2 Override" — if so, drop to the
  next tier down for a genuinely organic match, the way this run used Vostok instead of Belgrano/Palmer
  City/Zukelli/Casey.
- **All five lenses still produced usable, well-differentiated ideas.** No lens came up empty, matching
  the Cancer run.
- **Verdict: template holds up, with one addition.** Recommend folding the feeder-city Stage-2-override
  guidance from the point above directly into `../District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Template.md`
  itself before starting the real 13-district pass — this isn't Scorpio-specific, since the same reverse
  index shows several other districts (per the Stage 2 Overrides section) have the identical Belgrano/
  Palmer City/Zukelli/Casey entries mixed into their own top ranks.

## Round 2 — re-run with `District_Vision_Notes/Scorpio.md` added, 2026-07-17

**Files added:** `District_Vision_Notes/Scorpio.md` (full read).

**This file turned out to be the sharpest real-world validation yet of the standing Stage 2 exclusion
rule.** Its own "recap of what was already established" paragraph freely mixes genuine Stage 1 material
(the mask culture, steam-as-privacy, the Goth subculture entanglement) with material that's explicitly
Stage 2 per Scorpio's own pre-war Mega_Init scope note — the Archive of Final Confessions, the near-death
simulation facilities, and the four-destroyed-cities refugee convergence are all named in the same recap
paragraph with no stage label distinguishing them from the founding-era detail sitting right next to them.
Filtering these out before using anything else from the file was the actual first step here, not an
afterthought — exactly the risk the standing rule exists to catch.

**What survived the filter and is genuinely new:**

- Specific atmosphere: dark reds and blacks, *genuine* fog rather than Cancer's ambient haze, and
  confirmation that "tightly-guarded" is social/cultural rather than physical security.
- **Access to Scorpio's deeper spaces is earned specifically through reputation** — not relationships,
  not which transformation mask someone wears — "built over time." Not stated anywhere in the four
  Megasheet documents.
- The "peace of knowing" gets a specific descriptive anchor (a settled acceptance of two hard facts,
  rather than despair or resignation) — elaboration on an already-Stage-1-confirmed trait, not new
  material requiring its own idea.
- **Explicitly flagged as still open:** what specifically earns reputation, and whether it differs for
  someone in active transformation versus a visitor; whether the "peace of knowing" is actively
  taught/cultivated or simply settles into long-term residents; whether the fog varies by area.

**Two lenses revised** to use this more strongly-grounded material, following the same principle the
Cancer re-run established — prefer a source's own explicitly-flagged-open question over a gap this pass
had to infer:

4. **District personality/geography/architecture → history (revised).** Vision Notes states plainly that
   access to Scorpio's invite-only deeper spaces is earned "specifically reputation, built over time" —
   not mask-phase, not relationships — but leaves genuinely open *what* that reputation is actually built
   from, and whether the path differs for a resident working through their own transformation versus an
   outsider seeking access. Nothing in the four Megasheet documents touches this mechanism at all. A chain
   establishing what founding-era Scorpio actually recognized as reputation-earning — successfully guiding
   others through their own descent/integration/emergence, surviving a documented personal transformation,
   something else entirely — would resolve a specific, explicitly-flagged gap in the district's own core
   access structure. *(The original Unburdening-booths-origin idea is still valid, kept as an alternate.)*

5. **Other (revised).** Vision Notes explicitly flags as open whether the "peace of knowing" — Scorpio's
   defining emotional register, already confirmed Stage 1 by the Mega_Init's own Enneagram section — is
   something residents actively teach and cultivate, or something that simply settles into anyone who
   stays long enough. This doesn't fit neatly under any of the other four lenses (it's neither economic,
   population-specific, nor a real-world precedent), making it a genuine "Other." A chain resolving
   whether the Descent/Integration/Emergence method itself functions as that teaching mechanism, or
   whether "peace of knowing" is understood as something separate that the method doesn't actually confer,
   would close a gap about the district's own core emotional inheritance that nothing else addresses.
   *(The original Leo/Scorpio rivalry idea is still valid, kept as an alternate.)*

**Verdict: same pattern as Cancer.** Vision Notes added nothing to Lenses 1, 2, or 3, but for Lenses 4 and
5 it outperformed every other source read for Scorpio — both because it's more specific, and because
(unlike every idea grounded elsewhere) it's explicitly self-flagged as unresolved rather than inferred.
Worth noting this run needed *two* lens swaps where Cancer only needed one — plausibly because Vision
Notes sessions vary in how much of their "still open" material lands on personality/atmosphere versus
other lenses, which argues for reading it fully rather than skimming, since a district's Vision Notes file
could turn out to matter more or less depending on what that particular conversation happened to cover.
