# Leo (Pre-War) — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** open questions from `Leo_Mega_Init.md` (this same pre-war folder) given proposed
answers, chosen specifically so they hold true whether or not the Star War eventually happens in its
currently-documented form. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece.
Written 2026-07-16.

---

## I. Demonym

**Proposed: Leonine.** Straightforward, consistent with standard demonym formation, and unlikely to be
affected by however the Star War question resolves.

---

## II. How the Scale-vs-Intimacy Tension Actually Shows Up, Day to Day, Pre-War

**Proposed:** not yet as a formal rivalry, but as a genuine, ongoing artistic disagreement that shapes
which venues thrive, which performers get patronage, and which recognition-level promotions feel
"earned" to the district at large. Grand-spectacle performers and intimate-form performers already
occupy recognizably different social circles, draw different patron interest, and periodically clash
over things like festival scheduling priority or which style gets showcased at citywide events — real,
lived friction, but expressed through the district's own ordinary social and economic channels rather
than through anything resembling an organized, named conflict. This gives the eventual Star War (in
whatever form it takes) a genuine runway of lived tension to escalate from, rather than requiring the
conflict to appear from nothing.

---

## III. The Recognition Hierarchy's Founding-Era Origin

**Proposed:** yes, present from very early in the founding period, though less formalized than its
post-war form. A district built specifically around proving personhood through undeniable performance
would need some way to track whose performance is actually landing — the known-face/crowd-stopper/
district-legend/city-legend gradation plausibly starts as informal social consensus long before it's
ever named as a real system, then simply becomes more explicit and codified as the district matures
across the interwar period. This is independent of the Star War question either way.

## Worth Your Attention

Section II is the most load-bearing proposal here — it gives Leo's eventual Star War (whatever form it
takes) a real, decades-deep social and economic runway to escalate from, rather than treating the
conflict as something that could plausibly erupt from a calm, undivided district. The tension already
exists; only its eventual political calcification remains genuinely open.
