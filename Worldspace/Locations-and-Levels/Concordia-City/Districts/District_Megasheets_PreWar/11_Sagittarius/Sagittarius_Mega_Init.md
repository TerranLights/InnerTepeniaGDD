# Sagittarius (Pre-War) — Mega-Init

**Step 1 of the Pre-War District Megasheet compilation process.** Companion document to the existing,
post-war `District_Megasheets/11_Sagittarius/Sagittarius_Mega_Init.md` — not a replacement for it.
Reconstructs Sagittarius as it stood during the Second Interwar Period (roughly 2564–2812), when Tepenia
was a full, functioning country, before the Long Night War. Synthesizes `Deep_Dives/
11_Sagittarius_Deep_Dive.md`'s own Stage 1 material, which the deep dive itself already isolates as
existing "before the war ever touched it." Compiled 2026-07-16.

**Scope note:** this is the most directly-transferring of this batch's three districts. Sagittarius'
post-war defining character — self-inflicted rather than war-inflicted, opportunity-seeking rather than
trauma-processing — was already fully formed centuries before the war arrived, per the deep dive's own
explicit Stage 1 / Stage 2 split. The Third Expansion Collapse and the Siligel Rush and Bust (c. 2772–
2777) both predate 2812 outright. Only "Long Night War Frontier Defense" and the "Post-War Resource
Scarcity" pressures are removed; nearly everything else carries over with light adaptation.

---

## The One-Line Pitch

*A district that never needed the exile's original wound to become who it is, and never needed a war to
prove that its own defining values could cost sixty-three lives and still not shake the "push further"
ethos loose — a frontier civilization already two centuries deep into answering the hard questions about
expansion, cost, and what's owed to the people who pay for it, entirely on its own terms.*

## Who Lives Here, and Why

Rugged explorers, prospectors, long-range scouts, frontier settlers, homesteader families, traders,
caravan merchants, archaeologists, adrenaline-seeking adventurers, exiles from stricter districts —
unchanged from the post-war population, since none of it was ever produced by the war. Robots: rugged
cold-weather navigation models, mobile survey and resource-scanning units, caravan escort and defense
frames, optimistic "hope engine" personalities designed for morale in isolation, plus the occasional
recovered or jury-rigged unit from a lost expedition — a category of loss Sagittarius has been
accumulating since its earliest founding years, not one the war introduced. Cultural lean, present from
founding: Instinctive (with Thinking undertones) + Assertive + Positive Outlook Enneagram.

Per the deep dive, Sagittarius is one of three districts (with Taurus and Capricorn) whose founding logic
was never a response to the original exile wound at all — it exists because crowded domes will always
produce a population that wants opportunity and space more than safety, and the cold turns philosophy
into a practical survival tool rather than an intellectual hobby. This is already fully true in the
district's earliest decades, not something the intervening centuries deepened into existence.

## What It Feels Like

Vast, open stretches of frozen wasteland, sprinkled with living arrangements and occasional Rastras —
genuinely wild territory, clustered into isolated clans rather than one continuous settlement, each
cluster operating independently but bound by a shared, unspoken understanding that they have each
other's backs. Most Rastras are parked; a small handful are out actively driving. The district reads as
generally quiet and sparse, punctuated by real bursts of activity. None of this atmosphere is
war-produced — it's the direct, lived consequence of building a frontier civilization from nothing in a
lethal environment, present from the district's earliest habitable years. Soundscape: wind, constant and
audible, with Rastras plowing directly through it during operation.

## Personality (Enneagram Read)

**Instinctive (with Thinking undertones) / Assertive / Positive Outlook** — unchanged from the post-war
read. Raw survival against the ice, expansion drive, philosophical confrontation with the wilderness;
bold pioneering, independence from central control; optimistic idealism, reframing harsh conditions as
adventure or philosophical growth. This is the district's own founding-era personality, not a trait the
war produced or sharpened.

## What's Actually Open

- **Pace** — not directly confirmed; whether there's real urgency to movement given the cold, or a
  different rhythm entirely, applies just as much pre-war as post.
- **What actually defines a "clan"** — family lineage, shared profession, or (per the post-war Full
  Extrapolation's own proposal) shared risk-history — already an open, live question in the founding era,
  since clan structure would have needed to form early, not late.
- **The living-arrangement structures' own detail** (materials, permanence, size) beyond being clustered
  and expedition-derived.
- **Who refines Sagittarius' extracted precursor materials into finished siligel** — the deep dive's own
  proposed answer (Capricorn) is a pre-war mechanism from the moment the Rush begins, c. 2772; worth
  carrying into this document's own Full Extrapolation.
- **Whether the oral-tradition preservation crisis has already claimed anything specific** by the end of
  this period — a live, ongoing risk throughout the interwar centuries, not a post-war development.

## Worth Your Attention

The single most load-bearing fact carried over unchanged from the post-war Megasheet is that Sagittarius'
bitterness toward central authority was never generic frontier resentment to begin with — it's a specific,
repeatable grievance about proving the district's own value and not being credited for it. Pre-war,
that grievance has exactly one confirmed instance (the Siligel Rush's stranded political power). The
post-war district's version of the same grievance has two (the Rush, then the war's own caravan
logistics). This document is therefore the origin point of a pattern that only fully compounds once the
war arrives — worth reading as the "before" half of a two-part story whose second half doesn't exist yet.
