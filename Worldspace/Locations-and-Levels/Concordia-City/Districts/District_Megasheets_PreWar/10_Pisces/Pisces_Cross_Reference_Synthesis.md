# Pisces (Pre-War) — Cross-Reference Synthesis

**What this is:** the deliberate cross-referencing pass across this pre-war Megasheet's own Mega-Init and
Full Extrapolation, plus the existing post-war Pisces Megasheet and deep dive, looking for connections
between facts that live in different documents and were never actually placed next to each other before.
This document stays purely descriptive. Written 2026-07-16.

---

## Finding 1: Pisces is the one founding-era district whose entire identity is compatible with all three of Concordia's other deliberately-unresolved pre-war events

**Combining:** this pre-war Megasheet's own status as a second-order district (shaped by the excess of
the other five direct-response districts, not the founding wound directly) + the fact that Pisces itself
carried two of Concordia's three then-flagged uncertain pre-war events (the Tolerance Pact and the
Flood), with the third (Leo's Star War) sitting in a district Pisces already has an established relationship
with via ex-Leo burnouts. *(Update 2026-07-29 — all three since resolved: the Tolerance Pact and Leo's Star
War fully, the Flood's mechanism (not its exact date) via Candidate D, "The Mosaic." See `Deep_Dives/10d_Pisces_Tolerance_Pact.md`, `Deep_Dives/03b_Leo_Star_War_Alternatives.md`, and `Deep_Dives/10b_Pisces_Flood_Mechanism.md`.)*

**2nd-order effect:** Pisces' own founding character — catching whoever the other districts' methods
don't work for — means it's structurally the one district whose pre-war identity doesn't depend on any
of the three open questions being resolved in any particular way. Whatever Leo's Star War turns out to
be, whatever Capricorn's 2761 exposure turns out to be, whatever the Flood turns out to be, Pisces
receives some of the overflow either way.

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Pisces' own pre-war Megasheet the single most future-proof of the three
"deliberately unresolved" documents built so far — its core identity doesn't just tolerate the other
open questions staying open, it's actively strengthened by them staying open, since an unresolved Star
War or The Narrow Door still plausibly produces ex-Leo or ex-Capricorn arrivals in Pisces regardless of
the specific final shape either conflict takes.

**4th-order effect:** worth treating Pisces as a genuine structural pressure valve for this whole
pre-war Megasheet project, not just a district in it — any future pre-war district whose own central
conflict stays genuinely undecided can plausibly route its own overflow population through Pisces
without requiring Pisces' own document to be revised.

---

## Finding 2: The Tolerance Pact's own pre-war precedent (Full Extrapolation §II) mirrors Capricorn's own pre-war calcification pattern, just moving in the opposite direction

**Combining:** this document's own proposed pre-Pact relationship (scattered individual accommodations,
not yet a citywide arrangement) + Capricorn's pre-war Cross-Reference Finding 1 (a genuine, temporary
crisis measure that calcifies into permanent structure because nobody with power to end it has any
incentive to).

**2nd-order effect:** where Capricorn's pattern describes something that starts necessary and hardens
into unjust, Pisces' own Libra relationship describes something that starts as scattered, individual,
low-stakes accommodation and — if it ever becomes the Tolerance Pact — hardens into a deliberate,
citywide institutional arrangement. Both are calcification stories, but Capricorn's is a story of
drift (nobody chooses the outcome, it simply never gets revisited), while Pisces' potential Pact would
be a story of consolidation (individual precedents adding up to something eventually worth
formalizing).

**3rd-order effect:** this gives Concordia's founding-era history two genuinely distinct models for how
an informal arrangement becomes a lasting institution — worth keeping both in mind as templates for any
other district's own still-open pre-war questions, rather than assuming every calcification story follows
Capricorn's drift-based shape.

---

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across Both Findings

Both findings position Pisces as a genuinely structural, connective piece of Concordia's own pre-war
history rather than an isolated district. Finding 1 shows Pisces receiving overflow from the other
currently-unresolved questions regardless of how they resolve. Finding 2 shows Pisces' own potential
Tolerance Pact following a different, equally plausible calcification model than Capricorn's, expanding
this project's own understanding of how Concordia's founding-era institutions actually form.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Finding 1 is the more significant of the two — it's a genuine structural insight about how this whole
"deliberately unresolved" approach interacts across multiple districts at once, not just within Pisces'
own document. Pisces isn't merely tolerant of open questions elsewhere in Concordia; its own founding
character actively depends on other districts having imperfect, exclusionary methods, whether or not any
specific flagged event ever gets pinned down.
