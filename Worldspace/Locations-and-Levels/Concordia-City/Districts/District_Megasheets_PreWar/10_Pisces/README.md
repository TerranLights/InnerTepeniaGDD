# Pisces (Pre-War) — Mega-Init

**Step 1 of the Pre-War District Megasheet compilation process.** Companion document to the existing,
post-war `District_Megasheets/10_Pisces/Pisces_Mega_Init.md` — not a replacement for it. Reconstructs
Pisces as it stood during the Second Interwar Period (roughly 2564–2812), before the Long Night War.
Synthesizes `Deep_Dives/10_Pisces_Deep_Dive.md`. Compiled 2026-07-16.

**Scope note — Pisces carried two separate open items at the time this was written, both since resolved.** (1) **The Tolerance
Pact** carried the same developer-flagged uncertainty as Leo's Star War and Capricorn's core injustice — "not sure yet what this actually is or whether it stays in canon." This document, written before that resolution, treated the Pact as **not yet formalized**, while building Pisces' relationship with Libra so
that an arrangement shaped like it remains a natural outcome. *(Update 2026-07-29 — resolved: the Pact stays in canon, unrenamed, reframed as a never-negotiated equilibrium individual Libra operatives quietly arrived at over decades, rather than a deliberate deal — see `Deep_Dives/10d_Pisces_Tolerance_Pact.md`. This document's own pre-war build-up already supports that reading without needing revision.)* (2) **The Flood** was a separate,
already-canon event whose own *timing and mechanical nature* — not its existence — were flagged as
needing a dedicated pass (`10_Pisces_Deep_Dive.md`, `Pisces_Full_Extrapolation.md`, both explicit). This
document followed that same existing discipline: it didn't invent a mechanism or confirm a date, but it
did build Pisces' pre-war conditions (underground clinics routing unregulated Aquarius technology,
already-porous population) so that *some* Flood-shaped event remains a natural outcome whenever it's
eventually pinned down. *(Update 2026-07-29 — the mechanism is now resolved, Candidate D "The Mosaic," see `Deep_Dives/10b_Pisces_Flood_Mechanism.md`; the exact date remains deliberately open, still compatible with this document's own hedged framing.)*

tests any of those rules — a second-order district, shaped not by the founding wound directly but by the
necessary excess of everyone else's own particular, exclusionary way of coping with it.*

## Who Lives Here, and Why

The same founding-era population the post-war district describes: failed artists, trauma cases too
intense for Scorpio's own confrontation-based methods, smugglers, and those seeking chemical or digital
escape — drawn here from the very start by the district's own porous architecture and lower enforcement
priority. Robots, present from early in the interwar period: early fluid-frame and reconfiguration-
capable units, neural-interface specialists (already working, already somewhat unregulated, well before
any specific incident forces the question of regulation). Cultural lean, present from founding: Feeling +
Withdrawn/Reactive with strong Instinctive undercurrents — compassion mixed with self-destruction, and a
belief that rigid categories eventually fail everyone, already true of the population Pisces draws from
founding, not a trait the district develops later.

Per the deep dive, Pisces is structurally a second-order district from the very start — not organized
around the original exile wound directly, the way Cancer, Scorpio, Aries, Aquarius, and Libra each are,
but around catching whoever those five founding-era methods don't work for. This makes Pisces' own
founding character genuinely parasitic on the other five districts' own founding choices, in the most
literal, structural sense, from day one.

---

## What It Feels Like

The porous, shifting, difficult-to-navigate physical character already established for post-war
Pisces — walls that move, routes that don't stay reliable — is already present pre-war, since it's
partly practical (low-visibility architecture serves a marginal, informally-tolerated population well
before there's any specific black-market economy to protect) and partly the district's own emerging
philosophical relationship to identity and permanence. What's different from the post-war district: no
fully-formed Syncretic Religion yet (its later doctrine, per the deep dive, plausibly crystallizes only
after some kind of formative experiential event — deliberately not narrated here), and no proven,
war-tested claim to being the city's actual economic lifeline. Pre-war Pisces is genuinely marginal, not
yet indispensable.

## What's Actually Open

- **Whether, or in what form, the Tolerance Pact eventually formalizes** — deliberately unresolved here.
  This document builds the underlying condition (Libra's periodic, inconsistent crackdowns; Pisces'
  own quiet usefulness to individual Libra operatives even before any citywide arrangement) without
  committing to the specific unwritten, ~60-year-old arrangement currently on record.
- **The Flood's own timing and mechanism** — deliberately unresolved here, consistent with the existing
  flag in `Pisces_Full_Extrapolation.md` and the deep dive. This document adds nothing new to that
  question beyond establishing that Pisces' own pre-war conditions (underground clinics, unregulated
  neural-interface work routed from Aquarius) make some such event a natural outcome whenever it's
  pinned down.
- **Whether the Syncretic Religion has any pre-Flood precedent**, given the deep dive's own proposal that
  the religion's core doctrine is likely a *retrospective* framework built to explain the Flood after the
  fact — if so, pre-war Pisces (before whatever event eventually happens) plausibly has no organized
  syncretic doctrine at all yet, only the raw ingredients (Goth death practices, Cymaticist sound work)
  it will later draw from.

# Pisces (Pre-War) — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** open questions from `Pisces_Mega_Init.md` (this same pre-war folder) given proposed
answers, chosen specifically so they hold true regardless of how the Tolerance Pact and the Flood
questions are eventually resolved. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by
piece. Written 2026-07-16.

---

## I. Demonym

**Proposed: Piscean.** Straightforward, consistent with standard demonym formation, and independent of
either open question.

---

## II. The Pre-Pact Relationship With Libra

**Proposed:** an ongoing, genuinely inconsistent pattern rather than a settled arrangement — periods of
real enforcement effort from Libra, alternating with periods of quiet neglect, alongside individual
Libra operatives who, unofficially and on their own initiative, occasionally use Pisces' own
information-economy channels for things they can't officially acquire. No citywide understanding exists
yet; what exists is a scattered pattern of individual accommodations that a later, more centralized
Tolerance Pact (if it happens) would plausibly formalize and generalize, rather than invent from
nothing. This gives the eventual Pact, whatever form it takes, a genuine decades-long precedent to
crystallize out of.

---

## III. What Pisces' Underground Clinics Actually Do, Pre-Flood

**Proposed:** legitimate-adjacent work that simply doesn't fit inside other districts' own more
regulated frameworks — Aquarius-originated experimental technology that failed to clear ethical review
elsewhere, offered here with less oversight and correspondingly higher risk, alongside ordinary
underground medical and psychological care for people other districts' formal systems don't adequately
serve. This is already recognizably the shape of what the post-war district's dream-tech dens and
neural-interface specialists do — the Flood, whenever and however it's eventually pinned down, would be
this same already-existing practice producing an unusually severe, district-altering outcome, not a
wholly new kind of activity appearing from nowhere.

# Pisces (Pre-War) — Cross-Reference Synthesis

**What this is:** the deliberate cross-referencing pass across this pre-war Megasheet's own Mega-Init and
Full Extrapolation, plus the existing post-war Pisces Megasheet and deep dive, looking for connections
between facts that live in different documents and were never actually placed next to each other before.
This document stays purely descriptive. Written 2026-07-16.

---

## Finding 1: Pisces is the one founding-era district whose entire identity is compatible with all three of Concordia's other deliberately-unresolved pre-war events

**Combining:** this pre-war Megasheet's own status as a second-order district (shaped by the excess of
the other five direct-response districts, not the founding wound directly) + the fact that Pisces itself
carried two of Concordia's three then-flagged uncertain pre-war events (the Tolerance Pact and the
Flood), with the third (Leo's Star War) sitting in a district Pisces already has an established relationship
with via ex-Leo burnouts. *(Update 2026-07-29 — all three since resolved: the Tolerance Pact and Leo's Star
War fully, the Flood's mechanism (not its exact date) via Candidate D, "The Mosaic." See `Deep_Dives/10d_Pisces_Tolerance_Pact.md`, `Deep_Dives/03b_Leo_Star_War_Alternatives.md`, and `Deep_Dives/10b_Pisces_Flood_Mechanism.md`.)*

**2nd-order effect:** Pisces' own founding character — catching whoever the other districts' methods
don't work for — means it's structurally the one district whose pre-war identity doesn't depend on any
of the three open questions being resolved in any particular way. Whatever Leo's Star War turns out to
be, whatever Capricorn's 2761 exposure turns out to be, whatever the Flood turns out to be, Pisces
receives some of the overflow either way.

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Pisces' own pre-war Megasheet the single most future-proof of the three
"deliberately unresolved" documents built so far — its core identity doesn't just tolerate the other
open questions staying open, it's actively strengthened by them staying open, since an unresolved Star
War or The Narrow Door still plausibly produces ex-Leo or ex-Capricorn arrivals in Pisces regardless of
the specific final shape either conflict takes.

**4th-order effect:** worth treating Pisces as a genuine structural pressure valve for this whole
pre-war Megasheet project, not just a district in it — any future pre-war district whose own central
conflict stays genuinely undecided can plausibly route its own overflow population through Pisces
without requiring Pisces' own document to be revised.

---

## Finding 2: The Tolerance Pact's own pre-war precedent (Full Extrapolation §II) mirrors Capricorn's own pre-war calcification pattern, just moving in the opposite direction

**Combining:** this document's own proposed pre-Pact relationship (scattered individual accommodations,
not yet a citywide arrangement) + Capricorn's pre-war Cross-Reference Finding 1 (a genuine, temporary
crisis measure that calcifies into permanent structure because nobody with power to end it has any
incentive to).

**2nd-order effect:** where Capricorn's pattern describes something that starts necessary and hardens
into unjust, Pisces' own Libra relationship describes something that starts as scattered, individual,
low-stakes accommodation and — if it ever becomes the Tolerance Pact — hardens into a deliberate,
citywide institutional arrangement. Both are calcification stories, but Capricorn's is a story of
drift (nobody chooses the outcome, it simply never gets revisited), while Pisces' potential Pact would
be a story of consolidation (individual precedents adding up to something eventually worth
formalizing).

**3rd-order effect:** this gives Concordia's founding-era history two genuinely distinct models for how
an informal arrangement becomes a lasting institution — worth keeping both in mind as templates for any
other district's own still-open pre-war questions, rather than assuming every calcification story follows
Capricorn's drift-based shape.

---

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across Both Findings

Both findings position Pisces as a genuinely structural, connective piece of Concordia's own pre-war
history rather than an isolated district. Finding 1 shows Pisces receiving overflow from the other
currently-unresolved questions regardless of how they resolve. Finding 2 shows Pisces' own potential
Tolerance Pact following a different, equally plausible calcification model than Capricorn's, expanding
this project's own understanding of how Concordia's founding-era institutions actually form.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Finding 1 is the more significant of the two — it's a genuine structural insight about how this whole
"deliberately unresolved" approach interacts across multiple districts at once, not just within Pisces'
own document. Pisces isn't merely tolerant of open questions elsewhere in Concordia; its own founding
character actively depends on other districts having imperfect, exclusionary methods, whether or not any
specific flagged event ever gets pinned down.
