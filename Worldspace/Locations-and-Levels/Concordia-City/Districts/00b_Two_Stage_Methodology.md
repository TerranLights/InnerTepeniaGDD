# Methodology Note — Two-Stage District Analysis

**Established:** 2026-07-09, mid-analysis, at the developer's own correction.

## The distinction

Every district-by-district deep dive from here forward should separate two genuinely different causal layers, rather than treating a district's history as one continuous chain from founding to present day:

**Stage 1 — Organic formation (2564–2812, the Second Interwar Period, ~250 years):** how did this specific community actually become itself, on its own terms, through 250 years of lived experience — independent of the Long Night War, which hadn't happened yet? This is the same kind of question `City_Origin_Factions_Second_Interwar.md` already asks and answers for Tepenia's 35 actual cities: a living culture shaped by its own circumstances, not yet touched by the war that ends the period.

**Stage 2 — The war's fallout as a second, later force (2812 onward):** how did the Long Night War's actual fallout land on that *already-mature*, 250-year-old identity, and reshape, test, deepen, or partially break it into the specific present-day (≈2822–2827) district the player actually encounters? This is the same kind of question `City_Origin_Factions_PostWar_Refugee.md` already asks for the same 35 cities — a separate, later layer, not a continuation of the same story by other means.

## Why this matters

A district's current-day character is a **"double result"** — the outcome of the war's pressure acting on a population that was *already something specific*, not a blank community that the war alone shaped from scratch. Skipping Stage 1 makes every district's war-era trauma feel arbitrary (why did *this* population respond to catastrophe *this* way?) rather than earned by 250 years of pre-existing cultural formation.

Concretely, for Cancer: the existing `District_Canon_Reference.md` entry jumps almost straight from "designated as sanctuary zone" (a zoning fact) to "absorbed enormous collective trauma" during the Long Night War (2812) — with barely any exploration of what actually happened in the ~250 years *between* those two points, when the district was already a living community with its own accumulated culture, before the war ever touched it.

## How to apply this going forward

For each district, ask Stage 1 and Stage 2 as genuinely separate questions:
1. Given the founding-era zoning/circumstance, what population actually arrived, and why would *they specifically* (not a generic population) develop the district's established cultural texture over the following ~250 years, through ordinary Second-Interwar-Period life — not war, just time, proximity, and lived circumstance?
2. Only then: how did the Long Night War's fallout act on that already-formed community specifically — testing it, breaking parts of it, deepening other parts, adding a second layer of trauma or transformation on top of (not instead of) the first?

This doesn't discard anything already written — it's a lens for reading the existing material more carefully, and for filling in what's genuinely missing between "founding" and "war."
