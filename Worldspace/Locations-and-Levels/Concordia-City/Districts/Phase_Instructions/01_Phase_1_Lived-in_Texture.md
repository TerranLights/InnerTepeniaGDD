# Phase 1 — Lived-in Texture (Architecture, Sensory Profile, Export Culture)

**Purpose:** close the three "present but inconsistent" gaps from the original city-template comparison —
Architecture (4/13 districts had it), Sensory Profile (2/13), Export Culture (1/13, and even that one
misplaced). This is the lowest-lift phase and the most direct physical foundation for everything after it:
Phase 6 (Thematic Breadth) needs to know what a district looks/sounds/smells like before placing things in it,
and Phase 8 (Robot-Specific Culture) needs to know what a district produces and where robots would physically
be before describing their culture.

**Status as of 2026-08-16:** Cancer, Taurus, and Leo are done. **Read `00b_General_Population_Discipline.md`
first** — Sensory Profile's "first impressions" sub-field is a real risk for the same narrow-context-as-default
mistake caught in Fashion (Phase 2).

## 0. Do the real-world research ONCE, up front, before Phase 1 — it feeds all eight phases

**Added 2026-08-16, from Cancer's from-scratch rewrite.** `Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md` was
originally scoped mainly to Phase 6, on the assumption that places/things/people were where real-world picks
paid off. Cancer's rewrite disproved that: properly-researched picks fed **every phase**, and several fed
multiple at once. Hundertwasser's Window Right became Architecture *and* Sensory Profile *and* Ordinary Daily
Life *and* Thematic Breadth; Arcosanti's bell foundry became Architecture, Sensory Profile, Export Culture, and
a location; Findhorn's Attunement became the backbone of Ordinary Daily Life and a Robot-Specific Culture
finding.

**So: before writing anything for a district, research all of its `District-Inspirational-Influences.md` picks
— every tier, Primary through Supporting — at the concrete level, and keep the results to hand for the whole
run.** Doing it per-phase wastes effort and, worse, means later phases never see material that would have
answered them. Cancer's own research summary is recorded at the head of its Phase 1-7 block in
`Cancer_Full_Extrapolation.md` as the format to follow.

## 1. What each category actually asks for

Pulled directly from `Outside-World/.../Local_Cultures/CITY_CULTURE_TEMPLATE.md` — districts are held to the
same bar cities were, adapted only where a district-specific translation is genuinely needed:

- **Architecture (template §9):** *"building styles; urban character; how the built environment reflects the
  culture; what layers of history are visible; how the city relates to its physical terrain."* District
  translation: "physical terrain" doesn't apply the way it does to an outer city (all 13 districts sit on the
  same city, no independent geology) — read this as *how the built environment relates to the district's
  physical constraints within Concordia* (available footprint, proximity to the Hub, whatever structural role
  the district plays in the city's own layout) rather than natural terrain.
- **Sensory Profile (template §14):** four sub-fields, answer each explicitly — **Sound**, **Smell**, **Feel**,
  **First impressions for visitors**. Don't collapse these into one paragraph; the template's own bulleted
  structure exists because a district can be visually calm and acoustically tense, or vice versa, and losing
  that distinction loses the finding.
- **Export Culture (template §25):** *"what this [district] is known for producing and sending to the rest of
  [Concordia]: goods, ideas, art, people, technology, a particular type of person."* District translation:
  "rest of Tepenia" becomes "rest of Concordia's other 12 districts" — a district's export target is the city
  around it, not the outer Federation (though a district's export could in principle also reach outer cities via
  Concordia's own outward connections; note this if it comes up, but don't force it).

## 3. Sources to read, per district, before writing anything

Per the Compilation Guide's own Step 0 discipline ("read everything before writing anything" — the value of
later cross-referencing depends on holding all sources in mind at once), pull these in full for the target
district before starting:

1. **`District_Megasheets/{NN}_{Name}/{Name}_Mega_Init.md`** — especially the "What It Feels Like" section
   (built directly from Vision Notes language) and the Hard Facts table (founding era, founding population).
   This is usually the richest existing architecture/sensory source, even when not labeled as such.
2. **`District_Megasheets/{NN}_{Name}/{Name}_Full_Extrapolation.md`** — read every existing Finding. Cancer's
   own Finding II (haze from plant-transpiration scale) and Finding IV (strain hidden behind institutional
   walls, not street-level) are exactly the kind of incidental architecture/sensory material that's already
   there and just needs formalizing — expect the same pattern in most districts.
3. **`District_Canon_Reference.md`** — that district's own "What It Really Is," "Cultural Texture," and
   Community Infrastructure & Social Life section. Named physical spaces (Cancer's Threshold Waiting Rooms,
   Communal Warm-Soak Houses) are concrete architecture/sensory material hiding in a section not labeled either.
4. **`Regional-Characteristics/District_Prominent_Features.md`** and
   **`Regional-Characteristics/district_summaries_with_traits_and_features.md`** — quick-reference landmark and
   trait data.
5. **`District-Inspirational-Influences.md`**, that district's own Primary/Secondary/Supporting picks — apply
   `Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md` here specifically for Architecture: research each real-world
   pick's actual building materials, construction style, and urban layout, not just its general vibe.
6. **`Deep_Dives/{NN}_{Name}_Deep_Dive.md`** and, if it exists, that district's `District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities/{NN}_{Name}.md`
   entry — Stage 1 (pre-war, organic) material relevant to how the district was originally built, distinct from
   any Stage 2 (war-era) additions or damage.
7. **`District_Vision_Notes/{Name}.md`** — per the History Enhancement Template's own finding, this sometimes
   holds a fuller version of a detail the Mega-Init only names in passing. Check before assuming a detail is
   fully resolved elsewhere.
8. **Export Culture specifically:** that district's stated civic function/economy (Hard Facts "Zodiac / Role"
   row), `District_Refugee_Diaspora_Composition.md` (what flows *in* — useful contrast for reasoning about what
   flows *out*), and any existing Notable Figures or named crafts/trades already established (Cancer's Second
   Chair Workshops).
9. **The color-coded district map** (`Reference/Images/Maps/Concordia_City_-_Extended_map_-_with_labels_-_Color-Coded_by_District.jpeg`) —
   directly relevant to all three Phase 1 categories. Confirms whether the target district is a small,
   Hub-adjacent wedge (Gemini, Leo, Aquarius, Scorpio, Aries, Virgo, Libra) or one of the two huge outer-ring
   giants with no Hub contact (Sagittarius, Capricorn) or a middle-ring district (Cancer, Taurus, Pisces) — this
   directly shapes Architecture (a tiny central district's built environment reads differently than a sprawling
   peripheral one) and Export Culture (Sagittarius and Capricorn are Concordia's only two districts touching an
   actual highway ramp to the rest of Tepenia — see `District_Culture_Development_Plan.md`'s Governing
   Methodology §4 for the full breakdown).

## 4. Process

**Step A — Formalize before inventing.** For all three categories, first check whether the district already has
scattered-but-real material (per Cancer's own precedent — its Architecture was real but unlabeled, spread
between Mega_Init and two Full_Extrapolation Findings). If so, the first move is consolidation: name the
existing pattern, cite exactly where each piece already lives, and only then add what's genuinely missing. Don't
write a from-scratch Architecture section for a district that already has one, just uncredited.

**Step B — Apply the Two-Stage lens to Architecture specifically.** Architecture is the one Phase 1 category
where Stage 1 vs. Stage 2 (`00b_Two_Stage_Methodology.md`) routinely produces a genuine finding, not just a
grounding check: a district's original ~250-year Stage 1 construction (built with time and no war pressure) is
plausibly visually/structurally distinct from anything added or damaged during Stage 2's war-era fallout. Ask
explicitly: does this district have a visible architectural "seam" between its founding-era design philosophy
and anything added under later pressure? Cancer's answer (Finding VIII) was yes — the original curved,
plant-integrated design vs. faster, more clinical Overcrowding-Decision-era institutional additions.

**Step C — Sensory Profile: answer all four sub-fields separately, then check for an institutional/street
split.** Per Cancer's Finding IV precedent (strain hidden behind institutional walls, not visible on the
street), many districts likely have a public-register sensory profile that differs from what their institutions
feel/sound/smell like internally. Check whether this split exists for the target district before assuming one
uniform sensory identity throughout.

**Step D — Export Culture: use the district's civic function as the anchor, not a blank brainstorm.** A
district's Hard Facts "Zodiac / Role" row already states its civic function — ask what that function would
plausibly produce as an export in each of Export Culture's own categories (goods, ideas, art, people,
technology, "a particular type of person"). Favor concrete, named exports over generic claims ("Cancer exports
comfort" is too vague; "Cancer exports trained companion-model caregiving robots" and "Cancer-trained
integration specialists" are specific and traceable). Where a district's Stage 2 Override status is relevant
(Scorpio, Gemini — see `District_Culture_Development_Plan.md`'s per-district notes), consider whether the
district's export identity is shaped by that trauma-driven population rather than by an ordinary Stage 1
economy.

**Step D.5 — For Sagittarius and Capricorn specifically, factor in gateway status.** These are Concordia's only
two districts physically touching a highway ramp to the rest of Tepenia (color-coded map). Their Export Culture
plausibly includes literal goods-in-transit, not just internally-consumed-by-Concordia exports — and their
Architecture/Sensory Profile plausibly show real gateway/customs/logistics character (checkpoints, freight
staging, the sensory signature of through-traffic) that a Hub-adjacent interior district wouldn't have.

**Step E — Apply the 5-lens technique from `District_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Template.md` if any
category feels short on material:** district civic function/economy, feeder-city population culture (checking
Stage 2 Overrides first), real-world historical precedent, district personality/geography/architecture, other
(`Historical_Pressures.md`, `Historical_Inter-District_Effects.md`, open Mega-Init questions). Not every lens
needs to produce a finding for every category — use it as a checklist when stuck, not a mandatory five-for-five
quota.

## 6. Output format and placement

- New content goes into `District_Megasheets/{NN}_{Name}/{Name}_Full_Extrapolation.md` as new Roman-numeral
  Findings, continuing the district's own existing numbering (Cancer's example: existing I-VII, new VIII-X).
- Each new Finding's heading should name what it covers, followed by a tag identifying the source pass — Cancer's
  convention: `## VIII. Architecture — formalizing what's already established, not inventing from scratch
  (District Culture Development Plan, Phase 1)`.
- Each Finding opens with **Proposed:**, matching every existing Finding in the file — this is not locked canon,
  per the Compilation Guide's Step 2 framing rule.
- Name things specifically (proper nouns) wherever the finding produces a nameable object, place, or practice.
- After all three (or however many are still needed) new Findings are added, update the file's own **Worth Your
  Attention** section — either note the new material's own standout (per Cancer's example, which flagged Finding
  VIII specifically) or explicitly confirm the existing callout still holds and why.

---

## 7. Rules and pitfalls specific to this phase

- **Don't treat "verify depth" districts as free passes.** A keyword-match "has it" is not the same as "done."
- **Don't force Export Culture into goods if the district's real export is people or expertise** — Cancer's own
  worked example produced zero physical-good exports and three people/practice exports, which is the correct
  outcome for a caregiving-function district, not an incomplete one.
- **Check Stage 2 Overrides before using feeder-city data for Export Culture's population-lens reasoning**
  (Scorpio, Gemini confirmed cases — see `District_Culture_Development_Plan.md`).
- **Capricorn caution:** its core injustice mechanism is still undecided (`Deep_Dives/06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md`).
  Architecture and Sensory Profile can likely proceed without it, but Export Culture reasoning that depends on
  Capricorn's specific institutional character may need to stay provisional until that decision lands.
