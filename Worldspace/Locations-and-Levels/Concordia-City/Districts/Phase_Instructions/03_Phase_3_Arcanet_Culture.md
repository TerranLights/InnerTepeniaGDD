# Phase 3 — Arcanet Culture

**Purpose:** close the last of the "present but inconsistent" gaps. Arcanet Culture had zero districts with a
genuinely *dedicated* section — Gemini and Sagittarius only had incidental mentions scattered across
Full_Extrapolation/Mega_Init/README. This phase formalizes a real, dedicated Arcanet Culture Finding for all 13.

**Read `00b_General_Population_Discipline.md` first.** If the strongest existing material is a specific
institution's Arcanet use (like Cancer's Mother's Circuit — genuinely caregiving-robot-specific), state the
district's own general information-culture baseline separately and explicitly — don't let the specific
institution stand in for the general answer. Cancer's own Finding XIII does this correctly; use it as the model.

---

## 1. What this category actually asks for

Template §24: *"how this city uses and relates to its subnet's Arcanet; heavy information culture vs. oral
culture; archival vs. social; how privacy norms shape network behavior."* District translation: districts don't
belong to a subnet the way outer cities do — Concordia's own Arcanet presence is the relevant frame, and Gemini
(the district literally named/themed "Data Nexus") is the obvious center of gravity for the whole city's network
character, the way a subnet hub city anchors its own subnet's Arcanet culture.

---

## 2. Prerequisites

None strictly required, but Phase 1's Architecture/Sensory Profile work (if done for the target district) gives
Arcanet Culture something physical to attach to — network infrastructure has a location and a feel.

---

## 3. Sources to read, per district

1. **Gemini's own material first, regardless of which district you're working on.** `District_Megasheets/09_Gemini/`
   (all 4 files) plus its README/Mega_Init incidental Arcanet mentions — Gemini is Concordia's Data Nexus, so
   every other district's Arcanet Culture finding should read as a relationship *to* Gemini (how connected,
   how dependent, how resistant), not as an independent invention.
2. **Sagittarius's own incidental mentions** (Mega_Init/README) — the second district with existing material,
   also needing promotion into a dedicated Finding.
3. **`feedback_extreme_altitude_arcanet` memory / its underlying source** — established canon that
   extreme-altitude outer locations (Dome Fuji, Kunlun, Vostok) have basically no Arcanet presence. Check
   whether an analogous "low-connectivity" district exists within Concordia (a plausible candidate: Virgo/the
   Undergrid, given its underground, isolated character per the Source Index's own findings) before assuming
   uniform high connectivity across all 13.
4. **`feedback_arcanet_solarnet_naming` memory** — "Arcanet"/"Solarnet" are always standalone terms, never used
   as a modifier with an article. Apply this terminology rule in every new Finding.
5. **The color-coded district map** — Gemini sits in the innermost ring, directly Hub-adjacent, which is a
   literal, physical confirmation (not just thematic naming) that it's Concordia's network center. Distance
   from Gemini/the Hub (inner-ring neighbor vs. outer-ring giant like Sagittarius or Capricorn) is a plausible,
   concrete driver of how strong/reliable a district's own Arcanet connection actually is — the two outer-ring
   districts with no Hub contact are the most physically remote from Gemini and are worth checking as
   lower-connectivity candidates for exactly that reason.
6. **`District_Refugee_Diaspora_Composition.md`** — a district's feeder-city population may already have an
   established oral-vs-archival information culture (from that city's own Local_Cultures file) worth carrying
   through as a real Deep Culture inheritance, per the "heavy information culture vs. oral culture" prompt.

---

## 4. Process

**Step A — Establish Gemini's Finding first if it doesn't already have a proper dedicated one**, since every
other district's Arcanet Culture content should be written *relative to* Gemini, not independently. Promote
Gemini's existing incidental mentions into one real Finding before writing any of the other 12.

**Step B — For every other district, ask: how connected is this district to Gemini's network, and what does
that connection feel like day to day?** Use the map's inner-ring/outer-ring distinction as a starting hypothesis
(closer = plausibly more integrated/dependent; the two outer-ring giants = plausibly more independent or
under-served), then check whether the district's own established civic function supports or complicates that
hypothesis — a district whose whole function is information-adjacent (Libra/Government, plausibly heavy
archival use) might be highly connected despite physical distance, while a purely industrial district
(Capricorn) might have real infrastructure but low social/cultural engagement with it.

**Step C — Decide oral vs. archival, social vs. private, for each district specifically**, per the template's
own four contrast pairs. Don't default every district to the same answer — Cancer's established care-debt
economy (informal, unwritten, socially-tracked) already implies a real oral-culture lean; a district like Gemini
or Libra plausibly leans hard archival instead. Let each district's existing established character answer this
rather than picking generically.

**Step D — Check the Extreme-Altitude Arcanet precedent before assuming universal high connectivity.** If a
district (Virgo is the leading candidate, given its Undergrid/underground character) plausibly has a real,
low-connectivity Arcanet relationship, write that as a genuine finding rather than defaulting every district to
the same connected baseline the way a first, un-differentiated pass might.

**Step E — Terminology check before finalizing:** "Arcanet" and "Solarnet" always stand alone, never modified
with an article or turned into a compound adjective phrase (per `feedback_arcanet_solarnet_naming`).

## 6. Output format and placement

Same convention: one new Roman-numeral Finding per district in `{Name}_Full_Extrapolation.md`, tagged
`(District Culture Development Plan, Phase 3)`, opening with **Proposed:**. For Gemini and Sagittarius, the
Finding should explicitly note it's a promotion/formalization of existing material (cite exactly where the
incidental mentions currently live) rather than presenting itself as entirely new.

---

## 7. Rules and pitfalls specific to this phase

- **Don't write all 13 districts' Arcanet Culture as structurally identical "connected, informative, useful"
  filler.** The whole point of this phase is differentiation — use Step C's four contrast pairs to actually
  vary the answer.
- **Don't skip Gemini.** Every other district's finding implicitly or explicitly references it; writing the
  other 12 first risks contradicting whatever Gemini's own finding eventually settles on.
- **Watch the terminology rule** — this is an easy, easy-to-miss slip ("the Arcanet's own district" reads fine
  casually but violates the standalone-term rule).
