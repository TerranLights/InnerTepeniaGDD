# Pisces — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/10_Pisces_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Pisces ("The Markets") is Concordia's black-market/dissolution district — where all the overflow the rest of the city can't accommodate ends up. Established texture: physical navigation is genuinely difficult (walls move, entrances appear and disappear, routes shift week to week), access to anything significant runs on an information economy (sharing something true and vulnerable about yourself) rather than a credit one, fluid-frame robots common here and rare elsewhere, and the district's underground spiritualist community and its black-market operation are actually the same community expressing two faces of the same practice.

---

## The developer's vision

**Loosely-organized chaos — always something happening.** A great place to land jobs and make money if morally-questionable work doesn't bother you. Confirmed the sense that if something can't be bought here directly, someone in Pisces can get it for you regardless.

**Crowded and cluttered at street level, bathed in a citywide wash of purple, blue, and red light** — a distinct palette from Gemini's yellow/blue/orange/green circuit-glow. Not loud, but not quiet either — a persistent moderate hum of activity.

**Conditionally dangerous rather than outright threatening.** People generally mind their own business; the danger surfaces specifically if you step on the wrong toes, not as an ambient, constant threat.

**The navigational instability is genuinely felt, not just implied** — the developer confirmed actually feeling lost from time to time while picturing moving through the district, not merely an abstract awareness that it might be possible to get lost.

**Pace: loitering, lingering** — not people moving briskly on business, but a slower, more lingering energy overall.
