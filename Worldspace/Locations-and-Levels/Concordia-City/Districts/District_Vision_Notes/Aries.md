# Aries — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/05_Aries_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Aries is Concordia's raw survival/energy district — literally the power core, and the district that keeps everyone else alive whether they acknowledge it or not. Established texture: overclocked heroism as a core value ("push it until it breaks" engineering), "running hot" (voluntarily taking dangerous shifts) as a status symbol, a barracks culture where crew loyalty supersedes district, family, or ideology, genuine contempt for "soft" districts that consume power but criticize how it's made, and a devotional-but-not-religious relationship with the machinery itself. It never goes dark and never goes quiet — silence is specifically what Aries avoids, the way Leo avoids darkness. It also carries real scars: the Overclock Martyrs and the Black Silence.

---

## The developer's vision

**Genuinely hot — hot enough to beat back Antarctica's own cold.** Constant active machinery throughout. Confirmed not intentionally oppressive, but a real environmental fact visitors would need to acclimate to in order to tolerate comfortably; not weaponized or hostile, just genuinely demanding.

**A culture of duty rather than pride — and quiet, unspoken self-respect rather than anything performed or stern.** People take their work seriously in a way that doesn't need to announce itself. Confirmed explicitly as quiet self-respect over intimidation or theatrical dedication.

**Nobody stops to acknowledge you.** Not from unfriendliness — everyone is genuinely absorbed in tending to their own obligations, heads-down, busy. Distinct in kind from Taurus' reserved not-the-welcoming-type quality; this reads more like everyone simply being occupied.

**Soundscape: humming from the energy cores, grinding machinery, dense industrial ambience** throughout.
