# Hub (Axis Mundi) — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/13_Hub_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: the Hub (Axis Mundi) is Concordia's neutral crossroads/governance nexus — the city's proof of concept that all these wildly different districts can physically coexist, and also a place where nobody is ever fully at home, which produced a specific Hub-born identity built around the pride of homelessness. Established texture: the Grand Transit Exchange, where every district's own culture visibly collides daily; the Bridge Memorial, deliberately uninscribed because every district reads it completely differently; visibly layered "patchwork" architecture from multiple eras of hasty wartime repair; and Hub-born residents who take real pride in professional detachment and adaptability, while privately holding strong opinions they rarely voice.

---

## The developer's vision

**A moderate population — not crowded in the usual sense, but a good number of people, both human and robot.** People visibly read as different from each other (reflecting their varied district origins), while it's also possible to pick out genuine "Hub people" from the mix, even amid that diversity — though what specifically marks someone as a Hub person, as opposed to a visitor passing through, was left open rather than pinned down this session.

**A mixed-use area** — lots of transit, but also shopping and office space woven in alongside it, not a purely transit-focused zone.

**The patchwork architecture reads as "semi-uniform."** Confirmed directly: you can tell the district was originally aiming for something more cohesive, but history had other plans, and the result is what could actually be made to work rather than the original vision.

**Soundscape: a general wash of conversation.**
