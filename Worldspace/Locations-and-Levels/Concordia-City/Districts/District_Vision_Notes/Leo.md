# Leo — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/03_Leo_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Leo is Concordia's cultural/performative district, where morale itself is treated as infrastructure. Established texture: a rigid but entirely unwritten recognition hierarchy (known face → crowd-stopper → district legend → city legend, with real informal privileges attached), the Golden Ring promenade functioning as the district's social nervous system, no real audience/performer distinction (everyone is always both), artistic lineages taken as seriously as blood lineages, and it's the district's warmest and brightest — deliberately so, since dark spaces are understood as psychologically dangerous after The Dimming (a six-month energy-crisis blackout Aries once inflicted on it). It also carries the scars of the Star War, a decades-long feud between rival performance houses still shaping today's "grand vs. intimate" aesthetic divide.

---

## The developer's vision

**Lights, activity, and constant hustle.** Everything reads as bright and loud. Multicolored lighting throughout, leaning slightly more blue and pink than the rest of the spectrum, but roughly evenly distributed across the full rainbow — confirmed as the district's overall, consistent color palette, not something that shifts noticeably by area.

**People actively draw you toward what they're doing.** Mostly in an atmospheric, metaphorical sense — the whole place has a pull to it — but occasionally literal, with people physically pulling you toward their specific activity.

**A welcoming feeling whose target is genuinely ambiguous.** It's unclear, even to the developer, whether this pull feels personal (people wanting *you* specifically involved) or general (the district built to pull everyone in regardless of who they are). Left open rather than forced to a decision — worth revisiting once more of Leo is built out, especially given the district's own established "no audience/performer distinction," which this ambiguity sits naturally alongside.

**Overwhelming, but genuinely welcoming rather than exhausting.** "Kind of a lot," but not in a way that reads as unpleasant.

**Soundscape: a mix of live music and crowd noise**, not one or the other — performance and audience sound blending together rather than staying separate.

## Still open from this session

- Whether the "pull toward participation" feeling is personal or general — explicitly left unresolved this session
- Whether specific areas of the Golden Ring or wider district have any distinguishable character within the overall bright/loud/colorful atmosphere, or whether it's uniform throughout
- How the recognition hierarchy (known face/crowd-stopper/district legend/city legend) would actually be felt or noticed by a first-time visitor, versus a repeat visitor
