# Gemini — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/09_Gemini_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Gemini (Janbogo Subnet Nexus) is Concordia's information/communication district — built on the belief that information solves problems, never fully reckoning that more information increases the need for judgment rather than reducing it. Established texture: the Twin Channels (an official public network and an unofficial shadow one, staffed by the same people, nobody officially acknowledging the shadow network exists), Truth Markets trading rumored and verified data side by side with reliability prices attached, a culture treating speed as a moral value even against accuracy, and constant organized noise. Also carries the Great Corruption, a still-mysterious event that wiped a huge portion of Janbogo's pre-war archives.

---

## The developer's vision

**The entire district reads as a literal, glowing circuit** — light-tracks in yellow, blue, orange, and green running along buildings, houses, and walls, as if the whole place were a circuit board. Confirmed the movement is genuine, not decorative: the lights represent actual data moving through the district. The fact that it's visually beautiful is a pleasant side-effect, not the point.

**Whether the specific colors carry deliberate meaning (different data types, sources, etc.) is genuinely uncertain — even to the developer.** Framed honestly as something a local, or someone who'd spent real time in Gemini, would be able to explain, but not something a newcomer (or the developer, picturing themselves as one) would necessarily know.

**The Twin Channels' hidden layer isn't visible from ground level, but wouldn't be surprising given how the district is laid out.** Not confirmed outright — held as a plausible impression based on the city's overall structure, not a directly observed detail.

**Soundscape: a general din of conversation — the computers themselves are mostly quiet.** The noise of Gemini comes from people talking, not from machinery, a notable contrast to Aries' and Capricorn's industrial soundscapes.
