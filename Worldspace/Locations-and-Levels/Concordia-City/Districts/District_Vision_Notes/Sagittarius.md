# Sagittarius — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/11_Sagittarius_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Sagittarius ("The Frostlands") is Concordia's frontier/exploration district — the one place where philosophical questions about what civilization is for aren't intellectual exercises but survival imperatives. Established texture: the cold is never abstract, it's the fundamental organizing fact of daily existence; storytelling is the district's primary cultural currency; almost no written institutional culture, knowledge transmitted through story and mentorship, creating a real vulnerability when experienced scouts die. Also carries its own self-inflicted wound: the Third Expansion Collapse, which killed sixty-three people, sitting in permanent tension with the district's "push further" ethos.

---

## The developer's vision

**Vast, open stretches of frozen wasteland, sprinkled with living arrangements and occasional Rastras.** Genuinely wild territory.

**Habitation is clustered into isolated clans, not one continuous settlement.** The clusters are separate and operate independently, but there's a shared understanding across all of Sagittarius that they have each other's backs — no hostility between clans, despite the isolation between them.

**Most Rastras are parked; a small handful are out actively driving.** The district reads as generally quiet and sparse rather than constantly bustling, with occasional pockets of real activity.

**The "wild" quality is specifically about emptiness, exposure, and a lack of visible infrastructure — not social danger.** Explicitly confirmed: the threat here isn't other people or wildlife, it's the cold itself. The environment is the district's central, ever-present concern.

**Soundscape: wind, constant and audible.** Rastras plow directly through it during operation, but the wind itself is a persistent, defining sound of the place.
