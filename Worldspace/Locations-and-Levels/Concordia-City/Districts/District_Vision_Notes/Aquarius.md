# Aquarius — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/07_Aquarius_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Aquarius ("The Labs") is Concordia's visionary/experimental district — full of people genuinely trying to save the world, with a systematic inability to consider what their good intentions look like from outside. Established texture: buildings showing four or five layered eras of experimental architecture, never torn down, just built around; social organization project-based rather than neighborhood-based; ethical review boards everyone participates in and routinely routes around; the highest rate of experimental augmentation in the city. Also carries the weight of the Failed Utopias — at least three serious attempts at internal communal societies, each collapsing in its own way.

---

## The developer's vision

**Laboratory complexes genuinely built out in the ice and snow — confirmed literally, not just decoratively.** Some facilities are more exposed/less fully enclosed than the rest of Concordia, specifically because certain experiments require real outdoor cold conditions; testing them at room temperature would invalidate the results. Confirmed this outdoor cold-testing is occasional, done specifically when the work calls for it — the general norm is that most of what happens in Aquarius happens indoors.

**Clustered facility-complexes at wildly varying scale** — some huge, some single-room. Confirmed the district reads as a genuine visual patchwork rather than one coherent whole: different construction eras are immediately, obviously apparent, even to someone with no architectural knowledge. This directly reinforces the district's own established "four or five layered eras, never torn down" character.

**Current activity read as mostly indoors** — during this particular visit, there wasn't visible outdoor activity to describe, consistent with the general pattern of most work happening inside rather than the exception of occasional outdoor cold-testing.
