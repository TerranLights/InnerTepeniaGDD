# Taurus — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this district — distinct from `District_Canon_Reference.md` (established facts) and the district's own two-stage deep dive (`Deep_Dives/02_Taurus_Deep_Dive.md`). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal Megasheet process when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-09

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Taurus is Concordia's residential/domestic-stability district — the heart of everyday bonded life. Established texture: multi-generational homes with private courtyards, heirloom objects carrying real genealogical weight (a grandparent's chair as non-negotiable furniture), showing up unannounced at a household is completely normal and expected (doors answered, tea produced — a warmth mechanism that doubles as informal surveillance), extraordinary preserved-food and fermentation cuisine developed out of necessity, and fiercely maintained noise ordinances/quiet hours (a real source of friction with Leo's festival culture). Also carries two real wounds: the Insulation Schism (Coldsiders living in the literally colder, less-connected parts of the district based on when their family arrived and how much political capital they had) and the Bonded Pair Registry Collapse, which created an Unverified underclass whose bonds could never be fully re-verified.

---

## The developer's vision

**Lots of housing, at varying — but mostly even — degrees of maintenance and weather erosion.** There is some disparity across the district, plausibly tracking the established Coldsiders divide, but it reads as a subtle gradient rather than a stark, dramatic split. Not a district visibly cut in half; more an uneven wear pattern you'd have to actually look for.

**People don't greet you, but they're not hostile or unfriendly about it.** Confirmed specifically: they're just not the welcoming type. This tracks with, rather than contradicts, Taurus' established household-level warmth — the door-answered, tea-produced hospitality is real, but it's private and directed inward at known/approached households, not extended to strangers passing by in the street.

**Pace: "slow-brisk-walk" speed.** Not slow the way Cancer is slow. People clearly have things to do, but they're also not rushing — a purposeful, unhurried-but-not-idle rhythm, distinct in kind from Cancer's own "slow enough."

## Still open from this session

- Whether the Coldsiders divide's visual subtlety (mostly even, not stark) says anything about how socially significant the line actually still is day-to-day, versus being more a matter of historical record
- Whether the private, household-directed warmth changes at all for a repeat visitor/known face, as opposed to a first-time stranger
- Soundscape — not yet explored for this district the way Cancer's ambient singing was
