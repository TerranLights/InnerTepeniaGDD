# Per-Subnet Regional Personality — Guide (index and organizing framework, v0 proposal)

**Created 2026-10-05.** Status: **PROPOSAL for the developer's review; nothing here is canon.** It organizes **categories only**: no subnet's personality is written here. Values are filled **after the 38 cities have completed the ULM** (and possibly the CST and RWBEM). Companions: `Per-Subnet_Metacategories.md` (the developer's skeleton, not edited) and `Per-Subnet_Overview_Reference_Data.md` (a fact layer: members, hubs, Census I, founders, infrastructure; **not** the personality guide).

**Why this is classification, not worldbuilding** (developer, 2026-10-05): it defines the *slots* and the *rules for filling them*. **Scope of the one-location law:** comparing cities to each other is **required** when amalgamating a subnet into a meta-culture; the Law of One Location still binds every ULM/CST/RWBEM *city* pass unchanged.

## 1 · What a "regional personality" is

What a place **feels like** as the player explores and becomes familiar with it: the way the American West Coast, the Rockies, the Deep South, Appalachia and the Bayou each feel different inside one country. It is **not** a tally of facts, and **not** quests or side-content. It sits between the city and the nation (*differentiate locally, converge nationally*, `No_National_Stereotypes`): a subnet's personality is what its cities share, shaded by who dominates.

**The blind-drop test** (each personality's summary): *if a player were dropped here with no map, which three things would tell them which subnet they are in?*

| **F** | **Temperament** | How do people carry themselves? | sociability toward strangers · formality · humor and directness · risk tolerance · attitude to authority · generosity and pride. **Excludes human-versus-robot treatment** (developer: not expected to vary between subnets) | Phases 4, 9 |

| **I** | **Meaning** | What does the place believe, mourn and celebrate? | belief climate · observances · its core promise and how it fails · (death is reserved corpus-wide, `RV-4`) | Phase 6 |

| **K** | **Stance** | How does the subnet see itself, and the others? | self-image · view of each other subnet · reputation and in-world nicknames · **how locals answer when the player asks about `[[Location-X]]`** *(the stock of phrasings about the other subnets: later, developer)* | Phase 5 (relation) |

**Plus one structural slot, outside the layers:** **Sub-regions** — a subnet may be several regions in one (as "Appalachia" has sub-areas). Examples already in canon: Janbogo is the Ross Sea coast, the Adélie and George V coast, and the Concordia plateau; Mirny is the Prydz Bay and Wilkes coast and the plateau (Kunlun, Ariun Nuur). Any slot may carry a per-sub-region value.

## 3 · Deriving a subnet's value from its cities

**The blend** (developer, 2026-10-05). The "broad-scale culture" is set by whoever has the majority, as **a weighted blend of three things**: *"partially from numerical majority, partially from aggregated majority, and partially from founders (similar to how the Dutch concept of multiculturalism helped produce modern-day New York City)."* The exact weights are **undetermined, probably a set of parameters**, so the framework records all three inputs per subnet and leaves the blend tunable:

| Input | Meaning | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **W1 · Numerical majority** | each city's share of the subnet's Census I population | computed (table below) |
| **W2 · Aggregated majority** | the majority taken across the cities together: how many member cities lean each way, one city one vote. *(My reading of "aggregated"; please correct if it means something else.)* | known (cities per subnet) |
| **W3 · Founders' weight** | the **early-founding stock's influence beyond its numbers**: a founding group's institutions and habits can outlast its share of the population (the Dutch in New York). **Source: the Founding Register only** (`DR-19`: founders stand on geography and access, never on the station's operator). *Guard:* the divergence operator still applies, so founders set a **starting stock**, and time and place then change it. | to map from the Register (founders per city, per subnet) |

*Why the three inputs will disagree:* in Palmer one city (Esperanza) holds 43.5 % of the people while eight cities each get one vote; in Mawson one city holds 83.8 %; and a subnet's founders (W3) need not be its most numerous stock. The blend rule decides how loud each is.

**Each slot's value is stored as four parts**, so averaging never erases a minority:
1. **Dominant** — what the weighted blend says the subnet is.
2. **Shared floor** — what *every* member city has.
3. **Outliers** — member cities that diverge, named.
4. **Confidence** — how many member cities' passes feed this slot (so a value built from 2 of 9 cities is visibly provisional).

## 4 · Readiness: what can be framed now, and what waits

| Layer | Can be framed from existing canon now? | Waits for |
|---|---|---|
| Ground, Year, Rhythm (the physical and logistical halves) | **Yes** — climate, specs, highways, airports, ports, the extent and density runs | the city passes (for the felt half) |
| Standard of living | **Partly** — definitions can be set now; inputs exist (the per-city workforce tiers; the food and heat layers) | the definition, then each city's value; **city first, subnet second** |
| Inheritance | the **label** and the Register's founders now | the weighted blend (W3 mapped from the Register; parameters undetermined) |
| Livelihood, Temperament, Expression, Meaning, Order, Stance | **Slots only** | the city ULM (and any CST/RWBEM the developer wants) |
| Signature | — | all of the above |

**Pilot candidate:** the Mirny subnet — four of its nine cities (Shirayuki, Sinheung, Zhongshan, Davis) have completed the ULM and Mirny is mid-pass; no other subnet is that far.

## 5 · Non-standard subnets

- **Byrd (one city).** There is nothing to blend, so Byrd's personality is its city's. An **alternate layout** is needed. The towns research (Batch 1, 2026-10-04) names two candidate sites nearest Byrd (each about 713 km away; **town-to-subnet membership is not assigned**, `DR-52` open item 6):
  - **Russkaya**, a bare-rock nunatak on the Marie Byrd Land coast; qualifies on stability; ship access only, about three weeks a year; the only station on a coastal stretch of more than 1,500 km; the only one of the three Russian-program stations with a documented revival effort.
  - **Arturo Parodi / Union Glacier** (the Ellsworth Land interior), air access only; **fails** the stability test (ice moving about 20 m a year; the project has set no speed threshold).

  *Proposed layout for Byrd:* the city as the core, plus an **outposts** slot (remote rock and interior sites) and a **long-empty-coast** slot, because Byrd's feel may come as much from distance and emptiness as from the city.
- **Mawson (three cities, sharply different).** Population says Mawson; city count says three equal voices. Outlier handling matters most here: Dome Fuji is a robot-only plateau pilgrim city and Temirötkel a heavy-industry port.
- **The South Pole (one station, 6,857 people).** A neutral inter-subnet relay, not a region in the usual sense. *Proposed layout:* a short form with Ground, Rhythm, Order and Stance, and **Inheritance** read as "unification and science", the developer's label.
- **Subnets with sub-regions** (Janbogo, Mirny): use the Sub-regions slot (§2).

## 6 · The developer's broad-scale culture labels (Layer G; kept verbatim)

Palmer — Latin-American · Halley — European · Mawson — Altaic · Mirny — Sino-Australian · Janbogo — East Sinitic · Byrd — Anglo-American · South Pole — Unification/Scientific. Under the culture law each label names the **starting stock**, never the culture itself; the weighted blend is what lets a label be defended from its cities. *(Read-across against the Founding Register: `Per-Subnet_Overview_Reference_Data.md` §2.)*
