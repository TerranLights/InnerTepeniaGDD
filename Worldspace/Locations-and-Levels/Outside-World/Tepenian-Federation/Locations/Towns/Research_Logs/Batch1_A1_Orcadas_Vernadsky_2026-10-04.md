## Correction to the inventory/brief
**"Sandefjord Bay Station" is not within 25 km of Orcadas.** It is on the west coast of Coronation Island (60°37'S 46°03'W), about 73 km from Orcadas and about 27 km from Signy. A British hut built there in 1945 was never occupied and had collapsed by 1955. The real facility near Orcadas is **Station C at Cape Geddes** (British FIDS, Jan 1946 to Mar 1947; about 10.5 km NW; closed). Naming is unresolved: the BAS page is titled "Sandefjord Bay Station C" and a snippet says Cape Geddes was built "after abandoning plans for a base at Sandefjord Bay".

4. Vernadsky: 2017 sheet "65°14'74.5''" is invalid as written but matches decimal minutes of the 2024 CSV. COMNAP year 1996 vs site history 1947/1954. Wordie House predecessor swept away by a "tidal wave" (guide) vs "possibly a tsunami" (Wikipedia): cause not established. Rasmussen Hut 9 km (NASC) vs 8.1 km (computed from the island's coordinates).
5. The Vernadsky closest station by distance is Yelcho (Chile, 51.9 km), not Palmer (53.3 km).
