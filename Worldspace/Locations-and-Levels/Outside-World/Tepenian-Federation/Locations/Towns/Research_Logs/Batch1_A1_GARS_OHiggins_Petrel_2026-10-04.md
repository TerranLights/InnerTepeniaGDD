## Contradictions between sources (unresolved)
1. Petrel seasonality: COMNAP 2024 year-round (peak 45); COMNAP 2017 seasonal Oct to Mar; Wikipedia summer 25 / winter 0; AAD (Mar 2023) "summer base since 1976"; CEE + 2024 to 2026 press: permanent since the 2021/22 reopening. **Treat as year-round since 2022, winter crews 22 to 30.**
2. Petrel dates: reopening 9 Dec 2021 vs 15 Nov 2021; summer-only from 1978 (CEE, COMNAP) vs 1976; origin 1950/51 (CEE) vs Jan 1952 vs Dec 1952.
3. Petrel coordinates: CEE "63°28'S 56°17'W" is about 2.9 km off COMNAP.
4. Petrel ground: COMNAP "ice-free ground", permafrost "sporadic"; CEE continuous permafrost on a moraine. **CEE preferred (measured geotechnics).**
5. Petrel wind: COMNAP mean 8 / max 70 km/h; CEE monthly means 18 to 30 km/h, recorded extremes 157 to 176 km/h.
6. Petrel runway: 850 m (1967), 1,000 m, about 1,400 m, 1,600 m, 1,300 m. Unresolved.
7. Petrel capacity: 25 beds / peak 45 (COMNAP); design 60 + 80 transit (CEE); press 60 winter / 120 summer; present crew 22 to 30.
8. Petrel distances: Ushuaia 1,100 vs 1,300 km; Marambio about 80 vs 87 km (computed); Esperanza 37 vs 39 km (computed).
9. O'Higgins elevation 10 m (Chilean Met), 12 m (COMNAP), 13 m (Wikipedia, DLR datasheet).
10. O'Higgins population: winter 24 / summer 52 (Wikipedia citing COMNAP); winter 21+3 / summer 44+8, 60 beds (the catalogue itself); 21 / 50 (INACH); peak 70 (COMNAP 2024).
11. O'Higgins climate: COMNAP catalogue -9.8 °C mean, July -17.8, 621 mm; DMC normals (via Wikipedia table) -3.5 °C, July -7.9, 558.3 mm; Wikipedia prose July -9, 771 mm. DMC is the most traceable.
12. O'Higgins ice runway: Wikipedia "on sea ice"; DLR "continental glacier 3 km SE"; COMNAP 800 m runway. DLR preferred.
13. Islet: DLR about 150 by 200 m "Isabel Riquelme islet"; Wikipedia 300 by 200 m, 50 m from mainland; USGS: Schmidt Peninsula, tied to Cape Legoupil by a low isthmus.
14. GARS year-round start: "beginning of 2010" vs "June 2010". GARS elevation: 17.56 m (DLR), 17 m (COMNAP), 13 m (datasheet), 32.15 m ellipsoidal (BKG pillar). GARS to Chilean base: 30 m (Monuments Council) vs about 60 m (computed).
15. Rada Covadonga is "Seasonal" in COMNAP, not year-round (the batch assumed year-round).
