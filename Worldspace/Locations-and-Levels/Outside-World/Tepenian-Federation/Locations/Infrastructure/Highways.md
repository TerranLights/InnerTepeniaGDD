# Tepenian Highway Network

**Purpose:** Dedicated reference for Tepenia's overland highway network — full routes, junctions, and endpoints. Built 2026-07-06 from the corrected Highway Quick Reference in `Cities/City_Relationship_Database.md` §1, which remains the source of truth for any future corrections; update both files together.

**Status:** All highways are pre-Long Night War infrastructure. Post-war, coastal sections are partially or fully non-operational. Inland sections (particularly those connecting Concordia, Byrd, Vostok, and the South Pole ruins) may still be functional or partially maintained.

## Hwy 1 — Rothera Highway ("Palmer Highway")

**Route:** Esperanza *(northern terminus)* → Marambio → *(ramp: road to Port Lockroy; boat to Palmer City)* → *(ramp: road to Rothera)* → Byrd *(western terminus)*

**Endpoints:** Esperanza (north) ↔ Byrd (west)

- Northern terminus is **Esperanza**, not Marambio.
- Port Lockroy, Palmer City, and Rothera are **not main-line stops** — all three are reached via connecting ramps. Palmer City specifically requires a **boat crossing** from its ramp, not a road.
- Marambio itself sits on the main line, reached via a causeway/bridge chain across the Seymour Island / Snow Hill Island / James Ross Island group and the Prince Gustav Channel.
- Western terminus (Byrd) **connects directly to Hwy 22's endpoint** there.
- The only land route connecting the Antarctic Peninsula to the rest of Tepenia.
- Hitchhiking-valid on the Marambio–Rothera segment specifically.

## Hwy 2 — Dumont Coast Highway ("DCH")

**Route:** *(junction with Hwy 110)* → Casey → Dumont d'Urville

**Endpoints:** Junction with Hwy 110 (west) ↔ Dumont d'Urville (east)

- Western end is a **junction with Hwy 110**, not a bare starting point.
- Cape Denison is **not** on this route — it belongs to Hwy 183 instead.
- Dumont d'Urville end **connects directly to Hwy 183's endpoint** there.
- Short coastal route along the Dumont d'Urville Sea.
- Hitchhiking-valid.

## Hwy 4 — Mawson-Soyuz Highway

**Route:** Sayowa → Mawson → Soyuz → *(ex-Bharati Japanese city, name TBD)*

**Endpoints:** Sayowa (west) ↔ Ex-Bharati Japanese city (east)

- Sayowa end **connects directly to Hwy 7-ext's endpoint** there.
- Far end sits at a **tri-junction** connecting directly to both Hwy 110's and Hwy 22's endpoints.
- Hitchhiking-valid.

## Hwy 7 — Belgrano Highway ("Atlantic Highway")

**Route:** Belgrano → Halley → Abowasa → *(ramp to Neumayer, between Abowasa and Sanay)* → Sanay → Troll → Lazar

**Endpoints:** Belgrano (west) ↔ Lazar (east)

- Does **not** pass through Neumayer directly — Neumayer is reached via a separate unnamed connector road (ramp located between Abowasa and Sanay).
- Atlantic / Queen Maud Land coast spine.
- Hitchhiking-valid.

---

## Hwy 7-ext — Belgrano Highway Extension

**Route:** Lazar → Princess Elisabeth → *(junction with Hwy 37, shortly before Sayowa)* → Sayowa

**Endpoints:** Lazar (west) ↔ Sayowa (east)

- Junctions with **Hwy 37** shortly before reaching Sayowa.
- Sayowa terminus **connects directly to Hwy 4's endpoint** there.
- Built **2611–2614** — the only highway with confirmed in-world construction dates.
- Extends Hwy 7 eastward from its terminus at Lazar.

## Hwy 22 — Transcontinental Highway

**Route:** Byrd *(Amundsen Sea end)* → South Pole (Amundsen Station) → *(junction with Hwy 175)* → *(dual-junction with Hwy 37, bidirectional)* → *(junction with Hwy 59)* → Zhongshan/Soyuz/ex-Bharati tri-junction

**Endpoints:** Byrd (west) ↔ Zhongshan/Soyuz/ex-Bharati tri-junction (east)

- Byrd end **connects directly to Hwy 1's endpoint** there.
- Passes directly through the South Pole (Amundsen Station).
- Junctions with Hwy 175 and, separately, a **bidirectional dual-junction with Hwy 37** along its interior stretch.
- Eastern end is a genuine **tri-junction** connecting directly to both Hwy 4's and Hwy 110's endpoints.
- Does **not** pass through Sayowa or Mawson directly.
- Cross-continent spine from West Antarctica to the East Antarctic coast.

## Hwy 37 — Mountain Cut Throughway

**Route:** *(junction with Hwy 7-ext, shortly before Sayowa)* → Dome Fuji → *(dual-junction with Hwy 22, bidirectional)* → Kunlun → Vostok → Concordia

**Endpoints:** Junction with Hwy 7-ext, just before Sayowa (northeast) ↔ Concordia (southwest)

- **Dome Fuji is a confirmed stop** on this highway *(Dome Fuji's own file previously said "no highway access" — that claim is stale and needs a corresponding fix)*.
- Kunlun comes before Vostok along this route (order reversed from an earlier, incorrect listing).
- Concordia end **connects directly to both Hwy 110's and Hwy 183's endpoints**, via the outer ring linking Concordia's Capricorn and Sagittarius districts.
- East Antarctic **plateau** traverse — not through the Transantarctic Mountains.
- Connects the Indian Ocean coast (via Sayowa) to the inland stations and Concordia.

## Hwy 59 — Atlantic Throughway ("Arcanet Line")

**Route:** *(ramp with Hwy 7, between Halley and Abowasa)* → *(ramp with Hwy 22, farther from the South Pole than Hwy 175's ramp)*

**Endpoints:** Ramp with Hwy 7, between Halley and Abowasa ↔ Ramp with Hwy 22, farther from South Pole than Hwy 175's ramp

- **Connector highway, not a city-to-city road.**
- Hwy 7 ramp sits specifically between Halley and Abowasa.
- Its Hwy 22 ramp is farther from the South Pole than Hwy 175's own ramp with Hwy 22.
- Also carries the Arcanet cable along its full length.
- Does not reach the South Pole or Concordia directly.

## Hwy 110 — Coastal Cut Highway

**Route:** Zhongshan → Davis → Mirny → Casey → Concordia

**Endpoints:** Zhongshan (northwest) ↔ Concordia (south)

- Zhongshan end sits at a **tri-junction** with Hwy 4's and Hwy 22's endpoints.
- Concordia is a genuine full terminus of this route — **not** an inland spur off Casey.
- Concordia end connects directly to Hwy 37's and Hwy 183's endpoints via the outer ring linking Concordia's Capricorn and Sagittarius districts.
- Main East Antarctic coastal route.
- Hitchhiking-valid.

---

## Hwy 175 — Central Cut Throughway

**Route:** *(ramp with Hwy 183, near Janbogo)* → *(ramp with Hwy 22, closer to the South Pole than Hwy 59's ramp)*

**Endpoints:** Ramp with Hwy 183, near Janbogo ↔ Ramp with Hwy 22, closer to South Pole than Hwy 59's ramp

- **Connector highway.**
- Its Hwy 183 ramp is located near Janbogo — **not** near Concordia, despite Hwy 183 itself originating at Concordia.
- Its Hwy 22 ramp sits closer to the South Pole than Hwy 59's does.
- Connects the Janbogo subnet region to the South Pole.

---

## Hwy 183 — Janbogo Highway

**Route:** Concordia → *(junction with Hwy 175, near Janbogo)* → passes near Janbogo/Zukelli *(connecting road, not direct)* → passes near Cape Adare *(connecting road, not direct)* → Denison → Dumont d'Urville

**Endpoints:** Concordia (south) ↔ Dumont d'Urville (north)

- Concordia end connects directly to Hwy 110's and Hwy 37's endpoints via the outer ring linking Concordia's Capricorn and Sagittarius districts.
- Janbogo, Zukelli, and Cape Adare are reached via **connecting roads, not main-line stops** — the same spur-access pattern already established for Fort McMurdo and Scott (unaffected by this correction).
- Denison is confirmed on this route (moved here from Hwy 2).
- Dumont d'Urville end connects directly to Hwy 2's endpoint there.
- Connects Concordia to the Ross Sea coastal cities.

## Route to Byrd from Concordia

Hwy 183 → junction with Hwy 175 (near Janbogo) → Hwy 175 → junction with Hwy 22 → Hwy 22 (Amundsen Sea direction) → Byrd. Multiple transfers; a very long journey.
