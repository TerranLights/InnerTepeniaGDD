## 3 · Nuclear power: what canon and the treaty say

- **Canon places nuclear generation in Concordia's Power Core, and the Tower design file infers a nuclear or fusion array at the Tower itself** (not a ruling).
- **The Falkland Treaty draft (Article V.1)** bans nuclear explosive devices and the disposal of radioactive waste within Tepenian territory, "without exception and without term". **It does not ban reactors.** (The draft is marked "not locked canon".)
- **The treaty scaffold's gloss** says the article "retroactively explains why Tepenia's civilization runs on the Amundsen Tower's grid-regulation model instead of nuclear power: not a technological gap, a treaty-mandated one." **This sits in tension with Concordia's reactors, with the Tower design file's inferred reactor array, and with the draft's narrower text.** Unresolved.
- **The real Antarctic Treaty's Article V** is the model: explosions and waste disposal are banned, reactors are not. The McMurdo reactor (PM-3A, 1962–1972) was operated and then removed, with 12,000 to 14,000 tons of soil and the dismantled plant shipped out.
- **Consequence for design:** if reactors are allowed, **spent fuel and decommissioning waste must leave the territory**, which ties Tepenia to Upper Earth shipping.

Research for this section: `Cities/Research_Logs/Tepenia_Nuclear_Power_Logistics_Research_2026-11-02.md`. Key facts from it (SOURCED or DERIVED there): reactors have cold-region precedent (PM-3A at 1.75 MW; Bilibino at 4 × 12 MW; the floating Akademik Lomonosov at about 64–70 MW plus heat); microreactors of 1–5 MW are designed to ship in containers but are in demonstration only; fuel is about 25 t of enriched uranium per gigawatt-year against about 2.07 million t of diesel; waste and decommissioning are the heavy logistics; high-voltage DC loses about 3.5% per 1,000 km (AC about 6.7%); the longest clearly sourced nuclear district-heat pipe is 23 km; no nuclear plant has been sited on an Antarctic ice shelf or sea-ice coast.

## 5 · Open questions *(the developer's)*

1. **Is nuclear power allowed in Tepenia at all?** Reconcile Concordia's reactors (and the Tower's inferred reactor array) with the scaffold's gloss and the draft's narrower Article V.1, or revise one.
2. **Where does spent fuel go?** (Presumably out on the Upper Earth freighters; this should be ruled, because it sets how nuclear can be.)
3. **Were there other plants before the war?** Which cities fed the grid, and which fed it from where? **Scale:** the Tower alone drew about 324 GW and the national model says about 101.7 GW of electricity; a floating-unit count in the hundreds would be needed for either, so the question is how the setting generated that power (the research file gives per-unit sizes).
4. **Plants per cluster, per city, or none outside Concordia?** The cluster table above is the geography; no cluster is ruled.
5. **Is the geothermal heat also power?** (Open since 2026-09-05; the geothermal file recommends heat plus limited local power.)
6. **Reconcile Concordia's geothermal with the "only six" line** in `Geothermal_Heating.md` (flagged there 2026-11-02; options A, B, C).
7. **Relung Panen's power**: the canon food model already assumes "mature" 10 kWh/kg for all indoor food (about 0.66 t per person per year), which puts Relung's export food at roughly 0.5 GW average; the 6–16 kW per person in the research file is the present-day staple-crop range, which the model calls impossible. Which assumption governs is the developer's call.
8. **What does the grid look like now**, and does anything feed Relung, Mawson or Byrd across long distances after the Tower?
