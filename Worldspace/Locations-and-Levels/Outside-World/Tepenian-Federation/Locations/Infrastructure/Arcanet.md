# The Arcanet

**Confirmed 2026-08-07.** The Arcanet is, functionally, the Antarctican Internet — its name is a shortened
form of exactly that. Tepenia is a sovereign nation in Antarctica, populated by both robots and humans, and
functions for practical purposes as "New Antarctica." Real-world intercontinental internet connectivity
depends on physical undersea cable infrastructure — the same way Australia connects to the rest of the
world, and North America and Europe connect to each other. Tepenia has no such physical link to Upper
Earth's own internet, so it necessarily runs its own separate, self-contained network.

**What populates it:** both contemporary Tepenian data and salvaged pre-war Upper Earth datadrives — the
Arcanet was never built from nothing, it absorbed whatever internet-era information Tepenians brought with
them or later recovered, alongside everything generated within Tepenia itself since.

**Structural character:** the Arcanet is roughly, structurally analogous to the real-world TOR Network —
though for a different purpose. Where TOR is fundamentally about privacy and secrecy, the Arcanet is about
having a functional, self-sufficient internet-connectivity infrastructure for an isolated population with no
physical link to the rest of the world's own network.

**Relevance to robot language (see `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/Human_Universals_Culture_Framework.md`):**
because the Arcanet is a data/information network, not an alternate spoken-language medium, it has no
bearing on vocal phonology — robot vernacular languages stay within the same human-style phonemic/vocal-tract
constraints as whatever surrounding human culture they're drawn from, regardless of also having Arcanet
access.
