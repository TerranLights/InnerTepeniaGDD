# 1 · THE RULING

> ***"The closest land connections would be: French Southern and Antarctic Lands, and Heard Island and
> McDonald Islands. Both of those would serve as viable politically-neutral shipping hubs that Tepenia could
> work out arrangements with in order to (indirectly) trade with both Australia and South Africa. The city of
> Sayowa is also geographically near enough (along with Mawson, even though that's a resort town with more
> fishing), which means that Sayowa, Davis, and the Tri-Cities could all equally benefit from the French
> Southern and Antarctic Lands and Heard Island and McDonald Islands."***

# 2 · ⭐ THE MECHANISM

| | |
|---|---|
| **What they are** | **Subantarctic island territories sitting between Tepenia's Indian Ocean coast and the nearest mainlands** |
| ⭐⭐ **What they do** | ***TRANSSHIPMENT.*** **Tepenia negotiates arrangements with the territories themselves, and trades INDIRECTLY with Australia and South Africa through them** |

## 2.0 ⭐⭐⭐ THE DIRECTION IS **INBOUND** — *developer correction, 2026-09-16*

> ⛔⛔ **TEPENIA IS NOT EXPORTING FOOD THROUGH THESE HUBS.**
> ***"Davis does not ship food 'out of Tepenia'. It imports raw materials it needs from Upper-Earth countries."***

| ⛔ NOT this | ✅ THIS |
|---|---|
| **Agricultural output leaving the continent** | ⭐⭐ **RAW MATERIALS COMING IN** — the inputs a closed continental economy cannot manufacture for itself |

⭐⭐ **And it is consistent with what canon already says the constraints ARE.**
**`10_Validation_Findings` §5e: the Davis greenhouse has *"no ecological ceiling — limited by ENERGY, GLASS,
CO₂, labor, logistics: engineering problems, all solvable with capital."*** ⇒ ***Those are import
requirements.*** **`Division_of_Industry/README.md` separately records that metal is scarce and recovery is a
necessary industry.**

> ### ⇒ **The hubs feed the greenhouses. They do not empty them.**
> ⭐ **Davis's agricultural output is a DOMESTIC supply — it feeds the Federation, per the coalition finding —
> and never needed a foreign market.**

⏸️ **What specifically comes in is NOT enumerated here** — *glass, CO₂, energy hardware, nutrient and mineral
feedstock and metal are the obvious candidates from the constraint list, but the manifest is unruled.* **`H66`.**

# 3 · ⚠⚠ TWO THINGS FLAGGED, NOT SMOOTHED OVER

## 3.1 ⭐⭐ THE TWO HUBS ARE NOT EQUALLY NEUTRAL — **and that may be a feature**

> ⚠ **`HIMI` is an AUSTRALIAN EXTERNAL TERRITORY.** **`TAAF` is FRENCH.**

⇒ **For trade with South Africa, both are genuinely third-party.**
⇒ ⛔ **For trade with AUSTRALIA, routing through an Australian territory is not indirect — it is Australia.**

| Hub | Neutral vs Australia | Neutral vs South Africa |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **TAAF** *(French)* | ✅ **Yes — true third party** | ✅ **Yes** |
| ⚠ **HIMI** *(Australian)* | ⛔ **No — it IS Australian sovereign territory** | ✅ **Yes** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ WHICH MAKES THEM DO DIFFERENT JOBS, and that is more interesting than making them equivalent
> **`TAAF` is the POLITICAL instrument — the genuinely neutral door.**
> **`HIMI` is the GEOGRAPHIC one — closest, but it buys proximity rather than deniability.**
> ⏸️ **NOT RESOLVED HERE.** **Flagged for the developer: whether HIMI is treated as a neutral hub in-world
> despite its sovereignty, or whether it is specifically the Australia-facing door while TAAF is the
> everything-else door.**
