# Tepenia Airport Network

Which Tepenian cities/locations retain functional airports in the post-war/present-day setting. A short, deliberately curated list — confirmed 2026-07-06 directly from the developer's own "Tepenian Airport Map" reference image (`Reference/Images/Maps/Tepenian airport and flight map.jpeg`), with locations refined 2026-07-07 against a companion highway-overlay version of the same map, which shows each airport marker sitting directly on the highway network — also mirrored into this same folder as `Tepenian airport and flight map - highway overlay.jpeg` for convenience. Every other city, including several with real-world Antarctic stations that do have airstrips today, is **highway-only** in Tepenia — this list does not track 1:1 with the real-world research pass in `Reference/Real-World/Stations/Antarctic_Stations_With_Airstrips.md`, which should be treated as background reference only, not a predictor of Tepenia's own network.

| **Machu Picchu Airport** | Near Sejong, also reasonably close to Juan Carlos | **International** — connects directly to Ushuaia and the already-established Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority (see `World_History_Reference.md` and `Specs/Juan_Carlos.md`). Real-world basis: Peru's Machu Picchu Base sits on King George Island, near both King Sejong Station and Juan Carlos I Station — resolving the previously-open question of why the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority sat so far from Ushuaia geographically; the Authority's actual physical processing point is here. **Location refined 2026-07-07:** closest to Sejong, but sits close enough to Juan Carlos that the developer's own map places its marker right at Juan Carlos's label — same airport, not a separate one. |
| **Mountain Pass Airport** | Not a city — a small, isolated industrial outpost | On Hwy 37, between Kunlun and Vostok. Not a highway-to-highway junction (that's the separate Hwy 22/Hwy 37 dual-junction, positioned between Dome Fuji and Kunlun). Reasonably accessible to Kunlun, Dome Fuji, Amundsen Station (via the nearby Hwy 22 dual-junction), and Concordia to an extent. **Confirmed 2026-07-07: the outpost here was a joint Vostok-Kunlun venture** — a fabrication-synthesis chamber (the individual apparatus that creates robots, part of the nationwide Cradle infrastructure) manufacturing site, established and staffed by scientists and engineers from both cities working together. **Historical, not current, per the same-day power-source correction:** too remote for its own dedicated power infrastructure, the outpost ran on residual overflow from Amundsen Tower's continent-wide regulated grid. Amundsen Tower's destruction during the Long Night War ended that supply permanently, along with the outpost's own manufacturing capability — no new chambers have been built here since. Chambers built here before the war shipped out via the highway/airport network and still function wherever they ended up; the outpost itself is simply dark now, not destroyed. See `Locations/Infrastructure/Highways.md`'s Hwy 37 section, `Specs/Vostok.md`, `Specs/Kunlun.md`, and `Energy_Grid_Failure_Rationale.md` #11. |

## Everything Else Is Highway-Only

Every other Tepenian city — including ones whose real-world station has a genuine airstrip today (Amundsen Station, Belgrano, Byrd, Casey, Concordia, Davis, Dome Fuji, Dumont d'Urville, Fort McMurdo, Halley, Kunlun, Lazar, Mawson, Neumayer, Palmer City, Princess Elisabeth, Sanay, Sayowa, Scott, Vostok, Juan Carlos, and others) — has no functional airport in Tepenia. Access to these cities is via the highway network only (`Locations/Infrastructure/Highways.md`).
