# Tepenia Airport Network

Which Tepenian cities/locations retain functional airports in the post-war/present-day setting. A short, deliberately curated list — confirmed 2026-07-06 directly from the developer's own "Tepenian Airport Map" reference image (`Reference/Images/Maps/Tepenian airport and flight map.jpeg`), with locations refined 2026-07-07 against a companion highway-overlay version of the same map, which shows each airport marker sitting directly on the highway network — also mirrored into this same folder as `Tepenian airport and flight map - highway overlay.jpeg` for convenience. **⭐ Updated 2026-09-02: the developer revised BOTH map images to add Byrd** *(see its row below)*. **The table below now reconciles 1:1 with the maps — thirteen markers, thirteen rows** *(ten on the developer's own two images; the last three are on the updated copies of 2026-10-29)*: Zukelli/Janbogo · Mirny · Tri-Cities · Troll · Rothera · Marambio · Machu Picchu · Mountain Pass · Belgrano · Byrd · Relung Panen Passenger · Relung Panen Cargo · Winter Intake Airport. Every other city, including several with real-world Antarctic stations that do have airstrips today, is **highway-only** in Tepenia — this list does not track 1:1 with the real-world research pass in `Reference/Real-World/Stations/Antarctic_Stations_With_Airstrips.md`, which should be treated as background reference only, not a predictor of Tepenia's own network. **⭐ Updated 2026-10-29 (developer, `DR-81`): Relung Panen hosts two airports, and both map images were copied and extended with two markers** — `Reference/Images/Maps/Tepenian airport and flight map - with Relung Panen.jpeg` and `…highway overlay - with Relung Panen.jpeg` (the markers are copies of the developer's own marker; the originals are untouched). *Markers sit between Mirny and Casey on the coast, inland, at the Bunger Hills; their exact spacing and position are illustrative, not surveyed.* **⭐ Updated 2026-10-29 (developer, `DR-82`): the Australian-side international intake.** In summer, immigration and supply shipping run through a seasonal sea gateway (`Ports.md` §3e); in winter, immigration arrives by air at a **separate, fully isolated coastal airport reachable only by air**, where customs and immigration processing happen, and processed arrivals fly on to **Mirny**. One marker was added to the same two copies (and to the overlay copy in this folder) on the coast between Davis and Mirny, **off the highway network**, because the airport has no ground link. **Its position is a placeholder only: the site is not ruled** (research under way, `Cities/Research_Logs/Relung_Panen_Intake_Gateway_Research_BRIEF_2026-10-29.md`).

### ⭐ Three cases where "no air access" is load-bearing rather than incidental

**1. ⛔ THE MAWSON SUBNET HAS NO AIRPORTS AT ALL. Confirmed 2026-09-03, developer.**
**Dome Fuji · Mawson · Temirötkel.** ***It is the only subnet with zero air access*** — Halley has two (Troll,
Belgrano), Palmer three, Mirny four (Mirny, the Tri-Cities, and Relung Panen's two, from 2026-10-29; the Winter Intake Airport, `DR-82`, serves no city and its site is not ruled, so it is not counted here), Janbogo one, Byrd one. **The subnet is entirely road-dependent, and its
gateway is the three-way Temirötkel Junction, which Temirötkel reaches by spur rather than sitting on.**
⚠ **Bears on Dome Fuji, a continent-wide pilgrimage site** *(`Official_Population_Census.md`)*: **every
pilgrim arrives overland.**

**2. ⛔ PALMER CITY IS AIR-DISCONNECTED ON PURPOSE — and the reason CHANGED. Established 2026-09-03, developer.**
**Palmer City has never hosted an airport, and it is `SEA-LINK` by road** — reached by boat crossing from a
Hwy 1 ramp *(`Specs/Palmer_City.md`)*. ***So every arrival, without exception, comes by water.***

> **The two eras, and they are different facts:**
> - **Early — incapacity.** *"At first, it was because Tepenians simply didn't have the materials and
>   infrastructure to build one."*
> - **Later — deliberate policy.** *"As it became apparent that Palmer City was easily-accessible by
>   Upper-Earthers, it was deliberately kept 'air-disconnected' from the rest of the country to make it
>   harder for anyone from Upper Earth with ill intentions to sneak in."*
>
> ⭐ **A constraint forced by poverty, later re-read as a chosen security posture** — `Cultural_Synthesis_
> Techniques.md` §6, **Necessity Before Meaning**, occurring in canon rather than as a derivation.
> **And the boat crossing is what makes the policy enforceable: a city you can only reach by water is a city
> where every arrival is observed.**
>
> ⚠ **Note the asymmetry, which is deliberate:** **the Palmer subnet hosts Tepenia's ONLY international
> airport that carries passengers on the current maps** *(Machu Picchu → Ushuaia; note 2026-10-29: Relung Panen's cargo airport is international freight only, and the Australian side's Winter Intake Airport, `DR-82`, carries passengers in winter only, so Machu Picchu is now the only YEAR-ROUND international passenger airport; `DR-51` leaves the count in each era open)*, **while the subnet's own hub city is the one sealed off from the air
> network.** **The gateway and the guarded city are neighbors.**

**3. Signy has neither road nor air access.** **`Access type: NONE`** *(`Specs/Signy.md`: "entirely
maritime-access only; South Orkney Islands have no overland connection")* **and no airport.** ***It is the
only Tepenian city reachable by neither road nor air — sea access only.***
