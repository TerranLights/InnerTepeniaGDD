## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep] — added in the 2026-08-10 Kinship re-scan, applying the revised shared-experience-first
  framing.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident: every robot who lives
  and works inside Mirny's integrated residential-industrial windbreak ring shares the same ongoing, everyday
  condition — a life where the line between "home" and "the structure that's protecting everyone from a
  katabatic wind" barely exists, and where the ring's real structural performance is a constant, ambient,
  lived fact rather than an abstraction anyone could be insulated from. That ongoing condition, not any single
  storm or gathering, is what actually binds Mirny's robots together horizontally — it holds between two
  robots who never happened to share a specific crisis, as long as they both live inside the same ring. The
  already-established Relay Room Vigils custom (quiet, cross-rank gatherings when the subnet's internal Arcanet
  connection is genuinely at risk, §9) is best understood as a recurring, visible punctuation of this ongoing
  condition — worth keeping on record as a specific historical/civic occurrence in its own right (candidate
  municipal-history material), rather than as the Kinship mechanism itself.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** A second, guild-specific shape the same underlying shared-experience logic can take: the
  Windwright Guildhall's apprenticeship structure, carrying a specific named teaching-lineage marker — Talia
  Voskresenskaya's founding-era insistence on live-storm testing over chamber simulation ("what the chamber
  isn't telling them," per the Historical Vignette material) is still institutionally attached to her name in
  how trials are run today. A Mirny robot trained into that same real-condition-testing discipline shares a
  mentor/mentee bond with her trainer — but per the revised framing, this is properly understood as one
  particular *shape* shared experience takes here (the shared experience of learning to distrust untested
  elegance together), not a separate or competing kinship mechanism from the Vigils above. (Whether
  Voskresenskaya herself was robot or human isn't established anywhere in the source material — flagged in the
  Reference Note below rather than assumed.)

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "competence over romance" and the founding-era Bellandry incident (an untested,
  conventionally-elegant square-cornered windbreak design collapsed; an unfamiliar rounded design, tested for
  real, held) already establish Mirny's core emergent ethic: trust real conditions over inherited elegance. For
  robots specifically, this plausibly sharpens into something closer to a checkable standard than a general
  attitude — a robot's own sensors give her a literal, verifiable read on whether a claim has actually been
  tested against real conditions or not, making "has this actually been checked, or just assumed" a genuinely
  robot-flavored expression of the city's own founding lesson.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Distinct from Janbogo's thermal-cycling marker (per the Swap Test — this
  needed to be genuinely different, not a reskin): Mirny robots plausibly develop an ambient, low-level habit
  of structural/wind-load awareness as functional second nature. Living and working inside a windbreak whose
  own real near-failure history (Bellandry's incident) is common civic knowledge, and whose integrated
  residential-industrial housing means the building's structural health is never abstract, a Mirny robot's
  attention plausibly stays lightly, habitually tuned to how the structure around her is actually bearing load
  — something no human resident tracks the same way, and something a robot from a steadier-engineered city
  wouldn't show either.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Robots participate in §18's existing landscape essentially unchanged — Russian
  Orthodox heritage and Chinese secular/philosophical traditions persisting side by side without merging,
  consistent with the "coexistence, not fusion" civic doctrine (see Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller
  robot-specific extension of that doctrine). §18's own explicit flag — "no dominant robot philosophical
  tradition" — is confirmed as accurate by this pass, not filled in with an invented one; see Cross-Reference
  Synthesis below for the genuinely new, non-devotional material that fills the gap without contradicting this.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep] — corrected 2026-08-10.** This file's original Water (primary) and Metal
  (secondary) proposals are withdrawn. Mirny's actual pre-existing pick, per `city-symbol-pairs.md`, is
  **Solar symbol Saturn + Robo-Element Earth**, reasoned there as "Built as its own windbreak, unforced pride,
  doesn't need outside validation" — the identical Enneagram material ("unforced pride... calm, settled
  confidence needing no defense") this file had already independently pulled to argue for Water, now correctly
  grounding Earth instead. Earth fits with real precision once seen this way: Mirny's own windbreak-ring is,
  literally, an engineered earthwork the city built to shelter itself — Earth's positive valence (patience,
  groundedness, unshowy support, already used at Davis) reads just as cleanly here as it did there, but through
  structural self-sufficiency rather than agricultural role. Saturn plausibly reinforces the same self-
  contained, doesn't-need-outside-validation register. Recorded as a confirmed pre-existing pick, not invented;
  the Water/Metal proposals aren't kept as secondary options, since neither was grounded in an actual
  assignment.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing note stands and is confirmed by this pass: siligel/coolant
  service is genuinely no-frills, served without separate ceremony, consistent with "competence over romance"
  — the real expansion for this city is the Glitch-Coolant finding below and the fuller Cross-Reference
  Synthesis treatment.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Mirny is **not** one of the canon-named bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety Glitch-Coolant
  cities (that's Janbogo, Zhongshan, and Shirayuki) — this pass positively places it in the working-class/
  potency category instead (Byrd/Rothera's pattern: limited selection, noticeably stronger effect), directly
  grounded in the already-established Rough Bar Nights custom ("unpolished, function-over-comfort... real
  kinship, expressed without much ceremony"). Worth flagging as a genuine differentiation success: Mirny's own
  subnet-mate Zhongshan is bohemian/variety — the two cities diverge here rather than defaulting to a uniform
  subnet-wide answer, consistent with their already-established rivalry (§23).

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's own named civic principle — competence over romance — gives an unusually direct
  local condition for Ch.17's explicitly locality-variable courtship customs. A plausible local expression:
  demonstrated reliability functions as the primary courtship signal here, more than romantic gesture in the
  conventional sense — working alongside someone through an actual hard shift or storm, or personally vouching
  for their competence, plausibly carries the weight a more conventional city would put into a romantic
  gesture. This isn't invented from nothing; it's a direct extension of a principle the city already names for
  itself.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Mirny's founding institutional continuity came specifically from written records
  (station logs, orientation manuals) rather than living teaching, per Specs' own founding note ("learning
  from a written record isn't the same as being taught by a living institution") — a genuinely distinct
  transmission mode from Janbogo's oral hosting-phrase fossils. A plausible local effect: Windwright trainees
  (and by extension the technical/industrial vocabulary generally) may show unusually precise, almost
  archivally-consistent terminology, since the tradition's own origin is document-based rather than casual
  oral transmission — even as the *living* mentor-lineage (§1's finding) now layers real person-to-person
  teaching on top of that original document-based inheritance. This is somewhat more speculative than the
  other findings in this sheet and should be weighted accordingly.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24 already establishes Mirny as the Mirny Subnet's literal intra-subnet
  Arcanet routing hub (connecting Zhongshan, Casey, Davis, Vostok, and Kunlun to each other — not to
  Concordia, that link was severed subnet-wide by the Planetary Split Brain), carrying "the sober
  responsibility of being the single point of failure for the whole subnet's internal cohesion." This is a
  different *kind* of Arcanet relationship than Janbogo's usage-pattern finding — Mirny's distinctive fact is
  about being the infrastructure itself. Given robots concentrate in "communications, technical" roles (§17)
  and the Relay Hardware Vaults already have a semi-public viewing area reflecting "quiet pride in the role"
  plus the existing Relay Room Vigils custom (quiet gatherings when the subnet connection is at risk), robot
  relay technicians plausibly hold a specific, quietly weighty civic status — never celebrated loudly, per
  "unforced pride," but genuinely, visibly respected. Expands already-present canon rather than inventing new
  material.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Robots participate in the Dual-Table Suppers alongside humans, per the
  general egalitarian Human-Robot Relations Baseline — no indication of separate or excluded participation.
  See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the more distinctly robot-specific extension of the underlying
  "coexistence, not fusion" doctrine.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A plausible fashion/maintenance-aesthetic marker distinct from Janbogo's
  thermal-venting ornamentation (per the Swap Test): visible reinforcement plating or wind-load stress-
  indicator markings, functioning simultaneously as a practical quick structural-health check (echoing §3's
  ambient wind-load-sensing habit) and as a badge-like aesthetic marker of experience and service — comparable
  to how a quarry worker's visibly worn gear reads as a mark of pride rather than something to hide.
- **No music/performance finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check) — nothing in the gathered material
  establishes an equivalent human music scene to anchor a robot-specific extension against (unlike Janbogo's
  Majyao-fusion anchor), and inventing one without any local hook would risk exactly the generic-reskin
  failure mode the Swap Test exists to catch.

## Closing Check — The Swap Test (Step 6)

**Swap Test result: Pass, with one flagged tension worth re-checking once Casey is actually run.** Mirny's
own source material explicitly describes its general temperament ("practical, function-first," "quiet
functional excellence") as *resembling* Casey's — meaning a pure-temperament finding here could risk also
fitting Casey. Checked against this directly: every one of this sheet's strongest findings (the Windwright
kinship structure, the wind-load-awareness identity marker, both Cross-Reference Synthesis entries, the
Glitch-Coolant placement, the Sinheung-consulting relationship) is anchored to a fact specifically and
concretely Mirny's own — the named founding-era incidents, the windbreak's real near-failure history, the
Relay Hub's single-point-of-failure role, the Dual-Kitchen doctrine's own origin story — none of which Casey is
established as sharing. Passes on that basis. Flagged rather than asserted with full confidence: worth an
explicit re-check once Casey's own pass is complete, specifically on whether any Casey-side finding ends up
close enough to Mirny's general-competence-culture material to warrant revisiting either city's Cooperation/
Morality (§2) finding.
