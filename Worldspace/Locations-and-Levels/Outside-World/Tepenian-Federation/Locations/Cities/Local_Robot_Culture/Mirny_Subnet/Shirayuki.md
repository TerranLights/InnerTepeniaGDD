## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep] — added in the 2026-08-10 Kinship re-scan, applying the revised shared-experience-first
  framing.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience: every Shirayuki robot, regardless of when she
  arrived or which sector she works in (research, education, or the arts, per §6b's own cross-sector mix),
  lives the same ongoing condition of practicing Ashiato's "individual expression that still reads as
  collectively legible" — an everyday creative practice, not a one-time event, that's genuinely horizontal
  across the whole population. That shared, continuing life of visibly, personally claiming something you
  didn't originate — living the city's own "made by others first, self-authored after" arc daily, through
  style rather than through housing status — is what actually binds Shirayuki's robots together, and it holds
  between two robots who never lived in the same Circuit House at all.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes for newcomers: the Alumni Circuit Houses — a
  standing network of graduates hosting newcomers — plausibly function as a housing-based lineage genuinely
  distinct in mechanism from every prior city's teaching-lineage version. Since students are drawn nationwide
  "purely for the culture and the soul" (§7) and a meaningful fraction stay to become the next generation's
  hosts, a robot's specific housing "family" is whichever Circuit House took her in, and whoever she later
  takes in herself — a structural pay-it-forward chain that's one particular institutional form of the
  broader shared creative life above, not a separate mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Shirayuki's central founding tension — an imposed, diplomatically-decreed origin that
  took generations to become genuinely, authentically claimed — plausibly generalizes into a lived civic
  ethic: belonging doesn't require having been born into something to be genuinely, fully yours. This carries
  unusual weight for robots specifically. Ch.11 already establishes that robots have no biological "belonging
  by birth" mechanism at all — their kinship is always chosen. A city whose entire founding myth validates
  exactly that logic (claimed belonging as fully legitimate, not a lesser substitute for inherited belonging)
  plausibly resonates with robots in a way it wouldn't for humans, who at least have the *option* of
  biological family elsewhere. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** The first city in this run whose identity marker isn't environmental —
  Shirayuki's climate is genuinely mild (a real, deliberate break from every prior Mirny-subnet city's
  wind/cold/terrain-driven logic). The marker here instead extends the Ashiato ethos into the body itself:
  given robots have a literal physical canvas — frame, plating, surface — that clothing-only self-expression
  can't reach, a plausible, genuinely robot-only marker is visible self-modification (paint, plating pattern,
  accessory placement) as a direct, embodied extension of Ashiato's "individual expression that still reads as
  collectively legible" logic. No human resident has an equivalent option — this is specifically about the
  body, not just what's worn on it.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** A third distinct mechanism for genuine Gen/Mark diversity, after Casey's transit-
  driven and Kunlun's recruitment-driven versions: educational draw. Students relocate to Shirayuki nationwide
  specifically for the Institute (§7: "purely for the culture and the soul of the place"), and the Alumni
  Circuit House pattern implies meaningful retention rather than pure pass-through — meaning Shirayuki's
  robot population plausibly shows real Gen/Mark variety through youth migration-for-education, a genuinely
  different local condition from either prior "Yes" finding in this run.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's no-dominant-tradition landscape is confirmed accurate by this pass. Its
  own "quiet, layered awareness of the city's own diplomatic origin" is real material, though not itself
  devotional — see Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new robot-specific extension.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep] — corrected 2026-08-10.** This file's original Electromagnetism (primary) /
  Fire (secondary) proposal is withdrawn as a proposal — but its own secondary candidate turns out to be the
  actual answer. Shirayuki's real pre-existing pick, per `city-symbol-pairs.md`, is **Solar symbol Uranus +
  Robo-Element Fire**. Fire's positive valence (passion, charisma, the courage to act and be seen) tracks the
  city's own Feeling/Compliant/Positive-Outlook register — "by a wide margin the warmest" of its Tri-Cities
  cluster — a direct, well-grounded fit this file had already half-recognized before reaching past it for a
  more elaborate Electromagnetism reading. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the reworked treatment of the
  Ashiato-crystallization material without the withdrawn Electromagnetism framing.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing note stands but is thin ("substantial... real
  institutional and creative infrastructure," no specifics) — confirmed rather than contradicted, with real
  expansion below.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** A sixth distinct Glitch-Coolant variant across this run, after bohemian/variety,
  working-class/potency, Casey's transience-driven pattern, Davis's genuine absence, and Kunlun's temporally-
  scattered pattern: home-crafted, small-batch, personally-formulated and traded within creative circles —
  directly mirroring the DIY ethos already established citywide for music (Home Recording Dens) and print
  (the Print & Zine Collective). Not curated-cosmopolitan, not mass-produced, not absent — made and shared the
  same way everything else creative gets made and shared here.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from Davis's collaboration-based courtship signal (deliberately differentiated,
  not a reskin): given the city's core "imposed becomes genuinely, visibly claimed" thematic DNA, a plausible
  Shirayuki-specific courtship gesture is a partner deliberately, permanently incorporating an element of the
  other's personal Ashiato style into their own — a color, pattern, or accessory type — worn not because it
  was given, but visibly made genuinely one's own, directly mirroring the city's own foundational arc at
  interpersonal scale rather than extending a generic "repeated collaboration" logic.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Given the population converged partly by diplomatic decree and partly by nationwide
  student draw, no single heritage-national vernacular fits the way it did at more organically-settled cities.
  The honest local source is Ashiato-movement vocabulary itself — genre/subculture-specific terminology (per
  the Catalog's own note on "subculture-specific vocabulary") that crosses every represented nation, since the
  movement's own origin was a *naming* act as much as a stylistic one. Robot angle: given robots hold real,
  valued standing across research, education, and the arts equally here (rather than concentrating in one
  economic sector, unlike most prior cities), robot artists specifically — not researchers or educators — are
  the more plausible primary coiners and spreaders of this vocabulary, since it originates from creative
  practice.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §24 is confirmed thin and generic in the source material — a genuine gap, unlike
  every prior city's rich, specific Arcanet finding. This pass proposes real expansion rather than leaving it
  unaddressed: given the city's extensive creative-distribution infrastructure (Album Drop Nights, the Print &
  Zine Collective, Ashiato look documentation), Shirayuki's Arcanet use plausibly skews toward creative
  sharing and distribution — closer to a genuine creative-community social layer than any other city's
  practical, research, or logistics-oriented use pattern found so far.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** The visible-craft manifestation of §3's identity finding: frame
  customization (paint patterns, accessory placement) plausibly functions as a recognized craft specialty
  within the Ashiato Ateliers' existing economy, alongside clothing — a genuine robot-specific trade skill,
  not just individual decoration.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Distinct from every prior use of the "enhanced robot perception" trope
  in this run (Janbogo/Davis/Kunlun all used heightened sound/vibration perception): given the Home Recording
  Dens' bedroom-studio culture, robot producers plausibly work through a structurally different production
  workflow than human producers — direct digital signal handling rather than an analog-instrument-plus-ear
  process — a genuine difference in *method*, not just perceptual acuity, giving this a distinct mechanism
  from the pattern used at the other three cities.
