## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct in mechanism from the prior three cities' teaching-lineage kinship structures
  (Janbogo's craft apprenticeship, Mirny's engineering apprenticeship, Casey's dispatch-judgment transfer):
  Davis's robot kinship structure is plausibly built on **collective research credit**, not individual
  teaching. Naturalist Ratna Wirawan's founding-era decision to decline solo credit for a major discovery —
  the origin point of the standing custom, kept on record as a specific historical occurrence in its own
  right — established an *ongoing* civic norm where a robot's genuine "family," in the Ch.11 sense, is
  whoever she shares discovery-credit with as a continuing, everyday practice across every project she works,
  not a one-off event and not who trained her technically. This resonates with Ch.11's own point that robots
  lack the genetics axis that produces hierarchy in human kinship — a credit-diffusing, explicitly
  non-hierarchical structure fits that absence unusually well.
- **Re-checked 2026-08-10 against the revised shared-experience-first Kinship framing — no change needed.**
  Unlike Mirny, Casey, Janbogo, and Byrd's original passes, Davis's Kinship finding was never mentor/mentee-
  shaped to begin with: it's already a horizontal, ongoing shared practice (collective credit across repeated
  collaboration) with no teaching hierarchy in it. The honest outcome here is confirmation, not revision.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "do the work well; let the work speak" gives an ethic genuinely distinct from
  Mirny's (skepticism toward *external*, untested claims) and Casey's (proactive disclosure for *someone
  else's* safety): Davis's is about self-effacement applied to *one's own* contribution. Given robots'
  precise internal logging capability, a plausible local expression is that a Davis robot's actual
  contribution gets verified through her own logged data rather than self-reported claims — trustworthiness
  measured by consistency between what she says she did and what her own record shows, not by external
  disclosure or skepticism toward others. A genuinely different target (self-claims, not others' claims or
  others' safety) from either prior city's version of "practical honesty."

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Davis is the first city in this run whose defining identity marker isn't
  wind-centric — its architecture is explicitly terrain-responsive rather than wind-first (Course of Events'
  own framing: "Building With the Land, Not Against It," a stated contrast to Tepenia's default approach used
  everywhere else so far). A plausible robot-only marker following that same logic: Davis robots, concentrated
  in environmental monitoring across a genuinely varied oasis (freshwater lakes, hypersaline lakes, fjords,
  each with distinct ice chemistry), plausibly develop unusually fine-grained, place-specific terrain-reading
  — not a generic environmental sense, but literally distinguishing which of many specific lakes is safe or
  stable at a glance or touch. Directly connects to §8's existing "that's a [Lake X] situation" idiom — this
  finding is the literal, technical version of what that folk phrase gestures at loosely.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). The
  population's human-majority reversal is a species-ratio fact, not a Gen/Mark fact, and doesn't substitute
  for a real finding here — recorded honestly rather than stretched to fill the section.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's no-dominant-tradition landscape is confirmed as accurate by this pass.
  See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new material that fills the gap without contradicting this.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Earth is already established canon for Davis (Davis = Earth/the breadbasket,
  per `city-symbol-pairs.md`) — a confirmation, not a new proposal, unlike Janbogo's and Mirny's first-ever
  informal picks. Robot angle: Earth's positive valence (reliability, patience, groundedness, unshowy
  support) plausibly validates robot researchers' and naturalists' own professional ethos directly — see
  Cross-Reference Synthesis for how Earth's *negative* valence maps onto an already-established internal
  tension with unusual precision.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing note stands: precision-oriented siligel preparation, "a
  matter of careful craft," similar in seriousness to Zhongshan's approach but without the ceremonial weight
  — consistent with the city's broader "attention to detail as civic virtue" ethos.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** No established human drinking-culture institution appears anywhere in the gathered
  material for Davis — no equivalent to Splinters (Casey) or Rough Bar Nights (Mirny). Rather than force a fit
  into one of the two canon Glitch-Coolant categories, the more honest local finding is genuine muted absence:
  minimal, private, institutionally invisible consumption, directly consistent with a civic ethos built
  entirely around "let the work speak" rather than any showy consumption culture. Tagged Deep rather than
  Surface since the finding itself is about what the absence *means* (a value statement), not about a visible
  practice.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from Mirny's action/reliability-demonstration, Casey's staying-vs-leaving
  signal, and Janbogo's sustained-hosting-gesture: given The Quiet Credit's collaborative-authorship norm
  (§1), a plausible Davis-specific courtship signal is repeated, voluntary collaboration choice — partnering
  on a specific research project or survey with the same person repeatedly, when other collaborators are
  genuinely available, functions as a real, socially readable signal in a culture that already tracks who
  works with whom for credit purposes. A direct extension of the city's own existing social currency
  (collaborative credit) into the one universal explicitly flagged as locality-variable.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes "that's a [Lake X] situation" as terrain-feature idiom for
  reliability or unpredictability. Robot angle: given robots' concentration in research and environmental
  monitoring (§17) and their fine-grained lake-reading expertise (§3), robot researchers plausibly coined and
  remain the most precise users of this idiom — deploying specific, accurate lake names rather than using it
  as loose generic shorthand, functioning almost as a technical classification system wearing the disguise of
  folk speech.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: research- and data-oriented,
  environmental monitoring and scientific record-keeping, explicitly noted in-canon as sharing more in common
  with Zhongshan's research orientation than with Casey's logistics focus or Mirny's infrastructure/relay
  focus. Nothing new to derive — recorded as confirmed, pre-existing canon. **Worth flagging forward:** since
  this similarity to Zhongshan is explicit in the source material itself, it's worth an explicit re-check once
  Zhongshan's own pass is run, the same way Mirny flagged Casey forward.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A fashion/maintenance-aesthetic marker keyed to terrain variety rather
  than wind exposure (distinct from every prior city's wind-derived marker): given the oasis's genuinely
  different lake chemistries (freshwater vs. hypersaline), robots specializing in different parts of the
  Vestfold Hills plausibly show visible differences in waterproofing or salinity-resistant surface treatment
  — a practical marker of which specific terrain a robot works, structurally similar to other cities'
  practical-markers-as-fashion but keyed to Davis's own defining variety (terrain/water) rather than wind.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §13's existing naturalist/observational visual-arts tradition, combined
  with §14's note that water sounds (lake ice shifting, fjord tidal movement) are unusually prominent here,
  and robots' genuinely wider sound/vibration perception (Ch.15) suggests a plausible robot-specific
  extension: field-recording or sound-naturalism as an art form parallel to the existing visual naturalist
  tradition, capturing ice/water acoustic detail humans structurally can't perceive the same way. Deliberately
  differs in both source (ice/water, not wind) and form (naturalist field-recording, not live performance or
  early-warning) from Janbogo's and Casey's own uses of the same physiological fact.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: A (§18; the Historical Vignette "The Lake That Told Two
  Different Stories") + C (the Sediment Core Archive) + D (the UK/Windermere-Lea Valley cautionary
  undercurrent)*
  The fuller treatment of §5's confirmed-absence finding. A founding-era methodological incident — sediment
  cores from two differently-salinated lakes showed a genuine discrepancy for the same historical period,
  determined to reflect real physical difference rather than researcher error — became a named civic lesson
  for Davis's research community. Extended as a belief-adjacent finding: rather than needing consistency or
  a single master narrative, Davis's closest thing to a shared ethic is genuine, lived comfort with true but
  contradictory data existing side by side without needing reconciliation. This is a fourth, genuinely
  distinct treatment of the Religion/Belief axis across this run — not reverence (Janbogo), not an
  undramatic verification-creed (Mirny), not anti-mythologizing of lost things (Casey), but comfort with
  unresolved multiplicity itself.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: A (§5's fault lines; Course of Events chain #8, "The Wish
  Nobody Says Out Loud") + E (Earth's own established dual valence) + C (the infrastructure record of Davis's
  deliberately modest, anti-glory civic architecture)*
  Earth's positive valence (reliability, patience, groundedness, unshowy support) and negative valence
  (stagnation, paralysis, hoarding, smothering) map onto Davis's own already-established internal fault line
  with unusual precision: satisfied quiet-competence vs. resentment of a *permanent* support role, and the
  specific private awareness (per the Course of Events material) that Davis could plausibly have been an
  assertive, Vladivostok-style port city and chose humility instead. This gives a real mechanism connecting
  folk-Elemental belief to lived civic tension, rather than treating them as two unrelated facts — and, like
  Casey's Wilkes irony, reads as genuinely strong, ready-made hook material for future questline or character
  work, not purely a culture finding.
