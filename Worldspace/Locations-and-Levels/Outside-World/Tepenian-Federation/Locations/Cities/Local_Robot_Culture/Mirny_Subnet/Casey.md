## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep] — added in the 2026-08-10 Kinship re-scan, applying the revised
  shared-experience-first framing.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  dispatchers, drivers, and logistics robots share the ongoing, everyday condition of *being the last stop* —
  living inside a constant rhythm of other people's arrivals and departures, where nobody around you is ever
  quite staying, and every day repeats the same basic shape (someone passing through needs something honest
  and fast from you) rather than converging toward a single settled community life the way a Windwright at
  Mirny's does. That ongoing condition, not any one storm or rescue, is what actually binds Casey's robots
  together horizontally — it holds between two robots who never worked the same shift, as long as both live
  the same transit-city rhythm. The trucker's-wrong-turn incident that produced §2's civic ethic is better kept
  as a specific historical/municipal occurrence in Casey's own record (real, worth preserving) rather than
  treated as the Kinship mechanism itself.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** A second, dispatch-specific shape the same underlying logic can take: the Junction
  Dispatch Office's training structure, where "Long Odds" Okonkwo-Hale's personal forecasting reputation ("no
  forecast on record was ever confirmed wrong") got formalized, per the candidate 2nd-order-change material,
  into **"stated margins"** — a transferable civic standard other dispatchers measure themselves against. A
  Casey robot trained into trusting stated margins the way the standard's own namesake did shares a mentor/
  mentee bond with her trainer — but per the revised framing, this is one particular *shape* the shared
  last-stop life above takes for dispatchers specifically, not a separate mechanism competing with it.
  (Okonkwo-Hale's own robot/human status isn't established anywhere in the source material — see the Reference
  Note below rather than an assumed resolution.)

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** This directly resolves the tension flagged at the end of Mirny's run. §6a's cardinal
  value — "be useful, be quick, be honest about the route" — reads similarly practical to Mirny's "competence
  over romance" on the surface, but the underlying *mechanism* is genuinely different once traced to its root:
  Mirny's ethic (per Bellandry's incident) is about **skepticism toward untested claims** — a self-directed
  engineering standard for a settled population managing its own environment. Casey's ethic (per the trucker's
  wrong-turn incident and the resulting dispatch-confirmation procedure) is about **proactive, accurate
  disclosure in service of someone else's safety** — a hospitality-to-strangers standard, because Casey's
  entire civic function is serving other people's journeys, not managing its own settled conditions. For
  robots specifically: given Arcanet-connected real-time access to road/supply data (§24), a Casey robot's
  version of "honest about the route" plausibly extends into actively surfacing relevant data unprompted
  rather than waiting to be asked — since in a junction city, delay itself can cost a stranger's safety in a
  way it wouldn't in a self-contained industrial city like Mirny.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Distinct from both Janbogo's thermal-cycling marker and Mirny's wind-load-
  sensing marker (the source material itself flags Casey's climate as "a genuinely different combined-exposure
  problem from Mirny's own purely katabatic-dominated profile"): Casey robots plausibly develop an unusually
  fast-cycling weather-model recalibration habit, tracking and rapidly distinguishing between two structurally
  different wind signatures (katabatic vs. maritime storm) that can arrive back-to-back, rather than one
  steady signature like Mirny's robots need to track. A robot from a single-wind-source city would plausibly
  need real, noticeable adjustment time to operate comfortably at Casey — a clean, city-specific, genuinely
  robot-only marker with no human equivalent.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Unlike Janbogo's and Mirny's clean nulls, Casey plausibly shows modest, genuine
  local flavor here — not identity-forming (consistent with Ch.14's non-hierarchical rule), but a more common,
  unremarkable social occurrence than elsewhere: as a junction city with constant robot-and-human transit,
  Casey robots regularly meet others from wildly different Gen/Mark origins as an ordinary fact of daily life,
  in a way a robot from an overwhelmingly locally-fabricated population wouldn't. Plausibly makes casual
  "which chamber are you from" comparison-talk a genuinely more common conversational move at Casey than at a
  settled city — still comparison, not identity-formation, so it doesn't contradict the universal's own
  non-hierarchical framing.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §18 confirms no dominant organized tradition ("the road doesn't wait for philosophy") —
  this pass fills the gap with material genuinely distinct from both Janbogo's quiet accreted reverence and
  Mirny's undramatic verification-creed: an explicitly **anti-mythologizing** civic creed, traced directly to
  the Wilkes excavation-abandonment incident (an early attempt to dig out the buried pre-exile ruins was
  abandoned once its real scale became clear, settling Wilkes's status as "a curiosity, not a destination"
  through direct lived experience, not received wisdom). Worth flagging explicitly: the post-war irony here is
  sharp and possibly worth developer attention — Casey's own physical infrastructure already notes (Finding C)
  that Casey is now exactly the same kind of thing to outsiders that Wilkes always was to Casey, meaning this
  creed's own logic now applies self-referentially to the city that built it.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Fire is already established canon for Casey (the Mars+Fire symbol pairing in
  Historical Vignettes, corrected 2026-08-06) — not a new proposal, a confirmation. Robot-specific angle: given
  the city's own internal tension between its quiet junction-service register and its loud Splinters-fame
  register (per the City Enneagram Personality section's own flagged contrast), robots who work Splinters
  specifically plausibly embody Fire's charismatic, publicly-seen register more consciously than robots in
  logistics/dispatch roles — a real, lived split within the robot population itself, not just an abstract
  civic duality.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing note stands: siligel resupply treated with "efficient
  professionalism" as a transit good, not a distinct craft tradition.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Casey's Glitch-Coolant profile is worth flagging as a genuinely new observation about
  how the two existing canon categories work: Casey doesn't fit bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety (which implies
  settled, connoisseur-style refinement across a fixed multinational population — Janbogo, Zhongshan,
  Shirayuki's pattern) or working-class/potency (limited selection, Mirny/Byrd/Rothera's pattern). See
  Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment — Casey's variety is driven by constant transient
  turnover, not by settled composition, a different mechanism arriving at superficially similar
  variety-outcomes to the bohemian category through a genuinely different local condition.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Emergent, Deep]** Distinct from both Mirny's action/reliability-demonstration courtship and Janbogo's
  sustained-hosting-gesture courtship: given Casey's own explicit "fast in, fast out, by design" hospitality
  ethos (§6b, directly traced to the failed lingering-lodging-house incident) and its population's genuine mix
  of transients and settled residents, a real, socially-recognized distinction plausibly exists between
  transient/traveler romance (understood as bounded, no expectation of permanence) and "staying" romance — the
  act of choosing not to leave when you could have functions as Casey's own version of a real courtship/
  commitment signal, since it's specifically a *transit* city's answer to what commitment looks like. This
  makes less sense transplanted onto a settled city like Mirny or Janbogo, where everyone's already staying by
  default — a genuine product of Casey's own specific civic condition.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes road/junction vocabulary bleeding into general speech ("at the
  junction" as recognizable Casey phrasing for an irreversible choice point) — a genuinely different
  vocabulary *source* than Janbogo's heritage-language hosting-phrase fossils or Mirny's technical-craft
  terminology: functional, operational road-jargon rather than either. Robot angle: dispatchers and logistics
  robots (§17's concentration sectors) plausibly coin and actively transmit most of this vocabulary, the same
  structural role Janbogo's hospitality workers and Mirny's Windwright trainees play for their own cities'
  vocabularies, just drawing from an operational rather than heritage or craft source.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: Casey's Arcanet is "practically
  oriented around road conditions, supply status, and travel coordination... a distinct third specialization"
  — explicitly self-differentiated in canon from Zhongshan's archival/social focus and Denison's
  hazard-monitoring focus. Nothing new to derive; recorded as confirmed, pre-existing canon, consistent with
  how Janbogo's own Arcanet finding worked.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Distinct from Janbogo's environmental-sound-incorporation music finding
  (a different mechanism, not a repeat): robot performers and sound-technicians at Splinters, given robots'
  genuinely wider sound/vibration perception range (Ch.15), plausibly excel at the kind of demanding,
  genre-spanning live-sound engineering Splinters' Classic Rock/Jazz/Blues/Acoustic Folk scene requires —
  technical precision applied to professional live performance, not aesthetic incorporation of ambient
  environmental sound the way Janbogo's finding works.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A fashion/maintenance-aesthetic marker distinct from both Janbogo's
  thermal-venting ornamentation and Mirny's static reinforcement-plating badge-marks: given the dual-source,
  rapid-transition wind exposure (§3, above), junction/dispatch-role robots plausibly favor visibly
  reconfigurable or rapid-adjustment weatherproofing gear — a marker of adaptability-in-use rather than
  accumulated wear, reading as a recognizable "junction worker" aesthetic distinct from a Windwright's more
  static badge-of-service look.
