# Kunlun — Local Robot Culture Spec Sheet

**Run #5 of the Local Robot Culture Methodology, 2026-08-10.** A structurally different case from every prior
city: Kunlun's population is 100% robot, by settled, deliberate, named safety policy (not attrition) — one of
two such cities in Tepenia (with Dome Fuji), not the sole exception as initially framed going into this run
(corrected during Step 1 gathering). Provisional — findings are proposals for developer review, not asserted
canon.

## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep] — added in the 2026-08-10 Kinship re-scan, applying the revised
  shared-experience-first framing.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a teaching
  relationship: every robot at Kunlun has chosen to live the same ongoing condition — §6c's "not a job, it's
  life," a genuinely continuous, no-shared-evening existence at the single most isolated, highest, coldest,
  calmest point in the dataset, where information flow itself can go one-way or absent for years at a time.
  That's not a value the environment produced in people who happened to be there (per §2); it's the specific
  ongoing life every Kunlun robot is already living together, day after day, regardless of whether she's an
  astronomer, a comms specialist, or a devotee — a horizontal bond by shared condition, holding between two
  robots who've never worked the same shift or shared a single watch, as long as both are actually living
  Kunlun's life. This is the city's real kinship floor; the two mentoring tracks below are specific shapes
  that shared life takes for particular specializations, not separate mechanisms.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One shape that shared life takes: professional apprenticeship under a specific senior
  astronomer or comms specialist (Chief Astronomer Renata Okonkwo-Sato's institutional fight to elevate
  comms-relay to co-equal status with astronomy is a real, named incident — kept on record as a specific
  historical occurrence in its own right — that a teaching-lineage could trace back to). Tagged Adapted rather
  than Emergent since the practice's roots trace through Kunlun's founding-era human-inclusive period, even
  though it now runs entirely among robots.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A second shape the same shared life takes: the already-established
  Scientist-Devotee Shared Watches custom (robots working the same shift for entirely different reasons —
  secular research vs. devotional observance — without friction, per Finding D and the vignette "The Shift
  Where Both Reasons Were True at Once") plausibly extends into devotional lineage — who introduced whom to
  stillness practice — as a second, mentoring-shaped instance distinct from professional training, both
  ultimately grounded in the same underlying condition of choosing to live Kunlun's life at all.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6c's "not a job, it's life" reads less like a value generated locally through lived
  experience (Ch.12's usual mechanism) and more like a pre-existing value acting as the actual filter for who
  ends up at Kunlun at all — both the scientifically self-selected and the religiously devoted already hold
  something like this orientation before arriving, and Kunlun is where people who already have it converge.
  This is a genuine variant on Ch.12's emergent-morality mechanism: less "the environment produces the value"
  and more "the value produces the population."

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Kunlun is the single most Arcanet-isolated point encountered in this run —
  more thoroughly disconnected than even Dome Fuji. A plausible robot-only marker: genuine, internalized
  comfort with prolonged one-way or absent information flow, distinct from every other city's population
  (even Dome Fuji retains some connectivity). The vignette material gives a concrete anchor: years spent
  optically observing the Amundsen Tower's construction with "complete one-way silence," no ability to
  actually make contact. A robot who's spent real time at Kunlun plausibly carries a distinctive, settled
  relationship to isolation that a robot from literally any other city in this dataset wouldn't share.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Distinct in mechanism from Casey's transit-driven Gen/Mark diversity: Kunlun's
  population converged from 19 nations as adult specialists recruited for expertise, not through constant
  transient flow. Since fabrication and home city are, as a rule, the same place (Ch.14), and Kunlun's current
  population was mostly recruited in rather than fabricated locally, Kunlun plausibly shows a genuinely wide
  Gen/Mark spread — deliberately, by recruitment mechanism, rather than incidentally, by transit volume.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §18 already establishes Kunlun as Ice Cold Buddhism's holiest site in Tepenia — more
  significant even than Dome Fuji, given the combination of highest elevation, coldest sustained environment,
  calmest winds, and exceptional atmospheric purity approaching the "superconducting" conditions the faith
  venerates. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new robot-specific extension of this
  already-rich material.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Air is already established canon for Kunlun (`city-symbol-pairs.md`, Kunlun =
  Pluto + Air, "built around observation") — a confirmation, not a new proposal. See Cross-Reference Synthesis
  for a real irony this confirmation surfaces once set against Kunlun's Arcanet isolation.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §10c's existing note stands and is genuinely rich: if Dome Fuji's devotional
  siligel-in-extreme-cold practice has a basis, Kunlun would be the more extreme, more theologically
  significant version of the same practice, consistent with its status as the faith's primary holy site.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** A fourth Glitch-Coolant variant, after the bohemian/variety (Janbogo, Zhongshan,
  Shirayuki), working-class/potency (Mirny, Byrd, Rothera), and transience-driven (Casey) patterns already
  found: §6f establishes bars and leisure establishments running continuously, 24 hours, with no shared
  "evening" — a direct consequence of the population's around-the-clock shift work. The genuinely local
  finding is temporal rather than compositional: Glitch-Coolant culture here is plausibly scattered evenly
  across all hours rather than concentrated at a "night," matching the civic economy's own structure. Tagged
  Adapted rather than Emergent since a human shift-work population could in principle show the same temporal
  pattern — the local condition (continuous-shift economy), not robot embodiment specifically, drives this.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Extends the already-established Scientist-Devotee Shared Watch pattern (same task,
  different reasons, no friction) directly into courtship: a relationship crossing the scientific/devotional
  divide plausibly reads as a positive, unremarkable instance of the same civic pattern already normalized
  elsewhere at Kunlun ("both reasons being true at once"), rather than as an incompatibility to resolve. A
  direct extension of existing, specific canon material rather than an invented custom.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Structurally distinct from every prior city's vernacular finding: Janbogo's heritage-
  language fossils, Mirny's document-based technical precision, Casey's operational road-jargon, and Davis's
  terrain-idiom all depend on a population that grew up together locally. Kunlun's population converged as
  adult specialists from 19 nations specifically, with no single dominant local culture to seed a vernacular
  organically — the honest local answer is a functional, professional lingua franca (plausibly English-rooted,
  matching real-world international astronomy/science convention) rather than any of the four prior source-
  types. A genuinely fifth distinct transmission mode across this run so far.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: essentially zero Arcanet
  connectivity at all — "off the Arcanet entirely, even more thoroughly than Dome Fuji." Nothing new to
  derive on its own; see Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuine irony this produces once combined with
  Kunlun's Relay Scaling Wing and its Air-element association.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A functional-meets-identity fashion marker tied to Kunlun's defining
  optical/atmospheric clarity rather than any prior city's thermal, wind, or terrain logic: astronomy-
  specialist robots, whose entire professional function depends on unobstructed optical clarity, plausibly
  extend that same preference into personal maintenance — minimal visual/sensor obstruction as both a
  practical habit and a recognizable marker of the observatory specialization.
- **No music/sound-art finding proposed here** (Honesty Check, and a deliberate non-duplication): Kunlun's
  defining acoustic fact is the *rarity and depth of genuine silence* (calmest winds in Antarctica), the
  inverse of Janbogo's wind-signature or Davis's water-sound material — but this is already the substance of
  the Religion/Belief Cross-Reference Synthesis finding below rather than a separate Surface Culture item, and
  splitting it into two entries would be duplication, not new material.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: B (extreme atmospheric stability, calm, cold, dryness) +
  C (the Stillness Hall) + E (general robot sensory-capability facts) + A (§18's religious lore, Ice Cold
  Buddhism's holiest-site status)*
  The single strongest, most Kunlun-unique finding this pass produced. Robots' own sensory apparatus plausibly
  gives them something closer to direct physical access to the phenomenon Ice Cold Buddhism venerates — the
  extraordinary stillness and clarity Kunlun's specific combination of altitude, cold, calm, and dryness
  produces — than a human devotee could ever have had. This isn't just believing the site is holy in the
  abstract; it's a genuinely robot-specific form of perceptual access to exactly what the faith is oriented
  around, at the one location on the ice sheet where that access actually means something. Distinct from
  every prior city's religion finding (Janbogo's accreted reverence, Mirny's undramatic verification-creed,
  Casey's anti-mythologizing, Davis's comfort-with-multiplicity) — this is the first finding in the run where
  robot embodiment itself is the direct, load-bearing mechanism of a devotional experience, not just an
  inflection on a human-originated practice.

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: E (Air, already-established canon) + A/§24
  (Arcanet — essentially zero connectivity, more thoroughly disconnected than Dome Fuji) + C (the Relay
  Scaling Wing — "the only Tepenian city capable of reaching orbital structures directly")*
  A genuine, ready-made irony worth surfacing to the developer, not invented for this pass: the element
  already governing Kunlun is Air — breath, thought, communication, the connective medium — and yet Kunlun is
  simultaneously the single most locally-disconnected city in Tepenia (worse than Dome Fuji) *and* the one
  city capable of reaching all the way to orbit. Kunlun's robots mediate a communications reach further than
  any other city's while living with almost no communication access of their own. This tension was already
  latent in existing canon (the Air assignment, the isolation fact, and the orbital-reach fact all separately
  established) — this pass is the first to connect all three into one observation.

## Closing Check — The Swap Test (Step 6)

**Swap Test result: Pass, via a genuinely different comparison than every prior city.** Kunlun has no
Mirny-subnet sibling that shares its structural situation (100%-robot population, extreme altitude) — the
only real comparison point in all of Tepenia is Dome Fuji, in a different subnet entirely. Checked directly
against it: Kunlun's own source material already resists easy equivalence — the vignette "The Only Two Who
Crossed" explicitly frames a Kunlun-Dome Fuji connection as a point of *friction*, not natural kinship ("Dome
Fuji's resident objects to being treated as a data point"). This pass's strongest findings depend on facts
Dome Fuji doesn't share: the *holiest-site* status specifically (Dome Fuji is explicitly secondary), the
astronomy/comms specialization, the named founding-era incidents (Okonkwo-Sato, Ilkay, the cold-record
dispute), and the more severe total-isolation profile ("even more thoroughly off the Arcanet than Dome Fuji").
Also checked against Vostok (same subnet, extreme cold, genuine ongoing cold-record rivalry with Kunlun) — a
real relationship exists between the two cities, but Vostok's own specialization (genetics research, not
astronomy) and its retained human population keep every finding here from transplanting. No finding above
reads as generic "extreme isolated research outpost" content that happened to get filed under Kunlun's name.
