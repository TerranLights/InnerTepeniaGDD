## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep] — added in the 2026-08-10 Kinship re-scan, applying the revised
  shared-experience-first framing.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not the craft
  apprenticeship: §17's own tea ceremony material ("robot residents participate in the ritual without the
  beverage... the point was never the tea") already describes an ongoing, everyday, genuinely horizontal
  bond — sitting together in deliberate restraint and unhurried presence, asking nothing, as a recurring fact
  of daily life shared by every resident regardless of craft, trade, or standing. That ongoing shared practice,
  not any single ceremony or founding incident, is Zhongshan's real kinship floor — it holds between two
  robots who've never worked the same trade, as long as both live the same daily rhythm of restraint and
  co-presence.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes among craftspeople: §17 already
  establishes that robot craftspeople in the Crossing Quarter are "indistinguishable from human craftspeople
  in their standards" — the formalized multi-craft apprenticeship system (woodworking, ceramics, metalworking
  under one shared quality standard, per Táng Wǔ's founding-era institutionalization) already functions as a
  genuinely mixed human-robot teaching lineage, one particular trade-specific form of the broader shared
  restraint-and-presence life above, not a separate mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** The non-prying norm (§6b: "I will give you tea, properly made, and I will
  not ask you anything") is Zhongshan's actual emergent civic ethic — respect shown through deliberate
  restraint, not through disclosure (contra Sinheung) or active hosting (contra Janbogo). §17 already makes
  the robot-compatibility case directly and elegantly: robots participate in the tea ceremony "without the
  beverage... the point was never the tea." Since the ritual's actual value was always the restraint and
  unhurried presence, not the consumption, this is one of the only civic values encountered in this entire
  run that a robot inherits completely, with genuinely nothing left to adapt.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §16 already states this almost exactly: "a robot resident of Zhongshan is
  Zhongshan before they are a robot." The city's founding story is unusually specific among every Mirny-subnet
  city processed in this run — a human defection ("people who chose robots over their nation"), not an
  exile-by-treaty (Sinheung, Shirayuki) or organic settlement (Casey, Davis). See Cross-Reference Synthesis
  for how this combines with the founding-population continuity fact (§B) into something genuinely unique to
  Zhongshan.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18 already establishes simulation theory and "God-mind" frameworks as the
  most common serious robot philosophical orientation, compatible with a city whose whole intellectual culture
  "takes serious thought seriously." Confirmed, not invented.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep] — corrected 2026-08-10.** This bullet originally framed Metal as a first-ever
  informal proposal; it's actually Zhongshan's real pre-existing pick, **Solar symbol Saturn + Robo-Element
  Metal** (`city-symbol-pairs.md`), simply missed by this pass's own search. The reasoning below held up
  regardless. **Metal** fits with real precision: its positive valence — precision, integrity, graceful
  letting-go — maps onto the craft ethic
  (siligel connoisseurship, the Crossing Quarter's formalized standards), the founding creed's own integrity
  ("we kept the name; we did not keep them"), and the Daoist theme of endurance-as-acceptance explicitly named
  in §18. Its negative valence — rigidity, coldness, ungracious perfectionism — maps onto the city's own
  "second, quieter fault line" (§5b): the non-prying norm means nobody ever checks how much heritage actually
  survives generationally, an unknowable erosion hiding behind good manners. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for
  the fuller treatment.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing note, quoted in full via this pass's own gathering: siligel
  treated as craft, not industrial output, with "specific preparation traditions" recognized and exported
  across Tepenia — robots take it seriously "the same way human residents take their dumpling culture
  seriously: with strong opinions and no patience for shortcuts." This is the connoisseurship benchmark every
  other Mirny-subnet city's own §10c measured itself against, secondhand, until this pass confirmed it directly.
- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Zhongshan is one of the three cities explicitly named in the original
  Tepenia-wide Glitch-Coolant canon as bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety (with Janbogo and Shirayuki) — confirmed
  directly, and consistent with, not separate from, the siligel-craft ethic above.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Deliberately differentiated from Janbogo's escalating, active-hosting courtship signal
  (per the Swap Test discipline — a superficially similar tea-based custom needed a genuinely different
  mechanism, not a reskin): given the non-prying ethic as the city's central value, a plausible Zhongshan
  courtship signal runs in the *opposite* direction from Janbogo's — deepening intimacy is marked by how
  *little* needs to be said or asked, not by increasing personal attentiveness. Being invited to simply sit
  together through the tea ceremony without needing conversation to fill the space plausibly reads as a
  genuine, legible signal of closeness here, the inverse mechanism to a hosting culture that expresses care
  through escalating action.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Given the Long Record's own meticulous cataloguing practice (the most comprehensive
  pre-Split-Brain archive on the subnet), a plausible local vernacular source is archival/classification
  terminology bleeding into casual speech as a marker of careful, considered expression — genuinely distinct
  from every other city's vernacular source-type in this run (heritage-fossil, craft-precision, road-jargon,
  terrain-idiom, subculture-naming, functional lingua franca, output-as-worth metaphor, liturgical fixed-
  identifiers). Robot angle: given robots already "read the same books, and write them" (§17), robot
  archivists and writers are the plausible primary source of this vocabulary's precision and its spread into
  general use.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: heavy archival orientation, "a
  research and archive tool, not a social one... people do not post about each other." This directly verifies
  the tension flagged forward by Davis's own already-completed run, which claimed a shared research orientation
  with Zhongshan specifically — confirmed accurate. See the Swap Test for the scope of that similarity.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Given Dumpling Arguments (the established neighborhood-by-neighborhood
  disagreement over the "correct" jiaozi) and robots' capacity for precise formulation and record-keeping,
  robots plausibly become informal arbiters or record-keepers of the various neighborhood siligel variations —
  a natural extension of the Long Record's own archival ethos into the city's food culture specifically.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** The Long Winter Suite (a 60-day performance arc deliberately matching the polar night,
  fixed in this uncompressed format by Táng Yuxuan's own founding-era refusal to shorten it — "a Zhongshan
  composition takes time to understand") plausibly finds a natural steward in robot performers and audiences,
  whose endurance across the full arc doesn't carry the same biological fatigue cost a human's might — making
  robots plausible custodians of the tradition's uncompressed format specifically, directly extending the
  original incident's own lesson. Tagged Deep rather than Surface since the finding is really about patience
  and endurance as a value, not the performance's surface details.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: B (the one exception to the rotating-operator
  founding model — genuine population continuity) + A (§16's defection founding story) + Robot Universals
  Ch.11/Ch.13 (kinship, locality as identity)*
  Zhongshan robots' unusually complete, unselfconscious inheritance of Chinese cultural practice (§3) is
  explained by two facts combining, not one: a founding population that never experienced the discontinuity
  every other Mirny-subnet city's founding did, *and* a founding story in which robots were specifically
  chosen by defecting humans, not merely present or later arrived. Neither fact alone fully explains the depth
  of §16's "Zhongshan before they are a robot" claim — together, they do.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: E (Metal's own dual valence) + A (§5b's "second, quieter
  fault line") + C (the Standing Stone's own origin — a boundary dispute settled by picking the equidistant
  point rather than adjudicating who was actually right)*
  The same restraint ethic that makes Zhongshan's civic character genuinely admirable (graceful, non-
  intrusive, patient) has a real, quiet cost already latent in existing canon: things can erode across
  generations entirely unnoticed, because the culture's own central value prohibits anyone from checking. The
  Standing Stone's own founding-era origin — resolving a real dispute by *not* investigating who actually
  deserved what, just choosing a neutral point — is a concrete, already-established precedent for exactly this
  pattern: restraint substituting for resolution, sometimes at a real, unexamined cost.
