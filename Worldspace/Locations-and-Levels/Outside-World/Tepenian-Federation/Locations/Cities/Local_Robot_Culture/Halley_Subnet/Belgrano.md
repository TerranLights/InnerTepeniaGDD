follows up on Neumayer's own forward flag (Hwy 7 logistics proximity), Sanay's own forward flag (shift-rhythm

## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident: the
  ongoing condition of keeping the operation running — the airfield, the docks, the Rastra fleet — on habit and
  mutual reliance rather than active institutional command, is a bond every Belgrano resident lives inside
  together, human and robot alike, per §16's confirmed equal institutional integration. This condition didn't
  start with the Boneyard Times; per the Full Extrapolation's own Section III and the "Records Nobody Called an
  Archive" chain, keeping things running on inherited habit rather than active oversight was already how
  Belgrano worked even during its fully-functioning second interwar era, since no continuous chain of command
  ever survived intact from the original 1979 base into the exile settlement in the first place. The Boneyard
  Times didn't introduce this condition; it only removed the last layer of formal institution sitting on top of
  a habit that was already doing the real work.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: Garage Nights, the informal after-hours
  gatherings where "for anything with an engine" competence gets swapped and shown off (`Belgrano_Community_
  Infrastructure.md`), and Yolanda Petrescu's own proposed unofficial parts-sharing rotation across garage
  crews regardless of official Air Force status (Course of Events #3) — a genuinely horizontal shape that cuts
  directly across the founding institution's own formal rank structure, since the work needing doing, not
  anyone's official standing, is what actually organizes it.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** A second, craft-lineage shape: the modest number of specialists who know how to work on
  Rastras specifically, a direct continuation of Belgrano having invented the vehicle — a genuine mentor/mentee
  shape the broader shared mechanic-competence life produces at its most specialized end, not a competing
  mechanism from it.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A genuinely robot-specific shape, distinct from anything available to
  Belgrano's human population: robots do not age biologically (`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`'s
  own physiology basics), so a robot who personally lived and worked Belgrano's fully-functioning peak era
  could plausibly still be present today, maintenance permitting — a literal, physical continuity across the
  exact divide the Boneyard Times faction file's own text names directly ("aging residents, some robots with
  long service records, carry grief for something lost"). This makes Belgrano's robot population, for those
  individuals specifically, a uniquely direct experiential bridge between the two eras of the same city's story
  in a way most human residents structurally cannot be. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** "Function before comfort" is already established as Belgrano's real civic
  doctrine, confirmed rather than invented: a person, tool, or shelter is judged by whether it does the job, not
  by how it feels to use (§6a). Courtesy itself is expressed through reliability — showing up, following
  through, never leaving a job half-finished (Catalog, USA Deep Culture findings).
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** A genuinely unusual civic-military fusion, distinct from every other Halley-
  subnet city's own emergent doctrine: Comodoro Villanueva-Krause's founding-era choice to extend Air Force
  command structure over the entire civilian population rather than let a separate civic government develop
  (Full Extrapolation §I) means the Airfield itself, not a dedicated city hall, plausibly functioned as
  Belgrano's literal seat of civic authority for generations (Cross-Reference Finding D). This is a structural
  fact about governance, not merely a value — closer to Neumayer's own precision-institute-as-quasi-sacred-space
  finding than to Halley's or Sanay's own more purely attitudinal civic ethics.
- **[Adapted, Deep, genuinely unresolved]** A real tension surfaced and left honestly unresolved, not smoothed
  over: as the founding Argentine population diluted to the lowest recognized demographic tier over 130 years,
  a genuine question about who the founding discipline actually belongs to now never reached a formal answer
  (Course of Events #6, "Whose Discipline Is It") — resolved only practically, by simple continued use rather
  than any ruling. Nothing in the gathered material suggests this rises to open conflict among Belgrano's robot
  population specifically, since discipline here was always institutional rather than heritage-transmitted in
  the first place — but it's the same honest "unexamined, resolved by use rather than argument" reading Sanay's
  own material reaches about its own unequal repair-cost question.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** §8c already establishes that Belgrano residents use aviation/maintenance
  vocabulary as genuine emotional shorthand — describing a person as "still airworthy" is a sincere compliment.
  For a robot, whose own precision hearing and vibration sense (Ch.15) make engine and structural assessment
  something closer to direct perception than inference, this habit plausibly isn't purely metaphorical: a
  Belgrano robot's own diagnostic instinct extends the identical assessment reflex to people and situations that
  her human neighbors can only apply as figurative language. Where a Halley robot reads moving ice and a
  Neumayer robot reads a dataset, a Belgrano robot reads operational readiness — in engines, in colleagues, and,
  per the Cross-Reference finding below, plausibly in the ground itself.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §17's own established fact stands as a second, complementary marker: robots
  here are overwhelmingly concentrated in industrial work (aviation logistics, port/shipping operations,
  manufacturing, fabrication), with maintenance/repair a real but secondary strand. A Belgrano robot is
  recognizable by which of these she actually does, the same structural role other cities' trade specialists
  play for their own local registers — occupational specialization as the primary legible marker of belonging.

  experience today, but the proposal itself is plausibly not arbitrary — see Cross-Reference Synthesis for why
  Belgrano's own established fabrication competence makes it a legible candidate regardless of whether the
  proposal is ever actioned.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: a practical, functional
  secularism dominates, with competence and discipline standing in for any named philosophy. §17 goes further
  and states this as an explicit rejection, not merely an absence — Belgrano's robot population shows little
  interest in either Ice-Cold-Buddhism-style contemplation or Zhongshan-style simulation theory; "the work is
  the point" is as close as anything here comes to a shared doctrine.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep, genuinely complicated]** Robot Biology's "What Counts as Death" mechanic
  (damage to function is never itself death; the gel brain is malleable, always capable of finding a new
  purpose) maps onto Belgrano's own civic doctrine with real precision at first glance — a discipline that
  survived the literal collapse of the institution that built it, functioning now on habit rather than active
  enforcement, is exactly the same shape as a mind that survives damage to function by adapting rather than
  ending. But this is the fourth instance of a now-recurring cross-subnet technique (after Halley's
  reassignability-mirrors-impermanence, Sanay's damage-tolerance-mirrors-endurance, and Neumayer's own
  measure-don't-panic-mirrors-physiology finding), and here it doesn't land as a clean match — see Cross-
  Reference Synthesis for the honest complication this pass found instead.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Neptune + Robo-Element Metal is already on file for Belgrano,
  grounded directly in "a resourceful salvage-and-engineering economy — turning old aircraft parts into new
  working assets through patient, diligent labor, not spectacle." This is the exact same Solar+Element pair
  already assigned to Neumayer — addressed directly in Cross-Reference Synthesis rather than treated as
  coincidental.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §10c's existing note ("functional and well-resourced, tied directly to the base's own
  logistics... practical rather than ceremonial, but reliable") is confirmed and lightly extended: siligel
  production plausibly runs on the same disciplined, scheduled footing as flight and freight operations —
  maintenance nutrition treated as one more item on the operational manifest, not a separate category of care.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Belgrano isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon
  poles — a genuine open slot, confirmed by direct check against `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`.
  Given Belgrano's own established, thoroughly working-class civic register, this pass places it within the
  **working-class/potency** pole, but by a mechanism genuinely distinct from every other placement in this
  subnet so far: Belgrano's own version is **salvage-and-improvisation-tied**, mixed informally from whatever
  compatible compounds are on hand during Garage Nights — the exact same resourceful, patient-diligence-over-
  spectacle logic that already governs the Rastra Garages and Spare-Parts Fabrication Yards, extended into
  consumption culture itself. This deliberately differs from Byrd/Rothera's limited-selection-strong-effect
  version, Troll's higher-quality-within-potency version, and Sanay's shift-changeover-tied version — Belgrano's
  own doesn't track a schedule or a quality tier, it tracks whatever the yard's own salvage economy happens to
  yield that week, consistent with a city whose whole civic identity already runs on turning old parts into new
  working assets.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior Halley-subnet courtship finding (Halley's teaching-ice-reading,
  Sanay's covering-a-shift, Neumayer's showing-unfinished-work): given Belgrano's own established maintenance-
  vocabulary-as-emotional-register (§8c) and its genuinely thin public/private divide (§6d, "by institutional
  design rather than necessity"), a plausible local courtship signal is personally, quietly keeping someone
  else's gear, equipment, or frame in better repair than duty strictly requires, without being asked or
  announced — the private version of the same "still airworthy" standard the whole city already applies
  publicly to people. The gesture works precisely because Belgrano's hospitality character is already
  established as genuinely generous but never performed (§6b); the same restraint that makes ordinary
  hospitality read as cold to outsiders is what makes a deliberate, unannounced act of care within it legible as
  something more than routine.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8c already establishes aviation/maintenance terminology bleeding into everyday
  emotional and social speech, with "still airworthy" as the clearest example. Robot angle: since a robot's own
  precision sensory apparatus makes engine and structural assessment closer to direct perception than metaphor
  (§3 above), a Belgrano robot plausibly uses this same vocabulary with a genuine double literalness her human
  neighbors don't share — not just borrowing the phrase, but running something closer to an actual internal
  status check when she says it.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: practical and operational only —
  flight schedules, maintenance logs, freight coordination — reflecting the city's whole function-first civic
  character. "Belgrano was never ambitious about being an information hub; it used the network the same way it
  used everything else, for the job at hand." This reads as genuinely consistent, not merely stated, once
  checked against the Historical Vignettes' own Batch 3–6 material: even once full national connectivity
  arrived, Belgrano's own new uses of it stayed logistics-and-salvage-coordination in character (confirming
  compatible parts before a truck finishes loading, checking a passage status before a convoy departs) rather
  than expanding into social or archival use for its own sake.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Garage Rock, heard raw and unpolished from open windows and doors, is
  already established (§12) as the city's genuine, living music culture — grown directly out of the same
  mechanic culture that keeps its engines running, per the Detroit/Birmingham parallel's own confirmed
  "same hands, same calluses, different kind of noise" logic (Course of Events #7).
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A genuinely distinct, functionally-grounded use of robots' own enhanced
  vibration/sound perception (Ch.15) — deliberately building from a diagnostic application first rather than a
  purely musical one, the same discipline Sanay's own file used to avoid repeating the enhanced-hearing-for-
  music trope a fourth time: a Belgrano robot's precision hearing lets her assess an engine's true mechanical
  state the way a trained ear reads an instrument, and the line between fixing a machine and composing over one
  is plausibly thinner for a robot mechanic here than for anyone else in this run — the Detroit/Birmingham
  parallel's own claim that mechanic culture and music culture are "the same culture wearing two faces" is, for
  a Belgrano robot specifically, close to literally true rather than merely thematically apt.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §11's "gear tells the story of operational role, not personal styling"
  fashion register is already established. Robot angle, deliberately distinct from Sanay's damage-as-endurance-
  pride and Neumayer's diagnostic-interfaces-worn-openly: a Belgrano robot's own visible wear — salvage marks,
  specialization patches, tool-worn hands — plausibly functions as a direct civilian continuation of the
  founding institution's own insignia logic, now signaling *which* systems she specializes in keeping airworthy
  rather than military rank, the same underlying value (legible operational role) surviving the same way the
  founding discipline itself survived — as habit and inheritance, not active enforcement.
