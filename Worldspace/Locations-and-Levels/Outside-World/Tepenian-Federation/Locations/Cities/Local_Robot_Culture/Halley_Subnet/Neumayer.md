## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  the deliberately-designed overlapping-shift work system — engineers, researchers, and technicians rotating
  through staggered shifts specifically so nobody burns out, per the City Vision Notes session — means no
  Neumayer worker, robot or human, ever finishes a piece of precision work entirely alone. Handoff to the next
  overlapping shift is a constant, structural fact of daily life: verifying, re-checking, and trusting the
  next set of hands to continue exactly where the last left off, in a city where sloppy work is treated as a
  genuine social lapse (§6a). That ongoing, mutual, load-bearing trust in each other's precision — not any
  single project or crisis — is Neumayer's real kinship floor, genuinely horizontal across every technical
  role. Distinct in mechanism from Sanay's own shift-rotation Kinship floor (§1, Sanay): Sanay's rotation is
  externally paced by freighter arrival schedules; Neumayer's is internally designed, specifically engineered
  against burnout rather than driven by any outside logistics rhythm.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: §17's own established tradition — robots who
  have personally maintained a single monitoring project across generations are treated with the same reverence
  other cities reserve for founding figures. This is a legacy-stewardship shape the broader overlapping-shift
  culture produces at its longest timescale, not a competing mechanism — the same structural respect Halley
  gives to relocation-cycle veterans and Sanay gives to reliable shift veterans, here earned through
  unbroken data-continuity rather than movement or rotation-endurance.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** "Measured, Not Debated" is already established as Neumayer's real civic
  doctrine, confirmed rather than invented — and per the Catalog's own Synthesis Notes, carries a specifically
  *stricter* register than Halley's own version of the shared Methodologists faction: "rigor is measurement,
  close to sacred; skeptical of interdisciplinary work that hasn't fully honored what it's drawing from,"
  versus Halley's more forgiving "rigor is whatever survives being wrong." Sloppy work and unverified claims
  are a real, checkable social lapse here (§6a), not merely a professional one.
- **[Adapted, Deep, genuinely unresolved]** A real internal friction, surfaced by the Catalog and not smoothed
  over: four distinct national research-prestige traditions (American mission-driven, French elite-education-
  adjacent, German institute-methodical, British campus-engineering) coexist at one city, each converging on
  the same underlying value — technical achievement as status — by genuinely different routes. Nothing in the
  gathered material suggests this rises to open conflict, but it's a real, plausible source of quiet
  status friction (whose research tradition's judgment carries more weight in a dispute) that the city's own
  otherwise-unified "rigor over credentials" doctrine doesn't fully resolve on its own.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Neumayer robot is recognizable by treating her own self-diagnostic and
  recalibration routines with the same scheduled rigor the city applies to its instruments — performed on a
  fixed cadence rather than only when something feels wrong, extending "Measured, Not Debated" reflexively
  into her own embodied maintenance rather than only her external work. Where a Sanay robot reads a city's
  repair history off its structure, and a Halley robot reads moving ice, a Neumayer robot reads herself with
  the identical discipline she applies to a dataset — the city's whole civic virtue turned inward.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A genuine, city-specific irony, not present at either Halley or Sanay: per
  `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`, functional chambers were historically installed in almost every
  Tepenian city, but ongoing *manufacturing* is confirmed at only Sinheung and Byrd — Neumayer designs the
  current-generation (Mark IV) schematic those two cities actually build from, but has never been established
  as a manufacturing site itself. A Neumayer-fabricated robot's own honest answer to "what Mark are you" quite
  plausibly points to an older, installed-generations-ago chamber, not the current Mark IV design her own
  city's engineers are actively refining for everyone else. The city that authors the present generation's
  design doesn't necessarily build robots to it herself — the uncredited-engineering pattern (§2, §Cross-
  Reference Synthesis) recurring one level deeper than anywhere else it's been found.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: no dominant formal
  religion, the closest thing a near-secular faith in rigorous method itself — "patient, systematic measurement
  is how a civilization actually endures" — expressed through the unbroken climate record rather than explicit
  doctrine, complicated by the Tower's own quiet, un-publicly-grieved erasure of Neumayer's name from its most
  famous achievement.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Robot Biology's own confirmed fact that a gel brain's data "is not static —
  constantly moving, constantly shifting," never a fixed, readable snapshot, gives Neumayer's civic faith in
  perpetual, never-finished measurement a literal embodied counterpart its human residents can only hold as a
  practiced value: a robot's own cognition already *is* the kind of continuously-updating, never-final dataset
  the city spends its whole civic life maintaining about the ice shelf beneath it.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Neptune + Robo-Element Metal is already on file for Neumayer,
  grounded directly in "precise, self-contained work that rewards inquiry rather than announcing itself." Both
  halves track the city's whole civic identity with real precision — quiet capability, uncredited by design,
  and cool, exacting material discipline.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §10c's existing note ("precision-oriented, consistent with the city's whole
  engineering sensibility... siligel production treated as another instrumented, carefully-measured process")
  is confirmed and lightly extended: siligel batches here are plausibly documented and version-tracked the same
  way any other Neumayer data product would be, treating a robot's own maintenance nutrition with the identical
  rigor applied to atmospheric readings.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Neumayer isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon
  poles — a genuine open slot, confirmed by direct check against `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`.
  This project's own prior development (City Vision Notes, Full Extrapolation §VII, the "Brewer Who Wouldn't
  Serve by Feel" Historical Vignette, Course of Events candidate #9) already resolved it as a **third,
  precisely-calibrated category** — exact, reproducible effects rather than variety or raw potency, "Measured,
  Not Debated" applied to drinking culture. The genuinely robot-specific extension this pass adds: a robot's
  own established physiology (real-time internal thermal/energy-state monitoring, per Robot Biology's coolant-
  as-thermal-regulation mechanic) gives her a literal, built-in capacity to self-verify a calibrated dose with
  a precision no human drinker could match by feel alone — making robots the natural, de facto standard-setters
  and enforcers of the exact-specification brewing practice the human brewer in the founding Vignette had to
  learn from an apprentice's borrowed instruments.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from both subnet-mates' own courtship findings (Halley's teaching-ice-reading,
  Sanay's covering-a-shift): given Neumayer's own established social contract — rigor as public civic virtue,
  personal life kept genuinely private (§6d), and professional imprecision treated as a real social lapse
  (§6a) — a plausible local courtship signal is deliberately letting a specific person see unfinished,
  unverified work first, before it's checked and made presentable. In a culture where showing anyone flawed
  work is otherwise avoided almost reflexively, choosing to be seen mid-error by one particular person is a
  genuine, costly act of trust — the one context where Neumayer's usual precision-first self-presentation is
  deliberately set aside.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes referencing specific measurement/monitoring data points in
  casual conversation as a very Neumayer habit, one that reads as odd to outsiders. Robot angle: given a gel
  brain's own confirmed non-static, continuously-updating nature, a Neumayer robot plausibly extends this habit
  one step further than her human counterparts — hedging even personal, conversational claims with an implicit
  "as of my last check" register, treating her own memory the same provisional way the city treats a dataset,
  rather than as settled fact.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: data-heavy and archival use,
  dominated by the city's own scientific record-keeping, consistent with its whole institutional identity. See
  Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment tying this to the redundant off-site archival practice and
  the still-open Planetary Split Brain question.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Electronic, Metal, and Digital-Industrial music dominating the After-Hours
  Sound Venues is already established (§12) as the city's genuine emotional release valve — machinery-adjacent,
  precision-coded genres fitting the city's whole civic identity, confirmed via City Vision Notes rather than
  left as a gap.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §11's "clothing reads as equipment more than style" fashion register is
  already established. Robot angle, deliberately distinct from Sanay's own "visible repair welds worn as
  pride" finding: since robots don't need the same protective bulk humans do, a Neumayer robot's own visible
  diagnostic and calibration interfaces — normally functional, utilitarian hardware — plausibly get worn
  openly and legibly rather than concealed, the same civic value (precision, verifiability) expressed as a
  visible statement rather than Sanay's endurance-through-damage aesthetic.
- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §13's "data visualization and technical documentation carry genuine
  cultural weight here — a beautifully rendered atmospheric dataset is treated with the kind of appreciation
  other cities reserve for painting" is already established, giving Neumayer a genuinely distinct visual-arts
  register from anywhere else in the subnet.
