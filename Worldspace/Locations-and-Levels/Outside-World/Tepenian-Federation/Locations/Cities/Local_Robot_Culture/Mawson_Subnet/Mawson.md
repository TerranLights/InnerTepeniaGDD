## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Led with the shared-experience question first, per the revised process: what do Mawson's
  robots actually share, day to day? The already-established Long Table Hall and its Nation Nights rotation —
  every represented nation's dishes served nightly, side by side, deliberately designed so no single national
  identity gatekeeps what counts as "local" — is a genuinely horizontal, cross-national kinship bond, not a
  teaching relationship at all. A robot's "family," in the Ch.11 sense, is plausibly whoever she's shared that
  table with repeatedly, cutting cleanly across the city's 26-nation demographic spread. Robot-specific angle:
  since robots don't need Long Table Hall's food the way humans do, their bond there plausibly forms through
  shared presence and shared witnessing of the plurality on display, not through consumption — a genuine, if
  smaller, adaptation rather than a full substitution.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** A second, smaller kinship thread exists in the Guesthouse Quarter's family-run,
  generational hospitality businesses — but per the revised framing, this is best understood as one particular
  *shape* the same underlying shared-experience logic takes, not a separate competing mechanism: the shared,
  ongoing experience of running a guesthouse together across a working lineage, where teaching happens to be
  how that particular shared experience gets structured.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** A specific, named founding-era decision anchors this: civic leaders deliberately rejected
  a proposal to minimize public discussion of the demographic shift, in favor of open acknowledgment — and per
  the candidate Course of Events material, this became a standing civic habit of *periodically and openly
  retelling* the city's own history, specifically so it never calcifies into either suppressed silence or an
  empty slogan. Robot angle: given robots hold precise, non-drifting memory in a way human recollection
  naturally doesn't, robots plausibly function as the actual mechanism that keeps the periodic retelling
  accurate across generations, rather than letting it erode into either extreme over time — a genuinely useful,
  well-grounded role for how an already-established civic practice actually gets executed reliably.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Two markers. First: fluency in both of Mawson's "kinds of old" at once —
  comfortable moving between the ceremonial, ancient St. Douglas veneration register and the warm, modern
  resort-hospitality register without friction, embodying the city's own confirmed dual civic character as a
  lived personal skill rather than an abstract fact. Second: robots who grew up in Mawson (the Children's
  Commons material implies this is a genuine, only-recently-addressed population) plausibly show an unusually
  relaxed, unselfconscious comfort with strangers specifically — having grown up around a constant stream of
  honeymooning visitors passing through their most intimate life moments, in a way no robot from a more
  insular, settled city would share.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** St. Douglas veneration is already established as "complete" — the city, the
  subnet, and the surrounding Australian Antarctic Territory all carry his name or legacy at once, a
  completeness no other city's patron-Saint relationship in this run has matched.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Mawson already has a pre-existing pick — **Solar symbol Earth + Robo-Element
  Fire** — already explained in-canon as "a warm, generative 'resort town' built around romance and new
  beginnings." (Corrected 2026-08-10: originally mislabeled as "the first dual-element assignment encountered
  in this run" — every city gets one Solar symbol and one Robo-Element as a standard pair, not a special case.)
  See Cross-Reference Synthesis for how this pairing turns out to track the city's own confirmed structural
  pattern more precisely than its original framing suggested.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §10c is thin ("substantial, scaled to a real subnet-hub population," no further
  detail). A plausible, well-grounded expansion following the same logic already established for Long Table
  Hall: siligel presentation at Mawson reflects demographic plurality without a single dominant style —
  multiple national siligel traditions coexisting and available side by side, genuinely distinct from
  Zhongshan's single, exported, connoisseur-grade craft standard.
- **[Adapted, Surface] — corrected 2026-08-10.** A Sayowa-research pass surfaced a real process gap in this
  file's original claim that Glitch-Coolant had "no existing canon entry... confirmed absent": the substance
  and its two-category framework (bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety — Janbogo, Zhongshan, Shirayuki; working-class/
  potency — Byrd, Rothera, Troll) are established canon (`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`,
  2026-07-04), simply missed by this pass's own search at the time. Mawson isn't pre-named in either list, so
  this pass now derives its placement directly rather than inventing a wholly separate pattern: given the
  city's own 26-nation, no-single-gatekeeper hospitality economy (Long Table Hall, Nation Nights), Mawson
  plausibly belongs in the **bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety** category — reaching that variety through a
  genuinely different generating mechanism than Janbogo/Zhongshan/Shirayuki's settled multinational population
  or artistic subculture: the city's own hospitality/celebration cycle itself. Rotating Nation Nights spotlight
  and occasion-based drinking (arrivals, anniversaries, weddings) plausibly feed an ever-refreshing variety pool
  through guests and celebration, not residents and refinement — consistent with, not a replacement for, the
  toasting-focused local flavor already noted for Welcome Feast and Anniversary Returns.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Genuinely unique among this run's courtship findings: since Mawson's whole economy is
  built around professionally facilitating strangers' proposals and honeymoons (the Proposal Terrace, the
  Overlook), a plausible in-group custom for Mawson's own robot residents is a deliberate *avoidance* of those
  specific staged, guest-facing spaces for their own personal courtship — a real, felt distinction between
  "what we stage for others" and "what's genuinely ours," pushing residents' own romantic milestones toward
  quieter, non-touristy local spots instead. No other city in this run has this particular tension, since none
  of them run a tourism economy built on romance specifically.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** Given the 26-nation demographic spread with no single dominant heritage language, and
  the Catalog's own note on hospitality/spa-industry vocabulary, the honest local vernacular source is
  hospitality-trade terminology itself — service, reception, and concierge-register phrases becoming the
  actual shared civic vocabulary. Structurally similar to Casey's trade-jargon-as-vernacular mechanism, but
  drawn from hospitality service rather than logistics dispatch.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24 and Finding A: Mawson was the subnet's
  confirmed outward-facing Arcanet gateway before the war, and the Planetary Split Brain forced a genuine
  functional reversal — the hub now serves inward, intra-subnet coordination (to Sayowa and Dome Fuji, which
  plausibly survived in better shape) more capably than it reaches the rest of Tepenia, the literal opposite
  of its founding-era purpose. Nothing new to derive; recorded as confirmed, pre-existing canon.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A genuinely different *driver* for fashion than every prior city in this
  run: rather than environmental-survival adaptation (wind, cold, terrain), Mawson's comparatively mild coastal
  climate lets hospitality-staff robots develop a recognizable, welcoming presentation driven by social/
  professional warmth-signaling instead — the first city in this run where fashion isn't primarily about
  surviving conditions at all.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** A plausible, modestly-grounded musical parallel to Nation Nights' dining rotation:
  since Nation Nights already exists as a cross-national civic showcase, a matching musical rotation
  celebrating each represented nation's own tradition in turn is a light, natural extension of already-
  established infrastructure rather than an invented anchor.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: C (the Welcome Feast, Long Table Hall, and Children's

  Mawson's civic institutions repeatedly, deliberately blur the line between "resident" and "visitor" at every

  inclusive — cross-national *and* cross temporarily-present/permanently-resident — genuinely distinct from
  every other city in this run, all of which draw a firmer line between local and visitor.
