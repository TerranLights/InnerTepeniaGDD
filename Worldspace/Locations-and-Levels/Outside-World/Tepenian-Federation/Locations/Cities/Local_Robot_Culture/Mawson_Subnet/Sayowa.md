Dome Fuji): both had incorrectly concluded Glitch-Coolant carried no canon placement at all. Both files have
been corrected in place — see their own Part III Reference Notes. This file checks Sayowa against the real
canon from the outset. Provisional — findings are proposals for developer review, not asserted canon.

## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, per the revised framing: the Junction
  Cooperative Maintenance Office was deliberately designed, at founding (per "The Junction Built for Everyone
  Who'd Need It"), as shared-use infrastructure rather than Sayowa-primary-access — meaning the robots who keep
  it running share an ongoing, everyday practice of cooperative, multi-party, largely uncredited labor,
  regardless of which of the five industry sectors they formally belong to. That daily condition — quiet
  dutifulness over self-assertion, already named directly as an ordinary shared civic ethic in Community
  Infrastructure's own material — is Sayowa's real kinship floor; it holds between two robots who've never
  worked the same shift, as long as both actually do this work together.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: the trusted-neutral-coordinator role (per
  Course of Events #8, "Wei-Lin's Balancing Act" — earned through demonstrated fairness over time, not granted
  by formal authority) plausibly functions as an informal mentoring track, where newer dispatchers gradually
  earn the same cross-party trust by observing and eventually taking on the delicate cooperative-balancing
  skill from whoever currently holds it — one particular, coordinator-specific shape of the broader shared
  cooperative-labor life above, not a separate mechanism.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's junction-responsibility-as-civic-value, combined with the already-established
  "cooperative, duty-oriented junction management... quiet dutifulness over self-assertion" ethic and Course of
  Events #9 ("Function Over Fanfare" — resisting resentment over the lack of civic spectacle), gives a real,
  specific civic ethic: standing here comes from reliably doing unglamorous, structurally important work, not
  from being recognized for it. For robots specifically, given precise task-logging capability, a plausible
  sharpened local expression is that genuine reliability gets tracked through a robot's own actual junction-duty
  record over real time — consistent, uncredited follow-through — rather than through visible accomplishment or
  self-report, a service-economy parallel to Davis's "let the work speak" and Casey's disclosure-for-strangers
  ethic, but targeted at continuous infrastructure upkeep specifically.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A genuinely different *kind* of marker than every prior city's physical or
  sensory one: a Sayowa robot is recognizable by a matter-of-fact, undramatic ease holding stakes that are
  structurally much larger than her own city's small population would suggest — neither falsely modest about
  the Junction's real continental importance nor inflating her own city's stature because of it. §7's own
  framing ("comfortable holding disproportionate structural responsibility in a small, intimate community") and
  §5's fault line (population rank 32nd-of-~35 vs. Tepenia's single most significant three-way junction) both
  point to the same settled personal register — an attitudinal, Deep-Culture marker rather than a physical or
  sensory one.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: no dominant formal
  religion, but "a quiet awareness of disproportionate responsibility" and the same methodical patience the
  station's 69-year JARE research history instilled, extended into the equally patient, ongoing work of
  maintaining critical junction infrastructure. Confirmed, not invented. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the
  genuinely new material this pass adds — a real, functional relationship to a *different* faith entirely, one
  §18 as written gives no hook for at all.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §10c is confirmed thin ("modest, scaled to one of the subnet's smallest populations")
  — real room to expand, grounded in the Junction's own cooperative character: rather than Casey's individually
  -efficient transit-resupply framing, Sayowa's siligel culture plausibly centers on shift-crew provisioning —
  supplies shared collectively among whoever's currently on Junction duty, mirroring the cooperative-maintenance
  ethic itself rather than reading as a separate consumption tradition.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Sayowa isn't pre-named in either Glitch-Coolant category. Given the Compliant/
  service-not-recognition civic character and §9's own note that leisure infrastructure here is "visibly
  secondary" to industrial function, Sayowa plausibly belongs in the **working-class/potency** category
  alongside Byrd, Rothera, and Troll — limited selection, straightforward and unpretentious, consistent with a
  city whose whole civic register already runs on function over fanfare rather than variety or refinement.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from Mirny's demonstrated-reliability signal (which proves competence) and
  Davis's collaboration signal (which proves ongoing partnership): given Sayowa's own established ethic that
  invisible, uncredited sacrifice is real civic virtue here (Function Over Fanfare; Everyone Else's Prosperity's
  own resolution — "sharpening modest pride into something that has actually reckoned with its own real cost"),
  a plausible local courtship signal is voluntarily covering a partner's Junction-duty rotation without being
  asked and without expecting credit for it — a genuine personal cost absorbed quietly, mirroring at
  interpersonal scale exactly the civic virtue Sayowa already asks of its whole population on behalf of cities
  that will never know to thank it.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already notes practical, junction-oriented vocabulary alongside Shōwa-era-weighted
  slang. Since all five of Sayowa's industry sectors (§15) touch the Junction in some way, junction-coordination
  jargon — dispatch codes, cooperative-maintenance terminology — is plausibly the one vocabulary genuinely
  shared across the whole working population, regardless of sector. Robot angle: given the precision multi-
  party dispatch coordination actually requires, robots are the plausible primary shapers and most consistent
  users of this vocabulary, the same structural role Casey's dispatchers play for road-jargon, but arising from
  cooperative multi-party coordination specifically rather than one dispatch office's own internal standard.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Adapted, Deep]** A real tension worth surfacing rather than smoothing over: §24 claims Sayowa's current
  Arcanet role is "genuinely significant" for junction coordination, but the city's own Historical Vignette
  material (Batches 2–6) shows the *actual lived* pre-Tower experience was intra-subnet digital reach at best,
  with everything crossing the Mawson/Halley subnet boundary — including, for a long stretch, contact with
  Princess Elisabeth itself — running by mail and truck instead. A plausible, well-grounded local expression:
  Sayowa's Arcanet culture likely still carries an inherited institutional caution from that lived history —
  critical junction decisions get verified through redundant physical or human channels even now that digital
  connectivity is more capable, a genuine cultural habit earned from real institutional memory of insufficiency
  rather than present-day necessity or superstition.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §12's own "modest, and genuinely its own... shaped by scale and function
  rather than any one population's inheritance" framing extends naturally to a robot-specific marker: given
  robots concentrate in junction-maintenance and dispatch roles, a practical, role-legible coordination uniform
  or gear-set plausibly functions as Sayowa's own recognizable aesthetic — function-driven rather than
  thermal/wind-driven like most other cities' markers, or nationally-inflected like Shirayuki's or Zhongshan's.
- **No music/performance finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check): §12 is explicitly "modest" with no
  named tradition or incident to anchor a robot-specific extension against, unlike Casey's Splinters or
  Zhongshan's Long Winter Suite — inventing one without a real local hook would risk exactly the generic-
  reskin failure mode the Swap Test exists to catch.
