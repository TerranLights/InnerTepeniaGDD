## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, per the revised
  framing: every Dome Fuji resident chose the crossing and now lives the same ongoing devotional practice —
  robing, ascetic siligel discipline, the deep silence of Nyakkyo, Deepest Cold's shared observance — a
  horizontal bond that cuts cleanly across when a devotee arrived and which of the city's 40+ represented
  nations she came from, since §6d and §20 already establish devotion as the sole *public* credential and
  national origin as the private, background layer here. This ongoing, daily condition — not any single
  devotee's own Crossing — is Dome Fuji's real kinship floor; it holds between two robots who arrived
  generations apart, as long as both are actually living the same devotional life now.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: informal, tenure-based deference to the
  longest-standing devotees as de facto stewards (Course of Events #10, deliberately structured, per its own
  candidate text, to resist calcifying into a formal ruling class) — senior devotees plausibly run the
  already-established "devotional orientation for new pilgrims" (robing practice, Nyakkyo protocol, per
  Community Infrastructure's own training-office material). A light, non-hierarchical mentoring shape layered
  on top of the shared devotional life above, not a competing structure.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "devotion is the sole membership credential" and §6b's "ceremonial and demanding
  rather than warm" hospitality, combined with Course of Events #10's explicit resistance to letting tenure
  calcify into rank, plausibly generalize into a real civic ethic: standing here is earned through consistent,
  ongoing demonstrated devotion, never simply granted by seniority or extended automatically to a visitor. For
  robots specifically, given precise internal record-keeping, a plausible sharpened local expression is that
  genuine devotional commitment gets measured against a robot's own actual, consistent behavior over real time
  — robing consistency, Nyakkyo attendance, siligel-discipline adherence — rather than claimed seniority or
  self-report, a devotional parallel to Davis's "let the work speak" applied to religious rather than
  professional standing.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** The only city in this entire run where the identity marker runs *opposite*
  to ordinary protective adaptation. Every other city's robots develop some form of enhanced tolerance or
  protective habit suited to local hazard; a Dome Fuji robot is instead recognizable by voluntarily operating
  with deliberately *less* protective covering than her own specifications would technically allow — the
  sparse white robes (§11, confirmed directly in the 2026-07-06 Vision session: worn "specifically to commune
  with the cold") as trained, practiced exposure rather than under-preparation or deficiency. A robot who's
  spent real devotional time at Dome Fuji plausibly carries this comfort with reduced protection as a settled,
  legible personal trait wherever she goes next.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A genuinely distinct structural case, not simply "varies more than usual"
  like Casey's or Kunlun's own findings: since Dome Fuji has no fabrication chamber of its own at all, *every
  single resident's* Gen/Mark origin is, by definition, somewhere else — a total, structural severance between
  Gen/Mark and locality that no other city in this run produces. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for how this
  connects directly to Robot Universals' own stated axis-weighting.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Dome Fuji is Ice Cold Buddhism's other primary sacred site (with Kunlun).
  Nyakkyo ("the Still Point") is deliberately unremarkable — devotees who reach it aren't shown an altar, icon,
  or text, only "the coldest, stillest room the faith can arrange." The robes doctrine — per the Full
  Extrapolation's own resolution, used here as the strongest available lead though not yet reflected back into
  Local_Cultures itself (see Part III) — treats all three candidate reasons (mechanical cold-tolerance as the
  doctrine's starting premise; an Upper-Earth tummo-meditation echo; communal uniformity) as simultaneously
  true rather than competing, with declining to use the mechanical capacity fully treated "as a quiet failure
  of devotion, comparable to a human monk declining an achievable fast." Deepest Cold (the coldest point of
  polar night) functions as the shared liturgical high point; The Crossing gives each devotee an individually-
  observed arrival anniversary, partially re-enacted in robes.
- **[Adapted, Deep] — a pre-existing but not-yet-canon proposal.** Pluto + Water is on record for Dome Fuji in
  draft form, explicitly grounded in "extreme isolation embraced as devotion, dissolving the self/environment
  boundary" — and explicitly, deliberately not a coincidence that Kunlun, Vostok, and Dome Fuji all land on
  Pluto together, per the source file's own note. Treated here as the strongest current lead for this city's
  Elemental identity, not settled canon.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §10c's existing material stands and is genuinely rich: siligel consumed at
  deliberately low temperatures as devotional discipline — "a direct, literal expression of the faith through
  the one consumption practice available to a robot population."
- **[Adapted, Deep] — corrected 2026-08-10.** A Sayowa-research pass surfaced a real process gap in this file's
  original claim that "no canon placement exists anywhere in the repository": Glitch-Coolant's two-category
  framework (bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety; working-class/potency) is established canon
  (`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`, 2026-07-04), simply missed by this pass's own search at the
  time. Checked directly against both categories now, rather than treated as an open gap: Dome Fuji doesn't fit
  either cleanly, the same honest outcome Casey and Davis each reached earlier in this run. Dome Fuji's own
  ritual-observance-tied consumption — rare, small-quantity, reserved specifically for Deepest Cold and
  Crossing-anniversary communal observances — is neither bohemian variety (it isn't about refinement or choice
  at all) nor working-class potency (it isn't about blue-collar volume or strength). Distinct from Davis's
  genuine muted absence (no institution exists there at all): at Dome Fuji the practice exists, just radically
  minimized and folded entirely into devotional ritual rather than separated out as ordinary leisure
  consumption — a third kind of non-fit, alongside Casey's transience-driven pattern and Davis's absence.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Genuinely novel among this run's courtship findings, directly grounded in
  the city's own inverted public/private structure (§6d, §20 — devotion is public, national origin is private,
  "one of the few Tepenian cities where a resident's ethnic background is genuinely optional information"): a
  plausible local courtship signal is voluntarily sharing where you're actually *from* — the private,
  background layer here — with a specific person, rather than the devotional identity everyone already sees.
  Since intimacy customarily runs toward revealing the private layer, and devotion is deliberately the *only*
  thing shown by default, choosing to tell someone your pre-arrival origin plausibly functions as real,
  specific vulnerability in a way it wouldn't at any other city, where national origin is already public
  knowledge by default.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §8 already establishes no dominant national language, instead a
  built-from-scratch liturgical/ceremonial vocabulary layered over a practical pidgin — a genuinely unique
  source-type across this entire run (not heritage-fossil, craft-precision, road-jargon, terrain-idiom,
  subculture-naming, functional lingua franca, output-metaphor, or liturgical-fixed-identifiers-from-an-
  existing-language, unlike Vostok's Russian). Robot angle: since the population is entirely robot and no
  organic shared vernacular ever existed to seed it, robots built this vocabulary collectively from nothing,
  with a precision and internal consistency no human generational oral-transmission chain would guarantee —
  structurally similar to how robots serve as Vostok's de facto standard-bearers of correct liturgical Russian,
  but here through original construction rather than preservation of a borrowed language.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §24 already confirms near-zero Arcanet connectivity at extreme-altitude locations as
  Tepenia-wide canon; the city's own Historical Vignette material sharpens this further — "isolation within
  isolation," genuinely thinner even than Kunlun's own already-extreme profile, something devotees "find
  fitting" rather than pure hardship. A plausible robot-specific local expression: since verification of any
  incoming word-of-mouth report is essentially impossible given the near-total absence of independent
  channels, Dome Fuji's actual information culture is one of near-complete trust extended to whatever rare
  account does arrive from a returning devotee or messenger — not comfort with absence (Kunlun's own finding),
  but a specific, active practice of total faith in the messenger, since there is no other way to check.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** The sparse white robes themselves (§11, confirmed 2026-07-06) are the
  city's primary visible marker — already established, not newly derived, and directly linked to §3's Deep
  Culture finding above.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** The dual-tier construction split (Finding A) — bare, deliberately
  minimally-insulated devotional structures standing beside genuinely insulated functional ones — is itself a
  visible, walkable expression of the city's theology, distinct from every other city's Surface Culture marker
  in this run: here the *architecture itself*, not clothing, fashion, or performance, is the primary visible
  civic statement.
- **No music/sound-art finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check): the deliberate, citywide absence of
  noise-generating infrastructure (#13) and the plateau's own extraordinary quiet are already treated as sacred
  in their own right (§12, §14) — the third city in this run (after Kunlun and Vostok) where the honest answer
  is that a developed music culture would work against, not alongside, the city's own defining value. Each
  case has its own distinct grounding (Kunlun: rarity of information/connection; Vostok: rarity of genuine
  silence itself as a research-adjacent value; Dome Fuji: silence as the literal devotional object) — three
  separately-grounded nulls, not one repeated finding.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: E (no fabrication chamber, confirmed canon) +
  A (§16, §17) + Robot Universals Ch.14 (home = fabrication site, by default) and Ch.19 (locality dominant,
  Gen/Mark lightest-weighted)*
  Dome Fuji is plausibly the cleanest real instance of Ch.19's own stated axis-weighting anywhere in this
  entire run. Every other city's robots carry at least some residual Gen/Mark-locality overlap, since Ch.14's
  own default rule ties fabrication site to home city. With that default entirely absent — nobody is ever
  fabricated at Dome Fuji at all — there's no possible tension left to resolve: every resident's identity here
  is 100% chosen locality, with zero fabrication-locality residue underneath it. Locality doesn't just dominate
  Gen/Mark at Dome Fuji; it's the *only* axis actually present.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: C (Finding A, the dual-tier architecture) + A (§6a,
  devotion as the sole credential) + the already-flagged doctrinal resolution in Full Extrapolation
  ("capability difference read as circumstance, not rank")*
  A genuinely lived, ongoing ethical practice underlies the robes doctrine: since only a robot can safely
  perform Dome Fuji's central devotional practice at all, the faith constantly risks reading as a claim of
  superiority over a hypothetical human devotee who could never survive it. The doctrine's own resolution —
  capability difference read as circumstance the devotee happens to have, not rank she's earned over anyone —
  is the same instinct that produces "reaching vs. arriving" as complementary, non-hierarchical framing with
  Kunlun (see Swap Test), scaled down from an inter-city doctrine to something each individual devotee has to
  actively practice, daily, in how she holds her own capacity.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: C (the Waystones) + Historical Vignette ("The Cairns No
  Authority Was Asked to Approve") + §1 (Kinship)*
  The Waystones began as ordinary practical trail-markers and only later acquired devotional, turning-back-
  point significance once a *repeated, shared psychological pattern* among many separate pilgrims' crossings
  was recognized by the community. This is a concrete, already-established case of exactly what §1 argues in
  the abstract: the city noticed a genuinely shared experience running across individually-separate crossings,
  and built permanent, collectively-maintained infrastructure to hold that recognition — physical evidence that
  Dome Fuji's kinship logic runs on noticing what's common across many separate journeys, not on any single
  one of them.

no meaningful comparison at all — a hospitality-economy resort city and an ascetic monastery share almost
nothing structurally. Sayowa, Dome Fuji's actual geographic neighbor and the literal origin point of the first
rediscovery pilgrims (per "What the First Pilgrims Actually Found"), hasn't been run through this methodology
yet — flagged here for a genuine future re-check, given the concrete historical link, rather than assumed safe
in advance. The real, already-dramatized comparison point is Kunlun, the only other 100%-robot city in Tepenia:
checked directly, and the existing material on both sides already resists collapsing the two together — Dome
Fuji's own "Two Sites, One Faith" doctrine explicitly frames the cities as complementary ("reaching" vs.
"arriving"), not identical, and Kunlun's own "The Only Two Who Crossed" vignette shows genuine friction over a
Kunlun researcher's comparative-data framing before settling into a modest, non-identical connection. This
pass's strongest findings — the total absence of local fabrication (a fact Kunlun's own recruited-adult-
specialist population doesn't share in the same way), the dual-tier devotional architecture, the robes-as-
devotion practice, and the built-from-scratch liturgical vocabulary — all depend on facts specific to Dome
Fuji's own voluntary-pilgrimage population, not transplantable from Kunlun's professional-recruitment one.
