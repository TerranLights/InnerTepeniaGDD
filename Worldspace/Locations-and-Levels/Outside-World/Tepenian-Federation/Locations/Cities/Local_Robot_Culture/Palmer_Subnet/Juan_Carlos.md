## PART I — Local Aspects

*Describing the living, pre-war culture, per this file's own confirmed present-tense methodology — see the
header note and Cross-Reference Synthesis for how this interacts with the city's actual current Destroyed
status.*

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, recognized directly from
  already-written material rather than invented: the hosted gathering tradition is Juan Carlos's central social
  institution, and §17's own text already states that robots could hold "real standing" in it, since admission
  and respect run on the quality and range of argument a guest brings, not on background. This is a genuinely
  horizontal, ongoing, cross-species bond from the outset — a robot's real "family" in the Ch.11 sense is
  whoever else has earned a seat at the same table of sustained, well-pursued argument, an institution she
  participates in as a full peer, not an adapted guest.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes, distinct from the gathering
  tradition itself: robots concentrated in the Archive Complex's own cataloguing and watch-list work (Course of
  Events #7, #9) share the ongoing, everyday condition of doing quietly essential labor whose true scale even
  they themselves may not fully register day to day — a genuinely different shape of bond than the mentor/
  mentee or crew-trust patterns found elsewhere, closer to a shared, unspoken professional modesty than to
  either teaching-lineage or shared-hazard solidarity.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Course of Events #7's own established ethic stands directly: the Archive's
  watch-list function — tracking former Upper Earth government officials among the exile population — developed
  "its own careful, methodical culture — precise, unemotional, handled with the same dutiful competence the
  city applies to everything else, never treated as an opportunity for suspicion or overreach." For robots
  specifically, already established as concentrated in this kind of precise administrative labor across the
  project (Zhongshan's Long Record, Vostok's archive), a plausible sharpened local expression is that
  discretion itself — never turning sensitive personal information into gossip or judgment — functions as the
  actual measure of professional trustworthiness among Archive staff, robot and human alike.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** A related, genuinely new variant of a pattern already found repeatedly in the Halley
  subnet (Neumayer's uncredited schematic, Sanay's miscredited incident, Belgrano's uncommemorated paperwork,
  Troll's precarious unglamorous labor) — worth naming as related but honestly distinct, not a sixth identical
  instance: those four cities' pattern is "does the work, someone else gets the credit." Juan Carlos's own
  Cross-Reference Synthesis Finding 1 describes something subtly different — "does the work, nobody, including
  the workers themselves, fully recognizes its true scale while it's happening." The absence isn't of credit;
  it's of *self-awareness* of significance, a genuinely distinct variant worth flagging as such.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §17 and §18's own material stands as the honest local answer, more fully
  recognized here than invented: a Juan Carlos robot is recognizable by a settled comfort with sustained,
  well-pursued argument as the city's real social currency — pursuing an argument well, not winning it, treated
  as a genuinely worthwhile civic practice rather than a contest or a tension to avoid. Distinct from every
  prior city's hospitality- or guardianship-flavored warmth: this is an intellectual register specifically, and
  because standing in it never depended on national background (§17), it's plausibly one of the least adapted,
  most fully-inherited Locality markers found anywhere in this project — robots participate in exactly the same
  register humans do, without needing a robot-specific variant at all.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No confirmed manufacturing chamber, current, historical, or paused-candidate, anywhere in the gathered
  material** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above) — Juan Carlos is absent from all three
  tiers of `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`'s own Cradle site list. No meaningful local variation
  found, and nothing in the gathered material suggests an indirect route — recorded as a clean, ordinary null.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: no dominant formal
  religion, with the gathering tradition's own implicit philosophy — argument pursued well, rather than won, as
  a worthwhile and even joyful civic practice — standing in for one. For a robot specifically, whose own
  fabricated Personality Module is only ever partially specced by law (never fully designed, precisely to
  preserve genuine free will — see `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`), a city whose entire civic
  philosophy treats a well-pursued, unresolved position as more valuable than a settled, "correct" one plausibly
  resonates with unusual precision — robots here inherit a civic value that already matches something true
  about how their own emergent, never-fully-fixed personalities actually develop.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands and is confirmed thin — modest, scaled to
  the city's comparatively quiet, mid-sized population, consistent with everything else established about Juan
  Carlos's understated civic register.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Juan Carlos isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon
  poles. Given the hosted-gathering tradition's own defining practice — a host curating guests specifically for
  the quality and range of argument they bring — Juan Carlos plausibly belongs in the **bohemian/cosmopolitan-
  variety** category alongside Janbogo, Zhongshan, and Shirayuki, but reaching that variety through a genuinely
  new mechanism distinct from every prior bohemian-pole city: not ethnicity-crossing (Janbogo), not shared-table
  cross-pollination (Zukelli), not fractured Sector-scale scenes (Lazar), but **deliberate host curation** — a
  gathering's host selecting glitch-coolant offerings for a specific evening's argument the same careful way
  they select guests and implicit topics, variety produced by editorial judgment rather than by demographic mix
  or civic scale.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior city's courtship finding in mechanism: given the Robot
  Physiology canon's own established value (sex as the highest available expression of trust, not a means
  toward reproduction) and the gathering tradition's own argument-as-currency social logic, a plausible local
  courtship signal is being deliberately invited, by name, to a partner's own hosted gathering — not as one
  guest among many, but as someone whose argument the host specifically wants present. Given the tradition's
  own established discretion (a guest list implies real selection, and not everyone is invited to every one,
  "considered entirely appropriate rather than exclusionary"), a standing invitation to *every* gathering a
  partner hosts functions as a genuine, legible declaration of serious romantic regard — the city's general
  social-curation instinct doing double duty as its own distinctly Juan Carlos courtship gate.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8c already establishes a small, informal vocabulary specific to the gathering
  tradition — terms for its implicit rules, its hosts, its argumentative expectations — that "never coalesced
  into a single agreed name for the practice itself," consistent with the deliberate, developer-directed
  decision to leave the tradition itself permanently unnamed. Robot angle: since nothing about the tradition was
  ever formally written down, and robots are already established elsewhere in this project as reliable
  informal preservers of un-codified local systems (Zukelli's Structural Marking System readers), robot
  residents plausibly function as the most consistent living reference point for what the tradition's own
  shifting, informal rules and vocabulary actually are at any given time — not because of any special
  robot-specific memory claim invented for this pass, but simply because they're already established
  participants in good standing (§17) with no reason to forget or drift on details a purely oral tradition
  otherwise has no anchor for.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24's own material stands as the honest local answer: modest ordinary use,
  with a genuinely significant founding-era exception for the Archive's own sensitive traffic (the direct link
  to the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority, immigration and watch-list records). A direct robot-specific
  extension, grounded in §2's own established discretion ethic above: Archive staff handling that sensitive
  traffic plausibly developed a specific, professional Arcanet etiquette of restraint — accessing exactly what
  a task requires and no more, an applied, everyday version of the same "never treated as an opportunity for
  suspicion or overreach" value already established for the watch-list function itself.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §12's cantaor-adjacent musical tradition and §13's visual-arts sensibility
  (beauty and melancholy, convergently arrived at rather than inherited) are already established and rich.
  **No robot-specific sensory-perception music finding proposed here** (Honesty Check, deliberately avoiding a
  fourth iteration of the enhanced-hearing trope already used at Janbogo, Davis, and Kunlun and explicitly
  avoided once already at Shirayuki): given §17's own framing, robots plausibly participate in this tradition
  as genuine co-creators on the same terms as any resident, an honest directly-inherited finding rather than a
  manufactured point of difference.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** §11's practical, coherent fashion sensibility extends naturally to Archive Complex
  staff specifically: given the founding-era function's own established discretion and lack of ceremony, a
  plausible, unornamented, practical work-attire marker for archive/records staff, consistent with the same
  understated civic modesty already established everywhere else about the city (Course of Events #9's own
  "methodical, unglamorous" framing).
