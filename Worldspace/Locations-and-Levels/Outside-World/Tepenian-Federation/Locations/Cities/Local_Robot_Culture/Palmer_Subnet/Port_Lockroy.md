## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep, the richest finding in this file]** Leading with the actual shared *life*
  experience, not a single incident: the Communal Stone Wall — a genuine multi-generational artifact, one new
  panel added each year (the Long Memory custom) — is Port Lockroy's real Kinship floor, an ongoing project
  every resident who has ever added to it shares in common, regardless of era. The existing Cross-Reference
  Synthesis's own Finding 2 already establishes the emotional stakes directly: Old Toby doubted he'd live to
  see the wall's full civic maturity, and given Tepenia's own established human lifespan facts, he was
  correct — no human resident present at the wall's start could still be there when it finishes, "carried
  forward faithfully by people who never knew them." A robot resident, by contrast, plausibly *could* be
  present for the entire span — not because she set out to witness it, but simply because nothing about her
  own physiology requires her not to be. This gives Port Lockroy's robots a uniquely direct, literal answer to
  the city's whole founding tension (living memory vs. heritage spectacle): for a robot who's actually been
  there the whole time, it was never spectacle to begin with.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** One specific, already-established shape that shared life takes: Community
  Infrastructure's own confirmed detail of a robot courier who "jokes with [Old Toby] between chisel strokes"
  at his workshop — a small, genuine, cross-generational friendship grounded in shared, ongoing craft-adjacent
  labor (goat husbandry, stonework, postal rounds), not a single incident but an established, recurring social
  fact of the city's own small working life.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §18's own established civic value — continuous accountability to the past, "not nostalgia
  but a lived awareness that every era leaves something behind for the next one to carry" — is confirmed
  directly. For robots specifically, given their own established precision at record-keeping and long-duration
  memory, a plausible sharpened local expression is that robots plausibly function as the city's real, informal
  check against §5b's own named risk (living memory sliding into heritage-tourism spectacle) — not through any
  formal role, but simply by being reliable, unembellishing witnesses to what actually happened in each layer
  of the city's own palimpsest, a genuine civic service in a city that takes real pride in not papering over its
  own past.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: a quiet, specific
  philosophy around smallness and irrelevance as a kind of protection — "the same quality that made the city
  strategically unworthy of destruction gives it a modest, unbothered self-image today." A Port Lockroy robot
  is recognizable by a genuine, unforced comfort with holding multiple historical eras at once without needing
  to resolve them into one clean story — a Deep, attitudinal marker distinct from every larger Palmer-subnet
  city's own more assertive identity (Palmer City's spectacle, Marambio's motion, Juan Carlos's argument-
  culture).

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No confirmed manufacturing chamber, current, historical, or paused-candidate, anywhere in the gathered
  material** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above) — Port Lockroy is absent from all three
  tiers of `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`'s own Cradle site list. No meaningful local variation
  found — recorded as a clean, ordinary null.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: no dominant formal
  religion, with continuous accountability to the past standing in for one. For a robot, whose own gel brain is
  itself "malleable and ever-changing... always capable of finding a new purpose" (Robot Biology canon), a city
  whose central value is carrying forward what previous eras left behind, without needing to resolve or erase
  it, plausibly requires little translation — an honest, directly-inherited finding rather than a manufactured
  point of difference.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands and is confirmed thin — modest, scaled to
  the smallest population in the Palmer subnet.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Port Lockroy isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon
  poles. Given the city's own genuinely tiny population and its established total absence of dedicated leisure
  infrastructure, Port Lockroy plausibly belongs in the **working-class/potency** category, but through a
  genuinely new mechanism, distinct even from Marambio's own rare-lull version: rather than being folded into
  brief pauses in an otherwise busy schedule, consumption here is folded directly into the ordinary pace of
  work itself — shared casually between goat-tending, stonework, and postal rounds, the same register as the
  already-established Old Toby/robot-courier workshop friendship, since a city this small and this slow never
  really needed a separate leisure/work distinction to begin with.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior Palmer-subnet courtship finding: given the city's own genuine
  intimacy (§16) and its established communal-labor register (the Wall, the postal relay), a plausible local
  courtship signal is being given a real, personal role in that shared civic labor — adding a panel to the
  Communal Stone Wall together, or being handed a shared stretch of the postal relay to maintain as a pair — a
  gesture that means something specific here because the work itself is already how the city expresses
  belonging generally (§28's own "newcomers are known almost immediately"), extended into a deliberate romantic
  register.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8c already establishes era-specific shorthand ("the '44 wing") distinguishing the
  city's different historical strata. Given §1's own established finding that a robot resident could plausibly
  have personally lived through more than one of these labeled eras, robots are plausibly the vocabulary's most
  reliable users and preservers — not because of any special memory claim invented for this pass, but simply
  because a robot who was actually there for a given era has no reason to let its own shorthand drift the way
  living memory ordinarily does across a human generational handoff.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24's own material stands as the honest local answer: modest, centered on
  maintaining the historical/heritage record. A direct robot-specific extension, grounded in the Reduced Postal
  Relay's own established "partly hand-managed" character: robots maintaining personal, direct courier
  relationships with counterparts in other cities plausibly represent a genuine analog layer running alongside,
  not replacing, ordinary digital Arcanet use — consistent with a city whose whole civic character resists
  letting any one era's technology simply supersede the last.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §13's preservation-oriented crafts and documentary visual arts are already
  established. The already-confirmed Old Toby/robot-courier workshop scene extends this directly: robots as
  genuine co-crafters in the Communal Stone Wall's own stonework tradition, participating in the city's central
  material-culture practice on the same terms as any human resident, an honest directly-inherited finding
  rather than an invented point of difference.
