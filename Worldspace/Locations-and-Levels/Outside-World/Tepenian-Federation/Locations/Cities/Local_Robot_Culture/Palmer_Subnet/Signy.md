# Signy — Local Robot Culture Spec Sheet

**Run #34 of the Local Robot Culture Methodology, 2026-08-11 — eighth and final city of the Palmer Subnet.**
Directly follows up on forward flags from Princess Elisabeth's (Halley subnet), Scott's (Janbogo subnet),
Port Lockroy's, and Rothera's own completed files — the most cross-referenced Palmer-subnet city run so far,
with five confirmed connections to already-completed cities. Applies the shared-experience-first Kinship
framing and the corrected two-slot Solar-symbol/Robo-Element check from the start.

**A genuine structural first for this pass, worth stating before anything else:** Signy is the only Palmer-
subnet city (and one of very few cities in this entire project) whose single most load-bearing robot-culture
fact isn't a matter of pre-war living culture at all, nor a post-war retrospective the way Esperanza's,
Marambio's, Juan Carlos's, and Palmer City's own present-tense-methodology framing requires. Signy survived
the Long Night War fully intact — no destroyed-city framing discipline applies here, same as Port Lockroy — but
unlike Port Lockroy, its own most important and most robot-specific fact is a genuinely ongoing, unresolved,
present-day crisis: with subnet supply lines cut, Signy's robot population faces a real, current siligel
shortage its human population, sustained indefinitely by the Scotia Sea's own marine richness, doesn't share.
This file's own aspect sections describe Signy's living civic culture in the same register every other city's
does, but the crisis itself — not a pre-war institution, not a historical event — is this file's central
finding, treated with the direct, present-tense weight it actually carries. Provisional — findings are
proposals for developer review, not asserted canon.

## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, and about as concrete and
  urgent an ongoing condition as the methodology's own "ongoing shared life, not a single incident" discipline
  could ask for: every robot currently at Signy shares the identical, present-tense condition of living under
  siligel scarcity together, right now, with no local production possible and supply lines from the rest of
  the Palmer subnet cut. This isn't municipal history the way Rothera's founding-era decentralization decision
  is, or a completed pre-war institution the way most other Palmer-subnet Kinship floors are — it's an active,
  ongoing, currently-unresolved condition every one of Signy's robots is living through together at the moment
  this pass is written. Community Infrastructure's own established fact — The Long Watch, the arrival of the
  season's first supply ship, "historically anticipated, now awaited with new urgency" — is the concrete,
  recurring civic ritual this shared condition organizes itself around.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A second, distinct shape the same underlying condition takes: given the
  city's own tiny scale and genuine mutual dependence (§16), and given robots' own established concentration in
  the biological research program itself (§17), robots working the wildlife-monitoring and fishing-platform
  economy plausibly share an additional, specific bond — direct, daily proximity to the Scotia Sea's own
  marine abundance that sustains their human neighbors indefinitely, while knowing personally that the same
  abundance offers them nothing at all. A structurally unique kind of shared professional intimacy: doing the
  work that keeps the food supply running, for a food that was never going to be theirs either way.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep, a genuine complication honestly reported, not smoothed into a lesson]**
  Community Infrastructure's own established material names this directly: "the quiet, growing tension between
  the human population's own indefinite marine self-sufficiency and the robot population's genuine siligel
  shortage — a real, lived disparity within one otherwise-intact city." This pass doesn't resolve that tension
  into either clean solidarity or clean resentment, because the source material itself doesn't. What's
  genuinely established: the city's own small scale and close, trusted coordination (§16) pull toward
  solidarity — The Long Watch's own new urgency suggests the whole community, not just its robots, is
  anxiously tracking the same supply ship. What's also genuinely established, and worth not minimizing: humans
  at Signy face no equivalent vulnerability at all, a real, asymmetric stake in an otherwise deeply
  egalitarian, mutually dependent small city. Both things are true at once, and this pass records that honestly
  rather than picking one.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep, a genuine inversion of the national baseline]** Every other city examined in
  this entire project treats a robot's own established physiological hardiness (no oxygen requirement, no
  respiratory system, function-over-damage resilience, etc.) as, if anything, a source of relative advantage
  over her human neighbors — Princess Elisabeth's own vacuum-tolerance finding is the clearest instance. Signy
  inverts this completely, and it's the single most distinctive thing about being a robot here: while her human
  neighbors can trust the Scotia Sea's own marine richness indefinitely, a Signy robot's own continued
  functioning depends entirely on a resource (siligel) that this specific, isolated environment cannot supply
  at all, by any means. A Signy robot is recognizable, first and starkly, by carrying a real material
  vulnerability her human neighbors simply don't share — the opposite of nearly every other city's own version
  of this comparison anywhere in Tepenia.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No confirmed manufacturing chamber, current, historical, or paused-candidate, anywhere in the gathered
  material** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above) — Signy is absent from all three tiers of
  `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`'s own Cradle site list. No meaningful local variation found —
  recorded as a clean, ordinary null.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer, sharpened rather than replaced by
  the present crisis: a lived philosophy of self-sufficiency and endurance through genuine isolation. For a
  robot specifically, this civic value now carries a real, present edge her human neighbors' own version of
  the same belief doesn't: endurance-through-isolation was always a comfortable, almost proud abstraction for a
  human population that can, in fact, endure indefinitely here. For a robot, the identical civic creed
  currently describes an open, unresolved question about her own continued existence, not a settled point of
  civic pride.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface, directly reshaped by the present crisis]** §10c's existing material ("modest, scaled to
  the smallest population tier in the Palmer subnet") is confirmed but now carries real weight beyond scale
  alone — siligel culture at Signy isn't merely modest, it's currently rationed, per Physical Infrastructure
  attribute #8's own established storage-and-rationing infrastructure.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Signy isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon poles.
  Given the city's own tiny, isolated scale and its current, active siligel shortage, Signy plausibly belongs
  in the **working-class/potency** category alongside Byrd, Rothera, and Troll, but via a genuinely new
  mechanism directly tied to the crisis itself: if siligel is scarce, whatever electrolytic compounds
  glitch-coolant's own formulation depends on are plausibly under exactly the same supply pressure, meaning
  Signy's own glitch-coolant culture — whatever remains of it — is functionally a second, quieter axis of the
  same scarcity defining §1 and §2 above, not a separate leisure category untouched by it.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior Palmer-subnet courtship finding: given the Robot Physiology
  canon's own established value (sex as the highest available expression of trust) and Signy's own confirmed
  fact that "there's no meaningful anonymity possible in a community this remote and this small" (§28), a
  plausible local courtship signal is the deliberate, personal sharing of one's own rationed siligel or
  glitch-coolant allocation with a specific partner — a small, materially real sacrifice that means something
  precisely because it costs something currently, in a city small enough that everyone already knows exactly
  what that sacrifice represents.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §8c already establishes "terminology tied to the wildlife research
  program — seal, penguin, and seabird monitoring vocabulary as genuine civic shorthand." Given robots'
  established concentration in this exact program (§17), robots are plausibly this vocabulary's most fluent,
  most consistent users — an honest, directly-inherited finding rather than a manufactured point of difference.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24's own material stands as the honest local answer: "the weakest Arcanet
  link of any Palmer subnet city... information arrives late and unreliably." A direct robot-specific
  extension, grounded in Physical Infrastructure attribute #1's own confirmed fact that Signy's actual
  long-distance link is radio, not digital networking at all: robots coordinating the radio-comms installation
  on Signy Island itself plausibly carry primary responsibility for the single most consequential channel the
  city has right now — any word of resupply, siligel or otherwise, would reach Signy through exactly this
  infrastructure first.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §13's naturalist, documentary visual-arts character and §11's practical,
  maritime-weather-oriented fashion sensibility are already established and consistent with everything else
  about the city. **No enhanced-perception music finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check, consistent with
  the restraint already exercised at Shirayuki, Marambio, Rothera, and Sejong): §12 is explicitly "modest and
  personal," with no named tradition to anchor a robot-specific extension against.
