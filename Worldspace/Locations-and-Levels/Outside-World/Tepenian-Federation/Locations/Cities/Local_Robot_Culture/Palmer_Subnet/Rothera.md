## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  Rothera's industrial base is deliberately, permanently decentralized across dozens of kilometers of Adelaide
  Island (Physical Infrastructure Attribute #1) — meaning a Rothera robot's most immediate, ongoing shared-life
  condition isn't "working in Rothera" in the abstract, it's working *one specific site*, alongside the same
  crew, day after day, with the other Decentralized Industrial Sites functioning as genuinely separate working
  worlds rather than interchangeable extensions of the same shop floor. This is the real Kinship floor at the
  site level: an ongoing, ordinary condition of shared labor, physically bounded by which stretch of the island
  a given robot actually reports to.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A second, distinct, and genuinely city-wide shape layered directly on top of
  the site-level floor above: Course of Events #5 ("Two Shifts, One City") establishes that Rothera's human
  workers run 8–10 hour shifts while its robot workers run 10–12 hour shifts — two overlapping but genuinely
  mismatched working rhythms — and that residents actively, deliberately protect shared after-work leisure
  time against that mismatch, stopping at the same local bars "regardless of whose shift just finished." This
  isn't passive overlap; it's sustained, ongoing effort to keep two different-length working lives from
  splitting into two barely-overlapping social worlds. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for why this specific
  custom is doing more structural work for Rothera than a simple after-hours-ritual finding elsewhere in this
  project would.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §6a's own established civic value — function matters more than presentation —
  is confirmed directly, and the Historical Vignette material gives it real, specific grounding rather than
  leaving it an abstraction: Chief Engineer "Malcolm Ashgrove-Pryce" *(placeholder, not developer-chosen)*
  laid out Rothera's original decentralized industrial plan for purely practical reasons — moving less raw
  material further than it needed to go — with "no one involved framing the decision around resilience or
  survivability at all." A robot's own civic standing at Rothera plausibly runs on the same unglamorous
  standard: whether a given method actually works, not on how deliberately or dramatically it was chosen.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** A genuinely satisfying, decades-later confirmation of the same value, not a

  at one fabrication site stayed contained to that site alone, proving Ashgrove-Pryce's original efficiency-
  driven layout choice sound as a resilience strategy purely by accident of consequence, "decades after he
  first made it." For robots specifically — already established elsewhere in this project as reliable,
  unembellishing record-keepers (Port Lockroy, Zhongshan) — this incident plausibly functions as the concrete,
  legible proof-point behind an otherwise abstract civic value: quiet vindication earned by outcome, not
  declared in advance as virtue.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep, a genuine structural echo of Lazar's own finding, differently caused]**
  Checked directly against Lazar's own already-completed file (Halley subnet): Lazar's robots are recognizable
  first by *which Sector* they read as home, because Tepenia's single largest city is genuinely too big for
  any one citywide practice to be everyone's actual lived experience. Rothera's own version of the identical
  altitude-drop is real but has a genuinely different root cause — not sheer population scale, but a
  *deliberate resilience strategy*: decentralization was chosen specifically so that no single location could
  define, or endanger, the whole. A Rothera robot is recognizable first by *which site* she actually works —
  Bonner Airstrip ground crew, Marguerite Bay Harbor, one of the scattered fabrication sites, the vault
  program — before anything about her reads as generically "Rothera," the same structural shape Lazar's own
  finding takes, arrived at from population fragmentation there and engineered redundancy here.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** The one genuine city-wide exception, exactly paralleling the shape Lazar's own
  file found for its own single exception: §25's own established Export Culture material — "a specific *type*
  of person — practical, unglamorous, genuinely useful, recognizable by an ease with industrial work" — is a
  demeanor, not a site-specific practice, and it's the actual thing that reads as "Rothera" at full city scale
  regardless of which particular site a robot calls home.
