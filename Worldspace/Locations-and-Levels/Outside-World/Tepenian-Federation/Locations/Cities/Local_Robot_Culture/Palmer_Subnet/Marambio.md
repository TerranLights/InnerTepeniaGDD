## PART I — Local Aspects

*Describing the living, pre-war culture, per this file's own confirmed present-tense methodology — see the
header note and Cross-Reference Synthesis for how this interacts with the city's actual current Destroyed
status.*

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience: the ordinary, everyday condition of
  moving people and goods by both air and sea, regardless of weather (§6c) — a genuinely dual-register labor
  identity shared by every robot working either the airfield or the shipyards, since attribute #5's own
  established coordination infrastructure meant the two workforces were never fully separate. This is Marambio's
  real Kinship floor: not a craft lineage or a single dominant institution, but the shared, ongoing fact of
  keeping a city in constant motion, together.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes, small but genuine: §6f's own
  established smoke-break custom — dockworkers, human and robot, pausing together during rare quiet stretches
  between ship dockings — is the concrete, recurring ritual form the broader shared-motion condition takes when
  it briefly stops. A modest but real instance of the same "shared rest inside constant motion" logic the
  Glitch-Coolant finding below extends further.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §6a's own established civic value — movement as the norm, never built for
  permanence — is confirmed and needs no adaptation: a robot's own standing at Marambio runs on reliable
  participation in that motion, not on settling into any fixed role.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A genuinely different, robot-specific answer to the existing Cross-Reference
  Synthesis's own open question (whether Marambio's founding-institutional persistence — Argentine Air Force
  operational discipline outliving the founding population's own 0.74% demographic share — was ever noticed and
  named the way Janbogo's identical structural pattern was): checked directly against Janbogo's own already-
  completed file, where the teahouse-keeping apprenticeship is an *explicit, celebrated* teaching-lineage
  robots consciously recognize as their own kinship institution. Marambio's own robot population plausibly
  carries the Comandante's Operations Hall's founding discipline forward the same way — through consistent,
  trained procedure — but *unconsciously*, without the reflective naming Janbogo's version received. This is a
  genuine, honestly-differentiated confirmation of the CRS's own speculative reading: yes, the pattern runs
  here too, but true to Marambio's whole function-over-reflection civic register, nobody — human or robot —
  seems to have stopped long enough to notice it.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Adapted, Deep]** A Marambio robot is recognizable by genuine fluency across two entirely different
  logistics registers at once — aviation and maritime — treating neither as more "hers" than the other, a
  dual-competency identity with no clean equivalent found at any single-function Peninsula city.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §7's own established fact stands directly, without needing adaptation: some
  human dockworkers came to Marambio *specifically* because robot society treated them better than human society
  ever did. This gives Marambio's robots a genuinely distinct Locality marker among Palmer-subnet cities so
  far — recognized, by outsiders and newcomers alike, for a practical, undramatic fairness that drew people here
  on its own reputation, not merely tolerated as an incidental fact of egalitarian Tepenian life generally.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No confirmed manufacturing chamber, current, historical, or paused-candidate, anywhere in the gathered
  material** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above) — Marambio is absent from all three tiers of
  `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`'s own Cradle site list. No meaningful local variation found —
  recorded as a clean, ordinary null.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: no dominant formal
  religion, with a practical ethic of motion and connectivity standing in for one — Marambio's whole civic
  purpose was keeping people and goods moving through a frozen world. For a robot, whose own existence already
  runs on function without needing a supernatural frame to justify it, this ethic requires no translation at
  all — an honest, undramatic, fully directly-inherited finding, consistent with the city's own whole
  function-first character rather than a deliberately manufactured point of difference.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands and is confirmed thin — modest, scaled to
  the city's practical, logistics-first character, consistent with everything else established about Marambio.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Marambio isn't named in either of Glitch-Coolant's two official canon
  poles. Given the city's own established working-class, function-first register (aviation and dock labor, no
  cultural-capital ambition per §18/§25), Marambio plausibly belongs in the **working-class/potency** category
  alongside Byrd, Rothera, and Troll — but reaching it through a genuinely new mechanism within the pole,
  directly extending §6f's own established smoke-break custom: consumption isn't limited by selection so much
  as by *time* — a city paced by flight schedules and ship arrivals rather than daylight has few dedicated
  evening-social windows at all, so what gets consumed is consumed quickly, during the same rare quiet
  stretches the smoke-break custom already occupies, rather than across a longer, more leisurely session.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep, the richest finding in this file]** §16's own material stands as the honest,
  already-dramatized local answer, more fully recognized here than invented: many human dockworkers are in
  ordinary, unexceptional relationships with a robot coworker — a genuine, lived, civic-scale instance of the
  project's established Bonded Lattice romance theme, not an exceptional case requiring special explanation.
  Given the Robot Physiology canon's own established value (sex as the highest available expression of trust),
  a plausible local courtship signal grows directly out of the shared-labor Kinship floor in §1: a consistent,
  repeatedly-chosen dock or flight-line work pairing — being requested, shift after shift, as someone's specific
  partner in physically demanding, weather-exposed labor — functions as Marambio's own real courtship
  gesture, trust built the same way everything else here gets built: through reliable, repeated, shared
  physical work. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely weighted complication this finding carries.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8c already establishes two genuinely distinct professional vocabularies — aviation
  shorthand specific to the airfield, separate dock/shipping jargon among the port workforce. Given attribute
  #5's own established dual-mode coordination infrastructure, robots working across both systems (or serving as
  the coordination link between them) are plausibly the population most fluently bilingual in both
  vocabularies at once — functioning, in effect, as everyday translators between the airfield's and the
  shipyard's own separate working languages, the same structural role Sayowa's dispatchers play for junction
  jargon, applied here across two genuinely separate professional registers rather than one shared vocabulary.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24's own material stands as the honest local answer: practical and aviation-
  oriented, making Marambio "one of the network's most operationally active nodes despite its modest
  population." A direct robot-specific extension, grounded in attribute #5's own coordination infrastructure:
  robots plausibly carry the primary responsibility for the real-time Arcanet traffic actually synchronizing
  airfield and shipyard schedules against each other — the practical, moment-to-moment labor underneath the
  city's own "quiet, undramatized capability" reputation (per its own Solar+Element pick).

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §11's practical aviation/logistics work clothing extends naturally to a robot-specific
  marker: given robots' own established dual-competency identity (§3), a practical, cross-rated work uniform or
  gear-set — legibly marking someone trained and trusted across both the airfield and the shipyards, rather than
  one — plausibly functions as Marambio's own recognizable civic aesthetic, function-driven in the same register
  as Sayowa's dispatch-coordination gear.
- **No music or visual-arts finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check): §12 and §13 are both explicitly
  "modest, background rather than central" with no named tradition or incident to anchor a robot-specific
  extension against — inventing one without a real local hook would risk exactly the generic-reskin failure
  mode the Swap Test exists to catch, the same restraint already exercised at Sayowa.
