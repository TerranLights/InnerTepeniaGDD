## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  the founding population arrived as several genuinely unrelated groups converging within a short window, with
  no prior relationship to each other and no single dominant national community to organize around (per "The
  Ships That Just Happened to Arrive Together"). What they had to build together from day one, regardless of
  origin, was the rookery-boundary infrastructure itself — negotiating shared space with 250,000 birds who
  were there first and never cared about anyone's nationality. That ongoing, still-continuing negotiation
  (individual pet-keeping guidelines, boundary maintenance, the whole daily practice of living beside the
  colony as neighbors, not wildlife) is Cape Adare's real kinship floor — it holds between two robots who
  arrived generations apart, as long as both actually live that coexistence day to day, cutting cleanly across
  every one of the city's mixed national threads.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: the heritage-preservation and civic memory-
  keeping specialization (§17, already established as "a genuinely respected specialization here") plausibly
  carries its own teaching-lineage, anchored to Archivist Elena Manalo's founding-era insistence on redoing the
  hut's structural measurements with updated instrumentation — a precision standard newer archivists are
  plausibly still trained into. One particular professional shape the broader shared civic project of
  "remembering correctly" (§2) takes, not a separate or competing mechanism.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §18's own civic philosophy — "being first carries an obligation to remember correctly,
  not just to claim credit" — is already Cape Adare's real emergent ethic, directly traceable to St. Carsten's
  own significance being about staying through a winter, not merely arriving. For robots specifically, given
  precise internal record-keeping, a plausible sharpened local expression is that a robot's own civic standing
  gets measured against how accurately she maintains and transmits the precedence record, not how prominently
  she's associated with it — consistent with §6a's "earned by residence and reverence, not ancestry."

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Cape Adare robot is recognizable by a genuinely unbothered, practiced
  comfort operating inside the rookery's own rhythm and scale — 250,000 birds' constant sound, smell, and
  claimed ground treated as an ordinary environmental fact rather than something to route around or tolerate.
  Distinct from Dumont d'Urville's own penguin-colony-adjacent identity (per §23's own explicit contrast: Cape
  Adare's relationship is "reverent bystander," not Dumont d'Urville's "negotiated coexistence" civic
  philosophy) — this is quieter and more settled, closer to genuine neighborliness than active management.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** St. Carsten veneration is already established with real specificity: his
  significance was never arriving first, but being the first to remain through an entire winter — confirmed,
  not invented. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new robot-specific extension of this material.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Earth + Robo-Element Wood is already on file for Cape Adare.
  Given the identical pair also belongs to Zukelli, both halves are grounded here in facts specific to Cape
  Adare rather than the generic "community warmth" reading either city could otherwise share: **Wood** grounds
  literally in the preserved wooden hut structure at the city's own physical and civic center, and in the
  founding population's own organic growth from several unrelated arriving groups into one civic body — growth
  rings from genuinely separate origins, not one seed. **Earth** grounds in the heating infrastructure's real
  "oasis" microclimate (§9, "warm and green... against the surrounding cold") and the city's own vast-but-
  low-density physical footprint. Zukelli's own reasoning (its music scene) shares neither of these specific
  anchors.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §10c is confirmed thin ("practical and functional, without a distinctive local
  tradition") — real room to expand, grounded in §6c/§10b's own arrival/departure meal-ritual framing:
  siligel/coolant service at Cape Adare plausibly centers on greeting and farewell, not routine resupply —
  genuinely distinct from Casey's individually-efficient transit-good framing, since Cape Adare's version is
  ceremonial-in-miniature (a small ritual marking a threshold) rather than purely functional.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Cape Adare isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category — a genuine open
  slot, not a gap-fill error. Given the city's own no-single-tradition-dominant civic register (mirrored
  directly in its acoustic-folk music scene, "adopted, cross-pollinated... belongs to everyone equally, owned
  by no one specifically"), Cape Adare plausibly belongs in the **bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety** category,
  alongside Janbogo, Zhongshan, and Shirayuki — but reaching that variety through cross-pollination among a
  chance-converged population rather than a settled multinational one or an artistic subculture.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Genuinely unique among this run's courtship findings, grounded in a custom
  no other Tepenian city has established: individual penguin pet-keeping (§14, "an intimate extension of the
  city's basic coexistence with the rookery," uncommon but real). A plausible local courtship signal is
  introducing a partner to a kept bird, or taking on shared care of one together — a small, specific,
  genuinely Cape-Adare-only gesture that couldn't transplant onto any other city's own courtship customs,
  since none of them have this particular institution to draw on at all.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes "The Hut" (capitalized) as shared civic shorthand, working
  across a population with no single dominant heritage language. Robot angle: given robots concentrate in
  heritage-documentation and archival work (§17), robot archivists are the plausible primary preservers of
  precise historical terminology surrounding the precedence narrative — the same structural role other cities'
  vernacular-preservation findings give their own trade specialists, here specifically archive-grounded rather
  than craft- or logistics-grounded.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §24 already establishes Cape Adare's Arcanet use as heavily weighted toward
  historical/civic archive material, consistent with the city's whole memory-keeping orientation. See
  Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new material this pass adds regarding the city's specific,
  asymmetric warmth toward Zukelli via the subnet's instant line.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** The acoustic-folk music tradition (§12) is already established as the
  city's primary visible marker — unhurried, unamplified, genuinely cross-pollinated rather than any one
  tradition's inheritance. Robot angle: given robots' precise timing and pitch capability, robot musicians
  plausibly function as an informal calibration reference within ensembles that otherwise deliberately resist
  any single dominant tradition — a quiet technical role inside a genuinely communal art form, not a separate
  robot-only performance tradition layered on top of it.
- **No fashion-specific finding proposed this pass** (Honesty Check): §11 explicitly notes no strong national
  aesthetic marker, climate-function first — consistent with, not a gap in, a city whose whole civic register
  resists any single dominant identity being read off appearance.
