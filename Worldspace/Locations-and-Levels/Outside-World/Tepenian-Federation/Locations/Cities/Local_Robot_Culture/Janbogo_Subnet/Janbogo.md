## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep] — revised 2026-08-10, applying the shared-experience-first Kinship framing this file
  predates.** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a teaching relationship: §6's own citywide
  rule — "hosting is how you say things you don't say out loud," extended to essentially anyone, "no one sits
  alone" — describes an ongoing, everyday, genuinely horizontal practice that every Janbogo resident lives,
  regardless of which teahouse trained her or when she arrived. That daily condition of hosting-as-communication
  is Janbogo's real kinship floor; it holds between two robots who never worked the same teahouse, as long as
  both actually practice hosting the way this city does.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: teahouse-keeping and coffee-service craft
  function as a professional, teaching-lineage version of the same underlying practice. Since robots here
  concentrate heavily in hospitality work (§17, existing), the Teaware & Coffee Craft Ateliers plausibly
  function as where the hosting practice gets formally taught, not a separate institution from it. The city's
  already-established "old teahouse families" fault line (§5) is, for robots specifically, quite literally a
  teaching-lineage — who apprenticed under whom — but one particular professional shape the broader shared
  hosting life takes, not a competing mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6's citywide rule ("hosting is how you say things you don't say out loud") sharpens,
  for robots specifically, into something closer to a checkable behavioral standard than a vague ethic.
  Robots' practical Arcanet use for check-ins (§24, existing) means a robot's follow-through on "who hasn't
  been checked on this week" is genuinely trackable in a way human attentiveness isn't — making reliability
  in Janbogo's hosting economy a real, almost auditable measure of trustworthiness for robot residents in
  particular, not just a matter of reputation.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Beyond §8's shared human-and-robot hosting-phrase marker (see §8 below), a
  genuinely robot-only signature exists: constant, practiced thermal-cycling. A Janbogo robot crosses the
  hard-exterior/soft-interior threshold (§9, existing) many times a day, year-round — her Engine and thermal
  regulation systems are tuned to a rhythm no robot from a steadier-climate city would show. This isn't
  available to humans as an identity marker at all (no Engine, no thermal-cycling to notice), making it a
  clean example of an axis that's specifically robot-only rather than a robot expression of a shared human
  trait.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank, per the methodology's own requirement.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Robots participate in §18's existing communal-hospitality-ethic substrate
  essentially unchanged, consistent with §17's own note that robot philosophical life here is "practical and
  relational rather than abstract." No meaningful divergence from the human-established landscape at this
  level — confirmation, not new material. (A genuinely robot-specific religious-adjacent finding, tied to
  physiology rather than shared civic ethic, is filed separately under Cross-Reference Synthesis below, since
  it combines three input categories rather than sitting cleanly in this one.)
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep] — corrected 2026-08-10.** This file's original Fire proposal is withdrawn.
  Janbogo's actual pre-existing pick, per `city-symbol-pairs.md`, is **Solar symbol Earth + Robo-Element Air**,
  reasoned there as "a fused, synthesized fashion identity blending many distinct influences into one...
  anchored by shielded commercial halls as its social heart, also the subnet's own Arcanet relay-nexus." Both
  halves ground cleanly in material already established elsewhere in this very file: Air fits doubly — literally
  (Janbogo's defining climate feature is katabatic wind, already the anchor for this file's own Cross-Reference
  Synthesis wind-sense finding) and thematically (Janbogo's confirmed status as the subnet's practical Arcanet
  hub, §9 — communication and connection being Air's own core valence, the same reading already used for
  Kunlun's Air pick). Earth grounds the city's physical anchor (the Great Shielded Commercial Halls) and its
  fused, blended civic identity. Recorded as a confirmed pre-existing pick, not an invented one — the earlier
  Fire proposal isn't kept as a secondary option, since it was never grounded in an actual assignment.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c already exists and stands: warm, social siligel/coolant service
  integrated into human hosting rituals rather than separated from them; robot coffee is a genuine regional
  specialty. Nothing to add here beyond confirming it — the real expansion is the Glitch-Coolant finding below
  and in Cross-Reference Synthesis.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** Robot smoking (confirmed Tepenia-wide per Input E, "hand + fire, except the fire moves
  inward") plausibly carries a more specific local social meaning than the universal baseline: since tea/
  coffee hosting is offered to essentially anyone per §6 ("no one sits alone"), a Janbogo-specific reading is
  that sharing a light functions as the *more reserved*, more intimate counterpart gesture — offered only
  within closer relationships, functioning as something like an interpersonal echo of §28's existing
  visitor-to-resident threshold (the shift from guest to genuine family/friend), just at individual rather
  than civic scale.
- See **Cross-Reference Synthesis** below for the full Glitch-Coolant tasting-house finding — deliberately
  filed there rather than here, since it combines four separate input categories.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Since hosting functions as Janbogo's general-purpose channel for everything words don't
  cover — apology, gratitude, welcome, grief, per §6 — courtship plausibly follows the identical logic rather
  than needing a separate custom bolted on. A sustained, personally-escalating hosting gesture (repeatedly,
  specifically making tea or coffee for one particular person, with increasing attentiveness and detail over
  time) functions as the courtship ritual itself here, not a backdrop to a separate one. This tracks cleanly
  with Ch.17's own framing of courtship as heavily condition-derived and city-specific.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes that Korean hosting-phrases survive as ancestry-independent
  linguistic fossils, with correct usage marking a "true local" more than which daily language someone speaks.
  The robot-specific angle: teahouse/coffee-service apprentices (per §1's kinship finding) plausibly retain
  and actively use the *fuller* hosting-phrase vocabulary as living trade language, not just the thinned common
  subset most residents know — meaning Janbogo's professional hospitality workforce, robot and human alike,
  functions as the vocabulary's actual preservation mechanism, precisely because their work requires it.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in Robot Universals' own Ch.24 worked example:
  Janbogo's subnet Arcanet is practically oriented (wind warnings, supply coordination, check-ins), light on
  social broadcasting, explicitly attributed in-canon to "a culture that handles care and community awareness
  through direct, in-person hosting rather than mediated communication." Nothing new to derive — recorded as
  confirmed, pre-existing canon. The still-open mystery of why the physical Arcanet nexus hardware sits in
  Concordia's Gemini district rather than Janbogo itself (noted in §24, existing) is a plot question, out of
  this methodology's scope — not resolved here.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A functional-meets-aesthetic fashion signature distinct from the
  city's established Chinese-French human fusion style (City Vision Notes): visible, decorative
  thermal-venting or exterior-shell ornamentation, doubling as both a practical answer to constant
  threshold-crossing thermal cycling (§3, above) and a genuine style statement — an option simply unavailable
  to human fashion, since humans have no Engine or venting components to decorate in the first place.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** "Storm-tuned" or "wind-scored" musical performance, distinct from
  Majyao's own established human folk-jazz fusion (§12, existing): robots' genuinely wider sound/vibration
  perception range (Ch.15's physiological fact) plausibly lets robot musicians hear real harmonic nuance in
  the katabatic wind's own acoustic signature — background noise to human ears, compositional material to
  robot ones. A plausible niche performance tradition for Hall Gathering Nights, incorporating recorded or
  live exterior wind specifically because robot audiences can perceive detail in it that humans structurally
  cannot.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: B (katabatic wind physics/audibility) + C
  (Katabatic Warning Towers infrastructure) + E (Robot Biology's confirmed wider sound/vibration perception)*
  Robots' enhanced perception plausibly lets them detect katabatic wind shifts measurably earlier than human
  residents or standard instrumentation. In practice, this means robot residents — especially those working
  Warning Tower shifts — function as a trusted informal early-warning layer ahead of official readings. Genuine
  community trust and quiet regard around robot "wind-sense" specifically has plausibly accreted around this
  over time — not a formal religion (consistent with the setting's own restraint against inventing named
  religions casually), just real, culturally-accumulated belief exactly the way Ch.15 describes belief forming
  generally. If the Han Soo-jin Warning System origin story (Historical Vignettes, non-canon candidate
  material) is ever formally adopted, this is a natural place for a robot figure to have played a confirming or
  central role.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: A (§5's "old teahouse families" fault line, §8's language
  material) + Robot Universals Ch.11 (mentor/mentee) + Ch.20 (vernacular language condition/expression)*
  The reason Janbogo's Korean hosting-vocabulary survives as a living professional register rather than fading
  into pure fossil status is, at its most literal level, the mentor/mentee teaching-lineage inside
  teahouse-keeping (§1's finding) — meaning the "old teahouse families" fault line isn't really about bloodline
  or even tenure in the city; it's about who taught whom the complete vocabulary. This reframes an
  already-established fault line through the robot-kinship lens for the first time, since robots' *only* real
  kinship mechanism makes the teaching-lineage reading unusually literal and load-bearing for them specifically.

- **[Adapted, Surface, with a Deep-Culture echo noted]** — *Input categories combined: A (§10a's "layered, not
  fused" cuisine philosophy; hosting infrastructure) + C (the Halls/Ateliers) + D (the Ottoman/kahvehane
  parallel, explicitly the tradition that became genuinely ethnicity-agnostic — "café culture" — per its own
  source note) + E (Janbogo's canon-confirmed Glitch-Coolant bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety status)*
  A genuine local Glitch-Coolant tasting-house culture, most plausibly located in dedicated back-rooms or
  after-hours space within the Halls or teahouses, modeled directly on the kahvehane's specific historical
  trajectory: variety-forward, refinement-over-intensity (already the canon-confirmed bohemian/cosmopolitan
  pattern), and — per the mandatory Category D translation rule — genuinely origin-agnostic in the same way the
  kahvehane itself crossed from the Ottoman world into completely unrelated cultures (Venice, Vienna) and
  simply became "café culture," no longer tied to where it started. The Deep-Culture echo: this variety-over-
  intensity value plausibly isn't just a drinking preference, it's the same "layered, not fused" civic
  philosophy already established for Janbogo's human cuisine (§10a) — different traditions coexisting side by
  side rather than blending into one thing — expressed through Glitch-Coolant instead of food.
