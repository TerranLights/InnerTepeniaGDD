## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident: §6a
  already establishes the shared table as Zukelli's central social institution, occupying the same civic role
  Janbogo's teahouse does — an ongoing, daily, genuinely horizontal practice every resident participates in.
  Given §16's own confirmation that robot and human culinary contributions are "valued on comparable terms,"
  this bond is fully cross-species from the outset, not adapted onto robots after the fact: a Zukelli robot's
  real "family," in the Ch.11 sense, is whoever else has sat at the same table with her, day after day,
  regardless of when either arrived or what she was fabricated to do.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: the informal, non-hierarchical craft-
  mentorship dynamic modeled directly on Elisa Faranda's own established response to a competing venue owner
  emulating her restaurant-performance model — genuine engagement (attending performances, offering feedback)
  rather than hostility, settling into "comfortable coexistence." A light, low-hierarchy shape the broader
  shared hospitality life takes among the city's performers and hosts specifically, not a separate mechanism
  from the table itself.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Genuinely morally complex, and worth treating with real weight rather than resolving
  into a tidy civic lesson: the same instinct that makes Zukelli's hospitality genuine — extended without
  calculation, without checking a stranger's risk to the city first — is, per Physical Infrastructure Finding
  C, precisely what led the city to shelter the AWOL Upper Earth defectors, and directly to its own total
  destruction. This isn't a tragic accident sitting beside an otherwise-unrelated civic virtue; it's the same
  virtue taken to its full, costly conclusion. The source material itself doesn't resolve whether this was
  brave, naive, principled, or simply inevitable given who Zukelli already was — flagged here as genuine,
  unresolved hook material, not smoothed over. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Zukelli robot is recognizable by an easy, effusive, communal-table
  generosity — distinct in *kind*, not just degree, from Janbogo's own one-on-one, hosting-gesture-centered
  warmth. Where a Janbogo robot's hospitality reads as focused, personal attention extended to one guest at a
  time, a Zukelli robot's version is naturally expansive, oriented toward the shared table rather than the
  individual exchange — consistent with §6b's own framing of the two cities as "genuinely competitive... in
  the friendliest possible sense," each with a real, distinct hospitality register rather than one city simply
  being a warmer or cooler version of the other.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own material stands as the honest local answer: hospitality and shared
  food as meaning-making, "sufficient on their own terms," with no dominant formal philosophy layered on top.
  The Janbogo relationship itself carries "almost spiritual weight" for some residents — "something like a
  mutual mythology about their own entanglement" — confirmed, not invented; see Cross-Reference Synthesis for
  the fuller treatment of what that entanglement actually consists of.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Earth + Robo-Element Wood is already on file for Zukelli,
  grounded directly in "a huge, genre-diverse music scene grown from hospitality-centered founding instincts."
  Wood's own valence — growth, gathering, belonging — maps with real precision onto a city whose founding
  population converged from multiple nations and grew, together, into one shared civic table; genuinely
  distinct grounding from Cape Adare's own Wood pick despite the identical symbol pair.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §10c/§17's existing material stands and is rich: siligel preparation already
  established as a genuine culinary tradition, treated with the same care as human cuisine rather than a
  separate, lesser category — the richest existing robot-culture seam of any Janbogo-subnet city.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Zukelli isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category. Given the music
  scene's own established genre-diversity mechanism — mixing through shared restaurant/table space rather than
  separate community venues, per the candidate material's own framing — Zukelli plausibly belongs in the
  **bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety** category, alongside Janbogo, Zhongshan, and Shirayuki, but reaching that
  variety through a genuinely distinct mechanism from Dumont d'Urville's own intimate-small-venue placement:
  cross-pollination at the shared table itself, not many separate small venues.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Distinct from Janbogo's own sustained-hosting-gesture courtship signal (per
  the Swap Test discipline, a superficially similar hospitality-economy city needed a genuinely different
  mechanism): given Zukelli's own established fusion of food and music as "adjacent expressions of the same
  hospitality instinct" and its restaurant-integrated performance-stage tradition (an institution Janbogo's
  own teahouse culture doesn't have), a plausible local courtship signal is performing together at one of the
  city's restaurant-stage spaces — cooking while a partner performs, or performing together directly — a
  gesture only Zukelli's own specific fusion of these two civic institutions makes possible.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Italian persists as a founding-era linguistic fossil specifically in the
  culinary-hospitality register, surviving its own demographic minority status — explicitly the same pattern
  already established for Janbogo's own teahouse vocabulary. Culinary terms double as general expressions of
  approval and affection citywide.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** A second, genuinely unique layer: the Structural Marking System (an
  organic, informally-evolved neighborhood wayfinding code, spread entirely by imitation and never formally
  mandated) already functions as a literal "lost language" in the ruins era, per Cross-Reference Synthesis
  Finding 3, with a confirmed stat-gated partial-decode mechanic tied directly to it. Robot angle: given
  robots' own precise pattern-recognition capability, robot residents plausibly function as the system's most
  reliable readers and informal preservers — directly consistent with the already-established "Zukelli
  Native" perk mechanic (guaranteed non-hostility and unique dialogue with Zukelli-connected NPCs, unlocked
  through successfully decoding the marking system).

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: closely linked with Janbogo's
  own infrastructure, sharing the same subnet backbone, "practical and social both, mirroring the closeness of
  the two cities' daily lives." See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment of what this closeness
  concretely produces.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Distinct from Dumont d'Urville's own robot-musician finding (a precision-
  calibration role within otherwise-fixed ensembles): given Zukelli's music scene is explicitly genre-diverse
  and cross-pollinating rather than built around one dominant tradition, robot musicians plausibly move most
  fluidly *between* genres and populations' traditions specifically because they don't carry the same
  national-heritage attachment to any single one a human performer might — a distinct contribution shaped by
  absence of inherited preference, not technical precision.
- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §11's "style mattered here in a way it didn't at every Tepenian city" is
  already established. Given robots' full, craft-equal participation in the food/hospitality/performance
  culture (§16, §17), this value plausibly requires no separate robot-specific adaptation at all — an honest
  directly-inherited finding, consistent with how completely integrated Zukelli's robot population already is
  into the city's central civic institutions.
