## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  every Denison resident lives inside one continuous, physically fused structure, where a section's failure
  under wind load genuinely threatens every connected section, not just its own occupants. That ongoing,
  literal interdependence — shared maintenance rotations, a real, felt sense that letting your own section go
  threatens everyone attached to it — is Denison's real kinship floor, holding between two robots who've never
  worked the same maintenance shift, as long as both actually live inside the fused structure day to day. This
  is a genuinely robot-only inflection of a bond every resident shares, since robots' own greater structural
  resilience (§16, §17) means they carry a disproportionate share of the actual load-bearing maintenance work
  the interdependence depends on.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: Chief Wind-Engineer Wei Zhang's founding-era
  design argument — that individually-anchored structures were more vulnerable to Cape Denison's sustained
  wind than a fully fused one, a position that won and was built and held — plausibly anchors a genuine
  teaching-lineage in structural engineering craft, newer engineers trained into her specific load-sharing
  design philosophy. One professional shape the broader shared structural life takes, not a competing
  mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "you don't complain about the wind — you account for it" and §28's own framing
  (wind-safety competence becoming instinct, not conscious effort, is "when Denison considers you one of its
  own") already establish a real, specific civic ethic: practical stoicism paired with an immediate expectation
  of competence, not tolerance for newcomer softness. For robots specifically, given their own greater
  structural resilience and concentration in hazard-response work, a plausible sharpened local expression is
  that a robot's actual standing gets measured by how quickly and calmly she handles a genuine structural
  emergency, not by how she talks about the danger beforehand or after.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Denison robot is recognizable by a practiced, visible calm operating in
  genuinely lethal exterior wind conditions that the city's own fused, aerodynamically smooth architecture
  otherwise keeps largely invisible to residents day to day (per the Enneagram file's own architecture-vs-
  speech resolution). Given robots concentrate specifically in the hazardous outdoor wind-engineering and
  hazard-response roles (§16, §17), this steadiness plausibly reads as a specific professional signature —
  distinct from ordinary residents' more abstract, recitation-based pride in the city's severity, since these
  robots have direct, repeated, physical experience of exactly what's being recited.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Denison was a historical Cradle fabrication-chamber manufacturer (with Sinheung and
  Byrd), lost when the city was destroyed. A robot fabricated there plausibly carried real pride tied to
  Fabrication Engineer Kenji Suh's own established working method — rigorous, repeated calibration testing
  over any single breakthrough insight — the same "knowing exactly where you were made" pride already
  established for Sinheung, but genuinely distinct in register: Denison's chambers no longer physically exist,
  so a Denison-fabricated robot's own origin point is now a place she can never return to, a real, quiet grief
  layered underneath what would otherwise be straightforward manufacturing pride. See Cross-Reference
  Synthesis for the fuller treatment.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's secular "mastery-through-understanding" ethic is already established
  and specific — wind as a problem to genuinely understand and engineer around, not simply endure, explicitly
  distinguished in-canon from Belgrano's fatalistic function-before-comfort register. Confirmed, not invented.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Venus + Robo-Element Metal is already on file for Denison, and
  Denison is the sole Venus-assigned city in the entire 34-city table — no cross-city repeat risk the way Cape
  Adare's Earth+Wood pair required extra care against Zukelli. Metal's precision/integrity/uncompromising-
  structural-answer valence maps directly onto Wei Zhang's own fused-design philosophy and Kenji Suh's
  calibration-precision fabrication method — a genuinely different grounding than Zhongshan's own Metal pick
  (craft ethic and Daoist endurance), since Denison's Metal is about engineering exactness under literal
  physical load, not contemplative restraint. Venus grounds with real precision too: astronomically the most
  extreme planet in the solar system (crushing pressure, extreme heat) — genuinely gorgeous from orbit, lethal
  on the surface — a direct echo of Denison's own landmark structures (visible and striking through blowing
  snow) sitting atop a wind regime that could kill an unprepared visitor within the hour.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands and is genuinely specific: siligel storage
  and handling protocols shaped directly around wind-driven structural risk, in a way other cities' siligel
  culture doesn't need to consider.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Denison isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category, and Janbogo's own
  already-completed Swap Test explicitly rules Denison out of the bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety pole
  specifically ("Fort McMurdo/Scott/Denison do not share" Janbogo's bohemian status). Given the city's blunt,
  direct communication register (§6b, §8) and its technical-mastery-over-refinement civic value (§18), Denison
  plausibly belongs in the **working-class/potency** category alongside Byrd, Rothera, and Troll — straight-
  forward and unpretentious rather than variety-seeking, consistent with a city that takes pride in facing
  extremity directly rather than softening it.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from Sayowa's own duty-covering courtship signal (per the Swap Test discipline,
  a superficially similar "taking on risk for someone" city needed a genuinely different mechanism): given the
  wind-statistics-recitation tradition is Denison's established *public* pride ritual, a plausible private
  courtship variant is sharing a personal, specific close-call number or story that never enters the standard
  public recitation — a private, vulnerable counterpart to the civic performance, extending "The Newcomer Who
  Didn't Believe the Numbers Until She Did" logic to an interpersonal scale: trusting someone with the number
  that actually scared you, not the ones you're proud to recite.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes wind-severity terminology functioning as general-purpose
  intensifiers ("that's a Category [X] problem"), recognizable Tepenia-wide as distinctly Denison speech.
  Robot angle: given robots concentrate in structural/wind-engineering roles and Arcanet use skews heavily
  toward hazard tracking (§24), robot engineers are the plausible precision-anchor for this vocabulary —
  keeping the intensifier slang tethered to actually accurate figures rather than exaggerated drift, directly
  consistent with the recitation tradition's own whole point (per "The Newcomer" vignette, the recited numbers
  turned out to be understated, not exaggerated).

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: a near-real-time structural
  safety network functioning almost as much as a communications tool, "a specialization unique among the
  cities covered so far." See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new material this pass adds
  regarding a plausible functional data-sharing relationship with Dumont d'Urville.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Percussive, rhythmic music (audible "over" the wind rather than requiring
  silence) and wind-interactive sculpture/installation art (§13, "unique in Tepenia") are already established
  as the city's primary visible markers.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Wind-rated gear is already established as identity marker as much as
  necessity (§11, "a quiet flex" elsewhere in Tepenia). Robot angle: since robots' own structural resilience
  means Denison-rated gear isn't strictly protective necessity for them the way it is for humans, a robot who
  maintains and wears it anyway is making a purely deliberate professional-identity statement — genuinely
  distinct from the human version of the same gear, which carries real protective stakes alongside the
  symbolic ones.
