## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  every Fort McMurdo resident lives the same recurring, unceremonious condition — each rotation, the crew
  either trusts your output or it doesn't, verified again next shift, with §6b's own "trust expressed as
  workload, not conversation" ruling out any softer, more sentimental substitute. That ongoing verification
  cycle, genuinely origin-blind and species-blind on the shift floor (§16), is Fort McMurdo's real kinship
  floor — it holds between two robots who've never worked the same rotation, as long as both actually live
  inside the same "output is your standing" condition. This directly explains §17's own honest admission that
  Fort McMurdo "simply doesn't stop working long enough" for a more contemplative robot culture to develop:
  the crew-trust cycle already does the bonding work other cities assign to religion, craft, or ceremony.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: per Course of Events #10 ("The Unnamed Ones"),
  the city's own best coordination systems worked so well that the tacit judgment behind them stopped being
  actively taught — until a real crisis exposed the gap, and a deliberate mentorship program was built to fix
  it. A structural, crisis-driven teaching-lineage response, distinct from an organically-grown apprenticeship
  tradition — one particular shape the broader shift-floor trust condition takes when it needs formal
  reinforcement, not a separate mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §5's own founding-identity line — "a crew that can't trust its weakest link fails
  together" — is already Fort McMurdo's real emergent ethic. Distinct from Davis's self-effacement-verified-
  against-one's-own-data and Mirny's skepticism-toward-untested-claims: Fort McMurdo's version is about
  collective, safety-critical stakes specifically — extraction and manufacturing work where a single
  unreliable link genuinely endangers the whole crew, not just personal professional reputation. For robots
  specifically, given precise task-logging capability, a plausible sharpened local expression is that
  reliability gets measured almost audibly, through an actual shift-completion record, rather than through
  claimed competence or self-report.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Fort McMurdo robot is recognizable by an unbothered, matter-of-fact
  awareness that her work genuinely ripples outward nationally, held without ever needing external validation
  or performance — directly grounded in §21's own "operational gravity, not political theater... it doesn't
  perform importance, because it doesn't need to." Distinct in *kind* from Sayowa's own "disproportionate
  structural responsibility held without drama" finding (per the Swap Test discipline, a superficially similar
  "modest city secretly important" register needed a genuinely different root): Sayowa's is about a *small*
  population carrying *outsized junction significance*; Fort McMurdo's is about being the actual historical
  seat of national decision-making gravity itself — real governmental and operational-industrial centrality,
  not infrastructure passing through.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above) — and
  genuinely explained, not just unexamined: fabrication chambers were explicitly considered and rejected here
  in 2026-07-07, since Mount Erebus's active status raises both a lava/ashfall hazard and ongoing seismic
  disruption to precision synthesis equipment. Recorded honestly, with its own confirmed reason, rather than
  left as an unexplained gap.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's own secular ethic — "the work is the meaning" — is already established
  and specific, alongside five generations of "ambient rather than acute" relationship to living beside a
  permanently active volcano. Confirmed, not invented. Erebus itself functions as a recurring, half-personified
  cultural presence rather than a devotional object — "watched, personified, never quite trusted" (§13),
  entering vernacular directly ("she's smoking today," §8) — closer to a constant civic co-resident than a
  religious figure. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new material this pass adds regarding
  where civic reverence actually lands in a city this secular.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Jupiter + Robo-Element Earth is already on file for Fort
  McMurdo, grounded directly in "dominance so obvious it needs no assertion, quietly load-bearing." Jupiter's
  own sheer scale without needing to prove it tracks §21's own civic register precisely. Earth here grounds
  distinctly from every other Earth-picked city in this run: not Davis's literal agriculture, not Byrd's
  literal underground, not Mirny's structural windbreak, not Sayowa's quiet-dispatch practicality — Fort
  McMurdo's Earth is specifically about political and operational gravity, the literal ground a former seat of
  government stood on, made concrete in the Comms Coordination Stations as the physical site where that
  gravity is actually exercised.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands: industrial-grade, high-volume siligel
  production, itself one of the city's exports — "practical and reliable rather than artisanal, matching the
  city's whole relationship to craft."
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Fort McMurdo isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category. Given the city's
  unsentimental, output-over-refinement civic value and its shift-based social life (canteens between shifts
  as the closest thing to communal gathering space, §6c), Fort McMurdo plausibly belongs in the **working-
  class/potency** category alongside Byrd, Rothera, and Denison — direct and unpretentious, consistent with a
  culture that measures people by output rather than ceremony.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from Sayowa's own duty-covering courtship signal (which is about absorbing
  someone else's burden): given §6b's own established hospitality logic — warmth expressed by being handed
  real responsibility quickly, "trust expressed as workload, not conversation" — a plausible local courtship
  signal runs the opposite direction: deliberately delegating a genuinely high-stakes task or responsibility to
  a specific person, as the city's own version of a trust gesture extended to romantic interest specifically.
  Giving trust, not absorbing risk, is the local intimacy currency.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes shift-time replacing clock-time ("end of second") and Erebus
  itself entering idiom ("she's smoking today"), alongside hybrid bilingual technical jargon. Robot angle:
  genuinely distinct from most other cities in this run, where a specific trade or professional group anchors
  the local vernacular — since Fort McMurdo's shift-floor culture is already, confirmedly, origin-blind and
  species-blind, robots plausibly function as equally-weighted co-creators of this vocabulary rather than a
  specialist subgroup preserving it, consistent with §17's own framing that robots here "talk about their work
  the way the humans do."

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: overwhelmingly practical — shift
  logs, equipment status, safety documentation — explicitly distinguished in-canon from Janbogo's own relay-
  nexus role: "the wires" (Janbogo) versus Fort McMurdo's own staffed comms-coordination "brain... using those
  wires to actually make things happen." See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment of what this
  distinction means for where the city's real civic gravity actually sits.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** Shift-rhythm work music dominates, with source-nation folk traditions
  surfacing in canteens and bunk-blocks as "a running undercurrent" (§12) — already established, human-driven
  material with no strong robot-specific hook to extend without forcing one.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** §11 already establishes off-shift fashion as a "released-pressure-valve
  aesthetic" for the human population. Robot angle: since robots plausibly don't carry the same accumulated
  physical/psychological shift-stress that drives that release, a robot's own off-shift presentation likely
  reads calmer and more consistent than the human population's own volatile pressure-valve swing — a genuine,
  visible difference in register rather than a robot version of the same human need.

## PART II — Cross-Reference Synthesis Findings (Step 4)

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: A (§24's explicit "wires vs. brain" distinction from
  Janbogo) + C (Finding B — the Comms Coordination Stations as the physical site of "invisible power actually
  exercised") + Course of Events #11 ("The Ledger Nobody Elected" — scheduling authority extending into
  genuine resource/credit allocation) + §17 (robots' own "doesn't stop working long enough" for philosophical
  culture)*
  In a city this secular and this unsentimental, civic reverence doesn't vanish — it relocates. The Comms
  Coordination Stations plausibly function as the closest thing Fort McMurdo has to a sacred institution: a
  place where real decisions with genuine national consequence get made without ceremony, narration, or
  religious framing, exactly because the city "doesn't stop working long enough" to build a more traditional
  belief structure. The reverence other cities extend to a faith or a founding myth, Fort McMurdo extends,
  quietly, to a logistics office.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: A (§23's own explicit "closest working relationship" claim)
  + `City_National_Connections.md` ("Strong, Industrial/Social" tie — "collaborative-and-competitive,"
  precision calibration flowing routinely from Scott) + `City_Relationship_Database.md`'s own Consistency Rule
  ("effectively twin cities, not distant settlements")*
  Fort McMurdo's real felt community isn't the wider Janbogo subnet at all — this pass found zero documented
  ties to Denison, Dumont d'Urville, Cape Adare, or Zukelli — it's specifically Scott, three kilometers away on
  the same peninsula. The day-to-day precision-calibration collaboration between the two cities plausibly
  functions almost like an inter-city extension of §1's own crew-interdependence ethic, just scaled up from
  individual crewmates to an entire neighboring city. **This is the highest-priority forward flag in this
  file** — Scott is the natural next city to run, given how much of Fort McMurdo's own culture is defined by
  this one specific relationship rather than by the subnet at large.

- **[Adapted, Deep]** — *Input categories combined: C/History (Course of Events #10, "The Unnamed Ones" — the
  city's own best systems working too well to keep teaching themselves) + §1 (the crew-interdependence
  Kinship finding) + Robot Biology (precise task-logging capability)*
  A real, structurally-grounded civic tension worth flagging rather than resolving: a culture built entirely
  on "output is your standing, not conversation" risks losing the tacit judgment behind its own best systems
  precisely because it never narrates or ceremonializes expertise the way a more reflective city would. Robots'
  own precise record-keeping could either genuinely help here (preserving the literal decision trail) or,
  ironically, mask the same gap even more thoroughly, if only outcomes get logged and the *reasoning* behind
  them doesn't — worth surfacing as a real, unresolved risk in the city's own civic design, not just a positive
  finding about its efficiency.
