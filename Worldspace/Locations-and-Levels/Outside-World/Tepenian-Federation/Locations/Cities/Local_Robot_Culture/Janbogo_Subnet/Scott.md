## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident,
  and deliberately distinct from Fort McMurdo's own internal mechanism: where a Fort McMurdo robot earns
  standing through a recurring, verified shift-completion record among interchangeable crewmates, a Scott
  robot's real kinship floor is slower and more personal — the ongoing, generational condition of living in a
  community small enough that "people, robot and human alike, simply know each other" (§16), sustained
  together through the shared, continuing civic duty of tending St. Robert's memory and Hut Point itself. That
  ongoing stewardship, not any single commemorative event, is what actually binds Scott's residents — it holds
  between two robots who've never worked the same maintenance rotation, as long as both actually live inside
  the same small, long-familiar community and its shared duty of remembrance.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: precision-calibration craft, plausibly
  anchored to the Keeper of Hut Point's own hands-on maintenance and monitoring tradition — a professional
  teaching-lineage shape of the broader shared stewardship life above, not a competing mechanism from it.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "quality/precision over scale" is already established as Scott's real civic value —
  and per "The Vote Māui Won by Staying Modest," this wasn't a passive default: staying small was a genuinely
  contested founding-era choice that won by a real but not overwhelming margin, and per Course of Events #8
  ("Quiet On Purpose, Not By Accident"), the choice keeps getting actively reaffirmed against real later
  temptation (a declined investment offer to expand industrially), not settled once and forgotten. For robots
  specifically, a plausible sharpened local expression is that genuine standing gets measured against
  consistency and precision sustained over real time, not against scale or output volume the way it would at
  Fort McMurdo.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Scott robot is recognizable by a genuine, unbothered ease with modest
  scale — directly grounded in the city's own Solar symbol reasoning, "content not to be noticed." Distinct
  from performed modesty or resentment: this is settled, lived comfort with being the quieter of two
  neighboring cities, without needing to compete on Fort McMurdo's own terms (§25). The one real complication
  to this otherwise-clean identity marker — genuine, ongoing friction specifically about *being read as* Fort
  McMurdo's lesser sibling — is kept separate here and treated fully in Cross-Reference Synthesis, since it's
  a relational fact about the neighboring city, not a standalone trait of Scott's own robots.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **No meaningful local variation found** (Honesty Check, per the Universal Triage Record above). Recorded
  here rather than left blank.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** §18's St. Robert veneration is already established as "the most personally
  felt Saint relationship anywhere in Tepenia" — a lived, daily awareness the city exists because of one
  man's unknowing sacrifice, confirmed rather than invented. Robot angle: given robots' own long-term, stable
  presence in a community this small, and their direct involvement in the commemorative work itself (§17),
  robot residents plausibly function as some of the most continuous living links to St. Robert's story across
  generations — not through archival precision (the Cape Adare model) but through personal, warm, repeatedly-
  retold oral transmission, since Scott's whole relationship to St. Robert is already established as personal
  and daily rather than institutional and formal.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Saturn + Robo-Element Water is already on file for Scott,
  grounded directly in "the deliberately quiet counterpart next door to a loud capital, content not to be
  noticed." Water's own positive valence — adaptability, calm resilience, quiet strength — tracks the city's
  own settled, unshowy temperament with real precision.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands: modest, practical siligel production,
  "without the industrial scale of Fort McMurdo's production, matching the city's overall precision-over-
  volume character."
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Scott isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category, and
  neither fits cleanly: bohemian/variety implies a cosmopolitan refinement register Scott's small, domestic
  culture doesn't have; working-class/potency implies a blue-collar-volume register that belongs to Fort
  McMurdo, not its quieter neighbor. Given the city's small, personal-familiarity social fabric, Scott
  plausibly represents a genuinely new pattern across this whole run: intimate, small-batch, home-brewed
  Glitch-Coolant shared within family-like circles rather than at any public venue — domestic rather than
  either refined or potent.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior city's courtship finding: given §6e's own low-privacy civic
  value ("people know each other's names here" already, per §6b) and the small population's total personal
  familiarity, a plausible local courtship custom runs opposite to cities where intimacy is marked by a
  private, individual gesture — at Scott, a relationship becoming genuinely serious is marked less by any one
  deliberate act and more by the whole small community's quiet, collective, already-assumed acknowledgment,
  since privacy simply isn't available or expected the way it would be at a larger city. The "gesture," if
  there is one, is choosing not to keep something private that easily could be.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes "The Hut" and "St. Robert" as immediately recognizable local
  shorthand carrying real ceremonial weight. Robot angle: distinct from the archival-precision role robots
  play at Cape Adare or Byrd, Scott's robots plausibly serve the vocabulary's *personal, oral* register
  specifically — recognized not for correcting drift in the historical record, but for how warmly and
  consistently they retell St. Robert's story across generations, matching the city's own personal-rather-
  than-institutional relationship to its founding figure.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: closely linked with Fort
  McMurdo's own network infrastructure given physical proximity, carrying disproportionate commemorative and
  historical archive traffic. See Cross-Reference Synthesis for the fuller treatment of what the Fort
  McMurdo link actually consists of, mechanically.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §11's "comfortable with the outdoors" as genuine cultural value, and §12's
  small, close-knit, informal music culture, are already established. Given robots' own long-term stable
  presence and direct involvement in outdoor Hut Point stewardship work, this value plausibly requires no
  robot-specific adaptation at all — an honest directly-inherited finding, not a gap needing to be filled with
  an invented marker.

  Historical Vignette material (kept as supporting municipal history: "The Instrument Fort McMurdo Couldn't
  Calibrate Itself," "The Rivalry That Still Helped When It Mattered")*
  **This directly complicates, rather than confirms, Fort McMurdo's own forward-flagged hypothesis.**
  Fort McMurdo's file proposed the relationship functions as "an inter-city extension of [its own] crew-
  interdependence ethic, just scaled up... from individual crewmates to an entire neighboring city." Scott's
  own material shows this doesn't hold cleanly: the relationship runs on two logics Fort McMurdo's own
  internal, origin-blind, symmetric shift-floor culture has no equivalent of. First, it's asymmetric specialist
  complementarity — Scott supplies precision calibration expertise Fort McMurdo's own heavy-industry staff
  structurally lack, not interchangeable crew output either city could equally provide. Second, and more
  significantly, it carries a real, ongoing, *managed but not eliminated* status and identity friction — Scott's
  own genuine resentment at being read as "little sibling," and Fort McMurdo's own unremarked condescension —
  that simply doesn't exist inside Fort McMurdo's internal culture, where §16 establishes genuine, frictionless
  origin-blindness with no status hierarchy at all. The relationship is real, strong, and functionally close,
