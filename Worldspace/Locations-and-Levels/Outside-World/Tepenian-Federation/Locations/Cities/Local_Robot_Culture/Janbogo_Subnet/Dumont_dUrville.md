## PART I — Local Aspects

### 1. Kinship, Clan & Mentor/Mentee Structures — Local Expression

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** Leading with the actual shared *life* experience, not a single incident:
  the naturalist-observation practice at the Observation Blinds is already established as genuinely organic —
  it began as a small group monitoring one colony for practical reasons, was joined informally by others, and
  shifted from monitoring into "genuinely restorative" shared practice that "no one formally decided." That
  ongoing, unstructured, still-continuing practice — extended to visitors and newcomers as a real invitation,
  not a closed circle — is Dumont d'Urville's real kinship floor, holding between two robots who've never
  watched the colony together on the same day, as long as both actually live that quiet, recurring practice.
  Directly reinforced by the deeper civic condition underneath it: the colony's own boundary terms get
  renegotiated every breeding season, never settled once and forgotten — everyone who lives here participates
  in that renewal, whether they think of it as naturalist observation or not.
- **[Adapted, Deep]** One specific shape that shared life takes: given robots handle a disproportionate share
  of the most exposed outdoor work (§16), wind-engineering craft plausibly carries its own teaching-lineage
  among robot specialists — a professional shape of the broader shared negotiation-with-the-environment life
  every resident lives, not a separate or competing mechanism.

### 2. Cooperation, Morality & Emergent Social Convention — Local Expression

- **[Adapted, Deep]** §6a's "you don't fight the wind or the penguins; you plan around both," combined with
  newcomers who try to "tough it out" being "gently, repeatedly corrected until they adapt or leave," already
  establishes a real, specific civic ethic: patient correction over harsh judgment, accommodation treated as
  competence rather than weakness. The deeper logic underneath it, per §1's own renegotiation-every-season
  principle, is that no adaptation here is ever treated as permanently solved — a robot's own actual standing
  plausibly depends on staying genuinely responsive to changing conditions, not on having once proven herself
  capable and resting on it.

### 3. Locality as Identity — What Makes This City's Robots Recognizable

- **[Emergent, robot-only, Deep]** A Dumont d'Urville robot is recognizable by a settled, negotiation-minded
  calm — genuinely distinct from Denison's own pride-in-danger register and from Mirny's grim stoic endurance,
  both explicitly differentiated in-canon from Dumont d'Urville's own "Negotiated Ground" identity (§5). Where
  a Denison robot might recite the city's own extremity with visible pride, a Dumont d'Urville robot's version
  of the same underlying confidence reads as simple, practiced unbotheredness — per §14's own first-impression
  material, visitors notice "how unbothered residents are" by both the wind and the penguins, well before they
  notice anything else distinctive about the population.

### 4. Generational Identity (Gen/Mark) — Local Flavor

- **[Adapted, Surface]** A modest but genuinely locally-grounded finding: per Robot Universals' own note that
  build statistically reflects local economic character (hard physical labor toward thicker builds, agility-
  demanding work toward slender ones), Dumont d'Urville's own Division of Industry — marine/resource
  extraction and precision wind-engineering each at roughly a quarter of the economy — plausibly produces a
  real, visible mix of both build types side by side, rather than one dominant local physical type the way a
  single-industry city might show.

### 5. Religion & Belief — Robot-Specific Local Practice

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** §18's coexistence-not-dominance ethic — "the wind and the penguins as
  evidence that coexistence rather than dominance is the only workable relationship with a hostile
  environment" — is already established and specific, confirmed rather than invented. See Cross-Reference
  Synthesis for the genuinely new robot-specific extension of St. Jules Day's own outdoor-reading tradition.
- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Solar symbol Mercury + Robo-Element Fire is already on file for Dumont
  d'Urville, grounded directly in "small but intensely alive." Fire's positive valence (passion, vitality, the
  courage to be seen) tracks the downtown eateries-with-stages culture's own festive, Positive-Outlook civic
  register with real precision; Mercury's smallness-paired-with-intensity reading matches the city's own
  self-understanding as a small, easily-overlooked place that is nonetheless genuinely, vividly alive.

### 6. Consumption & Vice Culture — Local Siligel/Coolant/Glitch-Coolant Flavor

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** §10c's existing material stands: siligel production shaped around the
  wind's effect on outdoor equipment and material degradation, genuinely thinner than Denison's own more
  developed structural-risk framing but real and specific.
- **[Adapted, Surface]** Dumont d'Urville isn't named in either existing Glitch-Coolant category. Given the
  downtown eateries-with-stages culture's own established character — small, packed, genre-diverse, informal
  venues, "New Orleans at 1/20th scale" — Dumont d'Urville plausibly belongs in the **bohemian/cosmopolitan-
  variety** category, alongside Janbogo, Zhongshan, and Shirayuki, but reaching that variety through its own
  specific mechanism: an intimate, small-venue live-music-and-drink culture rather than Janbogo's hospitality
  economy, Zhongshan's connoisseurship, or Shirayuki's artistic subculture.

### 7. Sexuality & Courtship — Local Customs

- **[Adapted, Deep]** Distinct from every prior city's courtship finding, directly grounded in an institution
  no other Tepenian city has: given the Naturalist Observation Blinds already function as a genuine, standing
  invitation extended to newcomers (§17, Community Infrastructure), a plausible local courtship signal is
  inviting a specific person to a shared observation session — the city's own closest thing to a "date"
  custom, carrying real weight precisely because the practice is already established as contemplative and
  restorative rather than merely social.

### 8. Vernacular Language — Robot-Specific Speech Markers

- **[Adapted, Surface]** §8 already establishes a compact, French-rooted wind vocabulary — "a shorthand...
  that outsiders find bewilderingly precise" — genuinely distinct in source and civic function from Denison's
  own severity-as-general-intensifier slang, since Dumont d'Urville's version is tied specifically to the
  founding language's continued functional (not ceremonial) use, per the already-established finding that
  French persists here because it's actually spoken daily, not preserved as heritage. Robot angle: given
  robots' own concentration in exposed outdoor wind-work (§16), robot specialists are the plausible primary
  users and precision-keepers of this vocabulary, the same structural role other cities' trade specialists
  play for their own local registers.

### 9. Arcanet Etiquette — Local Network Behavior

- **[Directly-inherited, Deep]** Already confirmed directly in §24's own text: thin and practical, weather and
  ice-condition updates dominating local traffic, "little bandwidth left over for social or archival use." See
  Cross-Reference Synthesis for the genuinely new material this pass adds regarding a real, recurring
  subnet-wide pattern this city's own material states about itself directly.

### 10. Surface Culture — Robot-Specific Markers

- **[Directly-inherited, Surface]** The naturalist documentary visual-art tradition (§13, "more wildlife-
  focused visual art, relative to its size, than any other Tepenian city") is already established. Robot
  angle: given robots' concentration in naturalist observation and precise record-keeping, robot naturalists
  plausibly function as primary documentarians and illustrators — a nature-focused counterpart to Cape Adare's
  own heritage-archivist specialization, distinct in subject even though structurally similar in role.
- **[Emergent, robot-only, Surface]** Distinct from Denison's own robot-fashion finding (heavy gear worn as a
  deliberate identity statement despite not needing it): since robots face genuinely lower personal risk from
  the wind than humans do, Dumont d'Urville's robots plausibly lean the *opposite* direction — freed rather
  than burdened, favoring lighter, more expressive presentation that fits the downtown's own festive, "New
  Orleans at 1/20th scale" register, since they don't need to dress for survival the way human residents do.
