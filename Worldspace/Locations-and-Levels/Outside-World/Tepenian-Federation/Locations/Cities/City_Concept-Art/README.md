# Concept Art for Tepenian Cities

**What this is:** whole-city and general-neighborhood concept art — establishing shots, skyline/silhouette studies, district-level layout sketches, anything depicting a city (or a broad piece of one) as a place rather than a specific quest scene or asset. Organized by subnet, one subfolder per subnet plus a dedicated `Amundsen_Station/` folder, each subnet folder holding one subfolder per city. Expected source: primarily Grok-generated images, dropped in as they're produced.

**How this differs from the other concept-art location:** each city's own `City_Megasheets/[Subnet]/[City]/Questline_Asset_Concept_Art/` folder is for narrower, more specific material tied to that city's own Megasheet and eventual quest design — individual buildings, quest-specific scenes, items, characters encountered there. This folder is the wide shot; that one is the close-up.

**Organization:** drop images into the matching city subfolder (e.g. `Palmer_Subnet/Rothera/`, `Mawson_Subnet/Dome_Fuji/`) or `Amundsen_Station/` directly (a single city, no subnet). Every one of Tepenia's 35 cities already has its own subfolder, scaffolded ahead of any actual art existing for most of them — a `.gitkeep` marks the still-empty ones.

**Concordia is the one exception to the subnet structure:** it gets its own top-level `Concordia/` folder, one subfolder per district — the 12 zodiac districts (`Aries/`, `Taurus/`, `Gemini/`, `Cancer/`, `Leo/`, `Virgo/`, `Libra/`, `Scorpio/`, `Sagittarius/`, `Capricorn/`, `Aquarius/`, `Pisces/`) plus the neutral `Central_Hub_Axis_Mundi/` — rather than a single Concordia-wide folder, since Concordia's own Megasheet plan treats each district as its own full unit (see `City_Megasheets/Megasheet_Progress_Checklist.md`).
