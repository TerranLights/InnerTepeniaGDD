# Concept Art for Tepenian Cities

**What this is:** whole-city and general-neighborhood concept art — establishing shots, skyline/silhouette studies, district-level layout sketches, anything depicting a city (or a broad piece of one) as a place rather than a specific quest scene or asset. Organized by subnet, one subfolder per subnet plus a dedicated `Amundsen_Station/` folder. Expected source: primarily Grok-generated images, dropped in as they're produced.

**How this differs from the other concept-art location:** each city's own `City_Megasheets/[Subnet]/[City]/Questline_Asset_Concept_Art/` folder is for narrower, more specific material tied to that city's own Megasheet and eventual quest design — individual buildings, quest-specific scenes, items, characters encountered there. This folder is the wide shot; that one is the close-up.

**Organization:** drop images into the matching subnet folder (`Halley_Subnet/`, `Palmer_Subnet/`, etc.) or `Amundsen_Station/`. No per-city subfolders yet — add them if a subnet folder starts accumulating enough images that per-city sorting becomes useful.
