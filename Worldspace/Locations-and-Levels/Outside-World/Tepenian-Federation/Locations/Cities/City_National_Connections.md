# City-to-City National Connections (Post-Tower)

**What this is:** for every one of Tepenia's 35 outer cities, a reasoned list of the other cities it would
plausibly have a strong or medium connection to — industrial, economic, cultural, social, religious,
scientific, infrastructural, or historical — once the Amundsen Tower's completion connected all six subnets
through a single relay for the first time. **Time frame: the living Second Interwar Period after the Tower's
completion, before the Long Night War and the Planetary Split Brain** — the same setting this project's
Historical Vignettes batch 2 pass (`Historical_Vignettes_Progress_Tracker.md`) was written in. Destroyed-in-
the-war cities (Zukelli, Princess Elisabeth, Denison) are included as they stood during this living period, not
as their later ruined state.

**Relationship to `City_Cross_Subnet_Relationships.md`:** that file is the physical-infrastructure and
already-catalogued-connections foundation this one builds on and extends — its highway/aviation network
findings and Part 2/3/4 threads are folded in below rather than repeated in full. This file goes further in two
ways: it covers *every* city (that file left 15 of 35 without any cross-subnet connection identified), and it
reasons from each city's own established personality, economy, and culture — not just physical infrastructure
and shared founding nations — to what full national digital connectivity would plausibly produce.

**Method:** each city's own Full Extrapolation, Physical Infrastructure Attributes, Local Cultures, and Specs
material (read across this project's own Historical Vignettes and Megasheet work) supplied the economic
niche, civic personality, and any already-established connections used below. Connections are listed once per
city but are generally reciprocal — check the target city's own entry for its side of the same relationship.
**Strength key:** **Strong** = load-bearing, actively maintained; **Medium** = real but secondary, occasional,
or one-directional.

## Halley Subnet

### Halley (the city)
- **Neumayer** (Halley) — Strong, Infrastructure/Engineering. The subnet's only other ice-shelf city; both share
  the same structural-relocation engineering problem no bedrock-founded city faces, and both sit on the same
  Hwy 7 corridor.
- **Sanay** (Halley) — Strong, Infrastructure/Political. Halley's own residents personally built and staff the
  Halley subnet's actual Arcanet nexus at Sanay; namesake credit and physical infrastructure sit in two
  different cities by design.
- **Amundsen Station** — Strong, Infrastructure. Hwy 59 ("the Atlantic Throughway / Arcanet Line") runs directly
  from Halley to the Pole, carrying both highway traffic and the physical Arcanet trunk cable.
- **Belgrano / Troll** (Halley) — Medium, Logistics. Downstream of Halley's own no-dock/no-airstrip design;
  Halley's resupply depends entirely on whichever of Belgrano's or Sanay's passage is open, trucked in via Hwy 7.

### Neumayer
- **Amundsen Station** — Strong, Engineering/Historical. Neumayer's own precision-instrument infrastructure
  drafted the Amundsen Tower's schematics and structural design — the Tower itself was built at the Pole, but
  designed here.
- **Sinheung / Byrd** (Mirny / Byrd) — Strong, Industrial/Technical. Neumayer designed the current-generation
  (Mark IV) fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic that Sinheung's and Byrd's chamber-manufacturing floors
  actually build from — a real, load-bearing technical dependency neither manufacturing city gets public credit
  for crediting back to its actual source.
- **Mawson** (Mawson) — Medium, Technical/Cultural. Neumayer drafted the spec and schematic for Calethina,
  commissioned out of Mawson as a hospitality-host hologram — an uncredited engineering export, the same
  quiet pattern as the Tower and the Cradle chamber.
- **Halley** (Halley) — Strong, see Halley's entry above.
- **Davis** (Mirny) — Medium, Scientific. The two most genuinely comparable "hard science" civic identities in
  the country — long-term, multi-generational atmospheric/glaciological (Neumayer) and paleoclimate sediment-
  core (Davis) research — a natural professional-correspondence connection once full connectivity exists.

### Belgrano
- **Byrd** (Byrd) — Strong, Historical/Infrastructure. Maps recovered from Belgrano's own archives contributed
  directly to the expedition that located and founded Byrd — Tepenia's founding overland-exploration story.
- **Esperanza / Marambio** (Palmer) — Medium, Cultural/Demographic. Argentina's only three founding-population
  cities in the country, split across two subnets — a real, if thin, cross-subnet Argentine cultural thread.
- **Sanay / Troll** (Halley) — Strong, Logistics. The three-city Halley-subnet supply chain: South African
  freighters land at Belgrano or Sanay, goods truck overland via Hwy 7, and Troll flies the onward legs out.
- **Sinheung** (Mirny) — Medium, Economic/Gameplay. A specific aircraft part recoverable from Belgrano's own
  ruins-era garage bypasses a stat-gate on Sinheung's own plane-repair sidequest — a concrete, if narrow,
  cross-subnet material thread.

### Troll
- **Sanay / Belgrano** (Halley) — Strong, Logistics. Receives what Sanay and Belgrano bring in overland via
  Hwy 7 and flies it onward — the functional center of a major share of Tepenia's actual domestic import
  distribution.
- **Dome Fuji** (Mawson) — Strong, Infrastructure/Economic. One of Dome Fuji's three confirmed aviation supply
  routes, direct by air — genuine cross-subnet logistics lifeline to one of the country's most isolated cities.
- **Sinheung** (Mirny) — Strong, Infrastructure. The second of the two independent aviation routes (alongside
  Troll's own) that kept Dome Fuji supplied for a substantial stretch of Tepenian history — a real three-city
  logistics triangle (Troll–Sinheung–Dome Fuji).
- **Fort McMurdo** (Janbogo) — Medium, Cultural/Economic. Tepenia's two clearest "working-class logistics
  muscle" cities — Troll's freight hub and Fort McMurdo's industrial capital — share a comparable civic
  self-image even without direct physical infrastructure linking them.

### Abowasa
- **Sejong** (Palmer) — Medium, Cultural. Both cities solved a genuine multinational-coexistence problem at
  founding (Finnish-Swedish at Abowasa, a dozen communities at Sejong) — a natural point of practical
  governance comparison once full connectivity exists, though at very different scales.
- **Sanay / Troll / Belgrano** (Halley) — Medium, Infrastructure. Downstream of the same Hwy 7 supply corridor;
  Abowasa's own small-scale internal Halley subnet trade runs through this chain.
- **Mirny** (Mirny) — Medium, Cultural. Mirny's own Dual-Kitchen Halls institutionalized Russian-Chinese
  coexistence at civic scale the same deliberate way Abowasa's founding generation institutionalized
  Finnish-Swedish coexistence — a resonant, if never directly exchanged, parallel.

### Sanay
- **Halley** (Halley) — Strong, see Halley's entry above.
- **Belgrano / Troll** (Halley) — Strong, see Belgrano's/Troll's entries above.
- **Amundsen Station** — Medium, Infrastructure. Home to the Halley subnet's own actual Arcanet nexus, making
  Sanay (not Halley) the subnet's real technical peer to the Pole's own relay hub.

### Princess Elisabeth
- **Sayowa** (Mawson) — Strong, Social/Cultural. Tepenia's single most geographically adjacent inter-subnet
  proximity; a sustained, generations-deep Japanese-heritage family correspondence connects the two cities,
  predating and outlasting the formal Hwy 7-ext highway link.
- **Dome Fuji** (Mawson) — Medium, Geographic/Cultural. The closest Halley subnet city to Dome Fuji by
  longitude, though the exact highway link is unconfirmed; a plausible minor pilgrimage-adjacent connection
  given the proximity.
- **Shirayuki / Sinheung** (Mirny) — Medium, Demographic. Princess Elisabeth is the only Halley subnet city
  with genuine Japanese demographic presence (co-Primary with the USA); a real, if never dramatized,
  demographic kinship with Tepenia's other Japanese-heritage cities once full connectivity exists.
- **Signy** (Palmer) — Medium, Technical. Both cities built genuine civic identity around engineered,
  actively-maintained self-sufficient power (zero-emissions systems at Princess Elisabeth, wind-and-hydro at
  Signy) — a natural engineering-correspondence pairing.

### Lazar
- **Fort McMurdo** (Janbogo) — Medium, Political/Economic. Tepenia's largest city by population and its
  historical/political capital — the country's two biggest civic hubs, a natural point of comparison and trade
  even without dedicated infrastructure.
- **Palmer City** (Palmer) — Medium, Cultural. The two cities that read as genuine big-city megacenters —
  Lazar's maglev/holographic commercial density, Palmer City's entertainment-and-casino economy — share a
  scale and civic energy no other Tepenian city matches.
- **Sejong / Abowasa** — Medium, Cultural. Lazar's own coalescence from two separate founding settlements
  (Novolazarevskaya and the unoccupied Maitri site) parallels the multinational-coexistence-by-necessity
  pattern at Sejong and Abowasa, though Lazar's merger was demographic rather than a live negotiation.
- **Neumayer** (Halley) — Medium, Historical. The nearest confirmed Halley subnet neighbor; Novolazarevskaya's
  own real-world DROMLAN air-logistics-hub heritage plausibly gave founding-era Lazar and Neumayer a working
  logistics relationship neither city's own file has fully developed yet.

## Palmer Subnet

### Palmer City
- **Sejong / Juan Carlos** (Palmer) — Strong, Political/Economic. The Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority
  and Machu Picchu Airport, near Sejong, are Palmer City's own confirmed Upper Earth tourism gateway — the
  same physical corridor Juan Carlos's own immigration processing routes non-immigrant visitors through toward
  Palmer City.
- **Mawson** (Mawson) — Medium, Economic/Cultural. Tepenia's two deliberately-cultivated hospitality economies
  — entertainment/casino tourism at Palmer City, honeymoon-destination tourism at Mawson — a natural
  cross-subnet hospitality-industry correspondence.
- **Byrd** (Byrd) — Strong, Infrastructure. Palmer City's own over-cap population redistribution supplied a
  large share of Byrd's founding population; Byrd's only overland highway connection (Hwy 1 → Hwy 22) also
  runs through the Palmer subnet corridor Palmer City anchors.
- **Lazar** (Halley) — Medium, see Lazar's entry above.

### Esperanza
- **Marambio** (Palmer) — Strong, Economic. An established shipping division of labor: Marambio handles bulk,
  high-volume cargo; Esperanza's Hope Bay Harbor handles smaller-scale, higher-value shipments — including
  once serving as Marambio's own overflow receiving point during a dockside failure.
- **Belgrano** (Halley) — Medium, see Belgrano's entry above.
- **Janbogo** (Janbogo) — Medium, Demographic. Esperanza's own population was reduced (to 85% of its original
  total) in the same redistribution that enlarged Lazar, alongside Janbogo and Zukelli — three cities whose
  Census figures are now historically linked by that single administrative event.

### Rothera
- **Mirny** (Mirny) — Strong, Industrial. Rothera plays, for the Palmer subnet, almost exactly the role Mirny
  plays for the Mirny subnet — quarrying and manufacturing the vehicles and materials that build and maintain
  its own subnet's highway network. The two cities are structural twins.
- **Byrd** (Byrd) — Medium, Industrial. Rothera's own decentralized industrial model, and its underground vault
  sections, plausibly influenced Byrd's own early industrial planning — two of Tepenia's most industrially
  significant cities sharing a documented structural resemblance.
- **Denison** (Janbogo) — Medium, Engineering. Rothera's decentralized, wind-load-distributed industrial layout
  and Denison's fully-interlinked, wind-fused architecture solved the same underlying katabatic-wind
  engineering problem from two different structural philosophies — a natural cross-subnet engineering
  correspondence.

### Marambio
- **Esperanza** (Palmer) — Strong, see Esperanza's entry above.
- **Belgrano** (Halley) — Medium, Demographic/Aviation. Shares Argentine Air Force founding heritage with
  Belgrano; both cities are their own subnet's primary aviation hub, a natural professional correspondence
  between the two.
- **Byrd** (Byrd) — Medium, Infrastructure. A secondary/backup logistics link into the Palmer-Byrd-Mirny
  overland corridor alongside Hwy 1.

### Port Lockroy
- **Juan Carlos** (Palmer) — Medium, Cultural. Two cities whose entire civic identity is about moving
  information rather than goods — Port Lockroy's postal-correspondence network, Juan Carlos's own "Federation's
  memory" archive-keeping — a natural, if quiet, professional kinship.
- **Zhongshan** (Mirny) — Medium, Cultural. The Long Record (Zhongshan's own archive, the most complete
  pre-Split-Brain history in the Mirny subnet) and Port Lockroy's own accounted-for-history civic instinct
  ("account for the past rather than paper over it") share the same underlying value, worth a genuine
  correspondence once national connectivity exists.
- **Cape Adare** (Janbogo) — Medium, Cultural. Both cities built their whole civic identity around
  preservation-as-practice — Port Lockroy's Communal Stone Wall, Cape Adare's heritage-documentation archive —
  a natural professional exchange between the two.

### Sejong
- **Palmer City / Juan Carlos** (Palmer) — Strong, see Palmer City's entry above.
- **Janbogo / Sinheung** (Janbogo / Mirny) — Medium, Demographic. One of Tepenia's three Korean-founded cities
  — genuine but limited, ceremonial kinship rather than close operational ties, per the established "kin, not
  siblings" framing.
- **Abowasa** (Halley) — Medium, see Abowasa's entry above.

### Signy
- **Kunlun / Vostok** (Mirny) — Medium, Technical. Signy's own genuine self-sufficient wind-and-hydro power
  system drew direct engineering consultation from Tower-era energy-regulation planners; the same reliability-
  over-peak-output discipline is exactly what Kunlun's and Vostok's own extreme-condition engineering culture
  would recognize immediately.
- **Princess Elisabeth** (Halley) — Medium, see Princess Elisabeth's entry above.
- **Rothera** (Palmer) — Medium, Infrastructure. The only two Palmer subnet cities that survived the Long Night
  War intact/functional at meaningfully different scale than their neighbors — a natural point of comparison.

### Juan Carlos
- **Amundsen Station** — Strong, Historical. Juan Carlos's own formal Federation archive gradually relocated
  into Amundsen Station's unified national archive across the Second Interwar Period — the single most direct
  institutional link between any outer city and the Pole's own archive function.
- **Palmer City / Sejong** (Palmer) — Strong, see Palmer City's entry above.
- **Zhongshan** (Mirny) — Medium, Cultural. Two cities whose civic self-image is built entirely around being
  quiet custodians of history — the Long Record and the "Federation's memory" nickname are close enough in
  spirit to be genuine professional peers.
- **Port Lockroy** (Palmer) — Medium, see Port Lockroy's entry above.
