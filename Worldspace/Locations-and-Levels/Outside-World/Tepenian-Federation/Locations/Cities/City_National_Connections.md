# City-to-City National Connections (Post-Tower)

**What this is:** for every one of Tepenia's 35 outer cities, a reasoned list of the other cities it would
plausibly have a strong or medium connection to — industrial, economic, cultural, social, religious,
scientific, infrastructural, or historical — once the Amundsen Tower's completion connected all six subnets
through a single relay for the first time. **Time frame: the living Second Interwar Period after the Tower's
completion, before the Long Night War and the Planetary Split Brain** — the same setting this project's
Historical Vignettes batch 2 pass (`Historical_Vignettes_Progress_Tracker.md`) was written in. Destroyed-in-
the-war cities (Zukelli, Princess Elisabeth, Denison) are included as they stood during this living period, not
as their later ruined state.

**Relationship to `City_Cross_Subnet_Relationships.md`:** that file is the physical-infrastructure and
already-catalogued-connections foundation this one builds on and extends — its highway/aviation network
findings and Part 2/3/4 threads are folded in below rather than repeated in full. This file goes further in two
ways: it covers *every* city (that file left 15 of 35 without any cross-subnet connection identified), and it
reasons from each city's own established personality, economy, and culture — not just physical infrastructure
— to what full national digital connectivity would plausibly produce.

**Method:** each city's own Full Extrapolation, Physical Infrastructure Attributes, Local Cultures, and Specs
material (read across this project's own Historical Vignettes and Megasheet work) supplied the economic
niche, civic personality, and any already-established connections used below. **Every connection is written
out in full at both cities' own entries** — no "see X's entry above" shorthand — so each city's own section is
complete and self-contained on its own. **Strength key:** **Strong** = load-bearing, actively maintained;
**Medium** = real but secondary, occasional, or one-directional.

## Halley Subnet

### Halley (the city)
- **Neumayer** (Halley) — Strong, Infrastructure/Engineering. The subnet's only other ice-shelf city; both
  share the same structural-relocation engineering problem no bedrock-founded city faces, and both sit on the
  same Hwy 7 corridor.
- **Sanay** (Halley) — Strong, Infrastructure/Political. Halley's own residents personally built and staff the
  Halley subnet's actual Arcanet nexus at Sanay; namesake credit and physical infrastructure sit in two
  different cities by design.
- **Amundsen Station** — Strong, Infrastructure. Hwy 59 ("the Atlantic Throughway / Arcanet Line") runs
  directly from Halley to the Pole, carrying both highway traffic and the physical Arcanet trunk cable.
- **Belgrano** (Halley) — Medium, Logistics. Downstream of Halley's own no-dock/no-airstrip design; Halley's
  resupply depends entirely on whichever of Belgrano's or Sanay's passage is open, trucked in via Hwy 7.
- **Troll** (Halley) — Medium, Logistics. The same overland resupply chain — goods reaching Halley via Belgrano
  or Sanay were often bound onward through Troll's own aviation distribution first.

### Neumayer
- **Amundsen Station** — Strong, Engineering/Historical. Neumayer's own precision-instrument infrastructure
  drafted the Amundsen Tower's schematics and structural design — the Tower itself was built at the Pole, but
  designed here.
- **Sinheung** (Mirny) — Strong, Industrial/Technical. Neumayer designed the current-generation (Mark IV)
  fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic that Sinheung's own chamber-manufacturing floor actually builds
  from — a real, load-bearing technical dependency the manufacturing city gets no public credit for tracing
  back to its actual source.
- **Byrd** (Byrd) — Strong, Industrial/Technical. The same Mark IV chamber schematic Neumayer designed is also
  what Byrd's own Chamber Works builds from — the second of the Cradle's two active manufacturing sites quietly
  dependent on Neumayer's own uncredited engineering.
- **Mawson** (Mawson) — Medium, Technical/Cultural. Neumayer drafted the spec and schematic for Calethina,
  commissioned out of Mawson as a hospitality-host hologram — an uncredited engineering export, the same quiet
  pattern as the Tower and the Cradle chamber.
- **Halley** (Halley) — Strong, Infrastructure/Engineering. See Halley's own entry above.
- **Davis** (Mirny) — Medium, Scientific. The two most genuinely comparable "hard science" civic identities in
  the country — long-term, multi-generational atmospheric/glaciological research at Neumayer, paleoclimate
  sediment-core research at Davis — a natural professional correspondence once full connectivity exists.
- **Lazar** (Halley) — Medium, Historical. The nearest confirmed Halley subnet neighbor; Novolazarevskaya's own
  real-world DROMLAN air-logistics-hub heritage plausibly gave founding-era Lazar and Neumayer a working
  logistics relationship neither city's own file has fully developed yet.

### Belgrano

  aviation distribution — a core link in the three-city Halley-subnet supply chain.
- **Sinheung** (Mirny) — Medium, Economic/Gameplay. A specific aircraft part recoverable from Belgrano's own
  ruins-era garage bypasses a stat-gate on Sinheung's own plane-repair sidequest — a concrete, if narrow,

  downstream via the Hwy 7 connector.

  correspondence.
