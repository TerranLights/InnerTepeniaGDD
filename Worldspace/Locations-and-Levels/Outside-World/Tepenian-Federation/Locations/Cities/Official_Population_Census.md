## Section A — National Origin Composition by City

National communities are classified by tier based on long-run population share. Where a founding wave nation set the city's early cultural character prior to the arrival of larger national communities, this is noted.

**Tier definitions:**
- **Primary** — Dominant national community; shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity
- **Significant** — Distinct, visible communities with meaningful presence in city culture
- **Notable** — Present and recognizable; background cultural texture

Destroyed during the Long Night War. At time of destruction, Zukelli's combined population was approximately **1,302,000** — making it the second-largest city in the Federation by Orbital Era figures. Human retention rates immediately before the war were 70.63%; robot retention was 78.03%. Zukelli was not a depleted or declining city. It was near its peak population when it was destroyed. *(Dated 2026-10-31: the two retention percentages above were updated to the live rows (L491, L613).
