**Passenger processing (both):** at all real gateways (Hobart, Christchurch, Punta Arenas) border exit, check-in, cold-weather clothing and most biosecurity happen in the gateway city; medical clearance is done before travel; no source mentions a border presence at an Antarctic airfield. Wilkins has transit rooms and a heated emergency shelter for 40 or more people. Australian Antarctic Territory travel counts as an international movement under Australia's Migration Act.

**Alternates:** Australia's rules require two hours of holding fuel and a critical point past which a flight may not continue unless the forecast supports landing. No scheduled Antarctic service has used a second continental airfield as an alternate; Australia once considered an alternate at the Bunger Hills (about 250 nm from Wilkins) and chose round-trip fuel instead.
