# Input drift review: Zhongshan (Opus pass and Sonnet partial trial), 2026-11-03

**What this is.** A read-only review. It asks one question: has any data the Zhongshan pass absorbed changed since the pass ran, so that its results might come out differently if re-derived? Nothing was re-derived and no pass file, spec, census or source was edited. This file is the only thing written.

**Baselines used.** Pass time = commit `623049f3` (2026-09-13; the passes ran 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-11). "10-30" = commit `c3e31166` (the evening the manual input audit amendment notes were filed). "Now" = HEAD (`54dda306`, 2026-11-03).

**One caution about the baseline.** The pass files themselves were edited after `623049f3`: on 2026-09-23 (Metal's meaning re-grounded), 2026-10-01 (audit fixes: "habitation and administration" struck, the "64.17% had no standing" figure corrected to 47.17%, Founding Register cited, "Background-Lore" demoted to "never an input"), 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-30 (Pre-Trip notes, amendment notes), and 2026-11-02 (line-citation remaps and the Robot Universals address sweep only). So the pass text already absorbed those. This review looks only at input changes that the pass text does not yet reflect.

**Labels.** `Opus` = `Zhongshan_Opus/`, `Sonnet` = `Zhongshan_Sonnet/`. The Sonnet trial is a near-copy of the Opus files for Frame, Inherited, Spine (except the Saturn+Metal passage), Phase 2 (except one 3-line note), Phase 3, Step -1 contract and Research (all differ by 4 to 12 lines), so a finding on one of those files applies to both folders at the same text, with line numbers within 3 of each other. Where a row says "Opus+Sonnet" that is what it means.

## 1 Summary

1. Zhongshan's inputs have moved in four big ways since the pass: the city's headcount rose twice (the 2026-10-28 Santa Luce redistribution, then the 2026-11-02 Esperanza relocation); its composition table was rebuilt (18 nations became 35 rows; Russia, Germany, Thailand, Indonesia and Romania were split into successor rows; tiers were relabeled so Russia and Australia dropped out of Significant); Division of Industry file 16 was recounted with the robot-keyed term on 2026-11-03; and a language rule now says Hawaiian is dominant everywhere with Mandarin alone as Zhongshan's supporting tier.
2. The headline shares survive: the Sinian Federation is still 35.83% (Zhongshan was exempt from the Sinian decline), Korea 9.70%, Japan 7.30%. "Plurality, not a majority", the 3.69 times gap to second place, and the robot-majority to human-majority crossing between censuses all still hold.
3. Counts: 26 dependent items. 8 number or label only (N), 6 wording now false as written (W), 7 conclusions that might change on re-derivation (C), 5 cannot tell without re-running (U).
4. Most exposed: Phase 8 (Making), section 8.1 (the composition merge), which reasons from "five Significant entries" and "a twelve-entry tail inside 2.36 points"; the Phase 8 closing axis "The commons had no homeland" and QA Gate 4, which justify it by that roster shape; the Phase 8 language section 8.8, which predates the language rule; and the Phase 2 and Phase 9 claim that the inversion is "in the retention rates themselves", because two arithmetic scenario steps have since moved those rates.
5. Least exposed: Phases 3, 5, 6, 10 and Steps 5 and 10 depend on none of the changed inputs except through quoted numbers.
6. The 2026-10-30 amendment notes are stale again on nearly every "live" figure (section 5). Two further problems in the notes: the Sonnet note says the Sonnet trial has no division-of-industry figures, but its `02_Spine.md` L56 to L58 carries the 41.2 / 15.4 / 43.4 shares; and the "353 to 504 km2" figure was built from the pre-cut three-city sum, not the post-cut sum the Pre-Trip note says.
7. Recommendation (section 6): do not re-run the whole pass. Re-derive Phase 8 section 8.1, 8.8 and the closing axis (plus QA Gate 4 and the Step 6 composition cell), re-check the Phase 2 and Phase 9 inversion reasoning, and put a dated amendment note on everything else.
