# Zhongshan — Web Research Log

**One dedicated research record per location. Standing convention, established 2026-08-30 at the developer's
direction.** See `README.md` in this folder for the convention itself.

**What this file is for:** an exact record of **what was searched, what came back, which finding each fact
became, and what was deliberately left unexplored** — so that any future session can pick up the thread,
re-check a claim against its actual source, or go deeper without starting over.

**Read this alongside** `Universal_Location_Methodology/Test_Runs/2026-08-30_Zhongshan_Run3_Cold/`, which holds
the pass the research fed.

## Session 1 — 2026-08-30, Run 3 (cold single-location pass)

**Researcher context:** Universal Location Methodology, Step 3. **Research was run *after* the capability
profile named the deficit, never before it** (`00_RUNBOOK.md` Step 3 / Gate G). **The deficit being researched
against was:** *Zhongshan has no forcing function, and therefore no signal that a practice has quietly lapsed.*

### Query 1 — the physical site

> **Search string:** `Larsemann Hills Antarctica ice-free oasis lakes geology Zhongshan Station Progress Station`

**Sources returned and used:**
- ScienceDirect — *Larsemann Hills: Geomorphometric modeling and mapping* (S1873965223000671)
- The Cryosphere / Copernicus — *Retention time of lakes in the Larsemann Hills oasis, East Antarctica*
  (tc.copernicus.org/articles/15/2667/2021/)
- Antarctic Treaty ASMA 6 management plan (env.go.jp — `ASMA06_en.pdf`)
- Australian Antarctic Program magazine, issue 26 (June 2014) — *Managing activities in the Larsemann Hills*
- Wikipedia — *Zhongshan Station (Antarctica)*, *Progress Station*
- arXiv 2305.07523 — *Geomorphometric modeling and mapping of Antarctic oases*

**Facts extracted:**

| Fact | Where it went |
|---|---|
| ~**40 km²** ice-free coastal oasis on Prydz Bay, Ingrid Christensen Coast, Princess Elizabeth Land | Frame; Phase 3 |
| **Two large peninsulas** (Stornes, Broknes), three small (Grovnes, Brattnevet, Stinear), **~130 islands** (large: Fisher, Manning, McLeod) | **Finding V** (archipelago texture) |
| ⭐ **~150 lakes, "but drainage network is not developed"** | ⭐ **THE key fact of the entire pass.** Spine corroboration; **Findings V, VII** |
| Lake chemistry driven by **sea spray, local geology, and periodic seawater surges from calving of the Dålk glacier** (eastern corner of the oasis) | **Findings V** (the city smells different at its two ends), **VII** (why the water must be checked individually), **VIII** |
| Basement geology: **orthogneisses over pegmatites and granites** | **Phase 7a** — the non-thematic stone export |
| **Three year-round stations share the one oasis** — Progress (RU), Zhongshan (CN), Bharati (IN) | Confirms the real-world basis of the Tri-Cities cluster |
| Real Zhongshan Station is **the staging post for China's inland Kunlun station** | ⭐ **Recorded as a DIVERGENCE** — Tepenia gives that role to Hwy 37 instead. See §3.4 of the pass |
