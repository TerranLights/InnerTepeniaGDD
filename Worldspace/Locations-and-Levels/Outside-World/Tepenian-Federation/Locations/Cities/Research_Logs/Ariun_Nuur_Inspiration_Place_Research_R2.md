### A. Other settings (sub-areas of daily life)

**Berkeley**
- **The research workforce next door (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, an IGI partner).** The Berkeley Lab Postdoc Association is "run by and for about 1000 postdocs and early career scientists" at the lab. Source: https://postdoc.lbl.gov/activities/, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26.
- **Student and early-career housing pressure.** UC Berkeley houses only about 22% of its 45,000+ students, "the lowest rate in the UC system." Source: KQED, 2024-06-06, https://www.kqed.org/news/11989237/uc-berkeley-can-start-building-on-peoples-park-california-supreme-court-rules, **SOURCED**. In the November 2022 University of California academic-worker strike (nearly 48,000 workers on 10 campuses, United Auto Workers), graduate students were said to average about $24,000 a year, with over a third spending more than that on rent alone; over 90% of graduate student employees spent more than 30% of income on housing; postdoc Neal Sweeney: "every month I really had to think about if I would make it through that month." Demands were $54,000 for graduate workers and $70,000 for postdocs. Source: KQED (Nov. 2022), https://www.kqed.org/news/11932746/why-uc-berkeley-academic-workers-are-striking, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26. A 97% strike-authorization vote of 36,558 ballots: **UNVERIFIED** (search snippet, SF Standard / Labor Notes).
- **People's Park (Southside).** A 1969 community space and protest site for over 50 years; UC Berkeley's 2021 Long Range Development Plan aims to add nearly 12,000 student beds; two neighborhood groups (Make UC a Good Neighbor; People's Park Historic District Advocacy Group) sued, arguing that noise from future residents was pollution under California's environmental law; the California Supreme Court ruled for the university on 2024-06-06. KQED 2024-06-06, **SOURCED**. Project details (about 1,111 student beds, a separate building of about 125 beds for formerly homeless people, $312 million; in January 2024 police in riot gear cleared activists and crews walled the site with double-stacked shipping containers): **UNVERIFIED** (snippets, PBS / ABC7).
- **Family and graduate housing in Albany (UC Village).** Began as **Codornices Village**, a federal wartime housing project announced 1943 for shipyard workers' families, "racially integrated" at a time of severe housing restrictions on African Americans; first residents April 1944; 1,896 temporary units on 120 acres. The university bought part of it around 1954 for $44,000 and ran it as student family housing. Today 974 units on 77 acres in Albany, for students with families and postdocs with dependents; children attend Albany Unified schools. Between 1997 and 2007 the cheapest two-bedroom rose 330%, from $410 to $1,360 a month; rent protests in 1981 and the mid-1990s. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Village, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26. A community garden of 170 plots on 6.5 acres with orchards, berry patches and a children's garden for the 3,000+ residents; a new Albany Village graduate complex of 761 single bedrooms at below-market rent (opening about fall 2024): **UNVERIFIED** (snippets, edibleschoolyard.org; news.berkeley.edu 2022-08-22).
- **Outdoor and recreation space in the hills.** Strawberry Canyon Recreation Area, Centennial Drive above Memorial Stadium: a seasonal pool (end of May through September; three 25-yard lap lanes, water 80-81°F), picnic and fitness areas; open to students, RecWell members, and day-pass buyers ($15 adults, $5 children). Source: https://recwell.berkeley.edu/facilities/strawberry-canyon-recreation-pool/, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26. ### B. Subcultures

- **Academic workers as an organized group.** At Berkeley, graduate students, postdocs and academic researchers are unionized under the United Auto Workers and struck system-wide in November 2022 over housing costs (above). At Duke, about 2,500 PhD students working as research or teaching assistants voted 1,000 to 131 (88%) on 2023-08-22 to form the Duke Graduate Students Union, affiliated with Southern Region Workers United (SEIU), described as the largest graduate union at a private Southern university; a first attempt in 2017 had failed; demands included cost-of-living stipend raises, vision and dental benefits, support for parents and help for international students with visas. Co-secretary Kerry Eller: "a historic victory ... for the labor movement in North Carolina." Source: WUNC 2023-08-22, https://www.wunc.org/news/2023-08-22/duke-university-graduate-students-win-union-election-in-a-landslide, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26. Aim: "to strengthen our ties to the Lab, to each other and to the (Berkeley) Bay Area." postdoc.lbl.gov, **SOURCED**. - **Cameron Crazies and Krzyzewskiville (Duke undergraduates).** A tent city on the lawn outside Cameron Indoor Stadium before the Duke-UNC men's basketball game. Started 1986 when senior Kimberly Reed and friends from the Mirecourt selective living group pitched four or five tents on Thursday for a Sunday game; 70-75 tents by tip-off, national news. Named for coach Mike Krzyzewski (1980-2022), who bought pizza for tenters. Three registration tiers: **black** tenting (10 people sleeping nightly, line position set by trivia and attendance events), **blue**, and **white** (the "Race to the Secret Spots"); occupancy falls by thirds of the season (2 by day and 10 by night, then 1 and 6, then 1 and 2); "grace" (permitted absence) when temperatures drop below 32°F, snow exceeds 2 inches or winds reach 35 mph. By 2017 over 150 groups sought the 70 black/blue spots. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krzyzewskiville, **SOURCED**, accessed 2026-10-26. Coach Ronald Ludington directed the training center and coached in nine consecutive Olympics and 36 World Championships; skaters who trained there include Kimmie Meissner (2006 World Champion), Tara Lipinski (1998 Olympic Champion) and Johnny Weir. ### A. Other settings (sub-areas of daily life)

**How visitors live at the platforms**
