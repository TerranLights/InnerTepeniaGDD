## Session 4 — 2026-09-26 — Developer ruling: domestic-need exclusions, and a new import direction

**Not new research — a direct developer ruling, recorded per this log's own "input data, not conclusions"
discipline, but a ruling still needs to be on record since it changes which candidates above are live.**

**1 — Two categories struck from Tepenia's *export* candidate list, domestic need:** Tepenia's own citizens
need iron ore and bulk building materials to build their own cities, and need synthesized/recovered phosphate
(Cape Adare guano, per [[project_upper_earth_trade_justification]]) for their own agriculture — so neither
can be something Tepenia sells to Australia. Applied above to the main candidate list (phosphate, steel/
aluminium) and flagged as an open question against Session 3's Dufek-Intrusion/Antarctandes metals list
(chromium, vanadium, nickel, copper specifically — platinum and gold are not implicated, since neither is a
bulk structural material).

**2 — This doesn't kill iron ore and building materials as a trade good — it flips their direction.**
Session 3 already established, as real-world fact, that Australia is a dominant global exporter of iron ore
(Pilbara/Hamersley) and coal. Combined with ruling 1 above, the coherent shape is: **Tepenia would plausibly
*import* iron ore and bulk building materials from Australia**, alongside food — solving Tepenia's own
domestic-construction need from precisely the supplier who already, in the real world, produces these goods
at export scale. This strengthens rather than weakens the reciprocal trade relationship: Australia's export
list to Tepenia grows (food + iron ore/building materials), which sharpens rather than removes the open
question of what Tepenia sends back the other way.

**Still open:** which of Session 3's metals candidates (chromium/vanadium/nickel/copper specifically) survive
the domestic-need filter, if any — not decided here, flagged for a later pass.

### Two standout findings

**1 — Morocco/phosphate is the strongest speed case, but creates a canon tension worth flagging, not
resolving.** Existing canon has phosphorus flowing the *other* direction: Upper Earth sells it TO Tepenia,
because Tepenia has no domestic source and phosphorus can't be synthesized
([[project_upper_earth_trade_justification]]). For Tepenia to instead supply Australia's phosphate need, it
would need its own source — and Cape Adare's guano is already a named Tepenian nutrient-recovery project in
that same canon. Bird guano is literally how the real-world 19th-century phosphate-fertilizer trade began
(the Peru guano trade) — a real, non-coincidental parallel. **Whether Tepenia's guano supply could plausibly
reach national-import scale is unverified and NOT decided here.**

**2 — Singapore/Malaysia are a distinct case, not a speed win.** They're not beatable on time, but they carry
