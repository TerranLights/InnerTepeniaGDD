### 1.1 Latest national census: ethnic composition

**Census:** Seventh National Population Census of the People's Republic of China, reference moment 00:00, 1 November 2020. **SOURCED** (NBS Communiqué No. 2, https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202105/t20210510_1817187.html).

**Headline (SOURCED, Communiqué No. 2, verbatim):** *"Of the national population, 1286311334 persons or 91.11 percent were of the Han ethnic group and 125467390 persons or 8.89 percent were of ethnic minorities."* Since 2010: Han +60,378,693 (+4.93%); minorities +11,675,179 (+10.26%). ### 1.3 Weighted composition (DERIVED)

**Method:** each province's people weighted by that province's 2020 GRP per head. Weighted share of group *g* = Σ_p (count_g,p × GRPpc_p) ÷ Σ_p (pop_p × GRPpc_p). Inputs are the two tables above, and all 31 provinces are included. Script and outputs are in the agent scratchpad (not the repo). Servicemen (2,000,000) are excluded because the yearbook's provincial table excludes them.

**Regional income by ethnic group: NOT FOUND as an official series.** NBS publishes income by province, urban/rural and quintile, not by ethnic group. Academic estimates from household surveys exist (for example the China Household Income Project studies of rural Yi, Manchu and Han incomes, *China Quarterly*, https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/DCC7D279AEB89EE11A21D469C2508720/S0305741022001576a.pdf/incomplete_catching_up_income_among_yi_manchu_and_han_people_in_rural_china_20022018.pdf). They are not national, not official and not used here. The weighting therefore applies one province-wide per-head figure to every group in a province.

### 1.4 Languages and religions (census data only)

- **Language: NOT COLLECTED by the 2020 census.** The State Council notice on the census (国发〔2019〕24号, 31 Oct 2019) lists its content verbatim: *"普查主要调查人口和住户的基本情况，内容包括：姓名、公民身份号码、性别、年龄、民族、受教育程度、行业、职业、迁移流动、婚姻生育、死亡、住房情况等。"* (https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/国务院关于开展第七次全国人口普查的通知). **SOURCED.** There is no language or dialect item.
- **Religion: NOT COLLECTED** (same list, SOURCED). A secondary report (Bitter Winter) claims enumerators asked about religion off-form. That is **UNVERIFIED** and is not census data.
- Census language or religion shares for China are therefore **NOT FOUND**, by design of the census. Non-census sources (surveys, white papers) exist but are outside this item's "census data only" rule and were not used.

## 2 · Japan (draft city share 11.98%)

### 2.1 Latest national census: composition

**Census:** 2025 Population Census of Japan (令和7年国勢調査), reference date 1 October 2025. The preliminary population count was published 29 May 2026. The **Basic Complete Tabulation on Population and Households (人口等基本集計), which carries foreign nationals, was published 29 September 2026.** **SOURCED** (Statistics Bureau results page, https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kokusei/2025/kekka.html; summary PDF https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kokusei/2025/kekka/pdf/summary_01.pdf).

**The category system and its limits (SOURCED):**
- **Japan's census does not ask ethnicity, language or religion.** The 2020 census items (19 in total; the 2025 item list was not separately checked, **UNVERIFIED** whether identical) are, verbatim from https://www.stat.go.jp/data/kokusei/2020/gaiyou.html: household members: *氏名、男女の別、出生の年月、世帯主との続き柄、配偶の関係、**国籍**、現在の住居における居住期間、5年前の住居の所在地、在学、卒業等教育の状況、就業状態、所属の事業所の名称及び事業の種類、仕事の種類、従業上の地位、従業地又は通学地、従業地又は通学地までの利用交通手段*; households: *世帯の種類、世帯員の数、住居の種類、住宅の建て方*.
- The **only population-origin variable is nationality (国籍)**: Japanese, one of the listed foreign nationalities, or not stated. Naturalized citizens, Japan-born descendants of earlier immigrants (for example Zainichi Koreans who naturalized), Ainu and Ryukyuans all count as **日本人 (Japanese nationals)**, with no further breakdown. ("All count as Japanese nationals" is DERIVED from the item list: there is no other item that could separate them.)
- Census scope excludes the islands named in the enforcement regulation (Habomai, Shikotan, Kunashiri, Etorofu; Takeshima) and excludes foreign diplomats and foreign military personnel and their families. **SOURCED** (same page, sections 4 and 5).
- **Two counting bases exist.** *Raw values* (原数値): nationality "not stated" kept as its own line. *Imputed values* (不詳補完値): "not stated" distributed. Imputed: **Japanese 119,132 thousand (96.9%), foreign 3.84 million (3.1%)**, foreign +1,094 thousand (+39.8%) since 2020 (summary PDF, verbatim figures: *"日本人人口は１億 1913 万２千人（総人口の 96.9％）"*, *"外国人人口は 384 万人（総人口の 3.1％）"*). The prefecture-by-nationality table is published in **raw values**, so the work below uses raw values and reports shares both of the total and of persons whose status was stated.
- Total population (basic tabulation): **122,972,528**. The preliminary count published in May 2026 was 123,049,524 (search-result snippet only, **UNVERIFIED**). The two releases differ, and the reason was not checked.

**National counts, raw values (SOURCED: e-Stat, 2025 census basic tabulation, Table 49-1 男女、国籍別人口－全国、都道府県、市区町村, statInfId 000040506619, https://www.e-stat.go.jp/stat-search/file-download?statInfId=000040506619&fileKind=0):** total 122,972,528; Japanese 117,405,318; foreign 3,461,758; not stated 2,105,452. This is a count of people municipalities know of. ### 2.3 Weighted composition (DERIVED)

**Method:** as for China. Each prefecture's people are weighted by its GDP per head: weighted share of *g* = Σ_p (count_g,p × GDPpc_p) ÷ Σ_p (pop_p × GDPpc_p).
