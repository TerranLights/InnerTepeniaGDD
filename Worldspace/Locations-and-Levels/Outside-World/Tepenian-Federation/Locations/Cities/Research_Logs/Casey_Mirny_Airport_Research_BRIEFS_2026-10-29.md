## Brief 2: operations (full text as sent)
Context: the international passenger airport links to Hobart (and secondarily Fremantle): near Casey (about 110.5E, about 3,420 km from Hobart) or at Mirny (about 93.0E, about 4,160 km from Hobart, about 4,120 km from Fremantle). Items:

3. WEATHER AT CASEY/WILKINS: wind, crosswind, visibility, blizzards, katabatic effects, share of flights lost to weather.
4. WEATHER AT MIRNY: the same, plus the long-run climate record; sea-ice and polynya effects; Soviet and Russian aviation there (search in Russian too).
5. RANGE AND AIRCRAFT: flying distances from Hobart and Fremantle to each site; which aircraft classes (narrow-body, wide-body, C-17) can do each leg with a safe-return reserve; capacities; what the runway must offer.
6. PASSENGER PROCESSING AND BORDER CLEARANCE: how people are cleared into Antarctic programs from a gateway airport (customs, quarantine, biosecurity, medical); what a gateway terminal needs; real analogies (Hobart, Punta Arenas and Frei/Marsh, Christchurch).
7. WHAT THE FACTS IMPLY: factual only.
