# F.1 · ⭐⭐⭐ THE ANSWER — **PERSONS-VERSUS-WORK, IN FOUR LAYERS THAT DO NOT AGREE**

> # **The procedure reaches a PERSON. But the body that reaches the person is not the community's body.**
> # **And the community's own body is constituted, by its own long title and its own exhaustive schedule, over WORK, LAND, ANIMALS, AMENITIES AND TAXES.**

## LAYER 1 — THE CONSTITUTION: a right that exists in order to be deprivable

**s. 202, verbatim:**
> *"Protection from arbitrary deprivation of right of abode in Tristan da Cunha or of British citizenship —
> **No person shall be arbitrarily deprived of his or her right of abode in Tristan da Cunha or of his or her
> British citizenship, whether by legislation or otherwise.**"*

⭐ **Read it precisely.** The guarantee bars only **arbitrary** deprivation. **It therefore presupposes that a
right of abode exists as a legal object, that it CAN be deprived, and that non-arbitrary deprivation is
lawful** — **"whether by legislation or otherwise."**

**s. 214, verbatim, and it is one sentence:**
> *"There shall be an Island Council for Tristan da Cunha which shall be established and constituted in
> accordance with a law in force in Tristan da Cunha."*

⛔ **The constitution names the community's body and says nothing whatever about its powers.** *Everything is
delegated downward.*

# F.2 · ⭐ WHERE THE COUNCIL'S OWN BINDING DECISION DOES LAND ON AN INDIVIDUAL

**Three provisions, and all three are allocations — not decisions about the person.**

1. ⭐⭐ **Livestock Limitation By-Law 2, verbatim:**
   > **"No owner of livestock may retain in his or her possession livestock in excess of the number approved by
   > Island Council."**
   *"owner" means the person in whose name livestock are registered.* Contravention is an offence —
   **a fine of £2, and a further £1 for each day the offence continues after conviction.**
   ⭐ ***This is the single place in the entire corpus where a determination made by the community's own elected
   body binds a named individual. It is a CEILING ON A HOLDING.*** **It measures what a person has. It does not
   decide anything about the person.**
2. **The Agricultural Committee** — *Livestock, Forestry and Agriculture By-Laws, by-law 2* — consists of **the
   Administrator as Chair and 4 members of the Island Council "who must themselves be owners of livestock,"
   elected annually.** Its duties include **"(b) control of the number of types of livestock permitted for each
   owner."** ⭐ *A per-person allocation, made by a committee whose members are required to be materially
   affected by it.*
3. ⭐⭐ **Donkeys Limitation By-Law** — **the one written procedure in the corpus that decides how PERSONS are
   GROUPED.** *"family" means a person or persons separately owning and maintaining potato patches; "unit" is a
   group of three families.* **"The families on the Island of Tristan da Cunha are to be divided into units of
   3 families to each group. Each unit will be allowed to keep and own 2 jacks and one jinny."** Every unit
   **must register any change in the composition of the units within one month** *(failure: **25p each**)*;
   rearing a foal as a replacement requires **the Administrator's permission** and it may then be kept **2
   years without being counted**; a family may **opt out** and keep **one donkey**.
   ⛔ **And the dispute clause:** **"In the event of any dispute concerning the composition of units as defined
   or the use of animals within the group, the decision of the Administrator will be final."**
   ⭐ ***Who a person is grouped with is a question the written law answers — and it answers it by handing the
   final say to the appointed outsider.***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ THE JOINT FINDING NEITHER OF US COULD REACH ALONE
