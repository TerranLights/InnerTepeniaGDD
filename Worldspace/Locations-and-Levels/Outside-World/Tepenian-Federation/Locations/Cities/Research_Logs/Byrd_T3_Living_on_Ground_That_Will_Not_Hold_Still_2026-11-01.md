### 1a. Kiruna, Sweden (building on `Byrd_T10_…` §1.10; quake magnitude, church move and the August 2025 enlargement are not repeated)

**Pages read (WebFetch):** LKAB, "Residents" (for those affected), https://lkab.com/en/community-development/for-those-affected/residents/ · LKAB, "Updated impact area in Kiruna," https://lkab.com/en/community-development/updated-impact-area-in-kiruna/ · Government Offices of Sweden press release, 27 August 2026, https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/08/regeringen-medger-markforvarv-for-samhallsomvandlingen-i-kiruna/ · Fastighetstidningen, 18 January 2016, https://fastighetstidningen.se/nyhet/lkab-ger-fastighetsagarna-klara-besked-om-ersattning/ · TV4, 1 December 2025, https://www.tv4.se/artikel/1Nd6i7DidVJXpJWEVlomIf/tvingas-flytta-vaedjar-om-hoegre-ersaettning-vaeldigt-vemodigt · Euronews, 2 November 2025, https://www.euronews.com/2025/11/02/moving-a-town-in-the-arctic-why-residents-of-swedens-kiruna-feel-left-out-in-the-cold · Luleå University of Technology news, 27 October 2020, https://www.ltu.se/ltu/media/news/Gruvstadernas-stadsomvandlingar-genom-medborgarnas-ogon-1.202591?l=en

**Who pays and under what law**
- **LKAB pays, as the party causing the damage, not under a special public mandate. UNVERIFIED (search 2 summaries of Riksdag documents, not opened):** a National Audit Office (Riksrevisionen) report says LKAB grounds its role in the transformation on the Minerals Act's rules on compensation for damage and encroachment; a parliamentary motion says LKAB has no specially decided societal mandate and can compensate only private property owners, municipalities and authorities.
- **SOURCED (Government, 27 August 2026):** *"det är LKAB som har det direkta ansvaret att ersätta de sakägare"* ("it is LKAB that has the direct responsibility to compensate the affected parties"); *"en viktig del i LKAB:s ersättningsmodell är att kunna erbjuda ersättningsmark"* ("an important part of LKAB's compensation model is being able to offer replacement land").

**Who decides land: the state, between the company and the town**
- **SOURCED (Government, 27 August 2026):** the government allowed LKAB to buy **275 hectares** of state land in Kiruna *"för samhällsbyggnadsändamål"* ("for community-building purposes"), keeping back a strip for a future railway property for the Transport Administration (Trafikverket). **Kiruna Municipality had applied to buy the same land; the government rejected the municipality's application**, saying the extra administrative step of LKAB buying from the municipality *"bör undvikas"* ("should be avoided") and the municipality's reasons were *"inte så starka att LKAB ska hindras att förvärva markområdena direkt från staten"* ("not so strong that LKAB should be prevented from acquiring the land directly from the state"). Stated scale: *"Omkring 12 000 människor och 6 000 bostäder berörs"* ("About 12,000 people and 6,000 homes are affected").
- **SOURCED (LKAB, "Updated impact area"; press meeting 12 September, year not printed, 2025 by the page's image paths):** *"Since the transformation of Kiruna began to be planned in 2004, LKAB has made forecasts of the land impact"*; the extent *"has been updated over the years based on the shape and location of the ore body, the nature of the bedrock, measurement results and mining plans"*; the new phase was presented jointly by **Kiruna Municipality and LKAB** with *"a phased plan for the updated impact area and new compensation principles"*; *"The first area is around the school Triangelskolan"*; *"Relocation is not expected within the next few years, and the timing will depend on access to buildable land"*; the process *"must start now to secure operations until – and beyond – 2035."* (The 6,000 people / 2,700 homes figure is in `Byrd_T10_…`.)

**The compensation offer (verified against LKAB's own page)**
- **Original 2016 terms, SOURCED (Fastighetstidningen, 18 January 2016):** house owners and owners of rental buildings were offered two choices: *"ett ersättningshus som är likvärdigt med det nuvarande"* ("a replacement house equivalent to the current one"), or *"en summa pengar som motsvarar marknadsvärdet plus 25 procent"* ("a sum of money corresponding to the market value plus 25 percent"); *"Naturligtvis kommer huset inte att vara en kopia av det gamla, men det ska vara jämförbart"* ("Naturally the house will not be a copy of the old one, but it must be comparable"). Businesses that rent were offered *"en ny lokal med hyrestrappning upp till fem år"* ("new premises with stepped rent for up to five years"), and LKAB would cover relocation costs and lost earnings. The article says *"Men i mars förra året gjorde LKAB en kovändning"* ("But in March last year LKAB made a U-turn"; March 2015: DERIVED from the article date). UNVERIFIED (search 1 summary of another Fastighetstidningen item): the U-turn was toward replacing owners' houses with new ones, because market value plus 25 percent would not have gone far toward building new.
- **Current terms, SOURCED (LKAB "Residents" page, 2025 version):**
  - House owners: *"You will be offered a new property as a replacement for your original property"*; or a smaller house, where *"The number of square meters you give up is then multiplied by the square meter price plus 25%"*; or, if the owner chooses an apartment, *"a sum of money that corresponds to the market value of your house plus 25 percent."* A property-exchange agreement or a purchase agreement with LKAB is signed.
  - Condominium (tenant-owner) owners: *"Monetary base amount based on market value (average price per square meter) plus 25%"*, plus *"Individual assessment of the condition and standard"*; those redeemed after June 2022 get priority to buy a new condominium built under LKAB's management, at the stated price per square meter up to the same floor area.
  - Tenants: *"New rental and relocation compensation (possibly rent escalation for new construction)"*; *"For owners, a market valuation is done, for tenants, the housing situation is evaluated."* Rental priority with LKAB Fastigheter (LKAB's housing company); existing queue points are not used up.
  - Six steps: information meeting (*"Well in advance before the urban transformation affects your property, LKAB will contact you"*) → mapping and valuation *"regardless of whether you own or rent"* → offer → choice → decisions and agreements → move and handover (*"LKAB will then take over your previous accommodation"*).
- **The dispute, SOURCED (TV4, 1 December 2025):** the Kiruna homeowners' association (Villaägarna Kiruna, chair Peter Maagaard) says the 25 percent supplement is not enough and wants outbuildings excluded today to count, *"biutrymmen som källare, grillkåtor och skotergarage"* ("ancillary spaces such as basements, barbecue huts and snowmobile garages"). **850 villas**, plus rental buildings, schools and sports facilities, must be demolished in the new phase. A new expropriation law with **market value plus 100 percent** was due to take effect at the turn of the year but was stopped, *"av ekonomiska skäl enligt Villaägarna"* ("for financial reasons, according to Villaägarna"); the Justice Minister declined comment. Maagaard: if a quarter of the 850 owners objected, *"skulle LKAB få över 200 rättegångsprocesser"* ("LKAB would face over 200 court cases"). DERIVED: 850 × 0.25 = 212.5. The association's lawyer estimates compensation from *"några hundratusen upp till flera miljoner"* ("a few hundred thousand up to several million" kronor) per case.
- **UNVERIFIED (search 1 summary):** a 2016 government memorandum (Ds 2016:16, "Ersättning vid expropriation av bostäder") found expropriation compensation does not always suffice to obtain equivalent housing; a parliamentary written question says residents may have to take large loans for replacement homes, pay much higher rent, move to smaller homes or leave the area.

**Residents' experience: what was kept, what was lost**
- **SOURCED (LTU, 2020):** Eugenia Segerstedt's dissertation *Small Town, Big Move* (Human Work Science, Luleå University of Technology) drew on four studies with more than 3,500 participants, including three Kiruna surveys 2011 to 2016 and workshops with residents of Kiruna and Gällivare and long-distance commuters. Findings: in 2011 blue-collar workers were less likely than others to consider moving, a difference gone by 2016; young men and women were the most likely to want to move (*"Kiruna was becoming the city of the old men"*); social bonds replaced the community itself as a reason to stay; hope for an attractive post-transformation town declined and no longer held back those planning to leave; one recurring storyline was **"broken promises"** of faster change; residents who spoke of a good society did not mention the mine.
- **SOURCED (Euronews, 2 November 2025):** residents say the new town has lost *"the warmth that defined the old one"*; some call the new main square a *"bloody wind tunnel"*; a University of Gothenburg study (built-heritage specialist Jennie Sjöholm) found the new Kiruna **can be up to 10 °C colder in winter** than the old town, because it sits in a hollow where cold air collects and tall buildings block the low sun; the 1900 town plan by Per Olof Hallman used a sunny south-facing slope and curved streets against wind, while the new plan put transport links before microclimate, so playgrounds and balconies often face north. Sjöholm: *"Decision-makers did not optimize the design for the urban climate."* The mine, town, roads and railways cut across historic Sámi reindeer migration routes. Completion is expected in 2035.
- **UNVERIFIED (search 4 summaries, not opened):** an anthropology thesis (Elisa Maria López) on the Čohkkiras (Jukkasjärvi) Sámi herding community, on whose land Kiruna was built, describes continuing conflicts over who may use the mountains, rivers and lakes; a 2026 Lund student thesis concludes the relocation *"exposes the precarious position of tenants in Sweden"*; one participation study classes LKAB-led consultation as "tokenism" on Arnstein's ladder.

### 5d. When monitoring saw it coming and the decision came too late: Vajont, Italy, 1963 (neighbor; followed for late warnings)

**Page read:** English Wikipedia, "Vajont Dam," https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vajont_Dam (secondary)

- **SOURCED (Wikipedia):** in 1959 three experts separately warned the operator SADE that the side of Monte Toc was unstable; on 4 November 1960 a slide of about 800,000 m³ led SADE to lower the water about 50 m; the government sued journalists reporting the problems for "undermining the social order"; in April and May 1962 residents reported five "grade five" earthquakes, downplayed by SADE; in July 1962 SADE's model tests showed a slide wave could overtop the dam if the water stood within 20 m of the crest, and a reduced level 25 m below the crest was set; creep was about 1.01 cm a week before September 1963, **25.4 cm a day** in September and **1 m** the day before failure; on 26 September 1963 ENEL (the nationalized owner since March 1963) decided to empty the basin slowly to 240 m; on **9 October 1963 at 22:39** about **260 million m³** fell into the reservoir and about 50 million m³ of water overtopped the dam in a 250 m wave; official death toll **1,917** (estimates to 2,500). The page records no evacuation order.
