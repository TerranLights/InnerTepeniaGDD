- **A. Snapshot (UNVERIFIED, snippet):** It began as an Imperial Chinese military fort. It became a de jure enclave after the New Territories were leased to the United Kingdom in 1898. Its population grew sharply after the Japanese occupation ended, mostly with refugees from the renewed Chinese Civil War. By the late 1980s it held about 35,000 residents on 2.6 hectares (6.4 acres). From outside it looked like one solid block 12 to 14 stories high. Inside it was a dim maze of narrow corridors, with electric wires and water pipes running along the ceilings. *(Search snippet attributed to results including atlasobscura.com/articles/kowloon-walled-city and gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20170411-hong-kong-city-of-darkness-revisited.   - "Hundreds of factories" made things such as fish balls and golf balls.
