# The "(founding wave)" tag: what it would change if it means "Register founder" (2026-11-02, NOT APPLIED)

*Developer, 2026-11-02, sheet decisions, item 6: "all as recommended" (the tag means "Register founder"; show the change list first). Checking every tag in the 38 specs against the Founding Register showed the question reaches well beyond the five Peninsula cities: **27 cities carry the tag**, and in **16 of them at least one tag names a nation the Register does not list as that city's founder.** Nothing has been edited.*

## What the tag means in the Janbogo-subnet and Mirny-subnet specs today
In those cities the Australia and New Zealand tags carry a different meaning: "a direct sea route from Australia" (Casey's wording) or "earliest founding wave, geographically closest" (Cape Adare, New Zealand). That is a **founding-era arrival** meaning, not a Register meaning. So the choice is really between:

1. **Register founder only** (my recommendation): remove the tags above, and say in each spec what the sea-route nations are in plain words instead ("early arrivals by sea from Australia").
2. **Two tags:** keep "(founding wave)" for early arrivals and add a separate marker, "(Register founder)", for the Register's nations.

Either way the edit touches the tier line in each spec, the census Section A line (the same text), and the Phase 2 datasheet annotation rows; it does not change any share or count.
