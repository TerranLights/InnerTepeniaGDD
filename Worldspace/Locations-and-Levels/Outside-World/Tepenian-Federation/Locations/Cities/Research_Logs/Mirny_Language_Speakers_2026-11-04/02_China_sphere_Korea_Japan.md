# Language speakers, G2: China sphere, Korea, Japan (2026-11-04)

*Research agent G2. Budget 70 searches; used **65.5** (47 WebSearch + 37 WebFetch at half each). Status: complete within budget; gaps listed below.*

**How to read this file.** "Speaker" = the developer's definition: native plus fluent second-language speakers who could conduct business, social talk and general dealings in the language. Every figure says what its source actually measured. Where no survey measures the developer's definition, the BEST figure is **my derivation** from the sources named in the note, and is marked "derived". Recalled numbers that this run did **not** verify are marked "(recalled, not verified)". Treat them as guesses until checked.
