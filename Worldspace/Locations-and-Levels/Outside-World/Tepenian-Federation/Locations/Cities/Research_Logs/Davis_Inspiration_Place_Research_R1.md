## Pick 1 — Tyumen, Russia [PRIMARY]

### A. Snapshot

- **Location:** on the Tura River, Western Siberia; 57°09′N 65°32′E; elevation 102 m; about 1,700 km east of Moscow and about 300 km east of Yekaterinburg. **SOURCED** — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyumen.
- **What it is:** administrative center and largest city of Tyumen Oblast; widely called Russia's "oil and gas capital" since the West Siberian oil and gas discoveries of the 1960s. **SOURCED** (en.wikipedia Tyumen; utmn.ru/tmn/en/facts/oil). **Defining feature (as the sources frame it): the first Russian town in Siberia, and the rear base/"capital" of the West Siberian oil-and-gas north.**
- **Founding:** fortress ordered by Tsar Feodor I on 29 July (8 August N.S.) 1586, on or near the site of Chingi-Tura (Chimgi-Tura), capital of the Tyumen Khanate (13th–16th c.); "Tyumen" first mentioned 1406 (Arkhangelogorod Chronicle); early settlers came from Perm, Solvychegodsk and Veliky Ustyug; by the 1624 census over half of households were service people. **SOURCED** — en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Tyumen; ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Тюмень.
- **Population:** 847,488 (2021 census); 872,077 (2025 estimate, Russian Wikipedia citing Rosstat). **SOURCED** — ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Тюмень. ⚠ The English Wikipedia fetch returned a "2025 estimate 744,554" with an incoherent growth note; this conflicts with the census figure and is **not used** (likely a mis-extraction; unresolved).
- **Area:** 698.48 km² (urban okrug) / 490.82 km² (city limits) per ru.wikipedia; en.wikipedia gives 235 km². Figures differ by definition; **SOURCED** both, unreconciled.
- **Divisions:** four administrative okrugs — Vostochny, Kalininsky, Leninsky, Tsentralny. **SOURCED** — ru.wikipedia.
- **Climate:** humid continental (Dfb); January mean −16.7 °C, July +18.6 °C. **SOURCED** — en.wikipedia.
- **Ethnic makeup (2021):** Russians 83.4%, Tatars 7.1%, Ukrainians 1.7%. **SOURCED** — ru.wikipedia.
- **Population history:** the university's English pages say the city grew "from 150,000 to 600,000" over the oil era (no years given). **SOURCED** — utmn.ru/tmn/en/facts/oil.

### D. How the defining feature shaped those things

- **Oil-and-gas rear base:** the oil institutes and the 1963 industrial institute (now TIU), the oil-company offices, the refinery, the Palace of Culture "Neftyanik" named after V. I. Muravlenko, hotels named "Neftyanik", the pig and egg complexes built to feed the north, the air routes to the northern oil towns, and the private-sector money flow from KhMAO/YaNAO (2022 account) all follow from the city's role as the southern base for the northern fields. **SOURCED** as listed above.
- **First Russian town in Siberia / river gateway:** shipbuilding (largest 1915 employer category; later the 1928 plant), the 1896 river pier, the Ob-Irtysh Shipping Company (1963), the Vasilyevskaya fair, the coachmen/carter trade that sustained carpet demand, and City Day's founding reenactment, bell and merchant-history festivals and the "Kontora parokhodstva" multicenter all derive from the river-and-road gateway role. **SOURCED** as above; the "gateway" causal link is the sources' framing (Kupibilet/Tripster call it "ворота Сибири" — **UNVERIFIED** snippet).
- **Distance from the front:** Tyumen was chosen to hold Lenin's body; Tyumen cats sent to Leningrad (commemorated in 2008). **SOURCED**.

### E. Structural features bearing on industry, economy, arts and work life

- Income derives heavily from a resource north that is administratively part of the oblast but physically far away; the city hosts offices, training, refining and services rather than the fields themselves. **SOURCED** (en.wikipedia; t-j.ru 2022).
- Heavy industry has been pushed out of the center (the 47-ha shipyard sold for housing, 2011). **SOURCED**.
- A dense amateur and youth theater scene (15 named theaters/studios) alongside the state drama theater, puppet theater and philharmonic. **SOURCED**.
- Thermal water: the oblast markets itself as a "capital of thermal waters" — springs of +36 to +48 °C with bromine, iodine and iron; Verkhny Bor 5 km from the city (open-air pools ~+40 °C year-round); Sovetsky springs 35 km; "Feshenel" 60 km — **UNVERIFIED** (snippets, kupi.com blog). A planned "Pearl of Siberia" wellness resort with the "Ancient Sea" thermal complex: 89.2 bn rubles, ~3,000 rooms, ~5,000 jobs; oblast had 4.1 million visitors in 2025. **SOURCED** — rustourismnews.com, 2026-06-17.
- Exposure: the refinery has been the target of drone strikes (October 2025). **SOURCED** — Meduza 2026-06-06.
