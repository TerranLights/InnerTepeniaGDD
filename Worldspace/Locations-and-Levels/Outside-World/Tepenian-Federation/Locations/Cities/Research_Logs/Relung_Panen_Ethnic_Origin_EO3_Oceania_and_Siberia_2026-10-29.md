**Category system and its limits (SOURCED unless marked):**
- **Australia's census has no "ethnic group" or "race" question.** The nearest variables are **Ancestry** (self-identified, up to two responses), **Indigenous status** (Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander origin), country of birth, language used at home and religious affiliation. (The absence of an ethnicity question is **DERIVED** from the variable list in the 2021 DataPack metadata, `Metadata_2021_GCP_DataPack_R1_R2.xlsx`, tables G01 to G62, which has no ethnicity table.)
- Ancestry, verbatim from the ABS Census Dictionary (https://www.abs.gov.au/census/guide-census-data/census-dictionary/2021/variables-topic/cultural-diversity/ancestry-multi-response-ancp): *"This variable indicates how a person identifies their ancestry."* Question: *"What is the person's ancestry?"* *"Provide up to two ancestries only."* Responses are coded to the *"Australian Standard Classification of Cultural and Ethnic Groups (ASCCEG), 2019."* *"a total count of responses, not people, is produced"*, so *"the total count of responses for all ancestries in Australia exceeds Australia's total population."* *"The item non-response rate for the Ancestry question was 6.2% in 2021."* *"The 2021 Census includes mark boxes for both Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander ancestries"*, which the ABS expects to reduce "Australian" responses; 2016 and 2021 Aboriginal ancestry counts are therefore not directly comparable. **SOURCED.**
- The ABS applies *"small random adjustments ... to all cell values to protect the confidentiality of data"*, so rows and columns can differ slightly from totals. **SOURCED** (Table 4 footnote).

### 1.3 Weighted composition (DERIVED)

**Method (same as EO1):** each state's people weighted by that state's 2021-22 GSP per head. Because ancestry allows two answers, the shares are given on two bases:
- **% of persons:** Σ_s (responses_g,s × GSPpc_s) ÷ Σ_s (persons_s × GSPpc_s). Columns sum to more than 100% (about 131%), because one person can give two ancestries.
- **% of responses** (sums to 100%): Σ_s (responses_g,s × GSPpc_s) ÷ Σ_s (all stated responses_s × GSPpc_s). This basis is the one used for the 1.6 arithmetic, because it is the only one that sums to 100%. *Assumption, stated:* a person giving two ancestries counts as two half-weights only in the sense that every response carries equal weight. "Not stated" (6.23% of persons) is excluded from the pool.

**Result (DERIVED; full table in 1.6):** the weighting moves very little. North-West European ancestries go from 44.89% to 45.00% of responses; Oceanian (Australian, Aboriginal, Maori, New Zealander, Pacific) from 27.15% to 27.22%; Southern and Eastern European from 9.10% to 8.98%; Southern and Central Asian from 5.35% to 5.28%; North-East Asian from 4.92% to 4.87%; South-East Asian from 3.48% to 3.52%. The reason is arithmetic: the two highest-GSP units (WA, NT) differ from the national pattern in opposite directions, and the three largest states (NSW, Victoria, Queensland, 77.6% of the population) have GSP per head close to one another. Australian Aboriginal ancestry rises from 2.22% to 2.32% of responses, because the NT (24.5% Aboriginal ancestry) has the second-highest GSP per head.

**Regional income by ethnic or ancestry group: NOT FOUND as an official series.** The census has personal-income tables (G17), but no income-by-ancestry table appears in the state-level General Community Profile; a TableBuilder cross-tabulation (ancestry × income × state) would exist only as a custom request and was not pursued. The weighting therefore applies one state-wide per-head figure to every group in a state.

### 3.3 Weighted composition (DERIVED)

**Method:** each subject weighted by its 2021 GRP per head, on the two bases used above. **% of stated persons:** Σ (count × GRPpc) ÷ Σ (stated persons × GRPpc); this sums to about 100.12%. **% of responses** (sums to 100%): Σ (count × GRPpc) ÷ Σ (all nationality responses × GRPpc); 3.6 uses this one. Refused and no-data are excluded from the pool.

**Result for the UTC+5 to UTC+9 belt (DERIVED; full table in 3.6):** Russians 81.04% of stated persons unweighted, **78.41% of responses weighted** · Tatars 4.73% → 4.42% · Bashkirs 4.15% → 2.93% · Yakuts 1.29% → **2.52%** · Ukrainians 0.63% → 1.30% · Nenets 0.11% → **0.96%** · Buryats 1.22% → 0.71% · Kazakhs 0.76% → 0.64% · Khanty 0.08% → **0.47%** · Tuvans 0.79% → 0.28% · Altaians 0.21% → 0.09%.

*Reading (DERIVED, arithmetic only):* the GRP weighting is dominated by **Yamalo-Nenets AO (GRP per head 8,295.1k, 9.6 times the national 862.8k) and Khanty-Mansi AO (3,364.5k)**. Together they hold 5.4% of the belt's people but **23.8% of the weighted pool**. So groups concentrated there (Nenets, Khanty, Mansi, Selkups, and in-migrant groups such as Ukrainians, Azerbaijanis, Kumyks, Nogais, Lezgins, Tajiks and Kyrgyz) gain weight, and groups in low-GRP republics (Tuva 280.7k, Altai Republic 359.1k, Buryatia 371.9k) lose it. Sakha (1,705.2k) raises the Yakut share. **This is a property of the composition method's income factor applied to resource-extraction regions, and is stated so it is not mistaken for a population fact.** The unweighted column is given beside it in 3.6.

**Key facts that bear on how those numbers can be read (stated flatly):**
1. **No New Zealand region lies in the UTC+4 to UTC+10 window** (legal UTC+12; solar about UTC+11.1 to +11.9). Its 2.97% is computed as instructed, but no NZ population lies in the window. Not adjudicated.
2. **The categories are not alike across the three countries.** Australia counts *ancestry* (up to two answers; "Australian" is itself a category) and Indigenous status separately. New Zealand counts self-identified *ethnicity* (multiple answers). Russia counts *nationality* (one or two answers), with **11.3% nationally (11.1% in the belt) having no nationality recorded**. The three tables do not use one common taxonomy.
3. **The GRP weighting moves Russia's belt substantially:** Yamalo-Nenets and Khanty-Mansi hold 5.4% of the belt's people but 23.8% of its weighted pool. **It barely moves Australia** (no broad group shifts by more than 0.12 points) and moves New Zealand moderately (Asian +1.1, Pacific +0.6, European −1.0, Māori −0.8 points of responses).
4. **Australia's eastern seaboard lies past solar UTC+10.0** (east of 150°E, including the cities of Sydney and Brisbane). On legal standard time all states are inside the window. The weighting uses whole states.
5. **The Russian census primary (Rosstat) was unreachable** (TLS chain not trusted here; the bypass was refused by the permission system and not attempted otherwise). Every Russian figure is a secondary transcription, cross-checked at one subject (Sakha) to within 0.07%–0.4%.
6. **Flag for the developer, not applied:** project memory records a standing rule, "No Subcontinentals" (no South Asians ever came to Tepenia). The real-world Australian and New Zealand tables include South Asian ancestries and ethnicities: Indian, Punjabi, Nepalese, Sri Lankan, Pakistani, Fijian Indian, Sinhalese and others.
