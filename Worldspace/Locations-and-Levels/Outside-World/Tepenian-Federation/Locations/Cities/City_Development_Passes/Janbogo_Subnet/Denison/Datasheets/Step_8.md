# Denison — Datasheet · Step 8 (The Review Panel)

> **Tier U — the panel-position roster and disposition definitions, fixed corpus-wide.**

Citation: `Corpus_Reference_Sheets/Review_Panel_Full_Text_Quick_Reference.md`, in full — carries
`Disciplines/00f_Review_Panel.md`'s panel-position roster (Six Flat Archetypes plus the mandatory
Passer-Through and Neighbor, and the Lover faculty's question every time) plus the six disposition
definitions.

| Disposition | Definition |
|---|---|
| accepted | The objection is granted |
| noted | Recorded, not acted on |
| rejected | Refused, with reasoning |
| refereed | Two positions in genuine conflict, adjudicated |
| unmet | Refused as characterization — satisfying it would make the place generic |
| declined | The panel declines to rule |

⭐ The peer-free `unmet` test, city-scale: *"Would satisfying this objection replace something
SPECIFIC TO THIS PLACE with something that could be true anywhere?"*

**Not mechanical / not included here:** any actual panel run, any disposition assigned to any
objection about Denison — none of this exists until Denison's own Step 8 actually runs.
