## 5a.2 ⭐⭐ THE MECHANISM, WRITTEN AS A MECHANISM

**Canon's four facts, and they compose into one machine:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | **The water has no natural owner** — *a trench between two facing shores* |
| **2** | **The port is CONSTRUCTED** — ***somebody must command that labor, every year, forever*** |
| **3** | ⭐⭐ **The window is `~4` months** *(Nov–Mar)* — ***so berthing order decides whose cargo lands this year and whose waits a full year.*** **Canon: *"that is not a scheduling matter; it is an economy"*** |
| **4** | **One of the three has no shore at all** — *`~8 km` away on a different landform, dependent on a decision taken by two other governments* |

> ### ⛔ WHAT EACH SIDE REFUSES TO DEVELOP, AND WHO SUPPLIES IT — *the phase's actual question*
> ***Nobody here develops an independent sea access, because the geology does not offer one.*** **The horsts and
> grabens give the group exactly one defensible deep-water cut, and it is this one.** ⭐ **So the refusal is not
> a choice and there is no rivalry in it** — *the mechanism is that a single physical fact makes three separate
> maritime policies impossible, and somebody has to hold the queue.*
