## 6 · The contradictions and the both-are-true readings, worked

*Each pass finding named below **stays at full strength in the pass's files**. Where a culture file appears wrong the output is a docket row (§7), never a concession. Scales were checked in order (`04` Gate 6 note): (1) public/mainstream vs private/minority · (2) dominant culture vs its own counterculture · (3) earlier vs later generation · (4) legal/procedural vs narrative/emotional. Each case gives the single property, the scale, and three orders. Dual tags (inheritance × depth) follow the readers'. Cases are numbered in order of definition; the unmatched readers' own numbers are kept in the Credit tag.*

*Merge rule applied to this section:* where a reader worked a case on material that rule 3a puts OUT OF FRAME (the verification ethic and creed, the structural-load habit, the drink pole, the shared suppers, the consultancy tie, the whole personality read), the case is **not worked**; its shape is recorded in the note at the end of this section, and any part of it that stands on admissible ground is folded into the case that holds it.
