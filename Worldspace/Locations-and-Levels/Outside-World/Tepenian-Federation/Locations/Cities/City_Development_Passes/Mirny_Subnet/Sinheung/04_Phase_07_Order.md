# 7b — GOVERNANCE AND DECISION

> ## ⭐ THE SPEC'S STEER: ***"The most productive question here is not who holds power but WHAT IS UNADMINISTRABLE."***

## B.1 THE CANON STARTING POINT, AND IT IS ALREADY A CONTRADICTION

**The spec's own words:** the chambers are ***"a foundational, Federation-wide piece of infrastructure hiding
behind Sinheung's otherwise modest political profile."***

> # ***THE CITY HOLDS A NATIONAL CHOKEPOINT AND CARRIES NO NATIONAL WEIGHT.***
> **Every Tepenian city can build robots regardless of local manufacturing capability *because this city ships
> the apparatus.*** ⭐ **That is enormous structural leverage attached to a modest political profile — and canon
> states the modesty as a fact, not as a grievance.**

⚠ **`NO FORCED FIT` check:** *the obvious move is to write resentment, or a city quietly aware of its own leverage.*
⛔ **Canon does not say that, and the pass has not earned it.** ⭐ **What the pass HAS earned is the reason the
leverage is unusable, and it is structural rather than emotional — see `B.3`.**

## B.2 ⭐⭐⭐ THE THREE UNADMINISTRABLE THINGS — all already established, none of them invented here

| # | What cannot be administered | Established in | Why it resists administration |
|:--:|---|---|---|
| **1** | ⭐⭐ **THE STANDARD** | `Phase 3` | ***"The Standard has no office because an office could not do the job."*** **Freeze-thaw failure is DEFERRED and DISPLACED — the flaw is interior and the test is time, so inspection at the point of production has nothing to look at.** ⭐ *Not neglect. **Physics.*** |
| **2** | ⭐ **THE PORT RE-CUT** | `Phase 5` | **The port is CONSTRUCTED and re-cut every season — *"somebody must command that labor, every year, forever."*** ⛔ **Canon states the obligation and never names the party.** |
| **3** | ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ICE WORK** | `Step 3.6` | **The city stands mostly not on rock; the ice moves; no external party supplied a method.** ⛔ ***It is filed as `STANDING COST` — "re-leveling, mass balance and flow correction."*** **The city's largest continuous engineering effort has NO ADMINISTRATIVE EXISTENCE — it is booked as upkeep** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐ THE PATTERN ACROSS ALL THREE: **THIS CITY'S GOVERNMENT DOES NOT GOVERN ITS LOAD-BEARING ACTIVITIES.**
> **Not because it is weak, corrupt or absent — but because in each case the knowledge sits somewhere an office
> cannot reach:** *inside the maker at the moment of making; inside a seasonal obligation nobody was assigned;
> inside a practice misfiled as maintenance.*

## B.3 ⭐⭐ SO WHAT *IS* ADMINISTRABLE — and this is why the leverage is unusable

**Answer: the conversion, and only the conversion.** *Throughput, scheduling, the truck corridor, the port
calendar, the Institute's charter, the chamber count.* ⭐ **All of it real, all of it legitimate, and none of it
the thing the city is actually good at.**

> ### ***A city cannot bargain nationally with a chokepoint it does not administer.***
> **The chambers are leverage only if someone can credibly decide to slow them.** ⛔ **The decision that would
> matter — quality, integrity, whether a piece is right — lives in the trades and cannot be reached from a
> desk.** ⭐ ***The modest political profile is therefore not humility and not oversight. It is what a
> government looks like when its jurisdiction and its city's competence do not overlap.***

## B.4 LEGITIMACY AND SUCCESSION

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who decides?** | **An ordinary municipal administration**, competent within the conversion layer *(`B.3`)* |
| **By what legitimacy?** | ⭐ **The Jeju-do allocation** — canon's stated legacy: *"a claim secured by diplomacy before the exile era even began."* **This city's founding title is a DOCUMENT, not a settlement story** |
| **How does succession work?** | **Administratively, and it is uninteresting on purpose** — *the offices that turn over are the offices that were never load-bearing* |
| ⭐⭐ **Where does succession genuinely matter?** | ***In the trades*** — **because that is where the knowledge is, and it is transmitted person-to-person with no charter, no register and no office.** ⛔ **`7c` owns this, and it is the city's real continuity risk** |

## B.5 ⭐⭐ THE PROHIBITION — *derived, per `02` §3.3, and it has an author*

> **The spec:** *"a prohibition is a chosen policy with an author,"* distinct from the capability frame's
> grudging tolerance.

**The physics fixes what the one destructive act must be.** *If the only party ever positioned to know a piece's
integrity is its maker, at the moment of making, then the single act that empties the entire system is **someone
other than the maker vouching for the work.***

> # ⛔ ***YOU MAY NOT CERTIFY TO THE STANDARD ON ANOTHER'S BEHALF.***
> **Not a safety regulation. Not a guild rule.** ⭐ **The one prohibition a system with no inspector must have,
> because proxy-certification is indistinguishable from certification and destroys the only signal that exists.**

| Why this is a real prohibition and not a restatement of the physics | |
|---|---|
| **It forbids something people would otherwise plainly do** | *Vouching for a colleague is ordinary kindness, and here it is the one unforgivable act* |
| **It has an author** | **Each trade, on itself** — *the same body that enforces the Standard* |
| ⭐ **It is chosen** | *A city could instead have decided to accept proxy-certification and live with a degraded signal. This one did not* |
| ⭐⭐ **And it is cruel in a specific, non-obvious way** | ***It forbids the exact gesture that would relieve the closure problem `Phase 4` identified.*** **The one thing that would let a maker be told they got it right is the one thing prohibited** |

## B.6 ⚠ SANCTION PROPORTIONALITY — **the hard check, run BEFORE writing the penalty**

> **The spec:** *"price it in this location's physical conditions… Ask what it physically costs the excluded
> person HERE, and whether this place would actually pay that price for that offense."*

| Step | This location |
|---|---|
| **What does physical exclusion cost here?** | ⭐ **Survivable.** **Hwy 4 runs Mawson → here → onward; there is a port and an airstrip.** *A person expelled from this city can leave and live.* ⛔ **This is NOT an interior city where exclusion is a death sentence** |
| **So is expulsion the sanction?** | ⛔ **No — and it would be disproportionate.** *The offense is professional; the city has no mechanism to expel anyway, because the Standard has no office* |
| ⭐⭐ **What CAN be withdrawn?** | ***Recognition.*** **The trade stops counting your work.** *No hearing, no register to be struck from, no notice — the work simply stops being treated as work anyone will build on* |
| ⚠ **Is THAT proportionate?** | ⭐ **In principle yes — it lands on the only party who could have known.** ⛔ **In practice it is severe past proportion, for a reason the pass already established: `Step 1` found the Standard *"can condemn but cannot pardon."* There is no route back, because there is no office to readmit you** |

> ### ⛔⛔ **THE SANCTION IS PERMANENT NOT BY CHOICE BUT BY THE SAME ABSENCE THAT MAKES THE STANDARD WORK.**
> ⭐ *The city did not design an unappealable penalty. It has a judgment system with no appellate layer because it has
> no first instance either.* ⚠ **This is the single harshest thing in the pass, and it is worth writing plainly
> rather than softening: a person can lose their standing here and never be told by whom.**

⚠ **AND THE COUNTERWEIGHT, because `NO FORCED FIT` cuts both ways:** *this sanction is RARE.* **A system with no
inspector also has no enforcement apparatus generating cases.** ⭐ **Withdrawal of recognition requires the trade
to actually notice, agree, and act — which is slow, informal, and mostly does not happen.** ⛔ ***Do not write a
city full of the professionally condemned. Write a city where it is possible, known, and seldom.***

> **Denied jurisdiction over quality by physics, over the corpus by vocabulary, and over the seasonal labor by
