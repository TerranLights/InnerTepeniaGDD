### Panel A · the six Flat Archetypes

**The Child** (a born-here girl, about nine). *Asks: what am I not allowed to do here, and what happens if I do it anyway?*

- **S8C-201** · *"Nobody tells me no. I can walk off the rock if I want; the ice is what says no. In March the path I ran on in February is a sheet of ice, and that's the telling.
