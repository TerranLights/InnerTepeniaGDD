## The axis

**Claims without an arbiter.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could:* any place with several claimants and one resource has a priority question. What passes is the **pairing at Mirny's own values.** Nearly everything Mirny's economy does is owed to claimants outside the city and beside one another — the subnet's contact with itself (`SPEC` L184), materials for the eastern roads (`SPEC` L174), raw material for the chamber chain (`SPEC` L176), the inward sea supply (`SPEC` L143) — **while what would arbitrate between them is, in every admitted file, undefined (the treaty draft: "Nothing in this Article shall be read to define what authority sits above such regional groupings"), unwritten (no council, grouping or office is admitted), or placed where the claim cannot reach it (the road's hybrid authority governs the road, not the quarry).** And where the clock of a call is shorter than any authority's distance, the call falls to whoever is there, and the compact (`P6` P6-1) books the outcome to that person alone — which is also why creating an arbiter for the wind, the record or a crossing would give a loss a second answerer and change what Mirny is (`P6` P6-18). ## Sanction proportionality — a hard check (`00d`, generalized; `SPN3` L939–944) *[IND + D]*
*Before any exclusion or penalty is written it is **priced in Mirny's physical conditions, both bodies**. Exposure is a gradient and a preparation problem, never a gate; robots are ordinarily clothed (`RP` L38–57). What an exclusion removes is the **means of preparation** — shelter, a charging place — not a body's fitness. **Conditions used:** mean annual −11.2 °C; average night lows below 0 °C in all twelve months (−4.6 °C in January to −20.2 °C in August); record low −36.5 °C; winds averaging 10–14 m/s with storms beyond 40 m/s; katabatic events that make "outdoor exposure dangerous" (`SPEC` L139) and wind chill that "can drive apparent temperatures far below ambient" (`SPEC` L142), "dangerous regardless of cold protection" (`SPEC` L131); for a robot, charging below freezing is one of "three bad options" (`CP` L30–37) — spend reserve to warm the cells, charge 10–20 times slower and exposed throughout, or take "PERMANENT maximum-capacity loss" — and capacity falls "down to 50–60% at −20 °C" (`CP` L63). Outside waiting beside a road is "not survivable" (`HWY` L363); the national node model has people wait inside.*
