## 4 · Verdict

**Why.** The brief names "what the language row says" as one of the contract's admission decisions. `DR-125` item (6) is new national canon, dated after the contract. The row's statement of what the dominant language is (§5 L375) now gives only part of that canon, the English-and-Japanese core, taken from an earlier source (L1527 as the contract cites it). **The pull toward NO CHANGE.** The national fact grounds no Casey finding, so one could hold that nothing admitted actually moves. The brief says to choose CHANGES in that case, and the row's words do have to change.
