## The axis

**Tiered work, positional calls.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could.* Any large place divides its labor into necessary, owed and chosen work, and any exposed place lets whoever is present make the urgent call. - **The plant and the work.** Founding skill came from "skilled exiles among the founding population itself" or was rediscovered from the station's records, research and equipment (`NNS` L23; `DR-25`, `DR-26`); the sorting variable (a tie, a class) is orthogonal to skill, so "a stock does not predict who ran the quarry" (`P2` L368). After the first generation the plant's knowledge has living teachers, Mirny's own — the first link of a Mirny-made practice has nobody behind it, so what it holds is what the founders learned by doing (`P2` P2-3). It passes person to person like any trade. *Act 1:* learned from the exiles' own institutional backgrounds and the record. *Act 2:* learned from people who learned it here.
- **The wind.** No teacher, no instrument: "a record teaches the picture, not the place." (`RES` L94). Unlike the plant it **has** living holders in Act 2 and still does not pass on, because the compact makes the reading private and no file makes teaching it anyone's work: "Mirny's knowledge of its own wind does not accumulate across lives" (`P6` L162).
- **Third order [D].** The national training arithmetic funds the transmissible half only: trade and technical training is baseline work, 5,676 units at Mirny's residents (P7-11), and the Register's 22 industries have no slot for reading a local hazard (P7-3). So the half of Mirny's knowledge that matters most to survival is the half no tier is arithmetically responsible for teaching.

### P7-23 · Qualification is national, elsewhere and cohort-bound for care; absent in the files for everything else *[ADM + IND]*
- **Care.** "Every city staffs its own clinicians, always." (`MED` L16); every medic, nurse, field responder and robot-care technician was made at one of three national institutes, **none at Mirny**; an institute of robotic care stands in the Mirny subnet (`MED` L76) — relation only. The institutes are residential and cohort-bound: "These are not schools people attend. They are places people are FROM, for a while." (`MED` L210); intake and graduation follow the seasonal transport windows (`MED` L199–201). Mirny's clinicians therefore arrive as members of a national alumni network, after years away (`MED` L136–139).
- **Trades.** "In an isolated city you cannot hire in a replacement welder" (`RGR` L92): the trade pipeline is baseline work, 4.2 per 1,000 residents (`D08` L365). - **Qualification.** No admitted line describes a certificate, an examination or a recognition at Mirny. The Borrowed Form's "verified entry" (`RES` §6; `P2` §D) is a candidate form only [RW via CORR], not adopted.

### P7-24 · The long-lived robot, the Mentor/Mentee bond, and what no file says *[IND + CORR + D]*
- **A person to ask.** "a long-lived robot is a person to ask, not an archive" (`P2` L225) because "there is no static snapshot to copy in the first place" (`RP` L183). "no file says anyone asks" (`P4` L151). Her standing is memory, not rank (P7-14).

### P7-25 · The unread cues: the part that can be written, and the part that cannot *[CORR + RW + D]*
- "The cues are honest; they are unread" (`RES` L30); the first sign of the dangerous wind can read as an improvement (`P3` P3-2).
- **Writable.** Drift geometry is fixed by height and wind direction, "it is the half of the wind that can be written down." (`RES` L20); drift ridges record the wind's direction — "a compass for the feet when the eyes fail" (`RES` L87) — "The instrument exists; its reader does not." (`RES` L125).
- **Not writable.** The reading in the moment: how much loose snow lies upwind, how many days since snowfall, which of three winds (`RES` §1, §5). The record carries the thresholds, not the judgment.

- **Third order [D].** Because the tacit half can only be taught person to person and nothing makes teaching it anyone's work, the writable half — drift geometry and ridge direction — is the only part a record could carry. It is exactly what an amended record or a taught reading would carry (P7-21): the two candidates are the same instrument, aimed at the one writable half of the deficit.

### P7-30 · The dissent is not a population — it sits inside each person *[D on CORR]* · Act: Act 1, the compact may not yet have set; Act 2, stable
**Is the contradiction between groups here, or inside each person? Inside each person.**
1. **No group is advantaged by the compact against another.** It applies by crossing, not by stock, body or tier; the one asymmetry is a difference of *cost* — "the compact's price falls harder on the body whose mistakes can leave a permanent mark" (`P6` P6-1) — not of standing.
2. **The aggrieved share no circumstance and no time.** Each loss is private and dispersed (`P4` P4-7); the blamed are "a condition", not a group.
3. **The only medium for an organizing voice is the relay**, held by the keepers whose own practice is silence about its weakness (`SPN` §6 #8).
4. **Nothing to organize around.** "no failure is ever entered against the record" (`P6` L182); no office exists to petition (`SPN` §6 #5).
5. **The contradiction is internal:** to own a reading one was never taught; to answer to no one for it; to trust a medium one cannot check; to want an answerer and be one's own.
**What follows.** (a) There is **no counterculture, only seeds** — practices a single person could start (read the ridges, write in the record, publish a call, take the rest) each of which changes the compact. (b) **Reform comes by instruments, one-to-one acts, not by movements:** the Mentor/Mentee bond and the amended copy are the carriers (P7-21, P7-24). (c) **The place can be neither reformed nor opposed from within in the factional sense, because there is nobody there to organize;** (d) the only levers outside the person are the regional and national registers (P7-13), which answer for nothing local (P7-16). (e) For play: no faction quests; the seeds are hooks for individuals. `P6` RF-5's mechanism ([RW]: a compact that puts all blame on the individual can grow its own dissenters) stays a mechanism, not a movement.
