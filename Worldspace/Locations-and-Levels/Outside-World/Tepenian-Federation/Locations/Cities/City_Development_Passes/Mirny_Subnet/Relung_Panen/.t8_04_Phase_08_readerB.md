## The differentiation axis

**Made for the crossing.**

*What the words carry.* What this place's own conditions demand of the things people make is the crossing between a warm interior that has no season and an outdoors that changes twice a year. **The interior has no season:** the export and the city's own food are lit indoors year-round (spec L258; `DR-96` item 4, rulings L1633; `18` L12, L19 `[DoI corroboration]`), so there is no harvest day (Phase 4 P4-3); the warm rooms and the robot half's charging rooms keep one climate in every month (Phase 3 P3-18; S3-27). **The outdoors is two places:** for nine to ten months it is still and freezing, a mean wind of 4.6 m/s measured (spec L143) with the monthly means below 0 °C from February to November (spec L180–189), so the ordinary crossing is cold without wind (Phase 4 P4-7); for three months it is a melt on bare rock, meltwater, saturated regolith and slush (spec L171–172, L203), the average day above freezing and the average night below it (spec L179–180, L190 `[computed, REQ-52]`). **So what the household makes and keeps is a kit for the crossing:** the coat, the gloves and the storm layer that go on and off at every door; boots for dry rock and grit, then for water by day and refrozen slush by morning; the curtain that makes a dark the summer sky does not give; and, for the robot half, a warm room to charge in before the cold takes her reserve. Food crosses too: fresh produce is injured just below freezing and the air is below freezing most of the year, so freshness has to be kept warm in transit while frozen things keep for free (S3-37, S3-40). *(Function first, `03` L993–994.)*

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: a still, freezing outdoors for most of the year (spec L143, L180–189; calms in 39% of central-oasis observations, L146 `[DEM]` `[PA-3]`), so the crossing is short and windless rather than a struggle against weather; a genuine melt on bare rock inside ice walls that turns the same crossing wet and refreezing for three months (spec L113–116, L171–172, L203); and a seasonless interior holding the city's food, its warmth and its robots' charge (spec L258; S3-27; Phase 4 P4-6).
