**P2-2 · What the composition sources say about their own limits, and what Phase 2 therefore cannot read from them.** *Merges P2A-2 (a) to (h), B §A.2 items 1-10, P2C-1's limits 1-11.*
- **(a) Long-run, not a moment:** "National communities are classified by tier based on long-run population share." (census L33). The tiers summarize the whole frame's accumulation; they describe neither the founding party, whose size is in no admitted line (Frame L723), nor any season's arrivals (A, B, C).
- **(b) The founding tag is a label, not a date:** "the tag says nothing about when people arrived" (census L33), and since `DR-88` the tag "now means a nation named as a founder in the Founding Register, and nothing else" (rulings L1555) (A, B, C).
- **(c) Weighting, not counts:** "The tiers and the city totals are the information." (rulings L472); within-tier gaps were randomized for realism. In-world the census is a headcount, and stating a figure as a population is fine, but "deriving a characterization from a within-tier gap is not" (rulings L473); "A weighted ranking is not a census." (runbook L2113). This phase uses the city totals, the tier sums and the largest share only (A, B, C).
- **(d) Robots:** "Robot figures apply the same proportions as the human population."
