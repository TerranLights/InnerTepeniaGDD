# A · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE NAMING TECHNIQUE — **run first, and it came back FULL**

> **"Search the location's existing canon for religious-register language attached to non-religious objects.
> A registry described as 'close to sacred' is a theological statement about a filing system, and it is already
> written down. Only if the search comes back empty do you generate from scratch."**

## A.1 THE SEARCH RESULT

**One sentence in this city's own `Specs/` carries FIVE register terms at once, and every one of them is
attached to an object with no devotional content whatsoever:**

| Register word | Attached to |
|---|---|
| **"guarded"** | ⭐ **an archive** |
| **"preserving"** | ⭐ **a historical run of schematic revisions** |
| **"integrity"** | **refined materials** |
| **"taken seriously"** | **stone, chamber output, and a filing cabinet, *"in the same register"*** |
| **"expectation"** | ***"an informally shared civic expectation each trade enforces on itself"*** |

⛔ **So the search does not come back empty, and nothing is generated from scratch.** ✅ **The unnamed
load-bearing thing is already written down and it is `"The Sinheung Standard."`**

## A.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE PROPOSITION — *"as one sentence a resident would agree with"*

**What would have to be true for *guarded · preserving · integrity · taken seriously* to make sense applied to
graded stone, examined chamber output, and an archive of somebody else's revisions?**

> # ***"What I make will be used by someone who cannot check it, long after I can be asked."***

**Every earlier finding is already inside that sentence:**

| From | |
|---|---|
| **`Phase 3`** | *Freeze-thaw failure is **deferred and displaced** — a flawed piece fails seasons later, in a wall somebody else built* |
| **`Phase 4`** | *So **a maker can never be told they got it right**, and there is no office to tell them* |
| **`Phase 5`** | *And the port they all depend on is re-cut annually by shared labor nobody individually owns* |

## A.3 ⭐⭐⭐ THE COUNTER-CHECK — **"the name must not be portable"**

⚠ **Stated generally — *"make things well because others depend on them"* — that is portable to any
manufacturing town, and would be written too abstractly.** ⭐ **This city's version is not portable, because of
what it makes:**

> ## ***THE CHAMBERS ARE THE APPARATUS A ROBOT COMES INTO EXISTENCE IN.***
> **So a flaw here is not a flaw in a product.** ⭐⭐ ***It is a flaw in somebody's beginning*** — **and per
> `Phase 3`, neither that person nor the maker will ever be able to trace it.**
> ⚠ **AND THE FOUNDING POPULATION WAS EXILED FOR A RELATIONSHIP WITH ROBOTS** *(`Phase 2`)*.
> ⛔ **Not adopted as causation — canon does not connect them.** ⭐ **But it is why the sentence cannot be
> lifted to another city: the stake here is specific, and it is who the work becomes.**

## A.4 ⛔⛔ AND IT IS A **STANDARD OF WORTH**, NOT A CREED — *the phase names this exact case*

> **"Where the phase instead finds a STAKE, a COMPACT, a DEBT or a bare STANDARD OF WORTH, write it as what it
> is. A shared proposition is not a creed, and promoting one into the other is the same forcing error wearing a
> third costume."**

✅ **Written as what it is: a standard of worth.** ⛔ **NOT promoted into a faith.**
⏸️ **And the phase's own note applies verbatim:** *a location's non-devotional standard **may later become the
seed of a faith, or may stay secular indefinitely** — both are legitimate and **the pass does not decide it**.*

# C · DEATH AND THE DEAD — **two different questions, and only one of them is deferred**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **THE MORTUARY QUESTION — still DEFERRED** | *`§C.8c`'s Phase 6 row: "the deferred mortuary question — **do not answer it**."* ✅ **Honored. This phase writes no local rite, no local disposition, and no local funerary trade** |
| ✅ **THE OSSUARY-RECLAMATION QUESTION — RULED 2026-09-07** | *A separate tension, stated as unsettled in `Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`, and **ruled by the developer while this phase was being written*** |

⚠ **Keeping them apart matters:** ***"what does this city do with its dead" is deferred; "may anyone melt down
an ossuary" is now answered, nationally.*** ⛔ **The second does not license the first.**

## C.1 What canon already establishes, and it is national rather than local

| | |
|---|---|
| **Robot death is EPISODIC and COLLECTIVE** | *"There is no senescence and no background attrition. They die when something kills them — many at once, or none for a long while"* |
| ⭐⭐ **Ossuaries, and they are MIXED** *(ruled 2026-09-07)* | ***Metal bones AND the bones of the humans who loved those robots, in one structure.*** *One disposition among several.* **"A permanent and permanently growing physical record… legible, countable, and never diminishing"** ⚠ *though the two materials keep different time* |
| **Handled by religion and community, not a trade** | *"You cannot sustain a profession on demand that vanishes for months at a time"* |

## C.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE OSSUARY QUESTION WAS RULED MID-PHASE — **and the ruling produces this city's sharpest counterpoint**

> ### ⭐ DEVELOPER RULING, 2026-09-07, given while this phase was being written:
> > ***"The reason why nobody would 'recycle' metal from ossuaries is because that metal is the bones of dead robots. They mine their metal from quarries, rather than stealing from the dead."***

**Canon had left this open** — *"is it sacred and untouchable, or is it a reserve of last resort? Neither answer
is settled."* ✅ **It is settled: SACRED, and the reasoning is not economic.** ⭐ ***An ossuary is not an
un-exploited reserve. It is a body.*** **The alternative to grave-robbing is extraction, and Tepenia takes the
extraction.**

## C.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND HERE IS WHAT IT MEANS **IN THIS CITY**

| | |
|---|---|
| **1** | **This city runs a QUARRY TRADE** — *canon names it as one of the Standard's three pillars: "the quarry trade's own graded stone"* |
| **2** | ⭐⭐ **So the extraction that makes leaving the dead alone possible is, here, an ordinary local trade** — ***not an abstraction. Somebody's job*** |
| **3** | ⭐⭐⭐ **And this city's shared measure of worth is *"the quality and physical integrity of REFINED MATERIALS."*** *It grades stone. It grades chamber output. It grades an archive* |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SO THE CITY THAT GRADES EVERYTHING HAS ONE THING IT WILL NOT GRADE.**
> ***A large, permanent, permanently growing body of metal sits inside a culture whose entire measure of worth is
> the physical integrity of metal — and it is exempt.*** ⛔ **Not because it is low grade. Because grading it
> would be the wrong act.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE 2026-09-07 MIXED-OSSUARY RULING MAKES THE EXEMPTION SHARPER STILL
> **A Tepenian ossuary holds the metal bones of robots *and the bones of the humans who loved them*, together.**
> ⭐ ***So what the Standard is exempted from is not a material. It is a relationship.***
> ⛔ **There is no way to assay the metal that is not disassembling the bond it was interred with** — **which is
> why the prohibition needs no enforcement here, in the one city equipped to do the assaying.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THAT IS THE SHARPEST AVAILABLE STATEMENT OF WHAT THE STANDARD IS **NOT**
> **`A.4` established the Standard is a standard of worth and not a creed.** ⭐ **This is why that distinction
> holds:** ***a creed would extend to the dead. The Standard stops at them.*** **It is a measure of work, and
> the dead are not work.**

⚠ **AND THE HONEST LIMIT:** *this is what the ruling means here; it does not make this city special.* ⛔ **The
prohibition is national and the quarry is ordinary.** ⭐ **What is local is only the collision — that the
exemption is most visible in the place that grades the most.**

## C.4 ⚠ AND "IS THE ABSENCE ITSELF THE FINDING?" — **answered honestly: NO, because the absence is not local**
⛔ *This city has no distinctive mortuary practice in canon, and neither does the corpus generally, because the
question is deferred nationally.* ⭐ **So the absence here is an artifact of a standing deferral, not a fact
about this place** — ***and a thin invented rite would be worse than this sentence.***

# D · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FAILURE STATE OF THE CORE VALUE — **the phase's finding**

**The central promise:** ***work here is judged by the physical integrity of what is made.***

## D.1 UNDER WHAT CIRCUMSTANCE DOES IT BECOME IMPOSSIBLE TO KEEP?

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **WHEN THE THING IS WRONG BY DESIGN.**
> **This city grades EXECUTION. It does not own the design** — *`Step 1`: "the underlying engineering credit
> belongs elsewhere."*
> ## ***So a chamber can meet the Standard perfectly and still be wrong, and the Standard cannot see it.***
> ⛔ **A flawless build of a flawed schematic passes every test this city has.**

## D.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT GREW UP AROUND THAT CIRCUMSTANCE — **and canon already named it**

> ## **THE ARCHIVE.**
> ***"The guarded technical archive preserving the full historical run of `Mark IV` schematic REVISIONS."***

⭐⭐ **A manufacturing city keeping every version of a design it does not own is otherwise a strange thing to
do.** ⭐⭐⭐ **As an answer to the failure state it is exactly the right thing to do:**

> ### ***If you cannot govern the design, you can at least keep every version of it — so that when a fault finally shows, you can say WHICH REVISION PRODUCED IT.***
> ⭐ **The archive is not sentiment and it is not deference.** ***It is the only instrument available against the
> one failure the Standard structurally cannot catch.***

⚠ **And it re-reads `Step 3`'s open question rather than closing it.** *Canon does not say whose revisions those
are.* ⭐ **Under this reading the archive's PURPOSE is legible either way** — *a record of another city's
changes is still the evidence you need to place a fault.* ⏸️ **`DRQ` candidate stands.**

## D.3 ⭐⭐⭐ DOES THE CULTURE RITUALIZE THE FAILURE, OR REFUSE TO? — ***it refuses, and the refusal is the stronger half***

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔ **There is no rite for *"we built it right and it was wrong anyway."*** | *`Phase 4` established there is no closure mechanism at all — no verdict, no acquittal, no occasion* |
| ⭐⭐ **What exists instead is a FILING SYSTEM** | ***The city answers its deepest failure with record-keeping rather than with ceremony*** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THAT IS CHARACTERIZATION, NOT A GAP
> **A place that meets its central impossibility with an archive rather than a rite is making a claim about what
> would actually help.** ⚠ ***It is not consoling anybody. It is trying to make the fault findable.***
