# ⭐⭐⭐ HEADLINE FINDING

> # ***Davis's ordinary day is organized around getting somewhere warm before you stop — and for fifty-five days a year the whole population observes a night that is not there.***

**Three canon legs, none inferred:**

1. ⭐ **The shared night is national canon, and it is a mechanism, not a mood.** *"Robots do NOT work 100% of the
   time. Not remotely… they require downtime, most prominently OVERNIGHT RECHARGING."* ⇒ ***"Which gives Tepenia
   a shared night. Robots and humans stop at the same time, for different reasons. A city where everyone sleeps
   is a very different place from one where half the population works through the dark."***
2. ⛔⛔ **Stopping is the dangerous act — for half the population.** ***"A working robot generates heat. That heat
   is exactly what keeps its cells in usable range and its lubricants liquid… Stopping is the dangerous act."***
   **And the stop is also when charging happens, and *"charging a cold cell below freezing causes lithium
   plating — permanently reducing capacity. This is irreversible damage, not a temporary penalty."***
   ⇒ ***The mandatory nightly halt is simultaneously the thermally worst moment of a robot's day and the moment
   the irreversible damage is available.*** **Heated interior space at night is therefore not comfort. It is the
   condition of not accumulating permanent loss.**
3. ⭐⭐ **And Davis's own light cycle removes the cue for a substantial part of the year.** **Midnight sun ~55
   days** *(≈ Nov 25 → Jan 18)*; **polar night ~37 days** *(≈ Jun 4 → Jul 10)*; **two months at non-negative
   monthly means.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ WHICH MAKES THE FINDING THIS, AND IT IS ABOUT AN ORDINARY TUESDAY
> ***For roughly fifty-five days a year the sun does not set, the thermal deadline substantially relaxes, and
> the entire population stops anyway — together, on a schedule that nothing outside is enforcing.***
> ⭐ **The shared night at Davis is, for a meaningful stretch of the year, a pure convention: an agreement about
> when to stop, observed by a city that has no external reason to stop then.**
> ⛔ **And for ~37 days it is the inverse — a dark with no day inside it, on the same schedule.**
> ⚠ **Stated as structure.** ⛔ **What anyone at Davis calls this, feels about it, or does with it is Phase 6's
> and Phase 9's, not written here.**

⚠⚠ **NATIONAL / LOCAL SPLIT, OBSERVED.** **The shared night, the recharging requirement, the plating risk and
the inversion are ALL NATIONAL — true in every Tepenian city, therefore never a differentiator.** ⭐ **What is
Davis's own is the specific cycle they land on: a 55-day stretch with no darkness to anchor the stop, a 37-day
stretch with no daylight to anchor the start, and two months in which the cold penalty substantially lifts.**

## B.1 · ROUTINES — **the return, and the garments it requires**

- ⭐ **Dressing for outside is universal and unremarkable.** **Clothing is *"ordinary, near-universal, and both
  functional and fashionable"*; robots wear heavy winter coats and winter gloves; *"she just needs to be
  prepared for the weather, just like humans do."*** ⛔ **Nothing here is written as a body gated out by
  weather — exposure is PREPARATION, the same category it is for a human** *(`M-151`)*.
- ⭐⭐⭐ **And the garment trade-off lands precisely on Davis's own defining practice.**
  > ***"A human-shaped hand in a glove has a human-shaped hand's problem: insulation and dexterity trade against
  > each other. Heavier gloves cost fine motor control and tactile feedback; thin high-gauge gloves keep
  > precision and cost warmth."***
  **`02` §3.1 establishes Davis's characteristic authority as a dated observation, and its argument as *show me
  the entry*.** ⇒ ***Davis's civic instrument is a written record, and writing it outdoors is a
  dexterity-versus-warmth decision taken by hand, in the cold, repeatedly.*** **The city's method has a glove
  problem.**
  ⛔ **No notation practice, tool or convention is invented** — the constraint is stated; the solution is
  Phase 8's.
- **The day has real daylight structure for most of the year** — and for ~55 days none, and ~37 days none of the
  other kind *(headline)*.

## B.2 · MUNDANE CONCERNS — **charge state, and where you will be when you stop**

- ⭐ **This is the ordinary-life form of the cold file, and it is a daily arithmetic rather than an emergency.**
  **At Davis's mean of −10.0 °C, ambient capacity reduction runs *−20–30% near freezing*; in the coldest months
  at about −21 °C it falls toward the file's *50–60% at −20 °C* band.** ⚠ **Reversible — *"the tank is smaller
  while you are cold"*** — **but it is smaller every cold day.**
- **And peak output sags independently:** *"a cold robot cannot lift, sprint or strike as hard as a warm one at
  identical charge."* ⇒ **three numbers, not one, and two of them are down all winter.**
- ⛔ **The mundane concern is therefore not "will I freeze." It is *how much of me is available today, and will I
  be somewhere warm when I stop.*** ⭐ **A question with an answer that changes with the month, asked by half the
  population, every day.**
- ⚠ **For the human half the cold is a different axis entirely** — ordinary human cold exposure, and the same
  coats and gloves. ⛔ **Not collapsed into one account** *(Process C)*.

## B.3 · PERSONAL STRUGGLES — **a smaller tank, and a permanent scar available**

- ⭐⭐ **The struggle canon actually supplies is the gap between the two penalties.** ***A temporary squeeze and a
  permanent scar on the same bar*** — capacity returns with warmth; **plating never does.** ⇒ ***"The interesting
  play is in never letting the first force you into the second."***
- ⭐⭐⭐ **And Davis's own numbers put the second within reach on an ordinary bad day, not only in a crisis.**
  **Embrittlement's own worked example uses Davis's exact annual mean as its safe end — *"a knock that dents at
  −10 °C cracks at −50 °C"* — and Davis's April record of −41.8 °C sits past the ~−40 °C threshold at which
  *"standard greases exhibit properties similar to a solid, with catastrophic results."***
  ⚠ **A record extreme is not a typical day and is not written as one.** ⭐ **What it establishes is that
  Davis's envelope reaches the regime where the failure TYPE changes, not merely its amount** — **and Phase 3
  already found April to be the year's real edge, by a completely independent route.**
- ⚠⚠ **AND THE STRUGGLE WITH NO DETECTOR — flagged, not ruled.** **Canon: *"a robot that spends all its time
  working is virtually guaranteed to go insane. Leisure is not a perk… it is a condition of remaining sane,"*
  and the file flags that sustained overwork may sit close to the *faulty semantic signal* model of robot
  illness — *"labor conditions are a medical question. Flagged as a connection, not yet ruled."***
  ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **Set that against Davis's own established shape — obligations that are *"permitted, unpoliced, and
  charged to the individual,"* and a characteristic failure that is LAPSE with *"no moment at which anyone finds
  out."*** ***A city whose every obligation has no failure signal is a city where the one hazard canon calls
  medical would arrive without one either.***
  ⛔⛔ **NOT ASSERTED AS A DAVIS FINDING.** **The physiology file has not ruled the overwork/gel-brain link, and
  this phase does not rule it for them.** **The structural coincidence is recorded; the claim is not made.**
  Hole `H24`.

# E · PROCESS E — WHAT THE ORDINARY RHYTHM FAILS TO PROVIDE

> *"Ask what the ordinary rhythm fails to provide for people whose lives do not fit it."*

1. ⭐⭐ **It provides no occasion.** **The bars exist and the drinking institution does not; every national
   pleasure is available and none has been made into a rite.** ⇒ ***For a person who needs a scene — a night
   that is ABOUT something — Davis supplies the venue and not the reason.***
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **It provides no signal that you are falling behind.** **`02` §4.1's LAPSE is the city's characteristic
   failure and it has *"no moment at which anyone finds out."*** ⇒ ***The ordinary day at Davis will not tell
   you, or anybody else, that you have stopped managing it.*** **Work that is charged to the individual, an
   obligation with no completion state, and a rhythm with no checkpoint.**
3. ⭐⭐ **It provides no counterparty.** **`02` §5.2: *"Nobody at Davis has ever stood in front of a person who
   could have said no and did."*** ⇒ **For someone who needs a decision made about them — an admission, a

   exists and has no record of use.)*
4. ⚠ **And for the robot half specifically, it provides no margin on a bad month.** **Capacity is down all
   winter, output sags, and the one decision that would buy time — charging cold at full rate — is the one that

# F · BAND VARIANCE — Band 5

> *"Band 5–6: there is no single ordinary day either… ask instead what is common to ALL the ordinary days across
> the distribution, which is usually a very short list and is exactly the interesting part."*

⭐ **The list is short, and it is the phase:**

1. ⭐⭐ **Everybody stops at the same time** — *the shared night*, national canon.
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **And at Davis, for ~55 days a year, they stop without a darkness to stop for, and for ~37 days they
   stop without a daylight to have stopped from.**
