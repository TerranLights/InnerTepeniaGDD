## The differentiation axis

**Made for it, chose it, or born to it: how a person came to be here sorts this city's people before kind does.**

A robot built here was wanted before she existed, for a need the place had (Phase 2 P2-16; physiology L424). A later human arrival elected the place, the long way, by the spur (Phase 2 P2-10, P2-13).
