## The differentiation axis

**Membership by keeping, not by kind; kind divides only at the table, the night and the death.**

*What the words carry.* The line that sorts this city's people is not kind and not origin: it is whether a person has kept through a year in this place, and that line runs through both halves. On this place's own lines, belonging is "knowledge of the year, not a speech marker" (Phase 8 L310); "one belongs by having kept a year, not by being received" (Phase 6 L154); for a human arrival, membership in practice is one full water year lived through, unmarked (Phase 5 P5-24, PROPOSED, conditional); for an arriving robot, a regular warm charging room she returns to; and "the built-here robot never converts, having never been outside" (Phase 5 L237) *(B, C)*. **The way a person came sets where she starts on that line** *(A's modes, as the way in; B's joint form; C)*: the born-here human and the built-here robot keep from the start (Phase 6 hf6-7); the elector starts in her first year, new only to the year (Phase 5 P5-22); an arriving robot starts at her first warm room. **Kind still divides people in three places only** *(C; A, B)*: **the table** (the human half eats the city's own food; the robot half is sustained through the same doors the food leaves by, P9-26; Phase 8 P8-21), **the night** (one sleeps, the other charges warm and dreams, every month, P9-13, P9-33) and **the death** (continuous for one half, episodic for the other, P9-20). The short form "Kept together, fed apart" is C's Round 1 wording, which A and B now hold as the lead; the written form keeps its content and the lines' own word, membership (split 1).

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It rests on this place's water year (the drains cleared before the melt, the melt's morning ice and afternoon water, the refreeze), its made night under a sky with no polar night, and a cold that makes a robot's every charge a warm room's question in every month `[computed, REQ-52]`, and on a food vocation whose eaters are only one half. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** the human–robot baseline (physiology L377, national); being built in one's home city (Ch. 14 L35–36, national, P9-10); the modes of coming in themselves (born here, elected, built here: canon's general sorting, P9-3); origin kept in households in a many-origin place (P9-5). The axis is **PROPOSED and conditional as P5-24 is** (its gates: amendment G7-C1 and Phase 4 P4-21, both PROPOSED; where charging rooms stand, Phase 4 REQ-115), and it is written on DQ-53's PROVISIONAL answer. It goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.
