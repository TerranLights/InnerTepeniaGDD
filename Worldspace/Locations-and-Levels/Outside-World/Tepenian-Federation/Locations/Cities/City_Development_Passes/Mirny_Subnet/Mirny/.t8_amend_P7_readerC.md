## 7 · The sanction check, re-run (P7-38, P7-39)

**The check** (`SPN3` L942-947): price any exclusion or penalty "in this location's physical conditions", and never scale one person's temperament into a civic sanction. **Is guilt a sanction under the check? No.** The check prices penalties that someone imposes — exclusion, expulsion, confinement, denial. The ruling's guilt is "deep and immense personal guilt" (L1116): **personal** by the ruling's own word, arising in the person who was most able and did not go, and attached to an act that is explicitly not a legal obligation ("not as a result of any sort of 'legal obligation'", L1116). **What it costs each body.**
