## 4 · The axis

# **What stays, what refills.**

*Reached independently by readers A and C. Reader B's "Kept unseen, surfaces refill" is carried as the third order (P10-1). The label is a developer question (Q-89).*

**What it answers that no earlier Mirny phase answered.** `P3` asked what the place feels like; `P8` what it makes for itself; `P9` which kinds of people it distinguishes. **Phase 10 asks what is actually *there* — and the files sort it by one property: whether a thing outlasts the next storm.** The landmarks that stay — the ring, the core, the harbor, the relay hardware, the quarries, the inherited station and its record — are, without exception, things **nobody living at Mirny made** or things **made for someone else**; everything residents make for themselves — a cleared approach, a drift moved, a path reopened — is remade after every storm and leaves nothing to point at (`P8` P8-11: *"Mirny's own making for itself is upkeep, and upkeep leaves nothing to show"*). Names, where they ever come, can only attach to the first class: *"banks refill and move, while the ring, the core, the harbor, the relay and the quarries do not"* (`P3` L421).

**Third order (reader B).** The lasting things have no admitted face and are noticed only when they fail (`P6` P6-4); the visible things are surfaces the wind rewrites. **The things that stay cannot be seen; the things that can be seen do not stay.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could:* every snowy place has drifts that refill. What passes is the pairing at Mirny's own values: **its lasting landmarks are a medium no one here authored and works whose product leaves, while its residents' own making is the kind that refills.** Delete every other place's name from *a city whose standing landmarks are a record and a relay none of its people wrote or built and quarries whose stone leaves, while everything its people make for themselves is undone by the next storm* — the claim survives; it was never relational. **Not checked against any sibling** (terminal check).
**The column it would fill** (write-only, `SPN3` L146–147): Mirny's own column of the differentiation instrument, the Catalog row — *What stays, what refills.* No other location's row was read. *(The table write is carried by the merge; the brief allowed readers one output file.)*
