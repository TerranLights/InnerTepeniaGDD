## 5 · The reconciliation

*Union of the three readers' tables (M-246), one row per claim cluster; each cell carries a bare pointer, a short tag and a paraphrase, never a quoted string. Row numbers follow the finest-grained table (`LC-1`…, `LRC-1`…, `ENN-1`…); where another reader split or joined a cluster, the Credit cell names the reader, that reader's row, and the split. Dispositions come from the closed set `MATCH · BOTH-TRUE · CONTRADICTION · CULTURE-ONLY · OUT OF FRAME` (PASS-ONLY material is not row-listed here; it is ranked in §11). **Where the readers' dispositions differ, the row says so and the merged disposition is the most conservative one** — it concedes nothing to a Tier 3 file and gives no corroboration the pass did not earn. *Corroboration* means a match; it is never independent confirmation, and the row says what makes it non-independent. "Delete-the-name" is run on every comparative line: what survives is reconciled, what collapses is OUT OF FRAME.*
