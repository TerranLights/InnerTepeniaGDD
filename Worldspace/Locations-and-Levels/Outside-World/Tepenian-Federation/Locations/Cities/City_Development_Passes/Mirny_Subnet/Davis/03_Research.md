# 1 · ⭐⭐ PICK 1 — THE ABSENT INSTRUMENT OF REFUSAL

> ### **The instrument of refusal EXISTS in isolated communities. It is routinely NOT COMMUNAL.**

**B reached the primary legal texts** of the most isolated permanent civilian community on Earth *(Entry Control

# 4 · PICK 4 — THE PHYSICAL SITE

## 4.1 ⭐⭐⭐ THE SALT / FROST QUESTION — **settled by MECHANISM** · *changed a finding*

**The site carries a real, named boundary — a *salt line*** — dividing *"an area to the east that is rich in
vegetation and with relatively unweathered bedrock"* from *"an area to the west where physical weathering of the
metamorphic basement creates abundant sand."* **And: *"tafoni were only found in areas of the most salt-weathered
bedrock."***

**B supplied the reason:**

> ### ***Ice segregation requires the −3 to −8 °C window AND liquid water. "Unavailable moisture limits damage regardless of temperature." These soils are hyperarid; the lakes run 4–235 g/L, one at ~270.***
> # ⭐ **THE FROST REAGENT IS SCARCE. THE SALT REAGENT IS SUPERABUNDANT.**

⛔ **So the 60 days a year that cross zero are OCCASIONS, NOT DAMAGING CYCLES** — ***crossing 0 °C is not the
window.*** ⭐ **The missing variable was never the temperature. It was the water.**

### ⚠ Two caveats the researcher owned rather than being caught with
1. **The salt-transport threshold in the source is `>10 m/s`; Davis's canon figure is a `~5.6 m/s` MEAN.**
   ⛔ ***Mean ≠ peak; gust data unstated*** — `M-158` applied to its own corroboration.
2. **That paper's primary subject is a different oasis.**

## 4.2 ⭐⭐⭐ THE CAUSAL UNIFICATION — ⏸️ **HELD AS A HYPOTHESIS, NOT A FINDING**

**Combining all three independent source sets:** *lakes saline near the coast, fresh toward the plateau* · *wind
raising salt aerosol **from those marine inlets and saline lakes*** · *tafoni only in the most salt-weathered
bedrock* · **and varves forming only in fresh or brackish water, requiring anoxia.**

> # ***The saline basins are the source of the salt that eats the rock — and they are the same basins that cannot keep an annual ledger.***
> ### ***The archive-keeping half of the water and the sound-bedrock half of the land are the same half.***

⭐⭐ **And note what that rhymes with, reached from water chemistry rather than from obligation structure:**
`02_Spine.md` §4.1's **loud half / quiet half** — *the things that report their own failure, and the things that
do not.* ***Two independent routes to the same shape.***

> ## ⛔ WHY THIS IS NOT YET A FINDING — the researcher's own caveat, and it is decisive
> ***The salinity gradient is stated COAST→PLATEAU. The salt line is stated WEST|EAST.***
> # **No source says they are the same axis.**
> ⏸️ **Named test, unread by any researcher: `Adamson & Pickard 1986` / `Gore et al. 1996`.**
> ⭐ *A finding this good, held open because one axis identity is unsourced, is the discipline working.*
