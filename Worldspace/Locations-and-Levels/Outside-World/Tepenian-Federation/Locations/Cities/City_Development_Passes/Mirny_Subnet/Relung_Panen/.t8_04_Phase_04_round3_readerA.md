## 5 · Where the readings differ, and which view I now hold

**The three wordings:** A "A clock-kept stop"; B "The made stopping hour"; C "Made night, water year".

**What all three share:** the day is organized around the shared stop (physiology L336–338). The stop is kept by schedule, closing hour and darkened room (S3-28, L285), because the sky does not keep it: spec L151–156 and L179–190.

**Where they differ:**
