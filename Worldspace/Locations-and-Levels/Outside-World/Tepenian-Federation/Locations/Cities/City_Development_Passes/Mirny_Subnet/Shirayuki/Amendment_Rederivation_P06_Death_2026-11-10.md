The consequence sits outside the deferred block, and canon marks it "A place for a pass or a questline to find" (L99).
- P06_Death.32. So for a city whose standard is "how well it is kept" (Meaning L64), the dead, in the one practice canon describes, sit where that standard is not what decides.
