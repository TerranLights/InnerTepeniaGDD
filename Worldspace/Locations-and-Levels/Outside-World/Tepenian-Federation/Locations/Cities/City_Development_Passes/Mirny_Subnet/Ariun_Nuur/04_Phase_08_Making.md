**Making here starts from three conditions every maker meets: nothing can be dug or grown, so what is made is made of what the road brings; the cold keeps what it is given; and every act of making that needs warmth, liquid or light draws on the one supply.** (The differentiation axis, with its peer-free test, is at D.) *[C; narrowed in merge to the forms the union supports]*
