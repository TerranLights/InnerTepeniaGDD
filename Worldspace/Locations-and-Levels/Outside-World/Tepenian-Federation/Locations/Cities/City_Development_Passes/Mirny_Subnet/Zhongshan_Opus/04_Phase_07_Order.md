## 7d.2 — The specific pressure, named before anyone is assigned to it

> **The spine: *"Derive from the specific pressure, never from generic rebellion. What does this place require
> of everyone, and who cannot or will not give it?"***

**Two demands, and they compound:**

|---|---|

> ## **Who cannot give that? The one whose work is neither schedulable nor transferable to the next qualified person.**

## 7d.3 — ⭐⭐⭐ And canon already names them: the artisan class — which is the RARE second form

**`04_Phase_06`:402 handed this forward as canon: *the artisan class as a recognized civic institution*
(`Specs/` L173).** **`16` §18 supplies the economics: `Industrial/manufacturing 25%` = `127,531` workers,
*"precision manufacturing, with the craft ethic applying to industrial output as much as to art."***

⚠ **`00b` again, and it matters most here: the artisan class is NOT that sector.** **The sector is precision
manufacturing at scale** — SOC archetypes 3 *(skilled manual trade)*, 14 *(precision technical support)* and 1
*(supervisor/foreman)*, ordinary shift work in ordinary shops, producing ordinary output including the boron
line. **The recognized artisan institution is a scoped specialization inside it**, and the whole finding
depends on it being small.

**Now check it against the spine's three options.** The spine warns that a counterculture is usually written as
a **refusal**, and that the second form — ***"perform what the place cannot"*** — is *"rarer, more interesting,
and much likelier to be missed,"* and instructs: **check for it deliberately wherever the capability reading
has named a faculty the place lacks.**

> **The faculty this place lacks was named in Phase 6: *"A thing that works does not need someone's name on
> it."*** **The city's own civic standard is authorless competence.**
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ARTISAN SIGNS THE WORK.**
>
> **That is not a refusal of the city's standard. It is the performance of something the standard cannot
> produce** — and it is the second form exactly, found where the spine said to look for it.

### ⭐⭐ And the city LICENSED it. That is the finding.

**The artisan class is not tolerated, hidden, or marginal. It is a *recognized civic institution*, in canon, in
the city's own spec.**

> ## **A city whose standard is that working things need no author maintains, at public expense and under its own name, the one institution whose entire purpose is to attach an author to a made thing.**
>
> ## ⭐⭐ **The counterculture here is not opposed to the mainstream. It is the mainstream's own written exception to itself** — and nobody appears to have noticed that it is one.

**And it is the one authored thing in a city of authorless allocations.** ⭐ **In a place where you cannot bring
a case against the wind, the meter, the season or the rock, the maker's mark is the only signature that appears
anywhere at all.** *That is why the institution is load-bearing far out of proportion to its size, and why it
would be a mistake to write it as decoration.*

## 7d.4 — ⭐ Is the dissent a population, or is it inside each person?

> **The spine's fourth check, and it insists on being asked.**

**Both — and the relationship between the two halves is the useful part.**

- **Inside each person:** every one of the `127,531` workers in precision manufacturing faces the same daily
  question, because the craft ethic canon assigns to the sector *"applies to industrial output as much as to
  art."* **The tension is in the work itself, not between camps.** There is nothing to join and nobody to
  oppose.
- **As a population:** ⭐ **the recognized artisan institution is where the inside-each-person version acquired
  an address.** It is the small number who made the question their public identity, in a city that agreed to
  let them.

> ### **So this city is neither of the spine's two clean cases.** It is not factional, and it is not the
> unreformable place where *"there is nobody to organize."* **It is the third thing: a city that took a
> contradiction sitting inside everybody and gave it an institution to live in.**
>
> ⭐ **Which is why there is no dissent movement here and also no suppression.** **The pressure has somewhere to
> go, and the place it goes is a civic body with a budget.**
