### Reasoning

**Leg 1, "this city is not among [the designated gateway nodes]", no longer covers the table.** It is still true of the Australia
row (L129). But the same table now names this city's cluster as a Tepenian receiving node on the neutral transshipment route
(L130), for inbound raw materials, though not as the principal node. The table's own column heading is "Tepenian receiving
node". The pass's own word for that column is "designated gateway nodes". So in the pass's own terms the cluster is now a
designated node, a secondary one, on one route.
*And what does that cause?* The pass's sentence "Being served by a shared harbor on a coastal supply line is not being a
gateway" needs a narrower form: this city is not a direct Australian gateway node and not the neutral route's principal node,
but its shared harbor is a named, non-principal receiving node for inbound raw materials.
*And what does that cause?* Does that make the city a trade hub? No. The flow is inbound only, the node is not the principal,
and `16` §18 plus the corrected spec L180 both say "not a trade hub". **So the direction holds; the scope and the stated ground
narrow.** The closing line "Consistent on all three; recorded so it is not re-litigated" no longer stands as written: a
fourth source line (L130), ruled after the pass, has to be brought into the reconciliation.

**Leg 2, the shared constructed port, holds.** L202 still gives the cluster the shared Tri-Cities Port at Nella Fjord, long
haul and seasonal. Only the address and label details are new.

**Leg 3, "NOT a trade hub", holds, and is stronger:** the spec's L180 now carries the same ruling (change 6).

**Phase 7, "served by Australia out of [the two ports]", holds as one route but is now a partial description.** The
relationship database (L495, unchanged) still says the city's raw materials are staged via the two Australian ports, and
`Ports` L229-230 still model direct distances from both. The first port is now "ruled primary", the second "canon staging",
and the second is 60 km nearer (a label and a figure). But L130 adds a second, indirect inbound route for the cluster, through
the neutral transshipment hubs.
*And what does that cause?* L130's general clause says that route gives supply "without a direct bilateral relationship with
either mainland power". This city's cluster is absent from Australia's direct row (L129). So the inputs leave it open whether
this city has a direct Australian link at all. The relationship database's own wording, "as part of the [subnet's] coastal
supply line", fits both readings: Australian freight staged onward along the coast, or a direct sailing. The inputs do not
decide between them, and I do not decide it here (U3-Q1).
*And what does that cause?* Either reading supports the pass's "not an Australian gateway", and neither touches the inbound
direction. So this moves the description of the import route, not the verdict's direction.

**The Tower-route exclusion (L424-425) is untouched.** It rests on the Tower design document's distance from this junction to
the Tower site. That document is not on my list, and nothing on my list bears on it. The rows in the Ports file do not change
which coast carried Tower material. Nothing I read moves this part.

**The extra terminals (change 3) could change only a label.** If the block is canon, this city's supply might be staged through
a nearer terminal than either port the pass names. That would change "out of [the two ports]" (a label), not the direction
(U3-Q2).

**The other bullets in the cited span:** L323-324's figure changes from 706 to 881 lines, and its point, that the other file's
"empty" claim is stale, holds all the more. L325-330, the Hwy 22 hitchhiking list, rests on `Highways.md`, which is not on my
list. I cannot test it, and none of the note's U3 inputs bears on it.

**Why not HOLDS:** more than a figure or a label changes. One leg of the stated ground ("this city is not among them") and the
reconciliation's claim to be complete ("Consistent on all three; ... not re-litigated") no longer follow from the current table.
**Why not FLIPS:** the city is still absent from Australia's direct row. It still shares a seasonal, long-haul harbor. It is
still "not a trade hub" by `16` §18 and spec L180. And its corridor still carried no Tower material, as far as anything on my
list shows.
