# 5e · DEPENDENCY — ⭐⭐ **and the four-month window is the whole of it**

**`Ports.md`: the Tri-Cities port is `CONSTRUCTED` tier — *"rebuilt or cut open, every year," "paid, forever"*
— with a window of roughly **four months, Nov–Mar**.**

> ⛔ ***"Berthing order decides whose cargo lands this year and whose waits a full year. That is not a
> scheduling matter; it is an economy."***

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE WINDOW HOLDS **THREE** WORKLOADS, FOR ONE PHYSICAL REASON

| `Nov–Mar` holds | |
|---|---|
| **1** | ⭐ **the entire year's sea freight** |
| **2** | **the compulsory ground work** — *drainage against meltwater; ice re-levelling* |
| **3** | **the elective surface care** — *thin gloves, no scratching, light to see by* |

⭐⭐ **They do not merely overlap.** ***They are the same window for the same reason: it is the season the ice
permits a ship and the season light and low wind coincide*** *(`04_Phase_03` §E.1)*.

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THREE WORKLOADS, ONE WINDOW — AND ONLY ONE OF THEM CAN SLIP.**
> **Freight has an absolute deadline: the window closes and you wait a year.** **Ground work has a physical
> one: the meltwater arrives whether you are ready or not.** ⭐⭐ ***The upkeep is the only deferrable thing in
> the city's year.***
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐ AND IT IS ALSO THE THING THAT PUNISHES DEFERRAL HARDEST.
> **The damage accrues fastest in the dark season, unobserved** *(`04_Phase_03` §E.1)*. ***So what gets
> postponed in a busy summer is repaid, invisibly, all winter, and presented in full at the thaw.***
> ⭐⭐ **The thaw-audit acquires its social teeth here:** *it is the one enforcement mechanism on the one thing
> anybody could have skipped.*
