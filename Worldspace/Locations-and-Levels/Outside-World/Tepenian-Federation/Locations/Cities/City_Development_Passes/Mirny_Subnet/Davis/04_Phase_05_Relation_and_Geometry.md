## ⭐⭐⭐ The heat finding — **a 198-line zero that is load-bearing**

**Phase 3 established that Davis's ground is only strong while it stays cold, and that warm interiors are a
thermal load on it. Phase 4 established that every dwelling must be warm every night, for the robot half of the
population, or permanent capacity is lost.** ⇒ **Davis's heat demand is continuous, non-optional, and
structural.**

> ### ⛔ **AND THE NATIONAL GEOTHERMAL-HEATING FILE DOES NOT LIST DAVIS.**
> ⇒ ⭐⭐ ***Whatever keeps Davis warm is not the ground it stands on.*** **It is supplied — which makes the single
> most non-negotiable requirement in the city a dependency rather than a local resource.**
> ⚠ **Opened and empty is a RESULT.** ⛔ **What the actual supply is, is NOT stated in this phase's admitted set
> and is not invented** — hole `H29`.

## ⭐⭐ And the port is the inverse of a dependency

**`Ports.md` gives Davis's Prydz Bay port the tier ⛔ `CONSTRUCTED` — *"rebuilt or cut open, every year,"*
*"paid, forever."*** **Phase 4 established, from `16` §19, that Davis's maritime 15% was *considered for the
mandate and rejected* because Davis sits on the Hwy 110 main line and *"is not dependent on its own shipping"*
— its output moves **by road and air.**

> # ⇒ ***Davis rebuilds a port every year for cargo that does not leave through it.***
> ⭐ **The resolution is in the port entry's own wording:** *"when ice allows **delivery** directly to station
> coastal facilities."* ⇒ ⭐⭐ **the port reads as an INBOUND asset, not an outbound one** — **which is exactly
> where `D6`'s unresolved dependency would land: the plant and equipment Davis cannot make.**
> ⚠ **LABELED AS INFERENCE.** **Canon states the tier, the season, the word *delivery*, and the road/air routing
> separately.** ⛔ **It does not state that the port exists for imports, and this phase does not assert it as
> fact.** Hole `H30`.

## ⭐⭐⭐ And the answer to *"who knows?"* is the finding

**Two of Davis's four dependencies are UNNAMED** *(plant, heat)*, **one is unremediable** *(water)*, **and the
one that is named can only be named by conceding failure** *(shortfall)*.

> # ***Davis has no dependency it can discuss.***
> ⭐ **By the spine's own definition that makes every one of them a shadow and none of them politics** — **and
> `02` `D2` already supplies why: *no counterparty ⇒ no grievance ⇒ no critic is ever produced.***
> ⛔ **Not written as a defect. It is what a city with no instrument of refusal and no admissible vocabulary for
> shortfall structurally produces.**
