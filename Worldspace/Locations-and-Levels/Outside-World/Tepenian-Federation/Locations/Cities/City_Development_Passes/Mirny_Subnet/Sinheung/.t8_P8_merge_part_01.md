## 0. In short

1. **Stands, on a new ground.** No origin language dominates, because Hawaiian (later Tepenian) is the dominant
   language everywhere by ruling (DR-100 (1)). That Korea's plurality "cannot impose" is no longer the reason.
2. **Changes.** Korean is this city's only supporting-tier language (Korean 33.00; German, next, 10.06; the
   12-point line sits at 21.00). One origin language is therefore live here, as a working language.
3. **Narrowed.** The pass's claim that no origin language is a live identity marker now reads: Korean is not a mark
   of descent (at most 16.08% of residents can follow it by human Korean descent, and robots have no genetic
   descent), and inside the city it marks nobody as local. Whether it marks someone as from here, seen from
   anywhere else, is unwritten. PROVISIONAL.
4. **Narrowed.** F.2's method-vocabulary mechanism holds only for the origin languages other than Korean, and only
   in the city's shared settings. Korean words are borrowing or switching, not residue. That borrowing leans toward
   Korean is plausible and unmeasured.
5. **Narrowed.** The two named clusters survive only on 8a's preservation line, provisionally. As the general list
   of contributors, and at "near-equal weight", they fall for want of an admitted line. Whether German, Japanese
   and Mandarin contributed is open.
6. **New.** 8f has no general answer, never mentions the robot majority (51.27% of Census I), and never declares
   Band 5. The robots' day-to-day language is the dominant one (firm). Whether the Language Module delivers Korean
   is open.
7. **New.** A ruling records the Tepenian language's core as an English–Japanese pidgin (DR-85). No Japanese- or
   English-origin word is credited to this city's own rows without a check.
8. **Conditional.** The founding-stock clause waits on a Founding Register line. The grading words live in a language
   the whole city follows; which one is not settled. The line that the city's marker is not an accent stands, with
   a qualifier.
9. **Null.** DR-24 to DR-26 change no 8f sentence; they work only as a guard.
10. **Open.** The date of the name change; the Language Module; how Hawaiian relates to the pidgin core; where
    Korean is used (deferred by DR-4); who does not follow Korean (no speaker count exists).

    at home tracks descent is `null` for want of a speaker count (B).

    unwritten.

  narrowed and PROVISIONAL ({{REF:K:live_marker}}; {{REF:D:l398_conclusion}}).

- **Disposition:** `confirmed by all three` (live as a working language; not a mark of descent; A, B, C; K);
