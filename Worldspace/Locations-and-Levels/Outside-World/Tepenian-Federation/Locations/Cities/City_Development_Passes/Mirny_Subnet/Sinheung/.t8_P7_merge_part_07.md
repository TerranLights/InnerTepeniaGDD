- **Strength** follows the weakest any reader gave after Round 3. **Docket confidence** follows the lowest any reader gave after Round 3; a difference in confidence alone is not counted as a conflict.
- **Readers' own item numbers** (XA-, XB-, XC-) and the first check's (XU4-) appear only in this section, as source points.
