- **SRY-C09-17. The tier changed, and under `DR-12` the tier is information.** The pass listed the party in the Significant tier (`02_Spine.md` L123–L124, L132). The current spec places the Sinian Federation in Notable (L48, L65), and Significant now holds only "Australia, Korea" (L47). `LOG` L472 makes the tier information, not weighting. That depended on the party's order inside the Significant tier (`02_Spine.md` L123–L124). The current table puts the Sinian Federation in the fourth row (note L159; spec L62–L65), which is the top of the Notable tier, also an order within a tier. `LOG` L472 bars any finding that rests on a within-tier difference. So the old rank fails, and a new rank cannot take its place.
- **SRY-C09-19. The core of the conclusion survives.** Each reading in SRY-C09-15 leaves a share of the city descending from the refused party: the Sinian Federation row alone is 4.49% (L65). The split of the refused nation into a successor row plus detached pieces (spec L54) does not contradict "the category it insulted has dissolved" (`01_Inherited.md` L219).
