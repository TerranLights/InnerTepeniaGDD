## 4 · B · The arrival modes and their mix

**The axis: by what route each body came to be here, and what each route produces in the place.** Bounded throughout by: arrival after the treaty (brief §4 item 5); the Acts, the height undated (`DEC-698`); and the reserved founding date (RF-5), so **no arrival is dated and no order of arrival beyond "founding wave, then later arrivals" is written.**

### Humans

9. **Assigned / posted: the founding wave, by the treaty's station assignment, on the Register's geography and access** `[treaty draft]`. Q-A1.

10. **Chose it: at national scale, written; at city scale, unwritten.** Every human in Tepenia arrived by election, before the treaty (TD line 48) or at any time after it (TD line 50) `[treaty draft]`; none was compelled (TD line 49).     - **Attracts — by work:** "Science, almost entirely." (spec L167); it holds what cannot be moved (the lake, the archive understood only collectively, spec L70, L179), "so others must come or send to it" (spine §4); a specialized founding (`DEC-688`). And it attracts the robot body in one respect: altitude costs a robot nothing (frame §4 item 4).
    - **Repels — by body:** every arrival must be housed in heat at every place a body stops, with water melted at about an eighth of a kilowatt-hour a liter (spine §3a; research FIND-7); no ground, so food comes in (spine D-1); about 243 days with no sunrise or sunset (spine D-3); and, for the human body only, an altitude of about 3,900 m by the body's measure, highest in the cold, dark months, paid in sleep and chronic for the lowland-born (research FIND-11). The repulsion is split by body: it charges the human body twice (altitude and cold) and the robot body once (cold, in words only: capacity loss when charging in the cold, reduced capacity, output sag, embrittlement, safer moving than resting). **The place attracts by work and repels by body.** *Refused inference:* the robot majority (64.50%) is not read as caused by humans being repelled; the files give no basis for that causation, and the robot share is the census's, not a record of choices (RF-A6).
