⚠ **Stated with the precision the sources allow.** **The Vestfold Hills are exposed rock as well as periglacial

technique, code, trade or failure event is invented.**

## F.3 ⭐⭐⭐ The second-order consequence — **and the first draft got its shape wrong**

1. ⭐ **The obligation is PERMANENT and has no completion state.** **Trans-Alaska's lesson is *forever* — the

2. ⛔⛔ **But the FAILURE is not gradual, and the first draft said it was.** **The source is explicit:**
   ***"It holds, it holds, it holds — and then it does not. There is no degraded middle state to notice."***
   ⇒ **Correction: *the CAUSE accumulates invisibly; the FAILURE is a threshold event with no warning.***
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **And that distinction makes the finding sharper, not weaker.** **`02` §4.1's LAPSE is a slow decline
   nobody notices. This is its complement:** ***an obligation that must be met continuously, whose neglect
   produces no symptom at all until the ground changes state.***
   > ⭐⭐ ***Davis's culture fails by lapsing quietly. Davis's ground fails by holding, and holding, and then
