spread out, so the commons had to be built on this ground. Today the plurality is the same (35.83) and so is the gap to

- *And what does that cause?* The spread-out tier is now the city's largest tier, bigger than the plurality. The pass's
  own reading of a spread-out tail (L170: the default "**ERODES**, into something generic-but-local that nobody chose and
  everybody can use") now covers two-fifths of the city instead of under a third.
- *And what does that cause?* "Cannot impose" (L885) gets stronger at the tier level: the tail as a whole outweighs the
  plurality, though no single row in it does. The seams that negotiate (L173) drop from five named seams to three. The
  axis's clause "the seams between what was inherited are still load-bearing" does not count seams, so it still stands.
- *And what does that cause?* Nothing that reverses the direction. No party larger than the pass saw has appeared: the
  Primary row is unchanged and the Significant tier is smaller (24.10, against the pass's 34.85). Nothing I read gives the
  commons a homeland. Here the answer stops moving the verdict.
