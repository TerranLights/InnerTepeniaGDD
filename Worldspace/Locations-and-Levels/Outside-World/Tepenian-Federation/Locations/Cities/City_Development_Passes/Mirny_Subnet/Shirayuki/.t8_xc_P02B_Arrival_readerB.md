## 1 Summary (five lines at most: what changes, what stays)
1. The founding basis gains a second ground: geography, "in addition" to the court's allocation (Register L61; LOG L930). Every pass place that states the basis as the allocation alone is restated from the Register; nothing about who chose, who negotiated, or who can appeal changes.
2. Narrowed: "nothing to reach for" (spine L102) becomes one marginal ground nobody ever rehearsed; the answer to asking why you are here (spine L104) gains a half about the place; the inherited awkwardness (Inherited L215) holds for Act 2 and gets an Act 1 qualifier.
3. The arrival headline (Phase 2 L121) holds for the 2564 founding cohort. B.3's "could not have", L144's "only once" and B.4's "even the choosing" narrow from what was impossible to what is recorded, because humans could keep arriving after the signing (startup L65) and records can draw people (startup L67).
4. The inheritance lists (Frame L307; spine L95, L102) take in research, equipment, techniques and results (LOG L661), and keep "not people" and no tradition (LOG L646).
5. Unchanged: the terminus, the missing rotation clock, no founding hardship, the thin standing cost, no route back, the direction of the inverse mechanism, B.4 as the documented route, and B.5.
