### 4.2 `Specs/Davis.md` L143 — ADMITTED. The L135–181 blanket cut was wrong.

Step −1 excluded `Specs/Davis.md` **L135–181** wholesale because the range opens with `## Character & Culture`.
That is a **heading-level** cut, and `05` §6.1a forbids exactly this reasoning:

> ***"admissibility is a property of CONTENT, never filename"***

A heading is a filename with extra steps. And `§6.1d` says those headings — *"Character," "Culture,"
"Significance," "Developer vision"* — are **"suspect by default."** ⚠ **Step −1 read *suspect* as *excluded*
and cut 47 lines without examining one of them.** Suspicion is a trigger to check, not a verdict.

**L143 checked properly, in the order `05` L728–731 mandates — §6.1 FIRST, ratification SECOND:**

**Step 1 — §6.1 circularity.** The bar is: *"an input must not be a prior culture-pass CONCLUSION about the
same location."* L143 is stamped **"established 2026-07-05, revised 2026-07-16"** and its closing sentence is
***"Full detail in `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Davis.md`"*** — a pointer **TO** the culture sheet. It names
itself as the **upstream** source that the culture sheet elaborates. The L728 trap — *"downstream of a withheld
culture sheet is still out, ratified or not"* — tests for the **opposite** direction of flow and does not
catch it. ✅ **PASSES.**

**Step 2 — ratification.** `Cities/Specs/` is enumerated in the rule 6 table. ✅ **RATIFIED.**

**→ `Specs/Davis.md` L143 is ADMISSIBLE.**

**L141** (*"Nationally known for:"*) is **NOT admitted** — it is a claim about how the city is regarded, with no
provenance stamp and no date, and it is the genuine §6.1d case. **L135–140, L142, L144–181 remain unexamined
and stay OUT** pending the §5 re-adjudication.

## 5. ⛔ METHODOLOGY DEFECT — `M-218`, and a pass-side contamination declared against itself

### 5.1 The defect

**A range exclusion taken on a heading is a filename-class rule wearing line numbers.** `§6.1a` bans the
reasoning; `§6.1d` names the four suspect headings; **neither file says what to actually DO with a suspect
heading, and the gap defaulted to "cut the whole range."** Measured cost on Davis: the single sharpest
developer statement about the city — its City-Type resolution, its geographic justification, and its
supersession history — sat **9 lines** past a cut made without reading one line of what was cut.

**The rule this produces, and it generalizes past this corpus:**

> ⭐ **A suspect heading licenses a LINE-LEVEL adjudication, never a RANGE-LEVEL exclusion.** A range may be
> excluded only after each span inside it has been classified. **"Suspect" is an instruction to look, and an
> exclusion taken without looking cannot report what it cost — which is why it always reports zero.**

**And its companion, from `§6.1c`'s column-anchored technique:** *a provenance-stamped paragraph inside a
suspect section is a different object from the section around it.* **L143 announces its own date, its own
author-role, and its own supersession. Material that carries its provenance in its body does not inherit the
heading's suspicion — it overrides it.**

### 5.2 The contamination, declared

Adjudicating this required knowing what sits in L135–181. The orchestrator ran:

```
grep -n "^#\|^\*\*" Specs/Davis.md      ⛔ no line cap
```

which returned **L135, L141, and L143 in full.** The excluded range was entered. Under `§6.1a` —
***"you cannot un-see the second half"*** — this is recorded, not hidden.

**Aggravating, and the reason it is written up rather than noted:** the correct form
(`sed -n '1,134p' Specs/Davis.md | grep …`) was used **in the other command of the same parallel block.**
*The safe form was applied to the file's body and the unsafe form to its headings, in the same breath.* This is
the same class as the Zhongshan Round 2 `ls` event: **a command believed to return metadata returned content.**

> **Transferable rule:** ⚠ **a line-range restriction must be applied at the FIRST stage of a pipeline, not the
> last.** `grep | head` restricts what is *displayed*; `sed -n '1,Np' | grep` restricts what is *read*. **Only
> the second is a quarantine.** Any command touching a partially-admissible file must carry its cap in the
> reading stage — and `grep -n` over a whole file is a reading stage.

**Mitigation:** the three lines seen are L135 (a heading), L141 (**refused** — §4.2), and L143 (**admitted on
independent grounds** — §4.2). **No admitted content rests on the contamination**: L143 clears §6.1 and rule 6
on its own text, and would have been admitted identically had a dispatched reader surfaced it. The exposure
that remains unmitigated is **L141**, which is refused as an input but has been seen by the orchestrator.

⛔ **L135–181's remaining 44 unexamined lines are NOT adjudicated by this ruling** and must be handled by
dispatched readers under T8, reporting **classification only, never content**, so the orchestrator does not
compound this. Docketed as **`R-2`**, blocking on nothing — it can run alongside Step 0.

### 5.3 `M-218`'s other half, pre-declared before Round 2 and now confirmed

The **T8 `PROOF:` format assumes whole-file reads.** `Specs/Davis.md` is 181 lines with 135 admissible, so a
reader's mandatory `LLAST:` field points at **L181 — inside the forbidden range.** ⚠ **The verification
instrument requires the reader to violate the input contract in order to prove compliance with it.**

**Fix, to be carried into `PRE-TRIP_INSPECTION_RECIPE_TRIAL.md`:** where a file is partially admissible, the
`PROOF:` block takes the **admissible range** as its universe — `LINES:` reports the admissible count,
`LLAST:` quotes the last **admissible** line, and a new **`LRANGE:`** field states the contract
(`L1–134 ∪ {L143}`) so `triple_read_verify.py` can check the endpoints against the contract instead of
against the file length. **Not yet implemented.**
