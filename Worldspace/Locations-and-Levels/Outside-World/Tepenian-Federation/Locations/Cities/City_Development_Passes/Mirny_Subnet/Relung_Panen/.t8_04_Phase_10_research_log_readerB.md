## B3 pre-draft: this place's character from Phases 1 to 9, written BEFORE opening entries 31-33
A walled rock basin of lakes inside ice, still most days, lit every day, where nothing soft is given except the summer melt: soil, warmth, lit green, winter water and the summer dark are all made and must be kept. Its year is set by water and by dates the place itself bills (drains before the melt, the refreeze), not by any harvest; its interior holds one climate every month. It keeps for others (a reserve kept uneaten, a nutrient loop, a second supply valued for its difference) and lives on a line it does not hold. It relates through one neighbor and the whole nation. Membership is earned by keeping a year, not by kind or origin. Its ordinary success is an absence (the drain that did not block); its failures arrive on schedule. Character words: patient, disciplined, enduring, long-clocked, structured by consequence, quiet, interior, kept rather than given, reserved for the bad year. Weaker strands: the melt's water as the year's one loud natural force; the dependence on a single supply; half its people robots who dream in warm rooms.
