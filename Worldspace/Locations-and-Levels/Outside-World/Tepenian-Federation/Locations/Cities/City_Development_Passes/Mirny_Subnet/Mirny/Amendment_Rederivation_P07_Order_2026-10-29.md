## 6 · The sanction check, re-run

- **Is guilt a sanction? No.** (1) The check governs an exclusion or penalty — an act with someone imposing it; the ruled guilt is personal and "not as a result of any sort of 'legal obligation'". (2) It removes no shelter, heat, charging place or road.
