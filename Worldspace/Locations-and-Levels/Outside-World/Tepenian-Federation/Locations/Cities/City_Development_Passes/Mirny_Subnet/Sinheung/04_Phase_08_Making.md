**Because the whole city stops at one hour, the whole city eats at one hour.** ⛔ *There is no staggered-shift
food culture here, no late trade, no meal that exists because somebody is coming off work while others sleep.*

⚠ **`NO NATIONAL STEREOTYPES` holds in its CORRECT form** *(revised 2026-09-07)*: ⛔ *nothing here
characterizes a people's nature.* ✅ **But the loanwords are legitimately Korean-weighted** — *Korea is the
Primary nation and the founding stock, and §C.9b unlocks exactly this once composition is settled* — **with the
Baltic/Balkan and Southeast Asian clusters contributing their own process-words at their own near-equal weight.**
⭐ ***A vocabulary is the least divergent thing a migration carries: the ice changed the food, and left the word
for the step alone.***

## F.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT ACTUALLY MARKS SOMEONE AS FROM HERE

> # ***PEOPLE FROM THIS CITY USE MATERIALS-GRADING VOCABULARY ABOUT THINGS THAT ARE NOT MATERIALS.***

**`Phase 7` established the shared half:** *"Everyone means the same thing by 'physical integrity,' which is why
the measure works at all without an office."* ⭐⭐ **A vocabulary that precise, held that universally, and used
that constantly does not stay inside the works gate.**

| So people here describe, in the Standard's terms… | |
|---|---|
| **a plan, an argument, an arrangement, a person's account of themselves** | *as **sound** or **unsound**, as having or lacking **integrity**, as **finished** or **not finished*** |

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE TELL IS PRECISION, NOT VOCABULARY

> ⛔ **An outsider can learn the words in a week.** ⭐⭐ ***What marks a local is that they use them exactly*** —
> *because here the words are load-bearing, have an agreed referent, and carry a verdict.* **Someone using them
> loosely, as praise or as color, is immediately from somewhere else.**
> ### ⭐⭐⭐ ***The city's speech marker is not an accent. It is a refusal to use its own central words casually.***

## F.4 ⚠ AND ONE HONEST CONSEQUENCE

⭐ **This makes the city sound blunt to visitors and sound careful to itself.** *Both readings are correct.*
⛔ **A local declining to call something "good" is not withholding warmth** — ***there is no "good" available in
the measure; there is only met and not met, and pardon is not on offer for objects any more than for people.***
