## The differentiation axis

**One neighbor, whole nation.**

*What the words carry.* Every relation written on this city's own lines runs at one of two scales. **The near scale has exactly one counterparty:** Casey, the far end of the only overland link (spec L12; `Highways.md` L278), the junction onto the national road (`Highways.md` L207, L291), and the port the city's bulk uses both ways (spec L13; `Ports.md` L245; `DR-96` item 3, rulings L1633). **The far scale is the nation as a whole:** the food role, seed for "other cities", phosphorus and soil recovery as a national role "also serving other cities' waste streams" (`DR-71` L1374; `DR-75` L1421; `18` L30–33 `[DoI corroboration]`), the continental grid that reaches the city (`DR-138` item 9), the intake that processes its arrivals somewhere else (`DR-82` L1499), and the relation files' own reading that its role ties it "to the whole country, not to any one city" (`City_National_Connections.md` L398; `City_Cross_Subnet_Relationships.md` L207). **Between them, the middle distance is reached only through the near scale:** the science partner and the subnet's hub lie west, on Hwy 110, and the city reaches them overland only by going east to Casey first, because the Denman crossing is not built (spec L12; `Highways.md` L199, L278; `DR-137` item 13, rulings L2003). **The one relation that skips both scales** is freight with the founders' region abroad, by the city's own cargo airport on its own gulf ice, which carries no people (`DR-81` L1488; `Airports.md` L22; `DR-96` item 1). *(A; the city's own air door as the exception, B)*

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs four things at once, each on this place's own lines: a terminus spur to a single junction (spec L12); no berth, so the sea is also reached through that junction (spec L13; `DR-95` item 4, rulings L1623); outward roles addressed to the nation or to unnamed "other cities" (`DR-75` L1421); and a wall on the side where the middle distance lies (spec L113–116; `DR-137` item 13). Remove the wall and the hub and the partner are reached directly west; put the city on Hwy 110 and it has neighbors on both sides; give it a berth and it has sea relations of its own; name a city that receives its seed and it has a bilateral tie. The axis rests on ruled lines and the city's own relation files, not on `[PA-12]`, the held ground, `[PROV-8]` or any unruled door. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** belonging to a nation; drawing on a national grid; depending on imports; a road that ends; a food role. *The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.* *(A, B, C)*
