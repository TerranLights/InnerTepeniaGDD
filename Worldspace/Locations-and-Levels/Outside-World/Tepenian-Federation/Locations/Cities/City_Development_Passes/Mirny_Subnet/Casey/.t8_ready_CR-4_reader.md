## 2 · The ruling

**The developer's words** (rulings log L1912): *"humans could continue arriving into Tepenia after the signing. They just weren't allowed back to Upper Earth if they did"*.

**As recorded** (rulings log L1913, item 2; one name elided as `[…]`): "**(2) NEW CANON: humans could continue arriving into Tepenia after the signing of the Falkland Treaty; they were not allowed back to Upper Earth if they did.** This replaces the reading that the human channel closed at the signing (the […] Phase 2 piece's restatement and its recommended answer rested on that reading of article II.2.3; the article fixes who may accompany the robot population without being prevented, namely humans who had settled among robots before the signing, and does not itself bar later human arrival). The robot half (whether robots kept coming into being and arriving) was not answered; it stays as recorded: the robot channel is open in law, and no later arrival is recorded."

**As written into the treaty** (treaty L50, Article II.2.5): "Any human person who has not made the election described in paragraph 3 of this Article may, at any time after the effective date of this Treaty, freely elect to take up residence within the Federation of Tepenia, the Federation's existence not being limited to the population described in this Article. Such a person shall, by that election, be deemed under the law of each Assembly Power to have willingly and permanently relinquished any claim to home, residency, or quarter within Assembly Power territory, and shall for all purposes of this Treaty be treated as a person who has made the election described in paragraph 3." The no-return half is carried by Article IV.2.2 (L76, which now names "Article II.2.5") and IV.2.3 (L77): "No human person who has made the election described in paragraph 2 may thereafter return to the territory of any Assembly Power".

**Scope.** It does **not** name Casey. It is filed under another city's Phase 2 walkthrough (heading, L1908), but item 2 is not recorded for that city only: it is labeled "NEW CANON", its words are about Tepenia as a whole ("arriving into Tepenia"), and it now stands as an article of the national treaty that governs residence "within the Federation of Tepenia". **It is national canon, and so it reaches Casey as it reaches every city.** Items (1) and (4) of `DR-127` are recorded for the other city only; item (3) concerns sizing and robot national origin. None of them is part of this check, and none was assessed against the contract.
