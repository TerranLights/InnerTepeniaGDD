# B · THE FOUR SENSORY SUB-FIELDS — **answered separately, per the phase's own instruction**

## B.1 · SOUND

⭐⭐ **A constant acoustic floor: persistent, strong north-easterly katabatic flow.** *The ASMA management plan
calls it the defining climatic feature.* **Wind of that kind does not punctuate a soundscape — it masks one.**
**Underneath it, a `45%`-fabrication economy:** *plant, press, foundry, the truck route.*
⭐⭐⭐ **And for two months a year, a sound Antarctica almost never has: RUNNING WATER.** *The oasis holds
meltwater lakes, and the same wind "keeps the lakes well-mixed through the thaw."*

> ### ⛔⛔ AND WHAT THIS PHASE WILL **NOT** SAY ABOUT WINTER — `M-158`
> **The source is SUMMER-SCOPED:** *"persistent and strong… **on most summer days**."* ⛔ ***That is what was
> measured. It is not a claim about winter, and an absence of description is not a description of absence.***
> ⚠ **Katabatic flow is generally STRONGEST in the dark months.** ⭐ **So: winter wind is UNCHARACTERIZED here,
> and this pass records that rather than inventing a quiet season.**

## B.2 · SMELL

⭐ **Mineral dust, and this is stated by canon rather than inferred:** *"Exposed rock, not ice: **what the wind
moves here is dust and grit as well as snow**."*
**Beneath it the fabrication register** — *hot metal, coolant, siligel.*
⭐⭐ **And seasonally, wet rock and open water** — *a smell that exists here for two months and nowhere in the
year's other ten.*

## B.3 · FEEL

⭐⭐ **GRIT.** *Wind-carried dust and grit, on every exposed surface and every exposed face.* **Not the clean
abrasion of blown snow — mineral, because the ground is rock.**
⭐⭐⭐ **And for two months: WET.** *Above freezing by day, below it by night* — **so surfaces are damp in the
afternoon and glazed by morning.** ***That is a texture underfoot, on a handrail, and on a workpiece.***

## B.4 · FIRST IMPRESSIONS

**Approached by road** *(Hwy 4)* **or by air** — ⛔ **not from the water, which is a four-month window with a
fallback and no reliable near-shore landing.**
⭐⭐ **And the impression the ground gives:** *a hard, dark, ice-free core with meltwater on it — and a much
larger city standing on ice around and beyond it.* ***The rock reads as the middle of the city and is a
minority of it.***

# F · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ RETROACTIVE MECHANISM — **the phase's finding**

> **Asks: what is established as simply true here that nothing explains, and what physical system would produce
> exactly that? *It generates a second-order consequence for free.***

## F.1 THE UNEXPLAINED THING

**Canon asserts, without mechanism, that this city holds a citywide standard — *"The Sinheung Standard"* —
for the *"quality and **PHYSICAL INTEGRITY** of refined materials,"* spanning graded stone, examined chamber
output and a guarded archive, *"not administered by any single office."***
⛔ **Nothing in canon says WHY a city would converge on physical integrity as its shared measure of worth.**

## F.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE PHYSICAL SYSTEM THAT PRODUCES EXACTLY THAT

**Read the monthly table for the crossing, not the extremes:**

| Month | Avg high | Mean | Avg low | Crosses `0 °C` daily? |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| **Dec** | **`+2.8`** | `0` | **`−2.1`** | ⭐⭐ **YES** |
| **Jan** | **`+3.3`** | `0` | **`−1.6`** | ⭐⭐ **YES** |
| Feb | `0.0` | `−5` | `−4.6` | ⚠ touches |
| *(Mar–Nov)* | *below* | | | **no** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO MONTHS A YEAR, EVERY DAY, THIS CITY CROSSES THE FREEZING POINT IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.**
> ***That is the single most destructive thermal regime there is for stone, mortar, seal and structure.***
> **Water enters a flaw, freezes, expands, and widens it. A site at `−40` never does this. A temperate site
> does it a few times. ⭐ A site whose two warmest months sit exactly ON zero does it sixty times a year, to
> everything.**

⭐⭐ **AND THE WATER IS NOT RAIN — the arithmetic says so.** **December and January are the two DRIEST months of
the year** *(`10.3 mm` at `~14%` probability; `5.2 mm` at `~19%`)*. ⛔ **So the water doing the damage is not
falling on the city.** ✅ ***It is MELTWATER — the oasis's own lakes and the melt off its own exposed rock.***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE OASIS IS NOT A GIFT WITH A COST ATTACHED. **THE COST IS THE SAME PROPERTY AS THE GIFT.**
> **Ice-free ground exists here BECAUSE it melts.** ***The exposed rock, the liquid lakes and the freeze-thaw
> that destroys built work are one phenomenon, not three.*** ⛔ **The thing that made this site worth a
> diplomatic claim is the thing that eats what is built on it.**

## F.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE SECOND-ORDER CONSEQUENCE — **and it explains the thing `Step 1` could only describe**

**`Step 1` found that the Standard *"can condemn but cannot pardon,"* because canon gives it no administering
office — and recorded that as a structural asymmetry with no explanation.**

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ THE FREEZE-THAW REGIME SUPPLIES THE EXPLANATION: **AN OFFICE WOULD NOT WORK.**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Freeze-thaw failure is DEFERRED** | *A flawed piece does not fail at the works. It fails after some number of melt seasons* |
> | **And DISPLACED** | *It fails in a wall somebody else built, in a part of the city the maker never visited* |
> | ⛔ **So inspection at the point of production cannot catch it** | ***There is nothing to see. The flaw is interior, and the test is time*** |
> | ⭐⭐ **The only party positioned to know is the maker** | **At the moment of making, and never again afterwards** |
>
> # ***THE STANDARD HAS NO OFFICE BECAUSE AN OFFICE COULD NOT DO THE JOB. IT IS ENFORCED BY EACH TRADE ON ITSELF BECAUSE THAT IS THE ONLY PLACE THE KNOWLEDGE EVER EXISTS.***
> ⭐ **Not neglect. Not informality as a cultural preference. *Physics.***
