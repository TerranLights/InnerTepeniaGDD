## The axis

**Compelled, followed, then born.**

**Peer-free test ("could this be true anywhere?"):** the route alone fails it: robots removed as a class and humans admitted by a tie to robots is the treaty's route for every place the exile reached, so as a bare mode-mix it could be true anywhere. What passes is the route's meeting with this site: a population selected by ties and class and placed by assignment, **selected for nothing the site demands**, with no exit, in a place that has only its own residents to learn the wind from. That second half could not be true of a place without Mirny's wind, hub and unwritten-wind deficit (02_Spine §8). The finding is stated as that meeting, and the national part is flagged national, as parent-supplied relation (00_Frame RP-7).
