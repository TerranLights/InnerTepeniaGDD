## The sanction check, re-run

The check (entry 2, L942–947) applies "Before writing any exclusion or penalty". It asks "what it physically costs the excluded person here, and whether this place would actually pay that price for that" offense (L945). The conditions are unchanged (entry 16 L477), and so are rows 1–4, 7 and 9 of P7-38. The correction touches rows 5, 6, 8 and 10, and adds the guilt.

- **Is guilt a sanction under the check? No.** (1) The check governs an exclusion or a penalty, which is an act with someone imposing it. The ruled guilt is "deep and immense personal guilt" (L1116), and the duty is followed "not as a result of any sort of 'legal obligation'" (L1116). No one imposes it. (2) It removes no shelter, heat, charging place or road, so its physical cost to either body is nothing. (3) It attaches **before or during** a failure, to an omission. It does not attach after a loss to a misjudgment. It is personal and communal: an inner cost carried because of belonging to "a strong sense of community and tribe among Tepenians" (L1116). **It is not an institution, and it is not a sanction imposed by anyone.** No court, penalty, rota or rule is derived (trap 3). Criminal justice stays TBD (H42), and DR-55 defers it to research on the establishing peoples (entry 3 L1101).
- **What it costs each body.** *Human:* an inner cost, carried across a human life. *Robot:* the same inner cost, in a body without senescence ("there is no senescence and no background attrition", entry 6 L105). Whether a robot carries a not-going for as long as the body lasts is reserved (AF7-6; entry 14 L287). Entry 6 flags sustained overwork as possibly a medical hazard (L345–348, ending "connection, not yet ruled." at L348). **No admitted line connects guilt to any robot medical model, and none is derived.**
- **What the guilt pushes toward is what the check really prices.** The act it urges is a crossing into a storm in which outdoor exposure "is dangerous regardless of cold protection" (entry 16 L322, citing `SPEC` L131). The human cost is exposure; the robot cost is charge and possibly "PERMANENT maximum-capacity loss" (L324). Would Mirny pay that price? It does not *exact* it. Each person who judges herself most able pays it of her own accord. **The ruling carries its own proportionality guard:** the duty falls on whoever is most strategically able "to address a weather hazard" (L1431), with "capability and position for the hazard at hand" (L1432). So a person who cannot address the hazard is not the one the duty falls on [D]. Whether guilt reaches someone the storm itself kept from going is not ruled (AQ-2; AF6-1 reserved).
- **Rows touched.** *Row 5*, removal from a continuous post: still "a handoff, not a penalty" (L486), now with the ruling that no one is officially required to hand off or keep a post (L1116). *Row 6*, withholding the signal: the signal now carries the warnings (L1431), so withholding it would mean exposure to unwarned danger. Still **not writable**, and still no mechanism. *Row 8*, refusing mandated work: **ruled.** "People won't be punished for 'not pursuing' particular jobs. Simply that every choice has outcomes." (entry 3 L1098). *Row 10*, blame: **the first generation's.** The register's error 5 (entry 15 L16) is not repeated: guilt is **not** moved onto crossings.
- **What remains of "The one sanction that exists is a reading with no one doing it" (L494)?** For the first generation it stands. "Spoken only as blame" was the honest finding when there was nothing else to book a loss to (entry 14 L126). **After them, the admitted lines hold no sanction at all at Mirny.** How a crossing gone wrong is read is not ruled (AR-15). Guilt is a moral cost and not a sanction. Justice is TBD. One thing survives of the shape: **a cost with no one imposing it**, "a failure mode with no opponent in it" (entry 5 L176). Object, time and direction are all inverted. Blame was a reading by observers, laid on the injured person after a loss. Guilt is felt by the person who did not go, about the moment of need. "it also cannot be appealed" (L495) has an echo: there is no one to appeal guilt to. Whether anyone could release a person from the duty is reserved (AF7-3) and not derived.
- **The individual-to-civic check.** No one person's temperament is scaled into a sanction. The duty is a *communal* norm by ruling, and **it is not a civic sanction**. An individual's impatience still scales to "a smaller room and a shorter hearing" (entry 5 L167–168).

## The axis

**Phase 7's axis:** "**Claims without an arbiter.**" (L124), with the sub-axis "calls by position" (L127).

**Verdict: it survives with a changed half.**
- **The first half stands.** The admitted lines name no decider between the claims Mirny's work owes outward: the subnet's contact (L184 via L126), the eastern roads, the chamber chain, the inward sea supply. DR-59 sharpens it, because the quarry's two claims "may draw upon overlapping shares" (entry 3 L1220). The curating organization's remit is the weather protocol, and nothing extends it to the claims.
- **The second half fails.** The gloss (L126) says that the one authority that does act, "the person at the point", answers for the outcome alone, and that creating any arbiter for the wind "would give a loss a second answerer and change what Mirny is". Three things undo it. The wind's knowledge now has a curator (L1140). The duty falls by capability, not by presence (L1431–1432). And answering for the outcome alone was the first generation's (entry 14 L126). **The sub-axis "calls by position" must change too**, because "best placed" is "whoever's most strategically able to address a weather hazard" (entry 3 L1431), "not nearness" (L1432).

**Proposed wording (the developer chooses):**
1. **Knowledge curated; claims unarbitrated.** *(Recommended.)* It names the inversion the correction produced (A7-1, §6): Mirny's one curated decision is the knowledge its first generation made, and nobody decides between the claims its work serves. Sub-axis for the fast calls: **duty without compulsion** (DR-56's own heading, entry 3 L1111), replacing "calls by position".
2. **Claims unarbitrated; duty uncompelled.** Keeps Phase 7's own question and swaps the fast-call half. Its risk: it sits closer to Phase 6's "those who can, respond".
3. **Claims without an arbiter** kept word for word, with only the sub-axis changed to **duty without compulsion**. Its risk: the gloss would need a full rewrite and the axis would no longer name what the correction added.

**How it relates to DR-77 without copying it.** "Learned once; those who can, respond." (entry 3 L1443) answers Meaning: what Mirny holds about its wind and its duty. Phase 7 answers Order: who decides, what cannot be administered, and how the unwritten is learned and kept. *Knowledge curated* is the order-form of *learned once*: it says who keeps what was learned. *Claims unarbitrated* is what Phase 6 does not touch: between the claims on one quarry, one relay and the same hours, no admitted party decides. The order-form of *those who can, respond* is the sub-axis: nobody compels them.

**Peer-free test ("could this be true anywhere?").** Half of it could. Many places curate weather knowledge, and many have claims without a decider. What passes is the pairing at Mirny's own values. The curated thing is the knowledge an assigned first generation made with a record and no teacher. The unarbitrated claims all run through one quarry base and one relay. And the relay is both things at once: the channel of the curated knowledge (the warnings) and a claim with no arbiter (its redundancy). *Delete-the-name:* *a place whose only curated body of shared knowledge is the one its first generation made from nothing, while every claim its work owes outward, including the single path its warnings ride, has no admitted decider.* The claim survives and is not relational. **Not checked against any sibling** (write-only; the terminal check).
