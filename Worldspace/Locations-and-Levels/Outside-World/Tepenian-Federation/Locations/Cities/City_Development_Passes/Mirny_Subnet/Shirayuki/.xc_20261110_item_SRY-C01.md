## 3 Reasoning (each step cites a line read)

- SRY-C01-16. **The members that left the tier cannot be put back by adding successor rows.** The pass's Russia member (`04_Phase_02` L43) is now twelve separate origins (note L161; LOG L1712), with Russia (core state) in the Notable tier (spec L67). - SRY-C01-17. **The within-tier bar cuts both ways.** LOG L472 bars any finding resting on "one Significant nation outnumbering another". (a) So the gap between spec L63's "9.65%" and spec L64's "9.10%" cannot make either origin the single runner-up; at the level the ruling admits ("The tiers and the city totals are the information.", LOG L472), the city still has no single named runner-up. (b) The pass's own "unusually FLAT" reading (L60) and its "Four origins within" / "`1.6` points" (L60–L61), which also set the fifth member apart from the other four, were readings of within-tier values; under LOG L472–L474 a re-derivation can neither assert nor deny "flat" for the current tier. Only the membership and the tier boundaries carry information. (The pass itself says "*No finding here rests on the decimal*", `04_Phase_02` L53.)
- SRY-C01-18. **What the Act 1 ground no longer supports.** The pass derived "no natural opposition, no obvious coalition, and no group that could plausibly become the challenger" (`04_Phase_02` L65) from many equal middle groups (L64). The current input is a different structure: one Primary origin, two Significant origins, and 35 Notable origins (note L156; spec L47–L48).
