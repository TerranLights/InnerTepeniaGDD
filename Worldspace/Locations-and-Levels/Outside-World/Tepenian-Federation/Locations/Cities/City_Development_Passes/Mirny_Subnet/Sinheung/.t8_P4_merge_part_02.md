### {{ID:F:a_third_wording}} One third, "of a working life" and "of life" (C-5 question d)

**Found by:** A, B, C and K. **Disposition:** confirmed by all three.

**What the current inputs say.** D16 L1840 gives 30.5% (30.532% computed). The denominator is the workforce
headcount (D16 L27), not life, not time, and not the population.

**Re-derived statement (firm).**
- 30.532% is 0.916 of a third, 2.80 points short of it. It is 1.018 of three tenths.
- *About three in ten* is the accurate wording.
- Nearly a third is defensible by arithmetic, but it rounds up and invites the time reading (B, C). K adds that at
  30.5% the word overstates in the direction the pass itself warned against (P4 L51-54). At the pass's 32.6% the

- "of a working life" also fails (P4 L52, L94, L130; P8 L30; P9 L190-191). It reads a headcount share as a share of
  one person's working time.
- Proposed form: *about three in ten of the city's workers (30.5%)*.

**Pass locations.** Each place that says a third is false as worded: P4 L52, L94 and L202; P8 L30; P9 L190.

**What stays.** The figure, restated. Nothing of the time reading stays.
