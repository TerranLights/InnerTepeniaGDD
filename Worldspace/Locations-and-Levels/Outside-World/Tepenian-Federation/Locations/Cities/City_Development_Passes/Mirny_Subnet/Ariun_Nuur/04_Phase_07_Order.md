### Handoff sweep — method and convention

Entries 8–17 (the contract, frame, Step 1 audit, spine, research and Phases 2 to 6) were read whole, by eye, by each reader, for every
row addressed to `Phase 7`, normalized for emphasis marks and line wrapping. No search command was run, so a missed line is possible;
that is the method's limit. The merge writes the **union** of the three readers' places. One row per distinct line or block
(`DEC-715`, PROVISIONAL); a line that reaches Phase 7 only by reference to another row (*as 63*) is a place of its own, in Block 2.
Where readers' discharges differ, **the discharge that closes less is written** (carried closes less than answered in part, which
closes less than answered). In these rows the earlier files' capitalized hand-off table is written *handed-forward table*.
Discharges: **A** answered here · **P** answered in part, the remainder carried to a named phase or step · **C** carried to a named
later phase or step · **N** closed as not applicable, with the reason. Every rule, roster, priority, prohibition, author or authority
these places ask for waits for the DCI (`DR-137` item 11); what this phase writes for them is where the decision falls and who is
present. A place whose labels carry different discharges is counted as answered in part, as all three readers count it.

## 7c · Knowledge and transmission

**The axis of this section: survival is taught by the earlier arrival and checked by the cold; the archive is taught person to person and
checked by nothing; care cannot be learned here at all; and whoever cannot learn is kept, never sent away.**

**FIND-P7-25 · The founders learned from paper; records are not teachers.** [A FIND-P7-17; B FIND-P7-120; C FIND-P7-215 (i)] The founding

survival gives feedback at once (research FIND-23). → **The city's first induction was reading an orientation manual whose writers were
gone; every induction since is a person** (Phase 4 FIND-P4-45). → *Second order:* it cannot work for the archive, which nothing answers,
so the one body of knowledge the city exists for is the one its founding method could not have built; the archive had to be built the
other way, by people teaching people (Phase 3 FIND-P3-69) [B]. (G4.) PROPOSED.

**FIND-P7-26 · How someone learns to do what this place does: three teachers, three checks.** [B FIND-P7-119; C FIND-P7-215; A
FIND-P7-18]
1. **To stay alive here: from the earlier arrival, checked by the cold.** Every arrival must be taught a place that can kill, by people
   taught it the same way; the teacher is always the earlier arrival (spine G8 standing cost 3; Phase 2 FIND-P2-40); the warning a
   newcomer needs comes from the clock, the thermometer or someone who already knows (Phase 3 FIND-P3-40); a heat lapse is felt within
   hours (spine §5). **The survival lesson is the one lesson in the city corrected at once:** a wrong lesson is answered by the cold
   before it can be passed on (Phase 6 FIND-P6-8). Mentor and mentee bonds are a national robot institution (Phase 4 L13, citing the
   physiology file) [A].
2. **To hold part of the archive: from the earlier holder, checked by nothing** (spec L179; research FIND-23; FIND-P7-30). Research
   arrivals came up from member cities of the subnet to do research in the first place (Phase 5 FIND-P5-30, PROVISIONAL), so their
   research skill may arrive with them; survival they learn here [C].
3. **To care for either body: at the national institutes, elsewhere,** in residential cohorts, graduated when travel is possible
