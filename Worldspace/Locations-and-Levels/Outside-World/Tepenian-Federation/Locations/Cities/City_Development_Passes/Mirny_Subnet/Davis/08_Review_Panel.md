# 1 · PANEL A — THE SIX FLAT ARCHETYPES

**Cast as:** **Child** = a resident's kid, too young to work · **Lover** = a courting pair with nowhere private

## ⚠ The Child — ***`declined`***

> *"Nobody has told me what I'm not allowed to do. There's no list. When I ask, people tell me what's sensible,
> and everyone's sensible is a bit different. So how do I find out where the edge is — do I just have to walk
> into it?"*

**Substance:** ⛔ **Art. I.4's law-making power exists, granted 2564, with no recorded use on a person.**
**`H40` found no authored prohibition anywhere in the admitted set.** ⇒ ***A city with no authored prohibition
cannot tell a child what is forbidden.***

**Disposition — `declined`, not `unmet`.** ⭐⭐ **Davis is not protecting an unwritten constitution. Nobody has
noticed there is nothing to tell the child.** ⚠ **Written as characterization: *the edge is found by walking
into it, and the walking leaves no record, which is why it is never fixed.***

## ⭐⭐⭐ The Lover — ***`accepted`***

> *"There are bars. I've been in them. Nothing happens in them — nobody's rude, nobody's unwelcoming, there's
> just no evening that a bar is FOR. Where do two people go to be a pair in public? I can work beside her. I
> can put my name next to hers on a run. What I can't do is take her somewhere that means anything, because
> there isn't anywhere that means anything."*

**Substance:** ⭐ **Canon-affirmed at Phase 4 and Phase 8 §A.4 — *the bars exist; the culture around them does
not*, and Davis sits OFF the glitch-coolant axis entirely.** **Commercial is 15% of the distinctive tier: real
venues, real employment, no institution.** ⚠ **The only courtship form the corpus offers is *repeated voluntary
collaboration* — a Tier 3 candidate, and it is WORK-SHAPED.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ WHY THIS IS `accepted` AND NOT `declined`
> **Rule 3's test: would satisfying this make Davis more like the others?** ⛔ **NO — and that is the
> distinction.** **Giving Davis a signature drinking institution WOULD homogenize it.** ✅ **But the Lover is not
> asking for one.** ***She is asking where a pair goes, and the answer is that Davis has never built a place
> whose purpose is being together rather than doing something together.***
> ⇒ ⭐⭐ **That is a real, writable, characteristically-Davis hole: *the city has social VENUES and no social
> OCCASIONS.*** **`H71`.**

⚠⚠ **And the Impotent Lover shadow was run because of it** — *"what has gone dead here? Ask of any place that
reads as competent and joyless."* ⭐ **Davis reads exactly that way, and the sheet names this as the likeliest
single omission in *"a place built from structure outward"* — which is precisely how this pass was built.**

## The Parent — ***`noted`***

> *"Where are the children while I'm under glass? Somebody has them. Nobody has written down who."*

⛔ **Genuinely unwritten.** **Real, deferred, and not this pass's to invent.** **`H72`.**

## ⭐⭐ The Ruler — ***`accepted`***

> *"Everyone keeps telling me the harbor runs all year. Fine. An icebreaker is not a fact, it's a standing
> bill. Who owns the hull, who crews it, out of whose allocation, and what happens the first winter somebody
> decides the money is better spent? You have built a city that eats through a door you have not funded."*

**Substance:** ⭐ **The Ruler's brief specifically notices *"resource flow — imports as well as exports"* and
*"unadministrable rules."*** ✅ **The objection is correct: `Ports.md` now rates Davis `CONSTRUCTED`,
year-round, icebreaker-dependent — and `H65` records that the icebreaking is unsited and unpriced.**
⇒ ⭐ **`accepted` — the panel sharpens `H65` from a siting question into a FUNDING question.**

## ⭐⭐⭐ The Elder — ***`accepted` → `H69`***

> *"I kept the same run for sixty years. I can't any more — the hands go first. I handed it on, which is what
> you're supposed to do, and everyone was glad. And now I am not on anything. Nobody did that to me. There
> isn't a word for what I am now, because nothing happened."*

**Substance:** ⭐⭐⭐ **The Elder's brief: *what the place does with people it can no longer use.*** **Phase 9
§C.3 established the unattached as produced by an omission nobody authored. Gate 9 established there is no
written route back.** ⇒ ***The Elder is the person that mechanism produces, and Davis has no form for them.***

## ⭐⭐⭐ The Mentor — ***`accepted` → `H69`***

> *"I can teach anyone the work. What I can't do is put them on a line — a line isn't mine to give, it's a
> record of who was there. So a slow learner isn't failed, they're just never entered. And there's no second
> intake. There's no intake at all. You either got onto something or the seasons went past you."*

**Substance:** ⭐ **Phase 7 §C.2 answered the Mentor's back half — *what becomes of the ones who can't* — with
*unattached*. The Mentor asks the harder version and Davis has no answer: IS THERE A WAY BACK IN?**
