### Robots

**P4B-3 · A robot's day ends at a heated place, every night of every month; for about three months nothing else ends it.** [Inflected; national recharge made year-round by Casey's cold]

- →2 A robot's day length is set by her charge and the distance to heat, not by the light. In winter the light ends long before the day (2.7 h in June, spec L137); in the bright weeks it never ends it.
- →3 **At Casey the night is an indoor fact.** From late October to mid-February the robot majority's stop exists only inside heated rooms; in December the robot half goes indoors to recharge under full sun. **P4B-4 · A robot's outdoor work is planned around the next heated place, not around how long she can stand the cold.** [Determined physics, Casey's figures]
