### LANG-1 · F-3 · Mirny's two common layers: the conditions met with no language, the civic tongue national and learned

**Proposed finding (PROPOSED).** At Mirny two layers are common to residents, and they stand in opposite relations to translation. **The place's conditions** (wind and drift, light, the ring and sheltered core, the ten-month fast ice and its open-water window, the coastal rock, and the relay and the record as things: LANG-2) are the only native layer, and the only layer every resident, in either body and from any stock, meets with no language between her and it. **The civic tongue**, Hawaiian (later Tepenian; one language over time), stands beside them from the founding on, common and national, not native. Canon defines it as a pidgin with a primary base of English and Japanese and a supporting base of Korean, Tagalog, Ilocano and Mexican Spanish. It is met without translation by every resident who has it: a human born here learns it, a robot's dominant half reads it [D, conditional on AR-40], and a resident who arrives without it learns it. Whatever is common **and Mirny's own** is built on the site, the record and the relay, by its referents; the civic tongue is common without being built on them. (A, B, C.)
