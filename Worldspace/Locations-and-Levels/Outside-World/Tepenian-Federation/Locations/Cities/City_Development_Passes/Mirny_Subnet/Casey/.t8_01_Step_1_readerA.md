### S1A-1 · The sea gate: the open season is written, and only for ships coming in
- **Finding.** Spec L154: "the Budd Coast is accessible to shipping during the navigable season (roughly November–March) but ice-locked in winter; this directly determined Casey's supply windows". Spec L82: "This made Casey a viable destination for incoming ships during the exile period." Spec L135 and L141 (notes column): "sea ice forming" in April, "sea ice breaking up" in October.
- **Mechanism.** A seasonal gate on everything that comes to Casey or leaves it by sea. The ice makes the decision; no person does.
- **Written half.** The open season, and the inbound direction: ships could come in, and the season set the supply windows. The Frame adds that leaving by sea is possible only in that season (Frame L241), that the founders' access ground is open only in that season (Frame L170, conflict 5), and asks what is held at Casey between seasons (Frame L171).
- **Missing half.** The closed season, from the side of whoever is caught by it: a ship, a cargo or a person that misses the last weeks of open water; anyone at Casey who needs to leave by sea between about April and October. The spec's own lines write only "incoming ships" and "supply"; no admitted spec line writes a departure.
- **Lasting, and the way back.** Written by physics, not by any person: the ice breaks up in October (L141) and the sea opens about November (L154), so the closure lasts one winter and no longer, and the way back is the next season. What the wait costs, and whom it costs, is not written.
- **What it would reach (as questions, not findings).** If the roads came late in the frame (PA-5's sensitivity, Frame L501: "Act 1 Casey stands on sea arrival and the inherited base"), the closed season would close every route out of early Casey for about seven months. And whoever the ice holds at Casey over the winter is neither a summer arrival nor obviously part of the counted residents (see S1A-3), which is the case the Frame's Seasonal re-check trigger watches for (Frame L80).
- **Questions.**
  - **S1A-Q-1 (Step 2).** Frame L171 asks what is held between seasons. Ask the other half with it: what the closing of the sea does to people and goods caught on the wrong side of it, and whether anything at Casey answers to that wait.
  - **S1A-Q-2 (Phase 5, under REQ-3 / REQ-17 and AMD-4, AMD-5).** Within the frame, is there a winter way out of Casey other than the sea, and from what point? Do Casey's own port lines record ships leaving as well as arriving?
  - **S1A-Q-3 (Phase 5, then the Seasonal re-check at Frame L80).** Does the ice hold a population at Casey over the winter that is neither the summer flow nor the counted residents? If so, `01` L136's question of which population is the subject must be asked.
