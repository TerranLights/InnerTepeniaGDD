# 6 · ⭐⭐ WHAT THIS PASS IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH — for whoever picks it up next

**Shirayuki is a city that was handed everything it has and cannot say why it should exist. Its ground is
`87–91%` impermanent, its air abrades every exposed surface on a clock, and it has no shore on the harbor it
depends on. Almost nothing obliges it to do anything continuously except the weather — and it took on an
enormous voluntary obligation anyway, in exactly the register its own weather punishes most. Out of that it
built a standard of worth — *nothing here is old; things are only well-kept* — which is the one value under
which an inherited city is livable rather than resented, and which canon's own `Ashiato` scene turns out to be
named after. It grades people by their hands, kindly and without an author, and the one thing it cannot
maintain — access to water — it eventually secured by ceasing to exist as a separate city.**

⭐ **And per the Review Panel: read all of that as a place people like living in.** ***Loving the cleaning is
love.***
