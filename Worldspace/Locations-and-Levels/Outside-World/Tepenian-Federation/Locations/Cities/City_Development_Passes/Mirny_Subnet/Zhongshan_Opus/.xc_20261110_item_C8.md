no in-world act. The label it carries, "as arithmetic", is the same label item (4) gives Zhongshan's retention rates

not an in-world event. Read the same way, the ruling fixes **how much** rock is Zhongshan's. It does **not** say that anyone in
the world divided it. **This reading is mine.** The ruling's text does not settle it either way, so it goes to the developer
as C8-Q1.

"8–11%" (L293, and Phase 7 L473) becomes about 8 to 12%. That is a figure change. The Spine already marks this layer as
independent of the division (L296).

little more than a third. (ii) The Gate's MATCH (L63) compared 8.1 to 11.6% with Phase 0's 8 to 11% and passed. The ruling gives
8.4 to 12.0%. The low end now sits inside 8 to 11%, and the high end is about one point above it, where the Gate tolerated about

What L93 argued from the bound still follows: 88 to 92% of the city stands on ice, so "the overwhelming majority of it stands on
engineered apron", and the dense, shared-wall condition belongs to the rock core. What L69-70 says descends from the band
(the rock/apron gradient, the class structure, the axis in `Phase 9`) still **stands**. The conclusion keeps its direction.
Its stated ground ("a proportional third", "at most a third") and its figures need restating.
