## The differentiation axis

**Kept together, fed apart.**

Here the line that sorts people is not kind and not origin. It is whether a person has kept through a water year in this place, and that line runs through both halves. Kind still divides people in three places only: the table, the night and the way they die. The human half eats the city's own food. The robot half is sustained through the same doors the food leaves by. The axis is named on this place's lines alone and is checked against no other place.

## The lens decision

- **Human half: a shared constraint, with origin at household scale.**
  - **What the culture is organized around:** the keeping year: the drains before the melt, the refreeze, the made dark and the warm rooms (Phase 5 P5-24; Phase 6 P6-9; Phase 8 P8-60).
  - **Why origin cannot be the city-wide lens:**
    - No origin row is shared by one person in seven (spec L59; Phase 7 P7-44).
    - **Origin is ancestry, read under the divergence operator** (runbook §C.9b–e). At a height in the Census I era, nearer Act 1 (`DEC-583`), origin forms are still lived, but inside households (P9C-204, P9C-206).
    - Nothing is read from a nation (the stereotype law).
- **Robot half: city/locality, through the shared constraint and the local need she was built for.**
  - **The ranking:** city/locality is the seat of identity (Ch 13 L27–36). Build counts as circumstance, not body type (Ch 13 L38–40; physiology L424). The weighing is city first, Build second, Gen/Mark last (Ch 19 L22, L40–41).
  - **The anchor, stated as the brief requires:**
    - **What it is:** the founding population the Founding Register names: **Indonesia and Malaysia, jointly, Indonesia leading as a state with Malaysia in support** (Register L65; `DR-61`).
    - **Its ground:** geography and access, never the real station or its operator (Register L11; `DR-19`).
    - **What it carries:** the anchor reaches a robot only as mediated inheritance, "through the local culture the founding population built, not through any individual robot's origin" (runbook L2116–2119).
    - **What it does not carry:** the founding party's size and composition are open (REQ-84), and the founding "why" is not derived (`DEC-577`). So the anchor fixes **where the local culture was first laid down and by whom**, and no content beyond that. The founding-wave rows (Indonesia 12.60%, Malaysia 3.11%; spec L60, L68) are rows only, never the founding party's size and never a default for any robot (`DEC-588`).
- **Any category that matters more than kind:** the kept year (P9C-203). Neither half organizes around a shared exclusion: none is written (Phase 6 P6-27; row 3 above).
