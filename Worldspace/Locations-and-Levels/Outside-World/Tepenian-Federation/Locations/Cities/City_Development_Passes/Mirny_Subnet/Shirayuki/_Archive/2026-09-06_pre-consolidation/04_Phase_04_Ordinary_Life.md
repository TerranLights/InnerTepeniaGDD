# C · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CORRECTION — THE YEAR IS A DIAL, NOT A MODERATE MIDDLE**

**§4 wrote: *polar night `49` · midnight sun `62` · ordinary lit days `~254` — "`70%` of the year, and nobody

> ## ⛔ **PHASE 3's `C` BREAKS THAT, BECAUSE LIGHT AND WIND RUN ON OPPOSITE SCHEDULES.**
> **Katabatic forcing is MAXIMAL in the dark *(no solar input, largest radiative cooling)* and SUPPRESSED in
> summer *(solar heating of the ice slopes)*.**

| | Light | Wind |
|---|---|---|
| **Midsummer** | ⭐ maximum | ⭐ minimum |
| **Shoulder** | middling | middling |
| **Polar night** | ⛔ zero | ⛔ maximum |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **SO THERE IS NO MODERATE MAJORITY. THERE IS ONE DIAL, AND EVERY DAY SITS SOMEWHERE ON IT.**
> ***More light means less wind. Less light means more wind. There is no calm dark day and no bright windy
> peak beyond midsummer's own.***
> ⛔ **"Moderate, lit, variable and unremarkable" describes a temperate shoulder season. This city does not
> have one — it has a TRADE-OFF that never resolves.**
