## The axis

# **Exposure, not kind.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could:* every settlement has natives and newcomers, and every Tepenian city has two bodies. What passes is the pairing at Mirny's own values. At Mirny **nobody was native at the founding** (`P2` E1 [CORR]: no prior population, `DR-28`), and **the one competence the site demands of everyone who goes outside cannot be inherited by any channel** — not by descent, not from a teacher, not from the record (`SPN` §8; `P2` P2-3 [CORR]). So the only mark of being *from here* that the files show Mirny acting on is a practice acquired by exposure: *"The outsider Mirny is built to notice is the one who misreads the wind, not the one from elsewhere"* (`P5` L237 [CORR]), and *"Belonging at Mirny is legible at the threshold, not in the voice."* (`P8` L553 [CORR]). That practice is acquired by both bodies, written as *"a fork, never as a skill level"* (`P4` P4-2 [CORR]). Delete every other place's name from *Mirny's people are sorted, by the place itself, into insiders and newcomers according to how long they have lived in its wind, not according to whether they are human or robot, because no one was native at its founding and its one demanded competence can be acquired only by exposure* — and the claim survives; it was never relational. **Not checked against any sibling** (terminal check).

**What question it answers that no earlier Mirny phase answered.** `P2` asked who arrived and how; `P3` what the place feels like; `P4` what a day is made of; `P5` what is owed and received, and who counts as an outsider at the edge; `P6` who answers for a loss; `P7` who decides between claims; `P8` what the place makes for itself. **`P9` asks what sorts the people who live here into kinds, and how those kinds relate.** The files' answer: **the line the place draws is exposure to its wind, and it cuts across both bodies; kind (human or robot) does not decide who is inside — it decides the channel by which one gets inside, what a misjudgment costs, and how a life runs.**

### P9-2 · THE INFLECTION — the line Mirny draws is exposure, and it is body-blind *[CORR + IND + D]* · **emergent · deep** · both bodies · Act: the line is independent of the Act; who stands on each side of it is Act-sensitive · *provisional: rests on PA-31 (the compact as a derivation) and on no in-world speech (R-67, R-111)*
- *1st.* The files show Mirny acting on exactly one line between its own people and others, and it is the wind's: after an injury, the reflex question is what the injured person did (`P2` L417, `P4` P4-7 [CORR]); the outsider it notices is the one who misreads the wind, not the one from elsewhere (`P5` L237 [CORR]); what marks a resident is her reading and her preparation at the door, *"not an accent, a build or a stock"* (`P8` P8-26 [CORR]).
