### The dual assignment `Settlement + Installation` — considered, declined

`§C.8b` generalizes: *"everywhere founded as a station and now inhabited as a home carries it."* Declined here
specifically, because the dual assignment's value is the live tension between **staffed** and **settled** — a
place that was somebody's purpose before it was anybody's home. This city's own canon removes that tension:
continuous habitation, not a handover from an institution to a populace. There was never a staffed phase for
the settled phase to be in tension with.
