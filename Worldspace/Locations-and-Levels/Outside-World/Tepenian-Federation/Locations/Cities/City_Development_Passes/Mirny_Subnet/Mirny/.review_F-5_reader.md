## 5 · Verdict

**NARROWS**

The ground of part (i) no longer stands as written, for two separate reasons:
1. **Under `DR-111`, English is not Mirny's supporting tier. It is one of two languages in it,** set by special ruling. L394's "With English Mirny's supporting tier" carries L392's "English alone", which is the wording `DR-111` L1770 marks as written before the ruling.
2. **Under A1, the supporting tier is no longer the only route by which an English label could be taken for in-world speech.** English is now a primary base of the dominant tongue itself (L1895). An English authoring label could therefore also be read as an attested word of Hawaiian (later Tepenian), Mirny's dominant language for both bodies (P10 L392).

Both are true together, on different objects:
- English as a supporting-tier language of Mirny's residents (`DR-111`);
- English as a lexifying base of the national, dominant tongue (`DR-125` item 6).

**Part (ii), the operative rule, stands untouched.** It never rested on the tier: P08 L316 states it with no language-tier ground. If anything, A1 makes it more necessary. **The core does not flip.**

**The problem with the verdict set:** this change widens the hazard more than it shrinks the conclusion. NARROWS is chosen because the stated ground fails as written and needs re-deriving. HOLDS would leave a stale ground in place.
