## The axis

**Claims without an arbiter.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could:* any place with several claimants and one resource has a priority question. What passes is the **pairing at Mirny's own values**. Nearly everything Mirny's economy does is owed to claimants outside the city and beside one another — the subnet's contact with itself (`SPEC` L184), materials for the eastern roads (`SPEC` L174), raw material for the chamber chain (`SPEC` L176), the inward sea supply (`SPEC` L143) — **while the thing that would arbitrate between them is, in every file admitted, either undefined (the treaty: *"Nothing in this Article shall be read to define what authority sits above such regional groupings"*), unwritten (no council, grouping or office is admitted) or placed where the claim cannot reach it (the road's hybrid authority governs the road, not the quarry).** And where an arbiter would have to be created — to call the wind, to enter a loss against the record, to order a crossing — creating it would give a loss a second answerer and change what Mirny is (`P6` P6-18). Delete every other city's name from *Mirny's work is owed to several claimants at once; no arbiter stands between them in any admitted file; and the one authority that does act, the person at the point, answers for the outcome alone* and the claim survives; it was never relational. **Not checked against any sibling** (`SPN3` L463–466).
**What question it answers that no earlier Mirny phase answered.** Phase 2: who arrived and why. Phase 3: what the surface and season feel like. Phase 4: what the day is made of. *Who decides, by what legitimacy, how succession works — and, the more productive question, what is unadministrable. Everything Mirny-local below is **derived [D]** from Mirny's own pressures and described by FUNCTION; every institution or practice is tagged **[CAND]**. No office titles, no body names beyond the work-defined groups the pass already uses (the keepers, the harbor watch), no personal names, no schedules or frequencies, no Federation-level legislature, court or office. **Criminal justice at Mirny: TBD — undeveloped, not absent (`H42`, developer ruling 2026-09-16); no court, code, penalty ladder or enforcement body is derived anywhere in this phase.***

### P7-11 · The shape of order at Mirny, in one reading *[D on the findings below]* · Act-sensitive (P7-17)
Order at Mirny has three layers and they are of different kinds. **A thin national frame** with authorships and reasons (P7-12, P7-18) — **a layer of emergent convention** with no author (the compact, `P6` P6-1; *"Robot cooperation, reciprocity, and social convention develop emergently, through lived experience"*, ch. 12 L21) — **and a layer of positional authority** (whoever is at the point of work decides, and answers for the outcome alone). **The larger claims on the same people and ground (the quarry, the relay's restoration, the harbor's payer) fall between the layers** (P7-14). So the answer to *who decides* is: the person at the point; and for the larger claims the files name no one. The answer to *by what legitimacy* is competence and presence, not office. The answer to *how succession works* is two clocks and no authority by age (P7-17).
