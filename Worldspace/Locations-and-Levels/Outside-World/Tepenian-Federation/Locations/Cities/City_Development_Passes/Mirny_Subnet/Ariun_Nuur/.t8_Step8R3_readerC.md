## 4 · The Lover faculty, late

*Is this place alive, and could anyone love it?* R3's files add to what Phase 6 had: the polar night is sunless, not lightless, with a
noon twilight every day and a week when the moon circles the sky (CAT-B1-5, CAT-B1-6; RC-58); the first sunrise comes over a flat
horizon that nothing blocks (extends CAT-56, L1779–1781); sound may carry far in the still cold (CAT-B2-4, conditional); and warmth
given across kinds is paid from the giver's own food (FIND-P9-40), so care here has a literal price. Against that: no child, no elder's
way down and no pleasure in a stopped hour is written yet (items 201, 207; the pleasures wait for the CST), and every beauty in the
catalog is a setting or a candidate occasion, not something anyone is written doing.

### The Lover faculty, late
Late Lover-faculty verdict (R3): alive, and lovable in its sky and in what giving costs (a sunless but not lightless night, a first sunrise nothing blocks, warmth paid from the giver's own food); still not shown as a life someone would choose, because no child, no way down and no pleasure is written, and the beauty is all setting.

*Set beside Phase 6's early verdict (Phase 6 L506):* the early verdict was "alive, yes; lovable, not yet shown", with the air's glitter as
the one beauty given. R3 widens the beauty (the night's own light, the unblocked sunrise) and adds giving's price; it does not yet show a
life anyone is written loving. The direction is the same; the evidence for *alive* is broader.
