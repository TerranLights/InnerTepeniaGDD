## The differentiation axis

**Everything arrives already decided.**

*What the words carry.* Every relation that shapes this city is decided somewhere else and reaches it finished. **People:** no admission to the country happens in the city; international arrivals are processed on a reception ship at the ice edge in summer or at an isolated island airport in winter, outside it (`DR-82`, rulings L1499; `DR-96` items 2, 6, L1633; Phase 2 P2-10), and reach it already cleared. **Power:** the site gives no energy (spec L261), and the supply that lights the export food, heats the rooms and, `[PROV-8]`, charges the robot half is the continental grid, which the parent's ruling makes reach the city at its height (`DR-138` item 9, rulings L2014). **The sea:** there is no berth; the city's bulk trade rides another place's port at the far end of its one road (spec L13; `DR-95` item 4, rulings L1623; `DR-96` item 3). **The road:** a terminus; nothing passes through, and the junction where it meets the national road lies at its far end (spec L12; `Highways.md` L278). **The work it does for others:** its mandated shares are ruled outside the world (`DR-71`, `DR-72`, `DR-75`; Step 1 AS-15). What the city holds itself is narrow: the gulf ice under its two airports (`DR-96` item 1), whose keeper is not written.

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs four properties at once, each on this city's own lines: admission outside the city (`DR-82`); no energy from the site with a national supply in its place (spec L261; `DR-138` item 9); no berth, the sea trade on another place's port (spec L13); a terminus (spec L12). Put admission at its own door and arrivals are decided here; give the site energy and its largest load is its own; give it a berth and its export leaves by its own door; put it on a through-road and others pass. It rests on rulings and admitted lines, not on `[PA-12]`, `[PROV-8]` (the charge clause is not load-bearing), the held ground or the open one-door count. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** the national language, clinicians trained at national institutes, the national exile, the shared night. The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.

**The core move, run on the axis (`03` L655–657): what does each side refuse to develop, and who supplies it instead?** The city develops no sea door, no western road, no energy plant of its own written at the height, no admission, no training of its clinicians: Casey's port and junction, the parent's grid, the parent's intake and the national institutes supply them. The parent develops no second uncorrelated food region, no second seed stock and no nutrient-recovery role elsewhere on this list: the city supplies them (`DR-75`, rulings L1421; `18` L30–33 `[DoI corroboration]`). **A feedback loop, not a standoff:** the more of the nation's insurance the city holds, the more continuous bought power its doors must carry (P5C-209, P5C-220).
