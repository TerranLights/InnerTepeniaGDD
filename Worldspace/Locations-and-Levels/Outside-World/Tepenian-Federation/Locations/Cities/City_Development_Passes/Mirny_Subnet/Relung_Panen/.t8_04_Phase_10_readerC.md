## 6 · B · Real-world research (mechanisms only; a pick is a source, not a specification)

**Web calls: 32 of 100** (30 WebSearch, 2 WebFetch); every string, source, rejection and dead end is in the research log. **SECONDARY and SUPPORTING picks first**, as the brief directs; PRIMARY picks were not searched (absorbed earlier). No place, people or culture is transplanted; no real-world figure is a city figure.

- **P10C-B1 · One street's sun, then each household's own (L132, W1, W4, W30).** In the fjord town the sun is held to reach one named street on a set day, but residents keep the day three ways: on the fixed date, when the sun shines on that street, or "on the exact day the sun hits their home" (W30). **Mechanism taken:** a sun-return that is a property of each piece of ground, not of the town. **Transplanted: nothing** (no pancakes, no coffee; any occasion waits for the holidays stage, `DEC-610`). *The per-household form came only from the re-query of a pick already logged as covered (LAW 0-R).*
- **P10C-B2 · Which lakes keep their ice (site, W2, W5, W7).** The site's own primary record: the oasis "contains many lakes, only a few of which maintain an ice cover all year," and "the extent of glacier ice contact becomes the controlling factor in maintaining an ice cover all year" (Doran et al. 1996 abstract, W7). A secondary summary: lakes in the center mostly open in summer, lakes in contact with glacier ice mostly keep theirs (W5). **Taken as a site fact** (real geography, not station history): the margin lakes are the ones that hold. Algae Lake's own break-up date was **not found** (dead end at the sources).
- **P10C-B3 · Made ground on bare rock (L161, W3).** Sand and seaweed layered on bare limestone, topped with soil and dung over years; the cleared stone stacked into dry walls that also keep wind off the new soil. **Mechanism taken:** made ground has a visible boundary, and the boundary is built of the rock it was cleared from. Only the boundary is used (P10C-209); the materials of this city's made ground are its own (recovery works, Phase 7).
- **P10C-B4 · Shared ditch work's first water (L171, L172, W8, W18).** Spring clearing is debris work or silt-digging; labor owed in proportion to holdings; "for the first water a crew follow the water as it flows through the entire acequia" (W8); up to 200 workers mobilized "when the snow melts enough at altitude" (W18). **Mechanism taken:** the clearing is followed by a walk behind the first water. Who decides and who owes is governance and **is not written** (`DR-137` item 11); Phase 2 P2-30 keeps any borrowed form reserved (RF-102).
- **P10C-B5 · The coldest room that must not be eaten (L198, L199, W6, W14, W29).** A collection guarded in shifts by people who did not eat it, in rooms where "no one was allowed to remain alone" (secondary, unverified, W6); a backup store opened only a few times a year to limit exposure (W14), meant to stay frozen on rock and permafrost without power, then watched round the clock after meltwater reached its entrance (W29). **Mechanism taken:** a rarely opened door, and a cold that must be checked, not trusted. The guard regime is **not** taken (a rule; and not this city's).
- **P10C-B6 · The cold room between (W10, W31).** An entry room between outside and living space where coats, boots and often a freezer are kept and guests leave wet footwear (W10); a vestibule that "keeps out cold air, wind, and drifting snow," with the floor raised so doors swing above drift (W31, TM 5-852-9). **Mechanism taken, conditional:** the door may be a room, not a leaf; no line of this place's establishes one (P10C-219).
- **P10C-B7 · Room by room, not hall by hall (L181, W17).** The cultivation room is the unit (racks, lights, beds, air units, CO₂, water), with harvest, trimming, packing, precooling and shipping in **separate** rooms; the envelope insulated and near airtight to keep pests and dust out; 10 to 18 tiers. **Mechanism taken:** the growing rooms are sealed rooms with a chain of rooms behind them.
- **P10C-B8 · Light that shows from outside (L180, W23).** Glasshouses "lit from within" glow over a town at night. **Not adopted:** this city's racks are sealed rooms (B7); whether any light leaks to the street is a design question (P10C-RQ-203).
- **P10C-B9 · The made dark (W19).** Blackout curtains "essential for many people" under midnight sun; foil as a stopgap; the trade-off is that a blacked-out window cannot be opened for cooling. **Mechanism taken:** the dark is hung, by hand, every summer night. Here December's mean is about 0 °C, so the cooling trade-off is small (spec L190).
- **P10C-B10 · The warm room before the charge (W16), a real-world comparable only.** Lithium cells charged below 0 °C risk plating; packs are warmed before charging, which takes time. **Not a robot fact:** robot physiology is canon's (`DR-137` items 3–5); this only shows the shape of a need the city's own lines already state (Phase 4 P4-6).
- **P10C-B11 · A place a rescue ends in (W25).** Emergency-only shelters with sleeping benches, a hob, a gas heater, first aid, a beacon and provisions left by earlier users. **Mechanism taken:** a warm place kept stocked by its users for the next one. Siting **open** (P10C-RF-204).
- **P10C-B12 · The one road (L133, W27; L143, W13, W32; L138, W21).** One road carrying about 96% of travelers and 83% of goods (W27); a year's supply demanded at a pass before entry (W13); a single-lane tunnel run on a timetable (W21). **Taken:** only the one-road shape, which the city's own lines already give (P10C-221). **Not taken:** the year's-supply rule and the timetable (no line supports either). **W32 corrects the pick itself:** the barge in L143's town is **weekly**, not "a ten-day barge cycle" (P10C-RQ-205).
- **P10C-B13 · Tidal cracks at an ice shore (W26, W28).** Tides lift fast ice near shore and open cracks that snow hides; cross them square, probe often (US Antarctic field manual, W26); the site's epishelf lakes are tidal, with fresh water floating on marine water (W28, record only; full text not retrieved). **Taken, conditionally:** the apron's shore edge is a crack zone (P10C-202).
- **P10C-B14 · A margin lake that empties (L134, W22).** An ice-dammed basin that drains every summer; the warning is issued when a camera shows it draining; barriers along the river. **Mechanism taken:** a margin lake that is watched. The site's own record of ice-margin lake outbursts is S3-34.

## 8 · B3 · The Planet and Element determination

### The draft, written before any symbol file was opened (verbatim)

**This place's own character, from Phases 1 to 9 only:** a still, walled rock basin, lit every day of the year and ringed by ice, whose people live on things they made (soil from nothing, warmth, a summer dark, winter water, a warm charge for half of them) and keep them by hand, ahead of the need, against a summer that gives the most and takes the most back (Phase 3 axis; Phase 6 axis "Keeping against the summer"; Phase 4 "The made stopping hour"; Phase 8 "Made for the crossing"). Its largest life (the lit export, the heat, the robots' charge) rides one line it does not hold (spine S2-8; amendment S2-41; Phase 5 P5-30; Phase 6 P6-30), and it reaches the rest of the country through one neighbor and the whole nation (Phase 5 "One neighbor, whole nation"); its arrivals come cleared, as second stops (Phase 2), and belong by keeping a year, not by kind (Phase 9). Its worth to the nation is its difference, a hedge kept live and uneaten (spine S2-17; amendment F-G7-1). **Two words for it, before the files:** *keeping* and *shelter* (inward warmth in a still bowl), with *stillness* and *the made dark* close behind; *harvest* deliberately not used (the name is flavor, never data, rulings L866).

### The determination (after the files; members' meanings only; no incumbent pair)

- **P10C-290 · Planet: Earth, PROVISIONAL.** Its meaning is "Viability built from convergence, not from any one advantage" and its negative "A stability entirely contingent on everything continuing to line up at once ... it simply hasn't failed yet" (`Planetary_Symbols.md` L83–86): this basin lives because made soil, made warmth, a made dark, winter water and a line it does not hold all keep lining up, and nothing in it is natural plenty. *Tension recorded:* Earth's neutral, "the natural home," does not fit a made place; the fit is in the positive and negative, not the neutral.
- **P10C-291 · Element: Air, PROVISIONAL.** "It shelters by being held still, and that shelter costs nothing but stillness"; "Its shelter depends on a stillness it does not control" (`Robot_Elementals.md` L80–81, L85–86): the still bowl and the warm rooms, and their dependence on what the city does not hold.
