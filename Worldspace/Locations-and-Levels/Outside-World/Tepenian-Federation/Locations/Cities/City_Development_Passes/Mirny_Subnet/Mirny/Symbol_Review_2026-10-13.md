## 1 · Mirny as the established data gives it (Phases 2–10, with `DR-56` applied)

- **Physical:** a rocky, exposed coast on the Antarctic Circle; wind is the defining condition (mean 12.0 m/s, about 3 % calm-or-weak, ground blizzard under a clear sky); **heat follows the wind, not the thermometer** (`P3` P3-2); the thermometer holds a plateau May–September, so the **year is dated by light** (no polar night, about 29 midnight-sun days); the door where wind, noise and cold stop at once (`P3` P3-7); a shared indoor stop (`P4` P4-1).
- **Social:** assigned exiles, nobody chose the site (`P2` P2-1); fourteen stocks, no majority; two bodies at near parity; a hub of nine on a single relay (`P5`); the stone goes out to be finished elsewhere; delivered heat; what stays was made by others and what residents make refills (`P10`).
- **`DR-56` (the wind):** the first generation or so learned the wind alone; once they had it, they **taught each other and set up signifiers with a weather report/relay system** to communicate dangers. Duty without compulsion: people of both bodies feel deep guilt at not going out when best placed (Q-69). This community duty is **stated as a trait of Tepenians**, so by the national-trait rule it cannot discriminate Mirny from other places.

## 3 · The Planet: Saturn re-tested, and a contender appears

**Saturn** (rings are debris; least dense; a steep tilt with real seasons; *true nature may be unknowable*; positive: beauty from fragments rather than wholeness; negative: held together only loosely):
- **Survives, independent of `DR-56`:** fourteen stocks with no majority and continuity built from handoffs (*fragments*); seasons made by light while the thermometer holds a plateau (the tilt term).
- **Weakened by `DR-56`:** Phase 10 also leaned on *a private reading and a record that cannot name what it left out* (the unknowable-nature term). With the wind learned then taught and signifiers in place, that support is thinner.
- **Contradicted:** the negative term (*held together only loosely*) does not describe Mirny, and the community duty makes it less true still (Phase 10 had already caveated this).

**Neptune** (the farthest planet and home to the **fastest winds** in the system; its existence calculated from its effect on another body before it was seen; positive: being known through your effect on something else, intensity unrelated to proximity to the energy source; *quietly willing to share its secrets with anyone willing to do the brainwork*; negative: long existing only as an inference):
- Phase 10 reserved it as fitting *the relay rather than the place* (RF-1). `DR-56` changes that: the wind is **worked out through its effects** (drift, sky) by the first generation and then **shared** by teaching and signifiers, and Mirny's heat follows the wind, not the thermometer (intensity unrelated to the energy source). That is the city's central condition, not the relay.
- **Word-match guard:** *winds* is shared, but the property is also shared (a wind-dominated place).
- **Limits:** Neptune's *farthest, alone* flavor does not describe a coastal hub of nine; its terms about effect-known and inference are about knowing, which the pass has just been told to restate.

### Determination (proposed)
**Planet — leave open; Saturn stands provisionally but is no longer clearly the best fit.** Neptune is now a serious contender after `DR-56`. I would not confirm either until the amendment register (job 3) restates what the wind and the teaching actually are. The registered pairing was reasoned from the personality read, so its agreement with Saturn carries no weight (`NI-3`).
