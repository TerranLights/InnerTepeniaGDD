canon agrees: *"humans could continue arriving into Tepenia after the signing"* (`DR-127` item 2, quoted at `note` L139-140). So
the one-way half of the conclusion holds and the closed-system half flips. I agree with the first check (C10 output L66-69).

**Finding 102. On the robot side, the closure may never have had a ground.** Article II.2.1 sends to Tepenia *"every robot who may
hereafter come into being within such territory"*, *"in accordance with such schedule and such means of conveyance as the Assembly
Powers shall provide"* (`Treaty` L46). The pass quotes that same sentence's *"schedule and… means of conveyance"* at `P5` L134. The
draft's change note names only II.2.5 and the IV.2.2 cross-reference as new (L138). So nothing on my list dates II.2.1 as changed,
and the pass appears to have had the "hereafter" clause in front of it. If so, the robot half of the closure was an inference error
when it was written, not a change of input. This is a likelihood, not a fact: my list cannot show the Treaty as it read on the pass's
date (Question 128; Choice 137). The first check said only that it "cannot tell" (C10 output L58-59). Either way, as the input
stands today, II.2.1 opens a conditional robot channel, and `DR-127` left robot arrivals unanswered (`note` L141).

**Finding 103. Every later human arrival comes on the founders' own terms, so the inward channel is a continuing exile, not a
correction from the source.** II.2.5's last sentence says such a person *"shall for all purposes of this Treaty be treated as a
