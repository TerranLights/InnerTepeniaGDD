## ⭐⭐⭐ THE TYPE RULING — **the GPS law decides it, and that is the finding**

**`01` §1.1:** *"Anywhere founded as an installation and now inhabited as a home carries `Settlement + Installation`,
and the tension between 'staffed' and 'settled' is a live source of material."*

⚠ **This site LOOKS like the textbook dual assignment** — a polar research station with a city on it.
⛔ **It is not one, and the reason is binding rather than judgmental:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the dual assignment requires** | ***The LOCATION was founded as an installation and later became a home*** |
| **What actually happened here** | ***The CITY was founded by diplomatic allocation and settled by exiles.*** *The station was inherited PLANT, not the city's origin* |
| ⛔ **Why the station cannot supply the type** | **The GPS law: a real site's builders, flag, lineage, abandonment and fate are a COORDINATE, never a cause.** ***"It was a station" is site history, and site history is not an input*** |

> ### ⭐ SO THE LAW FORECLOSES A TYPE ASSIGNMENT THE SITE'S APPEARANCE WOULD HAVE SUGGESTED.
> ***`Settlement + Installation` here would be the GPS violation wearing a taxonomy label*** — **it would make
> the real station's identity structural to the city.** ✅ **`SETTLEMENT`, singular.**
> ⚠ **This is worth watching corpus-wide:** *every Tepenian city sits on a real station, so the dual assignment
> is available everywhere by appearance and earned almost nowhere by mechanism.*

| **Ruined / abandoned** | ⛔ **OUT OF FRAME.** *The damage is post-war* |
