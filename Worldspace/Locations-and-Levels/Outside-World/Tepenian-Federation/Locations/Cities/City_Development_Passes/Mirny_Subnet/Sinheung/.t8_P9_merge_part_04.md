## 3. New findings

### {{ID:N:net_change}} · The census rows are net counts, so "took the exit" is a reading, not an observation

- **Evidence.**
  - Retention is Census II divided by Census I, taken by kind, so it is a ratio of net counts.
  - Human death is continuous. RP L151: "Senescence is a steady rate that never pauses". Robot death is episodic (RP L150), and robots "do not age in the biological sense" (RP L67).
  - Robots are fabricated in each city's own chamber (Ch14 L35-36).
  - CENSUS L22-23: "Census I is the peak; Census II is post-orbital-migration." This dates Census II after the migration. It does not say that the whole difference is migration (B).
  - No admitted line gives the interval between the two counts, or the births, deaths and fabrications within it.
  - Phase 9 L88-89 rest "Nobody died." on Step 2 rule 2 as the Spine cites it (Spine L113-114). The rule's own text was in no reader's list.
- **Statement.** *The 1.47-point difference is a difference in net change. It equals a difference in who took the orbital exit only if births, deaths and fabrication were nil for both kinds, or equal in share. So the gap can carry a natural-change part of unknown sign and size. "Nobody died" holds as the reading convention, not as an observed count.* This piece does not reason from the interval between the censuses, which is not in any list (A).
  - **Strength:** `conditional`. It strengthens {{REF:F:residual}}: a residual that may include natural change cannot carry a sized cause.
- **Pass locations.** Spine L117 (qualifier); Phase 9 L88-89 (qualifier); Phase 9 L117, where "who stayed" should read "who remained in the count" (B).
- **Open.** How the amendment states the reading is put to the developer ({{REF:Q:q_net}}).
- **Disposition:** `found by two` (A, B); C adopted it in Round 3.
