- Noon elevation = 90 − (66.25 + 23.44) = 0.31° geometric, about 0.8° with ~0.5° refraction.
- Shadow per unit height = 1 ÷ tan 0.8° = 71.6 (C's ≈72), or 1 ÷ tan 0.31° = 184.8 without refraction.
- So a 100 m rise to the north shades about 7.2 km at June noon (18.5 km geometric).
