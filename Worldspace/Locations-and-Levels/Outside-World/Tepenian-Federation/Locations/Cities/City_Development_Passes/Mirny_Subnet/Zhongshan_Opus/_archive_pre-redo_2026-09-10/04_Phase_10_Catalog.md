# 1 · Named places and landmarks

**Canon-named, and this pass adds nothing to the names.**

- **Nella Fjord** — the fault trench between two horsts, rock-walled both sides, carrying deep water inland
  through Broknes Peninsula. **The working harbor is on it; dominion is not this city's.**
- **The tri-junction** — where Hwy 4, Hwy 22 and Hwy 110 endpoints meet. **The eastern terminus of the
  continent's single most structurally important corridor**, and, per `16` §18, **not an employer.**
- **The Tri-Cities Airport** — shared, domestic. This city is a **host**, not a served-not-host city.
- **The 6.7 km road** to the continental plateau behind the Broknes settlements.

**Described by function, left for the developer to name:**

- **The registry hall** — where C3 lives. The largest single baseline industry in the city, and the building
  that knows what nobody may ask.
- **The quay and its rebuild yard** — a CONSTRUCTED-tier harbor is *"a recurring act of construction."* The
  yard is where the act is staged, and it is busy in the months the water is not.
- **The road-end stop** — the hitchhiking node where Hwy 22 ends. **You wait inside, in the warm.**
- **The west shore looking across** — 1.16 km from the fjord head, in sight of the opposite bank at 0.71 km.
