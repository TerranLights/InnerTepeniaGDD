**P9B-10 · Yes: at Casey the category that matters more than kind, and more than origin, is whether a person has been held and warmed through a winter; and the city's two membership lines, the count and the winter, part in both directions, on the held stranger and on the newly built robot.** *The phase's strongest finding.* [Genuinely emergent; deep]
