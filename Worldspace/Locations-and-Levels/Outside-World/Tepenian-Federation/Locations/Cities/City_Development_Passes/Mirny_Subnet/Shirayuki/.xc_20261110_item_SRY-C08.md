## 1 The conclusion as the pass states it

- **SRY-C08-1. The basis, as the pass states it, is the court's allocation alone.** The spine's founding condition
  names as its ground "a claim already settled" (`02_Spine.md` L86), settled by the court and "pre-exile" (L87). No
  geographic ground appears in the founding condition (L84–L88). The amendment note lists `02_Spine.md` L87,
  `04_Phase_02_Composition_and_Arrival.md` L107 and `01_Inherited.md` L197–L204 among the "Pass places that state
  the founding basis as the allocation alone" (`Amendment_Notes_2026-11-10_Cross_Check.md` L67–L68).
- **SRY-C08-2. The spine (§2c, `G4`) draws its absences and quadrant from that basis.**
  - Without a founding negotiation: "Nobody in the founding generation ever had to make the case for being here."
    and "The argument was had by other people, on another continent, about somebody else" (`02_Spine.md` L94).
  - Without having chosen: "The founding generation's relationship to the place begins with someone else's
    decision" (L96).
  - Strength: "Legitimacy requiring no defense" (L101).
  - Deficit: "NO FOUNDING ARGUMENT." and "A city that never had to argue for itself has no rehearsed account of why
    it should exist — and when one is eventually needed there is nothing to reach for" (L102).
  - Grudging tolerance: "Nobody forbids the question." The permitted answer is "a court on another continent
    decided, for reasons about somebody else" (L104).
  - The absences with other grounds (L95, records not people; L97, no hardship; L103, thin standing cost) do not
    rest on who chose the site.
- **SRY-C08-3. Phase 2 (B and B.1), scoped to the founding era** (`04_Phase_02_Composition_and_Arrival.md` L101,
  L122 "Scoped to the founding era."):
  - "Inherited it", by an inverse mechanism: "a population whose membership was decided elsewhere" is said to be
    the allocation exactly, and "But it runs backwards: normally a population comes WITH a place; here the place was
    assigned TO a designated population." with "Same produced condition, opposite direction" (L107).
  - "Chose it" absent at founding, because "the Court chose the site" (L109); in B.1, "the Court picked" (L117).
  - B.1's reading: "NOBODY IN THIS CITY IS HERE BY A DECISION THEY MADE." (L121).
- **SRY-C08-4. The inherited-material asymmetry check (A1).** The allocation is treated as "(a GATE)"
  (`01_Inherited.md` L197). Written: the founding nation's admission, with "Three paragraphs, a contributing-factor
  analysis, and a vindication" (L201). Not written: the refusal of another party, where "Exclusion was not a side
  effect of the mechanism. It WAS the mechanism" (L202). Durability: "MORE durable." (L203). Route back: "NONE —
  and the reason is structural." because "The body that issued the exclusion has no successor with standing over
  this place." (L204). Consequences: "A permanent allocation nobody in-frame can appeal" (L212); "THE ALLOCATION
  OUTLIVES THE CATEGORY IT WAS WRITTEN IN." (L214); "It cannot be renounced any more than it can be appealed", and
  "So a city can inherit an advantage it no longer has the vocabulary to justify", an "INHERITED AWKWARDNESS"
  (L215). The pass leaves the inverse open: "Not answered here. Phases 2 and 5 own it." (L221).
- **SRY-C08-5. The conclusion in one line** (my summary of SRY-C08-1 to SRY-C08-4, not a quotation): the founding
  rests on the court's allocation alone. A body elsewhere chose the site for the founders, for reasons about
  another party. So the founding generation never argued and never chose. It has no founding argument to reach
  for, and it holds an allocation it can neither appeal nor renounce, and cannot justify in present vocabulary.

## 3 Reasoning (each step cites a line read)

- **SRY-C08-12. The allocation still stands, so the core survives.** The Founders column still reads "by the
  Jeju-do court's diplomatic allocation" (Register L61). Geography is "not a replacement" (L61), and the ruling is
  "Not a rewrite of the basis." (LOG L930). No admitted line says that the founding generation negotiated, chose
  the site or took it over in person, or that the allocation can be appealed or renounced. These therefore still
  follow:
  - the first sentence of `02_Spine.md` L94;
  - L96, L101 and the first clause of L102 ("A city that never had to argue for itself");
  - Phase 2's founding-era findings that the membership was "decided elsewhere" (L107), "the Court chose the
    site" (L109), "the Court picked" (L117) and L121;
  - A1's gate, its asymmetry and its lack of a route back (`01_Inherited.md` L197–L214).
- **SRY-C08-13. The basis has gained a second ground, and the change is in substance, not in a figure or a
  label.**
  - The Register requires each founding to stand "on the city's own geography and access." (L14), and a founding
    nation to stand "on geography and access" (L11).
  - `DR-19`'s general clause makes geography and access the ground of legitimacy (LOG L552).
  - Register L61 now states that ground for this city ("and, in addition, geography").
  - So the founding now rests partly on a fact about this place, and not only on a decision taken elsewhere about
    another party.
- **SRY-C08-14. In four places the pass's wording treats the allocation as the whole reason, and that part no
  longer follows as written.**
  - (a) `02_Spine.md` L104 gives the permitted answer as "a court on another continent decided, for reasons about
    somebody else". Under the current basis that answer leaves out a stated ground about the place itself
    (Register L61). Its allocation half follows; its implication that the reasons are only about somebody else does
    not.
  - (b) `02_Spine.md` L102 says "when one is eventually needed there is nothing to reach for". The Register now
    records a ground in the city's own geography (L61, L14). That ground is described as "(the edge of the rule)"
    (L61), so it may be thin, and no admitted line says how strong it is. But "nothing" no longer follows as
    written.
  - (c) `02_Spine.md` L94, second sentence: "The argument was had by other people, on another continent, about
    somebody else". This still follows for the allocation, but the added ground is about this place and not about
    somebody else.
  - (d) `01_Inherited.md` L215: "So a city can inherit an advantage it no longer has the vocabulary to justify".
    The admission now has a stated geographic ground (Register L61). That ground does not depend on the category the
    pass says has dissolved ("a category that no longer exists.", L208). No admitted line says whether it can
    justify the exclusion side of A1 (L202) as well as the admission (L201).
- **SRY-C08-15. Phase 2's inverse mechanism is stated on the allocation alone** ("here the place was assigned TO
  a designated population", L107). Register rule 2 makes a founding nation stand on geography and access (L11), a
  ground that ties the founding population to the site's position. No admitted line decides whether this addition
  changes "Same produced condition, opposite direction" (L107). The finding survives for the allocation, and its
  "opposite direction" wording needs re-checking.
- **SRY-C08-16. The pass's own statement of the basis (allocation alone) is not a form the Register holds.** It
  appears at `02_Spine.md` L86–L87, `04_Phase_02` L107 and `01_Inherited.md` L197–L204 (per note L67–L68). The
  Register holds the allocation plus geography (L61), and it forbids a pass to "restate a founding claim that this
  file doesn't hold" (L9). That is the input change the note records (`SRY-005`, note L65–L78), and the ground of
  every conclusion under this item has to be restated from it.
- **SRY-C08-17. Why this verdict.**
  - Not `FLIPS`: no admitted line contradicts the core. The allocation stands (Register L61; LOG L930), and nothing
    admitted makes the founders negotiators or choosers, or the allocation appealable.
  - Not `HOLDS`: the change is a new ground, not a figure or a label, and it removes part of the wording or ground
    of `02_Spine.md` L94 (second sentence), L102 (second clause) and L104, and of `01_Inherited.md` L215. It also
    leaves `04_Phase_02` L107's "opposite direction" open.
  - So the verdict is `NARROWS`.
