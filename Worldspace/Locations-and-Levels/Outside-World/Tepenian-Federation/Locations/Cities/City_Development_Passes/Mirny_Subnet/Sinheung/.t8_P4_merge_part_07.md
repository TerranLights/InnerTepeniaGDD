  the Standard reaches into work that does not make things.

  ({{REF:Q:dr4_scope}}) are interpretations of `DR-4`, 01 and the spine, offered as provisional.
- **The climate figures** come from FRAME L164 (a pass line citing GHCN-Daily), not from a source in the list.
- **Stale prose in D16.** Narrative sentences in D16 outside the admitted spans may still quote older percentages;
  D16 L1 says such sentences "were not rewritten" (B).
- **The `DR-68` census rows.** The ruling that applied `DR-68` to the census rows is in no list. The census was used
  as it stands (C).
- **No comparison.** No ranking, no other city's figure and no other city's answer was used. Where a listed line
  names another location, it is written (another location: not used).
