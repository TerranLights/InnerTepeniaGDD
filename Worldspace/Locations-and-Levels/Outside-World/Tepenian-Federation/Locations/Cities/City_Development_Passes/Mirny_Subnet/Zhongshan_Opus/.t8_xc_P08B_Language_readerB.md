**(b) What still holds, and why.**

- **Finding 104 (the guard holds and gains a ground).** *"No proportion, no feature list, and no invented dialect."* (L845) is

**(c) The re-derived conclusion: grounds, then proposed wording.**

  columns (`P02` Finding 23, L316-320; Finding 31, L393-395), so the 12-point rule gives Mandarin alone at Census I or Census II, for
  robots or humans. *And what does that cause?* The open question of which census governs §8.8a's figure (Question 125) cannot move
  the language set. *And then?* The set rests only on the equal shares. *Third order:* if robot origin percentages are ever set apart

  With a 26.13-point lead a change is unlikely but has not been computed.
- **Finding 108 (P-1 and L155: the language is named, and one fact is the city's own).** "Language family" becomes a named language,
  Hawaiian (later Tepenian; Hawaiian at the signing: L1632). The supporting tier is not supplied by the Federation: it is the national
  rule (`DR-100` item (3)) applied to the city's own composition. *And what does that cause?* `Phase 5`'s "Supplies" column (L155)
  carries the dominant language and the rule, and the city's own side carries Mandarin. *And then?* Because the supporting tier is
  computed from the composition, the city's language picture is exactly as provisional as its composition table (Finding 107's
  condition). *Third order:* P-1 is marked cheap to revise (L327) and keeps its direction, so nothing built on it flips; on my inputs
  only §8.8 and the table cell need the supporting tier written in. `DR-100`'s row "Not applied yet" lists *"how the rule enters a
  finished pass"* (L1670); D-6 answers for a re-derivation (*"Yes: a re-derivation uses the rulings as they stand"*, note L42), and
  `DR-101` sends the change to the amendment note, not into the pass files (note L33-35).
- **Finding 109 (where the two clocks are heard).** The two-branch marker is a marker of the stock's own language, which is Mandarin,
  and Mandarin is now this city's supporting tier: a language *"widely enough understood that business, social talk and general
  dealings can be done in it and people can be assumed to follow"* (`DR-100` item (2), rulings log L1669). *And what does that cause?*
  The marker has a living public medium, so "still audible" (L822) is grounded rather than asserted. *And then?* Because everyone in
  the city can be assumed to follow its supporting tier, not only the 35.83 of the Primary row, the two-clock Mandarin is heard by
  people of every origin here: the marker belongs to the city, not only to the stock (`Frame` L350: *"stock, people and local culture
  are named separately"*). *Third order:* the supporting tier is the layer people use beside Hawaiian, not instead of it, so how often
  the two clocks are heard depends on how much of daily life here runs in Mandarin, which no input sets. "Still audible" holds;
  "how loud" stays unwritten, consistent with "no proportion" (Finding 104). Whether the marker also carries into the city's Hawaiian
  (as accent or loanwords) is not set (Question 122).
- **Finding 110 (at the signing, the national language was already Hawaiian).** L1632's general sentences: at the signing the de
  facto national language *"it's Hawaiian"*. The founding branch came from 2564 (L829). *And what does that cause?* The newcomers of
  L833 arrived speaking the origin language into a Federation whose common language was already Hawaiian, so the two branches met with
  a third language present from the first day. *And then?* If the resident branch already spoke Hawaiian as its common tongue at the
  signing, the newcomers met residents for whom the stock's language was already a second layer, not only a drifted one; if it did
  not, the residents and the newcomers learned Hawaiian together. The inputs say neither (Question 123). *Third order:* either way,
  the stock's language here is a layer beside the national language, not the only speech, wherever in the frame the supporting tier
  applies (the rule states no starting date, so I do not claim it held from 2564); the meeting of L832-834 keeps its direction and
  gains a setting. No wording below claims which
  branch spoke Hawaiian.
- **Finding 111 (the closed door: `P02` taken as the current input).** `P02` proposes that the closure flips and survives per person:
  *"Every origin culture here is open inward and closed outward: the door closes behind each person, not behind the culture."* (`P02`

  take up Hawaiian, like everyone. *And then?* In the city's supporting tier her speech would be a third clock beside the two
  branches; whether the city takes her form as the better one is unwritten (`P02` Choice 55, L668-669). *Third order:* "two clocks"
  stays the city's founding fact; a third can be written only as a possibility until the stream is ruled (`P02` Question 74), so the

- **Finding 112 (one word in `P02`'s wording goes past its own open question).** `P02` restates L834 *"so that the source can reach the
  city only in newer exiles who cannot go back"* (L548-549) and the cell as *"neither can go back to the source, which reaches them
  only in newer exiles"* (L558). But `P02` itself leaves open whether *"recordings, letters or other carried information"* crossed and
  *"whether the source could reach the city without a person arriving"* (`P02` Question 77, L726-730), and the pass's own L839 says

  current speech. So the wording proposed here keeps `P02`'s substance (nobody who came can go back; the source can reach the city
  only by coming to it) and drops "only in newer exiles" (Choice 128). The two pieces should be reconciled on this word before either

  cause?* Both layers can be assumed here: Hawaiian everywhere, Mandarin in this city. *And then?* A switch between them is not a test
  of whether the listener understands; it is a choice of layer, the national one or the city's own. *Third order:* since the city's
  own layer is the plurality's stock language but is followed by everyone (Finding 109), switching into Mandarin here marks a speaker as
  of this city rather than of the stock, which is the separation the pass itself requires (`Frame` L350). Agrees with the first check
  on the inversion (first check L119-123) and goes one step further.
- **Finding 115 (the "built commons" is a named, sourced national language).** *"the genuinely shared layer was built here"* (L850-851)
  and *"the built layer — the one with no source at all"* (L854) no longer follow for speech. The universal layer is Hawaiian (`DR-100`
  item (1)); it has named sources, *"a Pidgin between a primary base of English and Japanese, with a supporting base of Korean, Tagalog,
