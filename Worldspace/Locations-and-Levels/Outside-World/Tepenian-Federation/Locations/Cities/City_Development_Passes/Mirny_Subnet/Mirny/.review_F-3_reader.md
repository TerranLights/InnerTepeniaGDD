## 6 · What exactly changes

**(i) No longer stands as written: the exclusivity in L283.** "These are **the only layer every resident, in either body and from any
stock, meets without translation.**" Under canon, Mirny has from the founding on a civic tongue for both bodies (P08 L269, L300), and
A1 now defines that tongue (L1894 to L1895). A medium every resident shares, in both bodies, is a second common layer beside the
conditions. The pass's own amendment layer already says so (P08 L304: "What the city shares in speech is the civic language plus a
conditions vocabulary of its own wind"). The word "only" excludes it.
- **Both-are-true reading, tried first.** The two layers differ in kind. The conditions need no language at all: a resident meets the
  wind, the drift, the light and the ice directly. The civic tongue is a language: it is shared, but it is learned, and it is national,
  not the place's own (P08 L307: "What is Mirny's in speech is the conditions vocabulary of its own wind"). So there is one property
  (shared by every resident) in two forms (unmediated for the conditions; a common, learned tongue for speech), on two objects. This
  reading saves the conditions' claim. It does not save the sentence as written, because L283 does not say in which sense "without
  translation" is meant, and in the plainer sense (a medium every resident follows) the civic tongue qualifies too. The scope has to be
  stated in re-derivation (IQ-301).
- **Act.** A1's "one language over time" puts the civic tongue in both Acts, so the narrowing is not limited to one Act.

**(ii) Also narrows: two clauses of L284.**

  L269 places at Mirny "from the founding on".

  relay. The clause survives only for what is common *and Mirny's own* (P08 L307).

**(iii) What survives.**

  civic tongue is national, not native, so it does not enter the native layer.
