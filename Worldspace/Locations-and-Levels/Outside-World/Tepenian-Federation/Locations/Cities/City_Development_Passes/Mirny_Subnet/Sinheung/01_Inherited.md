# 2 · ⛔⛔ THE ASYMMETRY CHECK — **seven of nine wrote only the favorable half**

## 2.1 ⭐⭐⭐ `G` · THE SINHEUNG STANDARD — **a verdict machine with no appeal, BY CONSTRUCTION**

**This is a GRADING system, so every clause of it is a verdict.** ✅ **And unusually, the inherited text DID
write part of the adverse side** — *"a distinct, less prestigious informal secondary market for lower-grade or
rejected material."* ⭐ **That is rarer than it looks; most inherited material stops at the favorable path.**

⛔ **But it grades MATERIAL. It never grades, or un-grades, a MAKER.**

| The mechanism | Written? |
|---|---|
| **What happens to rejected STONE** | ✅ **Written** — *it goes to a less prestigious secondary market* |
| **What happens to the PERSON whose work was rejected** | ⛔ **Not written** |
| **Whether that outcome is as durable as the favorable one** | ⛔ **Not written** |
| ⛔⛔ **Whether there is a route back** | ***STRUCTURALLY ABSENT — see below*** |

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ THE STANDARD CAN CONDEMN BUT IT CANNOT PARDON, AND THE REASON IS IN ITS OWN DEFINITION
> **Canon: *"Not administered by any single office; the standard exists as an informally shared civic
> expectation each trade enforces on itself."***
>
> ⛔ ***An unadministered standard has no appeal mechanism by construction — there is nobody to appeal TO.***
> **A grading body can be petitioned, can revise, can re-examine, can forgive. A shared civic expectation
> enforced by each trade on itself has no address at which any of that could happen.**
>
> ⭐ **So the asymmetry is not an oversight in the writing — it is a property of the institution as canon
> defines it.** ***The favorable path has an infrastructure and the adverse path has none.***

⚠ **This is `Step 1`'s highest-value catch and it is handed forward, not resolved here.** *`Phase 7` (Order)
owns what a place does with judgment; `Phase 4` owns what it is like to live under one.*

## 2.2 ⭐⭐⭐ `E` · THE INSTITUTE — **one premise, two implications, and canon wrote one**

**The Institutes file states its own governing logic plainly:**
> ***"Tepenia cannot evacuate a patient off-continent — so it cannot import a trained clinician either. There is
> no foreign residency to send someone to and no outside profession to recruit from."***

✅ **Canon draws the favorable implication:** *this makes the three institutes **load-bearing national
infrastructure**.*

⛔⛔ **The identical premise carries an adverse implication nobody drew:**

> ## ***IF A CLINICIAN CANNOT BE IMPORTED, THEN REJECTION FROM ONE OF THREE INSTITUTES IS NOT "TRAIN SOMEWHERE ELSE." IT IS EXCLUSION FROM THE PROFESSION, NATIONALLY AND PERMANENTLY.***
> **There is no fourth school, no foreign residency, and no outside profession to enter instead.**
> ⭐ ***The same sentence that makes the institutes precious makes their admissions gate absolute.***

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who is admitted, and what that gets them** | ✅ **Written** |
| **Who is refused** | ⛔ **Not written** |
| **What a refused applicant does instead** | ⛔ **Not written — and canon has closed every obvious answer** |
| **A route back** | ⛔ **Not written** |

⚠ **AND A SECOND, SHARPER ONE INSIDE THE SAME INSTITUTE.** *Its defining focus is **"the physical AND
EMOTIONAL care of, and for, robots,"** and it produces **robotic counselors, whose discipline is a robot's
inner life."*** ⛔ ***Nothing states what happens to a robot whose inner life the counselors cannot help.***
⭐ **A care institution's failure state is the most characterizing thing about it, and this one has none
written.** ⏸️ *Handed to `Phase 4` and `Phase 9`.*

## 2.3 ⭐⭐ `D` + `C` · **CANON DECLARES THIS CITY'S MAIN INDUSTRY REPLACEABLE — in passing, in a file about doctors**

> ***"Davis can be supplemented by fishing fleets; Sinheung's fabrication can be duplicated. A physician cannot
> be improvised, and cannot be imported."***

⭐ **The sentence exists to elevate the Institute. The verdict on Sinheung is a rhetorical foil.**
⛔ **Nobody wrote what it means FOR SINHEUNG to hold, in canon, the judgment that its dominant sector — 45%,
the corpus's highest — is duplicable.**

> ### ⚠⚠ AND THE CLAIM SITS IN TENSION WITH THE COUNT, IN THE SAME BODY OF CANON
> | Canon says | Canon also says |
> |---|---|
> | *"Sinheung's fabrication **can be duplicated**"* | **it is *"one of only TWO cities that manufacture chambers at all"*** |
>
> ⭐ ***Duplicable in principle; duplicated exactly once in practice.*** **Both are canon and both may be true —
> the runbook's own both-are-true instruction applies — but the reconciliation is not written, and the gap
> between "could be copied" and "has been copied once in 250 years" is exactly the kind of space a city's
> self-understanding lives in.**
>
> ⛔ **And `F` is the third data point: the ONE historical attempt to add a third site FAILED.** *`Step 2` gets
> this as a live tension rather than a settled fact.*

## 2.4 ⭐⭐ `F` · MOUNTAIN PASS — **the one inherited finding that DID write both ways, and it still has a hole**

✅ **This is the exception, and it is a good one.** *Canon supplies motive (kinship with both plateau
populations), means (only Sinheung had them), the act, the partners, **and the failure** — "the Tower fell, the
residual grid supply ended, and the capability died with it."* ⭐ **It even writes the residue: *"the ruin of
the attempt sits on Hwy 37 exactly halfway between the two cities it was built to serve. A monument to a failed
act of solidarity."***

⛔ **The hole: canon writes the RECIPIENTS' outcome and not the ACTOR'S.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the failure did to the plateau** | ✅ *"The plateau is dependent again"* |
| ⛔ **What the failure did to the city that pushed for it, supplied it, and watched it die** | **Not written** |

> ## ⛔⛔ AND A FRAME-INTEGRITY WARNING THAT COULD EASILY BE MISSED
> ***The Tower falls in 2812 — the Second Interwar's TERMINUS, not an event inside it.***
> ⭐ **So within this pass's frame, Mountain Pass is ALIVE.** **The plateau is being supplied. The act of
> solidarity is a working arrangement, not a monument.**
> ⛔ ***A pass that writes the ruin has written the wrong Act*** — **and the ruin is by far the more
> literarily attractive version, which is exactly why the warning is needed.**
> ⚠ **This is `Gate F` material and is pre-flagged here so it cannot arrive late.**

## 2.5 ⭐ `A` · THE ALLOCATION — **it confers belonging on a third of the city**

**The founding is a status conferral: Korean exiles hold this site by a diplomatic claim decided before the
exile era began.** ✅ **Canon writes what that gives the founding population.**

⛔ **`G8` says South Korea is `34.62%`.** ***So `65.38%` of this city is not of the founding stock, and the
founding story says nothing about what it confers on them.***

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the allocation gives the founding stock** | ✅ **Written** — *legitimacy, priority, the city's own account of why it is here* |
| **What it gives the other two-thirds** | ⛔ **Not written** |
| **Whether there is a route in** | ⛔ **Not written** |

⚠ **AND THE CHARACTERIZATION INHERITS THE SAME GAP.** *`Specs/` `## Character` calls it **"a singularly Korean
city"*** — ⭐ **a description of `34.62%` of it, applied to the whole.** ⛔ *`Technique 14`'s question exactly:
does this describe the general population, or one part's version?*
⏸️ **Handed to `Phase 2` and `Phase 9`. `NO FORCED FIT` applies — the answer may be that the allocation
genuinely confers nothing on the rest, and that would be a finding.**

## 2.6 ⚠ `C` · THE CREDIT VERDICT — **a permanent second place with no route out written**

> ***"Sinheung builds to the current-generation schematic (Mark IV), designed at Neumayer… Sinheung
> manufactures the chambers, but the underlying engineering credit belongs elsewhere."***

⛔ **A standing verdict about credit.** ✅ *What the city gets — the work, the volume, the Standard's own
archive of the schematic revisions — is written.* ⛔ **Whether the city can ever hold design credit, has tried,
or has stopped wanting to, is not.**

⭐ **And note what the Standard's third pillar actually is:** *"the guarded technical archive preserving
Neumayer's full historical run of Mark IV schematic revisions."* ***The city's own standard of craft is built
partly on custody of someone else's design history.***

## 2.7 ✅ `H` · THE AIRSTRIP — **symmetric enough, and the reason is instructive**

**Canon writes the route, its duration *("began sometime after Dome Fuji's pilgrimage resettlement… ran until
the Long Night War")*, and deliberately declines to pin a start date.** ⭐ **A declared looseness is not an
asymmetry** — *it is an open question written as open, which is the correct form.* ✅ **No finding needed.**

## 2.8 ⚠ `B` · THE 45% — **admissible with a mandatory tag**

**`Industrial fabrication: 45%` is `G3` and genuinely admissible, and it is PROJECT-INTERNAL DERIVATION.**
⛔ **Every finding resting on it carries the `05` §6.1 Column-3 tag** — *corroboration, not independent
confirmation.* ⚠ **And per `§C.6`: process-derived figures are baselined on CENSUS I** *(`962,415`)*, **so a
check against Census II will look `~20%` high and that is the convention working, not drift.**
