### A.2 · The source's own stated limits (what population, over what span, the source describes; `00b` L156-157)

1. **Long-run, not a moment.** "National communities are classified by tier based on long-run population share." (census L33). The tiers summarize the whole frame's accumulation. They describe neither the founding party, whose size is in no admitted line (Frame L723), nor any single season's arrivals.
2. **The founding tag is a label, not a date.** "the tag says nothing about when people arrived" (census L33), and since `DR-88` "(founding wave)" "now means a nation named as a founder in the Founding Register, and nothing else" (rulings L1555).
3. **The tiers describe presence, not standing.** Primary: "Dominant national community; shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity" (census L36). Significant: "Distinct, visible communities with meaningful presence in city culture" (L37). Notable: "Present and recognizable; background cultural texture" (L38). Language has since been taken out of the Primary clause by `DR-100` (contract L378, L482).
4. **Weighting, not counts.** "The tiers and the city totals are the information." (rulings L472). In-world the census is a headcount: stating a figure as a population is fine, but "deriving a characterization from a within-tier gap is not" (L473). Runbook L2113: "A weighted ranking is not a census."
5. **The table's own scope.** Shares are of Casey's own population and "identical between censuses"; "Robot figures apply the same proportions as the human population." (spec L74).
6. **Drawn tiers stand** as relabeled (`DR-131` item 7, for every city; rulings L1949). Seven rows sit at 4.5% or more, five Primary and two Significant, none Notable (Frame G0-38).
7. **Census operations are not events.** `DR-85`'s rework put every country in its latest-period form and moved shares by random draws (rulings L1527-1528); at Casey the Sinian Federation's share was multiplied by a draw (spec L27, DEMOTED). Nothing added or dropped by a recomputation is an in-world arrival or absence (brief note (a)).
