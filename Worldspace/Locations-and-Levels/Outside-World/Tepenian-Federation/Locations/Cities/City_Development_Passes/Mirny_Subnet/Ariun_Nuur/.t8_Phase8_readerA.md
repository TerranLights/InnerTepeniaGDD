### Food, for all populations present

**The axis of this category: the majority eats from the supply and the shipment, the minority from the far end of the road, and every liquid either body takes was ice until someone paid to melt it.**

**FIND-P8-1 · The robot version first: siligel, energy first, and warmth paid in it.** Siligel is robot food; its first function is energy replenishment, its second maintenance and repair (RP L238–247). Processing it is exothermic, so **warmth costs siligel**: a robot keeping herself, or someone else, warm in the cold burns more of it (RP L484–491). Here every outdoor hour is cold (no monthly mean above −31.3 °C, spec L105–116), and outdoors a robot is safe while moving, because work makes the heat that keeps her cells in range (RP L15–16). → **The robot majority's food bill rises with every outdoor hour and every warm act, and its food comes in by the road** unless the city's geography makes it (RP L478–482; PV-6; RF-21 stays reserved; Phase 5 FIND-P5-45). → *Expression:* a robot back from outdoor work has spent food the way a human back from the cold has spent heat. No rate, no figure (`DR-140` item 4). PROPOSED. [A]
