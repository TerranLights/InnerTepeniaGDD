The lake is about 230 km long and up to 50 km wide, its water about −3 °C (research FACT-58; spec L70's length differs, Q-56 open); its contents are reserved (RF-6). *[A1 · B101 · C206]*

**FIND-P3-2 · The air.** Mean wind about 5 m/s, 27 m/s in the strongest events (spec L82; CR-31); the plateau lacks the slope-to-coast gradient that drives katabatic wind (spec L127; CR-39, the closing comparison struck). "There is no whiteout-under-clear-sky here — when visibility closes, something is actually falling." (spec L93; CR-33, the comparison struck). Precipitation probability about 3–11% a month (spec L105–116). About 22 mm of precipitation a year (spec L83), and "What falls and what lands are the same size." (research FIND-4: the measured 22 mm and the stake-measured 22.5 kg m⁻² a year agree); the spec's own split between what falls and what is retained (L88–89) rests on no source found and is not used. Mean relative humidity about 71% (67% in the box) against 22 mm a year: the air is "humid by the gauge and empty by the liter" (research FIND-5; low weight). *[A2 · B102 · C206]*

**FIND-P3-3 · The sky.** Diamond dust is always present; on clear days the sky has a permanent faint shimmer, and halos, which the spec calls solar, and pillars are common; the visual quality of the air is distinctive (spec L128; CR-40; paraphrased). About 75 ± 16% of what accumulates at the site falls from a clear sky as diamond dust (research FACT-91, its UNVERIFIED mark kept), so snow arrives without weather (research FIND-4). *[A3 · B102 · C206]*
