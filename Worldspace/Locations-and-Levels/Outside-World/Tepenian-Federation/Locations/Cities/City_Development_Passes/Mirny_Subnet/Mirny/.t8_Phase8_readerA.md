## 4 · The axis

**Prepared, not predicted.**

**Peer-free test — "could this be true anywhere?"** *Half of it could:* every cold place prepares for its weather. What passes is the pairing at Mirny's own values. Canon makes exposure *"PREPARATION, the same category it is for a human"* (`RP` L50) for both bodies; and at Mirny prediction is not merely hard but structurally unavailable — no lead time is demonstrated (`RES` L123), *"The cues are honest; they are unread"* (`RES` L30), the one thing a caller could offer is *"a *now*, not a *soon*"* (`P7` P7-15), and a public wind call, a verified reading or an amended record would each *"give a loss a second answerer — and change the compact"* (`P6` P6-18). So what Mirny makes for itself — what it wears, keeps, eats, says and maintains — carries the whole weight a forecast would carry elsewhere: it is made to hold whatever the wind does, not to know what it will do. Delete every other place's name from *Mirny's people cannot forecast their wind and their compact declines the instruments that would let them, so what they make — the clothes, the stores, the words, the upkeep — is preparation for any wind rather than a reading of this one* and the claim survives; it was never relational. **Not checked against any sibling** (`SPN3` §0.4; terminal).

**What question it answers that no earlier Mirny phase answered.** `P2` asked who arrived; `P3` what the place feels like; `P4` what a day is made of; `P5` what is owed and received; `P6` who answers for a loss; `P7` who decides. **`P8` asks what the place makes for itself, and for what — and the answer the files support is: things that let a person cross without reading the wind; while the one thing Mirny could make out of the reading itself (a written entry, a public call) is the thing its compact refuses.** A sub-axis runs through play: *the predictable is played with, the unpredictable is prepared for* (P8-16).

**The column the axis would fill** (write-only, `SPN3` L146–147): Mirny's own column of the differentiation instrument, the Making row — *Prepared, not predicted*. No other location's row was read or is read.

### 8.4 · DRESS AND APPEARANCE — function first
