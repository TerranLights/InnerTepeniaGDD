# 0 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE PHYSIOLOGICAL FRAME — **cold is a preparation problem, not a gate**

**`Robot_Cold_Physiology.md` gives thresholds. Set them against this city's own numbers:**

| The threshold | Shirayuki |
|---|---|

| **Embrittlement — *"a knock that dents at `−10 °C` **cracks** at `−50 °C`"*** | ⭐ **at a `−9.9 °C` annual mean this city sits at the DENT end of the file's own example** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐ WHAT ACTUALLY BINDS: **THE GLOVE WEIGHT THE CLIMATE PERMITS.**
>
> **This city's upkeep is *skill, not effort* — *"polish… to remove oxidation **without scratching**."***
> **Precise, tactile, close work — and it is done in gloves, here as everywhere.**
> **The insulation/dexterity trade-off is universal to anything with human-shaped hands:** *thicker gloves
> reduce dexterity and touch sensitivity; fine motor tasks and small parts are the first casualties.* ⭐ **Thin
> high-gauge gloves exist precisely for precision work — and they are less warm.**
>
> ### ⭐ Tepenian robots inherit the trade-off BY DESIGN
> **`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`:** *"**the one constant across both eras is the human
> form/figure itself** — Tepenian robots still choose to build each other in that basic shape… because they
> see no compelling reason to depart from what evolution already optimized."* **A human-shaped hand in a glove
> has a human-shaped hand's problem.**
>
> ## **EVERYONE WEARS GLOVES. THE QUESTION IS HOW THICK — AND THAT IS SET BY THE SEASON.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE SECOND ORDER — **thin gloves in abrasive air are CONSUMABLES**
> ***A thin glove is the thing grit destroys fastest, and it is worn by everyone doing the work the city is
> organized around.*** ⭐ **So gloves are an ordinary recurring expense, visibly worn out, constantly replaced
> or mended** — **not equipment, closer to a staple.**
> ⭐⭐ **And they do DOUBLE DUTY.** *Under the seal loop (§B.3), for a robot a glove is also **seam
> protection**.* ***One garment, two populations, two entirely different reasons for wearing it.***
> ⏸️ **The textile-trade question is Phase 8's to earn or kill, not banked here.**

⚠ **And it cuts both ways** — *the physiology file names the opposite kit explicitly:* **"a player kitted for a
katabatic coast — drift shovels, whiteout lines, aerodynamic shelter — is carrying dead weight" inland.**
⭐ **Shirayuki IS a katabatic coast. That equipment register is this city's, and canon names it as a distinct
one.**

# B · THE FOUR SENSORY SUB-FIELDS — **answered separately, per `03` process B**

> *"A place can be visually calm and acoustically tense, and losing that loses the finding."*

## B.1 · SOUND — ⭐⭐⭐ **the wind has a direction, so the quiet has a map**

**Persistent, strong katabatic flow from the NORTH-EAST.** ⭐ **Constant, and — unlike gusting — *directional
and predictable*.**

> ## **EVERY STRUCTURE CASTS AN ACOUSTIC SHADOW, IN THE SAME PLACE, ALL YEAR.**
> ⭐ **Second order:** *the lee of anything is where conversation happens, and it is the same lee every year —
> so the quiet places are FIXED and everybody knows them.*
> ⭐⭐ **Third order:** ***a city whose sheltered spots are permanent, public and free does not need to build
> many.*** **Meeting happens in found lee, not in provided rooms** — *and where the wind drops is not a design
> decision anyone made.*

⭐⭐ **AND THE MAP MATTERS MOST WHEN IT IS LEAST USABLE.** *Winter is the wind's maximum (§E), so the lee is
most valuable in the season fewest people are out in it* — **which makes it a crossing route as much as a
meeting place.**

## B.2 · SMELL — ⭐ **dry, mineral, and mostly absent**

> ⭐⭐⭐ **THIS IS A DESERT.** *`~149 mm` of annual precipitation at `~45%` retention — `82 mm` lost to
> sublimation and wind transport — is **under the standard `<250 mm/yr` threshold.*** **Combined with sub-zero
> means for ten months, volatiles are suppressed almost year-round.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Most of the year** | ⭐ **very little.** *Cold, dry air carries almost nothing. **Rock dust** is the baseline* |
| **The melt season** *(Dec–Jan, above freezing)* | ⭐⭐ **the one olfactory event of the year** — *wet rock, meltwater, the lakes going ice-free* |
| **Indoors** | warm metal, coolant, charge |

⭐⭐ **Third order:** ***a city that smells of almost nothing for ten months has a population for whom smell is
NEWS.*** **The thaw is not merely visible — it is the moment the year acquires a smell**, and that lands on the
same weeks as the audit *(§E)*.

## B.3 · FEEL — ⭐⭐⭐ **grit, and pressure from one side**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Grit** | **Exposed rock + persistent wind = the air carries dust and grit, not only snow.** *It is on every surface, in every seam, continuously* |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Unidirectional pressure** | **You are leaned on from the north-east.** *Not buffeted — **leaned on**, steadily* |
| ⭐ **The windward/lee difference is TACTILE** | *Abrasion works the windward face; deflation the leeward.* **Both degrade — differently — so a hand run over the two sides of the same object reads two different textures** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ And the third order, which is the phase's sharpest
> ***YOU CLEAN A THING WHILE THE WIND RE-DIRTIES IT.***
> **The work is never finished across the year; this is the same fact at the scale of the hour — the act
> itself is provisional while you are performing it.**
> ⭐ **What that produces is not futility — it produces a different relationship to *completion*.** *Nothing
> outdoors here is ever "done." It is only recently attended to.*

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND FOR HALF THE POPULATION, "FEEL" INCLUDES THE SEAL LOOP

**Sourced practice:** **the cold degrades seals → thermal expansion and contraction *"sucks dust particles
through"* them → hard particles wear the seal lip → *the worn lip leaks fluid OUT and admits more dust IN* →
faster.** ⭐ **Wind-borne grit is worst *"in areas with no snow cover"* — which this oasis is, by canon.**

> ⭐⭐ **Under the governing physiology document — Ex Machina physiology, `siligel` and `coolant` — that is not
> industrial. It is bodily.** ***An ordinary, unremarkable awareness that the air is working on your seams,
> the way a human here knows their hands are chapping.*** ⛔ **Not dramatic. Not tragic. Weather, happening to
> a body.**
> ⭐ **And it validates `G2`'s standing-cost wording word-for-word** — *"every surface, seal, bearing, intake
> and joint"* **was written as inference at Step 2 and turns out to be the literal failure inventory.**
> ⚠ **`[DERIVED]` on the transfer to Tepenian robot bodies** — *the physiology document does not specify seal
> design.* **→ Phase 9 / canon.**

>
> | The fact | Visibility |

# E · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ SEASONAL VARIANCE — **the year is a DIAL, not a moderate middle**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Deep winter** *(Jul–Aug, `−16.1 °C`)* | **coldest, darkest.** *Polar night `~49` days, May 29 → Jul 16* |
| ⭐ **The daylight asymmetry** | **midnight sun `~62` days vs polar night `~49`.** *Refraction lengthens the light and shortens the dark at this latitude.* ***The bright season is 13 days longer*** — **any pairing that makes them equal is wrong** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Summer** *(Nov–Jan)* | **melt streams, ice-free lakes, `24`-hour light — and the wind's weakest state** |

> ## ⛔⛔ **LIGHT AND WIND RUN ON OPPOSITE SCHEDULES.**
> **Katabatic forcing is MAXIMAL in the dark** — *"the surface radiative cooling is largest and hence the
> katabatic forcing strongest"; "most pronounced during winter, when there is no incoming solar radiation"* —
> **and SUPPRESSED in summer** — *"summertime winds cannot be expected to contain a significant katabatic
> component, owing to enhanced solar heating of the ice slopes."*
>
> | | Light | Wind |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Midsummer** | ⭐ maximum | ⭐ minimum |
> | **Shoulder** | middling | middling |
> | **Polar night** | ⛔ zero | ⛔ maximum |
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THERE IS NO MODERATE MAJORITY. THERE IS ONE DIAL, AND EVERY DAY SITS SOMEWHERE ON IT.**
> ***More light means less wind. Less light means more wind. There is no calm dark day.***

⚠ **CANON IS COMPLETED, NOT CONTRADICTED.** *`Specs/` describes the wind as "persistent, strong, on most
summer days" and says nothing about winter.* ⭐⭐ **If the katabatic component is SUPPRESSED in summer and
canon still calls the summer wind persistent and strong, then *what this city experiences as its defining
climatic feature is the SUPPRESSED version.***

## E.1 · ⭐⭐⭐ THE WORKING WINDOW IS AN **INTERSECTION**

**Outdoor precision work needs BOTH:** **light** *(to see)* **and low wind** *(to hold a thin glove, and to not
re-dirty the surface as you clean it)*. ***Those peak together only in summer.***

> ## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE SEASON YOU CAN WORK OUTSIDE IS THE SEASON THE WIND IS WEAKEST — AND IT IS STILL STRONG ENOUGH TO ABRADE.**
> ⭐⭐ **And the consequence is the phase's center of gravity:** ***the damage accrues fastest in the season
> nobody can go outside. The wind does its worst work unobserved.***

## E.2 · ⭐⭐⭐ THE YEAR HAS A SHAPE — **four movements, two of them dates**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Late summer** | ⭐ **PREPARE.** *Conservation practice applies a protective layer **annually** — and the only sensible time is before the damaging season* |
| **Winter** | ⛔ **ENDURE, UNOBSERVED.** *Maximum katabatic forcing, minimum daylight, nobody outside* |
| ⭐⭐ **The thaw** *(`+0.2 °C` January mean)* | ## **REVEAL — and BEGIN** |
| **Summer** | ⭐ **REPAIR — in the wind's weakest state, which is still abrasive** |

⚠ **The PLACEMENT of the protective layer before winter is DERIVED, not sourced** — *conservation practice
states an annual cycle, not its position in the year.*

## E.3 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE THAW IS AN AUDIT **AND** A STARTING GUN

> **January mean `+0.2 °C` — a real thaw.** ***Once a year, publicly, and for everyone at once, the year's
> tending and the year's neglect become visible together*** — **and, per §B.2, that is also the week the year
> acquires a smell.**
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And it has a physical engine, not just a social one:** *the dark months are simultaneously maximum
> abrasion and minimum observation.* ***The thaw does not merely melt snow off a surface. It ends a season of
> unwatched damage.***
> ⭐⭐ **It is also the moment the working window opens.** ***A reckoning and a starting gun in the same week.***
> ⭐ **A voluntary obligation with a deadline nobody imposed.**

## E.4 · ⏸️ ONE THING THE SEASONAL CORRECTION DOES **NOT** SETTLE

**Whether the WINTER wind carries GRIT.** ⭐ *For: grit comes from exposed rock; this oasis is ice-free
bedrock; `~149 mm/yr` at `~45%` retention means thin cover; and katabatic flow SCOURS what cover there is.*
⚠ *Against: nothing read states it.* ⛔ **Recorded as an open inference, NOT a finding.**

# F · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ RETROACTIVE MECHANISM — **and it produces a second-order consequence for free**

> **`03` process F:** *"What is established as simply true here that nothing explains, and what physical system

> # **A CITY THAT MUST KEEP ITS DRAINAGE CLEAR OF GRIT HAS CLEAN STREETS AS A BYPRODUCT — NOT AS A VIRTUE.**
> **If the meltwater has nowhere to go, the thaw floods the city that just spent seven weeks freezing.**
> ***So grit clearance is compulsory infrastructure, and "orderly" is what compulsory infrastructure looks
