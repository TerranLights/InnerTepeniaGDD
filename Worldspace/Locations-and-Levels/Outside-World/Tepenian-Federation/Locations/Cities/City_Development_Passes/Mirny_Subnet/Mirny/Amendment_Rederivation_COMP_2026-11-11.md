### COMP-1 · The robot half of each origin row (F-6)

**Proposed finding, as it stands now.**
- Each of Mirny's 36 origin rows holds robots and humans, each at its own percentage (`DR-128` item 1).
- The spec states both for every row. The robot column is the robots' own stated figure, with the human column's standing (`DR-11` L459). Both columns are model output, and both are admitted-demoted alike (`DR-12` L472–L474).
- The two percentages coincide because the table filled the robot column at the human proportions (spec L69). **The coincidence is a value, not a rule.** In-world, the robot column is the headcount (rulings log L473). For derivation it is weighting, its equality with the human column is a construction, and no finding rests on the sameness or on a difference.
- A robot percentage set on the robots' own grounds is not stated at Mirny. That is an open slot, and the rulings log records "the robot percentages" as not decided (L1941: a record of an open matter, not a ruling).
- A row's practices are sized on its human count; any total that includes the robots is a conditional upper figure.

### COMP-2 · Naming the largest row (F-8)

**Proposed finding, as it stands now.**
- **No majority.** Mirny's composition has no majority row: 0 of 36 rows reach 50 %, no row is above 13.73 % (spec L32), and a majority would need 3.64 times the top row's figure. Six rows are Primary (spec L20), together 66.24 % (P08 L287).
- **Flat figures are fine.** Each row's own figure may be stated flatly, for example United Kingdom 13.73 % (spec L32) and Korea 13.71 % (spec L33).
- **No row is named "the largest",** and no group ranked by size inside the Primary tier (a "top two", a "top four") carries a characterization. The order between rows of one tier rests on gaps randomized for realism that carry no meaning.
- **In-world**, a row's count may be quoted as a population. The order of two near-equal rows is a true headcount fact on which nothing rests, and no Mirny line writes it as a characterization.
