## C · Death and the dead, as a question, not a rite

**The axis of this section: who the dead are here, what of them is kept, and by what; every answer stops at RF-14.**

**FIND-P6-210 · The bound.** Burial is impossible in the nation; an ossuary, mixed, is one possibility among several; the ordinary human disposition is deferred nationally; robots die in incidents, many at once or none for a long while, and humans die one at a time (frame L181; Phase 4 FIND-P4-61, from RP L103–108, L142–170). **FIND-P6-211 · The dead stay.** No arrival has a term, a human who came could not go back, and no departure is written (brief §4 item 5; RF-30; Phase 2 FIND-P2-32). → **The city cannot send its dead, or its dying, anywhere a term would have taken them;** every death here is a resident's (Phase 4 FIND-P4-61; research FIND-25, reserved as RF-64). *Who handles it:* not written for the city; nationally, for robots, community and religion, never a trade (FIND-P6-209); for humans, the national disposition is deferred. *Care at the end* is formed elsewhere: every carer in the nation is made at an institute, none here, and arrives by window (Phase 4 FIND-P4-23; the institutes' care runs *from delivery room to deathbed*, Phase 4 L18).

**FIND-P6-212 · The record against the rite.** The files hold **records** of the dead and no **rite**: (1) a name, the district honoring the city's own scientists who gave their lives to the work (FIND-P6-203); (2) the archive, where what a dead holder understood survives only in those she taught (spec L179; research FIND-23); (3) a number, the 1983 measurement, which the cold-record marker may honor as a record the settlers found at the station (`DR-143` item 2; its form Phase 10's). **So what the city keeps of its dead is their work, not their persons:** two of its three records are about knowing (a name for scientists; an understanding passed on), and the third is a measurement. **FIND-P6-213 · A death is also a loss to the archive, and makes no sound.** When a holder of the archive's understanding dies, her part survives only if it was taught; the loss announces nothing (spine §5, *a question no one can answer any more*; Phase 3 FIND-P3-19; Phase 4 FIND-P4-34). If the city had no working network link, what it knows can leave only in people (Phase 5 FIND-P5-48, conditional), so a person is the only form in which its purpose can travel, and a death the only form in which it is lost for good. → **In this city a death is counted twice, once as a person and once as what she knew, and only the first is ever noticed.** PROPOSED; no succession, recording or memory practice (RF-36, RF-49, RF-C204).

## D · The failure state of the core value

**The axis of this section: the city's two promises, and how each fails.**

**FIND-P6-216 · The central promise, and when it becomes impossible to keep.** *What is known here is not lost when its knower is.* It is kept only by being spread: no single holder has the whole (spec L179). **It becomes impossible to keep** when a holder dies before her part is taught (FIND-P6-213); when the archive outgrows the number of people who can hold it, since it is already larger than any one of them (spec L179); and **when the teaching drifts.** In the meme extraction's terms, a transmission can copy the instructions or copy the product (meme L56–70): the founders copied instructions (the records) and the cold corrected them; the archive's understanding passes as product, each learner rebuilding it from a teacher's account, where errors compound and nothing outside corrects them (research FIND-23; meme L58–63). → **The promise fails silently, by drift, long before it fails by death.** *What grew up around that circumstance, on the files:* the archive's own form (understanding spread across many, so no one death is its end), perpetual induction (every arrival taught by the earlier one, Phase 2 FIND-P2-40) and membership by the turn of instruction (Phase 5 FIND-P5-35). The city's structures are what grew up around the impossibility of anyone holding it alone. PROPOSED.

**FIND-P6-217 · The contract's losing side.** The contract converts by teaching. → **Whoever is never asked, never teaches, or never becomes the earlier arrival is never converted** (Phase 5 FIND-P5-35, which Gate 9 meets at Step 7), and the arrival who does not fit the specialization and cannot leave (Phase 2 SH-10) has no place in it. No admitted line treats that as a problem: **SH-C204**, a candidate, reserved, sharpening Phase 2's SH-10 and Phase 5's RF-131.

**FIND-P6-218 · The standard of worth turns on the edgeless robot.** The contract measures a person by being needed. For a robot outdoors, never stopping is safety; in her life, never stopping endangers her mind, and the sky never ends the day here (Phase 4 FIND-P4-27, FIND-P4-28; the medical link flagged nationally, not ruled). → **The robot who is always needed meets the city's standard of worth in full, and that is the danger** (SH-C205, sharpening Phase 4's SH-16). PROPOSED; no rule, illness or practice.

**FIND-P6-219 · Ritualize the failure, or refuse to?** No rite is written either way (`DR-140` item 2). What the files do write is a failure with no signal (spine §5; Phase 3 FIND-P3-19), so it cannot be ritualized without first being noticed. **The stance on file is a refusal by structure:** the city does not mark the failure, because it has arranged never to let understanding be singular, so that no one death has to be the failure. Written as a stance, PROPOSED, not as a custom (RF-C204). Whether that stance is the city's own belief or only its structure waits for the city's own culture files (Step 5, as a check).

**FIND-P6-220 · The second promise, and why its failure is different.** *No one who stops here freezes.* Heat at every place a body stops, on one supply, without a pause (spine §3a; §8e). It fails at a lapse of the supply, and the failure announces itself within hours: water, then charge, then bodies (spine §5). → **The city keeps two promises that fail in opposite registers:** the heat's failure cannot be ignored, and the archive's cannot be noticed (spine RF-49). The heat's failure needs no rite to be seen; the archive's would need one to be seen at all. No ritual is proposed for either.

**FIND-P6-221 · Counterculture seeds, from Phase 4 and Phase 5, named by what they lack, never by a doctrine.** In the mass-movement extraction's terms, people are drawn by a *shape of frustration*, not by doctrine (mass-movement L26–35), and those with no group to belong to are a movement's natural target (L83–86). The files write these shapes, each a seed and none a faction, movement or doctrine: **the keepers**, whose work keeps everyone alive and is named by no one (SH-C201); **whoever keeps her own clock against the institutions'** (Phase 4 FIND-P4-26, FIND-P4-52; spine SH-4); **the edgeless robot** (FIND-P6-218); **the never-asked** (FIND-P6-217); and **those outside the research population**, who hold the contract's survival half and none of its purpose (FIND-P6-206; Phase 4 FIND-P4-60). The city has no belonging by origin at city scale (spine D-9; Phase 2 FIND-P2-12), so the contract is the one bond the files write; whoever it leaves out is left with none. Reserved (RF-C205); no movement is written.
