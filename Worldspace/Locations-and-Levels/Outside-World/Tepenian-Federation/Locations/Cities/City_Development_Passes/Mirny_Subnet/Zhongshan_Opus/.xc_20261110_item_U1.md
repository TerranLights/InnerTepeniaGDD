### Reasoning

**Step 1. Phase 5, L89 (what the city supplies the cluster).** L89 is scoped to the cluster: the city and its two oasis neighbors,
with a shared harbor queue and airport (L90) and one fjord head (L91). Its grounds (`Highways.md`, relationship database L482) are
unchanged (note L14-17). The research partnership is a project recorded as another location's mandated role, with partners. One of the
partners named in `DR-72` L1386 is also one of the places L89 itself lists as reached by the coastal route "not at either neighbor", so
the partnership lies, at least in part, beyond the cluster. It adds nothing to "what this city supplies the cluster" and contradicts
nothing in L89-91. **L89 holds on its own scope.** One gap remains: from the lines on my list I cannot tell whether the project's lead or
its remaining partner is one of the two oasis neighbors (U1-Q4). If one is, L89's row gains an entry; it does not reverse.

*And what does that cause?* Phase 5 has a relation it does not record: a research partnership beyond the cluster. That is an
addition to the city's relations (what flows: Zhongshan's research work into a joint project; results shared among the partners). It
is not a change to L89's statement.

**Step 2. Phase 7's headline (L260; L274 first half; L278 "NATIONALLY SUBSTITUTABLE").** Is the partnership on the outsourceable side?
The non-outsourceable column (L266) holds water and sanitation, waste treatment, enclosure and atmosphere, construction and
maintenance, emergency services, thermal distribution, primary healthcare/obstetrics/childcare, and mortuary and decommissioning.
Agricultural research is none of these. It sits with Food or with higher education and specialist training, both outsourceable. The
rulings also make it a project shared among several cities and led from another location: the shape of an activity "a country
concentrates in a few places and can move" (L274). So "everything this city is nationally for is on the outsourceable side" (L260)
still follows. "NATIONALLY SUBSTITUTABLE" (L278) is, if anything, supported, since Zhongshan holds a partner's stake in a project led
elsewhere. "LOCALLY IRREPLACEABLE" (L276, L278) is untouched: nothing in the rulings bears on the survival column. **The direction
holds.**

**Step 3. The single-role claim (L271-272, L274 second half, L636, L638-639).** The pass states the national role as one thing:
"producing qualified people for somewhere else" (L271-272); "The city's entire national contribution sits in" the higher-education
row (L274); "The city's product is a qualified person" (L636); "the thing the city is for" (L639). A partner's stake in an *ongoing*
joint research project (`DR-72` L1386, "joint-venture ongoing research project") is a national contribution of another kind: research
done in the city on a standing basis, whose product is research results shared with partners, not a person who leaves. **The claim
that the whole national contribution is training no longer follows.** It now covers training plus a research partnership.

*And what does that cause?* **Step 4.** L280-282 makes departure the structural disposition: "the outsourceable half is the half that
leaves", and L638 speaks of "engineers and researchers who leave". A standing project needs researchers who stay to carry Zhongshan's
stake. So part of the outsourceable half stays in the city as a standing activity, and what leaves from it is its results, not its
people. The "fourth scale" of departure holds for the training share. For the research share it holds only for what flows out.
**This narrows L282's scope; it does not reverse it**, because the training share, which `16` §18 names and the pass quotes (L268-269),
is unchanged.

*And what does that cause?* **Step 5.** The city now has a group of qualified people whose national work keeps them here and ties them
to partners elsewhere. L639-640 says "the apprenticeship question and the economy question are the same question". That holds for the
training share. The research share is an economy question that is not an apprenticeship question. So the identity narrows to the
training share. Transmission stays central, because no share of Zhongshan's workforce is ruled for the partnership that could displace
the `16` §18 mandate as the main contribution.

*And what does that cause?* **Step 6.** L278's second clause, "Nothing it does for the Federation has to be done here", stays true for
training. For the research share the rulings give no reason why Zhongshan is a partner and do not say whether its part depends on
anything only this site has. So for that share the clause is unsupported, neither shown true nor shown false (U1-Q3). If the developer
answers that Zhongshan's part needs its site, the clause fails for that share. The verdict would narrow further, but would not flip:
the substitutable/irreplaceable split rests on the `04` §2 columns, agricultural research stays in the outsourceable column either way,
and training, the main role, stays movable.

*And what does that cause?* **Step 7.** Workforce. No share is ruled for Zhongshan's stake, and `16` is unchanged (note L14-17). So U1
does not move the mandated-sector figure. Whether the stake sits inside the 137,200 technical/scientific mandate, in a separate
mandated share, or in the free tier (U1-Q2) changes the wording of L271-272 and L638, not their direction. The figure change itself
(133,907 / 15.4% to 137,200 / 15.1%) is ZSO-012's.

**Step 8. The partnership's own scope.** Per "What changed" item 3, the rulings place Zhongshan only in the agricultural research
project. A re-derivation should say "a partner in the joint agricultural research project" and should not give Zhongshan seed breeding or
phosphorus and soil recovery unless the developer says so (U1-Q1).

The answer stops moving the verdict here. **The direction stands:** the national role is on the outsourceable side; the city is
nationally substitutable and locally irreplaceable; transmission is central; L89 holds on its cluster scope. **Part of the scope no
longer follows:** "entire national contribution", "the city's product is a qualified person", and "the outsourceable half is the half
that leaves" as stated for the whole role. And part of the ground is missing: the research partnership, which neither Phase 5 nor
Phase 7 records. That is NARROWS.
