## My own withdrawals and narrowings

- **P06_Death-C-R3-41, narrowing C-219 and C-222 (see P06_Death-C-R3-30).** For "the human dead are the part that cannot be kept",
  read: the human dead are held (they are "still there", PHYS L98), and what cannot be kept is their legibility
  (PHYS L96, L98, L114).
- **P06_Death-C-R3-42, narrowing C-221 (see P06_Death-C-R3-31).** I withdraw "So there need be no contradiction" and "the contradiction
  may still not exist, on a different ground". What stays is the fact that canon gives the ossuary a standing not
  earned by keeping (PHYS L122–L128). Whether that removes the tension the pass named, or moves it, is not shown by
  any admitted line, and is still the question of PASS-06 L178.
- **P06_Death-C-R3-43, narrowing C-217 and C-222.** For "An ossuary keeps its dead by holding them and by a prohibition
  on taking from them", read: an ossuary holds its dead, robot and human, in one structure, and may not be taken from
  (PHYS L81, L84, L92–L93, L123, L125). The word "keep" is kept for the proposition's own sense.
- **P06_Death-C-R3-44, extending C-222.** The "what canon supplies" row should also carry "Burial is impossible in
  Tepenia." (PHYS L108), as A proposed.

  no admitted line, so the claim must not be read as "costs the city nothing".

  the pass's own figure, "87–91% of the city stands on ice" (PASS-06 L58): 100 − 91 = 9 and 100 − 87 = 13. No rate,

  something else), so my word "ground" for the remainder is an assumption, and the figure should be read as "the
  share of the city not on ice, on whatever base L58 uses". Neither other reader derived it.
