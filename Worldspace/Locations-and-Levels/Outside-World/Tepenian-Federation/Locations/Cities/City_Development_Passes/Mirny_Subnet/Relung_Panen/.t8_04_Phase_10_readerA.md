## The differentiation axis

**Every place here is named by its season of failure: what the melt opens, what the cold keeps, what the hand must clear first.**

*What the words carry.* The catalog does not list institutions; it lists the things this place keeps, and each is enterable at the moment it is hardest to keep. The culverts are a place in the pre-melt weeks, when people stand at them "with heat and picks while the lakes are still white" (Phase 3 P3-36, L198). The gulf ice is a place by what the summer does to its surface (amendment S2-54; `DEC-586`). The seed store and the frozen stores are places the free cold keeps ten months and fails in the months of plenty (Phase 8 P8-12). The warm rooms are places because every month's average night is below freezing for the robot half (Phase 9 L363 `[computed, REQ-52]`). The lit rooms are the one place with no season at all, and the catalog's one place whose failure is not seasonal but a single interruption of the supply `[PROV-8]` (amendment S2-41). It is the theme of the earlier phases ("what we live on here was made, and it stays only as long as it is kept", Phase 6 P6-2) refracted through places, things, people and settings, not restated.

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It rests on this place's own year: a melt that is at once the season of plenty and the season of repair (Phase 3 P3-35); a refreeze that "fixes the summer's water where it stands" (Phase 3 P3-36); ten months of monthly means below 0 °C that keep frozen things without machinery and fail at midsummer (spec L179–190 `[computed, REQ-52]`; Phase 8 P8-12); no polar night, so the dark is made (Phase 3 P3-17); air doors on gulf ice (`DR-96` item 1); made ground on bare rock (spec L222). **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** that things need upkeep; that a city has stores, workshops and doors; that robots charge.
