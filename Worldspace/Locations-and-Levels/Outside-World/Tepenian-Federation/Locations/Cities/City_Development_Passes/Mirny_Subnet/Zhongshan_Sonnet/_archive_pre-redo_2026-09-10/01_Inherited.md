# 1.1 · ⛔⛔ THE ASYMMETRY CHECK — **`5` FIRES**

## ⭐⭐⭐ A-1 · THE COURT THAT CONFIRMED A CLAIM COULD ALSO WITHHOLD ONE — **and it did** `[I-1]`

**Written:** *the International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do coordinated Japan, Korea and the Sinian
Federation as regional peers and **confirmed China's existing presence as exclusively theirs**.*

> ## ⛔ **THE UNWRITTEN HALF: an admission mechanism has a decided-against side, and this one has a NAMED one.**

**`Specs/` L144, plainly:** ***"Russia was never a party to that court at all — whatever Russian presence exists
in Zhongshan today (`5.72%`, its smallest Significant-tier nation) arrived the same way Korea's, Germany's, and
Japan's did: as ordinary immigration into a city whose founding, and whose exclusive claim to its own site, was
already settled before any of them arrived."***

| Asymmetry question | Answer in the file |
|---|---|
| **Who does the mechanism decide against?** | ⭐ **Everyone who was not a party.** *Fifteen of the eighteen nations now living here* |
| **Is that outcome as durable as the favorable one?** | ⛔ **MORE durable.** *The claim was settled **before the exile era began**, so it was never available to be competed for* |
| ⭐⭐ **Is there a route back?** | ## ⛔ **NONE IS WRITTEN, AND NONE APPEARS POSSIBLE.** *The court sat once, before anyone arrived. There is no second sitting, no appeal, and no mechanism by which a later-arriving population could acquire a claim* |

⭐ **THE OPEN ASYMMETRY:** ***`64.17%` of this city's population belongs to nations that had no standing in the
settlement that founded it — and the settlement is the reason the city has never had to defend its own
existence.*** ⛔ **Not resolved here.** ⚠ *Phase 2 and Phase 7 own it. **And `00d` applies: if nobody experiences
this as a grievance, it is a candidate shadow, not a politics.***

## ⭐⭐⭐ A-2 · THE ORBITAL TIER IS A ONE-WAY DOOR, AND NOBODY HAS WRITTEN THE RETURN `[I-3]`

**Written:** *Census I → II is **migration to the orbital tier**, pre-war, not loss.* ⭐ **A documented
destination inside the setting's own future — which is why `Status` is `LIVING` and not `Declining`.**

> ## ⛔ **THE UNWRITTEN HALF: a migration is a GATE. Somebody passed it. The file never says who could, who could not, or whether anyone came back down.**

```
THIS CITY'S OWN RETENTION, Census I → II
  combined ......... 77.90%          humans ... 82.66%          robots ... 73.26%
  spread (H − R) ... +9.40 pp
  mix  Census I : 49.40% H / 50.60% R   →   Census II : 52.41% H / 47.59% R
```

| Asymmetry question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who does the mechanism decide against?** | ⛔ **UNWRITTEN.** *Nothing states what qualified a person for the orbital tier — cost, trade, selection, or simple choice* |
| **Is the outcome durable?** | ⭐⭐ **Apparently permanent. No return migration is recorded anywhere in the census** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Is there a route back?** | ## ⛔ **NOT WRITTEN — and the Tower's later destruction would foreclose it retroactively.** ⚠ *That is POST-WAR and outside this frame; **in-frame, the door is simply open and one-directional*** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE CENSUS SETTLES WHAT THE GATE ACTUALLY WAS** *(added on the full read)*
> **Section C, verbatim:** ***"Population is conserved between Census I and Census II — nobody was born or died
> in the transition; **they relocated**."*** ⭐ **Grand totals are identical: `32,026,600` both.**
> ⛔ **So the gate is not attrition and not loss. It is a door, and everyone who went through it is still alive
> on the other side of it** — `10,104,964` people in orbit at Census II, `~31.6%` of the nation.

> ## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THIS CITY WENT THROUGH IT BACKWARDS
> | | humans | robots | spread `H − R` |
> |---|--:|--:|--:|
> | ⭐ **The parent — national surface retention** | `67.12%` | `69.71%` | **`−2.59 pp`** |
> | ⭐⭐ **THIS CITY** | `82.66%` | `73.26%` | ## **`+9.40 pp`** |
>
> ⛔⛔ ***THE SIGN IS INVERTED.*** **Nationally, humans used the orbital exit more than robots. Here, robots
> used it more than humans** — *and this city crossed from robot-majority to human-majority doing it.*
> ⚠ **This is city-against-PARENT, which `01` §5.3a substitute 2 and `03` Phase 5c both permit.** ⛔ **It is
> NOT a city-to-city comparison and names no other city.**
> ⏸️ **What caused it is Step 2/Phase 5's to answer, not Step 1's.** ⚠ **And canon does not supply it:** *the
> census's own note calls the national orbital human-skew **"an open design question… not attributed to any
> specific structural cause here."*** ⭐ **So the parent's pattern is unexplained too — this city is not
> departing from a settled rule, it is a second unexplained case.**

⭐⭐ **AND THE FIGURE THAT MAKES THIS THIS CITY'S OWN, NOT A NATIONAL FACT:** ***robots used the exit at a
markedly higher rate, and the city crossed from robot-majority to human-majority while doing it.*** ⛔ **Stated
flatly; not ranked against anywhere.** ⏸️ **What it MEANS is Step 2's.** ⚠ **And `Robot_Physiology…` L215 makes
it sharper rather than answering it:** *"a robot built early in the era and one built late are **not carrying
the same culture**, even in the same city"* — **and the exit opened mid-frame, around `2688`.**
