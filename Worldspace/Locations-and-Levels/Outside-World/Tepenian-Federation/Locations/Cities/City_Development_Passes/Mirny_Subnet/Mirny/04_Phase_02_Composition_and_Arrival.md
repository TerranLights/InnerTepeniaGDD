## B. Arrival modes — the seven, human body and robot body separately

**B.0 Two levels, one text.** "Arrival" has two levels: **into the Federation** (the treaty) and **into Mirny** (the station
assignment). **Status.** `FTD` L3 says *"Not locked canon"*; Art. VIII.1 (L117) says the treaty *"shall enter into force upon
signature… without further requirement of ratification."* These answer different questions (the document's authority in this
project; the in-fiction entry into force), so they do not contradict; the pass files' word *"unratified"* conflates them and
is not used here. **Substance is [CONF]** (class-based exile, `FTS` L39; human forfeiture, `FTR` L27); **the wording of every
article is [DRAFT]**. Every treaty-grounded verdict below carries that split.

**B.1 The human admission route, exact wording and limits [DRAFT].**
- II.2.3 (L48): *"Any human person who has, prior to the date of this Treaty, taken up residence among robot communities, married
  or partnered with a robot, borne or raised children in a robot household, or otherwise established their life among the robot
  population, may elect to accompany that population to the Federation of Tepenia, and shall not be prevented from doing so by
  any Assembly Power."* Four grounds; the fourth is a broad residual. **Every ground is a tie to the robot population; none is a
  tie to a place, a skill, a nationality or a merit** (`DS2` L14 [CORR] reaches the same reading). II.2.4 (L49): *"No human person
  shall be compelled to depart under this Article who does not so elect."*
- **Cost:** IV.2.2 (L75) the human who elects is *"deemed… to have willingly and permanently relinquished their legal personhood within Assembly Power territory"*; IV.2.3 (L76) no return and no Assembly Power *"obliged to recognize, admit, or extend any right"*; IV.2.4 (L77)
  *"not a penalty… but a consequence… of the human person's own free choice."* The notes call the framing a rhetorical move
  (`FTD` L138): **the files give the legal frame, not the lived reason.**
- **Limits.** (i) *"prior to the date of this Treaty"*: **no written human channel exists after the treaty date**, so humans
  arriving later are read as born here or unread (R-17). (ii) `RP` L87–88 quotes the census's source as *"people who could afford
  robots, or who loved/supported someone who owned one"*; **the treaty text has no affordability ground.** The two differ; the
  treaty's four grounds are used and the difference logged (R-19; Q-36). (iii) Placement is by II.3.2, *"The population departing
  under Article II.2"*, which includes the II.2.3 humans.

**B.2 The robots, and the body.** II.2.1 (L46): *"Every robot presently residing within the territory of any Assembly Power, and
every robot who may hereafter come into being within such territory, shall depart"*; II.2.2 (L47): *"applies to robots as a class.
No individual designation, accusation, or finding of wrongdoing is required, sought, or relevant."* IV.1 (L68–71): personhood is
withdrawn **in Assembly territory**; inside the Federation *"the legal standing of robots shall be a matter for that Federation's
own law"* (IV.1.2); a robot who departed *"and does not thereafter return"* is unaffected (IV.1.3). **The body:** robots *"do not age
in the biological sense"* (`RP` L67) and die in incidents (`RP` L103–107); the gel brain's data *"is not static"* and cannot be read
out as a snapshot (`RP` L183); origin is learned by immersion, no childhood (`DR-11`). **Build place is unknown (R-13):** chambers
went to *"almost every city"* and Mirny is not named (`RP` L432).

## D. Borrowed Form

**Status: the gap is predicted; the move is prepared and conditional.** The rule (`SPN3` L454–455): run Borrowed Form where a later
category comes up empty **and** the capability reading already predicted the gap. `SPN` §6 predicted two: **#5 no inherited judgment
of the wind** (*"Diffuse"*) and **#7 no living teacher** (*"In its own past"*). **No later category has been written, so none can yet be
empty**; the likeliest receivers are Phase 8 (reading weather, naming winds) and Phase 6 (observances). The forms are therefore
**held for that trigger**, with the divergence stated now.

**The three forms (`RES` §6 [CORR]) — forms in an ancestral repertoire, never traits of a people:**
- **Russia stock — the coast book.** The Pomor *lotsia*: a family book of the coast, amended by each generation, whose key category is
  places *"where there is no escape"* from the weather; wind names that traveled and were reattached.
- **China stock — route books.** Hainan fishermen's route books: memorized first, written later, every copy amended by its keeper,
  entries marked *"this entry is verified,"* new books when the boats changed.
- **Australia stock — a published judgment.** Surf-patrol flags: a trained reader's judgment published as a marker and reset daily.
- **What transfers: the amended copy** — a record the survivors write in. **Divergence at Mirny:** the record **precedes** its
  practitioners and no living layer wrote back into it, so *"the first marks would be the founders' own."* The route book's *content*
  (sea routes) has no object where the sea is ice most of the year; its *form* (a verified-entry marker, a keeper who amends) survives.
  A written record can be amended by a human or a robot equally; a founding-cohort robot can be the keeper across a very long span (a
  derivation, not a claim about any person). **Act:** fresh repertoires in Act 1; in Act 2 a Tepenian practice descended from them
  (`NNS` L154), never the stock itself. Neither depends on the open founding Act (R-8).

**Said plainly: only three of the fourteen stocks were researched, and they are exactly the three founding stocks.** The other eleven
(Japan, UK, South Korea, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, South Africa, New Zealand, Belarus) **were not researched**
(`RES` §6) **and are not invented here.** The Borrowed Form speaks for the founding stratum and is silent on the eleven (R-22). **Three
cautions:** (1) that each form was carried by the founders in 2564 is **not established** by any admitted file: the forms are real-world
inspirations (`DR-15`), possibilities, not carried facts; (2) the Russia-stock form is justified by `DR-9` alone: the station's builder is
a coordinate and nothing about Mirny's coordinates explains a Russia-stock form (`NNS` L5, L154; `DR-19`, `DR-28`); (3) the practice is
never "what Russians/Chinese/Australians are like."
