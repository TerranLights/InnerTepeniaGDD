## 3 Reasoning (each step cites a line read)

**SRY-U03-12. The pass tested the relation's form; the current rule tests its source.** The pass's legality check for the kinship tie is the one-sentence test (Phase 7 L364–L365), which asks whether the claim survives with every other name deleted. The current rule (LOG L1868 item (5); note L16, L279) asks a different question: whether the relation is taken from Shirayuki's own lines. That rule was not in force when the pass ran (note L268). So the pass's finding "Survives deleting every other name — RELATION." (L365) does not by itself show the tie is legal under the current rule.

**SRY-U03-13. The kinship tie's stated ground comes from another city's lines.** The pass grounds the tie on the plateau city's tier: Phase 7 L361 (the parenthesis that the plateau city "is Japanese-Primary") and Phase 5 L82 ("the tier structures share an origin profile"). The current lines that carry that tier are the plateau city's census row (medical-institutes L107) and the tier list inside the airport row (Airports L18). The note classifies medical-institutes L104–L107 as "that city's lines, not Shirayuki's." (note L241–L242). Under LOG L1868 item (5), this ground no longer supports the tie. The figure itself is unchanged (SRY-U03-11), so this is not a figure problem; it is a source problem.

**SRY-U03-14. Whether the tie itself survives is not decided by my lines.** The tie is stated in clauses that name Shirayuki: Airports L18 ("Shirayuki's to" the plateau city) and medical-institutes L109 ("Shirayuki (Japan) ties to" it). Neither clause sits in Shirayuki's own entry: L18 is the outpost airport's row, and L97–L109 is a passage about the neighbor's institute (L98–L99). L18 also derives the tie by setting the cluster's compositions against the plateau cities' tiers, which are not Shirayuki's lines (note L241–L242). Shirayuki's own entry, within L388–L394, lists no plateau tie (SRY-U03-9). No admitted line says whether a clause naming Shirayuki inside another subject's row counts as "its own lines": the note names Airports L18 as the tie's source line (L235–L237) but does not classify it. I do not decide this (brief rule 2).

**SRY-U03-15. "Partial partner" rests on a clause about the neighbor.** Phase 5 L83–L85 conclude that Shirayuki "is the" "partial partner." The current support is the airport row's clause about the neighbor that pushed, "It had both reasons to push, and its neighbors were not equally invested." (Airports L18), and the medical-institutes passage on that neighbor's institute (L98–L102). None of these is a Shirayuki-own line, and no admitted Shirayuki-own line states Shirayuki's part in the outpost. Under LOG L1868 item (5) the sentence no longer follows as written.

**SRY-U03-16. The demographic tie is contradicted as stated.** Phase 5 L86 says the "Medium, Demographic" tie "is recorded in canon and UNEXPLAINED in its own source." and L87 disposes of it: "Not used. → Phase 9." Shirayuki's own entry records the same tie as "Medium, Demographic." (national-connections L391) and gives a ground on L391–L392 (SRY-U03-9); the note records the mismatch (L239–L240). Whether to use the tie, and how, stays with the re-derivation.

**SRY-U03-17. The set-aside cultural tie is untouched by my lines.** Phase 5 L89–L92 set aside a tie built on a station-descended founding and take nothing from it. No admitted current input bears on that disposition beyond the general rule; the line that builds the tie is not within national-connections L388–L394, and the queue item it names (`DRQ-14`) is not in my list.
