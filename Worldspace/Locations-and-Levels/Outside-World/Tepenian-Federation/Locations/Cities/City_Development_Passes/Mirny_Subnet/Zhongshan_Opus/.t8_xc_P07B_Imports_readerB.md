**Finding 105 · `Ports` L130's political clause describes the neutral strand, not the city's whole supply.** L130's general clauses give

- *And what does that cause?* On the sea reading, the city's maritime imports for a year, including the raw materials the economy cannot

  outside that season. Which of the two the city lives with is the difference between a supply that stops for most of the year and one
  that does not (Question 118).
