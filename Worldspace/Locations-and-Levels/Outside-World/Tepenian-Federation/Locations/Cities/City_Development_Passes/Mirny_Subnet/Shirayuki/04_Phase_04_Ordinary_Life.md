# 1 · THE HEADLINE FUNCTION, IN ONE SENTENCE — **then written away from**

> # **Shirayuki is the city that keeps its outdoor things.**

**Apply the test.** *Would tending constructed work reasonably be somebody's entire day, every day, forever?*
⛔ **No.** ⚠ **And `00b` forbids the shortcut anyway:** *the making is a visible minority; the upkeep is
general.* **So the ordinary day is elsewhere, and this phase has to go find it.**

# 2 · ⭐⭐⭐ WHERE THE ORDINARY DAY ACTUALLY IS — **keeping the ground usable**

**`Division_of_Industry/16` Half B:** **baseline `40.9%` · mandated `11.8%` · free `47.3%`** *(`D = 1.25`;
workforce `890,078` pre-cut → `~801,070` post-cut — a COUNT, so it moved; the percentages are ratios and did
not)*. ⚠ **`[COLUMN 3 — self-originated]`.**

> ## **`41%` OF WORKING LIFE IS THE COMPULSORY SURVIVAL LOAD — AND IN THIS CITY A LARGE PART OF IT IS THE GROUND ITSELF.**
> **`87–91%` of the built footprint stands on ice.** **Ice is not permanent ground. It has to be kept usable.**

## 2a · ⚠ BUT IT IS **CHEAP** ICE, AND THAT CHANGES THE WORK ENTIRELY

**`Extent_and_Density_Per_City.md` §10 distinguishes two kinds outright** — ***"cheap ice"*** *(buries slowly
and evenly, no horizontal motion)* **versus *"expensive ice"*** *(migrating shelves that a city must walk
upstream against, forever)*. ⭐ **This city's ice is the cheap kind, and its ruled difficulty is `D = 1.25`.**

> ### ⭐ SO THE ORDINARY WORK IS NOT DRAMATIC REBUILDING. IT IS INCREMENTAL.
> **Raising, re-levelling, digging out, extending, re-founding a little at a time.** ***Slow, constant,
> unremarkable, and never finished.***

⚠⚠ **AND TWO CANON INSTRUMENTS DISAGREE ABOUT THIS GROUND.** *`16` Half B classifies the city **"rock-founded,"
`D=1.25`, food term 100%**; `Step 2.6` finds `87–91%` of the footprint ON ICE.* ⭐ **Both can be true — the
SITE is rock, the CITY outgrew it — but if `D` was set from the site classification, `40.9%` is a FLOOR.**
⏸️ **→ `DRQ-13`, reached independently from two directions.** *`Extent` also states plainly that accumulation
and flow at several sites are "NOT YET MEASURED — do not assume," and Larsemann is not among the established
figures.*

## 2b · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FINDING — **the two largest workloads have the same shape, and the sameness is IMPOSED**

| | **COMPULSORY** — the `41%` | **ELECTIVE** — the famous thing |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | grit clearance for drainage · keeping the ice usable | maintained outdoor constructed work |
| **Rhythm** | continuous · incremental · never finished · undramatic | continuous · incremental · never finished · undramatic |
| **Season** | ⭐ the summer intersection | ⭐ the same summer intersection |
| **Hands** | ⭐ the same | ⭐ the same |

> # ***THE CITY DID NOT CHOOSE A TEMPERAMENT. IT HAS TWO ENORMOUS WORKLOADS WITH IDENTICAL RHYTHM, AND THE TEMPERAMENT IS WHAT YOU GET WHEN BOTH OF THEM DO.***
>
> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE SHARED RHYTHM IS NOT A COINCIDENCE — IT IS PHYSICALLY IMPOSED
> **Outdoor work needs light AND low wind, and those peak together only in summer** *(`04_Phase_03` §E.1)*.
> **Grit clearance matters most against the meltwater it must carry; ice re-levelling wants light and workable
> temperatures.** ⭐⭐ ***So the city does not have two workloads that happen to feel alike. It has two that
> must be done at the same time, because only one part of the year permits either.***
> ⭐ **`02` §3.2's chain completes:** *the elective obligation rides the compulsory one because they are the
> same motion, in the same weather, at the same time of year.*

⭐⭐ **Independent corroboration from a second document:** *canon places this city at the **"variety and
refinement over raw intensity"** pole of the national drinking culture* **— which is the same disposition as
*technique over effort*.** ***A labor analysis and a robot-culture document, two derivation chains, one
temperament.***

# 3 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE SKILL THAT SPANS BOTH — **and the majority of this city is a body that needs it**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The Dual Economy** *(the general section of the logistics file)* | *"**Robot maintenance and repair services are specialist industries**"* |
| **`04_Phase_03` §B.3** | **the air carries grit, continuously, into every seam — and cold-degraded seals admit it** |

> ## **THE PERSON WHO CLEANS A SURFACE WITHOUT SCRATCHING IT AND THE PERSON WHO SERVICES A JOINT WITHOUT SCORING IT ARE DOING THE SAME KIND OF WORK.**
> **The upkeep is *technique*, not effort — "polish without scratching," where doing it wrong does the damage
> faster than neglect would.** ***That is one skill register, and it applies to objects and to bodies alike.***
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And `51–54%` of this city IS a body** — **human-formed, clothed, gloved, moving through abrasive air
> every workable day.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND CANON NAMES THIS REGISTER, IN A SEPARATE DOCUMENT, AS THE MOST INTIMATE ONE IT HAS

> **`Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md`, on the same acts in their civilian identity:**
> **`Thermal`** — *"coolant-tending tools. This is arguably **the single most intimate category in the entire
> taxonomy**: a human tending a robot companion's coolant system, or a robot performing careful
> self-maintenance… **lean into this as TENDERNESS, not utility.**"*
> **`Drain`** — *"coolant-siphon and diagnostic **tending**… **AN ACT OF TRUST** — a caretaker relationship
> between two people, human or robot, one of whom is looking after the other's literal ability to function."*

⭐⭐ **So a maintenance sector that reads as a specialist industry elsewhere is, here, continuous with the
ordinary civic routine** — **and the act at its center is one canon calls tenderness.**

# 4 · THE SHAPE OF THE YEAR — **a dial, not a moderate middle**

| | Days | |
|---|--:|---|
| **Polar night** | **49** | *May 29 → Jul 16.* **The headline function is physically impossible** |
| **Midnight sun** | **62** | *Nov 22 → Jan 22.* **Mild, melting — and the wind's weakest state** |
| **Lit days between** | **~254** | ⭐ **but they are not a temperate shoulder — see below** |

> ## ⛔ **LIGHT AND WIND RUN ON OPPOSITE SCHEDULES** *(`04_Phase_03` §E)*.
> ***More light means less wind. Less light means more wind. There is no calm dark day.***
> ⭐⭐ **So the outdoor working window is the INTERSECTION — narrower than the lit season and narrower than the
> calm season.** ⛔ **"Moderate, lit, variable and unremarkable" describes a temperate shoulder season. This
> city does not have one.**

## 4a · ⭐⭐ THE SHARED NIGHT — **canon, and it lands hard on a polar city**

> **`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`:** *"Robots do NOT work 100% of the time. Not remotely…
> **they require downtime, most prominently OVERNIGHT RECHARGING.***" ⭐ ***"Which gives Tepenia a shared
> night. Robots and humans stop at the same time, for different reasons… it means the polar night falls on a
> population that is ALL resting, together."***

⭐ **Consequence here:** **for `49` days the sky supplies no cue, so the shared night runs on the CLOCK
alone** — *an agreed civic fact rather than an observed one.* ⚠ **And for `62` days the same is true in
reverse.** ***Only about `70%` of this city's year has a day and a night the sky agrees with.***

## 4b · ⛔⛔ **AND THE FAMOUS THING IS A SEASONAL ACTIVITY**

> ## ***THE CITY IS NOT DOING ITS DEFINING WORK FOR MOST OF THE YEAR.***
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And `04_Phase_03` §C says where everyone is instead:** *"Outside is a place the city has shaped and
> continuously re-shapes. **Inside is a place it INHERITED and adapted**" — the pre-exile station stock,
> "somebody else's proportions."*
> ## **FOR MOST OF THE YEAR THIS CITY LIVES INSIDE A BUILDING ENVELOPE IT DID NOT DESIGN; FOR A MINORITY OF IT, IT GOES OUTSIDE AND WORKS ON THE THINGS IT DID.**
> ⚠ **The inherited half gets most of the hours — which runs the wrong way from the city's reputation.**
> ⛔ **Canon's own Open Question — *"how did they adapt or modify it?"* — is therefore a question about where
> ordinary life mostly happens. Unanswered. Not invented here.**

# 5 · ⭐⭐⭐ LEISURE — **and what this city spends it on is the strange part**

> **Canon, and it is emphatic:** *on Upper Earth a robot's leisure "was afforded to them by their loving
> romantic partners… contingent, personal, and revocable."* **In Tepenia it is a FACT OF LIFE. Universal. Owed
> to nobody.*** ***"Leisure in Tepenia is not rest. It is the material proof that leaving worked."***

**This city's free tier is `47.3%` — `420,886` people. Canon's own name for it is *the character budget*.**

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ THE FINDING IS NOT THE SIZE OF THE SLACK. IT IS WHAT GETS DONE WITH IT.
> **Leisure here is hours owed to nobody, in a country founded on finally having them.** ***And a substantial
> share of those hours goes into unpaid upkeep of objects.*** **Not rest. Not consumption. More work,
> voluntarily, on things nobody requires anyone to maintain.**
> ⭐ **`02` §3.2 answered a third time:** *nobody experiences it as a cost because nobody experiences it as
> **labor** — it is what the free hours are for.*

## 5a · ⭐⭐ AND THE OTHER HALF OF THE EVENING — **canon, admitted**

> **`Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`:** *this city supports **a wide variety of glitch-coolant
> types**, valuing **variety and refinement over raw intensity**.*

⭐⭐⭐ ***"Variety and refinement over raw intensity" is the same disposition as "technique over effort."***
**A city graded on precision drinks the way it works: by discrimination, not by quantity.**
> ***Rest and upkeep are not two moods here. They are one register at two temperatures.***

⛔ **NO DISTINCTIVENESS CLAIMED.** *"This is what the place is like" is the finding; whether anywhere else is
also like it is a terminal question and not this pass's business* **(`M-159`)**.

⚠ **`00d` PROPORTION CHECK — run, and it does NOT return a shadow.** *This is not compulsion wearing a
friendly face.* **The hours are genuinely free, the participation is genuinely voluntary, and the ordinary
majority are ordinary people who look after their things and also rest, drink, smoke and see each other.**
⛔ **The tell `00d` names is a reader closing the file and calling the place sinister. It should not read that
way, because it is not.**

# 6 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THREE THINGS AN ORDINARY DAY CONTAINS THAT NOTHING ELSE IN THE PASS NAMED

## 6a · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PRIVACY HERE COSTS COMFORT**

**`04_Phase_03` `G.1`: the lee is simultaneously where the wind drops, where people gather, and the only
direction windows stay clear — *so the commons is structurally overlooked, and the windward side is private,
blind and abrasive.*** ⭐ **Set that against §4a's canon shared night — *a population that is ALL resting,
together*.**

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **TO BE UNOBSERVED HERE, YOU GO WHERE THE WIND IS.**
> **Not a rule, not a policy, not anybody's doing** — *simply that the sheltered ground and the seen ground are
> the same ground, and the synchronized rest cycle means the hours are shared too.*
> ⭐⭐ ***Wanting to be alone in Shirayuki means being cold.***

⚠ **`00d` PROPORTION, applied hard.** ⛔ **This is NOT surveillance and must never be written as such.** ✅ **It
is FAMILIARITY — being seen by neighbors, which is mostly pleasant and entirely ordinary.** ⭐ **The finding is
only that solitude has a price here and the price is physical.**

## 6b · ⭐⭐⭐ **TWO SMALL RECURRING PURCHASES, AND EVERYONE MAKES BOTH**

| Consumable | Why |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **Gloves** | *thin enough for precision work, and therefore the thing grit destroys fastest* |
| ⭐⭐ **Glazing** | *wind-borne grit frosts glass permanently — it does not break, it clouds* |

⭐⭐ **Two ordinary, unglamorous, recurring expenses, both caused by the same agent, both affecting everybody.**
***That is what a standing cost feels like from inside a household*** — **not a burden, a line item.**
⛔ **NOT extended into a trade claim here** — *that is Phase 8's to earn or kill.*
