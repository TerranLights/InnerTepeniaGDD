### Sound

- **P3-4 · On most days the loudest steady sound outdoors is the city's own machinery, unmasked by wind.** *Grounds:* a calm mean (4.6 m/s, spec L143; calms 39% `[DEM]` `[PA-3]`; two-thirds of readings at 5 m/s or less, `18` L15 `[DoI corroboration]`); little lying snow and about 92 mm ESTIMATED a year (L109; L157 `[DEM]`), so little weather sound; no open sea (L13), so no surf. *Against that,* the city runs a load that does not follow the sky (S3-1): the heat of about 0.5 GW of lit export food must be removed from the growing rooms (`DR-96` item 4; S3-43); heat against 3,987 freezing degree-days (`Climate.md` L24); winter water pumped from deep lakes through heated intakes into heated storage (S3-31, research L330); warm charging space in every month `[PROV-8]` (S3-27).
