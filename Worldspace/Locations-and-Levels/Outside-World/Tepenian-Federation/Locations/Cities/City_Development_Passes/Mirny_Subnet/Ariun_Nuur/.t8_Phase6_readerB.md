**FIND-P6-104 · The unnamed load-bearing thing: a debt of instruction, owed backward and paid forward.**

**The proposition a resident would agree with:** *Someone who came before me told me where it was safe to stop, and kept me alive through my first dark season; I cannot pay that back to her, so I pay it to whoever comes after me.*

**Why it must be true for the files to make sense:** every arrival must be taught a place that can kill, by people taught it the same way (spine G8 standing cost 3); the teacher here is always the earlier arrival (Phase 2 FIND-P2-40); the warning a newcomer needs comes from someone who already knows (Phase 3 FIND-P3-40); and a newcomer becomes a member when the direction of instruction turns around, when she first tells someone newer where to stop (Phase 5 FIND-P5-35). The debt cannot be paid back to its creditor, because the earlier arrival is already taught and nothing else is written passing back; it cannot be escaped by leaving, because a human who came cannot return and no departure is written (brief §4 item 5; RF-30); so **the only written way to discharge it is forward.**

**What it is:** a **debt**, in the spine's sense (what the place owes and does not discuss, phase spine L794–795): no admitted line has anyone name it, and the files write it only as structure. It is **not a creed**: no doctrine, oath, rite, title or teaching practice is written, and none may be (RF-45, RF-91, RF-109, RF-131).

**What makes it this place's and not portable (the counter-check, phase spine L796–797):**
1. **The currency is where to stop.** Here stopping is the act the cold punishes: for the robot majority, stopping outdoors is the danger, and for the human minority the whole stay is the cost; rest can happen only in heated, powered space (Phase 3 FIND-P3-25, FIND-P3-28; Phase 4 FIND-P4-12, FIND-P4-37). What is owed is not a trade or a skill but the location of safety.
2. **The cold collects at once.** A heat lapse is felt within hours (spine §5); the place gives warning only through temperature (Phase 3 FIND-P3-19). So the debt is called in on the first day, not over a career.
3. **The first holders owed it to paper.** The founding community re-learned survival from the station's journals, logs and orientation manuals, with no living institution (spec L145); every layer after was taught by someone (Phase 4 FIND-P4-45). **Only the founders arrived owing no person; everyone after arrived owing someone.**
