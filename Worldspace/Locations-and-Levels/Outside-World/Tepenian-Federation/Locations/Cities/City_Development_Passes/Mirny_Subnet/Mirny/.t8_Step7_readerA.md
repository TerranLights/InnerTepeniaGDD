## The closing test

> *Could a person live an entire life here — and would that life be unlike a life in any comparable place?*

**Yes to the second half; only partly to the first.**

*Unlike a life elsewhere — yes, and for reasons the gates confirm rather than flatter.* A child born at Mirny grows up in a wind that is almost never calm, under a sky that clears before the worst storms, in a city that dates its year by light because its thermometer will not move for five months. Nobody teaches her to read that sky; nobody taught anyone. When she misjudges a crossing, the question asked is what she did, never who sent her — and the record her city trusts, a book none of its people wrote, will never learn from her mistake, because no one writes in it. If she is a robot built here, she is native on her first day and a beginner with no childhood to be one in; if she lives long, she becomes the only person who remembers the founding, holding a memory the record cannot vouch for and that no rank will ever make anyone obliged to hear. That conjunction — a medium nobody here authored, held to never failing, and a private reading that dies with each reader — does not travel (Gate 4; `P6` P6-2). That is a place.

*An entire life — not yet.* A life is mostly made of the things Gate 1 measured as explained nulls: what she eats beyond the national pantry, what she sings, what she laughs at, what she calls the winds and the places, what she does with the hours that are hers, who she courts and how, what her childhood day is. The pass leaves about one honest, reasoned empty slot per finding. Each emptiness is lawful — no forced fit, no stereotype, no invented custom — but a person cannot live in a reason. The honest answer to the first half is therefore not *"probably, I suppose"*; it is *"we know the shape of the life and almost none of its days."* The empty middle `P4` named in its own words is still the largest empty middle of the pass at Step 7.

**What the gates could not see.** (1) **Thinness:** every null is explained, so no gate fails it. (2) **Monocausality:** the compact appears in nearly every phase's axis (`06` NS-11) — whether that is a place's deep coherence or one author's frame applied nine times cannot be decided by any in-run gate. (3) **A reading of absence:** the strongest finding's fourth leg is what the files do not say, asserted of 1.35 million people for 248 years (G11-F1); the gates can only call it unfalsified. (4) **The robot half's own texture:** both bodies are named in every claim, but the robot half has almost no practice of its own beyond national canon — robot leisure, drink scene, courtship and music are all REQUESTED. (5) **What the Neighbor would say** — possibly uncastable in-run (Gate 10).
