### How dispositions are counted

- Finders are counted from the Round 1 outputs of A, B and C and from K's output.
- `confirmed by all three` means that A, B and C each found the point in Round 1. K is named beside it where K also found it.
- `found by two` means that two finders found it. Where two readers and K found it, the row names all three finders; the closed set has no other word for that case.
- `found by one` means that one finder found it, including K alone.
- A Round 3 adoption is recorded in the Round 3 column but does not raise a disposition. A Round 3 withdrawal is recorded. Where a withdrawal leaves no contradiction, no conflict is recorded.
- `conflict resolved to the narrower reading` and `conflict sent to the developer` are used only where a contradiction remained after Round 3, including a contradiction with K.
- A row with more than one disposition gives each one with the part it covers.
