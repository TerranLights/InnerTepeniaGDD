### What converts a visitor into a member?

**P5-17 · The crossing changes hands** *[CORR + IND + D — offered for testing, NOT a custom]*
- *Mechanism.* A visitor's crossings are decided **for** them — by a host, by a driver, by the road's local call (`HWY` L30) — because a visitor has not learned the cue (`P3` §B.4: *"a visitor has not learned to read the sky"*). A member decides their own: a resident consults one of three channels — their own reading, the written record, or a relayed report no admitted file contains (`P4` P4-2 [CORR]) — and **owns the outcome**: *"A misjudged crossing is carried as the person's own"* (`P4` P4-7 [CORR]); *"each injury is read as the injured person's carelessness"* (`SPN` §4 [CORR]).
- *So [D]:* **a visitor becomes a member at the point where a misjudgment would be called theirs.** No announcement, no ceremony; nobody notices it happening, because nobody remarks on a crossing that went well. The evidence is counterfactual: the blame that would now land.
- *Robot reading:* the same, plus charge. A robot's crossing is paid in charge and the stop restores it (`P4` P4-5 [CORR]); a visiting robot's recharge happens in **someone else's** above-freezing place; a member's happens in a place that is hers [D, structural only — no dwelling is described, `DR-10`]. *Human reading:* a garment and a glove (`P4` P4-5).
