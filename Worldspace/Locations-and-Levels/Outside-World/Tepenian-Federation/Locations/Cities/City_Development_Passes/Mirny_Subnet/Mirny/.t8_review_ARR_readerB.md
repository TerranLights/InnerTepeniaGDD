## 3 · The re-derivation

### R-1 · The human route after the treaty date (F-1)

**Proposed finding:** *Two human routes into the Federation are written, not one: II.2.3 for those tied to robots before the treaty date, II.2.5 for any other human at any time after it. Both cost the same and neither has a way back. The door is the Federation's; Mirny neither opens nor closes it. Whether anyone came to Mirny through it, and how she was placed, is unread.*
- *1st [ADM].* II.2.3's grounds are all ties formed "prior to the date of this Treaty" (`FTD` L48). II.2.5 opens a second route "at any time after the effective date of this Treaty", with no ground beyond not having made the first election, and treats its elector "for all purposes of this Treaty" as a paragraph-3 elector (`FTD` L50). Both elections forfeit legal personhood in Assembly territory (L76), bar return (L77), and are framed as the person's "own free choice to depart" (L78). Ruled as canon: `DRL` L1913.
- *2nd [D].* The two bodies cross the Federation's edge differently in kind, not by "route or no route": a robot by compulsion as a class (`FTD` L46–47), a human only by her own act (L49, L50). So a human from Upper Earth at Mirny's edge (crews, observers, traders, `P5` L257) is not a person with no way in. She can come in only by an act that costs her home and personhood in Assembly territory and cannot be undone.
- *3rd [D].* The decision and its cost both fall outside Mirny: the law that takes her standing is the Assembly Powers' (L76), and the door is in the Federation's edge, "the only edge written in law" (`P5` L239). No Mirny residency rule is admitted (`P5` L286, M6; L499). What Mirny contributes is time. The ice and the wind decide when a sea visitor arrives (`P5` L261) and whether she is held through a winter (L282). If an election can be made inside Tepenia, a winter held at Mirny is a setting where an Upper Earth person lives Mirny's conditions before choosing them, and the ice holds mostly people whose only way in is a choice the city cannot make for them. If it can be made only before sailing, nobody chooses Tepenia from inside Mirny. **The text names no place (`FTD` L50): open slot (ARR-Q102).**
- **Survives:** II.2.3's reading and its four tie grounds (`P2` L156–160, of II.2.3); the cost (`P2` L162, widened); every Upper Earth visitor is human (`P5` L257); the P5-17 axes and their empty cell (`P5` L233–239); E2 and E4 (`P2` L338, L340).
- **Changes:** `P2` L155, L159–160 (scope), L165–166, L494; `P5` L255 (content), L258, L282, L499 (second half).
