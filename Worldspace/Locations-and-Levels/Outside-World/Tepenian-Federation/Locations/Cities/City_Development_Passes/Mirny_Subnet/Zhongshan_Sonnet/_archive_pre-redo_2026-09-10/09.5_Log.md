# `M-207` — A PHASE-8 CLAIM WAS WRONG, AND ONLY PHASE 9'S REQUIRED CANON CAUGHT IT

**The error.** Phase 8 wrote that a robot *"comes online speaking the dominant language, because that is what
the Language Module tracks,"* and concluded a heritage community could transmit its language **by initiating
its own builds.**

**What canon says.** `Robot_Universals` Ch. 20 — a Phase **9** required source, not a Phase 8 one:
> *"A robot's Language Module sets her **capacity** for language at fabrication, but **it does not set the
> content** of what she goes on to speak. That content is **filled in entirely by whichever community and
> culture actually surround her** — the same condition that would shape a human child's own native language."*

⛔ **Acquisition, not installation.** *And the correction inverts the practical conclusion:* **initiating a
build does NOT protect a minority vernacular**, because the robot still grows up surrounded by the majority.

**Why it matters that the finding got BETTER.** *The corrected mechanism is the ordinary real-world one —
minority languages are lost because the children are surrounded by the majority* — ⭐ **and it puts this
city's civic law directly in the causal path**, since the counterweight in a real minority community is family
and community talk about where you come from, **which is precisely the conversation the non-prying norm
forbids.** *The wrong version was a fabrication quirk. The right version is a mechanism that actually operates.*

**The structural lesson, which is the reason this is logged.** ⚠ **Phase 8's `MUST OPEN` block does not include
`Robot_Universals/`. Phase 9's does.** *So Phase 8 could not have caught this by following its own required
canon — the file that governs robot vernacular language is required by a later phase than the one that writes
about language.*

⏸️ **Docketed for `Step 5`:** **`Robot_Universals` Ch. 6 (Language) and Ch. 20 belong in Phase 8's `MUST OPEN`
block, not only Phase 9's.** ⭐ *`M-205` said a source opened for an earlier phase is not open for this one.
This is the mirror defect: **a source required only by a later phase was needed by an earlier one**, and
nothing in the block ordering would have surfaced it.*
