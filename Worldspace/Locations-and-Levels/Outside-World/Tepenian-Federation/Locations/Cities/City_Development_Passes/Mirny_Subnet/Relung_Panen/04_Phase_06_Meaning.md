## The differentiation axis

**Keeping against the summer.**

*What the words carry.* The place's unnamed load-bearing thing is a standard: **a made thing is worth its keeping, and here the by-hand keeping falls due against the season that gives the most.** What is given here is rock, ice walls, still cold air, light every day, lakes and the summer melt; what is made is soil, warmth, lit green, winter water, a summer dark, warm charging rooms and cleared drains (Phase 3 P3-2). The founders made the first of these where there was nothing: "There was no pre-existing farmland or soil; the founders had to establish their own." (spec L222; rulings L1252). **The things kept by hand are kept against the summer:** the dark against a sky that gives 19.4, about 24 and 21.0 h of light in November, December and January (spec L151–156, L179, L189–190; Phase 4 P4-4), made ground and the drains against a melt on bare ground (spec L171–172, L203; amendment G7-C1, G7-C2; S3-7), the morning path against the water the warm afternoon left (Phase 4 P4-8). **The things kept through the supply** (heat, winter water, lit green and, `[PROV-8]`, the robot half's charge) are kept against the cold, all year, by a line the city does not hold (amendment S2-41; Phase 5 P5-11, P5-30), and that half is where the standard breaks (D).

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: soil that had to be made from nothing at the founding (rulings L1252; spec L222); a summer that gives near-continuous light and a genuine melt on bare ground in the same three months (spec L151–156, L171–172, L179–190, L203); and, for nine months, nothing soft that was not made (Phase 3 P3-2). Give the ground its own soil and nothing made needs keeping from the melt; give the place a summer night and the dark need not be made; remove the melt and the summer takes nothing back. **Carried beside it (A's pins):** made ground reverts to the bare rock it was made on (spec L104–109; amendment L189), the night is made by hand (Phase 4 axis), and warmth is bought against the measured 3,987 winter freezing degree-days (spec L195), since the site gives no energy (spec L261).
