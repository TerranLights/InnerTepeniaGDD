## 5.1 Why this is a finding and not a bookkeeping note

**The binding anti-stereotype law supplies the correct form of words for a Tepenian city's origin:** *"A
Japan-FOUNDED TEPENIAN city. Stock: Japanese. People: Tepenian. Local culture: its own."* ⭐ **That form has one

## 5.2 And the founding instrument SEVERS the site from the origin in terms

**Falkland Treaty, Article II.3.3:**

> *"Nothing in this Article requires that a population assigned to a given station share the national origin of
> the Power that formerly maintained it; the purpose of assignment is the orderly and humane distribution of the
> exiled population across habitable ground, not the perpetuation of any prior national arrangement."*

> ### ⭐⭐ THE ANTI-STEREOTYPE LAW HAS AN IN-WORLD INSTRUMENT BEHIND IT, AND THIS IS IT.
> **The treaty that assigned Davis its people states, in its own text, that assignment does not track the site's

> from nowhere else*** — and the census is a production weight (limit 1). **Both of the phase's two natural

## 5.3 And what that causes — three orders

1. **First order.** ⭐ **No stock at Davis is a majority, and the largest is under a fifth.** `G8`'s own profile
   already draws the consequence: *"nothing load-bearing can be tacit; everything must be explicit."* **Applied

2. **Second order.** The ordinary mechanism by which a plural city organizes itself — **founding stock as host,

3. ⭐⭐ **Third order — and this is where the place stops resembling anywhere else.** **`D5` records that
