## The differentiation axis

**Seasonless mandate, seasonal upkeep.**

*What the words carry.* The work this place is ordered around comes in two regimes that answer to two different clocks. **The mandate** (21.4% of the modeled workforce, 161,514 workers, `18` L35 `[DoI corroboration]`) is set by national rulings outside the world (`DR-71` L1374; `DR-72` L1386–1387; `DR-73` L1398; `DR-75` L1421; `DR-96` item 5) and its largest part, the export food, is lit and enclosed with no season (spec L258; `DR-96` item 4, rulings L1633): there is no harvest day (Phase 4 P4-3), the food leaves as a daily flow on a supply that does not follow the sky (S3-1). **The upkeep** is set by the place: the drains put right before every melt and again at the refreeze (amendment G7-C1, PROPOSED; S3-4), made ground kept or it reverts (amendment G7-C2; S3-7), the gulf ice under both airports judged by its own temperature through December to February (S3-18, S3-20, S3-C6) `[DEC-586]`, the spur's culverts in the same weeks (S3-16; research RF-76 reserved), the made stop in a December with no night (Phase 4 axis). **So the nation decides how much of the city's work goes where, and the place decides when its own work falls due; no line puts either decision in the city's hands** (P7A-13). The mandate's work changes texture with the season (the cold chain is a warm chain for nine months and a cooling chain in the melt, S3-37; the seed store is machine-cooled about ten months and nearly free in July and August, S3-40, S3-C3), but nothing in it falls due on a date; the one seasonal pressure on its flow is the air door's melt-season risk, which is unruled and tagged (`DEC-586`). The upkeep's dates bind everyone, and no rotating staff exists to meet them (frame L21; Phase 2 P2-32 item 8). *(A)*

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: a ruled national mandate whose largest part is lit indoor food with no season (spec L258; `DR-71`; `DR-96` item 4); a genuine melt on bare rock inside ice walls, with a refreeze that fixes the summer's water in place, so that the place's own physics dates its upkeep (spec L113–116, L171–172, L180–181, L203–207); and an air door on lake ice whose risk months are the melt months (`DR-96` item 1; S3-18). Give the crop a season and the mandate gets dates of its own; take away the melt and the upkeep has no deadline; put the doors on rock and the export's flow has no seasonal edge. It rests on ruled and admitted lines and on PROPOSED entries tagged where used (G7-C1, S3-4, S3-7), not on `[PA-12]`, the held ground, `[PROV-8]` or any unruled door. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** a quota; a season; upkeep in general; a food role; indoor lit growing on its own (national, spec L258 "as everywhere"); a national grid; robots who recharge overnight; leisure owed to nobody. The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.
