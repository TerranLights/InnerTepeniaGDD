## The Lover's question

*Is this place alive, and could anyone love it?* — asked as the Lover **faculty** (Moore & Gillette), not the Lover position. Step 7's closing test (`07` L98: *structurally yes, and distinctively so; as a lived life, not yet*) and Phase 6's early verdict (`P6` L265) are inputs, not the answer.

**In the Lover's voice.** *"Is it alive? Yes — in two places, the stop and the door. At the stop a whole city goes still in full daylight because one of its two kinds of people has to charge and the other has chosen to rest at the same hour; that is the most beautiful fact in these files, and nobody in them remarks on it. At the door everyone's coat is a sentence about the day, said without a word. And the light — a June sun that only just clears the north; a sunset after four weeks of none, with no dark after it. Could anyone love it? Someone could love whom she lives with here: a robot who will remember every human who ever lived in her house; a human who learned the wind as a child beside someone who learned it, as an adult, on her first day. Someone could love being trusted with her own reading. But ask a resident whether she loves living at Mirny and the files give her nothing to answer with but the weather and her household — no song, no dish of her own, no game, no word for the wind she would teach a child, no day when the city gathers. Her love, as written, is for her people and for the light, not yet for the city. The place is alive in private and not yet in public."*

**Verdict.** **Alive** — in its conditions (the wind's ground tone, the light that dates the year) and in its households (two bodies, one stop). **Lovable privately** — a pair, a household, a person who wants to be trusted. **Not yet lovable publicly** — what a resident of either body would point to and say *this is ours* (a custom, a song, a dish, a gathering, a named place) is a reasoned null in every phase that could have held it (`P4` P4-10; `P8` 8b, 8e; `P10` §6.3). *The beauty the files do hold is all light* — the lit midnight, the June noon sun due north, the first sunset with no night after it (a candidate) — *and all of it is the sky's, not the city's making* (`P10` P10-7: the light's settings are dated; everything else is called on the day).

**Against Phase 6's early verdict** (T5, `P6` L265: *"lovable by someone who wants to be trusted with their own reading"*, and *"the love is for how the place holds its conditions more than for any custom"* — two substrings of the one line). **Not falsified; narrowed and moved.** Phases 7–10 did not supply a custom; they supplied **people to love the place through** — Phase 9's mixed household in which the robot is the continuity and the humans the turnover, Phase 8's *"a meal for one body, a room for the other"* (`P8` CAND-F2, a candidate), the cook who does not eat what she cooks. So the love the files make possible has moved from *how the place holds its conditions* to *the person one holds them with*. Phase 10 adds the reason the public love has nothing to attach to: *"The things that stay cannot be seen; the things that can be seen do not stay."* (`P10` L137) — no monument, no lasting thing of the residents' own.

**Against Step 7's closing test.** Consistent. The Lover adds *where* a life is already alive — at the stop and in the household — and *where* it is not yet — in public.

**The thinness flag, through this question.** Discharged as far as the panel can: the joy layer is empty **by request, not by finding** — no phase found joylessness; each found no admitted content. **Impotent Lover verdict:** *partially guilty — on the page, not proven of the place* (see below). Carried as a standing objection not resolved.
