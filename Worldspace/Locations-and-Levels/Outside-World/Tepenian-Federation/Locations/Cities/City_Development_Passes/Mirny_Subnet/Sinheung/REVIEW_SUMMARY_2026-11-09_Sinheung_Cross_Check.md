## What changed, in plain terms

- **Who is in the city and why (Phase 2).** The Founding Register now grounds Korea on the court's allocation *plus* geography, at the edge of the time-zone rule. So the pass's line that the founders are here "by instrument, not by reach" is killed, and so is its contrast with the nearest nation by reach. With the East Asian cluster as the developer ruled it (Korea, Japan and the Sinian Federation, 47.85%), the cluster is a plurality, not a majority, so the pass's "a majority that can, against a plurality that cannot" contrast falls. The "no bloc" verdict stands as a conditional one that rests on canon, with the rock allocation left open as the one seam. The starting stock for food preservation is now named by the spec's own tiers (Korea, Japan, Germany, the Sinian Federation first) and the practice claims are to be sourced and labeled as real-world proxies.
- **Retention (Phase 9 and the Spine).** The robot-versus-human gap is now 1.47 points, not 2.46, and part of that was moved by census steps the census itself calls arithmetic. The direction stands (robots stayed a little less often than humans) but no cause can be sized to the gap: the robot-side explanation survives only as a seed resting on a firm place fact (the apparatus is made here and shipped out), the human-side "the reason" is killed, and the Spine's "the retention figure is safe" is withdrawn.
- **The free share of the workforce and the band (Phase 4 and the Frame).** The free tier is a headcount of workers in work the city chooses (30.5%, about three in ten), not time or leisure, so "a third of life is free here" fails. The counterweight against reading the city as grim now rests on canon's own leisure rulings (for robots) and the city's chosen work. Fabrication is 26.2% of the workforce, not 45%. The Frame and phase headers declare Band 4; the ruled band is Band 5, and (per the band check) the statement the band ruling requires about the analysis it defers is missing.
- **Language (Phase 8).** "No origin language dominates" still holds, but now because Hawaiian (later Tepenian) is the dominant language everywhere by ruling. Korean is this city's only supporting-tier language, a living working language, so it leaves the "words that survive from origin" layer, and the identity clause narrows to "not a mark of descent".
- **Station lineage (Frame and Step 5).** The Frame's blanket "site history is not an input" falls: the station's plant, records, research, equipment and techniques are admissible. Every exclusion the pass actually made still stands on narrower ground (the "Resettled" refusal, singular settlement), and the Reconciliation's rejection of the station-sourced identity proves largely salvageable on the court's allocation plus geography. The Reconciliation also misnames the sheet sections it cites.
- **Raw materials (Phases 5 and 7).** "Raw material" splits into a domestic road stream and a provisional overseas stream of alloying and process inputs reached at one remove through the neutral hubs (era unruled; landing at the fjord only conditional). The spine sentence stands word for word, but three supporting rows fall.
