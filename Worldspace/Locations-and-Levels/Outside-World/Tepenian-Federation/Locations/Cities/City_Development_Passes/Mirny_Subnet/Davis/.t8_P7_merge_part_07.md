## 7. Limits

- **The model.** Everything here is re-derived on the Division of Industry model. The readers did not test its
  rates.
  - File 16 L45–46 marks four rate groups as estimates. Because the distinctive tier is workforce minus baseline,
    those estimates move 186,131.
  - "Firm" means firm on the files and rulings as they stand (A, C).
- **The national human total** (15,623,523) comes from file 19 L86, not from the census. Every fraction of the
  country's humans is conditional on it (all three).
- **The 44.2%.**
  - It cannot be given as a share of food, as opposed to a share of heads, because the other components' D
    values are in no list (A).
  - The share depends on other locations' figures, which entered only as inputs to the national total and were
    not examined (B).
- **Option A's composition** is inferred from arithmetic, not read from a line (A).
- **File 16's own formula text** lies outside its admitted spans. The confirmation that its baseline carries
  the 120.7 term rests on exact reproduction and on the formula as stated in files 17, 18 and 19 (all three).
- **People-fed figures are capacities.** Nothing in the files models where Davis's output actually goes (B).
- **D = 1.25** is taken as given. Its derivation lies in a file that is in no list (B).
- **What the readers did not see:**
  - Only the listed rulings-log spans were read, so a later ruling that reopened convention B would be
    invisible (C).
  - The pass-time quotations from file 16 were compared only with its current text; earlier versions were not
    read (C).
- **The workforce formula's meaning in persons** is not settled ({{REF:Q:workforce_unit_q}}).
- **The merge writer** re-derived nothing. It checked quotations and line citations only, within the admitted
  ranges. No location was compared with Davis; other locations' figures appear only as inputs to national
  totals, and are not named.
