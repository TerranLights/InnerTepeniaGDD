## The differentiation axis

**A still bowl the city fills.**

*What the words carry.* The site gives stillness: a measured mean wind of 4.6 m/s (spec L143), "an otherwise calm mean" (L203), calms in 39% of central-oasis observations (L146 `[DEM]` `[PA-3]`). It gives dry air with little to carry (polar arid, L163–166 `[DEM]`; dry valleys with little lying snow, L109; no soil before the founders made it, L222), and light every day of the year (L151), with long nights in winter (about 2.7 h of June daylight, L155, so about 21 h of night). Into that bowl, walled on every side (L113–116), the city puts a continuous load of its own: about 0.5 GW of light for the export food alone (`DR-96` item 4, rulings L1633), heat against 3,987 freezing degree-days (`Climate.md` L24), winter water moved by pump through heated lines (S3-31), and warm charging rooms every month for half its people (S3-27, `[PROV-8]`, `[computed, REQ-52]`; not load-bearing for the axis). **So what the senses meet on an ordinary day here is mostly the city's own output (its hum, its warmth, its light in the long winter nights, the smells of its work), and the site speaks in episodes: a storm with a passing low, the melt's water, the ice.** *(PROPOSED.)*

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each from this place's own lines: a walled basin whose mean air is still (L113–116, L143, L203); air and ground with little of their own to carry (L109, L163–166 `[DEM]`, L222); and a continuous bought load of light and heat on the order of half a gigawatt (`DR-96` item 4), which does not follow the sky (S3-1). Take away the stillness and the wind carries the city's output off; take away the load and the bowl is silent and dark through the winter nights; take away the walls and the air exchanges. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** cold; clothing for it; an indoor lit crop (national, "as everywhere", spec L258); the shared night (national, physiology L336–338). The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.

## F · Retroactive Mechanism

- **P3C-228 · What is simply true here that nothing explains: in October, with the air's mean at −13.7 °C, the lake ice is "beginning to work" (spec L188), while September, at −17.7 °C, is "still fully frozen" (L187).
