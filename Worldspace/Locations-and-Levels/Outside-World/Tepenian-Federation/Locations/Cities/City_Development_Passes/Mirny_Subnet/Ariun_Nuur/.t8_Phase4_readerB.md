## B · The four elements

### B1 · Routines: what the day is organized around

**The axis of this sub-section: the day is kept by a clock, and its night is a charge night held indoors.**

**FIND-P4-104 · For about 243 days a year, the day has no sky edge; the clock sets it.** Polar night runs April 23 to August 20 (120 days) and midnight sun October 21 to February 20 (123 days), from a published calculator (research FIND-3; the spec's L96–97 counts hold, its start dates a day or two later). **FIND-P4-105 · The city's night is the shared charge night, and it is held in heated, powered rooms.** National canon: robots need downtime, most of all overnight recharging, which gives the nation a shared night; robots and humans stop at the same time, for different reasons (physiology file L334–338); a charging robot dreams (L456). Here, charging in the cold costs capacity permanently and a robot cannot recover on her own past a threshold (cold file L23–26, L39–44, in words; asymmetry rows 81, 83), so **charge is taken warm**, and a charge draws on the electric supply, warming a cold cell included (`DR-140` item 4, no rate). → **The night is a time, not a darkness:** for about 243 days it is not bound to the dark (frame L177), and it can only be spent inside heated, powered space (spine L406). → For 64.50% of the city (284,693 robots, frame DL-4) the night is dreamed, in heat. → *Expression:* the city goes quiet at an hour on a clock, under its own lights or behind its own shades. *[B]*

**FIND-P4-106 · The shared night puts the robot load on the one supply in the same hours.** Charge for 284,693 robots is drawn in the hours everyone rests, beside heat for every resting body, all on one electric supply (spine §8e: *one supply carries both bodies' lives*), whose source is unwritten (`DEC-705`). → The hours when the whole city rests are the hours its supply carries both bodies' rest at once, and resting is the act the cold punishes most (spine standing cost 1). → **A lapse at night would find everyone stopped, both bodies, in the same hours.** PROPOSED; no rate, no source, no figure; nothing assumes a grid (PV-7).
