# ⭐⭐⭐ HEADLINE FINDINGS

**Three, each independently sourced, and all three survived the three-way cross-check.**

## H-1 — Nobody's presence at Davis was ever decided about them individually

> # ***Neither founding population was selected on any individual quality, and neither was selected on any relation to the destination.***

**Three sourced legs, none inferred:**

1. **Art. II.2.2** — *"This provision applies to robots as a class. No individual designation, accusation, or
   finding of wrongdoing is required, sought, or relevant to its application; nor shall the absence of any such
   finding be grounds for exception."*
2. **Art. II.3.2** — the departing population *"shall be settled at such existing scientific and research
   installations as are presently found upon that continent, **each population being assigned to a station**."*
3. ⭐⭐ **Art. II.2.3** — a human qualifies by having *"taken up residence among robot communities, married or
   partnered with a robot, borne or raised children in a robot household, **or otherwise established their life
   among the robot population**"* — **four grounds, and the fourth is a broad residual.**

> ### ⚠⚠ THE FOURTH GROUND IS LOAD-BEARING, AND THIS PHASE'S FIRST DRAFT MISREAD IT — developer correction, 2026-09-16. See §O item 12.
> ⛔ **The criterion is NOT "a relationship with a robot."** **Two of the four grounds are COMMUNAL rather than
> personal** — *residence among robot communities*, and the residual *otherwise established their life among the
> robot population* — **and the residual is broad enough to cover anyone whose life was already among them:
> neighbors, colleagues, anyone who lived there.**
> ⇒ ***A household tie is ONE qualifying route among several, not the criterion. Humans were free to self-exile
> alongside the robot population.***

**What the four grounds actually share, and it is the finding:** ⭐ ***every one of them is a tie to the robot
population — as a community or as particular persons — and not one is a tie to a place, a skill, a nationality,
or any individual merit.***

### What that causes

- **First order.** ⭐ **The founding instrument admitted humans on the basis of WHERE THEIR LIFE ALREADY WAS —
  not on who they were, what they could do, or where they were going.** Set against II.2.2's class rule for the
  robots: ***neither founding population was selected on an individual quality, and neither was selected on
  anything whatever to do with Davis.***
- **Second order.** ⇒ **The human founding cohort is not a cohort of individuals recruited for a destination. It
  is a cross-section of the people whose lives were already among the robot population** — households,
  neighbors, colleagues, and whoever else the residual clause covers. ⛔ **Its internal composition is UNSIZED
  and unsizeable from the admitted set.**
- ⭐⭐ **Third order.** **And that is why the human half of the founding cohort arrives already carrying a
  relation to the robot population and none at all to the ground.** ***The one thing every founding human had in
  common was the company they kept. The one thing none of them had was a reason to be at Davis.*** **Which is
  the same shape II.3.2 produced for the robots by a completely different route** *(§B.5)*.
- ⏸️ **Fourth order — HELD, not asserted.** A **mixed-household component is guaranteed to exist** by grounds 2
  and 3, which name marriage, partnership and children in terms. ⛔ **Its size is nowhere stated, `DR-4` forbids
  sizing it, and nothing establishes it at Davis 248 years later.** ⇒ ***Any reading that runs the 19.74 pp
  retention spread "through homes" rests on two unstated quantities and is a HYPOTHESIS, not a finding.***
  Holes `H5`, `H15`.
- ⛔ **AND THE PHASE STOPS THERE.** What Davis says about such a household, or whether it has a word for one, is
  Phase 6 and Phase 9 material. *Stating the constraint is legal; converting it into a practice is foreclosure.*

> **§7.3 compliance, checked on the headline.** The finding is about what the founding instrument did. ⛔ It
> nowhere characterizes the 781,596 who remained as having chosen, stayed, committed, or believed. **It is the
> structural, blameless, villain-free form §7.3 exists to admit.**

## H-2 — Davis's founding stock and Davis's plurality stock are two different communities, and canon writes nothing between them

**The founding-wave community carries the census's own `(founding wave)` tag at an inherited 4.93%. The
Primary-tier community sits at an inherited 19.04%.** Fire #3 states the gap in terms and this phase reproduces
it rather than performing a new comparison.

**The binding anti-stereotype law supplies the correct form of words for a Tepenian city's origin** — *"a
Japan-FOUNDED Tepenian city. Stock: Japanese. People: Tepenian. Local culture: its own."* ⭐ **That form has one
slot, and it silently assumes the founding stock and the demographic plurality are the same community.**

> # ***At Davis they are not, and the form has no correct single instantiation.***

**Davis is a founding-wave-Australian, plurality-Chinese Tepenian city, and those are facts about two different
populations.** ⛔ Neither term may be filled in for the other; the shorter form — picking one — is wrong in both
directions.

- **First order.** No stock at Davis is a majority and the largest is under a fifth ⇒ there is no *"the
  community"* and no *"the newcomers,"* and no stock can supply a default anybody may assume.
- **Second order.** The ordinary mechanism by which a plural city organizes itself — **founding stock as host,
  later stocks as guests** — is unavailable in *both* of its terms. The founding stock is too small to host, and
  there are no later stocks because there was no later arrival. ⇒ **`G8`'s deficit *"no seniority axis"* is not
  an omission in the record; it is a structural consequence of the founding arriving all at once and never being
  followed.**
- ⭐⭐ **Third order.** `D5` records that *"no precedence politics can form."* This phase supplies the mechanism
  it was missing: ***precedence requires that having-arrived be a difference that PERSISTS. A terminal
  assignment with no successor wave makes it a difference that dies with the first generation.***
- ⭐ **Fourth order.** A city that cannot form precedence politics also cannot form the **counter**-politics —
  there is no newcomers' grievance either. ⇒ one more counterparty Davis does not have.

## Q.5 ⛔⛔ **T8 PROVES A CLAUSE WAS READ. IT CANNOT PROVE THE CLAUSE WAS READ TO THE END.**

**Measured here, on the phase's single most load-bearing citation.** **Three isolated readers quoted Art.
II.2.3. `triple_read_verify.py` confirmed all three quotations against the real file byte-for-byte. All three —
and the orchestrator synthesizing them — still built on a reading that had elided the clause's fourth and
broadest ground, *"or otherwise established their life among the robot population."*** **The correction came
from the developer reading the output and quoting the clause back in full.**

> ### ⭐ THE SHAPE, STATED GENERALLY SO IT TRANSFERS
> ***A broad residual at the END of an enumeration — "or otherwise…", "or any other…", "including but not
> limited to…" — is exactly the span a reader elides while quoting the enumeration accurately.*** **The
> enumeration's first items are concrete and memorable; the residual is abstract and does the widest work.**
> **Every verification instrument in T8 operates on whether a quoted span matches the source. None operates on
> whether the quoted span is the WHOLE operative clause.**
>
> ⛔ **And the failure is silent in the worst way: the narrow reading is strictly MORE vivid than the correct
> one.** *"Admitted on the basis of who they loved"* is a better sentence than *"admitted on the basis of where
> their life already was,"* and it is wrong. **The incentive runs toward the elision.**
>
> ✅ **PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED:** *when a phase builds a headline on a single enumerated clause, quote the clause
> in full into the phase file — every ground, including the residual — before drawing from it, so that the
> elision is visible on the page rather than only in the reasoning.* ⛔ **Raised, not fixed.**
