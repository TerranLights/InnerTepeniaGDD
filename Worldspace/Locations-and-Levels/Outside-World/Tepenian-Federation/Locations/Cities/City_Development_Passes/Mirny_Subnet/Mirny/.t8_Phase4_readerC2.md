## A. The headline function, in one sentence — then written away from

**The headline function:** Mirny quarries and processes raw material — for the eastern highways, for infrastructure-maintenance machinery and, as the near-exclusive source, for the robot-synthesis chambers fabricated elsewhere — while its relay carries the subnet's own signal. *Sources:* `CON` C-3 (L386), *"subnet hub; quarrying; processing construction materials; manufacturing infrastructure-maintenance machinery; the near-exclusive raw-material source for robot-synthesis chambers"*; **[ADM]** `SPEC` L184, *"if it fails, the whole Mirny subnet loses contact with itself"*; **[ADM]** L174, *"the sheltered industrial core inside the city's outer wind-fortified ring"*. **The test** (`SPN3`): *would this reasonably be someone's entire day, every day, forever?* **No — and the one citable figure says why.** Mirny's own row, pasted as the file prints it (`DoI-16` L275):

## B. The four elements, separately

**B.0 The ordinary condition, stated before any environmental claim** (`SPN3` §0.2 item 9, rule 3; the `M-151` trap). *Clothed or not:* clothed, in both bodies — a robot *"nearly almost always does"* wear clothing, for fashion and function, and *"a robot can realistically remain standing still in most of the country"* when prepared for the weather **[IND, `RP`]**. *Sheltered or not:* no admitted file gives an interior; the night place for both bodies is a held-above-freezing place as a **default**, not a physical requirement (`P3` P3-6 as corrected 2026-10-03; the three-option table below) **[CORR]**. *Mobile or not:* both bodies are mobile; for a robot, stopping outdoors costs more than moving, a cost whose strong form (`COLD` §4, the death spiral) is written for a deep-cold plateau and is a **gradient**, never a gate, at Mirny's temperatures (`RP`: *"a GRADIENT across locations, never a gate"*). **Every claim below describes two bodies, a human body and a robot body, and says where they differ.**

### B.1 ROUTINES — what recurs

**P4-C2-1 · The shared stop is kept by something other than the sky** *[NAT + ADM + CORR + D]*
- **The canon.** *"Which gives Tepenia a shared night. Robots and humans stop at the same time, for different reasons."* **[NAT, `RP` L336]** — the human rests, the robot recharges (overnight, `RP` L334), and *"A city where everyone sleeps is a very different place from one where half the population works through the dark"*. The same passage glosses the shared night as the polar night falling on a population all resting; **Mirny has no polar night, so the gloss is not imported** (`FRM` RP-5); the shared-rest question was handed to this phase.
- *1st — the sky cannot be the cue.* Mirny's sun is below the horizon from 0 h a day (December) to 21.6 h a day (June) (§D.4), so the *length* of the sky's night swings by 21.6 h across the year on monthly means; a sunset exists on 336 days of 365 but wanders from 13:12 (June) to 22:39 (January) in solar time on monthly means, **a swing of at least 9 h 27 min** (the days beside the 29 lit days have sunsets near midnight, so the daily swing is wider), and does not exist at all for the 29 days of midnight sun (**[ADM]** `SPEC` L86: *"approximately December 8 → January 5 (~29 days)"*); and for about 80 days there is no darkness beyond civil twilight even where the sun does set (**[CORR]** `RES` §2: *"No darkness beyond civil twilight for about 80 days (Nov 11 → Jan 30)."*). The sky is an honest *calendar* and a dishonest *clock* — `SPN` already has it: *"The calendar tells the truth; the hour lies"*.
- *2nd — so the stop is kept by a convention.* Whatever keeps the shared stop at Mirny is in **no admitted file** (R-C2-2; Q-C2-1). `FRM` PA-8 sets no in-world calendar or clock, so "convention" here names only *whatever keeps time*, claims no device, and rests on nothing but the arithmetic above. A consequence: the same hour on whatever keeps time is daylight in one month and deep dark in another, so a resident holds two facts, the hour and the sky.
- *3rd.* **(a)** Whether the sun's short winter day lies *inside* anyone's waking span is a setting of the stop relative to solar noon, not a fact of the sky: June's 2.4 h of sun could fall wholly inside or wholly outside the shared day, and the files do not set it (R-C2-2). **(b)** The stop is the one synchronizer a visitor cannot read off the sky (Phase 5). **(c)** In the 29 lit days the stop is kept under a sun; `SPN` names the cost — *"no dusk ends the day in the one season that permits outdoor work"* (Backward check item 1 on "the one season") — and the only physical end-of-day cue the files document in the summer and transition seasons is the wind's evening build (**[CORR]** `RES` §1: the katabatic *"builds in the evening"*; local-time figures, no in-world clock): whether that build ends the outdoor day at Mirny is reserved (RF-C2-1). **(d)** Neither body's reason for the stop depends on the sun — a human rests, a robot recharges, and light has *no admitted effect on recharge* (`P3` §E) — so the nightless days change what people see and do outdoors and **nothing about what either body needs** (P4-C2-6).
- *Act:* neutral (the shared night is canon of the Second Interwar, `RP`).

**P4-C2-2 · The door: every outing is decided against the wind, not the thermometer** *[ADM + CORR + D]*
- **The facts.** **[ADM]** *"wind is the dominant environmental feature of this city"* (`SPEC` L71); *"sustained wind events that define outdoor conditions for much of the year"* (L57); *"even at relatively moderate temperatures, sustained katabatic events make outdoor exposure dangerous"* (L139); *"blizzard conditions can develop rapidly"* (L134). **[CORR]** the first sign of the dangerous wind can read as an improvement — a clearing sky and a rising thermometer (`P3` P3-2) — and the cues are honest and unread (`RES` §1, *"The cues are honest; they are unread"*).
- *1st.* A decision lives at every threshold: the monthly mean does not tell one winter month from another (the five May–September means span 1.0 °C), and a mild reading is where the warning can read as relief (`P3` P3-2); the two months with average highs above freezing, +0.4 and +1.1 °C, are the months a mild reading is most ordinary. The gate is the wind; the body is prepared for it with garments and gloves (both bodies), and a robot carries one more number (P4-C2-6).
- *2nd — what the resident consults is an empty slot.* The files make three channels available and establish none as Mirny's practice: **(i)** the resident's own reading of the cues (acquired by childhood immersion by the born-here human, by adult immersion by a robot or an adult arrival, `P2` P2-3 **[CORR]**); **(ii)** the written record, which carries procedure and thresholds and not judgment (`SPN` §4: *"Authority lives in writing; danger lives in the wind"*); **(iii)** a relayed report — `SPN` says *"the signal decides when people move"*, but **no admitted file says any wind or visibility report is published at Mirny** (the real-world analogs in `RES` §5 are comparables, not Mirny facts), and the relay is *"noisiest exactly when the city most needs it"* (`RES` §4, conditional on a relay technology that is not canon). **Which channel a resident consults is REQUESTED** (R-C2-3).
- *3rd — the asymmetry.* An injury is read as the injured person's carelessness while a trip not taken leaves no trace (`SPN` §6 #5, `P2` P2-1, `P3` P3-2). So whatever bias the door decision has, it has in **one direction for discretionary crossings: toward staying in** — direction only, no magnitude, and no claim about how often anyone stays in or how long anyone spends outdoors (**no time budget outdoors is claimed;** none is in any admitted file, R-C2-1). For crossings nobody can decline — work in the wind, §E — the bias has nowhere to go.
- **What the garments cannot fix.** *"outdoor activity in strong katabatic conditions is dangerous regardless of cold protection"* (**[ADM]** L131): in strong katabatic conditions it is the site, not a body, that closes the door, for both bodies alike. This is not the `M-151` error: `RP`'s gradient concerns what clothing does against cold, and L131 says clothing is not the remedy for this hazard; **no hazard it names is spelled out** (`P3` B.0).

**P4-C2-3 · Two kinds of day: crossing days and interior-bound stretches** *[ADM + CORR + D]*
- *1st.* **[CORR]** storms *"last up to about nine days (220 h)"* and calm and weak wind together are *"about 3 % of observations"* (`RES` §1; a regional source, `P3` PA-16): 220 / 24 = 9.17 days, 3 % of 365 = 10.95 days if observations are uniform (§D.5). A storm is *an ambient condition, not an occasion* (`P3` P3-1).
