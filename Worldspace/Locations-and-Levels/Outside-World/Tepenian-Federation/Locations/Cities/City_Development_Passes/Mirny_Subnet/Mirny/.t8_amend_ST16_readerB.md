### 2.1 The site and what it does

- **M2-1 [PHYS, LIVE].** Mirny sits on a rocky, exposed coast at 66°33'S: "For practical purposes, Mirny is on the line." (`SPEC` L75, L79).
- **M2-2 [PHYS, LIVE].** No polar night; about 29 days of continuous daylight, about December 8 to January 5 (`SPEC` L107, L108). Daylight runs from about 2.4 hours in June to a computed 24 in December while the winter temperature sits on a plateau from May to September (`ST13` L522, citing `SPEC` L134, L140 and `RD` L14). The light, not the thermometer, dates the year: Phase 3's axis "Wind-made, light-dated." stands (`ST13` L17).
- **M2-3 [PHYS, LIVE].** The cold is a backdrop: annual mean -11.2 °C (`RD` L8; `SPEC` L90); "WIND, decisively — the cold is NOT what defines this city." (`SPEC` L104).
- **M2-4 [PHYS, LIVE].** The wind is the defining hazard: katabatic, off the plateau from the south and southeast, averaging 10 to 14 m/s, with storms above 40 m/s recorded (`SPEC` L93); mean 12.0 m/s (`SPEC` L104); calm or weak in about 3 % of observations (`RES` L40, as `ST14` L792 cites it); a storm can run about nine days (`P4` L201: "storm up to 220 h = 9.17 days ; calm+weak 3 % of 365 d = 10.95 d").
- **M2-5 [PHYS, LIVE].** The snow is lifted, not falling: "Whiteout here routinely occurs under a clear sky" (`SPEC` L104), a ground blizzard in Step 3's words (`RES` L21 to L23, through `ST12` L899); about 38 % of what falls stays, and "residents dig out from what arrived from upwind, not from what fell on them." (`SPEC` L98 to L100, L104). Heat follows the wind, not the thermometer (P3-2, standing, `ST13` L17).
- **M2-6 [PHYS].** The same blowing snow degrades radio: "noise raised 50 dB or more, radio data lost 10–30 % of the time in winter and spring at one" (`RES` L72). The wind works on the relay's signal as well as on the people.
- **M2-7 [PHYS, OCT].** The sea freezes seasonally and is reachable only in the navigable window (`SPEC` L79); "an unschedulable open-water window; coastal rock with hidden crevasses inland." (`P2` L282).

(`RCP` L92); robots do not breathe (`RP`); the indoor default for robot nights holds in every month (P3-6, `ST13` L17). For a human the wind's cost is the familiar one of exposure.
