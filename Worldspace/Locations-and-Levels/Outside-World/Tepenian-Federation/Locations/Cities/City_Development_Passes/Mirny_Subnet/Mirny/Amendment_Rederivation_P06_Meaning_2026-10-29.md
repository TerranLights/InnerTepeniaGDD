## 4 · Re-derivation (merged)

**Corrected premises** (the developer's words, `DRL` L1115, L1116, L1140, L1142, L1150): **(a)** the first generation or so learned the wind alone, by doing, including getting it wrong; **(b)** afterward a knowledge system exists: taught person to person, signifiers, a weather report/relay system; **(c)** an organization of people of both bodies manages and curates the weather protocol, public and learnable by anyone; **(d)** the duty to restore the relay is communal, uncompelled, its neglect brings deep personal guilt (both bodies); **(e)** "Keepers" is the pass's coinage — the principle is endorsed, the label and its duties are not. **Not ruled:** the form of the weather report, who runs it, the signifiers, whether it rides the relay, the organization's name.
**What stays (trap 1):** the founding gap — "A **tradition** cannot cross: it passes person to person, and no one carried it across the gap" (`NNS` L21); the cues honest but, at the founding, unread ("The cues are honest; they are unread") (`RES` L30–33); the physics; and the opposite overstatement is refused — a taught wind is still a wind that is calm only a few percent of the time, whose visibility varies day to day at the same speed (`RES` L28, L40–41).
**Tags:** [ADM] stated in an admitted line · [IND] independent derivation · [CORR] corroborated by a second line · [D] derived, not stated · [RW] real-world mechanism · [CAND] candidate, not adopted. **Every finding is base-level (`DR-4`), both bodies named.**

### A6-1 · THE NAME, re-derived · replaces **P6-1**; folds missed #2, #3, #6, #22 · Act-sensitive · both bodies
> **The sentence a resident would agree with** *(proposed wording, not a quotation; three readers' drafts merged; R-67 still open)*: ***What the first of us had to learn the hard way is ours to learn and ours to pass on; a danger seen is a danger told; the call at your own door is still yours; and when the line goes down, whoever is best placed goes — nobody has to order it.***
- **What kind of thing it is** (`SPN3` L794, L812–815): a **SOCIAL CONTRACT** — agreed, never written as law ("nobody is officially required", `DRL` L1116). Inside it, a **STANDARD OF WORTH** — going when best placed; not going is "a cause for deep and immense personal guilt" [ADM]. And, as a **[CAND]** only, a **DEBT** to the first generation who paid for the learning (A, B, C; whether residents *feel* it is in no admitted line, R-67). The hub's older debt — the subnet's contact through its one site (`SPEC` L184) — survives, now with a stated motive. **Not a creed:** "A shared proposition is not a creed" (`SPN3` L813). **The weather protocol is not the Social Contract** (C): the protocol is a public body of knowledge with a curating organization; the Social Contract is the unwritten agreement that makes people learn it, teach it, tell danger and go.
- **Mechanism** [D on ADM + IND + CORR]: three admitted conditions force it together — **(G4)** the record came with "no living institutional knowledge" (`CON` L387) and no tradition crossed (`NNS` L21), so the first generation learned by doing (`P2` L224: "which includes getting it wrong"); **(G2)** the cues were honest but unread at the founding — "the sky tells the truth in a language nobody at the founding had learned" (`RES` L33); **(G5)** one relay, the subnet's single point of failure (`SPEC` L184), the object of the duty. What the first generation learned became the city's own tradition: "A city's own tradition is fine when it starts with the city" (`DRL` L646, `DR-25`) [IND].
- **Transmission, as mechanism only** [RW, `MM`]: fixed as signifiers it becomes a copy-the-instructions channel ("Errors do not compound", `MM` L65); taught person to person it rides vertical transmission "still loosely coupled to survival value" (`MM` L131). **So the old split — authority to what survives copying (the book), risk to what does not (the reader), P6 L91 — closes for the wind after the first generation: the reader's judgment was itself copied.** No claim about the protocol's form (AR-1).

The **learning channels** differ: a human born here can learn as a child; a robot learns as an adult by immersion ("learned from the cultures they are immersed in after coming online", `CON` L18; mentor/mentee, `P4` L176; "transmission runs through the community", `FRM` L269) [IND/CORR]. The knowledge system forms within "the first generation or so" and reaches Act 2 as a Mirny-made practice descended from Mirny's own founding, not from any stock. The duty's root is national and was "already IN PROGRESS" before the Tower (`NNS` L127–128); the Tower's completion (~2688) consolidates an identity already forming — **a gradient with a decisive moment, never a switch.**
