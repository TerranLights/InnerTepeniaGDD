## B.1 · SOUND — ⭐⭐⭐ **the wind has a direction, so the quiet has a map**

**Persistent, strong katabatic flow from the NORTH-EAST on most summer days.** ⭐ **Constant, and — unlike
gusting — *directional and predictable*.**

> ## **EVERY STRUCTURE CASTS AN ACOUSTIC SHADOW, IN THE SAME PLACE, ALL SUMMER.**
> ⭐ **Second order:** *the lee of anything is where conversation happens, and it is the same lee every year —
> so the quiet places are FIXED and everybody knows them.*
> ⭐⭐ **Third order:** ***a city whose sheltered spots are permanent, public and free does not need to build
> many.*** **Meeting happens in found lee, not in provided rooms** — *and where the wind drops is not a design
> decision anyone made.*

⚠ **In WINTER the wind is not described as persistent** *(the source names it "most summer days")*. **So the
acoustic map is SEASONAL: loud-and-mapped in summer, and something else in the dark months.** ⏸️ *What winter
sounds like is not established — flagged, not invented.*
