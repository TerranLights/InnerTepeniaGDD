## B · The four elements

### Routines

**The axis of this sub-section: the day runs between warm stops.**

**P4-202 · The day's skeleton is the street's, for every body.** Heat at every place a body stops, water melted from ice, charge for the robot majority, light for about 120 days, all on one supply, unnamed (spine §8e; Phase 3 FIND-P3-44). → A day here is a sequence of **warm stops joined by passages through the cold**: wake or come off charge in a heated place, cross, work, cross, stop again where it is heated. Whether the crossings are outdoors or inside one envelope is not written (enclosure open, `DEC-703`; Q-51), so the passage is written as a cost, never as a street or a corridor. → *Expression:* every plan in a day starts from where the next stop is. *(C)*

**P4-203 · The shared night is a charge night and a sleep night at the same clock hours, not at dark.** Robots require downtime, most of all overnight recharging, and robots and humans stop at the same time, for different reasons (RP L334–338). Here about 243 days have no sunrise or sunset (spine D-3), so *overnight* means the clock's night, not the sky's (frame L177; spine L406). Charge is taken warm: charging in the cold costs capacity, and warming a cold cell to charge it draws on the electric supply, no rate written (cold L24–37; brief §4 item 12). → **The robot majority's night is a charge taken in heated, powered space, beside the human minority's sleep in the same kind of space** (Phase 3 FIND-P3-20: in a shared place of rest the human breath is the only breath). A charging robot dreams (RP L456–458), so the robots' night is not blank time. *(C)*

**P4-204 · The manufactured day: the clock is the institutions'.** For about 243 days the sky sets nothing (spine D-3, L117, L129); in the polar night the day and night split narrows toward weather noise (spec L118). The one clock already on the site is a world clock: the real station's record was kept at four fixed hours (research FIND-39; UI-6, reserved as RF-70). → **Whose night it is here is everyone's, by clock** (research FIND-18 left it to this phase): the shared night exists because both bodies keep the same hours, not because the dark arrives. **P4-205 · The human place of rest changes with the season; the robot's does not.** For about 123 days of midnight sun human rest needs darkness at the place of rest as much as heat; in winter, light at set times is the known remedy (research FIND-17; Phase 3 FIND-P3-30). **P4-206 · Going out is decided indoors.** The air is still and honest: when visibility closes, something is actually falling (spec L93); exposure is temperature and time (spine §3a strength 1); the calm, clear day carries the full cost with no warning (SH-2). → **Outdoor time is set by clock and thermometer before the door, never read from the sky once out** (Phase 3 FIND-P3-25). *Expression:* a person of either body knows, before going out, how long she will be out and where she will next stop; how the city teaches this is unwritten (RF-45). *(C)*
