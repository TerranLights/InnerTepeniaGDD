## 0. In short

- **The governing split is the city's own determination, D16 §17** (L1838-1840; `DR-103` item 2, LOG L1694):
  321,100 baseline (41.8%), 211,975 mandated (27.6%), 234,288 free (30.5%), of a workforce of 767,363 on Census I
  1,014,533. Every split sentence in the pass carries the older fabrication-only 40.7 / 26.7 / 32.6 and the
  workforce 728,779. None cites §17.
- **The free tier is a headcount of work the city chooses, not time and not leisure** (D16 L27, L1840). It covers
  five sectors: diplomacy, technical and scientific work, trade, marine work, and the half of education and other
  that is not the Institute. The phrases "of life" and "of a working life" fail. *About three in ten workers* is
  the accurate wording; one third overstates the figure.
- **All of fabrication is mandated, and the 45% is a share of the distinctive tier** (`DR-73`). That makes
  fabrication 200,818 workers, about a quarter (26.2%) of the workforce. So P4 L111's 45% of the workforce is false,
  and P8 L27's claim that most people work materials with their hands loses its cited basis.
- **The road-closure crisis of purpose rests on the mandated fabrication works standing idle,** not on the free
  tier. The fabrication works are a floor of about a quarter of the workforce, not everyone.
- **The Section E counterweight against grimness still holds,** but on different ground. Canon makes robot
  leisure owed to nobody and gives everyone a shared night. About three in ten workers do work the city chose. The
  climate is stated in its own figures, and canon's human-robot baseline is warm. The free tier is not leisure.
- **The free share is the product 58.155% × 52.5%.** D = 1.25 acts on the first factor only. The pass's words low,
  so high and comparatively benign have no own-terms referent. Reading D as a 20% productivity loss is conditional.
- **The band is Band 5 by ruling** (`DR-4`; LOG L1713). The pass declares Band 4 at fourteen places and never
  states `DR-4`'s deferral. The full deferral statement belongs in this amendment, and a pass file gets at most an
  end-of-file pointer (AMEND rule 3).
- **Physical scale is set by Census I:** about 101-145 km² (EXTENT L620). That is far beyond the roughly 40 km² of
  shared rock, so most of the required footprint lies off the rock (firm). Whether the ordinary commute crosses
  the seam depends on internal placement, which `DR-4` defers (conditional).
- **The two later census increases (`DR-68`, `DR-90`) are arithmetic, not arrivals.** No finding may narrate
  growth or a moment of becoming regional.
- **Open, for the developer:** which measure canon's "the thing the exile was for" line belongs to; whether to keep
  "time that is your own"; the spec's wording of the two increases; how far `DR-4`'s interim clause reaches. Also
  open: the Band 5 density, this city's rock figure, and `DRQ-11` and `DRQ-17`, whose texts are in no list.

**Arithmetic** (identical in A's, B's, C's and K's commands, and recomputed by all three readers in Round 3):
520,192 + 0.5 × 494,341 = 767,362.5, printed 767,363. The tiers sum: 321,100 + 211,975 + 234,288 = 767,363. The

**Answer to (a).**
- No admitted pass line cites D16 §17.
- None carries the Half B row's current figures (26.2 / 32.0).

  Half B row, at an earlier date. 26.7 ÷ 59.3 = 45.03% of the distinctive tier. The Half B row today gives 26.2 ÷
  58.2 = 45.02%.
- B notes that question (a) treats the Half B row and the pass's split as two things. They are one row at two
  dates.

**Answer to (b).** The workforce is 767,363 on Census I 1,014,533 (firm). The label "CURRENT census" is false. The
pass's 495,143 + 233,636 belong to the Census I of 962,415, which has changed twice since, by `DR-68` and `DR-90`
(CENSUS L509).

**Why the shares moved** (C; A and B concur on the parts).
- Baseline 40.7 → 41.8: mainly the robot-keyed term (8,193 workers, 1.07 points). The pre-term baseline on today's
  workforce was 40.777% (D16 L1844).
- Mandated 26.7 → 27.6: the Institute's 2.5% of the distinctive tier joins fabrication.
- Free 32.6 → 30.5: both of the above.
- The `DR-68` and `DR-90` increments were not uniform between humans and robots (CENSUS L509). So the pass's
  argument that a uniform −10% left the percentages intact (P4 L43-44) no longer applies.

K's reconstruction of where the pass's figures came from is written at {{REF:N:older_inputs}}.
