### ARR-1 · Two written human routes into the Federation after the treaty date; who a later human at Mirny can be *(F-1)* · both bodies named · Act: from 2564 on
**Proposed finding.** The treaty writes **two** human routes: II.2.3, to accompany the robot population, on a tie formed before the treaty date; and II.2.5, to take up residence, open at any time after the effective date and naming no ground. Both carry one cost: legal personhood in Assembly Power territory, and the way back. A human at Mirny after the treaty date was born here, came as a founding tie-holder under II.2.3, moved from elsewhere in Tepenia, or elected under II.2.5; **which, and how many, is unread. None is recorded here** — *none recorded here*, never *none possible here*. *(A, B, C; the instead-rule is the union of the three lists, as B's Round 3 row 2 proposes.)*
- **1st [ADM].** II.2.3's four grounds are each a life established "prior to the date of this Treaty" (`FTD` L48). II.2.5 opens a second route "at any time after the effective date of this Treaty", treats its elector "for all purposes of this Treaty" as a paragraph-3 elector, and binds her to the loss of "any claim to home, residency, or quarter within Assembly Power territory" (L50). Both elections forfeit personhood in Assembly territory (L76) and bar return (L77), and the forfeiture is "a consequence, in the law of each Assembly Power, of the human person's own free choice to depart" (L78). Ruled as canon: `DRL` L1913. The robot route is unchanged: "every robot who may hereafter come into being within such territory, shall depart" (L46); no later robot arrival is recorded (`DRL` L1913; `P9` L305, "Not adopted"). *(A, B, C)*
- **2nd [D].** II.2.3's date limit falls on the **tie**, not on the **journey**: the human "has, prior to the date of this Treaty, taken up residence among robot communities" (L48), and the population departs "in accordance with such schedule and such means of conveyance as the Assembly Powers shall provide" (L46), so tie-holders reached Tepenia after the treaty date by construction. What the pass's reading closed was the human with no pre-treaty tie, or with one who had not elected; that is exactly the class II.2.5 names ("Any human person who has not made the election described in paragraph 3", L50). The new route does not widen when a tie-holder may come; it removes the tie as a condition. *(C; A and B confirm in Round 3.)*
- **2nd [D].** The two routes differ in what selects the person and agree in what it costs her. II.2.3 selects by a tie to the robot population (`P2` L159–160); II.2.5 by her own election ("freely elect", L50). After the treaty date a human is therefore selected by her **willingness to pay the price**; a tie is no longer a condition, though she may still have one (ARR-3). *(A; A's Round 1 "not by a relationship" is narrowed by C in Round 3, and A adopts the correction.)*
- **3rd [D].** Two properties the pass gives the founding human stratum hold of it by necessity, and of a later human only possibly: "selected, by the admission rule, for willingness to live among robots" (`P2` L338) and "every one of them is at Mirny **because of a robot**" (`P9` L199). The one property every human who came from Upper Earth now shares, at any date, is the cost: "a Federation person has **no legal elsewhere**" (`P5` L233), and E4's "**No resident of the founding stratum can leave without losing standing**" (`P2` L340) now reaches past the founding stratum (`FTD` L77). *(C; A and B state the widening.)*
- **3rd [D, conditional on any later elector at Mirny; none is recorded].** Mirny's founding human unit is "a household or community that included a robot" (`P9` L198, quoting `P2` L214), and robots are 50.63 % of Census I (`PP9` L120; [CALC] on `PP9` L9's counts: 710,578 ÷ 1,403,549 = 0.50627 → 50.63 %). A later elector, if one came, would be the one kind of human arrival who **need not** have come because of a robot; her tie to the robot half of the city could be formed at Mirny, after arrival. No count, date, stock or person is stated. *(A's third order in C's word "need not"; A's Round 1 "did not run through a particular robot" is withdrawn by A in Round 3.)*
