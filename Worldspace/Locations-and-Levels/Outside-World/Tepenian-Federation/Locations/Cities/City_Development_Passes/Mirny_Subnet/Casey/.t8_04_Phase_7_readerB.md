## 7c · Knowledge and transmission

**P7B-16 · How one learns what Casey does: by wintering, under someone who has.** [Originated; both bodies]

- →1 At Casey the curriculum is the year: the closing, the squeak, the store's draw-down, the morning reading, the charge margin, the exposure clock.
- →2 The teacher is whoever has wintered, and for a robot the mentor bond is canon's own form; the certificate, if any, is having come through (P5-27).
- →3 **What Casey teaches cannot be taught in the season it is needed,** because in the shut half nobody new can arrive by sea to learn it, and in the open half there is no winter to learn from. - →2 Because none is felt, each must be known; Casey's knowledge culture is a culture of thresholds held as knowledge (P6-4 →3).
- →3 **Casey's records hold the station and none of these** (P6-18 →3): the knowledge that keeps people whole is the unwritten part. *Records administration at its breadth:* the cargo count, the store's count, the road reports, the plant records and the inherited station records are one stewardship sector, and the inherited records are a small, scoped sliver of it (`00b` L212-215, L228-236); who keeps any of them is in no admitted line.

## 7d · Private life, minorities and counterculture

*Canon check first (spine L928): no counterculture or faction is sited at Casey (orchestrator-verified, 2026-11-15). None is created (RF-7). What follows derives from Casey's specific pressure and names dispositions and populations, never factions.*

**P7B-21 · What Casey requires of everyone, and who cannot or will not give it.**
- *The requirement:* be in the warm at the day's end (robots, every night of every month); know that mildness is not safety; lay in before April; accept being held if late; keep the stop in the bright weeks.
- *Who cannot:* the robot away from heat when the night or the blow comes; the household that cannot lay in a season at once (P4-11, conditional); the newcomer in her first shut half; the human who needs darkness in a household that keeps no schedule (P4-46).

- →3 **Those who cannot give it are mostly the uncounted and the newly arrived; those who will not are mostly the robots who take their owed hours in the light.** Neither is a group; each is a condition a person is in.

## Sanction proportionality

**P7B-25 · What an exclusion costs at Casey, priced by month, by route and by body.** (spine L942-947; `00d` L169-173; Frame L239-242)
- *Robot, any month:* excluded from heated rest, she cannot recharge without permanent capacity loss (Frame L272), every month's average low being below freezing (spec L132-143); safe cold charging runs at 5-10% of capacity (P4-15). One night of exclusion is a permanent injury; a season is a progressive one.
- *Human:* exposure on a 10-to-30-minute clock for uncovered skin on an ordinary winter morning, low risk on an average January night (P4-7, a present-day standard; canon states no human threshold, S2-69 (f)).
- *Leaving, by route and month:* by sea only from about November to March, a voyage of about 10 to 11 days (Ports L218 as P5-18 carries it); from about April to October, not at all; by road only if PA-5 holds and the weather allows, and the nearest road neighbor is at least about 438 km away (P5-1). A robot cut off from heat on a road leg must keep moving: "A robot is safer moving than resting" (Frame L273).
- *Comparables* [present-day]: a remote archipelago may expel residents who cannot support themselves, and has no social services (sourced, P7B-W1, P7B-W6), but expulsion there is a ticket to a survivable mainland; a village with no jail banishes people convicted of serious violence, by council vote, men of the community telling the person to leave (sourced, P7B-W7), again into a country one can survive in. *Divergence:* at Casey from April to October there is nowhere to send anyone; the mechanism (exclusion as a community's whole sanction) cannot be taken without its price.
- →1 **At Casey an exclusion in the shut half is, for a robot, a sentence to permanent injury and, for a human, a sentence to exposure, for about seven months.**
- →2 No offense written in Casey's material (setting out in doubtful weather, refusing a stranger heat, missing the last ship, skipping the stop) comes near that price; Casey would not pay it, and its contract, owed to whoever is here, forbids it in substance.
- →3 So **Casey's proportionate sanctions are inside the warm:** a smaller room, a shorter hearing, a lost turn at the store, a word at the door (`00d` L167-168). Exclusion is available only in the open half, and only by sea, so any expulsion-shaped rule at Casey would exist five months a year. Nothing here writes a sanction (criminal justice `TBD`); this is the price any later system must meet.

**P7B-26 · Cutting the flow, priced.** [PA-5]
- At Casey the flow cut first under shortfall (P7B-13) would be robot travelers refused recharge in the shut half, with the nearest road neighbor at least about 438 km away and the inland terminus at least about 1,084 km up a climb of more than 3 km (P5-1, P5-10).
- →3 **Cutting the flow at Casey is not routing traffic elsewhere, as in the comparable; for a robot traveler it is a choice between permanent loss at Casey and a journey she may not finish.** The contract's literalists (P7B-22 item 3) are pricing it correctly.
