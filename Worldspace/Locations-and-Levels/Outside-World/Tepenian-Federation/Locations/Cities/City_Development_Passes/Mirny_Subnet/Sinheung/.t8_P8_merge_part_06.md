## 7. Limits

- **No speaker counts.** Every share is a national-origin share (LOG L1669 (5)). Who actually speaks or follows
  Korean, and how well, is inferred from the ruling's definition, not measured. Where Korean is used, who does not
  follow it and how many arrivals there are cannot be shown; these are distributional questions at Band 5, deferred
  by DR-4.
- **The nation-to-language mapping is recalled and unverified** (LOG L1669 (5)). Every language-group figure inherits
  that limit. The bloc figures are used only as the ruling uses them, to set the tier, never as etymology.
- **Files outside every reader's list.** The pass's Phase 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9 and 10 files, Step 5, the Founding
  Register, the local-culture file named at Phase 8 L449, the Standard's canon source and Sets L75-77 were not read.
  Lines from them are quoted second-hand from Phase 8 or not at all. Pass locations outside Phase 8 that may repeat
  8f's language claims cannot be listed.
- **The pooled-region sums use the readers' own groupings,** not the pass's Phase 2 memberships.
- **The robots' share of the threshold is unknown.** The 67.79% figure is the whole workforce's; no line gives
  fabrication's own robot share.
- **One location, no comparison.** Whether following Korean marks a person as from here, seen from anywhere else,
  cannot be settled in a piece that may not compare.
- **The surviving processes are not re-derived.** Which origin processes, and so which method-words, survive is a
  food and craft question (8a, 8c) that this piece does not re-derive.
- **No web research was run.** Every real-world statement is a research question, not a fact.
- **The relation between Hawaiian, later Tepenian and DR-85's pidgin core is not ruled.** The Japanese and English
  caution is conditional on it.
- **Scope.** This piece re-derives F.1 and F.2. F.3, F.4 and 8a are touched only where the questions required, and
  are not re-derived. Every recommended answer in section 5 is PROVISIONAL, and nothing here is applied.
