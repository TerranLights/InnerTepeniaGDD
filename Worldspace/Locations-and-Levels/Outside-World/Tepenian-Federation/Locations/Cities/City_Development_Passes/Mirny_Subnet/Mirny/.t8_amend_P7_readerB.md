## The sanction check, re-run

`SPN3` L942-947: before writing any exclusion or penalty, price it in local physics and ask whether the place would pay that price for that offense; never scale one person's temperament into a civic sanction (`00d` L166-173).

**Is guilt a sanction under the check? No.** A sanction, as the check uses the word, is an exclusion or penalty *imposed* for an offense. The ruling's guilt is "deep and immense personal guilt" (the word that matters is *personal*) for not going when at "a foremost position", and the duty is "not as a result of any sort of 'legal obligation'" (`DRL` L1116). **No one imposes it, no procedure delivers it, no record of it is admitted, and no one can lift it by authority.** It is a cost the norm carries *inside* the person — in `00d`'s own phrase for its corrected example, "a failure mode with no opponent in it" (`00d` L176). It is also exactly what `DR-55` calls an outcome rather than a punishment, in another national ruling: "every choice has outcomes" (`DRL` L1098) [D, linking two national rulings; nothing local is derived].

**What it costs each body.** **Physically: nothing, for either body.** *Human:* a guilt carried for a lifetime, paced by senescence. *Robot:* a guilt carried "for as long as the body lasts" (`AMD6` L287, reserved), in a body with no senescence (`RP` L104-105) and a mind that is "fully conscious and sentient" (`RP` L340). Whether a long-carried guilt is a hazard to a gel brain is **not in any admitted line** and is not derived (the overwork mechanism at `RP` L344-348 is flagged, not ruled, and concerns work, not guilt).

**The check, turned on the duty instead [D].** The thing with a physical price is now the *going*, not a penalty: a crossing in conditions "dangerous regardless of cold protection" (`P4` L115) — exposure for a human, charge and possibly permanent capacity for a robot (`P3` L198). Would Mirny ask that of a person? **The duty's own definition prices it:** it falls on "whoever's most strategically able to address a weather hazard" (`DRL` L1431), and someone for whom the crossing is beyond survival is not able to address the hazard. **So the guilt attaches, by the ruling's words, only where going was within ability — proportionate by construction.** The edge where the storm leaves no one able is `AMD6` AF6-1, reserved.

**What remains of "one sanction that exists is a reading with no one doing it" (`P7` L494).** It **splits, and changes direction**:
1. **The old outward reading** — "each injury is read as the injured person's carelessness" (quoted at `P7` L495 [CORR via `P7`]) — is the first generation's finding. After it, how a crossing gone wrong is read is **not ruled** (AR-15, AR-24). It may persist as an inherited habit of speech, or not (`AMD6` L127, conditional). It is no longer written as standing.
2. **The ruled cost is an inward reading with no one doing it but the person herself.** That it "needs no executioner" stands. That "it also cannot be appealed" (`P7` L495) stands in a new sense: there is no one to appeal *to*, because no one imposed it; whether anything relieves it — a word, a practice — is not admitted (R-67).
3. **The direction flips [D].** The old reading fell on the one who **went** and was hurt; the ruled cost falls on the one who **did not go** when most able. Under the old account a restorer risked blame; under the corrected one, the person who stays back risks guilt. **The cost structure now favors going** — which is the duty's purpose, and the root of AF7-1's over-response risk.

**P7-38, row 10, proposed rewording:** *| 10 | The conscience — guilt for not going when most able | none physical; carried for a lifetime | none physical; carried as long as the body lasts | exists by ruling, imposed by no one | **not a sanction**: an unimposed cost (section 7) |*; the old blame row moves to an Act 1 note. **Rows 1-9 stand** (physics unchanged; row 5's "a keeper may hand off and leave" now rests on `DRL` L1116 for both bodies, not on Law 1 alone). **The `00d` byproduct (6) in L521** becomes: *guilt reaching someone who could not have gone, or who decides afterward she was the one* (reserved, AF6-1).
**Nothing is derived** of a court, penalty, rota, rule or enforcement body. **Criminal justice at Mirny: TBD** (`H42`; and `DR-55` defers Q-71-Q-73 to research on the establishing peoples, `DRL` L1101).
