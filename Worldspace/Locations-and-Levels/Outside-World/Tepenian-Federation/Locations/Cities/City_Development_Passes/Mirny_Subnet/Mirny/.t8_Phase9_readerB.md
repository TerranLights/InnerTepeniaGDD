### P9-1 · The general answer: two bodies at near parity, meeting one site, one record and one relay without translation *[ADM + CORR + CALC]* · both bodies · Act: both
- *General first (`00b`).* A resident of Mirny is a robot or a human at near-equal odds (50.73 / 49.27 [CALC]), clothed for the wind in the same outer register — "**Two bodies, one coat.**" (`P3` L218) — keeping the shared stop (`RP` L336, national), deciding each crossing alone (`P6` P6-1 [CORR]). ### P9-2 · THE INFLECTION — the first sky sorts Mirny's people more than the body does *[D on CORR + IND]* · both bodies · **Act-sensitive** · credit: the place's own minority named in `P7` L465 and `P8` L754, carried to a population structure here
- *1st.* The site's one standing demand cannot be inherited: no stock brought judgment of this wind and no teacher held it (`SPN` §8 [CORR]); the cues are honest and unread (`RES` §1 [CORR]). So competence with the place is acquired here, by exposure, in either body: a human by childhood if born here, a robot or an adult arrival by adult immersion (`P2` P2-3 [CORR]; `DR-11`) — **written as a fork, never as a skill level**: *"nothing says a born-here human reads the cue better than a built-here robot, or the reverse"* (`P4` L111 [CORR]).
- *2nd.* What separates residents is therefore **where the reading began**, not how good it is. A person whose first sky is Mirny's has no earlier sky to misread it by; a person who arrives carries another place's habits — the thermometer, a clear sky as reassurance — into a place where *"the first sign of the dangerous wind can read as an improvement"* (`P3` L172 [CORR]). The files already name the result from two directions: *"The outsider Mirny is built to notice is the one who misreads the wind, not the one from elsewhere"* (`P5` L237 [CORR]), and *"residents born or built here never cross into membership: they start inside"* (`P5` L396 [CORR]). Both sides of that line hold both bodies: born-here humans and built-here robots on one side; the founding stratum of both bodies, later arrivals and visitors on the other.
- *3rd [D].* **So the category that matters more than kind is itself distributed unevenly across the kinds, and the distribution moves with the Acts** (P9-3). The robot half can stand on both sides at once — robots built here and robots who arrived in the founding cohort, side by side in one body-kind — while the human half, as far as the admitted routes go, lies on one side after the founders die. **The line between beginning here and beginning elsewhere is a line inside the robot half more than a line between the two halves.**
- *Limits.* Not a rank, not a test, not an exclusion: nobody fails out (`P7` P7-31 [CORR]); no sanction is writable (`P7` P7-38). The category is the files' own, but **its size on either side is unreadable** (`DR-4`). It does not touch identity's seat — for robots that is city-locality (`RU13`), and an arrival who has lived the same struggles at Mirny is of Mirny by that canon; the first sky marks how her reading began, not whether she belongs.
- **Dual tag:** genuinely emergent · deep.
