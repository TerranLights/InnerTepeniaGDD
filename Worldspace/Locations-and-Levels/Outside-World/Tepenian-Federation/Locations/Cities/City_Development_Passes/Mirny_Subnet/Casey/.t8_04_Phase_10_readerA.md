### 8.1 Named places and landmarks

**P10A-1 · The bare crests and the white hollows [WN].** The garnet-bearing gneiss knolls (up to about 40 m) are kept bare by the wind; the hollows between them stay filled white (P3-2, P3-4; S3-29). [Determined; G2.4]
- →1 Bare rock is the one ground at Casey that does not have to be cleared, so foot paths and the lightest structures prefer the crests.
