# B · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ RESEARCH — **Process B, aimed at the two registers the pass left unspent**

## B.1 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE CONSERVATION VOCABULARY — **and it closes a gap Phase 8 flagged**

| Real-world finding | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ **Conservators use *"specialized terminology in condition and treatment reports **which is not defined in general dictionaries**"*** — **and *"a completely standardized vocabulary does not yet exist in the field"*** | **the register is real, occupational, and unstandardized** |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **PATINA is *"stable surface coloration and **AGE-CONSISTENT WEAR PATTERNS**, often accompanied by preserved original finishes"*** | ## **the central diagnostic** |
| **WEAR — *"gradual deterioration by USE or any continued process"*** vs **CORROSION — *"deterioration due to CHEMICAL processes"*** | ⭐ **two degradations, distinguished by cause, not by degree** |
| **Some corrosion products are PROTECTIVE** | *the patina again* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ DIVERGENCE 1 — **an occupational jargon has become the vernacular**
> **In the sources the register is a SPECIALIST one, held by conservators, and it is not standardized.**
> ⭐⭐ **Here everyone does the work** *(Ph 7's widened sector; Ph 4's general population)*, **so:**
> ## ***SHIRAYUKI IS A CITY WHERE A CONDITION-REPORT VOCABULARY IS ORDINARY SPEECH.***
> ⭐ **Which is exactly what Ph 8 §F claimed — *"distinctions ordinary residents make effortlessly and
> outsiders cannot hear"* — and it is now sourced as a real property of this kind of work rather than asserted.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ DIVERGENCE 2 — **the central diagnostic is UNAVAILABLE HERE, and its replacement is the city's whole proposition**
> **The conservator's organizing concept is *AGE-CONSISTENT wear*: does the wear match the age?**
> ⛔ ***Shirayuki cannot ask that question.*** **Nothing here is old** *(Ph 6 §A)* — **`87–91%` is rebuilt on a
> cycle, the air abrades everything on a clock, and the one genuinely old stratum predates the city and is not
> theirs.** **There is no age for wear to be consistent WITH.**
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **SO THE QUESTION BECOMES: DOES THE WEAR MATCH THE *CARE*?**
> ***ATTENTION-CONSISTENCY replaces AGE-CONSISTENCY, and that is not a metaphor — it is the operational form
> of "nothing here is old; things are only well-kept."***
> ⭐⭐ **The keptness vocabulary's organizing concept is therefore DERIVED, not invented:** *the city's central
> distinction is between wear that is consistent with the attention a thing has had, and wear that is not.*
> ⭐ **And Ph 8's "wear that was accepted vs wear that was missed" turns out to be exactly that distinction,
> reached independently.**

⛔ **NO TERMS MINTED.** *The CONCEPT is derived; the words remain the developer's to name.*

## B.2 · ⭐⭐⭐ PRESERVATION — **and which preservative is free decides the kitchen**

**The four traditional registers: *temperature control · curing (drying, salting, smoking) · fermentation ·
chemical preservation*.** ⭐ **Set each against this city's own physical facts:**

| Register | Here |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **DRYING** | ***Nearly free.*** *This is a **cold desert** — `~149 mm/yr`, sub-zero means for ten months. The air does the work* |
| ⭐⭐ **FREEZING** | ***Free for ten months.*** *Ambient, not powered* |
| ⛔⛔ **SALTING / BRINE** | ***SHIPPED.*** **This city has no shore** *(Ph 5 §0)* — **so salt arrives through the four-month window, in a queue it has no standing in, and up the `8 km` haul** |
| ⚠ **FERMENTATION** | **Requires managed warmth.** ⭐ *So it is an INDOOR, deliberate activity rather than an ambient one* |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE ONE PRESERVATION INPUT THIS CITY CANNOT MAKE ITSELF IS THE ONE THAT ARRIVES ON THE BOAT IT DOES NOT CONTROL.**
> ***A drying-and-freezing kitchen rather than a salting one*** — **with salt a marked, valued, carefully-spent
> import rather than a staple gesture.** ⭐ **The deferral axis, expressed in a spice rack.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND IT NAMES THE UNWRITTEN HALF OF THE YEAR
> **`04_Phase_04` §4b left a large hole: *the famous thing is a seasonal activity, and the city is not doing it
> for most of the year.*** ⭐ **Fermentation needs warmth; the interior is where warmth is; and the interior is
> where most of the year is spent.**
> ## ***WINTER IS THE KEEPING SEASON. SUMMER IS THE TENDING SEASON. TWO HALVES, ONE VERB.***
> ⭐⭐ **The indoor months are not empty — they are when the food is put up, the garments are mended, the
> inherited rooms are worked on, and the year's damage is repaired out of the weather.**
> ⭐⭐⭐ **Third convergence with the civic proposition:** *Ph 8 found the food and the proposition are the same
> sentence.* ***Now the CALENDAR is too — the city spends half its year literally keeping things.***
