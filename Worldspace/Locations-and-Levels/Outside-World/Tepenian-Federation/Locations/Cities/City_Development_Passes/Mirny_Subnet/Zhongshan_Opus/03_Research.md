across three sweeps — the convergence `LAW 0-R` predicts ("a three-pick convergence was invisible until the
third") but sharper, because these are not three readings of one pick, they are four different real-world
mechanisms independently describing the same site.

| Sweep | Instrument | What it says about who decides |
|---|---|---|

# 3.3 · CANNOT RETAIN PRECIPITATION — a deficit that bills itself twice, and a third time on the worst day

Three source facts, read together, produce a finding none of them states alone (the log's own "Derivation B,"
labeled as a derivation rather than a source fact, and reproduced here because it survives a fresh check):

1. **Salt is not removed by wind, only by rain, and this site gets almost none.** *"In Antarctica salt
   deposition is a particular problem and in the absence of rain these salt deposits are not washed off."*
   Crystallization stress runs `10–20 MPa`; hydration expansion up to `300%`.
2. **Water is the scarce resource.** At the real Davis station (same regional climate), a small saline tarn
   feeds `~70,000 L/24h` via microfiltration and reverse osmosis, and *"water restrictions are… a constant
   feature of station life."*
3. **The lake system is not a uniform reserve — it is dozens of independent clocks.** Retention times across
   the oasis's own lakes span an entire order of magnitude on the *same ~40 km²*: Lake Stepped under two
   years, Sarah Tarn twenty-one to twenty-two. Epiglacial lakes exchange fast, on surface runoff; endorheic
   landlocked lakes hold their water for decades, losing it mainly to evaporation.

**Fusion:** the only way to prevent salt damage is to spend the scarcest resource washing it off — the deficit
bills itself once as thirst and once as decay, from the same missing rain. **And it bills a third time on the
day something burns**, per `3.2`'s own source: the standard Antarctic firefighting doctrine is dry chemicals,
not water, precisely because *"if there is a fire there is unlikely to be very much liquid water around to
fight it with."* Handed to Phase 3 (Surface & Texture) and Phase 8 (Making) — a maintenance trade whose entire
job is a losing arithmetic against a resource it is also rationed on, and a fire-response culture built around
the assumption that water will not be the tool.

**And the lake-clock finding is its own texture source, independent of the deficit:** a city built around
dozens of water bodies that do not behave alike, some flushing in under two years and others holding water for
a generation, is not one water supply — it is a *portfolio* of them, aging at different rates, in one small
footprint. Handed to Phase 3 (neighborhoods differentiated by which lake feeds them, and how old that lake's
water genuinely is) and Phase 7 (a water-quality inspection regime that cannot treat the oasis as one source).

# 3.4 · CANNOT LAND SMALL BOATS — corroborated at source, and it reframes the network-position conflict

The ASMA states outright: *"no anchorages or barge landings are designated… vessels usually anchor
approximately 5 nm offshore,"* and small-boat access to eastern Broknes is *"difficult and sometimes impossible
due to ice debris up to hundreds of meters off shore, blown by the prevailing north-easterly winds. Helicopters
are therefore the only reliable means."* This independently corroborates §15's "not a trade hub" from a wholly
different register — physical rather than economic — which is exactly `02` §5.3's both-are-true shape landing
a third time (after G3 and G5) on the same underlying structural fact.

⚠ **CORRECTED 2026-09-10, per developer-directed research (`Cities/Research_Logs/Zhongshan_Research_Log.md`
Session 4).** The ASMA's north-easterly wind is the SECONDARY regime — an occasional, cyclonic-storm-driven
wind, responsible for over 80% of blowing-snow events but a minority of the station's actual wind-hours. The
PRIMARY wind, per Zhongshan's own station-specific numerical study (Haiyang Xuebao, 2016), is a constant,
ground-hugging katabatic flow from the SOUTHEAST. **So the boat-landing hazard is not a daily condition — it is
episodic, tied specifically to the NE storm systems**, not to the wind that blows here every day. This sharpens
rather than weakens the finding: a harbor that is unusable roughly when a storm arrives, and reliably usable
otherwise, is a different — and more specific — deficit than a harbor blocked by the site's own constant
weather.

**And the real fast-ice cycle adds a texture layer §2's profile didn't have:** fast ice forms late February,
reaches full coverage April–October (maximum extent in September, up to 100 km offshore), and the bay is
largely ice-free only January–March. **The resupply ship reaches the outer ice edge once a year, late
November/early December.** A settlement whose sea door is open roughly a quarter of the year, and whose one
scheduled maritime arrival is annual rather than routine, does not run its calendar around shipping — it runs
around the one window. Handed to Phase 5 (Relation & Geometry): the harbor named in the spec (`00_Frame.md`
§0.1c, `I-6`) is real, but its usable season is short, which changes what "having a harbor" means for this
city's actual rhythm.

# 3.5 · NOT A TRADE HUB — the category this city IS is a real, named thing

Query 73 found what Query 29 (a weak, thin parallel to a Haifa tech-park junction) did not: a genuine,
published real-world category of city built specifically around being a hub for something other than trade.
**"Antarctic gateway cities"** — five real cities (Hobart, Christchurch, Punta Arenas, Ushuaia, Cape Town) —
*"formally recognized"* as the coordination points through which *"all significant engagement with the South
Polar region is co-ordinated,"* described in the literature as *"important scientific hubs where the
diplomatic and logistical co-operation… take place."* They even signed a Statement of Intent with each other
in 2009, and a published thesis specifically asks what an "Antarctic identity" for a gateway city would even
be — a question this city's own capability profile (G3: technical/scientific identity, explicitly not
commercial) already sits inside.

**Per the one-sentence test and the ONE LOCATION law**, the admissible half of this pick is that the
*category* exists and that gateway status is coordinated peer-to-peer rather than won competitively — **not**
which real gateway city "wins," which would be a ranking this pass is forbidden from making. Fusion: this
city's own version of gateway status runs on people and expertise leaving, not goods arriving — consistent
with `16_Per_City_Three_Tier_Run.md` §18's own finding that the export is engineers and researchers, not
cargo. Handed to Phase 5c and Phase 7b, which already inherited the "coordination forum with no defined
superior" shape from `04_Phase_02`'s reading of the Falkland Treaty's own Article III.1.

# 3.6 · NO MAJORITY — what fills a diffuse slot is not a shared origin, it is a shared date

Already covered in full at `3.2` — Midwinter Day is the finding for this deficit specifically, not only for
the arbitration one. `02_Spine.md` names the address `diffuse`: no counterparty, no grievance ever forms. The
real record supplies what a diffuse population shares instead of an origin: **one universal date, marked by
exchange rather than by any single nation's own calendar.** And a second, corroborating fact sharpens the
texture rather than the mechanism: real Antarctic winter-team research (Query 51, PNAS wearable-sensor study)
finds *"multicultural teams fragment into national subgroups over time"* under prolonged physical confinement
— not from isolation itself, but from proximity. **So the shared observance and the national fragmentation
are not a contradiction; they are the same population doing two different things at two different scales** —
`02` §5.3's both-are-true test again, this time between two G8-adjacent findings rather than two generators.
Handed to Phase 2 (already carries the eighteen-nation composition) and Phase 6.

**And the language-culture split found at Query 66 belongs to the same shape:** *"while English serves as the
inter-station lingua franca, within individual stations, native languages dominate daily operations."* The
shared layer and the domestic layer run on different registers entirely — a structural fact, not a national
one (per `No_National_Stereotypes.md`'s sequencing rule, the *structure* is admissible, which specific nation
speaks what inside a given household is not, and the log itself already flagged and dropped that half).

## Austin — the "faded from memory" mechanism

Barton Creek Greenbelt is central to Austin's identity, yet *"the story of how it was preserved has largely
faded from public memory"* — a direct hit on a deficit-shaped finding: a defended thing whose defense is no
longer known even to the people benefiting from it. *"Keep Austin Weird"* itself was coined on a radio pledge
drive to name a quality that was *eroding with nobody deciding to erode it* — the same shape as this city's own
governance-by-expectation finding (§3.2), applied to identity rather than to dispute resolution. At Phase 8
depth: local slang built around moving between food stalls sampling as you go ("truckin'"), and humor that
trends self-aware and punny about the city's own quirks — both candidates already logged, not yet spent.

## Yekaterinburg — architectural monoculture arrived at without anyone choosing it

The world's highest concentration of Constructivist architecture (140+ structures) exists because an enormous
amount was built in one narrow window while one approach was simply the obvious one — nobody chose a style, a
moment produced one. Direct structural fusion for a city whose own capability profile shows things arriving and
not remaining (`02_Spine.md` §2.3): a monoculture that nobody designed is the built-environment version of the
same disposition. At Phase 8 depth: **Posikunchiki**, a genuine Ural-regional fusion dish — a hybrid of a fried
patty and a dumpling, born of local necessity rather than importing either form whole — is the log's own
proposed model for this city's general-population cuisine finding, and a quarterly rotating designer market
(fashion, crafts, food, never the same lineup twice) feeds a candidate crafts finding.

# 3.9 · A DEAD END, LOGGED BECAUSE IT KILLED A HYPOTHESIS RATHER THAN BUILT ONE

The log's Query 42 tried to find a real culture with no arbitration mechanism *because it never had scarcity* —
to support a proposed reading that this city's own missing arbitration follows from its uncontested founding.

the error: **the "never needed to" causal story for this city's own arbitration deficit is not supported by
the real-world record, and Phase 7 must find a cause that is not that one.** `3.2`'s convergence (governance by
expectation, sourced four ways) stands independently and does not depend on the killed hypothesis.
