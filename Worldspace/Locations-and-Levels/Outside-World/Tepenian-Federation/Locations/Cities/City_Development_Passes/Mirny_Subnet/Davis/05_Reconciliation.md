# 1 · ⭐⭐⭐ THE FINDING THAT SURVIVED BY CHANGING ITS SOURCE

⭐⭐⭐ **The finding does not need it.** **`Local_Cultures` §4, Tier 3 and admissible, states the same thing as

> ***"The Vestfold Hills' varied water bodies — freshwater lakes, saltwater lakes, fjord inlets — each freeze,
> thaw, and open on their own separate schedules… the ice conditions of one lake say nothing reliable about
> another, and residents track multiple, distinct local environmental calendars rather than one unified
> seasonal clock."***

> # ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **A RECORD IS TRUE BECAUSE IT WAS MADE AT THE TIME — AND TWO RECORDS MADE AT THE TIME MAY DISAGREE WITHOUT EITHER BEING FALSE.**

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Phase 6 keeps** | *What was written down when it happened is true; what was not written down cannot be said* |
| ⭐⭐ **Phase 6 gains** | ***Truth here is not singular.*** **Multiple concurrent true records, and no procedure for collapsing them into one** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The consequence** | **A city whose instrument of agreement is the record, and whose record does not resolve, is a city that AGREES ON ENTRIES WITHOUT AGREEING ON CONCLUSIONS.** ⭐ *That is why "show me the entry" ends arguments here and does not settle them* |

clocks — produces the epistemology continuously, rather than one remembered incident having taught it.**
