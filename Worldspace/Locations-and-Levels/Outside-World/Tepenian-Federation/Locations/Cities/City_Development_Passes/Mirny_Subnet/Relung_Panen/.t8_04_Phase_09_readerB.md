## The differentiation axis

**Membership by keeping, not by kind: what makes a person of this place, human or robot, is the year kept here (for a human, one full water year lived through; for a robot, a regular warm room to charge in), and that counts for more in daily life than which kind she is or which row she came from.**

*(Not checked against any other city. Ground: Phase 5 P5-24, Phase 6's standard "what we live on here was made, and it stays only as long as it is kept" (P6-28), and P9B-104 below. PROPOSED, conditional as P5-24 is.)*

## The lens decision

`03` L1062–1067 asks what each population organizes its culture around: origin, function, the built environment, a shared constraint or a shared exclusion.

- **Humans (492,215, Census I, spec L20): a shared constraint first, origin second and inside the household.** The constraint is the keeping: the dark, the dry path, the drains and the warm rooms are made and stay only while they are kept (Phase 6 P6-28; Phase 4's made stopping hour). Origin is real ancestry, and the height sits in the Census I era, nearer Act 1 (`DEC-583`), so people are still largely "X who live in Antarctica" (runbook L2234–2238); but no row reaches 13% (largest 12.86%, spec L59; Phase 2 P2-5) and every row is a minority (Phase 7 P7-44), so origin organizes households and small institutions, never the city (Step 3 S3-23, forms only; picks L152, L153, L194, L195 as sources, never specifications). **Not function:** the industry model spreads work across the full baseline (P9B-109), so no single trade is a human population's identity. **Not exclusion:** none is written (Phase 6 P6-27).
- **Robots (507,856, 50.78% of Census I, spec L20; datasheet L15): city-locality, organized around a shared constraint.** Canon seats a robot's identity in her city and subnet (Ch.13 L24–25), weighs city before Build before Gen/Mark (Ch.19), and makes the robot inheritance mediated through the local culture rather than direct (runbook L2116–2121). The constraint is the cold that makes a robot's nightly charge a question of which warm room, every month of the year (Phase 3 P3-25; Phase 4 P4-6; Phase 6 P6-8), and the reserve she keeps ahead of need (P6-8).
- **The anchor for the robot culture, and its ground:** the founding population the Founding Register names, **Indonesia and Malaysia jointly, Indonesia leading as a state and Malaysia in support** (Register L65; `DR-61`), standing on geography and access (Register L11; `DR-19`), never on any station's operator. **What the anchor is and is not:** it is the local culture the founders started, through which a robot's inheritance passes (runbook L2116–2121); it is not an ancestry assigned to the robot half. The founding-wave rows are 15.71% of the live table (Phase 2 L127, rows only), and a robot built here carries only the general ancestry principle and her own individual form (`DEC-588`; `DR-136` item 10; `DR-137` item 7): a robot who comes online in mixed company is of the place; one who develops inside one group is of that group. Nothing is read from either founding nation (the stereotype law; runbook L2232–2238 for the divergence operator). The founding "why" is not derived (`DEC-577`).
- **Any category that matters more than kind:** yes, on these lines: **whether a person keeps the year here** (P9B-104). Kind sets what the year costs her (two tables, two mortalities, two kinds of night); keeping sets whether she is of the place.
- **Representation categories 2–4 (anthros, elves, aliens):** none present; opened, n/a (representation file L10–14, L26–27).
