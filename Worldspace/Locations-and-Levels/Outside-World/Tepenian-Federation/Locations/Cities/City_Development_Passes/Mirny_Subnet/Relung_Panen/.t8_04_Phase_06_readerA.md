## The differentiation axis

**Nothing here keeps itself.**

*What the words carry.* On this place's own lines the things that sustain life were made, not found, and each lasts only while it is kept. The ground: "There was no pre-existing farmland or soil; the founders had to establish their own." (spec L222; rulings L1252), and made ground on bare rock "is not made once but kept" (amendment G7-C2, L189). The night: no polar night, and from November to January the sky gives about 4.6, about 0 and about 3.0 h of dark (spec L151–156, L179–190; Phase 4 P4-4), so the shared stop is made by hand, curtain and closing hour (S3-28; Phase 4 axis). The warmth: no energy from the site (spec L261), heat bought against 3,987 freezing degree-days (`Climate.md` via spine G2-C1), and for half the city a warm charge in every month (Phase 4 P4-6 `[computed, REQ-52]`). **Peer-free test, *could this be true anywhere?*** No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: a ground that did not exist until people made it from imported matter and local rock and that reverts to bare rock if not kept (L222; G7-C2); a sky that does not end the day for three months yet gives under six hours of light for three (L179–190); and a site that gives no energy, so warmth and the lit harvest are bought (L261; `DR-96` item 4). Remove the made ground and the reversion target is not bare rock; give the site a polar night or a soil and the night or the ground keeps itself; give it energy and the warmth does. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** upkeep in general; cold met by clothing; the shared night itself (national, physiology L336–338); leisure owed to nobody (national); sacred ossuaries (national). The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.
