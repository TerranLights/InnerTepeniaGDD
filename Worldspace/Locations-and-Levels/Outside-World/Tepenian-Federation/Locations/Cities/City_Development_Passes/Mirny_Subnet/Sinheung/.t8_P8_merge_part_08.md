### 9.3 Every conflict, and how it was decided

Eleven conflicts remained after Round 3: nine resolved to the narrower reading and two sent to the developer. A
reader's withdrawal was treated as evidence for the narrower reading, never as a tie-break, and no vote was counted.

1. **The unit of the middle tier** ({{REF:F:composition}}; {{REF:K:mid_clusters}}). A: the clusters are no longer
   the middle tier, so the composition sentence is killed by evidence. C: that mixes a tier of nations with pooled
   regions. B: no longer the second tier in the language sense. Resolved to the narrower reading: each unit's order
   stated, no middle tier claimed across units, the premise narrowed rather than killed.
2. **What a robot's origin row records** ({{REF:F:identity_marker}}). C: a census convention. A: spec L132 says only
   how the figures were made, and RP L426 ties a robot's vernacular to her building community. Resolved to the
   narrower reading: no genetic descent; what the row records is not stated.
3. **Whether the evening hour is one shared setting** ({{REF:F:daily_settings}}). A and B: it gathers the whole
   city (B: the seconds market too). C: the same hour is not the same room. Resolved to the narrower reading: a time
   shared by the whole city, not shown to be one setting.
4. **The strength of the least-divergent line** ({{REF:F:least_divergent}}; {{REF:K:least_divergent_claim}};
   {{REF:D:l412_least_divergent}}). B: `null`. A and C: `conditional`. Resolved to the narrower reading: `null` as a
   finding, held pending research.
5. **Whether the grading words must exist in the dominant language** ({{REF:F:grading_words}};
   {{REF:D:l425_grading}}). A and C (after C's W2): at least in the dominant language. B (after its withdrawals 1 to
   5): Hawaiian, Korean or both, which one not settled. Resolved to the narrower reading: B's.
6. **Whether L432 claims there is no accent** ({{REF:K:no_accent_reading}}). A: an implied claim, killed for want of
   a line. B: no such claim is made. Resolved to the narrower reading: no kill recorded; the qualifier carries it.
7. **Whether "live" is killed or the clause qualified** ({{REF:K:live_marker}}). B: "live" killed by evidence for
   Korean. A and C: narrowed, with a qualifier. Resolved to the narrower reading: a qualifier.
8. **The ground of the station guard** ({{REF:F:station_rulings}}; {{REF:R:station_word}}). A (after W8) and B:
   existing rules already settle it. C (after its W1): NNS L15 covers only the chain, and the builder may be stated
   as a bare fact (NNS L160), so it is not ruled. Resolved to the narrower reading: not ruled; the action unchanged.
9. **The L429 qualifier** ({{REF:D:l429_outsider}}). B found it and withdrew it; A and C adopted it. Resolved to the
   narrower reading: written in conditional form only.
10. **The Language Module's delivery of Korean** ({{REF:Q:module_korean}}). The recommended answers differ (A and B:
    leave the channel open; C: the Module gives the dominant language and a building community may add its own).
    Sent to the developer, with A's and B's narrower answer recommended.
