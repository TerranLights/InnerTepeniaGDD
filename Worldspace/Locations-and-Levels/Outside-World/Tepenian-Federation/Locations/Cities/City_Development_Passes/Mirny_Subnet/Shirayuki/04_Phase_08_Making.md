# C · ARTS, CRAFT AND MATERIAL CULTURE

⭐ **`04_Phase_07` §7a established the sector and its widening — *the making and keeping of the made
environment*, `25%`.** **Here, only the general practice:**

- ⭐⭐ **The competence is TECHNIQUE, and it is universal rather than specialist.** *"Polish without scratching"
  is something most adults can do adequately, a minority do beautifully, and everyone can see the difference in.*
- ⭐ **Materials are chosen for how they age in abrasion** — *bronze survives by forming a protective patina;

  earned.

  completed object here. It is an object with a maintenance history***, and the history is visible.
