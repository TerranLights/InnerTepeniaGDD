# C · PROCESS C — INTER-POPULATION RELATIONS

> ⭐⭐⭐ *"Find the LOCAL INFLECTION, not the national baseline. Restating the setting's baseline is not a

## C.1 ⛔ The baseline, stated once and NOT presented as a finding

**Tepenia-wide: *"egalitarian, skewed in favor of robots"* — robots tend to hold slightly better positions, a
quiet reflection of the country being theirs first; genuine, unforced appreciation running the other way.**
⛔ **That is the national baseline. It is not Davis's finding, and it is not restated as one.**

## C.2 ⭐⭐⭐ The answer: **yes, and it is ON A LINE / NOT ON A LINE**

**Three ratified facts:**

1. **Kinship at Davis is collective research credit, not teaching lineage** — *"a robot's family is whoever she
   shares discovery-credit with"* *(Phase 7 §C.1)*.
2. **Someone who never gets onto a credit line has no kin — UNATTACHED, by an omission nobody authored**
   *(Phase 7 §C.2)*.
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **A credit line records who did the work. It does not record what they are.**

> # ⇒ ***THE CREDIT LINE IS KIND-BLIND. A HUMAN AND A ROBOT ON THE SAME LINE ARE KIN.***

⭐⭐ **And it explains, rather than merely asserts, why kind does so little work here:** **at 51.3 / 48.7, with a
kinship mechanism that sorts by line rather than by kind, the human/robot axis has almost nothing left to
divide.** ⭐ **The inter-kind question answers itself underneath the real one, exactly as the spine predicts.**

⚠⚠ **PRECISION, and it matters:** ⛔ **This does NOT claim credit-kinship erases the national skew.** **It is a
DIFFERENT AXIS, not a correction to that one.** **Whether the two interact is unwritten — `H52`.**

## C.3 ⭐⭐⭐ And the category Davis actually produces: **a group that cannot become one**

**The unattached are sorted by a mechanism that operates identically on both kinds.** ⇒ **They share no origin,
no kind, no quarter, and no moment of becoming unattached** — *it is an omission, not an event.*

> ## ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ ***DAVIS'S MOST DISTINCTIVE SOCIAL CATEGORY IS ONE THAT CANNOT ORGANIZE.***
