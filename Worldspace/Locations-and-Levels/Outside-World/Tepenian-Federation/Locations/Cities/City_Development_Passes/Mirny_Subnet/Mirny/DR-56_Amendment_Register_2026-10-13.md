## 1 · What `DR-56` changes, and what it keeps

**The corrected account.** On arrival, **nobody knew the wind** and the first generation or so had to learn it on their own. Once they had it figured out, they **taught each other and set up signifiers with a weather report/relay system**, and the people learned to live with it and to communicate dangers. Duty is communal, not legal: people of both bodies feel deep personal guilt if, being best placed, they do not go out to restore the relay (Q-69).

**What the pass said instead (the premise to retire).** That the wind has *no teacher and no instrument, ever*; that *each resident re-learns it from nothing* (`P7` P7-27 3rd, `P6` P6-13, `P9` P9-5 3rd); that *nothing is posted, said or written* (leg 4; `P10` P10-8); that a loss is booked to *the person, the weather or silence, never to the medium, so the book never learns* (`P6` P6-17); and that the Social Contract survives *exactly as long as no instrument shares the answering* (`P6` P6-18).

**What survives untouched** (it was about the founding, or about physics, or about the system):
- The **Act 1 condition**: a working station and a record of journals, logs and manuals, with no living teacher; *a tradition cannot cross the gap* (`NNS` L21; `P6` P6-2 clause a); the first generation learned the plant and the wind by doing, including by getting it wrong (`P7` P7-28).
- The **physics**: wind-dominated, heat that follows the wind (`P3` P3-2), the light-dated year, the door (`P3` P3-7), the shared stop (`P4` P4-1).
- **Nobody chose the site; the founders were assigned; fourteen stocks, no majority** (`P2`).
- **The relay as the subnet's single path, and the storm that breaks it also stops people walking** (`P5` P5-14). This is now sharper: the warning system rides the one path that the worst storm breaks.
- **Two bodies at near parity, with different prices for a misjudgment** (`P9` P9-16).
- **The keepers' duty held without compulsion** (`P6` P6-6; `P7` P7-17): `DR-56` supplies the motive (communal duty and guilt) that the pass derived structurally.

## 3 · What remains distinctive after the change (a proposed shape, for review)

A re-derivation, not a rewrite. The old finding was *a place whose people carry the one thing that can't be handed on, alone.* The corrected account keeps a different, probably stronger Mirny:
1. **A place that remembers having had to learn the wind itself.** The founding gap is real and is Mirny's (assigned exiles, a record and no teacher, nobody chose the site), and the first generation paid for it.
2. **A learned, shared, signaled wind,** with a warning system that **rides the relay, the one path that the worst storm breaks.** The danger-communication system fails in the storm it exists for, and the people who go out to restore it do so from duty and would carry guilt if they did not. That is a Mirny-specific tension; it replaces the retired "refusal" as the pass's engine.
3. **Duty without compulsion, concentrated on the keepers and restorers** (Q-69), with the cost falling differently on the two bodies (P9-16).
4. **A founding memory held in persons** that thins as the founding cohort does (P9-17), instead of a skill that is re-lost every generation.

What I cannot say from the files: how the weather report is made, who runs it, what the signifiers are, or whether it is public. Those are requests for the developer, and nothing was assumed.
