## 1 · What `DR-56`, `DR-57` and `DR-76` change, and what they keep

**The corrected account.** On arrival, nobody knew the wind, and the first generation or so had to learn it on their own. Once they had it figured out they taught each other and set up signifiers with a weather report/relay system, and the people learned to live with it and to communicate dangers. **The warnings travel on the relay (`DR-76`).** An organization of people of both bodies manages and curates the weather protocol; the protocol is public and learnable by anyone (`DR-57`). The duty to restore the relay is communal, not legal: people of both bodies feel deep personal guilt if, being best placed, they do not go out to restore it (Q-69); **"best placed" means whoever is most strategically able to address a weather hazard (`DR-76`)**.
**Not ruled:** what the warning looks like and who runs it ("don't know yet"); the signifiers (to be researched in real-world sources); the organization's name; whether residents feel a debt to the first generation (it varies person by person).

**The premise to retire.** That the wind has *no teacher and no instrument, ever*; that *each resident re-learns it from nothing*; that *nothing is posted, said or written*; that a loss is booked to *the person, the weather or silence, never to the medium, so the book never learns*; and that the Social Contract survives *exactly as long as no instrument shares the answering*.

**What survives untouched** (about the founding, physics or the system):
- the **founding gap**: a working station and a record, no living teacher, assigned exiles, nobody chose the site; a tradition cannot cross a gap (`NNS` L21);
- the **physics**: wind-dominated, the light-dated year, the door, the shared stop;
- **fourteen stocks, no majority**; two bodies at near parity with different prices for a misjudgment;
- **the relay as the subnet's single path**, and the storm that breaks it also stops people walking. Sharper now: the warnings travel on that same path (`DR-76`).
