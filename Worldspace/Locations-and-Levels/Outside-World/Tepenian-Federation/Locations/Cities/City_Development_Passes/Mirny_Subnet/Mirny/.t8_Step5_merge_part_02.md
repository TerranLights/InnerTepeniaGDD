### 3.1 · Mirny as Phases 2–10 found it

Mirny, read only through its own pass, is a Band 5 Settlement of 1,351,430 at Census I (665,901 humans, 685,529 robots; robots 50.73 %) on the Antarctic Circle, a rocky, exposed coast whose one defining condition is **wind, not cold**: mean 12.0 m/s, about 3 % calm-or-weak, snow retention about 38 %, a ground blizzard that blinds under a clear sky, and cues that are honest but that nobody at the founding had been taught to read. Its year is dated by light — no polar night, about 29 days of midnight sun, a 2.4-hour June — because its thermometer holds a plateau from May to September. Its founders (Idelsk-Uralia as sponsoring founding nation outside the country; Russia, Australia and the Sinian Federation as the exile groups; `REG` L57, `DR-46`) were placed by assignment, not choice, on the treaty's terms, among fourteen long-run stocks with China the largest and no majority, and arrived to a working station and a written record (journals, logs, manuals) that no living person had made and no living teacher could explain. Mirny is the hub of nine members, and its relay is the subnet's single routing point.

Out of three admitted conditions — an unreadable but honest wind, a record without a teacher, a relay that is the only path — Phases 2–10 derive **one unwritten compact** (Phase 6): *the medium holds; the reading is yours.* The medium nobody here authored (the inherited record and the relay) is held to the standard of not failing; the reading of the wind is each person's own, unwritten, answered for privately; so a loss is booked to the person, to "weather," or to silence, and never to the medium, and **the book never learns**. Order follows it (Phase 7: claims on the quarry, the relay and the hours with no arbiter in any admitted file; fast calls fall to whoever is there and the person answers; the nation's mandate is a need-test with no mandator; no local prohibition has an author). Making follows it (Phase 8: *prepared, not predicted* — coats keyed to the wind, a bare hand that is an indoor hand, upkeep that refills after every storm and leaves nothing to show, a vocabulary of *now* with no word for *soon*). Populations follow it (Phase 9: sorted by **formation** — formed here or arrived; a wind line anyone can cross by exposure and a memory line only arrivals carry — and ranked by neither; by Act 2 the remembering side is the founding-cohort robots). The catalog follows it (Phase 10: what stays was made by no one here or is made for others; what residents make for themselves refills; no place is named, none is formed).

Throughout, ordinary life is the general answer: a robot or a human at near-equal odds, in the same coat at the same door, keeping a shared stop indoors and crossing between interiors, with leisure owed to nobody (established for the robot half) and an empty middle of what people actually do with their hours. No faith, faction, sub-location, currency or mortuary practice is sited. **Mirny works; its shadows (the blamed door, the book that never learns, the keepers invisible while they succeed) sit at the edges.** What the pass could not fill — local goals, speech, leisure content, any faith, food, music, dress idiom, the stocks' repertoires — it left explicitly open and REQUESTED for this check. `[merged: A, B, C]`

### 3.2 · The strongest finding and what it rests on (`RB` Step 5; `01` §5.2 rule 4)

**The finding: the compact of `P6` P6-1 with its enforcement and refusal (`P6` P6-3, P6-17):** *the medium holds; the reading is yours.* It is the operational form of the spine (`SPN` §8): Mirny's site demands the one thing it could not inherit — judgment of the wind — so the city holds its shared medium to the standard of not failing and treats the wind as each person's own reading, and **the inherited record therefore never learns**. It is the strongest because it is where the spine becomes a culture, because every later phase independently re-derives it in its own layer (P7-14 and P7-15 on claims without an arbiter and the unadministrable wind call; P7-20's fast/slow calls; P8's axis and P8-12, P8-18, *prepared, not predicted*; P9-14 and P9-15, *sorted in, never up*; P10's axis and P10-6), and because Phase 6's own counterfactual (P6-2) shows its conjunction does not travel. `[A,B,C]`

**What it rests on, leg by leg:**

1. **Admitted physics (G2), durable.** Mean wind 12.0 m/s and clear-sky whiteout (`SPEC` L82); danger regardless of cold protection (`SPEC` L131); wind as the defining hazard (`SPEC` L139); the research's *the cues are honest; they are unread* (`RES` §1, [CORR]). The ice sheet will not be retconned (`01` L437–438). `[A,B,C]`
2. **Admitted founding condition (G4), durable.** Records without a living institution (`SPEC` L150, the permitted spans only; `DR-25`, `DR-26`; `NNS` L21). `[A,B,C]`
3. **Admitted network fact (G5), durable in substance.** The relay as the subnet's single routing point (`SPEC` L184); membership by census and ruling (`DR-18`; `FRM` R-3). `[A,B,C]`
4. **A reading of absence — not durable, and testable here.** That no public wind call, no rite or marker for a storm loss, and no practice of writing in the record exist at Mirny (`P4` Q-42; `P6` R-67, R-68, R-77; `P7` P7-15). Phase 6 names its own falsifier: any rite, marker, record or spoken form for an outage or a storm loss in Mirny's own culture file (`P6` L184). **This is the leg entries 46–48 could contradict; §11 reports what happened to it.** `[B]`
