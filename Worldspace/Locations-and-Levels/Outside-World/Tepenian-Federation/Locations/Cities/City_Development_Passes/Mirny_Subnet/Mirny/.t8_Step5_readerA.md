## Mirny as Phases 2-10 found it

*(Written before entry 46 was opened; copied unchanged from the READING LOG, where it sits between entries 45 and 46.)*

Mirny, read only through its own pass, is a Band 5 settlement of 1,351,430 at Census I, robots 50.73 %, sitting on the Antarctic Circle on a rocky, exposed coast where the defining condition is wind and not cold: no polar night, about 29 days of midnight sun, a winter that holds flat for five months, moving snow at the ankle on an ordinary day, and cues that are honest but that nobody at the founding had been taught to read. Out of those three admitted conditions (an unreadable but honest wind, a record without a teacher, and a relay that is the subnet's only path) the pass derives an unwritten compact: the medium holds; the reading is yours. No faith is sited, no organized dissent forms, no rite marks the medium's failure, and what residents actually do with their free hours, speak, eat in their own idiom, or call their places is the largest empty slot. Mirny works; its shadows sit at the edges.
