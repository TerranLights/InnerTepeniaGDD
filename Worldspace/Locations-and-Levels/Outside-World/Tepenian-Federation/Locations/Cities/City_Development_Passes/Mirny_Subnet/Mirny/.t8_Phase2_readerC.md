## A. Composition established from canon

**A.1 The tiers, flat [ADM].** *"National communities are classified by tier based on long-run population share"* (`CEN` L33). Mirny (`CEN` L370–376): **Primary** — China · **Significant** — Japan, UK, South Korea, Russia, Indonesia, Australia *(founding wave)* · **Notable** — Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, South Africa, New Zealand, Belarus. Fourteen stocks: 1 + 6 + 7. Tier meanings (`CEN` L36–38): Primary *"shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity"*; Significant *"Distinct, visible communities with meaningful presence in city culture"*; Notable *"Present and recognizable; background cultural texture."*

**A.2 Census I governs (`DR-4`) [ADM].** 665,901 humans / 685,529 robots / **1,351,430** (`CEN` L488; `CON` L391). **My own arithmetic (raw):** robots 685,529 ÷ 1,351,430 = **50.73 %**; humans **49.27 %**; a robot majority of **19,628**. Two bodies, near parity, the slight majority robot. Every sentence below therefore describes two bodies (`03` §0.2 item 9; `RP` L18–57: human-looking, clothed, "a robot who is or is not DRESSED for it").

**A.3 Tier-share arithmetic, with its stated limit (`DR-12`).** Weights 8 / 3 / 1 per Primary / Significant / Notable nation (`DR-12`, as the brief and `CON` C-8 restate it): Primary 8 · Significant 6 × 3 = 18 · Notable 7 × 1 = 7 · total **33**. Tier shares: **24.24 % / 54.55 % / 21.21 %** (8/33, 18/33, 7/33; exact 24.2424 / 54.5455 / 21.2121). Each Significant stock is 3/33 = 9.09 % of the weights; each Notable 1/33 = 3.03 %. **The three founding stocks (China 8, Russia 3, Australia 3) carry 14 of 33 weights = 42.42 %; the eleven non-founding stocks carry 19 of 33 = 57.58 %** **[DRV]** (the same sum appears in `02_Spine` G8, **[CORR]**).
**Limits, stated with the figures and not after them:** (1) these are *weights for derivation*, a headcount only "in-world" (`CON` box (c)); (2) within-tier percentages were randomized, so *"the tiers and totals are the information"* and no finding here rests on a within-tier decimal (`DR-12`); (3) the per-nation Census I rows sum one short of the total (`CON` C-8), so *the totals govern*; (4) the weights describe long-run **origin-stock composition**, and for robots a stock is the culture a robot was immersed in after coming online, not a place it came from (`DR-11`, `CON` box (c)); (5) nothing here is a spread, a mode, or a statement about where in the city anyone lives (`DR-4`, RV-7).

**A.4 What the source says about its own limits.**
- *Tiers, not percentages, are what the census classifies by* (`CEN` L33). The "24.24 %" for the Primary stock is the arithmetic of A.3, not a census datum, and "dominant" (`CEN` L36) is therefore a **plurality** (about a quarter by weight), not a majority. **No stock is a majority under the weighting; that is a property of the model, not a measurement [DRV].**
- *Census I was "taken after the initial founding period and long-run immigration equilibrium was established, before construction of Amundsen Tower and the onset of orbital colonization"* (`CEN` L476). So the composition is the **equilibrium** one, not the founding one; and the phrase *"immigration equilibrium"* is the census itself saying people kept arriving after the founding. `CEN` L33 says the same of the tier note: a founding wave is one that set early character *"prior to the arrival of larger national communities."*
- The one annotation, *(founding wave)* on Australia (`CEN` L375), carries no date and is defined by arrival *before larger communities* (`CEN` L33). Under `DR-9` all three stocks are founders, but only Australia is annotated, and China is both a founding stock and the Primary. **The annotation cannot be read as an arrival order** (R-10, Q-35 in `00_Frame`).
- Census II (`CEN` L588–603) is history and is not used (`DR-16`); `CEN` L592 records that there is no Census III. The header's *"the last complete census taken before the outbreak of hostilities"* (`CEN` L5) describes Census II's position rather than Census I's; `DR-4` governs.
- I did not use the census's rank column or its revision marker `(revised 2026-07-04)` (`CEN` L488): the first is a comparison, the second an authoring edit.

## B. Arrival modes — the seven, each for the human body and the robot body

### B.0 What the instrument says (read from the treaty text itself, not from the datasheet's summary)

- **Robots.** *"Every robot presently residing within the territory of any Assembly Power, and every robot who may hereafter come into being within such territory, shall depart that territory and take up residence within the Federation of Tepenia, in accordance with such schedule and such means of conveyance as the Assembly Powers shall provide"* (`FTD` II.2.1) **[ADM-DRAFT]**. *"This provision applies to robots as a class. No individual designation, accusation, or finding of wrongdoing is required, sought, or relevant to its application; nor shall the absence of any such finding be grounds for exception"* (II.2.2). The class-based exile is **established canon** (`FTS` L39), the wording is draft.
- **Humans.** Admission is by election and by **ties to the robot population**, four grounds: a human who has *"taken up residence among robot communities, married or partnered with a robot, borne or raised children in a robot household, or otherwise established their life among the robot population"* **"prior to the date of this Treaty"** *"may elect to accompany that population"*; *"No human person shall be compelled to depart"* (II.2.3–4). The fourth ground is a broad residual. **Every ground is a tie to the robot population; none is a tie to a place, a skill, a nationality or a merit** (matches `Datasheets/Phase_2.md` L14, checked against the primary text; **[DRV]**). `RP` L88 states the same sort: eligibility was for *"people who could afford robots, or who loved/supported someone who owned one."* The human-ally personhood rule is **confirmed canon** in substance (`RWI` L27, "confirmed 2026-07-08"); the article's wording is draft.
- **Placement.** *"The population departing under Article II.2 shall be settled at such existing scientific and research installations … each population being assigned to a station according to arrangements to be concluded between the Assembly Powers and the government of the Federation of Tepenia in the years following this Treaty's signing"*; assignment need not share the former operator's national origin; *"the purpose of assignment is the orderly and humane distribution of the exiled population across habitable ground"* (II.3.2–3). **Two things follow and I note both:** (i) **the unit that is assigned is the population departing under II.2, that is, the robot population with the humans who accompany it**; (ii) **the "arrangements" are not in the text**, so the absence of skill-matching in the treaty is not evidence that none occurred. The Register's stated basis for founders is *"geography and access — time-zone proximity and practical reach — never on the station"* (`NNS` L9); I did not open the Register and do not reproduce its reasoning for Mirny.
- **The cost of leaving, exactly as written.** Humans: by election the human is *"deemed … to have willingly and permanently relinquished their legal personhood within Assembly Power territory"* and *"may [not] thereafter return"*; *"The forfeiture … is not a penalty … but a consequence … of the human person's own free choice"* (IV.2.2–4). Robots: personhood *"withdrawn"* as to Assembly territory; no standing there; *"A robot who has departed … and does not thereafter return to it is not affected"* (IV.1.1, IV.1.3). The draft's own notes: *"humans are never stripped of personhood for being human — only for the specific, voluntary act of emigrating"* and the register is *"deliberately voluntarist"*; of the robots, *"the actual choice was never real"* (`RWI` L17).

Second order: the place has *no self-selected type that "knows it"*; it has no founding self-image to maintain or defy, only a record and a wind. The mix is a **finding about a gap**, not a finding about a type.

- **Language is not written here.** The Primary tier *"shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity"* (`CEN` L36); robot vernacular *"tracks the dominant local culture and the community who had her built"* (`RP` L426); no national language is assumed (`00_Frame` PA-9, Q-24). A nation is not a language and no admitted file states which languages the founders carried, so **the language category is REQUESTED, not filled**, and no finding rests on it.
- The **later eleven stocks**, and their (unresearched) forms, and **what they brought into a record already begun** (D, below). **Write around them as present and plural; do not write them.** Six are Significant ("distinct, visible communities with meaningful presence in city culture") and seven Notable ("background cultural texture") (`CEN` L37–38): that gradation is the only guidance the files give.
- **Two bodies in one household unit.** The founding assignment was of populations of mixed bodies; later layers of unreadable mode may differ.
- **Act sensitivity.** Act 1 (origin cultures fresh): *"X who live in Antarctica."* Act 2: *"Properly TEPENIAN … Origin is now ANCESTRY, not identity"* (`NNS` L103–106); the founders' Act is **open** (R-8) and no finding here rests on it. Robots built early and late *"are not carrying the same culture, even in the same city"* (`RP` L215; `00_Frame` A.5: *"Robot cohorts run by build date, not by generation"*).

### C.2 What the place's own conditions make native **[CORR throughout — the values are from `02_Spine`/`03_Research`; the interpretation here is mine]**

- **Wind.** Mean about 11–12 m/s, storms beyond 40 m/s, prevailing east-southeast to southeast, storms up to about nine days, an afternoon lull in the warm seasons, three kinds (clear katabatic, cloudy cyclonic, the two combined); drift fills windward then lee at 12–15 times a structure's height and *refills if cleared*; blowing snow starts at about 5–10 m/s (`03_Research` §1). It is a **preparation problem for both bodies**, not a gate (`RP` L18–57).
- **Light.** No polar night; about 29 days of midnight sun today (`03_Research` §2 gives about 27 for the 2564–2812 frame; see Snags); no darkness beyond civil twilight from about mid-November to the end of January; June sunshine about 1.7 hours.
- **Sea.** Fast ice about ten months a surface, 30–40 km wide; break-up between Dec 17 and Mar 9 (average Feb 3), refreeze Mar 18 to May 5 (average Apr 6); *"The window cannot be scheduled"* (`03_Research` §3).
- **Hub, record, ring.** The subnet's relay routes through this site; a continuous stone flow; a sheltered core inside the wind ring; a station's records with *"no living institutional knowledge"* (`02_Spine` §3, §8).

### C.4 Finding F4 — the wind-judgment gap closes along two different channels, one per body *(understood to its third order)* **[DRV on `DR-11`, `RP` L67, L103–107; interprets `02_Spine` §6 deficits #5, #7, **[CORR]**]**

- **Second order.** The two bodies acquire it by different channels. ## D. Borrowed Form

**Status: registered against a predicted gap; NOT yet triggered.** The rule (spine L454–455): run Borrowed Form where a **later category comes up empty** and the capability reading already predicted the gap. The capability reading predicted the gap (`02_Spine` §6: #5 *"No inherited judgment of the wind"*, #7 *"No living teacher"*, *"the record is the only witness"*). **No later category has been written, so none can yet be empty.** The likeliest receivers are Phase 8 (techniques of reading weather; language and the naming of winds) and Phase 6 (observances). The three forms are therefore **held, not applied**:

- **The China stock — route books.** Hainan fishermen's route books (更路簿): memorized first, written later, every copy amended by its keeper, entries marked *"此条准"* (*"this entry is verified"*), new books written when the boats changed (`03_Research` §6).
- **The Russia stock — the coast book.** The Pomor *lotsia*: a family book of the coast amended by each generation, whose key category is places *"where there is no escape"* from the weather; wind names that traveled and were reattached (`03_Research` §6). Both Russia-stock and China-stock weather speech already split blowing snow at head height (*"white-hair wind"*, 白毛风).
- **The Australia stock — a published judgment.** Surf-patrol flags: a trained reader's judgment published as a marker and reset daily; a rule built to a ceiling set by the worst day its makers knew, and later exceeded (`03_Research` §6).

**What the three share, and the divergence:** the **amended copy**: a record the survivors write in. *Divergence at Mirny:* **the record precedes its practitioners**, and the first marks would be the founders' own (`03_Research` §6). Borrowed here means a *form in the ancestral repertoire*, never a trait of a people (`03_Research` §6; the sequencing rule, `NNS` L59–68, and the divergence operator, L72–91: stock is a starting point, never a trait of a people).

**Three cautions I add.** (1) That each form was **carried by the founders in 2564** is not established by any admitted file; the forms are real-world inspirations (`00_Frame` Q-21/`DR-15`: in the ULM they "can inspire things that we know for a fact exist"), possibilities and not carried facts. (2) The Russia-stock form must be justified by `DR-9` alone: **the real station's builder is a coordinate**, and nothing about Mirny's coordinates explains a Russia-stock form (`NNS` L5, L154; `DR-19`, `DR-28`). (3) **Eleven of fourteen stocks are unresearched** (Japan, UK, South Korea, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Malaysia, South Africa, New Zealand, Belarus): their forms for passing on unwritten skill or reading weather are an **open thread**, REQUESTED (P2-R8), **not invented here.** The three researched are the *founding* stocks, so the set researched is the set `DR-9` supports; the later 19-of-33 weights are the part with no researched form.

## E. Who is NOT here

2. **No human without a tie to the robot population, among the founders.** Every admission ground is such a tie (II.2.3); a human with no tie had no route, and nobody was compelled (II.2.4). *(Later humans: unreadable.)*
3. **No one with a standing way back.** Humans may not return (IV.2.3); robots would return without standing (IV.1.3). The population has **no member who can leave and be recognized as a person on the other side**, and **no visitor-by-origin from that side with an exile's status**.
4. **No prior population.** The station's occupants were never a prior population (`DR-28`); the place has no people who were here before the exiles, and so no *first natives other than the exiles' own born-here.* *(Grounds: `NNS` L13, L21.)*
5. **No living teacher of the station's work.** *"No institutional knowledge … survived"* (`01_Inherited` #1; `NNS` L21–23: no tradition crosses the gap). Records, research and equipment did cross (`NNS` L21).
6. **No robot made to a person's full specification, post-exile.** The Personality Module is *"only ever partially specced, by law"* (`RP` L422); a post-exile Tepenian rule, so the robot half has no member whose mind was fully designed here. *(National, not Mirny-specific; named because it excludes a population.)*
7. **No one the site forbids by body.** No admitted file forbids either body at Mirny; the human-forbidden category in `RP` L379 is defined by extreme altitude with extreme cold, and none of Mirny's admitted conditions reaches that. Exposure here is *"PREPARATION, the same category it is for a human"* (`RP` L49–51).
8. **Not claimed absent:** any nation not among the fourteen. Whether stocks below the Notable threshold are present is not stated; I claim nothing.
