**National, canon, and it is the winter solstice.** ⭐ **In this city that lands with unusual specificity,
and every element is already established:**

  wettest month is the darkest one* (`04_Phase_02` handoff → `Phase 3`).

  anniversary is observed when power is at its least expensive and the katabatic is at its peak.

> ⭐⭐ ***The nation's founding day is marked here in total darkness, in the rain-equivalent, at the loudest
> and cheapest hour of the year.*** **Nothing about that was arranged; it is what June 21 is at this
> latitude, under this wind, on this meter.**

Per `6.5`. ⛔ **Not written as a deficit.** *Recorded as a national framework whose organizing principle —
