## 5 · The Unrecognized Instrument (3.6; `02` §4.2)

**Candidate 1 (strongest) · D3, no darkness to end the day ← Casey already keeps a nightly stop that does not depend on darkness: heated rest.**

Overnight recharging is what gives the nation its shared night (physiology L336 via Frame L276). At Casey that stop has a fixed **place** (somewhere warm), imposed by the cold, whatever the light.
- *The method that generalizes.* A stop marked by a place, not by the dark. In the bright weeks (about 25 October to 18 February, S3A-7) nothing in the sky ends the day, yet 786,985 robots still have to go somewhere warm to rest. The heated room is already Casey's clock; Casey reads it as a cost (S2-13), not as a timekeeper.
- *Why it sharpens D3.* The made night exists for one body by physics and for the other by nothing. Humans in the bright weeks have no forced stop, and S3A-8 says the low-sun hours are the best working hours. So the shared stop of national canon is held together at Casey only by the robot body's heat need. The deficit is not "no night"; it is "a night that only one body is obliged to keep".
- *The lever* (`02` L566-567: "to connect two things it already has"): connect the heated rest (a cost) to the stop (a deficit), so the place that keeps robots from permanent loss becomes the place that ends everyone's day.
- *Standing.* Rests on admitted lines only (S2-10, S2-11, S2-13; Frame L272-276). Does not rest on PA-5. A candidate for Phase 1 and Step 7, not a finding about Casey's culture.

**Candidate 2 · D5, no living institution ← the junction already runs a living, daily knowledge exchange: for the road.** [F only; PA-5]
- *What Casey already does.* "Information exchange" is among the junction's trades (spec L193): current conditions pass person to person from those who have just been on the road, which is what a teaching institution does for tacit, changing knowledge (S3A-14's war stories; S3A-13's gates).

- *Why it sharpens D5.* Casey teaches strangers about the road every day and has never had anyone teach it about its own oldest buildings.
- *Standing.* Rests on spec L193 and so on PA-5; barred from any spine (Frame L515). Candidate only.

**Candidate 3 · D1, the sea's store empties in winter ← something else fills in winter: the drift.** [low weight; conditional on S2-20]
