# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 1 · THE GRIEVANCE HAS NO PARTIES BY ACT 2

**`01_Inherited.md` §4 `A1` found the pass's strongest asymmetry: the Jeju-do allocation admitted one claim and
refused another, and the refusal has *"no route back — the deciding body has no successor with standing over
this place."***

| | |
|---|---|
| **The decision happened** | ⭐ **BEFORE Act 1** — *on Upper Earth, pre-exile, between Upper Earth nations* |
| **The parties were** | **national actors, in a world that still had them** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **By Act 2** | ***Those categories do not exist in Tepenia.*** **There are Tepenians, whose ancestry differs** |

> # **THE GRIEVANCE DOES NOT GET RESOLVED. IT LOSES ITS PARTIES.**
> ***An exclusion enacted between two nations, inherited by two populations who have stopped being those
> nations, becomes a grievance nobody is standing on either side of.***
>
> ⭐⭐ **This is STRONGER than "no appellate body."** *A grievance with no court is still a grievance.*
> ***A grievance with no plaintiff and no defendant is a historical fact about a place.***
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And it explains something `04_Phase_05` could only date:** *the Tri-Cities merged `~2780s`, deep in
> Act 2* — **you cannot merge with a city you are still nationally aggrieved at, and by then nobody was.**

⚠ **AND THE ASYMMETRY CHECK STILL FIRES — differently.** ⛔ *The inverse is no longer "what became of the
refused party."* ⭐ **It is: *what becomes of a foundational injury when the people who could feel it have
become someone else?*** **Written as a Phase 6/9/10 question, not answered here.**

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 3 · THE SEAM IS ALSO A TIME BOUNDARY — **and there are THREE strata, not two**

**`04_Phase_03` §D found the seam is a GROUND boundary: the founding station sits on ROCK; everything after
sits on ICE. `03_Research.md` §C: *nothing on ice gets to be old*.**

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **SO THE PHYSICALLY OLDEST GROUND CARRIES THE ONLY PHYSICALLY OLD THINGS.**
> **`~9–13%` of this city can hold antiquity. The other `87–91%` structurally cannot.**

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE STRATIGRAPHY IS THREE-DEEP, BECAUSE THE OLDEST LAYER PREDATES THE CITY

| Stratum | Where | Whose |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **PRE-EXILE** | **on rock** — *the inherited station stock, standing and empty in 2564* | ⛔ ***Nobody's here. Built by people who never came, for a purpose nobody here had*** |
| ⭐ **ACT 1** | **on rock** — *what the founding generation added, because rock is where they were* | **the ethnic-fresh era** |
| **ACT 2** | ⛔ **on ice** — *everything else, rebuilt on a cycle* | **the Tepenian city** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE OLDEST THING IN SHIRAYUKI IS OLDER THAN SHIRAYUKI — AND IT IS THE PART THE CITY LIVES IN.**
> *(`04_Phase_04` §4b: the outdoor window is narrow, so most of the year is spent inside that inherited
> envelope.)*
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE CITY'S VALUE IS EXACTLY THE ONE THAT MAKES ALL THREE STRATA EQUAL.
> **Under *"standing is earned by attention, not duration,"* the pre-exile stock, the Act 1 build and last
> year's ice-founded street have precisely the same claim:** ***whatever attention they have been given.***
> ⭐⭐ **A city with a three-layer stratigraphy it did not build the bottom of has adopted the one standard
> under which that does not matter.**

⭐⭐ **AND IT SHARPENS THE STRATIFICATION FINDING.** *`Extent` §10: "an inherited address becomes inherited
security."* ***An inherited address on this city's rock is also an inherited address in the only era that
physically survives.*** ⚠ **`[DERIVED]`** — *canon gives the ground split and the Acts separately; the
alignment is this pass's.*

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 · "NOTHING HERE IS OLD" IS TRUE IN **FOUR** SENSES

**`04_Phase_06` §A's proposition was derived entirely from PHYSICAL facts. The Acts supply a cultural
derivation of the same sentence.**

| This city cannot reach a past, in four independent ways |
|---|
| ⛔ **Physically** — `87–91%` on ice; nothing outdoors is ever finished, only recently attended to |
| ⛔ **Foundationally** — *the claim was settled elsewhere by people who never came* |
| ⛔ ⭐ **Ethnically** — *by Act 2 the origin culture is **ancestry, not identity**. The deep past is real and is no longer THEIRS in the first person* |
| ⛔ ⭐⭐ **And the one era that IS theirs — Act 1 — survives only on the `9–13%` that is rock** *(§3)* |

> # ***THE PROPOSITION IS NOT ABOUT OBJECTS. IT IS THE CITY'S WHOLE RELATIONSHIP TO THE PAST.***
> **A place with no reachable antiquity of any kind will find its standard of worth in the only temporal
> register available to it: *the recent, the attended-to, the currently-kept*.**

⭐⭐ **AND IT STRENGTHENS THE CONSTITUTIVE CHECK** *(`04_Phase_06` §A.1)*. **The proposition required four
physical facts. It now requires those plus a fifth, cultural one — *a population whose deep past has become
ancestry* — and all five are this city's.** ⛔ **Whether anywhere else also has them is a TERMINAL question**
*(`M-156`)*.

# ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 · THE FOUNDING ARGUMENT DID NOT GO UNREHEARSED — **IT EXPIRED**

**`02_Spine.md` §2c: *"a city that never had to argue for itself has no rehearsed account of why it should
exist, and when one is eventually needed there is nothing to reach for."***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ THE ACTS MAKE THIS SHARPER AND SADDER.
> ***The only argument this city was ever handed was a NATIONAL one*** — *a claim settled against another
> claim, by an Upper Earth court.*
> ## **AND BY ACT 2 THAT ARGUMENT IS NOT MERELY UNPRACTICED. IT IS UNAVAILABLE IN PRINCIPLE.**
> **You cannot make a national-origin claim in a country where national origin has become ancestry.**

⭐ **So the deficit is not a skill the city failed to learn. It is a title deed written in a language that
stopped being legal tender.**

## ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5a · AND WHAT REPLACED IT IS A DIFFERENT **KIND** OF CLAIM — **which rhymes with the whole city**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What expired** | ⭐ **A TITLE** — *a claim of RIGHT, issued by an authority, enforceable in principle* |
| **What the city has instead** | ⭐⭐ **A REPUTATION** — *"nationally synonymous with Alternative Culture."* **`16`: the nation is *"poorer without it and not MATERIALLY harmed"*** |

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ **THE CITY TRADED A LEGAL CLAIM IT COULD NO LONGER MAKE FOR A SOCIAL ONE IT COULD.**
>
> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THAT IS THE SAME STRUCTURE AS ITS OWN CRAFT ECONOMY, AT CIVIC SCALE.
> **`04_Phase_07` `7c.2`–`7c.3`: this city has two standing-systems — a national CREDENTIAL and a local
> DEMONSTRATION — and its craft standing is *demonstrated, not certified*.**
> ## ***THE CITY RELATES TO THE NATION EXACTLY THE WAY ITS ARTISANS RELATE TO EACH OTHER: no certificate, no title, just a demonstrated record that anyone can inspect.***
> ⭐ **`03_Research.md` §J's Unrecognized Instrument lands harder for it:** ***the city built a new account of
> itself — the upkeep — precisely because the old one expired, and it never noticed it was doing so.***

⚠ **`[DERIVED]`** — *the components are canon; the rhyme is this pass's.*
