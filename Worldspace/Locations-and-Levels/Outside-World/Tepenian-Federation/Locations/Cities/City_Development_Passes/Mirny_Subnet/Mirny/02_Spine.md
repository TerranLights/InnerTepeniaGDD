### Conflicts — both true

- **Authority lives in writing; danger lives in the wind** (G2 × G4). The founding handed down procedure into a
  place that runs on judgment. The person best at surviving the wind has the least standing in the record, and
  each injury is read as the injured person's carelessness — the record had the right thresholds.
- **The south sends the stone and the wind** (G2 × G5). The quarries lie upwind (`M` L71, L174). **The work the
  nation values most is the most wind-exposed work in the city.**
- **Signal, not body** (G5 × G2). Mirny can always be reached by signal and only sometimes in person — and since
  the sky can't be trusted, the signal decides when people move.
- **Continuity is built from handoffs** (G2 × G5). People must stop; the relay must not. The founding loss
  happened in *"that chain of handoffs"* (`M` L150) — **the handoff is Mirny's most practiced act and the site of
  its founding loss.**
- **The calendar tells the truth; the hour lies** (G2). Day length is the one honest cue.
- **Out of the wind is under the snow** (G2) `[V]`.

judgment of the wind.

## 8 · THE SPINE

> **Mirny's site demands the one thing it could not inherit: judgment of the wind.** The sky lies here — whiteout
> comes under a clear sky — and so does the air. The founders arrived to a working station and centuries of
> manuals, but to no one who had done it. **So Mirny trusts what is written and what is relayed**: the record no
> founding stock owned, and the subnet's own signal passing through its relay. **It pays the wind's price at home
> for the stone and contact other places receive** — and the work the nation values most is its most exposed.
