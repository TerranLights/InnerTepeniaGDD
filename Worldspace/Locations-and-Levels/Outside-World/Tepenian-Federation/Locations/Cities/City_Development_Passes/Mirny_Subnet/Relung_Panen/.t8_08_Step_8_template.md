## §1 · What Step 8 is (runbook Step 8, L2978–3005; `Disciplines/00f_Review_Panel.md`, whole; both on your list)
1. **The panel, carried unchanged** (`00f`): **Panel A, all six Flat Archetypes, every time**, plus the mandatory **Passer-Through** and
   **Neighbor**, and **the Lover faculty's question every time** (runbook L2980–2981). Pull in Panels B to E where `00f` says they apply,
   and say why each is or is not used.
2. **Casting** (`00f` §6): each archetype becomes a specific person of this place, cast from this place's own lines, as a role, never a
   named person. **The Neighbor is cast from the shared things' own records** (the spur's record, the port's record, the subnet), never
   from another city's pass (Phase 5's REQ-136). A position that cannot be cast at all is a finding (`00f` L757–765): say so and why.
3. **Step 7's errata enter as facts** (runbook L2983, `M-278`): every erratum in `07_QA_Gates.md`'s errata section is a fact for the panel,
   with its source line, and **the panel runs on the corrected statements**, never on the errored ones.
4. **Each panelist raises objections** to the pass, specific and grounded in its lines. **Each objection gets one of six dispositions:**
   **accepted · noted · rejected · refereed · unmet · declined** (runbook L2985–2987; `00f` L746).
   - **The peer-free test decides whether to refuse** (runbook L2989–2997): *would satisfying this objection replace something specific
     to this place with something that could be true anywhere?* If yes, it is a refusal.
   - **`00f`'s self-knowledge test decides `unmet` against `declined`** (runbook L2999; `00f` L744–753). An `unmet` must name an admitted
     or ruled line that shows the place knowingly holding the thing; the pass's own coinage cannot be that line. A refusal grounded only
     in national canon is `declined`. A refusal grounded only in a question before the developer is `declined`, with the question named.
   - **Report the `unmet` count with its grounds, never as a measure of the place.**
   - **`refereed`** follows `00f` §4d's referee method.
5. **An accepted objection is written as a proposed amendment** (the file and line, the change, why). **No pass file is edited**; the
   amendments go to Step 9.
6. **Carried:** one location on its own terms (never compare Relung Panen with any other city); no forced fit; the real station is a
   coordinate only (`DR-19`); government, justice, law and law enforcement are not written (`DR-137` item 11); faith, holidays, dishes,
   music, dress, games, slang and the drinking scene are not written (`DR-140` item 2): an objection asking for one is `declined`, the
   ruling named. **The panel does not check plausibility** (`00f` L785–805). **No file is edited. Never declare anything complete.**
7. **Close:** a `HANDED FORWARD` table (first column the **receiving** step, `| **Step N** |`); one line `T4 - SHED: <one line>`;
   requests, reserved findings, developer questions (real choices only, PROVISIONAL).
