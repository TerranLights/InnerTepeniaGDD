**P6A-8 · Is the absence itself the finding? In part, and for two different reasons by body; none of the in-world reasons can be asserted for the human half.** (spine L819-823: lack of room · nobody yet to bury · would rather not look · looking would mean confronting a named civic failure)

- **And the fourth reason is turned inside out.** Casey's named civic value is the contract of P6A-2; its failure (a heat lapse, a stranding, a refused room) produces, on the material, **reduced robots, not dead ones**: "Recharging below freezing causes permanent capacity loss" (Frame L272), and the death spiral is "a far edge, not a daily threat" at Casey's temperatures (P4-15 →3). - →2 So the practices that mark failures elsewhere (mourning, a memorial, a record of the dead) never reach Casey's commonest failure; there is nothing to bury and nobody to name.

## D · The failure state of the core value

**P6A-11 · Casey's central promise is the warm night; it becomes impossible when the heat fails, the store runs low with strangers aboard, or someone is caught away; what grew up around that is a set of rooms and margins, and the culture neither ritualizes the failure nor visibly refuses it, because the failure leaves no mark anyone can see.** *Written as a structure, never as a rite.*
- **The central promise,** as the material writes it: nobody passes the night in the cold (P6A-2), the local form of the national promise that leisure, and rest, belong to nobody: "It is the material proof that leaving worked." (physiology L360).
- **When it becomes impossible to keep** (each from the material, none an event in frame):
  1. *The heat itself fails* in the shut half: a stopped network "freezes from the break outward" and restarting is a repair (S3-9); no sea brings parts or hands for about seven months, so repair capacity must already be at Casey (S3-9 →2); before about 2688 the supply is unwritten (S2-69 (b)).
  2. *The store runs low late in the shut half,* in lengthening light (S2-26), with the held and the flow drawing on it (S2-68); a gate can make arrivals bring their goods, "never their heat" (`03_Research` L462, S3-8), and under shortfall a real town cut off the passing trade first (S3-7, a comparable only).
  3. *Someone is caught away* by a blizzard that comes "very suddenly" (S3-18); "for a robot it means a night's recharge in a heated place that is not her own" (P4-39 →2).
  4. *The night feels safe:* the mildest nights are when a heated rest is likeliest to be skipped (S2-21 (b); S2-67), and a mild day may be the front of a storm (P3-37).
- **What grew up around those circumstances** (structures in the earlier phases, none a rite): every public interior that can hold people overnight is "its storm shelters for both bodies and its recharge network for one" (P4-39 →3); the warm stopping place where riders wait inside (P5-24); the hold sized to the late opening, not the average (S3-1 →2); the charge margin and the outdoor radius every robot keeps (P4-6, P4-15); and, if keeping the heat needs people at night, the keepers who do not share the night they keep (P4-38, conditional).
- **Ritualize or refuse?** The admitted material writes neither. **What it does write is a failure that leaves no mark:** the robot's loss "is invisible to others and may be invisible to her as a cause" (`04_Phase_04` L278); her struggle "is lived alone and forms no grievance" (`04_Phase_04` L286); it produces no dead (P6A-8); and the only record of a broken night is a smaller tank. **The refusal is structural, not chosen:** a culture cannot mark a failure it cannot see.
- →1 *First order:* Casey's promise can fail every winter without anyone being able to point at where it failed.
- →2 *Second order:* the failures accumulate as people, not as events: robots whose days end sooner than they were built for (P4-21 →3; P4-46 item 2), spread across the city, each one unremarked.
- →3 *Third order:* **the city's memory of its broken promises is carried in its people's bodies and in no record, rite or grievance;** the more faithfully the promise is kept, the less there is to see, and the less there is to see, the less anyone can tell how faithfully it is kept. Whether Casey's culture ever marks it (a count, a word, a remembering) is for **Step 5** and the step after the CST; nothing here assumes it does or does not.
- *Expression:* a robot who plans shorter days than her friends and cannot say why (P4-21); a room left heated through a blow for whoever comes. *Gameplay hook:* the player meets the failure only as a person, never as a monument.
- *Proportion* (`00d` L131-137): the promise is kept on almost every night for almost everyone; these are byproducts at its edge, unintended and, as far as the material shows, unnoticed. A shadow candidate (the unmarked failure) for **Step 7** only, after REQ-34.
- *Second core value, noted:* the documentary inheritance, keeping the base working from records; its failure state is record drift with no signal (S2-34) and a faithful reader breaking what worked (S3-24). It is Phase 2's and Step 7's; recorded so it is not mistaken for this one.
