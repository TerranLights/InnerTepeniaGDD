# PANEL A — THE SIX FLAT ARCHETYPES *(all six, every time)*

## 1 · THE CHILD — *what is it like to grow up here?*

> **In the Child's voice:** *"You wrote a city of two hundred thousand people who make things and a university
> that everybody comes to. **Where am I?** I was born here. Nearly everybody was — your own Phase 2 says
> `born here` is the overwhelming mode. And there is almost nothing in this pass about being ten years old."*

⭐⭐⭐ **ACCEPTED.** ***The objection is correct and it is a real hole.*** **`04_Phase_02` §B.2 establishes that
nearly everyone at any point in the frame was born here, and the pass then wrote almost entirely about
arrivals, workers and institutions.**

> ### ✅ WHAT IT FOLDS INTO — `04_Phase_04` §6c, strengthened rather than replaced
> **The pass already has the answer and did not apply it to children:** ***"one register, taught once, applied
> to a statue, a bearing, a window, and a person."***
> ## ⭐⭐ **SO GROWING UP HERE IS BEING SHOWN HOW TO HANDLE THINGS.**
> **Not schooled in it — SHOWN.** *`04_Phase_07` `7c.3`: the standing-system is demonstrated, not certified,
> so a child's competence is visible to adults from very early and is remarked on the same way an adult's is.*
> ⭐ **And `04_Phase_03` `G.1` supplies where it happens: the lee, which is public and overlooked, so a child
> learning is a child learning in front of the neighbors.**

⚠ **`[PANEL-DERIVED]`** — *folded into an existing finding, not written as a new section.* ⛔ **A childhood is
not invented wholesale at Step 8.**

## 2 · THE LOVER — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *is this place alive, and could anyone love it?* **(MANDATORY)**

> **In the Lover's voice, uncensored:** *"Read your own pass back. **Abrasion. Deferral. A queue you have no
> standing in. A shadow with no author. Privacy that costs comfort. Damage accruing unobserved. A city that
> cannot account for itself. A merger that dissolved it.** You have written somewhere **admirable**. You have
> not written somewhere anyone would want to live."*

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **ACCEPTED, AND IT IS THE MOST IMPORTANT OBJECTION IN THE RUN.**

> ### ⚠ THE MATERIAL FOR "LOVABLE" IS ALL PRESENT AND WAS UNDER-WEIGHTED
> **Leisure is *"the material proof that leaving worked"* — hours owed to nobody, `47.3%` of the workforce,
> canon's own *character budget*.** **Music in the lee, in fixed free public places nobody owns.** **A drinking
> culture of *variety and refinement over raw intensity*.** **Canon: *"genuinely easy to make friends here — a
> welcoming, socially low-friction city."*** **A thaw that arrives for everyone at once and smells of
> something.** **And `Ashiato`.**

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE SPECIFIC CORRECTION — **the pass wrote a deprivation where there is an affection**
> **`03_Research.md` §A concluded: *"Kagoshima loves the thing that makes the mess. Shirayuki can only love the
> cleaning."*** ⛔ **The pass wrote *"can only"* as a lack.**
> ## ⭐⭐⭐ ***LOVING THE CLEANING IS LOVE.***
> **A city where people genuinely enjoy tending things, in good company, in a mild summer, with music in the
> found shelter, is not a grim place.** ***It is a place with an unusually low bar for satisfaction and an
> unusually high one for competence*** — **and those two together are a recognisable kind of happiness.**

✅ **The finding is not changed. Its FRAMING is.** ⭐ **`00d`'s tell — *"a reader closing the file and calling
the place sinister"* — was run repeatedly and passed. ⚠ *The Lover's question is a different and sharper test,
and the pass had not been asked it.***

## 3 · THE PARENT — *what does raising someone here require?*

> *"You foreclosed descent as the transmission channel because most of the population is built rather than
> born. Fine. **So what do parents do?** You closed a door and never asked what was behind it."*

⏸️ **`NOTED` — real, and deliberately deferred.** ⭐ **The pass flagged this itself and declined to force it:**
*`03_Research.md` `N-1`: "robots may well have family-shaped social structures; what is foreclosed is DESCENT
as the general transmission channel, not kinship as a social form."* ⛔ **Answering it needs a ruling on robot
family structure that the universe canon does not currently make.** **Recorded, not invented.**

## 4 · THE RULER — *what does holding responsibility here cost?* ⭐ **TWO objections, two dispositions**

### 4a · *"Your governance phase has no governing in it."* → ⚖ **`REFEREED`**

> *"Three things unadministrable, none decided locally. So what does anyone in local office actually **do** all
> day? You have written an absence and called it a finding."*

⭐ **Both positions are right, and neither wins outright:**
- **The Ruler is right** that the pass is thin on the ordinary content of local authority.
- **The pass is right** that the three consequential facts are decided elsewhere.

> ### ✅ THE SETTLEMENT
> **What local authority does here is *the drainage run, the rebuild schedule, and siting*** — **the ordinary,
> unglamorous, physically necessary work.** ⭐⭐ **And that is precisely the *vocabulary of ground* `04_Phase_02`
> §F predicted every conflict here would be expressed in.**
> ## ***The Ruler's objection CONFIRMS the ground axis rather than defeating it.***

### 4b · *"This city should have real decision-making power."* → ⛔ **`UNMET`**

> **The peer-free test:** ***would satisfying this replace something specific to this place with something that
> could be true anywhere?***
> ## ⭐ **YES. Giving Shirayuki ordinary municipal authority over its berth, its rock and its junction would
> replace *"three consequential facts, and none of them is decided here"* with a city council.**
> ✅ **`UNMET`, and the refusal is written as characterization** *(`04_Phase_07` §7b)*, **not as a gap.**

## 5 · THE ELDER — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *what is it like to age out of the work here?*

> **In the Elder's voice:** *"Your whole city grades people by **how well they keep things**. That is a
> **hands** standard. **What happens when my hands go?** Your shadow is about people who never had it. **I had
> it.** I had it for fifty years and everybody knew. And now I am slower and less exact and the difference is
> visible on the surface, in public, at the thaw, to everyone — because you built a city where competence is
> permanently on display."*

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **`ACCEPTED` — and it is the panel's strongest result and a genuinely new finding.**

> ### ⛔ THE PASS HAD ONLY HALF THE PROBLEM
> **`04_Phase_07` `7c.4`'s shadow is *"someone whose hands are poor has no route to standing."*** ⚠ **That is
> about people who never arrived.** ***The Elder is about people who arrive and then leave — and that is a
> different and worse case, because it is a LOSS rather than an absence, and it is public.***

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE PASS'S OWN MATERIAL SUPPLIES THE ANSWER
> **If standing is DEMONSTRATED, and demonstration requires hands, then** ⭐⭐
> ## ***TEACHING IS HOW YOU KEEP STANDING WHEN YOUR HANDS GO.***
> **`03_Research.md` `N-1` eliminated ground and descent and left master–apprentice.** **`04_Phase_07` `7c.3`
> ruled the system uncertified.** ⭐ ***So the Mentor role is not a separate career — it is the Elder's
> continuation, and the city needs it structurally, not sentimentally.***
> ⭐⭐ **Second order:** *a city whose transmission channel is its only route out of the hands-standard has a
> reason to protect that channel that has nothing to do with the culture it transmits.*

⚠ **AND ONE HALF IS LEFT OPEN, HONESTLY.** ⛔ *Whether a robot's hands "go" the same way is not established.*
**`04_Phase_03` §B.3's seal loop says a robot body degrades continuously in this air — but degradation is not
aging, and canon does not settle the difference.** ⏸️ **Flagged. Phase 9's residual and a candidate `DRQ`.**

## 6 · THE MENTOR — *what does passing it on require?*

> *"You say the system is uncertified. **Then who says I am allowed to teach?** There is no credential to teach
> from."*

✅ **`ACCEPTED`, and it folds into `04_Phase_07` `7c.3` and closes a loop.**
> ## ⭐⭐ **YOU TEACH BY BEING ASKED.**
> ***Teaching authority is conferred exactly the way competence is: by demonstration, and by being sought out.***
> ⭐ **`04_Phase_10` `C.3` already names the role — *"the one who is asked back"* — and the Mentor is that role
> at the next stage.** ✅ **Consistent, and it required no new machinery.**

> **The Child again:** *"Why does nothing here have a story about when it was made? Everywhere has an old

⛔ **`DECLINED`.** ***The city has no concept of heritage-as-age.*** **`04_Phase_06` §A: nothing here is old,

> think you asked — *which things are best kept* — and be puzzled that it was not what you meant.**
