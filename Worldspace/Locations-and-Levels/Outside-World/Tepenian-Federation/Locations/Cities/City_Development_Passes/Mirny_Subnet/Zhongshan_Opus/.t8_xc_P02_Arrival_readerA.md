  has been refreshed from source"*.
- **Finding 6 (robots: a required stream, but a weak refresh; this is where I differ from the first check).** II.2.1 does more than
  permit. Every robot *"who may hereafter come into being within such territory, shall depart that territory and take up residence
  within the Federation of Tepenia, in accordance with such schedule and such means of conveyance as the Assembly Powers shall

  answered (note L141; Question 31). But a robot's origin is *"Learned, not built in"*: she absorbs the cultures she is immersed in

  what she absorbed between coming online and her conveyance. The Assembly Powers set that interval (L46), and no line on my list

  origin as it stood when the robot left"* (`first check C10` L131-132). Under `DR-11`'s mechanism that holds only as far as her
  immersion before she left.
- **Finding 7 (what the inward stream is, by the Treaty's own terms).** Every later human arrival has, by arriving, given up home
  for good. The II.2.5 election is deemed a willing and permanent relinquishing of *"any claim to home, residency, or quarter
  within Assembly Power territory"* (L50), and IV.2.3 bars return (L77). So the source reaches this city only through people who
  have left it for good. Each carries it as it stood on the day they left, and none can go back and check it again. **The source
  can arrive. It can never be visited.** This follows from the Treaty's terms and puts no size on the stream.
- **Finding 8 (the census can neither size the stream nor exclude it).** The census gives two counts per kind (`census` L502,

  - arrivals before Census I would sit inside that count, unmarked;
  - a conserved total carries no net arrival between the counts, but it cannot be read as evidence that nobody arrived (Question 34);
  - arrivals after Census II appear in neither count.
