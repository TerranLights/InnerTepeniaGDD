## B · Arrival modes and the mix

### B.1 What the list fixes about arrival

- **The founding:** shortly after the signing (`DR-61` L1250); arrival by air onto Transcription Gulf; the road spur to Casey later, "once the oasis can export"; soil made on site; "the first wave is a small founding party grown over decades, with no figure ruled" (spec L222; `DR-95` item 5; `DR-96` item 6).
- **Humans after the signing:** "humans could continue arriving into Tepenia after the signing of the Falkland Treaty; they were not allowed back to Upper Earth if they did" (`DR-127` item 2, L1913). The draft: any human "may, at any time after the effective date of this Treaty, freely elect to take up residence within the Federation of Tepenia", relinquishing any claim to home, residency or quarter in Assembly Power territory, and is then treated as an elector under paragraph 3 (II.2.5 `[treaty draft]`); an elector loses legal personhood in Assembly Power territory and may not return (IV.2.2–3 `[treaty draft]`).
- **Robots:** every robot in Assembly Power territory, "and every robot who may hereafter come into being within such territory, shall depart" to the Federation (II.2.1 `[treaty draft]`), as a class, with no individual finding (II.2.2 `[treaty draft]`). The rulings: the robot channel is open in law and no later robot arrival is recorded (`DR-127` item 2; "whether robots kept arriving after the signing" not decided, L1941).
- **Allocation:** the departing population is settled at existing installations, "each population being assigned to a station according to arrangements to be concluded" in the years after the signing (II.3.2 `[treaty draft]`), with no requirement to share the former operator's nationality (II.3.3 `[treaty draft]`).
- **Doors:** people still arrive overland through Casey (spec L265; `DR-81` L1488); the cargo airport carries no passengers; a domestic passenger airport links the city to the national network (spec L265; `DR-81`); international intake is processed outside the city, on a reception ship at the ice edge in summer and at an air-only island airport in winter with onward flights to Mirny (`DR-82` L1499; `DR-96` items 2, 6). **What the one-door reasoning counts stays open** (`DEC-587`; not settled here).
- **Robots built here:** a fabrication chamber is assumed present, provisionally (frame PA-17; robot file L432); a build is initiated by "a local robot community acting on a local interest, or a human commissioning one" (L422), with part of the personality "mandatorily randomized" by law (L422).

### B.3 Chose it: what the place attracts and what it repels

*None of these is written as anyone's reason; reasons are not stated (contract Tier 3, L70). Each is a property of the place that could attract or repel, from its own lines.*

- **Attracts:** a whole working city, not only a farm: a workforce of 753,964 with a baseline of 45.1%, mandated roles of 21.4% and a free tier of 33.5% (spec L263); the food role is 25% of the distinctive tier, 13.7% of the workforce (0.25 × 54.9%; `DR-73` L1398 with spec L263; frame S0-12). No majority stock to join or to stand outside (P2C-202).
- **Repels:** the end of one road (spec L12), the door at least 300 km away and about 430–450 km on real-world sources (L124 `[DEM]`; S3-C4); no berth (L13); processing elsewhere and a second leg (`DR-82`); a winter arrival landed west of the ice wall with an unwritten last leg (spine G5-D3); the cold, an August mean of −18.7 °C (L149), for the robot half a usable capacity of 50–60% by −20 °C (robot file L13–14) and a warm charging place in every month (spine S2-9; S3-27 `[PROV-8]`); the melt season's water work (spine G2-C2); the harvest's light on a bought supply (amendment G7-D2).
- **P2C-221 · An arrival comes to a city's ordinary work, not to the harvest.** The vivid vocation is a minority one: 13.7% of the workforce grows food for the nation, while 45.1% keeps the city itself going and 33.5% is in work the city chose (spec L263). Whatever draws people here, it cannot by the arithmetic be mainly farming work; a general answer of "people come here to farm" fails the general-population discipline ("A city's most *famous* work is almost never its *biggest* work.", 00b L223). The free tier is not leisure and is not time that is one's own (`DR-125` item 3, L1895).
- **P2C-208 · Which lands harder: the list cannot weigh them, but it shows how they act: the repels are a filter, not a barrier.** The repels are written in detail as physical costs of reaching and living here; the attraction shows only as an outcome, a small party grown over decades to 1,000,071 (spec L20, L222). Read together: the repels did not stop growth; they decide **who** comes, not **whether** people come. With nobody passing through (P2C-209), the people the place selects are those who will live at the end of a road, in the cold, under light every day, at the far end of a long haul, and **the filter is blind to origin**: every row meets the same road. "The place has a *type* and knows it" (`03` L440), and the type is set by the place. PROPOSED. *Physical expression (PROPOSED):* the question a newcomer is asked here is less "why Tepenia" than "why the end of the road", since every arrival already answered the first question elsewhere.
