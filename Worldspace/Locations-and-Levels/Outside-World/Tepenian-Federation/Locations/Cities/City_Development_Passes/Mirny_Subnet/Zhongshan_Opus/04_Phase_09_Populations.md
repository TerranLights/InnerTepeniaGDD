**And it is not a departure story.** The `−10%` census figure is stated in canon, twice, as *"an authoring

the inversion. The inversion is in the retention rates themselves.**

## 9.1b — ⭐⭐ Which creates the lens problem, and the spine demands the lens be stated

> **The spine: *"The outer-city version anchors robot culture in founding-nation threads; the district version
> anchors it in theme/role… **Neither is universal. Decide and state the anchor**: what does THIS location's
> non-dominant population organize its culture around?"***

> ## ⛔ **THE QUESTION CANNOT BE ANSWERED AS ASKED HERE, BECAUSE *WHICH POPULATION IS NON-DOMINANT* CHANGES DURING THE FRAME.**

**✅ THE LENS, DECLARED: `CITY/LOCALITY` — and specifically, this city's own ground.**

**Three reasons, and the third is the one particular to this place:**

1. **Canon supplies it as the default.** Ch. 19: *"City and locality is the dominant axis, **by a wide
   margin**, and should be treated as the default starting point for any robot population's identity."*
   Ch. 13 gives the mechanism — the Guadalajara analogy: four people of wildly unlike bodies, one city, one
   identity, **because identity tracks shared life rather than shared physical form.**
2. **The alternatives are ruled out for this location.** *Founding-nation threads* are already spent at
   `Phase 8` §8.1 as a composition reading, and Ch. 13 outranks them for identity. *Theme/role* is the
   district anchor and this is not a district.
3. ⭐⭐⭐ **And it is the only anchor stable across the crossing.** **The majority changed; the ground did not.**
   **A lens that had to name a dominant kind would have to change its answer halfway through the frame. The
   ground never does.**

> ### ⚠ **And the spine's departure rule is honored explicitly** *(Ch. 19: *"a default to depart from
> deliberately, not a rule to break accidentally"*)*: **this pass does NOT depart from it.** *`Gen/Mark` is
> weighted last and lightest here, exactly as the default prescribes, and §9.2c states why nothing in this
> city's own material argues for raising it.*
