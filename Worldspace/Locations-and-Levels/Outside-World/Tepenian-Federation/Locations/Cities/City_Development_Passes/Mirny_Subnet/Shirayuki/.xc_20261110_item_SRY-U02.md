## 3 Reasoning (each step cites a line read)

- SRY-U02-20. **The core holds.** The pass's central claim is that the mortuary question is asked and not answered (PASS-06 L168, L170; FRAME L326). The current source still defers it: PHYS L170, "The mortuary question remains DEFERRED; this note only states its shape."; PHYS L143, "this is a problem for the future."; NOTE L313, "the mortuary question deferred corpus-wide". The pass's refusal to "invent a Shirayuki funerary practice" (PASS-06 L177) is therefore still required, not merely permitted.
- SRY-U02-21. **The definition of death holds.** PASS-06 L176, "irreversible damage to the gel brain or to the energy-source architecture", matches PHYS L73, "irreversible damage to a robot's cognitive architecture (the gel brain) or to their energy-source architecture". The difference is wording only, and PHYS L73 adds "with no way of ever bringing the robot back online."
- SRY-U02-22. **"Ossuaries" as what canon supplies is true but incomplete.** Canon still supplies ossuaries (PHYS L75). It now also states, in the ruling area outside the deferred block, that the ossuary is "One possibility among several, not the only disposition." (PHYS L77–L78), that it is mixed, "metal AND calcium, together, in one structure." (PHYS L84), and that it is "SACRED AND UNTOUCHABLE." (PHYS L123). None of this contradicts PASS-06 L176, but L176 does not carry it.
- SRY-U02-23. **The observation still stands as a question, and the current input sharpens it.** PASS-06 L178 names the dead "who cannot be kept". PHYS L96 ("Calcium bone reduces.") and PHYS L98 ("They are simply less legible every century.") give that phrase a physical object inside the ossuary itself, the human half. Nothing admitted answers the question, so its status as "a QUESTION, not answered" (PASS-06 L178) survives. "for whom attention eventually stops" (L178) is neither confirmed nor contradicted: no admitted line speaks to attention at an ossuary.
- SRY-U02-24. **The refinement's ground no longer follows as written. This is the narrowing.** PASS-06 L179 states as fact ("already runs"), not as a hedge, that "the national practice already runs in the same register as the local value", and grounds it in the ossuary as "bones housed and attended to." Two current inputs qualify that ground:
  (a) Per PHYS L77–L78, the ossuary is "One possibility among several, not the only disposition.", and per PHYS L143 other post-death options "will still need" to exist for humans. So the ossuary is not "the national practice" for the dead in general. It is one practice, and the developer's words at L143 tie it to the robot side of the mortality mismatch. I do not rely on PHYS L153–L155 or L166 ("the ossuary cannot be the general human disposition"; "Most humans **do not** enter an ossuary at all"), because PHYS L170 says "Nothing above is adopted."
  (b) Under the mixed ruling, the ossuary keeps only part of what it holds in the "well-kept" register. Per PHYS L114, "The metal half stays exactly as legible as the day it was laid; the calcium half does not.", and per PHYS L97, "the more it looks as though only robots are in it." The keeping-practice keeps the robot dead and lets the human dead fade. The refinement's "may be smaller" (L179) can still be true for the robot dead. But "the national practice already runs in the same register as the local value" now holds for only part of the practice, and the part that does not hold is the very "cannot be kept" problem the observation at L178 names.
- SRY-U02-25. **Why NARROWS and not FLIPS.** No admitted line contradicts the item's conclusion as stated, "asked and deliberately not answered": PHYS L170 and FRAME L326 still require it. The refinement was already marked "still not an answer" and "NOT resolved" (PASS-06 L179). What fails is part of the refinement's wording ("the national practice") and part of its ground (the ossuary as a uniform keeping-practice). That is a reduced or qualified survival (brief §3), not a different conclusion.
- SRY-U02-26. **Why not HOLDS.** The difference is not a figure or a label. It is the substance of the refinement's "because" clause (SRY-U02-24). The note's row records only "The ossuary rulings" and "L75 onward" (NOTE L313); in my admitted note lines it does not record the mixed ruling, the "one possibility among several" clause, or the different aging of metal and calcium.
- SRY-U02-27. **§B is not affected by any admitted line.** PHYS L173–L174 places robot death with "religion and community rather than by a professional trade". This is a general mechanism and fits PASS-06 L162, "The general population practices whatever the national roster supplies, in ordinary proportions." It does not require siting a faith here, and PASS-06 L155 and L160 keep the faith slot open without deciding it. Per brief rule 2, I do not decide it either.
- SRY-U02-28. **Dating is not needed for the verdict.** PASS-06 L176 says "developer ruling" (singular), and L179 describes the ossuary only as "bones housed and attended to". Neither mentions the mixed or the untouchable ruling, both of which PHYS L80 and L122 date 2026-09-07, earlier than NOTE L11's "unchanged since 2026-10-20". No admitted line says whether the pass's author had the 2026-09-07 rulings in hand. The brief's test is whether the conclusion follows from the current inputs, and SRY-U02-24 answers that.
