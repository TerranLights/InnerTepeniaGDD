# A · CUISINE

## A.1 ⭐⭐⭐ The general answer — **food here has no season**

**Three ratified facts, combined:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Davis's light** | **37-day polar night · 55-day midnight sun** *(Phase 1)* |
| **Davis's production** | ***"Ice-free does NOT mean arable… production is greenhouse and hydroponic regardless of geology"*** *(`04` §6.2, via Phase 7)*. **Limited by *"energy, glass, CO₂, labor, logistics"*** |
| ⭐⭐ **The national rule** | **`13` retires ice-free area as a limit on everything except outdoor farming and surface construction — *"both need sky. Nothing else does — and per §12, the lit vaults need sky least of all."*** |

> ### ⇒ **ARITHMETIC NECESSITY, not speculation: a greenhouse cannot run through 37 sunless days on daylight.**
> **Davis's growing is under glass and under lights, and must be.** ⇒ ***The crop does not experience the polar
> night. The population does.***

⭐⭐ **So availability is a SCHEDULE, not a season.** **What is on a plate at Davis is a function of what the
vaults are currently running — which is a function of power allocation, a decision, not a weather event.**

## A.2 ⭐⭐⭐ And the second-order effect is the real finding

**If food has no season, the year has no food-marked events.**

| ⛔ What Davis does not get | Why |
|---|---|
| **A harvest** | **Production is continuous** |
| **A first-of-the-season** | **Nothing has a season to be first in** |
| **Preserving-for-winter** | **Winter changes the light, not the supply** |

> # ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ ***THE BREADBASKET HAS NO HARVEST.***
> **The calendar most agricultural cultures get for free from their own agriculture, Davis does not get — and
> Davis is the agricultural city.**

⭐ **This is DERIVED from the lit-vault fact, not read off Phase 6's holiday zero.** ⚠ **Phase 6's zero is
documentary and stays documentary; this is structural and stands on its own.**

# C · DRESS AND APPEARANCE

> **Process B: *start from FUNCTION, not aesthetics.* *What would someone doing this place's actual daily work
> need to wear, and what does that need becoming custom look like after two centuries?***

## C.1 ⭐⭐ The ordinary condition, stated first — **because the narrow companion file inverts without it**

✅ **Robots wear clothing, nearly always, functional and fashionable both — coats, layering, and gloves.**
⛔ **Exposure is a PREPARATION problem, exactly as for a human — never a species-level gate.** *(`M-151`.)*

## C.2 ⭐⭐⭐ The function, and it is unusual — **Davis's signature work is INDOORS**

**Agriculture 35% + half-mandated technical/scientific: greenhouse, hydroponic and instrument work.** ⇒ **The
city's defining daily labor happens under glass, in warmth, and demands precision.**

⭐⭐ **And canon supplies the exact trade-off this implies:** ***"heavier gloves cost fine motor control and
tactile feedback; thin high-gauge gloves keep precision and cost warmth… a GRADIENT across locations, never a
gate."***

> ## ⇒ ⭐⭐⭐ **DAVIS'S WORKING HANDWEAR ARGUES FOR DEXTERITY, NOT INSULATION — because the work is inside.**
> **And the outdoor register still exists for everybody, because the mean is −10.0 °C and April's record low is
> −41.8 °C.**
> ⇒ ⭐⭐ ***Davis is a two-register city with a daily transition between them.*** **The glasshouse is warm; forty
> metres away it is not.** **A place whose ordinary day crosses that boundary repeatedly dresses for the
> crossing, not for either end.**

⚠ **`00b` GUARD:** ⛔ **This is the GENERAL condition — a majority of residents cross that boundary, whatever
their work.** **It is not a description of farmers.**

## C.3 ⏸️ What is NOT derivable

## C.4 ⭐ Build — **and the honest answer is the un-dramatic one**

**Canon: build is statistically weighted by local economic character — hard-labor locales trend "thicker,"
