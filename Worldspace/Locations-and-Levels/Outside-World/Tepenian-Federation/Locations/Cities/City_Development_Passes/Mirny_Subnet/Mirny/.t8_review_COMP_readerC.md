The coincidence is a property of the table, not of Mirny's robots. No robot percentage set on the robots' own grounds is stated at Mirny, and none is decided for any city. A row's practices are sized on its human count, and its robots are a conditional upper figure.

**First order**

### R-2 · F-8: naming the largest row

**Proposed finding.** Mirny's composition has no largest community that any finding, characterization or descriptive line may use. What may be said:
- Each row's own figure, stated flatly (United Kingdom 13.73 %, Korea 13.71 %, and so on).
- The largest share is 13.73 %, and no row is above it.
- Zero of 36 rows hold a majority.
- Six rows are Primary, and together they hold 66.24 %.

The order between the top two rows rests on 0.02 points between two Primary rows and carries no meaning (`DR-12`; `DR-130` item 7).

**First order**
- [ADM] `DR-130` item 7 (L1940); `DR-12` L472–L473.
- [ADM] Spec L32 and L33, both Primary (L20).
- [CALC] The gap is 116 robots and 112 humans in Census I (0.0162 points of the combined city), and 87 and 87 in Census II.
- [ADM] Spec L28: the shares came out of splits into successor rows, new pools, a random draw ("the Sinian Federation's share was multiplied by 0.5616 (random 0.55 to 0.65)") and proportional reallocation. No admitted line gives the order between these two rows any cause, and `DR-12` L472 says such differences "carry no meaning".
- [CALC] 50 / 13.73 = 3.64 (P2c L72 holds). 13.73 + 13.71 + 12.03 + 12.00 + 7.81 + 6.96 = 66.24 (P08 L287 holds).

**Second order**
- [D] **No size-ranked unit below the tier is usable.** "The top two" rests on 13.71 against 12.03 (spec L33, L34), and "the top four" on 12.00 against 7.81 (L35, L36). Every grouping by size inside the Primary tier is a within-tier gap. So at Mirny the only units larger than a row that may carry a characterization are the tier (six rows jointly) and the city.
- [D] **Groupings on another basis stay legal.** A grouping by founding status is not a gap, for example "Two of the six Primaries are exile groups (18.96 % together, a tier fact, not a majority)" (P2c L93). Item 7 forbids ranking by size within a tier, not counting rows or summing their own figures.
- [D] **Each body would need its own superlative.** The top two come in the same order in both bodies only by construction (R-1). Under `DR-128` item 1, a superlative would have to name a body, and could differ between bodies.

**Third order**
- [ADM + D] **No row can carry the census's singular Primary definition.** The census defines Primary in the singular: "Dominant national community; shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity" (census L36).
  - Naming one row "the largest" is the one move that would give that definition a single referent at Mirny, and it would do so on 0.0162 points.
  - With that move closed, the definition can attach at Mirny only to the six Primary rows jointly, none of them a majority. That is the recommendation P2c already makes, and the developer has not yet answered it (AQ-23, P2c L222; open per L259).
  - Item 7 does not answer AQ-23, but at Mirny it rules out every reading of it that picks one row.
- [D] **This removes a contradiction in waiting.** Whatever "shapes the city's primary linguistic and cultural identity" points to at Mirny cannot be one stock. That is already where the pass's spine stands (P08 L247: "no stock's repertoire is Mirny's"). The narrowing removes a contradiction in waiting; it does not move the spine.
- [D] **Language guard.** P08 L293 already refuses to read a language from a nation: "A nation is not a language." So a reader may not explain any language's place in Mirny's supporting tier from "the largest row is the United Kingdom".
  - The tier is set by its own ruling, which the note names second-hand (`DR-111`, note L91 and L105–L106). That ruling is not admitted here.
  - With the rank gone, the two guards agree: neither a nation's name nor its order yields a language.
- [D] **In-world speech.** The census is a headcount in-world (`DR-12` L473), so any row's figure may be spoken as a population. A speaker who ranks the top two rows turns a gap that carries no meaning into a characterization, so no Mirny line should write one (COMP-Q202).

**What survives, what changes, what is open**
- **Survives:**
  - The bound (13.73 %), "no majority" as a datum, and every row's own figure.
  - P2c L72, L77, L78, L127, L141 and L183; P08 L244, L286 and L287.

- **Changes:**

  - The held candidate at Batch 3 L624 loses "the largest the United Kingdom at 13.73 %".

### The two items together
[D] The two items meet in one place. A superlative about rows assumes one order shared by both bodies, and that shared order exists only through the construction that F-6 narrows. Once each body's percentage is its own (`DR-128` item 1), and no characterization may rest on a within-tier gap (`DR-130` item 7), Mirny's composition can be stated as: two bodies, 36 rows, each row's own figure for each body, six rows in the Primary tier, and no majority anywhere. Nothing in that statement depends on which row comes first, or on the two bodies sharing proportions.
