*Answered both ways, as the runbook requires. The lists below are the union of the three readers' rankings, merged into one order; each item credits the readers who ranked it and their positions. Each item in (b) is a seed or a request, **never an adoption**.*

1. **The compact's mechanism, with its private ledger and its refusal** — the medium holds and the reading is yours; what fails without a sign has no one answering for it; losses are booked to the person, the weather or silence, so the record never learns (`P6` P6-1, P6-3, P6-4, P6-17, P6-18; `P7` P7-14, P7-15, P7-20). The culture files have only the public face of it, competence without drama, and an unstated civic ethic; they do not have its cost (`{{BT:competence}}`). `[A1, B1, C1]`

The culture files carry a single-stock founding built on the station and the ship (`{{C:founders}}`). `[A3, B2, C2]`
3. **Two bodies at near parity, in every claim** — the shared stop, charge as the crossing's budget, one coat for both bodies, intake that never crosses the table, two clocks in one household, the eldest who is not the elderly, the robot native who is never a beginner without being an answerer (`P3` P3-6; `P4`; `P8` P8-1–P8-4, P8-15–P8-18; `P9` P9-9, P9-10, P9-16). 5. **Formation, not kind or stock, as the population line** — the wind line anyone can cross and the memory line, neither carrying rank; the Act gradient that folds the memory line into the founding-cohort robots (`P9` P9-14–P9-17). `[A5, B5, C4]`

10. **The corrected work arithmetic** (23.6 % mandated; the free tier at most 35.3 %; `P7` P7-2) — whose sector shares, the check shows, are the culture file's own (`NI-1`; `{{D:sector-source}}`). `[A9]`

The one near line, a vigil while the connection is at risk (`LRC:102–105`), is held at the threat and books nothing to the medium after a failure, so **the falsifier is touched, not fired** (`{{BT:vigil}}`; `[[Q:vigil]]`). The verification ethic that two readers set against the compact is out of frame (rule 3a) and recorded only as a shape (§6.4).
- **The compact's surface is corroborated, its mechanism is not:** competence without drama and an unstated civic ethic (`LC:55`, `LC:86`, `LC:191`) are the compact's public face at tier 3 (`{{BT:competence}}`) — corroboration, not proof; a culture file written from the surface records no cost.
- **A strain, not a falsification:** Phase 10's claim that the lasting landmarks are, without exception, things nobody living at Mirny made or things made for others outruns the files on the ring and the core, whose builder and date are in no admitted file; the one culture line that names a ring builder is a withheld-source placeholder (`LC:294`, tier 3). Recorded as `[[S5RF:ring-strain]]`.
- **The strongest finding stands at full strength, and so do all of Phases 2–10.** `[A,B,C]`
