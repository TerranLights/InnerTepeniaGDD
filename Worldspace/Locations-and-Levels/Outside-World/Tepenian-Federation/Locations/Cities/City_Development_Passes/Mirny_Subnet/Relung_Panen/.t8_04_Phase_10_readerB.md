## 8 · B3 · The Planet and Element determination

**The draft, written before the symbol files were opened (copied from the research log, where it was written first):**
> A walled rock basin of lakes inside ice, still most days, lit every day, where nothing soft is given except the summer melt: soil, warmth, lit green, winter water and the summer dark are all made and must be kept. Its year is set by water and by dates the place itself bills (drains before the melt, the refreeze), not by any harvest; its interior holds one climate every month. It keeps for others (a reserve kept uneaten, a nutrient loop, a second supply valued for its difference) and lives on a line it does not hold. It relates through one neighbor and the whole nation. Membership is earned by keeping a year, not by kind or origin. Its ordinary success is an absence (the drain that did not block); its failures arrive on schedule.

**Then the symbol files, read for their members' meanings only.** None names this city; there is no incumbent pair to confirm. Each member was read from its own text, never from its one-word label (the labels of six elements are under review, entry 32 L16).
- **Planets.** Earth: "Viability built from convergence, not from any one advantage" and, negative, "A stability entirely contingent on everything continuing to line up at once. Nothing about the arrangement is guaranteed to last — it simply hasn't failed yet." This is the spine's own reading, the balance bought with built compensations, each with a standing cost (S2-32), and the draft's success-as-absence. Neptune: "real, provable influence that doesn't require visibility" and "does not shun, though also does not invite": the second supply felt only when another fails (P6-28), and the untaught year that nobody withholds and nobody teaches (P9-34). Pluto: one road and no rescue fits a strand, but the place relates through a neighbor and the whole nation (Phase 5 axis), not alone. Mercury ("nothing held back in reserve") is the opposite of a place that keeps a reserve. The Sun, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and the remaining member do not meet the draft (no single output, no compounded extremes, no revival, no dominance, no mystery, no defining event: G6 is null).
- **Elements.** Water: "it keeps one path open and unfrozen when everything around it has hardened, moving what it needs through that single channel"; "Conforming without conceding"; and, negative, "the damage never appears where the water was, only where the ice decided to gather." This is the culverts kept open before the melt, the one road kept open, a population of 32 rows none of which reaches one person in seven conforming to one year (Phase 9 L354), and the refreeze that fixes the summer's water where it stands (P3-36). Electricity: "Total dependency — a system that is completely helpless the instant its power source is removed", which is the spine's energy root (S2-8) and the lit crop; a strong strand, but the entry carries no physical-fact grounding of its own and reads the supply rather than the place. Air: "Held still it is the best insulator there is" reads Phase 3's still basin, but the stillness is the ground's condition, not what the people do. Earth (the element): made ground that "presents as the solid thing others are built on" fits the made ground, a narrower strand. Fire, Wood (a written record; the year here is not written down), Metal and Magnetism do not meet the draft.

**Determination (PROVISIONAL, put to the developer as P10B-DQ-101): Planet, Earth; Element, Water.** *Reasoning:* the place's character is a made convergence whose stability is contingent on every kept system lining up and whose success is that it has not yet failed (Earth), kept alive by holding open one channel through the cold while the refreeze sets the year's damage where the water stood (Water). **Reserved alternatives:** Planet, Neptune; Element, Electricity, then Air. **Peer-free test:** the pair reads this place's own spine, axis and findings; no other city's pair was a reason for or against any member. **Caution recorded:** the site is a real-world oasis, and Earth's one word is "Oasis"; the determination rests on the member's convergence and contingency text, not on that label (§19).
