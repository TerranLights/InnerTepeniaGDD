# 4.A · The headline function, and writing away from it

**§18 states it:** *Technical/scientific ~35%, of which **three quarters is the national mandate** —
*"Zhongshan produces engineers and researchers who end up across Tepenia."*

> ### ⛔ THE TEST: *"would this reasonably be someone's entire day, every day, forever?"*
> **A city of laboratories would fail it.** ⭐ **And §18 forecloses that reading itself, in the developer's own
> words:** *"it is not as though everybody in the city is a scientist and/or technician (because cities need
> other people doing other jobs, as well)."*

## The tiers, from §18 — **and the headline function is the smallest slice of the day**

| Tier | Share | Post-`−10%` headcount *(`M-199`)* |
|---|--:|--:|
| **BASELINE** — *keeping people alive* | **41.2%** | **~356,972** |
| **MANDATED** — *the national science export* | **15.4%** | **~133,907** |
| ⭐ **FREE — the character budget** | **43.4%** | **~376,217** |

⛔ **So the thing this city is famous for occupies about one worker in six.** ⭐ **The other five are feeding
it, warming it, rebuilding its harbor, teaching it, and doing whatever they chose.**

# 4.B · What the day is organized around

## ⭐⭐⭐ NOT THE LIGHT. THE LIGHT IS USELESS HERE AS A SCHEDULE.

**`49` days of polar night; `62` days of midnight sun.** ⛔ **For about a third of the year the sun does not
tell anyone when to work**, and canon closes the obvious workaround: ***"NOTHING IN THE INDOOR TIER USES
DAYLIGHT — anywhere in Tepenia, at any depth, including at the surface."***

## ⭐⭐ SO THE CLOCK IS THE WIND.

| ⭐ **The wind is year-round** | *this site is* ***"strongly influenced by the katabatic wind ALL THE YEAR ROUND"*** |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **And in summer it has a documented daily shape** | *it* ***"begins in the evening, reaches its MAXIMAL SPEED AT MID-NIGHT, and decreases before the next noon"*** — **`15–21 m/s` at the jet core** |
| ⚠⚠ **`M-158` SCOPE — NOT EXTENDED** | ⛔ **That cycle is documented for *"sunny summer days."* Whether the same evening-to-midnight clock runs through the polar night is NOT in the sources and is NOT inferred here.** ⏸️ *Asked at `Step 3` `Q43–44` and unanswered* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ THE ORDINARY DAY, THEN: **the quiet hours are the working hours.**
> **Afternoon is the calm.** ⭐ *And canon supplies the other half of the pairing:* ***robots require downtime,
> "most prominently OVERNIGHT RECHARGING"*** *— which gives Tepenia* ***"a shared night… robots and humans stop
> at the same time, for different reasons."***
>
> ## ⛔ **SO THE LOUDEST HOUR OF THE DAY IS THE HOUR EVERYONE IS ASLEEP.**
> ⚠ **And robots *"perceive sound and vibration across a meaningfully wider range than humans do,
> structurally."*** ⭐ ***The majority population hears the small hours more acutely than the minority does,
> and both are lying still through them.***

# 4.C · The four elements

## 1 · ROUTINES

| ⭐⭐ **Charging is the fixed point of the day, and it must be WARM** | *charging a cell below freezing causes **lithium plating** — canon calls it* ***"irreversible damage, not a temporary penalty."*** ⛔ **Only JANUARY's monthly mean is above freezing here.** *So for eleven months, overnight charge happens indoors or it costs capacity permanently* |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **And the heat that makes it safe is not this city's own** | ⛔ ***"THE NATIONAL GRID CAN FAIL. A VOLCANO CANNOT BE SWITCHED OFF."*** **Six cities have geothermal. This is not one.** *Here heat is* ***"a national service, delivered, and interruptible"*** |
| ⭐ **Dressing is ordinary and universal** | *a robot* ***"can (and nearly almost always does) wear clothing, both for fashionable as well as for functional purposes"*** *— coats, gloves, layering.* ⛔ **Never write exposure as a species gate; it is preparation, as it is for a human** |
| ⭐⭐ **The glove decision is a real daily one** | ***"Heavier gloves cost fine motor control and tactile feedback; thin high-gauge gloves keep precision and cost warmth."*** ⭐ **In a city whose largest sector is technical work, that trade-off is chosen every time someone goes outside to do something small** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The kit has a name in canon, and it is THIS city's** | *`Robot_Cold_Physiology.md` §5 describes a traveler* ***"kitted for a KATABATIC COAST — drift shovels, whiteout lines, aerodynamic shelter"*** *— naming it as the contrast to plateau equipment.* ⭐ **That is the local equipment register, stated by canon rather than invented here** |
| **Buildings clear themselves at night, partly** | *elevation lets the wind sweep snow from beneath — but it* ***"does little to prevent the buildup IN FRONT OF and AROUND them."*** ⭐ **So the drift shovel is not decorative. Somebody digs out a doorway most mornings** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **AND CLEARING THE GROUND IS A STAFFED, DAILY JOB** | ⛔ **ADDED 2026-09-08 — `Geothermal_Heating.md` §2.1, missed by an earlier grep.** *Canon states that cities on a geothermal loop have* ***"no packed snow, no ice underfoot, no clearing crews, no grit"*** *because the loop melts their pavement —* **and names the negative: *"the other 31 cities have NO geothermal heat."*** ⭐⭐ ***This city is one of the 31, so it "FIGHTS ITS OWN PAVEMENT," in canon's phrase — every street, stair and loading apron, by hand and machine, all winter*** |
| ⛔ **And everything outdoors is gritty and salty, always** | *no rain ever washes it off; **only a person with water does.*** ⭐ *`Phase 3` `F-1` — the maintenance trap* |

| ⭐⭐⭐ **The tank is smaller all winter** | *capacity falls **`20–30%` near freezing and to `50–60%` at `−20 °C`.*** **Winter means here sit at `−16.1 °C`** — ⭐ **so an ordinary winter day is lived at roughly two-thirds of a summer day's reserve.** ⚠ **Reversible — *"the tank is smaller while you are cold"*** |
|---|---|
| ✅ **But the joints are fine, and that is specific** | ⛔ *lubricants solidify* ***below `−40 °C`***; **this city's record low is `−33.8 °C`.** ⭐ ***HERE, COLD IS A CAPACITY PROBLEM AND NOT A MATERIALS PROBLEM.*** *Nothing seizes; the reserve just shrinks* |
| ⭐⭐ **Food is grown in the dark, and it is the city's largest power draw** | ***"These are SEALED, LIT, INSULATED GROWING VAULTS… Nobody in Tepenia grows food behind glass."*** ⭐ *And `11`: Tepenia **must stack**, because spread growing needs `31 km²` of floor per city — **more than this whole oasis***. ⛔ ***"The staple tier is a POWER LOAD before it is a labor pool"*** |
| ⭐ **The city feeds itself, and that was a choice** | *`13` §15 names this cluster* ***"all rock-founded, all capable,"*** *and rules that* ***"geology does not determine the national food map. ECONOMICS does."*** ⭐⭐ **This city carries a `GROWER` classification and a `100%` food term — it did NOT trade its farming away for its science, though it had the specialization to try** |
| **The plate itself** | *`2,800 kcal/day` population-average · **`50%` carbohydrate, `35%` fat, `15%` protein** · a potato-analog staple.* ⚠ *And canon is clear the cold is not the reason for the calories:* ***"shelter removes most of the cold penalty. The calories go to ACTIVITY"*** |

## 3 · PERSONAL STRUGGLES

| ⛔⛔ **The death spiral is the private fear, and it has a mechanism** | ***"Warming your own cells costs energy. Below some charge threshold, you no longer have enough energy to warm them enough to charge safely."*** ⭐ **Let charge fall too far in the cold and *"you cannot recover on your own. At all."*** ⚠ **In a city whose warmth is delivered and interruptible, that threshold is not only a wilderness problem** |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Stopping is the dangerous act** | ***"A working robot generates heat… Stopping is the dangerous act."*** ⛔ ***This is the exact opposite of human cold survival, where you shelter and conserve.*** ⭐ **Two populations, in one street, whose correct response to getting cold is the reverse of each other's** |
| ⭐⭐ **Overwork is a hazard, not a virtue** | *robots are* ***"fully conscious and sentient, so a robot that spends all its time working is virtually guaranteed to go insane. Leisure is not a perk… It is a condition of remaining sane"*** |
| ⭐ **And a job opens because somebody CHANGED THEIR MIND** | *robot turnover is **voluntary where human turnover is mortality** — "a technician leaves the trade by choosing to become something else."* ⭐ **In a two-thirds-robot workforce, vacancy usually means a decision rather than a death** |
