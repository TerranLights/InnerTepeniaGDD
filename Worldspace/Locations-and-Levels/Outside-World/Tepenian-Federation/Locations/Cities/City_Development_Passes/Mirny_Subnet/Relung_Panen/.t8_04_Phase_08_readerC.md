## The differentiation axis

**Rock, ice and arrivals.**

*What the words carry.* What this place cooks, wears, builds and plays with is drawn from two sources and from almost nothing between them. **The site gives** rock (felsic crystalline basement cut by mafic dykes, salt-weathered near and downwind of the bays, glacially polished at the ice margins; research L314–315, S3-30), ice (the walls on every side, the lakes' cover, the gulf's perennial ice about 3 m thick; spec L113–116; S3-12, S3-19), lake water, fresh in the south, at the periphery and on higher ground and saline low (S3-11), a cold that keeps frozen things without machinery nine to ten months of the year (S3-40), light every day of the year (spec L151–156), a melt for three months (spec L171–172, L203), and moss, lichen and algae thin at the wetter ice margins (S3-46, S3-57). **The site does not give** soil ("There was no pre-existing farmland or soil; the founders had to establish their own.", spec L222), a shore, because the shelf seals the sea (spec L13, L116; Step 3 records that no shore supplies seaweed here, research L99), or any ore or phosphate mineral found (research L316, S3-30); and no admitted line names timber or a fiber the place yields. **Everything else arrives:** by the one spur from Casey's port, by the two air doors on the gulf ice, or, for light and heat, by the line (spec L12–13, L265; `DR-96` items 1, 3; `DR-138` item 9). Even the table the city grows for itself is grown under bought light on imported phosphorus (P8C-207), and the robot half's sustenance arrives whole `[PA-16]` (P8C-209).

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: a site whose own materials are rock, ice, lake water, salt crust, cold and thin moss (spec L109, L113–116; S3-30, S3-46); a sealed coast, so that even the sea's materials do not reach it (spec L13, L116); and one road and two air doors as the only ways anything else comes, with nothing passing through (spec L12, L265; Phase 2's axis, L67). Remove the shelf and a shore supplies; give the ground soil of its own and the place grows its own soft things outdoors; put the city on a through route and goods pass it by the way. It rests on admitted, measured and ruled lines and on Step 3's PROPOSED site descriptions, tagged where used; not on `[PA-12]`, the held belt, animals, `[PROV-8]` or any unruled door. **What could be true anywhere, and so is not the axis:** dressing for cold (physiology L38–57); importing goods; cooking indoors; a national language; robots eating siligel and drinking coolant (national); indoor lit growing on its own (national, spec L258). The axis goes to this location's own row of the differentiation instrument at Step 6, write-only; no other row was read.
