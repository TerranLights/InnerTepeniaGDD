# A · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE NAMING TECHNIQUE — **run first, and it finds something**

> **The technique's real object is *"the location's unnamed load-bearing thing,"* and a belief system is only

> to be true for the existing language to make sense.**

## ⭐⭐⭐ THE PROPOSITION — one sentence, in the register a resident would use

> # **"NOTHING HERE IS OLD. THINGS ARE ONLY WELL-KEPT."**

**What has to be true for the existing language to make sense:** ***that standing is earned by attention rather
than by duration*** — **because in this specific place duration is not available.**

| The physical facts it grows out of — all verifiable, none invented |
|---|
| ⛔ **`87–91%` of the city stands on ice. Nothing on it gets to be old** |
| ⛔ **The air carries grit. Every exposed surface degrades on a clock** |
| ⛔ **The one permanent thing — ground on the water — the city does not have at all** |
| ⛔ **And the founding supplied no antiquity either: the claim was settled elsewhere, by people who never came** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AGE IS NOT AVAILABLE HERE, SO CARE SUBSTITUTES FOR IT.**
> ***A thing's standing comes from how well it is kept, not from how long it has stood*** — **and that is a
> theological statement about maintenance, already implicit in a filing system, a street-sweeping routine and
> a sculpture nobody was required to make.**

## A.1 · ⭐⭐⭐ THE NON-GENERICNESS CHECK — **constitutive, not comparative**

> **The question is NOT "would this work in some other city?"** *(A negative on a handful of cities proves
> nothing about the rest, and reads as assurance when it is not.)*
> ✅ **The question is: *does this proposition REQUIRE facts specific to this place, or could it be true
> anywhere?***

| The proposition requires | Present here? |
|---|---|
| **A place where duration is not available** | ✅ **`87–91%` on ice; nothing on it gets to be old** |
| **A place where surfaces degrade on a clock** | ✅ **the air carries grit, continuously** |
| **A place with no permanent thing to point at** | ✅ **no ground on the water at all** |
| **A place whose founding conferred no antiquity** | ✅ **the claim was settled elsewhere, by people who never came** |

⭐⭐ **All four are this city's own facts, and the proposition is a consequence of all four together.**
⛔ **Whether any other location is also like this is a TERMINAL question and is not answered here** *(`M-156`)*.

## A.2 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE ACT STAMP — **this is an ACT 2 proposition**

| | **ACT 1** *(2564 → early 2600s)* | **ACT 2** *(~late 2600s / 2700s on)* |
|---|---|---|
| **Is anything old?** | ⛔ **Nothing is old because nothing has had time to be** | ⭐ **Nothing is old because nothing here CAN be — and by now that is demonstrable** |
| **Is anything well-kept?** | *the compulsory upkeep exists from day one; there is nothing yet to say about it* | ⭐⭐ **enough has been kept long enough that "well-kept" becomes a CATEGORY** |
| **The elective layer** | ⛔ **does not exist** *(canon: "explicitly, canonically NOT founded for art/music")* | ⭐ **exists, and demonstrates the standard** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐ **"NOTHING HERE IS OLD; THINGS ARE ONLY WELL-KEPT" IS NOT A SENTENCE THE FOUNDING GENERATION COULD HAVE SAID.**
> ***It requires something to have been kept for a while.*** ⚠ **A pass writing an Act 1 scene must not put
> this value in anyone's mouth.**

## A.3 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE EXCEPTION — AND IT IS WHERE PEOPLE ACTUALLY LIVE**

| | |
|---|---|
| **§A** | ***"Nothing here is old."*** |
| **`04_Phase_03` §C + `04_Phase_04` §4b** | ⭐ **The interior is the PRE-EXILE STATION STOCK — standing and empty in 2564, so it PREDATES the city — and the city spends MOST OF ITS HOURS inside it** |

> ## ⛔⛔ **THE ONE GENUINELY OLD THING IN SHIRAYUKI IS THE PART IT LIVES IN.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE RESOLUTION IS BETTER THAN THE TENSION

**The interior is old, was never chosen, is somebody else's design — and it is still standing. So something is
keeping it.**

> # ***THE INTERIOR EARNS ITS STANDING EXACTLY THE WAY EVERYTHING ELSE DOES: BY BEING ATTENDED TO.***
> **The proposition does not break. It extends indoors** — **and canon's unanswered Open Question, *"how did
> they adapt or modify it?"*, is the long record of that attention.**
>
> ## ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **A CITY THAT BELIEVES STANDING IS EARNED BY ATTENTION HAS NO IDEOLOGICAL PROBLEM LIVING IN SOMEBODY ELSE'S BUILDING — BECAUSE THE BUILDING'S STANDING COMES FROM *THEIR* ATTENTION, NOT FROM ITS ORIGIN.**
>
> ⭐⭐⭐ **And it generalizes to the whole founding.** *This city was handed a site it did not choose, a claim it
> did not argue for, buildings it did not design, and a population it did not assemble.* ***Its value is
> precisely the one under which none of that confers standing anyway — and under which what it has done since
> DOES.***

⚠ **`[DERIVED]`** — *the interior's adaptation is canon's own unanswered Open Question; what is derived is that
the VALUE predicts an answer rather than resenting the absence of one.*

## A.4 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE INSIDE VIEW AND THE OUTSIDE VIEW ARE THE SAME FINDING**

| | |
|---|---|
| **From OUTSIDE** *(`04_Phase_03` §B.4)* | ⭐ **Both defining facts are invisible, so the city has no legible image — *it can only be known by what it DOES*** |
| **From INSIDE** *(§A)* | ⭐ ***Standing is earned by attention, not duration*** |

# B · BELIEF LANDSCAPE — ⭐ **the slot is left OPEN, and that is the finding**

> ## ⭐⭐ DEVELOPER RULING, 2026-09-06
> > ***"I am open to the possibility (in fact, the likelihood) that there will be more robot religions, so
> > don't force any community/society into a religion where it doesn't already naturally fit."***
>
> ⭐ **So the test is not "does one of the existing six apply?" but "does ANY belief naturally fit here?" — and
> the error to avoid is FORCING A FIT IN EITHER DIRECTION:** *assigning an existing faith that does not
> belong, **or** inventing one to fill a slot.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Six faiths currently exist** | **None is sited here** *(roster `grep` returns zero)*. *`Ice-Cold Buddhism` is scoped by `§C.9` to the plateau cities — **not** the coast* |
| ⭐ **What §A found is NOT a religion** | ***It is a standard of worth.*** **`03` offers exactly these alternatives — a stake, a compact, a debt — and warns *"do not invent a second religion to fill a slot already occupied"*** |
| ✅ **VERDICT** | ⭐⭐ **THIS PASS SITES NO FAITH HERE AND INVENTS NONE — and explicitly does NOT close the slot.** *What the city demonstrably has is a widely-held, unnamed, **non-devotional** proposition about how things earn standing, held by people who would be surprised to hear it called a belief* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ AND THE PROPOSITION IS DELIBERATELY LEFT UNRESOLVED BETWEEN TWO FUTURES
> **§A's proposition could later become the seed of a faith, or it could stay secular indefinitely.**

# D · ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE FAILURE STATE OF THE CORE VALUE

**Central promise:** ***anything can be kept.***

| When does it become impossible? | |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **Ordinary — and it is CHOSEN, not passive** | **Three workloads share one four-month window, and *the upkeep is the only deferrable one*** *(`04_Phase_05` §5e)*. ***So the ordinary failure is a choice, made under time pressure, every summer, by people who had a good reason*** |
| **Structural** | ⭐⭐⭐ **when the thing that matters cannot be maintained at all, because you do not have it.** ***The city has no shore. No amount of keeping produces one*** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THE CITY'S OWN HISTORY IS THE STRUCTURAL FAILURE STATE, PLAYED OUT.**
> **To secure the one thing it could not maintain — access to water — it did not maintain harder.** ***It
> dissolved itself into the two cities that had the shore.***
>
> ## **SOME THINGS CANNOT BE KEPT BY KEEPING THEM. THAT IS THE FAILURE STATE, AND THE CITY PAID IT ONCE, IN FULL, AND IT WORKED.**

## ⭐ Does the culture ritualize the failure, or refuse to?

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The ORDINARY failure IS ritualized** | **the thaw.** *Once a year, publicly, everyone sees what was and was not kept.* **A failure state with an annual, shared, non-punitive reckoning** |
| ⭐⭐⭐ **The STRUCTURAL one is NOT** | ***There is no observance of the merger.*** **The single largest thing this city ever did — trading separate existence for a claim on water — is the one event its own value has no way to mark**, *because the value is about keeping, and this was the opposite* |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ AND THE THAW IS THEREFORE **MORAL**, NOT MERELY INFORMATIONAL
> **What it reveals is not carelessness.** ***It is the accumulated record of everyone's summer priorities.***
> **Nobody was lazy. Everyone was busy with things that could not wait.**
> ⭐⭐ **A non-punitive annual reckoning on a failure nobody chose freely** — ✅ **`00d`-clean by construction:
> the shortfall is authored by the calendar rather than by anyone in the city.**

> **`03`: *"the refusal is often the stronger half."*** ⚠ **`[DERIVED — HIGH CONFIDENCE, NOT CANON]`** *on the
> merger going unmarked. Step 5 tests it against the read-last material.*
