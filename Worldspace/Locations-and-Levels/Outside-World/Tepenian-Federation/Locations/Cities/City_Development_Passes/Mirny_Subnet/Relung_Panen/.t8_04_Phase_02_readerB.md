### C.2 The native layer

A place whose only culture is its immigrants' cultures has no culture (`03` L453). What follows is what living here produces in anyone, of any row, human or robot.

- **P2B-117 · The city's common calendar comes from its water, not its crop. Proposed.** The crop has no season: all export food is lit indoors, year-round (spec L258; `DR-96` item 4). The year's shared deadlines are set by the water: the drains must be put right before every melt and again after it at the refreeze (amendment G7-C1, Proposed; spec L171–172, L203–204), and real practice clears culverts by hand immediately before the thaw (Step 3 S3-4). Where no origin predominates, shared ways form in shared work (Step 3 S3-23; amendment RF-73). **Third order:** the one date everyone in the city shares is set by the ground, and it falls before the good season, not in it (amendment S2-57: the bill is due before the season arrives). Who orders or pays for the work is not written (`DR-137` item 11; spine S2-25). Rests on G7-C1 (Proposed) and comparables. *Expression:* in the weeks before the melt, the whole city is out at the drains; a newcomer learns the date from the neighbors, not from a harvest.
- **P2B-118 · Four more native facts of life, each produced by the place and by no row.**
  1. **A night that people make.** There is no polar night; December's sun does not set (spec L151–156). The stop is set by schedule and darkened rooms (Step 3 S3-28), and canon's shared night falls in daylight for part of the year (frame S0-25; spine S2-4). *Expression:* blackout curtains and a fixed stopping hour, kept by everyone, in a season with no dark.
  2. **Warm rooms for the robot half every month.** Every month's computed average low is below freezing (spec L179–190, `[computed, REQ-52]`); charging cold costs a robot permanent capacity (frame S0-24); warm charging space is a year-round need (spine S2-9; Step 3 S3-27, `[PROV-8]`). *Expression:* a robot born here has never charged anywhere but in a heated room.
  3. **Which lakes hold.** Only a few lakes keep ice all year, glacier contact decides which (spec L205–207; Step 3 S3-12). *Expression:* knowing which lake ice holds in which month is a thing children here know and arrivals do not (P2B-RF-101).
  4. **Ground that was made and must be kept.** The founders had no soil and made their own (`DR-61` L1252); made ground is kept or it reverts (amendment G7-C2). *Expression:* the made ground is part of the yearly upkeep, not of the scenery.
  *(Wild birds nest at the rock margins where any settlement meets them, Step 3 S3-26; their presence at the height is the developer's, `DEC-593`, and is not written as a native fact here.)*

IV.1.1 `[treaty draft]`): for her, a law about a place she has never been. *Expression:* she has no arrival story, no crossing, no Upper Earth past; her oldest memory is this city's.
