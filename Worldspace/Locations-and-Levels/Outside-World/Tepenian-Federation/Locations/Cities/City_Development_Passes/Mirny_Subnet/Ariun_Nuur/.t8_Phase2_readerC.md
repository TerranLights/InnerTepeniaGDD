## C · The native layer against the transplanted layer

**Axis of this section: what the place made that no arrival carried.** The composition is read here to know what to write around, not to repeat. *A place whose only culture is its immigrants' cultures has no culture* (spine L452–453). (C237)

**The transplanted layer (written around, not repeated):** 25 origin rows, none above 15.97%; the founders' two rows together 7.90%; the founders' tongues outside the language of dealings (spine L187; research FIND-24, *unspeakable*: no listed line says they ever held dealings). No borrowed form is named in any composition line (spine L68). **So the transplanted layer cannot supply the city's shared forms by weight of numbers:** no stock is the one a newcomer must take on city-wide, and the founders' is a minority's. This rests on the largest row's own figure and on counting rows, as `DR-130` item 7 allows, never on a gap between rows. (C238)

**For the robot majority, the transplanted layer arrives only through the native one.** By the runbook's second surviving constraint, ethnic-cultural material reaches a robot-majority city through the local culture the founding population built, never through any one robot's origin (runbook L2116–2120, paraphrased); a robot's culture comes from "the humans and other robots they're in the community of" (runbook L2236); she is of the group she develops inside (`DR-137` item 7). In a city where no row predominates, that group is her raising community, not a city-wide origin (audit Q-33, recommended, PROVISIONAL; RF-32). **So for 64.50% of the city, origin is what the raising community carries, and the native layer is the first inheritance.** PROPOSED; the Act stays open. (C239)

**The native layer, as the city's own lines and the earlier steps give it** (each reserved where marked; none written here as culture):
1. **A scientific culture built from records, with no teacher.** The founding community re-learned survival from the station's records, using them as a foundation and independently building whatever scientific culture the city has (spec L145, paraphrased). Records are not teachers: survival teaches itself because the cold answers within hours, while the archive's understanding gets no such answer and must pass from person to person (research FIND-23; RF-57). (C240)
2. **An understanding no single holder has.** The archive is "so large and complex that no single individual — human or robot — fully understands it"; understanding is spread across the research population (spec L179). A bond that no origin row draws (research UI-4; RF-63, RF-36). (C241)
3. **The conditions every arrival meets the same way, whatever the stock:** heat at every place of rest, water as an energy product, a day made by the clock for about 243 days, rest located in heated, powered space (spine §3a, §8e; research FIND-7, FIND-17). 5. **A posting without rotation, and arrivals without return** (C223, C235): the native layer is held by people who cannot hand it back. For humans, every arrival is inducted by those who came before (spine G8 standing cost 3), so the teacher here is always the earlier arrival. (C244)

**The divergence operator, applied with this place's values** (§A item 6): separation by one highway and no road to the subnet's hub; an environment that costs heat at every pause and air at every human breath; struggles of supply and melting; a goal of research; a habit of learning from paper. **Same stock plus these inputs yields this place's culture; the mechanism needs no second city** (runbook L2227–2230).
