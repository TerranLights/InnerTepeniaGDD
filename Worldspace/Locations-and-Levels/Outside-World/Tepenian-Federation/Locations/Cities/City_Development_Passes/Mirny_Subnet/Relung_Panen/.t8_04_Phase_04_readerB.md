## The differentiation axis

**The made stopping hour.**

*What the words carry.* The day here is organized around the shared stop, and here the stop has to be made, because nothing else ends the day. **The sky does not:** no polar night; daylight runs 19.4, 24 and 21.0 h in November, December and January and 5.7, 2.7 and 4.1 h in May, June and July (spec L151–156, L179–190). **The work does not:** the export food is lit indoors with no season (spec L258; `DR-96` item 4), and no admitted line gives the racks a dark period (Phase 3 DQ-44). **The supply does not:** the racks' heat, the pumps under the lake ice and the warm rooms run on one continuous load (S3-1, S3-31; Phase 3 P3-5) `[PROV-8]`. What stops the day is canon's shared night, when humans and robots stop together for different reasons (physiology L336), kept here by schedule, a closing hour and a darkened room (S3-28; Phase 2 P2-25 item 3). *Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs three things at once, each on this place's own lines: a sky that gives near-continuous light for three months and under six hours for three (L179–190); a city whose largest work has no day and no season (L258); and a continuous bought load under every room (`DR-96` item 4).
