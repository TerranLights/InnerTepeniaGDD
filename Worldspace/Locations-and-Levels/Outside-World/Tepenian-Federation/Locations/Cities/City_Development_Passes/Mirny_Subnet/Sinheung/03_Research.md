# 2 · `Daegu` **[PRIMARY]** — *and it turned out to be a CONTRAST, not a parallel*

## 2.1 What came back

**Daegu holds the largest textile concentration in Korea** *(2002: `11.0%` of establishments, `14.5%` of
production)*. **Its rise came through the 1960s export drive — state-supported, and explicitly *"low-wage
assembly for global markets."*** ⭐ **And the city holds the WHOLE chain:** *"yarn manufacturing and weaving,
through dyeing, to distribution and trading."* **`Seomun Market` — `4,000+` shops, one of the Joseon dynasty's
three great markets — is the fabric hub.**

⚠ **The academic finding is `lock-in`:** *"locally induced lock-ins can be regarded as relatively strong in the
textile industry region of Daegu… due to this strong lock-in, renewal has been relatively unsuccessful."*

## 2.2 ⭐ WHAT IT IS A CONTRAST **FOR** — *`LAW 0-R`'s fifth angle, and the productive one here*

> ### **Daegu owns its whole chain. This city owns one link of its own.**
> **Daegu: yarn → weave → dye → distribute → trade.** **Here: materials arrive by road, the design arrives from
> elsewhere, and what happens locally is the fabrication in the middle.**
> ⭐ ***So the Daegu comparison does not supply a model to copy — it supplies the measure of what is missing,
> which is exactly what `Step 3.1` says a researched contrast is for.***

## 2.3 ⭐⭐⭐ THE SPECIFIC DETAIL THAT TRAVELS — **Seomun's alley names**

> ***"Though the market has grown from its small size, the names of the alleyways such as `Hongdukkaejeon` and
> `Dakjeon` remain unchanged. DESPITE RELOCATING, the alleyways of the original market still feature names of
> the items available there."***

**A naming layer that survived a physical relocation and kept describing an arrangement the ground no longer
has.** ⭐⭐ **The mechanism is portable and this city has two places for it:**
1. **Its own name was imposed and later reclaimed** — *a naming layer that outlived the arrangement that set it*
2. ⭐⭐ **It was allocated an OASIS and mostly does not stand on rock** — ***the allocation's language still names
   a basis the city has grown off***

⛔ **NOT ADOPTED — this is a candidate mechanism, not a finding.** *`Phase 3` and `Phase 6` own it.*

# 3 · `Córdoba` **[SECONDARY]** — ⭐⭐⭐ **the closest structural match on the list**

## 3.1 What came back

**`Industrias Kaiser Argentina` (IKA), founded Córdoba 1956.** **At its 1965 peak it assembled `55,269`
vehicles — *"about one-third Kaiser-Frazer based cars and Jeeps, the other two-thirds Nash Ramblers and (from
1960) **Renaults produced under license**."*** **`28%` of Argentina's total production; `8,500` employees.**

> ### ⭐ THIS IS THE `Mark IV` SITUATION, IN THE REAL WORLD: **a city assembling other people's designs under license, at national scale.**

## 3.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE SUB-PICK THAT PAID — **the `Torino`**

*(`LAW 0-R`: "a pick with a sub-part is at least two picks.")*

**The `IKA Torino` (1966–1981) originated in the `AMC Rambler American`** — ⭐ **and *"was far more than a
rebadge."***

| What Córdoba actually did to it |
|---|
| **Body extensively redesigned** *(Pininfarina — contributed **gratis**, at Juan Manuel Fangio's personal request, "as a gesture of respect")* |
| **Chassis reinforced** |
| **Mechanicals locally engineered** — *ZF gearbox, Alfa Romeo-inspired rear axle* |

✅ **Result: *"Argentina's national car,"* with a cult following that has not diminished.**

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE MECHANISM, AND IT IS THE ANSWER TO THE SPINE'S DEFICIT
> ***A place that builds someone else's design comes to own the object NOT by replacing the design, but by the
> cumulative weight of what it changed.***
> **Córdoba never originated the Rambler. It originated `everything done to the Rambler`, and after enough of
> that the object was Argentine.**

### ⚠ AND THE COROLLARY THE SAME PICK SUPPLIES
**IKA's `Bergantín` is recorded as *"the first locally conceived, designed and engineered car,"* with local
content made in Córdoba.** ⭐ ***So the licensee did eventually originate a design — and it happened AFTER
years of building other people's.*** **The accumulation came first.**

## 3.3 ⭐⭐ WHERE THIS LANDS ON THIS CITY'S OWN CANON — **and it re-reads a sentence already in the spec**

**"The Sinheung Standard"'s third pillar is *"the guarded technical archive preserving Neumayer's full
historical run of `Mark IV` schematic **REVISIONS**."***

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ **REVISIONS. PLURAL. A FULL HISTORICAL RUN OF THEM.**
> ⚠ **Canon does not say WHOSE.** *The possessive attaches to Neumayer, so the natural reading is that Neumayer
> revised and this city archives.* ⛔ **But the Córdoba mechanism makes a second reading available: the city
> that BUILDS a design for generations is the city that finds its faults**, and a revision history is what
> accumulated change looks like when it is written down.
>
> ⛔⛔ **NOT RESOLVED HERE, AND NOT ASSUMED.** ***The ambiguity is the finding.*** ⏸️ **→ `DRQ` candidate, and a
> `Step 5` docket row.** ⚠ *`NO FORCED FIT`: it is entirely legitimate for the answer to be "Neumayer revised
> it and this city keeps the papers," which would sharpen the deficit rather than relieve it.*

# 4 · `Volgograd` **[SECONDARY]** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **the pick that nearly died and then paid the most**

## 4.1 ⚠ THE FIRST ANGLE DIED ON FRAME, NOT ON QUALITY

**Angle 1 — "rebuilt city, inherited ruins" — returned rich, real material** *(the plant cleared and back in

belongs to a different pass.**

⚠ ***And this is precisely where a pass asserts redundancy and moves on.*** **`LAW 0-R` forbids it: query every
pick from more than one angle.** ✅ **The second angle is in-frame and it is the best return of the step.**

## 4.2 ⭐⭐⭐⭐ ANGLE 2 — **the plant was designed in Detroit and shipped in**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Designed by** | **`Albert Kahn Inc.`, Detroit** — *contracted 1929 by the Soviet government to design and oversee a tractor factory producing 40,000 units a year* |
| **Fabricated where** | ⭐ **New York.** *Steel structures manufactured by `McClintic-Marshall`, then **shipped to Stalingrad for field assembly*** |
| **How it arrived** | *"Everything from steel to skylights was coming from the U.S. by boat, special-built train, trucks, and, yes, camels"* — **Atlantic → Mediterranean → Black Sea → river → overland** |
| **Who assembled it** | **~`400` U.S. supervisors, mostly from Detroit. Installed in six months** |

> ### ⭐ A PLANT DESIGNED ELSEWHERE, BUILT ELSEWHERE, SHIPPED IN, AND ERECTED UNDER FOREIGN SUPERVISION.
> **That is this city's `G5` profile — materials in, design in, site granted — in the historical record.**

## 4.3 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ THE THIRD-ORDER CONSEQUENCE — **and it is the finding of the whole step**

> **Kahn's firm went on to design `521` factories in the USSR — *and to train **more than a thousand Soviet
> engineers** during the 1930s.***

> # ***THE TRANSFER WAS NEVER THE FACTORIES. IT WAS THE ENGINEERS — AND THAT IS WHAT ENDED THE ARRANGEMENT.***
> **A country that buys plants keeps buying plants. A country that acquires the people who can design plants
> stops needing to buy them.**
>
> ## ⭐⭐ SO THE ROUTE OUT OF "WE BUILD SOMEONE ELSE'S DESIGN" IS NOT ACQUIRING THE DESIGN. **IT IS ACQUIRING THE PEOPLE WHO CAN PRODUCE ONE.**

### ⛔⛔ AND THIS CITY ALREADY HAS THE INSTRUMENT — **canon has simply never connected it to the deficit**

**The `Sinheung Institute of Cybernetics and Robotic Care` produces, per canon:** *maintenance and repair
technicians · **frame and actuator specialists** · **sensor and processor service engineers** · coolant and
siligel practitioners · **chamber technicians** · and robotic counselors.*

| ✅ What canon states | ⛔ What canon has never asked |
|---|---|
| **The Institute's defining focus is CARE — physical and emotional. *"This is not a repair school."*** | ***Whether a school that trains the people who understand the chambers most deeply is, structurally, also the thing that could end the design dependency*** |

⛔⛔ **NOT ADOPTED. NOT A FINDING.** ⭐ **It is a QUESTION this step is entitled to raise and `Phase 7`/`Phase 9`
must answer** — *and `NO FORCED FIT` governs: "the Institute is a care school and has no design ambition
whatever" is a completely legitimate answer, and would make the deficit permanent and sharper.*

# 5 · NON-PICK RESEARCH — **the unadministered standard**

**Sought: a real analogue for a standard that grades work with no administering office.**

**What came back is the GUILD, and the useful part is where it FAILS to match:**

| ✅ Matches | ⛔ Does not match |
|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ ***"A guild's reputation was a COLLECTIVE ASSET; a single bad product could damage the standing of every member."*** | **Guilds had *inspectors* who examined workshops and finished goods against codified standards; goods that passed received a *mark*; guilds held *charters* from authorities** |

> ### ⭐⭐⭐ SO THIS CITY'S STANDARD IS RARER THAN A GUILD, AND WORSE-EQUIPPED
> ***It has the guild's collective EXPOSURE — one bad piece damages everyone — with none of the guild's
> MACHINERY.*** **No inspector, no mark, no charter, no office.**
> ⛔ **Which confirms `Step 1`'s structural finding from the outside:** *a guild could expel a member **and
> readmit him**. **A shared civic expectation can do neither**, because there is no body to do it.*
>
> ### ⭐⭐ AND THE CONSEQUENCE THAT FOLLOWS, FOR `Phase 4`
> **Collective reputation without an enforcement apparatus survives only on tight mutual observation** — *if
> nobody inspects, everybody must watch.* ⛔ **So the enforcement is not a procedure; it is a habit of looking
> at each other's work, constantly.** ⭐ ***That is a culture, not a policy*** — **and it means the "informal
> secondary market for lower-grade material" is where work goes when the watching has already happened.**
⏸️ **Candidate, handed to `Phase 4` and `Phase 7`. Not adopted here.**

# 9 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ `Step 3.6` — **THE UNRECOGNIZED INSTRUMENT**

## 9.4 · ⭐⭐⭐ WHY IT DID NOT SPREAD — *"the answer is the finding"*

**The technique predicts the reason is that *"nobody recognized it as an instance of anything — it was just how
that job is done."*** ⭐ **Here the mechanism is sharper than that, and it is structural:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⛔⛔ **MAINTENANCE IS THE OPPOSITE OF INVENTION, IN HOW PEOPLE FILE THEIR OWN WORK** | *Re-leveling a building is not experienced as designing anything. | ⛔ **AND THE CITY'S OWN INSTRUMENT CANNOT SEE IT** | ***The Standard grades MATERIALS*** — *"the quality and physical integrity of **refined materials**": graded stone, finished chamber output, an archived schematic.* **It has no category for a METHOD.** ⭐ *The one instrument that could have recognized the achievement is scoped to things, not to ways of doing things* |

> ### ⚠ AND NOTE WHAT THE STANDARD'S OWN VOCABULARY ALREADY CONTAINS
> ***"Quality and PHYSICAL INTEGRITY."*** ⭐ **Physical integrity is a structural criterion.** **The city
> already has the words. It applies them to stone and to chambers, and not to the thing holding the stone and
> the chambers up.**

## 9.5 · ⭐⭐ WHAT THIS CONVERTS — *the technique's stated purpose*
