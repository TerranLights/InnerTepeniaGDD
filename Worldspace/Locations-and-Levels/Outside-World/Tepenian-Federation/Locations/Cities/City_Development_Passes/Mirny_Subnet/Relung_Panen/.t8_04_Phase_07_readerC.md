## The differentiation axis

**Holding what it lacks.**

*What the words carry.* The work this city is given to do for the nation, and the work its own life needs, meet at one seam: **each national role it holds keeps, for others or as waste, the very thing the city itself is short of.** The lit export racks turn about 0.5 GW into heat that must be removed (`DR-96` item 4, rulings L1633; S3-43) in a city with no energy from its site (spec L261) that buys heat against 3,987 measured winter freezing degree-days (spec L195). The recovery role recovers phosphorus and soil as a national service, "also serving other cities' waste streams" (`18` L33 `[DoI corroboration]`; `DR-75` L1421), in a city that imports its own phosphorus (spec L234 `[DEM]`) and the organic matter for its made ground (spec L222). The seed role keeps a second stock for other cities, "insurance against a crop disease in one region" (`18` L32; `DR-75` L1421), and no line names who would back the city's own stock (Step 1 AS-12). The cold chain is, for nine months, a heated-handling trade keeping produce from freezing (S3-37), in a city whose robot half needs a warm room for every charge in every month (spine S2-9 `[computed, REQ-52]`; S3-27). **No line hands any of these across** (spine REQ-54; Step 3.6 items 1, 5 and 6, research L616–621), so the order's distinctive questions (who decides, who keeps, what cannot be administered) sit on that seam.

*Peer-free test, could this be true anywhere?* No. It needs, at once and each on this place's own lines: a ruled food role large enough to make half a gigawatt of waste heat (`DR-71` L1374; `DR-96` item 4); a site that gives no energy and a measured winter that demands heat (spec L195, L261); a national phosphorus and soil recovery role held by a city that imports phosphorus (`DR-75` L1421; spec L234 `[DEM]`); a seed backup kept for others beside no named backup for itself (`18` L32; Step 1 AS-12); and a population half robot whose charge must be warm in every month (spec L20; `[computed, REQ-52]`). Give the site its own energy and the heat is no want; take away the recovery role and phosphorus is only a lack; take away the food role's scale and the heat is small. **The axis rests chiefly on ruled and measured lines** (the heat: `DR-96` item 4, spec L195, L261; the phosphorus: `DR-75` L1421 with spec L234 at DEMOTED standing); the cold-chain and charging rows rest on Step 3's comparables and the computed columns and are carried beside it, not under it.
