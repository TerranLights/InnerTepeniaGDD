### ARR-1 · Two written human routes into the Federation after the treaty date; at Mirny, who a later human can be *(F-1)*
**Proposed finding.** The treaty writes two human routes, II.2.3 (accompaniment, on a tie formed before the treaty date) and II.2.5 (an election open at any time after it, naming no ground), at one cost: no legal elsewhere and no return. A human at Mirny after the treaty date is born here, a tie-holder who came under II.2.3, a II.2.5 elector, or unread. Whether any elector reached Mirny is **not stated**.
- **1st [ADM].** `TRT` L48 (II.2.3's four grounds, each a tie formed "prior to the date of this Treaty"); `TRT` L50 (II.2.5: "Any human person who has not made the election described in paragraph 3 […] may, at any time after the effective date of this Treaty, freely elect to take up residence within the Federation of Tepenia"); `TRT` L76–L77 (the forfeiture and the bar on return now reach both); `DRL` L1913 (new canon; "This replaces the reading that the human channel closed at the signing").
- **2nd [D].** II.2.3's limit sits on the tie, not on the journey (L48 with L46's "schedule"), so the class the pass's reading excluded was **the human with no pre-treaty tie, or with a tie who had not elected**, and that is the class II.2.5 names. The new route therefore does not widen when a tie-holder may come; it removes the tie as a condition. Its whole price is the loss: "relinquished any claim to home, residency, or quarter within Assembly Power territory" (L50) and personhood there (L76).
- **3rd [D].** So two properties the pass gave the founding human stratum become **founding-only by necessity, not by the treaty's closure**: "selected, by the admission rule, for willingness to live among robots" (`P2` L338) and "every one of them is at Mirny **because of a robot**" (`P9` L199). The one property every human who came from Upper Earth now shares, at any date, is the cost: **no legal elsewhere** (`P5` L233; `P2` L340, "**No resident of the founding stratum can leave without losing standing**", now extends past the founding stratum). At Mirny, where both bodies keep one stop and robots are 50.63 % of residents (`PP9` L9), a later elector, if one came, would be the one kind of human arrival who need not have come because of a robot. *Not claimed:* that any did.
- **Survives:** II.2.3's reading at `P2` L156–L160 (of II.2.3); the cost (`P2` L162); every founding-stratum absence in `P2` §E. **Changes:** `P2` L165–166, L494; the B.1 heading. **Open slot:** any later human arrival at Mirny: "none recorded here", never "none possible here".
