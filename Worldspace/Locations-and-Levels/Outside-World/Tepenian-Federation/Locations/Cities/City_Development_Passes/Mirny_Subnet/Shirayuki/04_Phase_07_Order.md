# 7c · KNOWLEDGE AND TRANSMISSION — ⭐⭐⭐ **and this is where the shadow is**

## 7c.1 · How the skill is learned

**The city's defining competence is a TECHNIQUE, not a body of knowledge** — *"polish without scratching."*
⭐ **You cannot learn it from a description; the difference between upkeep and damage is in the hand.**
**So: demonstration, correction, and repetition on real surfaces** — **and it is gradeable**, because the
result is visible, public, outdoors, and re-inspected annually at the thaw.
⭐ **Education at `20%`, drawing nationally, is the formal half of exactly this.**

## 7c.2 · ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **THE CITY HAS TWO STANDING-SYSTEMS, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME THING**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **The NATIONAL one** | **The mandated university — `20%`, `~160,210` people, drawing from across Tepenia.** *A formal institution that credentials* |
| ⭐⭐ **The LOCAL one** | **Demonstration on real surfaces, re-inspected publicly and annually at the thaw.** ***Uncertified, ungoverned, and visible to everybody*** |

> # ⭐⭐⭐⭐ **AND THIS EXPLAINS THE PIPELINE.**
> **Canon: students use the university *"as a deliberate stepping stone toward eventually integrating into and
> becoming part of the local arts and music culture."***
> ## ***THEY ARE TRADING A CERTIFICATE FOR A DEMONSTRATION.***
> **The "stepping stone" is not a move from school to work. It is a move from one standing-system to the
> other** — **from a credential the nation issues to a standing the street confers and nobody administers.**
> ⭐ **Which is also why the pipeline leaks nationally and nobody planned it:** ***the thing people come for
> cannot be granted by the institution that brought them.***

> ## ⭐⭐⭐ AND IT CONVERGES WITH `02_Spine.md` §5's INDEPENDENT RESULT — **four deficits, four addresses, no shared counterparty: *"four separate silences."***

⛔ **So: NO ORGANIZED COUNTERCULTURE IS WRITTEN.** ⭐ **The three shapes above are DISPOSITIONS, not factions —
