## D · The failure state of the core value

**The axis of this section: the city keeps two promises on one supply, and when they compete the body's promise wins and the purpose yields.**

**FIND-P6-17 · The central promise, in two halves.** *To the body:* everyone admitted is kept, warm at every stop, taught until she can stop safely (FIND-P6-3; Phase 4 FIND-P4-32). *To the purpose:* the lake is understood here, by many people together, none of whom holds it whole (spec L167, L179; spine §4, *be necessary*). *[A]*

**FIND-P6-18 · When each becomes impossible to keep.**
- **The body's promise** fails for whoever is alone when something goes wrong: a robot whose charge falls too far in deep cold cannot recover on her own and depends wholly on someone coming (Phase 4 row 82, FIND-P4-55); everyone stops in the same hours on one supply, so a lapse at night finds both bodies stopped at once (Phase 4 FIND-P4-7; SH-14).
- **The purpose's promise** fails silently: understanding thins as holders die (and, if they could, leave) faster than it passes (spine L264; RF-36); and if the city had no working network link, what it knows reaches anyone else only as people and samples carried along Hwy 37 (Phase 5 FIND-P5-48, conditional).
- **The two are ordered.** The city can schedule its research's dark periods and never its heat (spine L224); the only work that can be stopped is the work that is its purpose (spine §4; Phase 3 FIND-P3-18). → **When the two promises compete for the one supply, the body's wins and the purpose yields: the city's reason to exist is its first sacrifice.** The same order shows at the outpost on its road, where the chamber work ran on what the grid did not need (Phase 5 FIND-P5-14, an inference there).
