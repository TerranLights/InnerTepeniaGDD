## 3 · The re-derivation

### 3.1 · F-3 — the two common layers and translation

**Proposed finding, as it stands now.** At Mirny two layers are common to residents, and they stand in opposite relations to translation.
- **The place's conditions** (wind and drift, light, the ring and sheltered core, the fast ice and its window, the coastal rock, and the relay and the record **as things**) are the only layer every resident, in either body and from any stock, meets with no language between her and it.
- **The civic tongue**, Hawaiian (later Tepenian), is the shared speech for both bodies from the founding on. It is a pidgin formed between other languages, the layer through which residents of different stocks speak without translating **once they have it**. A child born here has it as the dominant language; a newcomer learns it.
- **The native layer is the first. The second is national, not native.**

**First order (the observation)**
- [ADM] The conditions: `Phase 2` L276–282.
- [ADM] The civic tongue: "**Hawaiian is the de-facto national language, and at Mirny it is the dominant, civic language for both bodies from the founding on.**" (`P08` L269).
- [ADM] Its definition: `DRL` L1895 (quoted in section 2).
- [D] The conditions are met directly. The civic tongue is itself a language, so it is met as a language, in one of three ways:
  - **had as a first language** by a human child born here: "a human born at Mirny learns Hawaiian, and a parent's language too where it differs" (`P08` L297) [ADM];
  - **had by derivation** by a robot: "Its dominant half now reads **Hawaiian**" (`P08` L277), conditional on AR-40 (`P08` L301) [D, as the pass states it];
  - **learned** by a newcomer who lacks it: "before she has the dominant language" (`P9 piece` L175) [D, the pass's own, conditional on AR-45].
- [D] Hence L283's "only" holds in the scoped sense and, on the pass's own reading, in the plain sense too. It fails only as a description of Mirny's whole common ground, which is how L284 reads it ("At the founding the shared medium was **site, record and relay**").
- [D] **Gap.** Whether each founder, human or robot, already had the civic tongue on arrival is stated in no admitted line. `P08` L269 places the tongue as civic "from the founding on"; it does not say who brought it. I write this as a gap, not filled.

**Second order (what that causes)**
- [D] **Every common thing Mirny makes in speech joins the two layers.** The pass's own "conditions vocabulary of its own wind" ("What the city shares in speech is the civic language plus a conditions vocabulary of its own wind", `P08` L304 [ADM]) takes its **referents** from the native layer and its **words** from a language. Which language a name is in is the developer's naming (`P10` L395 [ADM]), and no word is written (`P08` L279, L316 [ADM]). So L284's "whatever is common must be built on them" survives for referents and narrows for form.
- [ADM] **The conditions put every stock on one footing.** "no stock brought this wind's knowledge" (`P9 piece` L168); "A judgment of the wind that could not be inherited" (`Phase 2` L293).
- [D] **What separates residents at the native layer is years in the wind, not stock.** The pass already ranks a person by "the most years in Mirny's wind" (`P9 piece` L165 [ADM]).
- [D] **What separates residents at the civic tongue is whether they have it.** Whether some residents begin nearer it because their language is one of its bases is stated in no admitted line and is **not derived**. "A nation is not a language." (`P08` L293 [ADM]); no stock is converted to a language here.
- [D] The pass's own one-location test (`P08` L307) says the civic tongue is not what is Mirny's: "What is Mirny's in speech is the conditions vocabulary of its own wind". A1 does not change that. A pidgin defined for the whole nation is national by construction.

**Third order (and what that causes)**
- [D] **The two common layers meet at the relay and the record.** Both are inside L283's "these" (`Phase 2` L280–281 [ADM]). Both are met without translation as presences, and both carry language: the record's text is in the in-world owner's undetermined language, and "*if* the record's language differs from the civic language, reading it is a switch [D]" (`P08` L310 [ADM]). The warnings' language is open (`P08` L280, AR-45 [ADM]). **So the exact scope of "without translation" is the conditions themselves, plus the relay and the record as things, not their content.**
- [D] **At Mirny the native layer can cut off the national layer's farthest reach.**
  - [ADM] The relay is "**noisiest when it matters**" (`Phase 2` L280), and "a storm degrades the signal too" (`Phase 2` L291).
  - [ADM] When it is down, "the warnings that ride the relay are not received either when the relay is down, so the room has lost its warnings too" (`P08` L257). Speech goes on face to face: "In the long storm, sound is made in the room" (`P08` L257).
  - [D] So in the worst weather, beyond the room, what every resident still shares is the condition itself, met without translation. The civic tongue is held to the room.
  - [D] Delete-the-name test: no other place is named; the claim is built from Mirny's own relay, wind and storm, so it survives.
- [D] **The layer that needs no translation to be met is the one whose reading has to be passed on in words.** "For a newcomer or a visitor a cue is unread until learned, a condition of a person, not of the place" (`P08` L339 [ADM]). The protocol is kept and taught in a language that stays open (`P08` L280 [ADM]; the reserved AF8-6, "the protocol kept in the civic tongue because it is civic", is not adopted). So at Mirny the wind is met without a tongue and learned through one. **Which tongue: an open slot.**
- **Act.** [ADM] "one language over time" (`DRL` L1895); the change to Tepenian carries no date or Act (`P08` L269, `P9 piece` L176, AR-43). [D] The two-layer reading holds in both Acts. How many residents in either Act lack the civic tongue is not stated and is not sized here (the human arrival route after the treaty is another piece's ground, `NOTE` L168).

**What survives:** L283's claim for the conditions, with its scope stated; L284's "not a culture"; L285; L271; L295; `P08` L304 and L307, which already carry the narrowed form.
**What changes:** L283 takes a scope; L284's founding list gains the civic tongue as national speech; L284's "built on them" is limited to what is common and Mirny's own, by referent.

### 3.2 · F-5 — the caution on English authoring labels

**Proposed finding, as it stands now.** English is one of Mirny's two supporting-tier languages (English and Russian, by the developer's special ruling) and a primary base of the dominant tongue. So an English authoring label in this catalog could be read as attested in-world speech by two routes: as an English name, or as a word of the civic tongue of English origin. Every label stays an authoring label for an admitted referent, in English, never attested speech, and never a place name. Which language any name is in is the developer's naming.

**First order**
- [ADM] "Mirny's supporting tier is **English and Russian**, set by the developer as a Mirny special case (…), not by the 12-point rule, which by actual speakers gives English alone. Hawaiian (later Tepenian) remains dominant." (`DRL` L1769; the ellipsis omits other places' names).
- [ADM] "a primary base of English and Japanese" (`DRL` L1895).
- [D] At Mirny **one language holds two roles**: a supporting-tier language of residents, and a source of the national, dominant tongue. Both are true together, on two objects.
- [D, on the ordinary meaning of "base" for a pidgin; recalled, not verified] A word of the civic tongue can be of English origin, so an English-looking label could be read as one.

**Second order**
- [D] **The new route is the wider one.** The civic tongue is "the dominant, civic language for both bodies" (`P08` L269 [ADM]). A label read as a word of it would be read as what every resident says. Read as supporting-tier English, it would be what residents say "where business or talk calls for it" (`P9 piece` L179 [ADM]).
- [D] **A label read that way would get ahead of the language's own synthesis.** The language "is synthesized in its own repository" (`P10` L393 [ADM]; also `P08` L279).

**Third order**
- [D] **The rule now guards a second border.** The rule (`P08` L316, restated L348, applied at `P10` L394) is unchanged in its words. It now also keeps this catalog from writing the national language's vocabulary for it, not only from writing Mirny's supporting-tier speech.
- [D] **The answer to the collision is "never attested speech", not a change of label language.** The rule keeps labels "in English", the repository's authoring language, and it does not need to change that.
- [D] **Any named case is settled by R-118** (`P10` L395 [ADM]): the developer decides which language a name is in. An English-origin form cannot show by itself whether it is in the civic tongue or in the supporting tier's English.
- [D] **Russian and Japanese: none is sited here.** No label in the admitted lines is written in either, and no word is coined in any language (`P10` L393 [ADM]). So no parallel caution arises. This is a result, not a gap to fill.
