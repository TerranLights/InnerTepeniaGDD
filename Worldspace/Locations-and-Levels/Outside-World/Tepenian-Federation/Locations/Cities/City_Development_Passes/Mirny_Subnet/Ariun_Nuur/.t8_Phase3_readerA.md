## F · Retroactive Mechanism

**A-42 · F-1: the shimmer is the snowfall.** *Simply true, unexplained:* "ice crystals are always present; the sky … has a permanent faint shimmer on clear days" (spec L128), and "when visibility closes, something is actually falling" (spec L93). *The physical system that would produce exactly that:* snow made in the clear air itself, as diamond dust, about three quarters of what settles (research FACT-91), and almost none lifted again, since what falls and what lands are the same size and the wind is light (FIND-4; A-2). *Second order:* **the shimmer in the air and the slow burial of the city are one process.** *Third order:* the most beautiful thing in the city's sky is its slowest maintenance cost; every clear, shimmering day adds snow against every wall, with no storm to mark it. No custom is written from it (RF-A6).

**A-43 · F-2: the lake makes the plain featureless.** *Simply true, unexplained:* "flat, featureless" (spec L68). *The physical system:* the lake floats the ice above it, so the surface over the lake is flatter than the grounded ice around (about 0.01° against about 0.08°; research FIND-30). *Second order:* **the place's lack of a landmark and its reason to exist are one fact:** what makes the outdoors unreadable is the lake, and the one landmark is four kilometers down and readable only through the research (research UI-3, reserved as RF-62).
