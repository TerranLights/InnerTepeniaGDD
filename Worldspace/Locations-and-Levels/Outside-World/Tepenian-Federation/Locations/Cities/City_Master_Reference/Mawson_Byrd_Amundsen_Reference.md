### Canon facts

- **Geography/founding:** Named for Sir Douglas Mawson (St. Douglas in the Tepenian Saints framework). Australian Antarctic Territory (~42% of the continent, the largest national claim) administered here historically; claim persists post-war only as ceremonial memory, no functional authority.
- **Demographics:** China Primary (17.02%), Australia (the actual founder) Significant (8.29%) — "The Name That Outlasted the Founders." Confirmed as a **structural pattern recurring at three scales**: founding identity, demographics, and infrastructure function (hub role outlasted which specific links it coordinates).
- **Economy §15 (canon, `Local_Cultures` §15):** Subnet-hub logistics/Arcanet coordination 25% · Maritime trade 20% · Technical/scientific 20% · Commercial/trade 15% · **Hospitality/honeymoon tourism 10%** · Other 10%.
- **⭐ Distinctive economic identity:** Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination for newly-married human-robot couples — a *deliberately cultivated*, decades-earned reputation, not incidental. Typical stay ~1-2 weeks. Public libraries confirmed as a genuine civic institution.
- **Notable locations:** Holme Bay harbor; the subnet-hub Arcanet installation (took the brunt of Long Night War damage — inter-subnet links were the deliberate target per the Split Brain mechanism; intra-subnet links to Sayowa/Dome Fuji plausibly survived better); Prince Charles Mountains approach.
- **Demonym:** Mawsonite.
- **Highway:** Hwy 4, between the Sayowa Junction and Sinheung (western terminus at the Sayowa Junction, not inside Sayowa).

Connects the Mawson subnet, the inland Concordia route, and the Halley subnet — "the whole continent's connectivity plausibly depends on it."

### Canon facts

- **The founding Japanese population departed after the Falkland Treaty; the site sat unoccupied.** It was later relocated via old accounts/manifests/maps by "Ice Cold Buddhism" pilgrims — a rediscovery structure genuinely parallel to Byrd's founding. **No human has set foot at Dome Fuji since; human presence is outright forbidden today** (alongside Kunlun), per `Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` — altitude/cold lethality, not anti-human sentiment.
- **Current population (55,072, 100% robot) IS the devotee population of Ice Cold Buddhism**, one of the faith's two primary sacred sites (with Kunlun). Nationally blended, drawn from every Tepenian city — no curated heritage, unlike Kunlun.
- **Economy §15 (canon):** Ice core science/archival research 40% *(TBD whether central or secondary)* · **Religious practice/pilgrimage infrastructure 35%** · Facility maintenance/altitude-legacy infrastructure 20% · Other 5%.
- **⭐ Historical supply lifeline (canon):** for a substantial stretch of history, supplied by two direct aviation routes — Troll (Halley subnet) and a Sinheung-area airstrip (via Mawson) — not a relay chain, two separate direct flights into an isolated city with essentially no Arcanet connectivity.
- **Arcanet:** near-zero connectivity at extreme-altitude locations — an infrastructure baseline, not a cultural choice.
- Devotees wear white robes, sparsely, to commune with the cold — works because robots don't suffer frostbite/hypothermia.
