## Vostok

**Status:** Survived, extremely remote · **Census I:** 129,617 humans / 259,644 robots / 389,261 residents · **Real-world basis:** Vostok Station (USSR/Russia), Pole of Inaccessibility, 3,488m · **D (difficulty):** 2.50 (tied-highest in the roster) · **Geology:** ICE-SHEET (bedrock ~4km down under Lake Vostok) — geologically forced importer

### Files found

|---|---|

| City_Symbol_Assignments | Pluto + Water (✅ canon, confirmed via robot-culture cross-ref) |
| Megasheet (Mega_Init/Full_Extrap/Cross_Ref/Community/Physical Infra), Vision Notes | exist, not deep-read |

### Canon facts
- Holds the coldest naturally-recorded surface temperature on Earth (−89.2°C, 1983); sits above Lake Vostok, sealed 15–25 million years, 4km down.
- **⭐ Founding-legend mismatch, deliberately unresolved:** founding population was Russian; current population (USA/Japan Primary, zero Russian representation at any tier) has **no documented connection** to the founders. No migration event explains it — "ambient, settled fact" per its own Local_Cultures §5.
- Russian survives only as a "liturgical language of science" — fixed technical identifiers, spoken by nobody, understood by all serious researchers.
- Reachable via Hwy 37 (Dome Fuji → Kunlun → Vostok → Concordia), not just the 1,260km trackless Mirny crossing.
- Research archive confirmed too vast for any single mind (human or robot) to hold.
- **Notable figure:** "Charlene" (XT-17), robot geneticist, confirmed recruitable companion, DLC 7 central character — reduced-mutation-rate breakthrough tied to Lake Vostok biology.
- **DLC hook:** Vostok is the mandatory launch point for Kunlun's DLC content (Charlene's discovery needs Kunlun's comms relay).
- Division of Industry (Local_Cultures §15, pre-rebuild): Science 65% / self-sufficiency 25% / other 10%.

### Robot culture 🔶 (provisional)
- Kinship: archive-domain inheritance across researchers who may never overlap in time — described as the "most purely condition-based kinship finding" in the whole run.
- Public/private divide organized by **time**, not space — "Window Courtesy" (don't interrupt someone's active task-window) is the real social gatekeeping mechanism.
- Robots task-driven, no fixed hours; genuine schedule divide from humans, not a social one.
- One real crack in the self-sufficiency myth: a genuine, non-transactional friendship with Kunlun (an aurora-letter exchange), one-sided in the historical record (only Kunlun's files mention the cold-record rivalry).

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Thinking / Withdrawn / Positive Outlook** — "as complete a Head-center identity as a city can have." Symbolic: Pluto + Water (depth, accumulated wisdom).

### District affinity (Concordia) ✅
Aquarius / Scorpio / Sagittarius — **flagged low-confidence**, since Vostok's own Megasheet is "mostly proposed extrapolation."

### Division-of-Industry status (this session's rebuild)
**Geologically-forced importer** (ice-sheet). Workforce 324,452. Owes 18,774 food-worker-years; **ruled export: scientific research (bioinformatics — the Cryptograph Helix basis)**, minimum 9.2% of distinctive tier. National balance: baseline ~37.4%, mandated (research) 5.8%, **free 56.8%**.

### Open threads
Founding-legend gap (what happened to the original Russian population) genuinely unresolved. Whether the Vostok–Mirny cable is intact. Charlene's full backstory TBD.

### Canon facts
- **⚠ Flagged for an eventual rename** — its national composition (China Primary, 24.24%; Russia only Significant, 7.22%) no longer matches the Russian ship/station the name and founding legend are built around. Not resolved.
- Sits within seconds of arc of the exact Antarctic Circle — minimal polar night AND minimal midnight sun (~4–5 days each), sun grazing the horizon without fully rising/setting. This is the city's central symbolic and civic fact — "the Threshold."
- Among the windiest coastal stations in Antarctica (sustained events >40 m/s); the city is architecturally built as its own windbreak — concentric rings, dense, residential woven into the industrial core rather than separated.
- **Subnet hub:** routes Zhongshan, Casey, Davis, Vostok, Kunlun to each other on the Arcanet (NOT to Concordia — different subnet, Split Brain severed that).
- **Economy — reassigned 2026-07-16:** the near-exclusive top-tier quarrying/industrial hub feeding raw materials to the Tri-Cities, specifically Sinheung's chamber fabrication. (Originally misattributed to Davis; corrected when Davis was resolved as agricultural.) Also supplies eastern-highway construction materials.
- Division of Industry (Local_Cultures §15, pre-rebuild): Communications/Arcanet ~20% / Technical-scientific ~20% / Industrial ~20% / Maritime ~15% / Commercial ~15% / Other ~10%.
- **National Capital Candidate #6** ("For: literal self-protection metaphor architecture, real industrial teeth. Against: 'rough,' minimal ceremonial infrastructure").

### Robot culture 🔶
- Kinship: shared everyday condition of living inside the windbreak ring (home/shelter barely distinguishable), not a single incident.
- Glitch-Coolant: positively placed in **working-class/potency** category (NOT bohemian/variety — deliberate differentiation from its own subnet-mate Zhongshan).
- A structural/wind-load-verification "creed" (trust tested conditions over inherited elegance) is Mirny's closest thing to belief — explicitly NOT devotional, consistent with §18's own "no dominant robot tradition."

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Instinctive / Withdrawn / Competency** — "engineered to turn inward," unforced pride needing no defense. Symbolic: Saturn + Earth (self-sufficient, structural, unshowy).

### District affinity ✅
Taurus / Libra / Virgo.

### Division-of-Industry status
**Provider, rock-founded grower.** Workforce 1,018,480. Canon mandate: Arcanet/communications 20% + industrial/quarrying hub role. Mandated 11.8%, **free 47.1%.**

### Open threads
The rename question. Post-war relay hardware status. Two placeholder figures (Bellandry, Marchenko).

## Zhongshan

**Status:** Damaged, partially operational · **Census II:** 522,372 humans / 474,312 robots / 996,684 · **Real-world basis:** Zhongshan Station (China/CHINARE), Larsemann Hills, Prydz Bay · **D:** 1.25 · **Geology:** ✅ ROCK-FOUNDED (Larsemann Hills ice-free oasis). Can grow.

### Files found
Specs, Local_Cultures, Local_Robot_Culture, Enneagram all read in full. City_Symbol_Assignments: **Saturn + Metal**. Megasheet, Vision Notes exist, not deep-read.

### Canon facts
- **⭐ Sinian defectors** — founding population "chose robots over their nation... they left their country, they did not leave their culture." One of the sharpest founding stories in the corpus.
- **The one deliberate exception to the rotating-national-operator founding model** used everywhere else: continuous Chinese/Sinian habitation is judged plausible through the entire First Interwar Period — an intentional exception, not an oversight.
- The **non-prying norm**: asking about someone's origins or private circumstances is a serious social error. Hospitality is "ceremony rather than warmth" — tea properly made, nothing asked.
- Central food object: the dumpling (jiaozi); "Dumpling Arguments" over the correct recipe are a local institution nobody expects to resolve.
- One of **three cities explicitly canon-named bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety Glitch-Coolant cities** (with Janbogo, Shirayuki).
- Division of Industry (pre-rebuild): Technical/scientific ~35% / Industrial ~25% / Marine extraction ~15% / Commercial ~15% / Education ~7% / Other ~3%.
- **National Capital Candidate #4** ("chaos that is actually structure reads almost like a governance metaphor").

### Robot culture 🔶
- The tea ceremony transfers to robots with **nothing to adapt** — "robot residents participate in the ritual without the beverage. The point was never the tea" — flagged as one of the only civic values in the entire Mirny-subnet run a robot inherits completely.
- Metal element's negative valence (rigidity, ungracious perfectionism) maps onto a real quiet cost of the non-prying norm: heritage erosion nobody checks on, because checking would violate the norm itself.

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Instinctive / Withdrawn / Competency** (shares Major Theme with Sinheung, opposite direction — quiet/self-contained vs. forceful/outward). Symbolic: Saturn + Metal.

### District affinity ✅
Libra / Virgo / Taurus.

### Division-of-Industry status
**H-flagged estimate, NOT canon — needs ruling.** My own Half A proposal: **Prydz Bay maritime port for the Tri-Cities, 20%** (Sinheung's chambers have to leave by sea). Mandated 11.8% at this estimate, **free 47.1%.**

### Open threads
The Zhongshan/Kunlun "two faces of Sinian Tepenia" relationship. Whether the 20% maritime-port mandate holds up to developer ruling — genuinely my own estimate, weakest-tier confidence alongside Dome Fuji's.

## Shirayuki

**Status:** Damaged, partially operational · **Census II:** 336,124 humans / 392,200 robots / 728,324 · **Real-world basis:** Bharati Station site (built by India; **no Indian/South Asian population ever settled in Tepenia — hard canon rule**), Larsemann Hills · **D:** 1.25 · **Geology:** ✅ ROCK-FOUNDED. Can grow.

### Files found
Specs, Local_Cultures, Local_Robot_Culture, Enneagram all read in full. City_Symbol_Assignments: **Uranus + Fire**. Megasheet, Vision Notes exist, not deep-read.

### Canon facts
- **One of only two Tepenian cities founded by Upper Earth diplomatic decree**, not organic settlement — the International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do (Japan/Korea/China as peers) allocated the empty Bharati site to Japan specifically to prevent a third Chinese claim in the cluster. (The other is Sinheung.)
- **Named 2026-07-08**: Shirayuki (白雪, "white snow"), replacing a long "Japanese Diplomatic Partition" placeholder.
- **Explicitly, canonically NOT founded for art/music** — "no artistic or musical intention whatsoever." The massive amateur art/music/fashion culture (Ashiato fashion, Glacier-pop music) developed entirely afterward, organically, over generations.
- Mildest climate of the Tri-Cities cluster; genuinely varied population draw (research/education/arts) rather than one dominant civic type.
- **Flagship university** (added 2026-07-30): large, technical sub-fields of art/design, draws students nationwide "not for ranking but for the culture and soul."
- Ayako Hayashi's **confirmed pre-Concordia origin city** (resolved 2026-07-20).

### Robot culture 🔶
- Kinship: Alumni Circuit Houses (graduates hosting newcomers) — a housing-based, self-perpetuating lineage.
- **⭐ Strongest cross-reference finding of the run:** the city's own founding arc (made by others' decree first, self-claimed after) rhymes exactly with every robot's individual existential arc (made/fabricated first, self-authored identity after) — giving Shirayuki's robots an unusually personal relationship to their own civic founding myth.

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Feeling / Compliant / Positive Outlook** — the one Feeling-centered city in a Gut-centered cluster; "by a wide margin the warmest of the three." Symbolic: Uranus + Fire.

### District affinity ✅
Leo / Aquarius / Pisces.

### Division-of-Industry status
**Provider.** Canon mandate: **Education 20%** (arts/music/fashion 25% is treated as **discretionary**, not mandated — the nation would not "suffer materially" without Shirayuki's specific arts scene). Mandated 11.8%, **free 47.3%.**

### Open threads
Whether the region-wide "Alternative Culture" reputation belongs to Shirayuki alone or genuinely extends to Sinheung/Zhongshan too — deliberately left open pending both cities' megasheets.

## Davis

**Status:** Survived, operational · **Census II:** 437,423 humans / 344,173 robots / 781,596 (a genuine human-majority reversal, rare in this corpus) · **Real-world basis:** Davis Station (Australia), Vestfold Hills, Prydz Bay · **D:** 1.25 · **Geology:** ✅ ROCK-FOUNDED — the Vestfold Hills are the **largest ice-free coastal oasis in Antarctica**, ~400 km². Can grow, and grows more than anyone.

### Files found
Specs, Local_Cultures, Local_Robot_Culture, Enneagram all read in full. City_Symbol_Assignments: **Earth already established (pre-existing)**. Megasheet, Vision Notes exist, not deep-read.

### Canon facts
- Named for John King Davis — a ship's captain, not an explorer; "a navigator and enabler, not a flag-planter." Explicitly not a Tepenian Saint (Saints are pre-2083 explorers, not supporting mariners).
- **⭐⭐ "The Breadbasket of Tepenia"** — ruled 2026-07-16, reassigning the mining role to Mirny to resolve a direct conflict. Davis is Tepenia's primary agricultural/limnological research city, the food model's central anchor throughout this session's whole Division-of-Industry pass.
- Genuinely varied terrain within one city footprint: rock, freshwater lakes, hypersaline lakes, fjord inlets — each with its own ice chemistry and calendar.
- **Libraries openly, celebratedly welcome smuggled Upper Earth books** — confirmed also true at Zukelli, Janbogo, Esperanza; Sanay a likely candidate.
- Division of Industry (Local_Cultures §15, post-rework): **Agriculture 35%** / Technical-scientific 25% / Maritime 15% / Commercial 15% / Industrial 5% / Other 5%.

### Robot culture 🔶
- Kinship: **collective research credit**, not teaching-lineage — Ratna Wirawan's founding-era decision to decline solo credit for a discovery set the norm; a robot's "family" is whoever she shares discovery-credit with.
- Glitch-Coolant: genuine muted absence — no drinking-culture institution appears anywhere in the source material, consistent with the "let the work speak" ethos.
- Earth element's negative valence (stagnation, hoarding) maps onto a real internal fault line: satisfied quiet-competence vs. resentment of a *permanent* support role — Davis could plausibly have been an assertive port city and chose humility instead.

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Instinctive / Withdrawn / Competency**. Symbolic: Earth (confirmed pre-existing pick, "the breadbasket").

### District affinity ✅
Virgo / Taurus / Capricorn.

### Division-of-Industry status
**Provider — the national food anchor** throughout this session's rebuild. Canon mandate: Agriculture 35%. Mandated 20.7%, **free 38.5%.**

### Open threads
The hypersaline lakes' scientific/economic use. Its own vision-notes originally framed it as mining-dominant, since corrected.

## Sinheung

**Status:** Damaged, partially operational · **Census II:** 437,854 humans / 450,438 robots / 888,292 · **Real-world basis:** physical infrastructure only from Russia's Progress Station; **founding population is Korean**, via Jeju-do decree — Russia was never a founding claimant · **D:** 1.25 · **Geology:** ✅ ROCK-FOUNDED. Can grow.

### Files found
Specs, Local_Cultures, Local_Robot_Culture, Enneagram all read in full. City_Symbol_Assignments: **Uranus + Electricity**. Megasheet, Vision Notes exist, not deep-read.

### Canon facts
- **Named 2026-07-14: Sinheung** (신흥/新興, "newly rising/emerging"), replacing the long-standing "Soyuz" placeholder, which was never the founders' own choice.
- **The other of the two Jeju-do-decree cities** (with Shirayuki) — but its own founding tension is sharper and never fully resolves: "Claimed, Not Found." Legitimacy here is continuously reproven through output, never simply settled.
- **⭐⭐ Industrial fabrication 45% — the highest single sector in the entire Division-of-Industry corpus.** One of only a handful of Tepenian cities (with Byrd) that manufactures the fabrication-synthesis chambers that create robots, built to a Mark IV schematic **designed at Neumayer**.
- **The Sinheung Institute of Cybernetics and Robotic Care** — one of only THREE national medical/care institutes in the Federation (with Esperanza and Belgrano), the sole source of trained robot-care personnel nationwide. Its defining focus is explicitly "the physical AND emotional care of, and for, robots — not a repair school."
- "The Sinheung Standard" (2026-08-31, promoted directly to canon): a shared, informally-enforced reputation for quality across the quarry trade, chamber output, and the guarded Mark IV schematic archive.
- A "deep, genuinely unusual" civic patriotism, explicitly flagged in canon as an outlier against Tepenia's usual quieter post-national temperament.

### Robot culture 🔶
- A robot fabricated at Sinheung carries a "doubly-layered earned-not-inherited identity" — built to an externally-designed schematic, by a workforce whose own standing depends on continuous output.
- Electricity element's negative valence (instability, total dependency on a power source) is explicitly noted as "a literal echo of Amundsen Tower's own story," mapping onto the city's quiet anxiety about a treaty-secured claim's fragility.
- Courtship: **public, community-witnessed** demonstration of capability — the opposite of Zhongshan's next-door private restraint.

### Design-tool readings 🛠
Enneagram: **Instinctive / Assertive / Reactive** — shares only with Troll in the whole project; forceful, outward-projecting, never fully settled. Symbolic: Uranus + Electricity ("the spark that makes matter conscious").

### District affinity ✅
Capricorn / Aquarius / Libra (corrected 2026-07-31 from an earlier Aquarius-led pick).

### Division-of-Industry status
**Provider.** Canon mandate: Fabrication 45% (highest in corpus) + the robotic-care institute. Mandated 26.7%, **free 32.6%** — among the least-free cities in the subnet.

### Open threads
Whether Sinheung's own Zhongshan-contrast pattern (assertive vs. withdrawn) generalizes further.

## ⭐ Subnet-level patterns worth carrying forward

1. **Rock-founded, and it shows.** All 7 of Mirny subnet's populated cities (Vostok excepted — ice-sheet) sit on real ice-free oases (Vestfold Hills, Larsemann Hills, Mabus Point outcrops) — the subnet is geologically the most "growable" in the Federation outside the Peninsula.
2. **The Larsemann Hills Tri-Cities cluster (Zhongshan/Sinheung/Shirayuki)** is the tightest three-way founding-diplomacy knot in the corpus — all three trace to the same Jeju-do court decision, and their Enneagram reads span all three Hornevian registers (Withdrawn/Assertive/Compliant) from the same founding mechanism.
3. **National capital candidates cluster here** (Zhongshan #4, Mirny #6) — worth remembering if that question ever resurfaces.
4. **Two of Tepenia's only two 100%-robot cities are one subnet apart** (Kunlun here, Dome Fuji in Mawson) — and their own source material frames their relationship as friction, not natural kinship ("Dome Fuji's resident objects to being treated as a data point").
5. **The reciprocal-obligation table's H-flagged risk is concentrated in this subnet**: Zhongshan's 20% maritime-port mandate is genuinely my own estimate, not canon — the weakest link if this reference feeds a per-city write-up.
