# City Symbolic Substrate

**Status: official reference framework, promoted from `to-be-integrated/` 2026-08-30.** This is the same
kind of foundational, load-bearing symbol layer that `Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/` provides for Concordia's
twelve districts, built for Tepenia's 35 outer cities instead. It was already being cited as settled reference
material by multiple `Local_Robot_Culture/` city files before this promotion — the promotion formalizes that
working status by moving the source files out of staging, not by changing what they say. **Like any other
canon reference in this project, it remains open to future revision** — promotion out of `to-be-integrated/`
means "confirmed working reference," not "frozen forever."

**Two independent symbol systems, paired per-city:**
- `Planetary_Symbols.md` — the nine planets (Mercury through Pluto) plus a tenth symbol, the Asteroid Belt.
  Invented from scratch rather than drawn from real-world astrology; each planet's meaning is instead grounded
  in a real, verifiable astronomical or physical fact about that specific planet.
- `Robot_Elementals.md` — eight elements: the Chinese Wu Xing five (Earth, Fire, Water, Wood, Metal) plus Air
  (the Greek/Western import, kept deliberately outside the Wu Xing correspondence system rather than forced
  into an artificial slot), plus two "robot elements" grounded in real physics rather than any existing
  tradition (Electricity, Electromagnetism).
- `City_Symbol_Assignments.md` — a Planet + Element pair for each of the 35 outer cities (34 assigned; Abowasa
  excluded pending its own founding-nation fix), each pairing derived from and justified against that city's
  own already-established personality — cross-checked specifically against the three-axis reads in the
  sibling `City_Enneagram_Personalities/` folder, not assigned arbitrarily.

**Relationship to the Concordia district system.** Structurally parallel to `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/
Concordia-City/Districts/Zodiac_Personality_Substrate/`, but a distinct system for a distinct scale — this one
covers Tepenia's outer cities, not Concordia's districts, and uses Planet+Element pairs rather than zodiac
signs. The two should not be cross-applied to each other's territory.

**Relationship to the subnet-scale system.** `Storyline/DLC-Questlines/Subnet_Symbolic_Associations.md` is a
separate, subnet-scale (not city-scale) symbolic layer — five multi-city subnets plus Byrd and Amundsen, using
Elementals and a "Six Perfections" system. It predates and partly sources this folder's `Robot_Elementals.md`
(the Wu Xing five and Electricity were originally drafted there) and remains in place at its own scale; it was
not folded into this promotion, since it addresses a different unit of geography with its own DLC-questline
context.

**Already in active use.** At least a dozen `Local_Robot_Culture/` city files across Halley, Palmer, Mirny,
Janbogo, Mawson, and Byrd subnets already cite their city's Planet+Element pairing as established reference
material — this promotion updates those citations to point here rather than to the old `to-be-integrated/`
paths.

## Distribution and Overlap

With only 10 planetary symbols and 8 elements across 34 assigned cities, some repetition is mathematically
inevitable — see `City_Symbol_Assignments.md`'s own distribution notes for the exact counts. As with the
Zodiac substrate's own overlap cases, a handful of repeats are flagged as deliberate echoes of already-
established kinship rather than coincidence (Kunlun, Vostok, and Dome Fuji all sharing Pluto mirrors their
already-established extreme-isolation kinship and Ice-Cold Buddhism connection).
