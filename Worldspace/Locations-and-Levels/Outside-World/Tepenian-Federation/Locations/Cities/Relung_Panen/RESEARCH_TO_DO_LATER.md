## 2 · Geology and tunnels: research note

**Developer, 2026-10-26:** research the **geological composition of the area for the possibility of tunnels that go under the ice and possibly through rock and land.**

**Questions to research (real-world only):**
1. **The rock.** What is the bedrock of the Bunger Hills and the surrounding Knox Coast (the ground research found gneiss under gravelly till): rock types, strength, jointing and fracturing, faults, weathering depth, any published geotechnical or structural-geology work (Australian and Russian geological surveys, the Bunger Hills geology literature, Antarctic geological maps).
2. **The ground and ice.** Ice thickness and bed topography around the oasis and along the Casey route; permafrost depth and temperature; ice-shelf thickness and what lies beneath it; subglacial water and lakes; where the oasis's rock continues under the ice (airborne radar, BedMachine, ice-penetrating radar surveys).
3. **Tunnels in ice.** What has actually been built and how it behaves: Camp Century (Greenland), the ice tunnels at McMurdo and at South Pole, the Jungfraujoch, and other cut-and-cover or mined ice tunnels; creep and closure rates of ice at different temperatures; maintenance and lifetimes.
4. **Tunnels in rock and permafrost.** Polar rock tunnelling precedents (Svalbard mines, Norwegian and Greenland tunnels, the Alaska and Siberian permafrost tunnels), costs, methods, ventilation and heat, and failure cases.
5. **Under-glacier and under-shelf routes.** Whether anything has ever been tunnelled beneath a glacier or ice shelf; what is known about crevasse-zone avoidance by going below the surface.
6. **Routes of interest, as questions only:** within the oasis (rock between districts); from the oasis toward the coast or the shelf front; toward Casey (the road ruling is a single spur; this is a possibility to examine, not a ruling).

**Related files:** `../Research_Logs/Relung_Panen_Port_Research_2026-10-25_C_Casey_to_Bunger_Hills_Terrain.md` (distances, glaciers, oasis area figures) and `…_Soil_Research_2026-10-25_E_Oasis_Ground_Water_and_Light.md` (bedrock, till, permafrost, lakes).
