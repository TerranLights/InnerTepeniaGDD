## 3.1 What the deviations say — LAW F

- **⭐ Vostok's robot maintenance (13.0%) exceeds its food + healthcare + education combined (5.0%).** At 67%
  robot — well above the ~51% national average — **Vostok spends more keeping its machines running than
  keeping its people alive, fed and schooled.** That is not a rounding artifact; it is the city's character
  falling out of the census.
- **⭐ Kunlun has no schools, no clinics and no farms, and 17.1% robot maintenance** — its single largest
  sector after thermal. **A city that has no need of childhood.**
- **⭐ {{Abowasa}}'s education (3.1%) is the highest share of baseline in the set** — 5.8% of its civic load,
  against Casey's 4.0% and Vostok's 2.0%. **Exactly the predicted rotational signature: adults rotate out,
  children do not, so the school inflates as a share of in-city labor.** The sweep's matrix already recorded
  education as {{Abowasa}}'s *only* named necessary industry. **The model reproduced a canon fact it was not
  told about.** Strongest single validation in this run.
- **Vostok's science: 65% → ~25%.** Rescaling its existing Distinctive entries into the 29% envelope. Still
  overwhelmingly its dominant distinctive sector. **The sweep's §3 "purpose-dominant" concern resolves without
  a separate pass**, as predicted.
- **Cold and wind run INVERSELY in this corpus** — the coldest cities (Vostok, Kunlun) are the calmest, the
  mild coastal cities are the windiest. **Real Antarctic physics** (plateau domes are wind minima; katabatic
  drainage accelerates at the coast). It partially damps the spread, and it means **wind is a differentiator
  that runs opposite to every other driver** — worth exploiting rather than smoothing.

# 4. Problems found — recorded per Step 9.5, not smoothed over

1. **⚠ THE FAT MIDDLE IS STILL UNTESTED, and this is the run's real weakness.** Casey was selected as the
   "deliberately ordinary" city required by LAW C. **It landed at the floor (40%), not the middle** — −9.3 °C,
   no polar night at all, and the best highway connectivity in the corpus make it one of the mildest cities in
   Tepenia, not a typical one. **The instrument is now validated at both extremes and nowhere in between**,
   which is precisely the LAW C failure the fourth pilot slot existed to prevent. **A genuine mid-pack city
   must be run before the other 31.** Candidates: Neumayer, Sejong, Denison, Princess Elisabeth.
2. **⚠ The instrument was changed mid-run.** B4 coolant/siligel had no explicit weight; it is marked `FOLD`
   into B3 in the register but the model never said at what magnitude. **Assigned W = 10, scaling with robot
   share, during computation.** This is a real instrument change made while using it, and it materially drives
   Kunlun's and Vostok's headline results. **It needs to be written back into `01_Burden_Scoring_Model.md` §3
   as a decision, not left as an artifact of this run.**
3. **⚠ Existing §15 entries cannot simply be rescaled — they need deconfliction first.** Cape Adare's
   `Technical / scientific: 20%` explicitly names *medicine* among its contents, which now **double-counts
   against B2 Human healthcare.** Rescaling alone would preserve the overlap. **Every city's existing entries
   must be read for baseline content before being mapped into the Distinctive tier.** This is per-city hand
   work and it is not cheap.
4. **The envelope was fitted to this set, not to the corpus.** Casey anchors the 40% floor and Kunlun the 75%
   ceiling *because they are the extremes of these five*. If a milder Peninsula city (Palmer City, Signy,
   Esperanza) or a harsher case turns up outside that band, **the mapping is re-fitted and every prior figure
   moves.** Treat all numbers above as provisional until the full set is scored.
