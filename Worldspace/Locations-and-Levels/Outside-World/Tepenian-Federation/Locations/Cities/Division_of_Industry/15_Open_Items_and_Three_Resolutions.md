# ✅ RESOLUTION 1 — THE FUNGI TIER, SIZED

**Substrate is the binding input: 769,849 t/yr of dry fibrous crop residue** *(`11`)* —
the stream pigs physiologically cannot digest.

| Biological efficiency | Fresh yield | Calories | Protein | **Vitamin D** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 45% *(low)* | 346,432 t | 0.65% | 1.91% | 52.0 kg |
| **60% *(mid — use this)*** | **461,909 t** | **0.87%** | **2.55%** | **69.3 kg** |
| 90% *(high)* | 692,864 t | 1.30% | 3.82% | 103.9 kg |

*(Biological efficiency of 45–166% is documented for* Pleurotus *on straw and cereal
residues; 60% is a conservative working figure. Fresh mushrooms are ~90% water, ~300
kcal/kg, ~33 g protein/kg.)*

**Labour at ~25 t per FTE-year** *(commercial mushroom growing is hand-harvested and
genuinely labour-intensive)*: **18,476 FTE — 0.08% of the national workforce.**

## ⭐⭐ The verdict: it is not a calorie tier, and pretending otherwise would be wrong

> ## **~0.9% of national calories. ~2.5% of protein. Mushrooms are water.**
>
> ## **BUT: national vitamin D requirement is 114 kg/yr, and UV-treated fungi at BE=60%
> supply 69.3 kg — SIXTY-ONE PERCENT OF IT.**

> ### **So the fungal tier is not a food tier. It is TEPENIA'S VITAMIN D SYSTEM — and
> it produces it from waste, in the dark, at zero lighting cost, in tunnels the
> rock-founded cities are excavating anyway.**

**⚠ Which means it should be sited and staffed as a PUBLIC HEALTH function**, alongside
marine oils, rather than as agriculture. *(Recall the measured baseline: deficiency ran
10% → 35% over a polar winter at Neumayer and McMurdo — both Tepenian cities.)*

**⏸️ Open:** whether the residue stream is genuinely available *(it competes with the
ruminant tier for the same fibrous material — `11` allocated it to ruminants first)*.
**The two tiers must split 769,849 t between them, and nobody has ruled the split.**

# ✅ RESOLUTION 2 — CAPE ADARE'S PHOSPHATE: a stock, not a flow

**And the distinction is the entire finding.**

## Sustainable annual yield

**~250,000 breeding pairs at Cape Adare / Ridley Beach**, ~500,000 birds, ~50 g dry
guano/bird/day across a ~120-day season → **~3,000 t/yr of guano at 8–12% P₂O₅:**

> ### **~240–360 t P₂O₅ per year, sustainably. Call it 300.**

| Against | |
|---|--:|
| National make-up need *(77% recycling)* | 4,067 t/yr → **~7%** |
| National gross uptake | 17,682 t/yr → **~1.7%** |

## The accumulated deposit

| Colony area × depth | Deposit | **P₂O₅ stock at ~10%** | **Years of make-up** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 0.2 km² × 0.5 m | 100,000 t | 10,000 t | **2.5** |
| 0.2 km² × 1.5 m | 300,000 t | 30,000 t | **7** |
| 0.5 km² × 1.5 m | 750,000 t | 75,000 t | **18** |

## ⭐⭐ The dilemma, stated exactly

> ## **Tepenia can buy roughly 7 to 18 years of phosphate independence by mining out
> Cape Adare's accumulated deposit — ONCE.**
>
> ## **And at the end of it, it has neither the deposit NOR the colony that made it,
> because the sustainable flow is only ~7% of what the nation needs.**

**⛔ So the developer's "marginal" is confirmed on the flow and dramatically understated
on the temptation.** **It is a one-time windfall wearing the costume of a resource** —
which is precisely the Nauru and Christmas Island pattern already noted in `14`, arriving
inside Tepenia's own borders.

> ### ⭐ **This is the sharpest form of the import-substitution story: a nation held by
> the throat over a mineral, sitting on a deposit that would free it for a decade and
> cost it a bird colony and every year after that. Both sides of the argument are
> correct, and that is what makes it usable.**

**⚠ Cape Adare now carries three roles, none of which is "fishing town":** port and
shipping · marine science · **strategic mineral city with a decision to make.**

# ✅ RESOLUTION 3 — SEED VAULT SITING

## The governing fact, and it inverts the real-world problem

**Svalbard maintains −18 °C artificially, with permafrost as passive backup at −3 to
−4 °C.** **The Antarctic plateau is already far below seed-storage temperature:**

| Site | Annual mean |
|---|--:|
| **Kunlun** *(Dome A, ~4,090 m)* | coldest in the roster |
| **Dome Fuji** *(~3,810 m)* | ~−54 °C |
| Vostok | ~−55 °C |
| Concordia *(Dome C)* | **−52.7 °C** *(canon)* |

> ## ⭐⭐ **On the Tepenian plateau, keeping seeds cold costs NOTHING. The energy is spent
> keeping the room warm enough for someone to work in.** **That is the exact inverse of
> Svalbard's problem, and it is the whole siting argument.**

## ✅ Recommended: TWO sites, because the criteria genuinely conflict

**A deep archive wants cold and isolation. A working collection wants access. No single
site is both.**

### 1. ⭐⭐ DEEP ARCHIVE — **Dome Fuji** *(Mawson subnet)*

- **~−54 °C annual mean — passive storage, no refrigeration, indefinitely.**
- **⭐⭐ ZERO HUMANS.** Dome Fuji is robot-only. **A city of robots, who do not eat,
  keeping the seeds of everything humans eat.** **No population to endanger, no
  population to evacuate, and no conflict of interest with a food store.**
- Canon already gives the plateau cities *"basically no Arcanet"* — **isolation is a
  FEATURE for an archive**, not a liability.
- On the Hwy 22 / Hwy 37 network, so retrievable by road when it must be.

*(**Kunlun** is the alternative and is colder still — Dome A is the highest and coldest
site on the continent. **Dome Fuji is chosen for marginally better access; Kunlun is the
better vault and the worse door.**)*

### 2. ⭐ WORKING COLLECTION — **Esperanza** *(Palmer subnet)*

- **The DRQ-11 ruling already put the *Deschampsia antarctica* breeding program and its
  imported comparative genetic stock here.** **The working collection should live where
  the breeding happens.**
- Mildest site in the Federation, best access, and the city's real-world basis is a
  **civilian settlement with families and a school** — the right texture for an
  institution that must be maintained across generations.
- **⚠ Requires active refrigeration** at +1.5 °C January mean. **That is the cost of
  accessibility, and it is the correct place to pay it.**

### ⏸️ 3. A third, once founded — **{{Bunger Hills City}}**

**The second agricultural region should hold a copy of what it grows.** Deferred until
the city exists.

## ⭐ The design principle worth keeping

> ## **The archive is passive, cold, robot-kept and hard to reach. The collection is
> warm, staffed, human and easy to reach. Losing either one does not lose the seeds.**
>
> **And note what that arrangement says: the deepest guarantee of human food in Tepenia
> is held by a city with no humans in it.**

# ✅ RESOLUTION 4 — THE RECIPROCAL-OBLIGATION TERM. List A item 1, closed.

## ⭐⭐ The fix is a THREE-way split, not a reciprocal charge on importers

**The problem was framed as "importers get food free."** Working it through, that is a

MANDATED provider work — *"a stevedore at Sanay is not there by preference; the
Federation needs a port"* — and the model never separated it out.**

> ## **So the character budget was overstated for EVERY city, not only for importers.**

| Tier | What it is | Free? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. BASELINE** | local necessities — the city keeps itself alive | ⛔ no |
