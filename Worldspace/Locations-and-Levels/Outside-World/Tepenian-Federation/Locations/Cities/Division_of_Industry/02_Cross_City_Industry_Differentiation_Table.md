# Cross-City Industry Differentiation Table

**Created 2026-09-01, empty and unfilled.** The mandatory anti-convergence guard for the necessary-industries
bulk pass (`Canon_Gap_Resolution_Method/05_Bulk_Mode_for_Repeated_Shape_Gaps.md` **B6**).

> ## ⚠ THE ONE RULE
>
> ### Cells hold **the local form in one phrase — never the percentage.**
>
> **Two cells in a row that read the same mean at least one of them is wrong.** The number is in
> `01_Burden_Scoring_Model.md`; this table exists to answer a different question — *is this city's version of
> this industry actually distinguishable from the other thirty-five?*

**Why this file exists at all.** It is the direct analog of
`Concordia-City/Districts/Cross_District_Differentiation_Table.md`, which exists because **thirteen districts
quietly converged without it and Gate 6b failed once as a result.** Nothing about thirty-six cities makes that
risk smaller.

**Binding procedure, inherited verbatim from the district rule:**

1. **Read the relevant row BEFORE writing that industry for any city.**
2. **Add the city's column in the SAME COMMIT that completes it.** Not afterward, not in a cleanup pass.
3. **A collision is resolved by changing the content, not by rewording the cell.** If two cities genuinely do
   an industry the same way, one of them needs a different answer — or a stated reason why sameness is correct
   here *(e.g. a level-1 universal per LAW D, which is legitimate and should be marked as such)*.
