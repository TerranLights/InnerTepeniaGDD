- **B. [port]** *Geography/access: zone gap 0; reached across the Drake Passage from Ushuaia (the primary port, 984 km); Uruguay's Río de la Plata coast, about 3,100 km to the north-northeast, is zone-aligned and open to the South Atlantic.*

- **D.** *Zone gap 0; Río de la Plata, about 3,100 km.*

# ROUND 2: updated wording from the web-search RERUN (2026-10-03, later the same day)

**Why a second round.** The first pass above was written when web search was unavailable. The three RERUN logs (`Research_Logs/Founding_Gateway_Research_A2_…`, `B2_…`, `C2_…`) used about 250 searches and primary sources (the Japanese ministry's JARE-66 plan, NGA Pub. 151, Malaysia's DOSM table, Australian Antarctic Division schedules, INDEC and INE censuses, IPEV, IAATO). Each log ends with one **updated one-line basis** per row, reproduced below as **option E**. **Rounds 1 and 2 are all kept.** Nothing is ruled; the Register's Basis column is unchanged.

**Facts that changed since Round 1 (they affect options A–D above):**
- Ushuaia's population is **79,409** (city, INDEC 2022 definitive; 80,371 for the department). The earlier 79,538 and 89,606 are wrong or a 2026 estimate. Río Grande **99,241**; Tierra del Fuego Province **185,732**. Punta Arenas **132,363** confirmed (INE 2024). Puerto Williams **1,608** (2024 town; 1,868 in 2017); the "2,874 with naval personnel" has no source.
- The Cape Horn–Antarctica "650 km" is unsupported; land-to-land is about **815–840 km** (BAS bathymetry about 850 km for the passage).
- Punta Arenas to King George by air is **about 2 h by jet** (DAP), **2.5–3 h by Hercules**; "about 4 h" has no support.
- Crossing times (sourced): Ushuaia to the South Shetlands **about 2 days**; Ushuaia to Port Lockroy **about 4 days elapsed**; Punta Arenas to King George about 60 h by the Chilean Navy icebreaker; Cape Town to the Weddell Sea ice edge 10 days.
- **Montevideo** is a logistics node (Uruguayan Hercules and ship; a 2025 call by RRS Sir David Attenborough), **not** a regular Peninsula cruise departure. Wikipedia's "Ushuaia serves only Argentina's program" is contradicted (Spain's Hespérides made 3 of 5 logistic calls there in 2019–20).
- **Signy** is reached by sea only; no Brazilian, South African or Uruguayan operational link to the South Orkneys was found.
- **Zukelli:** a Hobart air link (AAD A319, 3,650 km, flown 2018) and a Hobart sea route (Korea's Araon, 11 days, 2015) are documented; the "not how the bay is served" caution was too strong. Ross Island is **due south** of Lyttelton (bearing 182°).
- **Japan's gateway:** the Shirase goes via **Fremantle**; the advance party flies via **Cape Town**; the Umitaka Maru returns via **Hobart** (Feb 2024 and 2025), so "no Hobart call found" was wrong. Japan reached Adélie Land waters once (2007–08 survey).
- **Hobart to Cape Denison:** 2,600 km (AAD 2006) and 2,670 km (NMA) match the computed 2,700 km. Commonwealth Bay access is **intermittent** (the Douglas Mawson's first sailing was blocked in Dec 2025).
- **Winds:** Cape Denison averages about 70–75 km/h (not 80); Dumont d'Urville's official record is 214.9 km/h (the "320 km/h" is not official).
- **Bunger Hills is on the Knox Coast of Wilkes Land** (AADC gazetteer); "Queen Mary" belongs only to the Shackleton Ice Shelf in front. A **ship route to the ice-shelf front exists** (Nuyina, March 2025, 2,060 nm from Hobart, about a week); the oasis is reached by air or helicopter. **No ship route from Southeast Asia to Antarctica exists in any source**; the Sumatra match is geometric only.
