# Halley Subnet — Cross-City Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors that pass through multiple Halley subnet cities — not things the cities have in common (see Cross-City Patterns for that), but chains showing how a fact in one city leads through another to a consequence in a third, sometimes reaching outside the subnet entirely. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Throughway 1: Sanay → Troll → Halley — one contested airfield, three cities' fates tied together

**Sanay is the Halley subnet's actual supply-chain entry point, receiving incoming South African freighter shipments** (Sanay Full Extrapolation, Section II) → **from Sanay, goods split two ways: trucked directly to nearby cities via Hwy 7, or trucked onward to Troll, whose airfield flies goods out to more remote parts of Tepenia** (Sanay Full Extrapolation, Section II) → **Troll has nothing to fly out without Sanay's incoming freight, and Sanay has no way to reach the subnet's more remote destinations without Troll's airfield — a direct, material mutual dependency** (Sanay Full Extrapolation, Section II) → **Troll Airfield's own control is genuinely fractured among competing factions, no single authority holding all of it** (Troll Full Extrapolation, Section I) → **Halley, having deliberately forgone its own docks and airstrip, depends entirely on overland resupply through Belgrano and Sanay, meaning Halley determines nothing about who controls Troll but depends completely on the outcome** (Halley Full Extrapolation, Section I) → **making Halley's own survival hostage to an ongoing, unresolved conflict between two other cities entirely, with Halley's own supply situation serving as a direct, visible barometer of how that fractured control is actually playing out at any given moment** (Troll Cross-Reference, Finding 1).

## Throughway 2: Neumayer's inherited equipment branches into three of Tepenia's most consequential engineering legacies, spanning its entire history

**Neumayer inherited a full settlement's worth of scientific and engineering equipment from the original AWI research station, essentially from the moment of its own founding** (Neumayer Mega-Init, Hard Facts) → **in its own very first years, before that capacity had matured into anything resembling a reputation, a small Belgrano crew arrived driving a crude first-generation tracked hauler, hoping to reach a triangulated location deep in the West Antarctic interior; Neumayer's founding-era scientists reverse-engineered it and redesigned it into something that could survive the distance, and the rebuilt vehicle became the template for every Rastra built across Tepenia since** *(established 2026-07-10)* → **generations later, once that same engineering capacity had grown into the most capable in the entire Halley subnet, it produced the schematics and structural planning behind the Amundsen Tower itself** (Neumayer Mega-Init, "What It Feels Like") → **and more recently still, the same lineage produced the current-generation ("Mark IV") fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic — the design the Cradle's two active manufacturing sites (a currently-unnamed Korean city and Byrd) actually build from today** (Neumayer Cross-Reference, Finding 1, confirmed as fact) → **none of the three — not the Rastra, not the Tower, not the chamber — carries Neumayer's name forward anywhere in the country** (Neumayer Mega-Init, One-Line Pitch; Neumayer Local Cultures) → **meaning a single city's own inherited research equipment quietly became the structural ancestor of a nationwide vehicle everyone drives, the country's greatest engineering achievement, and the literal ongoing mechanism of robot creation, across three different eras of its own history, with nobody standing at any of the three results aware of where it actually came from.**

## Throughway 3: Belgrano's own contribution to Byrd runs through Neumayer, and sits alongside Palmer subnet's — the same city owes two different subnets the same kind of debt

**Belgrano's crews had built a rough first-generation hauler, but nothing tested for the distance the Byrd crossing would require, so a small crew drove it north to Neumayer for a redesign before the expedition itself even began** *(established 2026-07-10; see Throughway 2)* → **the rebuilt vehicle — the true first Arrastradora, ancestor of the international "Rastra" — is what an all-robot crew then drove out from Belgrano to discover the buried ruins of Byrd Station** (Belgrano Mega-Init, "What It Feels Like") → **that expedition also used maps drawn from Belgrano's own archives, with material support shipped in from Palmer City and Rothera — two Palmer subnet cities** (Belgrano Mega-Init, "What It Feels Like") → **this means Byrd's own present-day rediscovery-story identity traces back to at least four different cities across two entirely separate subnets: Belgrano's own expedition and archives and Neumayer's own quiet redesign (both Halley subnet), and Palmer City's and Rothera's own contributions (Palmer subnet, per this Ultra-Megasheet's own Palmer subnet Throughways)** → **making Byrd, once both subnets' own Ultra-Megasheets are read together, a genuinely multi-subnet creation — found by one subnet's own initiative, engineered by a second city within that same subnet, and supplied and mapped with help from a second subnet entirely — with Byrd's own established culture currently reflecting none of these debts explicitly.**

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 3 is the most significant item in this file, because it's the first concrete evidence that Byrd's own unacknowledged debts — already flagged as a strong Palmer subnet finding — actually run deeper than one subnet alone, and deeper than a single act of generosity. Byrd owes its own existence to at least four cities across two subnets: one that searched, one that quietly rebuilt the vehicle that got there, and two more that supplied the records that pointed the way — a fact worth carrying forward explicitly once the promised full re-examination pass, informed by the Questline material, eventually happens.
