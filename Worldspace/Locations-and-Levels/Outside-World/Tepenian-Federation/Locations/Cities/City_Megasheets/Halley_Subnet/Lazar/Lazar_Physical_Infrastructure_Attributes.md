# Lazar — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Lazar would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Lazar.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Lazar.md`, and `Lazar_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Lazar_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** the city's newer district is referred to
below only as "the Overflow District," a purely descriptive placeholder — the real "Maitri" name is banned
from carrying forward per this project's own No-Subcontinentals canon, and the existing source files'
repeated use of it anyway is a flagged, unresolved inconsistency, not something to propagate further here.

**Governing facts:** Tepenia's single largest city by population (~1.8-2.6 million), both pre- and
post-war; genuinely coalesced from two originally separate settlements — an older, denser core (inherited
from the real, continuously-operated Novolazarevskaya Station) and a newer, sprawling expansion (built on
the unoccupied former Maitri site); sits in the Schirmacher Oasis, ice-free terrain with genuine summer
meltwater lakes; status is damaged but partially operational — survived heavy Long Night War bombardment
specifically *because* of its own sheer scale, a form of survival unique to Lazar among Tepenian cities;
maglev and elevated transit form the primary transit network; visible megacity-scale commercial density
(holographic advertising, constant activity) exists, though what actually sustains an economy that large is
explicitly flagged as still needing real development.

---

## Twin-Settlement Coalescence Infrastructure

1. **A visibly distinct twin-district urban layout** — the Old Core Institutional Quarter (established,
   denser, Novolazarevskaya-descended) and the Overflow District (established, newer and more sprawling) —
   physically legible evidence of the city's own two-settlement origin.
2. **Connective development infrastructure bridging the two originally separate settlements**, built as
   they physically and socially coalesced into one continuous urban area over successive generations.

## Megacity-Scale Transit Infrastructure

3. **A maglev and elevated transit network** (established) — the primary transit mode, genuinely distinct
   from the ground-level transit most other Tepenian cities examined in this pass rely on.
4. **Transit infrastructure scaled to Tepenia's single largest population** — the only maglev system found
   in this pass so far, a materially different order of magnitude from any other DLC city's own transit
   needs.

## Ice-Free Oasis Terrain (Schirmacher Oasis)

5. **Foundation and structural engineering on exposed bedrock**, the same general oasis-terrain problem
   Davis, Shirayuki, and Zhongshan all face, but here at genuine megacity scale rather than a
   moderate-sized city's footprint.
6. **Meltwater lake infrastructure** — the Schirmacher Oasis's own genuine summer liquid water, a
   distinctive resource and visual feature unique to this city within the Halley subnet.

## Highway Junction Commercial Infrastructure

7. **The Hwy 7 / Belgrano Extension Junction Markets** (established) — massive commercial density built
   directly around the convergence of both highways.
8. **Holographic advertisement and multicolored lighting infrastructure** (established) — genuine
   megacity-scale commercial signaling, visually distinct from any other city examined in this pass.

## Survival-by-Scale Infrastructure

9. **Structural redundancy built into the city's own sheer size** — the established in-fiction explanation
   for Lazar's survival under heavy bombardment that would have destroyed a smaller city outright; a form
   of resilience genuinely unique to Lazar's scale among Tepenian cities.
10. **Uneven damage distribution across the two founding-era districts**, plausibly reflecting whichever of
    the Old Core or the Overflow District took the worse of the war's own targeting — a real, physically
    legible historical record layered onto the twin-settlement layout itself.

## Records & Continuity Infrastructure

11. **The Unbroken Operations Hall** (established) — maintaining and marking the city's own continuous
    operational record since its actual Tepenian founding.

## Cross-City Trade Infrastructure

12. **The Neumayer Trade Exchange** (established) — an informal commercial link supporting Neumayer's own
    reliance on Lazar as its nearest genuine megacity market.
