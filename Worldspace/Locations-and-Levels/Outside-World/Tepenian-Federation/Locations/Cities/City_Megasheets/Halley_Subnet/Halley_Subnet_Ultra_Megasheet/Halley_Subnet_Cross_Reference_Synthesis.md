# Halley Subnet — Cross-Reference Synthesis (Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Step 3)

**What this is:** as with Concordia's and Palmer subnet's own Ultra-Megasheets, this step is largely already complete — the cross-city Patterns and Throughways work in this same folder already did the implication-hunting this step exists to produce. The work here is selecting, ranking, and presenting the strongest of that material as Halley subnet's own headline findings. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Finding 1: One city's uncredited engineering spans the entire subnet's history, in three separate eras

**Combining:** Neumayer's own founding-era redesign of Belgrano's crude first-generation hauler into the vehicle that became the Rastra, in Neumayer's very first years after the Falkland Treaty (`Halley_Cross_City_Patterns.md`, Pattern 2; `Halley_Cross_City_Throughways.md`, Throughway 2) + its later, matured-capacity design of the Amundsen Tower's own schematics + its most recent design of the current-generation Cradle fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic.

**2nd-order effect:** Neumayer isn't simply a city with one uncredited achievement and an unlucky pattern of not getting named for it — it has now produced three, spanning from its own earliest years through to the present day, at three completely different scales (a vehicle every Tepenian drives, the nation's single greatest engineering project, the literal ongoing mechanism of robot creation).

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Neumayer's own established character — quiet, precision-first, allergic to public credit-seeking — not merely a personality trait but a demonstrated, repeated behavior across the city's entire existence, present before it had any reputation to protect and unchanged after it earned one.

**4th-order effect:** Neumayer is, by a wide margin, the single most load-bearing uncredited city in this entire Megasheet project so far — not one of several similar cases, but the clearest possible example of what "does the real work, someone else gets the name" looks like when it isn't a coincidence but a whole city's defining civic behavior, sustained across centuries.

## Finding 2: Byrd's founding runs through four cities and two subnets, and Neumayer's own role was previously invisible

**Combining:** Belgrano's need for a working hauler and its own expedition, archives, and all-robot crew (Belgrano Mega-Init) + Neumayer's quiet redesign of that hauler before the expedition ever departed (`Halley_Cross_City_Throughways.md`, Throughway 2 and 3) + Palmer City's and Rothera's own supporting records, shipped across the Weddell Sea (per this project's own Palmer Subnet Ultra-Megasheet).

**2nd-order effect:** Byrd's own rediscovery-story identity, previously understood as owing a debt to three cities across two subnets, now owes a debt to four — Belgrano's own contribution was never singular, it was itself dependent on a fifth city's quiet assistance that no existing record, until this session, connected to the expedition at all.

**3rd-order effect:** this means Byrd's own established culture — which currently reflects none of these debts — is missing not three points of origin but four, and the newest of those four (Neumayer) was invisible even to this project's own prior cross-referencing work until the developer's own direct addition surfaced it.

**4th-order effect:** worth flagging as a genuine demonstration of how this whole Ultra-Megasheet process is meant to work — even a thoroughly cross-referenced chain can still be missing a link, and the right piece of new information can extend it further than the existing synthesis alone would have found.

## Finding 3: The Troll-Sanay-Halley dependency chain now has a fourth stake riding on it

**Combining:** Sanay's own established role as the subnet's freight entry point, splitting goods toward Troll's airfield or directly along Hwy 7 (`Halley_Cross_City_Throughways.md`, Throughway 1) + Troll's own fractured, contested control + Halley's total dependence on that same chain, having deliberately forgone its own docks and airstrip + this Ultra-Megasheet's own Full Extrapolation proposal (Section III) that Rastra parts, manufactured at Belgrano, plausibly pass through the same freight network as incidental cargo.

**2nd-order effect:** Troll's own contested control doesn't just determine whether Sanay's imports reach Halley — it now also plausibly touches the supply of spare parts for Tepenia's single most common vehicle, a stake with genuinely nationwide relevance rather than one confined to the Halley subnet's own internal politics.

**3rd-order effect:** this raises Troll's own already-established stakes (DLC 5's central conflict, per Troll's own Megasheet) one level further — a resolution here doesn't just decide Halley's winter or Sanay's distribution reach, it plausibly has some bearing on Rastra maintenance and replacement capacity for cities well outside the Halley subnet entirely.

**4th-order effect:** worth flagging as a concrete way to raise DLC 5's own perceived stakes without inventing anything new — the existing supply chain already reaches this far; the only new step is naming it explicitly.

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across All Three Findings

All three findings trace back to the same underlying shape: things Halley subnet produces reach much further than anyone standing inside the subnet, or even inside this project's own prior research, initially accounted for. Neumayer's engineering reaches across three centuries and every corner of the country (Finding 1). Byrd's own founding reaches across two subnets and now a newly-discovered fourth city (Finding 2). Troll's contested airfield reaches, however indirectly, into the maintenance of a vehicle every Tepenian depends on (Finding 3). Halley subnet's real significance, at every scale this synthesis has examined, keeps turning out to be larger than its own public reputation — the same finding this Ultra-Megasheet's own Mega-Init named as the subnet's defining trait, now confirmed a third and fourth time over.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Finding 1 is the most significant of the three, and specifically because of how it came to be found: it started as a modest addition to an already-completed research pass, and turned out to reveal that Neumayer's uncredited legacy was even deeper and older than this Ultra-Megasheet's own first draft had already established. Worth remembering for the remaining subnets still to come — this kind of synthesis is never fully finished until the developer has had a chance to add the pieces that only they know are missing.
