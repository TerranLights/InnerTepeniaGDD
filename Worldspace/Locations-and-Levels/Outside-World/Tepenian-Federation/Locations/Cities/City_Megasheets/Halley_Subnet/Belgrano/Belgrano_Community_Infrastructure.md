# Belgrano — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Belgrano's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for all 8 Mirny subnet cities plus Halley and Abowasa, extended here. **Note:** the existing `Belgrano_Full_Extrapolation.md` Section VI proposes a "Bandera Day" honoring Manuel Belgrano (the real historical figure and Argentine flag creator the city's real-world namesake station honors) — this is the same GPS-only violation as Abowasa's "Turku Remembrance" (see `feedback_no_national_stereotypes` memory, variant 6) and is dropped here, not carried forward. Drawn from `Belgrano_Mega_Init.md`, `Belgrano_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Belgrano_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Belgrano.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **The Belgrano Airfield Hangars** — the active, maintained runway and hangar complex, the reason the city exists.
- **The Rastra Garages** — specialist mechanic workshops that work on Rastras specifically, a direct continuation of having invented the vehicle.
- **Spare-Parts Fabrication Yards** — light manufacturing producing replacement components for Rastras and aircraft.
- **The Working Records Hall** — the ordinary institutional recordkeeping space (flight charts, aerial survey photography, meteorological data) that, in hindsight, held the richest documentary record pointing to Byrd's location.
- **The Boneyard Maintenance Rotations** — the informal, organized system of maintenance shifts that kept the city functioning once institutional command backing collapsed.

## Small offices for educational training

- Rastra-specific mechanical repair and light vehicle manufacturing
- aviation/airfield maintenance and flight operations
- maritime freight logistics and intermodal coordination

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Expedition Day** — commemorating the departure of the original Arrastradoras and the all-robot crew on the Byrd-founding crossing.
- **Maintenance Rotation Handoffs** — the informal practice of one crew shift handing off to the next, embodying the Boneyard Times' organized-survival ethic.
- **Garage Nights** — informal gatherings among Rastra mechanics and specialists, where "for anything with an engine" competence is swapped and shown off.
- people cooperatively working on some sort of engine/vehicle repair or build project together, the same low-key, competence-driven social glue as Capricorn's own welding-project gatherings.

## Worth Your Attention

The Rastra Garages and the cooperative engine/vehicle project entry together are the standout items — they give Belgrano's genuine, established mechanic subculture ("for anything with an engine, there's reliably somebody in Belgrano who can repair it") a concrete social form, the same way Capricorn's welding projects did for its own craft-pride culture.
