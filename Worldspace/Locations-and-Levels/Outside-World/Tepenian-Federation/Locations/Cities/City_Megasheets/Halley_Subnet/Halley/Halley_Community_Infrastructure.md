# Halley — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Halley's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for all 8 Mirny subnet cities, extended here. This begins the Halley subnet. Drawn from `Halley_Mega_Init.md`, `Halley_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Halley_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Halley.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **The Relocation Engineering Guild** — where ski-mounted platform craft is practiced and taught, tracing to Chief Engineer Astrid Vance-Mokoena's founding legacy *(placeholder name — proposed in `Halley_Full_Extrapolation.md` Section VIII, not a developer-chosen name; flag before treating as settled)*.
- **The Position Archive** — housing Position Day's accumulated survey records, plausibly Halley's only surviving trace of its own lost history.
- **The Old Positions Walk** — an informal path visiting the abandoned prior sites still accessible on the ice.
- **Resupply Dock Watch** — the informal gathering point where residents wait for seasonal resupply-ship arrivals.
- **The Relocation Documentary Archive** — the civic visual-arts tradition of photographing and recording each old position as it's abandoned.

## Small offices for educational training

- ski-mounted platform engineering and relocation-system maintenance
- engineering/logistics documentation and collective decision-making process-writing
- maritime resupply logistics and seasonal-window planning

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **The Crossing** — commemorating the founding generation's arrival onto the ice shelf.
- **Position Day** — the annual survey and record-keeping ritual, both practical and ceremonial.
- **Resupply Feasts** — meals clustering around a fresh shipment's arrival, a genuine civic occasion here.
- **Old Position Photography Walks** — the community practice of documenting an old position before it's finally lost to the sea.
