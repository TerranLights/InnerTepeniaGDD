# Belgrano — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Belgrano would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Belgrano.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Belgrano.md`, and
`Belgrano_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**A direct cross-reference worth flagging:** Belgrano is the confirmed birthplace of the Arrastradora/Rastra
vehicle lineage — the same tracked vehicle class that carried the all-robot expedition crew that found and
founded Byrd Station (see `Byrd_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` chain #2, and Byrd's own attribute #44).
Belgrano's own attributes below are the source-city half of that already-established connection.

**Governing facts:** status is ruins, not war-destruction — Belgrano survived the Long Night War intact and
declined into ruin only afterward, a genuinely distinct status from a city destroyed outright; southernmost
Halley subnet city, deep Weddell Sea position, one of the harshest climates in the subnet; the primary
Halley subnet aviation hub, paralleling Marambio's own role in the Palmer subnet; the confirmed invention
site of the Rastra vehicle lineage; a coastal receiving port (alongside Sanay) for South African freighter
shipments, trucked overland to Halley and onward to Troll for national distribution; its own ordinary
records hall held the documentary record that helped locate Byrd, generations after Belgrano itself
contributed it; post-decline, an organized "Boneyard Times" maintenance culture kept the city functioning
without formal institutional command.

---

## Aviation Hub Infrastructure

1. **The Belgrano Airfield Hangars** (established) — the runway and hangar complex, the reason the city
   exists at all.
2. **Substantial aircraft maintenance and fueling infrastructure**, sized to Belgrano's own established
   role as the primary Halley subnet aviation access point, directly paralleling Marambio's own role in the
   Palmer subnet.

## Rastra/Arrastradora Vehicle Manufacturing Infrastructure

3. **The Rastra Garages** (established) — specialist mechanic workshops dedicated to the vehicle lineage
   invented here, a direct continuation of the founding-era achievement.
4. **Spare-Parts Fabrication Yards** (established) — light manufacturing producing replacement components
   for Rastras and aircraft alike.
5. **Original vehicle-design and prototyping infrastructure from the founding era** — given this city
   literally invented Tepenia's first native tracked overland vehicle class, the earliest workshops where
   that design work actually happened plausibly still exist in some form, distinct from the ongoing repair
   infrastructure that followed.

## Records & Archive Infrastructure

6. **The Working Records Hall** (established) — ordinary institutional recordkeeping (flight charts, aerial
   survey photography, meteorological data) that, in hindsight, held the richest documentary record
   pointing to Byrd's own location — a genuinely consequential archive that was never built with that
   purpose in mind.

## Maritime Freight Infrastructure

7. **Coastal receiving-port infrastructure** (established, alongside Sanay) — South African freighter
   shipments landing here or at Sanay, whichever passage is open.
8. **Overland trucking infrastructure via Hwy 7**, moving goods onward to Halley (which has no docks of its
   own) and further on to Troll for aviation-based national distribution.

## Organized-Decline Infrastructure ("Boneyard Times")

9. **Infrastructure supporting organized, rotation-based maintenance rather than simple decay** —
   consistent with the established Boneyard Maintenance Rotations, a genuine "managed decline" system
   distinct from ordinary ruin, kept functioning without formal institutional command backing it.

## Weddell Sea / Deep Interior Climate Infrastructure

10. **Cold-engineering calibrated to the harshest climate profile examined in the Halley subnet so far** —
    the combination of southern latitude, deep Weddell Sea exposure, and continental influence gives
    Belgrano a materially more severe environment than the subnet's other coastal cities.

## Ruins-Specific Infrastructure

11. **Infrastructure that remained physically standing well past the point of active habitation** —
    consistent with the established "the archive outlasted the city" framing; Belgrano's own decline was
    gradual enough that its records were still usable, and used, generations after the decline began.
