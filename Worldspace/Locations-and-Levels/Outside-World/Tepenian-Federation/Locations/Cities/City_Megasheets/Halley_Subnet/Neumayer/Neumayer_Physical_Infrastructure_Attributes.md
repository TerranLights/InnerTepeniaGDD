# Neumayer — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Neumayer would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Neumayer.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Neumayer.md`, and
`Neumayer_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**A note on Cradle-adjacent infrastructure below:** Neumayer designed the current-generation
fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic that Sinheung and Byrd actually build from — already-established,
national-scope background canon, treated factually here the same way it's treated everywhere else in the
project (real, but unmarked, never a main-quest driver).

**Governing facts:** built on the Ekström Ice Shelf — floating ice, not bedrock — with hydraulic-leg
elevated architecture extended from the original station design to the whole city; status is damaged but
partially operational; Neumayer's own defining civic pattern is "designs, doesn't build, doesn't get
credited" — the Amundsen Tower's own structural schematics originated here, as did the current-generation
Cradle chamber design and (unmarked lore) Calethina's own spec/schematic, none of which carry Neumayer's
name forward with them; economy is precision scientific research (atmospheric, oceanographic,
glaciological) and structural/instrument engineering; culture is rigorous, methodical, "Measured, Not
Debated," balanced by a genuine After-Hours music scene as release valve.

---

## Floating Ice Shelf Foundation Infrastructure

1. **Hydraulic-leg elevated architecture** (established) — structures raised to account for snow
   accumulation, extended from the original single-station design principle to the whole city's own
   architecture.
2. **Long-term structural monitoring for measurable ice-shelf drift**, distinct from Halley's own actively
   self-propelled architecture — Neumayer's buildings are elevated and periodically adjusted in place
   rather than continuously moved toward the mainland.

## Precision Engineering & Scientific Infrastructure

3. **The Ice Shelf Observatory** (established) — the city's primary scientific infrastructure, atmospheric
   and glaciological monitoring.
4. **The Precision Institute** (established, renamed from the real Alfred Wegener Institute) — the formally
   preserved founding research and engineering institution.
5. **Instrument-Craft Workshops** (established) — dedicated space for precision engineering and calibrated
   tool-making, practiced as genuinely admired craft.

## Amundsen Tower Design Legacy Infrastructure

6. **The Amundsen Tower Design Archive** (established) — wherever the original schematics and structural
   plans for the Tower itself were kept, distinct from the Tower's own physical location at the South Pole.
7. **Large-scale structural engineering and computational design infrastructure**, capable of a project as
   consequential as the Tower's own structural planning — the specific capability that made Neumayer the
   natural home for that work in the first place.

## Cradle Chamber Schematic Infrastructure (design-only, factual)

8. **Design and schematic-drafting infrastructure for the current-generation fabrication-synthesis chamber**
   — Neumayer designs the Mark IV schematic that Sinheung and Byrd actually manufacture from; a
   design-and-engineering facility distinct from any actual manufacturing site.

## Redundant Archival Infrastructure

9. **Redundant, plausibly off-site-transmitted archival infrastructure** for the city's own centuries of
   unbroken atmospheric and climate data — consistent with the established open question of whether this
   record was transmitted elsewhere pre-war, implying some form of national data-distribution capability
   existed here beyond simple local storage.

## Social & Cultural Release Infrastructure

10. **After-Hours Sound Venues** (established) — Electronic/Metal/Digital-Industrial bars and music spaces,
    the city's genuine emotional release valve after high-focus technical work.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

11. **Ongoing repair infrastructure specific to ice-shelf-founded structures**, distinct from a
    bedrock-founded damaged city's own repair challenges — consistent with the established "damaged but
    partially operational" status.
