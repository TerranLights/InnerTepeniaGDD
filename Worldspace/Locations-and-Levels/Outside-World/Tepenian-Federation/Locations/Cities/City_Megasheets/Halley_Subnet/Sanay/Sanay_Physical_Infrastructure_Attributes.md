# Sanay — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Sanay would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Sanay.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Sanay.md`, and `Sanay_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Sanay_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** the source Local Cultures file frames
"Founders' Bedrock" around the real SANAE expedition's own name directly — a GPS-only violation matching
the same issue already caught and routed around for Abowasa, Belgrano, Lazar, and Neumayer. No attribute
below repeats that framing; where founding-era infrastructure is described, it's described by its physical
and structural facts, not by the founding nation's institutional identity.

**Why Sanay's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every other Halley subnet city has
either a passive terrain disadvantage (Princess Elisabeth's total wind exposure), a moving foundation
(Halley, Neumayer), or a single defining hazard. Sanay is the subnet's structural outlier in the other
direction — genuine bedrock stability let it take real war damage without being erased, and its economy is
built around function (shipping, logistics, a relay nexus it didn't build for itself) rather than around
managing an ongoing environmental threat.

**Governing facts:** built on Vesleskarvet nunatak, Ahlmannryggen range, Queen Maud Land — genuine bedrock,
inland from the coast; status is damaged but partially operational, the bedrock foundation giving it real
structural resilience without full immunity; Tepenia's actual major shipping/logistics port city (not
necessarily largest by population, but a genuine major hub by function), receiving South African freighter
shipments alongside Belgrano; hosts the Halley subnet's own Arcanet relay nexus, physically located in
Sanay but built and named for Halley, whose residents did the hands-on construction; work is organized
around rotating freighter-arrival crews rather than a fixed day/night schedule; Hwy 7 connects Sanay to the
rest of the subnet and onward to Troll for aviation-based distribution.

---

## Bedrock Foundation Infrastructure

1. **Structural foundation engineering built directly into stable Vesleskarvet rock**, distinct from any
   ice-shelf or ice-sheet city's own construction constraints — the specific physical advantage that let
   Sanay take real damage without being erased outright.
2. **Vesleskarvet Summit** (established) — the highest point of Sanay's own terrain, panoramic views across
   the Queen Maud Land ice plateau.

## Maritime Shipping & Port Infrastructure

3. **The Shipyard Complex** (established) — the lower city's primary maritime freight infrastructure,
   rebuilt first and fastest after the war per the city's own function-over-comfort priorities.
4. **The Trade Yard** (established) — a physically demarcated port section where incoming Upper Earth
   freighter crews dock and conduct business under defined, bounded protocols, distinct from Palmer City's
   own more open, tourism-facing Upper Earth relationship.
5. **Round-the-clock dockside operations infrastructure**, organized around rotating crews tied to
   freighter arrival and departure times rather than any fixed day/night civic schedule.

## Arcanet Relay Nexus Infrastructure

6. **The Halley Subnet Arcanet Relay Nexus** (established) — semi-distributed across several points along
   the upper clifftops; physically sited in Sanay specifically because Vesleskarvet's bedrock offers a
   stability no ice-founded city could guarantee for permanent critical infrastructure, though built and
   named for Halley, whose residents did the hands-on construction labor.
7. **Bedrock-integrated relay housing and cabling infrastructure**, distinct from any city that would have
   to run comparable infrastructure through or over unstable ice.

## Overland Logistics Infrastructure

8. **The clifftop trucking/shipping depots and import-export business district** (established) —
   coordinating the onward Hwy 7 logistics chain toward Halley and Troll from the upper clifftops, alongside
   the Arcanet nexus itself.
9. **Overland trucking infrastructure via Hwy 7**, distributing goods onward to Halley (which has no docks
   or airstrip of its own) and further to Troll for aviation-based national distribution — the same overland
   chain Belgrano's own attributes describe from its own end.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

10. **Ongoing repair infrastructure calibrated to bedrock-founded damage**, distinct from an ice-founded
    city's own repair challenges — consistent with the established "damage without erasure" status, real
    but non-total harm.
11. **Legible but non-catastrophic damage distribution across the city**, consistent with the established
    "scarred but standing" framing — distinguishing Sanay's ruins-free damage profile from both Princess
    Elisabeth's total destruction and Belgrano's post-war decline.

## Residential & Social Infrastructure

12. **Residential, commercial, and leisure districts stretched along the cliffside** (established) —
    off-shift worker spaces distinct from the shipyard/depot working areas, consistent with the established
    round-the-clock work rhythm requiring genuine off-hours release space.
