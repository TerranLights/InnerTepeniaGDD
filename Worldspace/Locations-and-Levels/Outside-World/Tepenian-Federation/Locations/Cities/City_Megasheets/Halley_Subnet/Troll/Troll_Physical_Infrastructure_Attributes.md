# Troll — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Troll would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Troll.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Troll.md`, and `Troll_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. **Closes out the Halley
subnet.**

**Why Troll's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every other Halley subnet city's central
physical fact is environmental (ice mobility, wind exposure, bedrock stability) or economic (a port, a
market). Troll's central physical fact is political — a single, functional, strategically irreplaceable
piece of infrastructure (the airfield) under fractured, overlapping three-faction control, with no single
faction able to operate it alone. The infrastructure below is shaped as much by that fracture as by the
Jutulsessen terrain or the subnet's coldest inland climate.

**Governing facts:** built on Jutulsessen nunatak, inland Queen Maud Land — colder and more continental
than any coastal Halley subnet city; status is damaged but partially operational; Troll Airfield's ~3,000m
runway is the single most significant aviation infrastructure in the subnet, functional post-war but
contested, split across three overlapping factional claims (runway/control tower, fuel depot, trucking
dispatch yard) that cannot fully function without cooperating; genuine intermodal freight hub — goods
arrive overland via Hwy 7 from Sanay or Belgrano and fly out via Troll Airfield to the rest of Tepenia,
including the historical direct-aviation supply link to Dome Fuji; major working-class city with
above-average spending power; St. Roald (Amundsen) veneration persists but has been increasingly
overshadowed by the airfield's own newer civic identity.

---

## Airfield Infrastructure (the defining, contested feature)

1. **The 3,000m Troll Airfield runway** (established) — capable of receiving intercontinental aircraft, an
   extraordinary capability for an inland Antarctic position; the single most significant aviation asset in
   the Halley subnet.
2. **The Runway and Control Tower** (established) — the physical operational center of the airfield,
   currently held by one of the three competing factions.
3. **The Grounded Fleet Hangars** (established) — housing the mostly non-operational cargo/passenger
   aircraft fleet, with a genuine handful maintained airworthy.

## Fractured Three-Faction Control Infrastructure

4. **The Fuel Depot** (established) — held by a second faction, distinct from and physically separate from
   the runway/tower complex — the airfield's control is genuinely split across separate physical sites, not
   just divided authority over one building.
5. **The Trucking Dispatch Yard** (established) — held by a third faction, the actual freight-coordination
   infrastructure that moves goods to and from the field; without it, neither the runway nor the fuel depot
   alone can move cargo.
6. **Necessary cross-faction coordination infrastructure**, distinct from any single-authority city's own
   dispatch systems — the physical and procedural handoff points where the three factions are forced to
   cooperate daily just to keep freight moving at all.

## Intermodal Freight Hub Infrastructure

7. **Overland freight-receiving infrastructure tied to Hwy 7**, accepting goods trucked in from Sanay or
   Belgrano before they're flown onward — the inbound half of the intermodal chain the airfield exists to
   complete.
8. **Historical direct-aviation route infrastructure to Dome Fuji**, consistent with the established fact
   that Troll's freight network was one of two direct supply lines that kept Tepenia's most isolated interior
   city supplied for a substantial stretch of its history.

## Jutulsessen Nunatak & Extreme Interior Climate Infrastructure

9. **Foundation engineering on Jutulsessen's exposed rocky outcrops**, distinct from Sanay's own
   more moderate nunatak siting — Troll's terrain is more dramatic and more thoroughly interior.
10. **Cold-engineering calibrated to the coldest, most continental climate profile examined in the Halley
    subnet** — winters here run colder than any coastal subnet city, a genuinely distinct hazard class from
    Belgrano's or Sanay's own maritime-influenced exposure.

## St. Roald Veneration Infrastructure

11. **St. Roald's Chapel** (established) — the older, quieter site of continuing Saint veneration tied to
    Amundsen's historical expeditions through this landscape, increasingly overshadowed by the airfield's own
    newer, civically dominant public identity.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

12. **Ongoing repair infrastructure specific to the airfield's own contested, multi-faction control** —
    distinct from a single-authority damaged city's own repair challenges, since damage assessment and
    repair priority themselves are subject to the same three-way fracture as everything else here.

## Cross-Referenced Extrapolation Findings (Methodology #2)

**What this is:** cross-referencing the 12 attributes above against Troll's own existing lore —
`Troll_Full_Extrapolation.md` and `Troll_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md` (which already holds 3 Findings of
its own, written 2026-07-09). Troll is one of this subnet's three deep-treatment cities per direct developer
instruction (alongside Sanay and Neumayer) — fittingly, since it's also the DLC's own established working
central conflict/MacGuffin. This pass runs at Mawson's own depth. Written 2026-07-30.

### Finding A: the Fuel Depot and Dispatch Yard are the exact facilities behind Halley's own real-time vulnerability

**Combining:** attributes #4–5 and #7 (the Fuel Depot, the Trucking Dispatch Yard, overland freight-receiving
infrastructure tied to Hwy 7) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 1 (Troll's fractured control
makes Halley's own dependency ongoing and precarious, not merely a future outcome to wait on).

**2nd-order effect:** these specific facilities are the concrete physical mechanism behind Finding 1's own
cross-city cause-and-effect claim — the Fuel Depot's and Dispatch Yard's fractured control is exactly what
makes Halley's own supply situation precarious in real time, not just contingent on some eventual outcome.

**3rd-order effect:** gives Finding 1 — already flagged as the most structurally significant Finding in this
subnet's existing material — a specific, attribute-level anchor for exactly which pieces of Troll's own
infrastructure carry that cross-city weight.

### Finding B: the cross-faction coordination infrastructure is the thin thread Halley's supply line currently hangs from

**Combining:** attribute #6 (necessary cross-faction coordination infrastructure) + the established Cross-
Faction Dispatch Handoffs tradition (`Troll_Community_Infrastructure.md`) + the existing Cross-Reference
Synthesis's Finding 1 (Halley's ongoing, precarious dependency on Troll's own current fractured state).

**2nd-order effect:** without this specific daily coordination mechanism, Finding 1's own "precarious but
functioning" dependency would plausibly collapse into complete supply failure rather than merely ongoing
instability.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming directly: this coordination infrastructure isn't background texture, it's
the actual thread Halley's own survival currently hangs from — the daily, tense handoffs between three
factions who'd otherwise have no reason to cooperate are what keeps a whole neighboring city fed.

### Finding C: the historical Dome Fuji route and the incidental chamber traffic are two genuinely separate flows

**Combining:** attribute #8 (historical direct-aviation route infrastructure to Dome Fuji) + the Full
Extrapolation's Section VI (fabrication-synthesis chambers as plausible, secondary freight traffic through
Troll's broader network, distinct from Sinheung's own dedicated Dome Fuji route).

**2nd-order effect:** these are explicitly two different flows through the same airfield — the established
Dome Fuji supply line (attribute #8) and the incidental chamber traffic Section VI describes are not the
same shipments.

**3rd-order effect:** worth clarifying directly since the two are easy to conflate — Sinheung's own dedicated
route bypasses Troll's general freight network entirely, while any chamber traffic passing through Troll
would be bound for cities other than Dome Fuji, layered onto everything else the airfield already moves.

### Finding D: Troll's dual holidays now each have their own separate, concrete physical anchor

**Combining:** attribute #11 (St. Roald's Chapel) and attribute #2 (the Runway and Control Tower) + the
existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 2 (Troll's dual holidays as a uniquely self-aware version of a
founding-heritage-versus-functional-identity pattern found elsewhere in the project).

**2nd-order effect:** the Chapel is the natural site for St. Roald's Landing, the older, quieter observance;
the Runway and Control Tower is the natural site for Runway Day, the newer, civically dominant one — giving
both halves of Finding 2's own dual-identity observation their own separate physical location.

**3rd-order effect:** completes a clean architectural expression of Finding 2 in full — unlike Rothera's or
Fort McMurdo's own quiet, undocumented identity drift, Troll's whole civic split is now visible as two
actual buildings a visitor could walk between, not just two dates on a calendar.

### Finding E: the Grounded Fleet Hangars are the legible physical site of the fleet status already established

**Combining:** attribute #3 (the Grounded Fleet Hangars) + the Full Extrapolation's Section II (a mostly
grounded cargo fleet, with a genuine handful kept airworthy through continuous, necessity-driven
maintenance).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #3 is the concrete location where Section II's already-detailed fleet status
would actually be visible — rows of dead aircraft standing alongside the few still being actively worked on.

**3rd-order effect:** a strong, ready-made environmental-storytelling location, showing precisely what
Section II already establishes without needing narration to explain it.

### Finding F: Troll's underlying geography and climate engineering exist entirely independent of the airfield conflict

**Combining:** attributes #9–10 (foundation engineering on Jutulsessen's exposed rocky outcrops, cold-
engineering for the coldest, most continental climate profile in the subnet) + the airfield-conflict material
dominating every other established Troll finding.

**2nd-order effect:** unlike nearly everything else established about Troll, these two attributes have no
connection whatsoever to the political fracture defining the city's present-day identity.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming as a useful reminder for any future writing — Troll's physical bones
(the rock it's built on, the climate it engineers against) predate the airfield conflict and will outlast
whatever DLC 5 eventually resolves it to; the terrain isn't a stakeholder in the fight happening on top of
it.
