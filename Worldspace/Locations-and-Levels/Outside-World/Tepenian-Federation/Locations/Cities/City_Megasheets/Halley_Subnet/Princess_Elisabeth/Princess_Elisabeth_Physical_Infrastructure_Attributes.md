# Princess Elisabeth — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Princess Elisabeth would logically
have to possess, built directly from `Specs/Princess_Elisabeth.md`, `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/
Princess_Elisabeth.md`, and `Princess_Elisabeth_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**;
Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked
canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Why Princess Elisabeth's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every other Halley subnet
city examined has at least one passive advantage — Halley's designed-for-relocation philosophy, Abowasa's
bedrock foundation, Belgrano's gradual post-war decline rather than destruction, Lazar's sheer
survival-by-scale. Princess Elisabeth had none. Its entire founding premise was that a human presence here
could only exist through continuous, active engineering on one of the most wind-exposed sites in the
subnet — and when that engineering failed or was destroyed, nothing protected what remained. This city is
also, per its own finalized status, **Destroyed** rather than damaged or ruins-by-decline — framed here the
same way Casey and Belgrano's own ruins framing works, but distinct from both: Casey never had working
infrastructure to lose, Belgrano's systems declined gradually, and Princess Elisabeth's systems failed
outright, in circumstances still TBD for DLC 5.

**Governing facts:** built on Utsteinen nunatak in the Sør Rondane Mountains, Queen Maud Land — genuine
exposed bedrock, but unlike Abowasa's own bedrock siting, the terrain here offers zero passive wind
shelter; the real station this city is based on faces gales up to 300 km/h and survives only through
deliberate aerodynamic engineering and deep permafrost anchoring; designed from inception as Tepenia's
first zero-emissions station — wind and solar power, no fossil fuels, zero waste discharge, later
expanded to full city scale; status is Destroyed — ruins, explorable for loot/data/audio logs, with a
small straggling survivor population who can anchor questline content; eastern Queen Maud Land position
puts it geographically closer to Dome Fuji than to the rest of the Halley subnet; no highway confirmed
beyond the eastern Hwy 7 extension, with a Dome Fuji corridor connection still TBD.

---

## Zero-Emissions Energy Infrastructure (established, now ruins)

1. **The Turbine Fields** (established) — the city-scale wind and solar arrays, expanded from the original
   station's own zero-emissions design, now a dominant, haunting ruined feature of the site, once again
   exposed to the same extreme winds they were built to manage.
2. **Integrated no-discharge waste management infrastructure**, consistent with the founding zero-waste
   design principle — physical systems for processing and containing waste on-site rather than any form of
   external disposal, a founding-era engineering commitment distinct from any other Tepenian city examined
   in this pass so far.

## Underground Energy Reserve Infrastructure

3. **The Underground Reserve** (established) — the buried flywheel/battery/thermal storage system;
   its access and control conduits were severed by the strike that destroyed the city, but the reserve
   itself plausibly still sits intact below the ruins.
4. **The Maintenance Corps Hall** (established) — where the underground energy-storage crew once
   coordinated system upkeep, now among the ruins.

## Extreme-Wind Survival Engineering

5. **Deep permafrost anchoring infrastructure**, engineered to hold structures against gales up to 300
   km/h — the specific physical mechanism, distinct from any passive terrain shelter, that let the city
   exist here at all while it was intact.
6. **Aerodynamically shaped structural design**, minimizing wind resistance across the built city rather
   than relying on bulk or shelter — a design discipline the real station is defined by, extended here to
   full city scale.

## Utsteinen Nunatak Bedrock Infrastructure

7. **Bedrock foundation engineering on exposed rock**, structurally distinct from Abowasa's own bedrock
   siting in one critical way — the rock here offers no wind shelter at all, meaning the structural
   advantage of solid ground came with none of Abowasa's passive protection.
8. **Utsteinen Summit** (established) — the elevated overlook with panoramic ice plateau and Sør Rondane
   mountain views, occupying the same exposed, commanding vantage the ruins now sit on.

## Ruins-Specific Hazard Infrastructure

9. **Unmanaged extreme-wind hazard conditions**, now that the founding zero-emissions systems (and
   whatever aerodynamic/anchoring engineering depended on active maintenance) have failed or been
   destroyed — plausibly the single most physically dangerous wind environment among Tepenian ruins,
   distinct from the milder katabatic exposure other Halley subnet ruins carry.
10. **Legible failure-not-decay environmental storytelling infrastructure** — dead turbines and solar
    arrays that stopped rather than degraded gracefully, visually distinguishing this city's ruins from
    Belgrano's own gradual "Boneyard Times" decline.

## Straggling Survivor Infrastructure

11. **Whatever shelter and subsistence infrastructure the straggling survivor population has adapted or
    salvaged from the ruins**, distinct from any functioning city's own civic infrastructure — a small,
    precarious foothold rather than an operating settlement.
12. **Survivor-held practical knowledge of the Underground Reserve's own access points**, per the
    Community Infrastructure file's own established survivor-relevance note — firsthand, lived memory
    rather than documentary record, since the founding institution itself didn't survive to pass it down
    formally.

## Eastern Queen Maud Land Position

13. **Whatever physical routing exists (or once existed) toward a Dome Fuji corridor connection**,
    consistent with the established open question of a TBD highway link — the only Halley subnet city
    positioned closer to another subnet's hub than to its own subnet's cluster.
