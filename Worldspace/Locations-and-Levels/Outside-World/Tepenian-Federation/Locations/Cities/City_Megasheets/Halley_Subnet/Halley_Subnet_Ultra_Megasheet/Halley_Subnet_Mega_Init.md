# Halley Subnet — Mega-Init (Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Step 1)

**What this is:** Halley subnet treated as one single subject, the same way each of its 8 cities was treated in its own City Megasheet, and mirroring the process already completed for Concordia's 13 districts and Palmer subnet's 8 cities. Synthesizing all 8 completed City Megasheets (Halley, Neumayer, Troll, Princess Elisabeth, Belgrano, Sanay, Lazar, Abowasa), `Official_Population_Census.md`'s Halley subnet rows, and the cross-city Patterns and Throughways produced in this same folder. Compiled 2026-07-10.

## The One-Line Pitch

*The subnet named for a city that gave away its own nerve center — Halley traded real control for naming honor, Sanay does the uncredited work underneath it, and Neumayer's own schematics quietly redesigned the vehicle every Tepenian drives, then built the nation's greatest engineering achievement, then designed its literal robot-creation infrastructure, across three different eras of its own history, without anyone remembering whose design any of it was. The whole arrangement now holds together, however precariously, on a single contested airfield three separate cities' survival depends on.*

## Who Lives Here, and Why

Halley subnet's demographic story is the most nation-diverse founding pattern of any Tepenian subnet examined so far — British (Halley), German (Neumayer), Norwegian (Troll), Belgian (Princess Elisabeth), Argentine (Belgrano), South African (Sanay), Russian-and-unclaimed (Lazar's Novolazarevskaya/Maitri coalescence), and jointly Finnish-Swedish (Abowasa) founding populations, eight genuinely distinct national or dual-national origins across eight cities. Almost every one of these founding populations was later diluted by the same eventual American-led demographic majority that recurs across most of Tepenia — except Sanay, whose own dilution broke pattern and produced a German plurality instead, and Lazar and Abowasa, whose plural founding stories never had one clean national population to dilute in the first place.

This produces a subnet whose residents arrived for the widest range of original reasons of any Tepenian subnet — scientific mission (Neumayer, Abowasa, Sanay), military aviation (Belgrano, Troll), pure geographic accommodation (Halley, built specifically to move with unstable ice), and sheer administrative overflow (Lazar's Maitri absorption) — and whose people, across nearly every one of these origins, ended up doing something for the rest of Tepenia that the rest of Tepenia doesn't fully credit them for.

## What It Feels Like

Moving through Halley subnet means moving through a landscape defined by unstable ground and the wildly different ways its cities chose to answer that instability. Halley's own ski-mounted modules dig into drifting ice and continuously propel the city toward the mainland, staying perpetually ahead of the shelf's own seaward creep. Neumayer's hydraulic-leg architecture, extended from a single research station's design into a whole city's built character, treats the same instability as a measurement-and-engineering problem rather than a reason to keep moving. Abowasa sits on genuine bedrock, needing neither trick, and has turned that stability into its own quiet export. Princess Elisabeth built its entire survival on total, actively-managed self-sufficiency and lost everything the moment its own internal connections failed rather than the systems themselves.

Then the subnet's economic backbone reveals itself as a genuine, load-bearing chain: freight lands at Sanay's enormous working port, splits toward Troll's contested airfield or directly along Hwy 7, and from there reaches Halley, Neumayer, Abowasa, and the rest of the subnet's interior. Lazar towers over all of it — Tepenia's single largest city, holographic advertisements glowing over a skyline that is simultaneously the subnet's most vibrant district and, in its former-Maitri half, its most visibly war-scarred one. And south of all of it, Belgrano's Boneyard Times quietly persist: an airbase that survived the war intact and lost the peace instead, run today by people whose discipline outlasted the institution that built it.

## Personality (A Whole-Subnet Read)

Halley subnet doesn't reduce to one Enneagram profile any more than Concordia's districts or Palmer subnet's cities did, but its own recurring pattern is unusually legible: a subnet whose defining civic instinct, across at least five of its eight cities, is to do genuinely consequential work and treat drawing attention to that work as a mild civic failure. Neumayer processes its own uncredited national-scale engineering legacy through meticulous private record-keeping rather than public advocacy. Sanay's own faction identity is explicitly named "Competence Without Commentary." Halley itself gave away its own subnet's technical nerve center in exchange for a naming honor and appears to feel no ongoing grievance about it. Belgrano's post-war survival runs on inherited habit rather than institutional pride. Even Abowasa's whole founding story — two nations, quietly working out a shared life at household scale — carries the same undramatic, function-first register. Halley subnet's collective personality, read across all eight cities, is Competency turned inward: capability that consistently prefers to simply keep functioning over being recognized for it.

## Real-World Inspirations, Researched

### Primary: Queen Maud Land's real multinational research presence

The real Queen Maud Land sector hosts research stations from at least nine different nations within a relatively compact geographic area — Norway, Britain, Germany, Belgium, South Africa, Russia, Finland, Sweden, and others, each pursuing largely independent scientific missions within genuine physical proximity to each other.

**What this gives Halley subnet:** direct grounding for its own unusually high founding-nation diversity relative to other Tepenian subnets — eight genuinely distinct national or dual-national origins across eight cities is a fictionalized but structurally honest reflection of how densely multinational this specific real-world region actually is.

### Secondary: Institutions whose fame exceeds their own name recognition — Bell Labs, JPL, Bletchley Park

Already researched individually for Neumayer, these three institutions apply at whole-subnet scale: each demonstrates that genuinely world-changing work can be performed by an institution the wider public never learns to associate with the result — exactly the pattern recurring across Neumayer, Sanay, and Halley all at once.

### Secondary: Cities and regions defined by contested infrastructure chokepoints — the Berlin Airlift

Already researched for Troll specifically, the Berlin Airlift's core lesson (control of one airfield determining whether an entire population eats) applies at full subnet scale — Troll's own contested control doesn't just determine Troll's fate, per this Ultra-Megasheet's own cross-city Throughways, it determines Halley's and shapes Sanay's distribution reach as well.

### Supporting: Chatham Dockyard and other "survived the disaster, declined in the peace" precedents

Already researched for Belgrano specifically, this pattern is worth naming as a genuine subnet-scale possibility — Belgrano's own Boneyard Times may not be a unique story within Halley subnet so much as the clearest, most fully developed instance of a decline-after-survival arc other lightly-damaged subnet cities could plausibly share in smaller ways.

## Worth Your Attention

The single most load-bearing fact in this Mega-Init is Pattern 1 from this Ultra-Megasheet's own cross-city Patterns file: Halley subnet alone contains a complete, four-category example of every war-damage pattern this whole Megasheet project has identified anywhere. That completeness isn't a coincidence worth simply noting — it makes Halley subnet the natural reference point any future city's own damage pattern should be checked against first, since between Lazar, Halley, Princess Elisabeth, and Abowasa, every currently known way a Tepenian city can take damage already has a clean, worked example sitting inside this one subnet.
