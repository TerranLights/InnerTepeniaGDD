# Neumayer — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Neumayer given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Neumayer_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-09.

## III. AWI's Institutional Continuity: Formally Preserved, Not Dissolved

**Proposed:** the Alfred Wegener Institute survived as a genuine, distinct institutional entity within the city rather than dissolving quietly into general governance. This follows directly from Neumayer's own established rigor-first culture — a city organized around "Measured, Not Debated" would treat formalizing its own founding institution's continuity as exactly the kind of thing worth doing properly, rather than letting it informally fade.

---

## IV. Ice Shelf Movement: Measured, Monitored, Not Catastrophic

**Proposed:** the Ekström Ice Shelf has moved a measurable but non-catastrophic distance since the Long Night War, with no confirmed loss of any city section to calving. Consistent with the city's own established precision-first culture, Neumayer would maintain detailed, continuous monitoring records of exactly how far it has moved — treated as routine data collection, not a source of civic anxiety, matching the established "quiet capability... no anxious hand-wringing" read.

## V. The Two Placeholder Holidays, Developed

- **Founders' Instrument** — commemorating the founding generation's decision to treat the inherited AWI research and engineering equipment as a genuine ongoing mission rather than scrap, the day the work that eventually became the Amundsen Tower project formally began.
- **The Schematic** — a specifically Tower-related observance, marking the day Neumayer's own design work was formally completed and handed off to the actual construction site at the South Pole — a quietly bittersweet civic holiday, since almost nobody outside the city remembers what it actually commemorates.

---

## VI. The Climate Data Record: Survived, Backed Up Well Before the War

**Proposed:** yes, the centuries-long atmospheric and climate data record survived, transmitted to multiple redundant off-site locations as standard practice for a precision-first institution long before the Long Night War ever reached Neumayer. A city this organized around rigorous measurement would treat backing up its most valuable asset as an obvious, unremarkable precaution, not an afterthought — meaning even total destruction of Neumayer itself would never have put the record genuinely at risk.

---

## VII. The Glitch-Coolant Variant: Precisely Calibrated, Confirmed

**Proposed:** the developer's own floated idea — glitch-coolant defined by exact, reproducible, engineered effects rather than variety or raw potency — is the correct resolution, since it's the only version of the concept that actually fits everything else established about this city. A robot drinking culture organized around precision and repeatability is simply "Measured, Not Debated" applied to one more domain of daily life.

---

## VIII. How Neumayer Feels About the Uncredited Tower Work: Documented Internally, Not Publicly Advocated

**Proposed, directly answering the question surfaced in the Mega-Init:** yes, Neumayer's residents likely do feel a genuine, complicated mix of pride and quiet frustration about the Amundsen Tower situation, similar to Bletchley Park's own veterans. But consistent with the city's whole established character, this feeling is processed the way everything else here is processed — through precise, meticulously kept internal record-keeping (the city maintains its own exact documentation of exactly what it contributed) rather than through public grievance or a campaign for outside recognition. Where Bletchley's veterans eventually pushed for public acknowledgment decades later, Neumayer's own instinct would more plausibly be to keep the record complete and exact, and let that completeness itself be the answer to the unfairness, rather than campaigning for anyone else to notice.

## Worth Your Attention

Section VIII is the most emotionally significant proposal here — it takes the genuine real-world parallel the Mega-Init raised and resolves it in a way that's specific to Neumayer's own established character, rather than assuming the city would react to injustice the same way Bletchley's veterans eventually did. Section VI is the second most valuable, since it resolves the "did the data survive" lean with an actual mechanism (redundant off-site backup as routine precision-institution practice) rather than leaving it as an unexplained probability.
