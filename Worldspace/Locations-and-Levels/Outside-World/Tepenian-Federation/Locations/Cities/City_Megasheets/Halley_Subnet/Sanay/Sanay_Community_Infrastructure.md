# Sanay — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Sanay's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for all 8 Mirny subnet cities plus Halley, Abowasa, Belgrano, Lazar, Neumayer, and Princess Elisabeth, extended here. **Note:** the existing `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Sanay.md` describes "Founders' Bedrock" as commemorating "the SANAE founding" directly — the real South African expedition's real name, the same GPS-only issue already caught for Abowasa/Belgrano/Lazar/Neumayer. Reframed below to commemorate the founding generation's own structural choice (building into stable rock) instead. Drawn from `Sanay_Mega_Init.md`, `Sanay_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Sanay_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Sanay.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **The Shipyard Complex** — the primary maritime freight infrastructure, rebuilt first and fastest after the war per the city's own function-over-comfort priorities.
- **The Trade Yard** — the physically demarcated port section where incoming Upper Earth freighter crews dock and conduct business under defined, bounded protocols.
- **Vesleskarvet Summit** — the highest point of Sanay's terrain, panoramic views of the Queen Maud Land ice plateau.
- **The Bedrock Foundation Halls** — built directly into stable rock, the structural choice that gave the city its passive resilience during the war.

## Small offices for educational training

- shipyard/dockside engineering and maritime freight logistics
- bounded trade-protocol administration, the Trade Yard's own procedures
- structural/bedrock construction technique

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Founders' Bedrock** *(reframed)* — commemorating the founding generation's own decision to build directly into stable rock, the structural choice that has defined the city's resilience ever since.
- **The Standing** — a quiet, unglamorous observance marking the city's continued function after the Long Night War, deliberately understated rather than triumphant.
- **Competence Without Commentary Gatherings** — informal recognition of steady, unglamorous persistence, explicitly declining to frame survival as exceptional or fortunate.
