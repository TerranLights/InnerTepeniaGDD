# Troll — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Troll's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for all 8 Mirny subnet cities plus Halley, Abowasa, Belgrano, Lazar, Neumayer, Princess Elisabeth, and Sanay, extended here. This closes out the Halley subnet. Drawn from `Troll_Mega_Init.md`, `Troll_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Troll_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Troll.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

---

## Additions

- **The Runway and Control Tower** — the physical center of the fractured airfield conflict, held by one of the competing factions.
- **The Fuel Depot** — controlled by a separate faction, one of three overlapping claims fracturing control of the airfield.
- **The Trucking Dispatch Yard** — the freight-coordination hub controlled by a third faction, the piece that actually moves goods to and from the field.
- **St. Roald's Chapel** — the quieter, older site of continuing Saint veneration, increasingly overshadowed by the airfield's own newer public identity.
- **The Grounded Fleet Hangars** — where the mostly non-operational cargo/passenger aircraft are kept, a genuine handful maintained airworthy.

## Small offices for educational training

- aviation/airfield maintenance and flight operations
- freight dispatch and intermodal coordination
- fuel-depot management and logistics

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **St. Roald's Landing** — the older, quieter holiday tied to the city's founding heritage, now practiced more privately.
- **Runway Day** — the newer, civically dominant holiday celebrating the airfield's own founding or a significant expansion.
- **Cross-Faction Dispatch Handoffs** — the necessary, tense daily coordination between the three competing factions just to keep freight moving at all, since no single faction can fully function without the others' cooperation.

## Worth Your Attention

Cross-Faction Dispatch Handoffs is the standout item — it gives Full Extrapolation Section I's own "fractured, overlapping partial control" premise a genuine, recurring social mechanism, showing the tension as something residents live through daily rather than an abstract political fact.
