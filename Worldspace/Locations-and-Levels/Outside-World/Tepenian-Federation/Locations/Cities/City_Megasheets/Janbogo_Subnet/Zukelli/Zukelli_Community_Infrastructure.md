# Zukelli — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Zukelli's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 16 Mirny and Halley subnet cities, Janbogo, Cape Adare, Denison, Dumont d'Urville, Fort McMurdo, and Scott, extended here. This closes out the Janbogo subnet. **Zukelli is destroyed** — everything below is framed as pre-war, now among the ruins, plus its present-day relevance to Janbogo's own diaspora and vigil-keeping practice. Drawn from `Zukelli_Mega_Init.md`, `Zukelli_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Zukelli_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Zukelli.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions (pre-war, now ruins)

- **Elisa Faranda's Restaurant-and-Performance-Space** *(name flagged for future renaming — not a developer-chosen name)* — the city's single most famous venue, exactly the kind of dedicated-stage-in-a-restaurant space the music scene grew out of, recognized well beyond Zukelli's own borders.
- **The Cultural Quarter** — the food halls and communal gathering spaces, the center of civic life, now ruins.
- **The Structural Marking System** — the color- and pattern-marked heating-pipe conduits threading between buildings, an organic navigation code that today functions as a stat-gated discovery mechanic in the ruins.
- **The Shared Polynya Shoreline** — where Zukelli's waterfront met the Terra Nova Bay polynya, still running alongside the ruins.
- **The Ruins Visible From Janbogo** — the single most distinctive geographic fact about the city's destruction, sitting in daily sightline of a living city across the water.

## Small offices for educational training (pre-war)

- food craft and communal-hospitality tradition (tableware, communal-space furnishing)
- music performance craft, integrated directly into restaurant and hospitality spaces
- structural/architectural craft supporting the organic, unplanned street layout

## Social cohesion mechanisms (pre-war)

- communal meals as the absolute center of civic life, where the hospitality-and-rivalry identity with Janbogo played out most visibly.
- music and food treated as adjacent expressions of the same hospitality instinct, performed in the same communal spaces.
- the pre-war "Crossing" tradition with Janbogo — informal visits blurring who was hosting whom.

## Present-day relevance, in Janbogo

The vigil-keeper practice — unofficial, unscheduled crossings to the waterfront at personal anniversaries to look across the water — sits in quiet, unresolved tension with the larger majority who've made peace with not looking. The Zukelli diaspora within Janbogo's own civic fabric carries the specific, unusually direct trauma of having personally watched the city's destruction happen.
