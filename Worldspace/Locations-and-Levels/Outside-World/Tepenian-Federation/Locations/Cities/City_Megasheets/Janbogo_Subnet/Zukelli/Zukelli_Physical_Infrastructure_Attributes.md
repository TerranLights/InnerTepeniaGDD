# Zukelli — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Zukelli would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Zukelli.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Zukelli.md`, and `Zukelli_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. **Closes out the Janbogo
subnet.**

**Why Zukelli's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every other Destroyed city examined
(Casey, Princess Elisabeth, Cape Adare, Denison) sits in genuine geographic isolation from any living city.
Zukelli is the only one visible in daily sightline from a still-inhabited neighbor 8km away — a deliberate
outcome of a strike calibrated for total, absolute obliteration specifically so a surviving witness could
carry its message forward. It's also the only city examined so far with an organically grown, deliberately
unplanned street layout requiring an actual learned navigation code to traverse, and the only one whose
ruins already carry a confirmed, designed stat-gated gameplay mechanic (the structural-language
decode/"Zukelli Native" perk).

**Governing facts:** Terra Nova Bay, Victoria Land — approximately 8km from Janbogo, sharing the same
katabatic climate and Terra Nova Bay polynya; status is Destroyed, obliterated by a strike deliberately
calibrated for total destruction (to guarantee the deaths of sheltered AWOL Upper Earth defectors and to
send a deterrent message to the rest of Tepenia) while Janbogo was left standing specifically to witness
and carry that message; pre-war built environment was tangled and organically grown, navigable to outsiders
only via a learned color/pattern structural-marking system on covered connections and heating-pipe
conduits; food and hospitality culture was the city's single defining civic feature, with music performance
integrated directly into restaurant spaces; destroyed near its demographic peak (72.5% human retention),
not in decline.

## Organic Urban Layout & Structural-Language Infrastructure

1. **The Structural Marking System** (established) — color- and pattern-marked heating-pipe conduits and
   covered connections functioning as an informal, organically-emerged navigation code, legible only to
   residents; in the ruins, a confirmed stat-gated discovery mechanic (10 Calculation, 10 Investigation, 6+
   Engine, 6+ Nerve) tied to the game's hold-to-highlight system.
2. **Tangled, unplanned street infrastructure**, built as civic needs arose rather than to any master plan
   — a genuinely different urban-design discipline from any other Janbogo subnet city examined.

## Plantlines & Elevated Walkway Infrastructure

3. **Plantlines and "bridger-footroads"** (established) — elevated walkways threading through elaborate,
   largely artificial or cold-engineered plant life, giving residents something resembling equatorial
   nature to walk among, a genuinely distinctive amenity among Tepenian cities examined so far.

## Food & Hospitality Culture Infrastructure

4. **The Cultural Quarter** (established) — food halls and communal gathering spaces at the center of civic
   life, the city's most exported cultural product within the subnet.
5. **Restaurant-integrated performance-stage infrastructure** (established) — most entertainment happening
   in dedicated performance stages built into restaurants and among friends, rather than at standalone
   venues, growing directly out of the city's food-and-hospitality civic core.

## Katabatic Wind & Courtyard Infrastructure

6. **Sheltered courtyards and wind-oriented street layouts**, distinct from Janbogo's own more civic-hall-
   centered response to the same katabatic wind regime — the same underlying hazard producing a genuinely
   different architectural answer just 8km away.
7. **Covered inter-building connections**, allowing outdoor movement without full wind exposure, physically
   carrying the structural-marking system described above.

## Shared Terra Nova Bay Polynya Infrastructure

8. **Waterfront and port infrastructure along the shared Terra Nova Bay polynya**, distinct from Janbogo's
   own port function but drawing on the same katabatic-wind-maintained open water.

## Ruins-Specific / Witness-Proximity Infrastructure

9. **Whatever survives at the scale consistent with a deliberately total-obliteration strike**, distinct
   from any partial-damage or gradual-decline ruins examined so far — the destruction here was calibrated
   for certainty, not area denial, which plausibly shapes what's actually left to explore.
10. **The Ruins Visible From Janbogo** (established) — sitting in permanent daily sightline of a living
    city across still-open water, the single most geographically unique fact among all of Tepenia's
    destroyed cities, and the physical basis for Janbogo's own established vigil-keeper/"made peace with
    not looking" civic tension.

## Cross-Referenced Extrapolation Findings (Methodology #2)

**What this is:** cross-referencing the 10 attributes above against Zukelli's own existing lore —
`Zukelli_Full_Extrapolation.md` and `Zukelli_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md` (which already holds 4 Findings
of its own, written 2026-07-07 — the richest existing base of any Janbogo subnet city). Proportionate,
moderate depth per this subnet's own weighting — Janbogo and Fort McMurdo carry the deepest treatment.
Written 2026-07-30. Closes out the Janbogo subnet's Methodology #2 pass.

### Finding A: attribute #1 and Finding 3's own "lost language" are the same fact, derived independently twice

**Combining:** attribute #1 (the Structural Marking System) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's
Finding 3 (Zukelli's ruins present a literal lost language, illegible in a way categorically different from
a planned city's own ruins).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #1, derived through pure Methodology #1 first-principles reasoning, and
Finding 3, derived through a separate cross-referencing pass written weeks earlier, describe the identical
system without either analysis referencing the other directly.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming as a genuine convergence rather than restating the same fact twice for
its own sake — two independent methods arriving at the same conclusion is itself evidence the underlying
system is real and load-bearing, not an artifact of one particular analytical approach.

### Finding B: the covered connections and the marking system are physically inseparable for ruins-exploration purposes

**Combining:** attribute #7 (covered inter-building connections, physically carrying the marking system) +
attribute #1 (the Structural Marking System itself).

**2nd-order effect:** the marking system has no substrate to exist on without attribute #7's own physical
structure — any surviving fragment of the covered connections is automatically a potential fragment of the
lost code.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming as one physical unit for exploration and level-design purposes rather
than two separate systems — wherever attribute #7 survives in the ruins, attribute #1 potentially survives
with it.

### Finding C: the Cultural Quarter is where the instinct that destroyed Zukelli was also its greatest civic joy

**Combining:** attributes #4–5 (the Cultural Quarter, restaurant-integrated performance-stage
infrastructure) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 2 (Zukelli's destruction was the direct
extension of the same hospitality-and-care civic instinct that defined it in life, taken to its furthest
possible extreme).

**2nd-order effect:** these buildings are the physical infrastructure of exactly the civic character Finding
2 identifies as both Zukelli's defining trait and the direct cause of its own destruction.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming directly: the same instinct expressed as warmth and welcome in the
Cultural Quarter is what led, at its furthest extreme, to sheltering the AWOL defectors — the city's
greatest joy and its final act trace to the identical civic character, not two separate facts about who
Zukelli was.

### Finding D: total obliteration is precisely what forecloses the Kazimierz possibility Finding 1 already names

**Combining:** attribute #9 (whatever survives at the scale consistent with a deliberately total-obliteration
strike) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 1 (Zukelli's tragedy is specifically the denial
of a Kazimierz-style second life, since no survivors remain to attempt any revival).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #9's own framing — asking only what physical structures remain — is possible
precisely because the population, not just the buildings, was eliminated; a partial destruction would have
left both.

**3rd-order effect:** confirms Finding 1's own point at the attribute level — there being nothing left to ask
about except structures, with no possibility of anyone returning, is the specific, total-obliteration fact
that forecloses the second-life possibility Finding 1 names directly.

### Finding E: the Ruins Visible From Janbogo is the single physical object both sides of Janbogo's own civic tension relate to oppositely

**Combining:** attribute #10 (the Ruins Visible From Janbogo) + the Full Extrapolation's Section IV
(Janbogo's own internal, unresolved tension between vigil-keepers who deliberately look and a majority who
have made quiet peace with not looking).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #10 is the single shared physical referent both sides of that ongoing civic
disagreement relate to in opposite ways — one group seeks it out, the other avoids it, but both are reacting
to the identical view.

**3rd-order effect:** gives Section IV's own "quiet friction" a concrete, walkable, physical anchor rather
than an abstract civic mood — any future scene depicting either side of this tension could stage it directly
at this one location.
