# Cape Adare — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Cape Adare's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 16 Mirny and Halley subnet cities, and Janbogo, extended here. **Cape Adare's status is Destroyed — historical site**, with no straggling survivor population noted (unlike Casey or Princess Elisabeth) — everything below is framed as pre-war, now among the ruins. Note: "St. Carsten's Landing" fits the same established Tepenian Saints framework already confirmed legitimate for Troll's St. Roald (`National_Holidays.md`), extended here to Borchgrevink — not a GPS-only violation. Drawn from `Cape_Adare_Mega_Init.md`, `Cape_Adare_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Cape_Adare_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Cape_Adare.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions (pre-war, now ruins)

- **Borchgrevink's Hut Site** — the oldest human structure on the Antarctic continent; the structure itself didn't survive the war, but centuries of heritage documentation plausibly did.
- **The St. Carsten Memorial Site** — connected to the hut heritage.
- **The Adélie Rookery** — one of the largest penguin colonies in the world, continuing undisturbed in the ruins even after the city's destruction.
- **The Harbor Landing Site** — the most accessible point on the Ross Sea coast from open ocean, the gateway that defined the city's whole founding character.
- **The Heritage Documentation Archive** — where centuries-long preservation work on the hut was kept.

## Small offices for educational training (pre-war)

- heritage documentation and historical archiving craft
- penguin-rookery coexistence and individual pet-keeping practice
- acoustic/folk music craft (guitars, violins, cellos, tagelharpas)

## Social cohesion mechanisms (pre-war)

- **St. Carsten's Landing** — February 17th, marking the day the first deliberate overwintering on the continent began.
- arrival/departure meal rituals — the city's own gateway-function version of hospitality, marking comings and goings rather than daily sustenance alone.
- acoustic folk music gatherings — unhurried, unamplified, "belongs to everyone equally" cultural performance.
