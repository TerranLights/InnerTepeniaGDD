# Scott — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Scott given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Scott_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-08.

---

## I. Demonym: Scottian

Following the standard adjectival pattern already used across Tepenia's shorter-named cities.

---

## II. Economic Character: Precision, Calibration, and Monitoring

**Proposed:** Scott's technical/research economy specializes in precision equipment calibration, maintenance, and environmental and geological monitoring for the whole Hut Point Peninsula — including a share of the ongoing Erebus monitoring program, complementing rather than competing with Fort McMurdo's own established heavy-industry focus. This traces directly from the real New Zealand Antarctic program's genuine emphasis on scientific and environmental monitoring, and it gives Scott a specific, non-overlapping economic niche alongside Fort McMurdo rather than a vague "smaller version of the same thing."

---

## III. Scott and Fort McMurdo Today: Still in Active Contact, Cooperation Intensified

**Proposed:** yes, the two cities' surviving sections remain in active contact — their established shared emergency infrastructure and physical proximity meant that, if anything, post-war cooperation intensified out of pure necessity, since neither damaged city could sustain itself with fewer resources working entirely alone. The established "genuinely collaborative and genuinely competitive" relationship persists, but the war stripped away much of the friendly rivalry's earlier luxury.

---

## IV. Hut Point Discovery Hut: Survived Intact

**Proposed:** yes, the hut survived the Long Night War undamaged, protected by its distance from Fort McMurdo's own more heavily targeted industrial infrastructure and by its small, simple, non-strategic physical footprint — the same reasoning already proposed for Cape Evans and Cape Royds surviving near Fort McMurdo. The Independence Day remembrance tradition continues post-war, now carrying even more weight given how much both cities have lost since it began.

## VI. Scott and Fort McMurdo During the War Itself: Coordinated, Not Separate

**Proposed:** the two cities' wartime experience was functionally coordinated rather than separate — their established shared emergency infrastructure meant defense and survival efforts merged out of necessity during the actual crisis, regardless of how politically distinct the two cities normally kept themselves during peacetime. The rivalry paused; the shared infrastructure didn't care whose city it technically belonged to.

---

## VII. How Scott Reacted to Fort McMurdo's Formal Capital Status: A Sting, Not a Rupture

**Proposed, directly answering the question surfaced in the Mega-Init:** mixed but ultimately unbothered. Scott's own established character — "somewhat allergic to being perceived as Fort McMurdo's smaller sibling" — means the formal designation likely stung at first, a concrete, official confirmation of an asymmetry Scott residents already resented being reminded of. But Scott's own established Positive Outlook temperament kept this from curdling into lasting resentment: Scott's civic identity was never built around wanting to *be* the capital in the first place, so a formal title it never sought and never held wasn't really a loss — just an official name finally put on an imbalance that had always existed.

## Worth Your Attention

Section VII is the second most valuable, since it resolves the new question the Mega-Init raised using Scott's own established emotional temperament rather than inventing a new civic reaction from nothing.
