# Scott — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Scott's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 16 Mirny and Halley subnet cities, Janbogo, Cape Adare, Denison, Dumont d'Urville, and Fort McMurdo, extended here. Note: "St. Robert" and the Hut Point remembrance are established Tepenian Saints framework content (`National_Holidays.md`), not a GPS-only violation. Drawn from `Scott_Mega_Init.md`, `Scott_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Scott_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Scott.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **Hut Point** — Scott's most sacred civic location, Robert Falcon Scott's 1902 discovery hut, among the oldest surviving human-made structures in Antarctica.
- **The Scott–Fort McMurdo Border** — a formally maintained but practically porous line between the two cities.
- **The Calibration & Monitoring Labs** — precision equipment calibration and environmental/geological monitoring for the whole Hut Point Peninsula, including a share of the Erebus program.
- **Shared Emergency Infrastructure** — the physical systems tying Scott and Fort McMurdo together, merged out of necessity during the war and still active post-war.

## Small offices for educational training

- precision equipment calibration and maintenance
- environmental and geological monitoring, in ongoing coordination with Fort McMurdo's own Erebus program
- emergency infrastructure coordination between the two cities

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Tepenian Independence Day — Hut Point Remembrance** — residents of both Scott and Fort McMurdo gather at Hut Point to lay candles, flowers, and personal tokens in memory of St. Robert, never mandated, happening simply because both cities feel it should.
- the friendly rivalry with Fort McMurdo — genuinely collaborative and genuinely competitive, a defining, ongoing social dynamic.
- informal border crossing — the practically porous line between the two cities as a normal, everyday social fact.

## Worth Your Attention

Tepenian Independence Day — Hut Point Remembrance is the standout item — it's the clearest example in this subnet of a shared civic ritual belonging to two cities at once, rather than one city hosting and the other merely attending.
