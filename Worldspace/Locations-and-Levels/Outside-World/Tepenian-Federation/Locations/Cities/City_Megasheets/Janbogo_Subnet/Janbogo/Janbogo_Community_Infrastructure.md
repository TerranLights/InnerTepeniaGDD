# Janbogo — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Janbogo's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for all 16 Mirny and Halley subnet cities, extended here. This begins the Janbogo subnet. Drawn from `Janbogo_Mega_Init.md`, `Janbogo_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Janbogo_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Janbogo.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **Majyao's Original Teahouse** *(needs an official, proper name — currently only known by its former keeper's name; flagged for future naming)* — the site famous throughout Tepenia before its keeper relocated to Concordia, preserved as civic memory by the community.
- **The Great Shielded Commercial Halls** — huge indoor shopping and social districts, the architectural fulfillment of the outside-hostile/inside-warm design principle.
- **The Katabatic Warning Towers** — where the city's forecasting system operates, the practical origin of "the wind signals incoming events."
- **Teaware & Coffee Craft Ateliers** — where the respected artisan tradition of tea and coffee service objects is practiced.
- **The Zukelli View** — the waterfront sightline to Zukelli's ruins, a somber, unique landmark.
- **The Smuggled Book Reading Rooms** — Janbogo's own confirmed, openly welcoming library tradition for smuggled Upper Earth books.

## Small offices for educational training

- katabatic wind forecasting and safety coordination
- teaware and coffee-service craft
- hospitality and hosting-tradition training for newcomers integrating into the city's civic culture

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- hosting someone who's had a hard week without them having to ask — food or drink simply arrives.
- **Threshold Crossings** — the felt, repeated daily act of moving between the violent exterior and the deliberately soft interior, a shared civic experience in itself.
- **Hall Gathering Nights** — informal social time spent in the shielded commercial halls, where most of daily Janbogo life actually happens.
- **Teahouse Story Circles** — the oral, anecdotal tradition of specific teahouses, hosts, and acts of kindness during hard winters, kept alive through speech.
