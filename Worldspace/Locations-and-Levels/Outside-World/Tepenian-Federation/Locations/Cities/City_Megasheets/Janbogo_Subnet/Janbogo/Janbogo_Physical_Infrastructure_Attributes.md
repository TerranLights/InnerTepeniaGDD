# Janbogo — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Janbogo would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Janbogo.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Janbogo.md`, and `Janbogo_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. Begins the Janbogo subnet.

**Why Janbogo's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every prior city's defining hazard or
advantage has been structural (ice mobility, bedrock, wind exposure as a survival threat). Janbogo's
katabatic wind is genuinely double-edged — it's the primary outdoor lethality hazard, but the same
gravity-driven airflow also maintains the Terra Nova Bay polynya, a year-round ice-free maritime access
point no other Tepenian coastal city has. Janbogo is also DLC 6's subnet hub and Tepenia's single most
critical surviving external link to Concordia, giving its infrastructure a strategic weight distinct from
any city examined in the Mawson, Mirny, or Halley subnets.

**Governing facts:** Terra Nova Bay, Ross Sea coast; status is damaged but partially operational — the last
coastal city still actively supplying Concordia; katabatic winds are the defining climate feature, both the
primary lethal outdoor hazard and the mechanism that keeps the Terra Nova Bay polynya open year-round;
reached via a connecting road off Hwy 183 rather than a direct main-line stop, but still the most actively
maintained highway corridor in Tepenia given what depends on it; approximately 8km from Zukelli's ruins,
visible from Janbogo's own waterfront — an established deliberate warning, not incidental; culture centers
on interior communal warmth (teahouses, the shielded commercial halls) as a direct design response to the
violent exterior; the Janbogo subnet's own Arcanet relay nexus is unusually located inside Concordia's
Gemini district rather than in Janbogo itself.

---

## Katabatic Wind Hazard Infrastructure

1. **The Katabatic Warning Towers** (established) — the city's forecasting and alert infrastructure,
   the practical mechanism behind the established "the wind signals incoming events" design principle.
2. **Rime-ice-resistant structural design and specialized building orientation**, engineered against the
   heavy structural icing sea spray combined with subfreezing temperatures produces on any surface facing
   the bay — a materially different structural discipline from any interior or non-coastal city examined so
   far.
3. **Shelter-point routing infrastructure for exterior movement**, given the established design principle
   that outdoor activity during a katabatic event requires planned cover, not simply adequate protection
   rating.

## Terra Nova Bay Polynya / Year-Round Maritime Infrastructure

4. **Year-round port and shipping infrastructure**, uniquely enabled by the katabatic-wind-maintained
   Terra Nova Bay polynya — the only location on the Ross Sea coast where ships can arrive regardless of
   season, a strategic asset no other Tepenian coastal city examined in this pass can match.
5. **Ice-edge navigational monitoring infrastructure**, tracking the polynya's variable boundary against
   the surrounding winter sea ice for approaching vessels.

## Interior Warmth Infrastructure (the defining cultural-physical contrast)

6. **The Great Shielded Commercial Halls** (established) — huge indoor shopping and social districts, the
   architectural fulfillment of the outside-hostile/inside-warm design principle at civic scale.
7. **Majyao's Original Teahouse site** (established) — Janbogo's single most historically significant
   landmark, preserved as civic memory despite its former keeper's relocation to Concordia; still lacking an
   official name of its own beyond the keeper's name.
8. **Teaware & Coffee Craft Ateliers** (established) — dedicated artisan-craft workspace for the
   nationally respected tea/coffee service-object tradition.
9. **Threshold infrastructure**, physically marking the transition between exterior and interior spaces —
   consistent with the established "Threshold Crossings" social mechanism, since a genuinely felt daily
   transition needs an actual physical boundary to cross.

## Zukelli View / Memorial Infrastructure

10. **The Zukelli View** (established) — the waterfront sightline to Zukelli's ruins roughly 8km north,
    unique in Tepenia as the only surviving city within sight of a destroyed one; per established canon, not
    an incidental view but the intended target audience of a deliberate warning strike.

## Hwy 183 Supply Lifeline Infrastructure

11. **Primary overland freight infrastructure along the Hwy 183 connector**, moving supplies to Concordia —
    the single most critical surviving external supply route in Tepenia, whose disruption would
    significantly worsen Concordia's own already-strained situation.
12. **Whatever governance and security infrastructure protects the Hwy 183 supply chain**, consistent with
    the established fact that Janbogo has likely been a contested location post-war precisely because
    whoever controls it controls Concordia's most critical external artery.

## Diaspora Connection Infrastructure

13. **Whatever communication or coordination infrastructure links Janbogo to its own diaspora community in
    Concordia's Gemini district**, distinct from any physical Arcanet nexus (the subnet's own relay hardware
    is established as sitting in Gemini itself, not in Janbogo) — a genuinely unusual arrangement reflecting
    the depth of the Janbogo-Concordia relationship.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

14. **Ongoing repair infrastructure specific to a katabatic-exposed coastal city under active strategic
    contest**, distinct from any interior or less-contested damaged city's own repair priorities.
