# Janbogo — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Janbogo would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Janbogo.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Janbogo.md`, and `Janbogo_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. Begins the Janbogo subnet.

**Why Janbogo's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every prior city's defining hazard or
advantage has been structural (ice mobility, bedrock, wind exposure as a survival threat). Janbogo's
katabatic wind is genuinely double-edged — it's the primary outdoor lethality hazard, but the same
gravity-driven airflow also maintains the Terra Nova Bay polynya, a year-round ice-free maritime access
point no other Tepenian coastal city has. Janbogo is also DLC 6's subnet hub and Tepenia's single most
critical surviving external link to Concordia, giving its infrastructure a strategic weight distinct from
any city examined in the Mawson, Mirny, or Halley subnets.

**Governing facts:** Terra Nova Bay, Ross Sea coast; status is damaged but partially operational — the last
coastal city still actively supplying Concordia; katabatic winds are the defining climate feature, both the
primary lethal outdoor hazard and the mechanism that keeps the Terra Nova Bay polynya open year-round;
reached via a connecting road off Hwy 183 rather than a direct main-line stop, but still the most actively
maintained highway corridor in Tepenia given what depends on it; approximately 8km from Zukelli's ruins,
visible from Janbogo's own waterfront — an established deliberate warning, not incidental; culture centers
on interior communal warmth (teahouses, the shielded commercial halls) as a direct design response to the
violent exterior; the Janbogo subnet's own Arcanet relay nexus is unusually located inside Concordia's
Gemini district rather than in Janbogo itself.

---

## Katabatic Wind Hazard Infrastructure

1. **The Katabatic Warning Towers** (established) — the city's forecasting and alert infrastructure,
   the practical mechanism behind the established "the wind signals incoming events" design principle.
2. **Rime-ice-resistant structural design and specialized building orientation**, engineered against the
   heavy structural icing sea spray combined with subfreezing temperatures produces on any surface facing
   the bay — a materially different structural discipline from any interior or non-coastal city examined so
   far.
3. **Shelter-point routing infrastructure for exterior movement**, given the established design principle
   that outdoor activity during a katabatic event requires planned cover, not simply adequate protection
   rating.

## Terra Nova Bay Polynya / Year-Round Maritime Infrastructure

4. **Year-round port and shipping infrastructure**, uniquely enabled by the katabatic-wind-maintained
   Terra Nova Bay polynya — the only location on the Ross Sea coast where ships can arrive regardless of
   season, a strategic asset no other Tepenian coastal city examined in this pass can match.
5. **Ice-edge navigational monitoring infrastructure**, tracking the polynya's variable boundary against
   the surrounding winter sea ice for approaching vessels.

## Interior Warmth Infrastructure (the defining cultural-physical contrast)

6. **The Great Shielded Commercial Halls** (established) — huge indoor shopping and social districts, the
   architectural fulfillment of the outside-hostile/inside-warm design principle at civic scale.
7. **Majyao's Original Teahouse site** (established) — Janbogo's single most historically significant
   landmark, preserved as civic memory despite its former keeper's relocation to Concordia; still lacking an
   official name of its own beyond the keeper's name.
8. **Teaware & Coffee Craft Ateliers** (established) — dedicated artisan-craft workspace for the
   nationally respected tea/coffee service-object tradition.
9. **Threshold infrastructure**, physically marking the transition between exterior and interior spaces —
   consistent with the established "Threshold Crossings" social mechanism, since a genuinely felt daily
   transition needs an actual physical boundary to cross.

## Zukelli View / Memorial Infrastructure

10. **The Zukelli View** (established) — the waterfront sightline to Zukelli's ruins roughly 8km north,
    unique in Tepenia as the only surviving city within sight of a destroyed one; per established canon, not
    an incidental view but the intended target audience of a deliberate warning strike.

## Hwy 183 Supply Lifeline Infrastructure

11. **Primary overland freight infrastructure along the Hwy 183 connector**, moving supplies to Concordia —
    the single most critical surviving external supply route in Tepenia, whose disruption would
    significantly worsen Concordia's own already-strained situation.
12. **Whatever governance and security infrastructure protects the Hwy 183 supply chain**, consistent with
    the established fact that Janbogo has likely been a contested location post-war precisely because
    whoever controls it controls Concordia's most critical external artery.

## Diaspora Connection Infrastructure

13. **Whatever communication or coordination infrastructure links Janbogo to its own diaspora community in
    Concordia's Gemini district**, distinct from any physical Arcanet nexus (the subnet's own relay hardware
    is established as sitting in Gemini itself, not in Janbogo) — a genuinely unusual arrangement reflecting
    the depth of the Janbogo-Concordia relationship.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

14. **Ongoing repair infrastructure specific to a katabatic-exposed coastal city under active strategic
    contest**, distinct from any interior or less-contested damaged city's own repair priorities.

## Cross-Referenced Extrapolation Findings (Methodology #2)

**What this is:** cross-referencing the 14 attributes above against Janbogo's own existing lore —
`Janbogo_Full_Extrapolation.md` and `Janbogo_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md` (which already holds 3 Findings
of its own, written 2026-07-08). Per direct developer instruction, this subnet's deepest treatment is split
between Janbogo and Fort McMurdo, unlike Mawson/Mirny's single clean hub city — DLC_Overview.md itself
leaves DLC 6's central location undecided between them. This pass runs at Mawson's own depth. Written
2026-07-30.

### Finding A: the port and monitoring infrastructure is the physical mechanism behind Janbogo's outward-transmission role

**Combining:** attributes #4–5 (year-round port infrastructure enabled by the polynya, ice-edge navigational
monitoring) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 1 (the polynya and the Zukelli-witness role
may be two halves of a single strategic calculation — Janbogo chosen as witness specifically because it
could transmit the deterrent message further than any other city could).

**2nd-order effect:** attributes #4–5 are the concrete physical infrastructure behind Finding 1's own claim
that Janbogo's value as a witness extended beyond simple proximity — the port and monitoring systems are
literally the mechanism by which any message, deterrent or otherwise, could travel onward via maritime
contact.

**3rd-order effect:** gives Finding 1's own richest speculative claim (a message potentially reaching beyond
Tepenia itself) a physical anchor rather than resting purely on inference from the polynya's existence.

### Finding B: three separate buildings each independently confirm Janbogo's "absorb and re-originate" civic trait

**Combining:** attributes #6–8 (the Great Shielded Commercial Halls, Majyao's Original Teahouse site, the
Teaware & Coffee Craft Ateliers) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 2 (Janbogo's real civic
trait is how readily it absorbs whatever reaches it, not any single cultural content — already shown across
fashion and hospitality).

**2nd-order effect:** these three attributes are three separate physical instances of the identical pattern
Finding 2 identifies — architecture (the Halls), institution (the Teahouse), and craft (the Ateliers) all
independently expressing the same absorb-and-re-originate civic behavior.

**3rd-order effect:** strengthens Finding 2 considerably — a pattern showing up in two domains reads as a
real trait; showing up in three physically distinct, independently-derived attributes makes it close to a
structural law of how Janbogo builds anything at all.

### Finding C: the Hwy 183 apparatus is the concrete infrastructure-level confirmation of why the defectors weren't sheltered here

**Combining:** attributes #11–13 (primary Hwy 183 freight infrastructure, governance/security protecting the
supply chain, diaspora coordination infrastructure to Concordia's Gemini district) + the existing
Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 3 (Janbogo's own heavy-traffic administrative visibility made it
structurally the wrong place to conceal the AWOL defectors, explaining why Zukelli sheltered them instead).

**2nd-order effect:** these three attributes collectively describe exactly the high-oversight, high-traffic
administrative apparatus Finding 3 already identifies as structurally incompatible with concealment.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming directly — these attributes, derived independently through Methodology #1
without reference to Finding 3, arrive at the identical structural picture Finding 3 reasons its way toward,
strengthening its conclusion through independent convergence.

### Finding D: the concentration-risk question applies specifically to the Halls, not to the threshold infrastructure connecting them

**Combining:** attribute #9 (threshold infrastructure marking exterior/interior transitions, physically
distinct from the Halls themselves) + the Full Extrapolation's Section IX (the Shielded Halls' own
concentration risk is real but untested, since the war's actual damage pattern suggests the halls weren't
the strike's target).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #9 clarifies that Section IX's own open concentration-risk question applies
specifically to attribute #6 (the Halls), not to the connective threshold infrastructure between them —
these are genuinely separate systems with separate vulnerability profiles.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming for any future strike or crisis scenario touching Janbogo — the Halls and
the thresholds connecting them would need to be assessed as distinct targets, not treated as one
undifferentiated "indoor civic space" risk category.

### Finding E: the Katabatic Warning Towers are the concrete legacy of Janbogo's own placeholder Notable Figure

**Combining:** attribute #1 (the Katabatic Warning Towers) + the Full Extrapolation's Section III
(Meteorologist Han Soo-jin, credited with formalizing the wind-warning system, plausibly the reason outdoor
katabatic fatalities remain rare rather than routine).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #1 is the concrete physical facility that placeholder figure is credited with
establishing — a real institutional legacy, not just an abstract credit.

**3rd-order effect:** gives Han Soo-jin a genuine physical location tied to her legacy, the same upgrade
several other placeholder Notable Figures have received across this Methodology #2 pass.

### Finding F: the Zukelli View has apparently succeeded at its intended function for generations

**Combining:** attribute #10 (the Zukelli View, established as the intended target audience of a deliberate
warning strike) + the Full Extrapolation's Section VIII (the deterrent message functionally worked —
no other Tepenian city is documented to have sheltered AWOL Upper Earth defectors since Zukelli's
destruction).

**2nd-order effect:** attribute #10's own established purpose — a permanent, intended reminder — has a
testable, confirmed outcome via Section VIII's own resolved question: the message has apparently been
received and heeded continuously since the war.

**3rd-order effect:** worth naming directly: this single piece of memorial infrastructure has been doing
exactly the psychological work it was designed for, generation after generation, with no confirmed lapse —
a rare case of a deliberately weaponized piece of environmental storytelling actually working as intended,
on record.

### Finding G: repair-priority patterns are a checkable physical marker for what the war's strike actually targeted

**Combining:** attribute #14 (ongoing repair infrastructure specific to a katabatic-exposed, strategically
contested coastal city) + the Full Extrapolation's Section IX (the halls' concentration risk untested, since
the war's damage pattern suggests port and highway infrastructure mattered more strategically than indoor
civic space).

**2nd-order effect:** if the Great Shielded Commercial Halls (attribute #6) show comparatively light repair
need relative to the port and Hwy 183 infrastructure, that repair-priority pattern is itself a specific,
checkable piece of evidence about what the original strike actually hit.

**3rd-order effect:** gives Section IX's own open question ("the halls weren't targeted, or mattered less
strategically") a concrete way to be confirmed or falsified through future environmental design — relative
damage levels across attribute #6 versus attributes #4/#11 would settle it either way.
