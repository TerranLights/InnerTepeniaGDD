# Fort McMurdo — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Fort McMurdo's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 16 Mirny and Halley subnet cities, Janbogo, Cape Adare, Denison, and Dumont d'Urville, extended here. Drawn from `Fort_McMurdo_Mega_Init.md`, `Fort_McMurdo_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Fort_McMurdo_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Fort_McMurdo.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

---

## Additions

- **The Harbor & Pier Core** — the original, structurally intact McMurdo Station-era infrastructure, now home to the surviving functioning population.
- **The Comms Coordination Stations** — where supply, extraction scheduling, and inter-city logistics are formalized, the operational backbone of the city's capital-scale influence.
- **The Dry Valleys Extraction Camps** — the lighter, more resilient raw-extraction operation across the Sound, still running post-war.
- **Cape Evans and Cape Royds** — the preserved pre-exile expedition huts, deliberately kept separate from the working city, a heritage contrast.
- **The Erebus Monitoring Station** — the long-running volcanology program tracking the summit's baseline, stable lava-lake activity.
- **The McMurdo Sound sea ice road** — the seasonal overland crossing to the Dry Valleys, usable only in winter. **Flagged:** the actual logistics of the summer/winter transition (ice road in winter, presumably boat crossing in summer, per the same dual-mode pattern as Dumont d'Urville's Channel Crossing) haven't been worked out — how the switchover itself happens, what the shoulder-season gap looks like, whether it constrains the Dry Valleys operation's own year-round output. See matching flag on Dumont d'Urville's Channel Crossing.

## Small offices for educational training

- fuel processing, equipment manufacturing and repair, construction-materials production
- inter-city logistics coordination and comms-station operation
- volcanology and long-term geological monitoring

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Founders' Shift** — a work-appreciation observance where crews are publicly recognized for the preceding year's output rather than narrating the city's history.
- **The Watching** — an informal gathering during heightened Erebus activity, watching the summit glow together, a small ritual acknowledgment of living beside something that could end them and hasn't.
- sea-ice-road crossing season — the seasonal rhythm of overland access to the Dry Valleys, itself a recurring shared civic event.

## Worth Your Attention

The sea ice road's flagged summer/winter logistics gap is the most consequential open item here — it's not just a Fort McMurdo question, it's a shared, unresolved mechanic that also applies to Dumont d'Urville's Channel Crossing, worth resolving once rather than separately for each city.
