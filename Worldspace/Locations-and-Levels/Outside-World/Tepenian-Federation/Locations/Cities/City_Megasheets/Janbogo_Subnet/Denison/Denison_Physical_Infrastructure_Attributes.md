# Denison — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Denison would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Denison.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Denison.md`, and `Denison_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**A note on Cradle-adjacent infrastructure below:** Denison was a confirmed historical fabrication-synthesis
chamber manufacturer during the Second Interwar Period, lost when the city was destroyed — already-
established, national-scope background canon, treated factually here the same way it's treated everywhere
else in the project (real, but unmarked, never a main-quest driver), and explicitly distinct from the
present-day network (Sinheung, Byrd, the Mountain Pass outpost).

**Why Denison's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** every prior wind-exposed city (Janbogo,
Troll, Belgrano) treats extreme wind as a serious hazard to survive around. Denison is categorically more
extreme than any of them — among the windiest permanent sea-level habitation sites on Earth, wind that's
functionally constant rather than episodic — and its founders responded not by minimizing exposure but by
building the entire city as a single continuous, load-sharing structure and making open pride in that
extremity the city's defining civic character, the explicit opposite of Tepenia's usual "competence without
commentary" ethos.

**Governing facts:** Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay, George V Land — a rare exposed-bedrock outcrop on an
otherwise ice-buried coast; status is Destroyed; near-constant katabatic wind, average ~80 km/h with gusts
recorded well over 300 km/h, intensifying further in winter, essentially disrespecting normal seasonal
patterns; founders inherited no substantial modern station infrastructure, unlike Casey — only a legendary,
nearly-unsurvivable expedition hut and a reputation for extremity, and built a genuine city there anyway;
the city's most distinctive built feature is functioning as one continuous, aerodynamically coherent
structure rather than separate buildings, city-wide, more comprehensively interlinked than any other wind-
exposed Tepenian city; historically a fabrication-synthesis chamber manufacturer.

---

## Extreme Wind Survival Infrastructure

1. **Near-continuous wind-monitoring infrastructure**, distinct from any episodic-wind city's own warning
   systems — Denison's wind doesn't fully respect season, so monitoring here is closer to constant
   surveillance than event-triggered alerting.
2. **Wind-window logistics infrastructure**, coordinating movement, construction, and outdoor activity
   around the gaps between wind events rather than any clean day/night or seasonal schedule.

## Interlinked City Structure Infrastructure (the defining, unique feature)

3. **The Interlinked City Structure** (established) — a comprehensive, city-wide physical joining of
   buildings into one continuous, load-sharing, aerodynamically coherent system, distinct from Janbogo's or
   Zukelli's own occasional covered connections.
4. **Landmark structures engineered for distant visibility through blowing snow**, giving Denison a
   deliberate, unified skyline legible even in severe weather — a design achievement no other wind-exposed
   Tepenian city has matched.

## Exposed Bedrock Foundation Infrastructure

5. **Structural foundation engineering on the rare exposed Cape Denison bedrock outcrop**, the specific
   geological feature that made any settlement here possible at all on an otherwise ice-buried coast.

## Historical Cradle Chamber Manufacturing Infrastructure (design-only, factual)

6. **Former fabrication-synthesis chamber manufacturing infrastructure**, consistent with Denison's
   established historical role producing chambers for the nationwide Cradle network during the Second
   Interwar Period — lost with the city's destruction, distinct from the present-day network's own active
   sites.

## Pride-in-Extremity Cultural Infrastructure

7. **Wind-Sculpture Installation Grounds** (established) — dedicated space for wind-interactive sculpture
   and installation art, a recognized local specialty turning the city's defining hazard into a civic art
   form.
8. **Whatever physical or ritual infrastructure supports the established "reciting wind statistics loudly
   and proudly" social tradition** — a genuine architecture-vs-speech compensation, since the interlinked
   structure itself keeps the wind's severity largely invisible to residents day-to-day.

## Heritage & Founding Infrastructure

9. **The Original Expedition Hut Site** (established) — preserved as the city's founding historical
   landmark tied directly to the Mawson expedition legend, distinct from Cape Adare's own older
   Borchgrevink heritage site elsewhere in the subnet.

## Ruins-Specific Infrastructure

10. **Whatever survives of the interlinked structure's own load-sharing architecture post-destruction** —
    a genuinely distinct ruins-exploration environment, since damage to a continuously joined structure
    would propagate and concentrate differently than damage to a city of separate buildings.
