# Scott — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Scott would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Scott.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Scott.md`, and `Scott_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Why Scott's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it shares Fort McMurdo's entire
geography — same island, same volcano, same climate, same seasonal ice road — but its own civic identity
is defined by scale and pace rather than environment: small, precise, overwhelmingly residential, the
direct experiential counterpart to its neighbor's constant operational churn. It's also the only city
examined so far whose founding Saint veneration (St. Robert) is the single most personally load-bearing
piece of its entire civic identity, and the only one whose physical infrastructure is genuinely,
functionally merged with a separate, distinct neighboring city.

**Governing facts:** Hut Point Peninsula, Ross Island — approximately 3km from Fort McMurdo on the same
volcanic promontory, sharing its climate, Mount Erebus visibility, McMurdo Sound sea ice dynamics, and spur
road connection to Hwy 183; status is damaged but partially operational, its war experience likely
intertwined with Fort McMurdo's given physical proximity and shared infrastructure; overwhelmingly
residential in physical footprint, with a modest handful of public gathering spaces — a genuinely quiet
place to live, not a working city; home to Hut Point itself, Robert Falcon Scott's 1902 discovery hut and
Tepenia's most personally significant Saint-veneration site; one small confirmed industry — collecting
volcanic material fallen from Erebus and forwarding it to Fort McMurdo's trucking network — alongside a
technical/research base, neither of which defines the city's own quiet daily character.

## Twin-City Shared Infrastructure

1. **The Scott–Fort McMurdo Border** (established) — a formally maintained but practically porous line
   between the two cities, observed by both sides even where functionally arbitrary.
2. **Shared Emergency Infrastructure** (established) — physical systems tying Scott and Fort McMurdo
   together, merged out of wartime necessity and still active post-war, distinct from either city's own
   independent civic systems.

## Hut Point / St. Robert Heritage Infrastructure

3. **Hut Point** (established) — Scott's most sacred civic location, the 1902 discovery hut itself, among
   the oldest surviving human-made structures in Antarctica; not a museum but a living remembrance site.
4. **Whatever physical infrastructure supports the established Tepenian Independence Day Hut Point
   Remembrance**, a shared civic ritual belonging to both Scott and Fort McMurdo at once — candles, flowers,
   and personal tokens laid at the site by residents of both cities.

## Residential-Dominant Civic Infrastructure

5. **Housing infrastructure dominating the city's own physical footprint**, distinct from Fort McMurdo's
   own industrial-dominant land use — the direct experiential counterpart to its neighbor's constant
   operational churn.
6. **A modest handful of public gathering spaces (restaurants, general leisure)**, consistent with the
   established "genuinely decent, quiet place to raise a family" civic character.

## Precision Calibration & Monitoring Infrastructure

7. **The Calibration & Monitoring Labs** (established) — precision equipment calibration and
   environmental/geological monitoring for the whole Hut Point Peninsula, including a genuine share of Fort
   McMurdo's own Erebus program.

## Small-Scale Volcanic Material Industry

8. **Volcanic-material collection infrastructure**, gathering fallen Erebus material and forwarding it to
   Fort McMurdo's own trucking network for onward processing — a small, contained operation consistent with
   Scott's established civic identity, real economic activity that doesn't define the city's own daily
   texture.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

9. **Damage-assessment and repair infrastructure calibrated to Scott's own smaller physical scale**,
   distinct from Fort McMurdo's own enormous, only-partially-collapsed industrial footprint — genuinely
   likely intertwined with Fort McMurdo's own war experience given the two cities' physical proximity and
   shared systems.
