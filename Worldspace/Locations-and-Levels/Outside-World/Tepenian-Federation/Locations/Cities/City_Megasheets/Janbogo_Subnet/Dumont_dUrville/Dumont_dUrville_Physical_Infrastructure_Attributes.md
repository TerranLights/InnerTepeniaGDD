# Dumont d'Urville — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Dumont d'Urville would logically have
to possess, built directly from `Specs/Dumont_dUrville.md`, `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Dumont_dUrville.md`,
and `Dumont_dUrville_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately
deferred until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Why Dumont d'Urville's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only Janbogo subnet
city built on an offshore island rather than the continental mainland or a nunatak, giving it a genuinely
different isolation profile — reachable only by seasonal sea-ice crossing or boat, with a permanent bridge
an explicitly open engineering question rather than settled fact. It's also the only city in this pass with
an established dense, "New Orleans at 1/20th scale" downtown character, and shares its daily environment
with one of Antarctica's largest Adélie penguin breeding grounds as an ordinary, year-round social fact
rather than a ruins-only feature (unlike Cape Adare).

**Governing facts:** built on Petrel Island, Géologie Archipelago, ~5km off the Adélie Land coast; status is
damaged but partially operational, not destroyed; katabatic wind here is even more extreme than Mirny's,
among the most severe on Earth, though the island position provides some archipelago shelter relative to
the open coast; a genuine two-highway junction (Hwy 2's eastern terminus, Hwy 183's northern terminus); the
downtown core on Petrel Island itself is dense, expensive, and genuinely alive, with heating infrastructure
running between buildings, not just inside them; the Adélie penguin colonies are an unavoidable, valued
year-round presence; whether a permanent bridge to the mainland exists is an explicitly unresolved open
question (aerodynamic flutter risk, pack ice pier loads, maintenance access all genuine unsolved
engineering problems); Pink Lucy's origin city.

---

## Island Foundation & Isolation Infrastructure

1. **Foundation and structural engineering on Petrel Island's own exposed rock**, distinct from a
   continental nunatak's own bedrock siting — genuinely bounded on all sides by water/ice channels rather
   than open plateau.
2. **The Channel Crossing** (established) — the ~5km route between Petrel Island and the continental
   coast, seasonally dual-mode (sea ice in winter, boat in summer), with the shoulder-season transition
   logistics explicitly flagged as unresolved, the same open flag as Fort McMurdo's own sea-ice road.
3. **Whatever partial bridge infrastructure exists, if any** — an explicitly open question given the real
   unsolved engineering problems (wind-flutter risk on a ~5km span, pack ice pier loading, maintenance
   access under extreme wind) that no real-world Antarctic engineering has actually solved to date.

## Extreme Wind Infrastructure

4. **Wind-resistant engineering exceeding even Mirny's own established standard**, consistent with
   Dumont d'Urville sitting in the same extreme Adélie Land katabatic regime as Denison, moderated only
   slightly by archipelago shelter.
5. **Low, anchored, wind-oriented architectural design**, distinct from Denison's own fully-interlinked
   continuous structure — Dumont d'Urville's own approach is described as negotiation with the wind rather
   than total structural fusion against it.

## Downtown Density & Heated Interstitial Infrastructure

6. **Dedicated heating infrastructure running between buildings downtown**, not just inside them — keeping
   the spaces between structures genuinely livable against the wind, a materially different engineering
   commitment than any city examined so far that only heats interiors.
7. **The Downtown Eateries-with-Stages district** (established) — dense, small, packed live-music venues
   built into ordinary eateries, the "New Orleans at 1/20th scale" civic character made physical.
8. **Spatially stratified real-estate infrastructure**, running outward from the sheltered, walkable
   downtown core (highest value) to cheaper residential territory across the channel crossing, to the
   highway network beyond.

## Adélie Penguin Colony Coexistence Infrastructure

9. **The Adélie Breeding Grounds** (established) — colonies occupying the Géologie Archipelago, an
   unavoidable, year-round environmental and food-supplement presence distinct from Cape Adare's own
   ruins-only rookery.
10. **Naturalist Observation Blinds** (established) — dedicated space for the established robot
    naturalist-observation tradition, the city's closest thing to a shared contemplative practice.

## Maritime Freight Infrastructure

11. **The Dumont d'Urville Port** (established) — receiving Australian freighter shipments during the
    seasonal navigable window, distinct from Janbogo's own year-round polynya access.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

12. **Ongoing repair infrastructure reflecting a mix of damaged and intact sections**, consistent with the
    established "damaged but standing" status — a geographically complete, bounded exploration environment
    given the island's own natural borders.
