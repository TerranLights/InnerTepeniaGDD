# Mawson — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every genuinely open question about Mawson given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Mawson_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-07. Given how developed this city already is, this document resolves placeholders rather than inventing large new structure. One item is deliberately left untouched: whether Mawson's own founding-nation demographic imbalance gets the same correction treatment Shirayuki and Soyuz received — already explicitly flagged in `City_Vision_Notes/Mawson.md` as the developer's own call, not yet decided, and forcing an answer here would work against that.

---

## I. The Arcanet Hub's Damage, Specifically

**Proposed:** the Planetary Split Brain severed *inter-subnet* Arcanet links specifically, not intra-subnet ones — the same mechanism already used to explain why Davis, Casey, and Mirny's own Australian heritage plausibly survived within the Mirny subnet. Applied here: Mawson's hub function connecting the wider Mawson subnet to the rest of Tepenia was a deliberate strategic target and took the brunt of the damage, consistent with "damaged but partially operational." But Mawson's own intra-subnet connections — to Sayowa and Dome Fuji specifically — plausibly survived in better shape, or proved more repairable, since that was never the kind of link the Split Brain itself severed. The subnet's own internal coordination could very plausibly still function even where its outward connection to the rest of Tepenia does not.

---

## II. The Australian Antarctic Territory Claim, Post-War

**Proposed:** the claim persists as historical and ceremonial memory — bound up entirely with St. Douglas veneration and civic pride in Mawson's own founding story — but carries no functional governmental authority in the post-Split-Brain reality. This is consistent with Fort McMurdo's own resolved status as historical-only national capital, holding the title through the Long Night War but with no present-day authority transferring anywhere. Mawson's relationship to the old Territory claim is the same shape: real, honored, remembered, and no longer operative.

---

## III. Demonym: Mawsonite

Following the same "-ite" suffix pattern already established at Kunlun (Kunlunite) and Davis (Davisite) — **Mawsonite**, a clean, natural continuation of the pattern rather than the "-er" suffix floated as an alternative candidate in `Specs/Mawson.md`.

## V. Honeymoon Traditions, Specifically

**Proposed:** a customary visit to the Prince Charles Mountains overlook above Horseshoe Harbour, timed for whichever of polar night or midnight sun happens to be in season during the stay — couples are said to make the trip regardless of which extreme they encounter, treating whichever one it is as the honeymoon's own defining shared memory. This gives the already-established "one to two week" stay a concrete anchor activity, consistent with the city's own established hospitality-forward, resort-adjacent character, without requiring an invented festival or elaborate ceremony that would sit oddly against Mawson's otherwise understated civic tone.

---

## VI. The Jeju Parallel — A Contrast, Not a Hidden History

**Proposed, resolved carefully:** Jeju City's own real history — a warm, decades-earned honeymoon reputation sitting atop a genuinely dark, long-suppressed historical layer — is not proposed here as a hidden trauma Mawson also secretly carries. Instead, the real value of the comparison is in the contrast it throws into relief: Mawson's own established fault line (Section 5b, Local Cultures) is explicitly *not* hidden or suppressed — it's described as "a genuinely accepted one," openly acknowledged rather than buried. Read against Jeju's real pattern of suppression-until-decades-later, Mawson's own choice to stay open about the gap between St. Douglas's founding legacy and its actual Chinese-Primary present reads as a deliberate, meaningful civic choice, not a passive default — the city could have gone the way Jeju's real history did, and specifically didn't.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Section I is the most structurally useful proposal here — it gives Mawson's own "damaged but partially operational" hub status a real, specific mechanism (inter-subnet link severed, intra-subnet connections plausibly intact) rather than leaving "the hub took damage" as an undifferentiated blank. Section VI is worth flagging as the most delicate: it deliberately resolves the Jeju comparison as a contrast rather than an invitation to invent unearned trauma for a city whose whole established character is warmth and genuine acceptance.
