# Mawson Subnet — Full Extrapolation (Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Step 2)

**What this is:** every open question about Mawson subnet as a whole given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established across all 3 City Megasheets rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Mawson_Subnet_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-10. Scaled to a 3-city subnet — fewer sections than the larger subnets' own Full Extrapolations, since there are genuinely fewer subnet-scale gaps to resolve here.

---

## I. Each city's own specific role within the subnet's "looking outward" identity

Mirroring the district-level and other-subnet exercises, but adapted to this subnet's own dominant trait — a subnet defined more by its outward connections than by any shared internal identity:

- **Mawson carries the hub's own quiet acceptance** — a founding gap made peace with, a hospitality reputation built to serve people passing through on their way to build lives elsewhere.
- **Sayowa carries the connective function itself** — a small city whose entire significance is what runs through it, deliberately spared by an enemy who needed that function too much to end it.
- **Dome Fuji carries the subnet's one truly self-contained identity, oriented entirely toward a religious relationship outside the subnet** — a population that arrived from everywhere and looks, doctrinally, toward Kunlun rather than toward its own subnet-mates.

## II. Whether Mawson's Own Founding-Nation Imbalance Gets the Shirayuki/Soyuz Correction Treatment — Left Genuinely Open

Consistent with the explicit note already on record in `City_Vision_Notes/Mawson.md` (the developer's own call, not yet decided), this document does not propose an answer. Forcing one here would work directly against the developer's own stated intent to decide this separately, on its own terms, rather than as an automatic extension of the Shirayuki/Soyuz precedent. Worth noting only that this Ultra-Megasheet's own Pattern 1 (founding-population displacement in three shapes) already treats Mawson's *current* peaceful dilution as a settled, accepted civic fact — if the correction is ever made, that acceptance narrative would need to be revisited alongside the numbers themselves, not left standing on its own.

---

## III. Extending the "Reaching Versus Arriving" Theology Beyond Dome Fuji and Kunlun

**Proposed, extending Dome Fuji's own Cross-Reference Finding 6 directly:** if "reaching versus arriving" is genuinely general enough to describe secular Tepenian sites (the Full Extrapolation source material already proposes the Amundsen Tower and Lake Vostok as candidates), Mawson subnet's own three cities can be read through the same lens without strain. Mawson's own honeymoon-destination reputation is a "reaching" identity — built deliberately, decades-earned, an achievement the city worked to construct. Dome Fuji is, naturally, "arriving." Sayowa sits in between, and interestingly doesn't resolve cleanly to either: its significance was never built (no one designed the Sayowa Junction to matter) nor is it a matter of pure presence (the junction's importance depends entirely on function, not stillness) — making Sayowa a genuine third case the duality doesn't yet account for, worth flagging as a limit on how far "reaching versus arriving" can be stretched before it stops being a clean binary.

---

## IV. A Proposed Notable Figure: The Sayowa–Dome Fuji Supply Liaison

**Proposed, new figure:** a Sayowa-based logistics coordinator — placeholder title **Aviation Liaison**, currently unnamed — responsible for Sayowa's own end of the historical Troll–Soyuz–Dome Fuji aviation supply route (Mawson Cross-City Throughways, Throughway 3), working alongside the already-proposed Junction Coordinator Wei-Lin Andersen (Sayowa's own Full Extrapolation, Section III). Given Sayowa's own established civic character — quiet, dutiful, valued for function rather than visibility — this figure would never have been publicly credited for keeping one of Tepenia's most isolated populations supplied, consistent with the same "quietly load-bearing" theme already confirmed project-wide in Halley, Janbogo/Ross, and Mirny subnets alike.

---

## Worth Your Attention

Section III is the most interesting proposal here, not because it resolves cleanly, but because it doesn't — finding a real limit to an otherwise elegant theological duality (Sayowa fitting neither "reaching" nor "arriving") is a more honest and more useful outcome than forcing a fit that isn't actually there. Section II is the most important to leave alone: the developer's own explicit "not yet decided" on Mawson's founding-nation correction deserves to stay genuinely open, not quietly resolved by inertia.
