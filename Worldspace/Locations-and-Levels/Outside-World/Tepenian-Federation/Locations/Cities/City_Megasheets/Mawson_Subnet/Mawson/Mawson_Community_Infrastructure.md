# Mawson — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Mawson's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and all 31 Mirny, Halley, Janbogo, and Palmer subnet cities, extended here. This begins the Mawson subnet, the last one in this push. **Note:** "The Unbroken Watch" cites "continuous operation since 1954" — the real Mawson Station's actual founding year — as the object of celebration, the same GPS-only pattern already caught repeatedly (most recently Lazar's own "Unbroken Watch"). Reframed below to honor the city's own continuous Tepenian-era operation instead.
