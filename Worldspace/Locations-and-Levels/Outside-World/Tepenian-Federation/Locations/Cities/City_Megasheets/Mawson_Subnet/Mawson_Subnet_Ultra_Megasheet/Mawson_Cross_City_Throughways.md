# Mawson Subnet — Cross-City Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors that pass through Mawson subnet's cities — not things the cities have in common (see Cross-City Patterns for that), but chains showing how a fact in one city leads through another to a consequence in a third, most often reaching outside the subnet entirely, consistent with this subnet's own established "connective tissue" character (Pattern 5). Written 2026-07-10.

## Throughway 1: Dome Fuji's relationship to Kunlun ties this subnet directly into Mirny subnet's own established Vostok-Kunlun material

**Dome Fuji and Kunlun are the two primary sites of Ice Cold Buddhism, related as complementary theological lenses rather than rival or hierarchical ones — Kunlun expressing devotion through vocation and construction (reaching), Dome Fuji through pure arrival and stillness (arriving)** (Dome Fuji Full Extrapolation, Section VII; Cross-Reference, Finding 6) → **this "reaching versus arriving" duality was proposed as general enough to extend past the faith itself, offering interpretive language for other built-versus-natural sacred or significant sites in Tepenia** (Dome Fuji Cross-Reference, Finding 6, citing the Amundsen Tower and Lake Vostok as secular parallels) → **meanwhile, Dome Fuji's own closest personality match by Enneagram is Vostok, not Kunlun — the identical Instinctive/Withdrawn/Positive Outlook triple, distinguished only by which direction each city's devotion faces (sky-and-orbit for Kunlun-adjacent Vostok's own comms work, subglacial biology for Vostok itself)** (Dome Fuji Cross-Reference, Finding 5) → **meaning Dome Fuji has two different Mirny subnet "siblings" depending on which axis is measured — temperament (Vostok) and purpose (Kunlun) — and neither overlaps with the other** → **making Dome Fuji this subnet's single deepest point of connection into Mirny subnet's own already-developed material, a genuine three-way relationship (Dome Fuji, Vostok, Kunlun) spanning two subnets that neither subnet's own Ultra-Megasheet could have fully captured working alone.**

## Throughway 2: This subnet's own war-damage logic draws one example from each of the project's other subnets' own frameworks, rather than sharing one internally

**Mawson's own "damaged but partially operational" hub status resolves, per this Ultra-Megasheet's own Full Extrapolation, into a severed inter-subnet Arcanet link alongside plausibly-intact intra-subnet connections — the same dispersed-function survival logic already established for Fort McMurdo in Janbogo/Ross subnet** (Mawson Full Extrapolation, Section I) → **Sayowa's own survival traces to a mechanism this project had never catalogued before — an attacker's own calculated restraint, preserving a structurally critical junction specifically because destroying it fully would have cost the attacker access they still needed** (Sayowa Cross-Reference, Finding 2) → **Dome Fuji's own survival traces to sheer remoteness and lack of strategic value, the identical mechanism already established for Vostok and Kunlun in Mirny subnet** (Dome Fuji Cross-Reference; `Mirny_Cross_City_Patterns.md`, Pattern 2) → **meaning this subnet's own three cities each demonstrate a different mechanism already established (or newly established) somewhere else in the project's own accumulated damage-logic framework, rather than sharing one internally consistent principle the way Janbogo/Ross subnet's two-principle taxonomy or Mirny subnet's single-principle taxonomy each did** → **making Mawson subnet, war-damage-wise, less a fifth independent case and more a working microcosm of everything the project has already established, worth treating as the natural closing comparison point now that all five subnets have been examined.**
