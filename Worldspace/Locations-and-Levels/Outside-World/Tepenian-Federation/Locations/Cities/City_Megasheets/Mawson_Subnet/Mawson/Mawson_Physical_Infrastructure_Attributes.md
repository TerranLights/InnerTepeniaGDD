# Mawson — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Mawson would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Mawson.md`, `Local_Cultures/Mawson_Subnet/Mawson.md`, and
`Mawson_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 (Cross-Referenced
Extrapolation Findings) is deliberately deferred until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not
locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Why this city's attributes look nothing like Byrd's or Dome Fuji's:** Mawson is coastal, not underground
or high-plateau; it's **damaged but partially operational** (real, ongoing war damage), not intact like
Byrd or Dome Fuji; it has a large, mixed human/robot population (~950k-1.4M) rather than a small or
100%-robot one; and its economy runs on hub logistics, maritime trade, and hospitality/honeymoon tourism
rather than fabrication or devotion. The categories below are derived fresh from these facts.

**Governing facts:** coastal position on Horseshoe Harbour, Holme Bay, Mac.Robertson Land, facing the
Indian Ocean; East Antarctic coastal climate — katabatic winds off the plateau, milder than interior
cities (mean annual −11.2°C, coldest month avg −20°C); status is **damaged but partially operational**,
not fully intact; the Mawson subnet's own Arcanet hub, its inter-subnet link severed by the Planetary
Split Brain while intra-subnet links to Sayowa and Dome Fuji plausibly survived better; population
~950k-1.4M combined human/robot, largest of the three Mawson subnet cities by a wide margin; economy is
subnet hub logistics, maritime trade, scientific research, and hospitality/honeymoon tourism — Tepenia's
go-to honeymoon destination for human-robot couples; sits on Hwy 4 between the Sayowa Junction and
Sinheung.

---

## Maritime/Coastal Infrastructure

1. **Harbor infrastructure at Holme Bay** (established: Holme Bay Harbor) — docking, freight receiving,
   and maritime coordination, the city's primary point of entry.
2. **Katabatic wind-resistant structural engineering** — the plateau's cold air draining directly down
   onto the coast is a genuinely distinct structural stressor from the calm plateau winds at Dome Fuji or
   the buried, wind-irrelevant construction at Byrd; buildings here plausibly need real wind-loading
   engineering none of those other cities do.
3. **Seasonal harbor operations infrastructure** — coastal Antarctic harbors typically see real seasonal
   ice variation; Holme Bay's own operational capacity plausibly varies meaningfully across the year,
   requiring genuine seasonal scheduling rather than year-round uniform access.

## War-Damage & Partial-Operation Infrastructure

4. **Ongoing damage-assessment and repair-prioritization infrastructure** — "damaged but partially
   operational" isn't a one-time status, it's an active, managed condition; a city functioning at this
   level plausibly maintains a genuine triage process for what gets fixed first given real, ongoing
   resource constraints.
5. **Redundant or backup Arcanet infrastructure for the hub's own severed inter-subnet link** — given the
   subnet hub's primary outward connection took the brunt of Planetary Split Brain damage, some form of
   contingency routing or backup relay plausibly exists, even if only partially functional, given how
   consequential losing hub function entirely would be for the whole subnet.
6. **Visible, unrepaired damage as an ordinary part of the cityscape** — unlike Byrd or Dome Fuji, both
   confirmed intact, Mawson's residents plausibly live alongside real, physically present war damage as
   simply part of daily environment, not a crisis in active resolution.

## Arcanet Hub Infrastructure

7. **The Subnet Hub Arcanet Installation** (established) — the physical communications node itself,
   damaged but partially functional.
8. **Intra-subnet relay infrastructure to Sayowa and Dome Fuji**, plausibly the hub's own best-preserved
   connections — per the established mechanism (the Split Brain severed *inter*-subnet links specifically,
   not intra-subnet ones), Mawson's physical relay equipment serving its own two subnet-mates is a genuine
   distinct asset from the badly damaged outward-facing infrastructure.

## Hospitality & Honeymoon Tourism Infrastructure

9. **Dedicated honeymoon-destination accommodations** (established: "The Hospitality Infrastructure") —
   formalized lodging built specifically for this economic sector, distinct from ordinary residential
   housing.
10. **The Prince Charles Mountains Overlook** (established) — a maintained scenic site above Horseshoe
    Harbour, timed to whichever of polar night or midnight sun is in season during a given stay.
11. **Guest reception and hospitality-service infrastructure**, sized to Mawson's own established status
    as Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination — a real, sustained visitor-facing service economy distinct
    from the reserved, visitor-is-a-rare-event hospitality register seen at isolated cities like Byrd.

## Subnet Hub & Logistics Coordination Infrastructure

12. **A logistics coordination center for the Mawson subnet's own internal economy** — the physical seat
    of the hub function itself, distinct from the Arcanet installation (communications) and the harbor
    (maritime freight specifically).
13. **Highway junction infrastructure on Hwy 4**, between the Sayowa Junction and Sinheung — freight and
    transit coordination for the overland route connecting Mawson to the rest of its own subnet and,
    beyond it, to Sinheung and Shirayuki.

## Population-Scale Infrastructure

14. **Large-scale residential and civic infrastructure**, sized to a population in the high hundreds of
    thousands to low millions — an order of magnitude larger than either Byrd or Dome Fuji, requiring
    genuinely different infrastructure scale (water, waste, housing density) than either of those two
    smaller cities' own attribute sets would suggest.
15. **Water production and waste management at coastal-city scale** — a coastal location gives Mawson
    real advantages over interior cities (direct seawater access, less extreme ambient cold reducing some
    heating burden), but the sheer population size means the underlying infrastructure still needs to
    operate at real civic scale, not a small-settlement scale.

## Civic & Institutional Infrastructure

16. **The Public Libraries** (established, plural) — a genuine civic institution consistent with the
    city's hospitality-forward character; the plural form itself implies multiple branches, appropriate to
    a population this large.
17. **St. Douglas's Landing** (established, name itself flagged for eventual renaming, veneration
    unaffected) — a physical commemorative site tied to the Tepenian Saints framework, honoring the city's
    own namesake.

## Legacy/Continuity Infrastructure

18. **Physical markers or maintained sites tied to "The Unbroken Watch"** — the observance marking
    Mawson's own continuous Tepenian-era operation (the longest of any Mawson subnet city) plausibly has
    some maintained physical anchor, not just a calendar date, given how central this continuity is to the
    city's own established civic identity.

## Cross-Referenced Extrapolation Findings (Methodology #2)

**What this is:** cross-referencing the 18 attributes above against Mawson's own existing lore —
`Mawson_Mega_Init.md`, `Mawson_Full_Extrapolation.md`, and `Mawson_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md` (which
already holds 3 Findings of its own, written 2026-07-07). Given **direct developer instruction** that
Mawson needs to carry disproportionate weight within its own subnet — Sayowa has comparatively little
content and Dome Fuji, however deep, is entirely optional/non-main-questline — this pass runs deeper than
Dome Fuji's own Methodology #2 pass, though still nowhere near Byrd's own 57-Finding scale. Written
2026-07-30.

### Finding A: the war forced a structural pivot from outward gateway to inward household-keeper

**Combining:** attribute #5 (redundant/backup Arcanet infrastructure for the severed inter-subnet link) +
attribute #8 (intra-subnet relay to Sayowa and Dome Fuji, plausibly the hub's best-preserved connection) +
the Full Extrapolation's own established mechanism (the Split Brain severed inter-subnet links specifically,
not intra-subnet ones).

**2nd-order effect:** Mawson's hub function today is materially better at coordinating its own subnet's
internal life than at reaching the rest of Tepenia — the reverse of its founding-era purpose as Tepenia's
own gateway to the Indian Ocean.

**3rd-order effect:** this is a genuine, forced identity shift worth naming directly — post-war Mawson is
less "the door Tepenia opens outward through" and more "the household that keeps its own two rooms
(Sayowa, Dome Fuji) running," a quieter, more inward-facing role than the city's own founding-era
self-image ever anticipated.

### Finding B: newlyweds arrive into a city that never hid its own scars

**Combining:** attribute #6 (visible, unrepaired war damage as ordinary part of the cityscape) + attributes
#9–11 (dedicated honeymoon accommodations, guest reception infrastructure) + the established honeymoon-
destination reputation.

**2nd-order effect:** couples arriving for a one-to-two-week honeymoon walk directly past real, lived-with
war damage on their way to the city's own cultivated hospitality infrastructure — the warmth and the
wreckage share the same streets.

**3rd-order effect:** this gives Mawson a genuinely distinctive tonal texture among Tepenia's
hospitality-oriented locations — not a polished, damage-free resort bubble, but a warmth that coexists
openly with visible cost, consistent with the same "genuinely accepted, not suppressed" civic honesty
Section VI of the Full Extrapolation already establishes for the city's demographic fault line.

### Finding C: Founding Administrator Warrick Zhao's legacy has a concrete physical address

**Combining:** attribute #12 (the logistics coordination center, the physical seat of the hub function) +
the Full Extrapolation's Notable Figure Warrick Zhao (credited with steering the transition from AAD
operations to genuine Tepenian civic institution, "hub function mattering more than any single national
identity").

**2nd-order effect:** the coordination center is plausibly the direct institutional descendant of Zhao's own
founding-era work — not just a historical credit, but a specific, still-functioning building.

**3rd-order effect:** gives a currently-abstract placeholder figure a concrete physical location to be
written into a scene around, the same kind of upgrade Dome Fuji's own Finding D gave its ancient-caretaker
figure.

### Finding D: the Public Libraries are load-bearing evidence for Finding 2's own "two separately-earned traits" claim

**Combining:** attribute #16 (the Public Libraries, established plural) + the existing Cross-Reference
Synthesis's Finding 2 (hospitality and demographic plurality are two separately-earned civic traits, not
one causing the other) + the Mega-Init's Odessa/Varna real-world grounding for genuine, institutionally
cultivated hospitality.

**2nd-order effect:** the libraries are a concrete physical instance of Finding 2's own abstract claim — a
civic institution built for community and access, not tourism, that happens to belong to the exact same
deliberate hospitality-building project as the honeymoon industry.

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Finding 2's own distinction checkable against a real building rather than
resting purely on stated civic character — the libraries are what "hospitality as institutional priority,
not demographic byproduct" actually looks like on the ground.

### Finding E: St. Douglas's Landing is the physical shape of "real, honored, and no longer operative"

**Combining:** attribute #17 (St. Douglas's Landing) + the Full Extrapolation's Section II (the Australian
Antarctic Territory claim persists as ceremonial and historical memory, with no functional governmental
authority post-Split-Brain).

**2nd-order effect:** the Landing is specifically where that resolved, historical-only claim gets a physical
anchor — a site that carries real emotional and civic weight while asserting zero present-day governmental
authority.

**3rd-order effect:** this mirrors Fort McMurdo's own resolved capital status almost exactly (real,
honored, historical, non-operative) — worth naming as a small, recurring Tepenian civic pattern: founding-
era authority claims that survive as honored memory long after they stop meaning anything functionally.

### Finding F: the Unbroken Watch's most plausible physical anchor is a piece of infrastructure still actually running

**Combining:** attribute #18 (physical markers tied to "The Unbroken Watch") + attributes #7–8 (the Subnet
Hub Arcanet Installation and its intra-subnet relay) + the existing Cross-Reference Synthesis's Finding 1
("the name that outlasted the founders" operating infrastructurally, not just demographically).

**2nd-order effect:** the Arcanet hub installation is the single piece of Mawson infrastructure that has
plausibly been continuously, functionally staffed since the city's own Tepenian founding — making it the
most literal possible anchor for an observance about unbroken continuity.

**3rd-order effect:** this ties the observance to something the player (or any in-world visitor) could
actually be shown still working, rather than a purely commemorative plaque or date on a calendar —
continuity made visible and operational, not just stated.

### Finding G: Mawson's harbor seasonality and its honeymoon seasonality are the same civic rhythm

**Combining:** attribute #3 (seasonal harbor operations, real ice variation across the year) + the
established Prince Charles Mountains Overlook tradition (timed to whichever of polar night or midnight sun
is in season during a given stay).

**2nd-order effect:** two independently-established seasonal facts about Mawson — the harbor's own
ice-driven operational calendar and the honeymoon industry's own polar-night/midnight-sun framing — turn
out to run on the same underlying clock.

**3rd-order effect:** this is worth naming directly as a single integrated civic rhythm rather than two
coincidentally seasonal systems: Mawson's whole year, freight and tourism alike, moves to the same seasonal
beat, giving the city a coherence between its practical and ceremonial economies that neither fact alone
implies.

### Finding H: rebuilding priorities create a real, unaddressed point of quiet civic friction

**Combining:** attribute #4 (ongoing damage-assessment and repair-prioritization infrastructure) +
attributes #9–11 (the honeymoon hospitality economy's own premium guest-facing infrastructure).

**2nd-order effect:** the same constrained post-war rebuilding capacity has to be split between ordinary
residential/civic repair and maintaining the honeymoon economy's own guest-facing polish — a genuine
resource-allocation tension nothing in the existing lore currently addresses.

**3rd-order effect:** whether repair priority quietly favors guest-facing areas (protecting the revenue the
honeymoon economy brings in) or residential ones (protecting residents first) is a realistic, currently
open point of civic tension — a small, believable seam in an otherwise warm, harmonious civic self-image.

### Finding I: Mawson's coastal engineering knowledge is a direct, practical export to Sayowa

**Combining:** attribute #2 (katabatic wind-resistant structural engineering) + Sayowa's own newly-derived
Base Attributes (this session — industrial/residential split, a critical highway junction physically near
but not inside the city) + the shared Hwy 4 connection between the two.

**2nd-order effect:** both subnet cities face the same katabatic coastal exposure, meaning Mawson's own
storm-resistant construction expertise is directly transferable to Sayowa's own rebuilding needs.

**3rd-order effect:** this gives the hub-and-junction relationship between Mawson and Sayowa a concrete,
practical dimension beyond simple highway connectivity — genuine technical exchange, not just freight
passing through.

### Finding J: Mawson's population scale is the actual precondition for its honeymoon economy, not just a cultural choice

**Combining:** attributes #14–15 (large-scale residential, water, and waste infrastructure sized to a
population in the high hundreds of thousands to low millions) + the established fact that Mawson is by far
the largest of the three Mawson subnet cities.

**2nd-order effect:** sustaining a genuine, year-round guest-services economy requires a civic infrastructure
base that neither Sayowa nor Dome Fuji actually has at their own respective scales.

**3rd-order effect:** this reframes "why Mawson became the honeymoon destination and not one of its
subnet-mates" as partly a hard infrastructure answer, not only a cultural one — the city's own sheer scale
is a genuine precondition for the hospitality economy Findings B, D, and G all describe, not just a
backdrop to it.
