## Who Lives Here, and Why

Mawson subnet's three cities carry three genuinely different relationships to their own founding populations, spanning the full range this project has documented anywhere: Mawson's peaceful, accepted dilution (Australian founders, Chinese-Primary present, no unresolved tension); Sayowa's institutional-weight-without-demographic-weight split (Japanese founders diluted to Significant tier, the Shōwa-era name still meaningful only to a shrinking few); and Dome Fuji's total population succession (the entire founding Japanese research population gone by ordinary attrition, replaced by a 100%-robot devotee population for whom national origin isn't even a relevant category anymore).

This makes Mawson subnet, more than any other in the project, the place where the question "what happens to a founding population over centuries" gets answered in its most varied and most extreme forms — not a subnet with one demographic story and small variations on it, but three genuinely different endings to the same kind of beginning.

---

## What It Feels Like

Moving through Mawson subnet means moving between three places that share almost no register with each other. Mawson itself is warm, hospitable, genuinely resort-like — Tepenia's honeymoon capital, a city that decided decades ago to be good news and has stayed that way. Sayowa is quiet, dutiful, and modest — a small city carrying an entire Japanese imperial era in its own name, valued for what runs through it rather than what it is, deliberately spared by an enemy who needed it too much to finish destroying it. And Dome Fuji, reached only through a genuinely dangerous interior plateau crossing, is something closer to a held breath — total flatness, near-total silence, a population of robot devotees who arrived one at a time from every corner of Tepenia and share nothing but the cold they came to commune with. Nothing about moving from one of these cities to the next feels like staying within the same subnet; each one feels like arriving somewhere that could just as easily belong to a different part of the country entirely.

## Personality (A Whole-Subnet Read)

Unlike Halley subnet's "Competency turned inward," Janbogo/Ross subnet's "Survival read as a verdict," or Mirny subnet's "Function outliving the need for credit," Mawson subnet genuinely doesn't reduce to one collective personality, even loosely — per this Ultra-Megasheet's own Pattern 3, its three cities share no single Enneagram trait across all three, only a shifting web of partial overlaps (Mawson and Sayowa's shared Compliant orientation toward serving an external need; Mawson and Dome Fuji's shared Positive Outlook toward embracing rather than enduring their circumstance; Sayowa and Dome Fuji's shared Instinctive, physically-grounded engagement with their environment). Where every other subnet examined so far produced a genuine unifying civic character, Mawson subnet's honest personality is that it doesn't have one — three cities connected more by what lies beyond their own borders than by anything shared between them.

## Real-World Inspirations, Researched

### Primary: The real geographic spread of Mac.Robertson Land through the East Antarctic interior

The real region spanned by this subnet's three sites — from Mawson Station's coastal harbor through Syowa's island position to Dome Fuji's remote interior plateau — covers a genuinely wider range of terrain and climate extremity than any other subnet's own real-world basis, mirroring this subnet's own established lack of a single unifying character.

**What this gives the subnet:** direct grounding for why three cities founded within the same broad region could feel so unrelated to each other — the real geography itself spans coastal harbor, island, and remote high-altitude interior, three genuinely different kinds of place sharing a regional label more than a lived character.

### Secondary: Trogir, Varna, Jeju City, and Odessa (already researched for Mawson specifically)

Give Mawson's own "name that outlasted the founders" identity and its hospitality-driven civic character concrete real-world grounding, worth reading alongside Shirayuki's own Harajuku/Shibuya research (Mirny subnet) as two different real-world-grounded models for how an imposed or displaced origin becomes something a population genuinely chooses to keep.

### Secondary: Felixstowe, Djibouti City, Trois-Rivières, and Perpignan (already researched for Sayowa specifically)

Give Sayowa's own small-population, outsized-function identity concrete precedent, and directly inform this Ultra-Megasheet's own Pattern 5 — every one of these real-world picks describes a place whose significance depends entirely on what connects through it, not what it contains within itself.

### Secondary: Paro Taktsang, the Potala Palace, and Angkor Wat (already researched for Dome Fuji specifically)

Already the richest single research thread in this subnet, and the source of this Ultra-Megasheet's own Throughway 1 — the "reaching versus arriving" theological duality between Kunlun and Dome Fuji, general enough to extend past Ice Cold Buddhism into secular Tepenian geography and infrastructure as well.
