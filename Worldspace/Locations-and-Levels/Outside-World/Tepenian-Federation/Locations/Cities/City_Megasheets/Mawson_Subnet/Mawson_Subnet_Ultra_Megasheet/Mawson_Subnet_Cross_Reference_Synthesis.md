# Mawson Subnet — Cross-Reference Synthesis (Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Step 3)

**What this is:** as with the four subnet Ultra-Megasheets before it, this step is largely already complete — the cross-city Patterns and Throughways work in this same folder already did the implication-hunting this step exists to produce. The work here is selecting, ranking, and presenting the strongest of that material as Mawson subnet's own headline findings. Written 2026-07-10.

## Finding 1: This subnet completes Japan's full Tepenian diaspora range, and does so by comparison rather than causation

**Combining:** Sayowa's own established Cross-Reference Finding 1 (three diaspora outcomes: Shirayuki protected, Princess Elisabeth organic-strong, Sayowa organic-diluted) + Dome Fuji's own original JARE founding, previously undiscussed in relation to Sayowa's own finding + Dome Fuji's total population succession, the most extreme organic outcome of all (`Mawson_Cross_City_Patterns.md`, Pattern 2).

**2nd-order effect:** the same national-origin diaspora now has four documented outcomes across the whole project, and two of the most extreme organic ones — Sayowa's dilution and Dome Fuji's total succession — sit inside this single subnet, a pairing no prior document had connected.

**3rd-order effect:** this is a comparative finding, not a causal one — worth stating plainly, since an earlier draft of this Ultra-Megasheet's own Throughways file mistakenly framed it as a causal chain running through all four cities. Only one genuine causal link exists in the underlying material (Sayowa's own file proposing that Japan's diplomats anticipated Sayowa-style dilution and secured Shirayuki as a hedge against it); Princess Elisabeth and Dome Fuji are independent parallel outcomes, not sequential consequences of each other.

**4th-order effect:** worth flagging as a small but genuine methodological lesson for the project's own remaining synthesis work — a real, valuable finding can still be misclassified as the wrong *kind* of finding (a causal throughway instead of a comparative pattern), and catching that distinction matters for keeping this whole Megasheet series honest about what it's actually claiming.

## Finding 2: This subnet's own war-damage logic is a working sampler of the whole project's framework, not a fifth independent case

**Combining:** Mawson's own split inter/intra-subnet Arcanet damage, echoing Fort McMurdo's dispersed-function survival in Janbogo/Ross subnet + Sayowa's own genuinely new "strategic value preserved by restraint" mechanism (Sayowa Cross-Reference, Finding 2) + Dome Fuji's remoteness-protected survival, echoing Vostok and Kunlun in Mirny subnet (`Mawson_Cross_City_Patterns.md`, Pattern 4).

**2nd-order effect:** unlike Janbogo/Ross subnet's two-principle taxonomy or Mirny subnet's single-principle taxonomy, Mawson subnet's own three cities don't share one damage logic internally at all — each one instances a different mechanism already established (or, in Sayowa's case, newly established) elsewhere in the project.

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Mawson subnet less a fifth independent data point in the project's own damage-logic framework and more a compressed sampler of everything the other four subnets already established — a genuinely different kind of contribution than the other subnets made, worth recognizing as such rather than searching for a single unifying principle that isn't actually there.

**4th-order effect:** now that all five subnets have been examined, the project's own complete damage-logic framework stands at: Halley subnet's four-category damage-*mechanism* taxonomy, Janbogo/Ross subnet's two-principle damage-*logic* taxonomy, Mirny subnet's single-principle damage-logic case, and Mawson subnet's own three-mechanism sampler — a genuinely complete, cross-referenced picture of how the Long Night War played out differently by region, ready for the developer's own planned full re-examination pass to draw on directly.

## Finding 3: This subnet's own cities are more deeply connected to their neighbors than to each other

**Combining:** Sayowa's own established bridging role toward Halley subnet (Princess Elisabeth, the Hwy 7-ext corridor) + Dome Fuji's own theological and personality relationship to Kunlun, a Mirny subnet city (`Mawson_Cross_City_Throughways.md`, Throughway 1) + the historical Troll–Sinheung–Dome Fuji aviation supply chain, spanning three separate subnets (Throughway 3) + Mawson's own Hwy 4 connection toward the same Mirny subnet cluster.

**2nd-order effect:** every one of this subnet's three cities has a more developed, more consequential relationship with a city in a neighboring subnet than with either of its own two subnet-mates.

**3rd-order effect:** this makes Mawson subnet functionally closer to connective tissue between Halley and Mirny subnets than to a self-contained region in its own right — a genuinely different structural role than any other subnet examined in this project, none of which showed this same degree of outward orientation.

**4th-order effect:** worth flagging directly for the developer's own planned full re-examination pass — Mawson subnet's own story was arguably never fully containable within its own three cities to begin with, and any future whole-picture pass should read this subnet's material alongside Halley's and Mirny's own Ultra-Megasheets rather than in isolation.

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across All Three Findings

All three findings converge on the same underlying observation: Mawson subnet, more than any other subnet examined in this project, resists being read as a single self-contained thing. Its own demographic story only makes full sense set beside a finding from a different subnet's own research (Finding 1). Its own war-damage pattern is a sampler of the whole project's framework rather than an independent case (Finding 2). And its own cities' most meaningful relationships point outward rather than inward (Finding 3). Mawson subnet reads, across all three findings, as the subnet that most clearly demonstrates this entire Megasheet series was never actually five separate stories — it was always one continuous picture, examined five pieces at a time.
