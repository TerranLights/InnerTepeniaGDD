## Who Lives Here, and Why

China leads as demographic Primary, with Japan, South Korea, Indonesia, and the founding-wave Australians all holding genuine Significant-tier weight — an Indian-Ocean-facing immigration current entirely distinct from the American-Primary pattern most of the rest of Tepenia eventually settled into. ## What It Feels Like

Institutionally deep and continuous — the oldest, most settled place in its subnet, because it genuinely is. But the defining surprise, established directly through developer vision: Mawson is, relatively speaking, the closest thing to a resort town Antarctica can produce — genuinely warm hospitality, public libraries, real ease of making friends, and status as Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination for newly-married human-robot couples, a reputation earned deliberately over decades rather than stumbled into. A real share of the city's visitors aren't here on hub business at all — they're couples on a one-to-two-week honeymoon before returning home to build their actual lives elsewhere.

## Real-World Inspirations, Researched

### Primary: Odessa

A real port city built on genuine, centuries-deep multicultural layering — Ukrainians, Greeks, Italians, Bulgarians, Armenians, Germans, and Russians living side by side, producing what's documented as "legendary local hospitality" as an actual defining civic trait, not incidental color. Also a genuine resort city in its own right, with sanatoriums, holiday camps, and beaches drawing visitors for their own sake.

**What this gives Mawson:** direct, earned precedent for the combination that defines this city — genuine demographic plurality *and* a real, documented hospitality reputation existing together, rather than one undercutting the other. Odessa's own real "legendary hospitality" gives Mawson's resort-town character concrete historical grounding rather than leaving it as an invented contrast against Antarctica's harshness alone.

### Secondary: Varna

Bulgaria's real "summer capital" — royal and government sessions historically convened there each summer — and a nearly three-thousand-year-old continuous cultural and economic center, home to the oldest worked gold ever found (the Varna Necropolis treasure, ~4600–4200 BC). A major seaport blending deep historical continuity with genuine modern tourism and festival culture.

**What this gives Mawson:** real-world grounding for the "continuity that never broke" half of the city's identity — Varna's own multi-millennial unbroken cultural centrality, still functioning as a real, living resort destination today rather than a preserved museum piece, mirrors Mawson's own combination of the oldest continuous occupation in its subnet with a genuinely lived, current hospitality character.

### Secondary: Trogir

A UNESCO-listed town with 2,300 years of unbroken urban continuity — founded by Greek colonists in the 3rd century BC, its original Hellenistic street grid (the cardo maximus and decumanus) still in actual use today, even as the population and rulers above it changed completely and repeatedly: Greeks, Romans, Byzantines, Venetians, the Habsburg Empire, the French, Yugoslavs, and finally modern Croatians.

**What this gives Mawson:** an almost exact structural mirror of the city's own defining theme, "The Name That Outlasted the Founders." Trogir's real urban structure persisting continuously beneath wave after wave of completely different rulers and populations is precisely the shape of Mawson's own story — a place whose underlying continuity was never about which nation happened to be living there at any given time.

### Secondary: Jeju City

The real, actual honeymoon capital of South Korea — a reputation earned deliberately starting in the 1970s–80s as Korean economic growth gave newlyweds the means to travel, cultivated over decades into "Honeymoon Island" status specifically, not incidental tourism. Notably, this cheerful modern reputation sits atop a genuinely dark, long-suppressed history (the 1948 uprising and subsequent killing of up to 30,000 civilians, not widely discussed until the late 1980s).

**What this gives Mawson:** the single most direct match of any pick in this pass — Jeju's real honeymoon reputation is deliberately built, decades-earned, exactly matching the already-established Vision Notes detail that Mawson's own honeymoon status is "genuinely a planned destination... earned over decades," not incidental. Jeju's own real layering — a warm, celebratory surface reputation sitting atop serious, only-partially-acknowledged history — also offers a genuine, worth-flagging structural echo of Mawson's own established quiet tension between founding memory and demographic present, worth developing further rather than copying directly.

## I. The Arcanet Hub's Damage, Specifically

**Proposed:** the Planetary Split Brain severed *inter-subnet* Arcanet links specifically, not intra-subnet ones — the same mechanism already used to explain why Davis, Casey, and Mirny's own Australian heritage plausibly survived within the Mirny subnet. Applied here: Mawson's hub function connecting the wider Mawson subnet to the rest of Tepenia was a deliberate strategic target and took the brunt of the damage, consistent with "damaged but partially operational." But Mawson's own intra-subnet connections — to Sayowa and Dome Fuji specifically — plausibly survived in better shape, or proved more repairable, since that was never the kind of link the Split Brain itself severed. The subnet's own internal coordination could very plausibly still function even where its outward connection to the rest of Tepenia does not.

---

## II. The Australian Antarctic Territory Claim, Post-War

**Proposed:** the claim persists as historical and ceremonial memory — bound up entirely with St. Douglas veneration and civic pride in Mawson's own founding story — but carries no functional governmental authority in the post-Split-Brain reality. This is consistent with Fort McMurdo's own resolved status as historical-only national capital, holding the title through the Long Night War but with no present-day authority transferring anywhere. Mawson's relationship to the old Territory claim is the same shape: real, honored, remembered, and no longer operative.

**Proposed:** a customary visit to the Prince Charles Mountains overlook above Horseshoe Harbour, timed for whichever of polar night or midnight sun happens to be in season during the stay — couples are said to make the trip regardless of which extreme they encounter, treating whichever one it is as the honeymoon's own defining shared memory. # Mawson — Cross-Reference Synthesis: Gaps, Combinations, and Downstream Effects

## Finding 1: "the name that outlasted the founders" operates at the infrastructural level too, not just demographically

**Combining:** Trogir's real precedent of an underlying urban structure persisting continuously through repeated, total changes in population and rulership + Mawson's own established "damaged but partially operational" hub status + the newly-proposed distinction between the city's severed inter-subnet link and its plausibly-intact intra-subnet connections (Full Extrapolation, Section I).

**2nd-order effect:** Trogir's real pattern — the underlying structure outlasting the specific people occupying it — applies to more than Mawson's already-established demographic story. It also describes what happened to the city's own infrastructure during and after the war: the hub *function* persisted even as the specific connections that function depended on changed dramatically.

**3rd-order effect:** "The Name That Outlasted the Founders" isn't only a demographic theme — it's a structural principle operating at multiple scales of the same city simultaneously: national founding identity, present-day demographic reality, and now infrastructural function, all following the identical shape of persistence-through-changed-occupancy.

**4th-order effect:** this gives Mawson a genuinely elegant, self-consistent identity, and a useful test for any future material about the city — does a proposed detail follow the "underlying structure persists, specific occupants or connections change" pattern the rest of Mawson's established lore already follows?

---

## Finding 2: Mawson's hospitality and its demographic plurality are two separately-earned civic traits, not one caused by the other

**Combining:** Odessa's real, explicitly documented "legendary hospitality," a real-world echo worth noting as coincidence rather than mechanism + Mawson's own established "genuinely easy to make friends here" trait + its honeymoon-destination reputation, already established elsewhere as something the city built deliberately, "earned over decades," the same way it built its hub function.

**2nd-order effect:** Odessa's own real hospitality reputation is a useful real-world texture reference, but Mawson's own established history is explicit that its hospitality character was a deliberate civic project, not a byproduct of who happens to live there. Applied to Mawson: its hospitality character and its demographic plurality read as two traits that grew up side by side in the same actively-cultivated civic culture, not one producing the other.

**3rd-order effect:** this keeps Mawson's two defining traits as complementary results of the same deliberate civic effort — a hub city that made hospitality a genuine institutional priority — rather than reducing the honeymoon-destination reputation to a downstream effect of population statistics.

**4th-order effect:** this is worth checking against other genuinely multinational Tepenian cities (Davis's own long Notable tier, Palmer City's all-43-nation composition) — whether they, too, deliberately built distinctly welcoming civic characters through their own institutional choices, rather than assuming hospitality follows automatically from demographic plurality wherever it appears.

---

## Finding 3: Mawson holds two genuinely different kinds of civic prestige on two different timescales

**Combining:** Varna's real ~3,000-year continuous cultural centrality, held simultaneously with a genuinely lived, current resort function + Jeju's real, comparatively recent honeymoon reputation, deliberately earned within living memory rather than ancient + Mawson's own two-tier timeline (a 1954 founding, essentially as old as Tepenia gets, alongside an honeymoon reputation explicitly "earned over decades").

**2nd-order effect:** Mawson's two defining reputations operate on genuinely different timescales — the "oldest continuously-occupied site" fact is a founding-era credential, while the honeymoon-destination reputation is a comparatively recent, actively cultivated development, much closer to Jeju's real decades-long timeline than to Varna's own millennia-deep resort identity.

**3rd-order effect:** Mawson's civic identity holds two different kinds of prestige at once — an ancient, inherited kind (continuity, St. Douglas, founding-era legitimacy) and a modern, earned kind (the honeymoon reputation, built deliberately within living memory) — without the established lore treating either one as more "real" than the other.

**4th-order effect:** this is a useful distinction to check for in other Tepenian cities' own Megasheets — whether a city's whole civic identity was fixed at founding and never added onto, or whether (like Mawson) it also developed a genuinely modern, separately-earned reputation layered on top of older founding-era facts.

---

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across All Three Findings

All three findings trace back to the same underlying observation: Mawson's established civic identity is genuinely load-bearing at more than one level simultaneously. Finding 1 shows the "outlasted the founders" theme operating infrastructurally, not just demographically. Finding 2 shows two of the city's defining traits growing out of the same deliberate civic effort, side by side rather than one causing the other. Finding 3 shows the city holding two different kinds of prestige, from two different eras, without contradiction. None of this required inventing new facts — it's what emerges from reading the established material carefully against itself and against real-world precedent.
