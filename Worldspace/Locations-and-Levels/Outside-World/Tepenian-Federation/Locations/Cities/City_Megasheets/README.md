# City Megasheets

**Status:** Ongoing since 2026-07-06 — see `Megasheet_Progress_Checklist.md` in this folder for current progress (15/35 as of 2026-07-08). This is step 7 of the City Vision Notes pipeline (see `TODO.md`'s "Active Initiative" section), downstream of steps 1–6 for every city. The actual compilation process is documented in full in `City_Megasheet_Compilation_Guide.md`.

**Purpose:** Once a city has been through the full pipeline — municipal Specs, `Local_Cultures/` post-culture draft, the developer's own City Vision Notes session, and real-world "inspirational cities" research — everything gets consolidated here into one single-page mega-datasheet per city: the definitive, all-in-one reference pulling together every exercise, every research pass, and every design decision made about that city across the whole project.

**Structure:** Mirrors `Local_Cultures/`'s subnet organization exactly — one folder per subnet, one folder per city inside it. Each completed city folder contains:
- **`[City]_Mega_Init.md`** — Step 1, the synthesis pass
- **`[City]_Full_Extrapolation.md`** — Step 2, the invention pass
- **`[City]_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`** — Step 3, the implication-hunting pass
- **`README.md`** — the concatenation of all three above, in order; the definitive, all-in-one mega-datasheet
- **`Questline_Asset_Concept_Art/`** *(renamed 2026-07-08, formerly `Concept_Art/`)* — narrow, close-up concept art tied to this specific city's own quest design: individual buildings, quest scenes, items, characters encountered here. For whole-city or general-neighborhood concept art (establishing shots, skylines, district layouts), see the separate top-level `Cities/City_Concept-Art/` folder instead, organized by subnet rather than by individual city.

**Do not populate any city's megasheet until that city has completed the full upstream pipeline** — the whole point is that this is the *last* step, synthesizing everything already established rather than introducing new lore of its own.
