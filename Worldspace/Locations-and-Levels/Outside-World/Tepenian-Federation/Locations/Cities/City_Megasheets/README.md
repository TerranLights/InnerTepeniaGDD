# City Megasheets

**Status:** Scaffolded 2026-07-06 — folder structure only, no content yet. This is step 7 of the City Vision Notes pipeline (see `TODO.md`'s "Active Initiative" section), downstream of steps 1–6 for every city.

**Purpose:** Once a city has been through the full pipeline — municipal Specs, `Local_Cultures/` post-culture draft, the developer's own City Vision Notes session, and (new, step 6) real-world "inspirational cities" research — everything gets consolidated here into one single-page mega-datasheet per city: the definitive, all-in-one reference pulling together every exercise, every research pass, and every design decision made about that city across the whole project.

**Structure:** Mirrors `Local_Cultures/`'s subnet organization exactly — one folder per subnet, one folder per city inside it. Each city folder will eventually contain exactly two things:
- **`README.md`** — the mega-datasheet itself. Named `README.md` rather than a per-city filename deliberately: each city folder holds exactly one text document, so there's no naming ambiguity to resolve, and it matches this folder's own convention.
- `Concept_Art/` — a subfolder for concept art the developer generates (via Grok) based on the megasheet's content, once it exists

**Do not populate any city's megasheet until that city has completed the full upstream pipeline** — the whole point is that this is the *last* step, synthesizing everything already established rather than introducing new lore of its own.
