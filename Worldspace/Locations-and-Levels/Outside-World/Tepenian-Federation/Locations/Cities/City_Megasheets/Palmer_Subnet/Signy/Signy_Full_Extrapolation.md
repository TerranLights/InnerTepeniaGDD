# Signy — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Signy given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Signy_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-08.

---

## I. Demonym: Signian

Following the simple, direct adjectival pattern already used for other short-named Tepenian cities — a natural, unforced derivation from the city's own name.

---

## II. Notable Figures (proposed, unconfirmed)

- **Dr. Naledi van Zyl-Osei** *(placeholder)* — a founding-era biological researcher continuing the BAS's original marine-and-terrestrial-biology mission into the exile era, drawing on Signy's unique South African founding-wave presence for her own background.

## III. The Bridge's Name: The Endurance Span

**Proposed**, drawing directly on Signy's own established strongest civic thread — its claim on St. Ernest (Shackleton) veneration, stronger here than anywhere else in Tepenia given the South Orkney Islands' proximity to the historic Endurance expedition route. A bridge that itself embodies endurance — a single crossing holding two islands, and by extension a whole city, together — earns its name from the same ship and the same act of persistence the city's strongest spiritual thread already honors.

---

## IV. The Siligel Shortage — Real, Not Yet Critical, and the DLC 3 Hook

**Proposed:** yes, this becomes the actual DLC 3 questline hook, as the Specs file itself already suggests as a strong candidate. The severity: not yet a full crisis by the time a player could plausibly reach Signy, but on a genuine countdown — weeks to a couple of months of reserve supply remaining, enough to create real narrative urgency without demanding instant catastrophe. This gives Signy's whole "untouched but not unaffected" identity a concrete, playable shape: a city that looks fine on arrival, until the player learns what's actually running out beneath the surface.

---

## V. St. Ernest Veneration — Confirmed, and Tied to the Bridge

**Proposed:** yes, confirmed as Signy's genuine strongest civic-spiritual thread, expressed concretely through the newly-named Endurance Span (Section III) and plausibly through a specific commemorative practice tied to the historic 1916 open-boat journey — perhaps an annual observance timed to the anniversary of the Endurance crew's own crossing, held at the bridge itself.

---

## VI. Ice Cold Buddhism — Minimal to No Presence

**Proposed:** Ice Cold Buddhism's established thematic core is built around cold, extreme, austere environments — its confirmed presence at Kunlun and Dome Fuji ties directly to those cities' genuinely harsh, high-altitude character. Signy is the opposite: the mildest Palmer subnet climate, biologically rich rather than austere, sub-Antarctic rather than deep-interior. The religion's thematic register simply doesn't fit Signy's own established identity — St. Ernest veneration (Section V) is the far more natural spiritual thread for this specific city.

---

## VII. Reaching Concordia — Technically Possible, Genuinely Difficult

**Proposed:** yes, still technically possible post-war, but severely degraded — the confirmed radio-based comms setup, alongside whatever residual Arcanet link survives, could still relay a message toward Concordia, just slowly and unreliably. This is consistent with the established framing that "if Signy has something important to say to Concordia, it has to work for it" — the connection exists, but using it is itself an achievement, not a given.

---

## VIII. The Two Placeholder Holidays, Developed

- **The Long Watch** — an observance built around Signy's whole established identity of patient, unhurried endurance; plausibly marking the arrival of the first supply ship of the season, a day the community historically waited for and now, post-war, waits for with new urgency given the siligel shortage.
- **St. Ernest's Passage** — directly commemorating the historic Endurance open-boat journey, observed at the Endurance Span itself (Section III), tying Signy's strongest spiritual thread to a specific, concrete civic ritual rather than an abstract veneration.

---

## IX. The Research Station Contradiction — Resolved in Favor of "Intact"

The 2026-07-03 status upgrade to "Survived; fully operational" is the more recent and more thoroughly reasoned correction (see the War Impact Note's own dating); any surviving "damaged but partially functional" language describing the biological research station is stale and should defer to the confirmed intact status.

As with the identical low-priority flags already noted on several other Palmer subnet Megasheets, this may already have been resolved since the Vision Notes session, or may live in a file this pass didn't directly check.

## XI. Signy's Own Power Self-Sufficiency

**Proposed, directly answering the question surfaced in the Mega-Init:** yes, tentatively self-sufficient, via a small-scale wind-and-hydro combination modeled on El Hierro's own real-world precedent (Section on Primary picks). Signy's established gale-exposed, open-ocean position provides exactly the kind of abundant, reliable wind resource El Hierro's own system depends on, giving the radio comms, dock operations, and floating platforms a plausible local power source independent of any wider grid — one less unresolved post-war vulnerability sitting alongside the siligel shortage, rather than a second, undiscussed crisis.

## Worth Your Attention

Section III is the most structurally satisfying proposal here — naming the bridge after the Endurance ties together three previously separate established facts (the two-island structure, the unnamed bridge, and Signy's own strongest spiritual claim) into one single, concrete landmark, rather than leaving them as three unconnected details. Section IV is the second most valuable, since it gives the Specs file's own flagged "strong DLC 3 candidate" a genuine playable shape rather than leaving it as an open possibility.
