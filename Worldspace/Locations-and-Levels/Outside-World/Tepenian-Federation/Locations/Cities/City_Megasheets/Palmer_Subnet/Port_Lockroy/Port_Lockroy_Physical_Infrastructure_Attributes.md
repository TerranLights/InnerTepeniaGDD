# Port Lockroy — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Port Lockroy would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Port_Lockroy.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Port_Lockroy.md`, and
`Port_Lockroy_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Port_Lockroy_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** "Founders' Watch" is reframed
below to honor the founding generation's own vigilant-watchfulness tradition rather than Operation
Tabarin's real 1944 wartime purpose directly — the same GPS-only routing already applied for Abowasa,
Belgrano, Lazar, Neumayer, Sanay, and Marambio.

**A note on unmarked background lore below:** Port Lockroy's own courier network handled shipping for what
became Calethina during the pre-war/Long-Night-War era — already-established, discoverable-only background
canon (no in-game quest markers), treated factually here the same way other unmarked lore is treated
elsewhere in the project.

**Why Port Lockroy's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only city examined in
this entire pass whose founding progression runs military → memorial → community, arriving at its exile-era
identity already carrying a wartime, then heritage-museum, layer before any Tepenian settlement began. It
survived the Long Night War not through physical resilience (its tiny island offers even less decentralize-
or-shelter potential than Marambio's) but through genuine strategic irrelevance and probable
proximity-driven misidentification with neighboring Palmer City — a survival mechanism distinct from every
hazard-driven or terrain-driven survival story examined elsewhere in this pass.

**Governing facts:** Goudier Island, inside the sheltered natural harbor of Port Lockroy, Wiencke Island,
western Antarctic Peninsula; status is damaged but partially operational, one of the few functioning nodes
in an otherwise mostly-destroyed Palmer subnet; built on the site of a 1944 British wartime intelligence
installation (Operation Tabarin), later a heritage museum, before becoming a living exile city — every era
physically incorporated rather than demolished, a genuine architectural palimpsest; Tepenia's second-
smallest city by population; the harbor's exceptional natural shelter gave it a maritime trade niche
distinct from any open-coast Peninsula city; culture built around continuous accountability to its own
layered history.

---

## Layered-History / Palimpsest Infrastructure (the defining, unique feature)

1. **The 1944 Base Structures** (established) — Operation Tabarin's original buildings, incorporated into
   the later city rather than demolished, now the most structurally intact part of the city given their
   original wartime-grade construction.
2. **The Communal Stone Wall** (established) — a genuine multi-generational artifact, a new panel added
   each year, the palimpsest character made into something residents actively build onto rather than a
   static inherited landmark.

## Natural Harbor Infrastructure

3. **Port Lockroy Harbor** (established) — the defining geographic feature, exceptional natural shelter
   from open ocean swells, distinct from Rothera's own more exposed coastal position.
4. **Small-craft maritime trade infrastructure**, taking specific advantage of the harbor's own protected
   waters — the city's genuine economic niche, distinct from any bulk-freight port examined elsewhere in
   the subnet.

## Postal & Courier Infrastructure

5. **The Reduced Postal Relay** (established) — a scaled-down, partly hand-managed correspondence network
   still running post-war, distinct from any bulk-goods logistics system; confirmed to have handled genuine
   wartime-era courier traffic historically.
6. **Whatever physical infrastructure supported the pre-war postal network's own full operational scale**,
   before its wartime-era reduction — the post office's own established status as a genuine functioning
   civic institution, not merely a heritage artifact.

## Small-Scale Subsistence Infrastructure

7. **Old Toby's Goat Pens and Workshop** (established) — small-scale goat husbandry and stonework craft
   space, a genuinely modest subsistence-and-craft operation fitting the city's own small population and
   quiet civic character.

## Strategic-Irrelevance Survival Infrastructure

8. **Whatever civic infrastructure reflects Port Lockroy's own established lack of strategic or industrial
   military value**, distinct from Marambio's high-value airfield/shipyard combination or Rothera's
   industrial base — the specific absence of anything worth deliberately targeting, combined with proximity
   to Palmer City's own strike zone, plausibly explaining the city's survival.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

9. **Ongoing repair infrastructure calibrated to real but non-total damage**, consistent with the
   established "damaged rather than destroyed" status — genuinely distinct in cause from Marambio's or
   Zukelli's own deliberately-targeted destruction.
