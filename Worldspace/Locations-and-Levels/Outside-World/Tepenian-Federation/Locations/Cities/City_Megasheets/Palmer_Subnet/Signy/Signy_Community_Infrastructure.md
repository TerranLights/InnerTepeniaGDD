# Signy — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Signy's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 23 Mirny, Halley, and Janbogo subnet cities, and Palmer City, Esperanza, Juan Carlos, Marambio, Port Lockroy, Rothera, and Sejong, extended here. This closes out the Palmer subnet. Signy is the one Palmer subnet city that came through the Long Night War fully intact. Drawn from `Signy_Mega_Init.md`, `Signy_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Signy_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Signy.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

---

## Additions

- **The Endurance Span** *(flagged for possible future renaming — Tepenia's own founders would probably have called it something else)* — the named bridge holding the city's two islands together, tied to Signy's own strongest spiritual thread.
- **The Biological Research Station** — the founding scientific infrastructure, fully intact, still functional, undamaged by the war.
- **The Floating Extension Platforms** — where Signy's fishing economy reaches beyond the coastline itself.
- **The Wildlife Territories** — seal haul-out areas, penguin colonies, seabird nesting grounds, present regardless of the city's own status.
- **The Wind-and-Hydro Power Works** — the small-scale, locally self-sufficient power system independent of any wider grid.

## Small offices for educational training

- marine and terrestrial biology research, the founding scientific mission
- floating-platform engineering and fishing-economy craft
- wind-and-hydro power system maintenance

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **The Long Watch** — marking the arrival of the first supply ship of the season, historically anticipated, now awaited with new urgency given the siligel shortage.
- **St. Ernest's Passage** — commemorating the historic Endurance open-boat journey, observed at the Endurance Span itself.
- the quiet, growing tension between the human population's own indefinite marine self-sufficiency and the robot population's genuine siligel shortage — a real, lived disparity within one otherwise-intact city.
