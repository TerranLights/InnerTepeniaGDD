# Marambio — Post-Culture Identity Rename Options (Pending Decision)

**Status: flagged 2026-07-21, not resolved.** Not canon — nothing here is chosen. This document exists
to hold the options in one place until the developer picks one (or something else entirely).

---

## Why this needs a decision

Marambio's Post-Culture Identity name/concept, "Standing on Warmer Ground" (`Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Marambio.md`, Section 5), only means anything because of the real-world Eocene fossil record on Seymour Island — "warmer ground" refers to the geological fact that Antarctica was once ice-free and warm, tens of millions of years ago. That material was struck across the whole project on 2026-07-21 (see `TODO.md`, "RESOLVED — Marambio Fossil/Paleontology Strike") per the developer's own correction that the fossil record is a real-world geological fact about the location, not a driver of Marambio's culture or a piece of Tepenia's own fiction.

Nothing else currently established about Marambio supports the name — Section 3 of the same file confirms the city is *colder* than its neighbors, not warmer. The name is now a leftover pointing at a concept that no longer exists in the city's own culture.

**Where the name is currently referenced**, all needing an update once a replacement is chosen:

- `Cities/Full_City_Integrity_Check.md` (tracking table)

## Option 1: "Never Just One Thing"

Reuses the title of Course of Events chain #10 (`Marambio_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`), which is
already, specifically, about the airfield-and-shipyard diversification strategy: "Marambio's centrality
ends up genuinely durable, resting on diversification rather than any single irreplaceable function."

**Why it fits:** costs nothing new — the phrase and its meaning already exist in established lore,
just not yet promoted to the top-level identity slot. Captures the dual-mode identity directly (never
just an airfield, never just a shipyard) and extends naturally to the population itself (never just
Argentine, given the founding nation's own extreme dilution) and to the city's whole function-first,
unexamined character (Marambio Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 2 — the "unexamined Janbogo").

## Option 2: "Built for Motion, Not for Staying"

A new phrase, leaning on the already-established "transient, never a place to settle" character running
through nearly every section of the city's own culture sheet (Section 4 Seasonal Rhythms, Section 6a,
Section 7, Section 14, Section 28).

**Why it fits:** most directly names the single trait every other section of the culture sheet already
converges on — motion itself, not any specific mode of it. Costs a small amount of new invention (the
phrase itself), though the underlying idea is entirely already-established.

## Option 3: "Whoever Runs It Now"

Reuses the title of Course of Events chain #3, about Marambio's founding-era operational discipline
persisting long after Argentina's own demographic presence eroded to the deepest dilution documented
anywhere in the Palmer subnet, "unremarked" and never named as significant by the city's own culture.

**Why it fits:** also costs nothing new. Points at a different, quieter established fact than Option 1 —
not the dual-mode logistics identity, but the founding-culture-outliving-its-founders theme (echoed
directly in Cross-Reference Synthesis Finding 2's "unexamined Janbogo" reading). Arguably a more
melancholy, less immediately legible name for a DLC-facing city identity than Option 1.

---

## Recommendation (not a decision)

Option 1, "Never Just One Thing," was the first pick offered when this was originally raised — it's the
most directly legible as a civic identity name, and it's the only one of the three that already
functions as a chain title *specifically about* the dual-mode identity itself, not an adjacent theme.
Kept here only as a recommendation; the developer hasn't chosen yet.
