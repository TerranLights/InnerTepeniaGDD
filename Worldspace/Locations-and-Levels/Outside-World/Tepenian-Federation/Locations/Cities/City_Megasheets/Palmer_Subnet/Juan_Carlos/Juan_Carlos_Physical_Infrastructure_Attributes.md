# Juan Carlos — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Juan Carlos would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Juan_Carlos.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Juan_Carlos.md`, and
`Juan_Carlos_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Juan_Carlos_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** the city's defining gathering
tradition — previously called "the tertulia" before the developer removed that name at their own direction
2026-07-22 — stays genuinely, deliberately unnamed here too; no replacement name is invented.

**Why Juan Carlos's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only city examined in this
entire pass destroyed by a strike deliberately targeting a specific bureaucratic/intelligence function
(tracking former Upper Earth government officials among the exile population) rather than any strategic or
symbolic value — the same deliberate-strike logic already established for Zukelli, but here aimed at an
archive rather than a sheltered defector population. It's also the direct historical origin point of
Amundsen Station's own pre-Split-Brain unified archive, tying a quiet, historically under-examined city
into DLC 1's deepest lore stakes, and the only Peninsula city confirmed to have no highway connection of any
kind — genuinely maritime/aviation-only, more isolated than any bridge-adjacent island city in the subnet.

**Governing facts:** Livingston Island, South Shetland Islands; status is Destroyed, specifically targeted
for its ongoing archive/intelligence function rather than generalized area damage; no highway connection
exists or is remotely plausible (110km open water to the mainland Peninsula, 95.4km to Sejong, both roughly
100x too wide for any causeway even under this project's generous engineering multiplier); maritime
sub-Antarctic climate nearly identical to Sejong's; quieter and less crowded than King George Island's own
national concentration, letting Juan Carlos's own culture develop with more coherence; was the origin site
of Tepenia's first bureaucratic archive (immigration, customs, Palmer City visas, and a specific watch on
former Upper Earth government officials), tied to the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority, before the
archive was gradually relocated and consolidated into Amundsen Station's own unified archive over the
Second Interwar Period; culture centered on a deliberately unnamed, guest-curated, argument-driven hosted
gathering tradition tied to café and discussion culture.

---

## Archive & Intelligence Infrastructure (the defining, unique feature)

1. **The Archive Complex** (established) — the formal Federation archive and its Arcanet link, the
   specific administrative/intelligence function that made Juan Carlos a deliberate strike target
   independent of the older bulk archive's later relocation to Amundsen Station.
2. **Whatever secure record-processing infrastructure supported the ongoing watch on former Upper Earth
   government officials among the exile population**, distinct from the general immigration/customs archive
   — the specific function established as the actual reason for the city's targeted destruction.

## Border & Customs Administration Infrastructure

3. **Whatever administrative infrastructure connected Juan Carlos to the Machu Picchu Border & Customs
   Authority**, given the established immigration-processing function centered here — arrivals routed
   through Ushuaia, non-immigrant visitors physically separated from immigration processing via a
   designated corridor to a Palmer-City-bound sealiner.

## Island Isolation Infrastructure

4. **Maritime and aviation-only access infrastructure**, distinct from every other Peninsula city examined
   so far — no highway connection exists or is plausible given the genuine open-water distances involved,
   making Juan Carlos more isolated than any bridge-adjacent island city in the subnet.
5. **Livingston Island harbor infrastructure** (established) — the primary maritime entry point, serving
   both the city's own maritime/fishing economy and the archive's own administrative traffic.

## Hosted Gathering & Café Culture Infrastructure

6. **Hosted Gathering Houses** (established) — private homes and cafés where the city's own defining,
   deliberately unnamed, guest-curated argument-driven gathering tradition took place.
7. **The Café Discussion Halls** (established) — where the gathering tradition and communal eating were
   inseparable, organized around hours of informal argument and storytelling.

## Ruins-Specific / Deliberate-Strike Infrastructure

8. **Whatever remains at a scale consistent with a strike calibrated against a specific administrative
   target**, distinct from any area-damage or gradual-decline ruins examined so far — the same targeted-
   destruction register established for Zukelli, here aimed at a records function rather than a sheltered
   population.
9. **The Archivist's Personal Notes site** (established) — one archivist's own off-network backup, the
   single record confirmed to have survived the strike outside the destroyed formal archive system.
