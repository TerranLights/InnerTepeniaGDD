# Rothera — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Rothera's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 23 Mirny, Halley, and Janbogo subnet cities, and Palmer City, Esperanza, Juan Carlos, Marambio, and Port Lockroy, extended here. Rothera is damaged but functional. **Note:** "Founders' Airstrip" cited BAS (the real British Antarctic Survey) directly as what's being honored — the same GPS-only pattern already caught repeatedly; reframed below to honor the founding generation's own competence instead. **Also flagged for future renaming:** "The Bonner Airstrip" and "Marguerite Bay Harbor" both carry their real-world geographic names forward unchanged — per direct developer instruction, Tepenia's own founders would plausibly have coined their own names for these places at founding rather than keeping the real-world ones three centuries later. Not resolved here, kept as working placeholder names. ## Additions

- **The Bonner Airstrip** *(placeholder name, flagged for future renaming)* — the 900m paved runway, damaged but still functional in some capacity.
- **The Decentralized Industrial Sites** — processing and manufacturing facilities spread across Adelaide Island, the literal reason the city survived the war.
- **The Underground Vault Sections** — large-scale excavated chambers, functioning as shelters and protected production space, a surface-ruins-above/still-functioning-city-below arrangement.
- **Marguerite Bay Harbor** *(placeholder name, flagged for future renaming)* — the maritime entry point, damaged but still functional.
- **Vault Architect's Original Excavation** — the earliest chambers dug by the vault program's own founding figure, before anyone knew they'd become a wartime shelter network.

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Founders' Airstrip** *(reframed)* — commemorating the founding generation's own inherited and maintained runway competence, observed with a maintenance-crew open house.
- **The Standing Works** — marking the city's continued post-war industrial function, timed to the anniversary of the Long Night War strikes, read as quiet, earned pride in having kept working through it.
- decentralized production as shared civic identity — a city that survived because no single strike could take out everything at once, felt as ordinary daily competence rather than dramatic resilience.
