# Juan Carlos — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Juan Carlos's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, all 23 Mirny, Halley, and Janbogo subnet cities, and Palmer City and Esperanza, extended here. **Juan Carlos is destroyed**, deliberately targeted for its ongoing archive/intelligence function — everything below is framed as pre-war, now among the ruins. **Note:** the city's defining gathering tradition was previously called "the tertulia," a name the developer removed at their own direction 2026-07-22 — the tradition stays genuinely, deliberately unnamed, and no replacement name is invented here. Drawn from `Juan_Carlos_Mega_Init.md`, `Juan_Carlos_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Juan_Carlos_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Juan_Carlos.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

---

## Additions (pre-war, now ruins)

- **The Archive Complex** — the formal Federation archive and its Arcanet link, destroyed in the deliberate strike targeting the city's intelligence function.
- **Hosted Gathering Houses** — the private homes and cafés where the city's own defining, deliberately unnamed argument-driven gathering tradition took place.
- **The Café Discussion Halls** — where the gathering tradition and communal eating were inseparable, hours of informal argument and storytelling organized around food and drink.
- **The Archivist's Personal Notes** — one archivist's own off-network backup, the single record that survived the strike outside the destroyed formal system.

## Small offices for educational training (pre-war)

- archival preservation and intelligence-record cataloguing
- hosted-gathering hosting craft (guest curation, argument facilitation, the tradition's own implicit rules)
- storytelling and political-commentary craft, treated as living literary culture

## Social cohesion mechanisms (pre-war)

- the hosted gathering itself — private, guest-listed, argument-driven, deliberately unnamed by the residents themselves.
- café-and-discussion culture — hours of informal argument and storytelling organized around food and drink.
- the informal, half-joking civic self-nickname "the Federation's memory," worn with wry pride rather than solemn reverence.

## Worth Your Attention

The Archivist's Personal Notes is the standout item — it gives Full Extrapolation Section II's own carefully-reasoned resolution (the formal archive was destroyed exactly as intended; what survived was one person's off-network backup, not a failure of the strike) a concrete physical object, turning an abstract "what survived" question into an actual questline artifact.
