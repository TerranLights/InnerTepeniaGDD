# Rothera — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Rothera would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Rothera.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Rothera.md`, and `Rothera_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Rothera_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** "the Bonner Airstrip" and "Marguerite
Bay Harbor" are both flagged in that file as real-world geographic names carried forward unchanged into a
300-year-old fictional civilization — working placeholder names, not resolved here, kept for consistency
with the established flag rather than invented around.

**Why Rothera's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only Palmer subnet city that
survived the Long Night War through genuine physical decentralization rather than strategic irrelevance
(Port Lockroy) or targeted sparing (Janbogo) — Adelaide Island's sheer size (~120km, mountainous, peaks over
2,500m) let its industrial base spread across dozens of kilometers rather than concentrate in one strikeable
urban core, the same abundance of space that also made genuinely large-scale underground vault construction
realistic here in a way no smaller island city in the subnet could support. It's also the Palmer subnet's
actual industrial center — the place that makes the finished infrastructure components the rest of the
subnet, including Palmer City itself, needed to build anything at all.

**Governing facts:** Rothera Point, Adelaide Island, western Antarctic Peninsula, Marguerite Bay; status is
damaged but partially operational, one of the few functioning nodes in an otherwise ruin-dominated DLC 3
landscape; the Palmer subnet's industrial center, decentralized across dozens of kilometers of the island
rather than concentrated in one dense core — the specific physical fact that let it survive as
damaged-but-functional rather than being erased like Palmer City or Sejong; large-scale underground vault
sections, genre-atypically large given Adelaide Island's abundant landmass, functioning as shelters and
protected production space; one of the few Tepenian cities with a paved runway, inherited from the British
Antarctic Survey era; mildest, most maritime climate of any Peninsula city outside Palmer City itself, with
regular rain alongside snow.

---

## Decentralized Industrial Infrastructure (the defining, unique feature)

1. **The Decentralized Industrial Sites** (established) — processing and manufacturing facilities spread
   across Adelaide Island rather than concentrated at Rothera Point, the literal physical reason the city
   survived the war rather than being wiped out in a single strike.
2. **Raw-material processing infrastructure converting Adelaide Island's own mountainous-terrain resources
   into finished infrastructure components**, supplying the rest of the Palmer subnet including Palmer
   City's own construction and maintenance needs.
3. **Maritime import infrastructure for whatever raw material Adelaide Island itself couldn't supply**,
   plausibly routed via the same Ushuaia/Punta Arenas gateway that founded the subnet's earliest arrivals.

## Underground Vault Infrastructure

4. **The Underground Vault Sections** (established) — large-scale excavated chambers built into Adelaide
   Island's own abundant landmass, functioning as shelters and protected production space at a scale no
   smaller Peninsula island could support.
5. **Vault Architect's Original Excavation** (established) — the earliest chambers dug by the vault
   program's own founding figure, before the wartime shelter function was known to be needed, distinct from
   the fuller vault network's later wartime-driven expansion.

## Aviation Infrastructure

6. **The Bonner Airstrip** (established, placeholder name) — the 900m paved runway, damaged but functional
   in some capacity, distinct aviation infrastructure most Tepenian cities lack entirely.
7. **Runway maintenance infrastructure calibrated to Peninsula-internal flight operations**, distinct from
   Marambio's own larger-scale, higher-traffic aviation hub role.

## Maritime Harbor Infrastructure

8. **Marguerite Bay Harbor** (established, placeholder name) — the maritime entry point to Rothera,
   damaged but still functional, serving the industrial base's own import/export needs.

## Mild Maritime Climate Infrastructure

9. **Wet-weather structural and drainage infrastructure**, given the established regular rainfall
   alongside snow — a materially different engineering demand than any drier interior or katabatic-wind
   city examined elsewhere in the project.

## War-Damage & Decentralized-Survival Infrastructure

10. **Damage-assessment and repair infrastructure distributed across the island's own scattered industrial
    footprint**, distinct from any single-core damaged city's own repair logistics — genuinely different
    coordination challenge given how spread out the damage itself is.
