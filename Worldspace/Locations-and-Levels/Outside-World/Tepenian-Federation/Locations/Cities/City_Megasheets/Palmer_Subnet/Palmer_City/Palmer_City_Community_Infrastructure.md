# Palmer City — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Palmer City's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and all 23 Mirny, Halley, and Janbogo subnet cities, extended here. This begins the Palmer subnet. **Palmer City is destroyed** (DLC 3 ruins setting) — everything below is framed as pre-war, now among the ruins. **Expanded 2026-07-31** per direct developer instruction — Palmer City's own huge, defining entertainment/nightlife scene warranted a noticeably longer list than the original 2026-07-30 pass produced; new items below are integrated directly into the existing lists rather than kept separate. **Expanded again 2026-08-02** — a third pass filling in genuine gaps the first two left: the diplomatic/tourism-reception side of the city implied by Rue de la Réception had no actual institution attached to it, the "human-robot cohabitation as tourism spectacle" line existed only as a Social Cohesion Mechanism with no physical venue, the entertainment economy had music/gambling/fashion but no dedicated food scene, and nothing yet addressed competitive/combat-sport entertainment distinct from music and cards. ## Additions (pre-war, now ruins)

- **The Petrograd Room** *(name flagged for future renaming — not a developer-chosen name, likely would be called something else)* — Palmer City's grandest casino/entertainment hall, a half-joking, half-reverent nod to St. Petersburg's own imperial ambition.
- **Little Burgundy Quarter** — the district around the original station buildings where the found jazz records first got played, still the densest live-music neighborhood.
- **The Boardwalk** — elevated walkway letting visitors experience the city's rare above-freezing, rainy weather while doubling as prime nightlife real estate.
- **Rue de la Réception** — the formal, ceremonial approach street used for visiting dignitaries and Upper Earth tourism arrivals.
- **The Flag Preservation Hall** *(now empty)* — where the found pre-war flag was displayed before its removal. **The flag itself, present-day:** relocated to Concordia before Palmer City's destruction (per `Palmer_City_Full_Extrapolation.md` Section IV), now sealed in a glass frame on a wall — open question, not yet decided, whether it hangs in (A) a museum-type building in whichever Concordia district makes the most sense, or (B) a high-status official building in the Libra district specifically. Flagged for a future decision, not resolved here.
- **The Late Registry** *(added 2026-07-31)* — an unofficial, unlicensed after-hours strip that only really wakes up once Needle Down's main stages wind down for the night; where the city's most serious night owls, human and robot alike, actually live.
- **Neighborhood Casino Floors** *(added 2026-07-31)* — dozens of smaller, block-level gaming rooms scattered across the city beneath the Petrograd Room's own citywide prestige, genuinely local haunts rather than tourist-facing spectacle.
- **The Exchange Hall** *(added 2026-07-31)* — a standing venue built explicitly around the city's 43-nation composition, hosting a rotating weekly showcase of cuisine, music, and craft from whichever represented nation holds the floor.
- **Radio Anvers** *(added 2026-07-31)* — the city's own jazz-and-variety broadcast station, informally the soundtrack of the whole city, its signal reportedly picked up as far as Rothera and Signy on a clear night.
- **The Ballroom Circuit** *(added 2026-07-31)* — a network of formal and informal dance halls built specifically around the city's rockabilly and electroswing scenes, distinct from jazz's own Little Burgundy Quarter home turf.
- **The Diplomatic Quarter** *(added 2026-08-02)* — the consulate-adjacent quarter Rue de la Réception's ceremonial approach street actually leads into; formal reception halls, short-stay attaché housing, and negotiation rooms for the Upper Earth dignitaries and tourism officials the street was built to receive.
- **The Living Exhibition Halls** *(added 2026-08-02)* — the actual venue behind the city's own "open, easy human-robot cohabitation as itself the city's actual tourism product" identity, previously a social fact with nowhere to happen: guided observation walks through ordinary neighborhood blocks, packaged and lightly staged for Upper Earth visitors who came specifically to watch humans and robots simply live alongside each other.
- **The Late Kitchen Row** *(added 2026-08-02)* — all-night food stalls and diners clustered near the Late Registry, feeding the same after-hours crowd; Palmer City's entertainment economy had covered gambling, music, and lodging by 2026-07-31 but had no dedicated culinary Addition of its own until now.
- **The Boxing Gardens** *(added 2026-08-02)* — an open-air combat-sport venue, the city's one major entertainment institution built around neither music nor cards; matches are treated with the same showmanship as a Ballroom Circuit dance night rather than as a separate, rougher tradition.

## Social cohesion mechanisms (pre-war)

- **The Day We Didn't Wait** — commemorating June 21, 2564, the day the exiles arrived the same day the treaty was signed, without hesitation.
- **Needle Down** — the citywide, dusk-to-dawn live-music observance celebrating the discovery of the jazz records, spanning the city's full genre range.
- open, easy human-robot cohabitation as itself the city's actual tourism product, dressed up as spectacle for Upper Earth visitors.
- **Showcase Nights** *(added 2026-07-31)* — a rotating weekly slot spotlighting a different neighborhood casino or venue with citywide attention, genuine competitive pride at stake between them.
- **The Exchange Hall Rotation** *(added 2026-07-31)* — whichever of the 43 represented nations is "hosting" the Exchange Hall that week, a recurring and genuinely anticipated civic event.
- **New Arrivals Toast** *(added 2026-07-31)* — a standing custom of welcoming Upper Earth tourists and new immigrants alike with a shared first-night toast, treating "welcome" as close to the city's actual civic religion.
- **Radio Anvers Request Nights** *(added 2026-07-31)* — listeners calling in song requests live, an unglamorous but genuinely citywide shared ritual, one of the only Palmer City customs that costs nothing and excludes no one.
- **Style Nights** *(added 2026-07-31)* — informal, competitive fashion display tied directly to the rockabilly and electroswing scenes, worn and judged seriously rather than treated as costume.
- **Ambassador's Welcome** *(added 2026-08-02)* — the formal greeting ritual staged in the Diplomatic Quarter for visiting Upper Earth dignitaries, a deliberately ceremonial counterpart to New Arrivals Toast's informal, everyone-included register — the city keeps both, and residents are fluent in switching between them depending on who's arriving.
- **Living Exhibition Hours** *(added 2026-08-02)* — the Living Exhibition Halls run on a set, advertised schedule rather than being open-ended; the city treats "being watched living an ordinary life" as skilled, paid civic labor with actual shifts, not a passive condition.
- **The Late Kitchen Crawl** *(added 2026-08-02)* — the informal custom of sampling several Late Kitchen Row stalls across one long night out, a natural extension of the city's whole dusk-to-dawn entertainment rhythm into food specifically.
- **Fight Night Wagers** *(added 2026-08-02)* — informal betting on Boxing Gardens matches, pairing naturally with the Neighborhood Casino Floors' own gambling culture; a night out easily moves between the two without anyone treating it as two separate activities.

## Worth Your Attention

The 2026-07-31 expansion above brings Palmer City's own Additions and social-cohesion-mechanisms lists up to a length that actually matches its established character — 43-nation composition, a whole entertainment economy, open human-robot cohabitation as its own civic pride. The Exchange Hall and its Rotation mechanism are the standout new pair: they give the 43-nation composition, previously an abstract demographic fact, an actual recurring, dramatizable civic institution built directly around it. **The 2026-08-02 expansion closes a real gap the first two passes left standing:** the city's own established "human-robot cohabitation as tourism product" line had no venue behind it until the Living Exhibition Halls, and Rue de la Réception's dignitary-reception framing had nothing at the other end of the street until the Diplomatic Quarter — both are exactly the kind of thing that reads as an oversight once named, since the city's own file had been gesturing at them for two passes already.
