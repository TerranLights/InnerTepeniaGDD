# Palmer City — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Palmer City's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and all 23 Mirny, Halley, and Janbogo subnet cities, extended here. This begins the Palmer subnet. **Palmer City is destroyed** (DLC 3 ruins setting) — everything below is framed as pre-war, now among the ruins. **Status: floor met, but flagged for future expansion** — a heavily cultural city like Palmer City would plausibly support far more Additions and social cohesion mechanisms than listed below; per direct developer instruction, come back and add more later rather than treating this as a complete pass. Drawn from `Palmer_City_Mega_Init.md`, `Palmer_City_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Palmer_City_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Palmer_City.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions (pre-war, now ruins)

- **The Petrograd Room** *(name flagged for future renaming — not a developer-chosen name, likely would be called something else)* — Palmer City's grandest casino/entertainment hall, a half-joking, half-reverent nod to St. Petersburg's own imperial ambition.
- **Little Burgundy Quarter** — the district around the original station buildings where the found jazz records first got played, still the densest live-music neighborhood.
- **The Boardwalk** — elevated walkway letting visitors experience the city's rare above-freezing, rainy weather while doubling as prime nightlife real estate.
- **Rue de la Réception** — the formal, ceremonial approach street used for visiting dignitaries and Upper Earth tourism arrivals.
- **The Flag Preservation Hall** *(now empty)* — where the found pre-war flag was displayed before its removal. **The flag itself, present-day:** relocated to Concordia before Palmer City's destruction (per `Palmer_City_Full_Extrapolation.md` Section IV), now sealed in a glass frame on a wall — open question, not yet decided, whether it hangs in (A) a museum-type building in whichever Concordia district makes the most sense, or (B) a high-status official building in the Libra district specifically. Flagged for a future decision, not resolved here.

## Social cohesion mechanisms (pre-war)

- **The Day We Didn't Wait** — commemorating June 21, 2564, the day the exiles arrived the same day the treaty was signed, without hesitation.
- **Needle Down** — the citywide, dusk-to-dawn live-music observance celebrating the discovery of the jazz records, spanning the city's full genre range.
- open, easy human-robot cohabitation as itself the city's actual tourism product, dressed up as spectacle for Upper Earth visitors.
