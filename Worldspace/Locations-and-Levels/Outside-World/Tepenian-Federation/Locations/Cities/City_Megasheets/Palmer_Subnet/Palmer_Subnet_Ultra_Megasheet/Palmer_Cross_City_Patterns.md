# Palmer Subnet — Cross-City Patterns

**What this is:** common factors recurring across Palmer subnet's 8 City Megasheets (Palmer City, Esperanza, Juan Carlos, Marambio, Port Lockroy, Rothera, Sejong, Signy). Several of these patterns are already partially named inside individual cities' own Cross-Reference Syntheses — this document consolidates them into a single subnet-wide picture and, where the material allows, extends them further than any single city's own file does. Written 2026-07-10.

---

## Pattern 1: A recurring Enneagram documentation lag — three cities, one shared cause

Esperanza, Rothera, and Sejong each independently discovered, in their own Cross-Reference Syntheses, that their Enneagram profile was anchored to an early, secondary, easily-countable fact (a logistics partnership, a confirmed airport, proximity to a border authority) rather than the richer primary civic identity each city's own later City Vision Notes session established. Rothera's own Megasheet named this explicitly as a "recurring pattern... not a one-off gap" after finding it a second time; Sejong's Megasheet extended it further, proposing the shared trait ("administrative facilitation," negotiating relationships with neighbors) might be a real, underdeveloped signal rather than pure documentation error.

**Within Palmer subnet specifically, the full tally is 3-to-2:** Esperanza, Rothera, and Sejong show the lag; Juan Carlos's own profile (Thinking/Compliant/Competency, anchored directly to its actual primary identity as Tepenia's founding archive) does not; and Signy is the explicit positive counter-case — a thin, early profile that happened to remain fully compatible with everything its own later Vision Notes session established. Signy's own Megasheet frames this directly: unlike the other three, "every established fact... points toward the same underlying trait," making thin documentation and rich characterization two different things rather than opposites.

## Pattern 2: The permanent thing carries forward what the temporary thing cannot — two independent instances

*(Reduced from three instances — a third, Marambio's fossil-record/dockworker parallel, was struck along with the rest of Marambio's paleontology material per the developer's own correction; see `Marambio_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`.)*

Two separate Palmer subnet cities independently produced a version of the same underlying mechanism, without either of their own Cross-Reference Syntheses citing the other:

- **Esperanza:** given human lifespans carry no dramatic extension, "being from Hope" as a living identity is actually carried forward by the city's own long-lived robot caregivers, not by human bloodlines (Esperanza Full Extrapolation, Section IV).
- **Palmer City:** Solenne Vaskarre, given established robot lifespans, may still be alive in the present-day game era nearly 250 years after the Long Night War's inciting incident — the single most consequential person Palmer City ever produced, potentially still carrying that history in person (Palmer City Cross-Reference, Finding 7).

**Combined, these two cities show the same mechanism operating at two different scales** — institutional memory (Esperanza) and lived historical witness (Palmer City) — neither of which either city's own Megasheet connected to the other. Robots are not merely present throughout Palmer subnet's history; they are, structurally, the subnet's actual continuity mechanism, in more than one domain at once.

## Pattern 3: Two distinguishable destruction doctrines, visible once Palmer subnet's four destroyed cities are compared side by side

Juan Carlos's own Cross-Reference Synthesis (Finding 2) proposed a general distinction between two Upper Earth strike types — a **witness-dependent deterrent strike** (requiring a spared neighbor to carry the message forward, as at Zukelli) and a **witness-independent security strike** (requiring no witness at all, occurring wherever geographic isolation makes one unavailable, as at Juan Carlos itself) — but only compared itself to Zukelli, outside Palmer subnet entirely.

**Applying that same typology across Palmer subnet's own four destroyed cities produces a clean, previously undrawn split:**
- **Security strikes** (no possible witness, isolation-driven): Juan Carlos.
- **Efficiency strikes** (maximum value destroyed per strike, not requiring or avoiding a witness): Marambio, whose own destruction justification already needed updating once its shipyard identity was added alongside its airfield — a single strike eliminating two now-critical logistics nodes at once (Marambio Cross-Reference, Finding 1).
- **Morale strikes** (psychological rather than tactical, targeting symbolic significance): Esperanza, proposed as an early casualty specifically because it was Tepenia's second-largest city and the one most explicitly built around hope and children (Esperanza Full Extrapolation, Section V).
- **Sejong** does not yet have an established destruction mechanism of its own — a genuine gap this comparison surfaces, since every other destroyed Palmer city now has one.

## Pattern 4: A three-way division of logistics labor nobody has stated as a single system

Rothera, Port Lockroy, Marambio, and Esperanza each carry an established transport or trade role, and placed together, they form a coherent, non-overlapping logistics division for the whole subnet:

- **Rothera** moves materials — vehicle and transit-infrastructure components, general construction goods, distributed via Hwy 1 to every other Palmer city (Rothera Full Extrapolation, Section II).
- **Port Lockroy** moves words — correspondence and archival material, the postal-and-relay counterpart to Rothera's freight role, explicitly stated in its own Mega-Init as "Rothera moves the materials, Port Lockroy moves the words."
- **Marambio** moves bulk volume — high-volume, less-differentiated cargo through its dual air-and-sea logistics hub.
- **Esperanza** moves specific, documented shipments — smaller-scale, higher-value or individually tracked cargo, per the one concrete example already in the lore (a named artisan's instruments moving through Esperanza's harbor specifically, not Marambio's).

No single city's own Megasheet states this as one unified four-part system — each names only its own role, or at most its relationship to one neighbor. Read together, Palmer subnet has a complete, non-redundant internal logistics network: goods, words, bulk, and specificity, each handled by a different city.

## Pattern 5: Three different survival mechanisms, already partially compared, extended here to a clean typology

Signy's own Cross-Reference Synthesis (Finding 1) already directly compares itself to Rothera and Port Lockroy, establishing that Rothera survived through deliberate, engineered decentralization (a pure asset), Port Lockroy survived through strategic irrelevance and probable misidentification with Palmer City's own larger strike zone (also a pure asset), and Signy alone survived through raw isolation — the identical trait that is now, post-war, also its greatest vulnerability (the siligel shortage). This is the clearest three-way typology already assembled inside a single Palmer city's own file, extended here only by naming it explicitly as a subnet-wide pattern: Palmer subnet's three lightly-damaged-or-intact cities each survived by a genuinely different mechanism, and only one of the three (Signy) paid, and continues to pay, a real ongoing price for its own survival.

## Pattern 6: Palmer subnet feeds Byrd twice, in two unrelated domains, and neither city's own file connects the two

- **Palmer City → Byrd:** Palmer City (alongside Rothera) contributed historical records and maps that helped the Belgrano-led expedition locate Byrd Station in the first place — meaning Byrd's own present-day rediscovery-story identity is, in a small but real way, downstream of Palmer City's own founding-era generosity (Palmer City Cross-Reference, Finding 8).
- **Rothera → Byrd:** Rothera's own decentralized industrial model, or its surviving engineers' records, plausibly assisted Byrd's early industrial planning directly — the two cities share an otherwise unexplained structural similarity (heavy manufacturing combined with genuine underground infrastructure) that this proposal gives an actual cause (Rothera Full Extrapolation, Section XI).

**Neither Palmer City's nor Rothera's own Megasheet references the other's connection to Byrd.** Combined, they show Byrd — a single city in an entirely separate subnet — receiving two independent, unconnected legacies from Palmer subnet: the historical records that let it be found, and the industrial template that shaped what it became once it was. Byrd is, in a very real sense, partly a Palmer subnet creation, twice over, without Byrd's own established culture reflecting either debt.

## Worth Your Attention

Pattern 2 and Pattern 6 are the two strongest items in this file, for different reasons. Pattern 2 is the deepest — it reveals a single structural mechanism (robots as the durable carriers of what humans, human institutions, and human lifespans cannot hold onto) operating independently in two completely different registers within the same eight cities, visible only once all eight files sit side by side. Pattern 6 is the most immediately useful for future writing — it identifies a specific, concrete, currently-unacknowledged double debt one DLC's own city (Byrd) owes to a different DLC's subnet entirely, a ready-made hook for any future cross-DLC content.
