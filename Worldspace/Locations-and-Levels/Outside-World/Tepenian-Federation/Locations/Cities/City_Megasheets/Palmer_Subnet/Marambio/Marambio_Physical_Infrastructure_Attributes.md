# Marambio — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Marambio would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Marambio.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Marambio.md`, and
`Marambio_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Following `Marambio_Community_Infrastructure.md`'s own precedent:** "Founders' Flight" is reframed below
to commemorate the exile generation's own founding flight onto Seymour Island rather than the real 1969
Argentine Air Force arrival directly — the same GPS-only routing already applied for Abowasa, Belgrano,
Lazar, Neumayer, and Sanay.

**Why Marambio's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only city examined in this
entire pass built on genuinely flat, small, indefensible terrain concentrating two critical logistics assets
(the airfield and the shipyards) on the same ground — a single-point-of-failure geography closer to Palmer
City's or Sejong's situation than Rothera's own large, mountainous, decentralizable island. It's also the
only city whose two defining infrastructure systems serve genuinely different networks at once: the
airfield moves people and goods purely within Tepenia, while the port is simultaneously Marambio's link to
Upper Earth (as the confirmed South America receiving node) and to the rest of Tepenia (shipping onward
across the Weddell Sea to Belgrano and Sanay).

**Governing facts:** Seymour Island, Weddell Sea, eastern Antarctic Peninsula; status is Destroyed, taken
out in a single strike that eliminated both the airfield and the shipyards together, consistent with the
island's small, flat, concentrated terrain offering no way to decentralize either asset; the Palmer
subnet's primary aviation hub, serving internal Tepenian traffic exclusively (Upper Earth arrivals route
through Machu Picchu Base on King George Island instead); shipyards on the island's inner side facing the
mainland are the confirmed Palmer-subnet receiving node for South American shipping, Tepenia's Upper-Earth
shipping partner for this subnet; connected to Hwy 1 via the Picnic Passage causeway/bridge chain onward to
Snow Hill Island, James Ross Island, and the Trinity Peninsula mainland; no polar night or midnight sun;
culture was transient and logistics-oriented, comparable to Fort McMurdo's own operational character.

---

## Aviation Hub Infrastructure

1. **The Marambio Airfield** (established) — the Palmer subnet's primary aviation hub, the most developed
   airstrip infrastructure of any Peninsula city, serving exclusively internal Tepenian traffic between
   cities rather than Upper Earth arrivals.
2. **Aviation maintenance and fueling infrastructure**, sized to the airfield's own established role as the
   subnet's central node for moving people and goods by air.

## Maritime Shipyard Infrastructure

3. **The Marambio Shipyards** (established) — spanning the island's inner side facing the mainland, the
   confirmed Palmer-subnet receiving point for South American shipping, equally central to the city's
   identity as the airfield rather than a secondary function.
4. **Dual-role port infrastructure**, distinct from any single-direction port examined so far — the same
   docks serve both the Upper Earth shipping relationship (South America) and Tepenia's own internal Weddell
   Sea network (onward shipping to Belgrano and Sanay).

## Dual-Mode Logistics Coordination Infrastructure

5. **Coordination infrastructure linking the airfield and shipyard operations**, given the established
   fact that both systems, together rather than separately, defined Marambio's civic character as a
   transient, goods-and-people-in-motion hub.
6. **The Comandante's Operations Hall** (established) — where the founding-era operational discipline was
   established and outlasted the founding population's own demographic presence, independently re-derived by
   the exile generation from surviving records rather than inherited from a living institution.

## Picnic Passage Causeway Infrastructure

7. **The Picnic Passage Causeway** (established) — Marambio's own Hwy 1 crossing to Snow Hill Island and
   onward toward the Trinity Peninsula mainland, plausibly still structurally intact post-war even though the
   city it connects to no longer functions.

## Concentrated-Terrain / Single-Strike Vulnerability Infrastructure

8. **Structural engineering on genuinely flat, small, indefensible ground**, distinct from Rothera's own
   large, mountainous, decentralizable island — the specific terrain fact that let a single strike eliminate
   both the airfield and the shipyards together rather than requiring separate targeting.

## Ruins-Specific Infrastructure

9. **Whatever remains legible of both destroyed logistics systems together**, distinct from any
   single-asset destroyed city examined so far — a "bridge to nowhere" quality given the causeway's own
   plausible survival despite the city it once led to being gone.
