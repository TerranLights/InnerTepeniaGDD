# Signy — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Signy would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Signy.md`, `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Signy.md`, and `Signy_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. **Closes out the Palmer
subnet and completes Methodology #1 for all 34 non-Byrd DLC cities.**

**Why Signy's attributes look like nothing else examined so far:** it's the only Palmer subnet city that
came through the Long Night War fully intact — not through decentralization (Rothera), strategic
irrelevance (Port Lockroy), or deliberate sparing (Janbogo), but through genuine, total remoteness that put
it below Upper Earth's targeting threshold entirely. That same isolation, previously just a fact of daily
life, became a real post-war crisis instead: the city can sustain its human population indefinitely on the
Scotia Sea's marine richness alone, but its robot population faces a genuine, unresolved siligel shortage
with supply lines to the rest of the subnet cut. It's also the only city examined in this entire pass built
across two physically separate islands joined by a single bridge, each island serving a distinct function.

**Governing facts:** Signy Island and the much larger Coronation Island, South Orkney Islands, Scotia Sea —
joined by a single bridge; status is fully survived and operational, the one functioning city in an
otherwise ruin-dominated DLC 3 landscape, though "functioning" carries a real post-war asterisk; the most
isolated Palmer subnet city, maritime-access only, with the weakest Arcanet link in the Federation (radio,
not digital networking, confirmed as the actual long-distance link given the sheer open-ocean distance);
sub-Antarctic rather than full Antarctic, biologically rich (seals, penguins, seabirds, Scotia Sea marine
productivity); founding population independently developed a biological-science civic focus, converging on
the same mission the original BAS station had rather than inheriting it; the strongest Tepenian Saints
connection to St. Ernest (Shackleton) of any city in the Federation.

---

## Two-Island Structure Infrastructure (the defining, unique feature)

1. **Signy Island's shipping dock and radio comms installation** (established) — the smaller island's own
   dedicated function, confirmed as radio rather than Arcanet given the physical distance involved,
   reinforcing rather than contradicting the city's own weakest-link status.
2. **The Endurance Span** (established, flagged for possible future renaming) — the single bridge joining
   Signy Island to the much larger Coronation Island, the only crossing tying the city's two functional
   halves together.
3. **Coronation Island's residential and leisure infrastructure** (established) — where most of the city's
   actual living infrastructure sits, distinct from Signy Island's own dock-and-comms function; the
   established daily rhythm of fishing on one island and returning to downtime on the other runs directly
   through the bridge.

## Biological Research Infrastructure

4. **The Biological Research Station** (established) — the founding scientific infrastructure, fully
   intact and still functional, undamaged by the war — a genuinely unique status among all 34 non-Byrd
   cities examined in this pass, none of which retained fully undamaged founding-era infrastructure.
5. **The Wildlife Territories** (established) — seal haul-out areas, penguin colonies, and seabird nesting
   grounds, present and ongoing regardless of the city's own status, distinct from Cape Adare's or Dumont
   d'Urville's own penguin-specific coexistence infrastructure.

## Maritime Fishing Infrastructure

6. **The Floating Extension Platforms** (established) — where Signy's fishing economy reaches beyond the
   coastline itself, the specific engineered mechanism behind the marine diet that sustains the city's human
   population indefinitely.

## Self-Sufficient Power Infrastructure

7. **The Wind-and-Hydro Power Works** (established) — a small-scale, locally self-sufficient power system
   independent of any wider grid, consistent with Signy's own established total isolation from the rest of
   the Palmer subnet's infrastructure.

## Post-War Supply Crisis Infrastructure

8. **Whatever siligel storage and rationing infrastructure exists for the city's own robot population**,
   given the established genuine post-war shortage — humans can sustain themselves indefinitely on Scotia
   Sea marine resources, but siligel is not something Signy can produce or improvise locally, a real, lived
   disparity within one otherwise fully intact city.
