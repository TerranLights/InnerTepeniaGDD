# Mirny Subnet — Full Extrapolation (Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Step 2)

**What this is:** every open question about Mirny subnet as a whole given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established across all 8 City Megasheets rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Mirny_Subnet_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-10. As throughout this Ultra-Megasheet, the currently-unnamed Korean city is referred to by its working title "Soyuz" only where necessary for clarity, never as a settled name.

---

## I. Each city's own specific role within the subnet's "function outliving the need for credit" identity

Mirroring the district-level and other-subnet exercises, but adapted to this subnet's own dominant trait — competence that never asked to be noticed:

- **Mirny carries the doubled hub load** — communications and industrial function both, at equal established weight, more than Janbogo's own single-register hub role one subnet over.
- **Casey carried a transformation interrupted mid-arc** — a city becoming something more than its own function, cut down before it finished becoming it.
- **Davis carries the enabler role, three times reinforced** — namesake, real-world precedent, and current economy all independently converging on the same "makes other people's work possible" identity.
- **Vostok carries an inheritance nobody currently living there actually built** — a legend arrived at by accident, taken up anyway because the work itself was worth doing.
- **Kunlun carries the most complete Head-center identity in Tepenia** — a city that became one enormous distributed instrument, re-resolved from a single nation into whoever the actual expertise required.
- **Zhongshan carries the one unbroken continuity** — the only Tepenian city where the founding operator nation never lost its own demographic Primary status.
- **The currently-unnamed Korean city carries the nation's actual robot-creation capacity** — quietly load-bearing infrastructure sitting behind a modest political profile, so quiet that its own war damage suggests Upper Earth understood its significance better than most Tepenians did.
- **Shirayuki carries a diplomatic decision made real** — an imposed origin that became, over generations, something its own residents would choose again.

## II. Mirny's Rename and the Subnet's Own Name — A Proposed Resolution

**Proposed:** given Mirny's Cross-Reference Finding 1 already establishes that an eventual city rename would create a genuine mismatch with the six-city "Mirny subnet" regional label, the cleanest resolution is a deliberate decoupling — the city itself is renamed to reflect its actual current demographic character, while "Mirny subnet" persists unchanged as a purely administrative and geographic label, the way "Ross Sea" or "Wilkes Land" function as real-world regional names independent of any single settlement's own current name. This avoids forcing an awkward, simultaneous two-part renaming decision, and gives the subnet's own regional identity a stable anchor even while its hub city's own name changes — consistent with how real Antarctic regional naming already works, where a sector's name frequently outlives whichever specific station or claim it was originally drawn from.

## III. Which Mechanism Produced the Cluster's Present-Day Harmony — Extending the Currently-Unnamed Korean City's Own Proposal

**Proposed, extending the city's own Full Extrapolation Section VI:** the Larsemann Hills cluster's present-day harmony traces to all three cities' own internal founding structures being, in different ways, already built around negotiated coexistence rather than any single dominant thread — the currently-unnamed Korean city's own genuinely doubled dual founding, Shirayuki's engineered-but-then-genuinely-inhabited identity, and even Zhongshan's own established "double life" dynamic (a shared public rhythm, heritage kept quietly at home) all describe populations already practiced at holding more than one thing true about themselves at once. Three cities disposed toward internal negotiation, placed next to each other, plausibly negotiated their way into a shared neighborhood more easily than three more singularly-identified cities might have.

## IV. The Cradle Network's Full Present-Day Shape, Subnet by Subnet

**Proposed, synthesizing across three subnets' own established facts:** the present-day Cradle chamber-manufacturing network is now confirmed to consist of exactly two active sites (the currently-unnamed Korean city in this subnet, Byrd in its own single-city subnet), with three historical sites no longer producing (Mountain Pass, jointly built by Vostok and Kunlun, ended by the Tower's collapse; Denison, in Janbogo/Ross subnet, ended by its own wartime destruction; and Neumayer, in Halley subnet, credited with the current chamber's own design lineage rather than manufacturing it directly). This gives the Cradle's own nationwide infrastructure a complete, checkable map across four subnets at once — design (Neumayer), historical manufacturing (Mountain Pass, Denison), and present-day manufacturing (the currently-unnamed Korean city, Byrd) — worth treating as settled unless Zhongshan's own "strong candidate under active consideration" status (per the Korean city's own Full Extrapolation, Section III) is ever resolved either way.

## V. Whether the Subnet's Single-Principle War-Damage Logic Is a Regional Pattern or a Coincidence

**Proposed, flagged explicitly as provisional:** Mirny subnet's own clean, single-principle war-damage logic (Pattern 2; Throughway 4) is worth treating as a genuine regional pattern rather than coincidence, on the strength of one suggestive fact already established elsewhere in this project — Zhongshan's own Full Extrapolation (Section IV) proposed that Upper Earth's targeting logic in this subnet traced to real strategic-value assessment, the same logic borne out independently across Casey's and the Larsemann Hills cluster's own destruction/damage stories. Whether this reflects a genuinely different regional command decision on Upper Earth's part, or simply the fact that this subnet happened to contain fewer cities sheltering defectors or otherwise inviting punitive strikes, is not resolvable from currently established facts alone — worth revisiting once Mawson subnet's own Ultra-Megasheet provides a third comparison point.

## VI. A Proposed Notable Figure: The Subnet's Own Relay-and-Industry Coordinator

**Proposed, new figure:** a Mirny civic role — placeholder title **Subnet Coordinator**, currently unnamed — responsible for balancing Mirny's own doubled hub function (Arcanet relay routing for Zhongshan, the currently-unnamed Korean city, Shirayuki, Davis, Vostok, and Kunlun, alongside the city's own construction-materials and maintenance-machinery output) since the war damaged both systems at once. A natural counterpart to the already-proposed Relay Technician Zoya Marchenko (Mirny's own Full Extrapolation, Section VI), this role specifically addresses the doubled nature of Mirny's hub identity established in Pattern 3 — one person or office responsible for keeping both halves of the city's function running when either alone would already be a full-time civic priority.
