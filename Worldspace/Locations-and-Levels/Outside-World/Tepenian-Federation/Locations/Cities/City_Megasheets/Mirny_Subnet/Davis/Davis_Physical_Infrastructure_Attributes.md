# Davis — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Davis would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Davis.md`, `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Davis.md`, and `Davis_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Governing facts:** sits in the Vestfold Hills, the largest ice-free coastal oasis in Antarctica (~400
km² of exposed rock, lakes, and fjord-like inlets) — genuinely unusual terrain variety for a Tepenian city,
most of which sit directly on ice or snow; status is damaged but partially operational; economy is Tepenia's
own breadbasket role (sheltered agriculture/greenhouse cultivation) plus genuine paleoclimate/limnological
research; the lake system includes both freshwater and hypersaline/landlocked marine basins; Prydz Bay
gives direct, fjord-inlet maritime access; population ~780k-1.16 million; cultural identity is explicitly
"enabler, not protagonist" (John King Davis was a ship's captain who enabled others' expeditions, not an
explorer who led his own), expressed as a genuine anti-glory civic ethos; libraries here are openly,
celebratedly welcoming to smuggled Upper Earth books, distinct in tone from the same practice elsewhere in
Tepenia.

---

## Ice-Free Oasis Terrain Infrastructure (the defining feature)

1. **Foundation engineering built directly on exposed rock and varied terrain**, rather than ice or snow —
   a fundamentally different construction problem from most Tepenian cities, which sit on or within an ice
   sheet.
2. **Terrain-adapted road and path networks**, navigating rocky ground, lake edges, and fjord inlets rather
   than the uniform ice-sheet travel most Tepenian cities are built around.

## Agricultural/Breadbasket Infrastructure

3. **The Vestfold Greenhouses** (established) — sheltered-agriculture cultivation complexes, the physical
   heart of Davis's own national breadbasket role.
4. **Large-scale food distribution and export infrastructure**, sized to a genuine national breadbasket
   role — shipping harvested food outward to the rest of Tepenia, not simply local consumption.
5. **Irrigation infrastructure drawing on the lake system specifically** — the exceptional terrain variety
   that enables large-scale agriculture here at all depends directly on the lakes' own fresh water, a
   resource most Antarctic-interior cities simply don't have access to.

## Lake System Infrastructure

6. **Lake-ice monitoring and seasonal-access infrastructure**, given the established seasonal freeze-thaw
   cycle (formalized culturally as the Lake-Ice Watch tradition).
7. **Specialized water-chemistry-aware infrastructure** for the system's hypersaline and landlocked marine
   basin lakes, distinct from standard freshwater-only lake management most other cities would need.
8. **The Wirawan Lake Survey Office** (established) — dedicated cataloguing infrastructure for the varied
   lake system.

## Paleoclimate/Research Infrastructure

9. **The Sediment Core Archive** (established) — storage and study space for lake-sediment paleoclimate
   research.
10. **Field naturalist and research-staging infrastructure**, supporting ongoing terrain-based ecological
    study across the oasis's own genuinely varied environment.

## Maritime & Fjord Infrastructure

11. **Prydz Bay harbor infrastructure** (established) — the primary maritime entry point.
12. **The Fjord Mooring Docks** (established) — sheltered small-vessel moorings distinct from the main
    harbor, suited to the fjord terrain specifically.
13. **Specialized small-vessel navigation infrastructure for fjord travel**, distinct from open-bay
    shipping — the inlets' own terrain demands different vessel handling than Prydz Bay's main approach.

## Civic & Cultural Infrastructure

14. **The Open-Door Libraries** (established) — an openly celebrated acquisitions policy for smuggled
    Upper Earth books, distinct in tone from the quieter versions of the same practice at Zukelli, Janbogo,
    or Esperanza.
15. **A deliberately modest, functional civic building style** — consistent with the established "enabler,
    not protagonist" ethos and its anti-glory expression (Harvest Quiet, The Quiet Credit), Davis plausibly
    avoids grandiose or monumental civic architecture as a genuine design choice, not simply out of economy.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

16. **Ongoing repair infrastructure**, consistent with the established "damaged but partially operational"
    status — less centrally emphasized than at the subnet hub (Mirny), but still a real, active condition
    rather than a settled one.
