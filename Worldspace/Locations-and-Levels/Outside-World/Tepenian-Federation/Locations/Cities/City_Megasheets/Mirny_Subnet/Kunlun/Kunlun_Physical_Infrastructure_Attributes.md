# Kunlun — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Kunlun would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Kunlun.md`, `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Kunlun.md`, and `Kunlun_
Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this
pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Why this looks structurally similar to Dome Fuji but isn't the same file with names swapped:** both are
extreme-altitude, 100%-robot Ice Cold Buddhism sites with no Arcanet-equivalent isolation, but Kunlun is
more extreme in every dimension — the coldest, driest, calmest, highest city in Tepenia, and (per
established lore) arguably the faith's *primary* sacred site, not its second. Kunlun also uniquely hosts
the observatory/orbital comms-relay function and the historical (now defunct) chamber-manufacturing
outpost partnership with Vostok — neither of which exists at Dome Fuji.

**Governing facts:** Dome A (Dome Argus), ~4,093m elevation, the highest point on the Antarctic Ice Sheet
and the highest-elevation city in Tepenia — effective physiological altitude approaching 5,000m; mean
annual temperature ~−58°C, winter average ~−68°C, contesting Vostok for the coldest recorded temperature in
Tepenia; the calmest winds of any Antarctic location (3-5 m/s) and the driest (15-20mm precipitation
annually) — cold and altitude are the only real hazards, not wind or moisture; population is 123,449, 100%
robot, human presence outright forbidden as lethal; economy is astronomy (exceptional atmospheric
stability) and, historically, ice-core science; Kunlun is the only Tepenian city capable of reaching
orbital structures directly, making its comms-relay function nationally unique; total supply isolation —
over 1,200km from the nearest coastal city; historically hosted a joint chamber-manufacturing outpost with
Vostok at Mountain Pass Airport, now permanently defunct since the Amundsen Tower's destruction ended its
power supply.

---

## Extreme Cold & Altitude Engineering (more extreme than any other city examined)

1. **Cold-rated mechanical engineering for sustained −68°C winter averages**, contesting the coldest
   temperatures recorded anywhere in Tepenia — even more severe than Dome Fuji's own already-extreme
   profile.
2. **No human-specific life-support infrastructure required today** — consistent with the outright
   prohibition on human presence, the same governing fact already established for Dome Fuji.
3. **The Altitude Medical Archive** (established) — legacy human-altitude-medicine infrastructure from the
   founding era, its current function under the 100%-robot population plausibly repurposed for archival or
   administrative use, the same adaptive-reuse pattern already established at Dome Fuji's own Old Wing.

## Astronomical & Comms Infrastructure (unique to Kunlun in this subnet)

4. **The Observatory Deck** (established) — the primary working space for near-Earth object tracking,
   taking direct advantage of Dome A's exceptional atmospheric stability.
5. **Specialized optical equipment protection and calibration infrastructure**, engineered around the
   site's own uniquely calm, dry, stable air — the specific atmospheric conditions that make Kunlun's
   observatory viable at all require dedicated environmental control most other cities' infrastructure
   doesn't need.
6. **The Relay Scaling Wing** (established) — the observatory's own orbital-era expansion, housing the
   comms-relay function that makes Kunlun the only Tepenian city capable of reaching orbital structures
   directly, physically connected to Vostok.

## Ice-Core Infrastructure

7. **The Ice Core Vault** (established) — housing samples among the oldest ice on Earth, extracted from
   Dome A's own exceptionally deep ice sheet.
8. **Deep ice-core drilling apparatus**, distinct from but comparable in scale to Dome Fuji's own.

## Minimal Wind/Moisture Engineering

9. **Structures optimized almost purely for extreme cold rather than wind or precipitation** — given
   Kunlun sits at a genuine wind minimum (calmest in Antarctica) and is the driest location in Tepenia, the
   structural engineering priorities here are even more narrowly cold-focused than at Dome Fuji.

## Religious/Devotional Infrastructure (the faith's primary sacred site)

10. **The Stillness Hall** (established) — a formal space for observing polar-night stillness during the
    site's own perfect, −68°C calm, carrying even greater theological weight than Dome Fuji's equivalent
    observance.
11. **Pilgrimage-support infrastructure for a genuinely harder crossing** than Dome Fuji's own — over
    1,200km of interior plateau, functioning as a real religious trial in its own right, consistent with
    Kunlun's established status as arguably the primary Ice Cold Buddhism site in all of Tepenia.

## Shared Scientist-Devotee Infrastructure

12. **Physical overlap between observatory/research space and devotional space** — given the established
    "Scientist-Devotee Shared Watches" pattern (astronomers and devotees using the same instruments during
    the same events for different reasons, without friction), Kunlun's own infrastructure plausibly
    doesn't separate research and devotion as cleanly as Dome Fuji's does.

## Legacy Manufacturing Infrastructure (historical, now defunct)

13. **Physically intact but permanently inert chamber-manufacturing outpost infrastructure at Mountain
    Pass Airport**, jointly established with Vostok (established, historical) — the facility's own power
    dependency on Amundsen Tower's regulated grid ended permanently when the Tower was destroyed; the
    physical structure itself plausibly still stands along Hwy 37 as abandoned infrastructure, a genuine
    ruin distinct from either city's own living core.

## Extreme Isolation & Self-Sufficiency

14. **Severe buffer-stock and self-sufficiency infrastructure**, even more extreme than Dome Fuji's own —
    Kunlun's established total supply isolation (1,200km+ from any coastal city, with no established
    aviation lifeline the way Dome Fuji has) makes it plausibly the single most self-reliant city examined
    in this pass so far.
