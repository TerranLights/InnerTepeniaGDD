# Davis — Full Extrapolation

**What this is:** every open question about Davis given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Davis_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-07.

---

## I. What the Lake Ecosystems Are Actually Used For

**Proposed, deliberately distinct from Vostok's own genetics angle:** rather than genetics or biological research (Vostok's own established niche), Davis's varied lake system — freshwater, hypersaline, and landlocked marine basins, each freezing and thawing on its own schedule — gives its research community a genuinely different scientific specialty: paleoclimate reconstruction from layered lake sediment cores. Just as ice cores at Kunlun and Dome Fuji preserve long-term atmospheric history, Davis's differently-salinated lakes preserve differently-paced sediment records, letting researchers cross-reference multiple independent local climate histories within a single city's territory — a genuinely distinct methodology from ice-core science, using water rather than ice as the archival medium.

## II. The Fjord Inlets' Role in City Layout

**Proposed:** while Prydz Bay harbor handles the city's main shipping traffic, the smaller fjord-like inlets serve a specific secondary function — sheltered mooring for smaller fishing and maintenance vessels, distinct from the main harbor's larger commercial flow. Because different bodies of water freeze and thaw on separate schedules (already established in Section 4 of the Local Cultures file), certain fjord inlets plausibly remain navigable even when the main harbor ices over, giving Davis a genuine logistical advantage — alternative maritime access tied directly to the same terrain diversity that defines the city's whole character, not just a scenic feature.

## III. Davis, Casey, and Mirny: Does Shared Australian Heritage Survive Post-Split Brain?

**Proposed, resolved cleanly from an already-established mechanic:** yes, more plausibly than most Tepenian cross-city relationships — the Planetary Split Brain severed *inter-subnet* Arcanet links, not intra-subnet ones. Davis, Casey, and Mirny are all within the same Mirny subnet, meaning whatever functioning connectivity existed between them before the war was never the kind of link the Split Brain actually cut. A real, live, still-functioning Australian-heritage cultural network between these three cities is a reasonable, mechanically consistent outcome — distinct from relationships crossing subnet boundaries, which the Split Brain would have severed outright.

---

## IV. The Mawson Question — Administratively Separate, Symbolically Linked

**Proposed:** given Mawson-the-city sits in a different subnet entirely, any pre-war relationship with Davis would have run through occasional, mostly symbolic or ceremonial gestures — shared Australian Antarctic naming heritage acknowledged on national holidays or through cultural exchange, rather than genuine day-to-day operational ties. This makes the Split Brain's severance of this particular link low-cost: there wasn't a deep working relationship to lose, just an occasional historical nod that's now simply gone, unremarked and unmourned by most residents of either city.

## VII. The Recognition Question: A Real, Minority Fault Line

**Proposed, drawing on Guatapé's own real embrace of tourism built around its lake:** while Davis's dominant civic ethos genuinely doesn't seek outside attention, a minority of residents plausibly do feel a quiet, rarely-voiced wish that the Vestfold Hills' genuinely remarkable terrain drew more outside recognition — not fame for its own sake, but simple acknowledgment that what Davis has here is objectively unusual even by Tepenian standards. This sits naturally alongside the fault line already established in Local Cultures Section 5b (residents satisfied by quiet competence vs. those who resent the permanent support role) rather than replacing it — a second, related but distinct minority tension, not the city's dominant mood.

## Worth Your Attention

Section I is the second most valuable, since it gives Davis's own research community a genuine, distinct scientific identity of its own, rather than leaving its research culture as a vaguer echo of Vostok's or Kunlun's already-established specialties.
