# Mirny — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Mirny's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same kind of pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts, extended here to the DLC-city Megasheet family so future Under-Questline work has real, textured starting points to draw from. Drawn from `Mirny_Mega_Init.md`, `Mirny_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Mirny_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Mirny.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

## Additions

- **The Windwright Guildhall** — where the wind-engineering craft tradition (Chief Windwright Osric Bellandry's legacy *(placeholder name, `Mirny_Full_Extrapolation.md` Section VI — not developer-chosen)*) is taught and practiced, purely as competence, never mythologized.
- **The Relay Hardware Vaults** — the physical subnet-hub infrastructure Relay Technician Zoya Marchenko *(placeholder name, same section)* keeps running post-war, with a semi-public viewing area reflecting the quiet pride the role carries.
- **Dual-Kitchen Halls** — communal eating spaces serving Russian and Chinese food side by side, kept genuinely distinct rather than fused, matching the city's coexistence-not-fusion cultural pattern.
- **The Solstice Grazing Grounds** — the actual gathering site for the Winter and Summer Solstice Grazing observances.
- **Quarry-to-Yard Transit Sheds** — waystations along the raw-material supply route from the southern quarries to the industrial yards, feeding both the eastern-highway construction chain and Sinheung's robot-synthesis fabrication chain.
- **The Circle-Wall Marker Plaza** — a small gathering space built around the Antarctic Circle marker embedded directly in the windbreak's own outer wall.

## Small offices for educational training

- wind-engineering and structural-shell design
- subnet relay maintenance and Arcanet routing diagnostics
- quarrying, ore-processing, and industrial machining

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Dual-Table Suppers** — informal daily meals where Russian and Chinese dishes are served side by side at the same table, the coexistence-not-fusion ethos lived out in something as ordinary as dinner.
- **Windwright Apprentice Trials** — community-witnessed tests where new apprentices' work is judged against the actual wind, not a panel's opinion.
- **Relay Room Vigils** — quiet, informal gatherings near the relay hardware during any stretch where the subnet connection is genuinely at risk.
- **Rough Bar Nights** — the city's own unpolished, function-over-comfort drinking culture; real kinship, expressed without much ceremony.
- **Quarry Return Toasts** — informal recognition when a quarry crew makes it back safely from the harsher southern routes.

## Worth Your Attention

The Dual-Kitchen Halls and Dual-Table Suppers are the standout items — they take the city's own most-repeated cultural descriptor ("coexistence, not fusion") and give it a genuinely concrete, daily, physical form, rather than leaving it as an abstract cultural-analysis line.
