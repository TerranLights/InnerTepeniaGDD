**4th-order effect:** worth flagging as the single most consequential fact this whole Ultra-Megasheet series has produced to date — every prior subnet's own Cradle-network connection (Halley, Janbogo/Ross) was significant to Tepenia's own worldbuilding; this one is significant to the specific character the player spends the entire game as.

**3rd-order effect:** this is the first genuine, checkable regional comparison this whole Megasheet project has produced regarding how the Long Night War's own targeting logic actually varied — not merely two different subnets with different outcomes, but two subnets whose war conduct required different *numbers* of explanatory principles to fully account for.

**4th-order effect:** worth flagging as a live, open question for Mawson subnet's own upcoming Ultra-Megasheet — whether it resembles Janbogo/Ross subnet's two-principle logic, Mirny subnet's single-principle logic, or introduces a genuinely third pattern neither subnet has shown yet.

## Synthesis: The Pattern Across All Three Findings

Mirny subnet reads, across all three findings, as a place whose quiet, function-first surface — the same "competence without an audience" personality established in this Ultra-Megasheet's own Mega-Init — consistently undersells just how much is actually happening underneath it.
