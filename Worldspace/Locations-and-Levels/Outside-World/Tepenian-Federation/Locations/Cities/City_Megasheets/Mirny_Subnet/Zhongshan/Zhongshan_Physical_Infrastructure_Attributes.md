# Zhongshan — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Zhongshan would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Zhongshan.md`, `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Zhongshan.md`, and
`Zhongshan_Community_Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred
until this pass is complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30. Closes out the
Mirny subnet — all 8 cities (Mirny, Casey, Davis, Kunlun, Shirayuki, Sinheung, Vostok, Zhongshan) now have
this pass.

**Governing facts:** Larsemann Hills, Prydz Bay, immediate neighbor to Sinheung; Sinian/Chinese-founded,
confirmed exclusively so by the same Jeju-do court that allocated Shirayuki to Japan; **the one deliberate
exception to the project's general rotating-operator founding model** — Zhongshan plausibly saw continuous
Chinese/Sinian habitation straight through the First Interwar Period, not a chain of unrelated operators,
independently reinforcing the Jeju-do court's later formal confirmation; status is damaged but partially
operational; economy is Prydz Bay maritime logistics (inherited Chinese icebreaker heritage) and scientific
research; culture is contemplative, artisan-craft-forward (precision woodworking, ceramics, metalworking),
with a genuine archival tradition and a deliberately unofficial counterculture district.

---

## Continuous-Habitation Legacy Infrastructure (a genuine exception among DLC cities)

1. **The Founding Hall** (established) — the preserved original Zhongshan Station structure, genuinely
   integrated into the city's core rather than inherited cold and reinterpreted the way most other
   Tepenian cities' founding infrastructure was.
2. **A deeper, more complete pre-Split-Brain historical record infrastructure** (established: The Long
   Record, "the most complete pre-Split-Brain history in the Mirny subnet") — a direct consequence of the
   continuous-habitation exception, distinct from cities whose own institutional memory was broken by
   generations of unrelated operators.

## Maritime & Icebreaker Infrastructure

3. **Prydz Bay harbor infrastructure** (established) — the primary maritime entry, the historic route
   Chinese icebreakers ran pre-exile.
4. **Substantial icebreaker maintenance and staging infrastructure**, inherited from and continuing one of
   the most active pre-exile Chinese Antarctic logistics operations.

## Ice-Free Terrain Infrastructure (Larsemann Hills)

5. **Foundation engineering built around a natural bedrock outcrop** (established: The Standing Stone) —
   early construction organized itself around the feature rather than clearing it, giving the city's own
   core a genuinely different structural relationship to its terrain than a purely cleared-and-built site.

## Artisan & Craft Infrastructure

6. **The Crossing Quarter** (established) — a concentrated artisan neighborhood where woodworkers,
   ceramicists, and metalworkers practice precision craft.
7. **The Mending Houses** (established) — dedicated garment-repair workshops, where clothing maintenance
   is practiced as genuine craft rather than simple upkeep.

## Fermentation & Fishing Infrastructure

8. **Fermentation and preservation craft infrastructure**, tied directly to Prydz Bay fishing technique —
   an established economic sector distinct from general maritime logistics.
9. **Fishing fleet and dock infrastructure**, distinct from the general Prydz Bay harbor — a specific trade
   with its own physical footprint.

## Archive Infrastructure

10. **The Long Record** (established) — the city's primary archive, the custodian institution for its own
    unusually complete historical continuity.

## Cultural & Performance Infrastructure

11. **Dedicated performance space for the Long Winter Suite** (established) — the city's own defining
    multi-movement composition, performed across the roughly 60-day polar night arc.

## Counterculture Infrastructure

12. **The Loud Quarter** (established) — a deliberately unofficial, unnamed counterculture district,
    existing alongside the city's own formal, contemplative civic character as a genuine, distinct urban
    zone rather than a suppressed or hidden one.

## War-Damage Infrastructure

13. **Ongoing repair infrastructure**, consistent with the established "damaged but partially operational"
    status.
