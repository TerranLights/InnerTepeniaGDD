# Kunlun — Community Infrastructure & Social Life

**What this is:** concrete physical spaces, small institutions, and recurring social rituals that give Kunlun's already-established culture a tangible, ground-level presence — the same pass done for each of Concordia's 13 districts and for Mirny, Casey, and Davis, extended here. Drawn from `Kunlun_Mega_Init.md`, `Kunlun_Full_Extrapolation.md`, `Kunlun_Cross_Reference_Synthesis.md`, and `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Kunlun.md`. Written 2026-07-30.

---

## Additions

- **The Observatory Deck** — the observatory's primary working space, tracking near-Earth objects and plausibly holding a record of the Amundsen Tower's own collapse debris.
- **The Ice Core Vault** — housing samples from the ice core drill site, among the oldest ice on Earth.
- **The Altitude Medical Archive** — the repurposed altitude-sickness medical infrastructure; its current function under the 100%-robot population remains an open question.
- **The Stillness Hall** — where Ice Cold Buddhism devotees observe formal polar-night stillness during the −68°C perfect calm.
- **The Relay Scaling Wing** — the observatory's own orbital-era expansion, housing the comms-relay function connecting to Vostok.

## Small offices for educational training

- astronomical observation and near-Earth object tracking
- comms-relay engineering and orbital-signal maintenance
- ice-core extraction and cold-archive preservation

## Social cohesion mechanisms

- **Deepest Cold Observance** — the civic/religious observance at Kunlun's coldest point in July, carrying even greater theological weight than Dome Fuji's equivalent.
- **Scientist-Devotee Shared Watches** — the informal, unspoken overlap where astronomers/comms specialists and Ice Cold Buddhism devotees work the same instrument during the same event, for entirely different reasons, without friction.
- **Mind-and-Hand Gatherings** — informal recognitions of the "not a job, it's life" civic ethos.
- **Vostok Relay Toasts** — informal acknowledgment shared with Vostok whenever the mutual resource-sharing relay connection holds through a difficult stretch.
