# Vostok — Physical Infrastructure Attributes

**What this is:** a systematic derivation of physical/civic attributes Vostok would logically have to
possess, built directly from `Specs/Vostok.md`, `Vostok_Full_Extrapolation.md`, and `Vostok_Community_
Infrastructure.md` — **Methodology #1 only**; Methodology #2 deliberately deferred until this pass is
complete across all 34 DLC cities. Not locked canon. Written 2026-07-30.

**Governing facts:** the coldest city in Tepenia — holds the real-world record for the lowest naturally
occurring surface temperature ever measured (−89.2°C, 1983); sits above Lake Vostok, a subglacial
freshwater lake roughly 250km by 50km, buried under ~4km of ice, isolated 15-25 million years, kept liquid
by geothermal heat and ice-sheet pressure (the same mechanism this session already established for Byrd's
own new subglacial lake, "The Long Window"); the current population is a Pacific-corridor blend (USA/Japan
Primary) with no Russian representation, a founding community entirely disconnected from the current
redistributed one; economy is almost entirely genetics research — huge sections of the city given over to
lab space; robots here work task-driven schedules with no fixed shift boundaries, while the human
population keeps a conventional day/night schedule; reached via Hwy 37, the DLC 7 access route through
Kunlun; established this session — Vostok's own genetics program supplied the bioluminescent ecosystem
transplanted to Byrd's Long Window, treated as a rare, specific exception to Vostok's own "exported almost
nothing else" economic profile.

---

## Extreme Cold Engineering

1. **Cold-rated structural and mechanical engineering for the coldest sustained temperatures in Tepenia**,
   contesting or matching the real-world −89.2°C record — among the most extreme cold-engineering
   requirements of any city examined in this pass.
2. **Specialized climate-controlled laboratory environments**, distinct from general habitation space,
   given the genetics research economy's own precision requirements layered on top of the city's already
   extreme ambient cold.

## Subglacial Lake Access Infrastructure

3. **The Lake Vostok Access Shaft** (established, "the most significant structures in Vostok") — the
   drilling apparatus and access infrastructure reaching the subglacial lake, extended and expanded across
   500+ years of post-exile scientific access.
4. **A geothermal-adjacent power source, plausibly independent of the standard grid** — the same real-world
   mechanism keeping Lake Vostok itself liquid (geothermal heat plus ice-sheet pressure) is the identical
   principle this session already established for Byrd's own independent power supply, making an analogous
   geothermal tap a genuine possibility here too.

## Genetics Research Infrastructure

5. **Extensive, multi-floor laboratory space** (established) — genetics research and gene/allele
   experimentation occupying huge sections of the city.
6. **A centuries-accumulated, distributed research archive**, so vast no single individual — human or
   robot — fully understands it (established) — implying serious, ongoing archival and data-storage
   infrastructure sized to that scale.
7. **Cold-storage and sample-preservation infrastructure**, drawing directly on the extreme ambient cold
   already freely available as a natural resource, rather than requiring active refrigeration.

## Population-Schedule Infrastructure

8. **Communal and civic spaces designed to accommodate two genuinely different population rhythms
   simultaneously** — robots on pure task-driven schedules with no fixed shift boundaries, humans on a
   conventional day/night cycle — a distinct civic-infrastructure challenge from a single-schedule city.

## Isolation & Self-Sufficiency Infrastructure

9. **Self-sufficiency infrastructure calibrated to the most extreme isolation of any city examined in this
   pass** — consistent with the established framing that "survival is functionally indistinguishable from
   disappearance," plausibly exceeding even Kunlun's own already-severe isolation profile.
10. **Hwy 37 physical highway infrastructure** (established) — the city's own real link to Concordia via
    Kunlun, the confirmed DLC 7 access route.

## Cultivation & Export Infrastructure (established this session)

11. **A dedicated small-scale cultivation and export-preparation facility**, distinct from the main
    research labs — given the newly-established transplant relationship supplying Byrd's own Long Window,
    a specific space for cultivating and preparing organisms destined for transplant elsewhere plausibly
    exists separately from general research space.

## Legacy Founding Infrastructure

12. **Inherited Russian-language technical infrastructure and signage**, used functionally by the current
    population without necessarily reflecting any lived connection to its own origin — consistent with the
    established total disconnect between the founding Russian community and the current, unrelated
    redistributed population.
