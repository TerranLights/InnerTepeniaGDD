# City Enneagram Personalities

**Purpose:** applies the Enneagram's three sub-classifications — the same framework the developer uses to design individual Doll characters' personalities — to each Tepenian city's *collective* personality instead. Each city's own established culture (from its `Specs/`, `Local_Cultures/`, and City Vision Notes content) is read as a personality profile, scored across three axes. Started 2026-07-06, first applied to the Tri-Cities cluster (Zhongshan/Sinheung/Shirayuki), then extended to every other touched city in Tepenia.

**These are not profiles of individual residents** — they're readings of each city's own collective character, grounded entirely in what's already established about that city elsewhere in the project. No generic Enneagram trivia; every judgment cites a specific, already-canonical detail.

---

## The Three Axes

### 1. Major Theme (Center of Intelligence)

- **Thinking (Head)** — types 5, 6, 7. Oriented around security, analysis, information, and planning. Fear-based at its core.
- **Feeling (Heart)** — types 2, 3, 4. Oriented around image, identity, and emotional/aesthetic connection. Shame-based at its core.
- **Instinctive (Gut)** — types 8, 9, 1. Oriented around autonomy, presence, control, and *being* rather than analyzing or performing. Anger-based at its core.

### 2. Hornevian Group (Social Strategy)

- **Withdrawn** — types 4, 5, 9. Moves *away* from others. Values independence, self-sufficiency, and internal space.
- **Compliant** — types 1, 2, 6. Moves *toward* others. Oriented around duty, obligation, and meeting expectations.
- **Assertive** — types 3, 7, 8. Moves *against* others. Oriented around impact, expansion, and taking up space.

### 3. Harmonic Group (Response to Conflict/Stress)

- **Positive Outlook** — types 2, 7, 9. Reframes difficulty in optimistic terms; minimizes negativity.
- **Competency** — types 1, 3, 5. Handles problems by detaching from emotion and applying calm, capable, rational effort.
- **Reactive** — types 4, 6, 8. Responds to problems with visible emotional intensity, wanting that intensity acknowledged rather than smoothed over.

## When Cities Score Identically

With only 27 possible three-axis combinations across 36 profiled locations, overlap is inevitable — 34 of the 36 fall into one of 11 shared-result groups (only Dome Fuji and Abowasa are unique). See **`Distinguishing_Overlapping_Profiles.md`** for what actually tells each overlapping group apart — the specific detail, medium, or scale each city expresses its shared triple through.
