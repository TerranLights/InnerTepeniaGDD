# Neumayer

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Neumayer's real-world basis is famous for genuinely precise, modular, zero-emission engineering — a station designed around careful anticipation of exactly what its environment would demand of it. In Tepenia, that same precision-first character carries through: a smaller, technically self-contained node rather than a place defined by raw output (Gut) or emotional identity (Feeling). Its whole presence in the network is a product of careful planning rather than reactive necessity.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Neumayer sits off Hwy 7's main line, reached via its own connector rather than being a primary through-stop — a physically set-apart position that mirrors a self-contained civic character. It isn't positioned to draw traffic or attention through the subnet; it functions quietly on its own terms, needing little from the cities around it.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Everything established about Neumayer points toward understated technical efficiency rather than drama of any kind. A precision-engineered station handling its role capably, without needing to prove or announce that capability, is Competency in its cleanest form — the same quiet capability Zhongshan and Sanay both show, here expressed through technical self-sufficiency specifically.
