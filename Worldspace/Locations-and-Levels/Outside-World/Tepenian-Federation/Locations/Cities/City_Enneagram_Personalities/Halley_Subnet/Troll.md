# Troll

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Troll is a genuine intermodal freight linchpin — imports from Africa land at Sanay or Belgrano, get trucked in via Hwy 7, and fly out across the whole country via Troll Airfield, one of only eight functional airports confirmed anywhere in Tepenia. That's a city whose significance is entirely physical and functional: real goods, real throughput, real working-class labor, not analysis or self-presentation. Its established working-class character with above-average national spending power (and its own distinct, higher-quality glitch-coolant variant) reinforces a city defined by tangible, earned material reality rather than abstraction.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Troll's control is significant enough to be flagged as DLC 5's working central-conflict anchor — a city whose importance is real enough to fight over. That's Assertive energy: Troll isn't a quiet, self-contained node (Withdrawn) or a dutiful service function absorbed into someone else's story (Compliant); it's a genuine chokepoint whose control has real, contested weight in the wider Federation's logistics, the same kind of outward economic force Soyuz exerts through manufacturing rather than freight.

## Harmonic Group: Reactive

Unlike Sanay's calm, uncontested competence, Troll's stakes are live — its airfield is genuinely fought over, its economic importance is not just present but actively contested. That tension suggests a Reactive temperament: a place where real pressure exists just under the surface, prone to visible strain when its role or control is challenged, rather than absorbing conflict quietly (Competency) or reframing it away (Positive Outlook).
