# Sanay

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Sanay holds the actual technical backbone of the Halley subnet — the real Arcanet nexus, built into Vesleskarvet's stable bedrock — while Halley keeps the naming honor. That's a personality defined by genuine capability and function rather than reputation or analysis: Sanay doesn't need the credit to know it's the one actually holding the subnet together. Combined with its major shipping/receiving port role to South Africa, likely Tepenia's single largest such port, Sanay's identity is built entirely on real, physical, load-bearing presence.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Sanay is genuinely busy with its own real work — running the ports, managing shipping lanes, maintaining the nexus — while Halley's own residents get the subnet-naming credit for having built that nexus. Sanay doesn't seem to need or seek that recognition; it simply keeps functioning, the definition of Withdrawn energy. It doesn't assert itself onto the wider subnet's narrative the way a city seeking impact or validation would.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Holding both a subnet's entire communications infrastructure and one of Tepenia's largest ports is an enormous responsibility, and Sanay carries it without any established drama or self-promotion attached. This is about as clean an expression of Competency as a city gets: genuinely critical, load-bearing capability, handled quietly and effectively, with no need to make a show of how much is actually resting on it.
