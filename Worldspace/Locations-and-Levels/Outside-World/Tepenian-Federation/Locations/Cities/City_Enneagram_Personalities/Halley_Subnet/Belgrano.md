# Belgrano

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Belgrano's defining feature is a genuine split identity between its living Second Interwar-era culture and a distinct post-war decline, resolved into "The Boneyard Times" faction — an identity built specifically around memory, loyalty, and what the city used to be versus what it has become. That tension between past and present self-concept is Heart-center material almost by definition: this is a city wrestling with its own image and continuity, not simply enduring circumstances (Gut) or analyzing risk (Head).

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

The "Boneyarders" — the subgroup that stays loyal to Belgrano through its decline — represent a duty-and-loyalty-driven orientation: staying because of commitment to the place and to each other, not because the city offers obvious advantage anymore. That loyalty-under-hardship is Compliant energy, oriented around obligation and connection to others rather than self-sufficient withdrawal or outward assertion.

## Harmonic Group: Reactive

A city whose own defining faction is named for its bones — the skeletal remains of a more vital past — carries real, felt intensity rather than calm management or optimistic reframing. The tension between the Second Interwar identity and the post-war "Boneyard" reality reads as something Belgrano feels rather than something it has smoothly processed, the emotional charge of decline and loyalty both wanting to be acknowledged rather than quietly managed away.
