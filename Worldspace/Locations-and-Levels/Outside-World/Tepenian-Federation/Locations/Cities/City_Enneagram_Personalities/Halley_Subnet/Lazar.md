# Lazar

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Lazar is a genuine megacity, badly bombed but not destroyed — survival and sheer physical scale are its defining traits, not analysis or self-image. Its own name is a direct product of that scale: "Novolazarevskaya," the founding Russian station's name, was phonetically shortened over centuries by the sheer weight of USA/Germany/France/Brazil immigration overtaking the founding population. That's a city whose identity was reshaped by raw demographic mass and endurance rather than deliberate narrative-building — pure Gut-center presence.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Lazar's whole founding story is one of accommodation: two originally separate settlements — Russian-run Novolazarevskaya and the non-repopulated Maitri site — coalesced into a single city, and its very name evolved to reflect whoever actually came to live there over time, rather than staying fixed to its founders. That absorptive, adaptive openness to new populations reshaping the city's own identity is Compliant energy: accommodating others' needs and presence rather than staying self-contained or pushing its own founding character outward.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

"Badly bombed, not destroyed" is itself a Competency-flavored fact: Lazar took serious damage and kept functioning as a highway origin point (Hwy 7's eastern terminus, the Belgrano Highway Extension's starting point) regardless. There's no sign of this being processed as an open wound or reframed with forced positivity — Lazar simply absorbed the damage and continued operating, the same capable, low-drama resilience found in Zhongshan and Byrd, here expressed at megacity scale.
