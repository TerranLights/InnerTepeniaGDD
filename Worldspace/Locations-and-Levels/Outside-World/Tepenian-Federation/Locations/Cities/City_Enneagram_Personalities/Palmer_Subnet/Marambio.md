# Marambio

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Marambio is a genuine dual-role logistics powerhouse — a major shipyard and port receiving from South America, and one of only eight confirmed functional airports anywhere in Tepenia. That's an identity built on real physical throughput and capability, not analysis or self-presentation: Marambio matters because of what it moves and processes, full stop.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Holding both a major port and a rare functional airport gives Marambio genuine outward economic reach — goods and people flow through it into the wider Atlantic coastal network. That's Assertive energy, an active force shaping the wider Federation's logistics rather than a quiet, self-contained node or a role defined purely by serving others.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Running a dual shipyard-and-aviation hub is a demanding, complex responsibility, and nothing established about Marambio suggests it's handled with drama or visible strain. It's simply capable — real infrastructure, real throughput, managed effectively as a matter of course.
