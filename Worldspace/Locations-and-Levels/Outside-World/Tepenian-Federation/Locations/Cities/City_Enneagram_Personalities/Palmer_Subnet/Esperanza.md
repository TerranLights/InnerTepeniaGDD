## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Esperanza's entire civic identity is organized around image, identity, and emotional continuity rather
than analysis or raw presence — "The Guarded City" is not a functional description, it's the city's
whole sense of who it is. The founding compact (robots built to protect the children of humans who
sacrificed everything for exile) functions as something close to civic scripture, and the birth registry
is treated as living proof of purpose rather than bureaucratic recordkeeping. That's Heart-center
identity-and-meaning-making, not Head-center risk analysis or Gut-center autonomous presence.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Esperanza's whole founding logic is a debt owed and honored — humans who gave up everything deserved to
have their children looked after, "deliberately and as policy," and every civic institution answers to
that obligation. This is Compliant energy in its clearest form: a city moving toward others, organized
entirely around duty and meeting an expectation it set for itself rather than asserting its own interests
outward or withdrawing into self-sufficiency.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

The one fact that could easily read as a quiet crisis — robots now outnumbering the humans the whole city
exists to protect — is explicitly not treated as failure or irony locally; it's reframed as evidence the
compact is being honored at scale. The same pattern holds toward residents who don't personally share the
founding mission: met with patience, not friction. That's textbook Positive Outlook — reframing a
potentially destabilizing fact in optimistic terms rather than responding with visible strain (Reactive)
or detached, unemotional management (Competency).
