# Palmer City

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Palmer City is Tepenia's cultural capital, its most nationally diverse city (all 43 master-list nations present), and its established tourism/entertainment economy — an identity built entirely around image, culture, and felt experience rather than function or endurance. That its own founding-era history includes the Long Night War's actual inciting incident (a gynoid denied personhood, defending herself against an Upper Earth diplomat) only deepens the Heart-center reading: this is a city where identity, dignity, and lived experience carry the highest possible stakes.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

A city built around entertainment and tourism actively wants to be seen and experienced — Palmer City draws people in and puts on a show, the definition of Assertive energy, reaching outward for engagement and impact rather than serving others quietly (Compliant) or staying self-contained (Withdrawn). Its unmatched national diversity is itself a form of magnetism: people from everywhere are drawn to be part of what Palmer City is.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

Palmer City's day-to-day civic register is glamour and vibrancy — an entertainment-forward culture that leads with fun and spectacle. That's Positive Outlook energy at the civic level, even though the city's own founding-era history holds real darkness underneath it; the city's presented, felt temperament is warm and inviting rather than defensive or visibly strained.
