## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Sejong's identity is grounded in a constantly-exercised analytical and administrative skill, not raw
output or emotional self-expression: King George Island crowded nearly a dozen national communities
onto one small island from day one, and Sejong's whole civic art form became diplomatic coordination —
"a skill the community built for itself out of sheer necessity, constantly renegotiating the boundary
with whichever neighbor happens to be adjacent" (`Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Sejong.md`). That's
Head-center presence in its clearest form: the city matters because of what it navigates and manages,
not because of what it physically produces or how it wants to be seen.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Sejong's whole function is facilitating coexistence — its established "boundary zones" are real,
negotiated civic geography maintained through "practiced and diplomatic" hospitality shaped by decades
of close-quarters coexistence, moving toward and accommodating its many neighbors rather than either
asserting outward dominance or withdrawing into isolation it never had the luxury of.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

King George Island's density carries genuine "competitive tension... over King George Island's
favorable, mild terrain," per Sejong's own established fault line — but the city handles it through
practiced, capable diplomacy rather than visible drama or strain, a "genuinely distinct community
identity" maintained calmly and without needing to announce it. That's Competency: real, substantial
responsibility (a dozen simultaneous neighbor relationships) handled capably.
