# Sejong

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies. Note: Sejong has less established personality-level lore than most other Tepenian cities — this read leans on its geography and infrastructure connections rather than a full developer vision session, and should be revisited if one happens later.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Sejong sits immediately next to Machu Picchu Airport, which connects directly to the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority — a genuinely administrative, processing-oriented piece of infrastructure. Proximity to that function suggests a city whose practical role in the network leans toward coordination and process rather than raw output or emotional self-expression.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

A city positioned next to a border-and-customs processing point plays a supporting, facilitating role in the wider Federation's administration — helping arrivals move through the system smoothly, rather than asserting its own outward identity or withdrawing into self-sufficiency.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Nothing established about Sejong suggests drama or visible strain; its role, as far as it's currently defined, is quiet, administrative, and functional — handled capably alongside the nearby Machu Picchu Airport and Authority rather than being a source of tension in its own right.
