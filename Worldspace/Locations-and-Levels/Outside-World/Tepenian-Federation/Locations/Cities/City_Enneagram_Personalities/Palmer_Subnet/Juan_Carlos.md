# Juan Carlos

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Juan Carlos was Tepenia's first bureaucratic archive — tracking every arrival and departure, every visa and permanent-immigration application, and specifically maintaining a watch on anyone who had held a position in their home country's Upper Earth government. Few possible identities are more purely Head-centered than "the city whose entire founding function was information security and institutional memory."

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Juan Carlos's archive function existed entirely in service of the wider young Federation's administrative needs — tied directly to the Machu Picchu Border & Customs Authority, processing and protecting information on behalf of the whole exile population, not for its own benefit or self-expression. That's Compliant energy: a dutiful, service-oriented role at the very center of the city's identity.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Managing a Federation-wide bureaucratic archive — the record of everyone who passed through the exile administration — is an enormous responsibility, handled with methodical, unemotional precision rather than visible strain. Even its eventual destruction is framed in Head-center terms: Upper Earth forces targeted Juan Carlos specifically because of its intelligence value, a calculated act of war against a city whose whole significance was informational, not emotional or symbolic.
