# Puerto Abrigo

> **⚠ RENAMED 2026-10-03 (`DR-40`): formerly *Port Lockroy*, the real base's name; now **Puerto Abrigo**.** Any line below that still says "Port Lockroy" names the REAL site (the base, the harbor, Operation Tabarin) or tells the story of the FORMER name (the base was named for Édouard Lockroy). The latter are revisit items. Puerto Abrigo is Spanish, "sheltered harbor"; founders: Chile first (`DR-34`).

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Port Lockroy's real-world basis is a preserved historic base functioning as a museum — a site whose entire purpose is memory-keeping and heritage rather than active production or risk management. That orientation toward preserving and honoring the past, carried into Tepenia's own highway-only, quietly-positioned version of the city, points toward a Heart-center identity built on legacy and meaning.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Puerto Abrigo has no functional airport in Tepenia (highway-only, per the confirmed airport network) and no major established economic or logistics role — a small, quiet, self-contained presence rather than one reaching outward for engagement or influence. It exists more as a keeper of its own history than an active participant in the wider Federation's affairs.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

A heritage/memory-keeping identity, especially one rooted in a real-world museum-and-gift-shop character, carries a fond, nostalgic register rather than one of struggle or visible tension. Port Lockroy's quiet, small-scale role reads as content rather than strained — a gentle keeper of the past rather than a city defined by hardship.
