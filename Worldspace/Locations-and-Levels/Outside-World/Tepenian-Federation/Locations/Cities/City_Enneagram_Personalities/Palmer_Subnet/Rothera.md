## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Rothera's identity is grounded entirely in real physical function and capacity, not analysis or image —
a decentralized industrial base spread across Adelaide Island, turning raw materials into the finished
infrastructure components every other Palmer subnet city actually builds with, plus large-scale
underground vault sections adding a second, compounding layer of physical resilience. That's Gut-center
presence in its clearest form: the city matters because of what it physically makes and withstands, not
because of what it analyzes or how it wants to be seen.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Rothera's industrial output doesn't serve one neighbor quietly — its finished components "plausibly
reach every other city in the subnet, including Palmer City itself," a genuine, load-bearing economic
footprint across the entire Palmer subnet. That's real outward reach and impact, the definition of
Assertive energy, even though the city's own civic register (function over presentation, no
self-promotion) reads modest on the surface — the assertiveness is economic and structural, not
performative.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Rothera survived the Long Night War specifically *because* of decentralization, not luck, terrain, or
bedrock — a fact its own culture takes quiet, specific pride in, per `Local_Cultures/Palmer_Subnet/Rothera.md`.
Continuing to function, still producing, still supplying an entire subnet's infrastructure needs, without
any established drama or dramatized struggle attached, is Competency in its cleanest form: real,
substantial responsibility handled capably and without needing to announce it.
