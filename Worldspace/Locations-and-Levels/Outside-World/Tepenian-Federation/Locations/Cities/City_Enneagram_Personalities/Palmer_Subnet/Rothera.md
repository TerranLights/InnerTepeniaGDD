# Rothera

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Rothera holds one of only eight confirmed functional airports in all of Tepenia, with a wheeled-aircraft-capable gravel runway — real, rare logistics capability that most Tepenian cities simply don't have. That's an identity grounded in genuine physical function and capacity, the same kind of Gut-center presence found in Marambio and Troll, expressed here through Rothera's confirmed place in the country's small aviation network.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Being one of only eight airports nationwide gives Rothera real, outward-reaching significance in Tepenia's logistics network — it isn't a quiet, self-contained node, and it isn't defined by serving another city's needs the way Esperanza serves Marambio. It's a genuine point of leverage in the wider Federation's aviation map.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Nothing established about Rothera suggests visible strain or dramatized struggle — it functions as a confirmed, capable piece of critical national infrastructure, the same understated technical competence found throughout Tepenia's small handful of genuinely airport-equipped cities.
