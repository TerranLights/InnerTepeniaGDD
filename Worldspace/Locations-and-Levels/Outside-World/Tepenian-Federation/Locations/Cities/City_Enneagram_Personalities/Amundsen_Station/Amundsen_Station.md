# Amundsen Station

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies. Not one of Tepenia's 35 cities — included for completeness, per the developer's own "for the sake of total completion" request during its 2026-07-06 vision note.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Amundsen Station's vision session confirmed it directly: a place of scientific, technical, and mechanical maintenance. Its established political neutrality reinforces the same center — the one place in a Federation of distinct subnet identities that belonged to all of them and therefore to none, where disputes too difficult for any single subnet were brought for impartial mediation. That's Head-center energy exactly: analytical, neutral, defined by information and function rather than identity or output.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Amundsen Station never had a permanent civic community in the way an actual city does — "people came from their cities, did their work, and went home." Its entire purpose was service to the wider Federation: maintaining the Arcanet relay and the Amundsen Tower, hosting disputes it had no stake in, existing for everyone else's benefit rather than asserting an identity of its own. That's Compliant energy in its most complete form — a place defined entirely by what it did for others.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Nothing about Amundsen Station's established character carries emotional charge or performed positivity — its whole nature was quiet, capable upkeep: relay operations, Tower support, life support, handled as ongoing technical maintenance rather than drama. Even its fall is described in functional terms: "the place that connected everything — and, after its fall, the place whose absence disconnected everything." That's Competency measured by consequence rather than sentiment.
