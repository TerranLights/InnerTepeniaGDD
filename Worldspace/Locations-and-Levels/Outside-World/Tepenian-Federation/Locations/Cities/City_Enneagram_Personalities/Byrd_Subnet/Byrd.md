# Byrd

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Byrd's entire civic identity — "Built From Below, Then Left Alone" — is a description of raw endurance and presence rather than analysis or self-image. The city was founded by an expedition that chose the hardest possible route to reach it, and its people have lived underground, literally built into the ice, for generations. Its self-sufficiency began as sheer necessity ("less pride than necessity") and only became pride over time — that arc, hardship absorbed and metabolized into identity without much reflection or emotional processing along the way, is textbook Gut-center material. Byrd doesn't theorize about its own resilience; it simply *is* resilient, in the same unreflective way Zhongshan simply *is* quiet.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Byrd is the most isolated surviving city in Tepenia, and not just by circumstance — it's isolated by identity. Its own established hospitality character is "reserved but genuine," explicitly because visitors are rare enough to be treated as a real event rather than routine. This is Withdrawn energy at its most literal: a place that has built an entire self-concept around not needing others, reinforced now by the total breakdown of its once-established aviation route. Even its economic recovery (the vast underground fabrication plants, the import/export & dispatch offices) routes outward through highways rather than drawing people in — Byrd projects goods, not itself.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Byrd's defining civic value is self-sufficiency treated as plain necessity, handled without complaint or drama — "pride over time," not pride demanded. The staggering scale of its underground city is presented as something the city simply built and lives inside matter-of-factly, not as a a source of civic boasting or anxious self-justification. Even amid genuine isolation and a grounded, irreparable aviation fleet, the city's described temperament is a "genuine sense of shared community" holding steady under real hardship — competent, quiet endurance, not reactive distress or forced optimism about its situation.
