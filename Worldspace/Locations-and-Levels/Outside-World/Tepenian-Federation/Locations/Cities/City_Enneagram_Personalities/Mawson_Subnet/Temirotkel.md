## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Temirötkel's vision session gave it an almost entirely functional, physical identity: major fabrication industry, a huge trucking & dispatch operation, "things get made as well as routed here." Leisure culture exists but is explicitly secondary to the industrial core — this is a city defined by doing and producing, not by analysis (Thinking) or self-image (Feeling). Even its physical layout reinforces this — split cleanly into industrial and residential halves, a city organized around function rather than expression.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Unlike Sinheung's forceful, identity-driven industrial pride, Temirötkel's economy is fundamentally a service role: its trucking and dispatch operation exists to move goods *for* the wider highway network — Mawson, Sinheung, Shirayuki via Hwy 4, and the Halley subnet via Hwy 7-ext. This is a supporting, logistics-first function, done quietly and dutifully rather than for recognition. Temirötkel doesn't assert its own importance the way Sinheung does; it simply keeps the network's goods flowing, the definition of Compliant, duty-oriented energy.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Temirötkel handles its dual industrial/residential identity with plain practicality rather than drama. Leisure being "explicitly secondary" isn't presented as a sacrifice or a source of tension — it's simply how the city has organized its priorities, matter-of-factly. There's no sign of Temirötkel reframing its situation optimistically or reacting emotionally to its own workload; it just gets the fabrication and dispatch done, the same quiet, capable competence Zhongshan shows in a different register.
