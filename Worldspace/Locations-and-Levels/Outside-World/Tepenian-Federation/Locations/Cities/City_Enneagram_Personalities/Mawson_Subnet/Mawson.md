# Mawson

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Mawson's whole identity is built on emotional experience and connection: it's "relatively speaking, Antarctica's closest thing to a resort town," a place defined by hospitality and warmth rather than function or security. Most tellingly, it's Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination for newly-married human-robot couples — a role built entirely around facilitating other people's most significant emotional and relational moments. That's about as pure a Heart-center identity as a city can have: Mawson's value to the wider Federation is fundamentally about feeling, not producing or enduring.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

Mawson's whole civic function is oriented toward serving visitors — public libraries, genuine ease of friendship, and a hospitality character built and earned over decades specifically *for* other people's benefit. Its honeymoon-destination reputation didn't happen by accident; it's a role the city has actively cultivated to meet a need in the wider Federation (newly-married couples wanting somewhere to go before settling into their real lives). That's Compliant energy exactly — moving toward others, organizing the self around meeting an external need or expectation, rather than turning inward (Withdrawn) or pushing outward to make an impact on its own terms (Assertive).

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

Everything about Mawson reads as warm and optimistic rather than defensively competent or emotionally reactive. It's "genuinely easy to make friends" here; the honeymoon tradition is framed entirely in terms of romance and hope rather than logistics; and the city's hospitality character comes across as generous rather than dutiful or effortful. Where a Competency-type city would handle its role capably and quietly, Mawson leans into its warmth directly — a place that wants to be experienced as good news, consistent with a genuine Positive Outlook orientation.
