# Fort McMurdo

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Fort McMurdo's vision session put it plainly: the city's real mass is *presence*, not population or architecture. Visitors immediately sense real business happening and real decisions rippling outward to change what other cities can do. That is Gut-center energy in its most concentrated form — an effect on the world, felt directly, with no need for explanation or emotional framing to make its weight obvious.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Fort McMurdo's established "de facto capital"/"McMurdo Problem" status is a genuine gravitational pull on the rest of the subnet — dedicated comms stations coordinating supply, extraction, and logistics that ripple outward well past the city's own borders. That's Assertive energy exactly: a city whose influence actively reaches into and shapes what other places can do, rather than staying self-contained or existing to serve others' needs.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Crucially, this gravity is explicitly operational, not political — Fort McMurdo doesn't posture or campaign for influence in Federation debates, it's just quietly indispensable. That's Competency at its cleanest: real capability and real effect, carried without drama, without needing to announce or defend its own importance.
