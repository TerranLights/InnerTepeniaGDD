# Cape Adare

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Cape Adare's vision session gave it a warm, community-first identity — vast but low-density, a "big city, small-town feel," strongly community-driven, with an acoustic-instrument folk music culture (guitars, violins, cellos, tagelharpas) as a genuine civic strength. Music this central to a city's identity, paired with community connection as its defining trait, points squarely to Heart-center energy: this is a place organized around belonging and shared feeling, not function or risk.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

A community-driven culture is inherently oriented toward others — mutual presence, shared space, and connection rather than self-sufficient isolation (Withdrawn) or outward assertion (Assertive). Even the penguins occasionally kept as outdoor pets reflect a place organized around warmth and companionship rather than pure utility.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

Everything about Cape Adare reads as unhurried and content: a slow, easygoing pace of both life and work, penguins as pets rather than wildlife to be managed, and a folk music tradition played for its own sake. There's no sign of struggle being managed competently or emotional intensity demanding acknowledgment — just a genuinely pleasant, low-drama, contented way of living.
