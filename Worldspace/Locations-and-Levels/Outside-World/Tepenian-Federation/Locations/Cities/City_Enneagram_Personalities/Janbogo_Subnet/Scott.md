# Scott

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Scott's vision session described it as overwhelmingly residential, "a genuinely decent, quiet place to raise a family." That framing centers domestic warmth and quality of life as the city's defining trait — a Heart-center concern, oriented around belonging and home rather than raw output (Gut) or careful risk management (Head), even though a real technical/research economic base sits quietly underneath.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Scott is deliberately, explicitly the quiet counterpart to Fort McMurdo's loud, operational energy right next door — "not much visibly happening day to day." That's Withdrawn energy in a very literal sense: a city content to exist inward-facing and low-key, needing no external attention or engagement to feel complete, its whole identity partly defined by contrast with its far more assertive neighbor.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

There's no tension or strain in how Scott is described — just a calm, pleasant, "genuinely decent" domestic life sitting alongside its established economic base without conflict between the two. That untroubled, contented framing, free of both dramatized struggle and visible reactivity, is Positive Outlook energy at its simplest and most stable.
