# Janbogo

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Janbogo's civic identity is built on aesthetic fusion and social vibrancy — a genuine Chinese-French fashion fusion culture, expressed through great shielded commercial halls that function as real social/commercial gathering spaces. That's an identity organized around image, culture, and connection rather than pure function or risk-management. Its role as the subnet's physical Arcanet relay-nexus adds real technical weight, but the city's felt character comes from its aesthetic and social life first.

## Hornevian Group: Compliant

A fashion identity built from genuinely blending two national traditions rather than favoring one is itself an accommodating, both/and orientation — Janbogo doesn't assert a singular dominant character, it synthesizes. That same accommodating quality shows in its role as the subnet's Arcanet nexus, a service function performed for the whole subnet's benefit.

## Harmonic Group: Reactive

Janbogo carries a genuinely heavy emotional weight: it was deliberately left standing after Zukelli's destruction specifically to witness the strike and carry its deterrent message forward. That's not a burden a city processes with calm detachment or optimistic reframing — it's a real, intense thing to have happened to a place, and Janbogo's ongoing relationship to that memory reads as something felt and reacted to, not smoothly resolved.
