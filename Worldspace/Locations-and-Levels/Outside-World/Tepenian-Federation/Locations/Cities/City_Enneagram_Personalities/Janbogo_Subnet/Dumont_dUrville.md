# Dumont d'Urville

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Dumont d'Urville's vision session landed it a "New Orleans at 1/20th scale" identity — live music in almost every downtown eatery, a tight, alive, culturally rich core on Petrel Island. That's an identity defined by atmosphere, culture, and felt experience, the clearest Heart-center read among the Janbogo/Ross cities. Even its already-established "Negotiated Ground" culture is about relationship and identity negotiation, reinforcing the same center.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Downtown Dumont d'Urville is expensive, dense, and alive — heating units run *between* buildings, not just inside them, a visible, outward display of vitality rather than a modest, self-contained warmth. That's Assertive energy: a city that takes up space and projects real presence and energy into its own small footprint, rather than turning inward (Withdrawn) or organizing itself around serving others (Compliant).

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

The "New Orleans" comparison itself carries strong festive, joyful connotations, and nothing about Dumont d'Urville's downtown culture reads as struggle managed competently or tension needing acknowledgment — it's presented as genuinely vibrant and alive. A bridge/mainland-access engineering question remains genuinely unresolved, but the city's own felt temperament, as established, is warm and celebratory rather than anxious about it.
