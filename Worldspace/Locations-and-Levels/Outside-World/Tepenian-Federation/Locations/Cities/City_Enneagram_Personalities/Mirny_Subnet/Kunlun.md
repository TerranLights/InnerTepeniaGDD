# Kunlun

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Kunlun is an astronomy site first and a communications relay second, its whole civic identity built around a towering antenna and satellite array and instrument-packed labs running holographic displays through every corridor. That's a personality organized entirely around information, observation, and analysis — the clearest possible Head-center identity among all of Tepenia's cities.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Kunlun sits among the most physically isolated locations in Tepenia, its population there specifically for astronomy and comms work rather than social or economic engagement with the wider Federation. Its curated, multinational population was drawn together by shared vocational heritage, not a desire for outward influence — a genuinely self-contained scientific community, turned inward toward its own instruments and observations.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

The defining detail from Kunlun's vision session is that nobody experiences the work as "a job" — it's simply their life, so completely that bars, music halls, and restaurants run 24 hours because the science never stops. That's not competent endurance of a demanding role; it's genuine, sustained enthusiasm reframing what would be relentless work anywhere else into something joyfully all-consuming — Positive Outlook in its purest form.
