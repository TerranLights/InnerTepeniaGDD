## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Sinheung centers on Gut energy the same way its neighbor Zhongshan does, but forcefully and outward-facing
where Zhongshan's is quiet and self-contained. The city's entire post-rework economic identity is built around
*making* things — heavy industrial fabrication, and, most significantly, Sinheung is one of the handful of
Tepenian cities that manufactures the fabrication-synthesis chambers that physically create robots. That's
about as literal a Gut-center "effect on the world" as a city can express: not just producing goods, but
producing the apparatus of creation itself.

The city's founding gives that instinct a specific edge. Sinheung's site was secured for Korea by the
International Court of Diplomacy at Jeju-do — the same court that settled Zhongshan for China and allocated
Shirayuki to Japan — but unlike Zhongshan's organically-held claim, Sinheung's Korean founders inherited an
*already-operating* Russian research station, a claim made by treaty rather than found. The city's own thematic
identity, "Claimed, Not Found," names this directly: a population that had to prove its right to the ground it
stood on gravitated toward doing something nobody could dispute the value of. That's Gut-center presence
asserted through action rather than argued through analysis (Thinking) or performed through image (Feeling).

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Sinheung stands out sharply against both of its Larsemann Hills neighbors. Where Zhongshan's non-prying culture
keeps private life unbroadcast and Shirayuki's calm setting supports quiet reflection, Sinheung's own social
contract states the opposite directly: "Korean cultural life is proudly, visibly public here — the opposite of
Zhongshan's non-prying insularity, because this city performs its identity outward precisely because it has
something to prove." Privacy itself is explicitly "low, by Zhongshan's standards" — mutual visibility is part of
what makes the city's claim to its own ground feel solid.

The built environment makes the same move architecturally: new construction doesn't hide the inherited Russian
station structures, it deliberately outbuilds them — "taller, more numerous, more obviously productive... a
skyline that reads as busy on purpose." Where Zhongshan concentrates care in details nobody outside would
notice, Sinheung's architecture is "legible from a distance," a physical argument for its own legitimacy the
same way the fabrication-chamber industry is an economic one. Nationally, the city's chambers ship across all
of Tepenia — every other city that lacks its own chamber-manufacturing capability depends on cities like
Sinheung for it — meaning Sinheung's own capability and identity actively reaches outward into the rest of the
Federation, rather than staying self-sufficient (Zhongshan) or accommodating (a Compliant city would be).

## Harmonic Group: Reactive

Sinheung's harmonic read rests on one specific, developer-flagged fact: a "deep, genuinely unusual sense of
national pride/patriotism... a genuine outlier compared to most Tepenian cities' quieter post-national
identity." Being named an *outlier* in emotional intensity — standing out against a whole country's calmer norm
— is close to a textbook description of Reactive energy: visible intensity that wants to be seen and
acknowledged, not smoothed over or reframed away. The city's own political character section makes the
mechanism explicit: "a population that secured its home through diplomacy rather than inheriting or
discovering it holds onto that home more fiercely, not less" — a claim held onto with force, not held calmly.

**Worth flagging, since the newer material adds real nuance here:** Sinheung's pride doesn't surface as
outward emotional display — it channels into disciplined, demonstrable output instead ("a quiet, rarely-voiced
anxiety... shows up instead as the city's outsized civic pride in what it actually produces"). That channeling
*medium* reads as genuinely competent and controlled, which could argue for Competency instead. But the
underlying temperament the medium is expressing — an intensity flagged as atypical for the whole country,
fiercely held rather than calmly detached, wanting the city's worth *proven* and *seen* rather than simply
trusted — is still Reactive in character; it's just Reactive energy running through an unusually disciplined
channel. Kept as Reactive here, consistent with the original 2026-07-06 read and `Distinguishing_Overlapping_
Profiles.md`'s existing Group 3 placement, but flagged in case a future pass judges the output-as-legitimacy
evidence strongly enough to warrant reconsidering it.

reactivity is about proving a claim to itself and to the rest of Tepenia, not defending territory under attack.
