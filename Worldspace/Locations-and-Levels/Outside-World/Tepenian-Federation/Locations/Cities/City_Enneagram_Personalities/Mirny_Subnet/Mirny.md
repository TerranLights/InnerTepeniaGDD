# Mirny

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Mirny's vision session gave it a physically defined identity: the city is built as its own windbreak, dense concentric buildings shedding the katabatic wind to shelter huge industrial yards inside. Its established civic character — "a rough city with a genuine, unforced sense of pride and kinship" — is presence and endurance, not analysis or emotional display. That pride is explicitly *unforced*: felt and lived, not performed.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

A city literally engineered to turn inward against a hostile environment — a fortified outer ring sheltering industrial and residential space alike inside — is Withdrawn energy made architectural. Mirny doesn't reach outward for engagement or validation; it consolidates and protects its own interior, content with minimal entertainment (mostly rough bars) rather than needing external stimulation.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Mirny handles genuinely harsh wind conditions and heavy industrial operations without any established drama attached — a rough, practical, no-frills competence rather than visible strain or a hardship reframed as meaning. Its pride being "unforced" reinforces this: a calm, settled confidence in its own capability, not something that needs defending or dressing up.
