# Casey

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Feeling (Heart)

Casey's entire vision session centers on **Splinters** — Tepenia's largest and most famous bar, a genuine cultural landmark hosting a massive Classic Rock/Jazz/Blues/Acoustic Folk scene famous along the whole Dumont d'Urville Sea and Indian Ocean coastline. A city whose defining civic identity is a music venue is a city organized around felt experience, atmosphere, and shared culture — Heart-center territory through and through.

## Hornevian Group: Assertive

Splinters isn't just locally beloved — it's famous along an entire coastline, a reputation that reaches well beyond Casey's own borders. That's Assertive energy: a city actively projecting its own cultural identity outward and drawing attention and visitors from a wide surrounding region, rather than staying self-contained or existing purely to serve others.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

A bustling city built around live music, dancing, and a famous social scene reads as warm and celebratory rather than strained or quietly capable. Casey's whole established character is about genuine vibrancy and enjoyment — the "bustling, not just a waystation" framing makes clear this is a place that leans into festivity as its defining trait.
