# Vostok

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Thinking (Head)

Vostok mirrors Kunlun's "one enormous distributed instrument" identity, but built around genetics — gene and allele experimentation drawing on Lake Vostok's ancient, isolated biology instead of the sky. Its research archive is confirmed to be so vast and complex that no single individual, human or robot, fully understands it; understanding is distributed across the whole population. That's about as complete a Head-center identity as a city can have: pure, ongoing analysis, so extensive it exceeds any one mind.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Like Kunlun, Vostok is a self-contained research community rather than one seeking outward engagement — its population's schedules (flexible for humans, nonexistent for robots) are organized entirely around the research itself, not around social or economic interaction with the wider Federation. It exists inward-facing, turned toward its own subglacial mystery.

## Harmonic Group: Positive Outlook

Vostok shares Kunlun's explicit "not work, it's life" ethos — robots socialize in the gaps between experiments rather than at a fixed shift's end, treating the research as something lived rather than endured. That same genuine, sustained enthusiasm for the work, rather than dutiful competence or visible strain under an unfathomably large archive, is Positive Outlook energy.
