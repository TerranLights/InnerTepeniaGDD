# Davis

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

Davis's vision session established it as "a working city first" — mining, fabrication, manufacturing, and general industry now the clear majority of its economy, with its founding research heritage reduced to a genuine but smaller minority. That's an identity organized around labor and physical output, not analysis or self-image, even with a small arts/music community and most leisure time spent at bars and eateries layered on top.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Davis's libraries openly welcome books smuggled out of Upper Earth — a quiet, self-directed way of building up culture without needing anyone's permission or external validation. That understated, self-sufficient approach to enriching its own life (smuggled knowledge, a modest homegrown arts scene) rather than seeking outside recognition or engagement is Withdrawn energy.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Davis blends serious industrial labor with a modest cultural life without any established tension between the two — a working city that also reads, also makes music, handled as a simple matter of balance rather than struggle. That quiet, capable integration of hard work and small pleasures, free of drama, is Competency energy.
