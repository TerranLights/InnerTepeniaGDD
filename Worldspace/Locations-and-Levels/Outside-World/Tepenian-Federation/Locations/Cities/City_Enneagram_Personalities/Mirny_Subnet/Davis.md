# Davis

*(See `City_Enneagram_Personalities/README.md` for the framework this applies.)*

## Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut)

*(Corrected 2026-07-16 — this originally described Davis's vision session as establishing it as "a working city first," mining/fabrication/manufacturing-dominant. The developer resolved a direct conflict between that framing and the same-day "breadbasket of Tepenia" City-Type resolution by reassigning the mining/quarrying role to Mirny; see `City_Enneagram_Personalities/Mirny_Subnet/Mirny.md`.)* Davis is, above all, Tepenia's breadbasket and a prime ecological/limnological research hub — sheltered-agriculture and greenhouse cultivation alongside genuine research heritage now the clear majority of its economy. That's an identity organized around patient, physical, hands-in-the-soil labor and careful observation rather than analysis-for-its-own-sake or self-image, even with a small arts/music community and most leisure time spent at bars and eateries layered on top.

## Hornevian Group: Withdrawn

Davis's libraries openly welcome books smuggled out of Upper Earth — a quiet, self-directed way of building up culture without needing anyone's permission or external validation. That understated, self-sufficient approach to enriching its own life (smuggled knowledge, a modest homegrown arts scene) rather than seeking outside recognition or engagement is Withdrawn energy. This actually fits a research-and-agriculture identity even better than the original mining-city framing did — a city of ecologists and growers quietly building its own knowledge base fits the Withdrawn register naturally.

## Harmonic Group: Competency

Davis blends serious industrial labor with a modest cultural life without any established tension between the two — a working city that also reads, also makes music, handled as a simple matter of balance rather than struggle. That quiet, capable integration of hard work and small pleasures, free of drama, is Competency energy.
