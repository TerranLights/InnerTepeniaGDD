# Little America — REMOVED FROM CANON (2026-07-03)

Little America has been removed from Tepenian canon entirely. It is no longer a Tepenian city.

**Why:** Little America was based on Richard Byrd's Stations I–V (1929–1958), at the Bay of Whales — and, like Framheim, the Tepenian city was never built on surviving pre-exile infrastructure. All five historical stations had been lost to ice shelf calving and burial by 2564; the Tepenian city was a full reconstruction from Byrd's underground archives.

Real-world verification (2026-07-03) found that the Bay of Whales itself — the defining feature both Little America and Framheim were reconstructed near — was **entirely eliminated in 1987 when Iceberg B-9 (154km long) calved off the Ross Ice Shelf.** The location is still ice shelf, not open ocean, but the specific bay-shaped feature that gave the site its meaning is permanently gone, replaced by reshaped ice. With no surviving structure to inherit in the first place, and the site's own defining geographic feature now gone too, there was no remaining physical basis to justify keeping the city in canon.

**Population disposition:** Little America's Census I population (425,052 humans / 437,529 robots / 862,581 combined) was combined with Framheim's and redistributed across Vostok, Esperanza, Concordia, Byrd, Zukelli, Janbogo, and Cape Adare. Full math preserved in `Official_Population_Census.md`'s removal note and the `project_city_post_cultures` memory file. Census II figures were not redistributed (flagged gap, not yet resolved).

**Known downstream consequence, not yet resolved:** Little America shared the Byrd ↔ Janbogo aviation route's refueling stop with Framheim (see `Specs/Byrd.md`). Removing both cities breaks this route as currently written and needs a real fix (see `Specs/Byrd.md`'s Open Questions) — not yet resolved as of this file's writing.

*This file is intentionally kept (rather than deleted) as a record of the removal decision and reasoning, should it ever need revisiting.*
