# Framheim — REMOVED FROM CANON (2026-07-03)

Framheim has been removed from Tepenian canon entirely. It is no longer a Tepenian city.

**Why:** Framheim was based on Roald Amundsen's 1910–1912 Ross Ice Shelf base, at the Bay of Whales — and, critically, the Tepenian city was never built on surviving pre-exile infrastructure. Per its own founding lore, the original historical station was already lost to ice shelf calving and burial centuries before the exile era began; the Tepenian city was a full reconstruction from Byrd's underground archives, built at a calculated position approximating the historical site.

Real-world verification (2026-07-03) found that the Bay of Whales itself — the defining feature both Framheim and Little America were reconstructed near — was **entirely eliminated in 1987 when Iceberg B-9 (154km long) calved off the Ross Ice Shelf.** The location is still ice shelf, not open ocean, but the specific bay-shaped feature that gave the site its meaning and its name is permanently gone, replaced by reshaped ice. With no surviving structure to inherit in the first place, and the site's own defining geographic feature now gone too, there was no remaining physical basis to justify keeping the city in canon at all.

**Population disposition:** Framheim's Census I population (430,488 humans / 454,194 robots / 884,682 combined) was combined with Little America's and redistributed across Vostok, Esperanza, Concordia, Byrd, Zukelli, Janbogo, and Cape Adare. Full math preserved in `Official_Population_Census.md`'s removal note and the `project_city_post_cultures` memory file. Census II figures were not redistributed (flagged gap, not yet resolved).

**Known downstream consequence, not yet resolved:** Framheim was the established refueling stop on the Byrd ↔ Janbogo aviation route (see `Specs/Byrd.md`) — aircraft could not fly the full distance in one leg, and Janbogo had no refueling infrastructure of its own. Removing Framheim breaks this route as currently written. This needs a real fix (see `Specs/Byrd.md`'s Open Questions) — not yet resolved as of this file's writing.

*This file is intentionally kept (rather than deleted) as a record of the removal decision and reasoning, should it ever need revisiting.*
