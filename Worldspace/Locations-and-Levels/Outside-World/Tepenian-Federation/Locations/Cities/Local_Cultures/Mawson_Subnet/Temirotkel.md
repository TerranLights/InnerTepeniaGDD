East Ongul Island carried a pre-exile research station whose infrastructure the city inherited; who built that station is a coordinate, not a founder. *(Corrected 2026-11-03: [old: "Japanese exiles founded the city on Syowa Station's extensive physical infrastructure."]. The city's name is *Temirötkel* (темірөткел), "Iron Crossing" (`DR-37`, 2026-10-03), which names the crossing and the iron of its industrial work. *(Corrected 2026-11-03: [old: "The city's name evolved phonetically from Shōwa (昭和) — the Japanese imperial era spanning 1926 to 1989, the reign of Emperor Hirohito, a period of extraordinary transformation in Japanese history encompassing war, defeat, and reconstruction.
