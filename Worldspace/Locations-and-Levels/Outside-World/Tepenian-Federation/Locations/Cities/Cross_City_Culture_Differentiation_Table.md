# How this is used — a four-step loop, and it runs in BOTH directions

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| **`Step 6`, before writing each category** | **READ that category's table.** ***If your answer rhymes with any entry, differentiate it explicitly and inline — in the finding itself — or change it.*** A prose assurance that "this is different" is not checkable |
| **While writing** | **Name your axis in bold** and confirm no completed city already uses it |
| **`Step 9.2`, when the city is done** | ⭐ **ADD YOUR ROW — IN THE SAME COMMIT.** `CLAUDE.md` requires this |
| **Whenever you notice something about ANOTHER city** | ⭐ **Record it here too.** ***Propagation is part of the finding, not a follow-up*** |

> ### ⚠ WHY THE LAST ROW EXISTS — a measured failure, `04` III.0
> **Two cities in one cluster were assigned the SAME planetary symbol. The collision *was* noticed — recorded
> correctly, months earlier, in one city's robot-culture file.** ***But the cluster's own differentiation
> guide contained zero mentions of symbols, planets or elements.***
> **The one file whose entire job was keeping those cities apart did not know.**
