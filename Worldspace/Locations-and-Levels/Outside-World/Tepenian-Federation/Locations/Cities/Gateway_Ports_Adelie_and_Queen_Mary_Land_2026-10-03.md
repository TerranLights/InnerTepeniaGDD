# Gateway Ports: the Adélie Land, Commonwealth Bay and Bunger Hills sites (2026-10-03)

> **Updated after the RERUN (same day):** corrected to `Research_Logs/Founding_Gateway_Research_C2_Adelie_BungerHills_RERUN_2026-10-03.md` (about 70 searches, 90 pages, four primary PDFs). The file name still says "Queen Mary Land" for continuity; **Bunger Hills is on the Knox Coast of Wilkes Land** (see section 3).

**Sites covered:** Dumont d'Urville (Adélie Land) · Denison (Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay) · Relung Panen (Bunger Hills oasis).
**Purpose.** A reference list of **every candidate gateway port and coast** found for these three sites, with the numbers, so a later session can reread the options without redoing the research. **Source of every number:** `Research_Logs/Founding_Gateway_Research_C_Adelie_QueenMary_2026-10-03.md` (read it for sources, search strings, dead ends and conflicts).

## ⭐ Developer ruling (2026-10-03, `DR-48`): the primary port is **HOBART**

> *"set Hobart as the main/primary port."*

**Hobart (Tasmania) is the main (primary) port for all three sites above.** Every other port and coast below is a recorded **possibility**: a secondary or alternative gateway, kept for reference.

⚠ **What this does and does not decide.**
- It names a **port**, which is infrastructure and access. It does **not** change any city's **founders** (`Founding_Register.md`: Dumont d'Urville Japan, Denison Australia, Relung Panen Indonesia and Malaysia). A founding nation stands on geography and access, not on the port (`DR-19`).

## 3. Per-site possibilities and notes

### Dumont d'Urville (Adélie Land; station 66°39′46″S 140°00′04″E on Île des Pétrels; solar UTC+9)
- **Primary: Hobart, 2,682 km** (the nearest city of size). Other near candidates: Invercargill 2,793, Launceston 2,839, Dunedin 2,921, Melbourne 3,224, Christchurch 3,230 km.
- **Japan (founders ruled):** Tokyo is 139.69°E and the site 140.00°E (0.31° apart, about 28 km); the initial bearing Tokyo to the site is 179.9°; Tokyo to the site is **11,381 km (6,145 nmi)**.
- **But the 140°E line is not sailable:** it crosses Indonesian New Guinea (2°21′S to 8°14′S) and Australia (17°43′S to 37°29′S). Ships must go **east of Australia** (Philippine or Coral Sea, Tasman Sea, around Tasmania), about 11,800 km (6,400 nmi, researcher's waypoint estimate, 4% longer than the great circle), or **west of Australia** (Indonesian archipelago, Indian Ocean, Cape Leeuwin), about 13,300 km (7,200 nmi, 17% longer). Tokyo to Hobart: great circle 8,771 km; east-route about 9,170 km.
- **Japan's actual Antarctic gateways (context only; RERUN, resolved):** the *Shirase*'s main body uses **Fremantle** (outbound and return, JARE-66 and JARE-67 plans); the advance party flies via **Cape Town** (DROMLAN air route), which explains English Wikipedia's Cape Town listing; the research ship *Umitaka Maru* returns via **Hobart** (Feb 2024 and Feb 2025). The first pass's "no Hobart call found" was **wrong**. Earlier ships also called at Colombo, Singapore and Sydney. **Japan reached Adélie Land waters once**: the *Umitaka Maru* in the 2007–08 CEAMARC survey off Terre Adélie and George V Land; no Japanese station was found there. Japan to Hobart, sum of NGA Pub. 151 legs: Yokohama–Sydney 4,330 nm + Sydney–Hobart 638 nm = 4,968 nm (9,200 km).
- Access caveat (context): katabatic winds and ice often block landings; the station keeps a skiway and a coastal airstrip 10 km away (October to February). Adélie Land lies between 136°E and 142°E; shore about 350 km. **Winds (RERUN):** mean about 33 to 35 km/h at the station; the "320 km/h record" is not official, Météo-France's record is **214.9 km/h** (23 May 1988).

### Denison (Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay, George V Land; 67°00′S 142°40′E; solar UTC+10)
- **Primary: Hobart, 2,698 km** (1,457 nmi); Hobart to the mouth of the bay 2,686 km; Hobart to the Mertz Glacier Tongue area 2,742 km. "Almost due south" is fair (4.66° of longitude, about 380 km, west-southwest drift); "same meridian" is not true.
- **Published figures (RERUN, sorted):** AAD 2006 **2,600 km** and the Norwegian mapping authority **2,670 km** match the computed 2,700 km; Wikipedia's 3,000 km overstates; the AAD history site's "4,000 km south of mainland Australia" is not geometrically usable. The great-circle figures are 2,610 km (South East Cape) and 2,698 km (Hobart). **Use 2,700 km with the method stated.**
- **Alternatives:** Invercargill 2,732 (small), Dunedin 2,855, Launceston 2,856, Christchurch 3,162, Melbourne 3,250, Wellington 3,461, Adelaide 3,577, Sydney 3,727, Perth 4,271 km. Dumont d'Urville (French) is the nearest station, 123 km west.
- **By ship (historical, context):** Mawson's *Aurora* left Hobart 2 Dec 1911 and entered Commonwealth Bay 8 Jan 1912 (36 days including a Macquarie Island stop). Modern: Chimu Adventures runs a voyage to Mawson's Huts "every year from Hobart" via Macquarie Island. Operator itinerary: 24 days Hobart to Dunedin with Commonwealth Bay on days 14 to 15 (agent pages, not the operator's own). **Access since 2010 is intermittent:** iceberg B09B blocked *L'Astrolabe* from the bay in 2011-12 and 2012-13, and the new *Douglas Mawson*'s first sailing (Dec 2025) was blocked **22 nm out** (two journalistic accounts). **Winds (RERUN):** Cape Denison mean about 70 to 75 km/h (AAD 71 km/h average daily maximum; AAE 19.4 m/s), not 80.
- **By air:** not served (see section 1).

### Relung Panen (Bunger Hills oasis, 66°17′S 100°47′E; solar UTC+7)
- **Primary: Hobart, 3,819 km** (2,062 nmi). The research found **nearer populous coasts in Australia**: Albany 3,650 km, Cape Leeuwin 3,671 km, Hobart 3,819 km, Fremantle 3,935 km, Perth about 3,940 km (AAD: Casey is "3,880 km due south of Perth"; Casey is 437 km from the oasis).
- **Indonesia (founders ruled):** the **Sumatran Indian-Ocean coast lies on the site's meridian**: Padang 0.39° (about 44 km) west of it; Padang to the site **7,264 km (3,922 nmi)** at bearing 179.8°; the 100°E meridian is open water from 2°51′S all the way to the ice, about 62° of latitude. Sumatra's population 62,259,500 (mid-2025 estimate).
- **Malaysia (founders ruled):** George Town, Penang is 0.42° west of the meridian and Port Klang 0.64° east, but **Peninsular Malaysia's west coast faces the Strait of Malacca** (900 km long, 65 to 250 km wide) and reaches the Indian Ocean only by the northern mouth (Penang about 8,600 km, Port Klang about 8,790 km, researcher's estimates) or via Singapore and Sunda (Port Klang about 7,880 km). Singapore 342 km and Jakarta 675 km east of the meridian.
- **Antarctic end (RERUN, corrected):** the oasis lies behind the Shackleton Ice Shelf (95°E to 105°E, western section projecting about 145 km seaward). The first pass's "no ship route" was wrong: **a ship route to the ice-shelf front exists** (*Nuyina*, March 2025, about a week from Hobart, 2,060 nm logged). **The oasis itself** (about 50 to 85 km inland of the shelf) is reached by air (Hobart to Wilkins to Casey to Bunger by Basler) or by helicopter from Mirny (Russia; Poland 2022), never by berthing. The Soviet Oazis station (1956 to 1958) was supplied by air from Mirny, about 360 km west.
- **Other bases for the coast (context):** Kerguelen's Port-aux-Français 2,560 km (ship only, no airport); Mauritius (Port Louis 5,978 km); Cape Town 6,255 km.
- **Coast (RERUN, resolved):** the Australian Antarctic Data Centre gazetteer places Bunger Hills **"at the western end of Knox Coast in Wilkes Land"**. "Queen Mary" is correct only for the Shackleton Ice Shelf in front of the oasis (the Queen Mary Coast ends at Cape Hordern, 100°30′E). **Corrected 2026-10-03** in `Specs/Mirny subnet/Relung_Panen.md`, `Relung_Panen_Dossier_2026-10-03.md`, `Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md` and `Relung_Panen/ULM_Input_Status.md`.
- **Indonesian and Malaysian polar interest (context only; RERUN):** **Malaysia** acceded to the Antarctic Treaty on 31 October 2011 (non-consultative), has been a SCAR full member since 2008, and funds a program (YPASM 2012, NARC 2022) that works with BAS, INACH, KOPRI and NZ's Scott Base: none in East Antarctica, always by air to a gateway city. **Indonesia** is not a treaty party or SCAR member, with three small participations (AAD *Aurora Australis* to Davis 2002, JARE-58 boarding the *Shirase* at Fremantle 2016-17, Russia 2024); one Ukrainian-agency item says Indonesia declared intent to accede in 2025, **unconfirmed** (not on the Treaty Secretariat list). **No ship route from Southeast Asia to Antarctica exists in any source**; the Sumatra meridian match is geometric only. NGA Pub. 151 legs: Telukbayur to Selat Sunda 488 nm; Hobart to Cape Leeuwin 1,628 nm.
