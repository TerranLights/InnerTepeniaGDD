# Station-Heritage Removal Tracker

> ## ⛔ READ THIS FIRST — WHY THE MISTAKE KEPT COMING BACK
>
> **Every pass copied the specs, and the specs were written from the station.**
>
> - **The copy instruction.** `Neo-Races-and-Cultures/_Method/City_Catalog_Template.md` L10:
>   ***"Pull from `Specs/[City].md` — don't re-derive, just summarize."*** Two commit messages say the same in
>   practice: the spec tier tables were *"sourced from Official_Population_Census.md"* (`a8a409c`), and the catalog
>   snapshots came *"directly from each city's own Specs/ file and the official census"* (`54466cc`). The megasheet
>   guide makes the spec founding binding: *"nothing invented later is allowed to contradict it"*
>   (`City_Megasheet_Compilation_Guide.md` L17). Every downstream method treats the spec as the authority and
>   reproduces it.
> - **What the specs contain.** The very first spec pass (`9d12cf9`, 2026-06-29) turned each real station's
>   operating nation into the city's founders. The spec template still builds that pattern into every spec:
>   `Based on: [Real station name], [country]` (L3), then `Founding population: [Who arrived first, and why were
>   they here?]` (L63), with **no rule anywhere in the file**. Result, as of 2026-09-30:
>   - 35 of 38 specs name the operator nation in `Based on:`.
>   - 29 name the operator or program and its start year in `Settled:`.
>   - **28 of 38 give the operator's own nation as the founding population.**
> - **Two founding systems disagree.**
>   - **The composition and census system** derives founding waves from **geography** (gateways). It says operator
>     identity "is NOT used as a factor."
>   - **The spec system** derives them from **heritage**.
>
>   The spec system wins because it sits in every header and is echoed by the index files, the knowledge graph and
>   memory. Fixing downstream copies three times (07-13, 07-14, 07-24) changed nothing, because the next pass copied
>   the spec again.
> - **The rule never reached the writers.** `DR-19` (founding stands on geography and access) exists in **one**
>   repo file, the rulings log. It is missing from the spec template, the catalog, culture, megasheet and
>   robot-culture templates, the ULM step files, and the law file itself (§8a, I-24).
>
> ### ✅ The root-level fix (2026-10-01): the Founding Register
> Founders now have one home, **`Founding_Register.md`**. The spec, catalog, culture and robot-culture templates take
> founders only from it. **`Universal_Location_Methodology/Tools/check_founders_against_register.py`** lists every
> place a city's files disagree with it. First run: **238 founder disagreements** (all in the ten ⛔ cities) and
> **11 heritage-wording hits**. Those are places to **read**, not a verification.
>
> ### ⚠ The specs stay. The fix has to be root-level.
> **Developer, 2026-09-30:** *"The specs are extremely important, so we can't outright delete the spec files, but
> there definitely needs to be some sort of root-level adjustment somewhere somehow."*
>
> So the target is **the way specs carry station information and the way every method consumes them**, not the
> spec files' existence. **Options to weigh, starting 2026-10-01. Nothing here is decided:**
> 1. **Fence off the GPS data inside each spec.** Move the station name, operator, program and dates into one
>    clearly labeled block, e.g. `GPS / climate-lookup only — never a cause`. Keep it out of `Settled:`,
>    `Significance:` and `Founding population:`.
> 2. **Make `Founding population:` a ruled field.** Each entry cites the ruling that sets it (`DR-19`/`DR-20`/…)
>    on geography and access, or reads `⏸️ not yet ruled`. It is never a free-text answer to "why were they here?"
> 3. **Change the template** (`C/Specs/_TEMPLATE.md`) to match 1 and 2, with `DR-19` stated inline.
> 4. **Change the copy instruction** in every consuming method (catalog, culture, robot culture, megasheet,
>    Division of Industry, ULM Phase 1) from "pull from Specs, don't re-derive" to "**pull everything except the
>    GPS block, and take founders only from a ruled field.**"
> 5. **A write-time check** to replace the rescinded hook (`.claude/hooks/deny-national-content.py`). It would flag
>    program acronyms, "since 19xx" and "[nation] station" in city files outside the GPS block.
>
> **Founder candidates by time zone:** `Post-War_Nations_Founder_Reference.md` (from the developer's map files). In the maps, the **USA, Canada, Italy, Russia and Belgium no longer exist**. **That is fine for now** (`DR-23`): the census and composition stay on pre-war nations, and post-war founders are identified, with the specs adjusted, at the **CST** stage.
>
> **Where everything is:** §8 (all three investigations), §2 (upstream sources), §3 (memory), §5 (cities needing
> rulings), §8c (suggested order of work).

**Created 2026-09-30, at the developer's direction.** Lists every known place where real-world station or operator
heritage was used as a reason for a Tepenian city's founding, character, identity, institutions or ties, along with
every upstream source that kept putting it back. **Goal: all of it permanently gone.** Each item is removed one at
a time and checked off only when its **Check** comes back clean.

**The rule** (`DEVELOPER_RULINGS_LOG.md` `DR-19`): a real station, and the nation or program that ran it, is a
**coordinate only**. A founding nation is legitimate only on **geography and access**, never on the station.
Physical inheritance (buildings, infrastructure, records) is admissible (`DR-7`).

**Status key:** ☐ open · ✅ verified gone (with the date checked) · ⏸️ waits on a developer ruling or a city revisit ·
🔒 must not be touched until a stated later stage.

numbers are as of 2026-09-30 and **drift as files change**, so re-run the item's **Check** before editing.
