This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal files when ready.

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in (see the two files above for full detail): Schirmacher Oasis, Queen Maud Land (~70°46'S, 11°44'E) — one of Antarctica's rare ice-free areas. Genuinely unique founding: coalesced from two separate settlements (Russian-inherited Novolazarevskaya Station, continuously operated since 1961; and the real, unoccupied Maitri site — no Indian exiles ever came to Tepenia — absorbed via Novolazarevskaya's own overflow population), later renamed from "Novolazarevskaya" down to "Lazar." Population: Census I 1,287,003H/1,333,316R (2,620,319 combined) — Tepenia's single largest city, ~46% bigger than #2. Census II 861,206H/942,771R (1,803,977). Composition: USA Primary; Germany/France/UK/Russia (founding heritage)/Brazil all roughly equal Significant-tier — unusually flat compared to most cities' single dominant Primary. Status: Damaged, partially operational — survived the war on sheer megacity scale rather than luck, terrain, or bedrock. Eastern terminus of Hwy 7; origin of the Belgrano Highway Extension.

**Faction:** "Grown Together" (standalone) — Lazar's fault line is old-coalesced-core vs. newer-expansion, not founder-vs-majority like every other city; a live, low-grade tension between those who remember what it took to grow two places into one and those for whom that's calcifying into gatekeeping.

**Flagged gaps going in:** timeline of coalescence (how many generations between founding and merger); whether Novolazarevskaya's real DROMLAN air-logistics role carried forward; whether the old dividing line still registers today, and whether it correlates with war-damage severity; relationship to Neumayer (nearest neighbor); demonym and notable figures both TBD; economy beyond a thin placeholder.

## The developer's vision

**A genuine highrise megacity** — tall, huge buildings, extending in every direction, visually confirming Lazar's status as Tepenia's largest city. Heavily active — people constantly moving to and from work and obligations — but quieter than the scale would suggest, thanks to the transit network. Multicolored lights and holographic advertisements fill the air, giving it unmistakable megalithic-city presence.

**Transit, confirmed:** maglev ground transit as the primary mode, electric transit as a secondary layer, plus transit elevated above street level. This combination is specifically why the city reads as quieter than its size and activity level would otherwise suggest.

**The dual-personality idea — open, not locked in.** The developer hadn't originally thought about Lazar having a "dual personality" beyond its already-established two-toned architecture (old Novolazarevskaya core vs. newer Maitri expansion), but is open to considering extending that split into a genuine dual character for the city as a whole, not just its skyline. Flagged for further development, not decided.

**The commercial/economic engine behind the holographic advertisements — genuinely unresolved.** The developer has no current answer for what's actually driving an economy large and visible enough to support this much commercial presence. This is explicitly flagged as needing real exploration, not a placeholder gap to quietly leave as-is — Lazar's current Economy & Industry section (oasis resources, subnet trade, a vague DROMLAN legacy) doesn't obviously justify the megacity commercial density just established.
