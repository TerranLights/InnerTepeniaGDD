## The developer's vision

A working city first — mining (quarries toward the South Pole direction, separate from Mirny's own similarly-directioned source), fabrication, manufacturing, and general industry now the clear majority of Division of Industry (revised from a research-first breakdown), with founding research heritage as a smaller but genuine minority. Large enough for a small arts/music community; most leisure time at bars/eateries/social establishments. Davis's libraries openly welcome books smuggled out of Upper Earth.
