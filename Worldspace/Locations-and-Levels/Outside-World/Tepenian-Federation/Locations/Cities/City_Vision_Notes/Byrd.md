## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Byrd is one of only three cities in Tepenia still functioning as a city at meaningful scale (alongside Concordia and Janbogo), the sole city in its own subnet, and already had an established "founded underground before it ever stood on the surface" identity — layered stratigraphy, with founding-era neighborhoods buried by generations of snow accumulation and the surface city a newer addition.

At the start of this session, Byrd's tiering had Japan at Primary (13.57%) ahead of the USA itself (11.43%) — Byrd's actual founding-operator nation. This was resolved first, via a hand-specified rotation and swap (see `Specs/Byrd.md`'s Founding Population Adjustment note for full detail), landing USA and Canada at Primary.

## The developer's vision

**Vast icefields in all directions, with huge warehouses as a landscape centerpiece and a massive trucking depot.** Giant elevators lead underground, which is where the real activity is. The underground is enormous — absolutely enormous. For what is essentially a comparatively small city, the scale is initially staggering, though it becomes obvious in hindsight given the established buried-city history. Huge mechanized fabrication plants, office buildings for import/export and dispatch, and a genuine sense of shared community.

**Follow-up asked:** given Byrd's isolation (grounded aviation, no direct Hwy 37 connection), where does the massive trucking depot's output actually go — north, east, or also a coastal/maritime angle given the city's Amundsen Sea position? **Answer:** two directions overland. North along Hwy 1, toward the Peninsula (in the direction of Palmer City and, beyond it, South America). East along Hwy 22, the Transcontinental Highway, which then connects onward to the South Pole (Amundsen Station) and the Zhongshan coast, reaching the rest of Tepenia from there.
