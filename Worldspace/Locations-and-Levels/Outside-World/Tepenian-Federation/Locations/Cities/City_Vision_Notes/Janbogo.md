## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in (see the two files above for full detail): Terra Nova Bay, Ross Sea coast (~74°37'S, 164°13'E). The Terra Nova Bay polynya stays open year-round, making Janbogo the only Ross Sea coastal city with true year-round maritime access. Zukelli's ruins are visible from Janbogo's waterfront, ~8km north. Founding: Unified Korea's primary Tepenian city, built on Jang Bogo Station infrastructure. Population: Census I 646,541H/663,970R (1,310,511 combined); Census II 490,564H/467,006R (957,570). Composition: China/USA co-Primary; Japan/Germany/Italy (founding operator heritage)/South Korea/Canada/Indonesia/Australia all Significant. Status: Damaged, partially operational — the Tepenian Federation's most important surviving external link, the last coastal city still actively supplying Concordia via Hwy 183. Culture: renowned teahouse culture, embodied by Majyao Bisyugota (relocated to Concordia's Aquarius district). The Janbogo diaspora in Concordia's Gemini district physically hosts the Janbogo subnet's own Arcanet nexus hardware.

**Faction:** "The Crossroads People" (shared with Sayowa, Esperanza, Casey, Princess Elisabeth) — Janbogo's specific angle is being the center everything converges on, not a hinge between things.

**Flagged gaps going in:** who's actually running Janbogo now; what Majyao's original teahouse looks like today; other cultural landmarks/notable figures beyond Majyao; why the subnet nexus sits in Concordia rather than Janbogo itself; the Sejong relationship pre-Split-Brain; whether Pink Lucy's migration route passed through here.

## The developer's vision

**Fashionable people filling insulated-but-vivid streets and interiors.** Clothing is genuinely insulated but still richly colored — a blend of Chinese and French design. Much of active daily life happens inside huge shielded indoor areas: brightly lit, bustling, full of people in bright Chinese-fusion outfits, shopping, browsing merchant stalls, general commercial activity. Outside, by contrast, is dark with very few people out.

**This directly confirms and scales up what was already established** in Section 9 (Architecture) and Section 11 (Fashion) — the outside-hostile/inside-warm design principle, and the layered fashion sensibility established early by the founding population (functional outer layer, expressive warm inner layer). The vision extends this from individual teahouses to genuinely large-scale shielded commercial districts.

**The design aesthetic** — a genuine Chinese-French-inspired fusion — is the city's own indoor-warmth style choice, developed independently rather than population-driven. Any real-world echo of Chinese or French design traditions is coincidental, not a demographic claim about who lives in Janbogo.

## The Zukelli question — resolved the same session

Immediately after the fashion/vision discussion, the developer raised (and then resolved) one of the project's longest-standing open questions: why was Zukelli totally obliterated in the Long Night War while Janbogo, only ~8km away on the same bay with comparable terrain, survived merely damaged?

**Terrain check first:** both sites sit in the same rocky coastal foothill terrain around Terra Nova Bay — no mountain range or major barrier between them, ruling out a geographic-protection explanation (and invalidating an old, incorrect "island vulnerability" angle that had been sitting in `TODO.md`, since neither city is an island).

**Weapon-scale check:** real-world blast-radius reference points (tactical nuclear/heavy conventional yields) show that a weapon sized to guarantee total destruction of one specific target, rather than maximum-radius area denial, would plausibly still leave a city 8km away with real-but-survivable secondary damage rather than annihilation — meaning the outcome doesn't require luck, just a weapon sized for its actual target.

**The developer's resolution:** Zukelli was sheltering multiple Upper Earth soldiers who had fully defected and gone AWOL, aided in hiding by both the local human and robot population. When the defectors' home military (nationality TBD) found them, it ordered a strike calibrated for total, absolute obliteration — both to guarantee the defectors' deaths and to send an unmistakable message to the rest of Tepenia that harboring Upper Earth fugitives would be met with total violence. Janbogo surviving nearby wasn't incidental — a message needs a living witness to carry it forward. The existing "Zukelli view" detail (Janbogo can see the ruins daily) is now understood as the deliberate, permanent point of that message, not just proximity.

Written into `Specs/Zukelli.md` (Current Status and Legacy sections, plus the Open Questions resolution), `Specs/Janbogo.md` (Geographic Basis and Notable Locations), and `Local_Cultures/Janbogo_Subnet/Janbogo.md`'s own Zukelli-view entry.

**Still open:** the defectors' nationality/military of origin, how many defected, exact timing within the war, whether Janbogo was able to help or warn in the moments before the strike, and whether the deterrent message actually worked on other cities afterward.
