## The developer's vision

**The industrial landscape:** Stretches of fabrication yards dominate the city, alongside residential areas where humans and robots live, sleep, commune, and share leisure time together. Advanced, futuristic forklifts haul materials constantly. Rothera makes a fairly good amount of things — not literally everything, but a broad general-purpose catalog for both home use and construction — though nothing it's specifically, famously known for within that catalog has been decided yet.

**Work rhythm:** Humans run 8–10 hour shifts; robots run 10–12 hour shifts. When the day ends, everyone stops at one of a variety of local bars and pubs. Humans drink alcohol. At night, humans sleep and robots recharge; in the morning everyone wakes (or comes back online) and does it again.

**Glitch-coolant — resolved into genuine Tepenia-wide canon, not just a Rothera detail.** Robots at these bars drink a specially-formulated coolant, mixed with a concoction of electrolytic compounds, producing a "glitching" effect analogous to human drunkenness. This has been written directly into `Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` as an established robot cultural institution (alongside siligel, plain coolant, and robot coffee), with a confirmed city-variation pattern:
- **Bohemian/cosmopolitan cities** (Janbogo, Zhongshan, Shirayuki) support a wide *variety* of glitch-coolant types — the robot equivalent of a sophisticated drinking culture offering beers, ales, wines, and spirits side by side.
- **Working-class robot settings** (Byrd, and Rothera itself) have a much more limited selection, but what's available produces a noticeably *stronger* effect — fewer choices, more potency, a direct blue-collar drinking culture rather than a refined one.

Rothera's own bar scene fits squarely in the working-class/limited-but-strong category — consistent with its whole "function over presentation" civic identity.
