# Princess Elisabeth — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this city — distinct from `Specs/Princess_Elisabeth.md` (established facts) and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Princess_Elisabeth.md` (32-section post-culture spec). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal files when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-04

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in (see the two files above for full detail): Utsteinen nunatak, Sør Rondane Mountains, Queen Maud Land. Halley subnet — but geographically closer to Dome Fuji than to its own subnet neighbors. **Status: Destroyed** — the real station survives extreme conditions only through active engineering (300 km/h gales), not natural shelter, so once its systems failed there was nothing passive to fall back on. Belgian founding (station opened 2009, the most recently built founding-era station), zero-emissions design as a lived civic value, not just inherited hardware. Population (Census II) 401,403H/365,359R/766,762 combined. Composition: USA/Japan co-Primary (the only Halley subnet city with two Primary-tier founding populations, reflecting genuine Atlantic + Indian Ocean immigration currents); Belgium (founding heritage) diluted to Notable. Identity: "Leaving No Mark, Meeting in the Middle" — a literal demographic/geographic crossroads that never resolved which larger cultural body it belongs to, and never turned that ambiguity into a defended identity either (unlike Sayowa).

**Factions:** The Crossroads People (second interwar) — "the crossroads nobody resolved," alongside Sayowa, Esperanza, Janbogo, and Casey.

**Flagged gaps going in:** notable figures limited to unnamed "straggling survivors"; demonym unresolved; exact circumstances of destruction open (with an already-flagged, undesigned candidate questline: restoring the ruined zero-emissions systems); the Ice Cold Buddhism/Dome Fuji pilgrimage connection unresolved; whether residents still connected the name to the actual Princess Elisabeth of Belgium; two placeholder holiday names; and a recurring census gap (missing from "Lost and Ruined Cities," same pattern as Marambio and Sejong).

## The developer's vision

**Vast wind farms and solar farms dominate the landscape**, enriching the established zero-emissions identity with real infrastructure: wind farms send electricity to parts of the city, and solar farms power heating systems that connect directly into the ground. Humans and robots shuttle back and forth maintaining this infrastructure and operating machinery that runs underground.

**A deliberately mysterious underground element.** Something significant is happening below ground — described as some sort of "mini-infrastructure" with complicated arrangements the developer genuinely can't make sense of, even in their own creative process. This isn't a gap to be filled in casually; it's confirmed as real and worth treating as a genuine narrative hook, but its specifics (what it does, how it relates to the surface energy systems, whether it connects to the already-flagged "restoring the zero-emissions systems" candidate questline) are all open.

**The developer's vision is spatially limited to this one area of the city** — attempting to picture other parts of Princess Elisabeth currently comes up blank. This is noted as a normal, honest limit, not a narrative fact about the rest of the city not existing; just marking how far this session's vision extends.
