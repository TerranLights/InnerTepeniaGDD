**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this city — distinct from `Specs/Bharati_TBD.md` (established facts) and `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Japanese_Diplomatic_Partition_cf_Bharati.md` (32-section post-culture spec). **Session date:** 2026-07-06

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: the only Tepenian city founded by deliberate Upper Earth diplomatic allocation (the Jeju-do Court) rather than organic station inheritance — Japan was assigned this unoccupied site specifically to prevent China's regional demographic pull from defaulting into a third claim in the Larsemann Hills cluster. At the start of this session, the standard population/GDP tiering method had ironically buried Japan at Significant tier (3.28%) behind China's Primary (19.05%) anyway — the exact outcome the diplomatic allocation was meant to prevent.

---

## The developer's vision

**Cleanliness. Clean, orderly streets.** There are art pieces (sculptures, murals, statues, constructed art, etc.) throughout the various districts, especially in the downtown region. There are people dressed very, very fashionably, but this isn't "model runway" fashion; this is Alternative / Alt-Rock / Alt-Grunge / Alt-Pop homemade fashion. Although there are very high-quality schools in Bharati, Soyuz, and Zhongshan, the schools in Bharati in particular are where people come from other cities across Tepenia to study, purely for the culture and the soul of the place. There's a massive local culture of people doing art and music on their own time in their own lives. It's very easy to make friends here.

**Follow-up asked:** is the art civically commissioned or grassroots? **Answer:** a genuine mix of both; which one dominates partly depends on the specific neighborhood and context.
