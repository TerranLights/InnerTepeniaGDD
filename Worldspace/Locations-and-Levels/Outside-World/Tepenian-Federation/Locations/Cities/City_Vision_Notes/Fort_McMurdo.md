## The developer's vision

The city's real mass is *presence*, not population or architecture — visitors immediately sense real business happening and real decisions being made that ripple outward and change what other cities can do. Dedicated comms stations coordinate supply/extraction/logistics, distinct from Janbogo's own physical Arcanet relay-nexus role for the subnet. This gravity is explicitly operational, not political — Fort McMurdo doesn't posture in Federation debates, it's just quietly indispensable, reinforcing (not replacing) its already-established "de facto capital" status.
