## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Dome Fuji is entirely robot (0 humans, 55,072 robots), nationally blended across every Tepenian city (not curated by any specific heritage, unlike Kunlun). One of Ice Cold Buddhism's two primary sacred sites — its population *is* the devotee community, confirmed as the reason the city still has any population at all. The existing `Local_Cultures` sheet already speculatively anticipated much of this session's vision in hedged language ("likely," "plausibly").

## The developer's vision

**Peace.** A Zen-like peace, almost like that of the interior of a Shinto holy shrine or a Buddhist monastery. There's a sense of spiritual connection to something — something larger than anybody, or even the sum of everybody. It's almost as if the air and sky itself is participating in meditation along with everybody who's actually here, almost as if the Southern Lights could simply stop moving, just hanging frozen in the sky, and it wouldn't strike anyone as "out of character." The disciples are wearing white robes, sparsely covering their bodies in order to commune with the cold.

**Follow-up asked:** does the sparse-robes-in-extreme-cold practice work because robots don't suffer frostbite/hypothermia the way humans would, or is there some other reason the robes stay minimal? **Answer:** primarily yes, that's the reason — though the developer is open to additional contributing reasons. Three were proposed and all three were kept as live possibilities for future design: (1) a real-world parallel to Tibetan Buddhist *tummo* ("inner fire") meditation, where monks meditate in extreme cold as a demonstration of mastery through discipline; (2) a literal-mechanical reading tied to the faith's own "superconducting conditions" theology, where minimal robes let a devotee's systems get closer to a spiritually significant cold threshold; (3) simple communal uniformity, the way monastic robes function in many real-world traditions.
