## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Mawson is the Mawson subnet's hub city, Australia's oldest continuously operating Antarctic station (since 1954), with an existing "The Name That Outlasted the Founders" identity — Australia founded the city and gave it its name, but China is the actual long-run demographic Primary (17.02%), with Australia sitting at Significant tier (8.29%), the same "founding nation buried behind China" pattern later confirmed at Shirayuki and Soyuz.

---

## The developer's vision

**Hospitality.** Comparing to the rest of Antarctica, in relative terms, this place is almost on the level of a resort town (to the extent that such a thing as a "resort town" can even exist in such a brutal and unforgiving environment like Antarctica). There are public libraries here. It is extremely easy to make friends here, and is a popular spot for newly-married human-robot couples to go on their honeymoon before returning and settling down in whatever city it is that they'll be living in. There's definitely culture here, and it's easy to make friends.

**Follow-up asked:** is Mawson a planned honeymoon destination, or something couples end up at incidentally? And is there a customary length of stay? **Answer:** genuinely a planned destination — Mawson has earned its honeymoon reputation over decades. No codified or customary length of stay, but it probably ranges somewhere around one to two weeks.
