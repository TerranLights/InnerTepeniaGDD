# Concordia — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this city — distinct from `Specs/Concordia.md` (established facts) and Concordia's own cultural sheets (`Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Concordia_Second_Interwar_Cultural_Sheet.md`, Second Interwar era; a post-war equivalent is still pending). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal files when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-06

*(Deliberately deferred from the rest of the City Vision Notes initiative — the developer's explicit direction throughout has been to "develop the rest of the country first," since Concordia is the game's primary present-day setting. This session is the actual, present-day/post-war vision — distinct from the already-completed Second Interwar cultural sheet, which covers the earlier, ascendant-era version of the city.)*

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Concordia is the last functioning major city in the Tepenian Federation, the primary game setting — not thriving, energy grid failing, high district tensions, no single faction in control, twelve zodiac-named districts (the zodiac naming itself is the developer's own out-of-fiction organizational scheme, not in-world). The player arrives directly into this instability.

---

## The developer's vision

**A city that's struggling, but surviving.** There's a sense of community here among people who wanted to see what a city can truly be — not a collection of buildings, not a smattering of offices, but an actual city. A true city.

**Follow-up asked:** does this feeling apply uniformly across all twelve districts, or does it vary given their already-established distinct characters (Aquarius's research focus, Libra's governance, Leo's festivals, the dome/outdoor divide)? **Answer:** the feeling applies across all twelve districts — every district genuinely shares the goal of Concordia being a true city. What divides them is not the goal but the method: each district holds a differing, sometimes flatly conflicting, belief system, viewpoint, and opinion on how to actually accomplish it. This reframes the established district tensions as a conflict over *path*, not over *end state* — a meaningfully different shape of conflict than a simple values clash.
