**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this location — distinct from `Specs/Amundsen_Station.md` (established facts) and `Local_Cultures/Amundsen_Station/Amundsen_Station.md` (32-section post-culture spec). **Session date:** 2026-07-06

*(Added "for the sake of total completion" — Amundsen Station isn't one of the 6 subnets' 35 cities, and is explicitly established as "a facility, not a city," but the developer wanted its own vision captured the same way regardless, alongside Concordia's.)*

---

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Amundsen Station is destroyed (Long Night War), the most significant ruins in Tepenia, DLC 1's setting. Politically neutral ground for inter-subnet disputes while it stood. Its established Division of Industry already framed the station's activity as ~60% Arcanet relay/infrastructure maintenance, ~30% Amundsen Tower operations, ~10% life support — explicitly not a conventional economy, since it produced no exports and had no permanent civic population in the way an actual city does.

---

## The developer's vision

**Naturally, a place of scientific, technical, and mechanical maintenance.** Consistent with, and confirming, the already-established facility character above — the work here was upkeep and vigilance rather than production.
