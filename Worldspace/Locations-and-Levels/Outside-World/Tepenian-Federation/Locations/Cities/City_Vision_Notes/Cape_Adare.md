## The developer's vision

Geographically vast but low-density — a big city with a small-town feel. Strongly community-driven. A heating-infrastructure "oasis" microclimate. Penguins occasionally kept as outdoor pets. A slow, unhurried pace of life and work. An acoustic-instrument music culture (guitars, violins, cellos, tagelharpas), developed independently out of the city's own mixed, no-single-tradition-dominant population and unhurried civic character. *(It happens to echo the real-world American folk subculture that plays Scandinavian instruments — a coincidence of convergent development, not an inheritance from any founding nation.)*
