## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in (see the two files above for full detail): King George Island, South Shetland Islands. Palmer subnet. **Status: Destroyed.** No highway access — 160-177km from the mainland, maritime/aviation only. Named after King Sejong the Great (creator of Hangul). Unusual founding: multiple exile communities (Korean, Argentine, Brazilian, Chilean, Chinese, Polish, Russian, Czech, Uruguayan) had pre-existing footholds on the same island simultaneously — fate of the non-Korean communities specifically left open. Population (Census I) 316,691H/328,142R/644,833 combined. Composition, as understood at the time of this 2026-07-04 session: China/USA co-Primary; Germany/France/UK/Italy/South Korea/Russia/Mexico/Brazil Significant (South Korea unusually retaining Significant status — strong founder retention); Argentina/Chile/Uruguay Notable (founding wave). *(Corrected 2026-07-13: China's co-Primary status turned out to be a methodology-violating entry — China appears nowhere else in the Palmer subnet, and its inclusion traced to real-world Great Wall Station's presence on King George Island, which this project's own population methodology explicitly excludes. Removed; USA is Sejong's sole Primary (21.65%). See `Specs/Sejong.md` and `Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md` for the full resolution.)* Identity: "A City Defined By Its Neighbors" — the city's own sense of itself sharpened rather than diluted by neighbor density; fault line is cooperative coexistence vs. competitive tension over the island's uniquely mild, desirable terrain.

**Factions:** Coherence vs. Contrast (second interwar) — Sejong's position paired against Juan Carlos: isolation gave Juan Carlos a settled, coherent institution (the tertulia); density gave Sejong a different civic art form instead — diplomatic/coordination skill as an actual industry, since knowing what you are here means constantly renegotiating the boundary with whoever's currently adjacent.

**Flagged gaps going in:** no notable figures; demonym unresolved; economy beyond "trade/transit hub" TBD; fate of the other King George Island national communities unresolved; the Sejong-Janbogo relationship (believed at the time to be "the two Korean Tepenian cities" — *corrected 2026-07-13: there are actually three, alongside {{currently-unnamed Korean city}} in the Mirny subnet, and Sejong's own Korean population has since diluted to its smallest Significant-tier nation, 5.79% — see `Specs/Sejong.md`*) never developed; whether the whole multinational coexistence experiment "worked" left open; two placeholder holiday names; a recurring census gap (Sejong missing from "Lost and Ruined Cities," same pattern as Marambio).

## The developer's vision

**A bustling "mini-city," something like Taipei at a much smaller scale.** People going about tasks and obligations, dense and vertical rather than sprawling — land constraints on King George Island forced Sejong to build up where it couldn't build out. Infrastructure is anchored into solid bedrock rather than ice, a genuine structural point of contrast with ice-shelf cities like Halley, which have to design around the ground itself moving. High-rise buildings cluster together, which also serves a practical function — natural diversionary wind protection, not just density for its own sake. Much of the cuisine is seafood-based.

**Resolves the economy gap:** fishing/maritime seafood harvest is confirmed as a real economic pillar of Sejong, alongside its already-established trade/transit-hub role — not just a culinary detail sitting on top of an unrelated economy.
