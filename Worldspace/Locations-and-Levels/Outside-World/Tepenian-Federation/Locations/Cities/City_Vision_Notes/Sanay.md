# Sanay — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this city — distinct from `Specs/Sanay.md` (established facts) and `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Sanay.md` (32-section post-culture spec). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal files when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-04

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in (see the two files above for full detail): Vesleskarvet nunatak, Ahlmannryggen range, Queen Maud Land (~71°40'S, 2°51'W) — bedrock foundation, unlike the ice-shelf cities. Founding: South African National Antarctic Expedition (SANAE) heritage, operational since 1997 (SANAE IV); the only major South African community in Tepenia. Population: Census I 231,576H/232,093R (463,669 combined); Census II 145,798H/129,319R (275,117 combined). Composition: Germany Primary, UK/Brazil Significant, South Africa itself only Notable-tier. Status: Damaged, partially operational. Established this same session, just before this vision pass: Sanay hosts the Halley subnet's actual Arcanet relay nexus (built by Halley's own residents' labor); Sanay is, alongside Belgrano, one of the two receiving ports for South African freighter shipments, trucked onward to Halley via Hwy 7.

**Faction:** "Competence Without Commentary" — Sanay's specific angle is the refusal of exceptionalism: it took real structural damage and actively rejects any narrative framing its bedrock survival as luck or specialness worth talking about.

**Flagged gaps going in:** no notable figures; exact extent/location of war damage across the city; Sanay's specific social/economic role relative to its damaged-but-functioning neighbors; whether any Sanay-origin diaspora exists in Concordia.

## A structural correction made during this session

The existing Local_Cultures file characterized Sanay throughout (Architecture, Cuisine, Fashion, Music, Arts, Sensory Profile) as **small, quiet, and modest** — language like "small population," "small community doing upkeep work," "not much room for aesthetic ambition." This directly conflicted with the developer's vision of a huge, bustling, industrial port city. Clarified by the developer: Sanay isn't necessarily one of Tepenia's largest cities by population (Lazar holds that title) — but it is a **major, major shipping and transportation hub** by function, and the tone throughout the file needed to reflect that economic weight rather than undersell it as a quiet backwater.

**Fixed the same session:** Architecture, Cuisine, Fashion, Music, Arts & Aesthetics, and Sensory Profile all rewritten in `Local_Cultures/Halley_Subnet/Sanay.md` to reflect a genuinely busy, industrious working port, without inflating its actual population figures. A few smaller word-level fixes elsewhere in the file ("quiet, unglamorous" → "hardworking, unglamorous"; "small, practical community" → "practical, hardworking community").

## The developer's vision

**Endless shipyards, a huge port city.** Humans and robots work hard at what can appear to be seemingly all hours of the day. Industrialized, gritty. Truckyards and warehouses take up small sections of the city. Atop the high rocky cliffs sit the major trucking/shipping depots and the business district handling import/export. Along the cliffside and across the expanse are stretches of residential, commercial, and leisure districts where people spend their time after work. Ship horns periodically pierce the atmosphere and echo across the city.

**The logistics chain, clarified:** Sanay imports raw materials and dried food items from South Africa via freighter, keeps whatever share it needs to maintain its own city, and trucks the rest out along Hwy 7 — either directly to nearby cities, or onward to Troll, where its airfield flies goods to more remote locations across Tepenia.

**The Arcanet nexus** is semi-distributed across several points along the upper clifftops, woven into the same terrain as the trucking depots and business district, rather than sitting apart in an isolated facility.

**Work schedule:** essentially round-the-clock rotating crews, oriented around freighter arrival and departure times rather than a fixed day/night schedule.

**Human-robot labor division:** roughly equal across the docks, truckyards, and business district — not perfectly symmetrical, though. Robots tend to hold slightly better positions, a quiet reflection of Tepenia being their country first. This isn't resentful on either side: robots at Sanay hold a genuine, specific appreciation for the humans working alongside them, aware they gave up entire livelihoods back in their Upper Earth homelands to be here. That appreciation shows up as real, unforced good treatment day to day.

*(Generalized 2026-07-05:)* This turned out not to be a Sanay-specific dynamic — the developer confirmed it's the default baseline pattern across Tepenia as a whole, characterized as "egalitarian, skewed in favor of robots." Written into `Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md` as a project-wide fact, alongside the related, separately-confirmed exception: Kunlun and Dome Fuji forbid human presence entirely, purely as a protective safety measure against fatal altitude/cold combinations, not from any anti-human sentiment.

**The SANAE research heritage's current visibility:** genuinely undecided — the developer isn't sure whether/how the founding scientific legacy still shows up in the now heavily industrial port city. Left open.
