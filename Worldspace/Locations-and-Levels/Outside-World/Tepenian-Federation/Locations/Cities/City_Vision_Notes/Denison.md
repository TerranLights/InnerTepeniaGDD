## The developer's vision

The city functions as one continuous, interlinked structure rather than separate buildings — comprehensively joined throughout, not just occasional covered connections the way Zukelli/Janbogo use them — with a handful of landmark structures recognizable even at a distance through blowing snow. The most extreme wind-engineering identity of any Tepenian city.
