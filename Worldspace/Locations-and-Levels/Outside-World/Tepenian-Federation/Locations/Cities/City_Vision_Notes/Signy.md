## The developer's vision

**Fishing dominates — resolves the "economy beyond research" gap.** Floating extension-platforms expand the island's reach out to sea wherever the geography allows it, supporting a major, central fishing economy alongside the established biological research. Time moves slowly here; nobody's in a real rush to do anything, consistent with the established wildlife-cycle-paced, unhurried civic rhythm.

**A genuine two-island city structure, using real South Orkney geography.** Signy is not one island but two working together: the smaller **Signy Island** itself hosts the one shipping dock (on its east side) and a substantial radio comms setup, while the much larger **Coronation Island** — connected by a high bridge — is where most of the actual residential living and leisure infrastructure is built. Daily rhythm: humans and robots wake (or come back online) and travel south to the docking island to fish for the day, returning in the afternoon to spend downtime on Coronation Island.

**The comms setup — confirmed as radio, not Arcanet, resolving a potential tension cleanly.** Signy's substantial local communications infrastructure is specifically radio transmission, since internet/wifi-style digital networking (Arcanet) doesn't reach that far. This doesn't contradict Signy's established "weakest Arcanet link in the Federation" status — it reinforces it. Signy relies on old-fashioned radio precisely *because* modern network connectivity physically can't reach this far out.
