# Soyuz — Developer Vision Notes

**Purpose:** A running record of the developer's own creative vision for this city — distinct from `Specs/Soyuz.md` (established facts) and `Local_Cultures/Mirny_Subnet/Soyuz.md` (32-section post-culture spec). This file captures open questions asked, answers given, and anything newly established through direct conversation, so it can be referenced later and folded into the more formal files when ready.

**Session date:** 2026-07-06

## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Soyuz sits in the Larsemann Hills / Prydz Bay cluster alongside Zhongshan and Bharati, named for the Soyuz spacecraft program (a deliberate founding choice honoring Russian space-age achievement rather than the inherited Progress Station name). Russia is the genuine founding-operator nation, though — like the rest of the cluster — its population had settled into a China-Primary composition under the standard population/GDP tiering method.

---

## The developer's vision

**Industrial activity. Lots of it.** Raw materials trucked in from Davis are fabricated into things that people can use. This is one of the cities in the country (among a handful of others) where they make the "fabrication-synthesis chambers" (pending a proper name) that actually create robots. The chambers are shipped across the country — from Soyuz and from the other cities that craft them — so that robots can be "born" (or, more accurately, built) in any city in Tepenia. There's a deep, deep sense of national pride and patriotism in Soyuz that's hard to find a counterpart for in the majority of Tepenian cities.

**Follow-up asked:** how does the fabrication process actually work, mechanically? **Answer:** not entirely sure of the technical process, but the rough sketch draws on the film *Ex Machina* — both for the chamber's aesthetic and for the robot brain itself, described as a "glowy-blue" nano-architecture "gel" brain. There's absolutely a waiting period, since constructing a robot takes real time. Critically: on Upper Earth, humans order robots built to their own specifications — a commercial relationship. In Tepenia, this inverts: robots make each other according to their own interests, but still in the human form/shape, since they see no reason to try to "change" what evolution has already produced — the human form and figure has withstood the test of time in basically every possible earthly environment.
