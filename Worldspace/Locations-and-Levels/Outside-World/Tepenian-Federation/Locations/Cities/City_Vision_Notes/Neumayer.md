## The developer's vision

**Engineering labs and outdoor field laboratories, testing technology that benefits the rest of Tepenia.** Humans and robots work with great focus inside highly developed engineering facilities as well as field-testing sites — not just measuring and observing, but actively developing and field-testing technologies that get deployed elsewhere in Tepenia. This gives Neumayer's established "precision engineering export" identity a more concrete, ongoing R&D-testbed function, rather than just abstract expertise.

**Work culture is intense but sustainably managed, not burnout-driven.** Scientists, engineers, and technicians work in alternating/overlapping shifts specifically so nobody burns out, even though the facility itself runs at what can look like all hours of the day.

**Resolves the "thin music" gap directly.** After work, people head to bars and music establishments — dominated by **Electronic, Metal, and Digital-Industrial** genres. This is a strong, thematically coherent answer: machinery-adjacent, precision-and-technology-coded music genres fit Neumayer's whole civic identity far better than the previous "no dedicated civic musical tradition" gap did.

**Climate/atmospheric data survival — a lean, not a decision.** The developer's instinct is that Neumayer probably did hold onto its centuries-long data record, but this isn't locked in yet — logged as an open lean, not settled canon.

**Glitch-coolant variant — genuinely undecided.** Given the established Tepenia-wide framework (bohemian cities get wide variety, working-class cities get fewer but stronger options), Neumayer was floated as a possible third category — *precisely calibrated* glitch-coolant, where the appeal is exact, reproducible, engineered effects rather than variety or raw potency, mirroring the same rigor Neumayer applies to everything else. The developer doesn't have an answer for this yet; left fully open.
