## The developer's vision

**Troll is a genuine intermodal freight hub, not just a strategic aviation asset — resolves the economy gap outright.** Raw materials, goods, and supplies imported from Africa land at Sanay (or Belgrano), get trucked overland via Hwy 7 to Troll, and are then flown out via Troll Airfield to other locations across Tepenia. This reframes the whole DLC 5 airfield-control stakes: it's not just about symbolic outside-world access anymore, it's about controlling a major share of Tepenia's actual real-economy import/distribution network.

**The Dome Fuji connection — a genuinely significant cross-subnet finding, mechanism now resolved.** Together with a smaller, less advanced airstrip at or near Soyuz (Mawson subnet, itself receiving imports via the city of Mawson), Troll's freight network flew supplies directly into Dome Fuji — one of Tepenia's most isolated, nearly Arcanet-less interior cities. Two separate, direct aviation routes converging on the same city, not a relay chain. Written directly into `Specs/Dome_Fuji.md` and `Specs/Soyuz.md` (which gained its own airstrip as a new established fact in the process). **Duration resolved 2026-07-05, left deliberately loose:** the route began sometime after Dome Fuji's own pilgrimage resettlement and ran until the Long Night War — no exact start date pinned, since it can't have predated the resettlement (nobody was there to supply beforehand) but the timing beyond that stays open by design.

**Troll is a major working-class city with above-average national spending power**, thanks to the freight-hub economy. This has a direct, confirmed consequence for the established glitch-coolant framework (see `Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`): Troll stays in the working-class, limited-selection/strong-effect category (like Byrd and Rothera) rather than shifting toward bohemian variety, but what's available is noticeably higher-quality than the national standard — not ornate or extravagant, just better-made. Written directly into the canon file as a new sub-variant.
