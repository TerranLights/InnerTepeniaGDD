## The developer's vision

Overwhelmingly residential in physical footprint — housing dominates, plus casual/leisurely business (restaurants, general leisure) and modest public gathering spaces — with not much visibly happening day to day. A genuinely decent, quiet place to raise a family. This sits alongside (not replacing) Scott's established technical/research economic base — the work is real but contained, distinct from the city's lived, residential texture — and reinforces its established Fort McMurdo counterpart-city contrast: quiet/residential vs. loud/operational next door.
