## Facts and factions presented at the start of this session

Recap of what was already established going in: Sayowa sits on East Ongul Island (Prince Harald Coast), the westernmost city in the Mawson subnet, built on JARE (Japanese Antarctic Research Expedition) heritage. At the time this session began, Sayowa's file still described itself as "Tepenia's primary Japanese demographic presence" — a claim already known to be stale following the same-session Larsemann Hills demographic rework, which had made Bharati (not Sayowa) the genuine Japan-Primary city.

---

## The developer's vision

**Partially industrialized, partially residential.** There's a major fabrication industry here, along with a huge trucking & dispatch industry — things get made as well as routed here. There is a semi-developed leisure culture here, but it's very secondary to the industrialization.

**A factual challenge raised mid-session:** the developer questioned whether Sayowa is genuinely on an island, noting Google Maps appeared to show it near the mainland coast. Verified via web research: Syowa Station is confirmed to sit on East Ongul Island, approximately 4km offshore from the Antarctic mainland — the existing lore was correct; the Google Maps impression was likely a rendering/zoom-level artifact given how close the island sits to the coast.

**A follow-up correction to the highway network:** given Sayowa is now established as a genuinely developed industrial/residential city, the developer determined the "three-way crossing" (where Hwy 4, Hwy 7-ext, and Hwy 37 converge) should be *near* Sayowa rather than physically *in* it, with a large connecting road (**the Sayowa Spur**) linking the city to that junction (now named **the Sayowa Junction**) — rather than the highway crossing sitting directly downtown.
