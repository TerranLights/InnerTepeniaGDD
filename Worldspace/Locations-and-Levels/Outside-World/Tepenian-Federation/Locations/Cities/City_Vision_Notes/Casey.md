## The developer's vision

A genuinely bustling city, not just a waystation, centered on **Splinters** — on the old peninsula, a full city block, Tepenia's largest/most famous bar, with a stage, dance floor, and flank/rear seating, hosting a massive Classic Rock/Jazz/Blues/Acoustic Folk music scene famous along the whole Dumont d'Urville Sea and Indian Ocean coastline. Named after the real, historical "Splinters" bar at the pre-exile Casey Station, verified via web search and rediscovered in-world through old records.
