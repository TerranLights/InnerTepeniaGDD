## The developer's vision

A city built as its own windbreak — dense buildings in a scattered, concentric formation 2-3 layers deep, a heavily fortified/smooth-shaped outer ring shedding the katabatic wind, sheltering huge industrial yards inside. These yards process quarried raw materials from the south (toward the continental interior/South Pole direction, not shared with Davis's own nearer quarries) into construction materials for the eastern highways and infrastructure-maintenance machinery, giving Mirny's subnet-hub status real industrial teeth, not just communications. Residential space sits inside these same inner buildings alongside the industrial yards, wherever wind isn't as much of an issue, rather than a separate residential layer. Entertainment is minimal, mostly rough bars — a rough city with a genuine, unforced sense of pride and kinship.
