**Why this exists:** the 2026-07-12 No National Stereotypes sweep (see `TODO.md`) fixed the surface-level violations (stereotype language, "inherited from" phrasing) but did not verify the *demographic facts underneath* each city's cultural writing. Re-deriving Zhongshan's culture on 2026-07-13 surfaced a real, structural bug: Zhongshan's founding story cites "proximity to the former Russian Progress Station" to explain its Russian population — but Progress Station is Soyuz's own founding site, a separate city, and Soyuz's population was independently re-derived on 2026-07-06 to be **South Korean**-Primary (34.62%), not Russian (Russia sits at just 6.85% there, its smallest Significant-tier nation). Soyuz is already flagged for a Korean rename. Zhongshan's founding-story prose never got updated to reflect that, and its whole "Sino-Russian synthesis" framing was built on the stale assumption. This is a *specific bug class* — a city's founding/cultural narrative citing a real-world station's real-world nationality (GPS-only, per the binding rule) instead of what the fiction has actually, currently established that population to be — and it may not be limited to Zhongshan.

**Governing principle for every check below** (see `feedback_no_national_stereotypes` memory, refined 2026-07-13): a city's *founding station operator* is a GPS coordinate, never a cause. A city's *actual, current population composition* (its own Section 1 / tier table) is a real, legitimately causal fact — culture follows population, never the founding flag. Check every causal claim in a city's culture writing against that city's **own current population table**, not against inherited assumptions, comparisons to neighbors, or the real-world identity of whatever station it's built on.

**Full reusable methodology, written up 2026-07-13 after resolving the Larsemann Hills cluster:** see `Cities/Founding_Nation_Bug_Investigation_Methodology.md` for the complete investigation checklist, the three-way GPS/population/temperament distinction, the two named sub-bug-patterns (unsupported tier annotations; stale demographic assumptions in creative sections), the README-is-a-stale-concatenation gotcha, and the precedent index. Apply that process to every city below rather than re-deriving it.

**What "checked" means for each city below, in brief (full detail in the methodology doc above):**
1. Pull the city's own Population & Composition table (Specs file) — confirm what tier/percentage each nation actually holds *today*.
2. Read the city's founding story and "deeper cultural apparatus" (architecture, cuisine, fashion, music, arts/crafts, social contract) — flag any claim that traces a cultural detail to a nation that isn't backed by that city's own current table, or that cites a neighboring city's/station's real-world nationality instead of this city's own numbers.
3. Fix using the corrected framework: circumstance (terrain, isolation, the specific problem this population had to solve, historical turning points) explains *how* a practice takes its specific form; the city's own tracked population explains *whose* inheritance it genuinely is. Never let a founding-station-operator fact do either job.
