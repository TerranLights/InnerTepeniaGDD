## 3b · SEJONG — the "Quer-" round (developer request, 2026-10-03)

The developer asked to explore Spanish place-names beginning "Quer-" (seeded by *Querencia*), and asked whether it is spelled "Qüerencia". **It is not: the word is *querencia*.** Spanish uses the diaeresis only in *güe*/*güi*; the u after q is silent ("que" = /ke/). The one historical use of "qüe/qüi" (old texts, e.g. "qüestión") marks a PRONOUNCED u, so "Qüerencia" would be read "kweh-". English readers will say "kwer-" for the correct spelling; the Spanish sound is **keh-REN-see-ah**. *(Sources: es.wiktionary "querencia", es.wikipedia "Diéresis" and "Ortografía de la lengua española".)* **Option for later:** the naming-drift guideline's orthographic-simplification type (Zukelli ← Zucchelli) would allow a Tepenian respelling **"Kerencia"**, which English readers would say correctly. Not ruled.

The prefix index (es.wiktionary `Especial:PrefixIndex?prefix=quer`) gives a short list; the honest result is that **Querencia is far the strongest**.
