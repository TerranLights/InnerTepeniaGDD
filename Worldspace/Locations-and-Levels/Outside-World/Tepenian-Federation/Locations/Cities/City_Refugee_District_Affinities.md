# City → Concordia District Refugee Affinities

**Rewritten 2026-07-09.** The previous version of this file predates both the full 13-district two-stage deep-dive pass (`Districts/Deep_Dives/`) and the completed 35-city Megasheet project (`City_Megasheets/`). It was wiped and rebuilt from scratch rather than patched.

**Primary source, by explicit direction:** each city's own Megasheet (`City_Megasheets/[Subnet]/[City]/README.md`) — the full, concatenated synthesis + extrapolation + cross-reference for that city, representing the deepest and most current material this project has on what each city's population is actually like. The `City_Origin_Factions_*.md` files were consulted as a secondary cross-check, but wherever a Megasheet's own established character (Enneagram read, "what it feels like," cross-reference findings) differed from or sharpened the faction-file framing, the Megasheet took precedence. Several picks below meaningfully changed from an earlier draft once the actual Megasheets were read in full rather than the lighter faction-summary layer — see the per-city reasoning for specifics.

**Scope:** All 35 Tepenian cities except Concordia (the destination, not a source) and Amundsen Station (excluded per developer direction — a fixed, specialized station complement, not a source of ordinary civilian refugee flow the way the other 35 are).

**Method:** For each city, "top 3" reflects which three Concordia districts its own established Megasheet character — psychology, values, social organization, Enneagram read — would make it most likely to gravitate toward on relocation. Per project law (`feedback_no_national_stereotypes`), founding nation is never the reasoning; only established cultural identity is. This top-3 is deliberately a **Stage 1 read** — each city's own pre-war, ~250-year Second Interwar Period culture, since that's the enduring value system a refugee actually carries with them, not the specific Stage 2 wound their *destination* district may have suffered.

**A separate, explicit exception: Stage 2 overrides.** Three existing canon claims about specific cities' refugee destinations (Scorpio for the four destroyed/ruined cities; Gemini for Zukelli specifically; the Hub for Sayowa) are Stage 2 facts — about what actually happened after the war, not about pre-war cultural affinity. Checking each one against its city's own Stage 1 Megasheet character: Hub-for-Sayowa turns out to be genuinely Stage-1-grounded too (Sayowa's own pre-war crossroads identity already predicts it, no override needed). But Scorpio-for-the-four-destroyed-cities and Gemini-for-Zukelli are not — none of those four cities' own pre-war cultures are Scorpio-coded, and Zukelli's own pre-war culture isn't Gemini-coded either. Those two are genuine Stage 2 overrides: a trauma-driven institutional pull sitting on top of, not replacing, each city's real Stage 1 cultural home. They're kept out of the ranked top-3 below and documented separately in their own section, so the table stays a clean, consistent Stage 1 read throughout.

Two cities (Kunlun, Dome Fuji) are flagged as lower-confidence, atypical cases — both are curated, largely-robot devotional/vocational populations rather than organic home cities, so "where their refugees would settle" is a genuinely softer question than for the other 33. Vostok is flagged separately — its own Megasheet is honest that most of its content is proposed extrapolation rather than settled fact.

## Stage 2 Overrides — Where Trauma or Circumstance Redirects the Stage 1 Picture

Everything above is a Stage 1 read: each city's own pre-war cultural character. But two existing canon claims about specific refugee destinations are Stage 2 facts — about what actually happened after the war — and neither is explained by the affected cities' own pre-war culture. They're documented here, separately, rather than folded into the ranked top-3 above, so it stays clear these are trauma-driven or circumstantial pulls sitting *on top of* each city's real cultural home, not a replacement for it.

**Override 1 — Scorpio, for all four destroyed/ruined cities (Belgrano, Palmer City, Zukelli, Casey).** Fixed by existing canon (`District_Canon_Reference.md`'s Scorpio Development Notes: these four cities all identify Scorpio as a top-2 refugee destination). Checked individually against each city's own pre-war Megasheet culture: Belgrano is disciplined/functional (Virgo-coded), Palmer City is glamour/felt-experience (Leo-coded), Zukelli is food/hospitality/warmth (Cancer-coded), Casey is festive-function (Leo-coded) — **none of the four had anything resembling Scorpio's confrontational-transformation culture before the war.** The pull toward Scorpio isn't a values match at all; it's that the Archive of Final Confessions is the one Concordia institution actually built to receive mass-casualty civilizational grief, and these four communities are the only ones carrying that specific kind of loss (total, sudden, unprocessable destruction). This is a trauma-driven institutional pull layered on top of each city's real Stage 1 home, not a cultural affinity in the same sense as the rest of this file.

**Override 2 — Gemini, for Zukelli specifically.** Also fixed by existing canon (Gemini's own Development Notes: Janbogo-city and Zukelli survivors converge there). Zukelli's own pre-war culture isn't Gemini-coded any more than it's Scorpio-coded — its Gemini pull is purely circumstantial: Zukelli's own testimony is uniquely valuable source material for Gemini's data-archaeology recovery work (recovering what the Great Corruption took), and Zukelli refugees physically converge with Janbogo-city's own refugees at that same subnet-nexus location. Worth noting Janbogo-city's own Gemini placement, unlike Zukelli's, needed no override — see its own entry below.

**A third, weaker possibility, not locked canon like the other two — flagged here rather than asserted:** Janbogo-city's own Megasheet frames its post-war character around witness-trauma specifically — a city deliberately kept alive to watch Zukelli's destruction and carry the warning forward, with an internal, unresolved divide between residents who keep an informal vigil at the waterfront and those who've made quiet peace with not looking. It's plausible that the "vigil-keeping" subset of Janbogo-city's own refugees would be specifically drawn toward Scorpio's confrontation-with-loss culture in a way the rest of Janbogo-city's population wouldn't be. This is my own inference from the Megasheet's material, not an existing Development Notes claim the way the other two overrides are — worth considering, not worth treating as settled.

## Per-City Reasoning

*Note: reasoning below cites each city's own Megasheet directly. Where a pick changed from an earlier faction-summary-only draft, that's flagged explicitly.*

### Halley Subnet

**Lazar** — Tops Taurus/Hub/Cancer. The Megasheet's real throughline isn't diversity for its own sake — it's endurance and accommodation without ever building a defended narrative around it: two settlements coalescing, then five roughly-equal nations, with no single default culture ever imposed. That genuine lack of one template (not a performed cosmopolitanism, an actually-lived one) points to the Hub as much as it does to Taurus' own "old-core institutional memory, never gone dark" continuity thread. Cancer picks up the city's demonstrated comfort absorbing difference without friction.

**Halley** — Tops Aquarius/Libra/Virgo. *Revised from an earlier Sagittarius-leaning draft.* The Megasheet's real civic instinct is trading tangible control (giving away the Arcanet nexus, forgoing fixed docks) for symbolic or naming credit, and treating relocation as an ongoing civic conversation rather than a settled inheritance — Aquarius' comfort with provisional, revisable premises fits first, with Libra picking up the formal/symbolic-authority-consciousness and Virgo the underlying adaptive labor. Dropped Sagittarius: Halley is described as *dependent* on Belgrano/Sanay resupply, not frontier-self-reliant.

**Neumayer** — Tops Capricorn/Virgo/Libra. "Measured, Not Debated" — precision as near-religious civic virtue, designing the Amundsen Tower's and the robot-fabrication chambers' own schematics without public credit, responding to that with meticulous internal documentation rather than grievance. Capricorn's disciplined achievement-culture fits first; Virgo's uncredited-but-meticulous competence is arguably an even closer match to the specific "did the work, someone else got the name" throughline.

**Princess Elisabeth** — Tops Hub/Virgo/Gemini. *Revised — Virgo now outranks Pisces.* The Megasheet frames this city around total, actively-maintained dependency (zero-emissions, no passive advantage) and a genuinely unresolved demographic crossroads (USA/Japan co-Primary) that never became a defended identity — a purer, more passive version of Sayowa's own crossroads pride, hence Hub first. Its destruction via one severed connection point (rather than brute force) reflects real off-grid engineering-upkeep culture, which fits Virgo better than the dissolution-adjacent Pisces pick from the earlier draft.

**Belgrano** — Tops Virgo/Aries/Capricorn. *Revised — Scorpio moved to Stage 2 Overrides, below.* Belgrano's own pre-war (Second Interwar Period) culture is disciplined, function-over-comfort, Air Force-descended — "the archive is a duty roster," a maintained system checked on a schedule, not a passion project. That's close to Virgo's own unsupported, unglamorous maintenance culture first. The Rastra-inventing mechanic subculture supports Aries' hands-on toughness second and Capricorn's production-achievement third. None of this is Scorpio-coded on its own pre-war terms — see the Overrides section for why Scorpio still matters here.

**Abowasa** — Tops Taurus/Cancer/Libra. "Intimacy, not institution" — a genuinely jointly-founded city (two real, separate stations ~200m apart) where household-scale negotiation has run for generations. Its actual Enneagram read (Thinking/Compliant/Positive Outlook — the only fully unique profile in the whole 36-location set, shared only with Dome Fuji) suggests things resolved comfortably rather than through constant effortful diplomacy, softening but not undoing the Libra pick.

**Troll** — Tops Aries/Capricorn/Sagittarius. Confirmed directly: an identity that has shifted almost entirely onto the airfield itself as its structural and psychological center, genuinely contested (Instinctive/Assertive/Reactive — "besieged," not calmly self-contained). Aries' chokepoint-as-leverage logic fits first, with Capricorn's working-class achievement-prosperity and Sagittarius' aviation-frontier character as secondary fits.

**Sanay** — Tops Virgo/Taurus/Capricorn. One of the clearest Virgo matches in the entire set: physically hosts the subnet's own Arcanet relay nexus while Halley keeps the naming credit, and its post-war rebuilding priority was explicitly function-over-sentiment. "Competence Without Commentary" stated almost verbatim.

### Palmer Subnet

**Esperanza** — Tops Cancer/Gemini/Hub. Confirmed and strengthened: Esperanza was chartered on one absolute founding principle — that the children of people who sacrificed everything for exile deserved to be looked after — with robots as the primary at-home caregivers. About as clean a Cancer match as exists anywhere in this project. Its now-lost gateway/arrivals function (destroyed early in the war) gives Gemini and Hub their secondary claims.

**Sejong** — Tops Libra/Gemini/Capricorn. Confirmed directly: diplomatic and coordination skill as an actual civic industry, born of genuine necessity (a dozen national communities on one small island), with a coexistence experiment that succeeded functionally while remaining emotionally unresolved — precisely Libra's own territory.

**Marambio** — Tops Capricorn/Aquarius/Scorpio. *Substantially revised.* The Megasheet's real emphasis is dual-mode shipyard-and-airport industrial throughput and explicitly *functional, not self-reflective* civic energy — closer to Capricorn than the frontier-storytelling Sagittarius pick from the earlier draft. A genuinely striking detail — human dockworkers in relationships with robot coworkers, some having relocated specifically because robot society treated them better than human society had — gives Aquarius' idealistic-reinvention culture a real secondary claim. The paleontological deep-time archive still supports a (now tertiary) Scorpio fit.

**Juan Carlos** — Tops Libra/Leo/Aquarius. *Libra and Leo swapped from the earlier draft.* The Megasheet reveals the city's archive/records function as its single most consequential trait — quiet, ordinary administrative work that turned out to be the seed of Tepenia's entire institutional memory, and the specific reason Upper Earth destroyed it. That legitimacy-and-precedent-obsessed function is more Libra-coded than the tertulia's performative argument-culture itself, which still earns Leo a strong second place.

**Palmer City** — Tops Leo/Libra/Cancer. *Revised — Scorpio moved to Stage 2 Overrides, below.* The Megasheet's actual pre-war culture is unambiguously glamour and felt experience — jazz, rockabilly, electroswing, casinos, a whole civic economy built on hospitality-as-sovereignty. That's Leo, first and clearly. The founding-city's own archive-as-claim-to-legitimacy obsession (precedence as evidence for a position about what Tepenia is) gives Libra a genuine second-place claim, with Cancer picking up the city's universal-belonging civic myth third. None of this is Scorpio-coded on its own pre-war terms — see the Overrides section for why Scorpio still matters here.

**Rothera** — Tops Capricorn/Virgo/Sagittarius. *Capricorn now leads.* The Megasheet frames Rothera's survival as a genuine, stated source of pride — deliberate decentralization and engineering resilience, not luck — which is real achievement-pride (Capricorn) rather than Virgo's usual self-effacing modesty, though the underlying distributed-infrastructure competence still earns Virgo second place.

**Signy** — Tops Sagittarius/Virgo/Taurus. *Pisces dropped.* Confirmed: total self-sufficiency, a wildlife-and-weather civic rhythm, genuine peace with chronic isolation. Nothing in the Megasheet supports a Pisces-adjacent dissolution or vice angle — Signy's isolation reads as patient and content, not underworld-flavored, so Virgo (patient, self-managing competence) and Taurus (small-scale, intimate stability) replace it.

**Port Lockroy** — Tops Taurus/Cancer/Virgo. *Scorpio dropped for Virgo.* "A city that never demolishes its own history" — the communal stone wall, added to by generations who'll never see it finished, is the city's own stated emotional throughline: quiet, humble, ongoing, unglamorous labor. That's Virgo, not Scorpio's confrontational transformation. Taurus and Cancer (heirloom culture, lived memorial tradition) remain strongly confirmed.

### Janbogo Subnet

**Cape Adare** — Tops Taurus/Cancer/Libra. The Megasheet shows a slower, warmer city than the "continuity over advocacy" framing alone suggested — small-town intimacy despite massive scale, precedence and historical weight as genuine pride, penguins treated as neighbors. Its Enneagram triple (Feeling/Compliant/Positive Outlook) is shared with Zukelli and Mawson, both of which lean Cancer — hence Cancer's promotion to second place here, with Libra picking up the precedent/legitimacy-of-record angle third.

**Janbogo (city)** — Tops Gemini/Leo/Libra. *Revised — the Scorpio witness-trauma pull is real but belongs in Stage 2 Overrides, below, not the ranked Stage 1 top-3.* Gemini is fixed by canon, and also genuinely Stage-1-grounded independent of that: Janbogo's own pre-war identity — cosmopolitan centrality, "the place where everything happened," the largest and most-connected node in its subnet — is already the same function Gemini district was built to model. Its cultural centrality gives Leo a real second-place claim, with its historic political weight giving Libra third.

**Zukelli** — Tops Cancer/Leo/Taurus. *Revised — both Scorpio and Gemini moved to Stage 2 Overrides, below.* Zukelli's own pre-war culture centered entirely on food, hospitality, and music as one civic instinct — "people traveled from Janbogo specifically for it," described repeatedly as having genuine soul. That's Cancer, unambiguously first. Its music-and-festivity gives Leo second place, and its still-unresolved founding-nation-versus-majority question (never as fully settled as Janbogo's own version of the same argument) gives Taurus a real tertiary claim. Neither Scorpio nor Gemini reflects anything about Zukelli's own pre-war culture — both are purely post-destruction phenomena, documented separately below.

**Denison** — Tops Aries/Sagittarius/Leo. Confirmed directly and vividly: pride in extremity performed loudly (reciting wind statistics as social ritual), the explicit opposite of the "competence without commentary" ethos most other cities share.

**Dumont d'Urville** — Tops Leo/Virgo/Sagittarius. *Substantially revised.* The Megasheet's real throughline is a vibrant, festive live-music culture ("New Orleans at 1/20th scale," music in nearly every eatery) explicitly proposed as possibly load-bearing to residents' will to stay somewhere this physically demanding — Feeling/Assertive/Positive Outlook, the clearest Heart-center read in the subnet. That's Leo, not the more stoic "negotiation with the non-negotiable" framing the earlier draft leaned on, though the underlying accommodation-not-conquest relationship with wind and penguins still earns Virgo a strong second place.

**Fort McMurdo** — Tops Virgo/Capricorn/Libra. Working-class and meritocratic — status earned by capability, not origin — with power that's operational rather than political ("presence, not population"). The confirmed post-war loss of its former capital function is close to a direct match for Virgo's own wound (invisible until something breaks); Capricorn picks up the meritocratic achievement-culture, Libra the former administrative centrality.

**Scott** — Tops Taurus/Cancer/Scorpio. *Substantially revised from a Scorpio-led draft.* The Megasheet is clear that Scott's actual, lived population is quiet, domestic, and family-oriented — "a genuinely decent place to raise a family" — not defined day-to-day by its Saint's mortality-facing testimony. That historical veneration (shared Hut Point remembrance with Fort McMurdo) still earns Scorpio a real but secondary, more ceremonial claim.

### Mawson Subnet

**Mawson** — Tops Cancer/Taurus/Leo. *Substantially revised.* The Megasheet reframes this city around hospitality as explicit civic purpose — welcoming as the natural response to a demographic plurality with no single gatekeeper of "local" identity, expressed concretely as Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination. That's a near-dead-on Cancer match, stronger than the institutional-continuity framing the earlier draft leaned on (which still gives Taurus a real second-place claim, and the honeymoon-destination public reputation gives Leo a plausible tertiary one).

**Sayowa** — Tops Hub/Gemini/Virgo. Hub is fixed by canon and massively reinforced here — "the point where three roads meet," quiet functional service performed without any interest in credit, "small vessel, outsized cargo." That same uncredited-service instinct gives Virgo a stronger tertiary claim than the earlier Pisces pick.

**Dome Fuji** *(atypical — see note above)* — Tops Pisces/Cancer/Aquarius. *Scorpio dropped.* The Megasheet reveals a far richer picture than "devotion through hardship": an anti-hierarchical, one-devotee-at-a-time population where "arrival is the credential," embracing extreme cold as the entire spiritual point rather than something to master — a peaceful, zen-like *merging*, not effortful confrontation. That points toward Pisces' own dissolution-of-self doctrine even more strongly than before, with Cancer (communal, devotional warmth) and Aquarius (a transcendence-seeking, self-selected population) as secondary fits. Scorpio's confrontational framing no longer fits once the devotional culture is read as Positive Outlook rather than hardship-tested.

### Mirny Subnet

**Casey** — Tops Leo/Virgo/Hub. *Revised — Scorpio moved to Stage 2 Overrides, below.* Casey's own pre-war Enneagram read (Feeling/Assertive/Positive Outlook, shared with Dumont d'Urville and Palmer City) is warm and festive, built around one chosen civic landmark — Splinters, Tepenia's largest bar, revived deliberately as an act of civic self-definition, not an accident of fame. That's Leo's territory first. Its "useful, quick, honest about the route" unglamorous-service ethos gives Virgo second place, and its genuine function-without-mythology, waypoint-without-a-culture-of-its-own character gives Hub a real tertiary claim.

**Mirny (city)** — Tops Taurus/Libra/Virgo. Confirmed directly: "competence over romance," a fortified, function-first city whose Continuity Schism (a century of unbroken occupation as identity, tested by the war) is Taurus' own "the practice is what we're loyal to" logic. Libra picks up the Continuants-vs-Rupturists precedent dispute; Virgo the underlying quiet endurance.

**Zhongshan** — Tops Libra/Virgo/Taurus. Confirmed and deepened: a strict non-prying civic norm repeating at every scale ("non-acknowledgment as respect"), patient civic maturity built up across three architectural eras — a formal, procedural, close-quarters social code that is Libra's own territory first.

**Shirayuki** — Tops Leo/Aquarius/Pisces. Confirmed directly: "A Place Decided For You, Made Into a Place You'd Choose" — an imposed origin turned into genuine, chosen cultivation of art, fashion, and creative life, the Larsemann cluster's own Heart-center city (vs. Zhongshan's and Sinheung's Gut-center profiles).

**Davis** — Tops Virgo/Taurus/Capricorn. Confirmed almost verbatim: "help that's genuinely useful rather than performed," quiet competence over self-image, self-sufficient enrichment without seeking outside recognition.

**Sinheung** *(files: Sinheung)* — Tops Aquarius/Capricorn/Libra. Confirmed and deepened: a city that went generations without choosing its own name — "Soyuz" was only ever a leftover label from the Russian station its Korean founders inherited via the Jeju-do court, until the city was officially named Sinheung (신흥) on 2026-07-14 — "Claimed, Not Found," with unusually sharp patriotism embedded in a claim proven through output rather than a self-chosen identity *(corrected 2026-07-13 — previously said this city "named itself after a spacecraft" and carried "a self-chosen identity"; it never did, and the working title has since changed from "Chosen Ambition, Not Inherited Memory" to "Claimed, Not Found")* — Aquarius' forward-orientation first, reinforced by its own established role manufacturing Cradle fabrication chambers (Capricorn, second).

**Vostok** *(low-confidence — the Megasheet itself flags most of its own content as proposed, not settled)* — Tops Aquarius/Scorpio/Sagittarius. What is confirmed: a heavy genetics-research industry (Thinking/Withdrawn/Positive Outlook, mirroring Kunlun's structure) and a defining demographic mismatch — a population with zero Russian representation inheriting a Russian scientific legend that isn't theirs, "a legend living in someone else's house." That specific inherited-identity tension is genuinely Scorpio-flavored. Revisit once Vostok's own source material is less thin.

**Kunlun** *(atypical — see note above)* — Tops Aquarius/Capricorn/Libra. *Scorpio dropped.* The Megasheet describes a curated, 19-nation space/astronomy/comms-heritage population whose engagement with its own hardship is joyful and all-consuming (Thinking/Withdrawn/Positive Outlook) — not effortfully confrontational. Aquarius' visionary/scientific character fits first; Capricorn picks up the vocational-excellence-as-identity thread; Libra the curated, qualification-based nature of who's allowed to belong there at all.

### Byrd Subnet

**Byrd** — Tops Virgo/Sagittarius/Capricorn. Read directly from Byrd's own Megasheet: Instinctive/Withdrawn/Competency, an underground, tunnel-founded mechanized-fabrication city — about as literal a match for Virgo's own Undergrid identity as exists anywhere in Tepenia. Its expedition-founding mythology supports Sagittarius second; its massive integrated fabrication economy (one of only two active Cradle sites in Tepenia) supports Capricorn's achievement-through-production culture third.
