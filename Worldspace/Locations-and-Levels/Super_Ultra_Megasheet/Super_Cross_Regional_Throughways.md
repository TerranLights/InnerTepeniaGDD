# Super-Ultra-Megasheet — Cross-Regional Throughways

**What this is:** connecting factors that pass through multiple regions of the game — not things regions have in common (see Cross-Regional Patterns for that), but chains showing how a fact in one region leads through another to a consequence in a third, fourth, or fifth, at the largest scale this project has assembled anywhere. Written 2026-07-10. **This whole document is flagged TENTATIVE** — see the status note at the top of `Super_Cross_Regional_Patterns.md`, tied specifically to Byrd's own still-undetermined DLC 2 main questline.

---

## Throughway 1: The Cradle network's complete nationwide shape, running through six of the game's eight regions and ending at the player character's own origin

**Neumayer (Halley subnet) designs the current-generation fabrication-synthesis chamber schematic, uncredited, the third of three separate eras of the same city's own unclaimed engineering legacy** (Halley Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 2) → **Byrd and Sinheung (Mirny subnet), are the network's only two currently active manufacturing sites, building to that same schematic** (Byrd Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 1; Mirny Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Full Extrapolation Section IV) → **Denison (Janbogo/Ross subnet) and Mountain Pass (a joint Vostok-Kunlun venture, Mirny subnet) are the network's two historical, now-dark manufacturing sites** (Janbogo/Ross Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 3; Mirny Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 1) → **Mountain Pass depended on residual power overflow from the Amundsen Tower's own continent-wide regulated grid, tying its own capability directly to the Tower's continued operation** (Mirny Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 3) → **the Tower's destruction, the central catastrophic event whose ruins anchor Amundsen Station and DLC 1, ended Mountain Pass's manufacturing capability permanently** (same source) → **but not before Mountain Pass produced the specific chamber, installed at Calethina's own lab in Concordia, that created the player character** (Mirny Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 1, citing `Energy_Grid_Failure_Rationale.md` #11) → **making this single supply chain the one thread in the entire game connecting Halley subnet's own design lineage, Janbogo/Ross subnet's own historical manufacturing, Mirny subnet's own active production and its dead joint venture, the Amundsen Tower's own collapse, and Concordia's own present-day setting, into one unbroken, mechanically real line running directly to the player's own body.**

## Throughway 2: Byrd's founding debt, independently assembled from two directions and only completed here

**Belgrano (Halley subnet) built Tepenia's first native tracked vehicle, the Arrastradora, specifically to reach Byrd, crewed all-robot for the dangerous unmapped crossing** (Halley Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Throughway 3) → **Neumayer (also Halley subnet) quietly redesigned that same vehicle mid-expedition, before the crossing to Byrd itself ever began, giving the lineage that became the modern "Rastra" its true origin** (same source) → **Palmer City and Rothera (Palmer subnet) shipped supporting historical records across the Weddell Sea, sharpening the expedition's own heading** (Palmer Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Cross-Reference Finding 2) → **Byrd's own established culture, to this day, reflects none of these four cities' contributions — not Belgrano's expedition, not Neumayer's redesign, not Palmer City's or Rothera's records** (Byrd Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 2, extended by Halley Subnet Ultra-Megasheet's own Throughway 3) → **Palmer City's own subsequent destruction erased its own local copies of the very records that helped find Byrd in the first place, meaning Byrd may now be the only place in present-day Tepenia holding real primary-source material about a destroyed city from a completely different subnet** (Palmer Subnet Ultra-Megasheet, Full Extrapolation Section V) → **making Byrd, once every region's own independent research is read together, not merely a city with one unacknowledged debt but the physical inheritor of four separate contributions across two subnets, one of which no longer has any other copy of what it gave.**

## Throughway 3: A single coerced signature threads from Byrd's own resident companion through Concordia's own institutions into the game's central thematic territory

**Maggie Aarden, an unwilling leader of early robot resettlement, becomes the robot signatory of the Falkland Treaty under deliberately loaded, coercive circumstances at Stanley, Falkland Islands** (`Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/Maggie Aarden/README.md`) → **she eventually settles permanently in Byrd (DLC 2), now confirmed as its canonical recruitable and romanceable companion** (Byrd's own Cross-Reference Synthesis; `Storyline/DLC_Overview.md`, DLC 2 entry) → **the Falkland Treaty's own official narrative versus its coerced reality is the traceable root cause behind at least three living Concordia districts' own founding institutions — Libra's Suspended Compact, Gemini's Twin Channels, and Pisces' Tolerance Pact — none of which currently attribute their own origin to this correctly** (Concordia Ultra-Megasheet, Cross-Reference Finding 1) → **meaning a single act of coercion against one robot, at the very beginning of Tepenia's history, is still actively, if invisibly, structuring the present-day institutions of a genuine majority-adjacent share of Concordia's own thirteen districts** (same source) → **and the same underlying thematic territory — a robot denied full agency or personhood, with lasting consequences for everyone around her — is the explicit inciting incident of the entire Long Night War itself, and echoes forward into Kendra Heinrich's own DLC 1 story, a robot fighting, centuries later, at the opposite end of the continent, for the same basic recognition Maggie's own signature was never allowed to freely give.**

## Worth Your Attention

Throughway 1 is, without qualification, the single most significant finding this entire multi-week project has produced — it's the only chain in the whole game that runs through six of the eight major regions and terminates at the player character's own physical origin, rather than remaining confined to worldbuilding texture. Throughway 3 is the most thematically significant, since it's the first place any regional synthesis has connected Byrd's own companion, Concordia's own district politics, and the game's central DLC 1 thematic territory into one continuous line — proof that the "shared goal, conflicting methods" reading of Concordia and the "robot personhood" reading of the wider game's own founding wound are, underneath everything, the same story told at different scales.
