# Super-Ultra-Megasheet — Full Extrapolation (Step 2)

**What this is:** every open question about Tepenia as a whole given an actual proposed answer, built as far as possible directly out of what's already established across every region's own Ultra-Megasheet rather than invented from nothing. Not locked canon — meant for review, revision, or rejection piece by piece. Companion to `Super_Ultra_Megasheet_Mega_Init.md` in this same folder. Written 2026-07-10. **Status: TENTATIVE** — see the Mega-Init's own status banner, tied to Byrd's undetermined DLC 2 main questline.

## I. Each region's own specific role within the nation's "uncredited competence" identity

Mirroring every smaller-scale version of this exercise — district-level, city-level, subnet-level — one level higher:

- **Concordia carries the argument** — thirteen sincere theories of how to build a true city, none of them wrong, none of them fully reconciled in 250 years.
- **Palmer subnet carries the cost** — the clearest single record of what the war actually took, and what three different kinds of survival actually looked like.
- **Halley subnet carries the unclaimed legacy** — one city's engineering quietly running the whole country for three centuries, nobody outside it aware of whose design it was.
- **Janbogo/Ross subnet carries the verdict** — every city's fate a legible answer to whether it was worth more alive than gone.
- **Mirny subnet carries the personal stake** — the one region whose own infrastructure reaches, mechanically, into the player's own body.
- **Mawson subnet carries the connective tissue** — barely a region unto itself, more valuable for what it bridges than what it contains.
- **Byrd carries the debt nobody's paid** — four cities' worth of founding contribution, across two subnets, currently unacknowledged by the one city that owes it.
- **Amundsen Station carries the wound** — the single structural loss every surface region still feels, however differently each one processes it.

## II. Whether the "reaching versus arriving" theological duality extends to Concordia and Byrd

**Proposed, extending Dome Fuji's and Kunlun's own established duality (Mawson/Mirny subnets) to two more regions:** Concordia is a "reaching" region in the same register as Kunlun — deliberately built, argued over, constructed one theory of city-building at a time, straining toward something none of its thirteen districts has ever fully achieved. Byrd is an "arriving" region in the same register as Dome Fuji — found already complete, buried and waiting, requiring only the crossing to reach rather than the construction to build. This gives the whole game's own two largest present-day settlements a clean, complementary structural read: the nation's own last living city built itself into existence through argument, while its other major surviving city was simply discovered, fully formed, underneath the snow.

## III. Whether the nation's own "uncredited competence" pattern has a single point of origin

**Proposed:** rather than treating Pattern 1 (Cross-Regional Patterns) as six independent regional coincidences, the most economical explanation is that it traces to the same root Concordia's own Full Extrapolation already proposed for pre-war Concordia specifically — a nation founded entirely by coerced, unwilling exile, whose very first and most consequential act (the Falkland Treaty's signing) was itself an act of uncredited, unrecognized labor extracted from someone who never consented to give it. If the nation's founding document was itself built on invisible, unacknowledged work, it's a reasonable, earned proposal that the pattern simply propagated outward from there — every region's own version of "does the real work, gets none of the credit" tracing back to the same originating shape, rather than emerging six separate times by coincidence.

## IV. A Proposed Whole-Nation Demonym Note

No single whole-nation demonym currently exists in established canon beyond "Tepenian" itself, which already functions cleanly as both noun and adjective across every region's own material. Nothing here proposes to replace it — simply noting, for completeness at this scale, that "Tepenian" already does the job every smaller-scale demonym (Concordian, Peninsulans, Mawsonite, Byrdian, and so on) does at its own level, and needs no further resolution.

## V. The Full-Re-Examination Pass's Own Place in This Document

**Proposed:** this Super-Ultra-Megasheet is not a substitute for the developer's own standing, explicitly-deferred plan (first stated during Concordia's own Ultra-Megasheet) to go back through everything in one single pass, informed by the Questline material excluded from every synthesis document in this project so far. Rather, this document is what that future pass will need to work *from* — the complete, cross-referenced, bottom-up picture, assembled specifically so the top-down re-examination has a finished floor to stand on rather than needing to re-derive the whole shape of the game from scratch. Worth stating explicitly: this document's own existence is what makes that future pass tractable at all.

## Worth Your Attention

Section III is the most speculative proposal in this document, and the most worth flagging for developer review specifically — tracing the entire game's own most-repeated civic theme back to one single founding wound is an elegant unification, but elegant unifications are exactly the kind of claim that should be checked carefully rather than accepted on the strength of tidiness alone. Section V is the most practically important, since it directly answers what this document is *for*, distinct from every Ultra-Megasheet beneath it.
