# Moon, Lagrange Points, Cislunar Orbits, and Real Isolated-Habitat Mechanism Comparables

Research file. Real physics and engineering data only; no worldbuilding. Started 2026-10-10. American English.
Part 1 is physics. Part 2 is tagged [RW] (real-world comparable, mechanism only; never a fact about the fiction).

## 1. Scope

Part 1 (physics): the Moon (surface environment, resources, lava tubes, transfer time and delta-v), mass-driver and lunar launch physics with the O'Neill-era lunar-materials argument, Earth-Moon and Sun-Earth Lagrange points L1 to L5, and cislunar station orbits (NRHO, DRO, low lunar orbit).
Part 2 ([RW], mechanism only): Antarctic stations, nuclear submarines and the ISS as engineering and logistics comparables, plus a short list of closed-habitat maintenance-burden studies.
Established elsewhere and not re-derived here: `leo_and_orbital_habitats.md` sections 3 and 8 (read-only) already hold O'Neill 1974 Table 2 mass breakdown, the SP-413 colony build, lunar escape speed from O'Neill, LEO-to-lunar-orbit delta-v of 4,100 m/s from SP-413, and LEO radiation and ISS reboost. This file adds independent sources and the new topics; overlaps are noted as cross-checks.

Legend: P = primary (agency, peer-reviewed, dataset, handbook); S = secondary. "Raw" = I read the page text myself; "digest" = the number reached me through a WebFetch summary.
