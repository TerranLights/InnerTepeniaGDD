# Shopping list: sources the research agents could not open

**Compiled 2026-10-06 from the "Open threads" and "Rejected sources" sections of the three research files.** Citations are
**as the agents logged them**. They were not re-verified, so a title or journal reference may need a correction when you
look it up. Where an agent gave a DOI or URL, it is included.

**How to use this.** Put anything you obtain into `research/_inputs/` (PDF, scan or text). Name the file with the author,
year and a keyword. A new session then reads it with `RESUME_HERE.md`. **Every item below is optional.** The "unblocks"
column says what each would let a class say, so you can pick by what matters most to you.

## What the two biggest holes mean in practice

- **A robot's body in vacuum.** No numerical thermal figure for any humanoid machine in vacuum has been found (B12). This
  bears directly on the physics-fidelity rule for robots and is the most useful single gap to close.
- **Jovian radiation.** Without items 1–5, any Jovian infrastructure class can only say that radiation is severe, not how
  severe at which orbit. That is a gap for the *Outer Tepenia* era more than for the Cryptograph Helix era.
