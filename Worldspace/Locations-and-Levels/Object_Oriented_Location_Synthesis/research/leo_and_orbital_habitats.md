# Low Earth Orbit and Rotating Orbital Habitats: physics and engineering research

Research date: 2026-10-06. Brief: `_BRIEF.md` (LAW 0-R; no figure from memory).

## Scope

Low Earth orbit (LEO) as an environment, plus three engineering structure concepts: small robot-only staging stations (vacuum, solar power, shielding), O'Neill-cylinder-type rotating habitats, and Von Braun-wheel-type rotating wheels. Also orbit transfer delta-v and the published limits of space elevators and mass-flow towers. No societies or cultures.
