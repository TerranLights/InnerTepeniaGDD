# Life support, low-gravity biology, power and heat rejection, hull structures, shielding, rotation dynamics, in-situ construction: physics and engineering research

Research date: 2026-10-10. Brief: `_BRIEF.md` (LAW 0-R; no figure from memory). Status: file assembled incrementally during the session; if a section below says "pending", the session ended before it was written.

## 1. Scope

The engineering every sealed, inhabited structure must satisfy, whoever lives there: closed-loop life support for humans (air, water, food, waste, trace contaminants, thermal), partial-gravity and radiation biology, power and heat rejection at scale, pressure-vessel and spin stress in hulls and materials, meteoroid shielding, rotation dynamics, and regolith-based construction. Residents are humans (who breathe and eat) and human-shaped robots (who do not). The sibling files `leo_and_orbital_habitats.md` and `mars_venus_and_transfer.md` (their sections 3 and 8) were read for overlap; figures already established there are cited by file name and not re-derived unless a new angle was needed.

Tooling note. `WebSearch` returns a model-written summary plus links; any number in a summary is a lead only and was never copied unless I then read it in an opened page. `WebFetch` returns a small-model digest; numbers that reached me only that way are marked "digest". Most pages and PDFs were fetched with `curl` and read as raw text (`pdftotext -layout` for PDFs, tag-stripped text for HTML): marked "raw". Date of every entry: 2026-10-10 (system date), unless the cell says otherwise.
