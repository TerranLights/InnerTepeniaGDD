================================================================================================

CI carbonaceous chondrite: 183 kg Fe/t: 9.6-14.1 x Mercury's
Sun photosphere: 1.19 kg Fe/t = 11-16 x poorer than Mercury's surface in iron by mass
(grades are not recoverability: Mercury's iron is partly sulfide; lunar iron is mostly in silicates; CI iron is largely oxide/sulfide; native-metal grades: Moon ~0.5 wt% [SL 3F], average meteorite ~20 % free metal [PR 1b])
