# Dialogue TODO

**What this is:** the dialogue-writing companion to the repo's own `TODO.md`, scoped specifically to the
`Dialogue/` tree — three buckets, so it's always clear where things stand: who's done, who's started but not
finished, and who hasn't been touched at all. See `Character_Index.md` for the flat "what folders/sheets
currently exist" log; this file is the status/planning layer on top of that.

**How to move an entry:** when a character's first sheet is written, move them from **Not Yet Added** to
**Added & Incomplete**, and note which sheet(s) exist. When they hit the completion bar below, move them to
**Added & Complete**. Update `Character_Index.md` in the same pass either way — the two files should never
drift apart.

**Definition of "complete," established 2026-08-15:** a character counts as complete once they have `Greeting`,
all of their `Quest_Step` sheets, and (if applicable — romanceable characters only) `Romance_Gate2` and
`Romance_Beats`. **`Banter_<Trigger>` and `Ambient_<Location>` sheets are open-ended by nature — new triggers
and locations can always be added — so they never block "complete" status.** A character can be Complete with
zero Banter/Ambient sheets, and can keep gaining more of either after being marked Complete without becoming
incomplete again.
