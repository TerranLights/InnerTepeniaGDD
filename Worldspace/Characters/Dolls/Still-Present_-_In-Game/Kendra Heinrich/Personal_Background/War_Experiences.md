# Kendra Heinrich — War Experiences

**Status: proposed working direction, not yet locked.** See
`../DLC_South_Pole_Level_Design.md` for full reasoning and supporting math.

During the final evacuations before Amundsen Tower's destruction, Kendra held the
line at the base of the Tower with an SMG and ballistic weapons, standing between
Upper Earth military forces and the innocents making the last desperate rides up
before the line closed.

**The proposed account of what actually ended her stand:** the Upper Earth strike
that destroyed the Tower didn't target her, or the evacuees directly — it hit the
base accelerator/power complex, the single point that kept the whole structure
standing. Once that power was cut, the Tower's ~150 km of structure lost its support
and came down over the following several minutes, the bulk of the mass landing
centered almost exactly on the position she was holding. She wasn't beaten by an
enemy she failed to stop — she was standing under roughly a quarter-million tonnes of
collapsing megastructure when it fell. That she survived at all is itself consistent
with what she is; the DLC's premise is that surviving isn't the same as getting out.

She likely owes her survival to being caught near, or inside, the underground
foundation shaft rather than the open surface — the shaft was bored into bedrock and
structurally separate from the collapsing tube above it, and plausibly remained
largely intact even as the surface was buried. Injured, and with her way out now
sealed under a mountain of wreckage, she has been stranded there since.
