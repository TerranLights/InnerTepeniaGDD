# Calethina's Construction & Shipping Chain — Full Play-by-Play

**What this is:** the fully-detailed narrative version of the construction/shipping chain first
skeletoned in `Founding_Construction_Chain_UNMARKED.md` (2026-07-12), filling in both threads that
document explicitly left dangling. Source: the developer's own play-by-play notes, worked through
2026-07-16. This file is the reference to work from; `Founding_Construction_Chain_UNMARKED.md` remains
the actual in-repo statement of what stays unmarked, discoverable-only lore per the Cradle precedent
([[feedback_cradle_unmarked_lore]]) — this file exists so the full story doesn't have to be
re-derived every time it's needed, not to change how it's exposed to the player.

Eight locations, six subnets, plus the South Pole itself — nearly the whole nation touched Calethina
before she ever came online, and the chain's own final stretch runs directly into the two catastrophic
events that define her present-day condition.

---

## 1. Request — Mawson [Mawson subnet]

Originally commissioned as a "welcoming host" hologram for Mawson — greeting honeymooners and general
visitors arriving at the city. The founding requirement was breadth: she needed to be able to hold a
genuine conversation on basically any topic, with a wide working knowledge of the whole country, not
just Mawson's own local specifics.

*Consistency note:* this gives Mawson a real, specific historical connection to one of the game's most
significant Dolls, independent of Mawson's own current DLC-depth gap (see `TODO.md`'s "Mawson DLC — City
Depth Gap" entry) — worth keeping in mind as one genuine thread Mawson can point to, even though it
isn't enough on its own to close that gap.

---

## 2. Spec & Schematic Design — Neumayer [Halley subnet]

The actual drafting and design work happened at Neumayer's own labs — a natural fit, since Neumayer is
already established elsewhere in this project as the quiet, uncredited origin point of major Tepenian
engineering work (including the current-generation Cradle chamber schematic itself). The core technical
problem at this stage: how to actually fit that scale of general knowledge onto a limited hard drive.

---

## 3. Engineering & Programming — Kunlun [Mirny subnet]

Kunlun took Neumayer's design drafts and engineered them into an actual, buildable schematic — turning
the theoretical spec into something that could physically be constructed. Fits Kunlun's own established
observatory-and-comms technical capacity directly.

---

## 4. Full Construction — Byrd [Byrd subnet]

The actual machinery was built at Byrd, living up to Kunlun's engineering schematics. Fits Byrd's own
deep industrial/fabrication capacity — the same city already established as one of only two active
Cradle chamber-manufacturing sites in the country.

---

## 5. Stress-Testing — Sejong [Palmer subnet]

Sejong was chosen specifically for its wide variety of residential populations, which served as a
natural block against Upper-Earth tampering — the sheer demographic density and diversity gave the
project room to be developed and fine-tuned without unwanted outside interference. Fits Sejong's own
established King George Island multinational density.

---

## 6. Shipped Via — Port Lockroy [Palmer subnet]

The "finished" version (or so everyone thought at the time) shipped out through Port Lockroy's own
courier network-node. Fits Port Lockroy's established role as a genuine logistics/communications
waypoint — echoing the real Port Lockroy's own history as the world's most remote post office.

---

## 7. Shipping Reroute Ordered By — Fort McMurdo [Janbogo subnet]

This is the pivot point of the entire chain. It was at this exact moment that the Long Night War fully
came underway.

**The original planned route, established 2026-07-17:** before the war intervened, Calethina's shipment
was never meant to go anywhere near Amundsen-Scott Station or Concordia at all — it was heading back to
Mawson, the city that originally requested her (Step 1). From Port Lockroy, the plan was up Hwy 1 to
Marambio, then either of two equally plausible ways across to the Halley subnet: by ship to somewhere on
the coast — likely Belgrano or Sanay, both on Hwy 7 ("the Atlantic Highway") — or by air, out of
Marambio's own airfield, direct to Troll (also on Hwy 7, between Sanay and Lazar). Either way, the route
converges on the same overland tail from there: Hwy 7 onward to Lazar, Hwy 7-ext from Lazar through
Princess Elisabeth to the Sayowa Junction, and finally Hwy 4 from the Sayowa Junction into Mawson itself.
A long, entirely ordinary logistics route, four subnets and one crossing by sea or air, ending exactly
where the whole project began — and which specific crossing it would have been is genuinely irrelevant,
since neither ever happened. The onset of the war is what forced Tepenia's hand into the reroute
described below instead — without it, Calethina would have completed a perfectly mundane delivery to a
honeymoon destination and never come anywhere near the Tower, the Split Brain, or Upper Earth's
compromising data.

Orders came directly from the Capitol — Fort McMurdo was, at this point in the timeline, still Tepenia's
historical seat of government — to reroute Calethina's shipment through Amundsen-Scott Station, on her
way to a destination judged likely to remain safe from attack: Concordia.

But the reroute order carried a second, far heavier instruction: **wipe her existing knowledge of the
entire country, to make room on her datadrives for information regarding the governments of Upper
Earth.** Calethina — designed and built as a broad-knowledge welcoming host for a Mawson honeymoon
crowd — was being repurposed, mid-transit, into a wartime intelligence-carrying asset. Everything about
Tepenia she'd been built to know was deliberately overwritten to make room for what she was now being
asked to carry instead.

All of the transferrence and transportation from this point forward happened during active wartime
conditions.

*This resolves the first thread `Founding_Construction_Chain_UNMARKED.md` left explicitly dangling:*
*"Fort McMurdo — the historical national capital — personally ordered a shipping reroute for one unit's*
*construction chain. Not a small detail to leave unexplained." The reroute wasn't bureaucratic — it was*
*a wartime intelligence decision made at the seat of government itself, and it cost Calethina the entire*
*body of knowledge she was originally built to hold.*

## 8. Forward-Shipped Via — Amundsen-Scott Station [South Pole, no subnet]

The reroute succeeded — Calethina was routed through Amundsen-Scott Station, and it was here that she
collected information compromising Upper Earth, now carried alongside (or in place of) her original
purpose-built knowledge base. From here, she was forwarded onward to Concordia.

**The physical route, established 2026-07-17:** the reroute traveled Port Lockroy → Byrd via Hwy 1,
then Byrd → Amundsen-Scott Station via Hwy 22, then Amundsen-Scott Station → Concordia via Hwy 22 to
its junction with Hwy 175, then Hwy 175 to its junction with Hwy 183 near Janbogo, then Hwy 183 into
Concordia — the same highway network confirmed in `Locations/Infrastructure/Highways.md`. Hwy 1's
western terminus and Hwy 22's Amundsen Sea terminus are the same point, Byrd itself, which is what
makes this a single continuous overland route rather than requiring a change of transport mode.

This means the reroute carried Calethina's shipment back through Byrd — the exact city where she was
physically built (Step 4) — a second time, now mid-transit and already stripped of her original
knowledge, without anyone involved necessarily registering the coincidence. She passed through her own
birthplace again on the way to the place and moment that would destroy her.

---

## 9. Arrival in Concordia

The day Calethina arrived in Concordia happened, unfortunately, to coincide exactly with the assault on
the Amundsen Tower.

She had just been set up at what would become Calethina's Lab, hooked in and powered on, when the
assault triggered the Planetary Split Brain Event. The resulting power shock — the same continent-scale
electrical catastrophe that struck down the Tower itself — fried and permanently corrupted her
datadrives.

This is the direct, specific cause of her established present-day condition: the "glitchy" behavior, the
genuinely damaged and unreliable memory, and the reason she can't always help the player the way a fully
intact version of her would be able to.

*This resolves the second thread `Founding_Construction_Chain_UNMARKED.md` left explicitly dangling:*
*"Her final leg passed through Amundsen-Scott Station — the Tower's own site — putting her construction*
*timeline in direct physical proximity to the location whose later destruction caused her defining*
*trauma." The proximity wasn't coincidental scheduling — she physically passed through the Tower's own*
*site carrying compromised Upper Earth intelligence on her way to the exact place and exact moment the*
*Tower's own collapse would catch her.*

## Worth Your Attention

The single most load-bearing new fact in this whole play-by-play is the datadrive wipe ordered at Fort
McMurdo — it means Calethina's currently-fragmented, unreliable memory isn't only a story about the
Split Brain shock. It's a *second* loss layered on top of a *first, deliberate* one: she was stripped of
her original purpose-built knowledge before the war ever damaged her, then never got the chance to
properly integrate what replaced it before that was damaged too. Any future writing about her
personality or her own relationship to what she doesn't know should treat these as two distinct wounds,
not one.

The original planned route (Step 7) is the second most valuable addition — it establishes exactly how
close Calethina came to a completely uneventful fate. Absent the war, she was one sea crossing and two
highways away from a mundane arrival at the honeymoon city that commissioned her, fully intact,
knowledge base untouched. Everything that actually defines her — the wiped memory, the compromising
Upper Earth intelligence, the Split Brain corruption — only happened because the war intervened at the
one moment her shipment was still in transit. Worth keeping in mind for any writing that touches her own
sense of what she lost: she isn't just damaged, she's specifically a person whose entire life would have
been different by one twist of bad timing.
