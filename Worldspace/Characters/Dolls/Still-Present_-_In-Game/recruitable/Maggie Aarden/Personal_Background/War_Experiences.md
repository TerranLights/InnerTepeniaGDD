# Maggie Aarden — War Experiences

**Added 2026-07-11**, alongside the correction to her Falkland Treaty signatory circumstances (see `README.md`'s Historical Status section) — filling the gap between her pre-war resettlement leadership and the Treaty signing.

* Her pre-war "third option" resettlement effort (Midwestland/Colorado Republic) was already underway when the War of Upper Earth broke out — a war she did not cause and had no part in starting.
* The war's escalating anti-robot violence forced her small, quiet resettlement effort to become something much larger: the communities she had organized became a real refuge, then a rallying point, for robots displaced by the fighting.
* She did not seek a wartime command role. Circumstance and the same SP 5w6 sense of duty that made her lead the original resettlement effort made her the person people kept turning to as the war worsened — by its end, she was a recognized, credible leader among the robot population at large, not just her own original community.
* This is exactly why the Assembly Powers (Upper Earth's governments) identified her, specifically, as their choice of signatory once the war was won: an already-credible robot leader whose signature on the Falkland Treaty would make the exile look agreed-to, not simply imposed on a defeated population.
