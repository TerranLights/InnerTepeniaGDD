# Seica Cenilaithe — Personal Questline: "The One I Couldn't Stop"

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted; full step-by-step design deferred until Scorpio/Concordia is developed enough to anchor real locations and triggers.** Full design detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** Hatred with no target — what an 8w7 carries when the people responsible are structurally unreachable. Whether rage can be a form of fidelity. The limits of power. What remains of someone who is gone.

**Emotional Core:** Her husband was killed in the Long Night War; the people responsible are unreachable, and she would not take it out on innocents even if she could, so the hatred lives inside her. The questline is about what happens when something reachable finally appears, and whether what she does with it is justice or something else.

**Inciting hook:** the player uncovers something connected to Upper Earth's legacy inside Concordia — a decision, an agent, an institution with real culpability. See `README.md` for the full note; this reuses recruiting-hook direction #2 already sketched there.

**Mechanism:** her own established cinematic character beat (detecting a lie and putting the liar down instantly, without hesitation) is the categorical block — her reputation makes any patient or undercover approach structurally impossible for her, though not for the player. The player investigates in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 non-stat world-state routes (the Upper Earth Defector subcommunity, her own Scorpio community, the Archive of Final Confessions, Libra, Gemini, a remorseful defector NPC, a legacy item, and a Wild Child/Scorpio route) — see `README.md` for full detail. Per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule, this guarantees the questline can never structurally block a player from reaching her romance gate.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium-High — which of the 13 routes the player takes, and whether the resolution lands toward The Name, The Target, The Reckoning, or The Fire (see below), are real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`:
- **The Name:** she creates something through her art that honors him in the specific, true way she's never been able to do publicly. It doesn't resolve the hatred — it gives it a form.
- **The Target:** something connected to Upper Earth's decisions reaches into Concordia in a form she and the player can actually confront. The hatred finally has a direction; what she does with it, and what it costs, is the ending.
- **The Reckoning:** through her spiritual practice, she finds something she can live with about what happened to him — not clean peace, a way to carry it that isn't purely burning.
- **The Fire:** she decides to keep the rage deliberately, as fidelity rather than self-destruction — the hardest and most honest ending for an Sx-8w7. She walks away the same as she walked in, except now she knows that's a choice.
