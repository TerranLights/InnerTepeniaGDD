## Heather Wendell

**Role / Archetype:** Frostlander through and through — a fiercely independent wilderness scout/huntress, most at home in wide-open, untamed territory. Repeatedly courted by both corporate and governmental interests within Concordia for her skills, and has repeatedly turned all of it down; she answers to nobody. Hunts for ecological balance, not sport. Confirmed 2026-07-20, grounded in a developer-provided song ("Silicon Psalms of the Outback," see `Personal_Background/Inspiration/`) — real-world "Safari-Outback" inspiration translated into Tepenia's own terms via Sagittarius/the Frostlands, the closest thing this world has to wide-open wilderness and life at the edges of civilization. Plausibly carries Australian-descended heritage (already an established founding-population thread in several Tepenian cities) expressed through actual Frostlands survival rather than literal desert.
**Primary Location(s):** Sagittarius (the Frostlands) — confirmed 2026-07-20, absolutely and without question per the developer. **Placement TBD between two structural options, both acceptable:** (A) already a Frostlands resident in the main game, or (B) a DLC companion who, upon being recruited and completing that DLC, returns to Concordia and settles into a companion player-home specifically in the Frostlands (consistent with the established DLC-companion settling-in mechanic in `Companion_System.md`) — the pull toward Sagittarius specifically is non-negotiable either way, only the route she arrives by is undecided.
**Affiliation(s):** None formal — independence from institutional affiliation is a defining trait, not an oversight
**Companion Potential:** Yes — confirmed recruitable (part of the 2026-07-20 roster expansion)
**Romance Potential:** Yes — per the binding "all recruitable companions are romanceable" rule (`Companion_System.md`); thresholds TBD

## Visual Description

*(See `Reference_Images/` folder — FSD012 series.)*

Long, wavy platinum-blonde hair with blunt bangs. Pale blue-green eyes, a direct, appraising expression. A pair of leather-trimmed aviator goggles worn pushed up on her forehead, and a small boom-mic headset at one ear — genuine field/flight gear, not decorative. A cropped dark-green tank top with a light star graphic, worn under an open, well-broken-in brown leather bomber jacket; distressed denim cutoff shorts cinched with a heavy leather utility belt carrying pouches and a metal flask; a thigh strap on one leg; tall lace-up leather combat boots. In different shots she's shown holding a metal flask, a rolled map or scroll case, and what looks like an old analog radio or signal device clipped to her belt.

She reads as: someone who lives out of that jacket — gear chosen entirely for function, worn in exactly the way real field equipment wears in over years of actual use, not styled to look rugged. The goggles and headset aren't costume pieces; she's dressed like someone who is, or recently was, actually flying or driving something dangerous for a living.

## Personality & Voice
- Core summary: Self-possessed and unimpressed by power for its own sake. She has been approached, repeatedly, by institutions within Concordia wanting to make use of her — offers of purpose, status, security — and has turned every one of them down, not out of hostility toward them specifically, but because none of it is worth trading the wilderness for. Doesn't care who holds authority in Concordia so long as she's left free to be out in the Frostlands. Hunts with real skill and real purpose, but never for its own sake — for balance, for necessity, on her own terms.
- Speech patterns / quirks / recurring phrases: TBD — full voice pending Phase 3 personality work
- Behavior under stress or in different districts: TBD — but plausibly most herself, most at ease, specifically in Sagittarius/the Frostlands, and more guarded or withdrawn the further she is from open territory
- Moral tendencies & player influence: Reacts poorly to attempts to leash, own, or institutionally claim her — by the player or anyone else. Likely responds well to being treated as an equal free agent rather than a resource to be recruited or managed.

## Backstory Summary
TBD in detail — confirmed direction, grounded in a developer-provided song written for her ("Silicon Psalms of the Outback," full lyrics in `Personal_Background/Inspiration/Silicon_Psalms_of_the_Outback.md`): she has spent her existence resisting exactly the kind of institutional claim most robots in Tepenia eventually settle into — corporate and governmental interests within Concordia have both tried to recruit her, offering purpose, status, a side to belong to, and she has refused all of it, choosing instead a life of genuine self-determination in the Frostlands.
