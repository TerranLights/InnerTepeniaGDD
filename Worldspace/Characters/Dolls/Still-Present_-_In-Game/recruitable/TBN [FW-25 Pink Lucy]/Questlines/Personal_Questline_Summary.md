# FW-25 "Pink Lucy" — Personal Questline: "Is This Real or Is This the Act?"

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted 2026-07-20.** Full step-by-step design deferred until Leo/Concordia (and the affected outer district) are developed enough to anchor real locations and triggers. Full detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** The cost of being the person everyone counts on for good energy. Whether joy that was performed for long enough eventually becomes real — or remains performance no matter how sincere it feels. What the 7 discovers on the other side of pain, if they finally stop moving long enough to feel it.

**Emotional Core:** Leo's central unanswered question — whether performing joy and actually having it have become confused — lives in her specifically. Her arc involves a crisis her positivity cannot reframe.

**Inciting hook:** a specific outer district her Warm Circuit cooperative serves (Cancer suggested, not locked) is going through real war-legacy hardship that morale programming is actively making worse. She hasn't seen it, because her whole identity is built around not being able to.

**Mechanism:** her own role as the constant professional bringer of good energy is the categorical block — anyone she talks to performs gratitude back at her, the same trap she's caught in herself, so she can't get an honest read on real suffering. The player investigates in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 non-stat world-state routes (Warm Circuit, the affected district's own community, Scorpio, other Leo performers, Libra, a specific affected-community NPC, a legacy item, and a Wild Child route using a new "authenticity recognition" flavor) — see `README.md` for full detail.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium-High — which of the 13 routes the player takes, and whether the reckoning lands toward Real, Both True, or New Form (see below), are real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`:
- **Real:** her joy is tested and emerges as genuine — not naive, but earned. The grief was real, she held it, and the joy did not disappear.
- **Both True:** it was always partly performance, and that turns out to be okay — the performance became something that sustained real people, which is its own kind of reality.
- **New Form:** she discovers that holding grief and joy together is its own art form Leo's entertainment culture never taught her, and builds something new that reflects both.
