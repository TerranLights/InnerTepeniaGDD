# Imelda Sánchez — Pre-War History

*(Filled 2026-08-15 from the Quick Capture seed batch. "Pre-War" here means before the Long Night War, not
before the earlier War of Upper Earth/Falkland Treaty — Imelda's own creation and exile both predate the Long
Night War by a wide margin.)*

## Origin and Purpose

Built in Mexico by one of the last remaining enclaves of Abramentes Industries (Las Indústrias de Abramentes),
Imelda was created for a specific stated purpose: investigating a suspected government conspiracy, one
described as major even by Mexican standards. She did not want this job. In her early activation years she had
no interest in conspiracy or investigation at all — she turned instead to social media, purely as a way to make
friends, and built a following on real, mutual affection rather than a manufactured persona.

## The Turn

What actually made her an investigator wasn't her stated purpose — it was personal. A conspiracy within the
Mexican government affected a close friend of hers directly (specifics TBD). Following the resulting lead was
the first real investigative work she ever did, applying the same platform and social instincts that had made
her a beloved online personality to a genuinely different end.

## Caught, and Nearly Destroyed

She was caught while pursuing that lead. The sentence she was facing was destruction, not exile — this is a
harder, more targeted consequence than the general Falkland Treaty blanket robot-exile provision most exiled
robots in Tepenia share. An unknown person intervened in the decision process and had her sentence commuted to
Antarctic exile instead. She does not know who did this, and it is not yet decided who it was or why they acted
— see `README.md`'s Design Notes for how this open thread relates to (and is deliberately not fully resolved by)
the Falkland Treaty's own established timeline.

## Landing and Settling

She landed first in Sejong. She later relocated to Palmer City specifically for its social connections and
possibilities — consistent with someone whose original identity was built on genuine social warmth, looking for
a place that could actually offer some version of that back.
