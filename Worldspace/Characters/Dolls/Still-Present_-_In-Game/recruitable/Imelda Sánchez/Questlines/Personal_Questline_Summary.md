## Overview
**Themes:** Proof versus belief. A personal echo of her own original investigative arc (a conspiracy that hurt
someone she loved, chased down to a conclusive answer), now applied to someone else's story instead of her own.

can't let go of is the gap between "everyone accepts this happened" and "someone can actually prove what else
was going on." That gap is the same shape as the gap that got her nearly destroyed the first time.
