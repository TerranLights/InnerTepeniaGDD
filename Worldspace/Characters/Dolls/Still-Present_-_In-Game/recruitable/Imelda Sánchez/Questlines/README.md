# Imelda Sánchez — Questline Notes

*(Filled 2026-08-15 from the Quick Capture seed batch. Full step-by-step design deliberately deferred, same
reasoning as Ayako Hayashi's own questline notes — best rooted in real places once the relevant subnet/district
is developed further.)*

**Core hook (Sourced, 2026-08-15 seed):** Imelda wants conclusive, solid proof of "more to it" behind an
established robot-self-defense killing of an Upper Earth diplomat — see `README.md`'s Design Notes for the
significant, care-required connection to this project's own Long Night War inciting-incident canon (Akina, in
`Still-Present_-_In-Game/unsure and_or special cases/TBN [SE-031 Palmer City self-defense Akina]/`).

**Design caution, carried over from `README.md`:** this should not be built to overturn or re-litigate Akina's
already-canon, fully-favorable account. The player-facing content is Imelda's drive to chase down corroborating
proof and expose whatever fed into the diplomat's actions — not a mystery-box replacement for settled lore. Keep
resolution soft and ambient per the project's Mystery = Soft-Detail-Delivery law; full resolution, if one is
ever written, stays in private design notes.

**Accessibility constraint (Sourced):** side-content only. Structured so a violence-first playthrough likely
never meets her, while a talk-first playthrough is nearly guaranteed to. Per the Personal Questline Design Rule
(`Companion_System.md`), her eventual "something the player can do that she can't" step should have a
categorical exclusion established first — TBD what that is, likely related to her own established weaknesses
(rudimentary technical skills, uselessness in combat) leaving some part of the Akina-adjacent investigation
structurally out of her reach.

**Confirmed 2026-08-15:** she is DLC 3 (Palmer)'s first companion (see `README.md`). This questline's routing
and location work should lean directly into Palmer subnet geography — Rothera, Palmer City, Port Lockroy —
rather than Concordia.
