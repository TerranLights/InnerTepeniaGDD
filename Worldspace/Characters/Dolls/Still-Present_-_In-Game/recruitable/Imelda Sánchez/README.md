## Imelda Sánchez

**Role / Archetype:** Fallen social-media starlet turned exposer of corruption — confirmed 2026-07-20. Originally from Mexico; began as a genuinely beloved social-media personality who loved her audience sincerely, not just strategically. Her path eventually led her to expose real corruption between the Mexican government and Upper Earth megacorporations — unsurprising to anyone that the corruption existed, but costly for her specifically that she was the one who proved it. She was punished for it and exiled to Antarctica following the War of Upper Earth. This directly explains the camera in her Visual Description: not a hobbyist's prop, but her actual working tool, from influencer content through to investigative documentation.

## Visual Description

Long, warm auburn-brown hair with blunt bangs, half pulled back. Dark brown eyes, soft warm makeup, a silver ear cuff with a chain detail at one ear. Consistently shown with a compact mirrorless camera (a real Canon EOS-style body), often held or raised to shoot. Dressed in a plaid mini skirt/shorts, a soft grey knit top with a lace bra visible beneath an open, loosely-worn camel/tan trench coat, and knit-cuffed ankle boots. The setting across the set is a lived-in study/office: a wooden desk, stacked books with visible travel-adjacent titles, a vintage double-hemisphere world map on the wall, lit candles, a glass of whiskey, and newsprint scattered across the desk.

She reads as: someone who documents things for a living — the camera is never set down, and the room around her (map, travel books, newsprint) tells the same story the camera does. Warm and unhurried in expression rather than performative, like someone genuinely more interested in what's in front of her lens than in being looked at herself.

## Backstory Summary
**Confirmed direction, 2026-07-20:** Imelda began her career as a genuinely popular social-media starlet in Mexico — the kind of following built on real, mutual affection between her and her audience, not a manufactured or cynical persona. Her path eventually took her toward investigative work: exposing real, substantiated corruption between the Mexican government and Upper Earth megacorporations. What she found was true and provable, and telling her audience about it cost her everything — she was punished for it and exiled to Antarctica following the War of Upper Earth.
