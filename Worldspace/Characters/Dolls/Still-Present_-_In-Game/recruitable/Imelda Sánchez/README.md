## Imelda Sánchez

**Role / Archetype:** Fallen social-media starlet turned investigator, now chasing one specific unsolved case
personal to her adopted city. Originally from Mexico; began as a genuinely beloved social-media personality who
loved her audience sincerely, not just strategically. Built by a surviving enclave of Abramentes Industries
(Las Indústrias de Abramentes) specifically to investigate a suspected conspiracy — one major even by Mexican
standards — she initially wanted nothing to do with that purpose and used her platform to make friends instead.
A conspiracy affecting a close friend of hers pulled her into real investigation for the first time; following
a lead got her caught, and she was headed for destruction until an unknown benefactor intervened to have her
exiled to Antarctica instead. This directly explains the camera in her Visual Description: not a hobbyist's
prop, but her actual working tool, from influencer content through to investigative documentation. **[Sourced,
2026-08-15 seed]**
**Primary Location(s):** **Rothera, Palmer subnet — confirmed 2026-08-15**, resolving the previous open
question. She fled there when the Long Night War reached Port Lockroy (where she was living and shooting the
stone-carving art at the time) and has remained ever since. Earlier Tepenia residences, in order: Sejong
(initial forced exile landing) → Palmer City (moved for the social connections and possibilities) → Port
Lockroy (living/working there when the war hit) → Rothera (refuge, present day). **[Sourced]**
**Affiliation(s):** TBD — no faction given in the seed; her Palmer-subnet history (see Design Notes) plausibly
gives her informal social capital there even from Rothera.
**Companion Potential:** Yes — **re-designated 2026-08-15 as DLC 3 (Palmer)'s first companion**, filling that
DLC's previously-open "no companion identity chosen yet" slot (`Companion_System.md`). Originally part of the
main-game 44-companion roster from the 2026-07-20 expansion; moved to DLC 3 once her seed content confirmed a
personal history and companion mechanic entirely native to the Palmer subnet. `Companion_System.md`'s DLC
companion list and main-game roster count have both been updated to match.
**Romance Potential:** Yes — per the binding "all recruitable companions are romanceable" rule
(`Companion_System.md`); thresholds TBD.

## Core Identity

**Activation Date & Origin:** Mexico; built by one of the last remaining enclaves of Abramentes Industries
(Las Indústrias de Abramentes), specifically to investigate a suspected government conspiracy — one described
as major even by Mexican standards. **[Sourced]** Precise activation date TBD.

**Enneagram / Personality Framework:** Type 7w8 — Self-Preservation Subvariant ("The Realist" wing / "Getting
Mine," "Keeper of the Castle" instinctual variant). **[Sourced type + subvariant, 2026-08-15 seed]**
- **7w8, "The Realist":** earthy, practical, tough-minded, strategic — a sense of humor that runs toward biting
  wit and a taste for the outrageous. More workaholic and more direct-to-the-point-of-bluntness than the 7w6
  wing, pragmatic about relationships (looking for an actual partner, not a romantic fantasy), comfortable being
  alone, capable of becoming jaded and callous where 7w6 stays more openly, uncomplicatedly enthusiastic. This
  is exactly the shape of "friendly-bitchy" from her vision statement — warmth with real edges, and someone who
  wants solid, conclusive proof rather than a comfortable belief (a Realist's practicality applied to her own
  investigative streak).
- **Self-Preservation instinct, "Getting Mine"/"Keeper of the Castle":** channels the type's Gluttony into
  building a network of trusted allies — practical, opportunistic, genuinely skilled at spotting an advantage
  and making things happen through the right connections. Cheerful, warm, and talkative; less idealistic and
  more cynically practical than the other two instinctual subtypes; can be more comfortable with long-term
  commitment than average for the type, partly because close relationships function as a kind of investment.
  This matches both her original social-media-as-friend-making instinct and her present partnered, hand-pulling,
  frolics-off-to-take-photos warmth.
- **Why the disillusionment tracks the type exactly:** Sevens run on Gluttony — an insatiable hunger for
  stimulating, positive experience that functions as a defense against ever fully registering pain. The type's
  own named Trap is "focusing exclusively on happiness can lead to unhappiness" — deferred pain accumulates
  somewhere out of sight and eventually resurfaces anyway. A Seven who has been nearly destroyed, forcibly
  exiled, and has since lived through a war reaching her adopted city is exactly the profile for "still tries to
  keep being optimistic, though her experiences have left her somewhat disillusioned" — the vision statement's
  own words. **[Strong Inference]**

**MACHINE Stat Profile (rough baseline, proposed 2026-08-15; CHINE stats raised +1 each per developer
correction 2026-08-15):** Might 2 / Agility 4 / Calculation 4 / Humanity 9 / Investigation 9 / Nerve 6 / Engine 7.
**Reasoning (proposal, not asserted):** the seed explicitly states she is "hopelessly useless in combat" and
has "only rudimentary technical and/or computer skills" — hence low Might, low-to-mid Agility (mobile enough to
frolic through a city, not a fighter), and comparatively low (though not lowest) Calculation, since "rudimentary"
reads closer to Calculation, the tech/analytical stat, than to Investigation, which is about noticing and
connecting things rather than computing them. Investigation sits at the top of her profile — she built an
entire second career on following leads and proving things conclusively, and her current goal is explicitly
evidentiary, not just believed. Humanity sits equally high, matching her established sincerity and warmth (the
"genuinely, not performatively" distinction the original README already drew). Nerve was set middling rather
than low, then raised further per developer correction: a 7w8 Realist is direct to the point of bluntness and
not conflict-avoidant socially, even without combat-adjacent composure. Engine reflects a sustained,
decades-plus (likely centuries-plus, given her timeframe) working life across two careers and at least one war
survived — the developer's correction reinforces that this endurance runs across the whole non-physical stat
cluster, not just Investigation and Humanity individually.

**Key Traits / Notable Perks (companion-relevant) — Sourced directly from the 2026-08-15 seed, tentative
numbers as marked by the developer:**
- Possesses only rudimentary technical/computer skills; hopelessly useless in combat.
- **As an active companion, bypasses all Charisma- and general "people skills"-oriented checks within the
  Palmer subnet DLC with no gating requirement at all** — her mere presence in the party clears those checks
  outright in that content.
- **Additionally lowers the numerical requirement for every Charisma-gating check in every interaction,
  everywhere in the game** — tentative figure: **-25%, result rounded down.** Marked explicitly tentative by
  the developer; not to be treated as locked.

## Visual Description

*(See `Reference_Images/` folder — busty-sex-doll-16 through 22.)*

Long, warm auburn-brown hair with blunt bangs, half pulled back. Dark brown eyes, soft warm makeup, a silver ear
cuff with a chain detail at one ear. Consistently shown with a compact mirrorless camera (a real Canon
EOS-style body), often held or raised to shoot. Dressed in a plaid mini skirt/shorts, a soft grey knit top with
a lace bra visible beneath an open, loosely-worn camel/tan trench coat, and knit-cuffed ankle boots. The setting
across the set is a lived-in study/office: a wooden desk, stacked books with visible travel-adjacent titles, a
vintage double-hemisphere world map on the wall, lit candles, a glass of whiskey, and newsprint scattered across
the desk.

She reads as: someone who documents things for a living — the camera is never set down, and the room around her
(map, travel books, newsprint) tells the same story the camera does. Warm and unhurried in expression rather
than performative, like someone genuinely more interested in what's in front of her lens than in being looked
at herself.

## Backstory Summary

**Confirmed direction, 2026-07-20; substantially expanded and refined 2026-08-15 from the Quick Capture seed —
this version supersedes the earlier, vaguer draft below it.**

Imelda Sánchez was built in Mexico by one of the last remaining enclaves of Abramentes Industries (Las
Indústrias de Abramentes), created specifically to investigate a suspected government conspiracy — one
described as major even by Mexican standards. In her early life she wanted nothing to do with that stated
purpose: investigation and conspiracy held no appeal for her, and she took to social media instead, purely as a
way to make friends. That following became genuine and mutual — the kind of audience relationship built on real
affection, not a manufactured or cynical persona (this matches and is now explained by the original 2026-07-20
draft's "beloved starlet" framing).

What changed her path was personal, not professional: a conspiracy within the Mexican government actually
affected a close friend of hers (specifics TBD). Following up on the resulting lead was what turned her, for
the first time, into a real investigator — using the platform and the instincts she'd built as an influencer for
a genuinely different purpose. She was caught while pursuing that lead, and was on course to be destroyed for
it. An unknown person intervened in the decision process and had her sentence commuted to exile in Antarctica
instead — she does not know who did this or why, and neither does the developer yet; this is a deliberately
open thread (see Design Notes).

She landed first in Sejong, then relocated to Palmer City for its social connections and possibilities — a
natural fit for someone who built her original following on genuine social warmth. She was living in Port
Lockroy, photographing its stone-carving art, when the Long Night War reached her; she fled to Rothera and has
remained there ever since.

**Superseded earlier draft, kept for reference:** the original 2026-07-20 summary framed her investigative turn
as exposing "corruption between the Mexican government and Upper Earth megacorporations" in general terms. The
2026-08-15 seed is more specific and personal (a friend affected, not an abstract exposé) and is treated as the
authoritative version; the two are compatible in spirit, not contradictory — the megacorp angle may still be
part of what the conspiracy actually was, just not the reason she personally got pulled in.

Full backstory detail in `Personal_Background/`.

## Design Notes & Open Questions

- **Structural placement — RESOLVED 2026-08-15:** re-designated as DLC 3 (Palmer)'s first companion, confirmed
  by the developer. Her companion mechanic (scoped to "the Palmer subnet DLC") and her entire personal
  history — Sejong, Palmer City, Port Lockroy, Rothera, never touching Concordia — are now consistent with her
  formal status rather than in tension with it. `Companion_System.md`'s DLC companion list and main-game roster
  count have both been updated accordingly. Her location work above already covers what a main-game placement
  would have left open (present-day district, general vicinity) — what's still needed is DLC 3-specific
  development (the subnet's own level design, factions, and companion tier) rather than a Concordia district
  assignment.
- **The Akina connection — significant finding, flagged 2026-08-15, handle with care:** Imelda's stated goal —
  proving there's more to "the murder in self-defense between the robot and the diplomat" — matches this
  project's own established Long Night War inciting incident almost exactly: a gynoid (now named **Akina**, see
  `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/unsure and_or special cases/TBN [SE-031 Palmer City
  self-defense Akina]/`) killed an Upper Earth diplomat in self-defense in Palmer City, and the fallout from his
  death is the actual chain of escalation that led to the Long Night War. Imelda's own years living in Palmer
  City, specifically for its social connections, plausibly put her in the right place at the right time for
  this to have become personal to her — worth developing once Akina's own precise timeline is further along.
  **Caution, per project's own Mystery = Soft-Detail-Delivery law:** Akina's file already establishes this as
  "extremely delicate, requiring the utmost care," and the canon record already fully favors Akina's account.
  Imelda's quest should not be built to actually overturn or re-litigate that account — the value here is a
  companion whose personal drive is to chase down corroborating proof and expose whatever fed into the
  diplomat's actions, not a mystery-box replacement for settled canon. Full resolution, if any, belongs in
  private design notes; what reaches the player should stay soft, ambient detail consistent with how this
  project already handles Mystery.
- **Exile mechanism, refined 2026-08-15:** the 2026-07-20 draft's open question ("blanket Falkland sweep vs.
  targeted retaliation") is now more specific, not fully closed: the seed establishes she was headed for
  destruction over being caught investigating, and an unknown intervention commuted that to exile — not a
  blanket sweep applied regardless of what she'd done. Whether this happened before, at, or after the Falkland
  Treaty's 2564 blanket robot-exile provision, and how an individualized commutation like this could exist
  alongside or ahead of that treaty, is still open and worth deciding once Mexico/Upper-Earth-era legal
  mechanisms get more general development.
- **What she exposed, and who retaliated:** still not decided — the specific conspiracy, who was affected
  besides her friend, and who came after her are all open.
- Mechanical synergies: TBD beyond the Charisma-check perks above.
- Replayability ideas: TBD.
- **Accessibility design constraint — Sourced, 2026-08-15 seed:** she should be reachable only via side content,
  structured so a violence-first playthrough will likely never encounter her, while a talk-first playthrough is
  nearly guaranteed to. Her personality and skills should stay complementary to a speech-built character even
  though such a build (e.g., 10 Humanity / 10 Nerve / 10 Investigation / 100 Speech / 100 Narrative) derives no
  actual mechanical benefit from recruiting her, since her Charisma-check bypass would already be redundant for
  that build — she should still be someone that player enjoys having around for who she is, not just for what
  she unlocks.
