# Villena Hiresvett — Personal Questline: "The Last Stage" (working title)

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted; full step-by-step design deferred until Leo/Concordia is developed enough to anchor real locations and triggers.** Full design detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** Grief for a foreclosed future — the dream that was real and is now structurally impossible. The difference between performing joy and actually having it. What you become when the original answer is permanently unavailable. Whether a smaller stage can be enough.

**Emotional Core:** She's been moving forward since the war — performing, building, staying in motion — and it's worked. The questline is the moment it stops working: something forces her to sit with the full weight of what was permanently lost, and to decide what her life means now that there's no nation left to be famous in.

**Inciting hook:** Villena was separated, in the evacuation chaos, from a bandmate/creative partner from her Palmer City days who she believes made the last transport out of Amundsen Tower without her — off-world, not to another ground city. She's never known what happened to them since. See `README.md`'s full note on how this adds a third layer to "she will never see the stars."

**Mechanism:** Villena's independence — no faction, no institutional affiliation — is a categorical block on ever formally investigating this herself. The player does it in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 non-stat world-state routes (Leo standing, her own fan network, Sagittarius, Libra, Vosora's off-world comms work, the Palmer City diaspora, a legacy item, and a Wild Child/Libra route) — see `README.md` for full detail. Per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule, this guarantees the questline can never structurally block a player from reaching her romance gate.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium-High — which of the 13 routes the player takes, and whether the reckoning lands toward Peace, Rededication, Grief, or the hidden "Other Dream" ending (see below), are real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`:
- **Peace:** she decides the city is enough — not what she dreamed, but real — and performs the same as before, but differently.
- **Rededication:** she finds a new version of purpose inside Concordia's scale (Leo's morale infrastructure, the people who need her shows) — the dream is gone but the reason to do it isn't.
- **Grief:** she fully grieves for the first time, lets it land, and the quest ends quietly with her back on a stage — the audience can't tell the difference; she can.
- **The Other Dream (hidden):** she discovers something she wants more than she ever wanted fame, and the original loss stops being the center of gravity.
