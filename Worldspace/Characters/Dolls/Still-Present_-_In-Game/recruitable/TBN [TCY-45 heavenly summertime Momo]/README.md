## Core Identity

**Origin — Signy, Tepenian Federation.** Built and "grew up" there. **[Sourced, marked prospective in the seed —
strong working assumption, not locked.]** Her original job or life situation there is undecided.

Signy is the most isolated city in the Federation — the only Tepenian city in the South Orkney Islands,
sub-Antarctic rather than fully Antarctic, no highway connection, the weakest Arcanet link in the country
(`Specs/Signy.md`). It is also the only city in her history still standing.

**Timeframe:** Activated in the later half of the Second Interwar Period; alive to the in-game present
(≈2822–2827). **[Sourced]**

### Enneagram — an unusually important case

**Type: 4w5, Self-Preservation — 55%. Undercurrent: 9w1, Self-Pres *or* Sexual — 45%.** **[Sourced, seed]**

- Major Theme: Feeling · Hornevian: Withdrawn · Harmonic: Reactive *(deterministic from Type 4)*
- Undercurrent subvariant remains undecided between Self-Pres and Sexual.

**Two things make this profile notable, and both should be preserved rather than smoothed:**

**1. She is the project's first confirmed Undercurrent** (`Worldspace/Enneagram/Undercurrents.md`, established
2026-08-11 specifically for her).

**2. The split is 55/45, not the ~80/20 working baseline** her README previously carried. Her Undercurrent is
very nearly co-equal with her main Type. She should not read as "a 4 with a faint 9 underneath" — she should
read as someone running two personalities at close to equal strength.

**The load-bearing derivation: her stated flaw belongs to the Undercurrent, not the main Type.**
**[Strong Inference]** The seed gives her weakness as *notable difficulty saying "no" and standing up for
herself.* A Self-Preservation 4 is the most stoic, least dramatic expression of its type — it endures rather
than emotes, and has no particular trouble refusing people. **A 9 is defined by exactly that difficulty**:
self-forgetting, merging with whoever is present, avoiding the friction of assertion. The flaw comes from the
45%, not the 55%. That is strong internal evidence the Undercurrent is real and weighted correctly, and it means
her central failure mode is not a 4's failure mode.

## Personality & Voice

- **Core summary [Sourced].** Slow and steady in motion, though quick in thought. She takes her time formulating
  words — partly out of consideration for the person she is speaking to, partly out of bashful shyness.
  **Claustrophobic. Values open skies.** Both traits are explicitly consequences of having been made in Signy.
- **The environmental irony, and it is permanent [Strong Inference].** Concordia is a **sealed, enclosed city**.
  A claustrophobe who needs open sky has ended up in the one place in the Federation that offers neither, and
  she is not leaving, because there is nowhere else left. This is not a crisis — it is a permanent, daily,
  low-grade discomfort she does not raise, which is exactly what a Self-Pres 4 does with pain.
- **Speech patterns:** TBD in detail, but the shape is given — pauses before answering, sentences arriving fully

## Backstory Summary

**Sourced from the 2026-08-29 seed; city context verified against `Specs/`.**

> **Framing discipline — important.** Her moves happened during the **Second Interwar Period, in peacetime**,
> in a Tepenian Federation that was a full, functioning country. **None of her relocations had anything to do
> with the war.** She was not fleeing, not displaced, not a refugee — she was a person with a life making
> ordinary choices about where to build it. The war enters her story exactly once, at the very end, in Zukelli.
> Do not write the earlier moves with any shadow of the war on them.

She was built in **Signy** and grew up there — remote, sky-open, marginal. What set her life in motion was an
absence rather than an event: **she felt the longing emptiness of not having a family the way humans have one.**

So she went looking, and over the peacetime decades the search took her across three subnets.

**Esperanza** *(Palmer subnet)* — she moved there to work as a caretaker and caregiver for human families.
**[Sourced]**

**This is the single most important city in her history, and the fit is exact.** Esperanza was not merely a city
that happened to contain families. It was founded on a compact: when the robots and humans settling the
Peninsula learned that humans had been born at Hope Bay, they deliberately established Esperanza as a community
organized around **caring for the humans who had accepted permanent, lifetime exile on behalf of the robots**,
and above all for their children (`Specs/Esperanza.md`). Its charter was that those humans, who had given up
homeland and genetic family forever, deserved to have their children looked after. Births there were civic
events, not private ones; the birth registry was the city's living proof that the sacrifice had been worth it.

**A robot who ached at having no family of her own moved to the one city in the Federation that existed to make
families whole** — and took up precisely the work that city was founded to do. Whether she chose it knowing
that, or simply followed the work and found herself somewhere that fit, is an open and very good question.

> **⚠ This resolves a standing open question in Esperanza's own spec.** `Specs/Esperanza.md:146` asks: *"given
> that the founding principle was explicitly about caring for human children, what role did robots play in the
> city's daily life? Were they caregivers, administrators, protectors?"* **She is a concrete answer** — a robot
> caregiver to human families, working inside the compact. Worth cross-referencing when that question is taken
> up properly.

**Princess Elisabeth** *(Halley subnet)* — reasons undecided in the seed; the developer's leading hypothesis is
a wish to be useful, possibly toward a clean-and-renewable-energy cause. **[Sourced as hypothesis]** **This
lands on real canon [Strong Inference]:** Princess Elisabeth is the Federation's International Polar Foundation
inheritance and its zero-emissions station, and its ruined power systems are already flagged as a candidate
questline (`Specs/Princess_Elisabeth.md`). The clean-energy motive fits the city exactly and is the strongest of
the seed's three floated options.

**Zukelli** *(Janbogo subnet)* — drawn by Zukelli's widely-known sense of community. **[Sourced]** Zukelli's
civic life ran on communal meals and a restaurant-and-performance-space culture where food and music happened
together; `District_Refugee_Diaspora_Composition.md` records communal meals as the absolute center of civic life
there. For someone whose whole search was for a family-shaped belonging, Zukelli was the closest thing the
Federation had to an answer — and, by the seed's account, she found it.

**Then the Long Night War came, and Zukelli was destroyed. She is one of the very, very few who got out.**
**[Sourced]** She now lives in Concordia, district undecided.

### The shape of it

**Her losses arrive in two entirely different registers, and the distinction is the character.**
**[Strong Inference]**

- **Zukelli she lost to the war, present and first-hand.** That is the wound.
- **Signy, Esperanza, and Princess Elisabeth she had already left, years or decades earlier, for her own
  ordinary reasons — and then the war destroyed Esperanza and Princess Elisabeth behind her, long after she had
  gone.** She did not lose those cities. She lost the *possibility of return* to them, retroactively, without
  being there.

The second kind is the stranger grief, and the less written one: places she had every reason to assume would
still be there if she ever wanted to go back, quietly deleted from the map while she was living somewhere else.
Every door she ever left open was closed from the far side. Only Signy — the one she left first, and the one
that survived — still stands.

**And she is painfully, acutely lonely now** **[Sourced]** — despite a life spent accumulating found family.
The people are gone or scattered. What she has left of them is objects.

## The Hab — her defining scene

**[Sourced — the developer's Personal Vision, recorded closely because it is the character in one image.]**

A climate-controlled domicile hab. She is watering **a plant grown from Signy seeds** — her birthplace, kept
alive by hand indoors. Above an artificial fireplace hangs **an item she carried out of Zukelli**, one of a very
small number of things she managed to take. Her clothing and furniture are adorned with **small, light,
distinctive objects once owned by friends and adopted family** met across her life and given to her — each small
and light enough to carry without difficulty.

**The derivation that matters [Strong Inference]:** *everything she owns is portable.* Her life furnished
entirely in things that can be picked up and carried — a home that is also a packed bag, and a woman who keeps
**a museum of people instead of the people.**

Two further notes, both usable:
- **The plant is the only living thing in the arrangement**, and it comes from the one city that did not die.
- **An artificial fireplace** in a climate-controlled hab is not for heat. It is a hearth she does not need,
  which is the whole character in one object.

## Design Notes & Open Questions

- **District placement — derived argument, offered not decided.** The seed floats Cancer, Leo, Taurus, Scorpio,
  Sagittarius. **[Strong Inference]** On the sourced traits alone, **Sagittarius (The Frostlands) has the
  strongest claim nobody has yet made for it**: she is claustrophobic and values open skies, and the Frostlands
  is Concordia's outer-ring frontier district — the one place in a sealed city whose identity is the outside.
  **Cancer** is the strongest *thematic* fit (a caregiver by trade; the district that absorbs destroyed-city
  grief; Zukelli is one of Cancer's largest diaspora populations). **Taurus** fits the domestic-hab imagery.
  **Leo** fits least well on the evidence — nothing in the seed suggests performance or display, and the visual
  read explicitly contradicts it. Not resolved here.
- **Goal (broad-scale desire) — open by developer statement, not oversight.** Candidate directions, options only:
  *(a)* find and reassemble whatever remains of her scattered found families; *(b)* establish one permanent
  thing that cannot be carried — the deliberate opposite of everything she owns; *(c)* return something from
  Zukelli to the surviving Zukelli diaspora, making her private museum public; *(d)* something unrelated to her
  history entirely, which would be the more surprising choice.
- **Do not let the war creep backward into her biography.** See the framing note in Backstory. Three of her four
  moves are peacetime decisions by a woman with a life; only the last is war.
- **The 55/45 Enneagram split should not be normalized.** It is a genuine outlier against the ~80/20 baseline
  and is why her flaw comes from her Undercurrent rather than her Type.
