# "Momo" *(working name — placeholder)* · TCY-45 — Loyalties & Beliefs

**No faction loyalties are assigned, and none are invented here.** The seed gives no affiliations. What it does
give is enough to describe *how she holds loyalty*, which is more useful at this stage than guessing *to whom*.

---

## Primary loyalties

**To people, not institutions.** **[Strong Inference — from the sourced life record]**

Her entire biography is a sequence of moves toward *communities and individuals*: human families in Esperanza, a
possible cause at Princess Elisabeth, a communal culture in Zukelli. She never appears to have been loyal to a
government, a faction, or an ideology. She was loyal to whoever was in front of her, and she relocated across
three subnets to keep being so.

**To the memory of specific people, permanently.** Her home is furnished in objects given by friends and found
family, kept and carried across a lifetime and out of a destroyed city. She does not let go of people, including
people who are gone.

**To Signy, in a quiet and complicated way.** She grows a plant from Signy seeds indoors, by hand, in a
climate-controlled hab. It is the only living thing in the arrangement, and it is from the only city of hers
still standing. **[Sourced object; Strong Inference on meaning]** Whether this is homesickness, guilt, defiance,
or simple habit is open and worth deciding deliberately.

**To herself — barely, and this is the problem.** Her stated flaw. See below.

## Conflicts in loyalty

**[Strong Inference]** The central one is structural rather than situational:

> **She cannot refuse, so her loyalties cannot be prioritized.**

Someone with notable difficulty saying "no" and standing up for herself does not get to choose between competing
obligations — she accumulates all of them and then absorbs the cost of the conflict privately. This means:

- **She will not tell anyone when two loyalties collide.** The conflict will be invisible until it breaks.
- **Whoever asks most directly wins**, regardless of what she actually wants, which makes her genuinely
  vulnerable to anyone willing to lean on her — including the player.
- **Her loyalty is therefore easy to obtain and hard to interpret.** Earning it costs almost nothing. Knowing
  whether you actually have it is the hard part.

**The 55/45 split matters here.** Her Type 4 core has a strong sense of who she is; her nearly co-equal 9w1
Undercurrent dissolves that sense in the presence of other people's wants. She is not a person without an
identity — she is a person with a clear identity and no reliable mechanism for asserting it.

## How loyalties may shift based on player actions

**Design guidance, not canon. [Strong Inference]**

- **The meaningful axis is not whether she becomes loyal — it is whether she becomes capable of refusing.** Her
  companion arc almost certainly runs through the first genuine "no" she says to anyone, possibly to the player.
- **Two honest branches present themselves, and they should be genuine trade-offs** per the project's
  dual-outcome companion rule:
  - **She learns to refuse.** She becomes a harder, more separate, less endlessly available person — and the
    player loses the frictionless companion they had.
  - **She never does.** She remains the most agreeable person in the party, and the player has to live with
    having benefited from that.
- **Do not write the second as simply the bad ending.** A player who never pushed her toward assertion may have
  been being *gentle* rather than exploitative, and the two are hard to tell apart from the inside — which is
  precisely the interesting part.
- **She should be difficult to lose.** Making her walk away should require something quite serious, and should
  feel like a genuine achievement of cruelty rather than a threshold the player crossed by accident.
