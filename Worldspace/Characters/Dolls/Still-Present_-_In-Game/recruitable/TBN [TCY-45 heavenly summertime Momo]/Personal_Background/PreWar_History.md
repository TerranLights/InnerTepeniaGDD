# "Momo" *(working name — placeholder)* · TCY-45 — Pre-War History

**Template note:** the standard heading means "before the first global war." For her that is meaningless — she
was activated ~750 years after 2083 and ~250 years after the Falkland Treaty. Here it means **before the Long
Night War (~2812)**, which is the great majority of her life and effectively her entire biography.

**Everything in this file is peacetime.** The Tepenian Federation of her lifetime was a whole, functioning
country at peace. She is not a war story until the last page of one.

## Signy — built, and shaped

Built in Signy and grew up there. **[Sourced; prospective]** Her concurrent job or life position is **TBD** and

Signy is the Federation's most isolated city: the only Tepenian city in the South Orkney Islands, sub-Antarctic

**Two permanent traits come directly from it** **[Sourced]**:

- **Claustrophobia**
- **A need for open skies**

**[Strong Inference]** These are not decorative. A small, remote, sub-Antarctic island city with almost no
network connection is a place of physical openness and informational thinness — a lot of sky, very little
signal. She was formed by more weather than data. It is worth keeping in mind that her later moves take her
steadily toward denser, more connected, more enclosed places, and end in a sealed city.

## The motive

**She felt the longing emptiness of not having a family the way humans have one.** **[Sourced]**

This is the engine of her entire life, and it is worth stating precisely what it is and is not:

- It is **not** grief — she lost nothing to acquire it.
- It is **not** a malfunction or an implanted directive.
- It is an **absence noticed**, and then acted on, repeatedly, over decades, by someone with the patience to
  keep acting on it. **[Strong Inference]**

A Self-Pres 4 with a 9w1 Undercurrent pursuing this would not announce it or dramatize it. She would simply keep
quietly relocating toward it. That is exactly what the record shows.

## Esperanza — the closest fit, and the most important city in her history

She moved to Esperanza to work as a **caretaker and caregiver for human families.** **[Sourced]**

**The city was built for that exact thing.** Esperanza's founding was not incidental: when the robots and humans
settling the Peninsula discovered that humans had been born at Hope Bay, they deliberately established the city
around a charter — to foster and care for **the humans who had accepted permanent, lifetime exile on behalf of
the robots**, and above all their children. Those humans had given up homeland and genetic family forever; the
founding council resolved that their children would be looked after. Births there were civic events. The birth
registry was the city's living proof that the sacrifice had been worth it. (`Specs/Esperanza.md`)

**[Strong Inference]** So: a robot who ached at having no family of her own moved to the one city in the
Federation whose entire purpose was making families whole, and did the work it was founded to do. Whether she
knew the charter when she chose the city, or simply followed the work and found herself somewhere that fit, is
open — and both readings are good.

> **⚠ She answers a standing open question.** `Specs/Esperanza.md:146` asks what role robots actually played in
> a city founded on caring for human children — caregivers, administrators, protectors? **She is a concrete
> instance of the first.** Cross-reference when that question is developed.

**Deliberately unresolved:** whether the work satisfied the need or merely proximate it. Caring for other
people's families is not the same as having one, and the distinction is the most obvious available source of
why she eventually left. **The seed does not say she left unhappy, and this should not be assumed.**

**Explicit gap:** the seed marks the event path between Esperanza and her next move as **TBD**.

## Princess Elisabeth — the least-understood chapter

She moved there for reasons the seed leaves **open**. **[Sourced as open]** Developer's floated candidates:

1. A wish to be useful
2. A pull toward a clean/renewable-energy cause
3. Some other, yet-undetermined motivation

**[Strong Inference]** Option 2 has the strongest external support: Princess Elisabeth is the Federation's
International Polar Foundation inheritance and its **zero-emissions station**, the most recently built of the
founding stations, and its ruined power systems are already flagged as a candidate questline
(`Specs/Princess_Elisabeth.md`). A character motivated toward clean-energy work would fit the city precisely,
and would hand that questline a personal connection it currently lacks.

**Worth noting as a shape, not a conclusion:** Esperanza is care *for people*; Princess Elisabeth would be
contribution *to a cause*. If that is the actual sequence, it is a meaningful shift — someone who tried to solve
loneliness through service to individuals, then tried solving it through service to something larger. And then
left that too, for Zukelli, which is neither.

## Zukelli — where it worked

She moved to Zukelli, **likely drawn by the generally known Zukellian sense of community.** **[Sourced]**
Possibly compounded by an additional motive, TBD.

Zukelli's civic life ran on **communal meals** and a restaurant-and-performance-space culture in which food and
music happened together — communal meals recorded as *the absolute center of civic life*
(`District_Refugee_Diaspora_Composition.md`). It sat about 8km from Janbogo across Terra Nova Bay, close enough
to be visible from it.

**[Strong Inference]** Zukelli offered the thing the previous two cities structurally could not: not a family
she cared for, and not a cause she served, but **a community that simply included her by eating with her.** For
someone who cannot ask for things on her own behalf — her defining flaw — a culture where belonging is conferred
by showing up to a shared table, rather than by asking, is close to ideal. **She would never have had to
request it.**

By the seed's account, this is where the decades-long search substantially succeeded.
