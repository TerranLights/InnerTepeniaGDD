# "Momo" *(working name — placeholder)* · TCY-45 — War Experiences

**Her war is short and singular.** She had no war until ~2812. She was not a combatant, not evacuated in
advance, not part of any defense. She was a resident of Zukelli when Zukelli was attacked, and she got out when
almost nobody did.

---

## The one event

**Zukelli was destroyed in the Long Night War. She fled as one of the very, very few survivors.** **[Sourced]**

That phrasing is the developer's, doubled for emphasis — *"one of the very, very few."* Zukelli's survivor
population is small enough that being one is a defining public fact about her, not merely a private one.

**She carried out a very small number of items** — light enough to move with, chosen or grabbed under whatever
conditions applied. One of them now hangs above the artificial fireplace in her hab. **[Sourced]**

---

## What is deliberately not written

Almost all of it, and this is a feature rather than a deficit at this stage. The seed supplies the fact and not
the scene. **Unresolved and open:**

- **What the attack actually looked like from inside it.** Zukelli sat ~8km from Janbogo across Terra Nova Bay,
  visible from it — proximity that may matter for how the attack unfolded and what could be seen.
- **How she got out**, and whether it involved choice, luck, or someone else's decision.
- **Whether she left anyone behind**, and whether she knows what happened to them. Given that Zukelli is where
  her decades-long search for family had substantially succeeded, **this is the single highest-stakes open
  question in her entire file.**
- **What the carried item is.** Currently the most concrete unwritten object in her character.
- **Where she went between Zukelli and Concordia**, and how long it took.
- **Whether she was alone in the escape or part of a group**, and what became of them.

## Handling notes

**[Strong Inference — design guidance, not canon]**

- **Do not let this event colonize her biography.** She had a long, peaceful, self-directed life before it, and
  the great majority of who she is was formed in peacetime. A version of her that reads as primarily "a
  war survivor" flattens roughly 100+ years of a person into her worst afternoon. See the framing note in
  `Timeline.md`.
- **Her flaw and this event interact badly, and productively.** Someone who cannot say no, cannot assert
  herself, and cannot ask for things on her own behalf is exactly the sort of person who would not have pushed
  to the front of an evacuation. **How she survived when almost no one did is therefore genuinely load-bearing**
  — and if the answer is that someone else made it happen for her, that is a substantial and painful piece of
  characterization waiting to be written.
- **She does not narrate it.** A Self-Pres 4 endures rather than emotes, and a 9w1 Undercurrent avoids the
  friction of raising things. Whatever she carries from Zukelli, the player should have to earn.

## The losses she was not present for

Distinct from the above, and easy to conflate — worth keeping separate. **[Strong Inference]**

Esperanza and Princess Elisabeth were also destroyed in the war, but she had **already left both of them,
years or decades earlier, for her own unrelated peacetime reasons.** She did not lose those cities the way she
lost Zukelli. She lost the standing possibility of ever going back — retroactively, at a distance, without
witnessing any of it.

**Signy, which she left first, is the only one still standing** (`Specs/Signy.md` — survived, fully operational,
too remote to be worth striking).

Whether she has any information about the fate of the human families she cared for in Esperanza is **open, and
is probably the most emotionally direct thread available for a questline.**
