## The organizing fact

**She keeps her relationships as objects.** **[Sourced]**

Her clothing and furniture are adorned with **small, lightweight, distinctive items once owned by — and given to
her by — friends and "adopted family" met over the course of her life.** Every one is small and light enough to
carry without difficulty.

**[Strong Inference]** This means her relationship history is **already itemized and physically present in her
home**, and each object is a person, a place, and a period. This is the richest unexplored vein in her file: any
one of them can be picked up and become a thread, a memory, a quest, or a name. It also means the player can
learn her entire relational history by looking around her hab — if they think to ask about a thing rather than
about her, which suits a character who cannot volunteer.

**And she is painfully, acutely lonely anyway.** **[Sourced]** The collection is not a substitute that works.

## Relational dynamics — derived

**[Strong Inference, from the sourced Enneagram and flaw]**

- **She cannot say no.** Her defining flaw. In practice: she will agree to things she does not want, absorb
  imposition without signaling it, and accumulate obligations she never chose. Anyone in her life will find her
  extremely easy to get along with, which is a problem rather than a virtue.
- **Her agreement carries little information.** Because refusal is unavailable to her, consent and compliance
  look identical from the outside. Characters who care about her have to learn to read her silences instead.
- **She does not advocate for herself.** A 9w1 Undercurrent at 45% strength means self-forgetting is nearly
  co-equal with her sense of identity. She has no grammar for a grievance on her own behalf.
- **She bonds slowly and then permanently.** Slow, deliberate, considered in speech; decades-long residencies;
  keeps physical mementos of everyone. Nothing about her suggests she discards people.
- **She is bad at being the one who needs something** — which is the natural core of a companion arc, and the
  obvious place for the player to matter.
