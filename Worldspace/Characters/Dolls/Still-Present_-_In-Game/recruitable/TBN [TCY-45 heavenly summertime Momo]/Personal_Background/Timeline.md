## Second Interwar Period, later half (2564–2812; she falls after ~2688) — **peacetime**

> **Framing discipline.** Everything in this section happens in a Tepenian Federation that is a whole,
> functioning, peaceful country. Her relocations are ordinary life decisions by a person building a life.
> **None of them has anything to do with the war.** Do not shade them retrospectively.

- **Activation — Signy.** Built in Signy and "grew up" there. **[Sourced; origin marked prospective in the seed]**
  - Her concurrent job or life situation in Signy is **TBD**.
  - Signy shapes two permanent traits: **claustrophobia**, and a need for **open skies**. **[Sourced]**
- **The motive that starts everything.** She feels *the longing emptiness of not having a family the way humans
  have one.* **[Sourced]** This, not any external pressure, is what moves her.
- **Move 1 → Esperanza** *(Palmer subnet)*. Works as **caretaker/caregiver for human families**. **[Sourced]**
  - Esperanza is the city founded on the compact to care for the humans who accepted permanent exile on behalf
    of the robots, and above all for their children (`Specs/Esperanza.md`). Her work sits inside that charter.
  - **Gap: the event path between Esperanza and her next move is explicitly TBD in the seed.**
- **Move 2 → Princess Elisabeth** *(Halley subnet)*. Reasons **TBD**. **[Sourced as open]**
  - Developer's floated options: a wish to be useful; a pull toward a clean/renewable-energy cause; some other
    undetermined motive.
  - The clean-energy option is the best-supported — Princess Elisabeth is the Federation's zero-emissions
    station and IPF inheritance (`Specs/Princess_Elisabeth.md`).
- **Move 3 → Zukelli** *(Janbogo subnet)*. Drawn by the **Zukellian sense of community**. **[Sourced]** Possibly
  compounded by an additional motive, currently TBD.
  - Zukelli's civic life centered on communal meals and a food-and-music hospitality culture. By the seed's
    account, this is where the search substantially succeeded.

**Duration of each residency: entirely TBD.** The seed fixes only the order. The era is long enough to
accommodate decades in each city, and her Self-Pres 4 profile is consistent with long, quiet tenures rather than
rapid moves.
