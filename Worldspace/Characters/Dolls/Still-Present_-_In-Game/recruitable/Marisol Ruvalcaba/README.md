## Marisol Ruvalcaba

**Role / Archetype:** International Data Syndicate field operative — confirmed 2026-07-20. The IDS is established (if older, preliminary) lore: the faction responsible for locating, securing, and physically retrieving pre-war datashards, old hard drives, and other records from Upper Earth to build the Arcanet — dangerous, illegal work, since post-Falkland-Treaty robot presence on Upper Earth is globally illegal and human "defectors" who moved to Antarctica are forbidden from returning (see `General-Overview-Notes/aspects_of_consideration.md`). Her established combat/personality profile fits this role precisely: very skilled with a one-handed, non-revolver pistol (the same weapon-category convention already used for Favi della Torre and Seica Cenilaithe), but prefers talking her way through a situation over fighting through it — exactly the instinct a field operative on covert, high-stakes retrieval missions would need. Genuinely, deeply patriotic to Tepenia specifically — not a mercenary or thrill-seeker, someone who believes in what the missions are actually for.

## Design Notes & Open Questions
- **IDS canonicity check needed:** the International Data Syndicate is established in preliminary design notes (`General-Overview-Notes/aspects_of_consideration.md`, `Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/city-and-district_layout_-_preliminary_suggestions.md`) but predates the current, more fully-developed Gemini/Janbogo faction structure. Worth confirming it's still active canon (likely yes, as the field-operative complement to The Long Frequency's Concordia-based analysis work — see Role/Archetype above) before building her questline around it.
- **IDS founding timeline, confirmed 2026-07-20:** the IDS wasn't founded immediately after the Falkland Treaty (2564) — it took roughly one human generation to take shape (~2584-2594), which is still comparatively very early on Tepenia's overall multi-century timeline. By the game's present day, the IDS is a long-established institution with centuries of operational history — worth keeping in mind for Marisol's own backstory (she's part of a deeply entrenched, historic institution, not a scrappy upstart one) and for how she'd talk about the organization's legacy and reputation.
