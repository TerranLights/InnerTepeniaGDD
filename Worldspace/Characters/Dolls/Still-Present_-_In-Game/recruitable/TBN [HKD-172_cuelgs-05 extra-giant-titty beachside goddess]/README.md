## Personality & Voice
- **Core summary — confirmed 2026-08-11:** 9w8 Social. Genuinely warm and hardworking, earning belonging through generous, often-invisible service rather than seeking attention — the Social subtype's defining drive is an underlying sense of not quite belonging, overcompensated for by working hard, hiding pain, and rarely burdening others with how much the constant accommodation actually costs her. Makes her an unusually good leader precisely *because* the motivation is unselfish, not image-driven: she doesn't campaign, she gets pushed forward by people who trust her.
- **The central psychological throughline:** Mawson's own established robot culture draws a real local distinction between what's *staged for guests* (the Proposal Terrace, the Overlook) and what's *genuinely ours* — residents deliberately keep their own courtships away from those spaces. Layered against her own Nine-level uncertainty about belonging, this gives her a real, non-war engine: she spends her whole professional life expertly manufacturing strangers' most important romantic milestone, and has never let herself have that same staged moment for herself.
- **Speech patterns / quirks:** fluent code-switcher between Mawson's two "kinds of old" — the ceremonial St. Douglas veneration register and the warm, modern hospitality-service register — moving between them without friction, a lived personal skill rather than an affectation.
- **Behavior under stress, confirmed 2026-08-11:** per `Enneagram_Dynamics.md`'s Disintegration chart, a Nine under genuine, sustained stress moves toward average Type Six — uncharacteristically anxious and worried. This should be reserved for a real crisis, not everyday friction; the shift reads as alarming specifically *because* it's such a sharp departure from her baseline calm. Behavior in different districts not yet derived.
- **Moral tendencies & player influence:** leans toward inclusion and mediation; genuinely uncompetitive, no interest in building an image or claiming sole credit — consistent with a Nine's real discomfort with the spotlight.
- **Earliest warning sign, if ever written drifting toward a harder arc:** the Wake-up Call for Type Nine is "outwardly accommodating themselves to others" — she'd start deferring on small everyday preferences (where to eat, what plays) a little too readily, before anything bigger shows.
- **Under real security, confirmed 2026-08-11:** per the Integration chart, Nines move toward healthy Type Three — self-developing, energetic — and the source material is explicit this requires *conscious choice*, not passive drift. Her plausible arc direction: not becoming a different person, but finally trusting an ambition that isn't only in service of someone else's milestone, for the first time.

### Individuated Depth — Crossing Type-Level Foundations Against Her Specific Facts (confirmed 2026-08-11)

**Method, not just content:** the generic Type 9 tables (`Foundations_and_Practice.md`) describe a shared psychological *pattern* — the same fear/desire/passion structure any Nine doll in the roster would share. What makes it *hers* specifically is crossing each abstraction against facts unique to her file (her exact job, her exact setting) rather than stopping at the type-level statement. Demonstrated below on five of the base-level foundations, per the same technique the developer asked to see applied explicitly.

- **Basic Fear ("loss of connection, of fragmentation"), individuated:** not a fear of her own relationships ending — a structural fear that the whole delicate ecosystem of belonging she's personally built and maintains (the Long Table Hall rotation, the couples she's guided, the Guesthouse Quarter community) would come apart if she stopped being the reliable one holding it together.
- **Basic Desire ("to be at peace"), individuated:** she's professionally saturated in emotional intensity all day — proposals, weddings, arrivals — so her version of peace isn't the absence of intensity around her. It's specifically the peace of being allowed to *witness* rather than to perform intensity herself.
- **Lost Childhood Message ("Your presence matters," never adequately heard), individuated:** her literal job is professionally confirming, to strangers, that their presence together at this exact moment matters enormously — that's what a wedding *is*. The wound and the competence are the same fact, not two things sitting side by side: she's exceptional at her job precisely because she knows, from the inside, exactly how much that message is worth to someone who's never received it.
- **Core Identification (draws stability from disengaging from intense impulses; resists recognizing her own strength), individuated:** standing at the emotional center of a stranger's most overwhelming life moment, repeatedly, without being swept into it, is a genuine trained capacity — not everyone could do her job. Her own self-image means she wouldn't describe it that way. Asked what she does, she'd say "I just help people," with no framework for naming the specific, unusual skill she exercises constantly. That gap is hers alone.
- **Passion (Sloth — resisting being touched by reality), individuated, and the sharpest hook of the set:** the real occupational hazard isn't laziness — it's the possibility that she's gone years standing at the emotional peak of other people's defining moments and let every single one leave her completely unmarked: professionally warm in the room, genuinely untouched afterward, water off a stone. A companion arc built around the first time, in years, someone actually gets through would land hard specifically because of how much practice she's had at not letting things through.

### Alone / Off-Duty — What She Does Unobserved

**Confirmed 2026-08-11.** She's Social-subtype dominant, meaning she's professionally "on" for the group all day — alone is the one register she isn't performing for anyone, and the sharpest test of who she actually is underneath the job.

- Her weaker, mostly-unused Self-Preservation register plausibly surfaces here instead of Social: classic Nine "narcotization" behaviors (puttering, small absorbing tasks, zoning out) she'd never indulge in front of a guest, precisely because no one's there to be warm for.
- Per the Withdrawn Hornevian group's Inner Sanctum material (a private mental space of idealized memories rather than present, real problems): alone, she plausibly replays and quietly turns over the beautiful moments she's witnessed professionally — not possessively, just an internal echo of her own career running on a loop, since it's the material she has the most of in her own head.
- Physically, the two-front boundary-holding effort behind her chronic low-grade fatigue (`Instinctive Center` detail) is something she'd only actually let go of alone — an unglamorous, unposed looseness that would never happen in front of a guest or coworker.
- **A specific image the crossing produces that isn't in any Enneagram table at all — it only exists from combining the tables with a fact unique to her file:** her own courtship stays deliberately away from Mawson's staged spaces (the Overlook, the Proposal Terrace) per the city's own established local custom. Nothing stops her from visiting one of those spaces *alone*, off-hours, unstaged, with no couple to guide. The same ground that's professionally "not hers" becomes something she visits privately, on her own terms.
- **Still genuinely open, and this is the natural place to resolve it:** whether her baseline calm is healthy-range (alone, she relaxes *further* into the same ease) or professional composure over something quieter (alone, something flatter and a little sadder surfaces once there's no one left to be warm for). Both are fully consistent with everything above; they lead to a different character voice, so worth an explicit, deliberate choice rather than leaving it to default.

## Backstory Summary
**Confirmed 2026-07-20, role confirmed 2026-08-11:** she worked in Mawson's hospitality industry — a genuine, well-established economic sector for the city (`Specs/Mawson.md`'s Economy & Industry section: "Tepenia's go-to honeymoon destination for newly-married human-robot couples," a resort-town character alongside its hub-logistics role). **Her actual career arc is Option 4 (see Role Possibilities below):** she began as a one-on-one Honeymoon Field Guide, escorting individual couples through Mawson's signature experiences (most notably the Prince Charles Mountains overlook tradition) for years, building deep expertise and real emotional investment in the human-scale side of the work. Over time, having lived the hospitality-vs-hub-logistics civic tension firsthand — her sector's work keeping the city's resort reputation alive going comparatively unrecognized next to "serious" hub-logistics coordination — she became her sector's de facto advocate, without ever leaving the personal guide work that earned her the standing and passion for the cause in the first place. The advocacy is earned couple-by-couple, not theorized from an office, and never sought for its own sake.

**Everyday life & texture, confirmed 2026-08-11 — all directly grounded in Mawson's own established Local Robot Culture material:**
- **Plausibly lives in or near the Guesthouse Quarter**, among Mawson's family-run, generational hospitality businesses — community by proximity and shared trade, not isolation.
- **A likely presence at the Long Table Hall's Nation Nights rotation** — Mawson's actual established robot-kinship institution (every represented nation's dishes served nightly, side by side, no single identity gatekeeping what counts as "local"). Given her Social-Nine drive to earn belonging through service, she plausibly does real, invisible labor keeping that rotation running smoothly, not just attending it.
- **Steeped in Mawson's Glitch-Coolant culture**, which lands in the bohemian/cosmopolitan-variety category, fed by the city's own celebration cycles (arrivals, anniversaries, weddings) and toasting culture — professionally *and* personally present in her life, with room for an interesting wrinkle where she's quietly a little tired of a ritual that's also her workday.
- **A warm, uncompetitive connection to Calethina** (see Relationships & Hooks below) — a Nine wouldn't feel territorial about someone else carrying a version of the same original "welcoming host" ambition into a very different form.

**Not yet decided:** why or when she left Mawson (if she has), and where she is at the time of the game's events. Explicitly left open by the developer 2026-07-20 rather than defaulting to the war-displacement shape used for most of the existing roster — still open even with role and core personality now settled, and still worth a non-war reason for genuine variety from the rest of the cast.

## Design Notes & Open Questions

### Role Possibilities — 4 candidates originally printed to file 2026-07-20; **Option 4 chosen 2026-08-11**

**DECISION, 2026-08-11: Option 4 (Combination: Field Guide → Sector Advocate) confirmed.** Kept all four
write-ups below for design history and reasoning, per how they were originally developed.

#### Option 1 — Honeymoon Field Guide
**Premise:** She personally escorts newlywed couples through Mawson's signature honeymoon experiences — most notably the already-established Prince Charles Mountains overlook tradition (`Specs/Mawson.md`, timed to whichever of polar night or midnight sun is in season during the stay). Her work is deeply personal and relationship-driven: not administration at a distance, but walking individual couples through the most significant days of their post-wedding lives, one pair at a time.

**Personality implications:** this kind of work gives her an intimate window into hundreds of couples' relationships at their most tender — genuine warmth, real emotional intelligence, and plausibly a certain wistfulness or vicarious quality, since she's professionally present, over and over, for other people's most romantic moments. Strong potential connection to the game's own core creative questions (robot consciousness, love between robots and humans) — as a destination explicitly serving human-robot honeymoon couples, she would have witnessed countless such relationships up close, repeatedly, across years.

**Trade-off:** intimate and characterful, but narrower in scope — doesn't on its own explain any civic or political entanglement (like the hospitality-vs-hub-logistics tension below) without an added reason.

#### Option 2 — The Course-of-Events "Character A" Hospitality Figure
**Premise:** She steps directly into an already-written but unnamed role from Mawson's own Course of Events files (`Mawson_07_The_Hub_That_Chose_Kindness.md`, `Mawson_10_What_The_Guests_Never_See.md`) — a proud hospitality-sector veteran with a specific, textured grievance: real resentment that her sector's work (keeping Mawson's resort reputation, and by extension its outside relevance, alive) goes comparatively unrecognized next to the "serious" work of hub-logistics coordination that keeps the whole subnet running. Not petty — she genuinely believes the civic imbalance is real and worth contesting, not just a personal slight.

**Personality implications:** gives her real civic/political texture — not just "warm hospitality worker," but someone with an actual institutional cause, willing to advocate, who's thought hard about her own sector's undervalued importance. Directly reuses pre-existing, already-developed lore (both Course of Events files) rather than inventing anything new from scratch.

**Trade-off:** less intimate/romantic in flavor than Option 1 on its own — reads more as civic advocate than beloved personal guide. Pairs very well combined with Option 1 or 3, less strong standing entirely alone.

#### Option 3 — General Visitor Liaison / Hospitality Administrator
**Premise:** A broader, more institutional role — not honeymooners specifically, but coordinating hospitality for all of Mawson's visitors (researchers, traders, officials, tourists of every kind). Less a personal guide, more the person who keeps the whole hospitality apparatus running smoothly across many simultaneous stakeholders.

**Personality implications:** a more managerial, organized, diplomatically-skilled figure, used to juggling many needs at once — an interesting ironic parallel to hub-logistics work, since her own job would itself be a kind of logistics, just for hospitality specifically. Less one-on-one intimacy, more systems-thinking.

**Trade-off:** the least personally intimate of the four on its own, closer to a job title than a fully realized character unless given a specific distinguishing hook — best suited as a base layer combined with one of the more personal options rather than standing alone.

#### Option 4 — Combination: Field Guide → Sector Advocate — ✅ CHOSEN 2026-08-11
**Premise:** She began her career as an honeymoon field guide (Option 1), personally escorting couples for years and building deep expertise and emotional investment in the individual, human-scale side of the work — and over time, having lived the exact hospitality-vs-hub-logistics tension described in the Course of Events files firsthand, became her sector's de facto advocate or spokesperson (partially stepping into the Option 2 role) without ever fully leaving the personal guide work that gave her the standing and passion for the cause in the first place.

**Personality implications:** the richest option — gives her both the warm, intimate, relationship-driven texture of Option 1 and the civic-minded, principled advocate energy of Option 2, with the advocacy feeling earned and grounded (lived guide-by-guide and couple-by-couple, not theorized from an office) rather than abstract.

**Trade-off:** requires the most backstory to earn convincingly — a real career arc rather than a single job title — more design work to fully realize, but also the most rewarding payoff of the four.
