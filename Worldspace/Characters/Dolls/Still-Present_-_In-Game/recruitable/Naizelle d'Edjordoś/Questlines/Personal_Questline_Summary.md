# Naizelle d'Edjordoś — Personal Questline: "The Recovery" (working title, pending)

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted; full step-by-step design deferred until Pisces/Concordia is developed enough to anchor real locations and triggers.** Full design detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** What you become when the world that made you no longer exists. The difference between surviving and living — she's rebuilt survival; whether she's rebuilt a life is a harder question. What music means to someone who stepped back from it after everything changed. The compound as security and as limitation.

**Emotional Core:** She was embedded in her home city's Heavy Metal/Industrial scene before the war — not as a performer, but as someone for whom the community and the music were a complete world. A 5w6:Self-Pres doesn't process loss openly; she hoards what's left of things rather than grieving them out loud. The questline is about what she's been quietly holding onto since before the war, and whether she can actually do anything with it now.

**Inciting hook:** a recording she played on, likely still circulating as an informally-traded copy independent of the master lost when her home city was destroyed — an artifact rather than a lost person, deliberately distinct from Favi's, Villena's, and Ayako's shape. See `README.md` for the full note.

**Mechanism:** her defining "Low-Profile Movement" trait is a categorical block — pursuing this personally would mean exposing herself to old scene networks, directly endangering the compound community that depends on her staying unnoticed. The player does it in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 non-stat world-state routes (Virgo, Pisces trade networks, a surviving scene community, Leo, Gemini, a legacy item, a former scene acquaintance, and a Wild Child/Pisces route) — see `README.md` for full detail. Per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule, this guarantees the questline can never structurally block a player from reaching her romance gate.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium-High — which of the 13 routes the player takes, and whether the resolution lands toward Recovery, Acknowledgment, Release, or Ground (see below), are real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`:
- **The Recovery:** something from her pre-war life — the recording, specifically — is recovered and brought into her present. Not restored, but present. She keeps it.
- **The Acknowledgment:** she finally lets someone in — not the compound community, who are already there, but in the specific way she's never let anyone in since before the war.
- **The Release:** she actually plays guitar for someone — not occasionally, not privately — for the first time since the war in any meaningful sense.
- **The Ground:** she chooses the compound deliberately, consciously, for the first time — not as the place she ended up but as the place she wants to be.
