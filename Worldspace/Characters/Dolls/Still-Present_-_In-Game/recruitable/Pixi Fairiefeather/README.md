## Visual Description

*(See `Reference_Images/` folder — Bezlya series.)*

Bubblegum-pink hair in a chin-length bob with blunt center-parted bangs, held with two small navy-blue bow clips. Grey-blue eyes, a small white star-shaped gem applied just under one eye. A white satin-and-lace two-piece set — a halter-neck lace-up top with an open midriff cutout, over matching lace-trimmed bottoms — with a navy-blue ribbon bow at the collar. Sheer white opera gloves, one hand holding an oversized white feather-and-rose wrist corsage. White lace-trimmed thigh-high stockings. Long navy-blue ribbon streamers trail through every shot, along with an oversized paper flower prop, all staged in an all-white studio setting.

She reads as: deliberately whimsical and fairy-tale-coded — the name "Fairiefeather" isn't incidental, the whole visual (feathers, ribbons, bows, a star under the eye, an ethereal all-white palette) is built around a delicate, storybook aesthetic rather than a grounded professional or combat one. Her expression across the set is composed and knowing rather than innocent, though — she's aware of the tableau she's part of, not lost in it.
