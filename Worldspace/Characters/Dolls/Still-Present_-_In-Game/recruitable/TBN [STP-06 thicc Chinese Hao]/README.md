**Timeframe — Sourced, confirmed by developer 2026-08-24:** the seed places her activation "late during the
Second Interwar Period" (per project canon, 2564–2812, ending at the Long Night War). The developer has
confirmed directly that this means late in the Federation's *peacetime* stretch — she was built while the
Tepenian Federation was still at peace and a full, proper country, i.e. **before** 2812, not after it. She was
therefore already active by the Long Night War and lived through it directly, rather than being built in its
aftermath. Robots in this setting can plausibly date back to the First Interwar Period (2083 onward) and live
for centuries, so this still makes Hao genuinely, unusually young by robot standards — likely built within a few
decades of 2812, putting her somewhere in the range of a decade to a few decades old as of the present day
(~2822–2827), against a population where multi-century age is normal. This is exactly the seed's own stated
implication ("she's actually relatively very, very young, by usual robot standards").

**7w8 Self-Preservation, as it applies to Hao specifically — Strong Inference, reasoning shown:**
- **7w8, "The Realist":** practical, tough-minded, and more direct/workaholic than the 7w6 wing — comfortable
  being alone, oriented toward tangible outcomes over open-ended possibility for its own sake. This matches the
  seed's own framing almost exactly: "warm but not jolly, loving but not bubbly, dignified though not
  necessarily always polite" is the 8-wing's grounding and directness tempering the base type's usual
  enthusiasm-first register.
- **Self-Preservation instinct, "Keeper of the Castle":** where the Social subtype channels a Seven's Gluttony
  (an appetite for stimulating positive experience) into a wide network and the Sexual/One-to-One subtype
  channels it into intensity-seeking, the Self-Preservation subtype channels it into **building and maintaining
  a genuinely comfortable, well-provisioned home** — home as the actual site of the type's pleasure-seeking, not
  a place merely passed through between outward adventures. This is a near-literal match for the seed's vision
  statement: household duties handled thoroughly, then a *specific, ritualized* self-reward (the balcony, the
  glass of coolant wine, the sunset) rather than seeking a new external stimulus for the reward itself. The
  "reasonably organized... 'mostly plan, most of the way,' while still curious about surprises" balance is also
  a textbook expression of this subtype: enough structure to keep the castle actually maintained, not so much that the
  type's native appetite for possibility gets fully closed off.
- **Why this produces a farming-and-harvesting researcher, not just a housekeeper:** a Self-Pres Seven's
  "castle" doesn't have to stop at her own front door — the same appetite for tangible, provisioning security
  can scale outward to *material security for people she cares about*, which is exactly the shape of her stated
  goal (helping orbital Tepenians "better provide for themselves"). The Ghost driving that scale-up (a friend's
  illness) is what turns an inward-facing domestic virtue into an outward-facing engineering mission.

## Backstory Summary

**Sourced, 2026-08-24 seed; refined 2026-08-24 on the origin-city point per Core Identity above.**

Hao was built in Zhongshan — Tepenia's most concentratedly Chinese-founded city, still carrying the weight of a
major national program's continuous investment — late in the Second Interwar Period, making her genuinely young
by robot standards even in the present day. Her purpose at construction is not yet determined.

What set her actual life's direction wasn't her original purpose but a close human friend of hers, born and
raised in Zhongshan, who developed an illness there. Her friend managed to flee to Tepenia's low-earth orbital
society, where the lower gravity eased the physical pain the illness caused. That departure — and the gap it
opened between what her friend needed and what Hao herself could actually do about it from the ground — is what
turned Hao toward overseeing farming research: developing more material-efficient means of farming and
harvesting food, with the explicit goal of transmitting workable schematics to that orbital society so that
Tepenians living off-world can better provide for themselves.

That mission is very plausibly what pulled her out of Zhongshan and into Davis specifically — Tepenia's
established breadbasket and its foremost ecological/limnological research city, where sheltered-agriculture and
greenhouse cultivation are the dominant daily work (`Specs/Davis.md`). Her taste for "coolant wine" specifically,
rather than any glitch-coolant variety, also tracks her Zhongshan origin: Zhongshan is one of this project's
confirmed **bohemian-variety** glitch-coolant cities (wines, whiskeys, and other refined types side by side,
versus the working-class cities' narrower-but-stronger selections — see
`Worldspace/Robot_Biology_and_Culture/Robot_Physiology_and_Cultural_Practices.md`), so her specific preference
for a wine-type coolant is a genuinely native taste, not an arbitrary detail. **[Strong Inference for this
paragraph's connective reasoning; the underlying facts — the goal, the friend, the wine — are all Sourced.]**

Full detail in `Personal_Background/`.
