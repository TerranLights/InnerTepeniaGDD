### What that city is — canon, not inference

**`Specs/Esperanza.md`:** *"Esperanza" = Hope in Spanish. Historically the site of the first human birth in
Antarctica; the only pre-exile station designed for permanent family settlement; generations of Tepenian
children were born here who had no other home.* **The city was founded on a compact — when robots and humans
settling the Peninsula learned humans had been born at Hope Bay, they deliberately established Esperanza as a
community organized around caring for the humans who accepted permanent, lifetime exile on behalf of the
robots, and above all for their children.** *Births there were civic events, not private ones.*
