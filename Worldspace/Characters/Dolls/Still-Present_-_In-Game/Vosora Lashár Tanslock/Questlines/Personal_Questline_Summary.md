# Vosora Lashár Tanslock — Personal Questline: "What the Silence Says"

**Status: substantially developed already — four fully-designed endings with an established dual-outcome perk structure. The 2026-07-20 retrofit adds the missing investigative mechanism (per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule) without touching the endings.** Full detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** What a civilization that controls its own history does when someone starts recovering what it edited. The distinction between "lost" and "hidden" — and what it means when that distinction becomes provable. The personal cost of being the person who has dangerous proof.

**Emotional Core:** The shape of what is missing in the Great Corruption is itself evidence — its size, its location in the timeline, what it clusters around. Vosora has been tracing that shape toward an answer.

**The answer (established 2026-07-20, per the binding non-malice lock on Gemini's Great Corruption):** a genuine accident — founding-crisis storage triage deprioritized certain record categories onto older, lower-redundancy infrastructure, which later failed. It looks like sabotage because the affected record types clustered on the same vulnerable storage tier, not because anyone targeted them. It's still dangerous to prove: the lost records would provide hard proof of founding-era structural inequities the wider game's non-malice audit is surfacing district by district, and proving the accident happened at all means proving Libra's own negligence — real damage to their legitimacy either way.

**Retrofit target:** her data recovery work stays entirely hers (explicitly her unique specialty) — what's newly designed is the antagonist pressuring her: not a hostile organization, but a sympathetic, genuinely convinced Libra official acting from a mix of protective instinct and institutional self-interest. The mechanism also replaces the old "protect her during the vulnerable step" framing with a non-escort structure, consistent with the binding constraint established for Ji-Eun Kim.

**Mechanism:** three compounding reasons form the categorical block — divided attention risking the fragile recovery, tipping off Libra that their pressure provoked a response, and (most importantly) that openly antagonizing a Libra official would destroy the institutional credibility every one of her four endings depends on. The player investigates in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 non-stat world-state routes (The Long Frequency, Michelle Stanton, a sympathetic Libra official distinct from the antagonist, Gemini/Janbogo, Capricorn — via her own pre-war Amundsen Tower construction logistics career, her own flagged Kunlun connection, a legacy item, and a Wild Child/Janbogo route using a new "computational/systemic side-effect" flavor tied to WC-3's Truth Markets lore) — see `README.md` for full detail.

**Player Agency Level:** High — which of the 13 approaches the player takes to protect the investigation, and which of the four already-designed endings it resolves toward, are both real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already designed in `README.md` and the main character README's Design Notes (four-branch dual-outcome perk structure, "No Good Endings"-compliant), content updated 2026-07-20 to match the accident-not-sabotage resolution:
- **Publication:** the truth — accident, Libra's negligence, and the structural inequities the lost records would have proven — goes public. A political earthquake, but permanent personal exposure for Vosora.
- **Leverage:** the records are held privately over the officials who suppressed the investigation — quieter, but entangles her indefinitely with the power she's leveraging against.
- **The Shape of the Gap:** full recovery is impossible, but the shape of what's missing is published as its own document — no smoking gun, so implicated figures can more easily evade accountability.
- **The Wrong Answer:** what she recovers is worse than the theory precisely because it's mundane — no conspiracy, just negligence and institutional self-preservation. Nothing external gets resolved; the only gain is what she and the player carry together, privately.
