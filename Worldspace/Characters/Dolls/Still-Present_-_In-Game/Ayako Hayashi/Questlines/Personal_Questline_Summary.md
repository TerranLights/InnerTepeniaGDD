# Ayako Hayashi — Personal Questline: "[Quest Title — working title "The Unfinished Garment," not final]"

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction only, charted 2026-07-20.** Full step-by-step design is deliberately deferred until Concordia (and specifically Cancer district, where the Red Spiral is headquartered) is developed enough to root plot beats in real, established places rather than placeholders — same discipline as Calethina's own questline deferral. This section exists to give future design work a real throughline to build from, not to lock in specifics.

## Overview
**Themes:** Grief transmuted entirely into vocation, versus grief actually faced. Precision as both a genuine gift and a shield. What it means to finally finish something made in loss — or to consciously choose to leave it unfinished, honestly, rather than as an open wound.

**Emotional Core:** Ayako has spent years being unshakeable for other people — the shyness that governs her every other relationship simply does not exist in a medical crisis, because the cost of hesitation is a lesson she already paid for once. The questline's core movement is someone finally being present for *her* the way nobody was present for the man she couldn't save — not by rescuing her from her grief, but by being competent without taking over and patient without prying, long enough that she can choose, on her own terms, whether to actually feel what she's spent years converting directly into action. Her Type 4w5's core pattern (per her own file: "processes grief by turning it into action rather than expression") is the thing under examination, not a flaw to be fixed — the ending should respect that this coping style *worked*, without pretending it was ever the whole answer.

**Inciting hook (narrowed 2026-07-20, to support the mechanism below):** a current Red Spiral case echoes the shape of what happened to him closely enough that she can't treat it with her usual clean detachment — a new patient or situation whose circumstances parallel the original accident. This is the strongest of the original three candidates (a person surfacing, an object surfacing, or this) because it's the one that gives the categorical block below real, specific teeth rather than staying purely emotional.

**Categorical block (established 2026-07-20, per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule):** the Red Spiral's cross-district neutrality — the thing that lets them "go where they are needed regardless of politics" — almost certainly rests on an internal rule against members pursuing cases where they have an undisclosed personal conflict of interest; that's the kind of firewall a faction this trusted would need to actually earn that trust. Ayako has never formally disclosed her personal connection to this shape of case. Protocol requires her to recuse — investigating it herself, even quietly, even using her own considerable Investigation (9), would breach the same rule that gives the Red Spiral its access everywhere, and put both the current patient's care and her own standing at risk. **This is a structural exclusion, not an emotional one** — she is not merely reluctant to look into it, she is barred from doing so through the very faction resources that would otherwise make this trivial for her. The player has no such restriction and can pursue the parallel in her place.

**The kept garment as the connective thread:** Her home already has a planted hook — the single complete garment on the hook near the window, made for him or in the period after losing him, which she "doesn't volunteer what it is" (see her home design in the README). A natural culminating image for this questline is the player's relationship to that garment shifting across the arc — first unremarked, then acknowledged in one sentence (already scripted in her romance beats), then, by the questline's end, either genuinely finished or consciously left as-is — an honest artifact instead of something she has to resolve to move on.

**Structural parallel worth studying when this gets full design:** several other companions' romance/quest content already runs *through* their work rather than beside it (Vosora through the Great Corruption investigation, Michelle through the Arcanet, Villena through performance). Ayako's fits the same pattern — her personal questline runs through the actual current Red Spiral case, not as a separate emotional cutscene bolted onto her medical duties.

**5 stat-based approaches to investigating the case in her place (non-build-gated, deterministic, per `Companion_System.md`):**
1. **Investigation-driven:** methodically trace the physical and documentary evidence around the current case and the original accident's circumstances, without needing Red Spiral's own restricted files.
2. **Calculation-driven:** cross-reference dates, medical details, and inconsistencies between the current case and whatever fragments of the old record exist.
3. **Nerve-driven:** directly press a reluctant witness, official, or record-holder who'd rather this stay closed.
4. **Humanity-driven:** earn the trust of someone who was there, or who's carried the memory, so they share it willingly rather than under pressure.
5. **Engine-driven:** sheer persistence — returning, following up, outlasting whatever bureaucratic or personal resistance stands in the way.

**Non-stat, world-state-based approaches (target 7–12, floor of 3; grounded in what's already established about her world):**
1. **Broader Red Spiral reputation route:** other Red Spiral members — not bound by Ayako's specific recusal, since they don't share her conflict of interest — might informally share what they know if the player has built standing with the faction generally.
2. **Leo district route:** long-term Leo residents or institutional memory (a record-keeper, a retired figure who'd remember the era) could hold a piece of what happened, reachable through district standing rather than a faction channel.
3. **Art/fashion community route:** an old contact from her pre-Red-Spiral art and fashion circle — someone who knew her before medicine, possibly even knew him — reachable through the player's own standing in that world, somewhere she herself would find it too exposing to go asking.
4. **A witness NPC route:** someone else present at, or shortly after, the original accident, whom Ayako has never had the professional or emotional access to approach directly, but whom the player could build an independent relationship with.
5. **A legacy item route:** a personal effect of his surfacing somewhere unrelated (an estate sale, a pawn shop, a district heirloom situation) — discovered through unrelated content, no check required.
6. **Gemini archive route:** if fragments of the original accident record survive in Janbogo's information networks, the player could access them there — a route Ayako's Red Spiral recusal would still bar her from pursuing even through a different faction.
7. **A sympathetic official/clerk route:** someone inside whatever institution handled the original accident's paperwork, who remembers it and carries quiet guilt, willing to help if approached directly and treated well — no stat check, purely relational.
8. **Wild Child/Cancer route (added 2026-07-20):** a player holding Wild Child status in Cancer — Red Spiral's own home district — can't be cleanly filed by Cancer's community and institutional record-keeping apparatus. An administrator or Red Spiral record-keeper forced into individualized handling of an uncategorizable case may end up pulling far more files than intended while trying to make sense of the player, surfacing the sealed case Ayako herself is barred from touching as an incidental side effect.

**Faction-antagonism pattern (per `Companion_System.md`):** not applied here — the Red Spiral has no dedicated faction file yet and no established negative relationship with another faction/district to draw on honestly. Revisit this specifically once Red Spiral's own lore is written; forcing the pattern now would mean inventing an antagonism that doesn't exist yet.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium — the emotional throughline is specific to who she is, but which of the 13 routes above the player takes, and how directly they push vs. simply stay present once the truth surfaces, are real, live choices.
