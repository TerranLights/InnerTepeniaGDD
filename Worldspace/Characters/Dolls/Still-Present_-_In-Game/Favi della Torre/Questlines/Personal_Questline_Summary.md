# Favi della Torre — Personal Questline: "The Long Watch"

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted; full step-by-step design deferred until Taurus/Concordia is developed enough to anchor real locations and triggers.** Full design detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** The cost of constant vigilance; the difference between readiness and fear. Whether trust is something you build or something you risk. What "safe" feels like to someone who has never let herself believe it. The losses you carry without knowing their full shape.

**Emotional Core:** What does a 6w5:Self-Pres do when the danger she's spent years preparing for finally arrives — and her preparation either works or definitively doesn't? The security network she built exists because she knows exactly what the absence of one costs: she went to rescue the person she loved and found him already dead. Beneath that is a second, still-unresolved loss — the Italian scientist who couldn't follow her into exile, whose death she was never told about. She carries the separation but not its conclusion.

**How the unresolved loss resolves:** see `README.md`'s full mechanism note. In short — Eyes of Gold's own intelligence reach narrows down where the record of his death is and tells Favi directly, freely, as one of their own loyal members (no reason they'd withhold it or gate it behind anyone's separate standing) — but it sits in a corrupted, post-Split-Brain fragment of the Arcanet that ordinary robot architecture can't reach, Favi included, regardless of her own high Investigation (a categorical exclusion, not a stat gap). The player, as a Bridge Unit, is the one who actually retrieves it, via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 8 additional non-stat, world-state-based routes (Gemini, Calethina, Aquarius, Pisces, Virgo, Libra, a retired archivist NPC, and a legacy item — an earlier Eyes of Gold-reputation route was removed 2026-07-20 since it didn't hold up logically) — per `Companion_System.md`'s Personal Questline Design Rule, which also guarantees this step can never structurally block a player from reaching her romance gate.

**Player Agency Level:** Medium-High — the vigilance/trust arc is specific to who she is, but whether the player's approach reinforces or challenges her guardedness, and which of the 5+ approaches they take to retrieve the scientist's record, are real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`:
- **Good:** the threat her network prepared for arrives and her preparation matters — real security through demonstrated competence, and she lets someone in fully for the first time since her boyfriend.
- **Neutral:** the threat arrives and preparation turns out to be beside the point; she grieves the gap between plan and outcome and moves forward anyway.
- **The Shape of It:** she learns the full truth about the scientist (see mechanism above); whether that opens her or closes her is left as her choice, not decided by the quest.
- **Abandonment / Hidden:** she realizes her community never needed protection as much as it needed her trust — and recognizes she's never fully given it. Whether she acts on that recognition is left unresolved.
