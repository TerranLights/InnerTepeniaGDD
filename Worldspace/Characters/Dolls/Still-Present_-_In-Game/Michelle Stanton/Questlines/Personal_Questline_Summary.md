# Michelle Stanton — Personal Questline: "What the Corruption Took"

**Status: broad-scope guiding direction charted 2026-07-20, retrofitted onto an existing but less-developed draft than Vosora's.** Her MACHINE stats, personality/voice, and traits remain placeholder (separate Phase 3 task) — this pass covers the questline only. Full detail lives in `README.md` in this folder — this file is the short-form pointer matching the format used for the other companions' broad-scope summaries.

## Overview
**Themes:** What a civilization that controls its own history does when someone starts recovering what it edited. The difference between what is lost and what was hidden. The personal cost of finding something everyone powerful would prefer stay lost. What it means when the truth turns out to be negligence and institutional self-interest rather than conspiracy.

**Emotional Core:** Was the Great Corruption an accident or something else? Resolved: a genuine accident, per the binding non-malice lock on Gemini's canon — but still dangerous to prove, since the lost records would provide hard proof of founding-era structural inequities, and proving the accident happened at all means proving Libra's own negligence.

**Cross-questline structure:** shared with Vosora Lashár Tanslock's questline — the pressure on both of them comes from Libra itself, specifically different officials each independently convinced suppression is the right call (not a hostile organization). Completing one questline does not complete or shortcut the other; see `README.md` for the full correction history.

**Mechanism:** her own established identity — slow verification as a deliberate counter-culture to Janbogo's "speed as moral value" — is the categorical block; rushing to confront the pressure would betray the exact principle her work exists to prove, and would also cost her the institutional credibility her own endings depend on. The player investigates in her place via 5 non-build-gated stat approaches (Investigation, Calculation, Nerve, Humanity, Engine) plus 9 non-stat world-state routes (Vosora, The Long Frequency, a sympathetic Libra official, Gemini/Janbogo, Aries, a legacy item, the South Pole synchronized Arcanet archive, Virgo/Undergrid, and a Wild Child/Long Frequency route using an ideological-conviction flavor) — see `README.md` for full detail.

**Player Agency Level:** High — which of the 14 approaches the player takes, and which of the four endings it resolves toward, are both real, live choices.

## Endings & Variations
Already sketched in `README.md`, content updated 2026-07-20 to match the accident-not-sabotage resolution:
- **Publication:** the truth — accident, Libra's negligence, and the structural inequities the lost records would have proven — goes public. A political earthquake across Libra, Janbogo, and possibly the Falkland Treaty legacy.
- **Leverage:** the recovered records are held as private leverage over the officials who suppressed the investigation. Quieter, more controlled consequences.
- **The Shape of the Gap:** full recovery is impossible, but the shape of what's missing is published as its own document — no smoking gun, so implicated figures can more easily evade accountability.
- **Discovery / Twist:** the records exist somewhere else entirely — confirmed as the South Pole synchronized Arcanet archive; an earlier Hawaii/Doris Morikawa connection was dropped as too speculative to use as confirmed content.
