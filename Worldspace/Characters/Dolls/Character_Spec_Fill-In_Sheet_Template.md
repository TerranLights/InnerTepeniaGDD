# Doll Character Spec — Fill-In Sheet Template

**What this is:** a reusable version of the worksheet built for Maggie Aarden
(`Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/Maggie Aarden/Companion_and_Romance_Questline_Design_Inputs.md`),
generalized so it can be copied into any Doll's own folder as a starting point for gathering her personal
details. It's organized as a set of questions with a short rationale under each — fill in whatever you have a
clear sense of under "Your answer," and leave the rest as `TBD`. Nothing here needs to be answered all at
once, and nothing here needs to be answered in order.

**How this maps onto the existing structure:** this sheet's sections feed directly into the standard Doll
folder skeleton (`README.md`, `Personal_Background/PreWar_History.md`, `Personal_Background/Relationships.md`,
`Personal_Background/Loyalties.md`, `Personal_Background/Timeline.md`, `Personal_Background/War_Experiences.md`,
and — if she turns out to be recruitable — `Questlines/Personal_Questline_Summary.md`). You don't need to write
in that structured format yourself; write in plain prose here, in your own words, and the structured files get
built from your answers afterward.

**Where to put it:** copy this file into the Doll's own folder (alongside her `README.md`) once you're ready
to start developing her, the same way Maggie's own worksheet lives in her folder. Not every section applies to
every Doll — a background-only or off-world/MIA character, for instance, may not need Section 13 (Role in the
Story / Companion Potential) filled out in any depth.

**2026-08-06 update:** enriched with fields pulled from the `TepenianUniverseTimeline` repo's own
cross-project character template (`Worldspace/Characters/z-template/README.md`), which serves as the
canonical baseline shared across all Tepenia-branded projects. Deliberately **excluded**: Original Doll
Model, Original Doll Manufacturer, and Original Doll Website Catalog Page — those belong to the eventual
standalone "Doll Wiki" site, not to in-universe personality development, per direct instruction.

## 4. Personality, in Your Own Words

Don't worry about Enneagram terminology or any other framework — just describe her the way you'd describe
someone you know: what she's like, what she cares about, what irritates her, what she avoids, how she'd react
under pressure. Adjectives, a comparison to another character, a specific behavior you picture her doing —
anything concrete helps more than an abstract label. I'll take what you give me here and propose a matching
Enneagram type/subvariant and a rough MACHINE stat baseline from it, not the other way around.

While you're in here, four more specific things worth including if you have a sense of them:
- **Her fundamental flaw or weakness** — not a cute quirk, an actual liability that can cause her real problems.
- **What she's genuinely good at** — skills or strengths, practical or otherwise.
- **What she values most** — the people, ideals, or things she cares about above everything else.
- **Any internal contradiction** — two things she wants that can't both be true at once (Maggie Aarden's own
  SP 5w6 pull between wanting to withdraw and being unable to abandon people who need her is the reference
  example for what this looks like).

## 14. Character Craft Notes

A few reflective, meta-level questions — useful for keeping the overall cast varied and intentional, not
required for her to function as a character:
- **Inspiration mix** — roughly how much of her comes from a real person you know, pure imagination, a
  famous figure or literary character, versus just intuitive "this feels right"? A rough split is fine.
- **Round or flat, on purpose** — is she meant to be multidimensional and evolving, or functional and
  single-note by design? Flat isn't a flaw here — it can be the right call for a background, comic-relief, or
  pure world-building role.
- **Anti-stereotype check** — is she deliberately subverting a stereotype some part of her design might
  otherwise invite? (Tie-in: this project already has a standing rule against national stereotyping — GPS-only
  demographic logic, no cultural-personality assumptions from a country name — so this is also a natural place
  to flag if a given nationality/origin needs a deliberate counter-note.)
