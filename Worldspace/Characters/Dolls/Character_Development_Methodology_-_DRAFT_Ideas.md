# Character Development Methodology — DRAFT Ideas

**What this is:** a running scratchpad for *what needs to be in* a future "Character Development
Methodology" — a repeatable instruction sheet for producing genuine psychological depth and inner personal
conflict for any given Doll character, distinct from the existing fill-in templates
(`Character_Spec_Fill-In_Sheet_Template.md`, `Companion_and_Romance_Questline_Fill-In_Sheet_Template.md`),
which capture *what fields* to fill in but not *how* to generate something genuinely deep for them.

**Status: pure brainstorm, not yet organized into an actual methodology.** Record candidate mechanisms and
ideas here as they come up, in whatever order they arrive. Sorting these into a real structured document —
deciding what's a pillar, what's a check, what's a worked example — is deliberately deferred until this list
has enough material to organize.

**Confirmed gap, checked directly (2026-08-08):** no character-development/psychological-depth methodology
currently exists anywhere in the repo. The closest precedent is `Forbidden_Trait_Design_Method.md`, which
derives forbidden traits from three existing inputs (Enneagram personality, personal history, personal
sensibilities) rather than inventing new criteria per character — a real, reusable discipline pattern, but
scoped narrowly to one mechanic (romance dealbreaker traits), not general inner-conflict generation.

---

## Candidate mechanisms, as of 2026-08-08

- **Enneagram-Rooted Contradiction.** Every companion already has a type/wing/instinct assignment. Inner
  conflict should be *derived* from that type's own structural core-fear/core-desire tension, never invented
  independently of it — e.g. a 5w6's conflict is never generic "trust issues," it's specifically the pull
  between withdrawal-for-safety (5) and loyalty-anxiety (6 wing). Makes the conflict load-bearing on
  something already established, not decoration bolted on afterward.

- **The Robot-Universal Friction Bank.** Now that `Robot_Universals` (the reference book at
  `TepenianUniverseTimeline/Reference/Robot_Universals/`) exists, there's a real menu of grounded,
  robot-specific friction points to draw from — none of which were usable material before that book existed:
  - the seeded-vs-emergent Personality Module tension (Robot Universals Ch.5)
  - city/locality belonging pulling against personal desire or path (Ch.13)
  - a mentor/mentee bond lost, betrayed, or never had (Ch.11)
  - an outlier Gen/Mark identity — a robot whose generation doesn't match her own city or peer group (Ch.14)
  - what the trust-bond stakes of romance actually cost a robot specifically, not generic jealousy (Ch.10, Ch.17)
  - a vulnerability tied to the real, narrow definition of integrity/death (Ch.4)
  Pick one or two per character, not all of them — specificity is the point, not a checklist every Doll runs
  through identically.

- **The Anti-Stereotype / Round-vs-Flat Check.** Already a labeled field in the Character Spec sheet, but not
  yet a real discipline step. Formalize it as an actual pass: does this conflict read as *this specific
  person's* problem, or could it be swapped onto any Doll of the same Enneagram type without anyone
  noticing? If the latter, it isn't done yet.

- **The Branch-Justification Check.** Ties to `Companion_System.md`'s existing Dual-Outcome Companion Perk
  law (2-5 mutually exclusive perks/questline, genuine trade-offs). The inner conflict should be *why* a
  companion's questline branches diverge the way they do — not a flat character with game-mechanical
  branches bolted on after the fact, unconnected to anything she actually struggles with internally.
