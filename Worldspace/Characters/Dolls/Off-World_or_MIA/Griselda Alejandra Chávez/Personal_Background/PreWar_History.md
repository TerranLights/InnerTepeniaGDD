# [Character Name] — Pre-War History

(Background before the first global war, early activation if applicable, human creators/owners, initial purpose, etc.)

* Was originally built as a blacksmith robot in the Republic of Sonora — the Arizona desert region of post-2083-war "New North America"
* Exiled under the Falkland Treaty's blanket provision, but not immediately — she left for Tepenia only once news of the treaty reached Sonora, some time after its June 21, 2564 signing
* Settled and set up her smithy in Belgrano (Halley subnet, Coats Land/Weddell Sea coast) — her city of residence in Tepenia, not her origin — using her blacksmithing skills to craft musical instruments (specializing in pianos)
* Developed a personal signature of using whalebone in place of wood for her instruments. She did not cut or shape the whalebone herself — she sourced it already cut and shaped from someone else (identity TBD) — but incorporated it into the pianos and other instruments she built, alongside her own metalwork
* Shipped finished instruments by sea across the Weddell Sea from Belgrano to Esperanza (the one Palmer subnet city on the Antarctic Peninsula mainland, not an island, and itself Weddell Sea-facing), then onward via highway to Palmer City, feeding its musical and performance culture
* Used her knowledge of metalwork and repairs to keep her own physical body under constant maintenance
* Managed to catch a ride up Amundsen Tower into low-earth orbit
* Initially lived aboard one of the early low-orbit stations. Later joined one of the expeditions to Mars
* Set up shop establishing a scrapyard and trading business inside of Deimos where she continues to live and work today
