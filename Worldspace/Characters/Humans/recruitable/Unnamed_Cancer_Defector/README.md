**Role/Archetype:** The single highest-esteemed Upper Earth ex-military defector living in Cancer — one of
the narrow set of "proven wartime loyalty" defectors permitted to live and work there per
`Upper-Earth_Defectors/Upper_Earth_Defectors_-_Main_Summary.md` (Cancer allows this only for defectors who

contempt almost everywhere else in Concordia specifically because of what he used to be. Useful precisely
*because* of that past: knows how Upper-Earthers think, how to navigate touchy Upper-Earth-related
situations, firearms and survival skills, and is a strong source of Upper Earth lore and perspective the
player likely can't get anywhere else.

## Core Identity
**Origin:** Human, Upper Earth-born, ex-military. Defected during the Long Night War and proved his loyalty
to Tepenia through wartime action — specifics TBD (what he did, for whom, under what circumstances).

**Age, established 2026-07-24 (approximate, by design):** at the time of the Long Night War (2812) he had
already accumulated a modest amount of military fighting and field-survival experience — a young soldier, not
a raw recruit, but not a hardened veteran yet either. Since the game's present day sits roughly 10-15 years
after the war, he's currently in his **mid-to-late 30s, possibly reaching into his early 40s.**

**Confirmed 2026-07-24 — likely carries one or two chronic injuries from warfare and military training.**
Not yet specified which injuries or how they manifest mechanically, but worth building into his physicality,
voice, and possibly a Trait or two once those get designed.

**Enneagram / Personality Framework — confirmed 2026-07-24:**
- Type: **1w2 (Social Subvariant)** — a principled reformer whose defection reads as a genuine moral
  rebellion against an unjust war, not mere self-preservation; the Social subtype's "how things ought to be
  done" energy applied to the war itself, not to one relationship or his own survival. The 2-wing gives him a
  real, if sternly-expressed, service/helper impulse — consistent with how he's earned his (limited) place in
  Cancer specifically.
- Major Theme: Instinctive (Gut Triad — standard for Type 1)
- Hornevian Group: Compliant (standard for Type 1)
- Harmonic Group: Competency (standard for Type 1)

**MACHINE Stat Profile — confirmed 2026-07-24:**
- Might: 6
- Agility: 6 — steady and disciplined rather than nimble; competent, not acrobatic.
- Calculation: 6 — solid tactical thinker, not a genius data-cruncher.
- Humanity: 6 — real underlying care (the 2-wing's genuine service impulse), expressed through principled
  action rather than open warmth, consistent with a Type One's controlled, sometimes stern surface.
- Investigation: 7 — his single most valuable trait as a companion: reading people and situations is exactly
  what navigating touchy Upper-Earth-related situations requires, making this his highest non-headline stat.
- Nerve: 10 — the ceiling of the scale; exceptional mental resilience and willpower, exceeding even Lyuba
  Baranova's existing "highest Nerve gate in the roster" (`Companion_System.md`).
- Engine: 9 — extraordinary operational endurance, matching his field-survival background.
