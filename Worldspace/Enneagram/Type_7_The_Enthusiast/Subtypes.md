# Type Seven — Subtypes

**Sources:** *Wisdom* (Riso & Hudson) cross-referenced with *Complete Enneagram* (Chestnut). See
`README.md` for provenance/distillation notes and the real-world example names for each Wing.

## The Wings

### 7w6 — The Entertainer

**Healthy:** productive and playful, holding a real belief in life's goodness and the joy of existence — more
curious and positive than 7w8, with quick minds, cooperative instincts, and real organizational ability that
lets them accomplish a great deal with apparent ease. Natural fits for show business, media, or public
relations.

**Average:** fast-talking, witty, and genuinely engaging, with high energy that lifts others' spirits — but can
lose focus and become scattered, with less follow-through than 7w8. To the extent insecurity is present, this
Wing can take on a revved-up, manic quality; craves connection, dislikes being alone, and has real conflicts
between the pull toward "greener pastures" and the fear of losing what they already have.

### 7w8 — The Realist

**Healthy:** genuinely enjoys the material world, combining quickness with real drive — often leads to
material success and positions of prominence. Earthy, practical, tough-minded, strategic; a sense of humor
that runs toward biting wit and a taste for the outrageous.

**Average:** applies energy across many directions at once — multitasking, sometimes "multicareering" — more
aggressive than 7w6, with genuine willpower and drive behind meeting their own needs. More workaholic than the
other Wing, pragmatic about relationships (looking for an actual partner rather than a romantic fantasy),
comfortable being alone, and direct to the point of bluntness — can become jaded and callous where 7w6 stays
more openly enthusiastic.

## The Instinctual Subtypes

Both books track the same underlying split — Gluttony channeled into three different domains: security through
alliance (Self-Preservation), service that consciously resists gluttony (Social), and idealization of an ultimate
connection (Sexual).

### Self-Preservation Seven — "Getting Mine" / "Keeper of the Castle"

Channels Gluttony into building a network of trusted allies — a kind of surrogate family the Seven typically
occupies a privileged position within. Practical, opportunistic, and genuinely skilled at spotting an
advantage — good at networking, finding a deal, and making things happen through the right connections.
Cheerful, warm, and talkative, resembling a "playboy" or "playgirl" archetype in its love of pleasure, food,
and sensory enjoyment, with a real underlying self-interest running through even its most generous-seeming
alliances (Naranjo's summary: "I will serve thee, and thou will serve me"). Less idealistic and more cynically
practical than the other two subtypes, often skeptical of religion or belief systems generally; can be more
comfortable with long-term commitment than the other subtypes, partly because close relationships function as
a kind of investment — resources to call on later, if needed.

**Distinguishing from Type Six:** can display a fearful or even paranoid streak at times, but this isn't its
regular mode — the baseline temperament stays relentlessly positive rather than anxious.
**Distinguishing from Type Eight:** when the Social instinct runs second, this subtype can look people-oriented
and impulsive like an Eight — but a genuine survival-level fear or anxiety runs underneath, even when it rarely
surfaces consciously, which an Eight's own core motivation doesn't share.

### Social Seven — "Missing Out" / "Sacrifice" (Countertype)

Flagged as the **Countertype** — the version of the type that runs against its own core grain, consciously
resisting rather than indulging Gluttony. Where the other two subtypes pursue more, this one works to be pure,
altruistic, and of service — deferring personal desire in favor of an ideal, often taking on real responsibility
within a group or cause and genuinely giving more to others than to themselves. The underlying ego reward is
real, if usually unacknowledged: a hidden hunger for recognition of the sacrifice itself, since needing to be
seen as good functions as its own form of gluttony, just redirected. Genuinely idealistic and visionary — a
Seven-flavored New Age optimism about a better, freer, more peaceful world — though this idealism can also
function as an "intellectual drug," an activating story that papers over an underlying fear that losing the
idealism would mean sliding into apathy and emptiness. Enthusiasm and a genuine desire not to cause pain can
sit in real tension with a persistent fear of commitment.

**Distinguishing from Type Two:** both are enthusiastic, joyful, and genuinely service-oriented. This Seven
remains fundamentally self-referencing rather than other-referencing — the desire to help stems from resisting
self-interest, not from needing external approval the way a Two's does, so this Seven usually still knows what
they themselves want, even while choosing to set it aside.
**Distinguishing from Type One:** both can look genuinely "pure." A One's standard is internally generated;
this Seven's goodness is oriented toward social consensus and applause — an ideal of purity validated from
outside, not derived from within.

### Sexual Seven — "The Neophile" / "Suggestibility"

Channels Gluttony into idealization itself — a genuine hunger for things of "the higher world," embellishing
ordinary reality and viewing life as it could be rather than as it actually is. Dreamers by temperament, more
"heavenly" than earthy, prone to falling in love intensely and quickly, with real, disproportionate attention
paid to whatever's positive in a given moment or person. The name "Suggestibility" points at genuine mental
flexibility, but also at a real gullibility — trusting, easily infatuated, prone to seeing people and situations
in more beautiful terms than the evidence strictly supports. Verbose and expressive, drawn to wit, wordplay,
and ironic humor, often testing limits through charm and seduction rather than direct confrontation. Despite
the "Sexual" label, this subtype's core hunger is less for literal sex than for an idealized, almost mystical
sense of ultimate connection — sexuality itself often stays more mental than physical. Genuinely dislikes the
mundane, finding routine tasks disproportionately hard to bear compared to the effortless pleasure of simply
imagining something instead.

**Distinguishing from Type Four:** shares real romantic intensity and a longing for love. This Seven's
imaginative fantasy stays fundamentally upbeat and idealizing rather than melancholic — optimism rather than
longing is the dominant note, even when both types are equally absorbed in an inner emotional world.
