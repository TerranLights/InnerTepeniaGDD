## Overview

Nines build a life around never having to be the reason the peace breaks — a strategy so complete that it
frequently costs them contact with their own preferences, desires, and even their own sense of being a separate
self. Both books trace this to a childhood in which asserting a want or a position felt like it risked real
connection, teaching the child that the safest, most loved version of themselves was the one who took up the
least room. What follows is real and valuable — an unusually inclusive, unflappable, genuinely mediating
presence that other types come to rely on — but the underlying mechanism both books converge on is that Nines
don't so much resolve inner conflict as *sleep through* it, maintaining calm by quietly declining to fully show
up in their own lives. Ironically, the very self-forgetting meant to preserve connection to others is what
slowly erodes it, since a Nine who isn't known to themselves can't ultimately be known to anyone else either.

**In the stories both books use as touchstones:** Homer's *Odyssey* gives Nines the Lotus-Eaters — a pleasant,
harmless people who have entirely lost their capacity to want anything, having numbed themselves with a
self-deadening flower until even the idea of "home," of a true self worth returning to, no longer registers.
Odysseus's own men fall under the same spell and have to be forcibly dragged back to the ship, tied to the
rowing benches until the compulsion passes. Dante's *Inferno* places the Slothful in the same muddy river Styx
as the openly Wrathful — but where the Wrathful visibly tear at each other above the surface, the Slothful can
only be detected by bubbles breaking through the mud, sighing an unintelligible hymn that never quite reaches
the surface as actual words. Both images land the same point: the Nine's anger and desire aren't absent, only
buried deep enough that even the Nine themselves can lose track of where they went.

## Who This Type Looks Like

Real-world figures the source material itself cites as recognizable examples of each Wing (see `Subtypes.md`

  Loren, Whoopi Goldberg, Ringo Starr.
- **9w1, The Dreamer** (more idealistic, reserved, quietly principled): Abraham Lincoln, Queen Elizabeth II,
  Carl Jung, Audrey Hepburn, Walt Disney.
