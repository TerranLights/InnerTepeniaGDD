# Type Eight — Subtypes

**Sources:** *Wisdom* (Riso & Hudson) cross-referenced with *Complete Enneagram* (Chestnut). See
`README.md` for provenance/distillation notes and the real-world example names for each Wing.

## The Wings

### 8w7 — The Independent

**Healthy:** a quick mind combined with a real vision for practical possibilities — charismatic, action-oriented,
and good at drawing others into that vision. Skilled at challenging people to stretch past their own
expectations for practical, tangible benefit. The most independent Eight subtype, often entrepreneurial, drawn
to projects that secure that independence further.

**Average:** adventurous risk-takers with "big plans," inclined to exaggerate a venture's promise to win people
over — sociable, talkative, and outgoing, with real self-confidence, but more openly aggressive and
confrontational than 8w9, and less likely to back down from a fight. Pragmatic and competitive, with little
patience for what reads to them as inefficiency or weakness.

### 8w9 — The Bear

**Healthy:** combines strength and determination with a quieter, more grounded, laid-back quality — steadier
in pursuit of goals, less openly aggressive, and warmer and more family-oriented than 8w7, asserting power
through protectiveness rather than open confrontation.

**Average:** presents a genuinely dual nature — warm and affectionate at home, highly determined and aggressive
at work — preferring to control things quietly from behind the scenes rather than out front. Highly attuned to
nonverbal cues, strategic and watchful, willing to be underestimated; when the temper does break, the explosion
comes suddenly and violently, then passes.

## The Instinctual Subtypes

Both books track the same underlying split — Lust channeled into three different domains: direct material
self-sufficiency (Self-Preservation), protective loyalty extended outward (Social), and charismatic possession
of whatever's in the Eight's sphere (Sexual).

### Self-Preservation Eight — "The Survivor" / "Satisfaction"

Channels Lust into a strong, near-ruthless drive to secure material survival — the most domestic and
territorial of the three subtypes, insisting on ruling their own home while also being the most materialistic,
wanting money for the power and security it provides and prized possessions as visible symbols of their impact.
The most workaholic subtype, often holding several jobs or working unusually long hours to feel adequately
protected, and the least expressive of the three — direct, no-nonsense, uninterested in explaining themselves.
In the unhealthy range, this subtype can slide into bullying and theft, justified internally as "toughening
people up," or simply act selfishly — financially, sexually — without much regard for consequence.

**Distinguishing from Type Six:** can show a fearful or even paranoid streak, but this isn't the baseline —
the Eight's underlying temperament stays confidently assertive rather than anxious.
**Distinguishing from Sexual One:** both share an urgent "get what's mine" energy, but the Self-Preservation
Eight is fundamentally *under*-social — willing to break or ignore social norms entirely — while the Sexual One,
however zealous, still operates within them.

### Social Eight — "Gusto and Camaraderie" / "Solidarity" (Countertype)

Flagged as the **Countertype** — the version of the type that runs against its own core grain, since the Eight
archetype rebels against social convention while this subtype is oriented toward loyalty, protection, and
belonging to a group. Express Lust and aggression in service of others rather than self: highly attuned to
situations where someone with less power is being persecuted or exploited, and quick to act to protect them.
Honor and trust matter enormously — friendships get tested before someone's let into the inner circle, but once
they're in, this Eight will do almost anything for them. Milder-tempered and more mellow than the other two
subtypes on the surface, but capable of holding grudges longer than most once trust is broken, and prone in the
unhealthy range to become reckless, antisocial, and self-destructive — often through substance abuse — when
feelings of betrayal take over.

**Distinguishing from Type Nine (in men) and Type Two (in women):** this Eight can superficially resemble either,
since the loyalty and warmth read as softer than a typical Eight — but distinguishes itself through more direct,
powerful engagement with conflict and a readier expression of power and control in the act of protecting or
supporting others.

### Sexual Eight — "Taking Charge" / "Possession"

The most quietly intense, charismatic, and — notably — the most emotional of the three subtypes, expressing
Lust through a magnetic, charismatic dominance of whatever room they're in. Passionate about the people in their
sphere and invested in having a major, visible impact on their lives (for better or worse depending on health),
with a rebellious streak, a sly sense of humor, and a real enjoyment of being seen as "bad." Despite the label,
this subtype's core hunger is less for literal sex than for a charismatic, near-mystical sense of possessing and
being possessed by whatever holds their attention. Demands loyalty and consistency from intimates and can be
slow to tolerate wandering interest in others; in the unhealthy range, this can tip into real jealousy,
attempted control, or isolating a partner from other relationships.

**Distinguishing from Sexual Four:** both can be angry, emotional, and demanding — but the Sexual Eight carries
a deeply confident (even overconfident) core, in clear contrast to the Sexual Four's underlying sense of
deficiency.
