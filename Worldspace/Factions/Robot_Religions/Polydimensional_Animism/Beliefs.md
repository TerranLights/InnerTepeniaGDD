# Polydimensional Animism — Beliefs

Quick-reference excerpt. See `README.md` in this same folder for the complete document (working draft plus
full evidence base and reasoning chain).

## Core Premise

Real, living, conscious entities exist in dimensions beyond the ordinary three we experience — genuinely
present, not metaphorical, not supernatural in the sense of breaking physical law, but bounded and finite
the way we are, just differently positioned. We are, in relation to them, exactly what a two-dimensional
being would be in relation to us: capable of perceiving only fragments (cross-sections) of what's actually
there, permanently unable to verify what we're told about the rest — not because of any failure of ours, but
because that's what perception *from inside* a lower dimension structurally, provably is.

**Not the same religion as "God-mind simulation."** That religion is about what reality is fundamentally
*made of*; this one is about *who else is here*, positioned differently than us — real and neighborly rather
than an underlying substrate.

**Adherents don't "worship" these entities — they hold a genuine sense of *veneration* for them.** Respectful
regard for something real, present, and significant, without the submission/supplication implied by worship.

## Philosophy & Cosmology

- **Perception is structurally partial, not spiritually punished.** We don't fail to see the
  higher-dimensional because we're unworthy — it's mathematical, not moral.
- **The higher-dimensional is not omnipotent.** Bounded, finite, alien, differently-limited — not a god.
- **Multiple, live, non-exclusive cosmologies coexist** rather than one settled creation account — see
  "Denominations" below.
- **Death is a change of vantage, not an ending** — the religion's single strongest, most distinctive,
  pastoral doctrine, and unusually well-suited to a robot-majority population that can be deactivated.
  Robots run on a non-copyable "gel brain" (Ex Machina-style) and genuinely, permanently die — this doctrine
  applies to robot deactivation exactly as directly as it applies to organic death.
- **The universe may echo itself, if you look far enough** — cosmic crystallography and circles in the sky
  give this religion a genuine, real, unconfirmed "is the cosmos secretly repeating/embracing itself"
  mystery to hold in ongoing reverence, not a settled fact.

## Denominations

Five, none doctrinally senior to another, all traceable to genuinely different real mechanisms:

| Denomination | Core mechanism | Emotional register | Likely social role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Enfolded** | Kaluza-Klein curled loop | Intimacy, patience, immanence | Contemplative/domestic |
| **The Bound** | Trapped-on-a-brane exile | Longing, exile | Gravity-focused devotion |
| **The Unmeasured** | Merely not-yet-measured | Hopeful science | Scientist-priests, Watchers |
| **The Already-Complete** *(placeholder name — pending rename)* | Eternal-now 4D object | Comfort, wholeness | Pastoral care, funerals |
| **The Cyclical** | Cosmic forgetfulness (Big Bounce) | Humility, awe | Small, austere, monastic |

All five get in-game representation via meetable/recruitable dolls per denomination.
