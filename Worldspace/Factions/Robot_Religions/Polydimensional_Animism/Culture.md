# Polydimensional Animism — Culture, Practitioners, Places & Visual Identity

Quick-reference excerpt. See `README.md` in this same folder for the complete document.

## Key Practitioners / Archetypes

- **The Geometer-Priest** — Scholarly track, likely Unmeasured or Enfolded.
- **The Vision-Seeker** — Visionary/trance track, likely Enfolded or The Bound.
- **The Watcher** — Patient, research-embedded, likely Unmeasured; plausibly holds a real day job at an
  observatory or research station.
- **The Pastoral Figure** — Grief/comfort-focused, likely Already-Complete; serves adherents who've had a
  disbelieved encounter and need support processing it.

## Strongholds (candidates, not locked in)

- **Watchers** → **Kunlun** (confirmed) — science/observation identity fit; human Watchers excluded there
  only because of Kunlun's own separate rule against human presence, not the religion's own stance.
- **The Enfolded** → **Scott**, **Mawson**, **Esperanza** — domestic warmth, romance/hospitality, family
  devotion.
- **The Bound** → **Byrd**, **Belgrano** — isolation/exile made literal, hard-work/garage-culture devotion
  through labor.
- **The Already-Complete** → **Concordia's "Ossuary Quarter"** district — a Goth ossuary community, memorial
  gardens, contemplation halls already built around this denomination's territory. No second candidate
  proposed yet.
- **The Cyclical** → **Signy**, **Princess Elisabeth** — plainest/quietest city in Tepenia; a waystation
  role sheltering pilgrims en route to the holy mount of Dome Fuji.

## Visual & Sonic Identity

**Not yet designed in detail.**

- **"Ornament falls away toward the center"** — sacred architecture inverting the usual "more sacred = more
  ornate" instinct: richly detailed outer spaces giving way to progressively plainer, more minimal inner
  sanctums.
- **8-sided or 24-faceted, multi-symmetrical sacred geometry** — integral to the denomination's art and
  building design broadly, including devout dolls' own player-homes, not just formal spaces.
- **The cross-section image itself** — flagged as too vague as currently written; deferred for future
  review.
- **Each denomination's aesthetic inherits from its stronghold city/district** rather than being invented
  independently of geography (see the per-denomination sketch in `README.md`).
- **Sonic identity specifically is genuinely unstarted** — nothing proposed yet.

## Connection to Gameplay / Factions

Any questlines are very much optional side content, never main story. Primary touchpoint: lore delivered
through companion dolls themselves, one or more per denomination.

- **Holiday hook**: `Worldspace/National_Holidays.md`'s underdeveloped fourth category (celestial/
  faction-specific observances) is a confirmed, natural slot for a Watchers' rite or an 8/24-timed ceremony.
- **Ecumenical bridge to Adinkra Codex**: a real, grounded (not contrived) overlap point exists via
  supersymmetric quantum mechanics functioning as a topology proof technique — confirmed as worth writing
  in, flagged for possible future expansion, not built out yet.
- **Persecution/social reception**: revised from "bureaucratic suppression" to simply being regarded as
  **an oddity** — mildly eccentric, a curiosity, not dangerous or discrediting. No secret cabal, no invented
  villain.

## Early Access vs. Launch Scope

In Early Access, this religion appears only as a handful of examinable buildings — surface-level,
broad-strokes information only. Full dialogue paths, companion belief-explanations, and optional side-content
questlines are all Launch-scope.
