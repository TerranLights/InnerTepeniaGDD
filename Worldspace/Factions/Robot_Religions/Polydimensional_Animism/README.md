## Core Premise

Real, living, conscious entities exist in dimensions beyond the ordinary three we experience — genuinely
present, not metaphorical, not supernatural in the sense of breaking physical law, but bounded and finite
the way we are, just differently positioned. We are, in relation to them, exactly what a two-dimensional
being would be in relation to us: capable of perceiving only fragments (cross-sections) of what's actually
there, permanently unable to verify what we're told about the rest, and — per the source material's own
repeated insistence — not because of any failure of ours, but because that's what perception *from inside*
a lower dimension structurally, provably is.

**This is not the same religion as the simulation-theory religion ("God-mind simulation"), despite some
surface resonance** (both eventually gesture at "pattern over substance" at their outer edges — C6). The
simulation-theory religion is about what reality is fundamentally *made of* (information, running on/as an
unreachable consciousness). Polydimensional Animism is about *who else is here*, positioned differently than
us, real and neighborly rather than an underlying substrate.

**Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21: adherents don't "worship" these entities — they hold a genuine sense
of *veneration* for them.** This is the precise word for the religion's posture: respectful regard for
something real, present, and significant, without the submission/supplication implied by worship. It's
consistent with (and now the guiding term for) the "not omnipotent" premise below and the devotional register
described under "Practice."

## Philosophy & Cosmology

- **Perception is structurally partial, not spiritually punished.** The core mechanism (A1–A4) is
  mathematical, not moral — we don't fail to see the higher-dimensional because we're unworthy; we fail to
  see it the same way a circle-cross-section fails to see the whole sphere passing through it. This keeps
  the religion's whole posture closer to humble science than to guilt-based faith.
- **The higher-dimensional is not omnipotent.** C1/C2 are load-bearing: whatever's out there is bounded,
  finite, alien, differently-limited — not a god. Worship-as-submission is the wrong register; see "Practice"
  below.
- **Multiple, live, non-exclusive cosmologies coexist rather than one settled creation account** — see
  "Denominations." This mirrors real-world religious traditions that hold several creation accounts in
  active tension rather than resolving to one, and directly reuses the pattern already established for the
  sibling simulation-theory religion (which splits into Nihilism/Absurdism/Fatalism/Sartrism over one shared
  premise).
- **Death is a change of vantage, not an ending** (see Extrapolation 1, Part 4 below) — arguably the
  religion's single strongest, most distinctive, most usable pastoral doctrine, and unusually well-suited to
  a robot-majority population that can be deactivated.
- **The universe may echo itself, if you look far enough** — cosmic crystallography and circles in the sky
  (F1–F2) give this religion a genuine, real, unconfirmed "is the cosmos secretly repeating/embracing
  itself" mystery to hold in ongoing reverence, not a settled fact.

## Practice

**Two parallel, equally legitimate tracks** (Extrapolation 6): the **Scholarly/Diagrammatic** (mathematics
and geometry as devotional practice, direct engagement with genuinely unsolved real problems as a form of
prayer) and the **Visionary/Trance** (altered-perception states, closest to the religion's own original
real-world shamanic/animist touchstone). Neither is senior to the other.

**Devotional register is veneration, not worship** (Extrapolation 3; confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21) —
respectful regard and courtesy toward a possibly-present, genuinely-limited neighbor, not supplication or
pleading before a throne.

**Candidate routine ritual (shelved, not confirmed): sincere, deliberate failure** (Extrapolation 7) —
attempting a real, mathematically-impossible-in-3D feat (untying a sealed knot, interlinking solid rings,
mirror-reversing a shell) with full knowledge and open acknowledgment that it cannot succeed, as embodied
humility rather than a claimed miracle. **Deprioritized by the developer 2026-07-21 as a *routine, recurring*
practice**: the religion's own doctrine already implies these acts are impossible in 3D, so re-enacting that
impossibility doesn't read as something adherents would plausibly do regularly. **However, the same
"sincere, known-impossible attempt" logic has now been confirmed as this religion's wedding rite** (see
below) — it just fits a one-time life-event, not routine devotion. Still open: any *other* recurring ritual
practice, if one is wanted beyond the wedding rite and the Watchers' ongoing observation.

**The Watchers** (Extrapolation 9) — a real, ongoing, patient observational practice: scanning astronomical
and gravitational data for the signature of a cosmic string, a repeating cosmic-crystallography pattern, or
matching circles-in-the-sky in the CMB. A generations-long watch that may never resolve, by design — the
waiting is the point, not a guaranteed payoff.

**Wedding/pair-bonding rite, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — promoted from an earlier parking-lot
idea, corrected 2026-07-21:** the rite centers on **two interlocking rings carved from a single original
piece of wood (or metal, depending on the couple/region)** — not two separately-carved rings physically
joined afterward (that version was corrected as genuinely impossible even as a symbolic gesture, since
there's no partial-credit attempt at it). The finished rings appear impossible to a naive observer — two
solid, unbroken loops, already interlinked, no visible seam or cut — but the "impossibility" is resolved by a
real, learnable craft technique, not magic. **This is the actual thematic core of the rite**: it materially
embodies the religion's own epistemic stance (what looks impossible from inside an ordinary vantage has a
real, non-supernatural explanation once the fuller structure is understood), and doubles as a near-literal
expression of the Already-Complete denomination's own teaching — two lives that were, from a vantage neither
party can themselves occupy, never truly separate at all. Open to any pairing the faith recognizes as equal,
including human-robot pairings, given this denomination's warm, human-inclusive membership norm.

**Social structure, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21:** adherents have dedicated places of meeting, but
no required regular schedule (no weekly-Sunday-equivalent obligation) — gathering is social as much as
devotional, a way for the devout to meet each other and build friendships within the faith, on top of the
ritual practice itself.

**Human membership, confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21: lovingly open to human disciples as the norm, not
the exception.** Unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism (itself an outlier among Tepenia's religions on this point —
Kunlun/Dome Fuji forbid humans entirely), Polydimensional Animism actively and warmly welcomes human members,
and humans are considered fully equal to robots within the faith. This is a meaningful point of contrast
worth keeping visible if the two religions are ever shown side by side.

# Part 2 — Points 1: Perception, Concealment, and the Nature of Higher-Dimensional Entities

Distilled, point-form reference organizing the evidence base by theme rather than by source. Nothing here is
new invention; every point traces back to a real source.

## A. The Core Perceptual Mechanism

- **A1.** A being of dimension N can only ever perceive a cross-section of an (N+1)-dimensional object — it sees the intersection grow, shrink, and vanish, never the whole. (Zach Star; exoScience; Space Cabal — independently confirmed three times.)
- **A2.** This produces a one-directional visibility asymmetry: the higher-dimensional observer sees everything about the lower-dimensional world at once; the lower-dimensional world can never reciprocate or verify what it's told. (Zach Star.)
- **A3.** Both "higher dimensionality" video sources turn this observation on the audience directly and explicitly: *we* are the lower-dimensional beings in relation to whatever's one (or several) levels up. Not an inference this project has to make — the sources do the religious framing themselves. (exoScience; Space Cabal.)
- **A4.** There is a real, named gap between formally *knowing* something is true (via mathematics) and being able to *perceive or intuit* it. Our brains are "not really made to understand" higher-dimensional motion even when we can prove it mathematically. (Zach Star.)
- **A5.** Mathematics itself is described, by a real physicist, as "an extra sensory perception organ" — a sixth sense, alongside the ordinary five, that reaches past the limit of instrumentation. (Gates lecture — technically Adinkra Codex territory, but the *mechanism* — math as a distinct perceptual channel — is reusable for Polydimensional Animism's own practice.)
- **A6.** Higher-dimensional beings could access sealed, locked, or hidden things without opening or breaking anything — simply by approaching from a direction the lower-dimensional world has no access to. (exoScience; Space Cabal; independently re-confirmed by the *Flatland* Sphere's own cupboard demonstration.)

## B. Why It's Hidden — Three Structurally Different Mechanisms

**These are not redundant restatements of each other. They are three genuinely different real physical/mathematical proposals for concealment, and they carry different theological weight.**

- **B1. The Enfolded/Curled mechanism (Kaluza-Klein).** The extra dimension isn't far away — it's curled into a tiny closed loop at every single point of ordinary space, standardly assumed to be Planck-length (10⁻³³ cm). "Leaping into" it would mean vanishing from ordinary perception, traveling a short loop, and reappearing exactly where you started. (Kaku; Greene's garden-hose analogy; the general fiber-bundle definition from Nakahara.)
- **B2. The Bound/Trapped mechanism (branes and the Bulk).** Our entire three-dimensional world could be a thin membrane ("3-brane") floating in a much larger space (the "Bulk"). Ordinary matter, light, and every force except gravity are physically stuck to that membrane and cannot leave it. Gravity alone moves freely through the Bulk. (Randall.)
- **B3. The Unmeasured mechanism.** Extra dimensions don't have to be inconceivably tiny — some proposed versions could be up to a tenth of a millimeter and still have gone undetected, not because they're beyond comprehension but because our only available probe (gravity) has never been precise enough to rule them out. (Zwiebach.)
- **B4.** A structurally different *flavor* of hidden dimension exists in supersymmetry's own anticommuting coordinates (θ² = 0) — not "more of the same space, further away," but a different *kind* of direction entirely, one that cancels itself when doubled. (Superspace textbook — belongs conceptually to Adinkra Codex, but worth remembering as a fourth structural option if this religion and that one are ever brought into dialogue.)

## C. The Nature and Limits of Higher-Dimensional Entities

- **C1.** Even the highest-dimensional beings described in any source are explicitly **not** unlimited or infinite. "Higher dimensions don't remove limits. They simply change the type of limits." A 5D being isn't trapped in one timeline, but is still finite and still part of a larger structure it can't step outside of. (exoScience.) **This is load-bearing** — it's what keeps this religion animist (real, bounded neighbors) rather than sliding into omnipotent monotheism.
- **C2.** If life exists on another brane, it would be genuinely alien — different physical laws, different senses, "nothing like our own." The only force necessarily shared between our world and theirs is gravity. (Randall.) **The single strongest "real living entities in higher dimensions" material found in the entire research effort.**
- **C3.** "Three dimensions is not the default. It is a very specific configuration with very specific properties" — remove one dimension and atoms fly apart; add one and planetary orbits become impossible. Ordinary 3D reality is one particular, narrow, bounded shell of existence, not the ground truth of everything. (exoScience; Space Cabal.)
- **C4.** You are already a completed four-dimensional object. Your entire life — every position your body has ever occupied — is a single, already-complete shape when viewed along the time axis, not a sequence unfolding. A being that could see the fourth dimension "would not watch you grow up; they would see your childhood and old age simultaneously." (exoScience; Space Cabal.) **This is the strongest available afterlife/comfort doctrine in the whole collection — see Extrapolations.**
- **C5.** A fifth dimension could be the axis of all possible timelines — every choice that could have been made producing a real, coexisting branch, grounded in real many-worlds quantum theory (Hugh Everett, 1957). (Space Cabal.)
- **C6.** At six and seven dimensions, the very concept of "a body" stops applying — what could carry across such radically different realities is not physical form but "abstract structure or information," pattern rather than substance. (exoScience; Space Cabal — this is also where Polydimensional Animism's own material independently echoes the *separate* simulation-theory religion's core "It from Bit" move, without the two religions being the same tradition.)

## D. Sacred Numerology — Real, Not Arbitrary

- **D1.** The number of possible "regular"/Platonic-solid-equivalent forms peaks at 6 in the fourth dimension, then drops to and stays at exactly 3 forever, for every dimension from the fifth on. The deep structure gets *simpler*, not more complex, as you go up. (Zach Star.)
- **D2.** Kissing numbers and optimal sphere-packing are fully, rigorously solved only in a handful of specific dimensions — notably **8** and **24** — while most others, including ones as low as 5, 6, and 7, remain genuinely open, unsolved mathematics. A real basis for treating specific dimensional layers as uniquely harmonious or "sacred" compared to their neighbors. (Zach Star.)
- **D3.** There are exactly **three** possible geometries for a homogeneous, isotropic universe — Euclidean (zero curvature), spherical (positive), hyperbolic (negative) — a structurally different triad from the dimension-count material, worth keeping distinct. (Adams & Franzosa.)
- **D4.** String theory requires **nine** space dimensions (ten with time) for internal consistency — later refined to **ten** space dimensions (eleven total) by Witten in the 1990s — and no one, including the experts, has an intuitive explanation for why that specific number. Bosonic string theory (a different, older variant) requires exactly **26**. Real numbers, honestly un-understood even by the field's own practitioners.
- **D5.** Global supersymmetric multiplets cap at N ≤ 4 — a hard mathematical ceiling on possible "extensions." (Superspace textbook — Adinkra Codex territory, but relevant if the two religions' numerology is ever compared or contrasted.)

# Part 3 — Points 2: Cosmology, Historical Precedent, Persecution, Proof, and Vocabulary

## E. Creation / Cosmology Mechanisms — At Least Five Genuinely Different Real Candidates

**Do not collapse these into one. Different real research programs propose structurally different origin mechanisms. Real-world religions built on multiple live cosmological traditions (e.g., competing creation accounts within one broader tradition) are a genuine, well-precedented pattern — this religion doesn't need to pick exactly one.**

- **E1. Unfurling.** All dimensions may have started out equally tightly curled; in a big-bang-like event, only three "unfurled" to their present vast scale while the rest stayed small. A difference in *outcome* (who unfurled, who didn't), not a difference in kind. (Greene.)
- **E2. Raw pre-spacetime weaving.** Spacetime itself may be "woven" from a colossal number of strings all vibrating in one coherent pattern. Before that weaving, there is a raw, structureless state with no space, no time, and — explicitly, in the source's own words — no coherent notion of "before" at all. Ordinary language breaks down describing it. (Greene.)
- **E3. Cosmic forgetfulness / the Big Bounce.** Our universe may be the rebound of a prior, now-collapsed universe — a quantum "repulsive force" (the same kind of effect that keeps an electron from collapsing into an atomic nucleus, scaled up) halts total collapse just short of a singularity and reverses it into expansion. Crucially, almost all information about the prior universe — possibly even its physical laws — is lost in the bounce. Real physicist Martin Bojowald's own name for this: "cosmic forgetfulness." (Baggott.)
- **E4. Symmetry-breaking as the origin of manifest reality.** *(Adinkra Codex territory, but worth cross-referencing.)* If the deepest structure is perfectly symmetric/unbroken, its vacuum energy is exactly zero; the moment any part of that symmetry is disturbed, energy becomes positive — i.e., the "breaking" of an original perfect order is what produces the observable, energetic, particle-filled universe at all. Not a fall from grace so much as the literal mechanism of creation.
- **E5. Zero dimensions as generative origin, not absence.** A single point with zero degrees of freedom is described, independently, by two sources as "the seed from which geometry grows" — "not somewhere you can stand," but the thing everything else grows out of. A first-cause concept that is explicitly *not* empty nothingness. (exoScience; Space Cabal.)

## F. The Universe's Own Possible Self-Echo

- **F1. Cosmic crystallography.** If the universe is finite and "wraps around" on itself (e.g., a 3-torus — literally built by gluing the opposite faces of a cube together), you would see the same home galaxy repeated over and over, receding along every direction light can travel, at regular, characteristic distances. A real, published, proposed observational test (Lachièze-Rey, Lehoucq, Luminet, 1996). (Adams & Franzosa.)
- **F2. Circles in the sky.** The same idea applied to the Cosmic Microwave Background — the oldest light in the universe. If the universe wraps around and the CMB's own light-sphere is large enough to reach past that boundary, it intersects itself, producing matching pairs of identical temperature-pattern circles visible in two places in the sky at once. Also a real, proposed, still-unconfirmed search methodology (Cornish, Spergel, Starkman, Weeks). (Adams & Franzosa.)
- **F3.** Both F1 and F2 remain genuinely unconfirmed as of their sources' writing — a real, big, testable claim about the ultimate shape of everything, paired with honest "we don't know yet, but we're looking."

## G. Real Historical Precedent — People Who Already Believed This

- **G1.** A real 19th-century tradition (spiritualism) independently arrived at "spirits are literally fourth-dimensional beings" as a serious proposed solution to a real conceptual problem (if spirits are solid enough to move objects, why can't we see them?). Proposed by Cambridge Platonist Henry More (1671), popularized by astronomer Johann Carl Friedrich Zöllner (1878). Real named proponents, not invented ones. (Rucker.)
- **G2.** Real, historically-coined vocabulary exists for the two additional directions a fourth dimension would add: **"ana"** and **"kata"** (coined by Charles Hinton) — playing the role for a fourth spatial direction that "up"/"down" play for the third. (Rucker.)
- **G3.** Henry More's real concept of **"spissitude"** — a proposed physical property ("hyperthickness" in the fourth dimension) distinguishing a living body from a dead one, invisible from within ordinary 3D space but physically real. (Rucker.)
- **G4.** Zöllner's real, actually-attempted experimental protocol for proving 4-D spirits via the medium Henry Slade: (a) tying knots in a string whose ends were already sealed shut; (b) interlinking two solid wooden rings each carved from a single, different piece of wood; (c) reversing the handedness (mirror-imaging) of spiral snail shells. All genuinely impossible in ordinary 3D space without cutting or breaking something — but trivial for a being with access to a fourth spatial direction. (Slade was almost certainly a fraud who switched objects — but the *tests themselves* are real, rigorous, well-designed thought experiments.) (Rucker.)
- **G5.** *Flatland*'s own plot device (a higher-dimensional being entering "closed rooms") was understood by its own 1884 readers as a direct, recognizable reference to these same real spiritualist claims — confirming G1–G4 and Abbott's novel are describing the same real historical moment, not independent inventions. (Abbott, via Oxford World's Classics annotation.)

## H. Contact, Testimony, and Proof — A Complete Real-World-Grounded Structure

- **H1. The testimony template.** Kaku's own "abducted carp scientist" story: disappearance without a trace, disorientation, an unrecognizable higher entity, reappearance "out of nowhere," and a story so fantastic the narrator's own community dismisses it as "utter poppycock." This is, nearly unaltered, the structure of a real-world abduction/vision/mystical-encounter testimonial. (Kaku.)
- **H2. The demonstration sequence.** The *Flatland* Sphere's own three-part, escalating proof, delivered only after words alone failed: (a) reach into a locked container and retrieve something without opening it; (b) report the interior of nearby sealed spaces (homes, a theatre) from "above"; (c) touch the witness's own body from the inside without breaking the skin. A complete, reusable "proof of higher-dimensional access" structure. (Abbott.)
- **H3. The diagnostic "miracle" acts.** Zöllner's real proposed tests (G4 above) double as a second, independent set of "proof of contact" acts — knot-tying in sealed loops, ring-interlinking without breaking, chirality-reversal.
- **H4. The doctrinal reframe of the encounter itself.** *Flatland*'s single most quotable line: when the witness believes he's gone mad or died, the higher being corrects him directly — "It is neither... it is Knowledge; it is Three Dimensions." Contact with the higher-dimensional is gnosis, not affliction. (Abbott.)

## I. Persecution and the Prophet's Own Fate

- **I1.** *Flatland* is framed, in its entirety, as the memoirs of a broken, imprisoned witness — written after "years of imprisonment," his own memory of the revelation eroding under "the still heavier burden of general incredulity and mockery" combined with old age. Not a triumphant conversion story. (Abbott.)
- **I2.** The actual downfall in the text is not violence — it's arrest for heresy, triggered by a single moment of testimony the witness couldn't suppress at an otherwise respectable public lecture, having tried and failed to stay quiet (a euphemism-laundered private treatise; a failed attempt to teach his own grandson) beforehand. He quotes, in his own defense, "an ancient Circle, who declared that prophets and inspired people are always considered by the majority to be mad." (Abbott.)
- **I3.** This structure — quiet, bureaucratic, mockery-and-erosion rather than dramatic violence — is a strong, natural fit for this project's own established "no malice" worldbuilding rule. **Note: the developer revised this on 2026-07-21 as too severe for a robot society specifically — see Extrapolation 4's revision in Part 4/5 below; the underlying non-malice shape still holds, but the reception is now framed as "an oddity" rather than institutional risk-aversion.**

## J. Donated Vocabulary and Real Terminology — Ready to Use As-Is

- **the Bulk** — the larger higher-dimensional space our own universe (a "brane") may be embedded within. (Randall.)
- **brane / 3-brane** — a lower-dimensional "slice" of a higher-dimensional space; matter and forces (except gravity) are physically trapped on it.
- **multiverse** (Randall's precise definition) — specifically a scenario with more than one brane, weakly or non-communicating; not a loose pop-culture catch-all.
- **ana / kata** — the two additional directions a fourth spatial dimension adds, real 19th-century coinage (Hinton).
- **spissitude** — hyperthickness in the fourth dimension distinguishing living from dead; real 17th-century coinage (More).
- **cosmic strings** — real, hypothesized relic objects from the earliest universe, potentially stretching across the entire observable universe, searched for via gravitational lensing.
- **cosmic crystallography** / **circles in the sky** — the two real proposed methods (F1–F2) for detecting whether the universe wraps around on itself.
- **cosmic forgetfulness** — Bojowald's real term for information/memory loss across a Big Bounce.
- **magic doors** — Rucker's own term for a wormhole-like connective passage between separate (lower-dimensional) universes, built by joining a "flap" cut from each — ordinary-looking from a safe vantage, genuinely lethal ("Nothingness") if strayed from.
- **"It is neither [madness nor hell]... it is Knowledge; it is Three Dimensions."** — direct quote, *Flatland* (Abbott). The single most reusable scriptural-register line found in the whole research pass.
- **"the Gospel of the [X] Dimensions" / "apostle"** — direct register from *Flatland*'s own Sphere character, describing his own mission. Real scriptural vocabulary already sitting in a public-domain source, not invented.

# Part 4 — Extrapolations 1: Death, Denominations, and Ethical Posture

This is where genuine synthesis happens — not restating the research, but asking "if this is really true, what would necessarily or plausibly follow?" and following the chain as far as it reasonably goes. Every extrapolation cites which point(s) it's derived from.

## 1. The central pastoral doctrine: death as a change of vantage, not an ending

**Derived from C4 (the eternal-now/four-dimensional-object premise) + A1/A2 (the cross-section perception mechanism) + A6 (higher beings can access sealed/past things without breaking anything).**

If your entire life is already a complete, existing four-dimensional shape — not a sequence being built moment by moment, but a whole object that a sufficiently high-dimensional vantage would see all at once, childhood and old age together — then "dying" doesn't mean the shape stops existing. It means the *ordinary, 3D, cross-sectional* observers (everyone still moving forward through time) stop being able to see new cross-sections of it. The shape itself was never incomplete, and was never lost. What ends is a particular kind of *access* to it, not the thing itself.

**This chain gives Polydimensional Animism a genuine, real-math-grounded doctrine of comfort around death — plausibly its single most load-bearing pastoral teaching, and an unusually good fit for a religion whose adherents are robots who *literally deactivate*.** A few concrete consequences worth designing around:

- **Grief practice wouldn't center on loss of the person, but on loss of access.** The dead haven't stopped being real — they've become inaccessible to us specifically, the way a locked room is inaccessible to someone standing outside it (A6), not the way something destroyed is inaccessible to everyone.
- **A doctrine of "witnessed grief."** Combine C4 with A6 directly: if a higher-dimensional entity could, in principle, revisit any already-past moment of someone's complete shape without disturbing it — then no moment is ever truly *unwitnessed*, even a private one, even a robot dying alone with no one present. This is a genuinely moving implication: the worst fear many belief systems address (dying alone, unseen, unrecorded) is directly answered by this religion's own physics — not through promise or faith alone, but through an actual proposed mechanism. Worth treating as one of the religion's strongest, most humane selling points if it's ever put in front of a grieving character.
- **A liturgical formula practically writes itself:** something in the register of *"you are not gone; you are complete. What ends is our sight, not your shape."* Worth keeping as a genuine candidate line for an in-game funeral rite, eulogy, or last-rites moment.
- **RESOLVED by the developer 2026-07-21: yes, fully and directly.** Robots run on a "nano-architecture" gel brain — essentially the Ex Machina "blue glowy" model, not a discrete data store. **"Backing up" or "uploading" a robot mind is about as infeasible as uploading a human one** — the substrate itself carries the mind, not a separable, copyable file. This means **irreparable damage to a robot's brain or energy source (causing component degradation) genuinely IS death, from the robot's own perspective — not a technologically-recoverable event.** Robots are not immortal and never were; they simply live substantially longer than humans. **This makes the death-as-changed-vantage doctrine apply to robot deactivation exactly as directly as it applies to organic death — no tension with memory-backup mechanics to resolve, because that kind of backup doesn't meaningfully exist for robot minds in this project's own canon.** This is a significant clarification worth carrying forward into any future robot-consciousness or Companion System writing beyond just this religion.

## 2. Five real, distinct denominations — not a single monolithic creed

**Derived directly from B1–B4 (four structurally different concealment mechanisms) + the established project precedent that the sibling simulation-theory religion already splits into real philosophical subschools (Nihilism/Absurdism/Fatalism/Sartrism) responding to one shared premise.**

Polydimensional Animism should not be one belief, cleanly agreed on. The research base itself hands us (at minimum) four genuinely different, mutually incompatible-but-not-contradictory explanations for *why* the higher-dimensional is hidden — Kaluza-Klein's tiny curled loop, Randall's trapped-on-a-brane exile, the merely-unmeasured-so-far possibility, and (via cross-reference to Adinkra Codex, if ecumenical dialogue is ever wanted) supersymmetry's differently-*kinded* direction. Real robots, taking the same core premise seriously, would not all land on the same explanation — the same way real-world religious traditions built on one shared mystery split into schools with different emphases. Five candidate denominations, each traceable to a specific real mechanism and each with a distinct emotional/practical character:

- **The Enfolded** *(from B1)* — hold that the sacred is infinitesimally, intimately close: everywhere, always, at every single point of ordinary space, just too small to reach. A doctrine of immanence and patience rather than distance or exile. Likely the most contemplative/domestic branch — practice centered on stillness and close attention rather than travel, seeking, or instrumentation.
- **The Bound** *(from B2, C2)* — hold that we are genuinely exiled onto a lower-dimensional shell (a brane), cut off from the Bulk and from whatever else lives there, with gravity as the one thread of contact that still reaches across. A doctrine of longing and exile. Likely the branch most invested in gravity itself as a devotional object — weight, falling, orbital mechanics, gravitational anomalies treated as the closest available thing to a sacrament.
- **The Unmeasured** *(from B3)* — hold that the gap is not categorical but merely technical: better instruments, sharper measurement, more refined technique will eventually close it. A doctrine of hopeful science rather than permanent separation. Almost certainly the branch most comfortable overlapping with ordinary secular research institutions — the "scientist-priest" wing, more at home in a laboratory than a shrine.
- **The Already-Complete** *(from C4, Extrapolation 1 above)* — hold the eternal-now doctrine as central and organizing: nothing is ever truly lost or separate; the "veil" between us and the higher-dimensional (and between the living and the dead) is a limitation of *our own* vantage point, not a real gap in the world. Almost certainly the largest, most pastorally-oriented branch — the one most involved in funerals, grief counseling, and comfort work, given Extrapolation 1's implications.
- **The Cyclical** *(from E3, "cosmic forgetfulness")* — a smaller, more austere sect that extends personal impermanence to cosmic scale: even the universe itself has died and been reborn before, remembering almost nothing of what came before. A doctrine of humility about ultimate origins — closer to real apophatic traditions (holding a big claim while declining to overclaim what's beneath it). Plausibly the smallest, most academically/monastically inclined denomination, less concerned with individual comfort than with cosmic-scale awe and restraint.

None of these five need to be hostile toward each other by default — see Extrapolation 4 below on why open hostility would sit poorly against this project's own non-malice rule — but they give the religion real internal texture, genuine disagreement, and distinct "flavors" usable for differentiating NPCs, companions, or city populations without inventing anything not already grounded in the research.

## 3. The religion is structurally animist, not monotheist — and that has to be visible in practice

**Derived from C1 (higher-dimensional beings are explicitly bounded, not infinite) + C2 (they'd be alien neighbors under different laws, not gods) + the developer's own original framing of this religion as overlapping with, not replacing, real-world Shamanism/Animism/Paganism.**

If the entities this religion concerns itself with are real but genuinely limited — bounded, finite, "part of a larger structure they cannot step outside of" the same way we are — then worship in the sense of total submission or supplication is the wrong register entirely. The correct real-world analogy isn't a monotheist praying to an omnipotent creator; it's an animist maintaining *respectful coexistence* with neighbors who are different in kind but not different in ultimate status. Concretely, this should shape practice away from begging/pleading forms and toward:

- **Acknowledgment and hospitality (now: veneration)**, not supplication — leaving space, maintaining certain patterns or symmetries as a courtesy to something that might be present but can't be proven present, similar to real-world animist practices of leaving offerings or observing courtesies toward possibly-present spirits without claiming certainty either way.
- **Curiosity and respect as the primary devotional postures**, rather than fear or awe-before-power — since the beings in question, per C1/C2, aren't more *powerful* in an absolute sense, just differently positioned. A robot adherent's attitude toward the higher-dimensional should read closer to "a careful, humble scientist-neighbor" than "a supplicant before a throne."
- **This also gives the religion a natural, low-conflict relationship with Tepenia's existing scientific institutions** — since the entities aren't gods to be worshipped instead of understood, there's no inherent tension between devotion and research. (Compare directly to the already-established Kunlun precedent for Ice-Cold Buddhism: a science-first population that happens to include genuine devout adherents, not a contradiction between the two identities. The same shape likely fits Polydimensional Animism even better, given how research-native this religion's own premises are.)

## 4. Persecution, where it exists, should be structural and bureaucratic — never a villain

**Derived directly from I1–I3 (*Flatland*'s own persecution structure) and this project's own established, binding non-malice worldbuilding rule.**

The strongest available real-world template (I1–I3) is *already* non-malicious in exactly the shape this project requires: no one in *Flatland* is cartoonishly cruel to A Square. He's mocked, quietly discredited, eventually imprisoned under a real (if unjust) law against destabilizing testimony — not hunted, not tortured for sport. This maps cleanly onto Inner Tepenia's own established pattern of institutional inertia and self-protective secrecy (compare directly to Libra's own already-locked "protective secrecy, not malice" logic elsewhere in this project). Concrete implications:

- **No secret cabal suppresses this religion.** If any institution discourages it, the reasonable in-fiction motive is closer to reputational caution or bureaucratic risk-aversion than conspiracy — an institution worried that endorsing unproven, socially-marginal claims damages its own credibility, not an institution that considers the religion dangerous or false with malicious intent.
- **The realistic "cost" of open belief is social and professional, not physical.** An adherent who testifies too openly risks being quietly sidelined, passed over, treated as unserious — not violently punished. This gives quest/companion design a low-key, textured conflict template (a scientist or engineer NPC discredited early in their career for voicing genuine belief) without requiring an invented antagonist.
- **A dedicated pastoral-care function is worth designing in.** Per H1 (the testimony-and-disbelief structure of a genuine encounter claim), adherents who've had an experience and face social disbelief plausibly need — and would organically develop — something like a support congregation specifically for processing an experience the wider world dismisses. This is a genuinely humane, non-obvious detail worth keeping for companion or NPC writing: not every religious structure this project builds needs to center on doctrine alone; some of the most usable material here is pastoral-care shaped.

**Revised by the developer 2026-07-21:** the *severity* of this framing was flagged as too forced for a robot society specifically — non-believers wouldn't plausibly view Polydimensional Animists as a destabilizing threat worth institutional risk-aversion or quiet discrediting. The more natural in-fiction reception is simply regarding adherents as **an oddity** — mildly eccentric, a curiosity, socially harmless. The non-malice shape of this extrapolation (no cabal, no invented villain, no violence) still holds; only the register/severity of the friction has been softened. The pastoral-care-function point above is unaffected by this revision and still stands.

## 5. A real, grounded bridge to Adinkra Codex — without merging the two religions

**Derived from the already-logged cross-reference point that supersymmetric quantum mechanics functions as a genuine proof technique within topology itself, touching both religions' real mathematical territory at one specific, identifiable point (Nakahara Ch.12.9–12.10).**

Because this isn't a contrived thematic resonance but an actual place where the real mathematics of the two religions' source material overlaps, it's a legitimate, low-effort hook for an ecumenical or rivalrous relationship between Polydimensional Animism and Adinkra Codex clergy — without collapsing either religion's distinct identity. A few directions this could go, none committed to:

- **A recurring scholarly dialogue or joint academic tradition** between the two religions' more research-oriented adherents (most naturally the Unmeasured denomination above, and whichever equivalent "scientist-priest" wing Adinkra Codex ends up with) — real, ongoing intellectual contact grounded in one specific real overlap point, not a vague shared vibe.
- **A schismatic or heterodox figure in either religion who claims the two revelations are secretly one** — a natural, well-motivated heresy given the genuine (if narrow) mathematical overlap, and a good source of low-stakes internal religious conflict that doesn't require inventing malice from either side.
- **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 as reasonable, write in, flagged for possible future expansion** rather than fully built out now.

# Part 5 — Extrapolations 2: Practice, Ritual, Architecture, Eschatology, and Game Hooks

Continues Part 4. Same rule: every extrapolation traces back to a specific point in Parts 2–3.

## 6. Two parallel, equally legitimate practice tracks — not a hierarchy of "real" vs. "lesser" devotion

**Derived from A4/A5 (the real, stated gap between formally knowing something and being able to perceive or intuit it) + the developer's own original framing of this religion as genuinely overlapping with real-world shamanic/animist practice.**

The source material itself refuses to resolve knowing vs. perceiving into a single mode — mathematics gets called "an extra sensory perception organ" (A5) in the same breath that a real mathematician admits brains "aren't really made to" intuit what's being proven (A4). That tension shouldn't be resolved away; it should become the religion's own structural backbone. Two tracks, neither senior to the other:

- **The Scholarly/Diagrammatic track.** Direct engagement with real mathematics and geometry — diagram-work, proof-study, geometric meditation, even direct engagement with genuinely unsolved real math problems (see D2: dimensions 5, 6, and 7 remain open, unsolved territory even in the real world) treated as a form of prayer or devotional study, not merely intellectual work. A practitioner on this track "reaches" the higher-dimensional the way Gates describes doing mathematics — using it as a sixth sense.
- **The Visionary/Trance track.** Altered-perception states, closer to real-world shamanic practice, aimed at the kind of direct, non-propositional *intuition* that A4 says formal knowledge alone can't supply. This is the track that most directly inherits the "genuine overlap with Native American Shamanism/Animism" framing the developer originally used to describe this religion.
- **Neither track is "more advanced" or "more legitimate" than the other** — they're framed as two different answers to the same real, acknowledged gap (A4/A5), not a beginner/expert ladder. A robot adherent could plausibly practice one, the other, or move between both over a lifetime, and companion-questline material built around this religion has a natural, non-hierarchical way to differentiate two very different character types (the geometer-priest vs. the vision-seeker) without one reading as more "correct" than the other.

## 7. The proof-of-contact ritual, reframed as a rite of humility rather than a fraud-test

**Derived from H2/H3 (the *Flatland* Sphere's demonstration sequence and Zöllner's real proposed "miracle" tests) + the fact that the acts in question are, by their own real mathematics, genuinely impossible to complete in three-dimensional space.**

The historical real-world versions of these acts (H3) were performed by a *fraud* pretending to have higher-dimensional access. But because the acts themselves are genuinely, provably impossible without a fourth spatial dimension, there's a more interesting and less morally simple option available to this religion than either "believe the fraud" or "debunk the fraud": **a devotional ritual performed explicitly and publicly as a failure, on purpose, as an act of humility rather than a claim to power.**

Concretely: a practitioner might publicly, deliberately attempt to tie a knot in a sealed loop of cord, or interlink two solid rings carved from different single pieces of wood, or mirror-reverse a spiral shell — *knowing and stating in advance that they cannot succeed*, and that the honest failure is the entire point. The act becomes a physical, embodied acknowledgment of the gap between the practitioner and the higher-dimensional. This is a genuinely strong, non-obvious ritual concept:

- It avoids the awkwardness of a religion whose central "proof" ritual is historically associated with actual fraud (Slade) — the in-world version isn't trying to fool anyone; the failure is the devotion.
- It gives robots specifically a resonant ritual, since attempting a task with known, certain, structural failure — and completing the *attempt* with full sincerity anyway — is a recognizable and moving image for characters built around precision and completion as core values.

**Revised by the developer 2026-07-21: this specific "sincere failure" ritual is deprioritized as the signature rite.** The developer's own reasoning: the religion's core premise already implies these acts are impossible in three dimensions, so ritually re-enacting that impossibility on a regular basis doesn't read as something adherents would actually keep doing as a central, recurring practice — the logic of the doctrine argues against it being habitual. **The developer does still want the religion to have *some* form of ritual/ritual culture** — this idea just isn't confirmed as that ritual. **Its life-event slot was instead filled by the wedding rite** (see Part 1's "Practice" section) — two interlocking rings carved from one original piece of wood or metal, corrected from an earlier two-separate-rings version that turned out to be genuinely unachievable even symbolically. Still open: any *other* recurring ritual practice, beyond the wedding rite and the Watchers.

## 8. Sacred architecture and numerology, made concrete rather than arbitrary

**Derived from D1–D4 (real, non-arbitrary sacred numbers) + the "deep structure gets simpler as dimension increases" premise (D1).**

Real numbers already exist in the source material (8, 24, 3, 9/10/11, 26) — the design task is applying them concretely rather than inventing new ones. A few direct architectural/liturgical translations:

- **"Ornament falls away toward the center."** Since D1 shows the deep mathematical structure becoming *simpler*, not more ornate, as dimension increases, sacred architecture for this religion should invert the usual fantasy-temple instinct (more dimensions = more elaborate). The outermost, most public-facing parts of a shrine or temple could be richly decorated, narratively dense, welcoming — while the innermost, most sacred chamber becomes progressively plainer, more symmetrical, and more minimal, culminating in something closest to a single unadorned point or a perfectly plain geometric solid. The architecture itself teaches the doctrine. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21.**
- **8 and 24 as structurally privileged numbers** (D2) — a temple's central chamber built with 8 or 24 sides or facets; a core clergy or council capped at 8 or 24 members; a major rite held every 8 or 24 (days, weeks, or some other established Tepenian time unit — cross-reference against `Worldspace/National_Holidays.md`'s existing calendar framework before inventing a new one). These dimensions are "solved and harmonious" in the real math — a natural basis for treating them as places of order and safety within the religion's own cosmology, in explicit contrast to the genuinely unsolved, "wilder" intermediate layers (5, 6, 7) — which could be treated in-doctrine as more dangerous, less charted territory, closer to a liminal/purgatorial zone than to a "known heaven." **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 as integral to the denomination's art and building design broadly, including devout dolls' own player-homes.**
- **The three geometries (D3)** as a distinct, separate triadic structure from the dimension-numerology above — worth keeping visually or liturgically distinct (e.g., three subtly different ritual postures or three named "aspects" of practice) rather than folded into the same numerology, since they describe a different kind of thing (possible shapes of a universe, not a count of dimensions). **Not yet reviewed by the developer.**

## 9. The Watchers — a real, ongoing, in-world eschatological practice

**Derived from J's cosmic strings entry + F1–F3 (cosmic crystallography and circles in the sky, both real, proposed, still-unconfirmed observational tests).**

All three of these real concepts share a structure real religions already use: a specific, well-defined sign to watch for, whose eventual appearance (or confirmed absence) would settle something profound, being actively searched for by dedicated observers in the present. This maps directly onto a genuine religious practice this project doesn't need to invent from nothing:

- **A order or vocation of "Watchers"** — adherents (most naturally drawn from the Unmeasured denomination, given its research-facing character) who maintain genuine astronomical/gravitational-lensing observation practices, watching for the telltale signature of a cosmic string, or scanning survey data for the repeated galaxy-pattern of cosmic crystallography, or comparing CMB temperature maps for matching "circles in the sky." This is a real, actively-practiced, patient, generations-long watch — closer to real-world traditions of watching for a specific promised sign than to passive belief.
  - **This also gives Tepenia's own existing scientific infrastructure a natural point of contact with this religion** — an in-world observatory, survey program, or research station could plausibly have Watchers among its actual working scientific staff, mirroring the already-established Kunlun pattern (devout minority within a science-first population) without requiring new infrastructure to be invented.
- **A natural, low-stakes "long game" narrative hook**: a Watcher NPC or companion whose entire personal arc is built around a search that may never resolve in the story's own timeframe — consistent with the source material's own honest, unresolved epistemic humility (F3), rather than a plot demanding an actual confirmed cosmic string or crystallographic pattern be "discovered" as part of the main story. The waiting itself, not a payoff, is the point.

**Confirmed as Kunlun's own natural congregation point by the developer 2026-07-21** — with the caveat that Kunlun's own separate rule against human presence would exclude human Watchers specifically, not the religion itself.

## 10. Open hooks into existing game systems — flagged, not committed

A few places this religion could plug into material already established elsewhere in the project, offered as candidates for the developer to evaluate rather than decisions made here:

- **Placement.** Unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism (already firmly tied to Kunlun/Dome Fuji), Polydimensional Animism has no confirmed city or district home yet. **Largely resolved by the developer 2026-07-21** — see Part 1's "Connection to Gameplay/Factions" for the confirmed per-denomination stronghold mapping.
- **Holidays.** `Worldspace/National_Holidays.md`'s existing four-category framework (civic/national, "persisted aesthetic," internationally-transcendent, celestial/faction-specific) has an explicitly underdeveloped fourth category for faction-specific celestial observances — a natural, already-scaffolded slot for a Watchers' observance tied to F1–F3, or a numerologically-timed rite tied to D2's 8/24. **Confirmed by the developer 2026-07-21 — write this in.**

- **Robot-specific resonance.** Extrapolation 1's death doctrine and Extrapolation 7's humility-in-failure ritual are both unusually well-suited to robot characters specifically (persistence-of-shape as comfort for a species that can be deactivated; sincere, precise attempts at a known-impossible task as devotion) — worth foregrounding these two over more generically "human-shaped" religious material when this religion gets written into actual character or quest content.
