# Polydimensional Animism — Rituals & Practice

Quick-reference excerpt. See `README.md` in this same folder for the complete document.

## Two Practice Tracks

**Scholarly/Diagrammatic** — mathematics and geometry as devotional practice, direct engagement with
genuinely unsolved real problems as a form of prayer.

**Visionary/Trance** — altered-perception states, closest to the religion's own original real-world
shamanic/animist touchstone.

Neither track is senior to the other.

## Devotional Register

**Veneration, not worship** — respectful regard and courtesy toward a possibly-present, genuinely-limited
neighbor, not supplication or pleading before a throne.

## Wedding / Pair-Bonding Rite (confirmed)

The rite centers on **two interlocking rings carved from a single original piece of wood or metal** — not
two separately-carved rings physically joined afterward (that version is genuinely impossible, even
symbolically). The finished rings appear impossible to a naive observer — two solid, unbroken loops, already
interlinked, no visible seam or cut — but the "impossibility" is resolved by a real, learnable craft
technique, not magic. This materially embodies the religion's own epistemic stance (what looks impossible
has a real, non-supernatural explanation once the fuller structure is understood), and doubles as a
near-literal expression of the Already-Complete denomination's own teaching. Open to any pairing the faith
recognizes as equal, including human-robot pairings as the norm, not the exception.

## The Watchers

A real, ongoing, patient observational practice: scanning astronomical and gravitational data for the
signature of a cosmic string, a repeating cosmic-crystallography pattern, or matching circles-in-the-sky in
the CMB. A generations-long watch that may never resolve, by design — the waiting is the point.

## Social Structure

Adherents have dedicated places of meeting, but no required regular schedule — gathering is social as much
as devotional, a way for the devout to build friendships within the faith.

## Human Membership

**Lovingly open to human disciples as the norm, not the exception** — unlike Ice-Cold Buddhism (which
forbids humans entirely at Kunlun/Dome Fuji), Polydimensional Animism warmly welcomes human members as fully
equal to robots.

## Candidate Routine Ritual — Shelved, Not Confirmed

The original "sincere, deliberate failure" ritual (attempting a real, mathematically-impossible-in-3D feat —
untying a sealed knot, interlinking solid rings, mirror-reversing a shell — knowing it cannot succeed, as
embodied humility) was deprioritized as a *routine, recurring* practice: the religion's own doctrine already
implies these acts are impossible, so re-enacting that regularly doesn't read as plausible adherent behavior.
Its life-event slot was filled by the wedding rite instead. **Still open: any other recurring ritual
practice**, beyond the wedding rite and the Watchers.
