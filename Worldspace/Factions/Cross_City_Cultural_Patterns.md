# Cross-City Cultural Patterns — Candidate Factions

**Status: exploratory research synthesis, not locked canon.** Derived 2026-07-21 by reading across the
Super-Ultra-Megasheet (`Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Super_Ultra_Megasheet/`) and all 5 Subnet
Ultra-Megasheets' own Cross-City-Patterns files, now that every one of Tepenia's 35 cities has real cultural
depth. **Purpose:** identify recurring cultural/psychological patterns that cut across city and subnet lines
— the same synthesis motion used for the robot religions' denominations and God-mind simulation's Nihilism
subschools — specifically looking for patterns with genuine internal-conflict potential: one shared
condition, several real, distinguishable, non-hostile responses to it.

Every claim below traces back to a specific already-established city or district fact — nothing here is
invented from nothing. See the individual Subnet Cross-City-Patterns files and `District_Canon_Reference.md`
for the underlying source material.

## Pattern A: "Does genuinely consequential work, receives none of the visible credit"

### Why this is the strongest candidate

This isn't a single observation — it's been independently rediscovered, without cross-citation, by separate
research passes in *every region of the game*: Halley subnet (Neumayer alone accounts for three separate
historical instances — the original Rastra design, Amundsen Tower's own schematics, the modern
fabrication-synthesis chamber, none bearing its name; also Sanay, also Halley itself), Mirny subnet (Davis,
Sinheung, Mirny's own doubled communications-and-industrial hub function), Janbogo/Ross subnet (Janbogo's own
witness/relay role), Palmer subnet (robots as the actual continuity mechanism underneath Esperanza, Marambio,
and Palmer City alike, in three unrelated domains), and at district scale in Concordia (Virgo's entire
established identity in `District_Canon_Reference.md`, and the four-district Recognition Cascade the
Super-Ultra-Megasheet's own Cross-Reference Synthesis found — Cancer, Taurus, and Leo all underappreciate
Aries; Aries in turn underappreciates Virgo). The Super-Ultra-Megasheet's own Pattern 1 already names this as
Tepenia's single most-repeated civic theme in the entire game.

### Core premise

Real, essential, civilization-sustaining work — much of it done by robots specifically, given their
established role as Tepenia's actual continuity mechanism (people and institutions that outlast any single
human lifetime) — routinely goes uncredited, unnamed, or actively misattributed. This isn't a conspiracy or
anyone's deliberate injustice (consistent with the project's non-malice rule) — it's simply how institutional
memory, naming conventions, and public attention happen to work in a young, crisis-formed nation. But
different cities — and, by extension, different individuals who carry this same lived experience — have
resolved the psychological weight of it in genuinely different ways.

### Five real, distinguishable responses — not one monolithic reaction

- **Competence Without Commentary** *(Sanay — already an established faction name in
  `Worldspace/Factions/City_Origin_Factions_Second_Interwar.md`)* — refuses to even frame the question as
  worth discussing. Not suppression, not bitterness — a genuine, lived conviction that asking "don't we
  deserve more credit?" is itself a kind of category error. Silence here reads as dignity, not injury.
- **The Archive Response** *(Neumayer, and Virgo's exact district-level parallel — "if the city won't
  acknowledge what Virgo does, Virgo will at minimum know what it did")* — doesn't seek public credit, but
  insists on rigorous internal record-keeping, so the truth is at least provably knowable somewhere, even if
  never publicly claimed. A private form of self-respect through documentation rather than display.
- **The Witness's Burden** *(Janbogo)* — already has its own documented internal "vigil vs. moving on" split
  (see Janbogo's own Cross-Reference Synthesis) — oriented outward, toward the dead and lost, rather than
  inward toward self-worth, but built from the same root shape: an essential function nobody else can or will
  perform, carried without complaint, at real emotional cost.
- **Reserved Leverage** *(Virgo's "Year of Silence," `District_Canon_Reference.md`)* — quiet acceptance as
  the default, but a real, once-exercised, deliberately-restrained collective capacity to make the
  invisibility visible by withdrawing labor — a boundary asserted exactly once, under genuine grievance, and
  never repeated. Structural power held in reserve, not habitually exercised.
- **Civic Peace** *(Mawson — "The Name That Outlasted the Founders")* — warmer and more openly at ease than
  Sanay's clipped refusal to discuss it: genuine, freely-discussed contentment with the gap between real
  contribution and public recognition, rather than silence used as armor.

**Why this works as a faction concept:** none of these five positions are hostile to each other — they're
different temperaments responding to the same real, shared civic condition, which keeps this fully consistent
with the non-malice rule while still producing genuine internal disagreement and texture (a Sanay-tempered
character might find a Mawson-tempered one's openness almost indecent; a Witness's-Burden character might
find the Archive Response's private documentation cold and insufficient compared to actually being present
for grief). **Not yet checked against the 9 undesigned Tentative Factions** (FD-3, FD-4, FD-6, FD-8 through
FD-12 — see `TODO.md`'s "Tentative factions" entry) for possible overlap — worth doing before treating this
as a wholly new faction slot rather than a fit for an existing one.

## Pattern B: Cultures as living, distributed entities — spreading and evolving independent of founding nation

**Explicitly reframed 2026-07-21 at the developer's direction: founding-nation-of-origin is not a valid lens
here** — an earlier draft of this pattern cited the tertulia (a hosted, argument-driven gathering) as
originating at Juan Carlos because of Spain's Significant-tier presence there; that's the same
country-of-founding logic already ruled out project-wide (see `feedback_no_national_stereotypes`), so that
example is dropped entirely. What actually holds up, checked directly against city-level (not district-level)
material, is real cultural *practices* — mostly music, born from local material conditions rather than
imported national tradition — that spread, get imitated, get absorbed, and evolve across city lines on their
own terms.

### Two real, distinguishable modes of cross-city cultural life

- **Outward radiation with contested authenticity** *(Belgrano's garage rock scene —
  `Background-Lore/Cities/Halley_Subnet/Belgrano/Course_of_Events/Belgrano_07_The_Garage_That_Built_the_Sound.md`)*.
  Born from Belgrano's own mechanic culture (off-duty garage crews turning tool-sessions into music sessions,
  tolerated rather than formally sanctioned) — not imported from any nation. It "draws real subnet-wide travel
  and attention," but stays physically embedded in actual working garages, never migrating to purpose-built
  venues. The result: "outside acts that later try to imitate 'the Belgrano sound' in cleaner, purpose-built
  spaces get quietly written off by subnet audiences as missing the entire point." A culture radiates outward
  as reputation and aspiration, but authenticity stays anchored to its origin conditions — imitation elsewhere
  is recognized and judged as imitation.
- **Inward absorption with productive internal tension** *(Shirayuki's "Glacier-pop" —
  `Background-Lore/Cities/Mirny_Subnet/Shirayuki/Course_of_Events/Shirayuki_05_Glacier-pop.md`)*. Shirayuki's
  founding-era Japanese folk tradition absorbs ambient influence from its Larsemann Hills cluster neighbors
  (Sinheung, Zhongshan) — not a national import, a *geographic/social osmosis* from adjacent cities. This
  produces a genuine internal split between a traditionalist (protect the original form) and an innovator
  (a living tradition has to absorb its surroundings or calcify into a museum piece) — "neither side is simply
  right." The productive outcome: the traditionalist's own criticism sharpens the innovator's work, producing
  a fusion genre with an *articulable, teachable* relationship to its own component parts, rather than an
  unexamined blend — "not an unexamined blend but a genre with an articulated, teachable relationship to its
  own founding and adopted influences."

**Why this pair works well together:** they're structurally complementary, not redundant — Belgrano shows a
culture pushing *out* and policing its own authenticity against outside imitators; Shirayuki shows a culture
pulling *in* surrounding influence and resolving the resulting tension through genuine internal disagreement
rather than either purity-policing or uncritical absorption. Between them, they give real texture for a
purist/embracer tension (echoing Pattern A's own internal-conflict shape) grounded in actual established
lore rather than invented from nothing.

**Confirmed and flagged 2026-07-21, not yet resolved:**
- The Goth subculture's reactive formation against Scorpio's own therapeutic tradition
  (`District_Canon_Reference.md`, Scorpio section) — **confirmed valid** by the developer as a real instance of
  this broader "cultures as living entities" phenomenon, though it is district-scoped, not city-scoped.
- Pisces' own unnamed syncretic religion (recombining Goth death-practice, Cymaticist sound-work, and digital-
  consciousness doctrine, "no formal name, no clergy, transmitted person-to-person, dream-to-dream" —
  `District_Canon_Reference.md`, Pisces section) — **flagged by the developer as worth investigating further**,
  not yet developed beyond its existing one-paragraph description.
- **Note: 3 of the district-level examples originally proposed here (Cymaticism/Leo, Goth/Scorpio, Pisces'
  syncretic religion) are exclusive to Concordia's districts, not the outer cities** — this is why the two
  city-level examples above (Belgrano, Shirayuki) were specifically hunted down separately, per the
  developer's own direction to look at cities, not just districts, for this pattern.

### Flagged for future exploration: religion → worldview → culture → visible society

**Explicitly deferred 2026-07-21 at the developer's request, until all five robot religions are fully
developed AND their practitioner strongholds/locations are known.** The Cymaticism/Leo example above (a
religion's aesthetic sensibility shaping an entire district's built environment and sound culture, "even in
residents who would never call themselves religious") is the seed case for this — once every religion has a
real doctrine and a real home (Polydimensional Animism's own stronghold mapping is already done — see
`Worldspace/Factions/Robot_Religions/Polydimensional_Animism/`; Adinkra Codex, Ice-Cold Buddhism, God-mind
simulation, and Cymatics Reverence itself are not there yet), revisit this specific cascade — how a
religion's own worldview visibly shapes the ambient culture and society of the place(s) it's concentrated in,
independent of formal membership — as its own dedicated exploration. **Cross-referenced in `TODO.md`'s
"Robot religion design" entry.**

## Secondary patterns (flagged, less developed)

### Founding-moment civic identity

Cities that organize their whole identity around one dated founding moment or figure rather than ongoing
function or scale: **Scott** (St. Robert/Robert Falcon Scott, the 1902 Hut Point refuge), **Dumont d'Urville**
(St. Jules Day, January 21 1840), **Cape Adare** (its own "firstness" identity as the first landing site).
Nearly half of Janbogo/Ross subnet's cities (3 of 7) share this mechanism. Ties directly into the already-
flagged "Tepenian Saints" TODO item (`TODO.md`) — worth developing together rather than separately.

### Destruction-doctrine typologies

Multiple subnets have independently produced typologies of *why* the Long Night War damaged different cities
differently — Halley subnet's 4-category damage-*mechanism* taxonomy, Janbogo/Ross subnet's 2-principle
strategic-*logic* taxonomy (worth-more-alive vs. punitive/symbolic), Mirny subnet's single-principle
(infrastructure value alone), Palmer subnet's own 4-type strike doctrine (security/efficiency/morale/
ideological). Rich and real, but reads as historical fact rather than something with faction/internal-
conflict potential on its own.
