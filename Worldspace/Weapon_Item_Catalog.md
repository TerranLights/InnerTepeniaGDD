# Weapon Item Catalog

**STATUS: ONGOING — genuinely incomplete, not a finished deliverable.** Established 2026-08-16, but the
developer's own assessment is that this is a rough first pass, not close to done. This file (and its
siblings — `to-be-integrated/gear/gear_collection_DRAFT_-_weapons.md`,
`gear_collection_DRAFT_-_armor.md`, and the 8 `splatter_*` raw-brainstorm files) should be treated as a
standing, recurring work area to come back to repeatedly, not a checklist item to close out. Don't treat the
current item count or category coverage as anywhere near final.

**Established 2026-08-16.** Item-level detail sitting between `Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md` (the category-level
"why these exist, what they were for before combat" framing) and `Game-Mechanics/Combat/Damage_Types.md` (the
category-level mechanical/damage-type framing). Neither of those files goes down to named item archetypes —
this one does. **Goal, per the developer's own framing:** every weapon *type* Fallout: New Vegas implements,
present here in Tepenia's own vocabulary, plus genuinely new items the setting's own science and culture call
for that FNV never needed. Same governing rule as the category-level philosophy: **almost none of this was
designed to hurt anyone.** Every entry states its civilian identity first; combat use is what circumstance
does to it.

Organized by the same 13 categories `Damage_Types.md`'s Weapon Category Cross-Reference already uses (7
Conventional, 3 Energy-Adjacent, 3 Tepenia-Original) — this file adds the item layer under each, it doesn't
redefine the categories themselves.

## Conventional

### Small Guns

**Availability, confirmed 2026-08-16: DLC-specific, not base game.** Guns belong to border cities — the ones
in genuine physical proximity to Upper Earth — not Concordia. This is consistent with, and sharpens, the
Philosophy file's own framing below: not just institutional and rare in the abstract, but geographically
absent from the interior capital entirely. A Concordia-only playthrough never encounters this category at all.

Per `Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md`, guns are the one deliberate exception to "not designed as a weapon" —
institutional, rare, kept at arm's length from civilian life (a customs post, a border watch). These don't need
a repurposed-tool cover story; they're already the thing they look like.

- **Sidearm** *(9mm-pistol equivalent)* — standard-issue customs/border-post holdout.
- **Hand Cannon** *(.357/hunting-revolver equivalent)* — higher-stopping-power revolver-pattern sidearm, the
  rare piece a border post keeps for genuinely dangerous wildlife encounters as much as for people.
- **Machine Pistol** *(10mm SMG equivalent)* — rapid-fire small arm, the closest thing Tepenia's institutional
  arsenal has to a "spray" option.
- **Holdout Pistol** *(derringer equivalent)* — small enough to conceal; the rare piece that leaves the
  customs post at all, carried by a specific individual rather than racked at a station.

### Long Guns

**Availability, confirmed 2026-08-16: DLC-specific, not base game — same border-city logic as Small Guns above.**

- **Service Rifle** — standard border-watch long arm, the rifle-rack equivalent of the sidearm above.
- **Marksman Rifle** *(sniper equivalent)* — precision long-range piece; institutional, almost never fired at
  a person, used for perimeter observation and genuine wildlife-hazard response (a Weddell seal or leopard
  seal too close to a work crew).
- **Scattergun** *(shotgun equivalent)* — doubles diegetically as signal-flare hardware; the same weapon that
  can fire buckshot can fire a flare cartridge, and most of its institutional use is the latter.
- **Carbine** *(assault-carbine equivalent)* — the compact, faster-handling long gun a mobile border patrol
  carries instead of the full-size service rifle.

### Heavy Weapons

**Availability, confirmed 2026-08-16 — split within the category, not all-or-nothing like Small/Long Guns
above.** Thermal Lance is base-game, available in Concordia — an industrial ice-clearing/welding tool has
obvious everyday civilian need at the interior capital. Every other item below (Autocannon, Breach Launcher,
Support Launcher, and the open Fat Man question) is DLC-specific, tied to the same border-proximity/frontier
logic as Small and Long Guns — convoy escort, demolition-scale conflict, and genuine military-adjacent
infrastructure aren't things Concordia itself needs.

Per the Philosophy file, this category descends from convoy-escort and demolition equipment — collective
infrastructure defense, not personal armament.

- **Thermal Lance** *(flamer/incinerator equivalent)* — **base game, Concordia.** An industrial ice-clearing
  and welding tool at civilian scale; a sustained, directed heat source for melting blockages or cutting
  structural material.
- **Autocannon** *(minigun equivalent)* — **DLC-specific.** Convoy-escort-mounted, protecting a Rastra convoy
  rather than a person; almost never man-portable in its civilian role.
- **Breach Launcher** *(rocket-launcher equivalent)* — **DLC-specific.** Fires demolition charges at range for
  controlled structural collapse (clearing an unstable ice formation, breaching a blocked tunnel) rather than
  direct anti-personnel use.
- **Support Launcher** *(grenade-launcher equivalent)* — **DLC-specific.** Lobs the same avalanche-control/
  structural charges the Thrown/Explosives category below covers, just at range and volume a hand-thrown
  charge can't match.
- **Open question, flagged rather than resolved — also DLC-specific if it ends up existing at all:** FNV's Fat
  Man (a man-portable tactical nuke launcher) has no obvious non-genre-breaking Tepenia equivalent in a
  hard-sci-fi, no-magic setting — including it as-is would be a tonal outlier even relative to FNV's own
  already-outlier weapon. Worth a developer decision: omit entirely, or reinterpret as something non-nuclear
  (an extreme-yield structural charge, scaled up from the Breach Launcher above) that keeps the "rare,
  terrifying, city-block-scale" *role* without the actual nuclear framing.

### Blunt Melee

Per the Philosophy file, the most mundane category in the whole taxonomy — no narrative weight needed to
justify presence in a scene.

- **Ice Axe** — already established; every household, every work crew has one.
- **Pry Bar** — already established.
- **Sledgehammer** — already established.
- **Security Baton** *(nightstick equivalent)* — the one blunt item that *is* institutional rather than
  household, carried by customs/security staff alongside the Small Guns rack.
- **Pipe Wrench** — heavy maintenance tooling, industrial-district ubiquitous.
- **Structural-Stress Hammer** *(super sledge equivalent)* — the powered/reinforced version of the ordinary
  sledgehammer, the same device `Weapons_and_Tools_Philosophy.md`'s own worked example describes (bringing
  down an unstable ice shelf before it kills a work crew).

### Bladed Melee

- **Survival Knife** — the single most universally-carried Tepenian item in this entire catalog; basic
  ice-safety and utility gear, carried by nearly everyone regardless of profession.
- **Ice-Core Drill** — already established.
- **Flensing Knife** — already established (marine-resource processing).
- **Cable Stripper** *(machete equivalent)* — a long, single-edge blade for stripping insulation and clearing
  cable runs, filling the role a machete fills in dense vegetation with Tepenia's own dense wiring instead.
- **Mess Cleaver** — food-preparation tooling, present anywhere meals are made at scale.
- **Rotary Cutter** *(Ripper equivalent)* — a powered, toothed cutting tool for ice-core or cable work; the
  one bladed item whose civilian identity is already inherently violent-*looking* despite a fully mundane
  purpose, which is exactly the kind of gap the TV series' "before" framing exists to close.
- **Thermal Edge** *(Shishkebab equivalent)* — a plasma/thermal-assisted cutting blade for ice work, sitting
  at the direct crossover point between this category and Thermal Weapons below; worth flagging as one item
  that could reasonably live in either category's cross-reference table.

### Thrown/Explosives

- **Avalanche Charge** — already established; controlled destruction used to prevent a bigger disaster.
- **Signal Flare/Smoke Charge** — genuinely non-lethal in civilian use, distress-signal and marking equipment;
  the one item in this whole category that's a weapon only in the loosest sense even in-game.
- **Hazard Trip-Charge** *(mine equivalent)* — left in place rather than thrown, marking/triggering unstable
  ice for controlled collapse before a crew works nearby; genuinely closer to a safety-engineering tool than
  a mine's classic ambush framing, and worth writing that distinction into any scene that uses one.
- **Utility Hatchet/Throwing Knife** — covered by `Game-Mechanics/Combat/Throwing_Weapons.md`'s own mechanic
  (stays where it lands, must be retrieved); civilian identity is the same ice-work/utility toolkit as Bladed
  Melee above, just balanced for throwing specifically.
- **Demolition Charge** *(dynamite/C4 equivalent)* — hand-placed, civilian engineering-grade explosive;
  distinct from the Breach Launcher above by delivery method (placed vs. fired) rather than purpose.

### Unarmed

**The category most in need of this pass — flagged directly by the developer.** The existing category-level
entry only covers bare-frame/bare-hand combat; FNV's own Unarmed skill has a whole sub-category of worn/held
items that enhance an unarmed attack, and Tepenia's own cold-weather/industrial-labor context supplies
genuinely better tool-first justifications for most of them than FNV's original wasteland setting did.

- **Bare Fists/Frame** — already established; for robots, no exposed striking surface, just reinforced frame
  under synthetic skin.
- **Reinforced Work Gloves** *(brass knuckles equivalent)* — standard heavy-industry safety gear with
  knuckle-plate reinforcement; genuinely universal cold-weather/industrial equipment, not a specialty item.
- **Ice-Grip Gloves** *(spiked knuckles equivalent)* — crampon-style traction spikes worn on the hand for ice
  climbing and crevasse work — this is arguably a *better*, more organic tool-justification than FNV's own
  spiked knuckles ever had, since Antarctic ice work has a genuine, everyday need for hand-mounted traction
  spikes that has nothing to do with combat at all.
- **Powered Cargo Gauntlet** *(power fist equivalent)* — an exosuit hand actuator built for heavy cargo
  handling and construction work at the docks; the single strongest tool-first fit in this entire catalog,
  since powered lifting gauntlets are exactly the kind of equipment a Tepenian shipping/construction economy
  would already have in wide civilian use.
- **Boxing Wraps** — sport/recreation gear, assuming a district or subculture with an established boxing or
  hand-to-hand sporting tradition exists to hang this on; flagged as needing that grounding rather than
  invented from nothing.

## Energy-Adjacent

### Thermal Weapons

Already established as the most intimate category in the whole taxonomy — coolant-tending as an act of care,
not utility.

- **Freeze Injector** *(the Cold end)* — coolant-system diagnostic and maintenance tool.
- **Cutting Torch** *(the Fire/Thermal end)* — exothermic cutting tool, industrial and welding use.

### Arc Weapons

Arcanet and electrical maintenance work — unglamorous infrastructure labor.

- **Arc-Welding Rod** — standard electrical/structural maintenance tooling.
- **Line Tester Probe** — a handheld tool for testing cable-fault and voltage state on Arcanet/power
  infrastructure, the specific tool a "lineman" or "Arcanet relay technician" (per the Philosophy file's own
  framing) would actually carry.

### Cell-Fed Weapons

Vehicle and fabrication-chamber maintenance work — the closest thing to "high-tech" in the pre-war set, but
still fundamentally maintenance tooling.

- **Cell Diagnostic Rig** — energy-cell handling and testing equipment; per the Philosophy file, handling one
  of these is closer to handling currency than ammunition.
- **Laser Leveler/Cutter** *(Radiant end)* — precision alignment and cutting tool for fabrication-chamber
  construction and calibration work.

## Tepenia-Original

Already the deepest category-level philosophy in the existing files (the widest gap between pre-war civilian
identity and in-game weapon identity); adding the specific tool-items each category's own philosophy implies.

### Structural Ordnance

- **Glacier-Stress Driver** — the actual field tool used to trigger controlled structural venting; civil
  engineering hardware, not military ordnance.

### Drain Weapons

- **Coolant Siphon Kit** — the core item behind the Siphon mechanic (`Damage_Types.md`); a caretaker's tending
  tool, drawing and monitoring coolant rather than draining it adversarially.
- **Diagnostic Tap** — the Exposure Siphon's human-side equivalent tool: field-medicine equipment for reading
  body heat/hydration/stamina state, repurposed offensively rather than designed for it.

### Signal Weapons

- **Arcanet Diagnostic Probe** — network-administration hardware; the quiet, largely invisible labor of
  keeping the communication grid running, per the Philosophy file's own framing.
- **Network Tap Tool** — used for legitimate line-monitoring and fault-tracing, repurposed as the delivery
  mechanism for a hostile quickhack-style payload.

## Genuinely New Tepenia-Original Items — No FNV Equivalent

Items the setting's own science and culture supply that Fallout's Mojave wasteland never needed a tool for.
Flagged clearly as new proposals, not yet cross-referenced into `Damage_Types.md`'s own category table.

- **Crevasse Grapple** — a rescue-harness launcher for pulling a person (or retrieving equipment) out of a
  crevasse; repurposable as a hooking/pulling weapon with genuine tactical utility (dragging a target off
  balance or off cover) that has no real FNV analog, since nothing in the Mojave's own toolkit does rescue
  work quite like this.
- **Windbreak Turbine Fragment** — a broken-off blade from district windbreak/turbine infrastructure (per
  Mirny's own established architecture — a literal windbreak city); an improvised, heavy, aerodynamically
  vicious blunt-or-bladed hybrid, the kind of thing someone picks up in a fight rather than carries
  deliberately.
- **Seal-Processing Hook** — marine-resource-economy tooling (Zhongshan's Prydz Bay fishing economy and
  similar coastal cities); a heavy hooked implement for handling large marine catch, repurposable as a
  puncture/drag weapon distinct in profile from the Flensing Knife's cutting role.
- **Katabatic Vent Charge** — a controlled-release pressure charge used in wind-channeling infrastructure
  maintenance (relevant to any city whose architecture manages katabatic wind, per Mirny's own established
  windbreak design); a directional blast tool distinct from the Avalanche Charge's omnidirectional profile.

## Universal / Nationwide Items — Not City-Specific

**Established 2026-08-16.** Everything above this section is grounded in one specific city's own local
industry. These items instead come from what's common to *the whole country's* shared conditions — extreme
cold and six-month dark/light cycles, a standardized national vehicle fleet, robot-human coexistence, and the
shared exile history most of the population's own ancestry traces back to. A given item here could plausibly
show up in the hands of a character from almost any city, unlike the "Genuinely New" section above, where each
item is tied to one specific place.

**Snow/Ice Safety Gear** *(wherever there's ice terrain — i.e., everywhere)*
- **Avalanche Probe** — a collapsible pole in every safety kit; already spear-shaped by design, no
  reinterpretation needed at all.
- **Snow Shovel** — plausibly the single most universally-owned tool in the country; every household, every
  vehicle.
- **Crampons** — foot-spikes, distinct from the hand-worn Ice-Grip Gloves above; a kicking/stomping weapon
  nobody would think twice about someone carrying.
- **Ice-Climbing Rope + Carabiner** — whip/garrote potential, standard mountaineering-adjacent gear anywhere
  near a crevasse.

**National Vehicle (Rastra) Kit** *(the established convoy vehicle used everywhere, not one city's own gear)*
- **Tire Iron / Lug Wrench**
- **Jumper Cables** — whip-shaped by accident.
- **Windshield Ice-Scraper** — blunt/edge, in every vehicle in the country.

**Universal Household Items** *(present in literally every home regardless of city)*
- **Cast-Iron Skillet** — mundane bludgeoning, present wherever food gets cooked.
- **Mending Needle/Awl** — resource scarcity plus isolation makes clothing repair a near-universal household
  value, not just Zhongshan's own explicit culture around it (see its own file's fashion/clothing-maintenance
  section).

**Robot-Universal Items** *(present wherever robots are — everywhere — tied to already-established robot
culture: siligel, glitch-coolant, robot smoking)*
- **Glitch-Coolant Flask** — the robot alcohol-equivalent (see `project_glitch_coolant` context); a heavy
  glass bottle.
- **Siligel Applicator Spatula** — skin-maintenance tool with a real edge.
- **Smoking Pipe + Lighter** — small fire-starting tool, tied to established robot smoking culture.

**Blackout/Energy-Scarcity Preparedness** *(a national concern per the power-grid story mechanic, not
city-specific)*
- **Hand-Crank Emergency Light** — blunt.
- **Candle + Holder** — polar-night preparedness, broader than any one city's own candlelit culture.
- **Energy-Cell Swap Pry Tool**.

**Founding-Generation/Exile-History Items** *(universal to most of the population's own ancestry)*
- **Exile Ration Kit Multitool** — a literal Falkland Treaty–era artifact many families would still own;
  heirloom-weighted rather than generic, worth writing that way in any specific character's hands.
- **Evacuation Harness Clip** — tied to the Long Night War's own space-elevator evacuation; carries real
  historical weight for any character whose family actually went through it.

**National Arcanet/Comm Devices**
- **Comm-Device Antenna Whip** — a literal whip, no reinterpretation required.

## Open Items

- The Fat Man/mini-nuke question under Heavy Weapons, above — genuinely unresolved, needs a developer call.
- Boxing Wraps' cultural grounding (which district or subculture actually has a boxing/hand-to-hand sporting
  tradition) is asserted as a need, not yet answered.
- Whether the Thermal Edge belongs primarily under Bladed Melee or Thermal Weapons in `Damage_Types.md`'s own
  cross-reference table — flagged, not decided, since it's a genuine crossover item.
- None of the four "Genuinely New" items above have been cross-referenced into `Damage_Types.md`'s Weapon
  Category Cross-Reference table yet — that table still only reflects the original 13 categories' existing
  items, not this catalog's additions.
- **Base-game-vs-DLC availability, confirmed 2026-08-16 for Small Guns, Long Guns, and Heavy Weapons — not yet
  checked for the other 10 categories.** Small/Long Guns are entirely DLC-specific (border-city-only); Heavy
  Weapons splits within the category (Thermal Lance is Concordia/base-game, everything else DLC-specific).
  Worth a deliberate pass to confirm whether any other category (Blunt/Bladed Melee, Thrown/Explosives,
  Unarmed, the Energy-Adjacent trio, the Tepenia-Original trio) has a similar Concordia-vs-border-city split
  that hasn't been stated yet, rather than assuming every other category is uniformly base-game by default.
