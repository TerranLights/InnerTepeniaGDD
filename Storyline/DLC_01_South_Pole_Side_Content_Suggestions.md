**Status: collection of suggestions, not canon.** Started 2026-10-18. Gathers the developer's new idea for how the DLC's geography and side content could work, plus every related note that was already scattered across other files, so they live in one place. Confirmed design stays in `DLC_01_Echoes_of_Amundsen.md`; the physical site is in `Worldspace/Characters/Dolls/Still-Present_-_In-Game/recruitable/Kendra Heinrich/DLC_South_Pole_Level_Design.md`.

## 1 · The developer's idea, 2026-10-18: two pathways to the station, a town above and below

**The arrival.** The player reaches the edge of the DLC in their Rastra (the overland vehicle). From there, there are essentially **two pathways to the station: one overland, one below the ice.**

**Why there are two.** The population of the South Pole (Amundsen-Scott Station) would be in the **low four digits, perhaps a couple of thousand**, not the twenty or thirty people of the real station today. A couple of thousand people is **a town**: not a full city like the coastal cities, but a real town. So there would be **an underground network of tunnels, stations, actual living infrastructure, and so on.**

**Two sets of lethal dangers.** Depending on whether the player is above or below the surface, there is **an entirely different set of lethal, deadly dangers** to face on the way to Amundsen-Scott Station.

*(Paraphrased from the developer's message of 2026-10-18; the structure above is theirs, the wording is a summary.)*

### Answers from the developer, 2026-10-18
- **Population: the official census governs.** The town is the size the census says (Census I 6,857; Census II 4,891, per `Specs/Amundsen_Station.md`), not "low four digits." The earlier phrase was a rough guess.
- **When the town existed:** it was **definitely a full town during the Second Interwar Period**, when the Tepenian Federation was a proper country. **What it is like after the Long Night War is still to be determined,** and needs further design, worldbuilding and research.
- **The pathways and the eleven entry methods:** the two pathways (overland and below the ice) are **the routes to the station's door**; the eleven methods are **how the player gets inside the station itself.**
- **Surface and underground are compatible:** there can be **one general underground network below the ice, leading toward the station,** and a **further, deeper series of underground chambers, which is where the player eventually finds Kendra Heinrich.** (To reconcile later with the confirmed design that she is locked inside the Amundsen-Scott Station building, and with the level design's open question on her exact placement within the underground layer.)
- **What the dangers above and below are: held off** until it is known what the place is actually like.

## 2 · Related notes already scattered across the repo

### The frozen dead along the evacuation routes
- `TODO.md`, logged 2026-07-03: during the Long Night War, human and robot bodies would plausibly be found frozen in the ice along the routes various subnet populations took trying to reach Amundsen Tower. The Tower's own passenger capacity was never the bottleneck; the desperate journey to it was (`Theoretical-Calculations/Amundsen_Tower_Space_Fountain_Design.md`).
- Placed in the **Debris Apron** zone in the level design (evacuees who didn't make it in time).
- Supported by the release-order rule (`DLC_Overview.md`): the subnet DLCs come first, so a player who has met each subnet's culture will recognize what they are looking at.
- Candidate end-slide angle: whether the dead's stories made it home to their subnets.

### The zones of the site (level design file)
| Zone | Where | Content ideas already noted |
|---|---|---|
| The Mountain | ~50–100 m radius, ~27–109 m tall | Traversal and climbing challenge; likely where the buried underground access is; densest wreckage and highest-value salvage |
| Debris Apron | 2–4 km out (up to ~10 km in storms) | Scavenging content; environmental storytelling; the frozen dead; melted pellet-stream fragments |
| Clear Ground | beyond ~4–10 km | A present-day outpost or camp, possibly a hub; the Vigil connection |
| Underground | beneath the Mountain | The dungeon heart; largely intact shaft and tunnels; damaged near the power vault; corrupted systems and residual energy hazards |

### Lore content in the underground utility tunnels
The confirmed first entry method (the utility tunnel network) should be **populated with lore content** about the Long Night War at this site: terminal entries, personal journals, audio logs, environmental storytelling (abandoned equipment, signs of hasty evacuation or last stands, evidence of what the station was doing when the war hit). One of the few places where pre-war primary sources are physically present. To be developed in Phase 7 (`DLC_01_Echoes_of_Amundsen.md`).

### The Archivist's Trail (cross-DLC side content)
An optional breadcrumb chain running Pergamino, then Esperanza, then Sanay, ending in a physical decryption codekey that skips the archive's extreme decryption check. It pays off in this DLC. Full detail in `DLC_01_Echoes_of_Amundsen.md`.

### The Vigil
Their reverence for the site, and the existing rule that repurposing Tower scrap triggers Infamy with them, fit a present-day camp in the Clear Ground zone; scavenging reads as desecrating a mass grave and war memorial. The connection between the DLC and the Vigil faction in Concordia is still an open item.

### Vosora Lashár Tanslock
She organized the Tower's original construction logistics and is now in Concordia. A natural questline hook: unique knowledge of the original facility layout (accelerator geometry, tunnel network, power plant location) to help navigate the underground layer, a builder paired with Kendra's defender.

### Hazards and threats noted but not yet designed
- Residual electromagnetic wreckage hazards.
- A dedicated archive-guardian system.
- Upper Earth's own on-site personnel or assets, flagged 2026-07-05 and deferred pending an investigation of how they could be sustained for a decade.
- Corrupted automated systems in the tunnels (parallel to Concordia's corrupted control AIs).
- Unexploded ordnance buried in the debris.

### Cross-DLC bypass gifts
The Cross-DLC Bypass law means each other DLC contributes one optional bypass gift to this one. Only the base game's (cloaking technology), Mawson's (an extreme-cold survival perk) and Halley's (the Archivist's Trail codekey) are designed; Byrd, Palmer, Janbogo and Mirny are not.

### End-slide angles (five candidates, 2026-07-03)
Kendra's personal fate; the site's epilogue; the evacuation dead; the Arcanet archive and the Planetary Split Brain; a capstone reflection across all six other DLCs.
