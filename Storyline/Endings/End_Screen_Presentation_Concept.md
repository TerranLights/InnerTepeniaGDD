# End-Screen Presentation Concept — Holographic Epilogue Space

**Status:** 🟡 initial concept capture, not yet fleshed out. Records the developer's own idea as described;
nothing here is locked. Complements `Main-Endings/Climax_Structure_and_District_Ending_Consequences.md`,
which already anticipates its own per-district "Minor Negatives" lists being delivered as "epilogue slides"
— this document is the answer to how that delivery actually works.

---

## The core idea

Fallout: New Vegas closes with a series of 2D slideshow images, one per faction/location/character
thread, each narrated in voiceover describing the resulting fate based on the player's choices.

Tepenia's version keeps the same *function* (a sequence of vignettes covering the game's various threads,
shaped by player choice) but changes the *presentation*: instead of flat 2D images projected onto a screen,
each vignette is a **3D scene projected as a giant hologram** — diegetically plausible in-world, given
holographic tech is already established elsewhere in the setting (Leo's holographic performer posters,
Gemini's holographic data displays). The visual reference point is Cyberpunk 2077's Mikoshi/Cyberspace
sequences — an abstract, simulated environment given depth and definition by small glowing blue and red
particles/dots, used purely for visual texture rather than any coded meaning (confirmed explicitly: the
blue/red distinction is not a friendly/hostile signal or any other semantic marker, just how the space
reads as three-dimensional and alive rather than empty).

## The asset-variable mechanic

Because each vignette is a full 3D scene rather than a static image, it can encode information two ways at
once:

1. **Character-level narration** — the broad, general outcome for a given person or population, delivered
   the way FNV's own slides are (voiceover, or an in-fiction equivalent, describing what became of them).
2. **Physical asset variables, shown rather than told** — small material details within the 3D scene that
   silently reflect specific player-driven state, without needing their own line of narration. The
   example given: whether a specific door is open or closed. A detail like this can carry real
   informational weight (was this place abandoned in a hurry, is it still in use, is someone home) purely
   through set dressing, the way a scene's staging can imply things a script never says outright.

**Why this matters mechanically:** it lets the epilogue system account for a much wider range of granular,
player-specific outcomes than narration alone could carry without becoming bloated or repetitive. The
`Climax_Structure_and_District_Ending_Consequences.md` document's own per-district "Minor Negatives" lists
are full of exactly this kind of granular, specific consequence (a status change, a relationship shift, a
piece of infrastructure's new condition) — many of which could be conveyed as an asset variable inside a
holographic vignette rather than requiring their own dedicated narrated slide.

**Example, using existing material:** a Scorpio-ending vignette could narrate, broadly, what became of the
district's population under Scorpio's own "Only Way Through" victory — while a background asset variable
(a specific door, left open or closed) silently reflects some more granular outcome already established in
that district's own Minor Negatives list, without needing to be spoken aloud at all.

## Open for future development

- The diegetic framing device itself (what in-world system actually projects this — Calethina's Lab tech,
  an Arcanet-native visualization, something Hub-specific tied to the Bridge Memorial, or left
  unexplained/abstracted the way FNV's own slides never explain their own presentation) is not yet decided.
- Which specific outcomes get full narrated treatment vs. which get folded into background asset variables
  is not yet mapped per district.
- Whether every district ending gets its own full vignette, or whether some are folded together, is not
  yet decided — `Climax_Structure_and_District_Ending_Consequences.md` already has full Victory
  Condition / Main Negative / Minor Negatives writeups for all 13 districts to draw from either way.
