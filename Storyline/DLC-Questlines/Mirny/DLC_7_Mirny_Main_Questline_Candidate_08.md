# DLC 7 (Mirny Subnet) — Main Questline Candidate #8: "The Same Sky, Read Twice"

**What this is:** an eighth prospective But/Therefore chain for DLC 7's main questline, produced by running
`../DLC_Main_Questline_Design_Method.md` against Mirny subnet, built entirely around a single city's own
internal life rather than an inter-city relationship. **Not canon, not chosen, not the only candidate** —
the 8th of the "at least 10" the method calls for. Uses the Linear Escalation Chain schematic
(`../But_Therefore_Quest_Design_Method.md` §5a).

**Uses 1 of Mirny subnet's 8 cities (Kunlun alone)** — the second candidate, after #7, to deliberately use
fewer cities than the cast could technically support, because the actual tension doesn't require an
inter-city relationship to be complete.

---

## Inputs actually used (Step 1 of the design method)

- **Kunlun's own Full Extrapolation, Section IV** — "Scientists and Devotees: A Real Internal Distinction."
  Astronomers and comms specialists organize their time purely around instrument/signal schedules;
  Ice-Cold Buddhism devotees layer formal, observed stillness onto the same conditions — most significantly
  during the −68°C perfect calm of polar night — as devotional practice, not merely working conditions. "A
  scientist and a devotee can be doing the same task at the same instrument for entirely different reasons,
  and both readings are true at once."
- **Kunlun's own Full Extrapolation, Section II** — the observatory's real, established research focus
  includes near-Earth object monitoring (asteroid/comet tracking), giving this chain's triggering event a
  genuine, already-established scientific basis distinct from the Tower-debris material used in Candidate
  #4.
- **Kunlun's own Local Cultures, §6(c) and §6(f)** — Kunlun has no civic clock at all: nobody treats the
  astronomy/comms work as a "job" separate from their life, the city genuinely never closes, and there's no
  shared "evening" the way most cities have one.
- **Explicitly excluded:** any connection to the Amundsen Tower's collapse (deliberately kept separate from
  Candidate #4, which already uses that material); any resolution of Vosora Lashár Tanslock's own
  still-undetermined Kunlun connection (`TODO.md` — flagged 2026-07-06, exact nature TBD, a separate,
  developer-reserved question this chain doesn't touch or presume to answer); Ice-Cold Buddhism's own
  unresolved theological content beyond what's already concretely established (the stillness practice
  itself, not its wider doctrine).

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A rare, genuinely significant transient astronomical event — real, time-sensitive, and squarely
within the observatory's own established near-Earth-object monitoring focus — is calculated to occur during
a specific, narrow window: the −68°C perfect calm of polar night, the exact conditions Kunlun's own
devotional population treats as its most sacred stillness observance.

**BUT** — This isn't a scheduling conflict to simply mediate — the stillness the devotional practice
requires and the instrument activity full observation requires are readings of the *same physical
conditions*, not two different things competing for the same slot. Neither side is wrong about what the
moment actually is.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to find people who can genuinely hold both readings at once — task-focused
enough to run the observation correctly, devoted enough to do it with the reverence the moment is already
understood, by half the city, to deserve — rather than simply overruling one group in favor of the other.

**BUT** — Genuinely holding both at once turns out to be much rarer than the city's own comfortable,
usually-unexamined "both readings are true at once" self-image assumes. Asked directly, in a real,
high-stakes moment, most residents who'd have called themselves both scientist and devotee find themselves
actually leaning toward one when it matters — and are visibly uncomfortable discovering that about
themselves.

**THEREFORE** — What the player is actually doing shifts from "assemble the right team" to "help Kunlun's
own population discover, for the first time under real pressure, which of the two things they'd always
assumed coexisted easily actually comes first for each of them individually."

**BUT** — Time is still running out — the astronomical window won't wait for anyone's own internal
reckoning, however genuine or overdue.

**THEREFORE** — Consistent with this project's own No Good Endings law, a workable team is found in time —
the event genuinely is observed, and the observatory's own record grows by one real, earned entry — but the
resolution isn't a synthesis. It's a handful of individuals making a real, personal choice under pressure,
for the first time, about which of their two identities actually leads — and Kunlun's own comfortable
civic assumption that the two always simply coexist doesn't survive the DLC unchanged, even though nothing
about the city's actual daily life visibly changes afterward.

## Self-check (per the method's §7)

Reading only the conjunctions: SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. No repeated
conjunction in a row. Each BUT changes the shape of the obstacle: first BUT turns "resolve a scheduling
conflict" into "the two needs are structurally, not just logistically, incompatible"; second BUT turns
"find dual-identity people" into "genuine dual identity is rarer than assumed, forcing real self-discovery";
third BUT keeps real time pressure on a beat that could otherwise drift into pure introspection. Deleting
any one Complication changes what the player is actually doing afterward, so none reads as a disguised And
Then.

## Worth Your Attention

This is the smallest-cast candidate so far — one city, no inter-city travel at all — and it's the first to
locate its entire dramatic engine inside a single city's own established internal culture (Section IV's
scientist/devotee distinction) rather than a relationship between cities. Its core asset is that Kunlun's
own source material already states the tension in almost exactly the shape this chain needs ("both readings
are true at once") without ever testing whether that comfortable coexistence actually holds under real
pressure — this chain's only real contribution is asking that question directly. Worth noting for whoever
picks up DLC 7's actual cast decisions later: Candidates #4, #7, and #8 now demonstrate the method works at
three different cast sizes (3 cities, 2 cities, 1 city) without forcing material that isn't there.
