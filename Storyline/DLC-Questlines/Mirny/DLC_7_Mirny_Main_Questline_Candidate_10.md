# DLC 7 (Mirny Subnet) — Main Questline Candidate #10: "What the Tea Doesn't Ask"

**What this is:** a tenth prospective But/Therefore chain for DLC 7's main questline, produced by running
`../DLC_Main_Questline_Design_Method.md` against Mirny subnet, built around Zhongshan's own internal
generational fault line rather than any of its established inter-city rivalries (Candidate #2's tri-cities
identity conflict, Candidates #5/#6's logistics/temperament chains). **Not canon, not chosen, not the only
candidate** — the 10th of the "at least 10" the method calls for, completing the method's minimum count.
Uses the Linear Escalation Chain schematic (`../But_Therefore_Quest_Design_Method.md` §5a).

**Uses 2 of Mirny subnet's 8 cities (Zhongshan, Davis)** — a pairing no prior candidate has used. Distinct
from Candidate #7's Davis/Shirayuki pairing in cast, and from Candidate #3's Davis/Casey/Mirny pairing in
both cast and theme.

**Flag before use:** Zhongshan's own Local Cultures §23 establishes an explicit "friendly rivalry" with
Davis ("Zhongshan considers Davis somewhat loud; Davis considers Zhongshan somewhat cold. Both are
correct."). Davis's own Local Cultures §23 entry for Zhongshan is thinner and doesn't confirm this texture
("no strong established relationship beyond shared subnet membership and composition similarity") — the two
files aren't fully in sync on this specific point. This chain builds on Zhongshan's fuller, more specific
account rather than silently picking a side; worth a real cross-file consistency pass whenever this subnet's
Local Cultures files next get audited, separate from whether this candidate itself gets used.

## Inputs actually used (Step 1 of the design method)

- **Zhongshan's own Local Cultures, §5b (Fault lines)** — the established generational cost of the
  non-prying norm: some notable-tier families "know exactly" how much of their original heritage survives
  after several generations of quiet osmosis into Zhongshan's public Chinese culture; others "genuinely
  aren't sure anymore," and the culture that would never pry into it "is also the culture that will never
  help them find out."
- **Zhongshan's own Local Cultures, §20 (Private Life & Minority Communities)** — the same fact restated at
  the household level: a "slow grief" the city, consistent with its own character, "does not acknowledge and
  does not ask about."
- **Zhongshan's own Local Cultures, §24 (Arcanet Culture)** — the city's archive is explicitly a "research
  and archive tool, not a social one"; personal information about people is structurally outside its
  mandate, unlike its rigorous public/scientific record-keeping.
- **Zhongshan's own Local Cultures, §19 (Underground/Counterculture)** and Cross-Reference Synthesis
  Finding 2 — the "Loud Quarter," the city's unnamed counterculture district, is the one place in Zhongshan
  where personal disclosure isn't just tolerated but is the entire point; its namelessness is itself an
  expression of non-prying-as-respect operating at civic scale, not administrative neglect.
- **Zhongshan's own Local Cultures §1 and Davis's own Local Cultures §1** — both cities independently carry
  Australia as a Significant-tier, founding-wave nation, giving a family with branches in both cities a
  genuinely grounded shared origin rather than an invented one.
- **Davis's own Local Cultures, §6/§7** — Davis's contrasting civic register: "do the work well, let the
  work speak," quietly thorough rather than ceremonial, a culture that draws people who want useful,
  well-informed help delivered without much fuss — the established foil to Zhongshan's non-prying austerity.
- **Explicitly excluded:** any claim that the family's missing history connects to the Planetary Split
  Brain, the Cradle network, or Mountain Pass — none of that is touched. This chain is about an ordinary
  family, not a subnet-spanning mystery.

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A notable-tier, Australian-descended Zhongshan family asks the player to help re-establish
contact with a branch of the same family they believe settled in Davis generations ago — a workshop tool,
an heirloom recipe, or a claimed craft lineage the Zhongshan family wants formally recognized requires proof
of the actual connection. It looks like a short errand: cross to Davis, find the relatives, confirm the
link.

**BUT** — Nobody in the Zhongshan family, and no Zhongshan civic record, actually knows the specifics
anymore. Three or four generations of the non-prying norm mean the family's own private history was never
written down, never asked about, and never passed on in enough detail to prove anything — exactly the
fault line Zhongshan's own culture predicts (§5b) but has never had to confront this directly before.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to go around the norm rather than through it. Zhongshan's own archive can't
help (§24 — personal history isn't its mandate), and asking neighbors or extended family directly is a real
social violation in this city. The only lead left runs through the Loud Quarter — the one place in Zhongshan
where people talk about exactly this kind of thing, because refusing to be private is the district's whole
point.

**BUT** — What the counterculture contacts actually remember is partial and contested — different people
hold different fragments, none of them coordinated, none of them official. The player doesn't get an answer;
they get several competing half-answers, each plausible, none provable from the Zhongshan side alone.

**THEREFORE** — The player carries these fragments to Davis, expecting Davis's own more open, direct culture
to simply fill in the rest.

**BUT** — It does, and that's the reversal: the Davis branch has a complete, well-kept record, because
Davis's culture never had a non-prying taboo suppressing it in the first place. The player isn't just
missing a fact anymore — they're holding a full, clear answer that the Zhongshan family itself doesn't have
and, per its own culture, was never going to go looking for. Delivering it isn't a neutral errand-completion
anymore; it's handing a private family the exact thing their own city's social contract exists to spare
people from ever having to confront: precisely how much has actually been lost.

**THEREFORE** — Consistent with this project's own No Good Endings law, there's no clean resolution here,
only a real choice in how the player delivers what they found: hand it over quietly and let the family
decide privately what to do with it (honoring Zhongshan's own preferred mode, but risking that the answer
sits, unresolved, exactly like every fragment already living in that family); or actively broker a real
meeting between the Davis and Zhongshan branches (a genuinely reconnective act, but a visibly public one that
overrides the very cultural value — private, un-pried-into heritage — the family's own city has spent five
generations protecting). Either path costs the family something real; neither is presented as the "right"
answer.

## Self-check (per the method's §7)

Reading only the conjunctions: SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. No repeated
conjunction in a row. Each BUT changes the shape of the problem: the first BUT turns "confirm a family
link" into "the normal channels for finding out are all culturally closed"; the second BUT turns "get a
lead" into "the lead is fragmented and unresolvable from Zhongshan's side alone"; the third BUT turns "cross
-check in Davis" into "the missing information was never actually lost, it was just never asked about," which
converts the whole quest from a fact-finding errand into a question of whether the family should be told what
was found at all. Deleting any one Complication changes what the player is actually deciding at the end, so
none reads as a disguised And Then.

## Worth Your Attention

This candidate's core asset is that it's the first one to build entirely on Zhongshan's own *internal*
identity fault line (§5b/§20) rather than any of its inter-city relationships or archival-custodian role —
material every prior Zhongshan-involving candidate (#2, #5, #6) left untouched in favor of tri-cities
dynamics or subnet logistics. Pairing it with Davis isn't incidental: Davis is the one Mirny subnet city that
shares Zhongshan's specific Australian founding-wave demographic (§1 in both files) while having developed
the opposite civic answer to privacy, which is exactly what makes the reversal land — the same starting
material, two generations of divergent culture, and a player caught delivering the consequence of that
divergence to a family that never chose to have it measured. The Davis/Zhongshan relationship-entry mismatch
flagged above is worth resolving on its own merits regardless of whether this candidate is ever used.
