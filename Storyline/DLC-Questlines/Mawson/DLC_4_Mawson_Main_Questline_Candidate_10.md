# DLC 4 (Mawson Subnet) — Main Questline Candidate #10: "Neighbors, Not Kin"

**What this is:** a tenth prospective But/Therefore chain for DLC 4's main questline, produced by running
`../DLC_Main_Questline_Design_Method.md` against Mawson subnet, completing this subnet's own minimum count of
ten distinct candidates the same way Candidate #10 did for Mirny, Halley, Janbogo, and Palmer subnets. Built
around the subnet's own core identity thesis directly — "looking outward," no shared internal identity —
rather than any single city's own material. **Not canon, not chosen, not the only candidate** — the 10th of
the "at least 10" the method calls for. Uses the Linear Escalation Chain schematic
(`../But_Therefore_Quest_Design_Method.md` §5a).

**Uses all 3 of Mawson subnet's cities** (Mawson, Sayowa, Dome Fuji).

---

## Inputs actually used (Step 1 of the design method)

- **Mawson Subnet Full Extrapolation, Section I** — the subnet's own dominant trait, named directly: "a
  subnet defined more by its outward connections than by any shared internal identity." Mawson carries the
  hub's quiet acceptance, oriented toward the whole nation; Sayowa carries pure connective function, oriented
  toward Halley subnet and Concordia; Dome Fuji carries the subnet's one self-contained identity, oriented
  doctrinally toward Kunlun in an entirely different subnet.
- **Mawson Subnet Cross-City Throughways, Throughways 1 and 3** — Dome Fuji's deepest relationships reach
  into Mirny subnet (via Kunlun); its supply chain runs through Troll and Sinheung in two other subnets
  entirely; none of the subnet's three cities' most consequential relationships point at each other.
- **Established, project-wide reconstruction context** — post-war rebuilding, referenced across this whole
  set's own prior candidates (Candidate #1's severed-hub reconnection, Candidate #3's junction governance
  test), creates real practical pressure toward coordination that this subnet, uniquely among those covered
  so far, has no natural shared identity to build that coordination on top of.
- **Explicitly excluded:** any invented shared cultural or civic identity for the subnet as a whole — the
  method's own material is explicit that none currently exists, and this chain treats that absence as the
  actual subject, not a gap to quietly fill.

---

## The Chain

**SETUP** — Post-war reconstruction pressure raises a genuine administrative question: should Mawson
subnet's own three cities formalize some kind of shared subnet-level coordination now, given how much
rebuilding elsewhere in Tepenia has required exactly that kind of internal cooperation — or should they
continue exactly as they've always been, three places grouped by geography alone, each defined far more by
what it connects to outside the subnet than by anything shared among themselves. The player is asked to help
facilitate the conversation — a straightforward "decide your own future together" planning task.

**BUT** — The three cities genuinely don't share much to build a coordinated identity on. Mawson's whole
character points toward the nation at large; Sayowa's real significance points toward Halley subnet and
Concordia; Dome Fuji's deepest relationship points doctrinally toward Kunlun, in Mirny subnet, and its
population holds almost no lived connection to the rest of Tepenia at all, let alone to its own nominal
subnet-mates. Manufacturing a shared "Mawson subnet identity" from nothing would mean inventing something
none of these places has ever actually been.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to help the three cities figure out what, if anything, genuinely makes sense
to coordinate on together — not assuming a shared identity needs to exist to justify practical cooperation.

**BUT** — A real complication surfaces: despite sharing no internal identity, the three cities are, in fact,
each other's literal nearest neighbors, and in a genuinely damaged, resource-thin post-war landscape, that
plain geographic proximity carries real practical weight regardless of any emotional or cultural commonality
— Dome Fuji's own extreme isolation and resource scarcity, in particular, could benefit materially from
coordination with its two actual neighbors, whether or not any of the three ever come to feel like kin.

**THEREFORE** — What began as an identity question becomes a purely practical one: whether to build genuine,
functional coordination specifically *without* pretending it creates or requires the shared civic identity
these cities have simply never had.

**BUT** — The reversal cuts against a clean answer either way. Formalizing honest, purely functional
coordination — mutual logistics support, shared emergency planning, real practical help for Dome Fuji
especially — delivers genuine material benefit to all three cities, but does so as an openly transactional
arrangement, oddly cold next to how most other Tepenian subnets' own member cities relate to each other,
honest about being neighbors and nothing more. Declining to formalize anything honors each city's genuine,
established independence and its own different orientation fully — but leaves real, practical mutual benefit
on the table, particularly for Dome Fuji, the most isolated and least self-sufficient of the three.

**THEREFORE** — Consistent with this project's own No Good Endings law, the resolution is the player's own
role in which way the subnet actually goes: build honest, functional, explicitly non-sentimental coordination
between three cities that have never been more than neighbors, delivering real material benefit at the cost
of a coldly transactional shape none of Tepenia's other subnets share; or leave each city fully independent,
honoring what each has always actually been at the cost of whatever practical good coordination might have
done, especially for the one city with the least to spare. Neither choice invents a kinship these places
never had — it only decides whether being neighbors, and nothing more, is still worth something.

## Self-check (per the method's §7)

Reading only the conjunctions: SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. No repeated
conjunction in a row. Each BUT changes the shape of the problem: the first BUT turns "build a shared
identity" into "there is no shared identity here to build on, and manufacturing one would be dishonest"; the
second BUT turns "coordinate anyway" into "coordination is purely practical, not a route to feeling like
kin"; the third BUT turns "formalize it or don't" into "there is no version of the choice that doesn't cost
either honest independence or real, especially Dome Fuji's own, practical benefit." Deleting any one
Complication changes what the player is actually choosing at the end, so none reads as a disguised And
Then.

## Worth Your Attention

This candidate's core asset is that it's the first in this set to operate at full subnet scale, engaging
Mawson subnet's own defining thesis directly — a genuine absence of shared internal identity — rather than
working through any single city's own local material, the same structural closing move Mirny, Halley,
Janbogo, and Palmer subnets' own Candidate #10s each made toward their own subnet's defining idea. It's also
the only candidate in this set willing to conclude that a real, useful outcome doesn't require inventing
warmth or kinship where none has ever organically existed — sometimes neighbors are just neighbors, and the
practical question is only ever whether that's still worth building on.
