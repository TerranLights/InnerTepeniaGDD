# DLC 4 (Mawson Subnet) — Main Questline Candidate #7: "The Hedge They Never Got"

**What this is:** a seventh prospective But/Therefore chain for DLC 4's main questline, produced by running
`../DLC_Main_Questline_Design_Method.md` against Mawson subnet, built around Sayowa's own established
Japanese-diaspora history — untouched by Candidates #1 and #3's own use of Sayowa's junction material. **Not
canon, not chosen, not the only candidate** — the 7th of the "at least 10" the method calls for. Uses the
Linear Escalation Chain schematic (`../But_Therefore_Quest_Design_Method.md` §5a).

**Uses 1 of Mawson subnet's 3 cities (Sayowa alone)**.

---

## Inputs actually used (Step 1 of the design method)

- **Sayowa's own Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 1** — Japan's Tepenian diaspora produced three
  genuinely different outcomes from three genuinely different mechanisms: Shirayuki's deliberate diplomatic
  protection at Jeju-do, Princess Elisabeth's unplanned organic "eastern approach" current, and Sayowa's own
  organic settlement diluting, unprotected, down to a mere 2.71% share. Sayowa's own Full Extrapolation
  already proposes that Japan's Jeju-do diplomats may have anticipated exactly this kind of organic
  dilution and secured Shirayuki specifically as a hedge against it — meaning Sayowa's diluted outcome may be
  the very outcome that justified Shirayuki's protection being sought in the first place.
- **Sayowa's own Full Extrapolation, Section I** — the Sayowa name carries genuinely different weight
  depending on who's asked: an ordinary proper noun for the demographic majority, but real, felt generational
  gravity for the smaller Japanese-heritage community specifically, carriers of JARE's own institutional
  tradition rather than demographic weight.
- **Sayowa's own Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 3** — "small vessel, outsized cargo": Sayowa's whole
  character, at every scale, runs on modest presentation quietly carrying significance well beyond what its
  own size or self-image would suggest, and Sayowa itself would never be the one to point that out.
- **Explicitly excluded:** any claim about Shirayuki's own present-day culture or community beyond what's
  already established in its own files; this chain stays entirely on Sayowa's own side of the comparison.

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A member of Sayowa's small Japanese-heritage community wants to properly document and preserve
their own institutional history — the JARE tradition, the Shōwa-era naming's real generational weight — given
how thin that community has grown relative to the city's demographic majority. The player is asked to help
gather and verify the historical record, a straightforward heritage-preservation task.

**BUT** — The research surfaces something the community never had reason to look for: real, credible
evidence that Japan's own Jeju-do diplomats anticipated exactly this kind of organic dilution generations
ago, and responded by securing Shirayuki specifically as a hedge against it — not Sayowa. Sayowa's own
community wasn't simply an accident of demographics; it was, in effect, foreseen as the likely outcome and
left unprotected while a different site was actively safeguarded instead.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to decide what to do with a finding that reframes this community's entire
demographic story from misfortune to foresight-and-omission — a real, specific historical choice, not
random chance.

**BUT** — The reversal is that sharing this finding could land two genuinely different ways, and nobody can
predict which in advance. For some in the community, it could read as vindication — finally, a real
historical explanation instead of unexplained drift. For others, it could land as a fresh wound — learning
that their own ancestors' diplomats effectively accepted this community's cultural thinning as the cost of
protecting somewhere else.

**THEREFORE** — What began as a preservation project becomes a real decision about whether to pursue formal
recognition for the community now — generations later, a symbolic correction of the historical oversight —
or simply document the history quietly and let it stand as private, felt knowledge.

**BUT** — The reversal cuts against a clean answer either way. Pursuing formal recognition gives the
community real, if largely symbolic, institutional standing it never received, correcting an oversight that's
gone unaddressed for generations — but forces a public reckoning with the "unprotected" narrative, and asks
a community whose whole established civic character is modest and never self-important (Finding 3) to make
exactly the kind of show of its own significance it has never made before. Quietly preserving the history
without pursuing recognition respects that same established modesty completely — but leaves the historical
oversight exactly as unaddressed and unacknowledged as it has always been, known now but never formally
answered.

**THEREFORE** — Consistent with this project's own No Good Endings law, the resolution is the player's own
role in which path the community actually takes: pursue formal recognition, correcting a generations-old
oversight at the cost of a very public departure from the community's own established quiet character; or
preserve the history privately, honoring that quiet character fully at the cost of leaving the oversight
formally unaddressed, forever. Either way, Sayowa's own established "small vessel, outsized cargo" nature —
never claiming attention it's actually owed — gets tested against a piece of its own history that, for the
first time, genuinely warrants some.

## Self-check (per the method's §7)

Reading only the conjunctions: SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. No repeated
conjunction in a row. Each BUT changes the shape of the problem: the first BUT turns "preserve some history"
into "the history reveals a specific, foreseen abandonment, not random demographic drift"; the second BUT
turns "share the finding" into "sharing it could heal or wound, and there's no way to know which in
advance"; the third BUT turns "pursue recognition or don't" into "there is no version of the choice that
doesn't cost either the community's own established modesty or the oversight staying formally unanswered."
Deleting any one Complication changes what the player is actually choosing at the end, so none reads as a
disguised And Then.

## Worth Your Attention

This candidate's core asset is that it takes a comparative historical finding already proposed in Sayowa's
own established material — its dilution as the plausible justification for Shirayuki's own protection — and
gives it real, felt present-day stakes for the specific community it actually happened to, rather than
leaving it as an abstract piece of Tepenia-wide immigration history. It's also the first Mawson subnet
candidate whose climax directly tests the city's own defining civic principle — modest, never self-important
— against a piece of its own history that might, for once, actually deserve to be made visible.
