# Subnet Symbolic Associations: Elementals and the Six Perfections

**What this is:** a speculative symbolic framework layered on top of the subnet-level design work already
underway across `Storyline/DLC-Questlines/`, in the same spirit as Concordia's own zodiac-coded district
scheme (12 districts, one exception — the Central Hub). Two independent systems are applied here, at
**subnet scale** rather than city scale — each subnet (plus Byrd and Amundsen, both effectively single-city
"subnets" of their own) gets one symbolic association per system, derived from that subnet's own established
functionality, personality, and local culture, not the other way around. Not canon, not binding — a
reference layer for future creative use. See `Reference/Real-World/Symbolic_Sets_of_Six_and_Seven.md` for the
underlying real-world systems this draws from.

**Units covered:** Palmer, Halley, Mirny, Janbogo/Ross, Mawson (the five multi-city subnets), plus Byrd (a
genuine single-city subnet) and Amundsen/"Echoes of Amundsen" (DLC 1's own location, included for the
Elementals section only — see Part B for why it doesn't have a Six Perfections slot).

---

## Part A: The Elementals

The traditional Five Chinese Elements (Wu Xing: Earth, Fire, Water, Wood, Metal) cover the five multi-city
subnets exactly, one each. Byrd and Amundsen required going outside that tradition — both fit two
concepts the developer has separately used before as "robot elements" alongside the human-coded five:
**Magnetism** and **Electricity**. Air (the Greek/Western import) was considered for Mawson and explicitly
rejected in favor of Wood after weighing both directly — see Mawson's own entry below for that full
comparison.

---

### The Elements Themselves

Before applying any of these to a specific subnet, here's what each element actually means on its own terms
— including the shadow side every element carries in its own tradition, since a symbol that only has
positive attributes isn't really doing any interpretive work.

**Earth** — Direction: Center. Season: the transitional "fifth season" between the other four. Color:
Yellow. Virtue: Trustworthiness/Sincerity (*Xin*). Emotion: Worry/Overthinking (*Si*). Traditionally
associated with the spleen and stomach in Chinese medicine — the organs that process and distribute
nourishment to the rest of the body.
- *Positive:* Reliability, patience, nourishment, groundedness, practicality, the capacity to support and
  sustain others without needing credit for it, fairness, a stabilizing centeredness that keeps everything
  else from spinning out.
- *Negative:* In excess, Earth becomes stagnation — over-caution that curdles into paralysis, an inability
  to change or adapt when change is actually needed, stubbornness, hoarding, a smothering kind of nurturing
  that doesn't let anything else grow on its own terms, a heaviness that resists necessary movement.

**Fire** — Direction: South. Season: Summer. Color: Red. Virtue: Propriety/Etiquette (*Li*). Emotion: Joy
(and, in excess, mania). Traditionally associated with the heart and small intestine.
- *Positive:* Passion, warmth, illumination, transformation, charisma, vitality, the spark of inspiration and
  creative energy, the courage to act and actually be seen doing it.
- *Negative:* In excess, Fire becomes recklessness, burnout, mania, destructive volatility, arrogance
  (mistaking one's own light for the only light worth following), impatience, a scorching intensity that
  consumes whatever it touches, an inability to ever simply rest.

**Water** — Direction: North. Season: Winter. Color: Black/Blue. Virtue: Wisdom (*Zhi*). Emotion: Fear.
Traditionally associated with the kidneys and bladder.
- *Positive:* Deep wisdom, adaptability, calm resilience, the capacity to yield without breaking, patience,
  introspection, stillness, finding the path of least resistance without ever actually giving up, quiet
  strength that doesn't need to prove itself.
- *Negative:* In excess, Water becomes fear and paranoia, isolation, an inability to ever commit or take a
  stand (endlessly yielding rather than standing firm when it actually matters), coldness, secretiveness, a
  passivity that shades into helplessness, being overwhelmed and swept along by circumstance rather than
  navigating it.

**Wood** — Direction: East. Season: Spring. Color: Green. Virtue: Benevolence/Kindness (*Ren*). Emotion:
Anger. Traditionally associated with the liver and gallbladder.
- *Positive:* Growth, vision, generosity, flexibility, initiative, benevolence, the capacity to plan and
  pursue genuine new beginnings, ambition tempered by real care for others, resilience — bending in the wind
  without breaking.
- *Negative:* In excess, Wood becomes anger and frustration, a rigidity disguised as flexibility (bending so
  readily it never actually takes a firm shape), overreaching ambition, jealousy of others' own growth, an
  inability to ever sit still or be content, expansion pursued at other people's direct expense.

**Metal** — Direction: West. Season: Autumn. Color: White. Virtue: Righteousness/Integrity (*Yi*). Emotion:
Grief. Traditionally associated with the lungs and large intestine.
- *Positive:* Precision, integrity, clarity, the capacity to let go gracefully the way autumn releases its
  own leaves, discernment, structure, refinement, honoring what has genuinely ended, cutting away what's no
  longer necessary, uncompromising honesty.
- *Negative:* In excess, Metal becomes rigidity, coldness, grief that can't actually move on, harsh
  judgment, an inability to forgive imperfection in anyone, isolation through over-refinement (cutting away
  so much it severs real connection along with the excess), melancholy, a perfectionism that never lets
  anything simply be good enough.

**Magnetism** *(no ancient tradition to draw from — constructed here specifically as a "robot element," per
the developer's own earlier framing)*.
- *Positive:* Genuine attraction and connection that doesn't require physical proximity, alignment and
  orientation (a compass always pointing toward something true), the capacity to draw disparate, scattered
  things together into one coherent whole, invisible bonds that persist whether or not anyone acknowledges
  them, complementary opposites that define and complete each other — a north pole is only meaningful because
  a south pole exists.
- *Negative:* Obsessive fixation — an attraction that can't let go once it's taken hold. Polarization — poles
  that repel rather than complement, with no available middle ground. Being pulled off one's own intended
  course by a force outside one's own control. Dependency on an external field rather than one's own internal
  compass. Invisible influence exerted on something that never actually consented to or even noticed it.

**Electricity** *(also constructed, alongside Magnetism, as the second "robot element")*.
- *Positive:* Animation — the literal spark that turns inert matter into something conscious and active.
  Connection and communication (the electrical basis of any nervous system, biological or otherwise). Power
  and capability. Illumination. Rapid, decisive transformation. The single current that runs through and
  unifies an entire system end to end.
- *Negative:* Danger and destructive potential — shock, short-circuit, electrocution. Instability, where a
  surge and a total blackout are simply two faces of the same underlying fragility. Total dependency — a
  system that is completely helpless the instant its power source is removed. The same current that animates
  a whole system is, withdrawn or overloaded, exactly what collapses it — a direct, literal echo of the
  Amundsen Tower's own story.

### Halley Subnet → Earth

**The case for it:**
- Halley subnet's own established Cross-City Patterns file names an entire "ground-stability triangle"
  directly: Halley and Neumayer both live on unstable, drifting ice shelf, while Abowasa alone sits on
  genuine mainland bedrock with real structural-stability research expertise. This is a subnet literally
  organized around the question of what stable ground even means.
- The subnet's own defining civic trait — "does genuinely consequential work and receives none of the
  visible credit for it," treating self-promotion as "close to a mild civic failure" — matches Earth's
  traditional virtue, *trustworthiness*, almost exactly: the element that quietly supports everything else
  without announcing itself.
- Earth's traditional emotion, *worry/pensiveness*, tracks the subnet's own recurring anxiety about exactly
  this kind of structural vulnerability — Princess Elisabeth's disconnected reserve, Troll's fractured
  control, Lazar's own "first time it ever goes dark" crisis all turn on some version of "is the ground
  actually solid."
- In the Wu Xing wheel, Earth sits at the *center*, mediating the other four elements rather than opposing
  any of them — fitting for a subnet whose own damage taxonomy (Cross-City Patterns, Pattern 1) is explicitly
  named as "this whole project's own reference case" for how a war can land differently on similar cities,
  a genuinely central, comparative role relative to the other subnets.

**The case against it:**
- Halley (the city) itself is *not* built on stable ground — it sits on the same continuously-drifting ice
  shelf as Neumayer. The actual bedrock stability in this subnet belongs specifically to Abowasa. Assigning
  Earth to the whole subnet risks papering over the fact that its own namesake city is, quite literally,
  ungrounded.
- Earth's other traditional associations (harvest, nourishment, agricultural abundance) don't have an
  obvious home anywhere in Halley subnet's established material — the fit works entirely through the
  virtue/emotion correspondence, not through any literal, physical echo.
- A reasonable competing case exists for **Metal** instead, given Neumayer's own "Measured, Not Debated"
  precision-engineering culture — Metal's own connotations of refinement and exactness arguably suit
  Neumayer's specific character better than Earth's broader stability theme does. Metal was ultimately
  reserved for Janbogo/Ross, but the competing claim is genuine, not manufactured for balance.

### Mirny Subnet → Fire

**The case for it:**
- Mirny subnet's defining civic act, across multiple cities, is self-naming: Sinheung's first-ever
  deliberate name after 130 years without one, Shirayuki's fresh naming mid-project, Mirny's own flagged
  (still undecided) rename. Naming yourself is an assertive, illuminating, self-defining act — the closest
  thing to Fire's own core quality of active transformation.
- Fire's traditional virtue, *propriety* — doing or being the correct, proper thing — maps directly onto a
  subnet whose whole civic energy runs on getting its own identity right rather than accepting what it
  inherited.
- The subnet's own war-damage logic is the cleanest, most purely rational of any subnet covered so far —
  strategic value and reachability alone, no punitive or symbolic exceptions. That clarity, a kind of cold
  illumination cutting straight through to cause and effect, is a genuinely Fire-coded quality even though
  it isn't emotionally "hot."
- Kunlun's own astronomy work and the subnet's own deep, if carefully unremarked-upon, connection to the
  Amundsen Tower's own collapse tie the subnet to literal light-and-energy imagery running through the
  game's central mythology.

**The case against it:**
- Fire's traditional emotion is *joy* — and Mirny subnet's own established tone reads as considerably more
  solemn and function-first than joyful. Vostok's isolation, Kunlun's clinically curated population, and the
  subnet's own "does the work, no credit" register (shared with Halley) all skew quiet and restrained, not
  passionate.
- Fire's other traditional connotations (volatility, destruction, unpredictability) sit awkwardly against a
  subnet whose most-praised trait is precisely its *order* — Zhongshan's unbroken continuity, a single clean
  explanatory principle for every city's war status. A genuinely fiery subnet would probably look more
  chaotic than Mirny actually reads.
- A real competing claim exists for **Metal** here too, given Zhongshan's own precision-manufacturing
  economy and the subnet's overall rational clarity — Metal's connotations of refinement and cutting
  precision aren't obviously worse-suited to Mirny than to Janbogo/Ross, making this one of the two genuinely
  contested assignments in this whole set (the other being Halley's).

### Palmer Subnet → Water

**The case for it:**
- Palmer subnet has the highest destruction rate of any subnet covered (five of its eight cities are
  destroyed), yet its single most distinctive established finding is that continuity survives anyway, through
  quiet, adaptive, non-institutional means — specifically, long-lived robots carrying identity forward where
  human lifespans and human institutions can't (Esperanza's caregivers, Palmer City's own possibly-still-
  living witness). That's Water's own defining quality: finding a way around an obstacle rather than
  breaking against it.
- Water's traditional emotion, *fear*, matches the subnet's own real, ongoing vulnerability — Signy's
  siligel countdown chief among them — better than any other candidate emotion would.
- Water's traditional virtue, *wisdom*, fits Juan Carlos's own established role as the seed of Tepenia's
  entire archival tradition, and the broader theme of accumulated memory running through the subnet's own
  candidate material.
- Water's association with winter, depth, and stillness suits the subnet's own quiet, ongoing memory-keeping
  register — a subnet defined as much by what's remembered about its losses as by the losses themselves.

**The case against it:**
- Water's usual connotation of calm, yielding stillness sits oddly against the sheer, active violence of
  Palmer subnet's own destruction history — five cities lost to three distinct, deliberate strike types
  (security, efficiency, morale). This is not a gentle subnet's story; Water may undersell how much of its
  history is outright destruction rather than erosion.
- A real competing claim exists for **Metal** here as well, given Palmer subnet's own destruction rate
  actually exceeds Janbogo/Ross's — grief and precision-cutting verdict aren't obviously less apt for Palmer
  than for the subnet that currently holds Metal.
- Water's implied adaptability oversells how much genuine agency was involved in several of Palmer's own
  survival stories — Port Lockroy's survival plausibly traces to a targeting *error*, and Signy's traces to
  raw remoteness rather than any active choice. The "finding its own way around" reading fits Rothera's
  deliberate decentralization far better than it fits cities that simply weren't hit by chance.

### Janbogo/Ross Subnet → Metal

**The case for it:**
- Metal's traditional emotion, *grief*, matches this subnet's defining trait — Janbogo's own cumulative
  witness burden, the "vigil-vs-moving-on" split — about as precisely as any correspondence in this whole
  document.
- Metal's association with autumn, harvest, and decline fits the subnet's own established pattern of civic
  identity anchored to single historical dates rather than ongoing achievement (Scott's St. Robert, Dumont
  d'Urville's St. Jules Day, Cape Adare's firstness) — a subnet oriented toward what already happened, not
  what's still growing.
- A verdict is, quite literally, a "cutting-through" decision — and Metal's core association with the blade
  matches the subnet's own explicit defining thesis, "survival was a verdict," with unusual exactness.
- Metal's traditional virtue, *righteousness*, tracks the subnet's own recurring moral-reckoning quality —
  whether Renzo Adorni was right to shelter the defectors, whether Port Lockroy's survival was earned or a
  mistake, whether Fort McMurdo's historical capital claim actually holds up.

**The case against it:**
- Not every city in this subnet is defined by grief specifically — Scott and Fort McMurdo read as more
  rivalrous and institutional than mournful, which weakens a fully totalizing "grief" reading across all
  seven cities.
- Metal's other traditional connotations — wealth, refinement, precious value — don't have an obvious home
  in Janbogo/Ross's own established material, which centers on memory and judgment rather than material
  worth.
- A genuine alternative case exists for **Water** here: witness-bearing is arguably as much about slow,
  quiet accumulation over generations (a water-like quality) as it is about one sharp, cutting verdict
  (metal-like), and the subnet's own material supports both readings about equally well.

### Mawson Subnet → Wood

*(Full comparison against Air already conducted and resolved in favor of Wood — summarized here.)*

**The case for it:**
- Mawson city's hospitality is explicitly, institutionally *cultivated* over decades, not incidental — active
  tending, which is Wood's own virtue (benevolence) in a literal sense.
- Wood's rooted-but-far-reaching structure — a fixed base sending branches outward — answers the subnet's
  own "oriented outward" problem better than it first appears to: each city is a real, distinct root; the
  outward relationships are the branches. A tree doesn't stop being one thing because its branches reach
  past its own trunk.
- Wood opens the Wu Xing generative cycle (Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → back to Wood), and the very
  first link tracks genuine established lore: Mawson subnet's single most developed relationship really is
  with Mirny subnet, via Dome Fuji's inseparable connection to Kunlun.
- Wood's association with spring and renewal fits Mawson's own founding story — peaceful, generational
  demographic dilution, not violent rupture.

**The case against it:**
- Wood's traditional emotion, anger, doesn't match anything in this subnet's established temperament — if
  anything, Mawson subnet reads as unusually agreeable (Positive Outlook, Compliant).
- Dome Fuji actively resists the metaphor: total population *succession*, not organic growth from a
  continuous root, plus an explicitly Withdrawn temperament that's close to the opposite of Wood's outward
  reach.
- Sayowa reads more like a passive conduit — a junction letting traffic pass through it — than a living,
  growing thing extending its own branches.
- The subnet's own material states directly that it "doesn't reduce to one collective personality even
  loosely." Wood implies one coherent life-force with a single root; the developer's own established note
  insists there isn't one to find.

**Air was the genuine alternative**, and lost on balance: Air's own "connective tissue" framing tracks
Mawson subnet's own established language almost word for word, and would have accounted for Dome Fuji's
isolation as an inversion (a place defined by what doesn't reach it). But Air isn't part of the Wu Xing
tradition at all, its textual support rests on one phrase rather than several independent facts, and
adopting it would have severed Mawson from the one genuinely checkable win Wood offers — the Kunlun link at
the head of the generative cycle.

### Byrd → Magnetism

**The case for it:**
- Byrd shares Mirny's exact Enneagram profile, "solved along opposite spatial axes" — Mirny's windbreak ring
  wraps *around* its industrial core, Byrd is built *downward* before it ever stood on the surface. That's a
  north/south pole relationship in the most literal structural sense available anywhere in this project.
- Byrd's own origin story is one of active searching and rediscovery, not simple continuity — an invisible
  pull that drew an expedition toward it across real distance, the same shape as magnetic attraction.
- It owes real, unacknowledged debts to two other subnets entirely (Neumayer's engineering lineage, Palmer
  City's and Rothera's historical records) — disparate elements drawn together from elsewhere, the way iron
  filings align to an external field.
- As a "robot element" (per the developer's own prior framing, alongside Electricity), Magnetism fits Byrd's
  own all-robot founding population directly.

**The case against it:**
- The association leans heavily on one specific fact — the Mirny-mirror relationship — rather than several
  independently corroborating details, giving it a narrower evidentiary base than most of the other
  associations in this document.
- Magnetism's other traditional connotations (navigation, compass orientation, attraction/repulsion as a
  moral binary) don't have much additional textual support in Byrd's own established culture beyond that
  single mirrored-profile fact.
- A competing, more literal case exists for **Earth**, given Byrd's own genuinely physical "built downward,
  underground" character — though Earth's more thematic, civic-register claim already belongs to Halley, and
  Magnetism's specificity (the actual opposite-pole relationship, not just "underground") makes it the
  sharper, more distinctive fit of the two.

---

### Amundsen ("Echoes of Amundsen") → Electricity

**The case for it:**
- The Amundsen Tower's own established function was literal national energy-grid regulation — one of the
  most direct, unambiguous real-world-element correspondences anywhere in this document, requiring almost no
  interpretive leap at all.
- The Tower's collapse — the event that defeated Kendra Heinrich in DLC 1 — is explicitly an energy/grid
  catastrophe, not a metaphorical one.
- As a "robot element," Electricity fits Amundsen's own foundational role in the setting's central mythology
  — the animating current behind the whole downstream story, robot creation included.
- It positions Amundsen appropriately as the generative, originating unit at the head of any ordered
  sequence built from these associations — the thing everything else can be traced back to.

**The case against it:**
- This association is almost too literal compared to the more interpretive fits found elsewhere (Halley's
  Earth, Mirny's Fire) — it requires no real inference, just direct correspondence, which some may find less
  interesting or "discovered" than the others.
- Amundsen Station doesn't have nearly as much other established city-level cultural material to draw on as
  the five multi-city subnets do — the Electricity association rests almost entirely on the Tower/collapse
  fact alone, with little supporting texture from any broader established local culture.

## Part B: The Six Perfections (Paramitas)

This system covers six units — the five multi-city subnets plus Byrd — in their traditional cultivation
order, each perfection building on the one before it. **Amundsen has no slot here.** The Six Perfections
set was specifically built around exactly six units; Amundsen was the reason a *seventh* system (the Seven
Hermetic Principles, not reproduced in full here) was needed when it had to be included. Forcing a seventh
perfection into a six-part traditional system would be the kind of invented content this whole exercise has
otherwise avoided — better to leave the gap honest.

---

### The Perfections Themselves

Same principle as Part A: each perfection's own full meaning first, including what it looks like when it
curdles or overshoots, before applying any of them to a specific subnet. The six are traditionally cultivated
in this exact order, each building on the one before it — Generosity gives something to discipline;
Discipline gives Patience something worth defending; and so on, up to Wisdom, which only actually completes
the other five rather than replacing them.

**Generosity (Dana)** — selfless giving of material goods, protection, or teaching, and the loosening of
attachment to possessions and self that giving freely requires. The foundation the other five perfections are
built on top of.
- *Positive:* Real openhandedness, a willingness to give without keeping score, the capacity to actually let
  go of something in service of someone else's genuine need.
- *Negative:* Generosity without wisdom becomes self-destructive — giving away what one actually needs for
  one's own survival. It can also curdle into something performative: giving in order to be *seen* giving,
  which feeds the giver's own ego rather than truly serving whoever receives it. Taken further, it enables
  harmful dependency in the people it's meant to help, or shades into outright martyrdom.

**Discipline / Ethical Conduct (Sila)** — channeling generosity into consistent, structured, ethical action
rather than scattered good intentions; a stable, sustained code of conduct.
- *Positive:* Consistency, integrity, the self-restraint that turns a single good impulse into a genuinely
  reliable character.
- *Negative:* Discipline without compassion becomes rigidity — moralism, a self-righteous judgment of anyone
  who doesn't meet the identical standard. It can become control or domination dressed up as virtue, joyless
  rule-following with no understanding of *why* the rule exists in the first place — a dead letter instead of
  a living principle.

**Patience / Forbearance (Kshanti)** — sustaining disciplined effort through real hardship, insult, and
difficulty without collapsing into anger or despair.
- *Positive:* Genuine endurance under real pressure, the capacity to hold steady when collapse would be
  easier.
- *Negative:* Patience without genuine resolution becomes passivity — tolerating something that should
  actually be changed or resisted, mistaking suppressed anger for peace. Taken further, "patiently" enduring
  harm indefinitely, rather than ever actually confronting it, becomes a quiet way of enabling that harm to
  continue.

**Diligence / Energetic Effort (Virya)** — the sustained energy needed to keep practicing across a long
path without losing momentum, supplying the fuel that patience alone can't provide forever.
- *Positive:* Showing up consistently, sustained forward motion, the refusal to let effort quietly lapse.
- *Negative:* Diligence without direction becomes burnout — busyness mistaken for actual progress, constant
  activity used specifically to avoid the stillness that real reflection would require, or sustained,
  strenuous effort aimed at entirely the wrong goal.

**Meditative Concentration (Dhyana)** — focusing sustained effort into genuine, stable insight rather than
scattered activity; the capacity to actually sit still with something long enough to understand it.
- *Positive:* Real focus, a settled mind, the stability that turns raw effort into actual understanding.
- *Negative:* Concentration without wisdom becomes escapism — using stillness to avoid engaging with a real,
  unresolved problem, or growing attached to the pleasant states concentration itself can produce (a kind of
  spiritual bypassing). Withdrawal from the world can get mistaken for genuine progress, and stagnation can
  disguise itself convincingly as serenity.

**Wisdom (Prajna)** — the culmination: a direct, experiential understanding of the true nature of reality
(interdependence, the absence of a fixed, separate self) that the first five perfections make possible, and
without which they remain well-intentioned but incomplete.
- *Positive:* Genuine insight that actually completes the other five, rather than just decorating them —
  understanding, not merely believing or reciting.
- *Negative:* Intellectual understanding mistaken for the real thing — knowing the words without the
  realization behind them. Wisdom without compassion curdles into cold detachment or quiet superiority, and
  can be used to justify disengaging from ordinary ethical effort entirely — a spiritual bypass at the
  highest possible level, dressed up as having already arrived.

---

### Palmer Subnet → Generosity (Dana)

**The case for it:** Palmer subnet's most distinctive established cross-city finding is unacknowledged
giving — Palmer City's and Rothera's own historical records and industrial template, given freely and
without credit, are what let Byrd be found and shaped in the first place (a real, checkable debt spanning two
different subnets). Esperanza's whole founding charter is a form of institutional charity — raising
children for a mission larger than any one of them. Generosity given without expectation of return is, if
anything, the single clearest throughline in this subnet's own material.

**The case against it:** Not every city in Palmer subnet is generous by disposition. Signy's own defining
crisis is scarcity, not giving — there's nothing to spare, which is close to Generosity's own opposite.
Several of the subnet's survival stories (Port Lockroy's likely targeting error, Signy's raw remoteness) are
closer to lucky accidents than acts of generosity, meaning the "Generosity" read fits some of Palmer
subnet's cities far better than others.

---

### Halley Subnet → Discipline (Sila)

**The case for it:** "Measured, Not Debated" (Neumayer) and "Competence Without Commentary" (Sanay) are
about as direct a textual match for Discipline as this whole document produces — consistent, restrained,
quietly rule-following civic character showing up independently in at least two different cities' own
established personalities.

**The case against it:** Troll's own fractured, multi-claimant airfield control is arguably the *opposite*
of discipline — a genuine, ongoing failure of unified order that the rest of the subnet has to route around
rather than resolve. One city within the subnet actively resists this reading, even though most of the
others clearly support it.

---

### Janbogo/Ross Subnet → Patience (Kshanti)

**The case for it:** Generations of vigil-keeping, cumulative witness-bearing across at least two
destroyed neighbors (Zukelli, Cape Adare), and an internal split that's persisted, unresolved, for as long
as anyone can remember — this is a subnet whose entire emotional register runs on sustained forbearance
rather than resolution.

**The case against it:** Patience implies eventual, peaceful endurance, but this subnet's own defining
thesis — "survival was a verdict" — has a harsher, more *active* quality than pure passive waiting suggests;
someone or something is always being judged, not simply endured. Scott's own established rivalry with Fort
McMurdo also reads as more anxious and status-conscious than patient.

---

### Mawson Subnet → Diligence (Virya)

**The case for it:** Sayowa's own established "small vessel, outsized cargo" character is sustained,
uncomplaining effort in a nearly literal sense. Mawson city's decades-long, deliberately cultivated
hospitality tradition and Dome Fuji's own ongoing devotional practice both describe genuinely effortful,
sustained commitment rather than passive circumstance.

**The case against it:** Diligence implies active, ongoing striving toward a goal, but Sayowa's own real
significance arguably comes from *where it happens to sit* (the closest inter-subnet proximity in the whole
Federation) rather than from active effort — a passive geographic fact more than a diligently cultivated
one. The fit is considerably stronger for Mawson city and Dome Fuji than for Sayowa specifically.

---

### Byrd → Meditative Concentration (Dhyana)

**The case for it:** Built downward and inward rather than outward — a genuinely focused, depth-oriented
community, the structural opposite of a subnet that spreads itself thin across many external relationships.
That inward focus is close to Dhyana's own core meaning: stable, undistracted concentration.

**The case against it:** Meditative concentration implies calm, settled stillness, but Byrd's own founding
history is a story of active searching, expedition, and discovery — a much more effortful, outward-directed
process than the passive stillness the perfection usually implies. The "concentration" reading fits Byrd's
present, settled character better than the active history that actually produced it.

---

### Mirny Subnet → Wisdom (Prajna)

**The case for it:** The culminating perfection, fittingly assigned to the subnet most concerned with
correctly understanding and naming its own true identity (Sinheung, Shirayuki, Mirny's own still-undecided
rename), and the subnet with the deepest, if carefully unremarked-upon, connection to the game's own
foundational mythology.

**The case against it:** Wisdom, in the Buddhist sense this perfection actually describes, is direct
experiential insight into the true nature of reality itself — a considerably deeper, more transcendent claim
than "correctly naming one's own city." Applying it to Mirny risks overstating an administrative and civic
practice as though it were genuine spiritual realization, when it may really just be very good
self-awareness.
