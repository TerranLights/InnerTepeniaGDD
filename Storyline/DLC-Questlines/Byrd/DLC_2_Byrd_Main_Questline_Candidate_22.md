**What this is:** a candidate built around Finding 19's own explicitly-proposed "third position" — structural
authority earned through demonstrated engineering lineage, not founding descent — giving Finding 7's
demographic tension a concrete expertise-vs-genealogy test case. No Cradle content. **Not canon, not
chosen.**

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A genuine structural question arises — real stakes, not politics — and a specific character
whose authority comes from demonstrated engineering-family lineage, not founding descent, offers a technical
judgment that conflicts with the founding-descendant establishment's own accepted read.

**BUT** — The establishment doesn't formally recognize this figure's standing, despite objectively strong
competence — a quiet, real exclusion Finding 19 already flags as unresolved. The player has to decide whose
judgment to actually back, with a real structural outcome riding on the call.

**THEREFORE** — The player backs one side, and the choice has to be made on the merits, under pressure, not
simply by deferring to whoever holds more formal standing.

**BUT** — Backing the engineering-lineage figure, if it plays out, doesn't just resolve one technical
dispute — it sets a real precedent about whether expertise or genealogy should determine structural
authority in Byrd going forward, a consequential civic question the city has never had to formally settle.

**THEREFORE** — The player's choice ripples beyond the immediate crisis into how Byrd recognizes technical
authority from this point on.

**BUT (reversal)** — There's genuine risk on both sides. The engineering-lineage figure, however competent,
could still be wrong — expertise isn't infallibility, and the founding-descendant establishment's own
accumulated tacit knowledge (Finding 7's whole basis) might genuinely capture something an outsider's
training doesn't. Betting on the challenger carries real technical risk beyond the politics; deferring to
the establishment carries no such risk but actively reinforces the exact exclusionary pattern Finding 19
already flags as real and unresolved.

**THEREFORE (resolution)** — Per No Good Endings: back the engineering-lineage figure, rewarding genuine
expertise and challenging Finding 7's status quo, at real technical risk if they're wrong; or defer to
established founding-descendant authority, minimizing immediate risk while reinforcing the very exclusion
Finding 19 identifies as an open, unclaimed fault line.

beyond this one case. The third BUT means neither side is risk-free. No Complication is deletable without
