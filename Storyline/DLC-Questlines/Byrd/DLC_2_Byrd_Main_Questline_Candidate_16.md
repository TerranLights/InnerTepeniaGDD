# DLC 2 (Byrd) — Main Questline Candidate #16: "The Recipe Nobody Wrote Down"

**What this is:** a candidate built around Byrd's own food-culture material — heritage and craft pride
specifically, not general food security (Candidate #12 already covers supply-chain fragility). No Cradle
content. **Not canon, not chosen.**

---

## Inputs actually used

- **Attribute #55** — pest and contamination control infrastructure for the food-growth and food-storage
  sections, necessary because a sealed underground hydroponic system has no realistic outside remediation
  help.
- **Finding 52** — pest/contamination vigilance is a genuine, distinct craft specialty (a "growth-keeper"
  role), complementing fermentation as a second point of quiet pride Finding 38 already identifies.
- **Attribute #69** — a dedicated fermentation/preservation craft facility.
- **Finding 38** — fermentation-preserved food as a small, lived echo of Byrd's own "necessity into pride"

- **Finding 45** — the gap `Byrd_Catalog.md`'s own Food findings left open across every Primary/Significant
  nation entry, now directly fillable using this session's own food-infrastructure attributes.

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A contamination outbreak hits the Growing Sections, and the player is asked to help contain it
before it spreads. What looks at first like routine hazard response (attribute #55) turns out to threaten
something more specific: irreplaceable, generations-old fermentation starter cultures — living heritage, not
just crop yield.

**BUT** — Standard containment protocol means destroying or sterilizing the affected material outright,
including whatever heirloom cultures can't be cleanly separated from the contamination in time.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to race to save what can be saved before standard containment proceeds,
rather than simply letting the protocol run its course.

**BUT** — Rescuing the cultures without spreading the contamination further requires real, specific
expertise — the growth-keeper specialists Finding 52 identifies as a small, quietly unclaimed specialty, and
their knowledge, like so much else in Byrd (Finding 27), isn't centrally documented anywhere the player can
simply look up.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to work directly with these specialists, learning and relying on knowledge
that exists only in their own hands-on practice, under real time pressure.

**BUT (reversal)** — Even a fully committed rescue can't guarantee everything survives — some strains or
techniques may be permanently lost no matter how it's handled. This surfaces a real values question: is it
actually worth investing heavily in preserving this specific heritage, versus simply restarting with newer,
safer, more standardized cultivation that carries none of this risk going forward?

**THEREFORE (resolution)** — Per No Good Endings: commit real, ongoing resources to preserving as much of
the heritage culture as possible, protecting something Findings 38 and 45 already establish as meaningfully
tied to Byrd's own self-understanding, at genuine practical cost; or accept a safer, more standardized
restart, practical and resource-efficient, at the cost of a permanent, small loss to exactly the kind of
necessity-into-pride identity the rest of Byrd's own established culture is built from.

## Self-check

SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. The first BUT turns "contamination" into
"irreplaceable heritage at risk." The second BUT means the rescue itself requires rare, undocumented
expertise. The third BUT means even success doesn't remove the underlying values question. No Complication is
deletable without losing the final choice.

## Worth Your Attention

This is the most intimate, lowest-stakes-in-scale but highest-stakes-in-meaning candidate in the current
set — no citywide crisis, just a small, real loss that directly tests whether Byrd's own established
necessity-into-pride identity (Finding 38, `Byrd_Catalog.md` Synthesis Notes) is something the city actually
protects when it costs something, or only celebrates when it's free.
