# DLC 2 (Byrd) — Main Questline Candidate #9: "The One Who Drove It First"

**What this is:** a candidate built around the already-proposed founding-expedition robot crew member
Notable Figure — no Cradle/chamber content, and a direct engagement with one of this project's own two
north-star creative questions (robot consciousness, per `user_creative_principles.md`). **Not canon, not
chosen.**

## The Chain

**SETUP** — The founding-crew robot is failing — a genuine, worsening malfunction. The Foundation Keepers or
Grounded Wings bring the player in, expecting a routine repair job using Byrd's own considerable robot-
maintenance infrastructure.

**BUT** — It isn't routine. The figure refuses standard diagnostic and repair protocol (attribute #58),
because the fix would overwrite or lose decades-obsolete, never-backed-up memory data — the only living
record of what chain #3 already establishes as an experience no human ever witnessed and no other robot
shares.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to work with the figure directly, not as a repair job but as a genuine
negotiation, trying to find a way to preserve what matters before any technical fix proceeds.

**BUT** — Finding 7's own founding-descendant/redistributed-majority tension surfaces in a new, sharply
personal form. Founding-descendants treat this figure's memory as irreplaceable civic heritage worth real
sacrifice to protect; much of the far larger, later-arrived population sees an urgent malfunction being
delayed over sentiment, at real risk to the figure's own continued function.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to navigate both the technical/ethical rescue itself and the social conflict
over how much of the city's own limited resources this one figure's memory is actually worth.

**BUT (reversal)** — Even full success doesn't fully resolve what chain #3 already established: this
figure's own memory of the founding was always solitary, with no independent witness. Whatever gets
preserved can never be verified or fully understood by anyone who wasn't there — the rescue can succeed
completely and the deeper isolation (a memory genuinely no one else can ever fully access) still remains.

**THEREFORE (resolution)** — Per No Good Endings: commit heavily to preserving everything technically
possible, at real cost and real civic controversy, closing only part of the gap since the experience itself
stays permanently, uniquely the figure's own; or accept a more limited preservation, respecting both
resource constraints and the social division over how much this is worth, at the cost of permanently losing
some portion of Byrd's own founding memory for good.

## Self-check

SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. The first BUT turns "repair a malfunction" into
"the figure's own memory and autonomy are directly at stake." The second BUT turns "negotiate a fix" into "the
whole city has a stake and is divided over it." The third BUT means even a full success doesn't close the
underlying gap. No Complication is deletable without losing the final choice's weight.
