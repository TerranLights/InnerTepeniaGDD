# DLC 2 (Byrd) — Main Questline Candidate #7: "What the City Doesn't Say Out Loud"

**What this is:** a candidate built around Byrd's own established "uncredited competence" theme — medical
and epidemiological, not industrial — carrying no Cradle/chamber content at all. **Not canon, not chosen.**

## Inputs actually used

- **Finding 5** — Byrd plausibly holds a second uncredited competence nobody outside the city knows about:
  exceptional medical/surgical capability, forced by the total absence of any realistic evacuation option.
- **Finding 36** — a distinct, second layer to that competence: genuine epidemiological and outbreak-
  containment expertise, forced by the same isolation logic — Byrd can't evacuate a critical patient any

## The Chain

**SETUP** — A genuine outbreak, or a medical case beyond routine capacity, hits Byrd — dense, sealed, and
permanently unable to evacuate anyone (attribute #15's own governing logic). The player is pulled in through
a specific case, expecting a straightforward medical-emergency quest.

**BUT** — Standard treatment isn't enough. Quarantine protocol (attribute #51) kicks in, and the player
watches Byrd's medical establishment execute containment with a level of precision and calm that reads as
genuinely exceptional — not what anyone expects from an isolated city of this size.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to work directly with Byrd's medical establishment to actually manage the
outbreak, uncovering along the way just how far this quiet, unclaimed competence (Findings 5 and 36 together)
actually extends.

**BUT** — Containing it inside Byrd isn't the whole problem. If the outbreak's origin has any connection to
the outside world — freight, a rare visitor, or something surfaced from the city's own deep, pre-exile
layers (chain #9) — there's a real question of whether the rest of Tepenia needs to know, both for their own
safety and because Byrd's own exceptional capability might be exactly what's needed elsewhere too.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to decide whether to keep Byrd's competence quiet — protecting the city's
own autonomy, resources, and privacy from an outside world that's never had reason to call on it — or make
it known, potentially saving lives well beyond Byrd's own walls.

**BUT (reversal)** — Guideline 1's own "uncredited, load-bearing" shape cuts both ways here. Keeping it
quiet means it stays exactly what it's always been — real, but invisible, helping no one beyond Byrd's own
population. Revealing it doesn't just help others; it permanently changes how the rest of Tepenia sees and
treats Byrd, inviting outside expectation and dependency an isolated, self-sufficient city never asked for
and has no established mechanism to manage gracefully.

**THEREFORE (resolution)** — Per No Good Endings: reveal Byrd's medical competence, saving lives elsewhere at
the cost of the quiet autonomy that's defined the city for generations; or protect the secret, preserving
Byrd's own self-contained character at the cost of lives that could have been saved had the rest of Tepenia
known what this one city was quietly capable of the whole time.
