# DLC 7 (Mirny Subnet) — Main Questline Candidate #1: "The Relay's Own Weight"

**What this is:** a single prospective But/Therefore chain for DLC 7's main questline, produced by
running `DLC_Main_Questline_Design_Method.md` against Mirny subnet. **Not canon, not chosen, not the
only candidate** — a worked demonstration of the method, covering exactly one of the "at least 10"
possibilities that method calls for. Uses the Linear Escalation Chain schematic (`But_Therefore_Quest_
Design_Method.md` §5a), per the DLC-scale worked example already established there.

---

## Inputs actually used (Step 1 of the design method)

- **Vostok's & Kunlun's Local Cultures §23** — both already state, near-verbatim, that "Charlene"
  (XT-17), Vostok's resident geneticist, discovering something in Lake Vostok significant enough to
  require Kunlun's comms relay to transmit is "the primary, plot-critical reason the player makes this
  Hwy 37 journey." This chain starts here because the established material already hands it to us —
  not invented for this exercise.
- **Mirny's Local Cultures §23 / Full Extrapolation §VIII** — confirms Mirny's own subnet-hub relay
  routes *all six* other subnet cities' communications, Vostok and Kunlun included, and that this
  routing infrastructure runs at reduced, war-damaged capacity.
- **Mirny Subnet Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 3 (4th-order effect) / Cross-City Patterns,
  Pattern 4** — naming as a chosen, meaningful, repeatable civic act, uniquely concentrated in this
  subnet; Mirny's own eventual rename is explicitly flagged (`TODO.md`) as deliberately deferred, not a
  question this chain is allowed to resolve.
- **Mirny Subnet Cross-Reference Synthesis, Finding 2 / Cross-City Throughways, Throughway 4** —
  this subnet's single-principle (infrastructure-value-only) war-damage logic, meaning every city's
  post-war condition, Mirny's relay included, traces to a real strategic fact, not an invented one.
- **Super-Ultra-Megasheet, Cross-Regional Pattern 1** — "does genuinely consequential work and gets
  none of the visible credit" as the single most-repeated civic theme in the whole game, independently
  confirmed at Mirny subnet specifically via its own doubled hub function.
- **Explicitly excluded:** the Cradle chamber-manufacturing network / Mountain Pass / player-origin
  thread (Mirny Subnet Ultra-Megasheet Throughway 1, Super-Ultra-Megasheet Throughway 1). That material
  is separately, bindingly forbidden from ever becoming quest content, marked or unmarked
  (`Design_Principles.md` §IV, the Cradle Precedent). This chain deliberately routes through Vostok and
  Kunlun's relationship *without* touching that thread — a different, non-forbidden discovery running
  through the same two cities.

## The Chain

**SETUP** — The player is directed to Vostok, the DLC's established launch point. Vostok's resident
geneticist, "Charlene" (XT-17), has identified something in Lake Vostok's ancient, ice-sealed ecosystem
significant enough that it needs to reach comms authorities beyond her own city — but Vostok has no
long-range transmission capacity of its own. The only way to send it anywhere is via Kunlun's comms
relay, reached by the Hwy 37 crossing.

**BUT** — Kunlun's relay can receive and transmit the signal, but not on its own authority. Any
transmission leaving Kunlun that isn't routine astronomy/comms traffic has to route through Mirny's own
subnet-hub relay to reach anyone else — and Mirny's relay hardware, still running at reduced post-war
capacity, is already fully committed just keeping six other cities in daily contact with each other and
with Mirny.

**THEREFORE** — The player has to travel on to Mirny itself and make the case, in person, that
Charlene's discovery is worth bumping ahead of the subnet's own routine daily traffic.

**BUT** — Getting the discovery prioritized means Davis, Zhongshan, Sinheung, Shirayuki, and Casey's own
remaining outpost all lose real, felt communications capacity for the window it takes to transmit — and
word of *why* gets out, even though what Charlene actually found doesn't. The other cities don't learn
what was sent, only that Mirny, the subnet's own communications hub, just quietly deprioritized all of
them for something unexplained.

**THEREFORE** — Resentment that's been sitting under the surface of Mirny's whole "quiet, unglamorous,
never actually chosen" hub identity surfaces for arguably the first time since the war. The player —
and whoever at Mirny they sided with to get the transmission through — is forced to publicly account for
why the call was made.

**BUT** — This is where the chain reverses on itself rather than simply resolving: the accountability
moment doesn't stay contained to this one transmission decision. It reopens Mirny's own already-existing,
still-deliberately-unresolved tension — a city whose name honors a founding legacy it's since
demographically outgrown, and whose relay infrastructure just visibly, publicly failed the rest of the
subnet at a real moment of need, being trusted as the subnet's uncontested administrative center by
default, never by anything anyone actually chose.

**THEREFORE** — The climax isn't a fight or a clean winner, consistent with this project's own No Good
Endings law and with Mirny's rename staying exactly as deferred as `TODO.md` already has it: Charlene's
discovery does get transmitted, and does matter — a real, earned scientific payoff for the player's own
effort. But the cost is that Mirny's own quiet legitimacy as the subnet's hub, previously just assumed,
is now openly in question in a way it wasn't when the DLC began. The rename question isn't answered here
— it's made impossible to un-notice.

## Self-check (per the method's §7)

Reading only the conjunctions: SETUP → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE → BUT → THEREFORE. No repeated
conjunction in a row. Each BUT changes the shape of the next beat rather than just delaying it (relay
routing authority, not just "the relay is broken"; a subnet-wide resentment, not just "the other cities
are annoyed"; a reopened naming question, not just "people are upset at Mirny"). Deleting either
Complication changes what the player does afterward, so neither reads as a disguised And Then.

## Worth Your Attention

This candidate is deliberately narrow — three cities carrying the spine (Vostok, Kunlun, Mirny), with
Davis/Zhongshan/Sinheung/Shirayuki/Casey felt as consequence rather than forced into the plot directly,
per the design method's own "don't force it" instruction. Its strongest asset is that almost nothing in
it was invented: the Vostok→Kunlun trigger, the Mirny relay-routing bottleneck, and the rename tension
were all already sitting, separately, in established files — this chain's only real contribution is the
causal link connecting Charlene's discovery to Mirny's own civic-legitimacy question, which no existing
document currently draws. Worth noting for whichever candidate is eventually chosen: this is one of ten
the method calls for, not a recommendation.
