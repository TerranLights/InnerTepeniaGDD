**This folder:** `/home/kuroskalacs/Documents/Doll-Fi/media/games/Inner Tepenia/InnerTepeniaGDD/Hermes_research/`.
The project is a science-fiction role-playing game design repository. Its fiction is set in a fictional Antarctic society (the
Tepenian Federation) in the years 2564 to 2812. Hermes collects the real-world facts the fiction is built on.

**Mirror the project's folders (rule 3).** Put a findings file under `findings/` in subfolders copying the part of this repository the
data bears on, for example `findings/Reference/Real-World/Inspiration_Place_Research/<Place>/` or
`findings/Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/...`. The assignment names the part to mirror.

## 5.5 · Real stations and real places

For a real research station or settlement at a site, collect the physical site, the infrastructure, the climate and the kinds of work
actually done there. **Never carry an operator's history, politics or nationality into a finding as the cause of anything.** Record such
material in *Not used*, with this reason.

## 5.6 · No national or ethnic stereotypes

Record what a source says about a community's documented practices, with the source named. Never generalize a nation or an ethnicity,
and never write "typical of" a people.

## 5.7 · Slang goes elsewhere

Do not collect or write slang, colloquialisms, nicknames or local coinages into a place file. Note speech only very loosely: which
languages or broad dialect groups are in use, and where groups differ. Slang met by accident goes in a separate file,
`findings/slang_found_<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, one entry each, with its source, and nothing else about it.

## 5.8 · Content boundary

No excreta content. Where a source's subject needs it, record the source and write "omitted: content boundary".

## 5.9 · Never compare the project's cities with each other

Describe a real-world place on its own terms. Never rank, score, or set it beside another place, and never write "like" or "unlike"
another place unless the source itself makes that comparison (then quote it and name the source).

## 5.10 · The frame (adds to rule 5.10)

The fiction's period is 2564 to 2812. Web facts are present-day facts and stay labeled as such; turning one into a fact about that
period is Claude's work.

## 5.11 · Books (adds to rule 5.11)

Before starting a book, check `Reference/Real-World/Book_Extraction_Index.md` in this repository. A book already extracted there is
reported as already extracted, with the index's pointer, and not redone. Books for this project are in `Reference/Materials/books/`.

## 7 · Standing in this project

- **No Hermes file is an input to any location run, step or phase** (the Universal Location Methodology, the cultural-synthesis and
  real-world-basis methods) unless a brief names it, and only at a standing Claude gave it in `reviews/`.
- **Cold runs and isolated readers treat this folder as withheld,** like every other research folder, until the Lead Developer says
  otherwise.
- The quotation audit and the knowledge graph read this folder. That is expected; the standing above is what keeps its files from being
  mistaken for canon.
