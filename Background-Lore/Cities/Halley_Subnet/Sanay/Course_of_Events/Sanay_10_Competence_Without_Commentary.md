## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Sanay has, by now, already lived through and settled its own account of the great subnet-wide storm
(Suggestion #1) — "endured, not spared" is established locally as the correct way to describe what
happened, insisted upon by the people who did the work. Outside the city, though, that correction
hasn't necessarily traveled.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A visiting official or journalist, impressed by how Sanay came through the storm, publicly frames the
city as a fortunate exception — spared where others weren't. The framing directly contradicts
everything Sanay's own civic identity is built on, and it lands in front of exactly the kind of public
figure whose job is to answer for how the city presents itself. A local councilor responds in kind,
publicly and specifically declining the "exceptional" label, insisting on endurance rather than luck in
terms plain enough that the correction itself starts to travel on its own.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a public confrontation between a well-meaning
outside observer and a local figure defending the city's own account of itself. A isn't being
malicious — the "lucky survivor" framing is a natural, sympathetic way to talk about disaster survival,
and it's likely intended as a compliment. B's objection isn't vanity or thin skin; it's a defense of
something the city already fought for once (Suggestion #1) and isn't willing to let outsiders quietly
undo through careless language. The strongest version of this conflict has A genuinely surprised by how
firmly the correction lands, since nothing about the original comment was meant as an insult.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a subnet-level or
national press figure, or a visiting administrator from elsewhere in Halley, doing comparative coverage
or assessment of the subnet's storm recovery. Character B is plausibly the placeholder Councilor Pieter
Alsdorf himself, or a figure matching that exact established profile — a public figure whose specific,
recurring role is declining, on the record, any attempt to cast Sanay's resilience as luck rather than
work.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Correcting the record once doesn't stop outsiders from making the same assumption again elsewhere — but
the councilor's specific refusal, delivered in plain enough terms, becomes something close to civic
doctrine. "Competence Without Commentary" stops being just a description of how Sanay behaves and
becomes something residents can point to and quote, word for word, whenever the same framing comes up
again.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the exchange becomes locally famous, it gets folded into how the city introduces itself to
newcomers — new dockworkers, new city employees, even schoolchildren end up taught the phrase and the
incident together, as a specific origin story rather than a vague civic mood. An off-the-cuff public
correction turns into deliberate civic pedagogy.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the story is now actively taught rather than just remembered, newcomers arriving in Sanay for
the first time increasingly find themselves corrected pre-emptively — residents volunteering "endured,
not spared" before a visitor has even used the wrong word, because everyone already knows which word is
coming. The correction stops needing an actual outside provocation each time; the city has learned to
perform it in advance.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

"Competence Without Commentary" is no longer just a phrase locals can quote — it's taught, expected, and
now visibly practiced on newcomers before they've even said anything wrong. What began as one
councilor's public correction has become the mechanism by which Sanay actively, continuously reproduces
its own self-image, rather than simply reacting to outside provocation whenever it happens to arrive.
