## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Neumayer's coolant culture, quietly established over generations, runs entirely on precision — every batch
engineered for exact, reproducible effects, standing apart from both the bohemian variety-seeking and
working-class potency-seeking coolant cultures found elsewhere in Tepenia. Outside visitors arrive already
expecting one of those two other experiences, since that's the only real framework most of them know.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A visiting drinker from a working-class glitch-coolant culture, skeptical of Neumayer's reputation on
sight, expects something bloodless and unimpressive from a drink engineered instead of brewed for
character — and says so, out loud, in earshot of the people who make it. What might have stayed a passing
insult instead lands somewhere it wasn't expected to: this is a city that has spent generations having its
best work travel under someone else's name, and a stranger dismissing its coolant as gutless reads, to at
least one person in the room, like the same old pattern showing up in miniature.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation between the skeptical visiting
drinker, who openly dismisses Neumayer's coolant as "an engineer's drink, not a real one," and a Neumayer
coolant-maker or bartender who takes the dismissal personally and issues a challenge — drink it properly,
paying attention, before judging it. This works best as genuine cultural pride colliding with genuine
cultural pride, not correctness versus ignorance. A's skepticism comes from a real tradition of its own —
potency and variety aren't lesser values, they're what that visitor's own culture has organized its
coolant craft around, and Neumayer's buttoned-up reputation generally hasn't done much to make outsiders
feel welcome to it. B's defensiveness isn't mere vanity — it's the latest instance of a city that keeps
watching its best work get written off or carried away under someone else's terms, this time in miniature,
over a drink instead of a schematic.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a visitor from a
working-class glitch-coolant culture elsewhere in the Halley subnet or beyond, proud of their own
tradition's potency and unwilling to concede ground easily. Character B is plausibly a Neumayer bar owner
or coolant brewer, someone whose craft is a direct, personal expression of "Measured, Not Debated" and who
hears the dismissal as touching something larger than just the drink.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The visitor drinks it properly, as challenged, and concedes — grudgingly but genuinely — that it works
exactly as advertised: the precision produces an experience just as real as anything rowdier, simply more
exact about what it delivers. They finish the glass and ask for another.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

The now-genuinely-converted visitor doesn't just quietly enjoy the coolant privately — coming from a
culture with its own oral, status-driven tasting traditions, they start describing Neumayer's coolant back
home in their own community's vocabulary: ranking it, giving it a nickname, treating its exactness itself
as a kind of flex worth bragging about — an act of cross-cultural translation Neumayer's own coolant makers
never attempted or needed to attempt themselves.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that visitor's own community starts hearing about Neumayer coolant in terms they already
understand and respect — translated into their own status vocabulary rather than Neumayer's — real demand
starts arriving specifically from that community, rather than spreading evenly across the wider Federation.
Without the visitor's own translation work, no particular outside community would have any special reason
to know or care.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Neumayer's coolant now carries a small, genuine, but distinctly lopsided outside reputation — concentrated
specifically within whatever community its first real convert came from and carried the story back to,
told in that community's own terms, rather than being evenly or generally known across Tepenia.
