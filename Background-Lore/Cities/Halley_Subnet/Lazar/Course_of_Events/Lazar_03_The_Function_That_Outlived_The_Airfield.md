## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Novolazarevskaya carries forward its real-world DROMLAN heritage as an institutional habit — a
community that knows, in its bones, how to coordinate people and cargo moving at real regional scale.
That expertise was built around aviation, but the habit itself has always been bigger than any single
piece of infrastructure.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Tepenia's airport network locks at exactly eight functioning fields, and Lazar was never going to be
one of them — the aviation infrastructure the coordination habit was built to run on simply isn't
coming. The institutional knowledge has nothing left to coordinate unless it finds a new mechanism, or
it withers into local memory over a generation or two. What Lazar does have is geography: the only
point in the entire Halley subnet where Hwy 7 meets the Belgrano Highway Extension, a convergence no
other city in the subnet can claim, sitting there mostly unexploited for exactly what it could become.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement over whether the city's
aviation-legacy coordination institution should actively reinvent itself around ground transit, or
whether that would mean abandoning something the old model actually did better. This isn't
progress-versus-nostalgia — A's confidence that highway coordination can simply inherit the old
role is genuinely untested, and B's skepticism is a real, defensible concern about coverage, speed,
and reach that ground transit may or may not actually match. The trigger doesn't resolve which of
them is right; the conflict is what has to.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a younger
coordination-station professional, or someone who came up through Lazar's growing highway and
trucking world rather than its older aviation-adjacent institutional culture, eager to redirect the
city's coordination expertise onto the ground before it atrophies from disuse. Character B is
plausibly an old-guard coordinator trained in the aviation-legacy model, someone who genuinely built
their career and their standards around a kind of reach and scheduling precision that highway
logistics has never had to prove it can replicate.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The pivot happens, but not as a clean vindication of A over B — it happens because there is, in the
end, no alternative left to argue for. The logistics-hub function reinvents itself on the ground:
Lazar becomes the Halley subnet's actual distribution capital by highway, the institutional habit
outliving the specific infrastructure it was born on. B's concerns about coverage and reach turn out
to be legitimate in the details — ground routes really do move differently than flight paths ever
did — which is precisely why the coordination stations end up rebuilding their scheduling practices
from scratch around the new mechanism rather than simply porting the old ones over unchanged.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the highway convergence point is fixed, physical ground — not distributed across flight paths
the way an airfield's reach once was — warehousing, staging yards, and distribution infrastructure
concentrate hard around the literal Hwy 7 / Belgrano Extension junction, producing a genuine,
functionally distinct logistics district: not the old core, not simply an extension of the former-
Maitri expansion, but its own emerging piece of the city's geography, built around throughput rather
than residence.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

That logistics district, sitting geographically and economically closest to the faster-growing,
more commercially hungry former-Maitri expansion, becomes the literal engine feeding the expansion's
own commercial boom — goods moving off the highway junction directly into the district's markets and
storefronts with almost no intermediate step. The city's megacity-scale commercial density stops
being an unexplained fact of scale and starts having a specific, traceable source: a coordination
habit that outlived its original infrastructure, now feeding a boom it never set out to cause.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Lazar functions as the Halley subnet's ground-based distribution capital, its logistics district
grown up around the one highway convergence point the subnet has to offer, feeding directly into and
substantially driving the commercial density of the district next door — an institutional habit that
began with aircraft and ended up defining a city that has none.
