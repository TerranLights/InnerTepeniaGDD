# Amundsen Station — Enhancement Opportunities (Completeness Note)

**Not one of the 35 cities.** Added here purely for completeness, at the developer's own request, the
same way Amundsen Station was swept for consistency after the main 35-city pass finished (see
`City_History_Consistency_and_Enhancement_Tracker.md`). Amundsen Station was a rotating-staff
infrastructure installation, not a settlement — no founding-population-vs-majority dynamic, no
generational post-culture, and (per its own Mega_Init) deliberately not padded to the length of an
actual city's documentation. There is no Neo-Races Catalog and no Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md for
this location, so the standard per-city method (compare five lenses against ten existing chains) doesn't
apply here. What follows is a short, unranked list of flagged ideas grounded directly in
`Amundsen_Station_Mega_Init.md`, `Specs/Amundsen_Station.md`, and `Amundsen_Station_Full_Extrapolation.md`
— nothing invented, nothing dramatized into full chains.

---

1. **St. Roald's civic observance.** Specs's own Open Questions ask directly: "is there a Tepenian
   Independence Day tradition at Amundsen Station, analogous to Hut Point in Scott? If so, who maintains
   it?" The Full Extrapolation proposes no organized tradition survives *post*-destruction, but leaves the
   *pre*-war question of whether rotating crews observed anything at the actual site of Amundsen's
   achievement genuinely open. Worth a light, specific answer — not necessarily a formal holiday, but
   perhaps an informal marker or ritual passed between rotating crews rather than inherited from any
   settled population.

2. **The rotating-crew handoff.** The Mega_Init establishes "intense, temporary camaraderie" as the
   station's defining social texture, but never touches the mechanism by which one crew's knowledge and
   habits transferred to the next — a genuinely distinct problem from any settled city's generational
   continuity, since nobody here ever grew up in the place. A specific handoff practice (a logbook, an
   informal briefing custom) would be a small, unique-to-a-facility idea no other city in this project
   needs.

3. **The Amundsen Tower's secondary passenger role.** The Full Extrapolation proposes energy regulation
   and bulk material export as the Tower's two primary purposes, with "passenger transport... real but
   secondary" — a detail established but never given any texture. Worth a light note on what that
   secondary passenger use actually looked like in practice (who used it, how often), since it's the one
   part of the Tower's function that would have put ordinary people, not just cargo, through the station.

4. **A specific mediation case.** Both the Mega_Init and Specs establish, as settled fact, that
   "disputes too difficult for any single subnet to resolve internally were sometimes brought [to
   Amundsen Station] for impartial mediation" — but no specific instance of this is named anywhere. A
   single concrete example (even left unresolved in outcome) would ground an oft-repeated abstract claim
   in something a player or reader could actually picture.

5. **How the pre-Split-Brain archive was actually built.** The archive's *existence* and *significance*
   are both well established (Notable Locations, Legacy), but the ordinary, pre-destruction process of
   how the station accumulated a synchronized record from all six subnets — routine archiving work, not
   a dramatic act — is never described. Worth a brief note on this as unglamorous, ongoing maintenance
   work, consistent with the station's own established "upkeep and vigilance, not production" character.
