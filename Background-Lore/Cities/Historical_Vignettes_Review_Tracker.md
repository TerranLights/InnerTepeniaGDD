(added 2026-08-06).

**Review methodology:** if the `// adjustments` line has been removed entirely from a given vignette's
annotation block (leaving `// visions` alone, or removing the block altogether), that means the entry is
**accepted as-is** — with the possible exception of swapping a placeholder character name for something else.

`// adjustments` line, with actual notes under it, need a second look.

`// adjustments` line removed, or has real notes recorded there). Use the **Notes** space under each city for
short flags — which specific vignette title(s) need a closer look, and why — rather than writing that
per-entry.
