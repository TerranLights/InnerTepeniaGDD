## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Juan Carlos's archive function has relocated inland, taking the practical work with it — the city that was
once Tepenia's literal institutional memory no longer performs that function in any official capacity.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

The city's own residents don't simply stop thinking of themselves as record-keeping, memory-holding people
just because the physical function moved — but what to actually do with that self-image is genuinely
unsettled. Claiming a title this significant without the literal function to back it up risks sounding like
empty nostalgia, and not everyone in the city agrees on whether the identity should be claimed formally, let
quietly lapse, or worn some other way entirely.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement over how seriously to hold on to an
identity the city has technically earned but no longer practically performs. A wants the "Federation's
memory" title claimed with real civic weight — formal recognition, deliberate commemoration, something
solid to replace what was lost when the archive moved. B worries that kind of solemn claim, made without the
function to back it up, reads as hollow self-importance — clinging to former significance rather than
honestly reckoning with the city's now-quieter role. Both care about the same underlying truth; they
disagree about the register it deserves.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly an older resident or
civic figure who remembers the archive's active era and wants that legacy given lasting, formal shape.
Character B is plausibly a younger resident, or someone whose own work has nothing to do with archives at
all, skeptical that solemn commemoration suits a city whose whole established temperament is quiet and
uncontested rather than self-important.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

B's register wins, but not by defeating A's underlying claim — only its solemnity. Residents wear the title
lightly, half-joking about being "the Federation's memory," a claim with real historical truth behind it,
carried with the same wry, self-aware pride Hay-on-Wye's own self-mythologizing carried, rather than solemn
civic reverence.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the identity persists informally rather than through any formal ceremony or declaration, it gets
folded directly into the city's one remaining living institution: hosts of the city's own gathering
tradition start opening sessions with some variation of the joke, half-ritual and half-genuine, until it
becomes a small recurring piece of the practice itself rather than a one-off remark.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the joke becomes ritualized specifically inside the gathering tradition, it travels wherever that
tradition travels — a shared verbal shorthand that later diaspora hosts in Leo, Taurus, and Pisces recognize
each other by, a specific marker of having come from Juan Carlos rather than simply from the wider Palmer
subnet.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Juan Carlos carries "the Federation's memory" as a lived, ritualized in-joke inside its own unnamed
gathering tradition — worn lightly, but specific and genuine enough that it will later travel with the
city's diaspora as a recognizable marker of exactly where they came from.
