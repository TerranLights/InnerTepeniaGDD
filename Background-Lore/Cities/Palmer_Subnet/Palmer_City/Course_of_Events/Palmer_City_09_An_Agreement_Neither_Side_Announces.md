## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Palmer City's tourism economy has grown large enough that informal, ad hoc visitor arrangements stop being
workable — too many visitors, too much revenue, too much real legal exposure to keep running on handshake
understandings between whoever happens to be present on either side.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Neither Tepenia's own government nor whatever Upper Earth authority oversees Machu Picchu Base wants to
formally, publicly acknowledge the arrangement they're both quietly benefiting from — open acknowledgment
would force each side to answer politically uncomfortable questions at home. For Tepenia, formally
recognizing an Upper Earth presence on Tepenian soil risks friction with residents for whom the exile is
still a raw, defining wound. For the Upper Earth authority, formally negotiating with Tepenia at all means
tacitly legitimizing a nation its own government officially still disdains. Pressure nonetheless builds for
some kind of workable, reliable arrangement, precisely because the volume of actual traffic has outgrown
what improvisation can manage.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a negotiation, not a fight — both sides want the
same practical outcome (a working, reliable visitor system) but neither can be seen wanting it, since
either side's own domestic politics punishes open acknowledgment. Character A, representing Tepenia's
side, needs a system secure and dignified enough to satisfy a population still living inside the exile's
wound, without ever getting the formal national recognition that would make the arrangement easy to
defend at home. Character B, representing the Machu Picchu Base side, needs the same functional outcome
without ever being seen to legitimize Tepenia's sovereignty by name. The tension is entirely about how to
build something real while each side's own government requires plausible deniability about it.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a Tepenian civic
negotiator or Palmer City official, trusted to represent the city's and the nation's interest without
overstepping into a formality the national government isn't ready to sanction. Character B is plausibly an
Upper Earth administrator stationed at Machu Picchu Base, professionally invested in smooth, functioning
operations, but bound by instructions from a home government that cannot be seen legitimizing Tepenia.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A quiet, practical understanding gets reached — a visa-on-arrival, tracked-stay system, genuinely
significant diplomacy conducted without either side ever making a public announcement about it. It becomes
the first real, sustained point of negotiated contact between Tepenia and the world that exiled it,
significant precisely because nobody involved ever calls it that.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the agreement can never be publicly cited or formally amended without breaking its own founding
condition of deniability, the two sides develop an unusual, informal-but-durable maintenance channel
instead — recurring, low-visibility working contact between whoever holds the Palmer City and Machu Picchu
Base liaison roles, functioning almost like a standing back-channel despite officially not existing as an
institution at all.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that back-channel exists and reliably works, it quietly becomes the informal route other small,
unresolved points of Tepenia-Upper Earth friction get handled through as well, whenever a practical problem
needs solving without either government wanting to touch it directly. Palmer City's own liaison role
expands, in practice, well beyond tourism administration — entirely because the channel had already proven
itself reliable (2nd-order), not merely because the original visa problem once existed.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Palmer City sits, quietly and entirely unofficially, at the center of Tepenia's only real working contact
channel with Upper Earth — a back-channel born from the visa system's own need for deniable, reliable
maintenance, now handling more than tourism alone, off any official record on either side.
