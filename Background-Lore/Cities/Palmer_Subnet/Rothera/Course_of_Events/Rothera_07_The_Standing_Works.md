## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Rothera's decentralized industrial network takes decades to fully mature, expanding site by site across
Adelaide Island until the whole system is genuinely, completely operational — a transformation so
gradual that no one living through it experiences a single moment of arrival.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A transformation this gradual has no single dramatic moment to mark as its completion — the network
simply becomes complete, sometime, without ceremony, the way any sufficiently slow process does.
Residents eventually settle on a date anyway, the day the last major fabrication site came fully online,
formalizing it into The Standing Works. But celebrating industrial infrastructure risks feeling hollow —
buildings and output aren't, by themselves, the kind of thing a genuinely function-first city
instinctively wants to throw a holiday around, and an observance built on an arbitrary completion date
risks reading as empty ceremony dressed up after the fact.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement over what the new holiday should
actually be about. A finds the whole idea faintly absurd — celebrating factories, however impressive,
strikes A as civic theater dressed up as substance, and pushes to drop or minimize the observance rather
than let it calcify into something hollow. B doesn't disagree that celebrating buildings would be hollow —
B's argument is that the holiday shouldn't be about the buildings at all, but about the specific,
traceable, multi-generational labor that built them, and that framed that way, the observance has genuine
substance behind it.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a younger resident or a
visiting outside observer, skeptical of industrial pageantry on principle, pushing the city to either
drop the new holiday or keep it minimal. Character B is plausibly an older worker or engineer — perhaps a
descendant of Ashgrove-Pryce's original planning team — for whom the observance was never really about
the buildings, but about the people, generations of them, who actually built and maintained the network
site by site.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

B's framing prevails. The Standing Works settles into a holiday built around oral history rather than
industrial pageantry — workers recounting specific personal and family contributions to specific sites,
not generic tributes to output or infrastructure. Residents treat the observance as genuine pride in
something they built together, patiently, across generations, rather than empty ceremony for its own
sake.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the holiday's core content becomes personal, family-specific oral history tied to particular
fabrication sites, an informal but rigorously maintained oral archive develops around it — families
tracking which relative helped build which site or vault section, generation after generation, as a
living record kept alive specifically by the holiday's own yearly retelling.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that labor-genealogy tradition persists, specific families and lineages become informally
associated with specific sites or vault sections across the city — an unofficial, affectionate naming
practice growing up around otherwise identical utilitarian architecture, giving Rothera's uniform
industrial landscape small pockets of individual, human identity that outsiders often find unexpectedly
charming.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

The Standing Works has grown into an annual oral-history tradition anchored in specific families'
contributions to specific sites, giving Rothera's otherwise uniform, function-first landscape informal
names and lived history it wouldn't otherwise carry.
