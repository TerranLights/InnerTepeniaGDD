## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Rothera's earliest industrial planners have every ordinary reason to concentrate the city's fabrication
capacity in one efficient, centralized complex — the way most industrial cities naturally do, with no
established reason yet to do otherwise.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Chief Engineer Malcolm Ashgrove-Pryce sees a real risk in that ordinary approach: a single concentrated
facility is also a single point where everything could be lost at once. He begins arguing for an
alternative — Rothera's whole industrial plan laid out deliberately spread across Adelaide Island's much
larger physical scale, decentralized by design rather than by efficiency. The argument doesn't settle
cleanly. Spreading the plan out costs real coordination overhead a centralized plant would never need —
more transit time between sites, more redundant support staff, more opportunities for any one link in the
chain to slip — and the planners responsible for actually hitting production targets have every reason to
resist paying that cost for a risk that hasn't happened yet.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation between someone accountable for
near-term production output and someone arguing for a structural hedge against a catastrophe that hasn't
happened and might never happen. This shouldn't play as foresight versus short-sightedness — A's
resistance is materially reasonable; decentralization really does cost more, in real time and real
resources, for a risk that's genuinely speculative at this stage. B isn't wrong either; the case for
dispersal doesn't require a specific threat, only the recognition that concentrated capacity is
structurally fragile in a way distributed capacity isn't. Both are arguing from the same function-first
values Rothera will eventually be known for — they just disagree about what "function" requires here.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a production-planning
administrator or an early site foreman, responsible for hitting output targets with the resources
actually on hand, understandably resistant to a plan that makes their own job harder for a benefit they
can't point to yet. Character B is plausibly Ashgrove-Pryce himself, or an engineer working directly
under him, carrying something like the Bas 90 dispersal logic as doctrine rather than improvisation —
arguing from structural principle, not from any specific danger already on the horizon.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Ashgrove-Pryce's plan is adopted. Rothera commits to the tradeoff, betting long-term resilience against
short-term efficiency as a considered strategic choice, not an accident of geography — the fabrication
network laid out from its earliest years across Adelaide Island's full physical scale, deliberately
separated, deliberately harder to coordinate.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

That coordination overhead doesn't just get absorbed quietly — it becomes a permanent institutional
fixture. A dedicated Site Coordination Corps forms, a class of logistics specialists whose entire
profession is choreographing material and worker movement between physically separate fabrication sites,
an occupational role a concentrated industrial city would simply never need to invent.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that Corps exists and needs a genuine physical means of moving people and material quickly
between dispersed sites, it oversees construction of a dedicated inter-site connector road network across
Adelaide Island — distinct from Hwy 1 itself — which becomes a defining, visible feature of Rothera's
built landscape, and hones exactly the road-building expertise the city will later be known for producing
on the wider subnet's behalf.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Rothera's industrial capacity is now genuinely decentralized by design, held together by a standing
coordination profession and a purpose-built internal road grid neither of which a concentrated industrial
city would ever have had reason to develop — resilience built into the city's institutions, not just its
geography.
