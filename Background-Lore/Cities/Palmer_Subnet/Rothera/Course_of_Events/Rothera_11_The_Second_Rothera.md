## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Rothera's decentralized industrial model is mature and proven — deliberate dispersal across Adelaide
Island (chain #2), patient underground vault capacity (chain #3), a whole industrial base grown as a
layered composite rather than replaced generation to generation (chain #9), its resilience concretely
demonstrated when one contained accident stayed contained (chain #10). The knowledge behind all of it
lives almost entirely inside Rothera's own engineers.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Word reaches Rothera that a newer, still-forming industrial settlement elsewhere in the Federation has
just suffered its own version of exactly the vulnerability Rothera's decentralization was built to avoid
— a single concentrated facility damaged or lost, threatening the whole site's fragile early output.
Their own planners send word asking, plainly, how Rothera actually did it.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** not a conflict about whether to help — both sides
agree Rothera should respond. Character A wants to send real expertise, not just documentation: engineers
who actually lived the decentralization decision, willing to leave Rothera for a real stretch of time to
help design the other settlement's own version of it. Character B, responsible for Rothera's own ongoing
maintenance and expansion needs, isn't opposed to helping, but is genuinely worried about the cost —
Rothera's own network still needs the exact expertise Character A wants to send away, and generosity
elsewhere shouldn't come at real cost to what Rothera itself still has to keep running.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a senior engineer, old
enough to remember the earlier, harder years of Rothera's own decentralization program, motivated by a
genuine sense that hard-won knowledge like this shouldn't stay locked inside one city. Character B is
plausibly a production administrator responsible for Rothera's own day-to-day output targets, not
ungenerous, just accountable for what actually keeps Rothera itself running.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Rothera commits a modest, deliberately unglamorous advisory team rather than a token gesture or an
open-ended commitment — real engineers, a real but bounded stretch of time, accepting a genuine short-term
cost to Rothera's own capacity in exchange for actually transferring the knowledge rather than merely
describing it.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

The receiving settlement adopts a genuinely Rothera-echoing decentralized design of its own — not a
literal copy, since its own terrain and scale differ, but the same underlying logic: distributed capacity
over concentrated efficiency, resilience treated as worth its real cost. The same relationship, in
miniature, as the real Gary-to-Magnitogorsk connection this whole idea is grounded in — one city's model
genuinely informing another's, adapted rather than duplicated.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Rothera's own civic self-image quietly widens. It was already, in its own established terms, the Palmer
subnet's construction engine — but this adds something less visible and harder to measure: not goods
moved along Hwy 1, but a proven way of building industrial resilience, carried outward by the people who
actually built it the first time.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Rothera holds two kinds of export at once — the physical goods that reach every other Palmer subnet city
via Hwy 1, and a quieter, harder-to-trace export of design philosophy that reached at least one other
corner of the Federation, modest in scale, never formally announced, but genuinely real.
