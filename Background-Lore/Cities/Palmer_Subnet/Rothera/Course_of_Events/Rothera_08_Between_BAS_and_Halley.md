## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Rothera's British Antarctic Survey heritage traces back to the same institutional lineage as Halley's own
founding, despite the real geographic distance separating the two cities across entirely different
subnets — a shared origin with no established practice yet for what, if anything, it should mean day to
day.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Shared institutional origin doesn't automatically produce an ongoing relationship once two cities settle
into their own separate lives, tied to their own separate subnets, their own separate neighbors, their
own separate daily concerns. Left alone, the connection between Rothera and Halley could easily fade into
nothing more than a historical footnote — true, but functionally irrelevant, the way distant shared
ancestry often becomes once there's no longer any practical reason to keep it alive.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement over how much of Rothera's limited
institutional attention and resources should go toward sustaining a relationship with a city that's real,
meaningful, but genuinely far away, in a different subnet with its own separate priorities. This is a
resource-allocation conflict, not a personal one — neither side is against Halley. A wants to formalize
and invest in the connection — a standing exchange program, a dedicated communication channel — out of
real sentiment and institutional respect for the shared origin. B isn't dismissing the connection's
value, but argues that resources spent maintaining a distant, low-frequency relationship come directly at
the expense of urgent local and subnet priorities that are, by any practical measure, more pressing.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a Rothera or Halley
figure with a personal or institutional attachment to the shared BAS lineage — someone who wants that
history formally honored, not just privately remembered. Character B is plausibly a practically-minded
administrator on either side, not opposed to the relationship in principle, but responsible for weighing
it against more immediate Palmer- or Halley-subnet needs that any dedicated formal channel would compete
with for the same limited attention.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A modest compromise forms rather than either side's full position. The connection persists through
irregular, opportunistic contact — personnel or dispatches carried whenever convenient, piggybacking on
Hwy 1 traffic or Marambio's own aviation link, rather than through any dedicated standing office or
formal exchange program. It stays real but modest — acknowledged and valued, never as close or constant
as either city's relationship with its own immediate neighbors.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the connection persists through individual travelers making the trip when opportunity allows
rather than through any standing institution, each such traveler becomes a minor local figure in both
cities — informal ambassadors by circumstance, notable precisely because contact is rare enough that
every instance of it is genuinely an event.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the relationship depends on individual travelers rather than institutions, what actually gets
exchanged between the two cities skews personal and anecdotal — family news, individual craft techniques,
personal impressions — rather than official or political. Despite centuries of shared origin, the
Rothera–Halley connection never formalizes into anything resembling a trade agreement or political
alliance; it stays human-scaled by its own nature, not by anyone's deliberate restraint.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

The Rothera–Halley connection remains real, sustained by individual travelers who become minor local
figures each time they make the crossing, personal and anecdotal by nature rather than institutional —
genuinely valued, and genuinely modest, exactly as it settled into being.
