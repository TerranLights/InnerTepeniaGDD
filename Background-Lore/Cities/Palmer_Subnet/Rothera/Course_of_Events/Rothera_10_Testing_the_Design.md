## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

A serious industrial accident — a fire, a structural failure — breaks out at one of Rothera's fabrication
sites: the first real test, decades after Ashgrove-Pryce's original decentralization argument, of what
actually happens when something genuinely goes wrong.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Because the city's industrial capacity was deliberately spread across the island rather than
concentrated, the accident stays contained to its own site rather than threatening the wider network —
the rest of Rothera's production continues largely uninterrupted, the decentralization argument proven
directly, in practice, decades after it was first made on paper. But one contained accident, however
vindicating it feels in the moment, doesn't retroactively justify every coordination cost, every
inefficiency, every year of extra effort the decentralized design has demanded all along — a single data
point isn't the same thing as a settled argument, however much it might feel that way to the people who
lived through it.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement over what this one accident actually
proves. A is right on the merits, in a narrow sense — a single contained incident is a small sample, and
treating it as final vindication of decades of accumulated cost is a real overreach that the numbers alone
don't support. B isn't arguing with the statistics; B was there, or knows someone who was, and is arguing
that lived, concrete experience of survival carries a kind of truth that abstract cost-benefit accounting
can't fully capture — that having actually watched the rest of the city keep running while one site burned
means something, whether or not it settles the argument formally.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a budget-conscious
administrator or planner — perhaps institutionally descended from the same skepticism that met
Ashgrove-Pryce's original proposal — cautious about letting one favorable outcome close a debate that
decades of real cost should keep open. Character B is plausibly a worker who was actually at, or near,
the affected site during the accident — someone whose case rests on direct experience rather than
argument, insistent that what they watched happen is proof enough on its own terms.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The story wins out over the statistic, without the abstract argument ever actually being settled. The
incident becomes local legend — the moment residents point to, concretely, as exactly what the
decentralization was actually for — retold and believed, even though A's narrower point about sample size
was never really answered on its own terms.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because it's the story, not the formal argument, that takes hold, the incident gets deliberately
preserved as a physical marker rather than simply erased in the rebuild — the affected section only
partially restored, a scorched wall or a visibly repaired structural seam left standing as evidence,
incorporated into the site rather than covered over.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that physical marker remains in place rather than disappearing into a full rebuild, the site
becomes a standing stop on new workers' orientation and on the Founders' Airstrip open-house circuit — a
hands-on teaching fixture, not just a story told secondhand, used going forward to show new hires exactly
why the site's design works the way it does.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

The contained accident is now embedded both physically and institutionally into Rothera — a partially
preserved site standing as local legend and as a genuine orientation stop for new workers, the
decentralization argument no longer just formally understood but experientially real to everyone who
passes through.
