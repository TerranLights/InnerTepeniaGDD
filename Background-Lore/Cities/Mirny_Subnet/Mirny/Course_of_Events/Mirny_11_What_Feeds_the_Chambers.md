## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Mirny's quarrying and industrial yards already serve one clear, established purpose — the literal
construction engine for Hwy 110 and everything connected to it (chain #6). Separately, and more recently,
Mirny's own material output has also become the supply source behind Sinheung's fabrication-synthesis
chambers, shipped nationwide — a nationally significant relationship that, so far, exists only as a fact
on paper, never yet tested against real demand.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Sinheung's chamber production scales up to meet growing national demand, and the resulting material
request from Mirny is larger, and more urgent, than anything the city's existing quarrying capacity —
already committed to the subnet-local Hwy 110 role — was ever sized to handle.

matters — both sides recognize fabrication-synthesis chambers as nationally essential. Character A, a

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a senior quarrying or
logistics administrator, protective of Mirny's own established subnet obligations, wary of overcommitting
capacity the city doesn't actually have yet. Character B is plausibly a Sinheung industrial
representative, practical rather than demanding, simply carrying the real weight of a national supply
chain that depends on Mirny specifically.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Neither role gets sacrificed for the other. Mirny commits to genuine expansion of its own quarrying
capacity — real investment, not a reallocation that would starve one obligation to feed the other —
sized to supply both Hwy 110's local materials needs and Sinheung's national chamber-manufacturing demand
at once.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Mirny's industrial identity concretely gains a second, nationally significant tier alongside its existing
subnet-local role — no longer just the Mirny subnet's own construction engine, but a genuine link in a
national manufacturing chain reaching well beyond Hwy 110.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Residents' own relationship to the city's rough, unglamorous quarry work quietly shifts — the same
unpretentious daily labor now carries real, tangible stakes reaching all the way to Sinheung and the
robots whose fabrication depends on it, without the city's own plain, unforced self-image changing at all.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Mirny holds two concurrent, genuine industrial roles at once — subnet-local highway construction
materials, and a nationally significant supply line feeding Sinheung's fabrication-synthesis chambers —
both drawing on the same quarrying economy, now serving both scales simultaneously rather than one at the
other's expense.
