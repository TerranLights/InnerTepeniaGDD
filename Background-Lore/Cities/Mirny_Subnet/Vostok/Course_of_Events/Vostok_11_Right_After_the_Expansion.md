## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Vostok and Kunlun are both extreme-isolation research outposts, each straining at the edge of what a
single site can sustain alone — but with no established resource-sharing relationship between them yet.
Kunlun's comms-relay capacity, before its own expansion, exists mainly to serve its own astronomy and
signal-relay mission, with nothing to spare for outreach beyond that.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Kunlun's comms-relay facility undergoes its major expansion, scaled to match the astronomy program rather
than remaining permanently subordinate to it (Kunlun's own chain #6). For the first time, Kunlun has real
spare capacity — and real justification, having just proven the value of investing in comms
infrastructure — to reach outward rather than only inward and upward.

attempting — both sides recognize the potential value once it's actually proposed. Character A, from

absorbed in its own decades-long research, and doesn't obviously need contact from an astronomy-focused
outpost whose needs seem entirely unrelated.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a Kunlun comms
engineer, freshly enabled by the expansion and genuinely curious what else that new capacity could be
used for beyond its original mission. Character B is plausibly a senior Vostok researcher, not hostile to
the idea, just genuinely uncertain what two such differently-focused outposts could actually offer each
other.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The two sites work out a concrete first exchange rather than a vague gesture of solidarity — Kunlun's
comms carrying supply and equipment requests neither site can source alone, Vostok offering computational
modeling and cold-tolerance research genuinely relevant to Kunlun's own extreme-environment engineering
in return.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

The relationship chain #7 describes — "equipment that neither outpost can make alone" — becomes real and
dated rather than an undated, always-assumed fact, beginning specifically in the direct aftermath of
Kunlun's own comms-relay expansion.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

The partnership becomes durable precisely because it started as a concrete, practical exchange rather
than an abstract kinship — an established norm from that point onward, part of both cities' own
institutional memory, rather than a relationship that simply always seemed to have existed.
