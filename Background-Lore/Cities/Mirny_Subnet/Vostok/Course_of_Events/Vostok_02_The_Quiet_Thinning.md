## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Vostok's founding Russian community lives and works generation after generation under conditions few
people would choose voluntarily — a genuinely committed population, sustaining the Lake Vostok research
program through sheer accumulated dedication.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Each generation's own descendants are free to leave for less extreme cities elsewhere in Tepenia, and
many eventually do — nothing dramatic, just an accumulating pattern of quiet departures as each successive
generation weighs the founders' own commitment against the genuine hardship of actually living it
themselves. The population thins steadily, not through any single crisis but through ordinary attrition —
age, quiet departure, simply not being replaced at the rate people leave. But a shrinking community still
has to maintain the same research infrastructure with fewer and fewer hands — and at some point, someone
still committed to the Lake Vostok program has to reckon directly with a descendant who has decided,
without any dramatic rupture, that they simply don't want to inherit a life this isolated.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a quiet, unresentful confrontation between an older
Vostok researcher, personally invested in the Lake Vostok work and hoping a younger relative or protégé
will continue it, and that younger person, who has decided, after real consideration rather than
impulsiveness, to leave for a less extreme posting. This should avoid melodrama — neither character is
wrong, and there's no villain in ordinary generational attrition. A's disappointment is real and earned;
B's choice to leave is equally reasonable and doesn't require justification beyond simply not wanting the
life A chose.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a senior researcher of
the founding-era community, someone who has given their whole working life to the Lake Vostok program and
hoped to see it carried forward by the next generation specifically. Character B is plausibly their own
child, grandchild, or a younger researcher they mentored directly, who has genuine respect for the work
but no desire to spend their own life under the same conditions.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

B leaves, without rancor, and A doesn't try to stop them — the departure happens cleanly, on B's own
terms, exactly the way the pattern has already played out for other families before them. By the time
centuries have passed, there's remarkably little of the founding population left — a decline so gradual
nobody could point to the moment it became irreversible.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because departures like this one happen without institutional rupture or conflict, Vostok never develops
any formal retention policy or crisis response to its own shrinking population — the decline proceeds
entirely unaddressed at the administrative level, since no single departure, including this one, ever
looks urgent enough on its own to prompt an actual institutional response.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because no institutional response ever developed, the population's eventual near-total absence goes
entirely unaddressed — an accumulated absence nobody in the administration ever formally logged or
resolved. *(Note, 2026-07-31: this section previously continued into a follow-on redistribution event
tied to Framheim and Little America — retired per direct developer correction; see
`Vostok_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`'s own correction note. Neither city ever existed in Tepenia's
own history, so there was no displaced population and no crisis for Vostok's own gap to solve. What
became of that gap, if anything, is now an open question rather than a resolved one.)*

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Vostok's founding community has thinned to almost nothing, entirely through ordinary, ungoverned
attrition — an absence so gradual and so administratively unremarked that its true significance goes
unaddressed. The population living at Vostok today has no traceable connection to this decline at all;
this is simply the state the city was left in.
