## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Splinters exists as a modest revival — nothing more ambitious than a decent place for locals to unwind,
run informally by the community that built it, with no aspirations beyond serving Casey's own
residents.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Casey's position as the last coastal city before the long interior drive to Concordia means a constant
stream of travelers passes directly through, and enough of them stop at Splinters looking for exactly
the kind of place it already is that word starts spreading along the whole Dumont d'Urville Sea and
Indian Ocean coastline. Demand grows faster than the original modest space can comfortably absorb. Casey
now has to decide, in practice rather than in principle, what Splinters actually is: a local bar that
happens to get occasional through-traffic, or something the city lets grow into a genuine regional
draw. Growing it means real investment, real risk of losing whatever made the original revival feel
worth doing, and a fundamental change in what the space is for.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation between someone who wants to
expand Splinters aggressively to meet the traveler demand — more space, more staff, a genuine
destination — and someone who has been running or shaping the place since its modest early days and
fears that scale is exactly what kills what made it worth building in the first place. This should read
as two people who both love the same thing and disagree, in good faith, about what loving it actually
requires: growth as stewardship versus growth as betrayal. Neither side is chasing profit or purity for
its own sake; both are protecting the same original impulse from opposite directions.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a newer figure drawn
into Splinters' orbit by its growing reputation — someone with a practical eye for what unmet traveler
demand actually represents, maybe someone who's watched other coastal waypoints fail to capitalize on
exactly this kind of moment. Character B is plausibly one of the original volunteers or early regulars
from Splinters' founding days — someone with no formal ownership claim (per Suggestion #2, nobody
holds one) but real moral authority over what the place is supposed to feel like, whose fear isn't
change itself but a specific, concrete kind of change: strangers treated as customers instead of guests.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The expansion happens, but on Character B's terms rather than Character A's — Splinters grows to
full-city-block scale to meet demand, while deliberately keeping its genre-spanning, unpretentious
character intact rather than professionalizing it away. Popularity and genuine character prove not to
require trading off against each other after all.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the expansion preserves the original informal character rather than replacing it with something
more polished and commercial, Splinters ends up structurally unlike any comparable destination
elsewhere in Tepenia — a full-city-block venue that still runs on the same unpretentious, community
logic as the modest room it grew from, rather than transitioning into a conventional large-scale
hospitality operation.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because Splinters stays structurally informal even at massive scale, it becomes something other coastal
cities' own hospitality ventures actively study and try, usually unsuccessfully, to replicate — Casey's
name becomes attached not just to the bar itself but to a specific, difficult-to-copy model of growth
without loss of character. Delete the 2nd-order change — imagine the expansion had professionalized
Splinters instead — and there would be nothing distinctive left for other cities to try to imitate; the
reputation depends specifically on what kind of large venue Splinters became.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Splinters stands as a full-city-block landmark drawing travelers from along the entire coastline, its
scale never having displaced the unpretentious, genre-spanning character it started with — Casey's
single most famous feature, and a model other cities can point to but rarely successfully copy.
