## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Sinheung's fabrication economy starts broad and general — whatever finished goods the wider subnet
happens to need, produced competently but without any particular specialization distinguishing the city
from other industrial centers.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

General manufacturing doesn't give a city any particular claim to national significance the way a
genuinely specialized capability would — a competent generalist industrial center is replaceable in a way
a genuine specialist isn't. The economy narrows deliberately over generations, engineers and planners
pushing toward increasingly precise, high-value specialization rather than staying a generic industrial
center. But narrowing that far means betting the city's whole economic identity on one specific capability
actually paying off — and midway through the narrowing process, before the eventual chamber-manufacturing
success is anything but a distant hope, someone with real authority over the city's economic direction has
to decide whether to keep committing scarce resources to an increasingly narrow, unproven specialization,
or pull back toward the safer, more diversified breadth the city is walking away from.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation between an economic planner
committed to the narrowing strategy, convinced that genuine specialization is the only route to real
national significance, and a more risk-averse counterpart who sees an increasingly exposed, single-focus
economy as a dangerous gamble the city can't actually afford to lose. This should read as a legitimate
strategic disagreement between two people who both want Sinheung to succeed — A is betting on upside, B is
protecting against downside, and at the point this conflict occurs, neither can yet know which bet is
actually correct.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a forward-looking
industrial planner or senior engineer, an early architect of the narrowing strategy, personally invested
in seeing it through to whatever payoff it eventually produces. Character B is plausibly a more
conservative economic administrator, responsible for the city's overall stability, genuinely worried that
committing further to an unproven specialization risks the fabrication economy's hard-won general
competence for a payoff that isn't guaranteed.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A's strategy prevails, but only after a negotiated compromise — the narrowing proceeds, but with a
formally preserved fallback capacity, a smaller reserved portion of the city's industrial capability kept
generalist specifically as insurance against the specialization failing. The bet pays off completely once
fabrication-synthesis chamber manufacturing becomes the city's defining role — proof that demonstrated
precision, not raw industrial breadth, was always going to be the real path to significance.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because a formal fallback capacity was preserved as part of the original compromise, Sinheung retains a
small but genuine general-manufacturing sector even after chamber production becomes its dominant
industry — never fully specialized to the exclusion of everything else, unlike a city that bet its entire
economy on the narrowing strategy without B's original caution shaping the outcome.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that reserved general-manufacturing capacity still exists, it becomes the sector Sinheung actually
relies on during any future disruption specifically to chamber production itself — giving the city a real,
built-in resilience against exactly the kind of single-point failure a fully specialized economy would
have had no defense against, a direct, structural legacy of B's original, only partially overruled
caution.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Sinheung stands as a genuinely specialized chamber-manufacturing city, its national significance built on
precision rather than breadth — but it carries, deliberately preserved from its own internal debate over
the narrowing strategy, a small reserved general-manufacturing capacity that gives the city real
resilience the purest version of specialization would never have allowed for.
