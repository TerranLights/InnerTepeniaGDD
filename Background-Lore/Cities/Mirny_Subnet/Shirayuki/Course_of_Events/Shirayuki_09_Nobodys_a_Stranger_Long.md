## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Shirayuki's population grows from students, artists, and researchers arriving from across Tepenia, few
of them knowing anyone when they first arrive — a city continually absorbing strangers, by design, given
its own schools' nationwide draw.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A city that wants to feel like a genuine home rather than a waypoint needs newcomers to actually
integrate quickly — the city's whole social culture develops around making that easy, genuine,
uncomplicated friendliness that doesn't require months of proving yourself before anyone actually opens
up. But a culture this welcoming could easily stay shallow, friendly on the surface without real depth
underneath — and at some point, a newcomer who has experienced exactly that kind of easy but ultimately
thin welcome elsewhere in Tepenia arrives skeptical, testing whether Shirayuki's own famous friendliness
is actually different, or just another version of the same surface-level hospitality they've learned not
to trust.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation that isn't hostile so much as
quietly guarded — a skeptical newcomer who keeps a long-tenured Shirayuki resident's easy overtures at
arm's length, testing whether the friendliness has any actual substance behind it, against a resident
whose genuine warmth starts to feel, to them, unfairly doubted. The resident isn't wrong to feel a little
hurt; the newcomer isn't wrong to be cautious, given whatever experience taught them caution in the first
place. The conflict is really about trust needing to be actually earned even inside a culture built to
extend it quickly.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a new arrival —
perhaps a transfer student or a researcher relocating from a city where friendliness proved to be
performative or transactional — bringing real, earned skepticism with them. Character B is plausibly a
longtime Shirayuki resident, genuinely warm by the city's own established civic norms, gradually
realizing that this particular newcomer's continued distance isn't personal, just protective, and
choosing to keep showing up anyway rather than taking the hint to back off.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

B's sustained, patient consistency — not a single grand gesture, but simply continuing to show up,
unbothered by A's initial caution — eventually gets through. The same ease that makes new friendships
easy to start also makes them easy to deepen, consistent with a whole city built around residents
choosing to be here rather than simply enduring it, and A's skepticism resolves into genuine trust rather
than being argued out of them.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because the friendship survived a real, tested period of skepticism rather than forming instantly, it
becomes unusually durable and specific — A, once persuaded, becomes one of Shirayuki's more vocal
defenders of the city's friendliness against exactly the kind of doubt they themselves once held, since
they know firsthand what actually convinced them.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because A now actively vouches for Shirayuki's friendliness to other skeptical newcomers, drawing
directly on their own tested experience rather than simply repeating the city's own reputation secondhand,
Shirayuki's welcome culture gains a kind of credibility with guarded arrivals that the reputation alone
never could — someone who was once exactly as doubtful as they are, telling them directly that it held up.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Shirayuki's easy friendliness now carries its own quiet, informal support network of formerly-skeptical
converts — residents who once doubted the city's own reputation and were won over by patient consistency,
now doing the same work for the next wave of guarded arrivals, keeping the culture honest rather than
letting it calcify into empty reputation.
