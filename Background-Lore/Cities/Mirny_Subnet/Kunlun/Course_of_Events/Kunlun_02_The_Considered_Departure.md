## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Generations of human researchers at Kunlun live with the same chronic altitude toll Dome Fuji's own
population once endured — a slow, compounding physiological cost of permanent life at extreme elevation,
managed but never eliminated.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Unlike a slow, passive fade, Kunlun's own medical infrastructure tracks that cumulative cost with real
precision, generation after generation — logged, measured, cross-referenced against every prior cohort's
own data. The evidence eventually becomes undeniable enough that the community itself, or a wider
Federation safety authority, moves toward a deliberate call: phase out human habitation entirely rather
than let it continue at mounting biological cost. But a considered decision like that means someone
actually has to decide, not simply let circumstance take its course — someone has to stand behind the
data, put a timeline to it, and make the call stick against whatever resistance a population being asked
to leave the only home and vocation many of them have ever known is likely to raise.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a confrontation between someone whose entire
identity and life's work is bound up in being physically present at Kunlun, and someone charged with
actually enforcing a phase-out that the data supports but that no individual researcher necessarily wants
applied to themselves specifically. This isn't a conflict between someone in denial and someone
clear-eyed — A's resistance is genuinely sympathetic, since the work at Kunlun really is worth doing and
leaving really does mean losing a vocation, not just a residence; B's push is not cold bureaucracy either,
since the data is real and the harm is real and quantifiable. The tragedy of this cycle is that both
positions can be entirely reasonable and still point in opposite directions.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a senior human
researcher, deep into their own career and already showing measurable signs of the cumulative toll, who
argues their own case should be an exception — more careful monitoring, a reduced schedule, anything
short of total departure. Character B is plausibly a Federation safety-authority representative, or
just as plausibly a Kunlun administrator (human or robot) who has to weigh one researcher's individual
wish to stay against the precedent that granting exceptions would set for everyone else the data applies
to just as clearly.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

No exception is granted. The phase-out proceeds on schedule, and Kunlun's transition to a fully robot
population carries the weight of a deliberate, considered act rather than a quiet drift — evidence-driven,
consistent with a city whose whole civic identity already runs on function and proof rather than
sentiment.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Departing human researchers cannot simply hand their posts to robot successors and leave — decades of
uncatalogued, instinctive expertise (how a particular instrument actually behaves in practice, which
readings to distrust, which anomalies matter) exist only in individual memory, never fully written down.
Kunlun formalizes a mandatory knowledge-transfer period before the phase-out completes: structured
interviews, recorded procedure, and direct mentorship sessions between each departing human specialist
and their eventual robot successor.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

That mandatory mentorship period becomes, over time, a permanent institutional standard rather than a
one-time transition measure. Long after the last human researcher has left, no robot can be certified
into a senior technical post at Kunlun without first completing a formal apprenticeship structured
around the same archived human-authored methodology — an inherited teaching lineage that outlives the
teachers themselves by centuries.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Kunlun is now fully robot, but its technical training still runs formally through archived human
instructional material and mentorship record — no astronomer or comms engineer earns real seniority
without having worked through a "vanished" human teacher's own recorded methodology first. The city's
evidence-driven, function-first character now extends even to how it trains its own future generations.
