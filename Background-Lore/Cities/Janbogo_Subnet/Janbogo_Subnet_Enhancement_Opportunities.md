# Janbogo Subnet — But/Therefore Enhancement Opportunities

**Part of the City History Enhancement Opportunities pass** — see
`Cities/City_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Tracker.md` for the overall purpose, per-city method, and
progress index across all subnets. This file holds the Janbogo subnet's own flagged ideas only.

**Scope note, carried over from every city's own Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md:** all flagged ideas
below are meant for *pre*-Long Night War, Second Interwar Period content only — no war damage, no
post-war material. Flagged ideas that would require war-era framing are out of scope for this pass.

### Cape Adare

**Files read:** `Cape_Adare_Catalog.md`, `Cape_Adare_Mega_Init.md`,
`Cape_Adare_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` (all 10 existing chains). First Janbogo subnet city.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** consensus-built early civic institutions among strangers with no
shared founding story (#1), Rookery-Keeper Tomás Widjaja formalizing penguin-keeping norms (#2), the
deliberate, ongoing civic project of staying "big city, small-town feel" (#3), Archivist Elena Manalo's
early heritage-documentation tradition (#4), the acoustic folk music culture cross-pollinating from a
mixed population (#5), St. Carsten's Landing becoming the defining civic holiday (#6), a second
symbolic gathering using Cape Adare's own "the first place" weight for regional self-governance (#7), a
curated calendar of other civic "firsts" (#8), deliberately paced growth protecting the historic core
(#9), and Elena's recurring defense of archive funding against trade-focused priorities (#10). New
angles below are ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** None of the 10 chains address the actual gateway/trade economy
   at all — Cape Adare is explicitly established as "the most accessible landfall point on the Ross Sea
   coast from open ocean," yet chains #9 and #10 only invoke "the trade-focused gateway economy" as
   background pressure against heritage funding, never developing it. A chain establishing what actually
   moves through Cape Adare's port — first point of contact for incoming ships, goods, and new arrivals —
   would use an entirely undeveloped economic thread.

2. **Resident character cultures.** The Synthesis Notes explicitly flag Cape Adare's distinct pattern:
   national distinctiveness concentrates almost entirely in the Surface Culture "heritage landmark"
   dimension, where four populations (USA, Japan, Canada, UK) each hold a precisely-matched "oldest
   structure" precedent — Jamestown, Yoshinogari, L'Anse aux Meadows, Skara Brae. None of the 10 chains
   dramatize these four traditions actually meeting; the archival tradition (#4, #10) belongs to an
   unspecified figure, not tied to any of them. A chain about representatives of these four heritage
   traditions comparing their own homeland's "oldest structure" against Borchgrevink's hut — an actual
   cross-cultural exchange of preservation philosophy — would use texture currently sitting unused across
   four separate Catalog entries.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Mega_Init's own "New, surfaced by this research pass" section
   asks whether Cape Adare hosted "a second historically significant event or gathering... beyond
   Borchgrevink's original 1899 overwintering." Chain #7 answers this generally (a regional
   self-governance gathering), but doesn't use the specific mechanism of the Yalta precedent it's drawn
   from — a meeting "whose historical weight vastly outstripped the town's modest physical size,"
   implying genuinely national, not just Ross-Sea-coast-local, stakes. A chain about Cape Adare hosting a
   moment of that outsized diplomatic weight — representatives from well beyond the immediate coast,
   deciding something nationally consequential — would use the specific half of the Yalta precedent
   chain #7's more modest framing doesn't fully capture.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** The Mega_Init's "What It Feels Like" section
   establishes that heating infrastructure creates "a genuine warm microclimate... close to an oasis by
   Antarctic standards" — an established but entirely unused fact; none of the 10 chains touch it, focusing
   instead on the rookery, the hut, and growth pacing. A chain about the engineering history behind that
   warm-oasis effect — how residents actually achieved and maintained it — would ground this specific
   environmental detail in something load-bearing.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section flags "the exact road connection and
   distance from Cape Adare south to the Terra Nova Bay cities" as still unresolved. None of the 10 chains
   touch Cape Adare's relationship with its southern Terra Nova Bay neighbors specifically — they're either
   internally focused or gesture at the "Ross Sea coast" only in the abstract (chain #7). A chain about
   establishing or improving that specific overland connection would use this explicitly flagged, still-
   open geographic question.

### Denison

**Files read:** `Denison_Catalog.md`, `Denison_Mega_Init.md`, `Denison_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`
(all 10 existing chains).

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the founding commitment to a single fused structure rather than
separate buildings (#1), Wei Zhang's structural interlinkage design breakthrough (#2), the wind-
statistics recitation tradition compensating for the architecture's own outward quietness (#3), Kenji
Marchetti-Suh's chamber-manufacturing operation (#4), the close Denison-Dumont d'Urville relationship
against a more distant Cape Adare (#5), a visiting skeptic's astonishment validating the recitation
tradition (#6), the strict structural-upkeep civic expectation the fused architecture demands (#7),
Denison and Casey's shared demographic origin producing visibly different cities (#8), residents
enjoying the gap between their fearsome reputation and livable reality (#9), and the pressure to live up
to the legendary namesake (#10). New angles below are ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** None of the 10 chains address Denison's wind-engineering
   expertise being sought out *externally* — the same "exportable skill" pattern already established for
   bedrock-construction expertise at Halley-subnet cities like Sanay and Abowasa. Chain #2 covers the
   internal engineering breakthrough; chain #4 covers chamber manufacturing. Neither touches Denison's
   engineers being consulted by another Tepenian city facing its own, lesser wind problem. A chain about
   that external consultation would use an unused angle distinct from both existing industry-focused
   chains.

2. **Resident character cultures.** The Synthesis Notes list three genuinely distinct "why we build this
   way" registers side by side without ever dramatizing them meeting — USA's Boulder/NCAR-echoing
   atmospheric-research caution, China's Kowloon-echoing dense-fusion instinct, and Japan's Sanda-echoing
   top-down comprehensive planning. None of the 10 chains stage any tension or complementary interplay
   between these three approaches. A chain about a specific design decision where these three registers
   genuinely pull toward different solutions before converging would use population-specific distinctions
   currently only listed, never dramatized together.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** Montreal's RÉSO and Trieste's Bora-adapted architecture are both
   explicitly flagged as "concrete, level-design-ready detail... worth keeping," yet neither appears in any
   of the 10 chains. Chain #1 covers the general commitment to a fused structure, but not RÉSO's specific
   proof-of-scale (500,000+ daily users) or Trieste's granular details — chains and handrails bolted to
   buildings, roofs weighted with limestone blocks. A chain built around these specific, physical,
   currently-unused architectural details would ground Denison's engineering identity in concrete texture
   none of the existing chains include.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** The Full Extrapolation resolves Denison's
   Enneagram tension by noting the architecture is Withdrawn *on purpose* — sealed and quiet specifically
   because that erases outward drama, which is why the verbal recitation tradition exists to compensate.
   Chain #3 treats that verbal tradition as something that simply emerged; none of the 10 chains dramatize
   the actual design choice behind it — an early debate over whether Denison's architecture should visibly
   showcase its wind-defiance instead of staying sealed and quiet. A chain about that specific choice point,
   with the "sealed" school winning, would dramatize the decision the Enneagram resolution describes only
   as an already-settled fact.

5. **Other.** None of the 10 chains depict the very earliest period at Cape Denison — before Wei Zhang's
   structural breakthrough (#2), before any engineering solution existed at all. Chain #1 opens with
   settlers already committing to the fused-structure design; chain #8 covers demographic origin, not
   lived experience. A chain about the harrowing first months or years of pure exposure — before the
   wind-engineering identity that defines every other chain even began — would fill the origin gap the
   existing chains skip past on their way to the engineering triumph.

### Dumont d'Urville

**Files read:** `Dumont_dUrville_Catalog.md`, `Dumont_dUrville_Mega_Init.md`,
`Dumont_dUrville_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` (all 10 existing chains).

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** French persisting as a living civic default among newcomers with
no ancestral stake in it (#1), the earliest civic decisions negotiating space with the Adélie rookery
(#2), the "New Orleans at 1/20th scale" downtown music culture becoming genuinely load-bearing (#3),
heating infrastructure run visibly between buildings as an outward statement of vitality (#4), the
perpetually unresolved mainland-bridge debate (#5), the outdoor St. Jules Day journal reading held in
the wind (#6), the informal, shared penguin-observation practice (#7), Petrel Island's unglamorous
"buildable rock" founding logic (#8), newcomers picking up functional French through sheer necessity
(#9), and the small dense downtown as the city's whole protected vital core (#10). New angles below are
ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** None of the 10 chains address the wildlife-tourism-gateway
   function as an actual economic activity — six of seven populations hold City-Type matches tied to real
   tourism-gateway towns (Wòlóng, Homer, Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer, Shiretoko, Phillip Island, Labuan Bajo),
   yet chains #2 and #7 treat the rookery purely as private coexistence and quiet observation, never as
   visitor revenue or tourism infrastructure. A chain about Dumont d'Urville developing formal
   visitor-facing infrastructure around the rookery — distinct from the existing chains' informal framing —
   would use an entirely unaddressed economic angle.

2. **Resident character cultures.** The Catalog names Australia's population as holding "the closest,
   most direct real-world match found anywhere in this project for a city's own defining wildlife
   relationship" (Phillip Island's Penguin Parade), yet the penguin-coexistence chains (#2, #7) present it
   as shared citywide rather than crediting any specific population. A chain giving Australian-descended
   residents credit for pioneering a more formal, structured wildlife-viewing tradition — echoing the
   actual Penguin Parade format — distinct from chain #7's quiet, informal "everyone just does it"
   observation practice, would use this flagged but unused population-specific authenticity.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Tucumán parallel is used for its practical-founding-geography
   half (chain #8) and its single-date civic-identity half (chain #6), but its third detail — the city
   carrying an entirely separate, affectionate civic nickname ("El Jardín de la República") alongside its
   formal "Cradle of Independence" identity — goes unused. None of the 10 chains give Dumont d'Urville a
   second, more informal name coexisting with St. Jules Day's formal weight. A chain about residents
   developing their own affectionate nickname for the city, distinct from its founding-moment identity,
   would use this unused half of an already-cited precedent.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** The Catalog notes Petrel Island is "somewhat
   sheltered by the archipelago relative to the open coast" — a specific geographic nuance distinct from
   Denison's or Cape Adare's full exposure — but none of the 10 chains use it; chain #4 and others treat
   the wind simply as uniformly severe. A chain about the archipelago's other islands serving a specific
   windbreak function, or a civic relationship (practical or symbolic) with those smaller surrounding
   islands, would ground an established but unused piece of geography.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section flags Pink Lucy's story as "the single
   most significant open question" tied to this city — her migration route is now resolved elsewhere in
   this project, but her pre-war role and name derivation at Dumont d'Urville specifically remain open.
   None of the 10 chains touch her pre-war presence at all. A chain establishing formative pre-departure
   texture for her time at Dumont d'Urville — without resolving her full story, the same restrained
   approach already used for Palmer City's and Shirayuki's own open character candidates — would build
   toward this significant, still partially open question.

### Fort McMurdo

**Files read:** `Fort_McMurdo_Catalog.md`, `Fort_McMurdo_Mega_Init.md`,
`Fort_McMurdo_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` (all 10 existing chains). Note: this city's own
Course_of_Events file deliberately excludes founding-era content, picking up only once the city is
already settled — the existing-coverage summary below reflects that scope.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the informal-to-official capital transition driven by accountability
pressure (#1), the "stayed-behinds" seasonal-labor residency fight (#2), the Dry Valleys extraction/
processing split (#3), the old-core-versus-new-expansion construction-standards clash (#4), the China/USA
meritocratic-culture workaround hardening into genuine civic value (#5), the Erebus seismic scare (#6),
the Amundsen Tower/Concordia convergence-boom precedence crisis (#7), the Cape Evans/Cape Royds heritage
preservation (#8), the comms-station charter formalizing existing informal authority (#9), and the
mentorship program fixing a succession-knowledge gap (#10). New angles below are ones the existing 10
don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** The Synthesis Notes flag "a rare three-way convergence" — USA
   (Hawaii/Kīlauea), Japan (Sakurajima/Kagoshima), and Italy (Mount Etna/Catania) are all independently
   anchored to real communities living permanently in the shadow of active volcanism. None of the 10
   chains differentiate these populations; chain #6's Erebus seismic scare and chain #10's monitoring
   program both stay unspecified as "the city" or Dr. Marco Conti alone. A chain crediting Japanese- or
   Italian-descended residents with specific expertise in the Erebus monitoring/response culture — drawing
   on real ancestral experience with active volcanism — would use this flagged three-way convergence
   directly.

2. **Resident character cultures.** Germany, France, and UK all converge on heavy-industrial craft pride
   via genuinely distinct real-world routes (the Ruhr, Lorraine, Sheffield), but none of the 10 chains
   differentiate these three European traditions from each other or dramatize them meeting. Chains #3-#5
   handle the industrial economy at the China/USA level only. A chain about a specific technical dispute
   or collaboration among these three European industrial traditions — different national engineering
   cultures converging on the same processing facility — would use texture currently only listed in
   parallel, never staged together.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Geneva parallel is used only for its coordination/organization-
   density half; its other real detail — "one of the world's most sophisticated private-banking sectors" —
   goes unused. None of the 10 chains address any financial or resource-allocation function distinct from
   the physical extraction/processing split (#3) or the pure scheduling-priority dispute (#7). A chain
   about Fort McMurdo developing a genuine resource-allocation or credit-management function across the
   projects it coordinates would use the unused financial half of the Geneva precedent.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** None of the 10 chains dramatize the McMurdo
   Sound seasonal sea-ice crossing itself as a physical challenge — chain #3 mentions it only in passing as
   "a real vulnerability" within its economic-split framing. A chain about an actual crossing failure or
   near-failure — testing whether the seasonal ice road connecting Fort McMurdo to the Dry Valleys can
   genuinely be trusted — would dramatize the physical crossing as its own story, distinct from #3's
   economic framing.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section flags "exact industrial output and
   materials extracted — volcanic geology of Ross Island, Dry Valleys geology across the Sound" as
   unresolved. Chain #3 resolves that raw extraction relocated to the Dry Valleys, but never addresses
   whether Ross Island's own volcanic basalt still serves any secondary industrial purpose afterward. A
   chain about a modest, secondary use for Ross Island's own geology once the Dry Valleys became primary
   would use this explicitly flagged, still-open materials question.

### Janbogo

**Files read:** `Janbogo_Catalog.md`, `Janbogo_Mega_Init.md`, `Janbogo_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`
(all 10 existing chains). The city sharing its name with the subnet.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the Terra Nova Bay polynya driving Janbogo's maritime/hub economy
(#1), Majyao Bisyugota's teahouse growing famous throughout Tepenia (#2), architect Wu Lian-Marchetti's
shielded commercial halls (#3), the independent Chinese/French aesthetic fusion (#4), the general
"whatever arrives becomes Janbogo's own" absorption pattern (#5), meteorologist Han Soo-jin's katabatic
warning system (#6), the permanently distant-but-warm Janbogo/Sejong relationship (#7), the accidental,
undesigned accumulation of hub function (#8), teahouse culture spreading beyond Majyao's own founding
heritage (#9), and the shielded halls' staged expansion under real crowding pressure (#10). New angles
below are ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** The Mega_Init's own "New, surfaced by this research pass" section
   explicitly asks whether the shielded commercial halls carry the same "concentration-equals-vulnerability"
   tension already explored at Casey and Marambio. Chain #3 covers the halls' design and chain #10 covers
   crowding-driven expansion, but neither stages an actual incident testing whether that concentrated
   design creates real structural vulnerability. A chain about a specific incident (fire, structural
   failure) testing the halls' own vulnerability — distinct from #10's purely crowding-driven expansion —
   would use this explicitly flagged, still-open question.

2. **Resident character cultures.** Italy's population holds "one of the strongest mechanistic matches"
   in the whole project (the Gulf of Trieste's real Bora wind), yet chain #6 credits meteorologist Han
   Soo-jin's warning system generically, without tying it to any specific population's ancestral
   wind-forecasting expertise. A chain giving Italian-descended residents specific, credited input into
   that warning system — the one population whose homeland precedent is a documented, named katabatic wind
   phenomenon — would use this flagged but unused population-specific authenticity.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Ottoman Kahvehane parallel's specific, dated detail — spreading
   to distinct cultures at documented historical moments ("Venice by 1645, Vienna after 1683") — goes
   unused; chains #5 and #9 both describe the teahouse tradition's spread as vague, undated organic
   diffusion. A chain about the teahouse tradition reaching a specific other Tepenian city at a datable
   moment — echoing the Kahvehane's own documented city-by-city adoption — would use the more specific
   mechanism the existing chains' organic-diffusion framing skips past.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** The Mega_Init's own "New, surfaced" section also
   raises Murmansk's own cautionary half — "a uniquely valuable port is also a uniquely valuable target" —
   distinct from chain #1's purely advantageous framing of the polynya. None of the 10 chains show Janbogo's
   leadership grappling with the polynya as a strategic liability, not just an asset. A chain about that
   recognition and any resulting contingency planning would use the flagged cautionary half of an
   already-cited precedent chain #1 doesn't touch.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "What's Actually Open" section flags the relationship among all three
   Korean-founded Tepenian cities — Janbogo, Sejong, and Sinheung — as unresolved. Chain #7 covers only
   Janbogo and Sejong, entirely omitting Sinheung, the third and (per the project's own correction) most
   heavily Korean city by population. A chain about Janbogo's relationship with Sinheung specifically would
   fill the gap chain #7's two-city framing leaves untouched.

### Scott

**Files read:** `Scott_Catalog.md`, `Scott_Mega_Init.md`, `Scott_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` (all
10 existing chains).

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the founding choice to stay deliberately small (#1), Community
Organizer Fiona Māui's family-oriented civic planning (#2), the small, contained Erebus-fallen-material
collection industry (#3), Wiremu Tane's role as Keeper of Hut Point (#4), the unmandated shared
Independence Day tradition (#5), Scott's complementary precision calibration and monitoring niche (#6),
the quiet sting of Fort McMurdo's formal capital designation (#7), declining outside investment to
preserve deliberate smallness (#8), the friendly, sibling-like Scott/Fort McMurdo rivalry (#9), and "the
city that never wanted to be the capital" as its own settled identity (#10). New angles below are ones
the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** The Mega_Init notes New Zealand, the founding-operator nation,
   sits at just 3.52% — "one of the deepest founding-nation dilutions documented anywhere in Tepenia, even
   as the city's whole civic identity remains organized around the founding-era civic template." Chains
   #1 and #2 cover the founding choice to stay small generally, but neither addresses New Zealand's own
   population specifically or how its civic template survived such deep demographic dilution. A chain
   about that survival — echoing the "founding culture outlives its founders" pattern already established
   at Zhongshan and Mirny — would use this specific, flagged demographic fact directly.

2. **Resident character cultures.** Indonesia's population holds "arguably the single strongest 'living
   under an active volcano' geography match found anywhere in this project" (Merapi), despite the smallest
   population share at Scott. None of the 10 chains credit this population specifically; chains #3 and #6
   (the Erebus material collection and monitoring work) both stay unspecified as citywide activity. A chain
   giving Indonesian-descended residents specific, credited expertise in that Erebus-adjacent work — drawing
   on real ancestral experience living safely near an active volcano — would use this flagged but unused
   population-specific authenticity.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Hannibal, Missouri parallel's specific mechanism — Mark Twain
   quotes posted "on nearly every streetlamp," ubiquitous physical civic branding — is used only generally
   for chain #4's Keeper role, not for that specific everyday-omnipresence detail. None of the 10 chains
   show St. Robert's presence woven into Scott's built environment beyond Hut Point itself. A chain about
   St. Robert references becoming genuinely ubiquitous throughout the city's public spaces — not just
   concentrated at the one sacred site — would use the unused half of an already-cited precedent.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** None of the 10 chains address Hut Point
   Peninsula's own specific physical geography as a constraint — Scott shares Fort McMurdo's terrain and
   microclimate but at "much smaller, more compact scale," a geological fact distinct from the purely
   cultural "chose to stay small" framing chain #1 uses. A chain about a literal terrain limit unique to
   Scott's own compact peninsula footprint constraining its growth would complicate chain #1's
   choice-only framing with a physical fact none of the existing chains use.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init's own "New, surfaced by this research pass" section asks specifically whether
   Fort McMurdo's capital formalization "strain[ed] the established 'genuinely collaborative and genuinely
   competitive in equal measure' relationship" between the two cities — a relationship-level question.
   Chain #7 answers only Scott's own internal emotional reaction (a quiet sting that fades); it never tests
   whether the actual day-to-day sibling dynamic chain #9 describes changed in practice afterward. A chain
   specifically testing that relationship, not just Scott's private feelings, would use the relationship-
   level half of this explicitly flagged question.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the organic street tangle developing its own internal, lived-in

city's civic center by default (#3), Elisa Faranda's deliberately small, famous Elisa's Room (#4), the

   industries, grown out of its hospitality-centered civic culture" — but all 10 existing chains address
   only food and music, never craft or design as their own economic activity. A chain about Zukelli's
   craft/design industry (furniture, decor, the visual character of its own restaurants and spaces)
   developing as a distinct economic export would use an unaddressed half of the city's own established
   City-Type identity.

2. **Resident character cultures.** The Venice parallel is used for its crossroads-meeting-point half
