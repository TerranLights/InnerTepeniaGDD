## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Janbogo's vast shielded commercial halls (chain #3) are the real center of civic gravity — nearly all
daily life happens inside them, celebrated as the city's defining architectural achievement, already
expanded once under real crowding pressure (chain #10). Almost the entire population's daily activity
funnels through a small number of large, continuous structures.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A fire breaks out in one of the halls — contained and survivable, but genuinely serious — the first real
test of what happens when something actually goes wrong inside a structure this large and this central
to daily life.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** not a conflict about whether the fire was serious —
both sides agree it was. Character A, a civic planner confident in the halls' existing design, is
reluctant to commit to costly structural retrofits based on a single incident, worried about disrupting a
system that otherwise works exactly as intended. Character B, a safety official or resident directly
affected by the fire, argues the real risk isn't this one cause — it's the underlying concentration
itself, and that risk doesn't go away just because this particular fire was contained.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a senior civic
administrator, protective of the halls' celebrated design and genuinely worried about the cost and
disruption of major retrofits. Character B is plausibly a fire safety official or a resident who was
actually inside during the incident, practical rather than alarmist, focused on what concentration
specifically makes worse.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The city invests in genuine structural compartmentalization — fire barriers and independent sections
dividing the halls into a connected network rather than one continuous vulnerable space, keeping the
shielded, communal character intact while ensuring a single incident can no longer threaten the whole
system at once.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Janbogo's iconic halls become genuinely less monolithic — a network of independent, connected sections
rather than one singular structure — the concentration-equals-vulnerability tension addressed directly
rather than left as an untested risk.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

The same "absorb whatever arrives and adapt" pattern that already defines Janbogo's cultural life (chains
#5, #9) turns out to apply structurally too — proof the city's adaptability isn't only a cultural
reflex, but a genuine, load-bearing civic habit that extends to how it builds.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Janbogo's halls remain the city's beating heart, now genuinely more resilient — compartmentalized rather
than singular — the concentration-equals-vulnerability tension the Mega_Init itself raised named and
addressed directly, rather than left as an open question nobody in the city ever had reason to test.
