## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Janbogo's growing population brings genuinely distinct aesthetic sensibilities with it — Chinese and
French design habits arrive side by side, neither one dominant enough to simply replace the other,
visible in how residents furnish, dress, and decorate within the shielded halls.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Designing for indoor warmth against a climate this severe means the city needs a real, unified visual
identity for its shared civic spaces, not two competing ones cluttering the same halls — but as the halls
fill with residents from both traditions, decisions about how to decorate and furnish shared commercial
and civic space start surfacing genuine aesthetic disagreement, each community naturally gravitating
toward what's familiar rather than toward compromise.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Neither scheme wins outright — practical necessity forces the two proprietors to actually collaborate on
one shared stretch of hall space rather than dividing it into separate zones, and what comes out of that
forced collaboration is neither purely Chinese nor purely French, but a genuinely new combination neither
side originally proposed.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

The two traditions fuse, independently, into something new — richly colored streets and interiors that
belong to neither tradition alone — as other proprietors elsewhere in the halls, seeing the combined
result work, start adapting the same fused approach rather than picking one tradition over the other.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the fusion spread by other proprietors imitating a result they admired rather than through any
citywide design mandate, nobody ever plans the fusion deliberately — it just keeps happening, building by
building, each new adopter adding their own small variation rather than replicating a fixed template,
which is exactly what keeps the resulting style genuinely alive rather than calcifying into a rigid
"official" civic look.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Janbogo now carries a genuinely original aesthetic identity, grown rather than designed, that no other
Tepenian city quite replicates — a Chinese-French fusion born from one forced collaboration and spread
entirely through imitation, never through civic decree.
