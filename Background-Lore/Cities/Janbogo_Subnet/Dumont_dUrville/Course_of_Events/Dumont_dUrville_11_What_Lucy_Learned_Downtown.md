## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Dumont d'Urville's downtown culture is fully alive — live music an ordinary, expected feature of nearly
every eatery, something residents actively protect rather than take for granted (chain #3). A young
Pink Lucy grows up inside it, naturally, instinctively warm and inclusive — the same open, reaching-out
quality already established as core to her character — without yet having any real outlet for it beyond
simply being herself.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A downtown venue's regular crowd thins during a hard stretch — bad weather, competing pressures, the
ordinary strain small communities face — and Lucy sees, for the first time, that the warmth she'd always
assumed was simply how Dumont d'Urville worked actually takes real, deliberate effort to sustain.

---

## Conflict
###### Pink Lucy vs. [Character B] — role/stakes, not a name, for now.

**Character A (confirmed):** Pink Lucy (FW-25).

**Character B:** *(left blank)*

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** not a conflict about whether the venue is worth
saving — both agree it is. Lucy, young and instinctively inclusive, wants to jump in and start organizing
people back through the door, certain her natural gift for making people feel welcome can actually fix
this. Character B, the venue's own established owner or organizer, is skeptical — not unkind, just
tired, and inclined to read her enthusiasm as naive energy rather than a genuine asset, having watched
other well-meaning efforts fizzle before.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character B is plausibly a downtown eatery or
venue owner, worn down by a hard stretch rather than cynical by nature, someone who needs to actually see
results before extending real trust to an untested young enthusiast.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Lucy organizes the crowd back — not through any elaborate plan, just through the same instinctive,
unguarded inclusiveness that already defines her, applied deliberately for the first time rather than
simply lived. The venue fills again. Character B comes around, genuinely impressed rather than merely
placated.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Lucy has her first real, lived proof that community warmth isn't automatic — it's something built and
actively maintained, the exact insight chain #3 already establishes as true of Dumont d'Urville's whole
downtown culture, now personally and formatively hers rather than simply an ambient civic fact.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

The experience becomes a template she carries forward — the specific, lived understanding that inclusion
has to be organized, not just felt, ready to apply again wherever her path leads next, well before
anything about her eventual departure from Dumont d'Urville ever enters the picture.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Pink Lucy carries this formative lesson with her from Dumont d'Urville — community warmth as something
built and maintained, not simply felt — the earliest seed of the morale-infrastructure instinct her
already-established character carries forward, wherever the rest of her story goes.
