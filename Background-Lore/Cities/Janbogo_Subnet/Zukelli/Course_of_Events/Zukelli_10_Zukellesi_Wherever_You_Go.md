## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

"Zukellesi" starts as nothing more than the ordinary demonym for anyone born or living in the city — a
plain label, used the way any city's residents are labeled, carrying no meaning beyond simple residency.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Zukelli's specific hospitality-and-warmth civic character becomes distinctive enough, recognized widely
enough across Tepenia — through the Crossing (Suggestion #6), the food-and-music reputation (Suggestions
#3, #4, and #9), and the good-natured rivalry with Janbogo (Suggestion #7) — that travelers and visitors
from elsewhere start noticing the word "Zukellesi" gets used, informally, by people who have never actually
lived in the city at all, applied to describe a certain kind of warmth rather than a place of birth. That
usage puts real, if gentle, pressure on what the word is actually supposed to mean.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a good-natured but genuine disagreement between a
Zukelli resident who feels the word losing its connection to the actual city — objecting, gently, when they
hear "Zukellesi" applied to someone who's never set foot in Zukelli at all — and an outsider, or a resident
who's more relaxed about it, arguing that a word this widely recognized has earned the right to mean
something beyond strict residency, the same way other cities' own exported "type of person" descriptions
have traveled elsewhere in Tepenia. The resident's protectiveness is legitimate: the word means something
specific, earned through the city's own history. The more relaxed view is equally legitimate: language
this widely and warmly adopted is arguably a genuine compliment to the city, not a theft from it.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly an ordinary,
long-settled Zukelli resident, quietly proud of the city's specific identity and slightly protective of
what the demonym is supposed to mean. Character B is plausibly a visitor or a more cosmopolitan Zukelli
resident, someone with regular contact with how the word gets used elsewhere in Tepenia, more comfortable
with its broader adoption.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A workable distinction settles the disagreement without either side fully conceding: residents keep the
word's original, precise meaning alive among themselves — a Zukellesi, properly, is someone from Zukelli —
while accepting, without actively fighting it, that the word's informal, broader usage elsewhere is simply
outside their control and not worth a real fight to reverse.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

People elsewhere start using "Zukellesi" informally to describe anyone who carries that same warmth and
hospitality instinct, whether or not they were ever actually a resident — the broader usage spreads freely
outside the city, while inside it the word keeps its original, precise meaning fully intact.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because residents actively maintain the word's precise internal meaning even as its broader external usage
spreads unchecked, the two meanings coexist permanently side by side rather than the broader usage ever
fully replacing the original — Zukelli's own residents become the word's last reliable keepers of what it
actually, originally meant, a role they take on quietly rather than through any formal campaign.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Zukelli's own residents keep the word's precise distinction alive themselves, quietly proud that their
city's name became shorthand for a whole way of treating people, even as the word travels further than
most of them ever will.
