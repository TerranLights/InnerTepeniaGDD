## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Zukelli's hospitality culture is mature and increasingly famous — Elisa's Room known subnet-wide (chain
#4), "Zukellesi" spreading as shorthand for warmth itself (chain #10), constant informal traffic with
Janbogo across the bay (chains #6, #7). The population has grown steadily for generations, organically,
the same way everything else about the city grew.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Word of Zukelli's specific reputation — not just Antarctic life in general, but Zukelli's own particular
hospitality-centered warmth — starts drawing a genuinely new kind of arrival: people choosing the city
deliberately, for what it had specifically become, rather than simply ending up there by circumstance the
way most exile-era settlement happened.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The city commits to accommodating the new arrivals while deliberately protecting its hospitality-centered
core — new neighborhoods extending the same organic, need-driven growth pattern (chain #1) rather than
imposing anything foreign onto it, succeeding at growing without losing what it already was.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Zukelli's population climbs toward its highest point yet — not scene-setting, but the direct, traceable
result of people actively choosing to come, drawn specifically by a reputation the city earned and then
successfully protected even while growing because of it.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Zukelli becomes, demonstrably, a city succeeding completely at becoming more fully itself — new residents
absorbed into the same hospitality culture that drew them, the wave still arriving, the city still
growing into exactly what it was already becoming.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Zukelli continues growing, its hospitality-centered soul deliberately protected even as new arrivals
swell the population further — a city whose growth is a real, causal story of people choosing it
specifically, still very much underway, still very much arriving.
