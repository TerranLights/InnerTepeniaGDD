## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Mount Erebus's constant volcanic activity scatters material across the Hut Point Peninsula, useful raw
matter nobody's yet organized a way to actually collect — a resource sitting unclaimed between two
settlements that have already committed to very different economic paths.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Fort McMurdo's own industrial capacity is already stretched across its Dry Valleys processing operation
and doesn't need another collection job added to it — but the material itself is real and valuable enough
that someone eventually has to decide whether Scott, having chosen modesty over competing with its
neighbor (Suggestion #1), should now pursue an industry of its own, however small, or leave the
opportunity unclaimed to preserve its deliberately non-industrial character.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a conflict between a Scott resident who sees the
unclaimed volcanic material as a genuine, low-risk opportunity — one Fort McMurdo has effectively passed
on — and a resident invested in Scott's founding domestic character (perhaps directly shaped by Fiona
Māui's own vision, Suggestion #2), worried that any new industrial venture, however modest, risks
eroding the quiet, family-oriented identity the city deliberately chose. The opportunist isn't betraying
Scott's founding values; a small, contained operation doesn't have to mean abandoning modesty. The
traditionalist isn't reflexively anti-growth; they've simply watched enough founding commitments erode one
small exception at a time elsewhere in Tepenia to be wary of the first exception here.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a practically-minded
settler, perhaps with a materials-handling or logistics background, drawn to a genuine, useful opportunity
sitting unclaimed nearby. Character B is plausibly a civic figure invested in Scott's domestic character —
potentially someone who worked directly under Fiona Māui's early civic planning.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A carefully bounded compromise emerges: Scott develops a small, contained industry gathering the fallen
volcanic material and delivering it to a trucking facility across McMurdo Sound for onward processing —
explicitly scoped to stay modest, with no ambition to grow into anything resembling Fort McMurdo's scale,
satisfying the traditionalist's core concern while letting the opportunist's practical case go forward.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Scott gains a genuine, if modest, economic niche — gathering and delivering Erebus's fallen material —
distinct from Fort McMurdo's own industrial operations and requiring nothing like its scale, staffed by a
small dedicated crew rather than becoming a citywide economic pivot.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the operation stayed small and specifically scoped rather than expanding to fill available demand,
it becomes the template Scott later reaches for whenever a new opportunity presents itself — a working,
proven model of "real, useful, and deliberately unambitious" the city can point to directly, making it
easier to bound future opportunities (like the precision calibration niche of Suggestion #6) the same
deliberate way rather than relitigating the growth question each time from scratch.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Scott's Erebus-material collection stands as exactly the kind of niche the city's whole civic character
favors: real, useful, and deliberately unambitious — proof, now demonstrated rather than merely intended,
that Scott can pursue genuine opportunity without abandoning the modesty it chose.
