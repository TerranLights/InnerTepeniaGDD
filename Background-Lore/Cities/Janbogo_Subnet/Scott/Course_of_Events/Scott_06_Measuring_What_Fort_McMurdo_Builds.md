## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Fort McMurdo's industrial base grows fast, heavy, and increasingly complex, badly in need of precise
calibration and monitoring work its own operational focus doesn't prioritize — a genuine gap sitting right
beside a neighbor that has already proven, through the Erebus-material operation (Suggestion #3), it can
run a bounded, complementary economic niche without losing its founding modesty.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Duplicating Fort McMurdo's own heavy-industry approach would put Scott in direct, unwinnable competition
with its neighbor, repeating exactly the mistake the founding generation already rejected (Suggestion #1)
— but the calibration and monitoring gap is real, valuable, and growing, and some in Scott see it as a
genuine opportunity to build something more substantial than the small Erebus-material operation, pushing
the question of just how far Scott's "complementary niche" strategy should actually be allowed to scale.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a conflict between a Scott technical figure eager to
build a genuinely substantial calibration-and-monitoring economic sector — arguing the opportunity is
large enough that treating it as another small, contained niche wastes real potential — and a more
cautious civic voice insisting Scott stick to the same bounded, deliberately modest scale that's worked so
far, worried that "complementary, not competing" starts becoming a distinction without a difference once
the sector grows large enough. The ambitious figure isn't abandoning Scott's values; genuine expertise
this valuable arguably deserves real investment. The cautious voice isn't reflexively resistant to growth;
they're protecting a founding identity that's already proven fragile to erosion elsewhere in Tepenia when
one exception at a time goes unquestioned.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a technical specialist
or engineer, drawn to the genuine scale of opportunity Fort McMurdo's own growth is creating. Character B
is plausibly a civic figure invested in Scott's founding modesty commitment, perhaps directly descended
from or influenced by Fiona Māui's original vision.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The cautious voice's framing wins, but the ambitious figure's underlying case is genuinely honored:
Scott develops a real, substantial technical sector — precision equipment calibration, maintenance, and
environmental and geological monitoring, including a real share of the ongoing Erebus watch — deliberately
defined and marketed as complementary to Fort McMurdo's industry rather than competing with it, letting the
sector grow in depth of expertise without ever growing into a rival industrial identity.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Scott develops a genuinely complementary technical niche — precision, calibration, and monitoring — that
neither duplicates nor competes with Fort McMurdo's own established heavy industry, giving Scott a real,
substantial economic identity for the first time since its founding, without abandoning the deliberate
modesty that identity was built to protect.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because the sector's whole identity depends on staying defined *in relation to* Fort McMurdo's industry
rather than standing fully independent, Scott's own economic self-understanding becomes permanently
bound up with its neighbor's — a dependency the city accepts with open eyes, since the alternative (an
independent industrial identity) was the very outcome the founding generation deliberately chose against.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Scott settles into a role it's genuinely good at and genuinely needed for, even knowing it will never be
the more famous half of the partnership — its own economic identity permanently, and deliberately,
defined in relation to Fort McMurdo's rather than entirely on its own terms.
