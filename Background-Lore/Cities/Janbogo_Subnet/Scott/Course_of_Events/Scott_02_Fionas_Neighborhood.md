## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Having chosen modesty over competing with Fort McMurdo (Suggestion #1), Scott's earliest exile-era decades
could still easily drift toward whatever civic shape its inherited infrastructure happened to suggest —
modesty was chosen, but what modesty should actually look like day to day hasn't been decided.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A community organizer sees an opportunity to shape something more deliberate out of the founding
modesty commitment — a genuinely family-oriented residential community, not just a smaller, quieter
version of an industrial city defaulting to whatever infrastructure it happens to inherit. Pushing early
civic planning toward housing, leisure, and modest gathering spaces means actively de-prioritizing
economic ambition at a formative moment, and not every early settler is convinced that's the right
specific shape for the city's modesty to take.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The organizer's vision prevails as the dominant shape of early civic planning, but incorporates a real
concession to the economically-minded settler's concern: housing, leisure, and modest gathering spaces
take priority, but a deliberately small, sustainable domestic economic base gets planned in alongside
them rather than omitted entirely.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Early civic planning commits concretely to housing, leisure, and modest gathering spaces as Scott's
central priorities — prioritizing domestic life over economic ambition means Scott will never compete
with its neighbor on raw output, a tradeoff the community accepts openly rather than discovering later by
surprise.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because domestic life was prioritized deliberately, with the economic tradeoff named and accepted up
front rather than hidden, Scott's later generations grow up with a stable, unambiguous sense of what the
city is *for* — a genuine clarity of civic purpose that gives Scott's identity a durability the more
economically ambitious alternative might never have produced, since a compromised, half-domestic,
half-competitive city would have left every generation renegotiating the same argument from scratch.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Fiona's choices set the whole tone the city carries forward — a place defined by what it's like to live
there, not by what it produces, with the tradeoff against economic ambition understood and accepted as
the deliberate price of that identity.
