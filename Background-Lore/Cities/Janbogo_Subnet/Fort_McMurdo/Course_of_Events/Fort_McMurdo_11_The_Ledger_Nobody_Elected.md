## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Fort McMurdo's coordinators already decide shipment scheduling between competing national-scale projects
(chain #7) — a real, informal "shadow arbitration body." But that influence stops at scheduling. Actual
resource and credit allocation — who gets scarce material inputs, labor capacity, or processing time when
supply genuinely can't cover every demand — has no formal mechanism at all, handled ad hoc by whoever's
on shift.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

As coordination deepens, projects start needing more than scheduling priority — they need actual,
binding decisions about which gets scarce resources at all when supply falls short of total demand,
something Fort McMurdo's coordinators have been quietly deciding informally for years without anyone
naming it as a real function.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A formal resource-allocation framework gets established — transparent criteria, a real review process —
giving Fort McMurdo's existing informal financial influence genuine, accountable structure instead of
remaining an invisible, ad hoc practice nobody had to answer for.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Fort McMurdo becomes something closer to a genuine credit and resource clearinghouse for the Federation's
major projects — not just Calgary's decision-hub role, but Geneva's financial-coordination role too,
both halves of the Mega_Init's own real-world precedent now concretely true of the city at once.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Like chain #9's comms charter before it, the new framework turns out to be less a grant of new power and
more a belated confession of authority that was already real — Fort McMurdo's coordinators had been
making these calls quietly for years; the framework just finally puts a name and a process to what was
always actually happening.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Fort McMurdo holds a formal, accountable resource-allocation authority alongside its existing scheduling
role — the Geneva-style financial half of its own established real-world precedent now as concrete and
real as the Calgary-style coordination half already was.
