# Sayowa — Everyone Else's Prosperity
### Featuring [Character A] and [Character B] (placeholder figures — unnamed)

**Course of Events Suggestion #11**, translated from `Sayowa_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md` into
`z-template_-_city_histories_conflict_variant.md`. Grounded in the Mega_Init's own Djibouti City
parallel, called "perhaps the single sharpest real-world parallel" in the whole research pass — yet its
most specific, pointed detail (85% of Djibouti's own port throughput serves a *different* country's
economy, not its own) goes unused. Chains #1, #6, and #9 all frame Sayowa's outsized importance as
serving the whole continent generally; this suggestion names the sharper asymmetry directly — the
junction primarily builds up other cities' prosperity, not Sayowa's own. Character fields are
intentionally left blank — see each Conflict section's Design Notes for what kind of conflict, and what
kind of character(s), would make the following setting-condition become true.

---

## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Sayowa's junction identity is fully settled — small population, outsized structural importance, quiet
civic pride that never needs recognition (chains #6, #9). The pride has always stayed general: doing
something genuinely important without needing to be large to do it.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Someone actually works out, concretely, where the junction's traffic ends up benefiting — and the
numbers are stark: the overwhelming majority of what flows through Sayowa builds prosperity in Mawson,
Concordia, and the Halley subnet, while Sayowa itself stays exactly as modest as it's always been. The
city's own quiet pride suddenly has a harder, more specific shape to reckon with.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** not a conflict about whether the numbers are
real — both sides can see them. Character A feels the asymmetry should be addressed directly — Sayowa
deserves a real, material share of the prosperity it makes possible for everyone else, not just quiet
pride in facilitating it. Character B, closer to the neutral-coordinator ethos chains #4 and #8 already
establish, worries that pushing hard for a bigger cut risks the trusted, disinterested status that makes
the whole cooperative junction arrangement function in the first place — the moment Sayowa starts angling
for its own advantage, the other stakeholders' trust in its neutrality could genuinely erode.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly an economic-minded
resident or local administrator, not resentful, just newly clear-eyed about a real imbalance. Character B
is plausibly the junction coordinator role itself (echoing Wei-Lin, chain #8), protective of the delicate
trust the cooperative arrangement depends on.

---

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Sayowa doesn't fundamentally renegotiate its position, but does secure something modest and concrete —
proportionate, guaranteed local investment or preferential terms — without disrupting the neutral,
trusted coordination role that makes the junction work at all. The asymmetry gets named and slightly
softened, not eliminated.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Sayowa's civic pride deepens into something more clear-eyed — not simple contentment with modest
service, but a genuine, unromantic understanding that the arrangement is asymmetric, chosen anyway, with
its costs now actually named rather than quietly unexamined.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Knowing exactly what the city is owed, and choosing the service role anyway, becomes a quieter, more
mature version of chain #6's "small vessel, outsized cargo" pride — no longer simple modesty, but modesty
held with full awareness of its own real cost.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Sayowa continues its junction role, materially still modest despite building others' prosperity, but now
with the asymmetry named clearly and a small, real concession secured — pride grounded in full
awareness rather than simple, unexamined contentment.
