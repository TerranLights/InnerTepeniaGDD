ultimately prevailed held that the name's own gravity was exactly the kind of thing that lost meaning through

turned into civic ceremony for a population that would increasingly not share that specific inheritance. The
private, generational discussion that resulted is the same pattern the smaller Japanese-heritage community
still practices today, tracing directly back to that founding-era choice.
