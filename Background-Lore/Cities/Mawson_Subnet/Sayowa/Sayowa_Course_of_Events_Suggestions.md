# Sayowa — Candidate Courses of Events

**Scope note:** all ten chains below are set strictly *before* the Long Night War, during the Second
Interwar Period when Tepenia is a full, functioning country — no war damage, no post-war/present-day
material. 10 candidate But/Therefore chains built from Sayowa's Megasheet ("The Point Where Three
Roads Meet," the Shōwa-era name, the cooperative junction, the small-vessel-outsized-cargo civic
character). Post-settlement only; nothing here is canon.

---

## 1. The Point Where Three Roads Meet

Sayowa's earliest exile community settles on JARE's inherited infrastructure with modest ambitions,
never expecting to become anything more than a small research-descended town. **But** the city
happens to sit exactly where the Mawson subnet, the inland route to Concordia, and the Halley subnet
all physically converge. **Therefore** traffic, freight, and coordination accumulate here regardless
of the population's own size or ambition — the geography deciding Sayowa's importance long before its
residents do. **But** a city can't simply ignore that kind of accumulating responsibility once it
starts. **Therefore** Sayowa reorganizes around the junction deliberately, industrial and dispatch
infrastructure growing to match a role the city never set out to hold in the first place.

## 2. A Name Two Ways

"Sayowa" descends phonetically from Shōwa, the Japanese imperial era spanning war, defeat, and
reconstruction — a name carrying real historical weight for the community that chose it. **But** as
later Chinese immigration becomes demographic Primary, most residents have no personal connection to
that history at all. **Therefore** the name settles into two genuinely different meanings depending on
who's asked — an ordinary, unremarkable proper noun for the majority, and something discussed
generationally, with real felt gravity, within the smaller Japanese-heritage community that still
carries JARE's institutional memory. **But** neither group's relationship to the name is wrong.
**Therefore** Sayowa simply carries both meanings at once, permanently, without either one displacing
the other.

## 3. The Ice Road Season

Lützow-Holm Bay freezes solid enough for vehicle traffic in winter, and opens to icebreaker access in
summer — but a permanent bridge or causeway would face constant structural risk from the genuinely
shifting sea ice. **But** East Ongul Island still needs reliable mainland access year-round regardless
of which season it happens to be. **Therefore** Sayowa commits to a seasonal solution instead — an ice
road when the winter freeze allows it, maritime crossing when it doesn't — accepting that the city's
connection to the mainland will simply change character every year rather than staying fixed.
**But** that means real planning discipline around exactly when to switch between the two.
**Therefore** reading the ice correctly becomes one of Sayowa's own quietly essential civic skills,
practiced and refined generation after generation.

## 4. Cooperative by Necessity

As Sayowa's junction grows more critical to the whole region's freight and communications, multiple
parties — Mawson, the Concordia route, interests from the Halley subnet — all come to depend on it
running smoothly. **But** no single one of them actually controls it outright, and a fight over formal
control could easily disrupt the very function everyone depends on. **Therefore** the parties settle,
without any dramatic negotiation, into an informal cooperative arrangement — everyone maintaining
what they use, nobody claiming the whole. **But** informal arrangements only hold if every party keeps
choosing to honor them. **Therefore** the Sayowa Junction stays functional for generations not because
anyone forced a resolution, but because every stakeholder keeps finding it easier to cooperate than to
fight over something they'd all lose by breaking.

## 5. Kin Across the Corridor

Sayowa and Princess Elisabeth sit closer together than any other two cities on opposite sides of a
subnet boundary in Tepenia. **But** Sayowa carries Japanese institutional heritage without matching
demographic weight, while Princess Elisabeth's own Japanese population arrived through a completely
separate immigration current. **Therefore** individual Japanese-heritage families on both sides start
maintaining contact across the corridor anyway, drawn together by shared heritage even though neither
city's founding story quite matches the other's. **But** that contact stays personal and informal,
never becoming an official relationship between the two cities themselves. **Therefore** Sayowa and
Princess Elisabeth end up quietly connected by individual families long before any highway logistics
ever formalizes a relationship between the cities they belong to.

## 6. Small Vessel, Outsized Cargo

Sayowa's population stays modest, one of the smallest in the Mawson subnet, even as its junction role
grows more critical to the whole continent's connectivity. **But** residents could easily internalize
that gap as embarrassment — a city too small for the responsibility it's been handed. **Therefore**
the opposite happens instead: Sayowa's own civic character settles into quiet confidence about doing
something genuinely important without needing to be large to do it. **But** that confidence never
tips into self-importance, since the city's whole disposition runs toward service rather than
recognition. **Therefore** "small vessel, outsized cargo" becomes something residents understand about
themselves without ever needing to say it out loud.

## 7. Haruto's Ledger

JARE's original research tradition and the Shōwa-era naming history both risk fading as the founding
Japanese population dilutes generation after generation. **But** institutional memory doesn't
disappear just because the people who could carry it forward become a smaller share of the city.
**Therefore** a dedicated historian keeps deliberate records — the research tradition's own history,
what the name actually commemorates, who first settled here and why — specifically for the smaller
community still invested in it. **But** keeping that memory alive takes real, unglamorous, ongoing
effort nobody outside the tradition much notices. **Therefore** Haruto's own quiet, careful
recordkeeping becomes the reason Sayowa's institutional Japanese heritage survives as more than a name
nobody can explain.

## 8. Wei-Lin's Balancing Act

The Sayowa Junction's cooperative management works in principle, but somebody still has to handle the
actual day-to-day coordination between Mawson, the Concordia route, and Halley subnet interests.
**But** no single one of those parties can be trusted to run the junction fairly on everyone else's
behalf. **Therefore** a dedicated coordinator role emerges, answering to no single stakeholder,
responsible only for keeping the arrangement itself functioning. **But** a position with real
practical authority and no formal chain of command above it invites real pressure from every direction.
**Therefore** whoever holds the role has to earn ongoing trust continuously, since the cooperative
arrangement's whole stability depends on every party believing the coordinator isn't quietly favoring
somebody else.

## 9. Function Over Fanfare

Sayowa's economy splits cleanly into fabrication and trucking/dispatch on one side, ordinary
residential life on the other, with leisure culture present but visibly secondary. **But** a
population doing genuinely important work for the whole continent could easily start resenting how
little civic spectacle it gets in return. **Therefore** the city's own culture settles the question
before it becomes a real grievance — dutiful, practical service is simply what Sayowa does, without
needing recognition to feel worthwhile. **But** that quiet dutifulness could look, from outside, like
Sayowa simply not caring about its own importance. **Therefore** the city ends up genuinely proud of
work most of Tepenia never thinks to credit it for — competence without needing an audience.

## 10. The Diluted Founding

Japan's exile population settles Sayowa organically, without any diplomatic protection securing its
demographic position the way Shirayuki's founders later arrange for their own city. **But** the same
regional Chinese immigration current reshaping this whole corner of the continent reaches Sayowa too,
with nothing in place to slow it. **Therefore** Japanese demographic weight dilutes steadily,
generation after generation, until China holds clear Primary status and Japan sits diluted into
Significant tier alongside several other nations. **But** losing demographic weight doesn't mean losing
institutional heritage entirely. **Therefore** Sayowa ends up carrying real Japanese civic memory
without the population to match it — a quieter, less-protected version of the same story Shirayuki's
own founders would later look at and decide not to risk repeating.

## 11. Everyone Else's Prosperity

Someone works out concretely where Sayowa's junction traffic actually ends up benefiting — overwhelmingly
Mawson, Concordia, and the Halley subnet, while Sayowa itself stays exactly as modest as it's always
been. **But** a resident who wants that asymmetry addressed meets real resistance from the junction's own
coordinator, worried that pushing for a bigger cut risks the trusted neutrality the whole cooperative
arrangement depends on. **Therefore** a real disagreement opens over how much Sayowa is actually owed.
**But** the arrangement's stability depends on every stakeholder trusting Sayowa isn't angling for its own
advantage. **Therefore** the city secures something modest and proportionate instead of a full
renegotiation — the asymmetry named and slightly softened, not eliminated, Sayowa's own pride settling
into something more clear-eyed than simple contentment.

---

## Worth Your Attention

**#1 and #6** work as a pair — the accidental geography that made Sayowa matter, and the civic
temperament the city built around accepting that importance gracefully rather than resenting its own
modest size. **#4** is the most structurally distinctive thread of the ten, since it gives Sayowa's
own established contrast with Troll's fractured airfield conflict an actual originating shape — genuine
cooperation, chosen and maintained, rather than contested control that simply never happened to erupt.
**#11** uses the Mega_Init's own single most-praised real-world parallel to its fullest, sharpening
Sayowa's modest pride into something that has actually reckoned with its own real cost.
