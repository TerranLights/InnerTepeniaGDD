## The Transition Zhao Refused to Rush

**Founding Administrator "Warrick Zhao" *(placeholder, not developer-chosen)*** is credited with steering
Mawson's exile community through its earliest transition from inherited Australian Antarctic Division
operations to a genuine Tepenian civic institution — setting the precedent that hub function would always
matter more to Mawson's own self-image than any single national identity.

Zhao's own defining decision, per what's preserved of the founding-era record, was resisting pressure from
several founding-era factions who wanted the new civic institution modeled closely on the operational
structure inherited from the Australian Antarctic Division, arguing continuity would ease the transition.
Zhao's own counter-argument was that Mawson's own future depended on functioning as the wider subnet's actual
coordination hub, not on preserving any single inherited institutional identity — the founding administration
should be built around that hub function directly, drawing structure and practice from whatever worked, not
from loyalty to any one inherited model. The civic institution built under Zhao's own direction took
considerably longer to establish than a straightforward continuation would have, but set Mawson's own
founding precedent: hub function first, inherited identity secondary — a principle later generations
inherited without needing to relitigate it.

## The Hub Built to Talk to Neighbors First

Mawson's Arcanet hub function, connecting the wider subnet to the rest of Tepenia, was built alongside
genuine intra-subnet connections to Sayowa and Dome Fuji specifically — infrastructure serving both outward
and inward coordination from the very start of its own founding-era construction.

Founding-era planners, per what's preserved of the hub's own original design debate, faced a real choice about
where to prioritize initial construction investment — outward capacity toward the rest of Tepenia, or inward
capacity toward Mawson's own closer subnet neighbors. The planners who ultimately shaped the hub's own
construction sequence prioritized the intra-subnet connections first, reasoning that Sayowa's and Dome Fuji's
own practical, day-to-day coordination needs with Mawson mattered more immediately than outward connectivity a
still-developing settlement had less urgent use for yet. The outward-facing capacity was built out fully in
subsequent construction phases once the intra-subnet foundation was solidly established — a founding-era
sequencing choice, prioritizing near neighbors before the wider nation, that shaped the hub's own basic
architecture from its earliest construction onward.
