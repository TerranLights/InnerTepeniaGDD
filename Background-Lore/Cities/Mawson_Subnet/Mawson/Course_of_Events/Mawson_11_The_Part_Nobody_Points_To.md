## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Mawson's warm, hospitality-driven civic reputation is fully established and celebrated — genuine ease of
making friends, a deliberately cultivated honeymoon destination, a founding transition (chain #2)
remembered as a smooth, deliberate act of civic leadership. The warmth feels uncomplicated, close to
timeless.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

Someone researching the earliest exile-era records — for an anniversary observance, or simple personal
interest — finds that the actual transition from Australian Antarctic Division routine to full civic
life was genuinely harder than the smooth, celebratory version everyone now tells: real hardship, loss,
and conflict in the founding years, quietly absent from the city's own warm modern mythology.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** not a conflict about whether the harder history is
real — both sides can see the records. Character A wants it genuinely acknowledged, incorporated
honestly into how the city tells its own founding story, uneasy that presenting only the smooth version
does a disservice to what the earliest generation actually lived through. Character B, closer to the
hospitality and honeymoon industry the city has built so carefully, worries that surfacing this harder
history risks complicating a warm reputation decades in the making — not from dishonesty, but from real
concern about what the city stands to lose.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a civic archivist or
historian, sincere rather than provocative, motivated by accuracy rather than any wish to tarnish the
city's image. Character B is plausibly someone connected to Mawson's hospitality industry, protective of
something genuinely valuable rather than merely image-conscious.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

A middle path holds: the harder early history gets acknowledged honestly — a small memorial, an accurate
addendum available to anyone who actually looks for it — without becoming the city's dominant public
story or displacing the warmth that is, in the present, genuinely and currently true.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Mawson's own civic self-understanding gains a genuine third layer, distinct from chain #8's two
established kinds of civic prestige (ancient inherited continuity, modern earned reputation) — a
quieter, only partially examined founding-era layer underneath both.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

This becomes Mawson's own specific version of the Jeju parallel the Mega_Init flagged — a warm surface
with something real and difficult underneath, not erased, simply not loudly discussed, giving the city's
established warmth genuine depth rather than uncomplicated ease.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Mawson holds three layers of civic history at once — ancient continuity, modern earned warmth, and a
quieter, genuinely difficult founding-era layer acknowledged without being foregrounded — proof its
warmth, while entirely real, was never actually uncomplicated.
