## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

The earliest exile community operates, in practice, on operational habits carried into exile by former
AAD personnel among the founders — familiar, functional, and genuinely useful in the earliest, hardest
years of settling the reclaimed station, but not yet a genuine Tepenian civic institution in its own right.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A city can't keep organizing itself around one generation's personal memory of a foreign government's old
operational chain of command indefinitely — that knowledge dies with the people who carried it unless it's
deliberately converted into something the city owns for itself. A founding administrator figure begins
steering the community through a deliberate transition — those inherited AAD habits reshaped into something
that actually belongs to the people living there now — but doing that means letting go of some of the
founding identity's own institutional authority, and not everyone who built their standing on that personal
expertise is eager to see it go.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

The administrator's own choice sets a lasting precedent: Mawson's hub function, not any single national
identity, becomes what the city actually organizes itself around. The old-guard veteran's structure
doesn't survive intact, but the underlying competence it represented — coordination, dispatch,
reliability — gets carried forward as the new institution's actual substance, just no longer tied to
Australia's own chain of command.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because civic participation and standing are now tied explicitly to function — doing the hub's work
well — rather than to founding nationality, Mawson becomes structurally receptive to absorbing later
immigration waves into full civic participation on the same terms, rather than treating newcomers as
guests operating inside an Australian-derived order.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Generations later, when demographic weight shifts decisively and China becomes Mawson's demographic
Primary, the transition is absorbed through the exact same function-over-origin civic logic this
administrator's own choice established — meaning the city doesn't have to invent a new civic principle
from scratch to cope with that later, much larger demographic change. It already has one, tested and
working, from its own founding era.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Mawson's civic identity is now formally organized around hub function rather than founding nationality
— a precedent set by one administrator's early, costly choice that will quietly define how the city
absorbs every demographic change still to come.
