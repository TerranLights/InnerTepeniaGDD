## State of Affairs 1
###### The city as it stands before this cycle's turning point.

Immigration currents bring genuinely diverse populations to Mawson — Chinese, Japanese, South Korean,
Indonesian, alongside the founding Australian wave — layering the city's demographics more than most of
its Mawson subnet neighbors.

---

## Trigger
###### What disrupts the above baseline.

A city this demographically layered could easily default to polite coexistence without any real warmth
behind it. Civic leadership starts treating hospitality itself as something worth actively cultivating,
not something that simply follows from having lots of different people in one place. But building a
genuine culture of welcome takes sustained effort across generations, not one policy or one leader's
initiative — and the first attempts to formalize it don't all land the same way.

**Design notes — what kind of conflict this needs:** a disagreement between a civic leader pushing a
specific, structured program to cultivate warmth across communities — organized events, deliberate
cross-community contact, an explicit institutional push — and a skeptic, possibly from an older
generation or a specific heritage community, who worries that mandated warmth is artificial and even
faintly patronizing to the communities it's meant to welcome, and that real friendliness can't be
manufactured by policy. A's instinct is right that leaving it to chance risks only polite distance; B's
skepticism is right that a heavy-handed program can curdle into performance.

**Design notes — what kind of character(s) this needs:** Character A is plausibly a civic leader,
possibly from the founding wave or an early-arriving community, genuinely committed to making Mawson's
plurality feel like more than coexistence. Character B is plausibly a resident from one of the newer
immigration currents, wary of top-down efforts to engineer community feeling and more interested in
whether the city's institutions actually change day to day than in what any single program declares.

## Culmination
###### The conflict's actual outcome — specific and concrete.

Neither the flashy program nor pure skepticism wins outright. What actually works is sustained, humble,
unglamorous effort across generations rather than any single initiative — and Mawson's famous ease of
making friends becomes a real, earned civic achievement, deliberately built alongside its demographic
plurality rather than an automatic byproduct of it.

---

## 2nd-Order Change
###### The direct, immediate structural consequence of *this specific outcome*.

Because sustained, unglamorous effort is what actually worked, later civic leaders inherit a specific
institutional lesson: hospitality initiatives get judged by whether they're built to outlast the leader
who proposed them, not by how impressive they look at launch — filtering out one-off spectacle in favor
of habit.

---

## 3rd-Order Change
###### The downstream consequence of the 2nd-order change — caused by *it*, not by the original conflict.

Because that "built to outlast, not to impress" filter becomes the actual institutional standard,
Mawson accumulates, generation over generation, a body of quietly durable civic customs — small,
low-key practices rather than grand monuments or declared holidays — leaving the city's civic character
defined by an accretion of small durable habits instead of a handful of celebrated landmark policies.

---

## State of Affairs 2
###### The new baseline, feeding the next cycle.

Mawson's hospitality culture is now made of many small, quietly durable habits accumulated across
generations, deliberately filtered to outlast whoever first proposed them — built the same patient way
the city's institutional memory itself was originally built.
