# Mawson Subnet — But/Therefore Enhancement Opportunities

**Part of the City History Enhancement Opportunities pass** — see
`Cities/City_History_Enhancement_Opportunities_Tracker.md` for the overall purpose, per-city method, and
progress index across all subnets. This file holds the Mawson subnet's own flagged ideas only.

**Scope note, carried over from every city's own Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md:** all flagged ideas
below are meant for *pre*-Long Night War, Second Interwar Period content only — no war damage, no
post-war material. Flagged ideas that would require war-era framing are out of scope for this pass.

---

### Dome Fuji

**Files read:** `Dome_Fuji_Catalog.md`, `Dome_Fuji_Mega_Init.md`, `Dome_Fuji_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`
(all 10 existing chains). First Mawson subnet city. Dome Fuji has zero human residents; every finding
tracks robot heritage-inheritance rather than lived human culture, the same framing established at
Kunlun and Vostok.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** the founding researchers' altitude-adaptation culture (#1), the
slow, undramatic depopulation ending in the last transfer request (#2), the pilgrim expedition finding
the station preserved rather than ruined (#3), surveying and building Nyakkyo at the dome's geometric
high point (#4), the robes debate resolving into three layered, coexisting explanations (#5), the
"Deepest Cold" observance timed to polar night's coldest point (#6), the complementary Kunlun/Dome Fuji
theological doctrine ("reaching" vs. "arriving") (#7), the ice-core archive being tended rather than
actively studied (#8), the maintenance robot who never left bridging both eras (#9), and stewardship
falling informally to whoever has stayed longest (#10). New angles below are ones the existing 10 don't
touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** The Synthesis Notes note that USA, France, Germany, UK, and
   Russia all carry "the secondary, explicitly distant-second research-town dimension" — but chain #8
   treats the archive's tending generically, citywide, never through any specific population's own research
   tradition. A chain about one population's own real-world research heritage (Russia's Akademgorodok-
   echoing devotees, say) shaping the specific *methodology* of how the archive gets preserved — distinct
   from chain #8's generic "cared for like an old text" framing — would use unused population-specific
   research texture.

2. **Resident character cultures.** Japan and South Korea each hold "genuine, direct Buddhist-pilgrimage
   City-Type anchors" (Mount Kōya, Haeinsa Temple) distinct from China's own Tibetan-plateau-plus-Wutai/
   Emei anchor — three separate real-world Buddhist traditions. None of the 10 chains differentiate them;
   the theology chains (#4-#7) all treat devotional practice as uniform. A chain about these three distinct
   traditions each contributing a different specific theological or ritual element to Ice Cold Buddhism's
   actual practice — rather than one undifferentiated devotional culture — would use unused population-
   specific religious texture.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Mega_Init explicitly flags Paro Taktsang's real 1998 fire and
   subsequent royally-championed rebuilding as "worth adopting directly... if the developer wants one," and
   separately lists "whether a Taktsang-style disaster-and-rebuilding beat gets added to Dome Fuji's own
   history" as still open. None of the 10 chains use this. A chain about a specific disaster at Nyakkyo and
   its rebuilding becoming its own devotional story would use this explicitly flagged, still-open, directly
   invited addition.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** Chain #3 covers only the one-time discovery
   expedition's crossing; none of the 10 chains address the interior plateau crossing as an *ongoing*
   ritual challenge for later generations of individual devotees making the pilgrimage after the site was
   already known and established. A chain about the crossing itself becoming a ritualized rite of passage
   for later pilgrims — using "the crossing is the filter" theme the Mega_Init names explicitly — would use
   this established idea in its unused, recurring (not one-time) form.

5. **Other.** The City Snapshot notes Dome Fuji "has the widest Notable-tier spread of any Tepenian
   city" — 24 additional nations beyond its 11 Primary/Significant ones. None of the 10 chains touch this
   sheer demographic breadth at all; every chain treats the devotee population as a uniform whole. A chain
   about that breadth itself becoming a specific point of theological reflection or pride — a moment where
   devotees remark on just how many different national backgrounds converge here, treated as proof the
   faith genuinely transcends nationality — would use this established but unused demographic superlative.

### Mawson

**Files read:** `Mawson_Catalog.md`, `Mawson_Mega_Init.md`, `Mawson_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`
(all 10 existing chains). Second Mawson subnet city, sharing its name with the subnet.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** continuous institutional memory carried forward since 1954 (#1),
Founding Administrator Warrick Zhao's transition from Australian Antarctic Division routine to a genuine
civic institution (#2), Hostess Mei-Ling Sorensen's deliberate hospitality-infrastructure proposal (#3),
the Prince Charles Mountains Overlook honeymoon tradition (#4), keeping St. Douglas veneration and the
old Territory claim alive as honored history rather than living authority (#5), civic leadership
deliberately cultivating hospitality across a demographically diverse population (#6), Mawson balancing
hub function and hospitality as two equally real priorities (#7), the city holding two distinct kinds of
civic prestige (ancient continuity and modern earned reputation) without picking a winner (#8), "The
Name That Outlasted the Founders" as genuine, open peace rather than unresolved tension (#9), and
hospitality workers and hub-logistics workers as two barely-overlapping halves of the same city (#10).
New angles below are ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** None of the 10 chains address Mawson's specific technical/
   administrative history as the Mawson subnet's Arcanet communications hub, distinct from its highway/
   logistics hub role; chain #10 mentions "Arcanet traffic" only in passing alongside freight dispatch. A
   chain about how Mawson specifically became the subnet's Arcanet nexus — a distinct technical function
   from the highway-junction role chains #1, #7, and #10 already cover — would use an unaddressed civic
   function.

2. **Resident character cultures.** Every one of Mawson's eight populations anchors to a genuinely
   different real-world honeymoon-resort register — Sanya and Bali (beach), Baden-Baden and Bath (spa/
   wellness), Atami and Jeju (classic resort-town), Deauville and the Gold Coast (romantic getaway) — but
   none of the 10 chains differentiate these registers; the city's hospitality culture reads as one
   undifferentiated whole. A chain about Mawson developing distinct honeymoon venues or "packages" echoing
   these genuinely different resort traditions — spa-focused versus beach-focused versus scenic-getaway —
   would use unused population-specific resort-type texture.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Mega_Init's own "New, surfaced by this research pass" section
   explicitly asks whether Mawson's warm reputation "sits atop any unacknowledged historical weight of its
   own, echoing Jeju's real layered history" — its suppressed 1948 uprising. Chain #8 pairs two kinds of
   civic prestige, and chain #9 frames the founder/present gap as *openly acknowledged* peace — the
   opposite of anything suppressed. None of the 10 chains use the "unacknowledged" half of the Jeju
   parallel. A chain about some quieter, genuinely unexamined layer beneath Mawson's warm surface — distinct
   from chain #9's deliberately open peace — would use this explicitly flagged, still-unused half of an
   already-cited precedent.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** The Prince Charles Mountains appear only as
   scenery for the Overlook honeymoon tradition (#4); no chain addresses any other use of the range —
   research, resource extraction, or recreational activity distinct from the honeymoon-viewing function. A
   chain about a secondary use of the Prince Charles Mountains would use the geography for something beyond
   its already-used scenic role.

5. **Other.** The Mega_Init explicitly notes Australia's founding-wave retention at Significant tier
   follows "the same pattern already established for Rothera's UK, Sejong's South Korea, and Juan Carlos's
   Spain" — a named cross-city comparison. None of the 10 chains draw this comparison directly; chain #9
   covers the founder/present demographic gap only in the abstract, without addressing the specific
   mechanism that kept Australia at Significant tier rather than diluting further. A chain about that
   specific retention mechanism — echoing the same pattern at three other named cities — would use this
   explicitly flagged but undramatized comparison.

### Sayowa

**Files read:** `Sayowa_Catalog.md`, `Sayowa_Mega_Init.md`, `Sayowa_Course_of_Events_Suggestions.md`
(all 10 existing chains). Third and final Mawson subnet city — the subnet is now fully complete.

**Existing 10 chains already cover:** geography deciding Sayowa's importance regardless of ambition (#1),
the name "Sayowa" carrying two genuinely different meanings depending on heritage (#2), the seasonal
ice-road/maritime crossing solution (#3), the informal cooperative junction-management arrangement among
stakeholders (#4), quiet cross-corridor contact between Japanese-heritage families at Sayowa and Princess
Elisabeth (#5), "small vessel, outsized cargo" civic confidence (#6), Haruto's historian recordkeeping
preserving Japanese institutional heritage (#7), Wei-Lin's coordinator role balancing multiple
stakeholders (#8), function-over-fanfare civic pride (#9), and Japan's organic, undiplomatically-
protected founding-nation dilution (#10). New angles below are ones the existing 10 don't touch.

1. **Division of industry → history.** The Catalog establishes "a major fabrication industry paired with
   a huge trucking and dispatch operation... things get made as well as routed here," but all 10 existing
   chains focus entirely on the routing/junction half, never the fabrication half. The Mega_Init also
   flags its own Kryvyi Rih pick as "carried forward from earlier work on this file, without an annotation
   on file" — an acknowledged, unresolved research gap. A chain about what Sayowa's own fabrication
   industry actually produces, grounded by finally researching and applying the orphaned Kryvyi Rih
   precedent (a real Ukrainian mining/industrial city, with Ukraine present at Sayowa's own Notable tier),
   would fill both an economic gap and a flagged research gap at once.

2. **Resident character cultures.** France (Perpignan's cross-border consolidation), Germany (Duisburg's
   inland-junction culture), and UK (Felixstowe's small-population-outsized-weight identity) each anchor
   to a genuinely distinct logistics philosophy — but chains #4 and #8 both treat the junction's multi-
   stakeholder management generically, never crediting any specific population's own approach. A chain
   about these three populations' distinct logistics traditions actually informing different aspects of how
   the junction gets run would use unused population-specific texture.

3. **Real-world historical precedent.** The Djibouti City parallel is explicitly called "perhaps the
   single sharpest real-world parallel" found in this whole research pass, but its most specific detail —
   85% of Djibouti's own port throughput serves a *different* country's economy (Ethiopia), not its own —
   goes unused. Chains #1, #6, and #9 all frame Sayowa's outsized importance as serving the whole
   continent generally, never addressing the sharper asymmetry that the junction primarily builds up other
   cities' prosperity rather than Sayowa's own. A chain about residents grappling with that specific
   asymmetry would use the unused, most pointed half of an already-heavily-cited precedent.

4. **City personality / geography / geology → history.** None of the 10 chains address East Ongul
   Island's own physical size or land constraints as anything beyond a factor in the seasonal-crossing
   chain (#3). A chain about the island's own limited buildable land directly shaping how the established
   industrial-versus-residential split got physically laid out — distinct from #3's crossing-focused
   framing — would ground an unused geographic constraint in something concrete.

5. **Other.** None of the 10 chains address the practical strain of running such a structurally critical
   junction with "one of the smallest Mawson subnet cities" by population — chain #6 treats the size gap
   only as a matter of civic pride and confidence, never as an operational challenge. A chain about where
   Sayowa's own specialist labor actually comes from, given how few residents there are to staff work this
   consequential, would ground chain #6's sentiment in a genuine logistical problem.
