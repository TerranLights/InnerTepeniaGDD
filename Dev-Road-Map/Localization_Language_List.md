# Localization — Target Language List

**Status:** Draft, established 2026-07-03. Needs budget/scope confirmation before
treated as final — see Open Questions at the bottom.

---

## Approach

Two factors went into this list: **market ROI** (standard industry-priority
languages for a Steam-first CRPG) and **thematic resonance** (languages tied to
nations heavily represented in Tepenia's own founding populations, per
`Official_Population_Census.md` and `Upper_Earth_Immigration_Composition.md`).
Where both point the same direction, that language is an easy priority call.

Full simultaneous localization into every tier below is unlikely to be realistic
for a solo dev at launch — this list is meant to be worked top-down, not
all-at-once. See Open Questions.

---

## Tier 1 — Launch Priority

High Steam market share, strong PC/CRPG purchasing patterns, and/or deep thematic
ties to major Tepenian founding nations.

| Language | Why |
|---|---|
| **English** | Base/source language |
| **Simplified Chinese** | Largest Steam player base by most estimates; standard top priority for any Steam release. Also the single most common Primary-tier founding nation across Tepenian cities — China appears as Primary or co-Primary in nearly every Ross Sea/East Antarctic city documented so far. |
| **Spanish** | Large market; deep thematic ties — Belgrano and Esperanza (Argentina), Juan Carlos (Spain), Marambio (Argentina) are all founding-Spanish-speaking cities, and Belgrano is a major DLC 5 location. |
| **Russian** | Large PC/Steam market with strong CRPG-genre affinity; thematic ties — Mirny (subnet hub) and Vostok are Russian-founded. *(Corrected 2026-07-13 — previously also listed Soyuz here; that city (files: Soyuz) is singularly Korean-founded via the Jeju-do court, despite sitting on the real-world Russian-built Progress Station — station operator is a GPS fact only, not a founding claim. See `Specs/Soyuz.md`.)* |
| **German** | Large, high-purchasing-power PC market; thematic tie — Neumayer. |
| **Japanese** | Strong RPG-genre audience; thematic ties — Sayowa and Shirayuki are both Japanese-founded (Shirayuki genuinely Japan-Primary today, 36.27%), and Dome Fuji is religiously/narratively significant as one of Tepenia's two "Ice-Cold Buddhism" pilgrimage sites. *(Corrected 2026-07-13 — previously credited Sayowa itself with pilgrimage/religious significance; that's Dome Fuji's and Kunlun's established identity, not Sayowa's — Sayowa's own civic identity is a highway-junction/logistics city with no religious content in its own files. Sayowa is also no longer Tepenia's primary Japanese demographic presence — Japan is diluted to 2.71% there — so Shirayuki is the stronger genuine Japanese thematic tie.)* |
| **Brazilian Portuguese** | Large, fast-growing Steam market. |
| **French** | Large market; thematic tie — Dumont d'Urville, and French/Italian co-founding of Concordia itself, the primary game setting. |

## Tier 2 — Strong Secondary Candidates

Large markets or thematic ties strong enough to prioritize once Tier 1 is covered.

| Language | Why |
|---|---|
| **Korean** | Strong PC/RPG market; **the single strongest thematic tie in the game** — Janbogo and {{currently-unnamed Korean city}} (files: Soyuz) are Tepenia's two living Korean demographic centers (Janbogo one of the most developed cities in the GDD; the Mirny-subnet city genuinely Korean-Primary, 34.62%, via the Jeju-do court). Sejong shares a founding-era Korean naming heritage with them but is not a current Korean demographic tie — its own Korean population has diluted to 5.79%, its smallest Significant-tier nation, behind the USA's 21.65% Primary. *(Corrected 2026-07-13 — previously listed only Janbogo and Sejong, omitting {{currently-unnamed Korean city}} entirely and overstating Sejong's present-day relevance; see `Specs/Sejong.md`.)* |
| **Italian** | Thematic tie — Zukelli (Mario Zucchelli Station) and Italian co-founding of Concordia. |
| **Polish** | Smaller market by size, but CRPG-genre players are disproportionately represented there (CD Projekt's home market) — good ROI-per-capita for this specific genre. |
| **Traditional Chinese** | Taiwan/Hong Kong market, distinct localization from Simplified Chinese. |

## Tier 3 — Thematically Notable, Smaller Markets

Not standard high-ROI localization targets on their own, but worth flagging given
how much the game's own setting draws on these nations. Realistic candidates for
post-launch or community localization rather than day-one.

| Language | Why |
|---|---|
| **Indonesian** | Not a traditional PC/Steam priority market (mobile-first), but worth noting: Indonesia shows up as a Significant or Notable-tier nation in an unusually large number of Tepenian cities across the Ross Sea, Mirny, and Mawson subnets — a genuine, if unplanned, thematic pattern in the census data. |
| **Turkish** | Common indie/mid-size Steam localization target independent of thematic ties; regional pricing market. |
| **Ukrainian** | Market and thematic consideration. |
| **Norwegian / Finnish** | Thematic ties (Troll/Norway, Abowasa/Finland) but small markets with very high English fluency — commonly skipped even by studios that do localize those regions' *content*. |

## Open Questions

- **Realistic launch scope for a solo dev.** Full Tier 1 (8 languages including
  English) is a substantial localization budget/effort even before voice acting is
  considered. Needs an honest scope decision: full text-only localization for
  Tier 1, a smaller subset, or English-only at launch with post-launch expansion?
- **Voice acting scope**, if any — localization cost is dramatically different for
  text-only vs. full VO, and affects which languages are realistic at all.
- **Community/fan translation policy** — many indie CRPGs (including genre
  touchstones like the classic Fallout games) rely partly on community-driven
  translation for lower-tier languages. Worth deciding whether Inner Tepenia
  supports that officially (tooling, moddable string files) even if it can't fund
  professional localization for every language on this list.
- **Priority order within Tier 1** if not all 8 can happen simultaneously.
