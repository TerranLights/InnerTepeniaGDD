for progress.**

### 8a. ⚠ THE RESEARCH-LOG COLUMN IS A FORWARD REQUIREMENT, NOT A DEBT — corrected 2026-09-04

**`Step 3.7`: *"Log every search in this location's own research log, AS YOU RUN IT."*** **Not afterward.**
*(`Step 9.5` rule 1: "Write it when it happens. Precision decays; a snag logged hours later has lost the
file, the wording, and the reason it mattered.")*

> ## ⛔ SO THE 32 CITIES WITHOUT A LOG ARE NOT 32 ITEMS TO WRITE.
> ***Their culture material was written without one. The research that fed those passes is gone.***
> **A log written now would be a RECONSTRUCTION — someone recalling what they think was searched — which is
> exactly the thing the log exists to make unnecessary.** ***`04` Part IV would call that a self-graded
> record.*** **You cannot honestly backfill a research log.**

**What the column therefore means:**

| | |
|---|---|
| ✅ **A city we run from here** | **gets a log, written LIVE during the pass, appended never rewritten** — Step 3, and again at Phase 10 §B when research runs a second time |
| ⛔ **A city with pre-ULM material and no log** | **its provenance is unrecoverable.** *Not a task. Not a gap to close. A fact about what already happened* |
| ⭐ **The practical effect** | **it resolves itself** — those passes are being redone anyway, since pre-ULM material is read-last-as-a-check, not treated as complete |

**So the count to watch is not "5 of 37." It is: *did every city we ran get one?*** **Currently: 1 of 1
(Mawson).**
