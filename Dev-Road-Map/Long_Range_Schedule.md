**Set by the developer, 2026-11-17.** It replaces the 2026-10-21 plan of a vignette round after each 38-city
wave. The developer: *"I think that my original thinking and idea was wrong."* Durations are the developer's
estimates, not commitments. LAW 0 governs every stage: there is no credit for finishing quickly.

## Step 6: the four vignette meta-rounds

Each round is built **purely and entirely from one stage's data and results**, never from the others.

| Meta-round | Source, and only that source | Per city | Across 38 cities |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ULM | 12 to 36 | ~456 to 1,368 |
| 2 | DCI | 90 to 180 | ~3,420 to 6,840 |
| 3 | CST | 60 to 90 | ~2,280 to 3,420 |
| 4 | RWBEM | 30 to 90 | ~1,140 to 3,420 |

*"Each 'meta-round' of vignettes will use the Fable model. This will be the most (extremely) resource-heavy and
token-expensive. However, it will produce the best-quality results."*

## Step 7: the review

For each vignette, one at a time, together:
- **(A)** confirm as written;
- **(B)** make the necessary adjustments; or
- **(C)** discard.

---

## Standing rules this schedule carries

- **The old historical vignettes** (`Background-Lore/`) stay **not canon**, unread as inputs and unedited, until
  the new vignettes exist. Only then are they checked, since *"any number of those vignettes might well still
  be perfectly valid."*
- **The Generation 1 megasheets are archived, never edited.** Generation 2 is new files, built by the method
  approved 2026-11-10: derive in the component files, then assemble.

## Answered by the developer, 2026-11-17

1. **The terminal differentiation check** (the single cross-city comparison deferred out of every per-city
   pass, 2026-09-06): *"That actually is a good point worth considering. I suppose we can address the matter
   once the time comes."* **Not yet placed.** It must be placed before the Generation 2 megasheets, which
   depend on it.
2. **The DCI round's unit, per city.** A city's DCI vignettes draw on the **full DCI results of every culture
   present in that city at 5% or more of its municipal population**.
   - **Why:** a culture at that share is significant enough to influence the city's local culture. The
     developer: *"I just want to make sure that the reasoning process is clear."*
   - *Orchestrator's reading, not the developer's statement:* the number of cultures at 5% or more varies by
     city, which may be part of why the DCI round's range (90 to 180) is so wide.
3. **The DCI and the city runs can overlap, without a guarantee.** The DCI process for one city *may* run
   while a ULM run is under way on another city. It is possible, not promised, so the schedule does not count
   on it.
4. **The old historical vignettes get their own check, once the new vignettes are complete.**
   - Each old vignette is judged individually against that city's newer information. Some may be kept as
     they are, some edited and adjusted, some discarded.
   - Until then, the standing rule holds: not canon, never read as an input, never edited.
   - *"We'll just have to see how each of them individually hold up within the context of the
     newer-resulting information of each city."*
