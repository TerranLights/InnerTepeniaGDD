Cyberpunk 2077 (1.63/pre-2.0 era) — chosen perk trees, all 12 skills across 5 Attributes
Source: gamestegy.com's archived "-old" (Patch 1.63) build guides, retrieved 2026-08-02
(developer-located source — the live web had otherwise been entirely overwritten to
reflect the 2.0+ system, and web.archive.org was inaccessible; this site preserves
dedicated pre-2.0 build-guide pages per weapon/playstyle archetype, e.g.
gamestegy.com/post/cyberpunk-2077/897/engineer-builds-old).

NOTE: extracted from individual build-guide pages, each centered on a specific weapon
type or playstyle — so each tree below reflects that build's own coverage of its skill,
not necessarily a datamined 100%-complete tree. Perk names marked "x2"/"x3" in the source
tables denote multi-rank perks (2 or 3 ranks). This is DISTINCT from the already-captured
1.63 skill LEVEL rewards (cp2077-1.63-skill-level-rewards.txt, the automatic 1-20 ladder)
and from the 2.0-era material (cp2077-2.0-skill-rank-bonuses.txt,
cp2077-2.0-perk-trees.txt) — this file covers only the 1.63-era CHOSEN perk trees.

## BODY ATTRIBUTE

### Athletics
(Source: King Kong — Gorilla Arms Melee Build)
- **Regeneration** (Lv1) — Regenerate health when in combat.
- **Wolverine** (Lv7) — Health regenerates faster during combat.
- **Steel and Chrome** (Lv9) — More melee damage (Gorilla Arms-focused).
- **Invincible** (Lv12) — Increases max HP.
- **Dog of War** (Lv16) — More health regeneration during combat.
- **Cardio Cure** (Lv16) — More health regeneration while moving.
- **Steel Shell** (Lv18) — More armor.
- **Indestructible** (Lv20) — Reduce all damage by 10%.
- **The Rock** (Lv20) — Immune to being knocked down by any attack/damage type.

### Annihilation
(Source: Shotgun Build)
- **Hail of Bullets x3** (Lv1) — Shotguns deal +10% damage.
- **Pump It, Louder!** (Lv5) — -20% Shotgun recoil.
- **In Your Face** (Lv5) — -30% Shotgun reload time.
- **Dead Center x2** (Lv7) — +20% Shotgun torso damage.
- **Bloodrush x2** (Lv7) — +10% movement speed in combat while carrying a Shotgun.
- **Bulldozer** (Lv9) — +5% Shotgun Crit Chance.
- **Mongoose** (Lv9) — +25% Mitigation Chance while reloading Shotguns.
- **Momentum Shift x3** (Lv11) — Shotgun/LMG kill: +20% movement speed for 10 sec.
- **Massacre x3** (Lv11) — +30% Shotgun Crit Damage.
- **Heavy Lead** (Lv12) — Shotguns knock back enemies with more force.
- **Skeet Shooter** (Lv12) — +15% Shotgun damage vs. moving targets.
- **Manic** (Lv14) — Entering combat with a Shotgun: +20% movement speed for 10 sec.

- **Speed Demon** (Lv16) — More damage the faster you're moving.
- **Poppin' Off x2** (Lv18) — +50% Shotgun dismemberment chance.
- **Hit the Deck x2** (Lv18) — +20% Shotgun damage vs. staggered/knocked-down enemies.
- **Biathlete** (Lv20) — Shotgun spread doesn't increase while moving.
- **Bloodbath** (Lv20) — Shotgun/LMG dismemberment: -30% weapon recoil for 6 sec (+1%/perk level).

- **Unshakable** (Lv18) — Restore HP and Stamina on hitting Stunned enemies.
- **Guerilla** (Lv20) — +60% Critical Damage for 10 sec.

## INTELLIGENCE ATTRIBUTE

### Breach Protocol
(Source: Netrunner Build)
- **Big Sleep** (Lv1) — Unlocks Big Sleep daemon: disables all network cameras for 3 min.
- **Mass Vulnerability** (Lv1) — Unlocks daemon: -30% Physical Resistance, network-wide, 3 min.
- **Almost In!** (Lv5) — +20% Breach Protocol time.
- **Mass Vulnerability: Resistances** (Lv7) — Upgrades Mass Vulnerability to -30% all Resistances.
- **Total Recall** (Lv11) — ICEpick daemon: -1 additional RAM cost for quickhacks.
- **Turret Tamer** (Lv12) — Unlocks daemon: sets all network turrets friendly for 3/6 min.
- **Efficiency x2** (Lv14) — 3+ daemons in one Breach: +6 RAM recovery/60 sec for 5 min.
- **Cloud Cache x2** (Lv14) — Completing Breach: -2 RAM cost on next quickhack per daemon uploaded.
- **Mass Vulnerability: Quickhacks** (Lv16) — Upgrades Mass Vulnerability: +30% quickhack damage taken.
- **Hackathon** (Lv18) — 3+ daemons in one Breach: -33% quickhack cooldowns for 5 min.
- **Head Start** (Lv18) — Auto-uploads the first daemon at Breach start.
- **Compression** (Lv20) — -1 daemon sequence length (floor of 2).
- **Buffer Optimization** (Lv20) — +100% daemon effect duration.

### Quickhacking
(Source: Netrunner Build)
- **Biosynergy x3** (Lv1) — RAM recovers in combat: +12 units/60 sec.
- **Bloodware x3** (Lv1) — +30% quickhack damage.
- **Forget-me-not** (Lv5) — Eliminating a quickhacked target: +1 RAM instantly.
- **Hacker's Manual** (Lv5) — Unlocks crafting specs for Uncommon quickhacks.
- **Daisy Chain x3** (Lv7) — Eliminating a quickhacked target: -50% cooldown on all other active quickhacks.
- **Signal Support x2** (Lv9) — +50% quickhack duration.
- **Subliminal Message x2** (Lv11) — +100% quickhack damage to unaware targets.
- **Mnemonic** (Lv12) — 2× quickhack spread distance.
- **Diffusion** (Lv12) — -2 RAM cost on already-quickhacked enemies.
- **School of Hard Hacks** (Lv12) — Unlocks crafting specs for Rare quickhacks.
- **Plague x3** (Lv14) — Spreading quickhacks jump to 3 additional targets.
- **Critical Error** (Lv16) — Quickhacks can Crit (based on Crit Chance/Damage stats).
- **Hacker Overlord** (Lv16) — Unlocks crafting specs for Epic quickhacks.
- **Anamnesis x3** (Lv18) — Cyberdeck RAM cannot drop below 4.
- **Bartmoss' Legacy** (Lv20) — Unlocks crafting specs for Legendary quickhacks.
- **Optimization** (Lv20) — -1 RAM cost for quickhacks.
- **Master RAM Liberator** (Lv20) — +50% RAM recovery rate (+1%/perk level).

## COOL ATTRIBUTE

### Stealth ("Ninjutsu" tree)
(Source: Best Ninja Builds — Stealth Assassin)
- **Crouching Tiger** (Lv1) — +20% movement speed while sneaking.
- **Dagger Dealer** (Lv5) — Enables knife throwing (equipped knife).
- **Assassin** (Lv7) — +15% damage to humans.
- **Strike from the Shadows** (Lv7) — +7% Crit Chance while sneaking.
- **Leg Up** (Lv7) — Optional takedown enhancement.
- **Sniper** (Lv9) — +20% headshot damage outside combat.
- **Cutthroat** (Lv12) — Bonus critical damage after knife headshots.
- **From the Shadows** (Lv12) — +20% Crit Chance for the first 7 sec (of combat/detection, per source).
- **Ghost** (Lv12) — +40% detection time before an enemy spots you.
- **Venomous Fangs** (Lv14) — All knives apply Poison.
- **Looking Sharp** (Lv14) — Blinds targets if a knife headshot doesn't kill.
- **Juggler** (Lv16) — Instantly restores a thrown knife on enemy death or Crit.
- **Hasty Retreat** (Lv16) — +50% speed boost for 5 sec after being detected.
- **Cheat Death** (Lv18) — -50% damage taken below 50% Health, for 10 sec.
- **Toxicology** (Lv20) — Increases poison duration on bosses.

### Cold Blood
(Source: Best Ninja Builds — Stealth Assassin)
- **Cold Blood** (Lv1) — Movement speed increase; stacking buff system (core mechanic of the tree).
- **Will to Survive** (Lv5) — +3% Resistances.
- **Blood Brawl** (Lv5) — +10% melee damage while Cold Blood active.
- **Defensive Clotting** (Lv9) — Each Cold Blood stack: +8% armor.
- **Collagulant** (Lv11) — Cold Blood stacks remove individually, not all at once.
- **Frozen Precision** (Lv11) — Each stack: +5% headshot damage.
- **Predator** (Lv12) — Each stack: +30% attack speed.
- **Pain is an Illusion** (Lv14) — -5% damage taken while Cold Blood active.
- **Cold and Calculating** (Lv14) — Critical hits add a Cold Blood stack.
- **Coldest Blood** (Lv16) — +2 max Cold Blood stacks.
- **Critical Condition** (Lv16) — +50% Cold Blood duration.
- **Immunity** (Lv18) — Immune to Bleeding, Poison, Burn, Shock.
- **Bloodswell** (Lv20) — Prevents death once; grants 5 sec damage immunity (15 sec cooldown).
- **Merciless** (Lv20) — +5% Crit Chance, +25% Crit Damage while Cold Blood active.
