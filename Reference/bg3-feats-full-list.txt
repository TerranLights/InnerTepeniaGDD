Baldur's Gate 3 — Feats, full mechanical list
Source: https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Feats
Retrieved 2026-08-01. Mechanical/numeric facts only (game rules, not
copyrightable flavor text) — extracted for use as raw conversion material
toward Inner Tepenia's own perks/traits/challenge perks. Requires "extreme
reinterpretation" per the developer's own framing — Inner Tepenia is Hard
Sci-Fi, not D&D-style fantasy, so several of these may not translate at all.

////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

1. Ability Improvement — Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability
   scores by 1 (max 20).

2. Actor — Charisma +1 (max 20); gain expertise in Deception and
   Performance.

3. Alert — +5 initiative bonus; cannot be surprised.

4. Athlete: Standing Up — Strength or Dexterity +1 (max 20); standing from
   prone costs less movement; jump distance +50%.

5. Charger: Weapon Attack — Charge up to 9m and make melee attack with +5
   damage without provoking opportunity attacks (uses Action and Bonus
   Action; counts as single weapon attack for Extra Attack).

6. Charger: Shove — Charge and shove first enemy without provoking
   opportunity attacks; shove distance based on Strength and target weight.

7. Crossbow Expert: Point-Blank — Crossbow attacks in melee range don't
   have disadvantage.

8. Crossbow Expert: Wounding — Piercing Shot inflicts Gaping Wounds for
   twice as long.

9. Defensive Duellist — When attacked with melee while wielding proficient
   Finesse weapon, use Reaction to add proficiency bonus to AC. Requires
   Dexterity 13+.

10. Dual Wielder — Use Two-Weapon Fighting with non-Light weapons (except
    Two-Handed).

11. Dual Wielder: Bonus Armour Class — +1 AC while wielding melee weapons
    in both hands.

12. Dungeon Delver: Perception — Advantage on Perception checks for hidden
    objects and saving throws against traps.

13. Dungeon Delver: Resist Traps — Gain resistance to trap damage.

14. Durable — Constitution +1 (max 20); regain full hit points on short
    rest.

15. Elemental Adept — Choose one damage type (Acid, Cold, Lightning, Fire,
    or Thunder): spells ignore resistance to that type; cannot roll 1s on
    damage dice for that element.

16. Great Weapon Master: Bonus Attack — After critical hit or kill with
    melee weapon, make bonus melee attack as Bonus Action.

17. Great Weapon Master: All In — Two-Handed or Versatile melee attacks
    (two hands): -5 to hit, +10 damage (toggleable).

18. Heavily Armoured — Strength +1 (max 20); gain heavy armour proficiency.
    Requires medium armour proficiency.

19. Heavy Armour Master — Strength +1 (max 20); non-magical
    slashing/bludgeoning/piercing damage reduced by 3 while wearing heavy
    armour. Requires heavy armour proficiency.

20. Lightly Armoured — Strength or Dexterity +1 (max 20); gain light armour
    proficiency.

21. Lucky — Gain 3 Luck Points (recharge on Long Rest); spend to gain
    advantage on attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, or force
    enemy to reroll attack.

22. Mage Slayer: Saving Throw Advantage — Advantage on saving throws
    against spells cast within melee range.

23. Mage Slayer: Attack Caster — Use Reaction to make attack against caster
    casting spell within 1.5m.

24. Mage Slayer: Break Concentration — Enemies you hit have disadvantage on
    concentration saving throws.

25. Magic Initiate: Bard — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Bard spell
    (castable once per Long Rest); use Charisma as spellcasting ability.

26. Magic Initiate: Cleric — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Cleric
    spell (castable once per Long Rest); use Wisdom as spellcasting
    ability.

27. Magic Initiate: Druid — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Druid spell
    (castable once per Long Rest); use Wisdom as spellcasting ability.

28. Magic Initiate: Sorcerer — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Sorcerer
    spell (castable once per Long Rest); use Charisma as spellcasting
    ability.

29. Magic Initiate: Warlock — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Warlock
    spell (castable once per Long Rest); use Charisma as spellcasting
    ability.

30. Magic Initiate: Wizard — Learn 2 cantrips and one 1st-level Wizard
    spell (castable once per Long Rest); use Intelligence as spellcasting
    ability.

31. Martial Adept — Learn two Battle Master manoeuvres; gain 1 additional
    Superiority Die (recharge on Short or Long Rest).

32. Medium Armour Master — Medium armour no longer imposes Stealth
    disadvantage; Dexterity AC bonus becomes +3 instead of +2. Requires
    medium armour proficiency.

33. Mobile — Movement speed +3m (10 ft); Dash action unaffected by
    difficult terrain; no opportunity attacks when moving after melee
    attack.

34. Moderately Armoured — Strength or Dexterity +1 (max 20); gain medium
    armour and shield proficiency. Requires light armour proficiency.

35. Performer — Charisma +1 (max 20); gain musical instrument proficiency.

36. Polearm Master: Bonus Attack — Using Glaive, Halberd, Pike,
    Quarterstaff, or Spear: use Bonus Action to attack with weapon butt.

37. Polearm Master: Opportunity Attack — Make opportunity attacks when
    target enters range.

38. Resilient — Choose ability score: increase by 1 (max 20); gain
    proficiency in that ability's saving throws.

39. Ritual Caster: Free Spells — Learn two ritual spells of choice (Speak
    with Dead, Find Familiar, Longstrider, Enhance Leap, Disguise Self, or
    Speak with Animals).

40. Savage Attacker — Melee weapon damage: roll damage dice twice and use
    highest result.

41. Sentinel: Vengeance — When enemy in melee range attacks ally (without
    Sentinel feat), use Reaction to make weapon attack against attacker.

42. Sentinel: Snare — Opportunity attack hits prevent target movement for
    remainder of turn.

43. Sentinel: Opportunity Advantage — Gain advantage on opportunity
    attacks.

44. Sharpshooter: Low Ground — Ranged attacks unpenalized by high ground
    rules.

45. Sharpshooter: All In — Ranged attacks with proficient weapons: -5 to
    hit, +10 damage (toggleable).

46. Shield Master — +2 bonus to Dexterity saving throws when wielding
    shield; use Reaction to shield against spells forcing Dexterity saves
    (half damage on fail, no damage on success). Note: bug prevents
    half-damage reduction on failed saves (as documented on the wiki at
    time of retrieval).

47. Skilled — Gain proficiency in 3 skills of choice.

48. Spell Sniper — Learn one cantrip; critical hit threshold reduced by 1
    for spell attacks (effect stacks).

49. Tavern Brawler — Strength or Constitution +1 (max 20); unarmed
    attacks, improvised weapons, and thrown objects: add Strength modifier
    twice to damage and attack rolls.

50. Tough — Hit point maximum increased by 2 per level.

51. War Caster: Concentration — Advantage on saving throws to maintain
    concentration on spells.

52. War Caster: Opportunity Spell — Use Reaction to cast Shocking Grasp at
    target moving out of melee range (requires knowing spell from another
    source).

53. Weapon Master — Strength or Dexterity +1 (max 20); gain proficiency
    with four weapon types of choice.
