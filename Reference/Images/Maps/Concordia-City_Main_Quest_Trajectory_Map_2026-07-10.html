Opening

Calethina’s Lab
Cancer/Taurus border. Capricorn visible, not yet entered. Opening task optional — “The Heating Grid Failure” is the only one of 8 candidates that is the main plot itself.
Act 1 — The Spark
1
Capricorn
Industrial pressure, first major data. Plants evidence tying Capricorn’s self-image to a rigged historical judgment — mechanism undecided, 4 contenders shortlisted (see 06b_Capricorn_Alternative_Conditions.md), 2761. Not resolved here.
Act 2 — The City Fractures
2
Taurus / Cancer
Residential and emotional cost of the crisis. Unchanged from the original structure.
3
Virgo → Scorpio
Virgo becomes the first end of the Recognition Cascade — decades of unfunded, proven-essential labor. Scorpio is where the player can sit with what they’ve found so far.
4
Gemini
Michelle Stanton near-mandatory — she built the Arcanet, so the grid’s data-corruption layer needs her specifically. Twin Channels is the 1st Falkland Treaty breadcrumb.
5
Pisces
The Tolerance Pact is the 2nd, independent Falkland Treaty breadcrumb.
6
Sagittarius
A Frostlander clan’s Rastra breaks down. Pays off if the player learned repair from Michelle at Beat 4; stands alone otherwise.
7
Aquarius
Predictive-maintenance fix for Virgo’s grievance — but risks feeding Scorpio’s caseload. Ji-Eun Kim’s concealment is here, fully optional.
8
Aries, the Power Core
The Cascade’s other end. Accumulated choices — Aquarius pursued? Scorpio’s confrontation landed? — resolve whether it breaks or repeats.
9
Libra — new beat
Governance and distribution, right before Act 3. The Suspended Compact is the 3rd and clearest Falkland Treaty trace.
Act 3 — The Great Blackout
10
Breaking Point
No fixed district — citywide event, unchanged. Not plotted on the chart.
11
The Hub — Axis Mundi
Climax, scaled by accumulated district friction. Three resolutions, same fight: Talk (Calculation+Humanity), Sneak (Agility+Nerve), Fight (always available).
Still open: friction→fight-scale formula, whether districts can be recruited as Hub allies, whether Talk/Sneak/Fight diverge in outcome or just route, and whether the Beat 2–3 transit clips through Aries before its own Beat 8 stop.
Both source documents remain marked TENTATIVE — treat this chart as a working reference, not a locked script.
