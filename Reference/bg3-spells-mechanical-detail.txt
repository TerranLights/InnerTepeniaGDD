## CANTRIPS

### Fire Bolt — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Fire_Bolt
Damage: 1d10 fire (scales to 2d10 at char level 5, 3d10 at level 10).
Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: 1 Action. Attack roll required (no save).
Verbal + somatic components. Benefits from high ground (extends range).
Ignites flammable objects (torches, braziers) on hit.

### Eldritch Blast — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Eldritch_Blast
Damage: 1d10 Force per beam. Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: 1 Action. Attack
roll required per beam (no save). Scaling: +1 beam at char level 5, +1
more at level 10 (each beam separate attack roll). Repelling Blast
invocation: pushes target 4.5m (15ft), once per cast. Hex adds 1d6
Necrotic per beam. Verbal + somatic. Can be Counterspelled.

### Sacred Flame — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sacred_Flame
Damage: 1d8 radiant (scales to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 10). Range:
18m (60ft). Cost: 1 Action. Save: Dexterity (spell save DC) — negates
damage entirely on success (no half damage). Verbal + somatic. Evocation.

### Acid Splash — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Acid_Splash
Damage: 1d6 acid (scales to 2d6 at level 5, 3d6 at level 10). Range: 18m
(60ft). Area: 2m (7ft) radius. Save: Dexterity — negates damage. Cost: 1
Action. High-ground range extension.

### Blade Ward — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Blade_Ward
Effect: resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing (half damage from
these). Duration: 2 turns. Cost: 1 Action. Range: Self. Verbal + somatic.
Abjuration. Available: Bard/Cleric/Sorcerer/Warlock/Wizard L1; Eldritch
Knight/Arcane Trickster L3; High Elf/High Half-Elf race; Magic Initiate.
No scroll version exists.

### Bone Chill — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Bone_Chill
Damage: 1d8 necrotic (scales to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 10). Range:
18m (60ft). Attack roll required. Cost: 1 Action. Secondary: "Bone
Chilled" condition, 1 turn — target can't regain HP or help Downed
characters; undead targets get disadvantage on attack rolls. Verbal +
somatic. High-ground range extension.

### Booming Blade — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Booming_Blade
Damage: weapon damage + 1d8 Thunder (base); +2d8 Thunder at level 5,
+3d8 at level 11 if target moves (bonus damage only triggers on
movement, within 1 turn). Range: normal weapon range. Melee weapon
attack roll (requires weapon proficiency). Cost: 1 Action. Castable
while Silenced. Qualifies for Extra Attack (uses weapon attack roll).

### Bursting Sinew — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Bursting_Sinew
Necromancy. Cost: 1 Action. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 3m (10ft) radius.
Target: 1 fresh corpse (not ooze/plant/construct/elemental; can't be
killed by Fire/Radiant/Acid). Damage: 1d10 Piercing (scales to 2d10 at
level 5, 3d10 at level 10), non-magical (resisted normally). Save:
Dexterity — negates. Consumes the corpse (can't be reused or reanimated
afterward).

### Dancing Lights — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Dancing_Lights
Evocation. Cost: 1 Action. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 9m (30ft) radius
illumination. Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Verbal + somatic.
Functions as one light source (not 4 like tabletop). Movable via Bonus
Action. Doesn't break stealth. Can trigger traps/block vents.

### Friends — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Friends
Enchantment. Effect: advantage on Charisma checks vs. a non-hostile
creature. Range: 9m (30ft). Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Cost: 1
Action. Castable while Silenced. Drawback: -10 approval with companions
when it wears off; considered a crime on Tactician+ difficulty (target
may become hostile/accuse caster of enchantment).

### Guidance — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Guidance
Divination. Bonus: +1d4 to ability checks. Duration: 10 turns
(Concentration). Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Cost: 1 Action. Verbal +
somatic. Repeatable.

### Light — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Light
Evocation. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Duration: until Long Rest (on
characters) or 20 turns (on objects). Effect: 7.5m (25ft) radius light
aura on one target object. Non-ally creatures can Dex save (vs. caster's
spell save DC) to resist. Verbal component. Blocked by Darkness spell.

### Mage Hand — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Mage_Hand
Conjuration. Cost: 1 Action. Range: 18m (60ft). Recharge: 1/Short Rest.
Duration: 10 turns. Summons a Tiny hand creature: 3 HP, 5 AC, Str
10/Dex 10/Con 3/Int 2/Wis 1/Cha 1. Can Shove (bonus action) and Throw
(action); manipulates objects. Can't target characters/items directly.
Verbal + somatic. Arcane Tricksters get an invisible, permanent-duration
version (Mage Hand Legerdemain).

### Minor Illusion — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Minor_Illusion
Illusion. Cost: 1 Action. Range: 18m (60ft). Duration: 10 turns. No
concentration. Creates an illusory cat (1 HP, 10 AC, 0 speed, all stats
-5) that compels nearby creatures to investigate/turn toward it (out of
and in combat respectively). Castable hidden/silenced; doesn't break
invisibility. Can't target characters/items. Triggers traps/blocks
vents if placed on them. Illusion School Wizards get Improved Minor
Illusion (bonus action, 20-turn duration).

### Poison Spray — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Poison_Spray
Damage: 1d12 poison (scales to 2d12 at level 5, 3d12 at level 10).
Range: 3m (10ft). Save: Constitution — negates. Cost: 1 Action. Verbal +
somatic. High-ground range extension.

### Produce Flame — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Produce_Flame
Damage (when thrown): 1d8 Fire (scales to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level
10). Range: 9m (30ft) thrown. Cost: free to throw immediately after
conjuring; costs an Action to throw later or extinguish. Creates a blue
flame, 9m (30ft) light radius, persists until Long Rest. Ignites
flammable objects. Verbal + somatic.

### Ray of Frost — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Ray_of_Frost
Damage: 1d8 cold (scales to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 10). Range: 18m
(60ft). Attack roll required. Cost: 1 Action. Secondary: target's speed
-3m (10ft) for 1 turn on hit. Extinguishes fire surfaces/objects;
freezes liquid surfaces (creatures on frozen liquid Dex save or become
prone); double damage to Wet targets. Verbal + somatic.

### Resistance (cantrip) — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Resistance_(Cantrip)
Abjuration. Bonus: +1d4 to saving throws. Duration: 10 turns
(Concentration). Cost: 1 Action. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Verbal +
somatic. Learned by Cleric/Druid at L1, or via Magic Initiate.

### Shillelagh — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Shillelagh
Transmutation. Cost: Bonus Action. Range: Self. Requires quarterstaff or
club. Duration: 10 turns. Effect: weapon becomes magical bludgeoning;
1d8 + spellcasting modifier damage; attack rolls use spellcasting
modifier instead of Strength. Weapon can't be thrown/removed from
inventory during effect. Works on improvised club/staff weapons (e.g.
torches). Verbal + somatic.

### Shocking Grasp — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Shocking_Grasp
Damage: 1d8 Lightning (scales to 2d8 at level 5, 3d8 at level 10).
Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Melee spell attack roll. Secondary: target
can't use reactions for 1 turn. Advantage vs. targets in metal armor.
Verbal + somatic.

### Thaumaturgy — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Thaumaturgy
Transmutation. Cost: 1 Action. Range: Self. Effect: advantage on
Intimidation and Performance checks. Duration: 10 turns. Verbal
component. Available: Cleric L1, Giant Barbarian L3, Zariel Tiefling L1,
Magic Initiate: Cleric.

### Thorn Whip — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Thorn_Whip
Damage: 1d6 piercing (scales to 2d6 at level 5, 3d6 at level 10). Melee
spell attack roll (despite ranged distance — no melee disadvantage).
Range: 9m (30ft). Cost: 1 Action. Secondary: pulls target 3m (10ft)
closer (not if target is Huge+).

### Toll the Dead — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Toll_the_Dead
Damage: 1d12 Necrotic if target has taken damage, 1d8 Necrotic if
target is at/near full health (scales to 2d12/2d8 at level 5, 3d12/3d8
at level 10). Range: 18m (60ft). Save: Wisdom — negates. Cost: 1 Action.
Verbal + somatic. Bug: "full health" threshold is actually ≤99% HP.

### True Strike — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/True_Strike
Divination. Effect: advantage on caster's next attack roll (self only,
consumed after first attack). Range: 18m (60ft). Duration: 2 turns
(includes casting turn) (Concentration). Cost: 1 Action. Castable while
Silenced.

### Vicious Mockery — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Vicious_Mockery
Damage: 1d4 Psychic (scales to 2d4 at level 5, 3d4 at level 10). Range:
18m (60ft). Save: Wisdom — negates. Secondary (on failed save):
disadvantage on target's next attack roll, 1 turn (applies to any
"attack," including spells). Cost: 1 Action.

## LEVEL 1

### Animal Friendship — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Animal_Friendship
Enchantment. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Save:
Wisdom. Target must have Int ≤3. Effect: Charmed 10 turns — target can't
attack caster, caster gets advantage on Charisma checks with it. Ends
early if caster/ally damages target (may turn hostile on higher
difficulty). Upcast: +1 target per slot level above 1st. Verbal +
somatic.

### Armour of Agathys — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Armour_of_Agathys
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self. Effect: 5 temp HP; melee
attackers take 5 Cold damage. Duration: until Long Rest or temp HP lost.
Upcast: +5 temp HP/+5 Cold damage per slot level above 1st. Only one
temp-HP source active at a time. Verbal + somatic.

### Arms of Hadar — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Arms_of_Hadar
Damage: 2d6 Necrotic. Area: 3m (10ft) radius from self. Save: Strength
— half damage on success (reaction retained); full damage + can't take
Reactions for 1 turn on fail. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1d6
per slot level above 1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Bane — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Bane_(Spell)
Enchantment. Effect: up to 3 targets get -1d4 to attack rolls and
saving throws. Range: 9m (30ft). Save: Charisma. Duration: 10 turns
(Concentration). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1 target per slot
level above 1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Bless — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Bless
Effect: up to 3 non-hostile targets get +1d4 to attack rolls and saving
throws. Range: 9m (30ft). Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Cost: 1
Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1 target per slot level above 1st. Verbal +
somatic.

### Charm Person — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Charm_Person
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Save: Wisdom (enemies
have advantage on this save). Duration: 10 turns. Effect: charms a
humanoid — can't attack caster; caster gets advantage on Charisma
checks with it. Ends early if caster/ally damages target. Upcast: +1
target per slot level above 1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Chromatic Orb — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Chromatic_Orb
Damage: 3d8 (Thunder) or 2d8 (Acid/Cold/Fire/Lightning/Poison — player
chooses type). Range: 18m (60ft). Attack roll required. Cost: 1 Action +
1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1d8 of chosen type per slot level above 1st. Creates
a surface at impact. Verbal + somatic.

### Colour Spray — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Colour_Spray
Area: 5m (17ft) cone from self. No save — affects creatures up to a
combined 33 HP pool. Effect: Blinded 1 turn (disadvantage on attacks,
attack range capped at 3m/10ft, attackers get advantage vs. them). Cost:
1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: HP threshold +11 per slot level above
1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Command — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Command
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Save: Wisdom. Options:
Approach, Drop (weapon), Flee, Grovel, Halt. No effect on undead. Target
obeys indirectly-harmful commands but resists directly-harmful ones (can
be exploited, e.g. Approach into opportunity attacks). No concentration.
Upcast: +1 target per slot level above 1st. Sorcerer Extended Spell
metamagic: doubles duration, no re-roll on failed save. Verbal +
somatic.

### Compelled Duel — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Compelled_Duel
Enchantment. Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 9m (30ft). Save:
Wisdom. Duration: 3 turns (Concentration). Effect: target gets
disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but the caster (if cast on a
player character by an enemy, player loses control for the duration).
No upcast benefit. Verbal component.

### Create or Destroy Water — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Create_or_Destroy_Water
Transmutation. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 9m (30ft). Area: 4m
(13ft) radius. Effect: conjure rain or remove a water surface in area.
Upcast: area +2m (7ft) per slot level above 1st. Verbal + somatic.
Interacts with Wet/Shocked/ice-terrain/Electrified Steam mechanics.

### Disguise Self — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Disguise_Self
Illusion. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self. Duration: until Long
Rest. Ritual castable. Effect: change all aspects of appearance (no
mechanical benefits from disguised race/size beyond visual, though size
category does change). Dispel Disguise action ends it early. No upcast
benefit. Verbal + somatic.

### Dissonant Whispers — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Dissonant_Whispers
Damage: 3d6 Psychic. Range: 18m (60ft). Save: Wisdom — half damage on
success. Secondary: Frightened 2 turns (can't move, disadvantage on
ability checks/attacks) on fail. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast:
+1d6 per slot level above 1st. Verbal component.

### Divine Favour — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Divine_Favour
Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self. Effect: weapon attacks deal
+1d4 Radiant (not unarmed attacks). Duration: 3 turns (Concentration).
No upcast benefit. Verbal + somatic.

### Enhance Leap — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Enhance_Leap
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Duration: 10
turns. Ritual castable. Effect: triples jump distance (applied to base
jump distance before other modifiers). No upcast benefit. Githyanki get
a free racial version 1/Long Rest. Verbal + somatic.

### Ensnaring Strike — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Ensnaring_Strike
Conjuration. Cost: Action + 1 L1 slot (Bonus Action if triggered on
hit). Weapon attack roll (triggers Extra Attack). Damage: weapon damage
+ 1d6 Piercing on hit. Save: Strength — on fail, Ensnared 10 turns
(can't move, 1d6 Piercing/turn, attackers get advantage, target has
disadvantage on attacks/Dex saves; freeable via ally Help action).
Concentration required. Upcast: +1d6/turn per slot level above 1st.
Verbal component.

### Entangle — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Entangle
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 3m (10ft) radius.
Save: Strength. Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Effect: difficult
terrain (half speed); Entangled (can't move 1 turn, Str save to resist,
disadvantage on attacks/Dex saves, attackers get advantage; freeable via
ally Help). No upcast benefit. Can't directly target Grounded creatures
(but area can still catch them). Verbal + somatic.

### Expeditious Retreat — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Expeditious_Retreat
Transmutation. Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self. Duration:
until Long Rest (Concentration). Effect: Dash immediately + Dash as
bonus action each turn while active. No upcast benefit. Verbal +
somatic.

### Faerie Fire — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Faerie_Fire
Evocation. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 6m
(20ft) radius. Save: Dexterity. Duration: 10 turns (Concentration).
Effect: targets become visible (can't turn invisible), attack rolls
against them get advantage, they shed dim light. No upcast benefit.
Verbal component.

### False Life — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/False_Life
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self. Effect: 7 temp HP. Duration:
until Long Rest. Upcast: +5 temp HP per slot level above 1st. Only one
temp-HP source at a time. Warlocks with Fiendish Vigour cast at will, no
slot. Verbal + somatic.

### Feather Fall — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Feather_Fall
Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: Self, 9m (30ft) radius AoE.
Duration: 10 turns. Ritual castable. Effect: immunity to fall damage;
prevents Prone from falls over 8m (27ft). No upcast benefit. Verbal
component.

### Find Familiar — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Find_Familiar
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Ritual castable. Recharge:
1/Short Rest per version known. No upcast benefit. Summons a fey
familiar: standard forms Cat/Crab/Frog/Rat/Raven/Spider; Pact of the
Chain exclusive Imp/Quasit; special single-creature variants (Boo,
Cheeky Quasit, Scratch). Only one active familiar total across all
versions; dismissed if caster leaves active party. Familiar can examine/
attack/drag objects, can't use environment interactables except by
attacking them. Verbal + somatic.

### Fog Cloud — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Fog_Cloud
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 4.5m (15ft) radius
sphere. Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Effect: Heavily Obscures/
Blinds creatures within (disadvantage on attacks, attack range capped
3m/10ft, attackers vs. them get advantage). Upcast: area +2m (7ft) per
slot level above 1st (documented as buggy/inconsistent). Verbal +
somatic.

### Goodberry — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Goodberry
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Effect: conjures 4
berries; each heals 1d4 HP on consumption and counts as 1 camp supply.
Duration: berries vanish after Long Rest. No upcast benefit. Verbal +
somatic.

### Magic Missile — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Magic_Missile
Damage: 3 darts, each 1d4+1 Force (total 6-15). Range: 18m (60ft). Cost:
1 Action + 1 Level 1 slot. No save — darts auto-hit. Darts can target
different enemies individually. Upcast: +1 dart per slot level above 1st.
Verbal + somatic. Shield spell grants full immunity to this spell.

### Cure Wounds — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Cure_Wounds
Healing: 1d8 + spellcasting modifier. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft) touch.
Cost: 1 Action + 1 Level 1 slot. Upcast: +1d8 per slot level above 1st.
No effect on undead/constructs. Verbal component. Available at Level 1
(Bard/Cleric/Druid/Life Domain) or Level 2 (Paladin/Ranger).

### Shield (spell) — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Shield_(spell)
Effect: +5 Armour Class. Duration: until start of caster's next turn (1
turn). Cost: 1 Reaction + 1 Level 1 slot. Range: Self. Grants immunity to
Magic Missile for the duration. Verbal + somatic. No benefit from
upcasting.

### Burning Hands — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Burning_Hands
Damage: 3d6 fire (3-18 total). Area: 5m (17ft) cone, from self. Save:
Dexterity — half damage on success. Cost: 1 Action + 1 Level 1 slot.
Upcast: +1d6 per slot level above 1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Grease — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Grease
Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 4m (13ft) radius.
Save: Dexterity — fail = Prone 1 turn. Duration: 10 turns, no
concentration. Effect: creates a difficult-terrain, flammable surface
(half movement speed on it). No upcast benefit. Verbal + somatic.

### Guiding Bolt — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Guiding_Bolt
Damage: 4d6 Radiant. Range: 18m (60ft). Attack roll required. Secondary:
next attack roll against target gains Advantage, lasts 2 turns. Cost: 1
Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1d6 per slot level above 1st. Verbal +
somatic.

### Hail of Thorns — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Hail_of_Thorns
Damage: weapon damage (attack roll) + 1d10 Piercing explosion. Range:
18m (60ft). Area: 2m (7ft) radius around target. Save: Dexterity — halves
explosion damage only (explosion triggers even on missed attack). Cost:
1 Action + 1 Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: explosion +1d10 per slot
level above 1st. Verbal component. Does not benefit from Extra Attack.

### Healing Word — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Healing_Word
Healing: 1d4 + spellcasting modifier. Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: Bonus
Action + 1 L1 slot. Instantaneous. No effect on undead/constructs.
Upcast: +1d4 per slot level above 1st. Verbal component.

### Hellish Rebuke — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Hellish_Rebuke
Damage: 2d10 Fire. Save: Dexterity — half damage on success. Cost:
Reaction (triggered when caster takes damage from an attacker) + 1 L1
slot. Upcast: +1d10 per slot level above 1st. Somatic + verbal.

### Heroism — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Heroism
Effect: immunity to Frightened; 5 temp HP/turn (only one temp-HP source
active at a time). Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot.
Duration: 10 turns (Concentration). Upcast: +1 target per slot level
above 1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Hex — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Hex
Damage bonus: +1d6 Necrotic per caster attack that hits target. Range:
18m (60ft). Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Duration: until Long Rest
(Concentration). Secondary: target has disadvantage on one chosen
ability's checks (not saves/attacks). No upcast benefit. Somatic +
verbal. If target dies/knocked out, caster can Reapply Hex free.

### Hunter's Mark — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Hunter%27s_Mark
Damage bonus: +1d6 weapon damage per hit (weapon attacks only, not
unarmed). Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot. Duration:
until Long Rest (Concentration). No upcast benefit. Verbal component.
Reapply Hunter's Mark (bonus action, free) if marked target dies.

### Ice Knife — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Ice_Knife
Damage: 1d10 Piercing (attack roll, initial hit) + 2d6 Cold (explosion,
triggers even on miss). Range: 18m (60ft). Area: 2m (7ft) radius. Save:
Dexterity negates explosion damage only. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot.
Upcast: explosion +1d6 Cold per slot level above 1st. Somatic component.
Creates a 2-turn ice surface; can knock prone (Dex save).

### Inflict Wounds — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Inflict_Wounds
Damage: 3d10 Necrotic. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Melee spell attack roll
required. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1d10 per slot level above
1st. Verbal + somatic.

### Longstrider — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Longstrider
Transmutation. Effect: +3m (10ft) movement speed. Range: melee, 1.5m
(5ft). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Duration: until Long Rest. Ritual
castable. Upcast: +1 target per slot level above 1st. Somatic + verbal.

### Mage Armour — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Mage_Armour
Effect: sets AC to 13 + Dex modifier (doesn't stack with other base-AC
abilities). Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot.
Duration: until Long Rest. No upcast benefit. Verbal + somatic. Target
must be unarmoured (shields allowed); removed if armor equipped.

### Protection from Evil and Good — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Protection_from_Evil_and_Good
Effect: vs. Aberrations/Celestials/Elementals/Fey/Fiends/Undead — target
can't be Charmed/Frightened by them, they have disadvantage attacking
target. Range: melee, 1.5m (5ft). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Duration:
until Long Rest (Concentration). No upcast benefit. Somatic + verbal.

### Ray of Sickness — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Ray_of_Sickness
Damage: 2d8 Poison. Range: 18m (60ft). Attack roll, then target makes a
Constitution save vs. Poisoned (2 turns: disadvantage on attacks/ability
checks) on hit. Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot. Upcast: +1d8 per slot level
above 1st. Somatic + verbal.

### Sanctuary — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sanctuary
Effect: target can't be targeted by attacks until it attacks/harms a
creature (area damage still applies); breaking it early inflicts
Sanctuary Blocked 1 turn. Range: 18m (60ft). No save. Cost: Bonus Action
+ 1 L1 slot. Duration: 10 turns. No upcast benefit. Somatic + verbal.

### Searing Smite — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Searing_Smite
Damage: on melee weapon hit, +1d6 Fire, then 1d6 Fire/turn for up to 10
turns (Constitution save ends it early). Cost: 1 Action to cast + spell
slot consumed on hit. Concentration required. Upcast: +1d6 initial per
slot level above 1st. Verbal component.

### Shield of Faith — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Shield_of_Faith
Effect: +2 AC. Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: Bonus Action + 1 L1 slot.
Duration: until Long Rest (Concentration). No upcast benefit. Somatic +
verbal.

### Sleep — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Sleep
Effect: puts creatures to sleep up to a combined 24 HP pool (undead/
sleep-immune excluded). Range: 18m (60ft). Cost: 1 Action + 1 L1 slot.
Duration: 2 turns, ends on damage/Help/shove/getting wet. Sleeping =
can't move/act, auto-fail Str/Dex saves, attackers get advantage
(melee within 1.5m/5ft auto-crits). Upcast: +8 HP pool per slot level
above 1st. Somatic + verbal.

### Speak with Animals — https://bg3.wiki/wiki/Speak_with_Animals
Effect: comprehend/communicate with beasts. Range: Self. Cost: 1 Action
