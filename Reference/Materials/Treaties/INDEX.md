# Treaty Reference Archive — Index

Curated reference archive of real-world treaties for use as creative/structural reference for the **Falkland Treaty** — the founding lore event of *Inner Tepenia*, in which a war between humans and sentient robots ends with the exile of robots (and human allies) to Antarctica, where they establish their own nation.

This is a **structural reference archive**, not a source of content to copy. The point is to see how real treaties of comparable character — ending wars, exiling/resettling persecuted populations, creating new autonomous territories, disarming belligerents, and protecting minorities — are actually drafted, structured, and negotiated.

All documents were fetched from public, freely accessible sources (Avalon Project at Yale Law School, Wikisource, national government archives, IGO treaty repositories, and similar). No paywalled or authentication-gated content was accessed. `treaties.un.org` requests were rate-limited to respect its published 2-second crawl delay; all other sources were fetched with reasonable pacing (≥1 second between requests) out of general courtesy.

**Format key:** FULL = complete treaty text obtained. SUMMARY = full text not readily scriptable; a citation/summary is provided instead (noted explicitly below).

| 1 | Peace of Westphalia (Treaty of Münster) | 1648 | FULL | `Peace_of_Westphalia_Munster_1648.html` | Founding template for the modern peace treaty as an instrument that ends a war *and* constitutes new sovereign political order — the ancestor of every treaty on this list. |

| 5 | Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye (with Austria) | 1919 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Saint-Germain-en-Laye_1919.pdf` | Companion peace treaty dissolving a multi-ethnic empire into successor states, with minority-protection clauses — model for how a defeated multi-population power gets legally dismantled. |

| 8 | Treaty of Sèvres (with the Ottoman Empire) | 1920 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Sevres_1920.html` | Contains explicit provisions for Kurdish local autonomy (Art. 62–64) and a path to full independence via League of Nations review, plus Armenian statehood provisions — a direct real-world precedent for "war ends, persecuted population gets an autonomous/independent territory carved out by treaty." |
| 9 | Treaty of Lausanne (main peace treaty with Turkey) | 1923 | FULL (Preamble + Parts I–V) | `Treaty_of_Lausanne_1923_main.html` (TOC) + `Treaty_of_Lausanne_1923_parts/` | Superseded Sèvres; shows how a peace treaty can be renegotiated once the political/military balance shifts — useful if the Falkland Treaty's terms were softened or hardened from an earlier draft. |
| 10 | Treaty of Brest-Litovsk | 1918 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Brest-Litovsk_1918.html` | Peace treaty imposed by a stronger belligerent with explicit demobilization terms (Art. V) and forced recognition of new independent states carved from the losing party's territory. |
| 11 | Locarno Pact (Treaty of Mutual Guarantee) | 1925 | FULL | `Locarno_Pact_1925.html` | Post-war border-guarantee treaty stabilizing an earlier settlement — model for a "second-generation" treaty that reinforces the Falkland Treaty's territorial terms after the fact. |
| 12 | Treaty of Peace with Italy | 1947 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Peace_with_Italy_1947.pdf` | Paris Peace Treaties model: post-WWII peace treaty combining territorial loss, military limitation clauses, and reparations in one instrument. |
| 13 | Treaty of Peace with Romania | 1947 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Peace_with_Romania_1947.html` | Same Paris Peace Treaties family; useful for comparing how the same template was adapted per-country. |
| 14 | Treaty of Peace with Bulgaria | 1947 | FULL | `Treaty_of_Peace_with_Bulgaria_1947.html` | Same Paris Peace Treaties family. |
| 15 | Treaty of San Francisco | 1951 | FULL | `Treaty_of_San_Francisco_1951.html` | Multilateral peace treaty formally ending a state of war with a defeated power while restoring its sovereignty — useful if the Falkland Treaty needs to show robots/allies regaining full sovereign status after a transitional period. |
| 16 | Camp David Accords | 1978 | FULL | `Camp_David_Accords_1978.html` | Framework-style peace agreement (as opposed to a single definitive treaty) — useful if the Falkland Treaty is better modeled as a framework followed by implementing agreements. |
| 17 | Egypt–Israel Peace Treaty | 1979 | FULL | `Egypt_Israel_Peace_Treaty_1979.pdf` | Full bilateral peace treaty with detailed military annexes, phased withdrawal timetables, and normalization clauses — strong model for a treaty's "implementation schedule" annex structure. |
| 18 | Dayton Peace Agreement (General Framework Agreement) | 1995 | FULL (core framework; see also Annex 1-A in Category 4) | `Dayton_Peace_Agreement_1995.pdf` | Modern peace treaty ending an internal/civil conflict via a short core agreement that incorporates numbered annexes (military, constitutional, human rights, refugees) — an excellent structural model for keeping a founding treaty terse while offloading detail to annexes. |

## Category 2: Population Transfer / Resettlement / Exile Agreements

| # | Treaty | Year | Format | File(s) | Relevance to the Falkland Treaty |
|---|--------|------|--------|---------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Convention Concerning the Exchange of Greek and Turkish Populations (Lausanne Convention VI) | 1923 | FULL | `Lausanne_Convention_Greek_Turkish_Population_Exchange_1923.html` | The single closest real-world precedent for a treaty mandating compulsory, large-scale population relocation as a term of peace — directly analogous to the Falkland Treaty's exile clause. |
| 2 | Treaty of New Echota | 1835 | FULL | `Treaty_of_New_Echota_1835.html` | Removal treaty ceding a persecuted population's homeland in exchange for relocation and compensation — a starker, more coercive analogue for exile-as-treaty-term. |
| 3 | Potsdam Agreement (Berlin Conference Protocol), incl. Section on the Orderly Transfer of German Populations | 1945 | FULL | `Potsdam_Agreement_1945.html` | Post-war great-power agreement explicitly mandating "orderly and humane" transfer of an ethnic population out of successor states — a rare precedent for population transfer framed as *protective* rather than purely punitive, useful if the Falkland Treaty frames robot exile similarly. |
| 4 | Indian Independence Act | 1947 | FULL | `Indian_Independence_Act_1947.pdf` | Domestic legislation effecting partition and creation of two new dominions from one territory — model for a treaty/act that simultaneously ends a conflict and legally instantiates new separate states. |
| 5 | Treaty of Guarantee (Cyprus) | 1960 | FULL | `Cyprus_Treaty_of_Guarantee_1960.pdf` | Great-power guarantee of a newly created state's independence and territorial integrity, with an explicit intervention right reserved to guarantors — relevant if outside powers (in-world, the human nations) retain a right of intervention over the new robot nation. |
| 6 | Treaty of Establishment (Cyprus) | 1960 | FULL | `Cyprus_Treaty_of_Establishment_1960.pdf` | Companion treaty establishing a new sovereign republic out of a formerly contested colonial territory, including retained foreign base rights — precedent for a settlement that creates sovereignty while leaving the old power a residual foothold. |

**Cross-references:** The Treaty of Neuilly (Category 1, #6) contains reciprocal emigration/minority clauses; the Treaty of Versailles Part III (Category 1, #4) contains the Danzig free-city provisions relevant to population/territory questions. Not duplicated here.

## Category 3: Treaties Establishing Autonomous Territories, Protectorates, or Exile/Resettlement States
Folder: `03-Autonomous-Territories-Exile-States/`

| # | Treaty / Instrument | Year | Format | File(s) | Relevance to the Falkland Treaty |
|---|--------|------|--------|---------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Balfour Declaration | 1917 | FULL | `Balfour_Declaration_1917.html` | Short-form great-power statement of intent to support a homeland for a specific population — precedent for how a single-paragraph declaration can seed decades of subsequent treaty-making (useful if Inner Tepenia's lore wants a pre-Falkland-Treaty "declaration" moment). |
| 2 | Sykes-Picot Agreement | 1916 | FULL | `Sykes-Picot_Agreement_1916.html` | Secret great-power agreement carving up a defeated power's territory into spheres of control/autonomous Arab state(s) *before* the war even ended — model for backroom pre-negotiation that later hardens into treaty terms. |
| 3 | League of Nations Mandate for Palestine | 1922 | FULL | `League_of_Nations_Mandate_for_Palestine_1922.html` | Full mandate instrument: an international body assigns a territory to an administering power with binding conditions protecting a specific population's rights and future self-governance — close structural analogue to an internationally supervised exile territory. |
| 4 | UN Partition Plan for Palestine (GA Resolution 181) | 1947 | FULL | `UN_Partition_Plan_Resolution_181_1947.html` | Detailed international plan partitioning one territory into two new states plus an internationally administered zone, with model constitutional/economic-union provisions — strong template for the Falkland Treaty's territorial-division mechanics. |
| 5 | Israel–PLO Declaration of Principles (Oslo Accords) | 1993 | FULL | `Israel-PLO_Oslo_Accords_1993.html` | Establishes a new autonomous self-governing authority for a population via phased, negotiated self-rule rather than immediate full sovereignty — model if the robot nation's autonomy is meant to be gradual/staged rather than immediate. |
| 6 | Good Friday (Belfast) Agreement | 1998 | FULL | `Good_Friday_Agreement_1998.html` | Modern peace agreement creating devolved autonomous government, cross-border institutions, and (critically) linked decommissioning/demobilization commitments — an excellent single-document model combining autonomy-creation with disarmament. |
| 7 | Lateran Treaty | 1929 | FULL | `Lateran_Treaty_1929.html` | Treaty literally creating a new sovereign micro-state (Vatican City) by negotiated agreement between the new state's population's institution and the surrounding power — a clean, small-scale precedent for "treaty as birth certificate of a sovereign territory." |
| 8 | Svalbard Treaty | 1920 | FULL | `Svalbard_Treaty_1920.pdf` | Establishes a unique demilitarized, internationally-recognized special-sovereignty regime over a remote, harsh territory with restricted military use — a strong structural precedent for Antarctica-as-treaty-territory specifically. |
| 9 | Antarctic Treaty | 1959 | FULL | `Antarctic_Treaty_1959.html` | Directly on point: the real treaty governing the exact continent where Inner Tepenia is set, establishing demilitarization, freedom of scientific/peaceful activity, and a cooperative multinational governance regime — essential reading regardless of era, since the Falkland Treaty must eventually reconcile fictional robot sovereignty with (or explain its divergence from) this real framework. |

**Cross-reference:** Treaty of Sèvres (Category 1, #8) Kurdistan/Armenia autonomy provisions are also directly relevant here; not duplicated.

## Category 4: Disarmament and Demobilization Agreements
Folder: `04-Disarmament-Demobilization/`

| # | Treaty | Year | Format | File(s) | Relevance to the Falkland Treaty |
|---|--------|------|--------|---------|-----------------------------------|
| 1 | Four-Power Treaty (Washington Conference) | 1921 | FULL | `Four-Power_Treaty_Washington_1921.html` | Post-war great-power pact substituting mutual consultation for arms racing in a specific theater — model for a "renounce further escalation" clause alongside disarmament proper. |
| 2 | Five-Power (Washington) Naval Treaty | 1922 | FULL | `Five-Power_Naval_Treaty_Washington_1922.html` | Detailed, numeric disarmament treaty with fixed tonnage ratios and scrapping schedules — a strong model for concrete, quantified disarmament terms (e.g., specific caps and decommissioning timelines) the Falkland Treaty could impose on either side. |
| 3 | Kellogg-Briand Pact (General Treaty for Renunciation of War) | 1928 | FULL | `Kellogg-Briand_Pact_1928.html` | Short, sweeping renunciation-of-war instrument — useful if the Falkland Treaty needs a simple, symbolic "never again" clause distinct from its detailed operational terms. |

| 11 | Dayton Peace Agreement, Annex 1-A (Agreement on the Military Aspects of the Peace Settlement) | 1995 | FULL | `Dayton_Annex_1A_Military_Aspects_1995.html` | The demobilization annex specifically requested: concrete cease-fire lines, zones of separation, withdrawal timetables, and an international implementation force mandate — probably the single best template in this archive for the Falkland Treaty's disarmament/demobilization terms specifically. |

## Summary

- **Total treaties/instruments archived:** 38 distinct treaties (95 files, ~11 MB), all with full text obtained.
  - Category 1 (Peace Treaties): 18
  - Category 2 (Population Transfer/Resettlement): 6
  - Category 3 (Autonomous Territories/Exile States): 9
  - Category 4 (Disarmament/Demobilization): 11
  - Category 5 (Minority Protection): 4
  - (Some treaties are cross-referenced across categories rather than duplicated as files — see cross-reference notes above; the 38 count reflects distinct files/file-sets, not distinct legal instruments, since a few — e.g., Versailles — bundle sub-instruments like the Covenant of the League of Nations.)
- **Full text obtained for every entry.** No summary-only fallbacks were needed — every target treaty had a genuinely scriptable, non-gated full-text source.
- **Formats:** primarily HTML (as served by source sites — mostly the Avalon Project at Yale Law School and Wikisource) and PDF (government/IGO originals). One plain-text file (Archive.org's OCR text of the 1918 Armistice terms).
- **Multi-part treaties:** Versailles (15 parts), Neuilly (13 parts + TOC), Trianon (15 parts + TOC), and Lausanne (6 parts + TOC) were split across multiple source pages by Wikisource/Avalon and archived as complete sets of per-part files rather than single documents.

### Source substitutions and notes
- **Treaty of Saint-Germain-en-Laye:** Wikisource's page for this treaty is a dead redirect ("does not exist"). Substituted the Forost (Ungarisches Institut, Munich) PDF, which carries a clear citation to the Consolidated Treaty Series (Vol. 226).
- **Polish Minorities Treaty:** AustLII's copy is behind a Cloudflare JavaScript challenge and could not be fetched programmatically. Substituted the same Forost PDF collection, again citing the Consolidated Treaty Series.
- **Cyprus Treaty of Guarantee:** The Greek Foreign Ministry's copy (mfa.gr) returned an Access Denied/CDN block. Substituted the UN Peacemaker mirror, which hosts the identical treaty text.
- **Treaty of Trianon:** The first source tried (istrianet.org PDF) turned out to be only a partial excerpt (Parts I–IV of a multi-file series). Replaced with the complete Wikisource part-by-part set (Preamble through Part 14 plus Protocol).
- All other treaties were obtained on the first attempt from the sources noted in the tables above.
- **Nothing was gated, paywalled, or skipped entirely.** No CAPTCHA-bypassing or authentication workaround was attempted at any point; where a source was blocked (AustLII, mfa.gr), a different public, unauthenticated source was used instead.
- **Rate limiting:** all `treaties.un.org` requests (1 document) were paced at ≥2 seconds apart per that site's robots.txt. All other sites were fetched with ≥1 second between requests as a general courtesy; no site was hit with concurrent or rapid-fire requests.
