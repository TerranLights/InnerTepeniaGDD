# SOC 2018 Manual — Cross-Category Commonalities

**Companion file to `SOC_Table_of_Contents.md` and `SOC_Minor_Group_Summaries.md`.** Those two files catalog
the SOC's own hierarchy (23 major groups, 98 minor groups). This file identifies a second, orthogonal layer:
**shared attributes, connecting threads, and common through-lines that cut ACROSS different major groups** —
found by pattern-matching across all 98 minor-group summaries at once, not by reading the PDF further.

**Why this matters, per the developer's own framing:** building a genuine in-character hook for all 98 minor
groups individually would be enormous. Many of those 98 groups, despite sitting in entirely different major
groups, actually share the same underlying *shape* — the same kind of daily experience, stress, skill, or
identity. A player roleplaying "I was a Lighthouse Keeper" and a player roleplaying "I was a Prison Guard"
have almost nothing in common by SOC category (53-6000 Transportation vs. 33-3000 Law Enforcement), but a
player roleplaying "I was a Firefighter" and "I was an Infantryman" have a huge amount in common despite
sitting in different major groups too (33-2000 vs. 55-3000). Designing against these **shared archetypes
first** covers a large fraction of all 867 possible detailed occupations with a small number of actual game
hooks, before (or instead of) ever needing a fully bespoke per-occupation treatment.

**Note:** most minor groups appear under more than one archetype below — that's intentional, not an error.
Real jobs have multiple facets (a Surgeon is both a Licensed Professional and someone who does exacting
physical hands-on work), and a player roleplaying a given job should be able to draw on whichever archetype
fits the specific angle they want to play.

---

## 1. Supervisor / Foreman (leadership over a specific crew, not the whole enterprise)

The single most-repeated pattern in the entire SOC structure — nearly every major group from 33-0000 through
53-0000 has its own dedicated "Supervisors of X Workers" minor group as its first entry.

**Draws from:** 11-1000, 11-3000, 33-1000, 35-1000, 37-1000, 39-1000, 41-1000, 43-1000, 45-1000, 47-1000,
49-1000, 51-1000, 53-1000, 55-2000 (14 minor groups).

**The shared shape:** direct, hands-on responsibility for a small team's daily work, without the distance of
running a whole company — the "you were the crew boss" identity, regardless of what the crew actually did.

## 2. Licensed / Credentialed Professional

**Draws from:** 23-1000 (lawyers), 29-1000 (physicians, dentists, pharmacists, nurses, veterinarians),
17-1000/17-2000 (architects, engineers), 13-2000 (accountants/CPAs), 19-3000 (psychologists), 25-2000
(certified teachers), 39-5000 (licensed cosmetologists).

**The shared shape:** formal credentialing (exams, licensing boards, continuing education requirements),
personal legal/professional liability for mistakes, and a title that carries real social weight and public
trust independent of the specific field.

## 3. Skilled Manual Trade ("worked with your hands")

**Draws from:** 47-2000 (construction trades), 51-4000 (metal/plastic workers), 51-7000 (woodworkers), 49-2000
/ 49-3000 / 49-9000 (installation/maintenance/repair), 47-5000 (extraction), 51-2000 (assemblers/fabricators).

**The shared shape:** physical dexterity, tool mastery, and a trained eye for diagnosing and fixing a physical
problem — a genuinely different skillset from either supervision or credentialed professional work, and one
that maps naturally onto Inner Tepenia's existing Repair/Jury-Rigging-flavored perks.

## 4. Uniformed / Disciplined Service (chain of command, oath, operational readiness)

**Draws from:** 55-1000 / 55-2000 / 55-3000 (military, all three), 33-3000 (law enforcement), 33-2000
(firefighting), 33-1000 (protective service supervisors).

**The shared shape:** hierarchical command structure, physical/tactical training, high-stakes split-second
decision-making, and an identity built around duty and service rather than individual career advancement.

## 5. Direct Public-Facing / Customer Service

**Draws from:** 41-2000 (retail sales), 35-3000 (food/beverage service), 39-5000 (personal appearance), 39-6000
/ 39-7000 (hospitality/tour guiding), 43-4000 (information/record clerks who deal directly with the public).

**The shared shape:** constant, repetitive interaction with strangers, patience and de-escalation as a core
job skill, and performance under a kind of social pressure very different from technical or physical work.

## 6. Scientific / Research / Analytical

**Draws from:** 19-1000 / 19-2000 / 19-3000 (life, physical, social scientists), 19-4000 (science technicians),
15-2000 (mathematical scientists), 17-2000 (engineers, overlapping with Skilled Manual Trade above).

**The shared shape:** hypothesis-driven thinking, methodical rigor, comfort with uncertainty and iteration,
and an identity built around *understanding* a system rather than operating or fixing it.

## 7. Caregiving / Vulnerable-Population Work

**Draws from:** 31-1100 / 31-2000 / 31-9000 (healthcare support), 21-1000 (counselors/social workers), 39-4000
(funeral service), 21-2000 (religious workers), portions of 39-9000 (childcare).

**The shared shape:** sustained emotional labor, trust built over repeated contact with people at vulnerable
moments, and a confidentiality/bedside-manner skillset distinct from clinical credentialing itself (this
archetype is about the *caregiving*, not the medical license — see archetype 2 for that facet).

## 8. Working With Animals

**Draws from:** 29-1000 (veterinarians, a facet of that group), 39-2000 (animal care/training), portions of
45-2000 (livestock work), 19-1000 (wildlife biologists), 45-3000 (fishing/hunting/trapping).

**The shared shape:** nonverbal handling skill, patience with an unpredictable subject, and physical risk that
comes from the animal itself rather than from tools, weather, or other people.

## 9. Outdoor / Elements-Exposed Work

**Draws from:** 45-2000 / 45-3000 / 45-4000 (agriculture, fishing, forestry), 47-2000 / 47-5000 (construction,
extraction), 37-3000 (grounds maintenance), 53-2000 / 53-5000 (pilots, mariners, partially).

**The shared shape:** direct, sustained exposure to weather and terrain as a core job condition — this
archetype maps almost directly onto Inner Tepenia's own Frostlands/Survival-flavored systems already in
place, making it one of the cheapest, most natural cross-cutting hooks to actually build.

## 10. Vehicle / Heavy Equipment Operator

**Draws from:** 53-2000 (air), 53-3000 (motor vehicle), 53-4000 (rail), 53-5000 (water), 53-7000 (material
moving equipment), portions of 47-5000 / 51-8000 (heavy industrial/extraction equipment).

**The shared shape:** licensed operation of complex machinery, direct responsibility for cargo or passengers,
and a specific kind of sustained-attention, procedural-checklist mindset. **Note:** per the earlier
Piloting-skill decision this session, Inner Tepenia's own turn-based format means this archetype currently has
no in-context mechanical expression — same caveat applies here as it did there; flavor/roleplay only until
Outer Tepenia.

## 11. Creative / Expressive Occupations

**Draws from:** 27-1000 (art/design), 27-2000 (entertainers/performers), 27-3000 / 27-4000 (media and
communication, and the equipment behind it).

**The shared shape:** public creation or performance, a portfolio/reputation built over time rather than a
credential, and an identity oriented around audience and craft rather than procedure or duty.

## 12. Clerical / Information-Processing / Office-Based

**Draws from:** 43-1000 through 43-9000 (the whole office/administrative support major group), 13-1000
(business operations specialists), 15-1200 (computer occupations, overlapping with archetype 6).

**The shared shape:** indoor, sedentary, systems- and paperwork-literate work — precision and recordkeeping as
the core skill, a strong thematic fit for Inner Tepenia's Investigation-flavored skills (Repair, Lockpick,
Medicine already sit here; Cryptography and Hacking are adjacent).

## 13. Sales / Persuasion-Based

**Draws from:** 41-3000 / 41-4000 (sales representatives), 11-2000 (advertising/marketing/PR managers),
portions of 41-9000 (real estate, insurance).

**The shared shape:** negotiation, rapport-building, and performance under commission-driven or quota-driven
pressure — distinct from Direct Public-Facing Service above (that's about *volume and patience*, this is about
*persuasion and close rate*).

## 14. Precision Technical Support (assists a credentialed professional, without holding the credential)

**Draws from:** 17-3000 (drafters/engineering technicians), 19-4000 (science technicians, overlapping with
archetype 6), 29-2000 (health technologists), 23-2000 (legal support/paralegals).

**The shared shape:** deep domain literacy and exacting precision in service of someone else's licensed
judgment call — a distinct professional identity from both the credentialed professional (archetype 2) and
the general clerical worker (archetype 12).

---

## Coverage Note

These 14 archetypes are not mutually exclusive and don't need to be — a player roleplaying a Surgeon can pull
from Licensed Professional (2), Caregiving (7), and Precision Technical Support (14, if playing the "assisted
by" angle) all at once, depending on which facet of the job they want to lean into. The goal of this file is
giving the eventual perk/hook design pass a small, reusable set of shapes to build against, rather than
starting fresh for each of the 98 minor groups (or worse, all 867 detailed occupations) individually.

## Next Steps (not started)

Same status as the companion files — this is cataloging/synthesis only, no perks or traits have been designed
from this yet. The natural next step is a design pass that picks a subset of these 14 archetypes (or all of
them) and works out what an actual in-character perk/trait/dialogue hook looks like for each — likely a much
smaller, more tractable task now than tackling 98 minor groups head-on.
