# Cross-Category Meta-Archetypes × Concordia's 13 Districts

**Companion file to `SOC_Cross_Category_Commonalities.md`.** That file defines 14 cross-cutting occupational
archetypes drawn from the SOC manual. This file matches each archetype against Concordia's 13 established
districts (per `Districts/District_Canon_Reference.md`), grounded in each district's own actual identity,
culture, and inhabitants — not forced or evenly distributed. Districts are listed per archetype in rough
strength order (Strong fit → Moderate fit → Present but minor), and weak/absent fits are simply omitted
rather than padded in. The reverse mapping (which archetypes touch a given district) falls out naturally from
reading across all 14 sections.

**Future content-type marking, added 2026-07-26 — flagged for later, not built yet:** each tier below is now
explicitly tagged with the kind of future content it's earmarked to seed, once actual design work begins
(nothing in this file triggers that work — it's a marker for later, not a build instruction now):
- **Strong** matches → basis for a future **Under-Questline** (per `District_Under_Questline_Design_Method.md`'s
  existing non-main-quest structure).
- **Moderate** matches → basis for a future **Sidequest** — confirmed by the developer as a genuinely new,
  formally distinct tier in the project's questline taxonomy, separate from Under-Questline. Its own design
  method doesn't exist yet; see the flag added to `District_Under_Questline_Design_Method.md` itself.
- **Present but minor** matches → basis for future **general, minor, in-the-world side-content** (flavor text,
  incidental NPCs, ambient dialogue — not a discrete quest structure at all). **Working interpretation, not yet
  confirmed:** treated here as a fourth, non-quest ambient-content category, distinct from the "true
  side-content" tier `District_Under_Questline_Design_Method.md` already declares out of scope (that tier is
  about hard-to-find *quests*; this is about flavor that isn't a quest at all) — worth the developer's direct
  confirmation before treating that distinction as settled.

**Critical standing rule, per the developer's own explicit instruction:** none of the tier-marking above
changes, overrides, or gets merged into any previously-established methodology for deriving Under-Questlines,
Sidequests, or other content. This file (and the archetype/district matching it documents) is an
**additional reference input** to bring *into* whichever established method actually applies when real design
work begins — never a replacement methodology in its own right. `District_Main_Questlines.md` and
`District_Under_Questline_Design_Method.md` remain the authoritative processes; this file just adds one more
thing worth considering alongside their own existing input-gathering steps.

**Confirmed both ways, per the developer's own observation:** every one of the 14 archetypes below reaches at
least one district, and — reading across all 14 — every one of the 13 districts is reached by at least one
archetype. No district is a dead end for this system, and no archetype is homeless.

## 1. Supervisor / Foreman

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Capricorn (guild foremen, production planners — the district's entire social identity is built
around earned rank and crew leadership), Aries (shift supervisors; "shift crews are the primary social unit,"
loyalty to crew supersedes everything else), Virgo (shift supervisors, logistics coordinators running the
Undergrid's constant crisis response).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Leo ("loyalty to one's troupe/crew" is explicit, arena promoters and festival organizers run
real teams), Sagittarius (explorer guild leadership, caravan/expedition leads), Libra (department heads,
council staff structures), Pisces (syndicate/operation leads within the black-market hierarchy), Gemini (news
desk/network shift leads), Hub (transit coordinators, council facilitators — coordination is the district's
whole function).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Cancer (care-team leads), Taurus (small-scale hydroponic operation leads), Aquarius
(research cluster leads, though project-based rather than crew-based), Scorpio (rebirth clinic directors).

## 2. Licensed / Credentialed Professional

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Cancer (nurses, therapists, doctors — the whole district exists around clinical/emotional
credentialed care), Libra (lawyers, judges, policy analysts — the district's founding purpose), Scorpio
(trauma therapists, existential counselors, licensed rebirth-technique practitioners), Capricorn (master
engineers, the apprenticeship system is effectively a credentialing ladder).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Aquarius (the University of Concordia is here, but the district's "rogue" ethos actively cuts
against formal credentialing as a value — plenty of Aquarius practitioners are credentialed, but the
culture doesn't especially respect that fact), Gemini (data scientists/analysts, though the district's own
culture rewards speed and reach over formal expertise), Aries (engineers, though the culture frames them as
hardened instinctive operators rather than credentialed professionals).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Taurus (retired engineers appear in its inhabitant list, but it's not a district
identity), Virgo (systems analysts exist, but Virgo's whole culture transmits expertise informally,
person-to-person, specifically *not* through credentialing).

## 3. Skilled Manual Trade

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Capricorn (The Yards — this is the district's entire reason for existing), Virgo (maintenance
engineers, tunnel inspectors — literally keeps the city's infrastructure alive by hand), Aries (power-core
engineers and technicians, "push it until it breaks" hands-on culture).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Taurus (household-scale insulation engineering is explicitly called out as rivaling Capricorn's
industrial teams; maintenance bots specialized in structural reinforcement), Sagittarius (frontier
construction, expedition equipment upkeep, "recovered or jury-rigged units from lost expeditions").

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Cancer (hydroponic systems maintenance), Aquarius (hands-on experimental
prototyping/engineering, though R&D-flavored rather than trade-flavored), Pisces (fluid-frame modification and
underground clinic tech work is genuinely hands-on/mechanical), Gemini (Arcanet hardware technicians).

## 4. Uniformed / Disciplined Service

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Aries (barracks culture, shift crews, "warrior-engineer" identity forged in wartime — the
closest thing to a standing quasi-military culture in the city).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Libra (security checkpoints staffed by "polite, professional guards who are more dangerous than
they appear," judicial/enforcement infrastructure), Sagittarius (explorer guild has real discipline and
hierarchy, frontier-defense wartime history), Capricorn (guild apprenticeship systems carry real disciplinary
weight and binding ceremony), Virgo (disciplined crisis-response protocols, though culturally informal
otherwise).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Hub (security/compliance staff exist here as the neutral administrative center, though
not framed as a disciplined culture in its own right).

## 5. Direct Public-Facing / Customer Service

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Leo (the entire district's identity is built around audience and performance; "no
audience/performer distinction"), Hub (merchants catering to all districts, transit coordinators — literally
built to serve everyone who passes through).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Pisces (dream-tech dens, information brokers dealing face-to-face with clients from every
district), Gemini (journalists and information brokers whose whole function is public-facing), Sagittarius
(traders, caravan merchants).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Taurus (small-scale hydroponic farmers and comfort artisans selling directly to
neighbors), Cancer (community-facing caregiving, though framed as care rather than service-industry
transaction), Libra (diplomats/lobbyists are public-facing, but toward an elite/institutional public, not a
general one).

## 6. Scientific / Research / Analytical

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Aquarius (the district's entire founding purpose), Gemini (data archaeologists, information
verification specialists — analysis is the district's core skill, even if its culture undervalues rigor in
favor of speed).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Libra (policy analysts, resource allocation officers doing real analytical work), Capricorn
(production planners, quality-control auditors), Scorpio (developed its rebirth techniques through genuine
trial-and-error methodology, described in explicitly clinical/experimental terms), Sagittarius ("philosophical
debate halls," rigorous applied thinking about expansion and survival, resource-scanning prospecting).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Aries (engineers, though the culture is instinctive/hands-on rather than
lab-analytical), Cancer (medical science underlies the caregiving, though the district's own self-image is
compassion rather than analysis), Virgo (systems analysts appear in its inhabitant list).

## 7. Caregiving / Vulnerable-Population Work

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Cancer (the district's entire reason for existing — "No One Left Behind"), Scorpio (trauma
therapists, rebirth facilitation for the psychologically shattered).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Taurus (multi-generational family care, "duty to family/bonded units" as a core cultural value),
Pisces ("empathy-overloaded companion units," caring for burnouts and addicts as an actual community function,
not just tolerating them).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Aquarius (the district genuinely believes it's "trying to save the world," a systemic
rather than personal form of care), Hub (nobody's official caregiving mission, but the Hub-born "adaptability"
culture includes real informal community support for the district's many transients).

## 8. Working With Animals

**The weakest-supported archetype across the current lore** — Inner Tepenia's Antarctic setting doesn't
establish significant animal populations, and no district treats animal work as part of its identity the way
a real-world farming or veterinary community would. Worth flagging as a genuine content gap rather than
force-fitting matches that aren't really there.

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Sagittarius (frontier scouts/prospectors are the most plausible point of contact with
whatever wildlife exists, and caravan animals if any are ever established), Cancer and Taurus (small-scale
hydroponic operations could plausibly include limited small-animal husbandry for food, though this isn't
established anywhere in current canon).

## 9. Outdoor / Elements-Exposed Work

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Sagittarius (The Frostlands — "outside the domes is lethal" is the district's organizing fact),
Capricorn (The Yards are confirmed partially exterior, fabrication yards exposed to the elements).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Aries (extreme heat/cold tolerance built into the district's machinery and worker culture, even
if the Power Core itself is an enclosed facility).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Taurus (the district's whole insulation-engineering culture exists *because of*
constant engagement with cold exposure, even though residents are more insulated-in than exposed-out).

## 10. Vehicle / Heavy Equipment Operator

**Same caveat as the original Piloting-skill decision this session: Inner Tepenia's turn-based format gives
this archetype no in-context mechanical expression regardless of district — flavor/roleplay only until Outer
Tepenia.**

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Sagittarius (caravans, expedition vehicles, Rastras — the frontier's whole logistics model).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Capricorn (industrial equipment operation in the Yards), Aries (heavy equipment for power
systems), Hub (the Grand Transit Exchange is the city's literal vehicle/transit nexus).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Virgo (tunnel maintenance vehicles/equipment plausible, not explicitly established).

## 11. Creative / Expressive Occupations

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Leo (the district's entire identity), Pisces (illusion artists, dream-weavers, escapist
performers — creative expression as both art and vice).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Sagittarius (storytelling is explicitly called out as "the district's primary cultural
currency," with real social status attached), Gemini (journalists and writers, a genuine creative-expressive
facet of the information-broker identity).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Aquarius (visionary theorists could include artistic experimentalists, though the
district's core identity is scientific rather than artistic), Scorpio (the mask culture and ritual practices
edge into performance-art territory even though the district doesn't think of itself that way).

## 12. Clerical / Information-Processing / Office-Based

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Libra (bureaucratic administrators, policy analysts — the district's founding purpose), Gemini
(Arcanet technicians, information clerks — the district's core identity).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Capricorn (production planners and quality-control auditors are heavily recordkeeping-driven),
Virgo ("maintains meticulous documentation of everything," a specific, named cultural trait), Hub (transit
coordinators, neutral administrators).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Aquarius (research documentation, ethical review board paperwork).

## 13. Sales / Persuasion-Based

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Pisces (black-market dealers and information brokers, where every transaction is itself a
negotiation), Sagittarius (traders, caravan merchants — commerce is core to frontier life).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Leo (arena promoters and luxury service workers selling experience and access), Gemini
(information brokers, the "Truth Markets" institution is literally a trading floor for data), Hub (merchants
catering to every district that passes through).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Taurus (small-scale hydroponic farmers selling produce), Libra (lobbyists are
persuasion-based, though toward an elite/institutional audience rather than open commerce).

## 14. Precision Technical Support

**Strong (Under-Questline basis):** Aquarius (lab assistants and research support roles beneath the district's senior scientists).

**Moderate (Sidequest basis):** Cancer (nursing assistants and integration facilitators supporting doctors/therapists), Scorpio
(confession-recording archivists and shadow-augmentation specialists supporting senior rebirth practitioners),
Capricorn (quality-control auditors supporting master engineers), Virgo (systems analysts supporting senior
maintenance engineers).

**Present but minor (general in-world side-content basis):** Libra (paralegal-equivalent aides supporting judges and diplomats), Gemini (junior
Arcanet techs supporting senior data architects/archivists).
