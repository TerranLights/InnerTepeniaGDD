# Witness to War - watch list of clips that look humor-relevant (Tier C-type note)

**Made 2026-10-05 by the Tier B harvest. Companion to `TierB_storycorps_witness_awm.md`.**

**What this is.** WtW publishes video only. This list names clips whose *site-written descriptions* (or WtW's own "Humorous Events" tag) suggest humor as a practice: jokes under fire, pranks, nicknames, laughter near death, humor in captivity, humor after service. **Nothing here was watched or transcribed; relevance is judged from one-to-three-sentence descriptions only, so expect false positives and misses.** The full set of 841 candidates (all clips with a humor word in the description plus all 662 clips tagged Humorous Events) with descriptions is in `source_texts/oral_history/witness_to_war/witness_to_war_catalog_humor_candidates.tsv` (local only, not in git).

**URL pattern.** `https://www.witnesstowar.org/combat_stories/<path>/<id>` where `<path>` is `WWII`, `Korea`, `Vietnam` or `Other` (Other = Cold War, Gulf War, Iraq, Afghanistan, other conflicts). The table gives `path/id`. **To find more by hand:** open https://www.witnesstowar.org/advanced_search , tick *Experience > Humorous Events* (662 clips) and optionally a War or Branch filter, then click Show Result; or use the top-right keyword box ("humor", "prank", "nickname", "laugh", "joke", "funny").

**Cue column.** `F12` = carries WtW's Humorous Events tag. Other cues are my automatic reading of the description: prank, nickname, dead/wounded, command, enemy, captivity, coping, home.
