# Palmer City: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group, and never included or excluded for being in South Asia. The city's name, the street's name, any namesake and the real station's history are treated as flavor, and no facet or candidate is built on them. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the destruction, current-status and legacy-in-Concordia material ignored as not an input), this city's five existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and the first lines of the Mirny file for layout only. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets**
- **F9 Socially flat footing** has only a few candidates. Real places where ground is equally costly for everyone are rare; most unstable-ground cities still have a better-placed quarter. Christiania, the Uros islands, Burning Man and Letchworth reach it from different directions, none exactly.
- **F13 Economy beyond hospitality** is open in the spec, so the candidates stop at entertainment and hospitality labor. A later pass could look at what supplies an island entertainment city (ferry trades, provisioning, instrument and record repair) once the spec settles it.
- **Governance** gets no facet: the spec says nothing about how the city is run beyond its neutrality.
- **Humans and robots at near parity (F12)** has no real-world parallel beyond the mixed-city candidates; that is expected and left open.
- **No polar night and gray light (F7)** is covered only through the wet subpolar ports; no candidate was chosen for light alone.

**Candidates to verify**
- #14 Ærø's wedding trade and #15 the Cyprus civil weddings: the scale and how they are organized.
- #27 "Cunard Yanks": how much of the story of records brought in by seamen is documented and how much is legend.
- #31 Jazzhus Montmartre: dates, locations and which musicians settled.
- #52 Molde, #73 Madeline Island's shoulder-season transport, #75 Lord Howe Island's cap figure.
- #72 Longyearbyen: the current state of house relocation for permafrost and avalanche risk.
- #8 Galveston's distinct eras (immigration port, gambling era, 1865 announcement) should be separated by the research round.
- #82, #84, #87 are kinds of place whose examples still have to be chosen and checked.
