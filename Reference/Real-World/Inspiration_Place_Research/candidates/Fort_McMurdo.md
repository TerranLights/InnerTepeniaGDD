## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets**
- **F11, the clerics.** The spec puts clerics in the founding sequence right after the miners. The only candidates are the mining brotherhoods of Ouro Preto and the church presence of Potosí. Nothing yet speaks to clerics as administrators in a working town. Left open rather than forced.
- **F12, the documentary start.** York Factory's post journals and Adak's inherited base only touch it. No candidate yet shows a place running on documents left by earlier operators.
- **F16, heritage ground.** Only Dawson City. The heroic-age pride is specific, and its real counterparts in polar history were avoided on purpose.
- **F14, wind.** Wellington and Punta Arenas cover wind as a daily nuisance. Neither covers whiteout under a clear sky.
- **F17, "hard to narrate."** Candidates share the reputation but not the specific trait of being remembered through objects rather than stories.
