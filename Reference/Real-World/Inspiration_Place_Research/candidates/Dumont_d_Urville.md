Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. ## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec says nothing. #59 (an open assembly) and #87 (single-problem local bodies) hint at shapes but were not chosen for governance. Nothing is proposed here because nothing is given.
- **Work rhythms.** The spec gives no work pattern beyond maritime trade and the egg harvest. #13, #48 and #50 touch the crossing's effect on the working day; the research round should not invent more.
- **What the city withholds.** No spec content; left open.
