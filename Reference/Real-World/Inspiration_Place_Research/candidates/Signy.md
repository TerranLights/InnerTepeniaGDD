# Signy: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone. No candidate is included or excluded for the nation it sits in, for sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder group, or for being in South Asia. The real-world station's name and operator are GPS only and are not a reason for any row. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the War Impact Note, the post-war status line and the post-war clauses of Economy and Legacy ignored), this city's twelve existing picks (split across `Signy_a.md` and `Signy_b.md`, treated as one list), `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. ## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec says nothing on how Signy is governed. Only St Kilda (51), Newfoundland outports (8) and Eigg (42, power cap) speak to it, plus the mooring-permit systems of Amsterdam (19) and Seattle (21). A research round should look for how floating communities allocate berths and settle disputes.
- **Arts and music.** Thin: Sausalito (20), scrimshaw (41), Fair Isle knitting (49). The spec gives no arts life, so nothing stronger is supported yet.
- **Food beyond the marine diet.** Only general fishing-culture picks; no candidate speaks directly to the monotony of an all-marine diet or how a place varies it.
- **Wet climate and damp.** No candidate addresses drainage, damp-proofing or freeze-thaw life on a boat directly; Faroe (12), Kerguelen (60) and Shetland (7) are climate cousins only.
- **Leisure on the home island.** The spec places leisure on Coronation but says nothing about it; Herschel Island (24) is the only candidate about leisure in a moored community.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- 5 Tenerife: no reason note from the developer; the parallel is a guess.
- 14 Cửa Vạn: the scale and timing of relocation ashore need checking.
- 22 Bangkok raft houses: the share of the population that lived afloat is uncertain.
- 24 Herschel Island: the scale of the wintering fleet and the winter social life need verifying.
- 27 Mission to Deep Sea Fishermen: the range of services on the mission ships is from memory.
- 32 Santa Cruz del Islote: density figures are often overstated in popular accounts.
- 44 Graciosa and 48 Utsira: whether either system is still running as described.
- 46 Saltstraumen: the claim of the strongest tidal current should be checked against other channels.
- 69 Vega: the details of the seasonal move and eider shelters.
- 80 Shackleton Autumn School: whether it still runs under that name.
- 82 Lantau Link: the exact wind-closure rules.
- 84 Epos library boat: whether it still operates.
