## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **F13 light.** The site has no polar night, so sun-return festivals are a poor fit and were left out on purpose. The ~2.7 h winter day and the refracted December sun have no direct candidate; #86–#88 cover the melt and ice calendar only.
- **F6 walled by ice and glacier.** The existing seaside candidates (#13–#26) supply the sealing, but none is sealed by ice on all sides with a lake country inside. Nothing found fits that whole shape.
- **F12 power.** #33, #58–#61 and #30 bracket the question (low-power greenhouses, cheap-heat towns, megaproject power) but no candidate is an off-grid farming region at this scale.
- **F8 / F9 robot half of the population.** There is no real-world parallel by design. #50, #57, #78 and #80 could carry an `[R]` flag (shared upkeep and repair cultures) in research, flag only.
- **Governance.** The spec says nothing. Water boards and irrigators' courts (#1, #45–#49), co-ops (#80, #82, #83) and state farms (#27–#29) show possible shapes, but none was chosen for governance.
- **Arts and music.** Thin: #14, #62, #81, #82 and #85 only. "Thin" may be the answer; no forced fit.
- **Food culture as eaten.** Only #16, #40, #72 and #84 point at it.
- **The founding.** Open by ruling; nothing here is proposed for it.
- **The phosphorus import itself.** Phosphate-supplier regions other than #75 (for example in North Africa) were left out because of sensitive contested-territory histories; the research round may decide whether to add one.
