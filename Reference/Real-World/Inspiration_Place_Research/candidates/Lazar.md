# Lazar: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone. No candidate is included or excluded because of the nation it is in, the nation of the city's founders, or whether it is in South Asia. The spec's older founding text and the real stations' names were not used as a reason for any candidate. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the Status line's war material, the Population Note's war rationale, and the "Current Status / Damage" section ignored, as not an input), this city's six existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no developer-added rows. No other city's material was read.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F13, governance.** The spec says nothing about how the city is run. Rows 9, 12 and 13 (district councils keyed to old boundaries, demergers) are the only governance-shaped candidates; a later round could look at federated city models more deliberately.
- **F13, arts and music.** Only rows 15, 67 and 68 speak to how an arts scene starts here. No candidate ties arts to the oasis or the lakes themselves.
- **F12, the economy.** Rows 26, 27, 74, 83 and 84 are possible drivers for a hub or service city, but none is a strong single match, which is what the spec itself says. An empty slot is a valid result here.
- **F3, the lakes as a water source.** Rows 28–33 cover shared-water institutions in hot oases; no candidate covers lakes that are frozen most of the year and liquid only in summer, used as a city's supply. The real Antarctic ice-free oases were deliberately not proposed, because they risk overlapping other locations' own sites.
- **F7, a human and robot population of near-equal size.** No real-world parallel exists; left open on purpose.
- **K7 work rhythms and K14 competence communities** are thin overall.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- Row 43: ropes or chains along Trieste streets for the bora. I believe this is real but have not checked it.
- Row 48: ropes along Punta Arenas sidewalks. Least certain item in the table.
- Row 58: the Times Square zoning requirement for illuminated signs. I believe it dates from the late 1980s.
- Row 53: the Midnight Sun Game's start year (about 1906).
- Row 35: whether Kangerlussuaq's climate is shaped by katabatic flow as I describe it.
- Row 8: the exact merger dates for Wuhan.
- Row 75: whether the shortening of the Los Angeles name maps onto the demographic shift as neatly as stated.
- Row 3: Vancouver has no developer note, so its facets are inferred.
- Row 6: the existing London Blitz pick rests on war damage, which this drafting run excludes as an input. It is kept because it is the developer's own pick.
