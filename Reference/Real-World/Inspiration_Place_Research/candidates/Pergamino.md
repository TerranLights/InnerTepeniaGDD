# Pergamino: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group, and never included or excluded for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. The city's name, its meaning, its former name and namesake, and the real station's history are treated as flavor and are not the basis of any facet or candidate.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the Status rationale, Legacy, Current Status / Destruction and other war-damage or post-war material ignored), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no developer-added rows. The Mirny file was read for layout only (first 20 lines). No other city's material was read.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets**
- **F11 (humans and robots in near-equal numbers):** no real-world parallel exists for the robot half. The multilingual candidates (Trieste, Nuuk, the immigrant hotels, the Chinese Maritime Customs Service) speak only to many origins and languages.
- **F9 (density ~8,000/km² on a rugged island):** the island-city candidates (Malé, Monaco, Iquitos) differ sharply in climate or setting. Nothing found pairs this density with a wet, cold, glaciated island.
- **F6 (calm as what the city is known for):** the islands listed have coherent cultures, but none is clearly known *for calm*. A later round could look for places whose reputation is quietness itself.
- **Daily life of an archive and customs workforce:** apart from reading rooms, coffeehouses and the Bletchley-type secret-keeping towns, there is little on leisure, meeting places or work rhythms for clerical populations. K10 and K7 for clerks are a likely gap.
- **Food:** only stockfish and klippfisk towns, the Faroes and the Azores touch K12. Wet-climate fishing-town food culture needs more.

**Material deliberately not used**
- The founding-era passage about logs and manuals left by earlier station operators was not used, because the real station's site history is not an input.
- The war-targeting rationale (Status, Legacy, Current Status) was ignored. F2 rests only on the developer vision in Character & Culture.
- The sealing and whaling history in Geographic Basis was not used, because it is site history.

**Candidates whose details are unsure**
- Row 3: the date and completeness of the Wolfenbüttel court's move to Braunschweig.
- Row 4: how far upper-class passengers were inspected aboard rather than on Ellis Island.
- Row 15: how the Paris Okhrana files reached Hoover.
- Row 19: the 1377 quarantine date.
- Row 26: whether the Shōsōin's construction actually buffers humidity, a claim that has been contested.
- Row 28: the Tianyi Pavilion's family rules as I recalled them.
- Row 35: the dispersal-office locations.
- Row 40: the medieval date at Chaudes-Aigues.
- Row 41: whether Heimaey's lava heat scheme ran as described.
- Row 60: the Bryggen Kontor's residence rules, including the rule that merchants stay unmarried.
- Row 66: the reason Turku's central functions moved.
- Row 76: the terms under which the manuscripts were returned.
- Row 25: the Book of Kells' place of making is debated. The Iona-to-Kells move of the community is better attested than the book's origin.
