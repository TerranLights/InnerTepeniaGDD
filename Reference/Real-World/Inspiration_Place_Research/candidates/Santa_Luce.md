# Santa Luce: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world stations or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (war-damage and post-war sections ignored), this city's four existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no Santa Luce section. No other city's material was read.

**Exclusions applied for this city:** the spec carries a rename notice and older text written around a Finnish-Swedish founding that has been superseded. Neither that founding, nor the two real stations' names, nor any founding nation (old or current) is used as a reason for any candidate. The spec passage "built for winter, used only in summer" is station history tied to the superseded founding, and it is not used as a facet (see section 3). Facets come from geography, function, access, scale, economy and daily life only.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Existing picks against the facets found** (all four were chosen for an older "two peoples in one small town" idea, which this draft does not use as a facet):
- **Baarle-Hertog / Baarle-Nassau:** fits **none** of F1–F9 on its own. Its value was the interlocking two-administration structure, which belongs to a two-community facet the spec still mentions but marks "not yet derived." It also sits far below F5's scale. Keep only if the developer re-derives a two-community facet on the current founding; otherwise a strike candidate.
- **Sark:** fits F8 (household-scale governance) and nothing else. Scale contradicts F5 badly (a few hundred people against 0.6–1 million).
- **Whittier:** still fits F8 and partly F3/F9 (a whole community under one roof in a hard climate). Scale mismatch with F5, but the "one building" logic could translate to a block or district.
- **Ny-Ålesund:** fits better now than before, but for different reasons: F7 (a research settlement) and F4 (polar night, though longer than Santa Luce's). Its original reason (several national stations in one town) leans on station heritage and on the superseded two-station picture; that reason should be dropped if it stays.

**Scale contradiction in the spec (F5, F8):** the census gives about 1.03 million and then about 0.61 million, while the prose calls the city "small," says "everyone knew everyone," and says negotiation happened household by household. These cannot both be literal. Town-scale candidates (Sark, Whittier, Landsgemeinde, Acoma) suit the prose; city-scale candidates (Porto Alegre, kampung RT/RW, chōnaikai, Gemeindebau, Winnipeg, Edmonton) suit the numbers. The neighborhood-institution candidates are offered as the bridge: a large city that lives at neighborhood scale. This needs a developer ruling, not a research answer.

**Temperature inconsistency in the spec (F3):** the header gives a mean of about −15 °C and the measured monthly means do average to about −15.0 °C, but the WIND vs COLD box says −25.0 °C. Candidates were chosen for the −15 °C figure.

**Excluded spec material:** "built for winter, used only in summer" describes the real stations' staffing history and the superseded founding's reasoning. Site history is not an input, so no "seasonal outpost turned permanent" facet or candidates were drafted.

**Thin or empty facets:**
- **Near-even human and robot populations (F6):** no real-world parallel; left open.
- **Internal subnet trade (F7):** the spec says only "internal Halley subnet trade," with no goods named. No candidates drafted; an empty slot, not a gap to force.
- **Governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed beyond neighborhood-scale negotiation; F8 candidates cover civic life, not formal government.
- **"What the position offered" (F7):** the spec does not say what the nunatak provides. The rock-research candidates (rows 30–33) assume exposed rock is the resource; this is an inference.
- **F9 (leisure, arts, food):** wholly inferred from climate and light; the spec gives nothing. Treat rows 54, 59–61 and 70–75 as the most speculative.
- **F5 low profile:** rows 8 and 62 are weak; a city of this size that is minor in its network has few well-documented real parallels.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described:** row 14 (the society's name, the huts, the regular spring expedition); row 15 (the fireworks funding share); row 2 (which camps sit on nunataks); row 24 (the nationality count); row 29 (current enforcement); row 31 (town naming after mergers); row 36 (around-the-clock screenings); row 42 (whether Toonik Tyme is a sun-return festival); row 43 (Viganella's mirror); row 46 (the snow castle's origin); row 53 (the ropes); row 56 (the pool and garden); row 58 (the dry-riverbed boat race's current status); row 9 (whether participatory budgeting continues).

**Nation clustering check:** rows 26, 33, 35, 36, 43, 51 and 68 sit in nations tied to the superseded or the current founding. Each was included on structure alone, as instructed; the reviewer may strike any that look like a pull toward those nations.

**Wind overstatement risk:** the spec says cold and wind are "neither extreme." Rows 50–53 are about a dominant wind direction and drifting snow, not severity; if read as severity they overstate the city.
