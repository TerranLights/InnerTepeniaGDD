# Ariun Nuur: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or population group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the status line, the "Current Status" section and other post-war material ignored), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. No other city's material was read. Real sites on the Antarctic continent were left out on purpose, so that no candidate doubles as another location's real-world basis.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec leaves it open. #30 (company-run), #85 (agency-owned) and #56 (a member foundation) hint at shapes, but none was chosen to settle governance. Nothing is proposed because nothing is given.
- **Food.** The spec says nothing. #8, #65 and #66 are leads on shared eating and growing food only. No cold-plateau food tradition was proposed.
- **Arts and leisure.** The spec says nothing. #32, #33, #34, #40 and #43 show arts and leisure that scientists built for themselves in isolated science towns. Per the no-forced-fit law, a quiet, focused city may simply have little.
- **F10 (shared outpost on borrowed power).** Only #22 and #71 touch it, and only partly. A real-world joint facility set up by two remote institutions at a waypoint between them was not found from memory. It may not need a pick, since it is a relation the spec already sets.
- **F9 (two approach profiles).** Thin by design: highway character takes no picks, and #28 and #83 touch only the access shape.
- **F4 (sealed ecosystem).** Real sealed ecosystems are few. #20 is the strongest. Antarctic subglacial lakes were left out on purpose.
- **Robot majority (about two robots per human).** There is no real-world parallel by design. #4, #24, #26, #61 and #62 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance, test and repair cultures) in research, flag only.
- **Demonym and name.** A naming matter, outside this task.
