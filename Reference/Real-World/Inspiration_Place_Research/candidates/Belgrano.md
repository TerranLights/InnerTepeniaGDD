# Belgrano: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group, and never included or excluded for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **Governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed. No candidates proposed; an empty slot, not a gap to fill.
- **Humans and robots in near-equal number (F12):** no real-world parallel exists. Left open.
- **Leisure, arts and food life:** the spec is silent. Everything under F14 comes from the existing picks, and food has only #14, #34, #60 and #91. A later round could look for what aircrews, port workers and medics actually eat and where they gather.
- **F13 (civic name from homeland history):** only one candidate, on purpose, because this facet sits close to founder nationality. Treat it as weak.
- **F10 (maps and the archive):** the spec leaves open whether an archival institution existed. Candidates #53–56 suggest kinds of institution without settling that question.

**Deliberately left out**
- The ruins, the decline after the war, and the "archive outlasted the city" framing are post-war status, which is not an input. Closure-and-decline material in #4, #6 and #12 is flagged in the care notes.
- The open question about a cross-subnet exile network is about founders and other cities, so it was not used.
- A real Antarctic research station built on skis on a moving ice shelf would be a strong F7 parallel, but it shares its name with a sibling city in this setting. It was left out to avoid confusion; #71 covers the same physical idea.
- Highway character is excluded by ruling; #69 is used only for trucking work culture.

- #37 Rega's membership share, #38 Norwegian Air Ambulance's founding story, #44 Tromsø's founding date and purpose, #46 St John Ambulance's origins.
- #42 the exact name and remit of the Bolzano institute.
- #52 the number of Subic Bay volunteers and the order of events.
- #54 how the Padrón Real was actually updated.
- #63 the current seasonal split between Narvik and Luleå.
- #73 which body administered sand-drift control at Skagen.
- #78 the name and founding date of Murmansk's festival.
- #9 Kangerlussuaq's role may have changed since the new capital runway opened.
