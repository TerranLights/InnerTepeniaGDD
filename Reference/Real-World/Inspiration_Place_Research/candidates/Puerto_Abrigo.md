# Puerto Abrigo: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone. None is included or excluded for its nation, for the nation of the real-world station, for the nation of the founders, or for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the Population/Destructibility Note, which deals with war damage and survival, was ignored as not an input), this city's existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no developer-added rows. No other city's material was read.

**Deliberately not used as data:** the city's name and its meaning; the real station's name, namesake and history (its wartime origin, its whaling-era use, and its museum and heritage role, including the heritage post office of the real site); the rename notice and any founder nation. The spec's Economy line "heritage/cultural significance" comes from the real site's museum role and is a revisit item in the spec itself (`DR-28`), so no facet was built on it.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F9 (road spur off the trunk line):** thin. The highway rules treat corridors as defined by what they connect, so only the physical texture of the link is a fit (rows 6, 31, 54). An empty-ish facet is a result.
- **F10, the half-robot share:** no real-world parallel exists for a community about half of whose members are robots; the rows under F10 speak only to tiny size and mixed origins. The `[R]` flags (rows 8, 52, 57) mark repair and maintenance cultures only.
- **F11 (open slots):** governance, work culture, leisure, arts, food and the demonym are not set in the spec. Rows under F11 show kinds of answer and must not be taken as decisions.
- **F8 (ordinary sky):** only two clean latitude-and-climate fits (rows 55, 56). Most mild maritime harbor towns in the table lie either farther from the pole or north of the Arctic Circle.

**Not used, on purpose**
- The war-damage and survival note (strategic irrelevance, misidentification with the neighbor) was not an input, so it has no facet and no new candidates. Existing row 4 is kept only because it is an existing pick.
- The Economy line's "heritage/cultural significance" and every museum, heritage, whaling-era or wartime fact about the real site were treated as flavor and not used.
- The existing picks file holds a placeholder line, `x [SECONDARY]`, with no place named. It is not a candidate and is left for the developer.

- Row 9: whether the Horta café's mail and message role is documented or only told.
- Row 10: Seyðisfjörður's population and whether its arts festival still runs.
- Row 11: Kristiansund's harbor ferry start date and the opera's founding year.
- Row 12: what mail the Hurtigruten carries now.
- Rows 17, 27, 28: dates and methods of the Tin Can Mail, the pigeon post and the St Kilda mailboats.
- Row 30: Stromness's private piers.
- Row 43: the number of houses inside the Arles amphitheater and when they were cleared.
- Row 49: Migingo's population.
- Row 53: the present state of the Lau Lagoon islets.
- Row 55: Nuuk's annual mean against this city's −1.7 °C.
