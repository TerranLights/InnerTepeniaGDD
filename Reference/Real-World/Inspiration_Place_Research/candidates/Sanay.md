# Sanay: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder group, and never excluded for the nation they sit in. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. Nothing here uses the station's name or the city's naming history as a reason for any candidate.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the war-damage, post-war status and endurance-through-damage material ignored), this city's four existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no Sanay section, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read.

**Tags used:** [EXISTING] = already one of the city's picks. [SPEC] = named in the city's own spec as a real-world model. K-tags follow `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **F14 Scale and makeup:** thin. The Census I to II drop has no cause stated in the spec, so nothing is proposed for it. The robot-human balance has no real-world parallel by nature. The multinational mix has only Winnipeg; candidates here risk sliding into nation matching, so I kept it light.
- **Governance:** the spec says almost nothing about how the city is governed beyond the legal tie to its port. The candidates for that tie (rows 5–14, 36) are structural, not about local civic life.
- **F7, the credit-versus-location split** (another city built the nexus and took the name): only Porthcurno and the cable stations touch it, loosely. No strong parallel for "the builders' town took the name."
- **F12 The vantage:** thin (Acoma, Mount Washington, SvalSat, Jungfraujoch).
- **Endurance through damage:** left out on purpose. It comes from the war-damage and post-war material, which is not an input.
- **The corridor (F3):** every corridor row is for physical texture only (shelter, marking, provisioning, convoy form, drift control), as the rules header requires. The truck stops and cafés are included for the trucking workforce's daily life, not for the road's character.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- Row 12 (Hamburg's current hold on Neuwerk), row 41 (current status of Pionen and Lefdal), row 47 (Mount Washington's exact rotation), row 59 (whether Willkomm-Höft still plays anthems), row 70 (what the Alice Springs memorial includes), row 74 (bunny chow's origin story), row 76 (the ship-paint story in La Boca, which may be partly legend), row 31 (current status of the Punta Arenas free zone).
- Row 17 (City Deep) and row 9 (Santos rail): I am confident of the general picture but not of current operations.
