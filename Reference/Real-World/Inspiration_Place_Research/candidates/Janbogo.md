## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F12 Spur and hub:** no strong candidate. Whittier (28) touches it only through its single access. Being off the main line but next to a junction may not need a real-world pick.
- **F14 Governance:** the spec does not say how the city was governed in peacetime. Only Hamburg (67) and the diwaniya (39) touch it, both loosely. Left open on purpose.
- **F11 Built on what was there:** only Split (79), plus Valdez's relocation (9) as a possible lead.
- **F2 Wind-kept open water:** no real-world port is kept open by wind alone. All the candidates are ice-free for other reasons (currents, latitude) except the North Water Polynya (18), which has no port city.

**Facet that may be out of scope**
- **F13 A ruin in view:** the neighbor's ruin appears in Geographic Basis and Notable Locations, but it is the result of a strike, so it may count as war-damage or post-war material, which this run excludes. Rows 80–85 are marked ⚠ so the developer can strike them as a block.

**Excluded on purpose**
- Picks whose main tie is the real station's nation (for example its bathhouse and café-room traditions) were left out. Rows that share a nation with a *population* group are kept only where the structural parallel stands on its own, and their care notes say so.
- Corridor-type picks (supply roads, ice roads, airlift routes) were left out under the corridor scope note. Rows 7, 15, 56 and 78 use only the port or town end.

**Unsure as described (verify first)**
- 29 Norilsk: whether the wind-closed block layout was deliberate and widespread.
- 26 Fermont: the wall's exact length and what it holds.
- 35 Bryggen: whether the halls were kept unheated by rule, and how the heated assembly rooms were used.
- 17 Senj: how the Adriatic road and bridge wind closures work.
- 19 Hammerfest: the details of the visitors' society.
- 23 Great Plains: how common the house-to-barn rope really was.
- 51 Russian cabmen's tea rooms as a distinct institution.
- 77 Landsbjörg: what share of its funding comes from fireworks.

- The climate section gives polar night as ~95 days (May 6 → Aug 8), while Gameplay Notes says ~64 days.
