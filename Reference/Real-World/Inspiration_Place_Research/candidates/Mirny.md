# Mirny: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec leaves it open. Candidates hint at three shapes (company-run as in #3, union-run as in #59, company town turned municipality as in #72) but none was chosen *for* governance. Nothing is proposed here because nothing is given.
- **Food.** The spec says nothing. #4, #36, #46, #76 and #82 are worker-food leads only.
- **Arts beyond the rough bars.** The spec says entertainment is minimal. #38, #55, #57 and #59 show arts that *grew from* work communities. Per the no-forced-fit law, "minimal" may simply be the answer.
- **Robot half of the population (F11).** There is no real-world parallel by design. #19, #42, #43 and #66 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance and repair cultures) in research, flag only.
- **Sun-return festivals** (common in polar towns) are a poor fit, because Mirny has no polar night. They were left out on purpose.
- **"In-between" identity (F1).** Line markers (#5–#11) are easy to find; places whose *culture* is shaped by straddling a threshold are not. #11 is the closest.
- **Demonym and name.** These are a naming fact only and outside this task.

- #5: whether the Grímsey sphere is moved, and how often.

- #10: the size of the offset between the Mitad del Mundo monument and the true equator.
- #18: which Trieste bora ropes and rails are current and which are historical.
- #21: the Norwegian convoy-driving term and which passes use it.
- #26: which Ryukyu island's windbreak practice is best documented.

- #72: Longyearbyen's current alcohol and movement rules.
