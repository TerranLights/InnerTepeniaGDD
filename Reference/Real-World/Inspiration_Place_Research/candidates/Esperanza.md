## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F12 The rest of the economy:** the spec leaves eastern-Peninsula resources and maritime trade TBD. No candidates were proposed for resource industries on purpose, so as not to invent an economy the spec has not given. The harbor rows (76–80) cover only the transfer role.
- **F11 Scale:** few care-defined places reach a city of one to two million. Rows 48–49 are smaller, or are cities where care is one sector among several. This may be a real gap rather than a search gap.
- **F6 Robots as caregivers:** the real-world parallels are human caregiving systems (fosterage, wet nursing, nannies, time banks). Only row 93 involves machines, and it is thin. Flagged [R] for the later translation step.
- **F10 The sun every day:** the latitude-equivalent rows are weak as character sources; they are included for the daylight texture only.
- **K11 (speech):** not addressed; the multinational makeup is noted in F5 only, as the language caution requires.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- Row 18 (Florence Baptistery bean tally): I recall a medieval chronicle describing it; the source and wording need checking.
- Row 17 (Amsterdam orphans in city colors): the detail is recalled, not confirmed.
- Row 47 (Bethel): whether it had its own internal money is uncertain.
- Row 32 (Umeå) and row 31 (Tromsø, NOSM): founding dates and stated purposes need checking.
- Row 85 (Punta Arenas pedestrian ropes): recalled; may be overstated.
- Row 58 (Dutch playground associations): the early-1900s origin needs checking.
- Row 24 (Piplantri): current practice and numbers need checking.
- Row 72 (French Hospital, London): founding year and purpose need checking.

**Watch points**
- Several rows concern war, refugees, orphanages and exile (9, 12, 51, 71–74). They are included for structure (a community raising the children of those who gave something up) and must be reported neutrally, without importing any group's identity into the city.
- Rows 8–13 all express the "debt owed" logic (F2); a review may want to keep only two or three of them.
