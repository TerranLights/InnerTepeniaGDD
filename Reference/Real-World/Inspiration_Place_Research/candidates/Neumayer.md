## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets:**
- **F13 governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed. Rows 22 and 83 are the only candidates. A research round might look at how long-lived research institutions govern themselves.
- **F13 food:** the spec says nothing on food. Rows 47 and 48 are the only food candidates.
- **F12 the coast:** "whatever the coast offered" is vague. No candidates were added for a coastal side economy.
- **F11 scale and makeup:** few candidates match a city of over a million people built around research. Most science towns are small.
- **Namesake:** the spec says the namesake was an institution-builder. That facet was not used, under the rule that names are not a reason for any candidate.

**Doubts to check:**
- Row 14: the number of steps added to the column.
- Row 20: whether the year markers exist as described.
- Row 37: whether the ropes are still in use.
- Row 45: the radio-quiet rule.
- Rows 51, 53, 55: start dates.
- Row 61: construction status.
- Row 74: attribution of the engineering.
- Row 78: dates.
- Row 93: the posted rules.
- No candidate was included or excluded because of its nation.
