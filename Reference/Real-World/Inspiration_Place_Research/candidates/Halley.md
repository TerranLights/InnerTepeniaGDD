# Halley: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group, and never included or excluded for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the war-damage and post-war sections ignored), this city's seven existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read. Neighboring places the spec names only in relation (the subnet's receiving ports, the corridor's far end) are referred to by role, not by name.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F13 Scale and makeup.** No real place is a moving city of about a million people. The near ones are temporary (row 23 Kumbh Mela, row 61 Black Rock City) or much smaller (rows 13, 14, 3). The research should treat scale as an extrapolation, not something to find in a parallel. On a many-origin, near-parity population there are no candidates here, because the picks rules exclude nation-based choices.
- **F14 Economy.** The spec says "TBD" and names only a gateway role. Candidates (rows 19, 66, 70, 75) cover the supply and labor pattern, not a local industry. This facet should be redrafted once the economy is designed.
- **F9 Namesake but not the nexus.** No direct parallel found for a place that gives its name to a network without being its technical hub. Row 66 covers only the related fact that residents traveled to build elsewhere.
- **F4 Governance.** The spec gives few governance details beyond collective relocation decisions. Rows 64, 65, 13, 22 and 25 are forms of decision-making, not a match to a known Halley institution.
- **F11 Snowmobile life.** Only covered indirectly (rows 4, 18, 28). No candidate stands on its own for a city whose internal traffic is mostly snowmobiles.
- **Arts and music.** Rows 47, 59 and 60 touch art made of or about impermanence. No candidate covers how an arts or music scene started in a place like this (K13 is thin).
- **K6 everyday rules.** Only rows 4, 28 and 61 hold clear rules. A rule-focused gap fill is likely needed.

**Excluded on purpose**
- **Halley VI and the earlier Halley stations** are the city's own real-world basis, so they are not listed as picks.
- **The corridor's character** is not picked, per the rules header. Rows 67–69 and 71–72 are there only for the city's role at the head of a route or line, or for its supply pattern.
