# Zhongshan: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (war-damage and post-war status ignored), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and the developer-additions file (it has no section for this city, so no developer-added rows). No other city's material was read. The city is treated on its own terms; its neighbors and its interior partner appear only as *relation* (what flows, what is shared), never as comparison.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec gives none. #10, #69 and #20 hint at shapes (a maintenance board, a guild constituency, an internationally settled status), but none was chosen *for* governance.
- **Food.** The spec says nothing. #34 (hawker centers) and #18 (shared mess) are the only leads.
- **Everyday leisure.** The spec says nothing outside the proposed music tradition. #25, #60, #61, #65 and #66 are leads tied to light and season; per the no-forced-fit law, a thin result here may be correct.
- **F12 is placeholder material.** The music tradition, artisan class and chronicler are all marked "placeholder, proposed" in the spec. Candidates #6, #68–#75 are for those placeholders and fall away if the placeholders do.
- **F13 (the honored name).** Only #76 is offered. Places that share the name were left out on purpose (namesakes are flavor only, and a same-name place is not a structural parallel).
- **F14 (interior partner).** Only #78, and the spec does not say goods or people flow between the two, so it may not apply.
- **Robot half of the population (F10).** There is no real-world parallel by design. #7, #11, #57 and #59 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance and repair cultures) in research, as a flag only.
- **Dust and grit as a daily nuisance (F5).** #42 and #44 touch it; no candidate is chosen specifically for how a town lives with wind-blown grit.
- **Demonym.** A naming question, outside this task.
