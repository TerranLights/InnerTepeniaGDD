# Cape Adare: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or population group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the destruction, ruins and post-war material ignored; the spec's density comparisons against other Tepenian cities also ignored, since this city is taken on its own terms), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no Cape Adare section, so there are no developer-added rows. ## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F16 Gateway economy:** the spec leaves the economy "TBD" beyond maritime trade and the logistics of arrival and departure, so candidates (Mindelo, Cuxhaven, Stromness, Ushuaia) can only show the shape of a passage economy. Nothing proposes what the coast itself yields; that is the spec's open question, not a gap to fill here.
- **F15 Large, mixed, half-robot population:** no candidate addresses the sheer size (~1.5 million) against a small-town feel except Sitka, and that only by area. Mixed-port candidates (Trieste, Cobh) are chosen for structure, not for any nation.
- **F14 Spur at the end of the reach:** covered only indirectly (Churchill, Ushuaia, Utqiaġvik). Per the corridor ruling, the road itself takes no pick.
- **Governance:** the spec does not say how the city is governed. The civic-assembly rows (73–75, 4, 76) rest only on "community-driven."
- **The heritage-hut conservation trusts that work on real polar huts** were left out on purpose. They work on the real site itself, which makes them a source and not a parallel.

**Doubts to verify (from-memory claims)**
- Ropes or handrails along streets in Trieste (bora) and in Punta Arenas (gales): I believe both exist but have not confirmed them.
- The Montreal gold-headed cane: the start date (1840s) and whether the custom continues.
- Holland, Michigan, as the "largest" municipal snowmelt system.
- The scale and span of the Phillip Island Summerland buyback.
- The St Kilda daily "parliament" as a regular morning meeting. It is widely reported, but check the form.
- The roles of the Holy Sepulchre keyholder families as described.
- The bowed-lyre revival examples (row 70): the least certain row.
- The current stork numbers and town rules in Rust.
- Sitka's status as the largest US city by area depends on how "city" is defined.
- Existing picks Yalta and Lahti have no reason note in the input; the facet readings here are guesses for the developer to confirm or correct.

**Spec tensions noted, not resolved**
- Landing (Feb 17) versus Wintering as the central observance. Plymouth (11) and Bari (26) model the arrival side, while stabilitas (22), Skellig Michael (23) and Ise-style renewal (17) model the staying side. That ruling belongs to the developer.
