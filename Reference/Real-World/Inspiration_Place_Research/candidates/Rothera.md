# Rothera: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group, and never included or excluded for lying in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. The city's name, its namesake and the real station's history are treated as flavor, not data; no facet or candidate is built on them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the Population/Destructibility note's war-survival reasoning, the "Current Status / Damage" section and the "Legacy" section were not used as inputs; the decentralized works and underground chambers are taken from Economy & Industry and Notable Locations instead), this city's five existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read. The spec's founding-population line, its per-nation census table and its station-era notes were not used as reasons for any candidate.
