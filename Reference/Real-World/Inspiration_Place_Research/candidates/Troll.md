# Troll: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. A candidate is neither included nor excluded because of the nation it is in, the nation of any founders, or whether it is in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (war-damage and post-war sections ignored), this city's five existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. The developer-additions file has no section for this city. No other city's material was read.

**Deliberately not used as reasons for any candidate:** the spec's "Founding population" line (it names the real station's operator nation, which is not evidence of founders), the station's name and the folklore behind it, the territorial claim, the explorer and patron-saint associations, and the real station's own supply chain. ## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F9 Scale and makeup:** thin (Keflavík, Fort McMurray). The spec gives size and a many-nation mix but nothing about how the groups live together; no candidate was chosen by nation, and none should be. A research round could look for large many-nation working cities built around logistics.
- **F10 Research and what the site offers:** the spec says only "whatever the nunatak position otherwise offers." Mountain observatories cover research; nothing more is proposed, because guessing the site's other uses would mean reading the real station's history into the city.
- **Governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed or who runs the airfield in normal times; only the gateway-control candidates (63, 65, 17, 62) touch it.
- **Food, arts and music:** the spec has none. Candidates 8, 44, 52, 73 and 74 are the only cultural leads; this is the likeliest gap for a research round (K12, K13).
- **Daily life under polar night at a working airfield** (flying in the dark, or not flying): no direct real-world candidate proposed beyond the general cold-airport rows (55, 56).
