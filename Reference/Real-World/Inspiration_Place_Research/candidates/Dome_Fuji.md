# Dome Fuji: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone. No candidate is included or excluded because of the nation it sits in, the nation of the real-world station, or the origin of any founder. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the war-damage and post-war status lines ignored), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so no developer-added rows. No other city's material was read; the Mirny file was opened for its layout only (first 20 lines).

| Facet | What it is | Spec source |

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F12 Alive but compromised:** only indirect candidates (#3, #31, #49, #72). No clean real-world parallel for a place deliberately running at reduced capacity, neither ruin nor thriving. Left open; not forced.
- **F13 Governance:** the spec says only that informal stewards keep an observance calendar. Candidates #30, #48 and #54 show forms a devotee community has taken, but none is implied by the spec. Treat as possibilities only.
- **F14 Altitude medical center:** the candidates (#66 to #68) are human medicine for a city with no humans; useful only for the legacy building and its purpose.
- **Food and drink (K12) and leisure third places (K10):** almost nothing that fits a robot-only devotee city without guessing at its practices. #47 and #48 are the nearest. Left open.
- **Arts and music (K13):** #45, #46 and #81 are tied to cold or impermanence; no music candidate was strong enough to include.

**Candidates to verify (unsure as described)**
- #5: whether the deaths and the refusal to eat the collection happened as the popular account says.
- #7: the founding seeds and their immigrant origin.
- #8 and #9: the details and dates of variety repatriation.
- #12: the winter fig-wrapping custom as a widespread practice.
- #24: whether every grave is adopted and how the waiting list works.
- #26: the 2020 reciprocity and its framing.
- #34: the exact dates and number of daily cold-water ablutions.
- #36: the physiological claims made for the practice.
- #45: the ice-water hand-cooling technique.
- #53: the dates and route of the revival.
- #57: the length and season of the winter retreat as described.

**Doubts about fit**
- Several candidates (#30, #32, #33, #64) bar a category of human, not humans as such. Their value is the structure of a ban and its threshold institutions, not the reason for it.
- #4, #11, #14: a seed or core archive whose motive is insurance or science, not gratitude. Useful for form only; the city's own motive comes from the F2 candidates (#23 to #29).
- #17 and #73 are inverse or industrial and may not survive review.
