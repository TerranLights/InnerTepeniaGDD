# Amundsen Station: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder group, and never included or excluded for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. The city's name, its namesake, the meaning of the name, and the real station's history and operator are treated as flavor, not data: no facet and no new candidate is built on them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the war-damage, destruction and post-war sections ignored as inputs), this city's seven existing picks, the developer-added entry for this city in `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md`, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and the first 20 lines of `Mirny.md` for layout only. No other city's material was read.

**Developer direction for this draft:** the city is to be expanded into a much larger population and a far more complex, intricate and engaging location for a downloadable-content setting. Facets about scale, layered structure, hidden or buried spaces, and a rich civic and cultural life are therefore weighed alongside the spec's own facts (facet F13 below carries that direction; it is not from the spec).

- **F11 Population and makeup (robot majority):** almost nothing real is a machine-majority settlement. Rows 2 and 104 touch it only through the work. The robot-translation step is the method's own; this draft flags `[R]` and stops there.
- **F12 Governance as a facility:** candidates speak to neutrality (rows 20, 24–28, 35) and to infrastructure jurisdictions (38–40), but none is a clean model of a facility that governs itself while hosting outside staff in balanced proportions. A gap-fill could look at treaty-run scientific institutions and at how balanced staffing quotas are actually run.
- **Food and drink (K12) and arts and music (K13):** thin. Rows 31, 58, 79, 83, 85, 96, 97, 101 carry some; none is centered on how a rotating, supplied-from-outside population eats or makes art. This matters most for F13.
- **F10 the three highways:** by the rules header, highways have no character entries of their own; the converging routes are recorded here only as part of the city's access situation. Rows 13, 98, 99, 100 are crossroads places, not road picks.
- **F14 ground that rises:** this is derived from the spec's precipitation figures (about 75 mm/yr landing and staying), not stated in the spec as a design fact. Treat rows 63, 64, 92, 93, 94 as conditional on the developer accepting the derivation.

### Existing picks that lean on the real station
- **Row 1 (IceCube)** is chosen because it physically stands at the real station; it is kept as [EXISTING], but its value here rests on the structural trait (a huge buried array feeding a compact control building), not on location.
