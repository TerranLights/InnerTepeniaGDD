# Amundsen Station: candidate picks by setting type (draft from knowledge, unverified; 2026-11-21)

**Status:** drafted 2026-11-21 from general knowledge only, **no web research**, under the protocol's drafting step (`_RESUME_HERE.md`, ruling of 2026-10-26: candidates first, from the place's own facts; the developer approves, strikes, adds and tiers; only approved rows are researched, two rounds each). **Every row is a candidate to verify; every fact in the "why" column is a recollection, not a finding.** Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on nation. Tiers are not proposed; the developer sets them.

**What this file adds:** the developer's classification of 2026-11-21 sorts the station's settings into four types. This file (1) places rows of the existing 104-row list (`candidates/Amundsen_Station.md`) under those types, by number, and (2) adds **new** candidates for each type. Existing rows keep their own text and facets; they are only cross-filed here.

**The developer's own words are in `Amundsen_Station_Setting_-_original_text.txt` (same folder) and govern wherever this summary differs.**

**Developer's classification (2026-11-21), in brief:**
- **BELOW 1 · Technical maintenance and management:** not scientific research; highly technical settings that need regular management and maintenance; very advanced machinery and technology inside otherwise mundane surroundings.
- **BELOW 2 · Labyrinthine complexity:** (a) a residence of over a thousand people, each staying a few months to a couple of years, that should feel like one; (b) heat spread thin: a little local heat everywhere melts its surroundings less than a lot in one place; (c) after the war, an empty labyrinth is more mysterious than an empty centralized space.
- **BELOW 3 · First-person presence, at the height and after the war:** (a) at its height a highly advanced, beautiful, technologically impressive (perhaps tightly spaced) temporary residence for scientists, technicians, engineers, doctors, plumbers, maintenance workers, cooks, food-service and leisure staff ("we need to keep them from climbing the walls"); (b) after the war an unintended "Grand Mausoleum": real places that thrived and then became cities of the dead.
- **ABOVE · The space fountain:** what a space fountain entails, and so what belongs at the Tower's base.

**Excluded at this stage (developer, 2026-11-21):** Ice-Cold Buddhism. Religious sites appear below only where the setting itself (catacombs, a necropolis) is the parallel; belief systems are a later stage (`DR-140` item 2).

**Legend:** `[IN LIST #n]` = already row *n* of the 104-row list (cross-filed, not duplicated); `[NEW]` = new candidate. Care notes flag sensitive histories (to be reported neutrally) and doubts.
