# Denison: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the destruction, legacy and post-war sections ignored), this city's four existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and the opening lines of the Mirny candidate file for layout only. The developer-additions file has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read.

**Deliberately not proposed:** the real expedition at this site, its huts and the bodies that conserve them. The site's own history is not an input; the city's dare is treated as a civic premise, and the picks for it are other places whose identity rests on an ordeal.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets**
- **F14 governance:** the spec gives almost nothing beyond institutions named after the wind. Candidates 14, 45, 51, 66, 90 to 92 are proposals for how a place governs one hazard, not evidence of how this city does.
- **F12 access:** only one candidate (89), on purpose, because of the highway rule. Sea resupply has no dedicated pick.
- **F13 the diaspora:** only one candidate (62). A diaspora that carries a weather-hardened manner elsewhere is a thin kind in my knowledge.
- **F10 founded on a dare:** only two direct picks (61, 83), because the real site's own expedition is excluded. A research round may find better ordeal-identity towns.
- **F2 light and wind out of phase:** no candidate shares the specific combination of grazing winter sun and worst wind. Candidates here only cover living by weather windows.
- **F6 the inland ice zone:** no real place builds a large industrial district on an ablating, calving ice margin. Candidates 86 to 89 cover moving or freezing ground and freight on ice, not that whole condition.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- **2 Sanda:** I do not know the developer's reason. My guess (a fast-growing satellite new town) is unverified.
- **18 Winnipeg:** I believe the pedestrian barriers at Portage and Main were kept by a 2018 vote and later slated for reopening. Current status unknown.
- **19 Chicago:** the origin of the wind nickname is disputed.
- **26 Svappavaara and Resolute:** I am unsure how much of each wind-wall plan was actually built.
- **29 Shibam:** rooftop bridges between towers are from memory; verify.
- **36 Genoa:** whether the Tramontana is locally as defining as the Bora is in Trieste is uncertain.
- **41 Askov:** the dates and the electrician-training program are from memory.
- **62 St. John's:** "windiest, foggiest" rankings are from memory.
