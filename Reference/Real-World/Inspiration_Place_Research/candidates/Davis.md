# Davis: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the war-damage and post-war material ignored), this city's three existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. No other city's material was read. The spec's own mentions of neighboring cities were not used as inputs.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **F10 Scale and mix.** Only #4 and #80 speak to a many-origin port population. The robot half of the population has no real-world parallel by design. #19, #63, #74, #75 and #81 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance, repair and shared-rest cultures) in research, flag only.
- **F15 Not the hub.** Nothing was chosen for being a non-capital city on a trunk route; the spec says only that Davis has less administrative weight. Left open rather than forced.
- **Governance.** The spec leaves it open. #13, #15 and #30 show growers' cooperatives and water courts, #81 a union hall, #63 a state service; none was chosen *for* governance.
- **Food.** The spec says only "eateries." #11, #27 and #36 are produce leads; there is no candidate for what is cooked or drunk.
- **F6 Terrain-shaped layout.** #3, #8, #33 and #48 give terrain; nothing shows a city whose *street plan* follows lakes, rock and inlets in one place. A research round might look for one.
- **Arts and music.** Only #2, #17, #36, #66 and #79 show how a scene started. Per the spec, the scene is small, and that may simply be the answer.
- **The icebreaking question (F8, H65).** #63, #65 and #69 show who runs icebreaking and how it is paid for; none settles anything for the spec.
- **Light.** With ~37 days of polar night, sun-return festivals would fit, but only #87 was chosen, because the hazard the spec names is dryness and isolation, not darkness.
