# Byrd: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on the nation a place is in, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder group, and never excluded for being in South Asia. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. The city's name, its meaning, its namesake (the "St. Richard" material) and the real station's own history are treated as flavor, not data: no facet and no candidate is built on them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the Status line, Connection to Concordia, Current Status and Legacy sections, and the grounded-fleet material, are treated as war-damage/post-war and ignored), this city's ten existing picks (two input files, read as one list), `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed. Only Mondragon (78) and the company-town rows (6, 61, 62) touch it, and only as a lens. Left open on purpose.
- **Leisure, arts and food:** the spec gives only "a genuine sense of shared community, especially underground." Rows 28, 32–38 and 36 are proposals for what underground leisure could resemble, not readings of the spec.
- **What the city withholds and provides:** the spec names exports (chambers, fabricated goods, dispatch) but nothing withheld. No candidate addresses withholding.
- **Population about half robots:** no real place parallels this; row 60 is the nearest for robot-made-by-robot work only. Flagged [R].
- **Thermal conspicuousness (F3):** rows 16–18 and 11 are approximations; there is no clean real case of a settlement betrayed by its heat.

**Spec items not used, on purpose**
- The founding narrative (the vehicle chain, the expedition) is written around an earlier founding per the spec's own note, and touches the real station's history; no candidate is built on it. The "reached by a crossing" ethos in Character & Culture was also left out for that reason.
- The grounded fleet and the 10% salvage sector describe a post-war state; no boneyard or salvage-yard candidate was drafted. If the developer admits the fleet as an input, aircraft-boneyard sites would be the natural rows.
- "St. Richard" and the planned memorial site were not used (namesake material).

**Spec inconsistencies noticed (not resolved here)**
- Aviation: "one of only ten airports in Tepenia" versus "the only city in Tepenia to" maintain aviation.
- Polar night: ~129 days (Annual Climate) versus ~108 days (Notable Weather Phenomena); midnight sun "one of the longer" versus "the longest."

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- 15 (current status of both heat-reuse sites), 19 (Holland's heat source after plant replacement), 22 (DYE-2 skiway training use), 25 (cavern-hangar current use), 37 (Growing Underground's status), 49 (Barbarastollen access), 60 (Fanuc's unattended-run figure), 63 (both prestige projects), 67 (winter-road operation today), 11 (exact role of reactor heat in tunnel deformation).
