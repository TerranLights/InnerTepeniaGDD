# Marambio: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. A candidate is neither included nor excluded for the nation it is in, the nation of the city's founders, or for being in South Asia.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the destroyed status, war-damage, ruin and post-war material ignored), this city's two existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and the first 20 lines of the Mirny file for layout only. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so no developer-added rows. No other city's material was read.

**Deliberately not used as data:** the city's name, its namesake and its meaning; the real station's operating history; and the founder nation named in the spec's Founding section. None of these is the basis of any facet or candidate below.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets.**
- **F6 Light without extremes:** almost no candidate is chosen for it, because the spec gives the light no cultural weight. A research round could look for customs at similar latitudes, but nothing is forced.
- **F14 Mixed population:** no real-world place matches a near-even human and robot population. #9, #10 and #32 are [R] flags only. Multinational port workforces (#24, #62) touch the "no single majority" part.
- **Governance:** the spec says nothing about how the city is governed beyond "institutional character." No candidates are proposed for governance, and that slot is left open.
- **Leisure, food and the arts:** the spec is silent; candidates #17, #39–#44 and #47 are proposals from the transit-and-dock facets, not from anything the spec states.
- **F8 Strait as a choice:** only a few candidates (#54–#56), because the spec leaves it as an open question.

**Candidates whose details need checking.**
- #5 Keflavík: whether the base radio is documented as the origin of the neighboring town's rock scene.
- #7 Gibraltar: whether the road still crosses the runway or now runs through a tunnel.
- #43 Mindelo: whether its song tradition is documented as growing in the port bars specifically.
- #46 Punta Arenas: whether wind ropes along the streets are a documented fact or a tourist story.
- #47 Bryggen: the exact Kontor rules and the use of the heated assembly rooms in winter.
- #51 Estonian ice roads: the exact posted rules, which may have changed.
- #30 Memphis: the work-study arrangement for students working the night sort.
- #38: whether the Shuttleworth Collection is the best documented example of returning aircraft to flight from records.
- #69 is a contrast and not a parallel. Strike it if contrasts are not wanted.
- #70 is a kind of place without named examples yet.

**Excluded on purpose.** Nothing was excluded because of nation. The spec's founder nation, the city's name and namesake, and the real station's history were not used. The destroyed status and ruins were not used.
