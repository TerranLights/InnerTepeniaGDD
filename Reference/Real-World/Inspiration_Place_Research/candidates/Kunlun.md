# Kunlun: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (war-damage and post-war sections ignored), this city's four existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`, and `Mirny.md` for format only. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no developer-added rows. No other city's material was read.

**Scope choice.** Real Antarctic stations were left out on purpose, because they may stand behind other Tepenian cities. Arctic, alpine, Andean and desert sites carry the polar, altitude and remoteness facets instead.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec says nothing. Candidates hint at shapes (a member-state council as in #9, #52, #54; a directorate under a national umbrella as in #1; a monastic republic as in #80) but none was chosen *for* governance.
- **Food.** The spec says nothing. Only #95 is a food lead, plus whatever the observatory rows show about shared canteens (#5, #7, #52).
- **Leisure and arts.** The spec says nothing. #53, #55, #74 and #77 show arts and leisure that *grew from* science communities. Per the no-forced-fit law, an empty answer may stand.
- **The fabrication outpost at a waypoint (F11).** No good real-world parallel was found for a workshop co-staffed by two remote communities at a point between them that ran on borrowed power. #92 and #93 cover only the engineering temperament.
- **Bond with the one neighbor (F12).** Thin: #73 and #91 only.
- **Robot-only population.** There is no real-world parallel by design. #6, #31, #42, #65 and #76 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance and repair cultures) in research, flag only.
- **Polar night.** Sun-return festivals were left out because the spec's polar-night custom is stillness, not the sun's return. #87 and #88 carry that facet.
- **Spec notes, not touched:** the spec's wind box still says −58.0 °C after the mean was corrected to about −54 °C, and its polar-night line carries an unfinished aside. Facets here used the corrected figure.
