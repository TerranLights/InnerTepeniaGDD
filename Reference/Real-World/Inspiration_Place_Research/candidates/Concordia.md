# Concordia: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-26 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the "Current Status" section was read only for the city's present-day civic spirit and governance shape, not for war damage), this city's four existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city, so there are no [DEVELOPER-ADDED] rows. No other city's material was read.

**Scope:** the city as a whole, and the districts and settings the spec itself describes: the dome-and-corridor core, Sagittarius (the Frostlands), Capricorn (the industrial yards), Gemini (the diaspora enclave around network hardware) and Libra (the Government District, named only). The spec gives no content for the other districts, so they get no district-specific rows (see section 3).

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **District count.** The spec names **twelve** districts, but this run's dispatch note says thirteen. The spec was followed. The other seven districts (Aries, Taurus, Leo, Virgo, Scorpio, Aquarius, Pisces) and Cancer beyond its name ("The Sanctuary") have no spec content, so no district-specific rows were drafted for them. By the no-forced-fit law, this is left open, not filled.
- **Libra (the Government District).** It is named only. #79–#85 are city-level governance leads, not district leads.
- **The medical faction's base and clinic.** The district is not set and there is no content, so it is left open.
- **Arts, music and food.** The spec says nothing beyond the Frostlands' communal meal. #12, #13, #17, #47, #76 and #89 are leads only.
- **Robots as the city's mobility (F6) and the half-robot population (F19).** By design there is no real-world parallel. #44 and #45 could carry an `[R]` flag (shared rest and warmth).
