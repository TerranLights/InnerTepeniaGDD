Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on sharing a nation with the real-world station or with any founder or exile group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Facets with thin or no candidates**
- **Governance.** The spec says nothing. #32, #45 and #55 hint at civic bodies built around traffic, but nothing is proposed for governance because nothing is given.
- **Food.** The spec says nothing. #32, #46, #68 and #69 are traveler-food leads only.
- **Festivals.** The spec names none. #15, #18, #33, #50, #64 and #74 are leads for customs tied to transit and to the opening of access; none was chosen to fill a slot.
- **F15 Applied technical capacity.** Only #83 and #58. The spec marks this TBD, and per the no-forced-fit law it may stay thin.
- **F10 Liminal light.** Only #18 and #19, and #18's light is not Casey's. Places in the narrow band with midnight sun but no polar night are few, and their civic customs about light are hard to find from memory.
- **F12, the robot half of the population.** There is no real-world parallel by design. #16, #22, #24 and #48 could carry an `[R]` flag (maintenance and repair cultures, rest at stops) in research, flag only.
- **F16 Transit in crisis.** #32, #45 and #56 are hospitality cases, not refugee cases. The developer may want a refugee-route town, but none was added here, because war material was not an input.
- **Demonym and name.** These are a naming fact only and outside this task.

**Candidates whose details are unsure**
- #4: how central the jazz clubs on Ogden's 25th Street were, and in which decades.
- #8: Gilley's closing date and how far its "largest" claim held.
- #18: the start year of the Fairbanks Midnight Sun Game and whether it is always played without lights.
- #19: whether Mo i Rana is in the narrow band with midnight sun but no polar night, as Casey is.
- #39: how much the seamen's records shaped Liverpool's scene; the claim is debated.
- #42: which beer barns are best documented, and their capacities.
- #46: the dates of the duty-free and coffee-drink origins at Shannon and Foynes.
- #50: whether Duluth's first-ship contest is still run, and by whom.
- #53: whether "Crossroads of Australia" is an official or informal title.
- #65: whether the Zagora sign still stands and in what form.
- #67: the number of michi-no-eki and when the program began.
- #84: which parts of Moose Jaw's tunnel history are documented and which are legend.
