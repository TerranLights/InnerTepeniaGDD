# Utstein: candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-27 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone, never on the nation a candidate sits in, the nation of the real-world station, or the nation of any founder group. Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them. The spec's rename notice and its older text about a former name and founders were not used as a reason for any candidate.

**Inputs read:** this city's spec (the destruction, ruins, legacy and post-war sections ignored, so every facet describes the living city), this city's five existing picks, `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`, and the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`. `_DEVELOPER_ADDITIONS.md` has no section for this city. ## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin or empty facets**
- **F8 (eastern outlier at the end of a road):** few candidates map cleanly without comparing the city to its network. The spec gives only the road and the position; what flows along it and in which direction is not written. Left thin on purpose.
- **F9 (scale and makeup):** the population is large and many-nation, but the spec gives no districts, density or living patterns. Candidates (Ny-Ålesund, CERN, Auroville, Paranal) cover only a research-community angle, which may not fit a city of a million.
- **F10, the mountain resources:** the spec says "whatever the nunatak position offered" and nothing more. No mining, quarrying or geology candidates were drafted, because the spec gives no basis; a later research round should look at what the real mountain range actually holds before any are added.
- **F13 (governance, leisure, arts, food):** absent from the spec. Every candidate under it is a prompt derived from F1–F4, not from a stated fact.
- **What the city withholds:** the spec says what it provides (expertise) but nothing it withholds. No candidates.
- **Pilgrimage link:** the spec leaves a possible pilgrimage connection open (TBD). No candidates drafted, since nothing is decided.

**Doubts about the spec's inputs**
- **Wind exposure is stated two ways.** The Significance line and Geographic Basis say the site is defined by exposure to gales up to 300 km/h; the researched climate section says the mountains shelter the ridge and the mean wind is about 6 m/s, with exposure in extreme events. Candidates under F5 and F6 (Mount Washington, rock lighthouses, Wellington, Punta Arenas) rest on the exposure reading and should be re-weighed once the developer decides which reading governs. The 300 km/h figure itself is unverified.
- **Cheyenne Mountain's reason note** refers to an underground "mini-infrastructure" that this spec does not mention. It is kept as an existing pick; the referent should be checked.

**Candidates I am unsure exist as described**
- Eigg's per-household power cap (the figure and how it is enforced).
- Nashtifan's custodian and the windmills' current working state.
- Tvindkraft still running.
- Punta Arenas street ropes (whether they exist and are still used).
- Wellington's wind-assessment planning rule.
- Monte Rosa Hut's self-sufficiency figure.
- FOGBANK: only the public outline is known; details are classified.
