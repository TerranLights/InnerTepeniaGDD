Founders come only from the Founding Register, and nothing here touches them.

## 3. Gaps and doubts

**Thin facets**
- **F11 Spur access:** only one candidate (Whittier). The spec says little beyond the road arrangement, and a road gets its character from what it connects (developer ruling), so a thin list may be the correct state.
- **F13 Scale and makeup:** covered only indirectly by dense mixed-origin food cities (Singapore, Penang, Kowloon Walled City). The twelve-nation stock was deliberately not used to choose picks.
- **F15 Governance:** the spec names only a council. Candidates touch community self-rule (Christiania, txoko societies, Venetian confraternities) but none was chosen for governance alone. Leave open until the city's governance is written.
- **F10 Design industry:** the spec says "design" without saying what is designed; Lyon, Kyoto and Winnipeg are guesses at the shape.
- **What the city withholds:** the spec does not say, beyond its streets being illegible to outsiders. No candidates were forced.

**Considered and left out on purpose**
- **Melbourne** (laneways, café culture, live music in pubs) is a strong structural fit but is in the founders' nation. Left out so that the list cannot look nation-driven. The developer may add it.
- **The sheltering of defectors and the destruction** are in war and post-war sections and were not used. Sanctuary-town candidates were therefore not drafted.
- **The Census I to Census II drop** was not treated as an event (census operations are not events).

**Unsure they exist as described (verify first)**
- #4 the silk-worker origin of the traboules (may be partly legend); #9 the house count inside the Arles arena; #12 the Reykir banana house; #13 the Wintergarten's origin inside a hotel winter garden; #29 Senj roof weighting; #33 how widespread blizzard rope lines were; #35 how much hobo signs were really used; #42 the Harar alley count; #80 the Murmansk sun festival's name and date.
- Several rows (#18, #30, #33, #39) are kinds of things rather than single places; research should pin each to two or three documented examples.
