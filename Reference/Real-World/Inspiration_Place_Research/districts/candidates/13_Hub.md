# 13_Hub — Concordia Central Hub ("Axis Mundi"): candidate inspiration picks (draft from knowledge, unverified)

**Status:** drafted 2026-10-28 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone (local economy, arts and music, work and life culture, built form, physical situation), never on sharing a nation, a founders' nation or a name. War damage is not an input: the district's layered repair is treated as a built-form fact, and no candidate is chosen for having been damaged in war. No slang.

**Inputs read:** `District_Canon_Reference.md` lines 711–778 (the refugee-composition Development Notes subsection skipped); the design substrate file for this district (sections 8 and 9 skipped, other districts' names ignored; section 11 used as texture only); this district's entries in `G_Correspondences.md` and `E_Decans.md` (both say there is nothing; `F_Rulerships.md` has no entry); `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`; the rules header of `Inspirational-Influences.md`; the Mirny candidate file for format only; the developer's vision notes for this district; this district's section and the introductory lines of `district_by_Enneagram_group_series.md`. No other district's material, and no other finished candidate list, was read. Zodiac material is design input only and is named only in bracketed citations.

**Signature function:** transit / governance nexus / neutral crossroads. Facets that serve it are marked **(SIG)**.
