**Status:** drafted 2026-10-28 from general knowledge only, with no web research. Every row is a *candidate* for a later research round to verify. Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone (local economy, arts and music, work and life culture, built form, physical situation), never on sharing a nation, a founders' nation or a name. War damage is not an input. No slang.

**Inputs read:** `District_Canon_Reference.md` lines 10-67 (the refugee-composition notes skipped); the district's personality substrate file (sections 8 and 9 skipped; section 11 used as texture only); the district's row of the rulerships file and its entries in the correspondences and decans files; the district's section of the full-attributes reference; `WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`; the rules header (lines 1-21) of `Inspirational-Influences.md`; `candidates/Mirny.md` for format only; the district's vision notes; the district's section and the introduction of the Enneagram-group file. No developer-additions file exists for this district. No other district's material was read.

**Signature function:** sheltering and caring for the vulnerable (the district's "nurturing / protective" role line). Facets that serve it are prefixed **S** and marked [SIG]; every other facet is prefixed **F** (or **X** for a silence).
