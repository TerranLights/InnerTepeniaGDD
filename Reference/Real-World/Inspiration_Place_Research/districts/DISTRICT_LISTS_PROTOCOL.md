# Concordia districts: inspiration lists (protocol, written 2026-10-27)

**Status:** agreed in outline with the developer 2026-10-27; **nothing drafted yet.** Pilot starts in the morning (after 05:00), not before. This file is the method; `_DRAFTING_TRACKER.md` (same folder) is the work list.

**What this is.** The district version of the city candidate lists in `../candidates/`. Same idea (a long list of real-world places, institutions, communities and traditions that share a structural or thematic trait with the district; the developer strikes, adds and tiers; two research rounds follow), adapted for the thirteen districts of Concordia. The picks are **seed material for the extrapolation and expansion phase**: real landmarks, cultural sites, institutions, gathering spaces and traditions to dig into later.

## Why districts need their own version (developer, 2026-10-27)

A district is **not just its label.** Cancer is colloquially the medical district, but it is not only hospitals, pharmacies and clinics. Scorpio is not only people in masks; the masks are one very specific practice in one specific context. The old district picks (`../../../Worldspace/Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/Districts/District-Inspirational-Influences.md`) read each district through its label alone. **A list that does the same is the failure this version exists to prevent.**

## Rules that carry over from the city lists, unchanged

- **Picks are judged on structural and thematic parallel alone.** Never on a shared nation, a founders' nation, or a shared name. A city's or district's name and namesake are flavor, not data.
- **Neither included nor excluded for nation, founders' nation, or South Asia** (the no-South-Asians rule means no South Asians in Antarctica only).
- **Name coincidences are expected and fine** *(developer, 2026-10-28)*. A real-world thing or person whose name lines up with something or someone in the Tepenian universe is perfectly to be expected. It is neither a reason to include nor to exclude a pick. Make a note in the row's care note that the real-world information and the in-universe information are not directly related.
- **War damage is not an input.**
- **No slang** in any file; slang found by chance goes to `../slang_found/`.
- **One location on its own terms: PEER-FREE.** For now, no district list reads, mentions or compares to any other district's list or material. (Developer, 2026-10-27: "Later on in the future, during future design & development, we can start connecting the districts to each other, but before we can do that, we need to know how each district stands on its own terms.") Comparison belongs to a terminal check on the finished corpus. The district runbook's read-the-differentiation-table rule is untouched and applies when the culture passes are done, not to these lists.
- Tiers PRIMARY / SECONDARY / SUPPORTING are the developer's; the draft suggests only.
- American English.
- **No forced fit.** A facet with no good candidate stays open. An empty slot is a result.
- Optional tags and the K1 to K14 kinds are in `../WHAT_TO_LOOK_FOR.md`.

## What is different

1. **Facets come from the whole district, not its label.** Build them from the district's full set of traits (see Sources). **Beyond-the-label rule:** at most about a quarter of a district's candidates may serve its signature function. The rest must serve ordinary life: housing, food, work rhythms, getting around, schooling, leisure, small joys, how the people who are not exceptional spend a Tuesday (the runbook's general-population discipline).
2. **Silence facets.** Where the district's canon says nothing about leisure, food, music, meeting places or governance, make an explicit "silent" facet and propose candidates for it, as the city lists did. These stay candidates, not claims.
3. **The zodiac data is a generator for picks, never a vocabulary.** Developer, 2026-10-27: use the extracted zodiac information as a basis for identifying further real-world inspirations. So read the district's own sign file, its own row of the shared tables, and the sign's section in `../../../Reference/Real-World/Zodiac_Signs_Full_Attributes.md` for temperament, material texture, shadow expressions, myth and cultural associations, correspondences and design hooks, and let them suggest real places, trades, institutions and traditions that the canon entry alone would not. **But:** the zodiac is not in-fiction. In every file you write, zodiac words appear **only inside a bracketed source citation** (for example `[substrate §14]`) or in the file header. Every "why it parallels" sentence is written in the district's own in-world terms.
4. **Lists are not tied to a fixed number of sub-areas.** Each district will later get sub-areas (the developer's current guess is two to five, a different number per district, decided from each district's own character, not preset). The substrate's "decans" section proposes three sub-neighborhood textures per sign: **use that as texture to inspire facets, never as a three-part structure.** Tag every candidate with its facets so the list can be re-sorted into sub-areas once they exist. Later, each sub-area gets its own research (as Byrd's levels will).

## Method for one district (what the agent does)

1. Read the allowed sources for this district.
2. Write the **facets table**: facet, what it is, source (canon section or substrate section). Include silence facets and the beyond-the-label facets. Mark which facets serve the signature function.
3. Draft **40 to 100 candidates** (more for a rich district; do not pad, do not stop early), spread across the world and eras, including small, odd and historical ones and kinds of place with two or three documented examples. Illuminate daily life and culture, not only the signature function.
4. Table columns: `# | Candidate | Facet(s) | Why it parallels (one sentence, in-world terms, K-tag hint where one plainly applies) | Suggested tier | Care note`.
5. **Gaps and doubts:** facets with thin or no candidates; candidates the agent is unsure exist as described; a count of rows that serve the signature function (to check the beyond-the-label rule).
6. **No web searches** in drafting (the search budget is reserved for the research rounds). General knowledge only; a later round verifies.

## DEFERRED: the symbolic-layer deep pass (developer, 2026-10-28; do after the Max 20x plan is paid for)

**Why.** During the Cancer and Scorpio walk-through the developer asked whether the picks used the actual symbolic data of the zodiac signs as a base. Audit of the pilot (Cancer, 99 rows): the lists drew on the sign file's *behavioral* material (socialization, shadow, behavior under conditions, design hooks) but barely on its *symbolic* layer. Keyword check: shedding the shell or hiding in a cave 1 row; sideways motion 0; tides and moon phases 0; the two-way gate 6; silver, pearl, opal, moonstone 1; rivers, trees and parks about 12; nourishment and digestion 0; a borrowed shell or carried home 0; the show of strength over a soft interior 0; the second-decan clinical quarter 0; the third-decan hospice edge 3. Scorpio and the Hub were checked by facet source only (their facets cite sign data heavily) and the motifs were not measured.

**Developer's ruling (verbatim in substance).** *The pilot results are perfectly fine and ALL are kept.* This deep pass will need extensive computation, web searching and research, so it waits for the Max 20x plan. Then it must go into extensive depth, using all the data from the actual zodiac signs as the basis of reference, to **fill out each district with other details that are not what the lists already have, and are still in-world and internally consistent with what each district is.**

**Method sketch (to be finalized when the pass starts):**
1. **Symbolic inventory first.** Per district, the agent lists every symbolic element in its sign data: emblem animal and its biology, myth, metal, stone, plant, color, topography, body function and its metaphors, sky texture and star names, decans, thresholds, historical anchors. Each element gets picks or an explicit "none."
2. **Picks from each element**, as real places, trades, institutions and traditions, written in the district's own in-world terms. Zodiac words only inside bracketed citations or the file header.
3. **Additive block.** New rows are appended to the existing file as a tagged block (nothing deleted or renumbered), checked for in-world consistency with the district and the beyond-the-label rule.
4. **Web research is expected** (verify, and find examples the general-knowledge draft could not). Budget and hours rules as for the other research rounds.
5. **Verification must test the symbolic coverage itself,** not only counts and word scans (that gap is what this pass fixes).
Example directions for Cancer (illustrations, not picks): tidal communities, vacancy chains of borrowed homes, silver-and-pearl trades.

**Hub exception (developer, 2026-10-28).** The Central Hub will be the **hardest to research in the deep pass, specifically because there is no zodiac data to use as a basis of reference** (no rulership entry, no correspondences, no decans, no section in the attributes file; only the substrate file `13_Ophiuchus_Hub.md`, the canon, the vision notes and the Enneagram section). Decide the Hub's basis of reference when the deep pass starts; do not force a sign's data onto it (no forced fit).
