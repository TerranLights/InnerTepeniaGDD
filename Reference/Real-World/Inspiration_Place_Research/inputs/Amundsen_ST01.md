# Research unit `Amundsen_ST01`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Living in snow, ice and earth with heat spread thin)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **S2-01** The Icehotel, Jukkasjärvi, and other snow and ice hotels (e.g., Kemi SnowCastle) // claim to test: Rooms built of snow and ice where people sleep, kept below freezing on purpose and rebuilt each year; how heat, light, air and comfort are managed so the structure does not melt. // care: Verify the rebuilding cycle, the room temperatures, and which parts (if any) are permanent and heated.
- **S2-15** Snow-sheltered and earth-sheltered winter dwellings (sod houses; Inuit winter houses (qarmaq) and snow houses; Norse longhouses) // claim to test: Traditional ways of keeping people warm in snow and ground with small, spread heat sources. // care: A tradition pick: research the techniques and two or three well-documented sites or reconstructions. Indigenous heritage: use the communities' own names and describe respectfully.
- **S2-16** Distributed underfloor heating (Roman hypocausts; Korean ondol) // claim to test: Heat spread under floors through many small channels rather than one hot room; how much heat, where it goes, how it is fed and maintained, and what daily life it shaped. // care: A technique pick: research the technique and two or three well-documented places where it was or is used.
- **S2-02** Ghadames old town, Libya // claim to test: A desert town of covered, shaded passages with rooftop routes: a labyrinth designed for climate control, later largely left for a new town. // care: Recent conflict history: report neutrally. Verify the abandonment and any return or conservation.
