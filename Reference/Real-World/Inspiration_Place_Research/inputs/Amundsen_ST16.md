# Research unit `Amundsen_ST16`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Machines that throw mass, and the campuses above buried machines)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **A-03** Electromagnetic launchers in service or test (EMALS; railgun and coilgun test ranges; SpinLaunch's test site) // claim to test: Real hardware that throws mass electromagnetically, and the plant, power and crews around it. // care: Military sites: neutral.
- **A-04** SLAC's two-mile linear accelerator as a place // claim to test: A very long straight accelerator with its surface buildings, klystron gallery and campus above the beam line. // care: As a place and workplace, not the science.
- **A-05** Accelerator and fusion campuses (Fermilab; DESY; the ITER site) // claim to test: What the surface of a site looks like when its machine is underground or enclosed. // care: As places and workplaces.
- **A-06** Maglev lines (the Yamanashi Maglev Line; the Shanghai Maglev) // claim to test: Magnetically levitated passenger vehicles and their guideways, depots, control and maintenance. // care: Verify speeds, lengths and depots.
