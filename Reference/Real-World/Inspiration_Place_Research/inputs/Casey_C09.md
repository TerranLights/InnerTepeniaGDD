# Research unit `Casey_C09`: Casey, candidate list (Northern staging towns and the polar-circle threshold)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-10-26/27 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the row number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note (things to verify, and how to report sensitive history). Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

- **#17** Talkeetna, Alaska // claim to test: The small staging town where mountaineers register, wait for weather and depart for a great mountain, with a bar at its social center (K10, K14). // care: Nation note: structure only.
- **#18** Fairbanks, Alaska, and its Midnight Sun Game // claim to test: A gateway city to the far north that marks its brief midnight-sun period with a late-night baseball game played without lights (K2). // care: Nation note: structure only. Fairbanks lies inside the polar zone; the civic custom is the point of interest. Verify the game's start year and whether it is always played without lights.
- **#19** Mo i Rana, Norway // claim to test: A town just south of the polar circle at the junction of a north–south highway and a road east, the last town before a high mountain crossing (K5). // care: Its industrial base is not part of the claimed parallel; the junction and the latitude are. Verify whether the town has midnight sun but no polar night.
- **#96** Rovaniemi, Finland // claim to test: A road-hub town sitting on the polar-circle line that made the line itself a destination, with a marked crossing point visitors come to stand on (K3, K8). // care: Check whether its town center has midnight sun but no polar night. Its tourist economy is not part of the claimed parallel; the threshold identity is.
- **#97** Jokkmokk Winter Market, Sweden // claim to test: A market held for centuries in midwinter near the polar circle, timed to the season when frozen ground made travel easier, so access and light set the town's gathering (K2, K4). // care: Sámi tradition; record names of institutions only, and report the history of the state's relations with the Sámi plainly. Founding year and exact date to check.
- **#95** Kangerlussuaq, Greenland // claim to test: A settlement that exists as a transit hub, long the main arrival point for the whole island, and the starting point of one of the few roads that run to the edge of the ice sheet (K5, K9). // care: Former foreign air base. It lies just inside the polar circle. Its role shifted when a new international airport opened elsewhere (date to check); a K9 friction case.
