# Research unit `Amundsen_ST20`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Advanced machinery inside mundane surroundings)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **S1-21** SubTropolis, Kansas City // claim to test: A business park, warehouses and cold storage inside a working limestone mine: the most ordinary uses of space, underground. // care: Verify size, tenants and temperature.
- **S1-22** Automated freezer warehouses (about −20 to −30 °C) and the people who work in them // claim to test: Large cold stores where machines and people work at sub-zero temperatures; shifts, clothing, breaks, health rules. // care: Choose two or three documented operators.
- **S1-23** The Lucens underground experimental reactor, Switzerland // claim to test: A nuclear power plant built in caverns, damaged in a 1969 accident and sealed. // care: Verify the accident and the decommissioning.
- **S1-24** Under-stage and fly machinery of opera houses (e.g., Palais Garnier, including its water reservoir) // claim to test: A hidden world of machinery and crews beneath and above one of the most ornate public rooms. // care: Verify the reservoir's purpose and the machinery's age.
