# Research unit `Amundsen_ST02`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Labyrinthine dwelling towns)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **S2-04** Hakka earthen roundhouses (tulou), Fujian (e.g., Chengqi Lou) // claim to test: Single ring buildings housing hundreds of people of many households, inward-facing, with shared courtyards, wells and stores. // care: Verify room counts and populations; distinguish the famous examples from typical ones.
- **S2-03** Fez el-Bali (the old medina of Fez) // claim to test: A very large car-free medina of lanes, dead ends and courtyards: a whole working city as a maze. // care: Verify size, population and how goods and services move without vehicles.
- **S2-05** Matera's Sassi // claim to test: A cave town layered into a ravine, emptied by government order in the mid-20th century and later revived. // care: Displacement history: report neutrally. Verify the evacuation dates and law.
- **S2-11** Shibam, Hadhramaut // claim to test: A dense walled city of tall mud-brick tower houses, close-packed and connected. // care: Conflict history: report neutrally. Verify current condition and population.
