# Research unit `Casey_C11`: Casey, candidate list (End-of-road and arrival ports)

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-10-26/27 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the row number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note (things to verify, and how to report sensitive history). Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

- **#85** Magadan and the Kolyma Highway // claim to test: A coastal port that is the starting point of the one long road into a cold, remote interior; ships arrive only when the bay's ice allows, and everything bound inland passes through the port first (K5, K7). // care: Nation note: structure only. The road and port were built largely with forced labor in the camp system; report plainly. How long the bay is ice-locked is unsure.
- **#86** Ushuaia, Tierra del Fuego // claim to test: The last city before the polar sea, at the end of a national highway, whose visitor season runs on the southern-summer window; it grew from a waystation into a destination people travel to for itself (K5, K8). // care: Its origin as a penal colony (the old prison is now a museum) should be reported plainly.
- **#87** Puerto Montt and kilometer 0 of the Carretera Austral // claim to test: A port city where a long, thinly settled highway begins and where ferries, trucks and supplies are staged before heading south into the remote region (K5). // care: The region's 19th-century settler history and displacement of Indigenous people should be reported plainly.
- **#52** Punta Arenas, Chile // claim to test: A port that boomed as the obligatory stop on a sea route and had to find a new identity when the route changed (K9, K8). // care: none
- **#47** Halifax, Pier 21 // claim to test: An arrival port through which many immigrants entered and moved inland by train, an arrival point and not the destination (K5). // care: none
- **#106** Cape Town as the "Tavern of the Seas" // claim to test: A resupply station founded to feed and water passing ships that grew into a many-origin city known for its hospitality to travelers (K5, K4, K12). // care: Slavery and dispossession are central to its history; report plainly.
