# Research unit `Amundsen_ST05`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Labyrinthine places (2))

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **S2-08** Chungking Mansions, Hong Kong // claim to test: A single building as a dense, multi-origin world of guesthouses, shops and services. // care: Verify size, occupancy and management structure.
- **S2-10** Wieliczka Salt Mine // claim to test: Kilometers of galleries, chambers and carved halls built by its workers; a workplace that became a maze of rooms. // care: Chapels exist there: describe as spaces only. Verify lengths, depths and the working history.
- **S2-12** Ancestral Pueblo great houses and living pueblos (Pueblo Bonito; Taos Pueblo) // claim to test: Many-roomed, multi-story communal buildings housing a community in one connected structure. // care: Indigenous heritage: use the communities' own names, describe respectfully, respect what communities ask not to be shared.
- **S2-14** Underground concourses in cold cities (Sapporo's underground walkways and malls; Harbin's underground malls) // claim to test: Long heated concourses that let a city live through winter indoors. // care: Verify names, lengths and dates of the Sapporo and Harbin systems.
