# Research unit `Amundsen_ST09`: Amundsen Station, setting types (Cities of the dead (3))

This file holds ONLY the picks of this unit. Do not read any other unit's picks, any city's spec, or any other city's files.
The picks below were drafted from general knowledge on 2026-11-21 and have NOT been verified. Each line gives the pick number and the place, then a **claim to test** (an unverified drafting guess about why it might be a useful parallel; it is NOT a fact, and the research should confirm, narrow or correct it), then any care note. Tiers are the developer's; do not judge them. Every pick gets the same full depth.

**Context for every pick (neutral; do not write about any fictional setting):** the picks are studied as real-world settings: how people live, work and keep machinery running in dense, enclosed, buried or extreme places; how heat, light, space and daily life are managed; and how such places look and feel when they are thriving and after they are abandoned. Religious belief systems are out of scope at this stage; describe sacred or burial sites as places only.

- **S3-17** Towns of the Fukushima evacuation zone (e.g., Futaba, Namie) // claim to test: Ordinary towns emptied by an invisible hazard, homes and shops left mid-day; partial returns. // care: Recent disaster: neutral and careful. Verify return rates.
- **S3-18** Truk (Chuuk) Lagoon's sunken fleet // claim to test: Ships sunk with crews' belongings and cargo in place, explored in first person by divers. // care: War dead: neutral. Verify dates and numbers of wrecks.
- **S3-19** Ghost stations of metro systems (London; Paris; New York's City Hall station) // claim to test: Abandoned underground public spaces inside a living network: a dead layer within working infrastructure. // care: Choose two or three well-documented stations per city.
- **S3-20** Necropolis cities (Recoleta Cemetery, Buenos Aires; Dargavs, North Ossetia; the Valley of the Kings) // claim to test: Streets of tombs and houses for the dead: the city of the dead taken literally, as a built place. // care: Burial sites: describe as places only, no belief content.
