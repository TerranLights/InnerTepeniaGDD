# Byrd T4: slang, nicknames and trade words found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found while researching unit T4 (machine-building cities; building to someone else's design; machines that make
machines; industry in remote interiors and closed production towns). Real-world usage only; nothing here is assigned
to any fictional place. Source is the page or search (numbered as in the T4 log) where the word appeared.

| Word | Where | Meaning as used | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
