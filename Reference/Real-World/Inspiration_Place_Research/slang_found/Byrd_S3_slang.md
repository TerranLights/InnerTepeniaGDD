# Byrd S3 — slang and invented-sounding words found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found while researching unit S3 (waste heat, snow-clearing, ice runways, cold-weather airports). Real-world usage only;
each word is listed with where it was seen. Nothing here is a design proposal.

- **Rodwell** — short for "Rodriguez well", the South Pole station's water well melted into the ice with generator waste
  heat; an old one becomes the sewage outfall. Seen at https://brr.fyi/posts/south-pole-water-infrastructure
- **utilidor** — an insulated, heated box or corridor carrying water and sewer pipes above frozen ground (Inuvik and other
  northern towns). Seen at https://legacy.csce.ca/en/historic-site/town-of-inuvik-infrastructure/
- **cold topping** — a heat pipe that stops working at its top because non-condensable gas collects there (Trans-Alaska
  Pipeline heat pipes). Seen in snippets of https://ascelibrary.com/doi/10.1061/9780784483589.031
- **bypass mail** / **bypass** — Alaska's subsidized pallet freight to bush villages, shipped by private airlines "bypassing"
  the post office. Seen at https://alaskapublic.org/programs/alaska-economic-report/2025-08-21/in-rural-alaska-bypass-mail-rates-are-going-sky-high
- **combi** — a Boeing 737 configured half cargo, half passengers (Alaska Airlines to Deadhorse). Seen in a snippet of
  https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Prudhoe_Bay
- **proof roller** / **proof-rolling** — an 88-metric-ton roller dragged over an ice runway to find weak spots; each weak spot
  found is a "failure". Seen at https://www.antarctica.gov.au/news/2023/wilkins-aerodrome-ready-for-busy-summer-season/
- **clam tent** — the guest tents at Union Glacier Camp. Seen in a snippet of
  https://www.naturalworldsafaris.com/polar-regions/antarctica/safaris-and-planning/union-glacier-camp
- **open skiway** — National Guard term for an unprepared snow landing area. Seen in a snippet of
  https://www.nationalguard.mil/News/Article/3056481/ny-minnesota-airmen-hone-ice-runway-construction-skills/
- **Kavernenflugplatz** — German, "cavern airfield", the Swiss concept of a fighter base inside a mountain. Seen at
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aircraft_cavern (snippet)
- **"It'll work; it just needs to catch up"** — Holland, Michigan snowmelt staff's stock reply after light snow. Seen at
  https://pmmag.com/articles/105527-holland-michigans-35-year-old-snow-melt-system-makes-it-a-year-round-destination
