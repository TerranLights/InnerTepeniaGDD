# Slang and special terms found by chance in unit C7 (2026-11-01, afternoon session)
Real-world terms only; collected, not researched. Sources are in the C7 file (Research_Logs/Amundsen_C7_Faith_Ritual_and_Rites_of_Passage_2026-11-01.md).
- gash run: Australian Antarctic station Saturday waste-removal duty (AAD "Station duties and volunteer positions" page).
- blizz line: blizzard hand line between station buildings; its upkeep is a rostered Saturday duty (same AAD page).
- slushies: kitchen-help rostered duty at Australian stations (same AAD page; also in the C8 slang note).
- Fort Knox: the locked alcohol store at Australian stations, opened weekly (AAD "Alcohol on station").
- Macca: Macquarie Island station's name for itself (AAD "This week at Macquarie Island", 31 July 2015, in "our Macca version of Christmas in July").
- Copper Store: local nickname of the Red Bay Co-operative Society (Labrador, 1896), from the pennies saved to found it (Canadian Geographic, via search summary; unverified).
- Four Hells: IWW name for the Loyal Legion of Loggers and Lumbermen (the "4Ls"), 1917-18 (search summary; unverified).
- lower deck justice: sailors punishing a thief or wrongdoer among themselves, likened to "barracks justice" in the army (hobbyist forum, via search summary; unverified).
- blacklegs, scabs: insults for strike-breakers in the 1984-85 British miners' strike (Oxford oral-history project page).
- NRB ("not required back"): offshore-industry term for banning a contractor worker from an installation (Caithness Business 2026; Energy Voice via search summary).
- Wiffa Film Festival: name of a Mawson station winter film event, 2025 (AAD "This week at Mawson", 29 August 2025); meaning of "Wiffa" not given.
