# Slang found by chance during Amundsen unit B6 (2026-11-01)

Collected only; not used in the research file.

- "R1" - CERN physicists' name for Restaurant 1 (Maclean's, 2012: physicists "bristle" at the word "cafeteria"; "It's a 'restaurant:' or 'R1'").
- "Runglish" - informal hybrid of Russian and English among ISS crews (from a search-result summary of popular articles; not verified in a primary source).
- "ÜWI" / "ÜWIs" - German abbreviation for overwinterers at Neumayer Station (AWI blog, 2025, "old ÜWIs" meaning the previous winter team).
- "revolving door society" - phrase attributed to the Norwegian government for Longyearbyen's turnover (Wikipedia citing a government source); a descriptive phrase rather than slang.
