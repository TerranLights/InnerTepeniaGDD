# Slang and jargon found by chance: unit S2 (living and building inside ice), 2026-11-01

Real-world terms met while researching; kept here, not in the research file. Not evaluated, not proposed for use.

| Term | Meaning as used in the source | Where found |
|---|---|---|
| freshie shack | refrigerated room for perishable ("fresh") food at Summit Station's Big House | ERDC/CRREL TR-11-10 (2011), https://geo-summit.org/sites/default/files/docs/Summit-Station_ERDC-CRREL_TR-11-10.pdf |
| midrats | "midnight rations," a fourth meal served at the South Pole in summer | first-hand account, https://www.yesandyes.org/2014/02/true-story-i-lived-at-south-pole-for.html |
| fuelies | the fuel crew who offload aircraft fuel at the South Pole | https://www.jeffreydonenfeld.com/2012/12/the-south-poles-fuel-supply/ |
| brass board | board at the South Pole tunnel entrance showing who is inside, for emergencies | https://southpolestation.com/trivia/00s/utilidor2.html |
| banana sled | sled used to haul cut snow out of the South Pole tunnels | https://southpolestation.com/trivia/00s/utilidor2.html |
| hypertat | a type of insulated hut used for berthing in the South Pole dome era | https://www.yesandyes.org/2014/02/true-story-i-lived-at-south-pole-for.html |
| bermageddon | the oldest buried waste berms at the South Pole | Polar Journal, 15 February 2024, https://polarjournal.net/problems-at-the-pole/ |
| Beer Can | the South Pole elevated station's utility lift and stairway tower | search snippet (NSF/USAP page), not fetched |
| snice | snow and ice mix used to build the Icehotel | Guinness World Records, https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/largest-ice-structure |
| utilidor | "utility corridor": a buried tunnel carrying power, water and sewer lines (an engineering portmanteau) | https://southpolestation.com/trivia/90s/utilidor.html |
| Peter snow | snow milled by a Swiss Peter snow miller, used as a building material | CRREL TR 174 (Clark 1965) |
| Wonder Arch | corrugated metal arch used over wide trenches at Camp Century (a product name) | CRREL TR 174 (Clark 1965) |
| Sea Bass / Sob Story | radio nicknames of the DYE-2 and DYE-3 radar stations | search result for Wikipedia, DYE Stations |
| Main Street | Camp Century's main trench | Atomic Heritage Foundation, https://ahf.nuclearmuseum.org/?p=31378 |
