# Amundsen C3: slang and nicknames found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found while researching unit C3 (long cold roads, junction towns and leave hostels), afternoon session. Recorded here, not in the research file. Real-world usage only; meaning as given by the source.

| Term | Meaning as used | Where seen |
|---|---|---|
| kochkarka, kochkarnik, kochkodrom (кочкарка, кочкарник, кочкодром) | Russian drivers' words for a frozen washboard surface of ruts and bumps on a winter road, where speed falls to 10-15 km/h | Kolesa.ru, "Зимник Тополиное – Батагай: ледяная артерия Якутии" (Yakutia winter road article) |
| taryny (тарыны) | Local Yakutian word for naledi, the layered ice formed when groundwater or river water breaks through and freezes over a road | Same Kolesa.ru article |
| "road of life and death" (дорога жизни и смерти) | Truckers' nickname for the Topolinoe-Batagai winter road | Same Kolesa.ru article |
| reverse snowbirds | A historian's phrase for Yukon Overland Trail roadhouse operators who worked the winter road season and left for the summer | yukonhistorytrails.com, "The Dawson-Whitehorse Overland Trail - Overview" |
