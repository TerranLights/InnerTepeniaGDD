# Byrd T1: slang and invented-sounding words found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found during unit T1 (underground daily life at city scale). Real-world usage only; nothing here is a proposal.

| Term | Language | Meaning as found | Where found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 鼠族 (*shǔzú*), "rat tribe" or "mouse tribe" | Chinese | Press nickname for people renting rooms in Beijing basements and civil-defense shelters | Search results on Beijing basement housing (Gizmodo, The New Daily, Lee et al. 2017 as "mouse tribe") |
| 蚁族 (*yǐzú*), "ant tribe" | Chinese | Young, low-paid graduates living crowded in cheap housing; used of some basement tenants | NBD (每日经济新闻), 8 August 2012 |
| 北漂 (*běipiāo*), "northern drifters" | Chinese | Migrants who move to Beijing to work without local residence status | NBD, 8 August 2012 (quoting a microblog line) |
| 纳凉点 (*nàliáng diǎn*), "cool-off point" | Chinese | Shelter or public space opened free to residents during heat waves | The Paper (2024), Xinhua (2025), Chongqing |
| 群租 (*qúnzū*), "group renting" | Chinese | Subdividing one space (here a basement) into many small rented rooms | The Paper / Beijing Daily (2018) |
| dugout | Australian English | An underground home cut into the hillside at Coober Pedy | Il Globo; Guinness World Records |
| lithotecture | English (coined, academic) | Dwellings excavated into self-supporting rock ("dugouts") | Baggs and Wong, *Architectural Science Review* (1987) |
| terratecture | English (coined, academic) | Houses with walls and roof earth-covered | Baggs and Wong (1987) |
| *kayaşehir*, "rock city" | Turkish | A rock-cut settlement whose rooms each open to the surface, as distinct from an underground city | showcaves.com, Nevşehir |
| *fleurines* | French (regional, technical) | Natural chimneys in the limestone scree that ventilate the Roquefort caves | INAO product sheet |
| *chikagai* (地下街) | Japanese | Underground shopping street | Architectural Institute of Japan paper (2023) |
| オフィス町内会, "office neighborhood association" | Japanese (coined) | A dormant downtown neighborhood association restarted in 2019 with building owners and firms as members | Sapporo Ekimae-dori Machizukuri Co. presentation (2024) |
| Skyway Avoidance Society | American English (coined name) | A Minneapolis resident's campaign urging people to walk the streets instead of the skyways | Next City (2016) |
| Plus 15 level | Canadian English | The second floor of downtown Calgary buildings, where the walkway network runs, about 15 feet above the street | City of Calgary Plus 15 Policy (2021) |
| *subterraneotherapy* | English (medical, coined) | Treatment using the microclimate of underground salt chambers | Wieliczka Salt Mine Health Resort |
