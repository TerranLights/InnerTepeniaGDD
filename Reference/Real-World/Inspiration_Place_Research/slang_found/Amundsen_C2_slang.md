# Amundsen C2: slang and nicknames found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found while researching unit C2 (network relay and timekeeping institutions), afternoon session. Recorded here, not in the research file. Real-world usage only; meaning as given by the source.

| Term | Meaning as used | Where seen |
|---|---|---|
| Exiles | Cable & Wireless (and Eastern Telegraph) staff posted to cable stations overseas | PK Porthcurno, tennis courts page (https://pkporthcurno.com/tennis/): "Across the globe, Cable Stations had tennis courts where the Exiles enjoyed playing, practicing, and competing." |
| go round the bend | Folk etymology only: travel agents and journalists claim the phrase (to go mad) comes from British telegraph operators posted to Telegraph Island, Musandam, Oman (1864-1868), in extreme heat; UNVERIFIED | Wikipedia, "Telegraph Island" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telegraph_Island), lead section |
| Exiles Club | Name of the Cable & Wireless staff social club in the former Royal Navy barracks, Georgetown, Ascension (name origin unverified) | Search summary of a travel blog (theramblingwombat.com) and an Ascension presentation; page not opened |
