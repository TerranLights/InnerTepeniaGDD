# Slang and special terms found by chance in unit C8 (2026-11-01)
Real-world terms only; collected, not researched. Sources are in the C8 file.
- slushy: Australian Antarctic station kitchen-help roster member ("the slushy roster"); on Sundays "the slushy usually cooks" (AAD, Feeding the troops).
- catch and kill: AAD phrase for expeditioners fending for themselves on the chef's day off.
- GASH: "Galley and Scullery Help", described as a US Navy term, weekly galley cleanup team at an Antarctic station (UAB account, via search summary; unverified).
- freshies: fresh produce at Palmer Station (Roads & Kingdoms).
- the beige plate: Palmer chef's phrase for a plate of undifferentiated starch and chicken.
- crosstown dinner: Palmer tradition when the ship arrives and both crews eat together.
- midrats: midnight rations on submarines and ships (galley guide, via search summary).
- dong: Tristan da Cunha signal, ringing an empty fuel canister to call fishermen out when weather is fair (Discover Magazine).
- McMurdo Sometimez: joking name of the irregular McMurdo newspaper 1960-1980 (search summary; unverified).
- Baby Seagulls: name of the Mawson 2012 winter band.
- Solskinnsboller / solboller: sun buns eaten for the sun-return day in Longyearbyen and Tromsø.
