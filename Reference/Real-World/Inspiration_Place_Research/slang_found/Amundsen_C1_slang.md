# Amundsen C1: slang and nicknames found by chance (2026-11-01)

Found while researching unit C1 (megaproject, science and spaceport towns; afternoon items: hydropower towns and company towns). Recorded here, not in the research file. Real-world usage only; meaning as given by the source.

| Term | Meaning as used | Where seen |
|---|---|---|
| slusk | Norwegian: a laboring man lodging with a worker's family; paid 2.50 kroner including morning and evening coffee (Notodden, about 1913) | Rjukan-Notodden Industriarv sheet "11-grønnebyen" (industriarven.no) |
| rallar (pl. rallarar) | Norwegian: an itinerant construction navvy (railway and plant building) | Store norske leksikon, "Rjukan" |
| Tyskerbyen ("the German town") | Early name for Hydro's row-house districts, after the German plan Sam Eyde brought home | industriarven.no sheet; SNL "Rjukan" |
| Electric Mountain / Mynydd Gwefru | Local name for Dinorwig power station | Wikipedia, "Dinorwig Power Station" |
| the concert hall | Nickname for Dinorwig's main machine hall cavern | Wikipedia, "Dinorwig Power Station" |
| wet canteen | Australian: the pub/tavern in a works town (Cabramurra) | Wikipedia, "Cabramurra" |
| company's spotter | Pullman workers' word for a company informer | Richard T. Ely, Harper's, February 1885 |
| amazons | Insult a mill agent used for the 1834 Lowell strikers | NPS, "Early Strikes in Lowell" |
| daughters of freemen | Lowell strikers' name for themselves (1834) | NPS, "Early Strikes in Lowell" |
| Island of Innocence | Settlement upriver from Fordlândia with bars and brothels | Wikipedia, "Fordlândia" |
| Quebra-Panelas ("Breaking Pans") | The 1930 Fordlândia food riot | Wikipedia, "Fordlândia" |
| Green Hell | Nickname for Fordlândia (malaria) | search summary only (unverified) |
| Kennecott Kids | Former child residents of Kennecott, Alaska, as they call themselves | NPS Kennecott bulletin |
| miner's consumption | Silicosis, as Kennecott miners called it | NPS Kennecott bulletin |
| Baťamen / Baťawomen | Researchers' term for the standardized employees the Baťa system aimed to produce | search summary of a Cambridge Urban History review (unverified) |
| Chocolate Bar-B | Title of a 1936 Hershey union-organizing newsletter | Wikipedia, "Pennsylvania chocolate workers' strike, 1937" |
| the gauntlet | Hershey loyalists forced the 1937 strikers out through a gauntlet | Hershey Community Archives |
