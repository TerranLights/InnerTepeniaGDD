# Aboa (Basen nunatak) — measured monthly mean air temperature, 1989–2001

**Source.** Kärkäs, E. (2004), *Meteorological Conditions of the Basen Nunatak in Western Dronning Maud Land, Antarctica*, Geophysica 40(1–2), 39–52 (Finnish Meteorological Institute data from the Finnish station Aboa). Table 2. Retrieved 2026-10-21 from the Wayback Machine copy of `archive.geophysica.fi/pdf/geophysica_2004_40_1-2_039_karkas.pdf`. The PDF is a scan; the table was read by OCR and **every temperature value was then checked by eye against the page image** (all twelve match).

**Station.** Aboa weather station, 73°02'32.6"S, 13°24'23.6"W, **484 m** above sea level, on the Basen nunatak. Data months: **Feb 1989 – Oct 2001** (months missing in some years; the paper lists them). This is a **record-period mean over roughly 12 years, not a WMO 1991–2020 normal.**

**Annual mean (the paper's text, §4.2): about −15 °C** (one degree warmer than Neumayer in the same years). The highest recorded temperature was 9.7 °C (4 Jan 1991) and the lowest −42 °C (12 May 1995).

**Neighbouring records the paper cites:** Dutch AWS 5, 10 km away and 121 m lower, −17.1 °C (1998–2000); the paper's own comparison with Neumayer and a buoy station on the ice-shelf edge.

**Why this file exists.** The Santa Luce spec's `Mean` column (annual ≈ −23 °C, coldest month −36 °C) differs from this measured record by up to 16.6 °C. See `READER_Recomputed_Normals_2026-10-21.md` and `Research_Logs/Climate_Reconciliation_2026-10-21_Log.md`.
