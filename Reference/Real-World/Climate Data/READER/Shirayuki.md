## Precipitation

**~159 mm** water equivalent annually (AARI figure for the Larsemann Hills); the oasis "rarely exceeds
250 mm." **Notably dry — roughly a quarter of the maritime west-coast cities.**

## Daylight

Computed from 69°24'S, standard −0.833° refraction convention:
**Polar night ~May 29 → Jul 16 (~49 days). Midnight sun ~Nov 22 → Jan 22 (~62 days).**
⚠ *The two spans are strongly asymmetric this close to the Circle — refraction lengthens midnight sun and
shortens polar night. Any pairing that gives them near-equal lengths at this latitude is wrong.*

## ⚠ Note — this proxy covers THREE Tepenian cities

**Shirayuki** (this file), **Sinheung** (Progress Station site, ~1–2 km) and **Zhongshan** (~15 km) all
occupy one 40 km² ice-free oasis and share one climate. **Climate differentiates none of the three.**
