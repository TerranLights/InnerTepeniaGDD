# Climate Reference: Juan_Carlos

**READER status:** Not in BAS READER surface station database *(seasonal station — no continuous 30-year normal)*

**Site:** Hurd Peninsula, Livingston Island, South Shetland Islands — **62°39'S 60°23'W**

**Climate authority:** Spanish Antarctic Program — Juan Carlos I Station *(AWS year-round; manual observations in summer)*

> **⚠ RESOLVED 2026-09-04 by proxy.**

## Resolution — proxy station `King_Sejong`

**~95 km, King George Island — the same South Shetland arc, the same maritime regime.** Nearest READER
station with a full normal.

**Cross-check:** the Juan Carlos I station's own published annual mean is **−1.2 °C** against the proxy's
**−1.7 °C**. **The site is ~0.5 °C warmer than the proxy — well inside tolerance, so the proxy is used
unmodified.**

> ### ⚠ SOURCING TRAP — the −2.8 °C figure is NOT this station
> A mean annual temperature of **−2.8 °C** is widely reported for *Livingston Island* and is the number a
> casual search returns. **It belongs to Byers Peninsula**, the exposed far-western end of the island,
> ~30 km from and climatically distinct from the Hurd Peninsula station site. **Same island, wrong
> number.** *(A city spec built on it would run ~1.6 °C too cold.)*

## Precipitation

⚠ **No station figure obtained.** Regional maritime estimate **~600–700 mm** water equivalent, rain and
snow both *(anchored on Palmer Station's measured 658 mm at comparable latitude and exposure)*. **This is
the weakest number in this file — an open item, not a measurement.**

## Daylight

Computed from 62°39'S, standard −0.833° refraction convention:
⛔ **NO polar night and NO midnight sun.** The site is **~435 km north of the Antarctic Circle**. Winter
solstice ~4.8 h; summer solstice ~20.1 h.
