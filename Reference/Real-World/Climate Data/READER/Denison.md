# Climate Reference: Denison

**READER status:** Not in BAS READER surface station database

**Site:** Cape Denison, Commonwealth Bay, George V Land / Adélie Land coast — **67°00'S 142°40'E**

**Climate authority (temperature):** Institut polaire français Paul-Émile Victor (IPEV) — via proxy station
**Climate authority (wind):** Australasian Antarctic Expedition 1911–14 (Mawson), measured at the site

> **⚠ Created 2026-09-04.** This file did not previously exist — **Denison was the one city of the 37 with
> no READER entry of any kind**, stub or otherwise. Resolved by proxy; see below.

## Resolution — SPLIT SOURCING

**Temperature is proxied. Wind is measured at the site and must NOT be taken from the proxy.**

| Variable | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Temperature** | proxy: READER `Dumont_Durville`, ~120 km west along the same coast | annual **−11.0 °C** |
| **Wind** | **measured at Cape Denison**, AAE 1912–14 | annual mean **19.3 m/s** |
| *(contrast)* | READER `Dumont_Durville` wind | annual mean **~9.7 m/s** |

### Why the wind does not transfer

Dumont d'Urville sits on an island **offshore of** the base of the ice slope. **Cape Denison sits at the
foot of it**, at the outflow of a broad interior drainage basin. **The proxy is sound for temperature and
invalid for wind — nearly a factor of two.** Cape Denison's 19.3 m/s is the highest annual mean wind speed
recorded at any near-sea-level site on Earth.

## Precipitation

**~655 mm** water equivalent (ERA-Interim reanalysis) / **~679 mm** (climatology) for the Dumont d'Urville
area. ⚠ **A large fraction sublimates in the dry katabatic surface layer before reaching the ground** —
measured at DDU by profiling radar during the APRES3 campaigns. **The falling total substantially exceeds
the accumulating total, and more so at Cape Denison than at DDU.**

## Daylight

Computed from 67°00'S, standard −0.833° refraction convention:
**No polar night** *(the site is only ~48 km inside the Antarctic Circle — less than refraction; the sun
clears the horizon for ~1.5 h even at the winter solstice)*. **Midnight sun ~Dec 5 → Jan 9 (~36 days).**
