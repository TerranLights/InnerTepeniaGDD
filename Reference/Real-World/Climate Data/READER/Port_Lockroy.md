## Precipitation

**~658 mm** water equivalent annually (Palmer Station figure), rain and snow both. Among the wettest sites
in the Tepenian set.

## Daylight

Computed from 64°49'S, standard −0.833° refraction convention:
⛔ **NO polar night and NO midnight sun.** **The site is ~195 km NORTH of the Antarctic Circle** — the sun
rises and sets every day of the year. Winter solstice ~3.6 h; summer solstice ~21.8 h.

> ⚠ **The city spec previously claimed a ~14-day polar night and ~18-day midnight sun here.** Both were
> fabrications inherited from Rothera. Corrected 2026-09-04.
