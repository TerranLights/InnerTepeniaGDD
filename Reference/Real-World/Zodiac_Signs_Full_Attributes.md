# The Zodiac Signs: Full Attributes (Positive and Negative)

**What this is:** a reference catalog of the traditional Western zodiac's twelve signs, each with its
astrological basics (element, modality, ruling planet, symbol) and a full accounting of its positive and
negative attributes — the same treatment given to the Six and Seven symbolic sets in
`Symbolic_Sets_of_Six_and_Seven.md`. Compiled specifically to support future "test-run" comparisons of
Concordia's own 12 zodiac-coded districts (plus the zodiac-free Hub), run both without this material and
then again with it layered on top, to see what it actually adds. Reminder, consistent with standing project
convention: Concordia's own zodiac naming is the developer's own out-of-fiction organizational scheme, not
an in-world conceit any district is aware of — this file is a creative-input tool, not new in-world lore.

---

## Aries — The Ram

**Element:** Fire. **Modality:** Cardinal. **Ruling planet:** Mars. **Dates:** March 21 – April 19.

Cardinal signs initiate; Fire signs act on instinct and passion rather than deliberation. Aries is the
zodiac's own opening sign — the first spark of a new cycle, ruled by the planet of action, conflict, and
raw drive.

- **Positive:** Courage and initiative — a genuine willingness to go first, try the untested thing, take the
  hit nobody else wants to take. Directness and honesty, often blunt but rarely dishonest. Natural
  leadership that leads from the front rather than the rear. Competitive energy that pushes performance
  upward rather than tearing others down. Protective instinct toward people or causes it's committed to.
  Decisiveness — Aries rarely agonizes over a choice once it's actually facing one.
- **Negative:** Impulsiveness that skips past consequences entirely. Impatience with anything that requires
  sustained, unglamorous follow-through. A short temper that flares fast and burns out just as fast, often
  leaving real damage behind it. Genuine difficulty compromising — a need to win that can override the
  actual stakes of what's being won. Insensitivity to how directness lands on people who needed it
  delivered more gently. Recklessness that mistakes speed for courage.

## Taurus — The Bull

**Element:** Earth. **Modality:** Fixed. **Ruling planet:** Venus. **Dates:** April 20 – May 20.

Fixed signs sustain and stabilize; Earth signs are grounded in the material and the physically real.
Taurus, ruled by the planet of beauty and pleasure, is stability expressed through appetite, comfort, and
loyalty rather than through discipline for its own sake.

- **Positive:** Reliability that genuinely never wavers — Taurus is the sign people build long-term plans
  around because it actually shows up. Patience with long, slow processes other signs would abandon.
  Practicality grounded in what actually works, not what sounds impressive. Deep, unglamorous loyalty and
  devotion. A real, sensory appreciation for beauty, comfort, and pleasure, treated as legitimate rather
  than frivolous. Determination that simply doesn't quit once committed.
- **Negative:** Stubbornness severe enough to hold a position well past the point it stopped making sense.
  Possessiveness over people, objects, and territory alike. Materialism that measures worth by what can be
  held or owned. Active resistance to change, even change that's clearly needed. Complacency — comfort
  can calcify into inertia. Jealousy, and a self-indulgence that tips generosity toward oneself into
  genuine excess.

## Gemini — The Twins

**Element:** Air. **Modality:** Mutable. **Ruling planet:** Mercury. **Dates:** May 21 – June 20.

Mutable signs adapt and transition; Air signs live in thought, communication, and social connection.
Gemini, ruled by the planet of messages and quick wit, is duality itself — literally the twins — always
holding two ideas, two moods, two directions at once.

- **Positive:** Adaptability that thrives on change rather than merely tolerating it. Genuine intellectual
  curiosity, always reaching for the next idea or conversation. Communication skill and wit sharp enough to
  make any exchange more interesting than it would have been without them. Versatility across skills and
  contexts. A real enthusiasm for learning that never fully switches off.
- **Negative:** Inconsistency — commitments made with real enthusiasm that quietly evaporate. Superficiality,
  skimming many things instead of settling into one. Indecisiveness born from genuinely seeing every side at
  once. Nervous, restless energy that struggles to sit still, literally or mentally. A tendency toward
  gossip, since information is currency Gemini instinctively trades in. The duplicity the twins symbolism is
  named for — not necessarily malicious, but a real capacity to present two different faces without
  reconciling them.

## Cancer — The Crab

**Element:** Water. **Modality:** Cardinal. **Ruling planet:** the Moon. **Dates:** June 21 – July 22.

Cardinal signs initiate; Water signs feel first and reason second. Cancer, ruled by the Moon itself, is
the zodiac's own emotional foundation — home, family, and the instinct to protect the vulnerable, carried
inside a hard shell built specifically because what's underneath is so soft.

- **Positive:** Deep, genuine nurturing instinct — Cancer notices who's hurting before they say anything.
  Profound empathy and emotional intuition. Fierce loyalty and protectiveness, especially toward home and
  family, broadly defined. Real tenacity once committed to protecting someone or something. Resourcefulness
  under pressure, especially where safety or care is on the line.
- **Negative:** Moodiness that can shift the emotional temperature of a whole room without warning.
  Over-sensitivity to perceived slights, real or imagined. Clinginess and a genuine fear of being left.
  Manipulation through guilt, often unconscious rather than calculated. Defensiveness — the hard shell isn't
  just protective, it can also wall people out who never meant any harm. A tendency to live in the past,
  and real difficulty letting old hurts go.

## Leo — The Lion

**Element:** Fire. **Modality:** Fixed. **Ruling planet:** the Sun. **Dates:** July 23 – August 22.

Fixed signs sustain; Fire signs act on passion and instinct. Leo, ruled by the Sun itself, is warmth and
radiance made into a personality — the zodiac's own performer, generous with its light but needing to be
seen giving it.

- **Positive:** Real warmth and generosity, given freely rather than transactionally. Genuine confidence
  that draws people in rather than pushing them away. Creativity and a natural gift for drama and spectacle.
  Loyalty to those it loves. Natural leadership that inspires rather than commands. A real capacity to
  encourage others to shine alongside it, not just bask alone.
- **Negative:** Arrogance, and a need for constant attention and validation that can exhaust everyone
  supplying it. Stubbornness once an opinion is formed. Domineering behavior when leadership tips into
  control. Vanity and a genuine vulnerability to criticism that can land far harder than intended.
  Self-centeredness that can crowd out other people's own moments. Melodrama — treating ordinary stakes as
  though they were a stage production.

---

## Virgo — The Maiden

**Element:** Earth. **Modality:** Mutable. **Ruling planet:** Mercury. **Dates:** August 23 – September 22.

Mutable signs adapt; Earth signs are grounded in the practical and the real. Virgo, ruled by the planet of
analysis and detail, is precision in service of usefulness — the zodiac's own craftsperson, most
comfortable improving something real rather than admiring something abstract.

- **Positive:** Genuine analytical skill and precision. Real diligence and follow-through most signs can't
  match. Practicality grounded in what actually helps. A sincere, often self-effacing desire to be useful.
  Reliability built from attention to detail others skip past. Modesty that doesn't need credit to keep
  doing the work.
- **Negative:** Perfectionism severe enough to make "good enough" feel like failure. Over-criticism, turned
  on others but just as often turned inward. Anxiety and worry that can spiral over details nobody else
  noticed. Nitpicking that mistakes small flaws for large ones. Rigidity around how things "should" be
  done. Genuine difficulty relaxing or accepting imperfection, in itself or anyone else.

## Libra — The Scales

**Element:** Air. **Modality:** Cardinal. **Ruling planet:** Venus. **Dates:** September 23 – October 22.

Cardinal signs initiate; Air signs live in relationship and idea. Libra, ruled by the planet of beauty and
harmony, is balance itself — the zodiac's own diplomat, most alive in genuine partnership and most
uncomfortable with open conflict.

- **Positive:** A real, felt commitment to fairness and justice. Genuine diplomatic skill — finding the
  version of a solution most people can actually live with. Natural charm and social grace. A sincere
  appreciation for harmony and beauty in both people and spaces. Cooperative instinct that makes
  partnership, not solo achievement, feel like the actual point.
- **Negative:** Indecisiveness that can leave real decisions perpetually unmade. Conflict-avoidance severe
  enough to let real problems fester rather than be named. People-pleasing that quietly erodes its own
  stated opinions. Superficiality — smoothing surface harmony without resolving what's underneath.
  Dependency on others for a sense of identity or validation. Procrastination, particularly around anything
  requiring an unpopular call.

## Scorpio — The Scorpion

**Element:** Water. **Modality:** Fixed. **Ruling planet:** Mars (traditional) / Pluto (modern).
**Dates:** October 23 – November 21.

Fixed signs sustain; Water signs feel with real depth and intensity. Scorpio, ruled by the planet(s) of
conflict and transformation, is intensity itself — the zodiac's own investigator, drawn to what's hidden,
uncomfortable, or true beneath a polished surface.

- **Positive:** Genuine intensity and passion, rarely halfhearted about anything it commits to.
  Resourcefulness under real pressure. Fierce, once-earned loyalty. A real gift for investigation — finding
  what's actually true rather than what's comfortable. Resilience through hardship most signs wouldn't
  survive intact. An honest willingness to name hard truths other signs avoid. A transformative power that
  can turn real crisis into real change.
- **Negative:** Jealousy and possessiveness. Secretiveness that can shade into genuine dishonesty by
  omission. Vindictiveness — a real capacity to hold and act on a grudge. Controlling behavior, especially
  where trust has already been broken once. Obsessiveness that can fixate past the point of usefulness.
  All-or-nothing thinking that struggles to hold nuance. Manipulation, when directness feels too exposing
  to risk.

## Sagittarius — The Archer

**Element:** Fire. **Modality:** Mutable. **Ruling planet:** Jupiter. **Dates:** November 22 – December 21.

Mutable signs adapt; Fire signs act on passion and instinct. Sagittarius, ruled by the planet of expansion
and luck, is the zodiac's own wanderer and philosopher — always aiming at the next horizon, more interested
in the search for meaning than any single settled answer.

- **Positive:** Real optimism, genuinely expecting things to work out rather than performing positivity.
  A love of adventure and new experience. Philosophical curiosity — a real hunger to understand the bigger
  picture. Honesty that can be refreshingly blunt rather than politically managed. Generosity, especially
  with knowledge, opportunity, and enthusiasm. A deep love of freedom, for itself and for others.
- **Negative:** Tactlessness — bluntness that forgets to consider how it lands. Over-promising, since
  Sagittarius's own enthusiasm often outruns its actual follow-through. Restlessness that struggles to stay
  anywhere, literally or emotionally, once the novelty fades. A real fear of commitment. Impatience with
  fine detail once the big idea is already understood. A tendency to overreach, promising more than any
  single person or plan can actually deliver.

## Capricorn — The Sea-Goat

**Element:** Earth. **Modality:** Cardinal. **Ruling planet:** Saturn. **Dates:** December 22 – January 19.

Cardinal signs initiate; Earth signs are grounded in the practical and the real. Capricorn, ruled by the
planet of discipline, time, and consequence, is ambition given structure — the zodiac's own builder, most
comfortable climbing something real and durable over the long term.

- **Positive:** Genuine discipline and follow-through across years, not just moments. Real ambition paired
  with the patience to actually earn it. A strong sense of responsibility and reliability. Practicality that
  favors what actually lasts. Resilience through real setbacks. Long-term planning most other signs can't
  sustain. Integrity — Capricorn tends to actually do what it said it would.
- **Negative:** Pessimism, sometimes assuming the worst before it's earned. Rigidity around process and
  hierarchy. Workaholism that crowds out rest or relationship. Emotional coldness or distance, especially
  under pressure. A real vulnerability to status-obsession. Unforgiving standards, applied to itself as much
  as anyone else. Genuine difficulty accepting help, even when it's needed.

## Aquarius — The Water-Bearer

**Element:** Air. **Modality:** Fixed. **Ruling planet:** Saturn (traditional) / Uranus (modern).
**Dates:** January 20 – February 18.

Fixed signs sustain; Air signs live in idea and principle. Aquarius, ruled by the planet(s) of structure
and sudden disruption both, is the zodiac's own reformer — committed to a better system in the abstract,
sometimes at real cost to warmth in the particular.

- **Positive:** Genuine originality and independent thought. A real humanitarian streak — caring about
  people in the aggregate, systemically, not just individually. Intellectual curiosity paired with
  visionary, big-picture thinking. Friendliness that welcomes a wide range of people without much
  gatekeeping. Progressive values and a real comfort with unconventional solutions.
- **Negative:** Emotional detachment, sometimes mistaking principle for an excuse to avoid real intimacy.
  Stubbornness (a fixed-sign trait) around its own convictions. Aloofness that can read as coldness even
  when it isn't meant that way. Contrarianism, occasionally for its own sake rather than genuine
  disagreement. Unpredictability. Self-righteousness about being the one who sees further than everyone
  else.

## Pisces — The Fish

**Element:** Water. **Modality:** Mutable. **Ruling planet:** Jupiter (traditional) / Neptune (modern).
**Dates:** February 19 – March 20.

Mutable signs adapt; Water signs feel first, with real depth. Pisces, the zodiac's own final sign, is
symbolized by two fish swimming in opposite directions — dissolution of boundary itself, equally capable of
profound compassion and complete escape from reality.

- **Positive:** Deep, genuine compassion and empathy — a real capacity to feel what someone else is feeling.
  Artistic and imaginative gifts, often channeled into real creative work. Spiritual depth and intuition.
  Adaptability that can flow around almost any obstacle. Selflessness, sometimes to a fault, but genuinely
  felt. A gift for dreaming that, well-channeled, becomes real creative vision.
- **Negative:** Escapism — a real pull toward avoiding hard reality rather than facing it. A tendency toward
  victimhood or self-pity. Poor boundaries, absorbing other people's emotions until they're indistinguishable
  from its own. Indecisiveness, similar to Libra's but rooted in emotional overwhelm rather than diplomatic
  caution. A susceptibility to illusion, denial, and — in its more severe expressions — addiction or
  unhealthy dependency. A martyr streak that can quietly resent the very sacrifices it insists on making.

## Worth Your Attention

Read across all twelve, a pattern worth naming: nearly every sign's own listed negative attributes aren't a
separate, opposite trait bolted on beside the positive ones — they're the *same* trait pushed past its
useful range. Aries' courage becomes recklessness; Virgo's precision becomes paralysis; Cancer's protective
instinct becomes a wall nobody can get past; Libra's fairness becomes an inability to ever actually decide.
That's worth keeping in mind for the district test-runs this file was built to support: a district's own
"Zodiac-appended" version isn't necessarily a district gaining a new quality it didn't have — it may just be
naming which direction an already-established trait could tip toward, for better or worse, if pushed.
