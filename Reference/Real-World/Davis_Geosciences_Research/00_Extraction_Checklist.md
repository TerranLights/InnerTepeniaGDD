## Extraction checklist by source (Davis-relevant subset only)

- [x] `Groundwater Geochemistry A Practical Guide to Modeling of Natural and Contaminated Aquatic
      Systems.pdf` (Merkel, Planer-Friedrich) — **DONE, modest yield.** A technical PHREEQC modeling
      manual, not a narrative source — most content not usable without distortion. One real find: extreme
      halophiles/thermophiles as documented real inhabitants of hypersaline groundwater (a genuine,
      distinct extremophile angle for Davis's hypersaline lakes, separate from Vostok's ice-sealed
      premise), plus real cave-adapted blind-fish genera. Full writeup in `01`.
- [x] `Subsurface Hydrology.pdf` (Pinder, Celia) — **DONE 2026-09-15, MODERATE yield.** One sharp, quotable
      distinction (Fig. 3.21 caption, fractured Precambrian rock): ***"the rock blocks are of low permeability
      and porosity, the fractures have a high permeability and low porosity."*** ⭐ **That refines Kirsch:
      fractures CONDUCT water but barely STORE it — a conduit, not a reservoir.** Also supplies the term
      *secondary permeability*. ⚠ **Its `arctic`/`Antarctica` hits (15) are PALEOGEOGRAPHIC** — Gondwanaland
      continental positions — **not cold-region hydrogeology.** §3.4 "Igneous Rocks" is mostly basalt-flow
      aquifers, not granite.
- [x] `Groundwater Geophysics A Tool for Hydrogeology.pdf` (Kirsch) — **DONE 2026-09-15, MODERATE-HIGH yield.**
      Two load-bearing mechanisms: **groundwater in hard rock lives in joints and fissures** *(incl. cooling
      joints of igneous rock)* while the rock body is *"nearly impermeable"*; and **coastal fresh water sits as
      a density LENS on salt**, with an interface the book itself calls *balanced* or *disturbed*.
      ⛔ **ZERO permafrost coverage — a real source-death absence.** Full writeup:
      `../Groundwater_Geophysics_Extraction.md`.
- [x] `Introduction to Geochemistry.pdf` (Misra) — **DONE 2026-09-15, LOW yield, and the reason matters.**
      ⛔ ***`limnology` 0 hits · `meromictic` 0 · `lacustrine` 1.*** Its 61 `evaporite` and 584 `isotope` hits
      are **deep-time MARINE geochemistry** *(calcite-vs-aragonite seas, δ³⁴S seawater records)* and
      **mineral-pair geothermometry for ore and metamorphic rocks** — **not** lake-sediment paleoclimate.
      ⚠ **`City_and_District_Research_Topics.md` claims this folder *"fits Davis's own sediment-core/limnology
      research methodology directly."* For this book that is simply not true.** ⛔ **Albarede and Ottonello NOT
      opened** — the checklist's "read one, not all three" stands, and the one read did not earn the other two.
- [x] `World Geologic Atlas. Sheet 17 (Antarctica).zip` — **DONE 2026-09-15. ⭐ FORMAT QUESTION RESOLVED: a
      single 15,166 × 8,882 px JPEG page image**, not text. **Readable directly as an image** *(overview →
      quadrant → site crop)*, so a page-image plate is **NOT** a dead end for a multimodal reader.
      **Finding:** the Prydz Bay coastal outcrop is mapped as **`γPz`**, decoded **from the sheet's own legend**
      *(not from convention)* as **Paleozoic granite**, an isolated exposure ringed inland by ice-above-sea-level.
      ⚠ **Two caveats: 1:10,000,000 is REGIONAL detail — it cannot resolve the Vestfold Hills internally; and
      the sheet is 1979.** ⏸️ **OPEN THREAD: whether modern mapping revises that age assignment — NOT asserted
      from recall.** ⛔ `Sheet 18 (Antarctic ocean)` **not opened** — bathymetric/oceanic, no matching deficit.
- [x] `Environmental Science Demystified.pdf` (Williams) — ⭐⭐ **DONE 2026-09-15, HIGH yield — the highest in
      this folder, from the title this checklist rated LOWEST.** ⛔ ***It is the ONLY one of the five volumes
      that covers frozen ground at all*** *(`permafrost` 19, `frost` 36, `frozen` 26, `freeze-thaw` 3 — all four
      specialists score 0–4)*. Supplies: **permafrost as ground whose strength is THERMAL and whose failure is a
      PHASE CHANGE** *("nearly as hard as rock… if warmed, it becomes soupy")*; **the Trans-Alaska refrigeration
      case** — a structure must manage its own waste heat forever or destroy its own foundation; **"periglacial"**
      as the registered term for ice-free ground bordering ice, active layer *"only the top few centimeters"*;
      and **frost wedging operating in the JOINTS of rock.** Full writeup:
      `../Environmental_Science_Demystified_Extraction.md`.
