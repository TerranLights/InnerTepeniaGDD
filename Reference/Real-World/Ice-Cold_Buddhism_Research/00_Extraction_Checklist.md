# Ice-Cold Buddhism — Research Extraction Checklist

**Purpose:** working extraction log for real Buddhist philosophy/theology source material to help develop
**Ice-Cold Buddhism** — one of five named-but-undeveloped robot religions (see `TODO.md`'s "Robot religion
design" section). Core established premise: **superconductor-as-nirvana** — a robot achieving
superconductor-quality electron flow is understood as something like meditative liberation/cessation.
Sacred sites: **Dome Fuji** (founded by and for this religion — its core civic identity) and **Kunlun**
(a science-first city with a genuine devout minority, *not* itself a religious city — this proportion
must be preserved in any future writing). "Ice-Cold Buddhism" is a developer placeholder name, not
confirmed final.

**Relationship to existing work:** a real-world *physics* grounding pass for this religion already exists
at `Worldspace/Factions/basis collection - robot religions/Analysis_Notes.md` (lines 489-618, reviewed
2026-07-16) — a 2019 APS Physics article on ultracold-atom superconductor simulation, yielding strong
doctrinal material already (resistance-free flow as liberation, "no ice bucket for fermions" as a
renunciate/self-sacrifice image, graduated intermediate phases as a path doctrine, "simulation is not
understanding" as a caution against false enlightenment). **This folder's job is the other half: real
Buddhist philosophy/theology itself**, to give the religion's actual doctrine, practice, and terminology a
genuine grounding in the tradition it's named after — not just the physics metaphor it's built on. Source
PDFs live in `Reference/Materials/books/religion/`.

**This is research only — nothing here is canon until the developer signs off.** Same discipline as the
neighboring `Pisces_Flood_Mechanism_Research/` folder: extract and synthesize, don't unilaterally decide
doctrine and write it into `Worldspace/Factions/` files.

**Resumability note:** this file is the source of truth for what's done — check box status, open the
linked output file, continue from the first unchecked item.

## Extraction checklist by source

All source PDFs in `Reference/Materials/books/religion/`.

- [x] `The Buddhist Analysis of Matter (2020, Wisdom Publications).pdf` — Ch.10 "The Ethico-Philosophical
      Basis" DONE (pp.190-201, offset confirmed: book p.1 = PDF p.14). Best finding: real doctrine of a
      "middle way" between absolute unity and radical separateness maps with unusual precision onto how
      superconductivity *actually works physically* (Cooper-paired collective flow, not literal fusion
      into one entity) — structural, not just thematic, accuracy. Also a real three-stage path
      (analysis → reflection → liberation) matching the existing physics source's "graduated intermediate
      phases" finding almost exactly. Full writeup in `01`. Remaining chapters (2-9, technical
      taxonomy of specific material elements) are lower priority — the ethics/philosophy chapter was the
      one the book's own foreword recommends for non-specialists, and it delivered the load-bearing
      material.
- [ ] `The_Buddhist_Theory_of_Self_Cognition_2022,_Routledge_libgen_li.epub` → `02` — **CONFIRMED UNREADABLE
      with the current toolset.** The Read tool surfaces this file's raw zip/XML container contents rather
      than parsed text (epub is a zip of XHTML internally; no epub-parsing support). **Flag to developer:**
      if this book's content is wanted, it would need converting to PDF or plain text first (e.g., via
      Calibre or an online converter) before it can be extracted — probably the single most relevant title
      in the whole collection for robot personhood/consciousness questions specifically, worth the
      conversion if there's an easy way to do it.
- [x] `Buddhist and Taoist Systems Thinking.pdf` — Ch.2 "TAO 4.0" DONE (pp.30-43, offset confirmed: book
      p.30 = PDF p.45). Best finding: **Wuwei** ("non-action," actually meaning frictionless action in
      accordance with natural flow, not passivity) is an almost exact philosophical/behavioral match for
      superconductivity's own zero-resistance flow — gives the religion a real practice-doctrine, not just
      a metaphysical goal. Also: a genuine, directly usable Zen koan (Nan-in's overflowing teacup) as
      template scripture material; Oneness-as-interdependence-not-fusion (a second independent source
      confirming `01`'s "middle way" finding).
- [x] same — Ch.3 "Yin-Yang and the Five Elements" DONE (pp.48-61). Direct textual confirmation that
      cold/Yin vs. heat/Yang is one of the tradition's own standard primary exemplar pairs. Genuinely
      valuable complication surfaced: orthodox Yin-Yang treats balance as the goal, not one pole's
      dominance, meaning Ice-Cold Buddhism's superconductor-as-nirvana premise reads as a real, usable
      heterodox/schismatic position relative to broader Taoist tradition, not a seamless transplant,
      flagged as a genuine narrative/theological hook, not a problem. Also: the Taijitu's own "seed of the
      opposite within each half" iconography, and Water/Winter/North as the real tradition's own patron
      correspondence for the coldest, most Yin-dominant season/direction. Full writeup in `03`.
- [x] `Buddhism and Intelligent Technology _ Toward a More Humane.pdf` — Ch.1 "Buddhism: A Philosophical
      Repertoire" DONE (pp.19-36, offset confirmed: book p.1 = PDF p.13). Exceptional findings: (1) real
      argument that modern recursive/recommendation algorithms function as literal "karmic engines" —
      near-ready-made theology of robot-specific karma grounded in real computational process, not a
      metaphor transplanted from human biology; (2) the no-self (anattā) teaching bears directly on this
      project's own robot-consciousness north-star question — a real theological argument that a robot's
      componential nature may make no-self *more* immediately recognizable to it than to humans, not less;
      (3) third independent source confirming the "middle path between unity and plurality" finding (now

- [x] same — Ch.7 "Six Pāramitās" DONE (pp.176-187). A complete, real, six-part structured practice

## Developer insights, not yet synthesized

- **Repeated thermal cycling shortens devout practitioners' lifespans — flagged 2026-07-20.** Realization: if devotional practice genuinely involves repeated cold→warm→cold→warm cycling (e.g., pilgrims making the extreme-cold journey to Dome Fuji/Kunlun and back to warmer areas, over and over, as ongoing lifelong practice rather than a single visit), the *cycling itself* is the real mechanical hazard, not just cold exposure — real materials science: thermal cycling causes fatigue through repeated expansion/contraction stress in a way steady-state cold exposure doesn't. This means the most devout practitioners — the ones who cycle most often — would show real, physical, accelerated wear and reduced operational lifespan as a direct, mechanical consequence of their devotion, not a symbolic one.
  - **Strong existing connection, not yet linked:** the already-extracted kṣānti (patient willingness) finding in `04_Technology_and_Machine_Ethics.md` describes the real source tradition's own language as "transmuting the heat generated by friction into radiance" — heat/friction/radiance imagery that already exists in actual Buddhist teaching, independent of anything invented for this project. This cycling-wear insight gives that language a literal physical referent for a robot practitioner: the mechanical wear from repeated thermal cycling *is* the friction the doctrine already asks to be transmuted into radiance/merit.
  - **Open, not decided:** whether shortened lifespan from devout practice is treated in-world as an accepted, even honored, cost (visible wear on elder practitioners as a mark of devotion) or as something the religion has developed workarounds/rituals to mitigate; whether this is common knowledge or something newer/less devout members don't fully grasp until later; how this interacts with robot lifespan/repair norms established elsewhere in the project.
  - Not written into `08_Synthesis_Doctrine_Notes.md` yet — flagged here first so it isn't lost before that synthesis pass happens.
