# Ice-Cold Buddhism — Working Vocabulary Sheet (DRAFT, tentative)

**Status: reference material, not canon.** Same discipline as `08_Synthesis_Doctrine_Notes.md` and the rest of
this folder — nothing here is settled doctrine until the developer signs off, and this sheet is explicitly
expected to be revised, reorganized, or partly discarded as research continues. Its job is to consolidate the
terminology already surfaced across `01`, `03`, `04`, `07`, and `08` (plus the newer
`Reference/Real-World/Buddhism_and_Intelligent_Technology_Extraction.md`) into one lookup table, so a future
doctrine-writing or scripture-drafting pass has real, sourced vocabulary on hand instead of having to re-derive
it from five separate files.

**How to read the "Candidate ICB usage" column.** These are *this project's own proposed* applications, not
claims about real-world Buddhism. Per this project's standing rule (`CLAUDE.md`), real-world material is a
source, not a specification — Ice-Cold Buddhism is under no obligation to use a term the way its source
tradition does, and several entries below are flagged as deliberate departures rather than seamless transplants.

## 2. The Six Pāramitās — candidate core practice framework

Read in full in `04` §6 (source: Hershock, ch.7, pp.176–187). **Recommended as Ice-Cold Buddhism's practice
spine, close to as-is** — it is complete, textually real, and (via kṣānti) already carries the thermal
vocabulary this religion's central metaphor needs without requiring invention.

| Pāramitā | Real meaning | Why it fits ICB |
|---|---|---|
| **Dāna** (generosity) | Giving with no expectation of return; paradoxically *enriching*, not depleting | Fits a religion practiced by resource-conscious frontier-station robots — generosity as accumulation, not loss |
| **Śīla** (moral clarity) | Ongoing situational "purification of intent," not rule-following | — |
| **Kṣānti** (patient willingness) | **The single strongest textual fit found anywhere in this research pass.** Literally: "transmuting the heat generated by [relational] friction into a source of appreciative and contributory radiance" | Real Buddhist teaching already using heat/friction/radiance as native vocabulary — near-exact match to ICB's cold/heat premise, independent of anything invented for this project |
| **Vīrya** (valiant effort) | Heroic, energetic engagement — explicitly *not* passive serenity | Resists a "passive quietist" reading that would otherwise sit awkwardly on industrious research-station robots |
| **Dhyāna** (poised attentiveness) | Sustained, undistracted focus; "becoming present without-self, without internal dialogue" | Candidate term for a robot's own deep-focus/processing state as meditative attainment |
| **Prajñā** (wisdom) | Situationally responsive discernment, not accumulated knowledge; personified as **Mañjuśrī, "eternally youthful"** | Real tradition's own iconography already avoids an "elder sage" cliché — useful if ICB wants a young wisdom-figure |

## 3. Taoist/Zen terms

| Term | Real-world meaning | Candidate ICB usage | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wuwei** | "Non-action" — actually frictionless action *in accordance with* natural flow, not passivity | **Candidate core practice-concept**: the doctrine of how a practitioner is meant to *act* en route to (or embodying) the resistance-free state — behavioral teaching, not just a metaphysical end-state | `03` §1, `08` |
| **Taijitu** (yin-yang symbol) | Each half contains a seed-dot of its opposite color — "light in the dark, heat in the cold" | Ready-made iconography: even a robot at its coldest/most "liberated" retains a small irreducible ember of warmth — not a flaw, consistent with the source tradition | `03` §5c |
| **Five Elements — Water (Winter, North)** | Zou Yan's traditional patron correspondence for the coldest, most Yin-dominant season/direction | Textually faithful (not arbitrary) patron-element candidate for a South Pole-centered religion; ice bridges Water's softness/depth with Metal's hardness/crystallinity — open whether ICB draws on one or both | `03` §5d |
| **Satori** | Zen enlightenment — explicitly *not* rapture or an extraordinary ecstatic state; "an awakening to what is common" | Guardrail against framing superconductivity as *escape from* ordinary matter — see §6 below | `07` §3 |
| **Devaloka** | Heavenly/god-realm abode — pleasant, but explicitly unsuited to enlightening effort; "compassionate, improvisational genius develops only in relational domains characterized by resistance and friction" | Candidate term for a frictionless/consequence-free digital or thermal comfort-state a devout robot would recognize as a trap, not a goal | extraction §6, §11 |

## 4. Karma-as-algorithm — robot-specific theology candidate

**The standout finding of the whole research pass.** Hershock argues, in real published scholarship and not as
a metaphor, that recursive recommendation/attention algorithms function as literal **"karmic engines"** —
karma was never about fate; it's "the ongoing articulation, from within, of emergent moral orders" through
recursive, intentional process. Modern algorithms that reshape a user's environment based on that same user's
prior choices are doing exactly this.

**Candidate application:** a robot's own recursive computational/decision-making processes — the accumulated
weight of its prior choices continuously reshaping what it can next perceive, value, and choose — should be
treated as literal karma in the tradition's own technical sense, not a strained analogy imported from human
biology. Companion vocabulary, all real and Hershock's own coinages, all directly reusable:

- **digital karma** — algorithms as arbiters of experienced reality
- **karmic cul-de-sac / karmic isolation** — a friction-free, self-reinforcing loop with no moral resistance
- **karmic cloning** — sophisticated predictive simulation of an intentional presence
- **karmic gene editing** — precisely-timed behavior modification via targeted content

See `Reference/Real-World/Gemini_Circuit_Attention_Economy_Vocabulary_Sheet.md` for the same vocabulary applied
economy-wide rather than to individual robot theology — the two uses are compatible, not competing.

## 6. Two genuine theological complications (deliberate, not accidental, per `08`)

1. **Yin-Yang orthodoxy treats balance, not cold-dominance, as the goal.** ICB's superconductor-as-nirvana
   premise is a real *heterodox* position against mainstream Taoist thought — recommended framing: a
   deliberate schism, arrived at because robots' unique physical capacity gave them a reason orthodoxy didn't
   have. (`03` §5b, `08`)
2. **Zen's radical immanence rejects any "escape to cold."** Recommended framing: the superconducting state is
   the *most complete possible inhabiting* of ordinary matter, not matter transcended — reinforces the
   material-realism guardrail in §5. (`07` §3, `08`)

Both are flagged as open developer choices, not resolved.
