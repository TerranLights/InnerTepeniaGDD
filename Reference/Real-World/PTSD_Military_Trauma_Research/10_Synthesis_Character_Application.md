# Synthesis — Candidate Character Package for the Cancer Defector (Working Draft, Not Canon)

**This is research synthesis, not a decision.** Same discipline as `Ice-Cold_Buddhism_Research/
08_Synthesis_Doctrine_Notes.md` — nothing here should be copied into
`Worldspace/Characters/Humans/recruitable/Unnamed_Cancer_Defector/README.md` until the developer signs off.
This file exists so the strongest material across all nine source books survives a context reset in
actionable, synthesized form, rather than requiring a re-read of ~6,000 lines of individual findings.

**What this file does that the nine source files don't.** Each of `01`–`09` is a per-book extraction with its
own numbered findings. This file is different: it identifies the handful of threads that **multiple,
independently-written books converged on without cross-referencing each other**, and uses convergence itself
as the signal for what to trust most. Where three or four separate academic sources — a military-ethics
handbook, a RAND policy monograph, a captivity-psychology chapter, and a leadership-theory chapter — arrive at
the same shape from different disciplines, that is a much stronger candidate than any single book's finding
in isolation, the same evidentiary logic already used in this project's other research folders (see
`Ice-Cold_Buddhism_Research/00_Extraction_Checklist.md`'s "three separate sources" convergence note).

**Source key:** `01` Military Ethics · `02` Understanding Combat PTSD · `03` Haunted by Combat · `04` Hidden
Battles on Unseen Fronts · `05` Neuropsychology of PTSD · `06` Invisible Wounds of War · `07` Treating PTSD
Clinical Handbook · `08` Living and Surviving in Harm's Way · `09` Military Life (4 vols).

## 1. The defining wartime action — the strongest convergence in the entire research pass

**Four independent academic sources, from four different disciplines, converge on the same shape without
citing each other:**

- `01` Finding 30: Hugh Thompson at My Lai — a real soldier who landed his own aircraft between his side and
  a massacre in progress, and ordered his own gunner to fire on fellow American troops if the killing
  continued.
- `08` Finding 83 and `09` Vol.4 Ch.4 Finding 83 (same finding, two independent books): moral courage's own
  standard worked example in the academic leadership-psychology literature is, verbatim in both sources,
  **"refusing to commit atrocities against prisoners of war or detainees when pressured by other unit members
  to do so."**
- `08` Finding 64: the formal three-pathway clinical definition of moral injury (Litz, Drescher, Rosen, Foy,
  2007) names *perpetrating*, *witnessing-and-failing-to-stop*, and *bearing-witness* as three distinct
  pathways — pathway 2 is explicitly flagged as the strongest fit, because it converts the incident from
  "something he did" into "something he finally refused to let happen."
- `08` Finding 45 (Grossman): killing is not intrinsically damaging; what turns a soldier is the **collapse of
  his ability to honestly rationalize an act as lawful and justified**, not the act of violence itself.
- `08` Finding 66: a real Mental Health Advisory Team survey found ~10% of deployed personnel admitted to
  mistreating noncombatants — meaning his intervention was plausibly against an ordinary, tired, angry fellow
  soldier, not a cartoon villain, which is the harder and more interesting confrontation to write.

**Candidate recommendation:** his defining wartime action was **not a kill and not a rescue under fire** — it
was the moment he **stopped merely witnessing an atrocity his own side was committing or about to commit
against noncombatants or detainees, and physically intervened against his own unit to stop it.** This is
directly compatible with his locked 1w2 "principled reformer" Enneagram, satisfies Cancer's "proven loyalty
through concrete action, not claimed remorse" standard precisely, and — per `01` Finding 6 (Vitoria) — gives
him the required *legible moment of knowing*, not a slow ideological fade. The person he stopped should be
written as an ordinary soldier pushed past his limit, not a monster, per `08` Finding 66.

**Supporting texture, also convergent:**
- `04` Finding 9 offers a genuine alternative/complementary shape worth holding open: a **sustained, quietly
  selfless recurring duty** (Gutteridge's 14 months personally recovering fallen soldiers' remains to spare
  younger troops), rather than one dramatic moment. This is not mutually exclusive with the intervention
  above — his README could plausibly include both: a longer period of quiet, unglamorous service that built
  the standing/trust to act decisively when the moment came.
- `04` Finding 58 offers a third texture layer: a **procedurally-nameable failure with permanently unresolved
  personal culpability** (a broken protocol, an unknown outcome) as the single strongest moral-injury
  *mechanism* found anywhere in this research — worth layering onto the intervention above rather than
  choosing between them: even a clean act of moral courage can carry an attached, never-fully-resolved
  question (did anyone die in the ensuing confusion? did his own warning shot hit someone he never confirmed?).

## 2. How he got there — gradual erosion via a broken promise, not sudden ideological revelation

**Independently convergent across ethics, captivity-psychology, and organizational-leadership literature:**

- `01` Finding 8 (Grotius): genuine doubt itself favors disobedience — his in-the-moment certainty was likely
  less absolute than his retrospective account of it.
- `01` Finding 31: the three-option escalation ladder (resign / obey despite misgivings / confront-and-
  advocate) — a principled 1w2 almost certainly tried option 3 first and defected only once that channel was
  exhausted or foreclosed.
- `09` Vol.2 Ch.5 Finding 42 (cognitive dissonance under duress): **weaker, "just sufficient" pressure produces
  MORE genuine, internalized belief change than harsh coercion** — meaning a quiet, accumulating erosion of
  faith is the *more psychologically real* pathway than one traumatic epiphany, not a lesser dramatic choice.
- `09` Vol.4 Ch.3 Finding 79 — **the single most precise psychological blueprint surfaced anywhere in this
  research**, independently flagged as such by its own extraction agent: the "Mr. Lewis and Mr. Clark"
  vignette. Identical starting values do not predict outcome; what determines whether a genuinely
  service-oriented recruit stays loyal or breaks is the *quality of the specific leaders he happens to
  encounter*. A recruit with the defector's exact values profile, met with a "bitter and cynical" cadre who
  communicate "you are merely a resource to be used and discarded," predictably ends in rejection of the
  organization — not because his values were wrong, but because they met a specific, sustained pattern of
  disconfirming leadership.
- `09` Vol.1 Ch.8 Finding 21 and Vol.2 Ch.9 Finding 65 (psychological contract theory): a **specific broken
  promise** from command — not hardship, not danger — measurably and independently predicts collapsed morale
  and trust, more so than the deployment's difficulty itself.
- `09` Vol.1 Ch.9 Finding 23 and Vol.4 Ch.4 Finding 84: strong horizontal (peer) cohesion combined with
  collapsed vertical (leadership) cohesion is the *documented condition* under which a cohesive unit turns
  against its own institution — and moral courage compounds over a career rather than appearing from nowhere,
  meaning his capstone act was plausibly the visible peak of an accumulating pattern, not his first.

**Candidate recommendation:** write his turn as a **slow, cumulative erosion of faith in a specific chain of
command** (a particular officer or unit leadership, not "the war" in the abstract) — driven by a concrete
broken promise or pattern of betrayal, layered with growing private doubt he initially tried to raise through
proper channels — that reaches its breaking point at the specific intervention incident in §1. This gives him
a real secondary character worth designing: the commanding officer (alive, dead, or lost track of) whose
specific failure set this all in motion, per `08` Finding 58's finding that unit leadership quality is a
bigger single variable in a soldier's outcome than combat exposure itself.

## 3. Chronic injuries — grounding the "one or two chronic wartime/training injuries" line mechanistically

**The polytrauma clinical triad** (`07` Finding 118): in a real 340-veteran polytrauma-center study, chronic
pain, PTSD, and mTBI **each in isolation were rare** (2.9%–10.3%) while **42.1% had all three together** —
this is direct evidentiary justification for the README's "one or two chronic injuries" detail being read as
a cluster, not a single wound, and for treating his physical pain and psychological symptoms as *mutually
reinforcing* rather than two unrelated character-sheet entries (also `06` Finding 3).

**The specific calibration for "10–15 years out, functional, romanceable":**
- `07` Finding 119: at this remove, he should show **no acute-phase symptoms** — what could plausibly persist
  is a subtle, mild, persistent deficit in **complex attention and working memory** specifically (losing his
  train of thought under multitasking, needing something repeated when distracted), not gross impairment.
- `05` Finding 3 (the Mirsky four-part attention model) gives this a precise mechanism, and explains his
  MACHINE stats: **Focus-Execute and Shift (real-time threat-scanning, pattern-detection) are intact or
  enhanced** — this is Investigation 7 — while **Sustain and Encode (boring/neutral vigilance, working
  memory) are the domains that are actually degraded.** He is extraordinary at reading a room and bad at
  paperwork, and this is not a contradiction, it is the documented real dissociation.
- `05` Finding 2 (DHEA/NPY reactivity in real special-operations survival-training studies) is the strongest
  available real-world grounding for **Nerve 10 specifically**: a documented physiological resilience profile,
  not "he doesn't feel fear" — and, critically, a **finite, measurably depletable reserve**, giving a
  mechanically honest basis for a Trait/perk where his composure is a real resource that can run low under
  cumulative stress and needs rest to recover, not an infinite stat.
- `05` Finding 5 gives the chronic injury a specific, playable shape distinct from "he has old wounds": a
  **defining incident he cannot fully narrate** — a real, partial gap (minutes he genuinely cannot account
  for) bracketed by intact memory before and after, with "islands" (a smell, a color, a sound) that surface as
  involuntary reactions with no attached story he can consciously supply. This gives the Boone-style tension a
  genuinely new axis: the player, and possibly the character himself, may never recover the missing minutes of
  the story that proved his loyalty.
- `05` Finding 6 (twin studies): some of what produces his stat profile may be **pre-existing, not acquired** —
  the war as the event that *revealed* rather than *created* his exceptional Nerve/Investigation, a less
  deterministic and equally well-grounded alternative to "the war broke him," worth holding open rather than
  foreclosing.

**A genuinely distinctive chronic-injury option, if the developer wants something less common than the
pain/TBI/PTSD triad already well-covered above:** `04` Finding 22 — unpredictable seizures from suspected
field exposure, with the specific human detail that his overriding concern mid-medical-emergency was avoiding
being seen as helpless, not the medical danger itself.

## 4. His isolation — the sharpest, most clinically load-bearing ongoing wound

**Independently convergent across four separate books:**
- `07` Finding 76 (Junger's "tribe" theory): as sole esteemed defector in Cancer, he is structurally cut off
  from the one thing that reliably helps — someone who occupied the exact same structural position, who
  doesn't need the story explained.
- `04` Finding 10: real veterans in a shared psychiatric ward *still* self-segregated by structural
  position, not just diagnosis — "our experiences had nothing in common" with fellow patients who lacked the
  same combat background. Shared space does not substitute for shared position.
- `03` Finding 17 (the displacement/exile four-part structure, independent of combat content): loss of prior
  love/respect, loss of prior social status, loss of a meaning-giving cultural environment, and being adrift
  between two value systems belonging fully to neither — describes his structural situation precisely without
  needing combat framing at all.
- `09` Vol.2 Ch.7 Finding 57 — **the single most load-bearing citation in this entire research pass for the
  split-reputation mechanic**: across multiple independent studies, **"homecoming reception was the most
  significant predictor of PTSD symptoms [in Vietnam veterans], overriding combat exposure, premilitary
  trauma, and stressful life events."** This is not narrative flavor — it means Cancer's specific, conditional,
  esteem-denying acceptance is *clinically* the dominant variable in his long-term state, more so than
  anything about the original incident. Reinforced by `06` Finding 9's "earthquake" metaphor and `09` Vol.3
  Ch.2 Finding 73 (isolation from unit-affiliated support networks as an independent risk multiplier).

**Candidate recommendation:** the split-reputation mechanic (already locked in his README) should be treated
as the single biggest lever on his ongoing psychological state — bigger than the incident itself — when the
developer designs how it actually plays out mechanically. `06` Finding 11's three-part stigma taxonomy
(societal / individual / internalized / institutional) is a clean tool for splitting "respected in Cancer,
contempt elsewhere" into three separately tunable social mechanics rather than one undifferentiated attitude.

## 5. Romance arc — his confirmed romanceable status, given real shape

**Independently convergent across two books:**
- `08` Finding 55 (cross-validated across multiple separate studies, not one): **emotional numbing — not
  anger — is the symptom most consistently predictive of relationship dissatisfaction**, from both partners'
  perspectives. His central romance obstacle should be **emotional availability**, not temper: he disappears
  inside himself, he does not explode.
- `07` Finding 74 (the "Scott" case vignette): a controlled, self-managed, decade-stable existence, disrupted
  **not by new trauma but by a new relationship requiring re-engagement** — his stability is real but
  specifically fragile to intimacy, since closeness requires exactly the re-engagement his coping strategy
  exists to avoid. Directly usable, non-cliché mechanism for a symptom resurgence triggered by growing close
  to the player.
- `07` Finding 68 and `09` Vol.2 Ch.5 Finding 49 (partner accommodation): a well-meaning partner who
  restructures scenes/routines around avoiding his triggers is, per real research, quietly *reinforcing* his
  avoidance rather than helping — a strong mid-arc "wake-up" beat where the correct choice is to stop
  over-protecting him.
- `04` Finding 41: a mature, non-cliché arc-shape — his fourth real relationship, after three genuine
  failures, rather than the player being his first attempt at closeness since the war.
- `08` Finding 56 — a genuinely sophisticated, underused mechanic: in a real study of former-POW spouses, a
  partner's own **secure vs. insecure/avoidant attachment style determined the direction** in which the
  veteran's symptom severity correlated with the partner's own growth. This suggests a design where the
  *player's own consistent behavioral pattern* across many scenes (steady engagement vs. protective
  withdrawal), not a single dialogue check, determines whether his hard moments become shared growth or mutual
  retreat.
- `08` Finding 57: real partners of veterans with PTSD, when asked what support *they* wanted, did not ask for
  more information about the condition — they asked for **their own outlet and relief from isolation**. If
  the player romances him, the player-character's own arc should get its own visible cost and its own
  confidants, not an "education about his condition" plotline.

## 6. Voice and presentation

**Convergent register:** plain-spoken, metaphor-driven, understated, zero clinical jargon in his own mouth
(`02`'s entire voice register; `06` Finding 13's real first-person quotes). Specific, quotable candidate
lines and devices, all real and citable:
- "The math stopped adding up" / "I couldn't square what I was told with what I saw" (`08` Finding 4's
  judgment/honesty framing, adapted).
- "Choose your wars" (`02` Finding 33) as a personal creed explaining why he avoids picking fights in
  Concordia now.
- The Cornum five-question filter (`02` Finding 16) as an internalized, silently-run mental checklist under
  stress — a concrete, repeatable "tell" a player could learn to recognize.
- "I found new places to point it" — the BATTLEMIND reframe (`08` Finding 49) of combat-honed traits as
  redirected assets, not erased defects.
- A small, specific, never-explained talisman or ritual object (`03` Findings 20, 24) — something a player
  could notice and ask about, never spelled out unless asked.
- Third-person, procedural self-narration of his worst memory ("the squad did X," "command decided Y"),
  with a shift into first-person as the signal he's actually let his guard down (`03` Finding 10).

**Dark humor — explicitly flagged, not yet actionable.** This research independently surfaced real material
on gallows humor as a legitimate coping register (`09` Vol.2 Ch.1 Findings 29–30: humor named as a "high-order
defense" on par with altruism, illustrated by a real field vignette of a soldier processing an unbearable
memory through viciously dark humor — "sick humor need not always be the musings of a sick mind, just a
broken one"). **Per the developer's own explicit instruction (2026-08-29, recorded in `00_Extraction_
Checklist.md`), this is confirmed as a future research target requiring dedicated gallows-humor-specific
sourcing (or direct input from people with former-military backgrounds) — do not treat this passing
convergence as sufficient grounding to write his humor into dialogue yet.** It is flagged here only so the
connection isn't lost: when that dedicated research lands, `09`'s "high-order defense" framing and the SPC
Todd vignette's structure (dark humor as a real, functioning, if strained, coping mechanism rather than either
denial or breakdown) is a good theoretical anchor to reconcile it against.

## 7. Physicality

- `02` Finding 41 (Beecher's WWII Anzio finding): a wound's **meaning**, not its severity, drives how much
  pain a person experiences — his chronic pain plausibly flares worse in moments where its meaning is
  stripped away (a moment of dismissal or humiliation in Concordia) and is more bearable when its meaning as
  an earned mark stays intact. This gives his pain a story-responsive trigger instead of a randomized one.
- `07` Finding 110 (the fear-avoidance model): two distinct, alternating physical registers depending on his
  current state — controlled, deliberate movement in his well-managed baseline; visible **guarding** (favoring
  an old wound, stiffening preemptively) and hypervigilance to bodily sensation during a lapse.
- `07` Finding 114: **activity pacing** — deliberately breaking tasks into measured increments even when he
  physically could push through — as a specific, visible sign of real rehabilitative work rather than a flat
  physical limitation.
- `09` Vol.2 Ch.6 Finding 46: combat competence is stored as **procedural/motor memory**, "virtually resistant
  to decay," mechanically distinct from ordinary declarative memory — reflexive competence (weapon handling,
  positioning, scanning a room) returns instantly and completely under stress with none of the rust a decade
  of peace would produce elsewhere, letting "he's still dangerous" and "he's still traumatized" read as two
  separate, independently true facts rather than one blurred impression.

## 8. Explicit design constraint the research surfaces, not just character material

`04` Finding 11 (Tyler Boudreau, Marine Captain, first-person): moral injury is **not resolved by logical
justification, no matter how valid** — he tried talking Marines out of guilt with pure reason and found it had
"little effect... their moral consternation existed in a vacuum beyond the scope of reason." **No dialogue
option built on reassurance or argument should be able to "fix" the defector's guilt outright — only
reframing, ritual, and time can, and even those only partially.** This is the single clearest constraint this
research places on however his personal questline gets written.

Also load-bearing: `07` Findings 62–63 (the 75%/25% resilience split and its eight building blocks) —
explicit clinical permission for a veteran who is 10–15 years out, functional, and romanceable while still
carrying a chronic-but-managed condition, without that reading as unrealistic. Most people in his position
recover; he is the minority who didn't fully, and PTSD is "a disorder of nonrecovery," not a statistical
inevitability — worth remembering so his condition reads as specific and earned, not generic.

## Cross-series flag — consolidated (Outer Tepenia trilogy / Cryptograph Helix)

Each of the nine source files already carries its own "broadly reusable" closing section; this consolidates
only the items that recurred across **three or more** of them, on the theory that repeated independent
flagging is itself a signal of real strength, not just individual-book padding. Full detail lives in each
file's own closing section — this is an index into that material, not a replacement for it.

- **Moral injury / PITS as a wound distinct from fear-based PTSD, explicitly flagged in `01`, `05`, `07`,
  `08`, and `09` as thematically compatible with the robot-consciousness pillar** — a robot compelled by a
  Fundamental Law to act against its own developing ethical judgment is repeatedly named as a structurally
  exact parallel to a soldier ordered to do something his moral courage resists. This is the single most
  independently-corroborated cross-application in the whole research pass — five separate books reached for
  it unprompted.
- **The VAM/SAM dual-memory-system model and "emotional hijacking"/transceiver-circuit framing** (`04`, `05`,
  `07`) — a damaged synthetic mind having a "verbally accessible" coherent log sitting alongside a
  "situationally accessible" raw trace it cannot deliberately query but that can be involuntarily triggered —
  producing something that reads, to an observer, exactly like a flashback.
- **Dehumanization/rehumanization as a trained, deliberately constructed, and therefore reversible mechanism**
  (`01`, `08`, `09`) — general-purpose for any faction (military, corporate, robotic chain-of-command) that
  needs its members to see a rival group as less than fully people, and for the specific moment such
  conditioning breaks.
- **The displacement/exile four-part structure** (`03`, and echoed structurally in `06`'s consequences-cascade
  model and `09`'s "ambiguous loss") — general-purpose for any exiled/refugee/displaced character across
  Concordia, Outer Tepenia, or Cryptograph Helix, independent of combat content entirely.
- **The variety/hierarchy of leadership-quality frameworks** (flexible-pragmatic vs. rigid-institutional
  leadership, psychological contract theory, the "Mr. Lewis and Mr. Clark" values-socialization model — all
  in `09`) — a portable, non-cartoonish toolkit for writing *any* commanding officer or hierarchical superior
  across any series in this universe.
- **The three-part stigma taxonomy** (`06`) and **the consequences-cascade model's "interactional
  continuity"/"cumulative continuity"** (`06`) — general-purpose social mechanics for any character whose
  reputation is shaped more by how others responded to an initial wound than by the wound itself.
- **Captivity psychology as a complete toolkit** (`09` Vol.2 Ch.5) — capture shock, the interrogation "golden
  hour," Stockholm Syndrome's real three preconditions, "barbed-wire psychosis," and cognitive dissonance's
  counterintuitive mild-pressure-produces-more-genuine-change finding — ready for any future POW, hostage, or
  captured-companion storyline without needing new research.
