# Research Topics — 13 Districts + 36 Cities/Stations

**Purpose:** a shopping list of real-world subjects worth sourcing PDFs about, one location at a time,
following the same pattern that worked for Pisces (see `Pisces_Flood_Mechanism_Research/` in this same
folder) — each topic below is chosen because it maps onto something *already established* about that
location, not a generic city-building suggestion. These are fields/subjects to search for, not specific
titles. As PDFs get collected into `Reference/Materials/books/`, they can get their own dedicated
extraction-checklist folder the same way Pisces did, whenever there's a specific question worth answering.

**Not every topic needs its own book** — several are broad enough that a single well-chosen title could
cover multiple locations at once (e.g., anything on Antarctic station engineering touches a dozen entries
below). Read them as a prioritized wish list, not a shopping cart that all needs filling before anything
else can happen.

---

## Part 1 — The 13 Concordia Districts

### Cancer (sanctuary/caregiving, "Mother's Circuit," Overcrowding Decision)
- Grief and bereavement psychology — institutional/collective grief processing, not just individual
- Surveillance creep in caregiving technology — how monitoring systems drift from care into control
- Triage ethics — medical/resource-allocation literature on "who gets left behind" under scarcity
- Ritual and mourning anthropology — memorial practices across real-world cultures

### Taurus (domestic stability, Insulation Schism, Bonded Pair Registry Collapse)
- Sociology of scarcity-driven class stratification — real case studies of resource-driven status divides
- Records/registry systems and what happens when they fail — database integrity, administrative collapse
- Attachment psychology — pair-bonding literature (could analogize human-robot bonding)
- Rationing-era housing history — real precedent for heating/resource-driven neighborhood divides

### Leo (arts/performance, Star War rivalry, Cymaticist religion)
- Performance studies — theories of authenticity, spectacle vs. intimate form
- Cymatics — the real physics of visualizing sound/vibration patterns (direct grounding for the religion)
- History of art-movement schisms and rivalries
- Social psychology of charisma and stage presence

### Scorpio (confrontation-based trauma work, mask culture, rebirth facilitation)
- Depth psychology / shadow work — real psychoanalytic literature, direct fit for "shadow-augmentation"
- Ritual mask traditions — anthropology of masks in transformation/rebirth rites
- Post-traumatic identity reconstruction — clinical literature on identity after severe trauma
- Neural-interface/personality-modification hardware — could reuse the Pisces embedded-systems material

### Aries (power generation, Overclock Martyrs, blackout-threat politics)
- Reactor/radiation safety engineering — real meltdown case studies (Chernobyl, Fukushima)
- Workaholism and labor as trauma avoidance — clinical psychology literature
- Power grid cascading failure — directly reusable: the Pisces "Networks of Networks" material applies
  even more literally here, since Aries *is* a power district
- Sociology of workplace martyrdom and self-sacrifice

### Capricorn (heavy industry, Merit Board Audit, builds robot frames)
- Labor economics — wage discrimination and systemic undervaluation of labor, real case studies
- Industrial/assembly-line manufacturing engineering
- Robotics frame/actuator manufacturing
- Organizational behavior — why known institutional problems go uncorrected for decades

### Aquarius (experimental district, failed utopian communities, Living Network Experiment)
- Intentional community history — why real communes/utopian projects fail (large existing literature)
- Network resilience and distributed systems — the Pisces "Networks of Networks" material could double
  here directly for the "Living Network Experiment" specifically
- Experimental/off-grid architecture and energy systems
- Social psychology of collective failure under material dependence

### Libra (governance, Suspended Compact — emergency powers never relinquished)
- Emergency powers and constitutional law — real case studies of "temporary" powers becoming permanent
- Weberian bureaucracy theory / procedural legitimacy
- Game theory of institutional lock-in — why institutions can't voluntarily give back power
- States of exception / martial law history

### Gemini (data/archive hub, Twin Channels, the Great Corruption)
- Archival science and digital preservation — data integrity, authenticity of records
- Parallel/official-vs-real record-keeping — historiography of propaganda and suppressed archives
- Digital forensics and targeted data destruction — reusable from the Pisces book pool
- Real historical archive-destruction events used to erase inconvenient history

### Pisces (The Markets)
Already extensively researched — see `Pisces_Flood_Mechanism_Research/` in this folder. One open
secondary thread if more material surfaces:
- Informal/black-market economics — underground economy literature, relevant to the Tolerance Pact's
  unofficial trade dynamics beyond the Flood mechanism itself

### Sagittarius (frontier/expansion, Third Expansion Collapse, siligel rush/bust)
- Boomtown/gold-rush sociology and boom-bust resource-extraction economics
- Industrial disaster root-cause analysis — real mining/expansion accident case studies
- Frontier-culture risk psychology

### Virgo (infrastructure/maintenance, Year of Silence, sink district for failures)
- "Invisible labor" / maintenance studies — a real, distinct academic field (e.g., "The Maintainers"
  scholarly network) built exactly around this district's own thesis
- Work-to-rule labor action history and effectiveness
- "Normal accidents" / systems-failure theory (Charles Perrow's work is the classic real anchor text)
- Municipal utilities engineering — heating loops, recycling, tunnel infrastructure

### Hub (Axis Mundi — transit district, no zodiac identity, Bridge Memorial)
- "Non-places" — Marc Augé's real anthropological concept of transit spaces lacking identity, an
  almost exact match for Hub's own stated character
- Contested public monuments — real literature on memorials with disputed/multiple meanings
- Sociology of transient professions — rail/transit workers, liminal occupational identity
- Hub-and-spoke logistics network theory

## Part 2 — 35 Cities + Amundsen-Scott Station

### Halley Subnet

**Halley** (the relocatable city, traded control for the name)
- Relocatable/mobile station architecture — real precedent already exists (the actual Halley VI station
  moves on skis) and is worth researching directly
- Ice shelf glaciology — why a station needs to move at all
- Political science of figurehead/ceremonial authority

**Abowasa** (jointly-founded, two peoples negotiating)
- Binational/co-founded settlement history — condominium territories, jointly-administered towns
- Consociational power-sharing governance models
- Intercultural neighborhood sociology at the household level

**Belgrano** (airbase survived the war, declined after — "Boneyard Times")
- Post-military-base economic decline — company-town literature on bases outliving their purpose
- Institutional discipline that outlasts the institution itself
- Aircraft boneyards — real aviation industry practice, a literal existing parallel

**Lazar** (grown together, no single founding story, largest city)
- Urban agglomeration / city-merger studies — how two settlements become one metro area
- Unplanned megacity growth dynamics

**Neumayer** (precision as civic virtue, uncredited engineering behind the Tower)
- Structural engineering on shifting ice shelves — the real Neumayer III station uses a hydraulic
  platform system for exactly this, a direct real-world parallel worth pulling
- History-of-science literature on erased/uncredited contributors
- Sociology and history of precision/metrology-obsessed institutions

**Princess Elisabeth** (total dependence on maintained engineering, collapsed under war)
- Zero-emission/passive Antarctic architecture — the real Princess Elisabeth Antarctica station is
  literally a zero-emission building, a direct existing parallel
- Reliability engineering — single points of failure in fragile systems

**Sanay** (technical backbone, major port, survived on bedrock)
- Port/harbor engineering on bedrock vs. ice foundations
- Real case studies of port cities surviving wartime due to geographic/geological advantage

**Troll** (runway/freight linchpin, contested chokepoint)
- Strategic literature on contested transport chokepoints
- Intermodal freight logistics engineering

### Palmer Subnet

**Esperanza** (rotational miners, robot caregivers, generational exile community)
- Company-town / rotational (FIFO) labor sociology
- Child development in isolated institutional settings
- The real Esperanza Base is the only Antarctic settlement with children actually born there — worth
  pulling real Antarctic-citizenship history directly

**Juan Carlos** (record-keeping origin, destroyed specifically for it)
- History of census/vital-records systems and their political weight
- Real historical cases of record-keeping institutions destroyed specifically to erase what they held

**Marambio** (dual transport hub, ancient warm-Antarctica geology, destroyed in one strike)
- Antarctic paleoclimate and geology — the real Marambio/Seymour Island is an actual major fossil site,
  directly relevant
- Strategic vulnerability of small, concentrated islands

**Palmer City** (first city, founded for joy, site of the war's start)
- Hedonic/pleasure-city urban planning history
- Historiography of single inciting incidents that trigger wars (comparable to WWI assassination
  literature)

**Port Lockroy** (preserves its own history, postal-hub destiny)
- Heritage preservation and adaptive reuse of former military sites — the real Port Lockroy is itself an
  actual preserved historic site/museum/post office, worth researching directly as precedent
- Postal service logistics history

**Rothera** (industrial spine, distributed surface-and-underground factories)
- Distributed manufacturing and redundant factory siting
- Subterranean industrial architecture

**Sejong** (dense multinational island settlement)
- High-density multinational enclave planning — real King George Island hosts several nations' stations
  clustered closely, a direct existing parallel
- Cohabitation sociology in crowded, multi-national settings

**Signy** (most isolated city, two islands/bridge, robots starving from isolation)
- Remote-island supply-chain fragility and logistics
- Cold-climate bridge engineering

### Mirny Subnet

**Mirny** (self-made windbreak, industrial yards)
- Katabatic wind engineering — the real Mirny Station is a famously wind-battered site, worth
  researching directly
- Industrial-yard fortification and windbreak architecture

**Casey** (highway junction, transient culture, famous bar)
- Truck-stop / waystation sociology — "non-place" transient culture literature (ties to Hub above)
- Highway logistics network design

**Davis** (breadbasket/multi-disciplinary research hub — identity settled 2026-07-16)
- Controlled-environment/greenhouse agriculture in extreme climates — real Antarctic/Arctic greenhouse
  research is directly and immediately relevant given the settled breadbasket identity
- Limnology and lake-sediment paleoclimate reconstruction — ties directly to Davis's own established
  lake-science research culture
- History of genuinely multi-disciplinary research institutions

**Kunlun** (highest/coldest point, 100%-robot city, specialist heritage)
- High-altitude, extreme-cold engineering — the real Kunlun Station is China's highest Antarctic station
- Post-national community formation — craft-guild/professional-identity sociology as a substitute for
  national identity

**Shirayuki** (imposed-then-chosen origin, research/education/art-fashion culture)
- Postcolonial identity reclamation — communities reclaiming an externally-imposed founding myth as
  genuinely their own
- Textile/fashion arts history tied to research and education culture

**Sinheung** (manufactures the apparatus creating new robot citizens)
- Large-scale advanced/additive manufacturing systems
- Sociology of communities that existed before being formally named (statelessness/undocumented
  community literature)

**Vostok** (coldest place on Earth, 25-million-year-sealed subglacial lake)
- Subglacial lake science — the real Lake Vostok is one of the most-studied subglacial lakes on Earth,
  extremely rich source material (extremophile biology, drilling-contamination protocols)
- Extremophile biology — life in extreme cold, sealed ecosystems
- Sociology of inheriting someone else's institutional legend

**Zhongshan** (stable founding-nation identity between two very different neighbors)
- Buffer-state / small-power-between-two-larger-neighbors geopolitics
- Cultural continuity and refinement (rather than change) over generations

### Janbogo Subnet

**Janbogo** (last living artery, kept standing to witness Zukelli's destruction)
- Survivor's-guilt / witness-community trauma — real literature on communities that survive while
  neighbors are destroyed
- Katabatic-wind-resistant shielded architecture

**Cape Adare** (first human footprint in Antarctica, penguin colony)
- Real Cape Adare/Borchgrevink expedition history — the actual first overwintering site in Antarctica
- Adélie penguin colony ecology — Cape Adare hosts one of the largest real colonies on the continent
- Acoustic/community music culture

**Denison** (windiest inhabited place, single fused structure, destroyed after outlasting the wind)
- Extreme wind engineering — the real Cape Denison is the windiest place at sea level on Earth
  (Mawson's original expedition site); directly worth researching
- Fused megastructure architecture for extreme climates

**Dumont d'Urville** (francophone city, penguin coexistence)
- Real Dumont d'Urville station history — French Antarctic policy, direct existing parallel
- Human-wildlife coexistence urban planning — the real station routes infrastructure around penguin
  colonies already

**Fort McMurdo** (historical capital, in the shadow of an active volcano)
- Active-volcano hazard engineering and civil defense — Mt. Erebus is a real, active volcano near the
  real McMurdo Station
- Political geography of informal-to-formal capital transitions

**Scott** (quiet neighbor to the loud capital, preserved Saint's hut)
- Preserved historic polar huts — the real Scott/Terra Nova hut is an actual heritage site, direct
  existing parallel
- Small-town-next-to-metropolis sociology

**Zukelli** (destroyed for sheltering people, Janbogo's sister city)
- Sanctuary-town history — real case studies of towns destroyed/punished for harboring refugees
- Sister-city/twin-settlement sociology

### Mawson Subnet

**Mawson** (oldest continuously-occupied site, population became something else)
- Real Mawson Station history — the actual oldest continuously operated station south of the Antarctic
  Circle
- Founder-population demographic drift and replacement sociology

**Dome Fuji** (ice-core research site, second-holiest robot-religion site)
- Real Dome Fuji ice-core science — deep ice-core drilling and paleoclimate records, rich source material
- Sacralization of scientific/technical sites — real literature on research sites becoming pilgrimage
  destinations

**Sayowa** ("where three roads meet," significant for transit not population)
- Crossroads/waystation town sociology
- Shōwa-era Japanese naming and historical memory

### Byrd Subnet

**Byrd** (buried mega-fabrication city, builds robots from nothing, single-route lifeline)
- Subterranean/under-ice city engineering
- Single-supply-corridor logistics vulnerability — real besieged-city/lifeline-route history
- Heavy industrial robotics fabrication from raw materials

### Amundsen-Scott Station
- Real Amundsen-Scott South Pole Station history and Antarctic Treaty neutrality
- Space elevator / space-fountain engineering theory — directly ties to the existing Amundsen Tower
  project notes
- Network topology and articulation points — Amundsen Station being the single node connecting all six
  subnets, whose destruction "severed Tepenia's ability to talk to itself," is literally a real graph-
  theory concept (a cut vertex/articulation point in a network) — the Pisces "Networks of Networks"
  material could be revisited specifically for this angle
