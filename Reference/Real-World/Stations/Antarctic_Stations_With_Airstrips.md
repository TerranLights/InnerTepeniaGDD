## Notes for Tepenia worldbuilding relevance

- **Real-world Byrd** has no listed airstrip of its own on this list — its associated "Byrd Surface Skiway" is a Marie Byrd Land facility, not necessarily tied to a currently active permanent station (the real Byrd Station was long since abandoned/buried). Worth checking against Tepenia's own already-established "grounded aviation fleet" / Byrd-Janbogo aviation-route-problem open question in `TODO.md`.
- **Real-world Concordia (Dome C)** does have a listed skiway (French/Italian operated) — relevant given Concordia's central importance as Tepenia's present-day primary setting.
- **Vostok, Kunlun (Dome A), and Dome Fuji** all have real listed skiways — consistent with these being real, currently-relevant scientific outposts in the source material, and worth cross-checking against Tepenia's own established "extreme-altitude Arcanet gap" and isolation framing for these three cities.
- **Troll Station** (Norway) has a genuinely notable blue-ice runway capable of wheeled aircraft (not just ski-equipped planes) — one of the more capable facilities on this list, consistent with Troll's real-world reputation as an aviation hub.
- Several names on this list use "skiway" specifically (a compacted snow or ice surface usable only by ski-equipped aircraft) as distinct from a genuine wheeled-aircraft runway (gravel or blue ice, usable by wheeled cargo planes) — this distinction may matter if Tepenia's own lore wants to differentiate between "reachable by any aircraft" and "reachable only by specialized ski-equipped planes."
