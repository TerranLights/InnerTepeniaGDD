NOTE: This is CP2077's POST-2.0-UPDATE system — five ROLE-based skills (Solo, Netrunner,
Headhunter, Shinobi, Engineer), each leveling automatically through play (not
perk-point-gated) and granting a passive rank bonus every 5 ranks, 5 through 60.
This is a DIFFERENT system from the pre-2.0 twelve-skill structure (see
cp2077-1.63-skill-level-rewards.txt) and from either era's PERK TREES (chosen,
point-spent abilities under the five Attributes) — this file covers only the
automatic role-skill rank bonuses.

////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////////

Each of Cyberpunk's five skill groups requires different actions to level up, but
most of the tasks involve things you'd do normally while playing anyway. These
upgrades aren't tied to perk points (aside from the +1 perk point rewards baked
into the ladder itself) — you rank up all five just by playing normally, and
progress from pre-2.0 saves carries over.

## SOLO
Groups heavy weapons, carrying capacity, and health. Levels via fists, Gorilla
Arms, clubs, and shotguns.

Rank 5: Carrying capacity +50
Rank 10: Health +10
Rank 15: +1 perk point
Rank 20: Fists and Gorilla Arms: +20% damage, +20% attack speed; Fists may apply Bleeding
Rank 25: Carrying capacity +100 (cumulative with Rank 5)
Rank 30: Health +15 (cumulative with Rank 10)
Rank 35: +1 perk point
Rank 40: +10% critical hit chance against enemies within 5m
Rank 45: Strong attacks and quick melee attacks deal +25% damage
Rank 50: +25% health after a finisher
Rank 55: While Adrenaline Rush is active, Adrenaline decays 50% more slowly and cannot decay below 10%
Rank 60: Berserk: +30% enemy health threshold to perform Finishers, +50% health from finishers, +30% Berserk duration

## NETRUNNER
Levels via using quickhacks on people and terminals (hacking-adjacent actions generally).

Rank 5: RAM recovery rate +5%
Rank 10: RAM +1
Rank 15: +1 perk point
Rank 20: +20% lock-on range with smart weapons
Rank 25: RAM recovery rate +5% (cumulative with Rank 5)
Rank 30: RAM +1 (cumulative with Rank 10)
Rank 35: +1 perk point
Rank 40: -20% RAM cost for quickhacks used on devices and vehicles
Rank 45: +15% duration for Control and Covert quickhacks; +5% damage with Combat quickhacks
Rank 50: +40% duration for Overclock
Rank 55: -10% health loss from using quickhacks during Overclock
Rank 60: While active, Overclock reveals enemies within 10m and allows quickhacking non-Netrunner enemies through cover

## HEADHUNTER
Levels via landing headshots, weapon techniques, targeting weak points; smaller gains from general handgun use.

Rank 5: Visibility to enemies -10%
Rank 10: +10% headshot damage, +10% damage against vulnerabilities
Rank 15: +1 perk point
Rank 20: No weapon sway when crouched
Rank 25: Visibility to enemies -10% (cumulative with Rank 5)
Rank 30: +15% headshot damage, +15% damage against vulnerabilities (cumulative with Rank 10)
Rank 35: +1 perk point
Rank 40: Optical Camo remains active while grappling enemies
Rank 45: Recovery time after a throw -30%
Rank 50: +30% faster crouch-walking and crouch-sprinting
Rank 55: Damage bonus from being undetected outside combat persists 3 seconds after entering combat
Rank 60: +15% Optical Camo charge when neutralizing an enemy via Focus Mode, Deadeye Mode, or a thrown weapon

## SHINOBI
Levels via movement abilities used in combat (dashing, dodging, climbing) and via katana/sword use.

Rank 5: Movement speed +5%
Rank 10: Stamina cost from dodging/dashing -5%
Rank 15: +1 perk point
Rank 20: Improved accuracy for a short time after dodging or dashing
Rank 25: Movement speed +5% (cumulative with Rank 5)
Rank 30: Stamina cost from dodging/dashing -10% (cumulative with Rank 10)
Rank 35: +1 perk point
Rank 40: Increased mitigation chance the faster you move
Rank 45: +25 damage with fast attacks
Rank 50: Air-dashing and sliding reload 20% of equipped weapon's ammo
Rank 55: Low stamina no longer affects ranged accuracy or melee attack speed when attacking from midair or while time is slowed
Rank 60: While Sandevistan is active: +40% critical hit chance, no stamina cost for any movement

## ENGINEER
Levels via smart weapons, throwables, and (fastest) crafting.

Rank 5: Armor +15
Rank 10: Cyberware capacity +5
Rank 15: +1 perk point
Rank 20: +10% grenade damage and explosion radius
Rank 25: Armor +25 (cumulative with Rank 5)
Rank 30: Cyberware capacity +10 (cumulative with Rank 10)
Rank 35: +1 perk point
Rank 40: Increased shock chance from charging a tech weapon or electric baton
Rank 45: Health item effectiveness +15%
Rank 50: +30% for all Cyberware stat modifiers
Rank 55: +10% damage from Bolt Shots, +40% burn chance with Bolt Shots; Chain Lightning sets enemies on fire
Rank 60: While Fury is active, may cause an EMP blast
