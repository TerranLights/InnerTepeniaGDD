## Summary

- **Level 9/Special (6 entries):** 1 item, 5 drops. This tier is BG3's most story-locked, deity-adjacent, single-companion-exclusive material — almost none of it survives Section 6's drop-list criteria intact.
- **NPC-Only (16 entries):** 12 land as earned-perk/enemy-signature ability material (Outcome 3, explicitly *not* player-facing per the assignment's framing), 4 drop as either deity-specific (Section 6's named callout) or too narrowly single-NPC-scripted to generalize.
- **Item-Granted (21 triaged, 2 cross-referenced elsewhere):** 12 land as Outcome 5 (item/weapon-mod/consumable properties), 9 drop — mostly deity-specific names from Section 6's explicit list (Kereska's Favour, Selûne's Dream, Shar's Aegis) plus body-horror transformations and redundant/narrow familiar-summons already better covered by Conjuration-school entries elsewhere in the conversion pass.
- **Overall across all 43 triaged entries:** 0 quickhacks, 0 level-up perks, 12 earned/enemy-signature perks (3), 0 traits, 13 items (5), 18 drops (6) — this batch skews heavily toward "drop" and "enemy-only," consistent with the assignment's note that Level 9/Special and NPC-only material was never meant to be broadly player-facing, and Item-Granted material is fundamentally equipment by its own BG3 category name.
