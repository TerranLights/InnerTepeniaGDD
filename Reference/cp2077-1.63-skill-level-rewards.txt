Cyberpunk 2077 (1.63/pre-2.0 era) — Skill level-up rewards, all 12 skills, levels 1-20
Source: outsidergaming.com's "How to Level Up Every Skill, All Skill Level Rewards" and
powerpyx.com's "All Skills List" (cross-checked for the Level 20 capstone clarification).
Retrieved 2026-08-02.

NOTE: This is CP2077's PRE-2.0-UPDATE system — twelve skills, two or three nested under
each of the five Attributes (Body/Reflexes/Technical Ability/Intelligence/Cool), each
leveling 1-20 through in-fiction use (not chosen) and granting automatic passive bonuses
at intermediate levels plus scattered "Perk Point" awards. This is DISTINCT from the
5-role-based 2.0-era rank bonuses (see cp2077-2.0-skill-rank-bonuses.txt) and from either
era's PERK TREES (the chosen, point-spent abilities) — this file covers only the
automatic per-skill level rewards.

Level 20 on every one of the 12 skills reads simply "Trait" in the source tables — per
powerpyx.com, this uniformly means "unlocks the last perk in that skill's own perk tree,"
not a standalone numeric bonus with its own description. It's a gate, not an effect.
