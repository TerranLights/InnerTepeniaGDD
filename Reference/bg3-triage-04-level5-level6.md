# BG3 Conversion Triage — Level 5 & Level 6 Spells

First-pass triage per `Game-Mechanics/Perks/BG3_Conversion_Basis_of_Translation.md`. Covers every entry in
the "## LEVEL 5" and "## LEVEL 6" sections of `Reference/bg3-spells-mechanical-detail.txt`. This is survey
work, not final design — no final AP costs, exact numbers, or final names are proposed here.

**Outcome key:** 1 = Quickhack · 2 = Level-up perk · 3 = Earned perk (Challenge/Companion/Quest/Capstone/
Milestone/World) · 4 = Trait · 5 = Item/equipment/weapon category · 6 = Doesn't translate — drop.

Damage types referenced below are drawn directly from `Game-Mechanics/Combat/Damage_Types.md` — not
re-derived. Weapon categories referenced for Outcome 5 entries are drawn from that same file's Weapon
Category Cross-Reference.

| Cloudkill | 5 | Item — Thrown/Explosives: a chemical-dispersal canister that blankets an area in a slow-moving toxic aerosol cloud (Poison damage/turn) and heavily obscures vision; the caster can steer the cloud's drift each turn via a remote trigger. |

| Contagion | 3 | Earned/quest-locked perk (Scorpio/Aquarius bio-hacking storyline): grants the ability to synthesize and deliver an engineered pathogen via melee contact — the target's own resistance is tested over a fixed number of exchanges (3 successes = cured, 3 failures = the condition persists until treated). | Dispel Evil And Good | 2 | Level-up perk, support-build: drop the creature-type-specific "disadvantage attacking caster" clause entirely (no aberration/celestial/fey/fiend taxonomy exists here) and keep only the salvageable core — a standing field letting the character break a nearby ally's active neural-coercion/hack status and grants them temporary resistance to further Psychic/Neural effects for the duration. |

| Hold Monster | 5 | Item — a heavy-tier Signal Weapon: an EMP/neural paralysis emitter that locks a target's motor systems entirely (can't move/act/react, melee attacks against them land clean), scaling to hit more targets with a better power cell — the escalated big sibling of a basic Hold Person-style quickhack per the framework's own Signal Weapon note. |
| Insect Plague | 5 | Item — a deployable micro-drone swarm canister: disperses a cloud of small aggressive maintenance/combat drones over an area (Piercing damage/turn), creating difficult terrain and sensor-jamming static that imposes a Perception penalty within. |

| Planar Binding | 3 | Earned/quest-locked perk: drop the Celestial/Elemental/Fey/Fiend creature-type framing entirely and keep the underlying shape — the character can hack and forcibly repurpose a defeated-but-recoverable hostile combat robot into a temporary ally, which breaks free (re-tests) each time it takes damage. | Seeming | 5 | Item — a squad-scale holo-disguise/biometric-spoofing rig: projects a randomized false appearance over the wearer and up to several companions simultaneously, each able to trigger one re-roll; lasts until the rig is removed or actively countered. |
| Telekinesis | 5 | Item — a gravitic/tractor-beam tool (heavy-duty magnetic-and-servo manipulator, weaponizable): lifts and throws an object or creature at range, with heavier payloads dealing more damage (Force/Gravitic-Inertial); a clean throw of an unattended object recharges the tool for a free follow-up use. |
| Wall of Stone | 5 | Item — Structural Ordnance: a rapid-deploy modular plating system that erects a segmented cover wall (each segment has its own HP pool) blocking movement and line of sight, vulnerable to Force/Thunder impacts and immune to Psychic/Neural effects since it's inert matter. |

## Level 6

| BG3 Name | Outcome (1-6) | Hard Sci-Fi Translation Concept |
|---|---|---|
| Arcane Gate | 6 | Drop — instant point-to-point matter transport via linked portals has no grounded Hard Sci-Fi mechanism even as near-future tech; falls squarely under the framework's Section 6 planar-travel/extradimensional drop category, unlike Cradle-style mundane transit which stays physically continuous. |
| Blade Barrier | 5 | Item — Structural Ordnance/Slashing hazard: a deployable line of rotating monofilament-wire or saw-disc emitters that shreds anything crossing or lingering in the line (Slashing damage), long-duration area denial. |
| Chain Lightning | 5 | Item — a heavy-tier Arc Weapon: a high-charge conduction weapon that arcs from the primary target to several nearby conductive targets in range (Lightning damage), the escalated big sibling of standard Arc Weapons. |
| Circle of Death | 5 | Item — rare/black-market ordnance (Scorpio/Pisces-sourced given the dark material): a nanite-charge grenade centered on a targeted creature that triggers localized cellular/molecular breakdown in the surrounding radius (Necrotic damage). |
| Create Undead | 3 | Earned/quest-locked perk (dark storyline, Scorpio/Aquarius-adjacent): grants the ability to reanimate a fresh corpse into a nanite-driven combat thrall (a repurposed, still-recognizably-human chassis rather than a "mummy") that fights for the player until deactivated or destroyed — kept deliberately dark and story-gated, matching the framework's explicit Necromancy guidance. |
| Disintegrate | 5 | Item — a rare, quest-locked disassembly weapon: a focused molecular-disruption beam dealing massive Force/Nanotech damage that's entirely negated if the target evades the beam in time (binary miss/hit outcome via the existing hit-chance system), high risk/high reward. |
| Eyebite | 2 | Level-up perk: a standing capability to operate a mode-switching neural-disruption tool on a single marked target, choosing between Panicked (fear-response overload), Sickened (nausea-inducing sensory feedback), or Asleep (forced shutdown/unconsciousness) — sustained via the single active-effect slot, with free mode-switches while it's running. |
| Flesh to Stone | 5 | Item — rare/quest-locked ordnance (dropping the literal "turns to stone" flavor per the framework's explicit flag): a hardening-nanite dispenser round that progressively stiffens and locks a target's joints/frame over 3 turns (each stage adding attacker advantage and defender disadvantage), culminating on the 4th failed resistance check in full-body lockdown reversible only by outside medical/repair intervention, never self-cured. |
| Globe of Invulnerability | 5 | Item — a high-tier deployable shielding generator (Abjuration-flavored): projects a small dome of hard energy shielding that renders anyone inside immune to incoming damage and improves their resistance checks for a short duration; rare/quest-locked given the power level. |
| Harm | 5 | Item — rare/dark bio-weapon (black-market or quest-locked): an engineered-pathogen or nanite payload dealing severe Necrotic damage that, on a failed resistance check, leaves lasting cellular damage reducing the target's effective max HP until treated at a proper medical facility. |
| Heal | 3 | Earned/milestone perk: automatically unlocked on crossing a high-tier Medicine/Investigation skill threshold, representing career-culmination trauma medicine — a full-spectrum trauma-nanite treatment that restores a flat, large amount of HP and cures Blinded/disease-type status. Reflects the framework's own note that automatic class-level unlocks can legitimately land as Earned rather than a menu pick. |
| Heroes' Feast | 5 | Item — a craftable consumable (a prepared "Field Feast" ration kit, consistent with Inner Tepenia's existing siligel/camp-supply crafting systems): grants the whole nearby squad temporary immunity to Poisoned/Diseased/Frightened-equivalent status and a max-HP boost, and produces a multi-day supply cache as a byproduct. |
| Otiluke's Freezing Sphere | 5 | Item — Thermal Weapons (Cold end): a thrown or melee-primed cryo-charge that deals heavy Cold damage on detonation, available in both a hand-delivered and a throwable/launched variant. |
| Otto's Irresistible Dance | 5 | Item — a Signal Weapon variant: a motor-control override emitter that locks a target into a forced repetitive movement loop on a guaranteed first hit (no resistance check), with the target able to break free on subsequent turns; weaker crowd control than Hold Monster since the target can still be attacked and fight back. |
| Planar Ally | 3 | Earned/quest-locked perk: reframed as calling in a favor from a completed questline — rather than summoning a Djinni/Deva/Cambion, the player calls in a specialist mercenary or faction specialist NPC ally for the remainder of the encounter. Companion-System-adjacent per the framework's Conjuration guidance. |
| Sights of the Seelie: Summon Deva | 6 | Drop — mechanically identical to Planar Ally: Deva (doesn't even stack with it in BG3), and its "Seelie"/fey-court framing is Forgotten-Realms-pantheon-specific per Section 6's naming rule; no separate conversion needed once Planar Ally is covered. |
| Sunbeam | 5 | Item — Cell-Fed Weapons: a line-beam laser rifle/turret dealing Radiant damage along its beam, with a sensor-overload secondary effect (Blinded-equivalent: attack penalty, effective range cap, defender disadvantage) on a failed resistance check. |
| Wall of Ice | 5 | Item — a deployable cryo-wall generator: flash-freezes a line of terrain into an ice barrier (Cold damage on creation) that continues venting freezing vapor into the surrounding area (Cold damage/turn) for its duration. |
| Wall of Thorns | 5 | Item — a deployable area-denial dispenser: launches a line of razor-wire/monofilament thicket that deals Piercing damage per turn to anything inside, quarters movement speed, and applies an Entangled-equivalent restraint status. |
| Wind Walk | 5 | Item — a rare squad-scale extraction/traversal tool (dropping the literal "incorporeal mist" and "immune to non-magical damage" framing as ungrounded): a combined aerosol-smoke and grav-harness system that lets the whole party move quickly and evade most conventional attacks while active, at the cost of being unable to fight or communicate normally until it's shut off. |
