# SOC Cross-Category Archetype Perks — First-Pass Brainstorm

**What this is:** an early ideation pass, not finalized design — one loose perk concept per cross-cutting
occupational archetype, growing directly out of the BLS SOC cataloging work in
`Reference/Real-World/jobs_professions_and_fields/`. Written 2026-07-26 in response to the developer's
"let's start throwing around some ideas and possibilities" request. **None of these are locked stat/skill
requirements, ranks, or exact numbers — they're concept sketches**, meant to be picked apart, refined, or
discarded individually before any of them actually enter `Regular_Perks_-_Level-Up.md` or another perk file.

**Purpose, unchanged from the source cataloging work:** giving a content creator doing a "Can you beat Inner
Tepenia as a [real-world profession]?" challenge run something the game genuinely recognizes, without needing
a bespoke hook for all 867 SOC detailed occupations. See `SOC_Cross_Category_Commonalities.md` for the full
definition and district-sourcing of each archetype referenced below.

---

## 1. Supervisor / Foreman

**Concept name:** *Chain of Command*

**Idea:** A unique dialogue option to direct an ally or companion in a crisis moment — something a former
crew boss would instinctively do. NPCs who worked the same kind of crew (regardless of *what* they
supervised — a foundry floor, a kitchen, a caravan) recognize the player's authority on sight, without it
needing to be earned through normal reputation channels.

**Notes:** the least specialized of the 14 concepts — deliberately so, since "Supervisor/Foreman" itself is
the archetype with the broadest district reach (14 minor groups feed into it). Could plausibly branch by
which district-flavor of supervision the player leans into.

---

## 2. Licensed / Credentialed Professional

**Concept name:** *The Right Papers*

**Idea:** Institutional NPCs (Libra clerks, Cancer clinicians, Capricorn guild officials) extend a baseline
trust and access that would otherwise have to be earned through normal reputation/skill-check means — the
social weight of a real credential, recognized on sight rather than demonstrated.

**Notes:** strongly tied to archetype 2's own Strong-tier districts (Cancer, Libra, Scorpio, Capricorn) — the
ones organized around formal credentialing as an actual cultural value, not just present-but-minor.

---

## 3. Skilled Manual Trade

**Concept name:** *Tradesman's Instinct*

**Idea:** A flavor-distinct cousin to the already-existing Repair/Jury-Rigging perks — represents "trained in
a real trade" identity rather than raw stat investment. Possibly a flat discount on a specific repair-action
type, or a dialogue-only recognition beat with fellow tradespeople.

**Notes:** needs real care to avoid simply duplicating Jury Rigging, Precision Maintenance, or the other
Repair-skill perks already in `Regular_Perks_-_Level-Up.md` — the differentiator has to be the *identity*
angle (this is who you were, not just a stat you invested in), not a second numeric bonus stacked on the same
mechanic.

---

## 4. Uniformed / Disciplined Service

**Concept name:** *Old Habits*

**Idea:** A combat-stance or AP bonus that only triggers when following a direct order from a companion or
authority NPC — muscle memory from institutional discipline, distinct from a build choice the player makes
unprompted.

**Notes:** the "only triggers when following an order" condition is the interesting design wrinkle here — it
would need a real in-combat mechanism for a companion/NPC to actually issue an "order" the game can detect,
which doesn't currently exist and would need its own small design pass.

---

## 5. Direct Public-Facing / Customer Service

**Concept name:** *Customer's Always Right*

**Idea:** A unique de-escalation dialogue option usable on hostile-but-not-yet-violent NPCs — trained
composure from years of repetitive public friction, distinct from Speech-based persuasion (this is about
absorbing hostility without escalating, not talking someone out of a position).

**Notes:** strongest fit for Leo and Hub (both Strong-tier for this archetype); could dovetail with the
already-established Reputation Matrix as a way to prevent a hostility spike rather than reverse one after the
fact.

---

## 6. Scientific / Research / Analytical

**Concept name:** *Show Your Work*

**Idea:** Reveals an object's or NPC's underlying mechanism/weakness before a normal Investigation threshold
would allow it — the habit of a trained analytical mind noticing structure before being told to look for it.

**Notes:** close in spirit to the existing NODE/Targeting weak-point mechanics (`Targeting_System.md`) — would
need a clear line drawn so this doesn't simply duplicate what Investigation-gated perks like Sniper or
Environmental Reader already do.

---

## 7. Caregiving / Vulnerable-Population Work

**Concept name:** *Bedside Manner*

**Idea:** Witnessing a companion's Grief-tier moments on the Fragmentation Matrix doesn't cost the player
character the usual Nerve/Humanity toll it normally would; a unique comforting dialogue option becomes
available at those moments.

**Notes:** this is the one concept here that ties directly into an already-designed system (the Fragmentation
Matrix) rather than a new mechanic — worth double-checking against that system's own numbers before locking
anything in, since it directly touches an emotionally load-bearing mechanic.

---

## 8. Working With Animals

**No concept proposed yet.** Per the district-matching file, this is the weakest-supported archetype in
current lore — Inner Tepenia's Antarctic setting doesn't establish meaningful animal populations. Held back
deliberately rather than forcing a perk onto thin lore.

---

## 9. Outdoor / Elements-Exposed Work

**Concept name:** *(unnamed — dialogue-only concept)*

**Idea:** No new mechanical bonus — Frontier Survival and Cold Adaptation already cover the numeric side of
this archetype. Instead, a unique dialogue beat where sustained Frostlands exposure reads in-fiction as
"nothing new" for this character, rather than a fresh stacked bonus.

**Notes:** deliberately the most conservative entry on this list — flagged to avoid redundant power creep
against perks that already exist.

---

## 10. Vehicle / Heavy Equipment Operator

**Concept name:** *(unnamed — dialogue-only concept)*

**Idea:** Same treatment as archetype 9 — dialogue/flavor only, no mechanical effect, per the standing
turn-based-format caveat already established for Piloting (`TODO.md`) and reaffirmed in the district-matching
file. Held for the Outer Tepenia trilogy, where operating a vehicle is a real in-context action.

---

## 11. Creative / Expressive Occupations

**Concept name:** *Command the Room*

**Idea:** A performance-flavored dialogue option, Leo-adjacent, that produces a brief crowd-reaction effect
in a social scene — the trained instinct of holding an audience's attention, applied outside a literal stage.

**Notes:** would need a real "crowd" mechanic to exist somewhere in dialogue/social scenes for this to be more
than flavor text — worth checking whether one already does before designing the effect further.

---

## 12. Clerical / Information-Processing / Office-Based

**Concept name:** *Paper Trail*

**Idea:** Finds falsified or altered records in Libra/Gemini archives that a normal Investigation check would
miss — the specific, trained eye of someone who has spent a career reading paperwork for what's *wrong* with
it, not just what it says.

**Notes:** strongest fit for Libra and Gemini (both Strong-tier here) — could plausibly become a quest-relevant
unlock (Category C, `Quest_and_Choice_Perks.md`) rather than a level-up perk, if it ends up gating a specific
investigation-flavored quest rather than being generally available.

---

## 13. Sales / Persuasion-Based

**Concept name:** *Closer*

**Idea:** Always gets one more round of negotiation in a Barter exchange, regardless of the normal cutoff
condition — the trained refusal to let a deal die on the first "no."

**Notes:** clean, simple, and doesn't obviously duplicate any existing perk — one of the more "ready to refine
into real numbers" concepts on this list.

---

## 14. Precision Technical Support

**Concept name:** *Second Chair*

**Idea:** Reduces AP cost or improves the odds when assisting a companion's own skill attempt — the "I've done
this as someone's assistant a thousand times" identity, distinct from doing the skill check yourself.

**Notes:** would need a real "assist a companion's skill check" mechanic to exist first — worth checking
whether the game has one anywhere before designing the effect size.

---

## Open Questions

- Which of these 14 (if any) should move first into an actual perk-pool file, and which file (base
  `Regular_Perks_-_Level-Up.md`, or the quest-gated `Quest_and_Choice_Perks.md`)?
- Several concepts (4, 6, 11, 14) depend on a game mechanic that may not exist yet (detecting an NPC "order,"
  a "crowd" social-scene effect, an "assist a companion's check" action) — each needs its own small
  feasibility check before being locked in as a real perk.
- Archetype 8 (Working With Animals) remains genuinely open — no concept proposed, pending either richer
  animal-population lore or a decision to leave this archetype without a perk entirely.
- Naming: none of these concept names are final. Several (particularly 9 and 10) don't even have a working
  name yet, being dialogue-only ideas rather than full perk concepts.
