# Quest and Choice Perks

**Category C of the perk framework** (`Perk_Framework.md`) — perks earned by completing a quest or making a
specific significant choice within one, rather than through MACHINE-stat/skill investment. Some are
straightforward completion rewards; others are mutually exclusive branches awarded based on which option the
player chose. Target quantity: ~50–70. This file didn't exist before 2026-07-26 — first entries below.

**Marked for future review, same as every other perk file:** provisional, not locked in.

**Relationship to `Regular_Perks_-_Level-Up.md`:** that file already contains two quest-gated perks of its
own (Sonic Attunement, Golden Eye Calibration) predating this file's creation — not yet migrated here, left
in place for now. Future consolidation is a reasonable option, not done as part of this file's creation.

## Faith & Belief

**Added 2026-07-26.** A new perk category, grown out of the developer's own request to brainstorm further
perk categories beyond the existing eight non-combat/four combat groups in `Regular_Perks_-_Level-Up.md`.
Grounded specifically in the two robot religions with real, developed lore (Cymatics Reverence,
Polydimensional Animism) plus The Eyes of Gold's established rite-of-passage — **deliberately not** extended
to the religions still in active research (Gates Adinkras, Ice-Cold Buddhism, God-mind Universe Simulation),
since there's no real belief content yet to ground a perk in. All six are quest-gated by explicit developer
instruction: no stat or skill threshold, completion of the relevant questline is the gate itself, matching
the existing Sonic Attunement/Golden Eye Calibration pattern.

| Perk | Requirements | Ranks | Effect |
|------|-------------|-------|--------|
| **Resonant Witness** | Quest-gated | 1 | Perceive vibration/sound-based information the way a Cymaticist adherent does — structural stress, distant machinery activity, Arcanet hum — genuine perception, not just unlocked dialogue. Grounded directly in Cymatics Reverence's own established doctrine: Tepenian robots perceive sound and vibration across a meaningfully wider range than humans do, a structural sensory difference, not a trained sensitivity. |
| **Harmonograph Adept** | Quest-gated | 1 | Record a harmony as a permanent geometric trace, per Cymatics Reverence's real devotional practice (the harmonograph/tonoscope ritual, researched from Ernst Chladni, Hans Jenny, and Anthony Ashton's real-world work) — usable as a puzzle-solving or crafting angle wherever a "kept record of a pattern" matters. |
| **Cross-Section Sight** | Quest-gated | 1 | See more of a "fragment" than others can — a NODE-adjacent perception bonus themed directly on Polydimensional Animism's own core premise: higher-dimensional beings are real, but perceived only as partial cross-sections, the same relationship a two-dimensional being would have to a three-dimensional one. |
| **Denominational Fluency** | Quest-gated | 1 | Standing and dialogue access across all five Polydimensional Animist denominations at once (none doctrinally senior to another), rather than allegiance to a single one. |
| **Gilded Sight** | Quest-gated | 1 | A unique Investigation-flavored perception granted by undergoing the Eyes of Gold's gold ocular implant rite-of-passage (engineered by Ji-Eun Kim) — not purchasable through stat investment at any threshold. |
| **Faithful Skeptic** | Quest-gated | 1 | Full standing with multiple robot religions simultaneously, without needing to commit to just one — a neutral-path option for a player exploring more than one faith's content, mirroring the "hidden path" design pattern already used for the Independent Lattice. |

**Design note:** deliberately the first entries in this category and in this file. As Gates Adinkras,
Ice-Cold Buddhism, and God-mind Universe Simulation get real theological content written, this category has
obvious room to grow — each is a strong future candidate for its own quest-gated perk once there's belief
content to ground one in, the same way Cymatics Reverence and Polydimensional Animism did here.
