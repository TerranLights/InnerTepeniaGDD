# Wild Wasteland (Trait)

**What this is:** a design draft, not a locked decision — written 2026-07-28. Ports Fallout: New Vegas's
**Wild Wasteland** trait, confirmed against the real mechanic: unlike every other FNV trait, it carries **zero
stat effect**. It's purely a content-flag — taking it swaps in and/or adds roughly 15 special encounters and
references throughout the game (real-world pop-culture nods: Star Wars, Monty Python, Indiana Jones, a
detective named after David Caruso, alien encounters), each signaled by a distinctive audiovisual cue (a
theremin sting, an on-screen "..." message, the Vault Boy shown with spiral eyes). Some of these encounters
*replace* a "straight"/serious version of existing content; others are pure additions with nothing removed.

---

## Confirmed for Inner Tepenia, 2026-07-28

**Tone — a genuine mix, not one lane:** content ranges from **in-universe absurdism** (bizarre Antarctic
cryptids, a glitchy AI with an oddly specific personality quirk, an absurd historical footnote nobody can
explain, a robot who insists it's something it isn't) to **real-world meta references** breaking the fourth
wall a little, the same way FNV's own gags do. Neither lane is favored over the other — mirrors FNV's own
actual variety, which wasn't purely one register either.

**Swap vs. addition — also a genuine mix:** some Wild Wasteland moments *replace* a straight/serious version
of an encounter (a real trade-off — taking the trait costs you the "sane" version), others are pure bonus
additions with nothing removed. Matches FNV's own real design exactly rather than picking one behavior.

**The audiovisual "tell":** Inner Tepenia adopts FNV's own underlying *design principle* — every Wild
Wasteland moment needs a consistent, recognizable audiovisual cue so the player knows "that was one of
those." The exact implementation (whether it reuses/adapts the glitch motif already established in
`Locations-and-Levels/Concordia-City/World_Map_Boundaries.md`'s map-boundary mechanic, or something
purpose-built for this trait specifically) is not yet decided — a detail to work out during actual content
design, not upfront.

**Quantity and placement — deliberately not fixed in advance.** No target encounter count, and no rule about
concentrating in thematically "weird" districts (Pisces, Sagittarius, Aquarius) versus spreading evenly
across all districts/DLCs the way FNV's own ~15 encounters are scattered fairly evenly across its map.
**Confirmed 2026-07-28: these decisions get made organically, one encounter at a time, as actual content
design work reaches each location** — not planned out as a fixed roster upfront.
