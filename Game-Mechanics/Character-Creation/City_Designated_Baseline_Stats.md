# City-Designated Baseline Stats

**Established 2026-08-16.** Certain Tepenian cities confer a **minimum floor** on specific MACHINE stats for
any character — Doll or human — confirmed to originate from that city. A character from a listed city can
exceed the floor freely; they can never fall below it on the stats that city designates. This is a floor, not
a fixed value and not a full stat block — everything about a character not covered by their origin city's own
floors is designed normally.

**Status: partial list, explicitly expandable.** The cities below are the ones currently identified. Every
other city plausibly has its own baseline floors too — they simply haven't been worked out yet. Absence from
this list means "not yet determined," never "this city confers no floor."

## Scope

**NPC/Doll rule only — does not apply to player character creation.** No new exception needed here: non-player
characters are already exempt from the player's 40-point budget, per `MACHINE_Stats.md`'s own established
player-vs-NPC rule (the same rule Ulysses' all-10s SPECIAL stats illustrate there). These city floors are just
a specific case of that existing, already-settled exemption. A player character's own chosen origin city, if
any of these, is flavor only and confers no mechanical floor.
